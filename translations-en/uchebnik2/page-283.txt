When you complete such a course, you will be great before God, and you will receive great blessedness and glory from Him in heaven and on earth; and you will stand with the holy martyrs of old; and in this present generation you will be called newly revealed martyrs of Christ.” Ivan instructed them from the Holy Gospel and from other books, and then blessed them for the feat. As provision for the journey he gave them Holy Communion of the Most Pure Flesh and Life-giving Blood of Christ our God. When they had partaken, the priest Ivan sent forth the two valiant warriors to fight and repel the enemy’s assault on the Holy Church. And the saints went into their homes, took from their property what they needed for the road, and peacefully said to their household: “The Lord be with you,” and then set out on the journey. Of their own will they hastened to the feat, each pondering in his heart: “As the hart longs for the springs of water, so my soul longs for You, O God. My soul thirsts for the mighty and living God; when shall I come and appear before You?” And they rushed like a lion upon a deer, and like hawks upon sparrows.

They passed through many lands; and this wondrous pair appeared in the Horde, and they reported them to the impious Tsar Batu, saying: “The great prince Michael of Chernigov has come to bow to you.” Not without cause was the man of Chernigov summoned. When the crafty Batu saw that by his deception he was darkening the brightest souls of the pious and surrounding them with fear, he rejoiced, the madman, that his design was coming true. And then, by God’s inscrutable providence, there appeared this God-sent radiant and great prince Michael, like the last small spark of divine understanding buried under ashes. He kindled a great fire of Orthodox faith, which enlightened all the faithful, darkened the shameless Tatar faces, obscured their godless decree, and burned up their impiety.

The lawless Batu summoned his priests, who knew how to work charms, and commanded them to arrange that the great prince Michael be led by them through fire, and bow to a bush and the sun, and come to him with honor.

The priests came to Prince Michael and said to him: “Tsar Batu summons you.”
