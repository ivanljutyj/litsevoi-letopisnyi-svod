And they came to the city with an army, bringing with them also the Muscovite host of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich. But by God’s intercession they did not capture any cities; they turned back, having conquered only the surrounding countryside and villages, though many people were led away captive. And the Muscovite army, like the Volotsk army, conquered all the Tver volosts and villages along the banks of the Volga and the church domains of the Holy Savior of the Tver episcopate, and took captives, burned, and laid waste everything.

In that same autumn, on the 27th day of October, Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver returned from Lithuania with his army and took captive the princesses—the wives of Princes Eremey and Vasily—and all their boyars, as well as the boyars and all the servants of his uncle Vasily Mikhailovich, and went with his own and the Lithuanian armies to the city of Kashin. The ambassadors of his uncle Prince Vasily Mikhailovich and of their lord Vasily of Tver met him in the village of Andreevskoe, and there the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God calmed his wrath, softened his heart, and granted him a good thought. And so he made peace and love in that place with his uncle Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, and also with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow, and peace and love came among them, and he sent them away with honors. And their boyars and all the nobles rejoiced, as did foreign merchants and merchants and hired workers, the clans and tribes of Adam. For all of them are one race and tribe of Adam. But kings and princes, boyars and nobles, foreign merchants and merchants, craftsmen and hired workers forgot that all of them are one race and tribe of Adam, and, having forgotten themselves, they are at enmity with one another, hate, gnaw, and bite one another, departing from God’s commandments, forgetting that one should love one’s neighbor as oneself.

And Prince Eremey made peace with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich; Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich concluded peace with him and released his princess to him.

And in winter, at Epiphany, Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich of Kashin came from his father Vasily Mikhailovich to make peace with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich.

Likewise Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver made peace with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow.

In the same year Prince Eremey renounced the terms of the peace agreement with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver and went to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich in Moscow.
