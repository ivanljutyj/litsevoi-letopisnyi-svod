And the Novgorodians, according to their custom, held a veche and elected three men: Ivan, abbot of Khutyn, Afanasy, abbot of the Nativity, and Partheny, abbot of the Annunciation; and they elected them on Ascension Day, the 7th day of the month of May, until the metropolitan should come from Tsargrad. For at that time Pimen was metropolitan in Tsargrad, summoned by the patriarch.

That same summer, before St Peter’s Day, the Tatars gathered and came unexpectedly in a raid upon Ryazan and ravaged it, and also ravaged Lyubutsk, and nearly took Prince Oleg captive.

In the same year Tsar Aksak Temir came and took Arnach from Takhtamysh, tsar of the Volga.

In the same year Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod sent his son Prince Ivan to the Horde.

In the same year Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Suzdal came from the Horde, granted by Tsar Takhtamysh: the tsar gave him Gorodets.

In the same year Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Suzdal and Gorodets, and his brother Prince Semyon Dmitrievich of Suzdal, gathered many troops from their patrimony, Suzdal and Gorodets, and moreover asked Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow for aid, the Zvenigorod and Volotsk forces.

And they went against their uncle, Prince Boris Konstantinovich, to Nizhny Novgorod; and having come, they stood by the city for 8 days; and then they made peace with their uncle, Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich.

And their uncle said to them: “My dear nephews, now I weep because of you, and afterward you too will weep because of your enemies.” And for their sake he renounced his Novgorod volosts, and they renounced his appanages; and so they made peace among themselves.

In the same year there was a great flood in the rivers from heavy rains. That same summer after St Peter’s Day, on the 6th day of the month of July, Metropolitan Pimen came to Rus from Tsargrad, but not to Kiev, only to Moscow; and he came without permission, because in Kiev was Metropolitan Cyprian, and in Moscow Metropolitan Pimen.

That same summer, on the 15th day of the month of August, on the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, in Vladimir Pimen, Metropolitan of Moscow, consecrated Theognast as bishop of Ryazan, and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich was there also. In the same year Gerasim, bishop of Kolomna, reposed.

In the same year the Novgorod posadniks and tysyatskys made civil strife among themselves, and three ends rose against the posadnik Osif Zakharyin, and struck the veche bell at Holy Sophia, and went to the Court in armor with weapons.
