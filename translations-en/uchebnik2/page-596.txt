In the same year the posadnik Bogdan Avvakumovich built a stone church in the name of Saint Simeon Stylites, and it was consecrated on his feast day.

In the year 6901 from Adam (the year 1401 from Christ)

On the first day of September, before early dawn, there was an eclipse of the moon. That same autumn, on the fifth day of September, Tsarevich Azi-baba was killed by Tsar Tokhtamysh in the Horde.

That same autumn there was a battle between Tsar Tokhtamysh and Tsar Aksak-Temir, and Tsar Tokhtamysh was defeated, fled himself, and his army was cut down.

In the same year the Germans came in arms to Vilna and wrought much evil.

That same autumn Tsar Tokhtamysh again seated himself in the Volga kingdom, and Tsar Aksak-Temir departed to his own land.

That same autumn, on the twenty-fourth day of November, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich came to Moscow from the Horde, from Tsar Tokhtamysh, where he had been received with honors and with a grant to the grand principality. And Nizhny Novgorod had already been given to him, and Gorodets with all its lands, and Meshchera, and Tarusa, and many other gifts; and he honored him, and there was great joy in the city of Moscow, and the people rejoiced and celebrated.

In the same year Danilo Feofanovich died, who for many years had served the grand prince in the Horde, in Rus, and in foreign lands. The monastic name given to him was also Danilo, and he was buried in the metropolitan monastery, near his uncle the blessed Alexis, Metropolitan of all Rus, by the monastery of the Miracle of the Holy Archangel Michael, in the center of the city of Moscow.

In the same year, on Easter Day itself after vespers, the Novotorzhians killed Maxim, a boyar of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.

Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich sent an army to Torzhok to seize the Novotorzhians, and, bringing them to Moscow, he put them to painful execution, but some escaped this fate.

In the same year the son of Amurat, Chelebiy Amiryatursky, seized the Bulgarian land and took their famous city of Tarnov. He captured their tsar, patriarch, metropolitan, and bishops, burned the relics of the saints, and turned the cathedral church into a mosque.

In the same year Prince Ivan died, brother of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and uncle of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich; in the holy angelic monastic rank the monastic name Asaph was given to him, and he was buried at the Holy Savior in the monastery, in the center of the city of Moscow.
