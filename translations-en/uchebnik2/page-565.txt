In the same year the Novgorodians went to a congress to make peace with the Germans. And, having failed to make peace, they dispersed.

On peace with the Novgorodians

The Novgorodians sent their envoys to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich: Yury Semenovich, grandson of Avramov, and Kiril Andrey anovich, and they concluded peace on the old terms. The grand prince sent his viceroy Ostafy Syta to Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians went with Prince Semyon Olgerdovich and an army against Pskov and halted at Soltsy. And there the Pskov envoys arrived and concluded peace, that the Pskovians would not intercede for debtors, bondsmen, slaves, or those who had gone to the Volga, but must hand them over.

Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich took Nizhny Novgorod, led away the princes with their princesses, and Prince Semyon Dmitrievich went to the Horde.

In the same year the Novgorodians founded the Monastery of St Nicholas on Skudelnitsa, at the end of Chudinova Street.

In the same year the Novgorodians built a stone Church of Constantine and Helena on Yaneva Street. Ivan, Archbishop of Novgorod, consecrated it. In the same year they built in Novgorod a stone Church of Constantine and Helena on Rostkina Street. In the same year there was a severe plague in Novgorod. It manifested itself thus: a swelling appeared, and after three days the person died.

In the year 6899 from Adam (the year 1399 from Christ)

The Novgorodians with their archbishop built a church in the name of St Athanasius in one day. Bishop Ivan consecrated it and celebrated the service, and by God's mercy the plague ceased in November.

That same autumn, during the Philip's Fast, the Tatars again came to make war on Ryazan.

That same autumn the boyars of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich: Alexander Pole, Alexander Veleut, and Selivan came from overseas, from the Germans, to Pskov and Novgorod with Princess Sophia Vitovtovna, daughter of Kestutievich, and with her Prince Ivan Olgimantovich.

After staying for some time at Gorodishche, they set out for Moscow. And they arrived with her in Moscow on the first day of December.
