At that time Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, heard that Mamai was encamped on the Voronezh within his Ryazan domains and intended to march against Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow.

And Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, sent his envoy to the impious Tsar Mamai with great honor and many gifts, and wrote many of his charters to him as follows: “To the free great Tsar of the East, Mamai. Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, installed by you and sworn to you, entreats you greatly. I have heard, lord, that you wish to go and frighten your servant, Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow. Now, all-radiant tsar, your time has come: there is much gold and wealth. And Prince Dmitry, a Christian, when he hears the name of your fury, will flee to distant places, either to Veliky Novgorod or to the Dvina, and then all Moscow’s wealth will be in your hands. But grant mercy to me, your servant Oleg of Ryazan. Furthermore, tsar, I beseech you: since both of us are your slaves, but I serve you with humility and submission, while he serves you with pride and disobedience, and I, your ulusman, have suffered many great wrongs from that Prince Dmitry; and moreover, tsar, not only that alone, but when I threatened him in your royal name for my wrong, he did not even care about that. He has also seized my city of Kolomna. And concerning all this, tsar, I pray and petition you that you punish him, so that he does not seize what is not his.”

After this, that Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, also sent to Grand Prince Yagailo Olgerdovich of Lithuania, saying: “I write to you of joyful things, Grand Prince Yagailo of Lithuania! I know that for a long time you have intended to drive out Prince Dmitry of Moscow and take possession of Moscow. Now our time has come, since the great Tsar Mamai is coming against him with many forces. Let us join him. But I have sent my envoy to him with great honor and many gifts. Do you also send your envoy, likewise with honor and gifts, and write him your message concerning what you know better than I.”

Yagailo, hearing this, was glad and warmly praised and thanked his friend Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, and sent his envoy to Tsar Mamai with great gifts and entreaty and petition, and wrote to him thus: “To the free great Tsar of the East, Mamai, Prince Yagailo of Lithuania, by your grace your sworn man, entreats you greatly and bows his head. I have heard, lord, that you wish to frighten your ulus, your servitor Prince Dmitry of Moscow. Therefore I beseech you, tsar, for I know that Prince Dmitry of Moscow does great wrong to your ulusman Prince Oleg of Ryazan, and also does me many injuries. Therefore both of us beseech you, all-radiant free tsar, that you punish him, so that he does not commit such injustice, and that you yourself bestir yourself, tsar, and, coming, see our humility and his pride, and then you will understand.
