The Lord’s imprint was stamped as though on wax at that very hour, and it is visible as though alive.

And on the right side of the great Mount of Olives is another mountain, the Galilean, where Christ commanded his disciples to see him after the Resurrection; there was a church there.

And from the city of Jerusalem, on the right side of the Mount of Olives, about five furlongs to Bethany, where our Lord Jesus Christ raised Lazarus. There is still there a great church, and the tomb of Lazarus, and the cells of Lazarus’s sisters. And in that church the service is Armenian; Armenians serve there. And to the south, near the city of Jerusalem, is the Pool of Siloam, where the Lord sent the blind man to wash. And near there is the cave of the great Anthony the Hermit. And near there is the Potter’s Field, which the Jews bought with the price of Christ. And further south from Jerusalem, about fifty furlongs away, is the Monastery of St Sabbas and the tomb of the great Sabbas. There were 14,000 monks; their cells can still be discerned today.

And beyond that Monastery of St Sabbas is Jehoshaphat’s ravine, which is also a valley. And many monks lived there as well.

And there is the cell of the great John of Damascus, in which he lived. And if one walks to the Monastery of St Sabbas, the distance is great. And on the right side is the monastery of the great Theodosius the Cenobiarch, six furlongs from the city of Jerusalem, on the mountain and also below the mountain. There were 16,000 monks there. And opposite from there, on the right side, is a monastery. There the great Eudocia lived. And from the city of Jerusalem eastward toward the Jordan, not far from Bethany, water flows from a rock. When Christ’s disciples asked him for water, Christ said to his disciples, “I will give you water from this rock,” and he struck the rock with his foot, and immediately water flowed, and it runs to this day. There too Keremen-Sarai stands, about five versts before the Jordan River; after passing behind the mountain, turn left for about three versts. And that mountain is very high, and on that mountain the devil tempted our Lord Jesus Christ: he took him up onto a very high mountain and showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory. And he said to him, “All this I will give you, if you fall down and worship me.” Then Jesus said to him, “Get behind me, Satan, for it is written: You shall worship the Lord your God, and him only shall you serve.” Then the devil left him, and behold, angels came and ministered to him. On that same mountain, below the summit, is a painted church, and on the summit of the mountain is another church, very large. On that mountain the Lord fasted forty days, as it is written: “Jesus was led up by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil, and after fasting forty days and forty nights, he was afterward hungry; and the tempter came to him and said,” and the rest, as written in the Gospel.
