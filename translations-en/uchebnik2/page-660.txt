To the local princes, with their princesses and children, I convey peace and blessing. Also to the God-loving bishops serving within the bounds of our church in the Russian metropolis, both those already departed and those still living, I convey my blessing, forgiveness, and love, and I ask the same of them. To the hieromonks and the entire priestly order, and to all who serve at the throne of the Lord, to all I convey forgiveness, blessing, and love. To the pious lesser and great princes, and to all who have already departed before, I also convey forgiveness and blessing, and I pray to the Lord God that He forgive them all their sins, by which, as mortal people, they have sinned. To noble and non-noble boyars, their wives, children, and the entire Christian people, I convey peace and blessing. To all monks living in various places, and to all the church clergy, I convey peace and blessing; and if I have laid a penance on anyone, or offended anyone by neglect, or by any other fault, or if I wished to forgive someone but he has already died, or if I taught someone and he disobeyed me, all of them in the Holy Spirit I forgive, absolve, and bless. I pray to the Lover of Mankind God to remit their transgressions, since when I counted my years that have passed since I was installed as metropolitan, I saw that the thirtieth year comes in the coming month of December on the 2nd day; and over so many past years, if anyone murmured against me, or renounced the episcopal, monastic, or priestly rank, or if any of the lay people joined with them, and those of them who repented and came to me to confess, to receive forgiveness and absolution of sins, are already forgiven and blessed from this moment. And may the Lord God not impute it to them as sin, but forgive them. And those who either are ashamed of me, or have forgotten, or are negligent, or fear me, or do so from poverty of mind, or because of the slander of enemies—anyone, priest or monk, or lay person male or female—anyone will be forgiven and blessed, and let it not be imputed to them as sin, since this is mine, and it is in my power to absolve it. Those who loved us and showed us mercy for the Lord’s sake in our misfortunes, journeys, and various countries—Christ-named people, nobles, men and women—they will be forgiven, blessed, and shown mercy by the Lover of Mankind God, and the Lord God will repay them according to their heart. And my soul and the house of the Holy Theotokos I entrust to my beloved in the Holy Spirit son, the great prince of all Rus, Vasily Dmitrievich: as he takes care, such reward will he receive from the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother Theotokos; also from his boyars, servants, and young people from small to great, as he grants favor and protects, so will he receive reward from the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother Theotokos. Peace, grace, and forgiveness from the Lord our God Jesus Christ be with all of you, and my blessing. This charter was written in the church of the Three Hierarchs, on the 12th day of the month of September, in the 15th indiction, and it is only not signed.
