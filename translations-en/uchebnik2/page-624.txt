When the end of his life drew near, he was at that time in Moscow with Metropolitan Cyprian on church affairs, and after being ill for several days he departed to God. He tended well the flock entrusted to him by God and fulfilled all the rules of the Church and the law: both in monasticism, in the priesthood, and in teaching. He was a bishop for 12 years, and he was like the holy apostles, and now with the hierarchs he stands before the throne of Christ, praying for his flock and for the whole world, with all the saints who have pleased God.

In the year 6905 from Adam (the year 1405 from Christ)

In the month of September the Turks waged war against the Greek land and did much evil.

That same autumn, in September, Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, grandson of Ivan, went with an army to Lyubech and returned home with a great number of captives.

In that same year the princess, wife of Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, daughter of Prince Vladimir Olgerdovich of Kiev, reposed, and was laid in the Church of the Holy Savior in Tver.
