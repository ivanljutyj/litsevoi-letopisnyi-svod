He wrote in the charter: “I, Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, accept from you the cross-kissing charter, and I remove the sin from you, and I bless you. And my son, the Grand Prince, accepts your petition and grants you peace according to the old custom. And you stand firm and unwavering in this, and I bless you.” And the Novgorodians sent 600 rubles to Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, in Moscow, and concluded peace according to the old custom, and great love came between them.

In the same year, Grand Princess Evdokia, widow of Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy, built in the center of the city of Moscow a stone church in the name of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God, in which there is a side-chapel of Saint Lazarus. And before that a wooden church had stood there.

In the same year, King Jagiello Olgerdovich, grandson of Gedimin, gave to his cousin brother Vitovt Kestutievich the Grand Principality of Lithuania.

And in the same year, Vitovt Kestutievich, grandson of Gedimin, sat upon the Grand Principality of Lithuania, and the Novgorodians concluded peace with him according to the old custom.

In the same year, Prince Konstantin Vasilievich of Beloozero came to Novgorod, and the Novgorodians received him with honor.

In the same year, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich went with an army to Nizhny Novgorod against Prince Vasily Dmitrievich Kirdyapa and his brother Semyon Dmitrievich.

On the Repose of Grand Prince Boris

Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod, Suzdal, and Gorodets reposed, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew.

And he was buried in his patrimony, in Suzdal, on the sixth day of May.

In the same year, Vladyka Arseny of Tver founded a monastery on the Tmaka River in the name of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, and a stone church, in which he was buried according to his command.

In the same monastery he built a heated church in the name of the holy fathers Anthony and Theodosius of the Kiev Caves.

In the same year, Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver, grandson of Michael, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, ordered the dilapidated wall of the city of Tver to be torn down, and in its place to build one of wooden beams, and in the next year it was built.

In the same year, in spring, there was a great flood. In the same year, they built a stone church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Veliky Novgorod on Lisya Hill, and another church, of Saint Demetrius.
