In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the venerable abbot Sergius founded a monastery on the Dubenka River at Stromyn, and placed in it a church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God, and brought as abbot from his own monastery of the Holy Trinity one named Leontius.

And the church was consecrated on the 1st day of the month of December; and he gathered monks and established the monastery with everything; and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich enriched and provided it with all necessities, to the glory of Christ God and of His Most Pure Mother the Mother of God, for the salvation of his soul and for the preservation of his realm.

In the same winter Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gathered many warriors and sent as an army his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and Prince Andrei Olgerdovich of Polotsk, and Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Volynsky, and other commanders, boyars, and many nobles, and sent them out on the 9th day of December, on Friday, against the Lithuanian land.

They, having gone, took the towns of Trubchevsk and Starodub, and many other regions, and laid waste the volosts, and returned to their own land with much wealth.

But Prince Dmitry Olgerdovich of Trubchevsk did not join battle, did not raise his hand against the grand prince, but with great humility came out of the city, with his princess, his children, and his boyars.

And he came to Moscow to negotiate with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and concluded an agreement with him, and gave an oath.

The grand prince confirmed his oath and agreement, and received him with great honor and much love, and gave him the town of Pereyaslavl with all its dues.

In the same winter the Vyatka men, having gone as an army into the Arsk land, killed the robber ushkuiniks, and captured their commander Ivan Ryazanets, son of Stanislav, and killed him.

That same summer Ivan Vasilyevich, the tysyatsky, set out from the Horde, and they, having deceived and outwitted him, seized him in Serpukhov and brought him to Moscow.

And on the 30th day of August, on the commemoration of the holy martyr Felix, on Tuesday before noon, at the 4th hour of the day, Ivan Vasilyevich, the tysyatsky, was killed, cut down with a sword on Kuchkovo Field by the city of Moscow, by command of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich. And there was a multitude of people standing by, and many shed tears for him, and grieved over his nobility and his greatness. For against many sons of men Satan from the beginning stretched out his wicked nets: and he instilled haughtiness, pride, and injustice.
