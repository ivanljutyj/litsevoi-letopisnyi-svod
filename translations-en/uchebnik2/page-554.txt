They cared for their salvation, with a clear conscience they concerned themselves with their earthly kingdom and prepared themselves for the heavenly. And like a helmsman who steers his ship against the wind, avoiding the waves, guided by the Highest Providence, and like a prophet standing guard over the divine building, so he cared for his kingdom. And the glory of his name multiplied, as once that of the holy great prince Vladimir. And the Russian land blossomed during his reign, as formerly the promised land of Israel. By the might of his authority he protected the whole Russian land. From east to west his name was glorified—from sea to sea, from the rivers to the ends of the universe his glory was extolled. Earthly kings, hearing of him, marveled, while his enemies living round about envied him. And they began to incite the impious Mamai against him, saying: “The great prince Dmitry of Moscow calls himself tsar of the Russian land and considers that he has surpassed you in glory and can withstand your kingdom.” Mamai, incited by crafty counselors who, though they held the Christian faith, did ungodly deeds, said to his princes and nobles: “I will seize the Russian land, destroy the Christian churches, and change their faith to my own, and I will command them to worship my Muhammad, and where churches were, I will set up mosques, and I will install basqaqs in all Russian cities, and I will kill the Russian princes.” As formerly Agat, king of Bashan, boasted against the ark of the Lord’s covenant in Shiloh. He boasted, and himself perished. And the godless Mamai sent first against Rus with war his accursed commander Begich with a vast army and with many princes of the Horde. Hearing of this, the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich came out to meet him with the great strength of the Russian land. And they fought the pagans in the Ryazan land, on the Vozha River. And God and the holy Theotokos helped the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and the impious Hagarenes were put to shame: some were slain, others fled. And the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich returned with a great victory. Thus he defended his patrimony, the Russian land, from the invasion of the impious. But the shameless Mamai was put to shame and instead of praise gained dishonor. Then he himself went against the Russian land, threatening the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich, having filled his heart with evil lawlessness. When the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich heard of this, sighing from the very depth of his heart, he turned to God and His Most Pure Mother: “O Most Pure Lady Theotokos, Sovereign Lady, intercessor and helper of the world, pray to your Son for me, a sinner, that I may worthily lay down my head and my life in the name of your Son and for you; I have no other helpers besides you, Lady! Let not my unrighteous enemies rejoice, and let not the impious say: ‘Where is their God, in whom they trusted,’—let all who bring evil upon your servants be put to shame.
