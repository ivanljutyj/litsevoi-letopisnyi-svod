They wept because they were being parted from the sweet love of their lord. Many guests from other lands who were there also wept, seeing such incomprehensible weeping among the people, and said to one another: “See how beloved he is to this city!” And they also said: “Truly blessed is the city of Tver, which for many years had such an adornment before its eyes!” The bishop and the whole cathedral clergy, in bright vestments with candles and censers, escorted him with all honors.

They brought his body and placed it in the cathedral church upon a bier, and the singing could not be heard because of the loud weeping. Thus, with abundant tears, they sang funeral hymns over him, gave the final kiss, blessed him according to custom, granted him remission of sins, and buried him in the great cathedral church of the Holy Savior on the right side. His noble son Ivan, seeing such unwavering love of the people for his father, wept and prayed to the Lord God to give him strength to follow in his father’s footsteps, and he asked for judgment and correction. With humble and gentle words he calmly gave consolation to all the people of Tver, and the sons of Tver were calmed. He conquered the hearts of all with sweet love for the noble one, and he became beloved by all for his good conduct.

In the year 6908 from Adam (1408 from Christ)

There was a solar sign in the heavens. That same autumn Prince Ivan Mikhailovich, grandson of Alexander, sent his envoys Fyodor Guslen and Constantine to Temir-Kutlui in the Horde, informing him of his father’s death and asking that he grant him his hereditary patrimony and his ulus—the great principality of Tver.

But in that same year Tsar Tumir-Kutlui died in the Horde, and after him Shadibek sat on the throne of the Great Volga Horde.

The envoys of Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver came to the Horde and gave gifts from Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver to Tsar Shadibek, to his tsaritsas, and to his princes. And he granted his ulus—the great principality of Tver—to Prince Ivan Mikhailovich by right of inheritance.

And Fyodor Guslen and Constantine came out of the Horde from Tsar Shadibek with honor, and with them the envoy Safryak, and they brought the yarlyk for the great principality of Tver to Prince Ivan Mikhailovich according to his patrimony and his ancestry.

That same autumn Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich of Tver came to an agreement with Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, and they came to Tver with their princesses and with their boyars, and concluded a peace agreement among themselves.
