In that year Smolensk also burned on the day of the Transfiguration.

In that same year Prince Semyon Ivanovich came out of the Horde, and with him his brother Prince Andrei Ivanovich, granted by the tsar to the great reign of Vladimir and Moscow. And he became Grand Prince of Vladimir and Moscow.

In that year evil traitors gathered at the Bryansk veche and killed their prince Gleb Svyatoslavovich. At that time Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, was also there, but could not restrain them.

In that year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich sent to Torzhok for tribute. His envoys, arriving there, began to commit offenses and acts of violence.

The Novotorzhtsy turned to Novgorod for help. The Novgorod posadniks sent boyars there with a large number of men. Arriving unexpectedly, they seized Prince Semyon’s governors: Prince Mikhail Davidovich, Ivan Rybkin, and Boris Semyonov, and others.

They took their wives and children into chains and remained for a month in Torzhok. And to the grand prince they sent from Novgorod Kozma Tverdislavich with these words: “You have not yet sat on the reign, and already your boyars show force.”

But the Novotorzhtsy were frightened, and the common people rose against the boyars, saying: “Why did we call the Novgorodians, when they have seized the grand prince’s governors? For this we shall all perish.” And the common people, having gathered, went to their courtyards, and freed Grand Prince Semyon’s governors from their chains, and, releasing them, escorted them out of Torzhok. And the Novgorod boyars fled to Novgorod. And the common people plundered the property of the Novotorzh boyars, and ruined their houses, and devastated their villages.

In that year there was an assembly in Moscow of all Russian princes. And they set out with an army to Torzhok: Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, Prince Konstantin of Suzdal, Prince Konstantin of Rostov, Prince Vasily of Yaroslavl. And the most reverend Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, went with them then. And they came to Torzhok with a great force.

And the Novgorodians, having gathered from all the land and taken counsel, sent with a petition Vladyka Vasily of Novgorod to Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and also sent posadnik Yakov and tysyatsky Avram with other boyars to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich. And they concluded peace according to the old ancestral charters, according to the will of all Novgorodians, and confirmed it by kissing the cross. And they allowed Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich to take the black bor from all the land of Novgorod, and imposed a tribute of a thousand rubles on the Novotorzhtsy.
