In the same year the Prince of Pskov, Dovmont, went to war by impassable ways against Viruem and seized their lands as far as the sea, and ravaged Pomorie, and returned with great plunder to Pskov.

In the same year Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Beloozero came from the Tatar land gravely ill, and was ill a long time, and again, by the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God, became healthy.

In the year 6777 from Adam (1277 from Christ)

Prince Dmitry Svyatoslavovich departed this life, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-forefather of Great Vladimir; and he took monastic vows, and the monastic rank of schema-monk, from Bishop Ignatius of Rostov. Prince Gleb Vasilkovich was there, and his mother sat with him; and suddenly his tongue went numb, and then he began to speak again; and Prince Dmitry looked at the bishop with joyful eyes, and said: “Master, father, lord Ignatius, the Lord God has fulfilled your labor, for you have prepared me for the long journey, to the Eternal Summer, as a warrior of the true King, Christ our God”; and after this the soul of Prince Dmitry departed to God quietly and meekly; and he was buried in the Church of Saint Michael in the Yuryev Monastery.

In the same year the Germans came in war to Pskov, on the Sunday of All Saints, and burned the settlement around the fortress, and stood ten days at the fortress, and did much harm; and they went to their own land, ravaging their cities.
