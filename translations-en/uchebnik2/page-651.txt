In that same year a bell was cast in Tver for the cathedral church of the Holy Transfiguration of the Lord by Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver.

In that same year, by God's mercy, the brothers made peace among themselves: Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver with his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, and they confirmed it by kissing the cross, and their enemies were put to shame. In that same year Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver married his son Ivan, and Bishop Arseny performed the wedding in Tver.

In that same year the Novgorodians made peace with Grand Prince Yury Svyatoslavich of Smolensk.

In that same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich released Semyon and Mikhail to Novgorod.

In that same year in Veliky Novgorod a stone church of the holy great martyr Barbara was built, and the Novgorod posadnik Kirill Andreevich built a stone church of the Three Youths with a side-chapel of the holy Archangel Michael on Prusskaya Street and a wooden church of the Holy Trinity at Vidogoshch in the coenobitic monastery in Veliky Novgorod. In that same winter in Veliky Novgorod people traveled across the Volkhov River on the ice from St Luke's Day until the month of March. In that same spring there was a drought, there was very little water, and all the ditches dried up.

In that same year in Veliky Novgorod the church of the Most Pure Mother of God on Lisya Gora was painted. In that same year Novgorod merchants built a stone church of the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb in Rusa.

In the year 6912 from Adam (1412 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich of Lithuania, grandson of Gediminas, together with the Olgerdovichs—with Koribut, Lugveny, Svidrigailo and the whole Lithuanian army—came to Smolensk.

And at that time Prince Alexander Ivanovich of Tver went to Vytautas and overtook Vytautas near Smolensk.

And Grand Prince Yury Svyatoslavich of Smolensk shut himself in the city with his Smolensk men and fought bravely.

Vytautas stood there all spring, making great efforts and firing cannons, but could not prevail, because Smolensk was well fortified. Vytautas stood for 7 weeks and departed to his own land, without doing harm to the city, but laying waste the environs of Smolensk.
