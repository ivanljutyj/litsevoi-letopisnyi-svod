And he went around everything as if on wings, leaving no one alive: neither rulers nor kings, but he killed and destroyed all.

And, boasting, he said: “Alexander of Macedon, as if in mockery, went around the whole earth, but few gifts were given to him and little homage was paid him.” And now Temir wished to go through the northern lands to the west, to attack India and Amazonia and all the ends of the universe, not knowing that when his spirit departs and he returns to the earth, on that same day all his thoughts will perish.

He returns to Persia, having captured Assyria and the kingdom of Babylon, Sebasteia and Armenia, and having taken captive all the hordes. Their names are: Khorasan, Gulistan, Shiraz, Isfahan, Ornach, Ginyan, Siz, Shebran, Sivas, Erzurum, Tiflis, Baghdad, the Blue Horde, which is near India, Great Sarai, Chagatai, Tabriz and Gorzustan, Obezi, Gurzi. And from there he went to Okhtai, and captured Shemakha too, and China and Crimea, and reached the Great Horde, and sowed millet for six months to feed such a multitude of his army, for there were more than four hundred thousand of them.

About Tokhtamysh
In the Great Horde, having conquered, he drove out King Tokhtamysh.

And he possessed many kingdoms and many lands. And they brought him gold and silver, and tribute, and obeyed him, fulfilling his will, and went with him everywhere to war, took many lands captive and reduced cities to desolation. He carried the Turkish king Bayezid with him in an iron cage.

About the capture of the city of Yelets

And, inflamed with fury and puffed up with pride, the accursed Temir dared to humiliate the Christian faith and the saints pleasing to God, the holy Russian wonderworkers, especially the great wonderworker among hierarchs, Peter. And he went against the Russian land, against the Christian flock, wishing to enslave it, like a second Batu, to whom before this, because of our sins, God permitted to enslave Christendom. Temir, seeing that no one resisted him, passed through the whole barbarian land, and, reaching the borders of the land of Ryazan, took the city of Yelets, captured the prince of Yelets, and tortured the people, for this persecutor of Christians was evil and fierce, cruel and merciless.

And, having planned not only to enslave but also to lay waste and reduce to desolation the Russian land, the tormentor boasted that he would go to Moscow, wishing to take it and raise a great persecution against Christians, to imprison, torment, burn and scorch, and to destroy holy places, like the ancient impious kings Maximian, Diocletian, Decius, and the apostate from God Julian. He was like them in his villainous nature.
