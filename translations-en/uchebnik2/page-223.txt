Rus (1225–1248 from the Birth of Christ). Book 5

In the year 6725 from Adam (A.D. 1225)

Grand Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich laid the foundation of the stone church of the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross in the square in Vladimir, on the 6th day of May; and in the same year it was completed; and it was consecrated on the 14th day of September.

In the same year the Bishop of Polotsk came to Vladimir to Grand Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich from Tsargrad; he knew the prince’s zeal and love for all things divine, for church building, for holy icons, for holy relics, and for all that is profitable to the soul and leads to Eternal Life; and the bishop brought the prince a certain portion of the Lord’s Passion, which for our sake the Lord God Jesus Christ suffered from the Jews.

Grand Prince Constantine rejoiced greatly and ordered the bishop to be lodged outside the city in the Monastery of the Ascension beyond the Golden Gate.

On the next day Grand Prince Constantine ordered the bishop, all the priests, the church servants, and all the people to go in procession with the cross from the cathedral church of the Most Holy Mother of God and from the church of Saint Demetrius; and he himself went with the princess, and with the children, and with the boyars, to the Monastery of the Holy Ascension.

The bishop took that sacred casket with the ineffable treasure upon his head, and all went into the city and placed it in the church of Saint Demetrius, singing and glorifying our Lord Jesus Christ. Then the bishop first kissed the holy relics, then Grand Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich, and likewise all the Orthodox.

In the morning the prince arranged a festival and celebration; it was then the Lord’s Day, and Saint Martyr Longinus was commemorated.
