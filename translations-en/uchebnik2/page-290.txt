Would he not submit to him, would he not be frightened by the sufferings of his prince, would he not wish to rule in the Chernigov region; but if he did not submit, he too would die in torments. And afterward Batu compelled others, and the accursed one fell into complete despair. Then he gave instructions to the executioner concerning the valiant counselor Theodore.

Then the pagans began to say to Theodore: “If only you would carry out the king’s command, go with the priests and bow to the sun, and the bush, and the fire; then you will not only remain alive, but will receive great glory from the king, and will stand before him, and enjoy his honor, and receive the domain of your lord, and be honored with authority, and be crowned to the grand-princely throne by the king’s hand; but if you do not do so, you will also suffer as your prince did.” But the valiant ascetic, with a most lucid soul and a clear voice, answered: “So tell the king, let him know that I, Theodore, was a like-minded counselor and companion in the authority and pious governance of my lord, the great prince Michael. I do not want Michael’s principality, nor the glory that the king promises me. And as in this life I was never without the mercies of my lord, so now I strive to follow also his honored suffering for Christ; I desire with joy to partake also of his glorious death, in which he received Eternal Life in the Heavenly Kingdom, and may Christ our God help me to reach it. But as for going with the priests and passing through the fire, and bowing to the bush and the sun—may this not be with me, nor with any Christian. I am a Christian, and I worship the Holy and Consubstantial Trinity. But you, do what you will.”

Having heard this sacred speech of the great Theodore, the foul Tatars went mad with rage, for they saw that he was inflexible, and like savage beasts they rushed upon him in fury; and with foul hands they seized him; and without mercy they tormented him just as the great prince; and they crushed his body; and finally they cut off his honored head. The pagans also said: “Those who do not wish to bow to the sun are unworthy even to look at it.” Thus they suffered and, giving thanks to Christ, gave their holy souls into the hands of God—the holy newly revealed confessors and martyrs—on the twentieth day of the month of September. Thus the valiant Theodore also finished his course and, following after his lord, the great prince Michael, ascended to Christ and received the same glory that he had desired.

Their holy bodies were thrown to the dogs to be devoured; and for many days they lay there; and by God’s grace their bodies remained unharmed. But God, the Lover of mankind, our Merciful Lord, glorified His saints who had suffered for Him and for the Orthodox Christian faith; and a pillar of fire appeared above their bodies, shining with most radiant beams.
