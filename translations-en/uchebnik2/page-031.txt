With the Lovetsk prince Emgazey and the Polovtsians he went against Prince Izyaslav Davydovich; and Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich prevailed.

Prince Izyaslav Davydovich fled to Izyaslav Mstislavich, Grand Prince of Kiev, and to his brothers; and they all went against Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich.

He fled also from that city of his, Karachev, with the Vyatichi. Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev took all his property and wealth, and distributed all his volosts and cities: to Prince Izyaslav Davydovich and his brother Vladimir Davydovich, and to one of their cousins, Prince Svyatoslav, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir; but whatever property and wealth belonged to Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich, all that Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev took for himself and sent to Kiev.

At that same time it happened that Prince Igor Olegovich, brother of Svyatoslav Olegovich, who was sitting, seized by Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, in Pereyaslavl, in the monastery of St John, in a stone undercell, under guard and in strict confinement, heard while there that Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, having gathered many troops, had gone against his brother, Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich, and was grieved almost to death, and grew faint, and, barely breathing, sent to Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, with tears and great weeping, saying: “Do me the kindness and order me to be tonsured into the monastic order—that I may not see the evil death of my brother, Grand Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich of Chernigov.”

Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev ordered His Grace Euthymius, Bishop of Pereyaslavl, to tonsure him into the monastic order. And coming, Bishop Euthymius tonsured him into the monastic order on the fifth day of January. And they brought him out of the prison in Pereyaslavl, and sent him from Pereyaslavl to Kiev, to the monastery of St Theodore, and there they placed the schema on him.

Prince Izyaslav Davydovich and his brother Vladimir Davydovich went after Prince Stanislav Olegovich to Bryansk, but his nephew, Prince Svyatoslav, son of Vsevolod Olegovich, remained in the city of Karachev.

Grand Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich went to Kozelsk, and from Kozelsk went to Dedoslavl, and there Prince Ivanko, son of Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh, fell ill.

Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich went to Ryazan, and was in Mtsensk and in Tula, and in Dubok on the Don, and in Yelets, and in Pronsk, and came to Ryazan on the Oka, and went up the Oka; and he remained in the city of Osetr, and there Prince Ivanko Berdladnik, son of Rostislav, grandson of Mstislav, deserted him,
