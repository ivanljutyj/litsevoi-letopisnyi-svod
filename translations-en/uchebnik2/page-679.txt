Christians gave much money in alms, honor, and gifts, and honored Luke as an apostle. But Luke was a simple man, descended from the humblest peasants, yet he possessed genuine virtue. And he returned to his region of Koloch with the miracle-working icon, having gathered much wealth.

And he built himself a house like a prince’s, established large and bright houses, and acquired many servants and young retainers in rich garments.

And on his table were many hearty and costly dishes and costly wines; he ate and drank together with those who served him.

And he rode out hunting with hawks, falcons, and gyrfalcons. He had many dogs and bears, and in this he amused himself.

Many miracles came from the icon of the Mother of God. Luke placed it in the church that he himself built on the place where the Koloch Monastery now stands. And sick people coming from everywhere received healing there.

Luke became cruel and shameless. When the hunters of Prince Andrei Dmitrievich, by the prince’s command, rode out hunting with hawks or falcons, he killed and robbed the falconers and took the hawks for himself. And this happened not once but continually. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich endured it. Sometimes he sent to him, but Luke answered the prince harshly and cruelly. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich bore everything with humility and patience. Then Luke began also to kill and rob Prince Andrei’s hunters, and he took bears with their cages for himself, amusing and consoling himself with this.

And Prince Andrei Dmitrievich had one bold hunter; he waited for the right time, caught a very vicious and fierce bear, and ordered it to be led not far from Luke’s courtyard. Luke saw the bear from his house, went out himself to the bear with his servants, and ordered Prince Andrei’s hunter to bring the bear into his courtyard. Prince Andrei’s hunter acted cunningly toward Luke and at that very moment released the bear upon him; Luke did not step aside, and the bear attacked Luke. They barely managed to take Luke away from the bear—only breath remained in his nostrils.

And at that same hour Prince Andrei Dmitrievich galloped up to Luke, and seeing Luke at his last breath, he said to him: “Why did you come to love demonic spectacles and dances and give yourself over to drunkenness? God glorified you with the miracle-working image of His Mother, the Mother of God, but you counted it as nothing and began to live a useless worldly life; therefore this has happened to you.” He began to weep and with tears begged him to take care of him. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich, using his enormous wealth, built on that place a monastery in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God, called Kolochskaya, and left the miracle-working icon of the Mother of God in it, where it works miracles to this day for all who come to it with faith.
