And at this time Prince Yagailo of Lithuania, who had come from the Lithuanian land with all the Lithuanian force to the pagan Mamai and the Tatars as aid, and to the harm of Christians, and God delivered us from him: he was a little late for the appointed time, by one day or less; but Prince Yagailo Olgerdovich and all his force only heard that there had been a battle between Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and Mamai, and that the Grand Prince had overcome, and Mamai had fled without waiting for anything.

And Prince Yagailo with all the Lithuanian force ran back with great speed, pursued by no one. For they did not see then either the Grand Prince, or his army, or his weapons; but they feared and trembled only at his name. After this, too, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich said to his brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich: “Let us go, brother, to our land of Zalesye, to the most glorious city of Moscow, and sit on our principality and on our patrimony and ancestral inheritance, and we have bought honor and glory for ourselves for future generations.” And they crossed over the Don River.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich set out through the Ryazan land. He was told that Prince Oleg of Ryazan had sent his force to Mamai as aid, and had himself destroyed the bridges over the rivers, and whoever rode home from the Don battle through his patrimony, the Ryazan land, whether boyars or servants, he ordered to be seized, robbed, and sent away naked. Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich wanted to send his army against Prince Oleg.

When Prince Oleg of Ryazan heard that the Grand Prince was coming, having defeated his enemies, he began to justify himself and weep, saying: “Woe to me, sinner and apostate from the Christian faith! How was I seduced? What was I thinking? I have joined the godless tsar!”

And he fled from his city of Ryazan and ran to Yagailo, Prince of Lithuania; and he came running to the Lithuanian border and stopped there, and said to his boyars: “I want to wait here for news: when the Grand Prince passes through my land and comes to his patrimony, then I will return home.” But his Ryazan boyars left Oleg and came to the Grand Prince and told him that Prince Oleg had abandoned his patrimony, the Ryazan land, and had fled himself with his princess, his children, and his boyars. And they begged him earnestly not to send an army against them, and they themselves petitioned him for an agreement and made an agreement with him. The Grand Prince listened to their petition and did not send an army against them, but placed his governors in the Ryazan principality and commanded all his army that if anyone went through the Ryazan land, no one should touch a single hair.
