In the year 6765 from Adam (1265 from Christ)

Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich of Vladimir, and Grand Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov, and Grand Prince Andrei Yaroslavovich of Suzdal went to the Horde with many gifts, and received honor from Ulavchii, and returned and took the seats that had come down to them from their fathers.

In the same year Prince Gleb Vasilkovich married in the Horde and came from the land of the khan, from the tsar, with great honor, and took the seat that had come to him from his father.

In the same winter census takers came from the Tatar land and registered the whole land of Suzdal, and Ryazan, and Murom, and appointed commanders over ten men, over a hundred, over a thousand, and over ten thousand; and having given all orders, the Tatars returned to the Horde. They did not, however, register the archimandrites, abbots, monks, priests, deacons, singers, and all church servants who stand before the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and are in the house of the Lord, and serve God’s churches.

In the year 6766 from Adam (1266 from Christ)

Grand Prince Oleg of Ryazan died, and before his death he became a monk and received the monastic rank of schema on the Wednesday of Holy Week; and he was laid in the Church of the Holy Savior on the eighth day of March, the day of the holy martyr Photinia the Samaritan. And after Oleg his son Roman began to reign on the grand-princely throne in Ryazan.

In the same year Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich of Vladimir, and Grand Prince Andrei Yaroslavovich of Suzdal, and Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov, and Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich of Tver went to the Horde, and showed honor to Ulavchii and to all the Horde princes and commanders; and they were sent back to Rus with great honor, and came in the same autumn, and each took the seat that had come to him from his father.

In that same autumn Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov and his brother Prince Gleb Vasilkovich with his princess came from the Horde to Rostov; and Prince Boris bowed to the Most Holy Mother of God and prayed before Her image; and his spiritual father Kirill and his mother Grand Princess Maria blessed him.

In the same year census takers came from the Tatar land to Vladimir.
