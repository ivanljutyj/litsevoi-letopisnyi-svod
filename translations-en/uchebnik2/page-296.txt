And Vladislav came with his nobles and with all his troops and occupied Batu’s camps. The Tatars were returning in bands to their camps, not knowing what had happened; but the Ugrians took their plunder from them and killed the Tatars themselves; and they took all the countless wealth of the Tatars. Those Tatars who wished to accept the Christian faith were left alive. Thus the Lord God, by His invisible power, destroyed the impious tsar and all his countless army, and what was written was fulfilled: “Vengeance is Mine, I will repay, says the Lord.”

A monument to King Vladislav was cast in copper, showing him riding on a horse and holding in his hand the battle-axe with which he killed Tsar Batu, and with him also his sister, by the help of the invisible power of God. And the monument was set upon the pillar that stands in the middle of the city, so that later generations might see and remember; and so it stands even to our days. And here the tale of Batu has now come to an end.

In that same year Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov married, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh; he married the daughter of Prince Yaroslav of Murom.

In that same year Prince Michael, nicknamed Khorobrit, son of Yaroslav, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, drove his uncle, Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, from the grand-princely throne of Vladimir, and himself began to reign on the grand-princely throne in Vladimir.

In that same year Grand Prince Michael Yaroslavich Khorobrit of Vladimir went to war against the Lithuanians, and the Lithuanians killed Grand Prince Michael Yaroslavich of Vladimir, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.

Blessed Cyril, Bishop of Rostov, sent to take the body of Prince Michael; and they laid him in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Vladimir.

The brothers of Prince Michael, the princes of Suzdal, went against the Lithuanians and defeated them at Zubtsov.

In the year 6757 from Adam (1257 years from Christ)

Prince Gleb Vasilkovich, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, went to the Horde to Batu’s son, to Tsar Sartak; and Tsar Sartak honored Prince Gleb greatly and sent him back to the land that had come to Gleb from his father.
