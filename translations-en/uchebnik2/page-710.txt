In the same year Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich left Veliky Novgorod for Moscow; he had been in Novgorod because his brother, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, wished to subject him to his son Prince Vasily. Prince Konstantin, not wishing to submit to the authority of his nephew, had gone to Novgorod. And Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, his brother, took away his entire patrimony, seized his boyars, took their villages and the property they had acquired, and put them in iron fetters.

In the same year, on the 15th day of the month of June, Vladyka Simeon, Archbishop of Novgorod, reposed, after being on the archiepiscopal throne five years and three months, and six years in all less two months and four days.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, grandson of Andrei and great-grandson of Ivan, came from Lithuania to Moscow.

In the same year there was civil war and bloodshed in Veliky Novgorod: two ends, the Nerevsky and the Slovensky, rose against Klementy Artemyevich; all the people were clad in armor. And they plundered his house, as well as the houses of other boyars, and many people were killed. Twenty men belonging to Andrei were killed, and some drowned in the river. Among the Nerevians fifteen men were killed; after that all calmed down. In the same year there was famine in Novgorod.

In the year 6930 from Adam (1430 from Christ)

In September Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver captured the Novotorzhsky boyar Ivan Kumgan and his son Foma and imprisoned them in Novy Gorodok in Tver.

In the same autumn two stone churches were built in Novgorod: Isaac Akinfovich built the Church of the Epiphany of the Lord in the monastery on the Field, and Vasily Filippovich and Lukyan Ontsifirovich built the Church of the Holy Transfiguration of the Lord.

In the same autumn, on the 8th day of the month of September, a disease accompanied by convulsions began among the people; there was famine all winter.

In the same autumn, on the 30th day of the month of September, the Novgorodians summoned to the archiepiscopal court Feodosy, the Trinity abbot from Klopsk, chosen by lot. In the same autumn the Novgorodians built a great bridge in Novgorod.

In the same autumn Princess Anastasia, wife of Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, was with her father in Moscow; she returned from Moscow a week before the Nativity of Christ.
