In the same year Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin came to an agreement with his brother Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, came from Pereyaslavl to Tver, and they concluded a peace treaty and kissed the precious cross, swearing that they would remain in peace and love.

In the same year the Church of the Holy Archangel Michael was painted in Tver. In the same year, on the eve of the Fast of Saints Peter and Paul, on Tuesday before early dawn, the moon disappeared and, not having reached full strength, set.

In the same year, during the Fast of Saints Peter and Paul, on the sixteenth day of July, on Wednesday, at the fourth hour of the day, there was an eclipse in the sky: the sun vanished, and only a small part of it remained, the size of a three-day-old moon; it became very gloomy and dark, but after an hour the sun shone again. And in those days the Lithuanians fought the Muscovites in their own land and defeated them.

In the same year, on St Peter’s Day, in the Novgorod land, in the region of Nizhny Novgorod, there was a strong storm and a terrible wind; and at that time and in that hour a certain man went out into a field, mounted a horse harnessed to a cart, and the wind rushed upon him, caught up the man together with the horse and the cart, and carried him through the sky amid the storm until he became invisible.

And then, on the next day, they barely found his cart hanging together with its wheels and everything else at the very top of a tree on the other bank of the Volga; and they found the horse lying dead on the ground, but the man vanished without a trace, and nowhere could they find him.

In the same year there were terrible thunderstorms with lightning. In the same year, on the twenty-sixth day of August, the Most Reverend Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, at Golyanishche consecrated Hilarion, Archimandrite of Simonov, as bishop of Kolomna.

In the same year Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, at Golyanishche appointed Mitrofan as bishop of Suzdal. At that time Metropolitan Cyprian was already ill.

In the year 6915 from Adam (1415 from Christ)

In September Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow gathered an army and went against Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich of Lithuania.

And having come, they halted on the Plava. And Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver sent to the aid of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow his brothers, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin and Prince Feodor Mikhailovich, his son Prince Ivan, Prince Ivan Eremeevich, and with him his boyars and voivodes, and their regiment became very great.
