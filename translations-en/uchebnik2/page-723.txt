Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich sent after them his uncles, Princes Andrei and Konstantin Dmitrievich, and with them Ivan Dmitrievich with his regiments; they reached Nizhny Novgorod and from there, not having overtaken the Tatars, returned back.

Prince Feodor Starodubsky Pestroy and Fedor Konstantinovich Dobrynsky, concealing themselves from the princes and commanders, set out after the Tatars with their regiments and caught up with their rearmost warriors: some they scattered and others they killed, but they did not overtake the tsarevich and the prince. In the same year in Great Novgorod the stone Church of the Holy Fathers at the Prince’s Court collapsed.

In the year 6938 from Adam (1438 from Christ)

In Smolensk there appeared a naked wolf, without hair, and it ate many people; and in Lithuania, in the city of Troki, Lake Zhidovskoe for three days was filled as though with blood.

In that same autumn, on the first day of the month of November, Evfimy, Archbishop of Novgorod, reposed. He remained in the episcopal rank five years and five weeks, and after being named bishop he remained at the lord’s court a year and two weeks; in the same month of November, by lot, the hieromonk Evfimy from Lisya Gorka was installed at the lord’s court of Holy Sophia.

In the same year Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, was in Troki with Vytautas, and then to Vytautas gathered the rulers of all lands: his grandson Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich of Moscow, King Jogaila Olgerdovich of Poland, Grand Prince Boris Alexandrovich of Tver, the Grand Prince of Mazovia, a papal cardinal from Rome, the Grand Prince of Vatma, the King of Bohemia, the Wallachian voivode Ilyash, the German voivode Kunshter, the Master of Prussia and Germany, and many other princes and voivodes. And Vytautas went with all his guests from Troki to Vilna, and there he showed them great honors over many days, and bestowed many gifts on them, and so dismissed them; and from there all dispersed, each to his own lands. But Photius, Metropolitan of all Rus, he kept with him and detained after that for another eleven days, then, having shown him honor, released him to Moscow.

Metropolitan Photius came to Novgorodok of Lithuania (Novogrudok), and there news came to him that Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich of Lithuania had died on the twenty-fourth day of the month of October, and after his departure he lived only three days; he had reigned thirty-eight years.
