In the same year the Olgovichi defeated the Lithuanians and killed 1,700 of them.

Hearing what was happening in the Russian land, Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich was greatly grieved by the fate of his kinsman by marriage and his children and wished to take revenge, but he postponed his revenge for the sake of Christians.

And he sent to the Grand Prince of Galich, Roman Mstislavich, asking him to release his son-in-law Rostislav with his brother Vladimir Rurikovich.

Roman, having heeded Grand Prince Vsevolod, released his son-in-law Rostislav and his brother Vladimir; and Grand Prince Vsevolod placed his son-in-law Rostislav on the throne in Kiev.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Galich, Roman Mstislavich, sent envoys to Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich with peace and love, saying: “I have released your son-in-law Rostislav Rurikovich, and I have given him peace and love, and now he rules on the throne of the great reign of his father in Kiev; for the sake of your love I hindered him in nothing, but assisted him and gave him help.”

And so great stillness, peace, and love came between them.

In the year 6712 from Adam (1212 from Christ)

Grand Prince Oleg of Chernigov, son of Svyatoslav, reposed. In the same year there were signs in the heavens, in the sun and in the moon.

On the Capture of God-Guarded Constantinople by the Latins.
In the year 6712 from Adam (1212 from Christ)

Alexios reigned in Constantinople, in the reign of his brother Isaac, for he had blinded Isaac and himself became emperor there, and he shut up Isaac’s son Alexios in a high fortress under guard, so that he might not go free.

And after some time Isaac dared to ask for his son, that he be released from the fortress, and Isaac entreated his brother and swore for his son that he would not think of the throne; and he was released from the fortress and went everywhere he wished, while Emperor Alexios was anxious about him, trusting his brother Isaac and his son, having received a promise from them.

But afterward Isaac wished to regain the throne and began to instruct his son, saying to him secretly: “I did good to my brother Alexios; I redeemed him from the infidels, and he repaid me with evil: he blinded me and took my throne for himself.” And his son wished, as he had been instructed, to leave the city for distant lands and there seek a throne for himself.
