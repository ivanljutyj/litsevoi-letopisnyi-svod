And Rurik kissed the cross to Grand Prince Vsevolod and to his son Konstantin, and to his brothers, and to Roman. And Roman said to him: “As for the oath you have already sworn on the cross, convey it to your matchmaker Grand Prince Vsevolod, and I will send to him also, to my father and lord, and we shall ask him to give Kiev back to you”; and having come to agreement with Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, they again installed Prince Rurik Rostislavich in the grand principality in Kiev.

In the year 6711 from Adam (the year 1211 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, and Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich, together with the Chernigov princes the Olegoviches, agreed and concluded a peace treaty among themselves, and on the cross swore an oath to remain in unity.

And Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich sent his boyar Mikhail Borisovich to bring the Olegoviches to the kissing of the cross.

Likewise the Olegoviches sent their boyars to Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, and brought him to the kissing of the cross; they also brought Roman Mstislavich of Galich to the kissing of the cross, and Prince Roman Mstislavich brought them to the kissing of the cross, and so peace and quiet came, but not for long.

That same winter there was a sign in the sky: three suns appeared in the east, and a fourth in the west, and in the middle of the heavens something like a great moon, resembling an arc, and this sign lasted from morning until noon; all the people looked and marveled.

On the war with the Polovtsians, and how Roman Mstislavich tonsured his father-in-law Rurik

In that same winter Grand Prince Rurik Rostislavich of Kiev and his son-in-law Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich, and Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, Grand Prince of Pereyaslavl, and other princes went to war against the Polovtsians; the winter was fierce then, and the Polovtsians were in great sorrow. And the Russian princes seized their tents and herds, and many captives, and returned home.
