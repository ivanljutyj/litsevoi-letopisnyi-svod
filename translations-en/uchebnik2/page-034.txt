And they kissed one another in love in Christ, and wept much over the misfortunes and calamities that had befallen them, and were comforted again, and honored each other with love, and having exchanged pleasant gifts, parted; and Svyatoslav Olegovich went to Nerilsk.

In the same year Prince David Svyatoslavich reposed in Ryazan, and after him his brother, Igor Svyatoslavich, sat on the great principality in Ryazan. In the same year the great prince of Kiev Izyaslav, son of Mstislav, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, consulted with his Russian bishops, that because of rebellion and many disturbances it was impossible to go to Constantinople to the patriarch to be appointed metropolitan for Rus. And then, having taken counsel, in accordance with the sacred apostolic rule which says: two or three bishops may ordain one bishop, and so, having assembled, six bishops themselves consecrated a metropolitan for Kiev and all Rus — the monk Clement of Smolensk, bringing him out of his silent cell.

For he was above all a lover of silence and withdrew from all, and devoted himself to prayer and the reading of divine writings, and was a schema-monk, and very learned in books and a teacher, and a great philosopher, and left many books written by him; and he was installed in Kiev on the 27th day of the month of July, on the memory of the holy great martyr Panteleimon.

Many were indignant at this, both among the bishops and among the other priests, and among the monks, and among the laity; but they were most indignant at the prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, saying that he had forced it to be done so without the blessing of the holy patriarch of Constantinople and the sacred council, although the law is that Russian metropolitans should be appointed by the holy patriarch of Constantinople and by the sacred council that resides in the church of Holy Sophia.

In the same year Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich made peace with the Polovtsians near the town of Voin in Ryazan; and the great prince Izyaslav Mstislavich went to Kiev to his throne, and the Polovtsians went to the Field, to their tents.

In the same year there was a sign in the sun; and that night there were thunders and lightnings, and very great winds. In the same year the Polovtsian prince Saimchyuga died. In the same year the great prince of Suzdal Yuri sent his son, Prince Gleb, to the great prince Svyatoslav Olegovich.

And Gleb, having come to Svyatoslav Olegovich, began to gather an army; the Polovtsians also came to Svyatoslav Olegovich.
