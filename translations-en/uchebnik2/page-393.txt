At that time Izborsk was hard pressed by the German host. And they sent Olgerd a request for help. But he did not want to march against the German force and said to them: “Sit in the city, do not surrender, and constantly join battle with them. And if there are no dissensions among you, they will do nothing to you. But if I go with all my force against their force, how many dead will fall! And who knows whose upper hand it will be? And if by God’s mercy ours is the upper hand, there will still be many killed, and what benefit is there in that? Therefore sit in the city and believe that they will do nothing to you.”

The Germans stood under the city for ten days and deprived the townspeople of water, but, driven by God’s wrath, they abandoned their camps and all their supplies, burned the wall-breaking engines, and fled to their own places, pursued by no one.

Then the Pskovians long entreated Grand Prince Olgerd to be baptized, wishing to install him as prince in Pskov. But he said to them: “I am already baptized, I am a Christian, and I do not wish to be baptized a second time, and I do not wish to sit as prince among you.” Then the Pskovians baptized his son Andrei in the cathedral church and installed him as prince in Pskov. And his father Olgerd Gediminovich and Keistut went to their own land. The Pskovians again made peace with the Novgorodians. In that year the Pskovians killed 300 Germans.

In that year, by command of Grand Prince Simeon, a stone church was built in the name of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God at Gorodishche, and Vasily, Archbishop of Novgorod, consecrated it. In that year there was a fire in Novgorod.

In that year the Novgorod posadnik Varfolomei Yuryevich died.

In that year Prince Yaroslav of Pronsk came from the Horde with the envoy Kindyak to the Ryazan principality. And he came with the Tatars to Pereslavl Ryazansky. And Prince Ivan Ivanovich Korotopol shut himself up in the city of Pereslavl and fought him all day, and at night fled from the city. The envoy Kindyak with the Tatars entered the city, and they did much evil to Christians: some they killed, and others they took captive. And Prince Yaroslav of Pronsk sat as prince in the city of Rostislavl Ryazansky.

In that year Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, went to the Horde to the new prince Janibek, son of Azbyak. And Prince Konstantin of Suzdal, Prince Konstantin of Tver, and Prince Konstantin of Rostov had come to the Horde before him. In that year Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, went to the Horde on affairs of the church clergy to the new tsar Janibek, son of Azbyak. In that year Grand Princess Ovdotya, widow of Prince Vasily Davydovich of Yaroslavl, died.

In that year Feodor, Bishop of Tver, died.
