The people of Kiev came to the Monastery of St Theodore to kill the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich, and they found him in the holy church, standing as was his custom at the liturgy at St Theodore’s; and they seized him in the church during the liturgy and led him out of the monastery. And Prince Vladimir Mstislavich met them at the monastery gates and said to them: “O lords and brothers of Kiev! Do not deal mercilessly with your lord, who was the Grand Prince of Kiev, Igor Olegovich, and has now taken the holy angelic form as a monk and schemamonk: if fear of sedition from him possesses you, let him be kept under guard.”

And taking the Prince-monk Igor, he covered him with his mantle, saying to the people of Kiev: “Lords and brothers of Kiev! Kill me, but spare him!” And he brought him into his mother’s courtyard. And the people of Kiev began to beat and push Prince Vladimir Mstislavich.

Prince Mikhalko, seeing this, leaped from his horse and began to help Prince Vladimir Mstislavich; and Prince Vladimir Mstislavich dragged the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich into his mother’s courtyard, and shut the gates, and brought him onto the gallery on the outer wall. And the people of Kiev beat Prince Mikhalko and tore from him his cross, his chains, and his golden grivnas.

And running to the courtyard of the Grand Princess, they broke down the gates, and entering the courtyard, they saw the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich in the entryway; and rushing in, they smashed the entryway, and dragged him down from it, and killed him.

And at that time Prince Mikhalko fled from them. They killed the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich, hooked a rope around his feet, and dragged him through the Babiy Market; then, coming to the Great Market by the marble church of the Most Pure Mother of God, they laid him on a cart and, bringing him to Podol, threw him down naked on the marketplace.

Many people walked past and wept, seeing their former lord, the Grand Prince of Kiev, now become a monk, lying naked and cast upon the marketplace. And there came from the Grand Prince of Kiev, Izyaslav Mstislavich, his tysyatsky Lazar, and from his brother Prince Vladimir Mstislavich his tysyatsky Roguilo, to the marketplace, and they took the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich, lying dead, naked, cast upon the marketplace; and they laid him in the Church of St Michael, in the Novgorod chapel. And on Saturday morning Clement, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, sent Abbot Ananias of the Monastery of St Theodore; and when the abbot came, he saw the Prince-monk Igor Olegovich lying naked and drenched in blood; and weeping, he said: “O cruel human vanity! What has he become like, and for what did he suffer? Alas, woe to us who live in this vain age! Where is love for one another? Where is mercy? Truly all has vanished and perished!”

And he clothed him in the garment befitting deceased monks, and they sang over him the customary hymns for the departed. And fear and trembling came upon all.
