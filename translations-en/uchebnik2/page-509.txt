And then the Grand Prince distributed much alms among churches and monasteries, and to the poor and the beggars, and he rejoiced together with his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, and with his brother, Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and with the surviving warriors.

And after this he dismissed them, and they went each to his own place. Also after this the Grand Prince went to the monastery of the Life-Giving Trinity in Radonezh to the venerable abbot Sergius, and prayed with tears to the Lord God, and to His Most Pure Mother, and to all His saints, and received a blessing from the venerable abbot Sergius, and said to him: “Father, through your holy prayers we have conquered the Ishmaelites.

And if your wondrous novice, the monk Peresvet-Alexander, had not killed the great Tatar champion, then indeed many would have had to drink the cup of death at his hands. Now, Father, by God’s permission, for our many sins a great multitude of Christian warriors has been slain by the Tatars. And you should sing memorial services and serve liturgies for all those slain.” And so it was; and he gave alms, and fed the venerable abbot Sergius and all his brethren. And again he returned to the city of Moscow. And he sat upon his patrimony and ancestral inheritance, upon the great principality, resting from many labors and great illnesses which he had endured for the Orthodox faith and for all Christendom. O great and strong zeal of your courage, Grand Prince Dmitry! How did you not fear and not dread to go beyond the Don, into the open field, against the great Tatar forces! And how did you yourself begin to fight before all others! And how did you cut down the Ishmaelites! But all this was by God’s help and mercy, and that of His Most Pure Mother, and of the great wonderworker Peter, and of all the saints, and by your parents’ prayer. O Lord Christ, do not be angry with us for our iniquities, and have mercy on us according to Your great mercy, and cast down our enemies! Most Pure Virgin, Mother of God, be merciful to us and pray to your Son and our God Jesus Christ, and calm and humble the barbarians, and their warfare, and the tumults of those who rise against us, and grant to us, your servants, stillness, and peace, and love.
