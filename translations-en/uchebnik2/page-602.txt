Thus this wonder-working image of the Lady Mother of God once came from Palestine to the reigning city, and from Tsargrad, in one ship with the merchant Pirogoshcha, it was brought to our Russian land, to the city of Kiev, to the pious great prince Georgy Vladimirovich Dolgoruky Monomakhovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-grandson of the equal-to-the-apostles Vladimir, who enlightened the whole Russian land with holy baptism.

And afterward that wonder-working image of the Lady Mother of God was placed, by the command of this pious great prince Georgy Vladimirovich, in Vyshgorod, in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God, in the Convent of the Maidens.

The Miracle of How the Clerics Saw the Icon of the Mother of God in the Air

In that monastery, in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God, where the wonder-working image of the Mother of God had been placed, it happened one day that when the clerics entered the church as usual, they saw that the holy icon of the Mother of God had come down from its place and stood in the middle of the church, in the air. Then they dared to place it in another place.

The Miracle of How the Icon of the Mother of God Turned Its Face Toward the Altar

And on another occasion they saw that wonder-working icon turned with its face toward the altar. And the clerics decided that the icon of the Holy Mother of God wished to stand in the altar. The priests took it, carried it into the altar, and placed it behind the holy table.

The Miracle of How the Icon of the Mother of God Stood by Itself in the Altar

And a third time they again saw that same holy icon of the Mother of God outside the holy table. And from it all sorts of miracles began to occur. They, however, were perplexed, not understanding where the Mother of God herself wished to choose a dwelling for herself.

The son of the great prince Yury Monomakhovich Dolgoruky, the great prince Andrey, called Bogolyubsky, conceived the desire to rule in the city of Rostov. And once he was speaking with the boyars about the holy icons, from which many miracles and various healings come to those who approach them with faith. They told him about this wonder-working icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, about how it had been brought from Tsargrad to Kiev and placed within the bounds of his domain, in Vyshgorod, in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God in the Convent of the Maidens, and about how the clerics of that monastery, entering the church, saw its wonder-working image standing in the air, and about how it stood by itself in the altar.
