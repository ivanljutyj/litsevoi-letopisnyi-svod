Christians gave much money in alms, in honor, and as gifts, and they honored Luka as an apostle. Luka was a simple man, descended from the lowliest peasants, but he possessed lawful virtue. And he returned to his own region of Koloch with the miracle-working icon, having gathered much wealth.

And he built himself a house like a prince’s, arranged large and bright buildings, and acquired many servants and youths in rich garments.

And on his table there were many hearty and costly dishes and costly wines; he ate and drank together with those who served him.

And he rode out hunting with hawks, falcons, and gyrfalcons. He had many dogs and bears, and in this he amused himself.

Many miracles occurred from the icon of the Mother of God. Luka placed it in the church that he himself had built on the place where the Koloch Monastery is now located. And the sick who came from everywhere received healing there.

Luka became cruel and shameless. When the hunters of Prince Andrei Dmitrievich, by the prince’s command, rode out hunting with hawks or falcons, he killed and robbed the falconers and took the hawks for himself. And this happened not once but constantly. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich endured it. Sometimes he sent to him, but Luka answered the prince harshly and cruelly. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich bore everything with humility and patience. Then Luka began also to kill and rob Prince Andrei’s hunters, and he took bears with their cages for himself, and in this he amused and comforted himself.

And Prince Andrei Dmitrievich had one bold hunter; he waited for the right time, caught a very vicious and savage bear, and ordered it to be led not far from Luka’s courtyard. Luka saw the bear from his house, went out himself to the bear with his servants, and ordered Prince Andrei’s hunter to bring the bear into his courtyard. Prince Andrei’s hunter acted cunningly toward Luka and at that very moment released the bear upon him; Luka did not step aside, and the bear attacked Luka. They barely managed to take Luka away from the bear—only breath remained in his nostrils.

And at that same hour Prince Andrei Dmitrievich rode up to Luka and, seeing Luka at his last breath, said to him: “Why did you love demonic spectacles and dances and give yourself over to drunkenness? How God glorified you with the miracle-working image of His Mother, the Theotokos, but you counted it as nothing and began to live a profitless worldly life; therefore this has happened to you.” And he began to weep and with tears begged him to care for him. Prince Andrei Dmitrievich, with his enormous wealth, built on that place a monastery in the name of the Most Pure Theotokos, called Koloch, and he left the miracle-working icon of the Mother of God in it, where it works miracles to this day for all who come to it with faith.
