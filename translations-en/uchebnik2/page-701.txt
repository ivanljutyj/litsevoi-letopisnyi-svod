In the same winter, on the 23rd day of the month of February, in Meatfare Week, Samson left Novgorod for Moscow to Metropolitan Photius for consecration as bishop of Novgorod, and with him came the Novgorod envoys: the posadnik Vasily Avvakumovich, the tysyatsky Vasily Osipovich, and Alexander Ignatyevich. And they arrived in Moscow on the 9th day of the month of March, on the commemoration of the holy martyrs, on the Sunday after the Council.

In the Week of the Veneration of the Cross, in the same month, on the 22nd day, on the commemoration of the holy father Basil, the Most Reverend Metropolitan Photius consecrated Samson as bishop of Novgorod and Pskov in the Church of the Holy Archangel Michael and gave him the name Simeon in place of Samson. At his consecration five hierarchs were present: first, Gregory of Rostov; second, Mitrofan of Suzdal; third, Antony of Tver; fourth, Timothy of Sarai; fifth, Isaac of Perm. The consecration took place in the presence of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich and his brothers Yuri and Konstantin. And the bishop returned to Novgorod on the 16th day of the month of April, on Great Thursday, and the whole sacred council of Veliky Novgorod met him with crosses, along with the posadnik Ivan Bogdanovich, the tysyatsky Boris Vasilyevich, and a great multitude of people; all rejoiced at the arrival of their new hierarch. And he entered the Church of Holy Sophia, served the liturgy, and sat upon his sacred episcopal throne, granted to him by God; great joy was throughout Novgorod. That same night Naum, the senior chorister of the Church of Holy Sophia, reposed.

In the same year the Tatars came to Kiev, plundered and burned the Pechersk Monastery.

In the same year the princes of Nizhny Novgorod came to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich in Moscow: Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, grandson of Dmitry; Prince Ivan Borisovich, and his son Prince Alexander had come two years before him.

In the same year Kostroma was founded.

In the same year, on the 3rd day of the month of May, Gregory, Archbishop of Rostov, reposed, having been on the archiepiscopal see in Rostov for 21 years, observing church laws and teaching the people the word of God. He was the second archbishop in Rostov; before that Rostov had bishops, not archbishops.

In the same year, in the month of August, the Tatars fought with Lithuania and returned with a great number of captives.
