In the same year, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver sent Grigorchuk and Korei to the Horde to the tsar with a complaint against his nephew, Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich. And there the tsar and tsaritsa, without trial, handed Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky over to his uncle Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver by the tsar’s command.

And Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich suffered great torment from his uncle: they wore down his boyars and servants with constant exactions and robberies, and gave the common people into bondage. That same winter after Epiphany, His Eminence Metropolitan Alexei went to Kiev, and the bishops accompanied him, including Theodore, bishop of Tver. And because of strife among the Tver princes, Bishop Theodore no longer wished to hold the bishopric in Tver. His Eminence Metropolitan Alexei conversed with him edifyingly and instructed him with great love in his soul to endure and wait, and the Lord God would arrange all as He wills. And with great love he sent him from Kolomna to Tver, to the Holy Saviour.

In the same year Prince Ivan Andreevich died. In the same year the Novgorodians agreed among themselves and kissed the cross that they would not tolerate demonic games and would not beat barrels.

In the year 6867 from Adam (1367 from Christ)

The blessed, Christ-loving, meek, quiet, and merciful Prince Ivan Ivanovich died, as a monk, in the schema, grandson of Daniel, great-grandson of Alexander, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, descendant of Yuri Dolgoruky, descendant of Vladimir Monomakh, descendant of Vsevolod Yaroslavich Vladimirovich. And he was laid in his patrimony, in the city of Moscow, in the church of the holy archangel Michael. He sat on the great principality for 6 years. His sons: Dmitry, Ivan.

In the same year Prince Andrei Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, built a stone church in the name of the holy archangel Michael in Nizhny Novgorod, in his patrimony. In the same year in the Horde Tsar Berdibek was killed, son of Janibek, grandson of Azbyak, with his accomplice Tavlubey, a sinister and powerful prince, and with his other counselors. And he drank the same cup with which he had made his father and his brothers drink.

After him Kulpa sat on the throne in the Horde, reigned 6 months and 5 days, and did much evil. And God’s judgment did not endure this, and Naurus killed him with his two sons, Michael and Ivan.

After Kulpa, Naurus sat on the throne in the Horde. In that time of war Prince Andrei Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, was in the Horde, and God barely saved him from a bitter death at the hands of the pagans.
