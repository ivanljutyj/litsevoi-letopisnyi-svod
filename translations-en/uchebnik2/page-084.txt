Many Cumans came to him, and six thousand warriors gathered under him, and they went to the city of Kuchelmin, and were glad to receive him, and from there he went to Ushitsa. Yaroslav’s garrison had also entered the city there, and they began to fight from the city, while the smerds galloped through the ostrog to Ivan, and three hundred of them ran over; the Cumans wanted to take the city, but Ivan would not let them; and the Cumans were angry with him and went away from him.

Izyaslav sent for Ivan, and he came to Kiev; and Izyaslav heard that Yaroslav of Galich, and Mstislav, and Vladimir Andreevich wanted to march against him because of Ivan.

And he sent to his brother Svyatoslav in Chernigov, giving him Mozyr and Chechersk, and told him that an army wanted to march against him because of Ivan. Svyatoslav said: “Brother, I was angry with you because you did not give me the Chernigov volost, but I did not wish you harm; but if an army is coming against you, then God deliver me from that volost; for you are my brother, and may God grant me to live well with you”; and they kissed the cross between themselves in all love. And then in Lutani met Izyaslav, and Svyatoslav Olegovich, and his sons Oleg and Igor, and Svyatoslav Vsevolodich, and Svyatoslav Vladimirovich, and they had feasting for three days and many gifts.

And then they sent their envoys to Galich and Vladimir to tell of their love, how God and the precious cross had joined them. But they, having learned of the union of the brothers, did not march against them.

Izyaslav began to plot against Yaroslav Vladimirovich of Galich
