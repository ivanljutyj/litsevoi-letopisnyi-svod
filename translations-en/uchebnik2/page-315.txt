And after Prince Yaroslav, his brother Prince Vasily of Kostroma became Grand Prince in Vladimir, the youngest son of Yaroslav, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, ancestor of Yaroslav, great-great-ancestor of the great Vladimir; and he became Grand Prince of Vladimir and Novgorod.

In the same year, after the death of Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, his son Michael was born.

At that time the Greeks had Emperor Manuel Palaiologos the First, who was a Latin; he drove the Latins out of Constantinople and reigned over the Greeks for 24 years.

About Dovmont

The Roman Master marveled at the courage and bravery of Prince Dovmont of Pskov and went to Pskov on campaign with a great host, in ships, on horses, and with missile engines.

Prince Dovmont of Pskov learned of this and entered the Church of the Holy Trinity, and took off his sword, and laid it before the holy altar, and fell to his knees, and began to pray with tears; and after his long prayers and tears, Abbot Sidor, in the presence of all the priests, took the sword and hung it on Dovmont’s belt, and blessed him, and released him.

Dovmont, without waiting for the Novgorod regiments, but with the Pskovians alone, went forth and, with the help of the Holy Trinity, the Most Pure Mother of God, and Their holy saints, and by his own bravery and courage, defeated the German troops, and wounded the Master himself in the face, and took some captive, while the rest fled; this was on the eighth day of the month of June.

In the same year the Germans again began to oppress people in the Pskov regions; Prince Dovmont of Pskov went and ravaged the Chud land, and returned to his land with great booty.

In the year 6780 from Adam (1280 from Christ)

The Novgorodians sent envoys to Pereyaslavl to Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, and invited him to come to Novgorod to reign over them. When Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, learned of this, he sent his voivode Semyon after him, while he himself went to Pereyaslavl, and from there went to Torzhok; and he came, and burned Torzhok, and installed his governors in it, and went back.
