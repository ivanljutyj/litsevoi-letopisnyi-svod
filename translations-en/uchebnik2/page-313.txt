His Eminence Kirill, Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Rus, sent his letter to Novgorod, in which it was said: “The Lord God gave His apostles authority in His place to bind and to loose, and after them to their heirs; and behold, we are the apostolic heirs, who bear the image of Christ and possess His authority; and behold, I, the chief pastor of all Rus, teach and instruct you. O my lords, fear the Lord God, honor the prince, do not stir up needless enmity, and do not shed blood. For every fault and every sin there is repentance and forgiveness; and Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, in whatever he is guilty before you, repents of it all and asks forgiveness, and does not wish to be such any more; and I vouch for him to you; and you should receive him with due honor. If, in your folly, you swore by kissing the cross and agreed among yourselves not to receive Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, then I release you from that oath, and forgive and bless you. Do not stir up enmity and do not shed blood; live in peace and love; if you do not obey me, I will impose spiritual penance upon you.”

The Novgorodians sent envoys to Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich for peace, and so they made peace and, by kissing the cross, agreed to observe all the ancient Novgorodian liberties that they had from the beginning; and Yaroslav Yaroslavovich began to reign in Novgorod, and they received him with great honor.

In the same year Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich set out from Novgorod to Vladimir.

In the same year, in the Horde before the tsar, they slandered Grand Prince Roman Olegovich of Ryazan, grandson of Ingvar, great-grandson of Igor, great-great-grandson of Gleb, and denounced him before the tsar: “He reviles you, great tsar, and mocks your faith.”

The tsar sent Tatars against him; and they began to force Prince Roman to accept their faith. But he said to them: “It is unworthy for Orthodox Christians to abandon their Orthodox faith and accept the faith of Muslims and pagans.” They began to beat him; but he said: “I am a Christian; and truly the Christian faith is holy, but your Tatar faith is pagan.” They did not want to hear such words from him, and they cut out his tongue and stopped his mouth with a cloth; and they began to cut off parts of his body.
