“...this terrible vision that I saw with such threats?” The princes and commanders who were near him began fearfully to ask why he had leapt up so unexpectedly, trembling all over and shouting absurd things. But from fear he could say nothing, only shook and groaned. And when he came to his senses, he ordered his princes and his army summoned to him. When all had gathered, he told them in order what terrible vision he had seen, and asked them, saying: “What does this mean, and who is that woman walking in such glory along the height of heaven, with a great multitude of lightning-like warriors who terrify us?” Hearing this from him, they said: “This is the true woman, the Mother of the blessed God, the Virgin Mary, Mother of God, helper of Christians and invincible provider. And as we understand, she wishes to intercede for the Christians.” Temir-Aksak said to them: “If Christians have such a helper, then it is vain even to begin a campaign against them; it will not end in success. If she sends even one of those who are beside her against us in wrath, he will conquer us all. And there will be no place for us to flee, even though we have many thousands of armed and strong warriors.” And as he said this, even greater fear fell upon him, trembling seized his bones, all the members of his body weakened, and he repented of making war against the Russian land.

On the Flight of Tsar Temir-Aksak

And soon he ordered all his countless force to turn back, and he rushed to flee with all his myriad warriors, pursued by no one.

He did not touch the Russian land, harming it in nothing. And he ran swiftly to the Horde, whence he had come, and before his eyes it seemed that a great multitude of warriors was chasing him from Rus. But it was not we who chased them, truly speaking; they were driven by the invincible power of God and the intercession and protection for us of our Lady the Mother of God, and by the prayers of the great saints of God, the new wonderworkers of Russia and all the saints.

On the Vladimir Icon

Such was the victory of the Mother of God against our enemies! Thus they overcame the godless Tatars, who did not see our regiments and did not hear the Russian princes coming against them with their army. But as soon as Christians cried out in sorrow to God and His Most Pure Mother, the Lord heard them and sent fear and horror upon the impious Hagarites, and destroyed their plans. Thus their cunning designs fell apart, and we were delivered by the help of the Lord our God, who granted us a bloodless victory through the prayers of our swift helper, the Most Pure Mother of God.
