He was angry with his tsar and, possessing sufficient forces, rose against his tsar Temir-Khozya, son of Khidyr, and sowed turmoil throughout the whole Volga Tsardom; he chose a tsar for himself, Avdul, and turmoil and war began in the Horde.

And at that same time Grand Prince Andrew Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod set out from the Horde for Rus. On the way Prince Retyakoz attacked him, but God helped Prince Andrew Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod, and he returned to Rus. Many Russian princes fled from the Horde at that time, and the Rostov princes fled after being plundered.

And the trans-Volga tsar Temir-Khozya, son of Khidyr, fled beyond the Volga River, where he was killed, having reigned only one month and seven days. He drank the same cup that he had given his father Khidyr and his younger brother Kutlui to drink.

And then Prince Mamai with a great host went beyond the Volga into the mountain country, and with him went the whole Horde and Tsar Avdul. And then a third tsar named Kildibek, son of Tsar Chanibek and grandson of Tsar Azbyak, rose up and began to fight for the Volga Tsardom; he destroyed many, and afterward he himself was killed.

And then many Sarai princes shut themselves up in Sarai and named as their tsar Amurat, brother of Tsar Khidyr.

And the Horde prince Bulat Temir conquered the Bulgarian Tsardom and all the towns along the Volga and the uluses, and took possession of the entire Volga route.

And another Horde prince named Tagai of Bezdezh conquered Naruchad and all that region and began to rule there.

And other Horde princes remained to rule in their domains; there began a great famine, turmoil, and disorder; the princes began ceaselessly to fight one another and shed blood. Thus God brought down His wrath upon them, while sparing His servants, the humble Orthodox Christians.

In that same year a Lithuanian army attacked the Tver lands. In that same year Prince Mamai began to fight with Tsar Amurat and with all the Sarai princes, and he killed many old Horde princes.

In the year 6870 from Adam (the year 1370 from Christ)

There was a great battle on the Volga between Prince Mamai and Tsar Amurat.

In that same year Tsar Amurat came in a raid against Prince Mamai and killed many of his Tatars. At that time two tsars ruled on the Volga throne: one was Tsar Avdul of Mamai’s Horde, whom Prince Mamai the temnik established in his own Horde, and the other was Tsar Amurat with the Sarai princes.

And thus those two tsars and those two Hordes rarely remained at peace, always being in a state of enmity and war.
