The Novgorodians came out of the city and killed some, while others they took alive with their hands; the rest barely escaped, and on that journey many died of hunger, while others, during the holy forty-day Great Lent, ate horseflesh and with difficulty made their way back to their own land. Then God not only did not let them overcome the city, but they themselves were wounded by God's punishment.

And around that city they captured countless people living there: some were cut down, others burned, from small to great, male and female, and their property was plundered. For the Novgorodians were wrong in that they forgot the great mercy of former grand princes, who allowed them to take a prince for themselves from the sons and grandsons of grand princes, from whomever they asked, to rule over them. But the Novgorodians turned to self-will and began to transgress the covenant established with their princes, and they acted outrageously against their princes, drove them away, shamed them, and robbed them. Therefore God then brought this punishment upon them and humbled them completely by the hand of Grand Prince Andrei.

And from that time they established the celebration of the feast of the miracle of the Most Pure Mother of God on the twenty-seventh day of November, the memory of the holy martyr James the Persian; thus did the Most Pure Mother of God deliver her city.

Three years before this, a sign appeared in Novgorod in three Novgorod churches: the Most Pure Mother of God wept and shed tears on three holy icons, foreseeing calamities and misfortunes over Novgorod and its territories, praying with tears to her Son that He not destroy them utterly like Sodom and Gomorrah, but that He have mercy on them as on the Ninevites. Such was God's mercy upon them; as holy David said: "Chastening, the Lord chastened me, but He did not give me over to death." For these Novgorodians did much evil, and broke the cross-kiss oath both among themselves and toward their princes, and shed much blood through murders; but, having humbled themselves and repented, they were saved, as the holy prophet Isaiah said: "When you turn and sigh, then you will be saved"; and he also said: "I humbled myself, and the Lord saved me." For humility raises from the abyss to the heavens, like a certain publican, while desire and pride bring down from the height of virtue to hell. Great indeed is the work of humility: it builds all, takes away sins, and succeeds in all.

In the same year the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God wrought a miracle in His house, the great Church of the Tithes in Kyiv, which the blessed Grand Prince Vladimir built, who baptized all the Russian land and gave a tithe to that holy great church throughout all the Russian land. The Most Pure Mother of God wrought such a miracle that it is truly beyond our merit and hope; for if the Lord God does not allow ordinary things to be wronged, much more so sacred and divine things; and this is truly fearful and full of horror for those who have understanding.
