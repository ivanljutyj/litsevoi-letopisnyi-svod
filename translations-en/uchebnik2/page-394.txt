That same winter Feodosia, wife of Ivan Ivanovich, died. That year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, came out of the Horde.

That year Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, came from the Horde from Tsar Jani Beg. He had gone on business concerning the church clergy, because all metropolitans and bishops had from the Horde tsars charters for their clergy. Someone from among the Rus people slandered Metropolitan Feognost before Tsar Jani Beg, saying that he “has countless income, much gold, silver, and every other kind of wealth, and therefore must give annual tribute to the Horde every year.” The tsar began demanding the annual tribute, but the metropolitan did not agree. For this the tsar detained him. The metropolitan gave the tsar, the tsaritsa, and the princes 600 rubles. And then the tsar released him with his people to Rus, and he returned in good health with all his own.
