In the same year Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich, grandson of Yaroslav Yaroslavich, came from Tsar Azbyak out of the Horde to the principality of Tver. In the same year the Novgorod envoy Feodor Kolesnitsa came from the Horde, from Tsar Azbyak, to Novgorod, and there was great rejoicing.

In the same year the Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich of Moscow, grandson of Alexander, sent his envoys to Pskov, by Tsar Azbyak’s command, to Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, that he should go to him in the Horde. And the Novgorodians also sent their envoys to Pskov to Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, by Tsar Azbyak’s command, that he should go to him in the Horde. And his brother Konstantin Mikhailovich of Tver sent his envoys to him in Pskov concerning the same. But he did not wish to go to Tsar Azbyak in the Horde and did not go.

During this Greek reign of Andronikos Palaiologos, Milutin was king in Serbia.

The Serbian Kingdom

Saint Simeon begot Stefan the King, and Stefan begot Stefan, who was named Uroš after his great-grandfather. This is the Hoarse King. He begot King Milutin, who was the fourth after Saint Simeon. And he, ruling Serbia, begot this Stefan the Great, pillar of piety and crown of kingship, treasure of virtue, who was good to all, approachable, and merciful.

And after the death of Stefan’s mother, King Milutin entered into a second marriage: he took the daughter of the Greek Tsar Andronikos Palaiologos. And by her he begot a son, Constantine. But the queen, seeing that Stefan was loved by all, hated him; and she came to his father, shedding tears and with a withered face, as if she were concerned for his salvation, and slandered the righteous man, saying that he lusted after her and plotted evil against his father.

The princes advised Stefan to gather soldiers and withdraw to another land and escape the fabricated lie. But he did not wish to; he placed all his hope in God. What happened after this? O loss of power! Womanly deceit prevailed and extinguished parental love! The righteous man was seized and deprived of his eyes. There was there a church of the holy wonderworker Nicholas. The righteous man lay there then, suffering greatly. And when he had dozed a little, Saint Nicholas appeared to him and said: “Do not grieve, Stefan; behold, your eyes are in my palm.” And he showed him.

Afterwards he is sent into exile to Constantinople with two of his children: one is called Dushman, and the other Dušan Stefan. Andronikos
