Through the Tatars a certain man told him of this calamity and guided him onto a safe path. And so, in good health, he reached his grand principality in Tver by the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God.

But Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich fled with his retinue to Pskov.

The Tatars wished to go to Novgorod and to Pskov. The Novgorodians sent their envoys, Semyon Klementyevich and Ivan Mikhailovich, to Tsar Duden and to all the Tatars with a multitude of countless gifts, so that they would not march with an army against Novgorod or ravage their lands. They, having taken countless gifts, returned to their own land, to the Field, and went to the Horde.

That same winter Prince Fyodor Rostislavich of Yaroslavl took the principality in Pereyaslavl, and Prince Andrey Alexandrovich of Gorodets took the principality in Novgorod.

In the same year a Tatar tsarevich named Takhtamir came from the Horde to Tver and inflicted great hardship on the people.

In the year 6802 from Adam (1302 from Christ)

Tsar Takhtamir assumed the throne in the Horde and defeated Nogai, tsar of the Horde.

In the same year Prince Andrey Alexandrovich came from Novgorod with the Novgorodians to Torzhok, intending to intercept his elder brother, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, on the way.

In the same year Prince Fyodor Romanovich of Ryazan died.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich went from Pskov to Tver past Torzhok.

His younger brother, Prince Andrey Alexandrovich of Gorodets, intercepted him at the ford. Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich himself crossed the river, but his treasury had not been ferried across, and his brother Prince Andrey Alexandrovich seized his treasury and took all his goods and pursued him with the Novgorodians; but he fled to Tver.

And from Tver he sent to him with entreaty Bishop Andrew of Tver and Prince Svyatoslav with submission. Prince Andrey Alexandrovich heeded Bishop Andrew and Prince Svyatoslav and returned to Torzhok, and sent the regiment back again to Novgorod. And Prince Ivan Dmitrievich took his seat in Kostroma, and Prince Andrey Alexandrovich went from Torzhok to Gorodets in the Lower Land, and Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich went from Tver to Volok and there fell ill.

In the same year Prince Fyodor Rostislavich of Yaroslavl burned the entire city of Pereyaslavl.
