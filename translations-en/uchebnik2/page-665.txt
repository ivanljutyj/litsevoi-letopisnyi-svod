She departed to God in peace in the year 6908 from Adam (1408 from Christ), on the seventh day of June; she was buried in her monastery in the Church of the Ascension of the Lord, and received the enjoyment of eternal blessings and of the Heavenly Kingdom.

Concerning the Candles That Light by Themselves at the Tomb of the Saint

The princess was glorified by God not only during her life, but also after her repose a candle many times lit of itself at her tomb. Through her prayers good things are multiplied in that monastery, and all necessities abound; even now an honorable common life continues there by the grace of Christ, to whom be glory with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages, amen.

In the same year Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich went with the Pskovians and an army against the Germans, seized many German lands, killed and captured many people, and captured the German town of Yavizna.

In the same year Bishop Theognostus of Ryazan reposed in Ryazan.

In the same year, on the twentieth day of July, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver went to the Horde by ships along the Volga to Tsar Shadibek.

And at that time there was turmoil in the Horde; before his arrival there had been a great rebellion in the Horde: Tsar Shadibek was driven out, and Bulat-Saltan was enthroned in his place, and many Horde princes were killed.

But by God’s mercy Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver came to the Horde whole and unharmed. And he and Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich of Tver contended before the new tsar, Bulat-Saltan, over the grand principality of Tver. Tsar Bulat-Saltan gave judgment; all the Horde princes upheld the right of Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, and Tsar Bulat-Saltan released him from the Horde with honors, while Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich remained in the Horde. In the same year in Tver they finished painting the church of the holy Archistrategus Michael at Gorodok. In the same year Vladyka Arsenius of Tver completed the narthex on the Tmaka River side in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God at Zheltikov.

In the same year, on the twenty-fifth day of May, early in the morning the moon in the sky disappeared and departed invisibly from the sky. In the same year it was very cloudy and rainy, and there were floods everywhere. In the same year worms grew wings and, flying from east to west, devoured all the trees, and they dried up.

In the same year, on the sixteenth day of August, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow gathered troops and went against the Lithuanian land, against Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich of Lithuania; he captured the town of Dmitrovets and burned it.
