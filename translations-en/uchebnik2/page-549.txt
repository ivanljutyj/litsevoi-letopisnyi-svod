Our Lord Jesus Christ was bound and beaten in His voluntary Passion, and they mocked Him. And He received forty complete wounds, undergoing deadly punishment. And the other half of that pillar is in Constantinople, in the Church of the Apostles.

And going further, here also is the prison of our Lord Jesus Christ. And behind the Greek altar there is an altar where James, the brother of the Lord, served; and from there is a place, if you go down into the crypt, as into a pit, by ten stone steps, where Queen Helena found the Life-giving Cross. And on the right side of the Greek service, on the upper gallery, is Golgotha, called the Place of the Skull, where the accursed Jews crucified our Lord Jesus Christ. There is Adam’s head, and the service there is Gurian; the Gurians serve. And beyond it is the Venetian service; the Venetians serve. And beyond those the Hungarians serve, and beyond those another Frankish service; the Franks serve. And beneath Golgotha, below on the ground, is the Iberian service; the Iberians serve. And on the left side by the Frankish service, lower and farther on, the magistri serve.

And in the middle of the church is the Holy of Holies, the navel of the earth, which King Nebrodos measured out; and over the Holy Sepulchre a small church is built, with an entrance to the Holy Sepulchre through two doors. And the Holy Sepulchre measures nine spans in length, and the width of the sepulchre is four spans. Over the Holy Sepulchre there is a large church. Its top is open, but in the small church over the Holy Sepulchre everything is covered, not the slightest opening is visible, and so everything in the church is arranged.

And to the east of the city of Jerusalem, at a distance, there is a village called Gethsemane, and here in that village is the tomb of the Most Pure Mother of God and a great church, entered by eight steps down into the crypt. And beneath the tomb of the Most Pure Mother of God a small church is made. And the tomb of the Most Pure is four and a half spans, and above the tomb eleven lamps burn. And behind the tomb, the couch on which the Most Pure Mother of God lay has been made into an altar. And entering that church, on the right is the Gurian service; the Gurians serve.

And then to the east is the Kidron brook, where Jesus entered with His disciples. And beyond the brook is a garden, the great cave of our Lord Jesus Christ, where He was betrayed to the Jews by His disciple Judas. There is a stone by which Christ stood, placing His hand. There also is the place where the Most Pure Mother of God, coming, prayed on the stone. And beyond that place is the great Mount of Olives, from which the Lord ascended into heaven. There is a very great church there. And in the middle of that church is a small church; in that church the Lord’s footprint was imprinted on a stone, so that one can see it as if living, as though imprinted on wax at this very hour. And in the same church on the right is the tomb of Pelagia the harlot, the Pelagia who anointed the Lord’s feet with myrrh and wiped them with her hair. And on her tomb is another footprint.
