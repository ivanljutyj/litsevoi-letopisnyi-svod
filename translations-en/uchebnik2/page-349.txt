In the same year Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, deposed Izmail, lord of Sarai.

In the same year Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Varsonofy bishop of Sarai.

In the same year he also sent him to Sarai. In the same year Nizhny Novgorod burned.

In the year 6821 from Adam (1321 from Christ)

Tsar Takhta of the Tatars died. In the same year Prince Alexander Glebovich of Smolensk died, grandson of Rostislav, great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-grandson of Davyd, great-great-great-grandson of Rostislav, forefather of Mstislav, forefather of Vladimir Monomakh. His sons: Vasily, Ivan.

In the same year Tsar Azbyak ascended the throne and became a Muslim.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver went to the Horde. Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, also went with him to the Horde, because at that time Tsar Takhta had died in the Horde, and the new Tsar Azbyak had ascended the throne, and everything was renewed.

And all came to the Horde and took yarlyks, each in his own name, both princes and bishops.

But by God's mercy Metropolitan Peter was held in great honor by the tsar in the Horde, and was released by the tsar with much honor, and soon came to Rus.

In the same year the posadnik of Ladoga went with the Ladogans on a campaign against the Germans; and while he was away, the Germans came, took and burned Ladoga, led people into captivity, and returned home with much wealth.

In the year 6822 from Adam (1322 from Christ)

Prince Vasily Alexandrovich of Bryansk died, grandson of Gleb, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of Davyd.

In the same year there was a great flood. In the same year there was famine in Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians held a veche, because the governors of Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver were not to their liking, since they suffered much offense and hardship from them. And they wanted to expel them. But Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver was then in the Horde. And they sent to Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich in Moscow.

He secretly sent to Prince Feodor Rzhevsky, asking him to go to Novgorod. He, having gathered, was still awaiting other news from Grand Prince Yuri.
