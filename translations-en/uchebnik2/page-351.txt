Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver summoned Prince Afanasy Danilovich and the Novgorod boyars to him and began to negotiate with them about confirming the oath according to their ancient custom. But they did not wish to give such an oath themselves.

He, falling into a rage, seized them all and sent them to Tver. And all the rest of the people he sold off as he could, and their horses, their armor, all their weapons, and all their craftsmen he took to Tver, and he ravaged Torzhok.

And in Novgorod he installed his own governors and gave the Novgorod posadnichestvo from his own hands to Mikhail Klimentyevich and Ivan Dmitriyevich. This was on the fifth day of February.

In the same year Andrei, Bishop of Tver, left his bishopric and went into a monastery.

A gap in the chronicle (three years)

And some said to Tsar Azbyak thus: “If his son is killed, his father will never come to you.” And Tsar Azbyak ordered his son to be released.

In the same year Kavgady sent many Tatars to intercept Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, to put him to death wherever they found him. That same summer, in the month of August, Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver went to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak.

In the year 6827 from Adam (the year 1327 from Christ)

Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver came to the Horde to the tsar on the sixth day of September, at the mouth of the Don River by the Surozh Sea, where the Don River flows into the sea, and that sea is called the Surozh Sea, on the sixth day of September, on the commemoration of the holy miracle of the great archangel Michael; and according to custom he gave gifts to all the princes and princesses, and afterward to the tsar himself and the tsaritsa.

Mikhail was in the Horde with the tsar for a month and a half, while Kavgady ceaselessly slandered him to the tsar. The tsar, having summoned his princes, said to them: “Judge Mikhail Yaroslavich with Yuri Danilovich, and when you have judged, tell me.”

And the princes sat in judgment and began to judge Mikhail with Yuri, and all helped Yuri, and they laid many charges upon Mikhail. Whatever Mikhail answered, his words were passed over. And even those words that were true they wholly despised, but they listened diligently to Kavgady’s words, for he was both judge and accuser, and took everything upon his soul, but made everyone believe him. Then they dispersed, and ordered them to be brought to trial the following week.
