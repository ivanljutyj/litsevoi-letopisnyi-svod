The Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, having heeded his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, and on his advice, sent a man chosen for such a task named Zakharia Tyutchev, giving him two interpreters skilled in the Tatar language, and sent much gold and silver with him to Tsar Mamai.

The envoy reached the land of Ryazan and heard that Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, and Jogaila of Lithuania had joined Tsar Mamai, and he secretly sent a swift messenger to the Grand Prince in Moscow.

Hearing this, the Grand Prince was both offended and greatly grieved, and taking with him his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich, he went to his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, and told him that Jogaila, Prince of Lithuania, and Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, had united with Mamai against us.

Metropolitan Cyprian said: “You, lord, my son in Christ, what offenses have you committed against them?” Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, shedding tears, said to him: “I, father, have sinned and am not worthy to live, yet in my dealings with them I have not transgressed a single letter of the law of my fathers. For you know yourself, father, that I am content with my own borders and do not wish to seize what is others’, and I have done them no offense whatsoever—I do not know why they have risen against me.”

The metropolitan said to him: “If such is the case, do not grieve, do not be troubled. The Lord is your protector and helper, for the Lord loves righteousness and fights for righteousness, and righteousness delivers from death. But do not act rashly, lest they suddenly fall upon you unawares. Rather, gather troops and send to all lands with all contrition, humility, and love, so that all people may come together, and the army may be greatly multiplied; and thus you will stand not with humility alone, but with humility you will join fear, and you will repel and terrify those who oppose you.”

The Grand Prince sent to all lands with humility and contrition, gathering all people into an army, and he cast grief and sorrow from his heart, but laid his sorrow upon the Lord, and upon His Most Pure Mother, and upon the holy wonderworker Peter, and upon all the saints. As the Lord wills, so He does. For who can oppose His will? It is fitting for us to ask forgiveness of our sins and mercy from Him. He does all things for our benefit and salvation.
