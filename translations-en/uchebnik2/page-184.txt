King Radoslav, hearing of this, ordered the bishops and abbots and all the noble people of the Serbian land to meet him; then Radoslav himself also went out to meet the saint.

Radoslav, although he had formerly been prudent in all things, later began to submit to his wife, and through her his mind became impaired. The rulers were displeased by the impairment of his mind and went over from him to his younger brother Vladislav. The saint left this to God's will, and when Radoslav was exiled, he fled together with his wife to the city of Drach, where the Latin who held the city was captivated by his wife's beauty; he took her from Radoslav and wanted to kill Radoslav himself. His wife became a new Delilah to her king.

The aforesaid King Radoslav, learning of this, fled and came to the holy archbishop; and he, having instructed Radoslav, tonsured him into the monastic habit in the Studenica monastery.

The saint crowned Vladislav as king and married him to the daughter of Asen, Tsar of Bulgaria and Zagora.

The Reign of Asen Ivan, Tsar of Bulgaria

After Tsar Boril, the old Tsar Ivan Asen ruled in Bulgaria, and after him his son Ivan Asen was tsar in Bulgaria; and at the beginning of his reign he rebuilt the Bulgarian cities that had fallen into decay during the time of Greek rule, and he subjugated many Greek cities.

He also subjugated the city of Sardica, otherwise called Sredets, where from ancient times, according to custom, Eastern and Western bishops, that is, Greek and Roman, gathered for a council, since this city was situated between both lands.

Tsar Asen took the relics of John of Rila that were in it and brought them to his capital city Tarnovo.

At that time the Latins ruled Constantinople, and there was no patriarch then; and he installed a patriarch at Tarnovo for himself, obtained from the Latins then ruling in Constantinople the relics of the venerable martyr Paraskeva and brought them to the city of Tarnovo, and went to other Greek cities; Tsar Theodore Laskaris came out against him for battle, in which the Bulgarian Tsar Asen won, and Theodore Laskaris was taken captive during the battle, and the Bulgarian Tsar Asen ordered him to be blinded.
