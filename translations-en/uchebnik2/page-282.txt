Batu did not dare, since the kingdom of the Russian land was great and populous. And then, fearing that he himself might suffer in some way, this crafty and most cunning ruler-tyrant devised another trick, and approached the people of God with deceit in order to accomplish his godless deed. And Batu gave such an ungodly law for all the great princes, the rulers of cities and those who took their place: those among them who wished to keep their rights and domains, he commanded to go to the Horde, so that each might receive authority from his hands and in submission and slavery to foreigners occupy his place. From such a cunning design the pious had no peace or relief, and all went to the Horde.

But the godless Batu did not begin openly to force them, nor did he command them to renounce Christ, but he tried to incline them to his evil design either by nets of deceit or by force of authority. And Batu appointed pagan priests to lead the arriving princes in to him; and he devised the introduction very cunningly, with evil intent: he ordered a certain fire to be produced, not as a real one, but by sorcery; and he ordered that none of the Russian princes be brought to him for an audience until they had passed through the fire and bowed to the sun, and the fire, and the bush, and the Tatar valleys. And whatever the princes brought with them as gifts to the tsar, from all of it the pagan priests took a portion and threw it into the fire. And afterward the princes themselves, together with the gifts, were led in to the tsar.

But our pious princes did not understand that people who truly honor God ought not to do this; and thus many, together with their boyars, fulfilled the cunning command of the ungodly, and each received what he asked of Batu. And the princes thought that by this they eased the torment from foreigners, if in every Christian city there would not be a foreigner as ruler. O foolish doubts and inadmissible submission of our princes! O devilish will of soul-destroying cunning! O crafty, evil, and cunning design of the tormentor! He wished to make the princes apostates and thus hoped to deal with the Church without labor and to destroy Christianity in Rus. However, the madman vainly learns vain things, for Christ is the Bridegroom of the Church, which even the very gates of hell shall not overcome; and now the Lord has given her a strong champion, this radiant Michael, the great prince. He was before God like the best arrow in the quiver, with which God shot and killed the enemy, and showed the vanity of his cunning.

And it became known throughout all the land what Batu had done to the Russian princes; and this news reached the great prince Michael himself. He alone did not wish then to go to the Horde, but left Kiev and began to reign on his independent ancestral throne in Chernigov.
