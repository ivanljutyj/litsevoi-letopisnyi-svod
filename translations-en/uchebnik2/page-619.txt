He gathered physicians. They gave him the prepared potion, and when he drank it, his belly was torn open. He lay for six days, and blood came from his mouth and from his backside.

And after three days he wickedly gave up his God-abhorrent soul. And as Pharaoh in the sea, so Temir-Aksak vanished in the desolate field. And his realm soon fell apart. What remained of his army scattered, each wherever he could.

So it was also in the time of King Hezekiah, when Sennacherib, king of Assyria, went to war against Jerusalem, vaunting evilly in great pride and uttering blasphemous words against God the Almighty. King Hezekiah, though he was sick at that time, nevertheless prayed to God with tears together with the prophet Isaiah and with all the people.

And God heard their prayer, and that same night the angel of the Lord destroyed the Assyrian hosts—one hundred eighty-five thousand. When they rose in the morning, they saw that dead corpses already lay there.

That same night King Sennacherib fled with the remnants of his army to Nineveh and there was killed by his own children. So Temir-Aksak too boasted that he would destroy Christianity, but by Christian prayer he was crushed.

Likewise the lawless Julian, who threatened Basil the Great, boasting that he would lay waste his city of Caesarea—Christian prayer killed him then. And as then, so now, one God and one grace works, doing as much as He wills, because great is God! So now by His great mercy He granted us a bloodless victory, struck and drove out our enemies, and delivered and saved us from many troubles, and always had mercy on us and preserved us, guarding us by His high hand from every harm of soul and body. And as in ancient times God was glorified by dealing with Pharaoh, with his chariots and his warriors, so He dealt with Temir-Aksak. And always we who believe in Him ceaselessly glorify His thrice-holy name, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, and our most pure Lady, the Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary, our Christian intercessor.

Now we celebrate the present miracle-working triumph of the Mother of God, the appearance of Her healing image, in all cities and places of the whole Russian realm. Therefore we celebrate, brothers, giving thanks and praising the Lord God and His most pure Mother, our hope, so that by Her prayers and those of all the saints we may strive, as far as we can, to pass the present time of our life in a God-pleasing manner, in stillness, in peace, and in repentance with compunction and a pure conscience, having cleansed ourselves from sins. And then we shall find here grace and mercy and be freed from visible and invisible enemies, and in the future endless age we shall be deemed worthy to receive ineffable blessings by the grace and love for mankind of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ, to whom be glory, honor, and dominion with the Father and with the Holy Spirit now, ever, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
