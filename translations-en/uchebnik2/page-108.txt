Also afterward Emperor Manuel sent two bishops for him, summoning him to himself; and Grand Prince Yaroslav Volodimirikovich of Galich released him with great honor, and assigned to him Bishop Kozma, and his boyars, and voivodes, and escorted him with honor.

In the same year, in Novgorod, the Swedes came against Ladoga in sixty ships; Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich with Posadnik Zakhary defeated them on the Voronaya River, killing some and taking others captive, on the twenty-eighth day of May.

In the year 6673 from Adam (the year 1173 from Christ)

There died in Chernigov Grand Prince Svyatoslav of Chernigov, son of Oleg, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great; and after him Prince Svyatoslav, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great, sat in Chernigov on the great principality; and in Novgorod Prince Oleg, son of Svyatoslav, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great, sat on the great principality.

In the same year the Polovtsians campaigned against Rus; and all the Russian princes gathered and met them far out in the Field, and there was a great battle, and God helped the Russian princes, and they prevailed. Some of the Polovtsians were killed, while others fled, scattering in the Field; and the Russian princes joyfully thanked God and His Most Pure Mother for the great victory over the pagans, and grieved for their slain, because few returned home; many were killed.

In the same year John, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, installed Elias as bishop in Novgorod. In the same year the Novgorodians built the Church of the Holy Trinity at Shatinitsa. In the same year Prince Svyatoslav Rostislavich built the Church of St Nicholas at Gorodishche.

In the year 6674 from Adam (the year 1174 from Christ)

Bishop Elias of Novgorod sent many gifts to Kiev to the Most Reverend John, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus.
