And Prince Mikhail of Tver then, in St Theodore's Week, sent them to the Horde, and after the Week of the Veneration of the Cross he himself went to Lithuania.

And after staying in Lithuania a short time, he returned to Tver.

And a little time later, on the 14th day of the month of July, Nekomat the Surozhian returned from Mamai's Horde, from Tsar Mamai, with the ambassador Achi-khoza, to Tver to Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver with a yarlyk for the grand principality of Vladimir.

And Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver that same day sent to Moscow to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich a declaration of the rupture of the peace treaty with him.

And he sent his viceroys to Torzhok, and to Uglich he sent an army.

And then in Tver, in the same month on the 27th day, in the Church of the Protection of the Most Pure Mother of God, at night before the icon a candle lit by itself. Then also in the same month on the 29th day, on Sunday early in the morning, the sun vanished.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow gathered all his army and, having united with all the Russian princes, went to Volok.

And there all the Russian princes came to him, and his father-in-law Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal with his son Prince Semyon, brother-in-law of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and with his brother Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich Nokhtem of Suzdal.

Then came also Prince Vladimir Andreevich, cousin of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and Prince Andrei Feodorovich of Rostov, and Prince Vasily Konstantinovich of Rostov, and his brother Prince Alexander Konstantinovich, and Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of Smolensk, and Prince Vasily Vasilyevich of Yaroslavl, and his brother Prince Roman Vasilyevich of Yaroslavl, and Prince Feodor Romanovich of Beloozero, and Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, and Prince Feodor Mikhailovich of Molozhsk, and Prince Andrei Feodorovich of Starodub, and Prince Roman Mikhailovich of Bryansk, and Prince Roman Semyonovich of Novosil, grandson of Grand Prince Mikhail of Chernigov, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-great-grandson of Oleg, forefather of Svyatoslav, great-forefather of Great Vladimir, and Prince Semyon Konstantinovich of Obolensk, grandson of Yury, great-grandson of Grand Prince Mikhail of Chernigov, and his brother Prince Ivan Konstantinovich of Tarusa, and many other princes with their armies. They were all angry with Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, saying: “How many times has he brought the army of his son-in-law, Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania, how much evil has he done to Christians, and now he has united with Mamai and with his tsar and with all Mamai's Horde!”
