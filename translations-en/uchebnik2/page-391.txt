That year the Lithuanians took Tishinov and went home with a great number of captives.

That year the Germans came and set up towns on the Pivzha River, in the Pskov land.

That year Prince Mikhailo Alexandrovich of Tver, godson of Vasily, Archbishop of Novgorod, came to him in Novgorod to learn to read and write.

That year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, had a son, Konstantin, who died the same day.

That year the Germans killed the best men of Pskov, although there was peace. And the Pskovians went against them with Prince Alexander Vsevolodovich and conquered Latygoda. Thus Prince Alexander Vsevolodovich, having taken vengeance, went away.

That year Vasily, Archbishop of Novgorod, ordered Saint Sophia to be covered with lead, icons to be painted, and icon cases to be installed.

In the year 6848 from Adam (the year 1348 from Christ)

Janibek, Tsar of the Horde, son of Azbyak, killed his elder brother Tinibek and himself took the throne. And even earlier he had killed his younger brother. And he came to rule the whole Horde, all the uluses and all the lands.

That year Luka Varfolomeev, not heeding the teaching and blessing of Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and of his own Archbishop Vasily of Novgorod, gathered around himself robber bands and wicked men and boyar bondsmen, went beyond the Portage to the Dvina, and built the fortress Orlets. And there, having gathered the Yem people, he took the Zavolochye land and conquered all the districts and villages along the Dvina.

At the same time his son Ontsifor went to the Volga. His father Luka then went to war with a small retinue, and the Zavolochians killed him.

When they learned of this in Novgorod, all the people grieved for him and rose up against the posadniks Fyodor and Andrei, saying, “You sent Luka to be killed,” and they plundered their houses and villages. The posadniks fled to Koporye and stayed there for some time.

And after this Luka’s son Ontsifor came to Novgorod and petitioned the Archbishop and all the Novgorodians against the posadniks Fyodor and Andrei, saying, “They sent my father to his death.”
