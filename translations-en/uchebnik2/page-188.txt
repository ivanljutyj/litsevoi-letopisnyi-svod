King Vladislav laid him with honors in his own monastery of the Ascension of the Lord, which he himself had founded, and many miracles always occurred there.

Then the saint appeared in a dream to a holy elder and ordered his body to be taken from the earth; and they placed it in the church, visible to all for veneration. And in the land of Zagorje, where his first grave had been, many miracles occurred to the glory of God.

On the Genealogy of the Serbian Despots

Belourosh begot Tehomil, Tehomil begot Nemanja, who is Saint Simeon, who established Orthodoxy and eradicated heresies. Simeon begot by his wife Anna three sons: Stefan, the first-crowned king; Volkan, grand prince of all Zeta and Hum; and Rastko, who was named Sava in monastic vows.

Simeon’s son King Stefan begot four sons: Rodoslav, Vladislav, Stefan, and Predislav. And Stefan was named Uroš in honor of his great-grandfather; he was the pockmarked king.

Predislav, the third son of Stefan, striving to imitate in all things his uncle Saint Sava, became a monk and was named Sava: in all things he was like his uncle Saint Sava, and after Saint Arsenius he became the third archbishop in the Serbian land.

Stefan, who was called Uroš and the pockmarked king, begot two sons: Stefan and King Milutin Bansago; and Milutin begot Constantine and Stefan of Dečani.

Stefan begot two sons: Dušman and Dušan; Dušan transgressed the covenants of his fathers, arbitrarily proclaimed himself tsar, and begot a son, Uroš. And Uroš had no children; the vine remained without offspring.

Stefan, the first son of the pockmarked King Uroš, begot two sons: King Vladislav and Urošica. Urošica was so virtuous that myrrh flows from his grave. His father Stefan reposed in the year 6825 from Adam (1325 from Christ).

The Second Genealogy

Grand Prince Volkan, son of Saint Simeon and brother of Saint Sava, begot a son, Župan Dmitar, in monastic vows David. This David begot Prince Vratislav, and Vratislav begot Prince Vratko.

Vratko begot a daughter, Milica. She was the wife of Grand Prince Lazar and bore three sons: the renowned Despot Stefan, Vuk, and Dobrovoy.
