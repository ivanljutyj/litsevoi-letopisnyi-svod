Of Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, came a multitude of Polovtsians; and they divided in two, and some went to Pereyaslavl and stood at Pesochna, while others came along this side of the Dnieper to Kiev and stood at Korsun.

And they sent to Kiev to Grand Prince Gleb Yuryevich, saying: “God has placed you on your patrimony, in the great principality in Kiev, that you may be confirmed with us according to your faith, and we with you according to our faith, in peace and love.” And Grand Prince Gleb Yuryevich said to the Polovtsian envoys: “I will go myself to your princes for peace and love.”
