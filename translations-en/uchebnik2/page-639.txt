The blessed Bishop Arsenius, seeing the man inflamed with divine fire for the love of Christ, burning in spirit, began to apply Divine Scripture to his virtuous heart, rejoicing that another all-powerful warrior of Christ would appear, who could more firmly reject the glory of this world and despise the honors of this vain, short-lived kingdom. The prince and the bishop conversed for a long time. And at last he said: "Great and pious father, through your prayers, by His mercy, as He wills, so He will do. And I trust in Him. But you, man, see to it that my sons and princesses do not grieve for me. Today command the whole holy council, let the archimandrites and abbots and all the others gather to you for the meeting of the holy icon."

And as soon as the bishop left the noble Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich, he ordered the envoys sent by the patriarch to enter the city with the honorable icon.

The envoys went into the city, carrying the honorable and priceless treasures. The bishop with the whole council and a multitude of people went out with candles and censers to meet the honorable icon, rejoicing and singing praises to the Lord. The wise Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich himself also came out, with great difficulty rising from his bed. And he met it in his courtyard, at the church of Saint Michael. And when he had honorably met the holy icon and saw it, he rejoiced in spirit, and his heart was gladdened. And with joyful tears he made obeisances, crossing himself before it with fear and faith, and thanked the Lord God, saying: "Do not hide my tears, O God, for I am a stranger before You and a sojourner. Like all my fathers, I am weak, and in You alone will I rest when I depart, and I will no longer be here. But now I will render to You, O Lord, for Your great love for mankind toward me, for You have granted me to receive the final blessing from the holy patriarch and from the great council, and have deemed me worthy to see Your Terrible Judgment, which is depicted on this holy icon! O glory to You, glory to You, most abundant in mercy, O man-loving God, that for such a sinner and unworthy one, according to Your immeasurable mercy, You have fulfilled the desire of my heart! Yes, glory to You, good God, glory to You!"

The envoys then gave him the letter from the venerable patriarch and from the whole holy council — peace and blessing, and in it was absolution for him. The bishop ordered the whole council to carry the honorable icon to the cathedral church of the Holy Savior, and they placed it there, and sang thanksgiving hymns to the Lord God, and then carried it before him.
