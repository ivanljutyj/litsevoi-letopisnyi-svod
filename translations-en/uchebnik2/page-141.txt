In the year 6686 from Adam (1186 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich of Vladimir made peace and love with the Novgorodians, and they confirmed it by kissing the cross, but they did not come to an agreement; he went against them to Torzhok, burned the volosts and villages, but did not burn the city, because the Novotorzhians had promised to give tribute, but they lied.

He captured their city, killed the people themselves, took the women and children captive, and burned their city on the eighth day of December, on the memory of our venerable father Potapius.

And so he sent all the captives and the booty to Vladimir, while he himself went from there to Volok Lamsky, and came there secretly, burned the volosts and villages, and took captive his nephew Prince Yaroslav Mstislavich, grandson of Rostislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.

And he burned the city of Volok Lamsky, and after taking many people captive and killing some, he went to Vladimir.

In the year 6687 from Adam (1187 from Christ)

Prince Mstislav Rostislavich gathered twenty thousand Novgorodians and went to conquer the Chud land and Ochula, and he stood there eight days; some he took captive, while others fled to the sea; he returned to Novgorod with great booty.

In the year 6688 from Adam (1188 from Christ)

Prince Mstislav Rostislavich went with the Novgorodians in arms against Polotsk, against his son-in-law Prince Vseslav, because his grandfather had once gone against Novgorod and had taken the church of Jerusalem and the service vessels, and had secured one pogost for Polotsk; Prince Mstislav Rostislavich wanted to return all the captured Novgorodian volosts.

And when he came to Luki, there his brother Prince Roman Rostislavich sent to him from Smolensk, saying: “Do not go, brother, to Polotsk against Prince Vseslav, for you have no offense from him; but if you wish, drive me out; if you wish, go with your army.”

He did not wish to grieve his elder brother Prince Roman Rostislavich and returned to Novgorod.

And a severe illness overcame him, and Prince Mstislav reposed, son of Rostislav, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and descendant of the great Vladimir, on the fourteenth day of June, on the memory of the holy prophet Elisha and of Saint Methodius, Patriarch of Constantinople, and he was laid in the vestibule of the Church of Holy Sophia in the tomb that Vladimir Yaroslavich had made. His son: Svyatoslav.
