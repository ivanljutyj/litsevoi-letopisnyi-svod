To his body, although an innumerable multitude of beasts was there, God preserved it, for it is written: “The Lord preserves all their bones; not one of them will be broken.” For death is cruel to sinners, as it came to the wretched Kavgady; not having survived a single year, the wretch vilely ended his life; and many faithful and unbelievers then saw two clouds overshadowing the body of the blessed Prince Michael, parting and coming together, shining like the sun, which they confessed to us with tears and with many oaths.

Then they sent the body from there to Mozhcharyk, and the Russian merchants who were there wished to place it in a church, covering it with a shroud; but they did not allow them, and instead placed it in a stable under guard. Then those living there saw at night a pillar of fire from earth to heaven, and others saw something like a heavenly rainbow bending down to the stable in which the body lay.

From there they took him to the city of Bezdezh, and when they were near the city, many in the city saw around the sledge a multitude of people with candles, and others on horses with lanterns, riding through the air; and so they brought him into the city.

And they did not place him in a church but in a courtyard, and two of those guarding him lay down on the sledge over his body. Great fear fell upon them, and by some unknown force they were thrown down from the sledge and flung far away, and they barely rose and came to themselves; and going, they confessed all that had happened to the priests who were there; and from them, having heard of this, the account was written down faithfully.

From there they took him through the Russian cities, and so little by little they brought him to Moscow, and brought him into the city of Moscow, and laid him in the monastery, in the church of the Holy Transfiguration; but in Tver they did not know of it.

In the year 6828 from Adam (1328 from Christ)

Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow came to the great reign and brought with him Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich, grandson of Yaroslav, and his boyars and servants, leading them with him as if captive; and the report spread through all the land.

And his sons met him in riverboats on the Volga: Dmitry, Alexander, Vasily, and Grand Princess Anna, their mother, and Bishop Varsonofy, and the whole order of clergy with candles and lamps, and a multitude of people; they met him at the Archangel Michael on the Bank, and laid him in the church of the Holy Saviour, on the sixth day of September. His body was whole and unharmed by anything, having been brought from distant lands on a cart and on a sledge; and in Moscow, however long it lay, it did not decay, but remained all whole and unharmed.
