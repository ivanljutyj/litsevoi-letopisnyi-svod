In the year 6637 from Adam (1137 from Christ)

Yaroslav Svyatoslavovich died in Murom, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vladimir the Great. In the same year Mikhalko Vyacheslavovich died in Ryazan. In the same year Prince Mstislav sent the Polotsk princes with their wives and children into exile to Tsargrad.

In the same year Danilo came from Kiev to Novgorod as posadnik.

In the year 6638 from Adam (1138 from Christ)

Mstislav sent his sons Vsevolod, Izyaslav, and Rostislav with their retinues and with the Novgorodians against the Chud; and they conquered them and imposed tribute on them.

In the same year Ivan relinquished the bishopric, having held it for 20 years. In the same year the Most Reverend Michael, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Nifont, a holy man and very God-fearing, as bishop of Novgorod.

In the same year the stone church of St Ivan was built; and Petrila was given the office of posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6639 from Adam (1139 from Christ)

Mstislav, son of Vladimir Monomakh, with all the Novgorod host and the Nizovians marched against Lithuania, and having waged much war and taken captives, returned home with great joy.

In the same year the princes of Ryazan, Pronsk, and Murom slew many Polovtsians. In the same year Mstislav Vladimirovich founded the Church of the Holy Theotokos in Pirogoshcha.

In the year 6640 from Adam (1140 from Christ)

Mstislav died, son of Vladimir Monomakh, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great, on the 14th day of April; he reigned in Kiev for six years. His sons: Vsevolod, Izyaslav, Rostislav, Svyatopolk.

The Reign of Yaropolk Vladimirovich in Kiev

In the same year, after the death of Mstislav Vladimirovich, his brother Yaropolk Vladimirovich Monomakh assumed the great reign in Kiev, because the people of Kiev had sent for him.
