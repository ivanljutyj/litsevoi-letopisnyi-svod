“From all of Novgorod with a petition, asking Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich to come to Novgorod to reign. And in everything the people of Novgorod acknowledged their guilt and said that they had unjustly raised a great rebellion and quarreled with the son of Yaroslav, with Grand Prince Alexander; they asked that for this Prince Yaroslav not be angry with Great Novgorod, but again send his son, Prince Alexander, to Novgorod to reign.”

And Prince Yaroslav sent his son Prince Alexander back to them.

In the same year the Lithuanians and Germans came in war against the Novgorod land and did much evil, and departed.

In the same year Batu’s Tatars captured the Bulgars of the Volga and the Kama.

In the year 6750 from Adam (1250 from Christ)

Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich came to Novgorod, and with him Archbishop Spiridon and the boyars; the Novgorodians met him with a procession of the cross at the city gates.

And the Novgorodians received Prince Alexander to reign among them with great honor; and there was great joy in Novgorod. And soon Prince Alexander set out with the Novgorodians, the Ladogans, the Karelians, and the Izhorians to the German city of Koporye.

And Prince Alexander destroyed the city to its foundations; and he killed the Germans themselves.

Some of the Germans Prince Alexander brought with him to Novgorod, and others he released to their German land, for Alexander was merciful beyond measure.

And the rebels from the tribes of the Vod and the Chud Alexander hanged, and then went to Pereslavl, which is on Lake Kleshchino.

After the victory of Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, when he defeated the king—it was in the third year, in winter—the Germans from this side of the sea gathered and came against Pskov, and defeated the Pskov troops, and set up their governors in Pskov.

Learning of this, Grand Prince Alexander was greatly grieved that Christian blood had been shed, and he did not delay at all, but his spirit rose, and so did the devotion to the Holy Trinity and Holy Sophia inherent in him.

And Alexander took with him his brother and all his warriors, and came to Novgorod; and he bowed to Holy Sophia with supplication and weeping.
