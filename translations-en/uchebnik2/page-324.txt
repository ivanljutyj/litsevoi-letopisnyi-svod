And Gleb built many churches and adorned them with icons and books; and he held priests and monks in great honor; and he had love and mercy toward all, and humility; but he hated pride and turned away from it as from a serpent.

When, by God’s command, Prince Gleb was departing from this vain and fleeting life, he died gently and quietly in the Lord, and joined his fathers, grandfathers, and great-grandfathers; he left great sorrow and weeping behind him to all who knew him.

Bishop Ignatius of Rostov, with all the priests, sang the appointed funeral hymns over him; and they laid him in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Rostov.

Gleb’s son Michael, who took after him the place that had come to Gleb from his father, in Beloozero; and in Rostov after Gleb Vasilkovich his nephews took the place: Prince Dmitry Borisovich, grandson of Vasilko, and his brother Konstantin Borisovich.

In that same year died Grand Princess Avdotya, wife of Vladimir Konstantinovich, in Uglich.

In that same year Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich of Vladimir took the city of Tetyakov.

In that same year many people died of various diseases.

In that same year the Tatars came to Ryazan and did much harm, and departed to their own land.

In the year 6787 from Adam (1287 from Christ)

There was a lunar sign: the moon disappeared completely, and was gone for a long time, and appeared before dawn, and when day broke it did not appear in full; this was on the 24th day of February. That same winter there was a solar sign.

In that same year died Grand Prince Yuri Andreevich of Suzdal, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of Great Vladimir.

And after Prince Yuri, his brother Prince Michael Andreevich became grand prince in Suzdal.

In that same year died Grand Prince Michael Rostislavovich of Smolensk, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of David, great-great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-great-grandson of Mstislav, forefather of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of Great Vladimir.
