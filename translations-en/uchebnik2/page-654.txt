He stayed for some time, receiving honors from Vytautas, from all the princes and lords, and from the whole country.

From there he went to Kiev and sent back to Moscow his vicar Archimandrite Timofey and his local servants, who had been ordered to keep various goods in Kiev and to manage affairs. And he left as his vicar Archimandrite Feodosy of the Savior, selected servants, and ordered them to remain in Kiev with the vicar archimandrite.

In the same year in Great Novgorod, Lyudin End and Prusskaya Street burned as far as the Church of Saint Michael, and in the Detinets as far as the archbishop’s court; in all, five wooden churches burned down, and five stone churches were scorched; the Church of Saints Boris and Gleb burned out within; in all, 30 people burned.

In the same year the Novgorod posadnik Vasily Ivanovich reposed. In the same year in Tver, a stone church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God was founded by Bishop Arseny on the Tmaka River.

In the year 6913 from Adam (1413 from Christ)

In September the Tatars came in a raid against Ryazan. Prince Fyodor Olegovich, grandson of Ivan, sent pursuit after them. The warriors sent by the prince in pursuit caught up with the Tatars, defeated them, recovered the captives, took many Tatars prisoner, and returned to Ryazan with great joy. In the same autumn, in the month of September, on the 13th day, at noon in Tver a fire began beyond the Tmaka; one hundred households burned, and the Church of Saint John the Forerunner. In the same autumn, in the month of October, on the 30th day, in Tver at Gorodok, Grand Princess Maria, wife of Prince Ivan Mikhailovich, reposed in the monastic habit; her name in monasticism was Martha, and she was buried in the Church of the Holy Savior in Tver.

In the same winter the venerable Archimandrite Evfimy of Suzdal reposed.

In the same winter, in the month of February, on the 13th day, Grigory, Bishop of Kolomna, reposed. In the same year Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, consecrated the priest Gogol as bishop of the Volhynian land in Vladimir-Volynsky.

In the same autumn, in the month of October, on the 1st day, the stone Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God at Simonovo was consecrated; it was founded by Archimandrite Feodor and built by Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and it was consecrated in the 29th year.

In the same winter Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin came to Tver, and his brother Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver captured him and all his boyars.
