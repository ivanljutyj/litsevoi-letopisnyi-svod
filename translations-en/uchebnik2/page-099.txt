We have commanded that the rules of the holy apostles and holy fathers concerning metropolitan sees and bishoprics be kept whole, unshaken, and inviolate according to their laws, and no one may depart from the holy divine and sacred canons unless he wishes to be cut off and estranged from God. We also read your second letter sent to us, from which we learned of the accusations against your God-loving bishop; and we were separately informed by the letter both from the Most Reverend Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus and the bishops under him, and from the envoy himself of our sovereign son the tsar, and from many others, that concerning such accusations against your bishop they had spoken many times at their council among you, and certain men who came from your Nobility had spoken of it before the Grand Prince of Kiev; and all those charges proved to be without evidence, such as might be brought against a bishop and for which he might be seized as guilty. And this bishop was justified by his own council under his Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, and we believe it would be unjust to condemn that sacred council and alter its decisions, because everything was examined there; and since the sacred canons do not permit us to do this, but command that each bishop be judged by his own council by his own metropolitan, so we too observe the sacred and divine canons which we received and were taught. But because the bishop, relying on his own righteousness, persistently with great entreaty and tears complained to us that his case should be examined again at a sacred and divine council, since he was much worn down by grief and sorrow, we heeded his entreaties and tears and read what was contained against him in the letter sent to us by your Nobility concerning the charges and other grievances connected with them. But since the bishop also answered confidently in his defense against each charge according to the sacred and divine canons, he is now justified by us, and we received him with us into the sacred and divine service, and he served the sacred and divine service with us; and we have sent his justification to your Nobility, so that you may receive him with love and honor, as one truly justified by God Himself and sent by our humility and by the sacred and divine great council. Do not wish to oppose the judgment of all the hierarchs and of our humility; if you wish to abide with Christ God, who united heavenly and earthly things by His descent to us, and by the grace and blessing of the holy great catholic Church, and by the prayers of the holy great hierarchs gathered here and of our humility, as a spiritual son, therefore set aside all doubt and sorrow, and lay off from your heart the hatred brought by the enemy which you hold against your bishop, and receive him with joy and honor as Christ, with all gentleness and meekness, humility, and love, as one worthy by God's grace to be a bishop, filled with understanding, enriched in reason, honored in word, and shining in virtue.
