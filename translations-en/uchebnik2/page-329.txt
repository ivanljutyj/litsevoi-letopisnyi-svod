In the same year Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich went from overseas to Pereyaslavl, and in Koporye were his boyars, his servants, all his treasury, and all his belongings.

And Prince Dmitry’s son-in-law, Prince Dovmont of Pskov, came out from Pskov and took from Koporye all his father-in-law’s treasury; and he brought his boyars and servants out of Koporye and sent them to his father-in-law, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.

And Dovmont went and took Ladoga, in which there were many of Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich’s people; Prince Dovmont brought them out and likewise sent them to his father-in-law, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.

The Novgorodians went and razed the town of Koporye to the ground, and sent their envoys to Vladimir to Prince Andrei Alexandrovich, and invited him to come to them in Novgorod.

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich came to them in Novgorod and began to reign, and the Novgorodians received him with great honor.

Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich came to the city of Pereyaslavl and began to gather an army and fortify the city, and from everywhere many people began to gather to him.

In the same year a messenger came to Novgorod to Prince Andrei Alexandrovich with these words: “Your elder brother, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, is reigning in the city of Pereyaslavl and gathering a great army, and behold, a multitude of people has gathered to him from everywhere.” Prince Andrei summoned the Novgorodians and reached agreement with them on everything.

In the year 6790 from Adam (1290 from Christ)

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich went from Novgorod to Vladimir, taking with him many Novgorodians, and the mayor Semyon Mikhailovich, and the other mayor Yakov Dmitrievich, with engines of war, fearing his elder brother, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, for he was in Pereyaslavl with a large army and was gathering warriors from everywhere.

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich came to Vladimir and honored the Novgorodians, and sent them back, and ordered them to guard Torzhok and Novgorod.

The mayors Semyon Mikhailovich and Yakov Dmitrievich took up position in Torzhok and did not allow the viceroys of Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich to enter Torzhok.

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich went from Vladimir to Gorodets, and from there went to the Horde to the tsar.
