On the 27th day of August. He remained in the great reign of Tver for 34 years, and lived 65 years in all.

This noble and Christ-loving Grand Prince Michael was born of Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich and his mother Anastasia in Pskov. At that time his father, Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich, after the murder of Shchelkan Dyudenievich, nephew of the Horde tsar Azbyak, could not live in Tver, fearing violence and oppression from the Tatars; and there his son Prince Michael was born, while he was living in great sorrow, and Vladyka Vasily, Archbishop of Novgorod, baptized him in the year 6841 from Adam (1341 from Christ). Grand Prince Alexander lived 13 years in Pskov, and afterward began to grieve and sorrow, to shed tears and weep bitterly, saying: “If I accept my death here, what will become of me and my children? All people know that I have abandoned my principality, and if I accept my death here, my children will lose their reign. It would be better for me to die for God than to live thus.” And he sent his boyars to Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, asking for his blessing and prayers and those of all the hierarchs, since he had resolved to go to the tsar in the Horde. And having received blessing and the promise of prayer from Metropolitan Theognost and all the Russian hierarchs, he began to prepare to go to the Horde with his boyars and servants, and set out from Pskov for the Horde.

And the prince came to Tsar Azbyak and said to him: “Free lord tsar, I have done you much harm, but now I have come to you to receive either death or life from your hand, as God instructs you. I am ready for all things: if by God’s will and your royal majesty you show me mercy, I will thank God and your mercy; but if you put me to death, I am worthy of death, and behold, I bow my head before you.” Tsar Azbyak marveled at the sweetness of his words and his humility and said to his princes: “Do you see Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, how by humble wisdom he has delivered himself from death?” And all marveled; Tsar Azbyak showed the prince honor, returned to him his patrimony, the great reign of Tver, and released him to Rus.

And he came to Tver to the great reign, and with him came envoys, Prince Kindyak and Avdul.

And he sent to Pskov for his princess and children. And he sent his son Prince Feodor with the tsar’s envoys Kindyak and Avdul to Tsar Azbyak in the Horde, but he did not conclude peace with Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich of Moscow. Prince Feodor Alexandrovich of Tver came to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak, but Tsar Azbyak became angry with Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich and his son Feodor, because someone had slandered them before him. And the tsar sent to Tver, summoning Prince Alexander to himself, and he was killed in the Horde together with his son Feodor.
