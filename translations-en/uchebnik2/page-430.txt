In the same year in Torzhok merchants who traded in foreign lands, and other merchants, and simple people built a stone church of the Holy Transfiguration, to the glory of Christ God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and for their own salvation.

In the same year Archbishop Alexius of Novgorod built a stone gate-church of the Meeting in the Antoniev Monastery.

In the same year, by the blessing of Archbishop Alexius, they began to build in Pskov a stone church of the Holy Trinity on the old foundation.

In the same year there was a sign from the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God—as though dew flowed from the icon in the church of the Holy Apostle Peter in Slavna.

In the same year there was a plague in Pskov. In the same year there was a severe plague also in Torzhok.

In the year 6874 from Adam (the year 1374 from Christ)

There was a sign in the sky. In the same year there was a severe plague in the city of Moscow and throughout all the Moscow regions.

In the same year there was a severe plague in Volok. In the same year there was a severe plague also in Lithuania. In the same year there was drought and great heat, the air became opaque, and the earth burned; everywhere grain became expensive, and famine came over all the land, and people died from it.

In the same year in Tver there was war between Prince Vasily Mikhailovich and Prince Eremei with his nephew Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich over the appanage of Prince Semyon Konstantinovich.

And in the same year, by the blessing and command of the Metropolitan, Bishop Basil judged them and vindicated Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich.

In the same year robbers from Novgorod the Great came; they had two hundred ushkui, and they went down the Volga River.

And they killed many Tatars and Armenians, Tatar and Novgorod merchants together with their wives and children, and plundered their goods, and destroyed their ships, and the pauzki, kerbaty, ladii, uchani, mishany, bafkhty, and strugi—all they gave to fire, and they themselves went away to the Kama. And so they went along the Kama, fighting the Bulgars, and killed many of them.

In the same year in Novgorod the Great they built the church of the Holy Trinity on Ryaditina Street.

In the same year young nobles from Novgorod the Great gathered an army without the permission of the Novgorodians, and their commanders were Osip Varfolomeevich, Vasily Feodorovich, and Alexander Avvakumovich.
