They brought Vasilko and laid him in the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God, where his mother also lies; and the hymns could not be heard because of the loud weeping. This Vasilko was handsome in face, with bright eyes and a stern gaze, and exceedingly bold, and he had a guileless heart. And whoever served him, ate his bread, drank from his cup, could in no way, because of his favors, be with or serve another prince: for Vasilko showed abundant mercy to his servants. He had courage and intelligence; righteousness and truth walked with him; and he was skilled in everything.

Then they also brought the head of Grand Prince Yuri and placed it in the coffin with his body. And so, by God’s will, that which you have learned came to an end.

The other accursed Ishmaelites went to Torzhok; and they came and besieged the city, demanding tribute.

And the Tatars pounded it with siege engines for two weeks; the people were exhausted in the city; and there was no help for them from Novgorod; and some were already in confusion and fear.

And then the pagans took the city of Torzhok and cut down everyone, both men and women, priests and monks; and, having lost everything, abused, the Russians accepted a terrible and painful death and gave up their souls to the Lord, on the fifth day of March, in the Week of the Veneration of the Cross. Among the slain were: Ivanko, posadnik of Torzhok, Yakim Vlunkovich, Gleb Borisovich, Mikhail Moiseevich.

Then the godless pursued from Torzhok along the Seliger road to Ignach Cross; and they cut down all people like grass; they were only one hundred versts short of Novgorod, for God and the Most Holy Mother of God preserved it.

From there Batu went and came to the city of Kozelsk; in Kozelsk there was a young prince whose name was Vasily.

The inhabitants of Kozelsk agreed not to surrender to Batu; and they said among themselves: “Though our prince is young, we will give our lives for him; we will receive glory in this world, and in the world to come we will receive heavenly crowns from Christ God.”

The Tatars fought at the city and wanted to capture it; and they broke the city walls and climbed onto the rampart.

Then the inhabitants of Kozelsk fought the Tatars with knives, and having agreed, they came out of the city to them and attacked their regiments; and they cut up the Tatar siege engines; and they killed four thousand Tatars; and they themselves were killed.
