And from Vladimir Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent their envoys to the Tatars at Voronezh.

The princes of Ryazan sent envoys to Prince Yuri of Vladimir, asking him to send them aid or to come himself; but Prince Yuri did not go himself, and did not heed the request of the princes of Ryazan; but he wished to wage the war separately. But it was already impossible to resist God's wrath: He brought confusion, horror, fear, and trembling upon us for our sins.

The princes of Ryazan, Murom, and Pron sk came out against the godless and fought them; and the battle was fierce.

And the godless Ishmaelites won; and the princes fled, each to his own city.

The Tatars were greatly enraged and began to ravage the land of Ryazan still more with great fury; and they destroyed their cities, burned villages, killed people, and took captives.

And the accursed foreigners came to their capital Ryazan; and they surrounded their capital Ryazan on the 6th day of December; and enclosed it with a stockade.

The princes of Ryazan shut themselves in the city with the people and fought stubbornly against the godless Tatars; and they were exhausted.

The Tatars took their city of Ryazan on the 21st day of the same month and burned it entirely; and they killed Grand Prince Yuri Ingvarevich, and his princess, and other princes, and their people with wives and children.

And monks, nuns, and priests the Tatars seized: some they cut down with the sword, and others they shot with arrows and threw into the fire.

And some they seized and bound, and slit open their breasts and took out their livers; and from some they flayed the skin; and into some they drove needles and splinters under the nails; and they committed outrage upon nuns, priests' wives, great princesses, boyars' wives, and simple women and girls before the eyes of mothers and sisters. But God preserved the bishop then; he was away that year when the army surrounded the city.

The Tatars gave many holy churches over to fire, and burned monasteries and villages, and took their property.

Then the Tatars went to Kolomna; and Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent against them his son Prince Vsevolod from Vladimir; and with him went Prince Roman Ingvarevich of Ryazan with his army.

And Grand Prince Yuri sent his commander Yeremey Glebovich ahead with guard detachments; and they joined Vsevolod and Roman Ingvarevich at Kolomna.
