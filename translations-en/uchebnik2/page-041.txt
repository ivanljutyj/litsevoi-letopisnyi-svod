And when he went to Dorogobuzh with Prince Mstislav Yuryevich, Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich fled from Dorogobuzh and came to Lutsk to his uncle Svyatopolk.

But Prince Volodimirko of Galich took away all those towns and came against Svyatopolk Mstislavich at Lutsk; and Svyatopolk Mstislavich and Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich shut themselves up in the city of Lutsk. He, having come, could do nothing to them and went to his domain, to Galich, and installed Prince Mstislav, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, at Peresopnitsa.

In the same year at Ryazan on the Great Vorona they defeated the Cumans. In the same year the Cumans came to aid Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh against Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich and did him much harm.

That same autumn Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky gave to his son, Prince Andrew Bogolyubsky: Turov, Pineg, Dorogobuzh, and Peresopnitsa; and he sat at Peresopnitsa. That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich sent many times to Prince Andrew Yuryevich, saying: “My lord and brother, bring me into peace and love with your father, Prince Yuri Vladimirovich.” But he sent this not sincerely, but cunningly and deceitfully; for the envoys coming from him were inspecting his host, his arms, how his city was fortified, and how and what was being built in it.

And again Izyaslav Mstislavich sent to Prince Andrew Yuryevich Bogolyubsky, saying: “I have no patrimony among the Czechs, nor among the Hungarians and Poles, only in the Russian land; ask peace and love for me from your father, Prince Yuri, and let him give me Pogoryna.” But Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh was angry with him and did not wish to receive him or give him a single volost.

That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich secretly learned that the people of Kiev wanted him to reign among them as grand prince in Kiev; likewise all Vyacheslav’s men and all the Berendei desired him for the great reign in Kiev. He rejoiced greatly and sent to them secretly, saying: “Nothing is so dear to me as your love, and I desire to see your salvation and health, and to please you in Christ in all things, and I love you so that I would lay down my soul for you.”

That same winter the Cumans came into Rus, and having done much harm, went back to their own lands. That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich gathered Hungarians, Poles, and Czechs, and a great host. And he went to Kiev against Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh, and passing by Peresopnitsa, he burned Zarechesk; and arriving, he halted at Mylsk.
