And the Novgorodians sent envoys to Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich in Chernigov. When the envoys were going and were in Smolensk, the Prince of Smolensk would not let them leave Smolensk; and all the roads were closed at the instigation of Yaroslav Vsevolodovich.

Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich of Chernigov learned of this, for he was then on campaign with his son, and he quickly went to Novgorod in Torzhok; and all the people were glad.

And from there Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich came to Novgorod; and the Novgorodians rejoiced, and agreed with him that he would uphold all the liberties of Novgorod and all the former charters of Yaroslav. And the prince gave relief to all the poor and debtors — five years without paying tribute; and those who had fled from the land as debtors were to pay tribute as the former princes had established, or annually without interest.

The Mordvins with Purgas came to Nizhny Novgorod; and the Novgorodians defeated them.

The Mordvins set fire to the Monastery of the Most Holy Mother of God outside the city, and fled away.

Puresh’s son with the Cumans defeated Purgas; and he killed all the Mordvins and all the warriors from Rus in Purgas’s force; and Purgas fled with a few.

About Bishop Kirill

Kirill, Bishop of Rostov, left his place of service and went to Suzdal, to the Monastery of Saint Demetrius, to his cell, intending to treat his illness, since he had pain within him, and his face changed and turned black, and his mouth and nose swelled.

Moreover, another trial befell him: in one day all his wealth was taken from him; Yaroslav adjudged it so in a single court case. Kirill was very rich in kuns, and villages, and all kinds of goods and books, and, plainly speaking, he was as rich as none of the bishops before him in the land of Rostov; and for all this he gave praise to God.

And Bishop Kirill received the monastic rank of schemamonk and distributed the remaining wealth to those who care for the sick and to the beggars.
