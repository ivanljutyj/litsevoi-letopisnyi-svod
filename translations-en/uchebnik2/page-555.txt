My God, against the adversary and my impious enemy. Give me strength of might before the face of my enemies, and exalt the Christian name above the ungodly Hagarenes, and put them to shame forever!" And, having summoned his nobles and all the princes of the Russian land who were under his authority, the prince said to the princes of the Russian land and to his nobles: "It is good for us, brothers, to lay down our heads for the true Christian faith. Let not our cities be captured by the heathen, and let not the holy churches of God be laid waste, and let us not be scattered over the face of the whole earth; let not our wives and children be led into captivity, and let not the ungodly torment us all our days, if the Most Pure Mother of God entreats her Son and our God for us." And the Russian princes and his nobles answered him: "Our lord, the Russian tsar, we have promised, serving you, to lay down our lives, and now for your sake we shall shed our blood and by our blood receive a second baptism." And, armed with the valor of Abraham, having prayed to God and having as helper the great saint, hierarch, and wonderworker Peter, intercessor of the Russian land, he went, like the ancient great prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, against the impious Mamai, a second Svyatopolk. And he met him in the Tatar fields on the river Don, and they clashed like mighty clouds. Weapons flashed like lightning on a rainy day. The warriors fight, entering into hand-to-hand combat; blood flows through the valleys, and the waters of the Don River were dyed with blood; everywhere Tatar heads fell like stones, and the corpses of the ungodly lay like felled oak groves. Many were eyewitnesses of how the angels of God helped the Christians. And God helped the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and so did his kinsmen, the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb. And the accursed Mamai fled from his face. The accursed Svyatopolk vanished no one knows where, and the impious Mamai perished without a trace. And the great prince Dmitry Ivanovich returned with a great victory, like Moses of old, who defeated Amalek. And silence came upon the Russian land. Thus were his enemies put to shame. Other countries, having heard of his victory over the enemy, granted to him by God, all acknowledged his hand, and all the schismatics and rebels of his kingdom perished. The great prince Dmitry Ivanovich had the custom, as David the forefather of God, who spared the children of Saul, so this great prince loved the innocent and forgave the guilty. Like the great Job he was a father to the world; an eye to the blind, a foot to the lame; a support; a guardian and example for all, guiding those subject to him toward the light, having received power by the higher providence, and delivering the human race from all worldly tumults; a high-flying eagle; fire that destroyed dishonor; a bath that washes away filth; a clean threshing floor; a wind that scatters the chaff; rest for those who labor by God's providence; a trumpet that awakens the sleeping; a peaceful commander; a crown of victory; a haven for those who sail; a ship for wealth; a weapon against enemies; a sword of wrath; an unbreakable wall; a net for the evil-minded; an unshakable foundation;
