Rus (1381–1388 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 9

In the year 6881 from Adam (the year 1381 from Christ)

Prince Vladimir Andreevich went to Veliky Novgorod and ruled in Novgorod from the Feast of the Intercession to St Peter’s Day, and then departed again.

In the same year, a ditch was dug around the citadel in Novgorod.

In the same year, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow sent his envoys to the Horde with great gifts; they seized Prince Ivan, son of Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, and brought him to Moscow, where they kept him in harsh conditions.

In the same winter, at the Nativity of Christ, Prince Mikhail Vasilievich of Kashin annulled his treaty with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver and went to Moscow, and from Moscow to the Horde.

In the same winter, Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk reposed. In the same winter, Prince Eremey of Tver reposed. In the same winter, Vasily, Bishop of Tver, reposed.

In the same year, there was great turmoil in the Horde; many Horde princes killed one another, and countless common Tatars were killed. Thus the wrath of God fell upon them for their lawlessness.

In the same year, the Novgorodians who were held in confinement in the cellars in Tver dug a tunnel and fled from Tver.

In the same year, Prince Mikhail Vasilievich of Kashin came from the Horde to Kashin.

In the same year, Tatars came from Mamai with an army against Ryazan, against Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan; they burned the cities subject to him, killed and captured many people, and returned with great booty.
