Osif Zakharyich, the posadnik, fled across the river to Plotnichy End, and the whole Torgovaya Side rose up in his support, and there was great strife for two weeks; and they took the office of posadnik from Osif Zakharyich, and so they made peace and love among themselves; and they gave the office of posadnik to Vasily Ivanovich.

In the year 6896 from Adam (the year 1396 from Christ)

In the month of September, Pimen the Metropolitan sent to Novgorod to the posadniks and the tysyatskys, that they should come to him in Moscow and bring with them those from among whom one was to be chosen as bishop in Novgorod.

That same winter, on the 8th day of the month of January, there came to Moscow with Ivan, abbot of Khutyn, who, God willing, was to be bishop in Novgorod, to the Metropolitan for consecration: the Novgorod posadnik Vasily Fedorovich, the tysyatsky Osif Faleleevich, Iev Avvakumovich, Timofey Ivanovich, and many other boyars and a great multitude of prominent people.

And he was consecrated by Pimen the Metropolitan as archbishop in Novgorod the Great on the 17th day of that same month of January; and at his consecration there were with the Metropolitan the bishops: Feognast, Bishop of Ryazan, Mikhail, Bishop of Smolensk, Savva, Bishop of Sarai, and Nil, Bishop of Zvenigorod; and they consecrated him during the reign of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

That same winter, on the 18th day of the month of January, a son was born to Prince Vladimir Andreevich, Afanasy, whom they nicknamed Yaroslav; from him they were called the Yaroslavichi.

That same winter there arose enmity between Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and his cousin, Prince Vladimir Andreevich; and the senior boyars of Prince Vladimir Andreevich were seized and dispersed to various cities, and they were held captive in great confinement and exhaustion; and very cruel overseers were set over each of them.

That same winter Feognast, Metropolitan of Trebizond, came to Moscow for alms.

That same spring, on the 25th day of the month of March, on the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich concluded peace and reconciliation with his cousin, Prince Vladimir Andreevich.

That same spring, on Palm Sunday, Pimen, Metropolitan of Moscow, consecrated Paul, Archimandrite of the Nativity in Vladimir, as bishop of Kolomna; and in Kiev at that time Cyprian was metropolitan.

That same spring after Easter, Prince Yuri Dmitrievich had a very grave illness, but God had mercy on him, and he recovered again. That same spring there was a great pestilence in Pskov, and the sign of the disease was a swelling.
