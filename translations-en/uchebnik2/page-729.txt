To Prince Vasily Vasilyevich. It was known, my son, also to your father, the son of our humility, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and to your mother, the daughter of our humility, Grand Princess Sophia, and to your uncles, boyars, nobles, and all Orthodox Christians, how I came to this most holy metropolitan see after my brother, Metropolitan Cyprian of Kyiv and All Rus'. And I found nothing in the metropolitan’s church house: everything had fallen into desolation, had been squandered, and had dispersed no one knows where. Therefore I, the humble one, had to undertake much labor. And all that, my son, was gathered into the church house of the Most Pure Mother of God in the patrimony of your great realm from other lands and great principalities, from the Lithuanian land, that was received from noble and pious princes, boyars, nobles, merchants, and Orthodox Christians, men and women, from those who gave for the redemption of the sins of their souls to the house of Christ and His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, gold, silver, pearls, precious stones, and silver vessels, and lands and waters—all this I charge to you, my son, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and to your children and grandchildren, from generation to generation until the end of the ages, that all this be preserved and taken away by no one, because all this was given to God and His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, for the redemption of the sins of Christians. As for my soul, I charge you, my son, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and your children to have care and to make memorial for it. Remember, my son, and do not forget all this: how I brought you forth from the divine font, with God as witness, and how I had care for you, and how I had compassion for you in many matters in these past times. I also bless you and entrust to you the guarding and protecting of my metropolitan boyars, monks, and all servants, from the least to the greatest, so that in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God all may be preserved and no one wronged in anything.
