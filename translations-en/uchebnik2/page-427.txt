Before this the plague was in Nizhny Novgorod, and it came from below, from Bezdezh, to Nizhny Novgorod, and from there to Ryazan and Kolomna, and from there to Pereyaslavl, and from there to Moscow. And so the plague spread through all the cities—to Tver, and Vladimir, and Suzdal, and Dmitrov, and Mozhaysk, and Volok—and a strong and terrible plague spread through all the cities. Alas, alas! Who will be able to tell the terrible and sorrowful tale? And in Beloozero at that time not a single living person remained. And there was great sorrow throughout the whole land, and the whole land became desolate and overgrown with forest; everywhere there were impassable thickets, and they buried ten and twenty people in one grave.

In the same year died Prince Ivan Ivanovich, called the Younger, brother of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, great-great-grandson of Alexander, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and still further descendant of Vladimir Monomakh, Vsevolod, Yaroslav, and Vladimir. And he was buried in Moscow in the Church of the Holy Archangel Michael on the square.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod and Gorodets took monastic vows. This prince was a man of deep spiritual life and had many virtues. In the same year there was a great drought throughout the whole land, and the air became murky, and the earth burned.

In the year 6873 from Adam (the year 1373 from Christ)

His Grace Alexius, Metropolitan of All Rus, laid the foundation of a stone church in the city of Moscow in honor of the miracle of the Archangel Michael that occurred at Colossae, which is in Chonae, and this church was called the Chudov Monastery, and in one year they began building and completed that church.

In the same year there was a sign in the sky: the sun became the color of blood, and black spots appeared on it; darkness lasted half a year; there was great heat and burning; forests with marshes and the earth burned; rivers grew shallow; some springs dried up completely. And there was fear and terror among the people, and great sorrow.

In the same year died the meek, quiet, humble, and renowned for many virtues Grand Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod and Gorodets, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrei, great-great-great-grandson of Alexander, great-great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and further descendant of Yuri Dolgoruky, Vladimir Monomakh, Vsevolod, Yaroslav, and Vladimir, in the monastic and schema order. And he was buried in the Church of the Holy Savior in Nizhny Novgorod, where his father, Grand Prince Konstantin Vasilyevich, was also buried.
