In the same spring, during Bright Week, in Novgorod the water in the Volkhov and in many other rivers turned backward. In the same spring there was a fire in Smolensk. In the same spring there was a fire in Moscow.

In the same year, on the 7th day of the month of June, there was a sign in the sun: first a green darkness descended, after some time it became red like blood; people could not see one another, and all stood as if in blood.

In the same year in Novgorod the posadnik Feodosii died in the monastic rank. In the same year in Novgorod they built a stone church of the Most Holy Trinity in the monastery at Vidogoshche.

In the same year the Novgorodians assembled a veche in Yaroslav’s Court and came with the whole veche to the church of Holy Sophia. And they placed three lots on the throne, having written three names on them: the first, the monk Samson from the monastery of the Holy Savior in Khutyn; the second, the abbot Michael from the church of St Michael on Skovorodka; the third, the abbot Lev from the church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Kolmovo. And the lot fell to the monk Samson. And the Novgorodians joyfully brought him with honors to the archbishop’s court on the 11th day of the month of August, on the commemoration of the holy martyr Euplus the deacon.

In the same month of August in Novgorod the church of the Holy Resurrection of Christ on Red Hill by Plotnitsky End was consecrated; a communal monastery was established there.

In the year 6924 from Adam (1424 from Christ)

In the month of September they built in Novgorod two stone churches — of the Holy Exaltation and of St Luke at Lyudin End.

In the same autumn, because of our sins, the Lord God allowed the Kievan metropolis to be divided into two parts, which ought not to have happened. It is said in the sacred canons that “it is not fitting for there to be two metropolitans in one province.” Vytautas Keistutovich, Grand Duke of Lithuania, gathered the Orthodox bishops of his land: Isaac of Chernigov, Theodosius of Polotsk, Dionysius of Lutsk, Gerasimus of Vladimir, John of Galich, Sebastian of Smolensk, Chariton of Kholm, Paul of Cherven, Euthymius of Turov, and said to them: “Install for me as metropolitan of Kiev the Bulgarian Gregory Tsamblak.” But they did not want to do this, fearing God and the sacred and divine canons handed down, and they said together in council to Vytautas: “We fear God and His sacred canons left to us, therefore we do not fulfill your command.” He said to them: “Tell me what is said in these canons.” They answered: “It is written in the sacred canons, in the first part, chapter 20, and in the canons…”
