In the same year Prince Andrei Dmitrievich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, came to Novgorod and remained in Novgorod from the day of St Vladimir to the day of Simeon the Summer-ender; great honors were shown him by the Vladyka and the Novgorodians, and then he returned to Moscow.

In the same year in Novgorod a side-chapel of the Holy Martyr Catherine and of St Alexis, the Man of God, was added to the stone church of the Most Pure Mother of God.

In the same year Vladyka John, Archbishop of Novgorod, together with the Novgorodians built the stone church of the Honorable and Glorious Veil of the Most Pure Mother of God at Zverinets, and Vladyka John himself consecrated it.

In the same year the stone church of the Holy Archangel Michael in Moscow was painted by the master Theophanes the Greek.

In the same year there was a sign in the heavens in the sun. In the same year Tsar Temir-Kutluy sent his envoys to the Grand Prince of Lithuania Vytautas Kestutovich, grandson of Gediminas, saying: “Hand over to me the fugitive Tsar Tokhtamysh; he is my enemy, and I cannot endure knowing that he is alive and lives with you. Life changes: today you are a tsar, and tomorrow a fugitive; today you are rich, and tomorrow poor; today you have friends, and tomorrow enemies. I fear my own, not strangers; Tsar Tokhtamysh is a stranger to me and a wicked enemy. Hand him over to me, and take for yourself all that he has.” Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich heard such words and gave his answer to Tsar Temir-Kutluy’s envoy, saying: “I will not hand over Tsar Tokhtamysh, but I wish to meet Tsar Temir-Kutluy myself.”

And Vytautas set out with his princes and with all the Lithuanian forces, and with him also Tsar Tokhtamysh with his court; their army was vast. And they went against the Tatar land to Temir-Kutluy with boasting and pride, saying boastfully: “We go to take the Tatar land captive, to defeat Tsar Temir-Kutluy, to seize his kingdom, to divide his wealth and property, to set Tsar Tokhtamysh to rule in the Horde, and in Caffa, and in Azov, and in the Crimea, and in Astrakhan, and in the Zayaitskaya Horde, and in all the coastal lands, and in Kazan. And all will belong to us, and the tsar will be ours, and we shall possess not only the Lithuanian and Polish lands, but also the North, Great Novgorod and Pskov, and the Germans, and all the great Russian principalities; from all the great Russian princes we shall collect tribute and dues, and they will submit to us, serve us, and fulfill our will, all that we desire and command them.” And with such words they went to conquer the Tatars and Tsar Temir-Kutluy and went far into the Tatar Field.
