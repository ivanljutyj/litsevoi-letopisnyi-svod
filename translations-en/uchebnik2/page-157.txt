In the same year the tonsure was held for Great Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, and for his son Prince Yuri in the city of Suzdal; on that same day he was also mounted on a horse. The blessed John, Bishop of Rostov, presided then.

Great Prince Vsevolod laid out Suzdal, and in the same year the city was built of timber.

On the Church of the Nativity of the [Mother of God] in Vladimir

In the same year Great Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, founded in Vladimir a stone church in the name of the Nativity of the Most Holy Mother of God, on the twenty-third day of August, with the blessing of his bishop Luke of Rostov.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, brother-in-law of Prince Vsevolod, went to Luki, summoned by the Polotsk princes and the people of Polotsk, and took the Novgorodians with him. They met at the frontier and decided among themselves in love and counsel that in winter all would gather and go against Lithuania or the Chud.

And that winter Yaroslav set out with the Novgorodians and Pskovians and with the whole region against the Chud, captured the city of Yuryev, burned their land, and brought back countless captives; they themselves all returned unharmed.

In the year 6700 from Adam (1200 from Christ)

Prince Yaroslav went to Pskov on St Peter’s Day, taking with him a few Novgorodians.

And he himself sat in Pskov, and sent his court to campaign together with the Pskovians.

And they captured the city of Bear’s Head (Odenpe), and burned it, and themselves returned unharmed.

In the same year there was a great fire in Vladimir on the twenty-third day of July; the city caught fire at midnight and burned almost until evening. Fourteen churches burned, and half the city was burned out, and the prince’s court was barely saved.

In the same year Abbot Martiry built a stone church of the Holy Savior in Russa, on the island.

On the Citadel in Vladimir. In the year 6701 from Adam (1201 from Christ)

Great Prince Vsevolod founded the citadel in Vladimir on the fourth day of June; then also the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God in Vladimir was renewed by Bishop John, which had burned in the great fire, and it was now like new.
