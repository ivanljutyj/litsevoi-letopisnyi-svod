In the same year the Germans came from across the sea with an army and attacked the Novgorod volosts and villages, and having done much evil, returned to their own lands.

In the same year Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich Kholmsky married—grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav Yaroslavich—and was wed in Tver in the Church of the Great Saviour by Vladyka Arsenius, former protodeacon of Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus.

In the same year Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver married his son in Moscow to the daughter of Fyodor Koshka, son of Andrei Sobakin, and he was wed by Vladyka Arsenius in Tver in the Church of the Great Saviour.

In the same year Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod came from the Horde from Tsar Tokhtamysh to his patrimony in Nizhny Novgorod with a charter of grant. In the same year the Tatars went against Ryazan.

In the year 6900 from Adam (the year 1400 from Christ)

Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, appointed Theodosius as bishop in Polotsk.

In the same autumn there died in Lithuania the Grand Princess Uliana, wife of Olgerd Gediminovich—daughter of Grand Prince Alexander of Tver, granddaughter of Michael, great-granddaughter of Yaroslav Yaroslavich—named Marina in monasticism, and she was buried in Kiev in the Pechersk Lavra.

There died Matthew the Greek, Metropolitan of Adrianople, on Friday of the sixth week after Easter, and he was buried in the Chudov Monastery.

There died Paul, Bishop of Kolomna. In the same year Metropolitan Cyprian appointed Gregory as bishop in Kolomna.

In the same autumn there died Euthymius Vislen, former Bishop of Tver, in the Chudov Monastery in Moscow, where Metropolitan Cyprian had ordered him to live in his monastery. In it he also died and was buried behind the altar.

In the same autumn there was a battle between Tsar Tokhtamysh and Tsar Aksak-Temir. And Tsar Tokhtamysh was defeated, and his numerous army was cut down by Tsar Aksak-Temir.

The stone church of the Dormition of the Theotokos in Kolomna was painted, which Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich had built.
