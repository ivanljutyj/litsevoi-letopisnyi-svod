At that time, by God's providence, Kirill, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, happened to be there, and he did not allow them to shed blood, but pacified them and brought them to agreement. And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich came to love Kirill.

And Prince Yuri entreated Metropolitan Kirill to go with him to Vladimir and bless him there; and Kirill went with him to Vladimir and blessed him.

And Metropolitan Kirill made Mitrofan, abbot of the Nativity Monastery, bishop of Suzdal, Vladimir, and Pereyaslavl.

And Metropolitan Kirill was greatly honored, and he left Vladimir. Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich himself escorted him, with his children and his brothers, with great honor.

Metropolitan Kirill also visited many other cities, and then went to Kiev.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent his brothers, Prince Svyatoslav and Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich, against the Mordvins; they took many villages and returned to their own land.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went from Novgorod with the Novgorodians overseas against the Yem tribe, and ravaged that land; and he took so many captives that they could not carry them all out because of the great number; and they cut down the captives, and some they released back.

In the same year in Novgorod they built a stone church of Saint James in the Nerevsky End.

In the year 6735 from Adam (1235 from Christ)

Sorcerers, healers, and wizards appeared in Novgorod, and they performed much sorcery, spells, and false signs, and did much evil, deceiving many. And the Novgorodians gathered and seized them and led them to the archbishop's house; and the people of Prince Yaroslav intervened in the inquiry about the sorcerers.

Then the Novgorodians brought the sorcerers to Yaroslav's Court; and they lit a great bonfire in Yaroslav's courtyard; and they bound all the sorcerers and threw them into the fire; and there they all burned.

In the same year the city of Vladimir burned, and 27 churches burned, and the court of the blessed Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich burned, and the Church of the Archangel Michael on his court, which he had built and adorned most wondrously and marvelously.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent his nephew Prince Vsevolod Konstantinovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, to Pereyaslavl-Russky to reign.
