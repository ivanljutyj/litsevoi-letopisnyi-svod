He also sent his nephew Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, grandson of Yuri and great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, to reign over them in Novgorod. They, having come with him to Novgorod, confirmed him by kissing the cross on all their terms, that he should reign over them.

That same year Prince Svyatoslav, son of Rostislav, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, fled from Ladoga to his father in Kiev.

That same year the Grand Prince of Kiev, Rostislav Mstislavich, took to himself from the Grand Prince of Chernigov, Svyatoslav Olegovich, his son Oleg, saying: “Let all people know that we have love and concord in Christ between us,” because the Grand Prince of Kiev, Rostislav Mstislavich, loved the Grand Prince of Chernigov, Svyatoslav Olegovich.

Prince Oleg Svyatoslavich, grandson of Oleg, stayed in Kiev with Rostislav Mstislavich for three days in love and great honor, and on the fourth day one of Rostislav’s former men, instructed by the devil, said to him: “I have, prince, a word for you; if you tell no one, I will reveal it to you.” He swore to him, and he said: “Prince, beware; they want to seize you.”

He was frightened and trembled, and went to the Grand Prince Rostislav Mstislavich, saying: “My mother is very ill; release me to Chernigov.” He long tried to dissuade him, but could not detain him and let him go, not knowing his thoughts.

He came to Chernigov and told everything to his father, the Grand Prince of Chernigov, Svyatoslav Olegovich; and he asked leave of him for Kursk, and conspired with Izyaslav Davidovich. The Grand Prince Izyaslav Davidovich sent him a message of love and joined with him; Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich also joined with him.

Hearing this, his father Svyatoslav Olegovich was offended and grieved; and his men said to him: “Why, lord prince, do you complain of your nephew, Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, and of your son, Prince Oleg, and do not care for yourself? Do you not know that Prince Roman, son of Rostislav, sent to Prince Izyaslav Davidovich, saying: ‘My father gives you the great reign of Chernigov; live with me in love and unity; and Prince Rostislav Mstislavich himself wanted to take your son Prince Oleg; for you are ruining your authority by holding to Rostislav Mstislavich, because he is crafty and stitches together deceit and sets a snare to catch you and your children.’ And so evil people set them at odds and forced them to renounce love.”

The Grand Prince of Chernigov, Svyatoslav Olegovich, believing this to be true, broke with the Grand Prince of Kiev, Rostislav Mstislavich, and joined Izyaslav Davidovich. Izyaslav Davidovich and Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich and Oleg Svyatoslavich went with the Polovtsians to Pereyaslavl.
