The princes Georgy and Yaroslav sent their army to Ingvar as aid, and with it the commander Yakun Timofeevich.

Prince Ingvar of Ryazan went with these troops against the Polovtsians; when the Polovtsians heard of this, they fled; and those of them who did not escape were killed by our men, and they returned to their own lands.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev, Mstislav Romanovich, grandson of Rostislav, great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, ancestor of Yaroslav, great-great-ancestor of the great Vladimir, sent to Novgorod for his elder son Svyatoslav, and gave the Novgorodians his younger son Vsevolod.

The Novgorodians, having obeyed Grand Prince Mstislav, fulfilled his will and received Vsevolod with honor to reign among them.

In the same year the foundation was laid for the stone Church of Saint Michael in Novgorod by Tverdislav and Feodor; and they founded another church—of the Three Youths.

In the same year Anthony, Archbishop of Novgorod, went to Torzhok; and the Novgorodians brought Archbishop Mitrofan again to the bishop’s house and installed him in the episcopal seat.

And to Anthony the Novgorodians sent envoys with the words: “Take a place where you wish.” Anthony came to them in Novgorod and took a place at the Church of the Holy Savior in Nereditsy.

The prince and the Novgorodians said to both archbishops, Mitrofan and Anthony: “Go to Kiev to Metropolitan Matthew of Kiev and all Rus; whomever he sends us shall be our lord.” And they sent with them the hieromonk Vassian, and also the priest Boris, and Yakun Matfeev.

In the same year in Novgorod they made Semyon Borisov posadnik, and Kozma Zhidyata tysyatsky.

In the year 6727 from Adam (1227 from Christ)

The Church of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God in the monastery was consecrated by Bishop Simon; in the time of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich and his brother Yaroslav Vsevolodovich of Pereyaslavl; the abbot then was Mitrofan.
