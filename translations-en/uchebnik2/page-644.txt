And he ordered his boyars to violate the cross-kissing oath and tear up the agreement with his brothers: with Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, with Prince Feodor Mikhailovich, and with Prince Ivan Borisovich. Prince Vasily Mikhailovich came to his mother, Grand Princess Avdotya, and said to her: “The boyars of our brother have renounced the cross-kissing oath. But our father bequeathed that he should be good to us.” And their mother, Grand Princess Avdotya, sent her boyars to her son Prince Ivan Mikhailovich, and her children—Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, Prince Feodor Mikhailovich—and her grandson Ivan Borisovich sent their boyars with these words: “Lord Grand Prince, against the will of our father, Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich, your boyars have renounced the cross-kissing oath; and you, Lord Grand Prince, should order your boyars to keep the cross-kissing oath and follow the testament of our father.” Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich answered them thus: “Our boyars renounced the cross-kissing oath at my word.”

And after this Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich began to be at enmity with his mother Evdokia and with his brothers Vasily and Feodor, and with his nephew Prince Ivan Borisovich, harboring hostility against them. Princess Borisova, by birth from Smolensk, took her son, while her boyar Voronets, like the ancient Brutus, came to Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich and petitioned him, saying: “We, lord, did not send our boyars to you.” And from that time the Grand Prince began to hold great hostility against his brothers, and he loved the flattery of these deceitful men.

In the same year Prince Vasily Mikhailovich came for the anniversary of his father’s death to Kashin, to his patrimony. In the same year Ivan, Archbishop of Novgorod, founded the detinets in the city.

In the same year in Novgorod the Church of the Holy Resurrection of Christ at the entrance was painted by command of Ivan, Archbishop of Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians sent their envoy Klimenty Vasilyevich to Lithuania to Vytautas Kestutovich concerning the conclusion of peace, and they signed peace according to the old custom.

In the same year, within the bounds of Chervleny Yar and at the watchposts near the Khoper along the Don, Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich with the princes of Pronsk, Murom, and Kozelsk killed many Tatars, captured Tsarevich Mamat-Saltan, and seized other Horde princes.

In the same year the supporters of Grand Prince Yuri Svyatoslavich of Smolensk grieved for him, wishing to see him among them at the grand principality of Smolensk. And they secretly sent to him in Ryazan, because he was then living with his father-in-law in Ryazan, while Vytautas held Smolensk.
