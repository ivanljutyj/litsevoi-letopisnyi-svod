And he himself went to Novgorod Seversky, intending to take it with bare hands; but, having exalted himself, he was humbled, for he accomplished nothing, since Novgorod Seversky was strongly fortified, and Grand Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich was invincible. And so Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev was afraid, and began to send to Grand Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich, asking for peace; and so, having made peace, he returned to Kiev, while Vasilko went to his father Yuri Dolgoruky in Suzdal.

In the same year the Lord God glorified His name and exalted the Christian race, granting help against the Cumans to Prince Mstislav, son of Izyaslav, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh: he slew many Cumans, and took others alive, and drove others far away, burned their tents and took much booty—horses and oxen, and camels, and sheep—and rescued a multitude of captive Christians, and returned to his grand principality in Pereyaslavl; and all the captives he had taken from the Cumans, a great multitude, he released, each to his own home.

And he himself went to Kiev to his father, Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, and glorified the Lord God for such help, and the Most Pure Mother of God, and all the saints, and rejoiced with great joy; and to the sorrowful, the grieving, the poor, and the needy, as to his own brothers, he showed great love and mercy.

In the same year Grand Prince Vladimirko of Galich reposed.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky of Suzdal was with his army before Chernigov, and returned to Suzdal to his grand principality, and, having come, built many churches: on the Nerl—[a church of] the holy passion-bearers Boris and Gleb; and in his own name he founded the city of Yuryev, called Polsky, and built in it a stone church of Saint George; and in Vladimir he built also a stone church of Saint George; and he moved the city of Pereyaslavl from Kleshchin and built it larger than the old one, and set up in it a stone church of the Holy Savior; and in Suzdal he set up a stone church of the Holy Savior.

In the same year the great cycle of the world ended.

In the year 6661 from Adam (the year 1161 from Christ)

Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev sent his son Prince Mstislav against the Cumans to the Desla, because much evil was being done by them; but he, not reaching them, turned back.

In the same year his father sent him to meet his new wife, who was his stepmother, with Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Monomakh, and with the Berendei; and they went as far as Oleshye, and, not finding her, returned back.
