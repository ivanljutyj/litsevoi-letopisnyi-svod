And we kissed the table on which the holy Passion of Christ is laid. And then we kissed St Arsenius the Patriarch and many saints. We also kissed Abraham’s table, at which he entertained Christ God, who appeared in the Trinity. We also kissed the iron bed on which Christ’s martyrs were burned. And we spent the whole morning in the church, bowing down and marveling at the miracles of the saints and at the grandeur and immeasurable beauty of the church.

After hearing the holy Liturgy, we went out of the church to the court of Constantine and saw there the imperial building. Here is the imperial arena, which is called the Hippodrome, and there stands a bronze column, as if twisted of three strands; at the top those strands were separated, and on each end of the strands was a serpent’s head adorned with stones and pearls. In that same column serpent’s venom was sealed up. There were also many other columns, of stone and bronze, and many wonders. And we looked at them for a long time, marveling.

Also, on the first day of the month of July, on the commemoration of the holy wonderworkers Cosmas and Damian, we went to the monastery of St John, which is called in Greek Prodromos, and in Russian the Forerunner. And there we bowed down and kissed, and the Russians living there well gave us rest. On the third day of the same month—the second day—on the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Pure Mother of God, we went to Blachernae and kissed there the holy reliquary in which lie the robe and belt of the Most Pure Mother of God. From there we also went to the Church of the Apostles and bowed down, and with great fear and trembling kissed the holy column to which our Lord Jesus Christ was bound and beaten in His saving voluntary Passion, and He received forty full wounds, showing forth death for our salvation. Here also is the stone of Peter the chief apostle, on which he wept bitterly over his denial during Christ’s voluntary Passion. We also bowed down to the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God which appeared to a holy elder in the desert. We also saw there the royal tombs of St Constantine the Great, equal to the apostles, and of Theodosius the Great, and of Theodosius the Small, and of many others.

We also saw in that same quarter a small church, in which is a great image of the Savior, from which came a voice of forgiveness to a man lying on a bed and repenting in faith of his sins.
