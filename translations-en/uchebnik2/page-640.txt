He walked after it, and with frequent sighs of the heart and abundant tears he prayed and thanked the Lord God that he had been vouchsafed to see this holy icon, on which the Last Judgment was painted, and which had been sent by the holy patriarch and the Ecumenical Council, as was written in the charter, and that a blessing had been granted to him. And having obtained his desire, as David said, he rejoiced as though he had found great spoil and wealth. And they placed the honorable icon near the holy altar on the right side. The bishop and the whole assembly, in festal vestments, offered up songs of thanksgiving, praying to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, and on that same day they held an honorable feast.

And after the liturgy he summoned to his dinner his bishop Arsenius, and with him the archimandrites and hegumens, priests and deacons, and all the church clergy, and some of the monastic order, and also the envoys of the holy patriarch, and he arranged an honorable dinner, a great feast for the bishop and the whole assembly. And he forced himself to sit with them at the dinner, though he was already greatly weakened and asked to be supported. He also ordered another meal to be prepared for the poor, the wretched, the lame, the blind, and the needy, and that they be fed to abundance, and he ordered alms to be distributed to them.

After dinner, having drunk together with all those present at the dinner, he began to take leave and to ask forgiveness of all, handing each one a cup from his own hands and kissing him, saying: “Forgive me and bless me.” And they all could not restrain themselves and wept from pity. And so, having kissed all in order, he took leave of all, from small to great, and dismissed them. They went away weeping.

Last of all he kissed his children, his boyars, his servants, and all his people, and instructing his children, he said: “My children! Make it your custom to have good love for God, give alms, love truth, and keep chastity and brotherly love.” And he enjoined them to love and honor brother, and not to offend him, and that they should all obey the elder brother. And to his boyars he said: “And you, brothers, remind my children to live in love, as I have instructed them.” And he assigned to each a portion of his patrimony: to his son, Prince Ivan, and to his children Alexander and Ivan — Tver, Novy Gorodok, Zubtsov, Radilov, Vbran, Opoki, and Vertyazin. And to Prince Vasily and Boris, and to his son Ivan — Kashin and Kosnyatin. And to his son Feodor — the two towns of Mikuliny with their volosts, and he wrote a spiritual charter, according to which they were to rule and live, not transgressing my word and the spiritual charter.

And rising, he went to the cathedral church of the Holy Savior, and prayed before the image of the Savior and of the Most Pure Theotokos, and before the other saints. And then he began to bow at the tombs of the great princes: his grandfather Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich and his father Alexander Mikhailovich. He came to the pillar where on the right side Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob are depicted, and beneath that place he indicated that he was to be laid.
