In the year 6746 from Adam (1246 from Christ)

Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, brother of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, came from Kiev to Vladimir and became grand prince; and the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God preserved him from the Tatars, and with him his eight sons: Alexander Nevsky, Andrew of Suzdal, Constantine, Athanasius, Daniel, Michael, Yaroslav of Tver, Vasily; and his brother Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, with his son Dmitry; and another brother of his, Ivan Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky; and his nephew Prince Vladimir Konstantinovich, grandson of Vsevolod; and two sons of his nephew Vasilko Konstantinovich of Rostov: Boris and Gleb; and the son of his nephew Prince Vsevolod Konstantinovich of Rostov, Vasily; and there were in all fifteen princes not killed by the Tatars.

Yaroslav, son of Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, revived the land of Suzdal; and he cleansed the churches of the bodies of the dead, and buried their bones; and he comforted refugees from other lands; and he gathered many people; and there was great joy among Christians, whom God delivered with His mighty hand from the godless Tatars.

Then Yaroslav gave Suzdal to his brother Svyatoslav, and to his other brother, Ivan, he gave Starodub, and sent each of them to rule in his own place.

And in Rostov began to rule Boris Vasilkovich, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of the great Vladimir; and Prince Boris’s brother, Prince Gleb Vasilkovich, began to rule in Belozersk. And that whole year was quiet and peaceful; and the Tatars did not come with war.

And after Yaroslav, Prince Michael of Chernigov, son of Vsevolod Chermny, became grand prince in Kiev.

In the year 6747 from Adam (1247 from Christ)

Grand Prince Yaroslav sent men to Rostov; and he commanded that the body of his brother, Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, be brought to Vladimir.
