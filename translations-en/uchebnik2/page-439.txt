In the year 6877 from Adam (1377 from Christ)

The Germans seized the Lithuanian city of Koven. In the same year Prince Lyub of Smolensk died. In the same year Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew, built in Gorodets the cathedral church of the Holy Archangel Michael. In the same year the city of Tver was rebuilt in wood and coated with clay.

In the same winter the Novgorodians with an army approached the German city of Oreshek, but, having no success there, returned back. In the same year Veliky Novgorod burned: the citadel and the archbishop’s court, and Holy Sophia, and the Nerevsky End, and the Plotnitsky End; many people perished.

In the same year the foundation was laid for the stone church of St Basil on Yaryshkina Street, and another, the church of St Evpaty on Rogatitsa.

In the same year the Novgorodians went with the Muscovites to the German New Town, but had no success there, and many of them were killed, and they withdrew.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich founded the city of Pereyaslavl, and in one year the city was built. In the same year Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich of Tver, grandson of Michael, great-grandson of Yaroslav, went to Moscow to Metropolitan Alexy to complain about his Tver hierarch Vasily.

In the same year Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania went with an army against the Germans and captured many prisoners. In the same year the Muscovites and Volochans fought over the Smolensk lands.

In the year 6878 from Adam (1378 from Christ)

The Horde prince Mamai installed another tsar, Mamat-Saltan, in his Horde.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gathered many warriors and sent an army to the city of Bryansk.

In the same autumn Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod gathered a large army and sent his brother Prince Boris Konstantinovich and his son Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and with them the tsar’s envoy Achi-Khoja, against the Bulgar prince Asan, who ruled the people now called Kazanians.

Prince Asan of Kazan sent out an envoy to meet them with a petition and many gifts. They did not accept the gifts, but installed Saltan, son of Bak, as prince, and themselves returned to Nizhny Novgorod to Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew, great-great-great-grandson of Alexander.

In the same autumn there were many rains, and there was a severe flood.
