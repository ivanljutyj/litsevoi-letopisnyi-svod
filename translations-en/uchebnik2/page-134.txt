Before reaching Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, the retinue lowered its banners to the ground, and they fled, driven by the wrath of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God, since the Mother of God did not permit the wrong done to Her House. If the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos, do not allow even simple people to be wronged, all the more do They not allow sacred and divine things to be wronged.

Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, however, fled to Novgorod, and his brother Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich to Ryazan, while the Vladimirians captured their mother together with her daughters-in-law; Mikhailko’s retinue set off in pursuit, fought, and took captives. Thus Prince Mikhailko Yuryevich entered Vladimir; priests and monks with crosses and a great multitude of people came out to meet him, and Prince Mikhailko Yuryevich entered with his brother Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich on the sixteenth day of June, a Sunday. Prince Mikhailko Yuryevich with his brother Vsevolod Yuryevich returned to the Most Pure Mother of God the cities, volosts, villages, and established tributes, all that Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich had taken from the Most Pure Mother of God, forgetting what is said: it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God. And there was great joy in the city of Vladimir, for the people saw in their city the great prince of all the Rostov land.

Truly one should marvel at this, for it came about not by human design but by God’s providence and that of His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos, that the weak people of Vladimir defeated the strong; because the ancient and strong cities of Rostov and Suzdal, and all their boyars, wanted to establish their own truth, not submitting to God’s truth, shamelessly saying: “The city of Vladimir is our suburb, and in it live our slaves, stonecutters and carpenters, and ploughmen.” So they spoke, opposing God and the Most Holy Theotokos, listening to evil men who wished good neither to us nor to this city nor to all who live in it. This city was founded by the great and blessed Vladimir, and later by Prince Andrew, son of Yuri Dolgoruky; and Prince Mikhailko was also chosen by the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos, as it is said in the Gospel: I confess to Thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that Thou hast hidden these things from the wise and understanding and revealed them unto babes. So too now the wise and understanding, the rich and strong, the ancient Rostovites and Suzdalians, and all the elders acted, not guided by God’s truth and not submitting to His will; but the new, small and insignificant people of Vladimir, having understood God’s will, fell before Him with fear and trembling and with tears, and the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother did not let them be wronged. For whatever a man asks of God with all his heart, the Lord God will not deprive him of it, just as the Lord God did not deprive the Vladimirians of their good desire and glorified them throughout all the land; saying: “Those who glorify Me,” He said, “I will glorify, and those who reproach Me shall be reproached and dishonored.”
