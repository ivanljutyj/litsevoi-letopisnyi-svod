In the year 6722 from Adam (the year 1222 from Christ)

There was a terrible thunder on Cheesefare Sunday, on the first day of February. In that same year there was a sign on the twenty-fifth day of March: at the Annunciation, in the morning, a three-day-old moon appeared above the sun, and it began to grow quickly, and before it had become full, a red cloud came over it, and after staying in it a little, withdrew, and the moon became small, as if three days old. And it began to grow, and before it had reached its fullness, again by three days, the same red cloud came over it. And so it happened four times, and all the people who saw it marveled and were terrified. In that same year John, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir, left his bishopric and went to a monastery at Bogolyubovo.

In that same year there was a fire in Vladimir; two hundred households and four churches burned.

In that same year the Hungarian king installed his son in Galich and turned the churches to the Latin rite.

In that same year Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov began to plan war. And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich of Vladimir began to gather a great host against him.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich went to Rostov with his brothers Svyatoslav, Yaroslav, and Ivan.

Hearing of this, Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich sent his regiment against Kostroma, burned it all, and carried off the people.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich came to Rostov and fought beyond the Ishna River. He stood there several days and did much harm to that land, putting districts and villages to fire.

And after this they made peace and confirmed it by kissing the cross. And from there he went to Moscow against his brother Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich.

And they removed him from Moscow and sent him to rule in Pereyaslavl Rusky, his patrimony.

In that same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavich went with the Novgorodians on campaign against the Chud and Nereva through Chud land to the sea. He ravaged their districts and villages, took their fortifications, and stood before their town of Vorobeyn, and the Chud submitted to him.

And Prince Mstislav Mstislavich took tribute from them and gave two parts of the tribute to the Novgorodians. And he gave the third part to the courtiers. Also with him went Prince Vsevolod Borisovich of Pskov with the Pskovians and Prince David of Toropets, brother of Vladimir, with the Toropchans, and the warriors returned home with great booty and wealth.
