In the same year the Grand Princess Theodosia, wife of Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, reposed in Novgorod, and she was laid in the Yuryev Monastery; in monasticism she received the name Euphrosyne, since names were then given not from the first word, but on whichever day they took monastic vows, according to that day the name was given, or afterward on the same day.

In the year 6753 from Adam (1253 from Christ)

Prince Konstantin Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, came from Batu’s Horde, from the khan’s sons, to Vladimir to his father Yaroslav Vsevolodovich with honor.

In the same year the Lithuanians conquered Torzhok and the districts of Bezhetsk, and Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich set out in pursuit of them with warriors from Torzhok; and there was a battle, and the Lithuanians prevailed, and killed many, and took horses, and went to their own land. And then the Tver voivodes Yavedey and Kerbet set out in pursuit of them with warriors from Tver and from Dmitrov, and Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich with warriors from Torzhok; and there was a great battle near Toropets, and they fought all day, and the Lithuanians prevailed, and the princes fled to Toropets.

That same night Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich came to their aid with the Novgorodians, and there was great joy, and embracing, they greeted one another; and in the morning they went out, arrayed against the Lithuanians; and there was a great battle and a fierce slaughter, and they defeated the Lithuanians, and took all their booty from them, and cut down the Lithuanian prince.

The Novgorodians returned from there; and Prince Alexander with his men set out in pursuit of the Lithuanians and defeated them near the town of Zhizhich; and there were few who escaped.

And Prince Alexander took his son from Vitebsk and went to Novgorod; and he met another army, and was not afraid to march against them, and joined battle with them, a great battle, and with God’s help and that of the Most Pure Mother of God he overcame the enemies, and came with joy to his own land.
