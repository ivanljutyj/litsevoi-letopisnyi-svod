They trusted that the Lady and Mistress, the Most Pure Mother of God, by her coming to the humble and sorrowful, could turn sorrow into joy and deliver from every evil now and forever. And with such faith and hope they made known their God-pleasing thoughts to the princes, boyars, and others who were with them, there where each of them was. And this God-pleasing deed pleased all.

Then the Grand Prince sent to his father, Metropolitan Cyprian, with news asking him to send to the glorious city of Vladimir for the icon of the Mother of God. His Eminence Metropolitan Cyprian thanked God that their God-like thoughts and desires coincided.

And having prayed, the metropolitan at once sent to the renowned city of Vladimir. Those sent arrived there just on the very feast of the all-honorable Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, and announced everything to the sacred council, and together with all performed the all-night vigil, and celebrated the divine liturgy in the cathedral church of the all-honorable Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God.

The Farewell to the Wonder-working Icon of the Mother of God from Vladimir

After the celebration of the divine liturgy, the archpriest and priests serving there went and took from the icon-case the most wondrous icon of the Mother of God, and carried it with honors from the city of Vladimir with crosses, icons, candles, and censers. And all, from the greatest to the least, escorted it for a long way with tears, because it was hard to part with such a priceless treasure, that healing icon. With fear and love they fell down before it, saying with tender compunction: “Where are you going, O Lady, leaving us orphans? Why, O Queen, do you turn your face away from us and forget our poverty and our sorrow? Drive away the fear and trembling that assail us, calm the wrath of God that advances upon us, and deliver us from destruction. Quell among us internecine strife and seditions; grant to us, your servants, stillness and peace. Let this miracle also be added to your deeds, and we will always proclaim Your miracles. And now come, O Lady, with Your pre-eternal Infant to this place where You were pleased to choose for Yourself a dwelling place of Your man-loving Son, Christ our God; save and preserve Your servant, the right-believing Grand Prince Vasily, and his children from generation to generation and for ever, and all his realm from the present Tatar invasion of Temir-Aksak. And always throughout the ages preserve your inheritance from all misfortunes and sorrows and from all enemies visible and invisible. And do not forsake us, O Lady, who hope in You!”

And after this, a great multitude of people, parted from the miraculous image of Christ and the Mother of God, which the eyes cannot gaze upon enough, wailing bitterly, with difficulty returned to their city of Vladimir.
