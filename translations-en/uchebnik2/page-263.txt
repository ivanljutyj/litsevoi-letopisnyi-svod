That same winter Batu’s Tatars took the Mordvin land, and they burned Murom, and they ravaged the lands along the Klyazma River.

And the city of the Most Holy Mother of God, Gorokhovets, the Tatars burned, and they went back to their own lands. There was then fear and terror throughout all the land; and the people themselves did not know who was fleeing where, from fear.

In the same year the church of the Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb in Kideksha was consecrated by Bishop Kirill of Rostov.

In the same year Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went to Kamenets; and he took the city; and he returned to his land with a multitude of captives.

In the same year Batu’s Tatars came into the Ryazan land, and they seized all of it.

In the year 6748 from Adam (the year 1248 from Christ)

Tsar Batu sent his commander Mengukak to look at the city of Kiev; and he came and stood on that side of the Dnieper River by the city of Pesochna.

And Mengukak saw the city of Kiev, and he marveled at its beauty and greatness, and he said to himself and to those close to him who were with him: “Truly this is a wondrous place, and the beauty of this city, and its greatness; and if these people knew the power and resolve of Tsar Batu, they would submit to him, and this city and this place would not be devastated.” At that time the Grand Prince in Kiev was Michael, son of Vsevolod Chermny.

And Mengukak sent envoys to Michael with these words: “If you want this city to remain intact, go and submit, and bow to our Tsar Batu; I advise you this as a dear friend.”

And Grand Prince Michael Vsevolodovich sent envoys to Mengukak with these words: “You yourself know, Mengukak, commander of ten thousand warriors, that Tsar Batu has his own faith, and I have the pious Christian faith of Christ. And since our faiths are at enmity, I will not submit to Tsar Batu or bow down to him. And what friendship can I have with you? The Apostle Paul teaches me and says: ‘Do not be partners with unbelievers. For what fellowship has righteousness with lawlessness? What communion has light with darkness? What agreement has Christ with the devil? What part has the faithful with the unbeliever? What accord has the Church of God with idols?’”

And Mengukak, hearing these words, was greatly enraged; and those close to him said: “It is unreasonable for you to be angry over this; but send to Michael again with deceit, and having outwitted him, you will execute him as you wish.”

And Mengukak sent his envoys to Michael with deceit and with these words: “I want to tell you something; come to me yourself.” And Michael seized his envoys and killed them, and he himself fled from the city of Kiev after his son
