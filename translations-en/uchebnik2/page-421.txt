In the same year Prince Konstantin of Rostov came from the Horde from the Tsar with honor and with a grant charter for the whole Principality of Rostov.

In the same year robbers came from Veliky Novgorod to Zhukotin, slew many Tatars, and seized their wealth. And because of that robbery of theirs, the Christians in Bulgar were plundered by the Tatars.

In the same year the princes of Zhukotin went to the Horde to the Tsar and petitioned that the Tsar protect himself and them from the robbers, who ceaselessly engaged in murders and robberies.

Tsar Khidyr sent three of his envoys — Urus, Kairbek, and Antyntsybey — to the Russian land, to the Russian princes, to catch the robbers and deliver them to him.

And all the princes gathered in Kostroma: Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Vladimir, his elder brother Grand Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod, and Prince Konstantin of Rostov.

And they caught the robbers, handed them over to the Tsar’s envoys with all their property, and sent them to the Horde.

In the same year Feodor, Bishop of Tver, left his episcopal see and withdrew to the Otroch Monastery. In the same year there was pestilence in Pskov.

In the year 6869 from Adam (1369 from Christ)

There was a heavenly sign: the sun diminished and became blood-red. In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow, grandson of Prince Ivan, great-grandson of Prince Daniel, great-great-grandson of Prince Alexander, went to the Horde to Tsar Khidyr, and, preserved by God’s mercy, left the Horde before the beginning of the turmoil, since after his departure great turmoil began in the Horde.

In the same year the Russian princes set out for the Horde to Tsar Khidyr: Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal from Vladimir and his elder brother Grand Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod, and Prince Konstantin of Rostov, and Prince Mikhail of Yaroslavl. And during their presence in the Horde turmoil began; the quiet, meek, and humble Tsar Khidyr was killed together with his younger son Kutluy by his elder son Temir-Khozya.

And Temir-Khozya, the elder son of Khidyr, ascended the Volga throne.

In the same year His Eminence Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, went to Tver and consecrated Vasily, abbot of the Spassky Monastery, as Bishop of Tver.
