In the same year Prince Vasily, called Kirdyapa, son of Prince Dmitry of Suzdal, grandson of Konstantin Vasilyevich, went to the Horde.

In the same year there was a fire in Moscow; there were then drought and great heat, a strong storm with a whirlwind began, fire spread everywhere, many people perished and burned in the fire, and everything burned. And that fire was called the Great Fire, which began at All Saints and spread everywhere with the help of wind and whirlwind.

In the same year there was a pestilence in Tver and Rostov. In the same year there died Prince Konstantin with his wife and children, and Vladyka Peter, and Grand Princess Anastasia, wife of Prince Alexander, also Princess Avdotya, wife of Prince Konstantin, and Prince Semyon Konstantinovich, and he left his appanage and his princess to Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich. Also afterwards there died Princess Sophia, wife of Prince Vsevolod, and then Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich himself. Also there died his brother Prince Andrei Alexandrovich, and his princess Evdokia, many boyars and noble people.

And then there died Prince Vladimir Alexandrovich, and great fear seized everyone. In the same year there died Grand Princess Alexandra, wife of Ivan Ivanovich, who lived as a nun and in the schema; in the monastic order her name was given as Maria, and she was buried in the monastery in the chapel of the Church of the Holy Savior in Moscow.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrei, great-great-great-grandson of Alexander Yaroslavich, came to Nizhny Novgorod to the grand principality together with his mother Elena, with his Vladyka Alexius of Suzdal and Novgorod, and of Gorodets. But his younger brother Prince Boris Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, did not give him the principality of Novgorod.

In the same year an ambassador came from the Horde from Tsar Barakhomza and from Tsaritsa Asan and seated Prince Boris Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrei, in the principality of Novgorod.

In the same year Prince Vasily Kirdyapa of Suzdal came from the Horde from Tsar Oziz, son of Dmitry, grandson of Konstantin, great-grandson of Vasily, and together with him the ambassador of Tsar Urusmanda, and they brought a yarlyk for the grand principality of Vladimir to Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal.

He did not wish to accept this and ceded the grand principality of Vladimir to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow, but asked him for help against Nizhny Novgorod, against his younger brother Prince Boris Konstantinovich.

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sent his ambassadors to them, so that they might make peace and divide their patrimony between themselves, but Prince Boris refused.
