In that same year Metropolitan Alexius took the episcopal see of Novgorod and Gorodets from Bishop Alexius of Suzdal.

At the same time there came from Moscow, from Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, an envoy—the Venerable Sergius, Abbot of Radonezh—to Nizhny Novgorod, to Prince Boris Konstantinovich, summoning him to Moscow; but he did not obey and did not go to Moscow.

The Venerable Abbot Sergius, by command of Metropolitan Alexius and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, shut all the churches.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gave a large army to the elder brother, Dmitry Konstantinovich, against his younger brother, Boris Konstantinovich.

Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich also gathered a large army in his own appanage in Suzdal and set out with his host for Nizhny Novgorod.

And when they reached Berezhie, his younger brother Prince Boris Konstantinovich met him there with his boyars, bowing and humbling himself, asking for peace and renouncing the principality. Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich did not reject his brother’s petition and entreaty, and made peace with him, and divided the rule of Suzdal, Nizhny Novgorod, and Gorodets. Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich himself took the grand princely throne in Nizhny Novgorod, and gave Gorodets to his younger brother, Prince Boris Konstantinovich. In that same year Alexius, Metropolitan of Suzdal, died.

In that same year Tagai, prince of the Horde, after the disintegration of the Horde came to Naruchad. And he began to rule in the land of Naruchad, and then wished to conquer Rus.

And he gathered all his army, his own and that of the land of Naruchad, and went with a numerous host against the land of Ryazan, and came secretly and unawares to Ryazan.

And he took the city of Pereyaslavl Ryazansky, and burned it, and seized all the districts and villages around it, and took many captives; thus advancing little by little, he returned to the Steppe with great tribute.

But Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, with his brothers, Vladimir of Pronsk and Titus of Kozelsk, gathered troops and went after Prince Tagai, and overtook him at a place called Shishevsky Forest, on the River Voyna.

And there was a fierce battle and a cruel slaughter, during which many on both sides perished. God helped Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan and his brothers, Prince Vladimir of Pronsk and Titus of Kozelsk.
