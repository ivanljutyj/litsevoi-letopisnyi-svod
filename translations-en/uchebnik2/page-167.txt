In the same year the nephew of Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, Prince Yaroslav Mstislavich, died in Pereyaslavl Russian.

In the same year there was a fire in the city of Vladimir on the 25th day of June, and most of the city burned down.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, grandson of Mstislav, founded the Church of the Holy Savior at Nereditsa; and Orefa founded the stone Church of the Holy Prophet Elijah on the Hill, and Polyazhai built the Church of Saint Euphemia in the monastery.

In the same year the princess, the wife of Prince Yaroslav, built the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God at Mikhalitsa.

In the same year two sons of Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich died: Izyaslav, who had been installed to reign at Luki, which served as a defense for Novgorod against Lithuania, and there he died; and Rostislav died in Novgorod; and both were laid in the Monastery of Saint George.

In the year 6708 from Adam (the year 1208 from Christ)

The great prince of Chernigov, Yaroslav, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, died. His son: Yaropolk.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, founded the stone Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God in the Knyaginin Monastery, with the blessing of his father John, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir.

About the Novgorodians

In the same autumn the best men of Novgorod, Miroshka’s retinue, came to Grand Prince Vsevolod with a petition and entreaty, with many gifts from all the Novgorodians, saying: “Your patrimony and ancestral inheritance, Great Novgorod, asks of you a son to reign in Novgorod.”

Then Archbishop Martiry of Novgorod died on the 24th day of August on Lake Seliger, on the way to Grand Prince Vsevolod; he was brought and laid in the narthex of Holy Sophia, and in his place Mitrofan was elevated and installed.

The Grand Prince, having heard the entreaty of the Novgorodians, sent to remove from Novgorod his kinsman by marriage Yaroslav.

And he confirmed the agreement on his terms with the Novgorodians by kissing the cross, and gave them his son Svyatoslav, who was then still a youth.

The Novgorodians received him from the wondrous Mother of God with great joy, and, having come to Novgorod, elevated him to the throne in Holy Sophia, and there was great joy in Novgorod.
