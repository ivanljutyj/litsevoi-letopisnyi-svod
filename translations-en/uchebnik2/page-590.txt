That Andronicus was quiet, meek, and humble. And many from everywhere began to come to him and take monastic vows. Having lived many years in virtues and pleasingly pasturing the flock of Christ, he died in deep old age, departing to the Lord. In his place in the monastery he left, having blessed him, his disciple Savva, who was greatly famed for his spiritual virtues.

About Athanasius, the Disciple of Sergius

Once Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Prince Ivan Danilovich, asked the venerable abbot Sergius to give him his disciple named Athanasius, a very virtuous man, by birth from Rostov. He wished that the venerable Sergius himself would go with him and found a monastery near the city of Serpukhov. The saint went on foot, because he loved hardships and humility and never rode a horse.

They came to the appointed place, which the prince, Vladimir Andreevich, liked, called Vysokoye. The place was very beautiful and truly high, on the bank of the Nara River, near the Oka River. He made prayer and founded a church in the name of the honorable Conception of the Most Pure Mother of God. And he gave him his disciple Athanasius, virtuous and very learned in the divine writings, as his manuscripts still testify today. He appointed him abbot, and he founded there a cenobitic monastery. A multitude of monks gathered there, and the monastery of this archimandrite was glorious, honorable, and filled with all necessities.

(9) How the Saint Was Compelled to Become Metropolitan

When His Grace Metropolitan Alexei grew old and began to weaken, he summoned the venerable Sergius and began to speak with him, wishing to leave him after himself on the metropolitan throne of all Rus. And taking a cross with a paramand adorned with gold, pearls, and precious stones, he wished to give it to the venerable Sergius. But he said: “Forgive me, great hierarch of God, this is not for my measure; from my youth I have not been a bearer of gold, and now in old age I wish to remain in poverty.” And so it happened several times. Then the metropolitan released him, grieved. But he went with joy to his monastery in Radonezh.
