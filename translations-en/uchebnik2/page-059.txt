Prince Yuri Dolgoruky began to entreat them, and prevailed, and made obeisance, and kissed the life-giving cross to them: “I am now going to the great principality in Suzdal with my children”; and he swore to them: “I shall never return here. I shall not remain here.”

They gave him leave to go. He left his son Prince Gleb in Gorodok, and himself went to Suzdal with his children. Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich and his uncle Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomash sat in Kiev: Vyacheslav in the Great Court, and Izyaslav in the Podugorsky Court; and he placed his son Mstislav in Pereyaslavl.

In the same year Izyaslav’s wife, the princess, reposed. In the same year Izyaslav went against Vladimir and returned at Karachev.
