He greatly honored the monastic and ascetic order, and was loved by the boyars and by all his own; he did not love drunkenness, and always desired the monastic order, and always told of the martyrs’ deeds, and drank the same cup for the Christians. If he had not gone to the Horde, having heard of so many calamities coming upon him, and had departed to other lands, then the Tatars, seeking him, would have come, and how many Christians they would have tortured and put to death, and desecrated holy churches; but this blessed Michael gave himself for all and departed this life by a violent death, and, rejoicing, came to his Lord Christ in the heavenly kingdom, for this life is mortal. If he had not died in this way, he would have died all the same.

That same autumn Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver married, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky.

In that same year Prince Boris Danilovich died, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky. And he was laid in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Vladimir.

In that same year Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich of Tver married, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and was wed in the Church of Saint Theodore in Kostroma.

In that same year Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver married, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky; he brought for himself from Lithuania the daughter of Prince Gedimin. And he was wed in the Church of the Holy Savior in Tver by Bishop Barsanuphius of Tver, and there was joy to all people in Tver.

In that same year the ambassador Baidere came from the Horde to Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich, and much evil did they do in Vladimir.

In that same year Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich sent his brother Afanasy Danilovich to Novgorod to the throne.

In that same year a son, Daniel, was born to Prince Ivan Danilovich. In that same year Prince Ivan Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, went to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak.

In that same year Prince Yuri Alexandrovich of Rostov died.

In the year 6829 from Adam (1329 from Christ)

From the Horde there came to Kashin Tayanchor the Tatar with a Jew, a collector of tribute arrears, and they inflicted great hardship on Kashin. That same summer the sun was eclipsed on the 26th day of June, on the commemoration of Saint David of Thessalonica, at the third hour of the day, and the moon was still young, two days old, and after one hour it was full again.
