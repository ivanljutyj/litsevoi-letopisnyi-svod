In the same year Prince Mstislav, son of Izyaslav, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, went to his great reign in Pereyaslavl.

In the same year the Novgorodians drove Prince Yaroslav Izyaslavich out of Novgorod, and seated Prince Roman Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, with them in Novgorod.

In the same year Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh of Suzdal set out from Suzdal with his sons and with all the Rostovites and Suzdalians into Rus; and there was a plague among them, not only among the horses but also among their host, such as had never been.

And he came to the Vyatichi and halted before reaching Kozelsk; and the Polovtsians came to him from the Field to help; but he, after consulting with his sons and boyars and fearing the wrath of God that had then befallen them, and the Polovtsians withdrew to their Field, while he himself returned back to Suzdal with great contrition and humility.

In the same year Prince Svyatopolk Mstislavich died in Vladimir, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, brother of the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich; and the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, in place of his brother, seated his son Yaroslav in Vladimir; in the same year the princess, wife of Prince Gleb Yuryevich, died in Suzdal.

In the year 6663 from Adam (1163 from Christ)

To the Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh a son, Dmitry, was born, and his father nicknamed him Vsevolod; and his father, Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, founded a city on the river Yakhroma and named it Dmitrov, after the name of his son Dmitry, called Vsevolod, because he was born there.

On the Repose of the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich died, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, on the thirteenth day of the month of November, on the commemoration of our holy father John, Patriarch of Constantinople, Chrysostom; and on the next day, on the commemoration of the holy Apostle Philip, his body was committed to burial in the earth, according to the word of the Lord, that “thou art earth and unto earth shalt thou return again”; and he was buried in his father’s monastery of Saint Theodore. He reigned ten years. His sons: Mstislav, Yaroslav.
