In that same year Alexis, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus', set out from Moscow to Vladimir. And there Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky came to him with complaints, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, against his uncle, Grand Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, concerning his grievances, which he had inflicted on him contrary to the existing treaty.

And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver sent envoys to Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich in Moscow and agreed to love and peace with Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel.

And then he went with Theodore, his bishop of Tver, to Metropolitan Alexis in Vladimir, and there were many conversations between them, but they did not reach agreement on peace and love.

And then an envoy came from the Horde to blessed Alexis, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus', from Taidula, wife of Chanibek, who summoned him to her in the Horde, to come to her because she had fallen ill. He prepared everything necessary for the journey.

And behold, a candle lit by itself in the metropolitan's cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God, at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter, and he with the sacred assembly celebrated a prayer service. And he divided that candle and distributed it to the people for a blessing, and he himself set out for the Horde that same day. And by the mercy of God, of the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the great wonderworker Peter, he healed Queen Taidula.

And soon he was released from the Horde, because at that time great enmity and turmoil had begun in the Horde. At that same time an envoy named Itkar was also released from the Horde to Moscow to collect tribute from all the Russian princes. In that same year an envoy from the tsar came to Rus' named Koshak.

In that same year the turmoil in the Horde not only continued but flared up even more strongly. There was then in the Horde, under Tsar Janibek, son of Uzbeg, a wise and mighty temnik, the accursed Tavlubiy. Having joined himself to Satan, he desired to possess the whole Horde and all lands. And he began to whisper into the ear of the tsar's son Berdibek, Janibek's son, praising and exalting him: "The time has come for you to sit on the throne, and it is already time for your father to come down from the throne." And little by little he began to advise him to kill his father Janibek, son of Uzbeg. And he drew many Horde princes to his side, promising to give each whatever he should.
