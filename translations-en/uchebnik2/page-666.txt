In the same year, in the month of August, there was a rebellion and civil strife in Lithuania; there was also famine throughout the Lithuanian land.

In the same year, in the month of August, on the 16th day, the Germans approached Pskov with an army and crossed the Velikaya River.

And then they withdrew a little from the city, but the Pskovians came out against them from the city.

And they met the Germans, and then the Germans overcame the Pskovians, killed 809 people and three posadniks: Efrem Kortoch, Elentiy Gubka, and Pankratiy.

In the same year, in the month of August, Vladyka Ioann, Archbishop of Novgorod, built a stone church of the Holy Transfiguration of the Lord at Verenda and at Vidogoshcha, and founded a monastery there.

In the same year, in the month of August, the Novgorodians built a stone church of St. Blaise on Volosovo; and in the same month of August, Posadnik Yuri Dmitrievich with his brother Yakov built a stone church of the Holy Miracle of Archangel Michael in the Arkazh Monastery.

In the same year, in the month of August, in Novgorod the Nerevsky End burned as far as the city itself, and the archbishop’s court; the Church of Holy Sophia burned, the Vladimirsky Bank, Lyudogoshcha Street, Khrevkova Street; in all, 12 stone holy churches burned, and 6 wooden ones burned down.

In the year 6917 from Adam (1417 from Christ)

On the 6th day of September, Prince Ivan Vladimirovich Pronsky came from the Horde from Tsar Bulat-Saltan with a charter of grant and gifts to Rus and sat in Pronsk, and with him came the tsar’s ambassador.

In the same autumn, in the month of September, the Tatars of Tsar Bulat-Saltan of the Great Horde conquered Lithuania.

In the same autumn, in the month of September, on the 4th day, died Grand Prince Yuri Svyatoslavich of Smolensk, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Alexander, great-great-grandson of Gleb, great-great-great-grandson of Rostislav, descendant of his ancestor Mstislav, of his great-ancestor David, of his great-great-ancestor Rostislav, Mstislav, and Vladimir Monomakh, on the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross, not in his grand principality of Smolensk, but wandering in a foreign land, roaming in exile, moving from place to place in the wilds of his grand principality of Smolensk, deprived of his patrimony and inheritance, of his grand princess, children and brothers, relatives, his princes and boyars, commanders and servants.
