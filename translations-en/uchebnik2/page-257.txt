And then the Tatars entered the city at the Golden Gate over the piled-up boards, and also from the Lybed River through the Irininy Gate and the Copper Gate, and likewise from the Klyazma River through the Volzhsky Gate, and finally they breached the whole city from all sides and entered it like demons.

And the Tatars took the New City before noon and burned it all; Prince Vsevolod with his brother Mstislav, and many boyars and people, and other princes, and all the people fled to the Middle City.

And Bishop Mitrofan, and the wife of Grand Prince Yuri, with her daughter, and with her daughters-in-law, and with her grandchildren, and with boyars and boyars' wives and many people fled into the church; and they partook of the Holy Mysteries of Christ our God; and they closed the doors of the church and went up to the upper tiers of the church and shut themselves in.

And the Tatars took this city too, and began to search for the princes and princesses and learned that they were in the church. The God-loving Bishop Mitrofan then stood up and prayed in tears with these words: "Lord and God of hosts, Giver of light, who sitteth upon the cherubim, Thou didst teach Joseph, and strengthen David against Goliath, and didst raise Lazarus, four days dead, from the dead; stretch forth Thy invisible hand and receive in peace the souls of Thy servants."

And the Tatars broke down the church doors and killed those who were in the church and resisted.

And they began to ask about the princes and princesses, and having learned that they were on the upper tiers, they began to lure them out and urge them to come down from the upper tiers; but they did not obey the Tatars.

And the Tatars dragged many trees into the church and around the church and set them on fire. Those in the church, with prayer and thanksgiving, gave their souls to the Lord, and burned in the fire, and joined the number of martyrs.

And the Tatars plundered the holy church and tore away the wonder-working icon of the Mother of God herself; and from there they went against Grand Prince Yuri.

And some Tatar detachments went to Rostov, others to Yaroslavl; and they took it.

And other Tatar detachments went to the Volga and to Gorodets, and they conquered all the lands along the Volga as far as the city of Galich Mersky.

And some Tatars went to Pereslavl; and they took that city and slaughtered the people.

And from there the Tatars conquered all those lands with many cities: Yuryev, Dmitrov, Volok, Tver, and there they also killed the son of Yaroslav; and as far as Torzhok there was no place where the Tatars did not come in war.

And in the land of Rostov and Suzdal they took 14 cities, not counting villages and hamlets, in the single month of February.
