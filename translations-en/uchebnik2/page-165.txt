Great Prince Vsevolod heard that the Olegovichi had beaten David’s regiment and had taken his matchmaker Mstislav captive, and that Roman had joined them as well; but Vsevolod Yuryevich delayed and gathered his army without haste.

In that same autumn the Chernigov princes sent an embassy proposing peace and love to the great prince of Kiev, Rurik Rostislavich.

But the great prince of Kiev, Rurik Rostislavich, without waiting for the decision of Great Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, made peace with the Olegovichi.

And in that same year he went to Chernigov after his matchmaker Mstislav, while another, Rurik Rostislavich, fortified Kiev, and with him were the Ryazan princes, and the Murom princes, and David from Smolensk.

But Great Prince Vsevolod, entering their land, took the cities of the Vyatichi and laid waste their land; the Olegovichi could not withstand his strength and sent to him, bowing and asking for peace; and he, having made peace with them, returned to Vladimir on the sixth day of October.

In that same autumn the Church of the Nativity of the Most Holy Mother of God in Vladimir was consecrated on the seventeenth day of October, which Great Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, had built.

Then also the Church of Joachim and Anna at the gates of the Monastery of the Nativity of the Mother of God was consecrated on the twenty-seventh day of December, which Bishop John of Rostov had built.

On the board from the tomb of Saint Demetrius

In that same winter a board from the tomb of Saint Demetrius was brought from Solun on the tenth day of January.

Concerning the Novgorodians

In that same year Yaropolk Yaroslavich came to Novgorod from Chernigov; he remained there six months, and the Novgorodians drove him out.

And again the best men of Novgorod sent to Yaroslav, Vsevolod’s kinsman, and received him with all truth and honor; and he came to Novgorod and sat upon his princely throne, and found great love among the Novgorodians, and then all was well in Novgorod.
