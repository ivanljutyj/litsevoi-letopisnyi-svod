Also, while digging by the front wall, they found a coffin covered with two boards, and opening it, they saw in it the body of Leontius, Bishop of Rostov, wonderworker, in no way corrupted, but wholly intact and emitting fragrance, and his vestments as if newly laid; and there was great joy in the city of Rostov for Prince Andrew and all the people.

When they had completed the stone church in the city of Rostov on the site of the burned church, by command of the pious lord Prince Andrew, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, they placed in it, in a shrine in the wall, the relics of the blessed Leontius, Bishop of Rostov, wonderworker, where they lie even now, and they set up great candles at his tomb.

Ivan, Bishop of Rostov, with the blessing of Theodore, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, established the feast of Saint Leontius, Bishop of Rostov, wonderworker, on the twenty-third day of May. And many miracles occurred.

After some time the feast of the saint arrived, and after Vespers, at the fourth hour of the night, the sexton had a dream: it seemed to him that he was in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God, and that angels came and began to sing, and the angels lit the candles at the saint’s tomb; and Leontius rose from the tomb, and taking a censer and incense, began to cense the whole church, and the fragrance spread through the whole church and the whole city of Rostov.

The sexton awoke early from his sleep, and quickly went to the church doors, and wanted to open them, and saw a great light, and heard angelic singing, and Saint Leontius standing and praying with his hands raised up.

The sexton could not stand for fear, and trembling, fell to the ground, and then after a long time lifted up his eyes and saw no one, only the burning candles, and quickly went to tell everything to Bishop Ivan.

He went to the church and ordered Matins to be sung, and from that fire lit all the other candles. When Matins ended, one of the clerics began to put out the candle at the saint’s tomb, and an angel came and pushed him, saying: “How dared you do such a thing?” He became paralyzed in arms and legs, and deaf and mute, unable to move at all, but only in his heart, with compunction, he prayed to the Lord God, and the Most Pure Mother of God, and the blessed Leontius, bishop and wonderworker.
