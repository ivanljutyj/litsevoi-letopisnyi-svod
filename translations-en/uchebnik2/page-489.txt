He, having taken counsel with his brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich and with the other princes and commanders, decided to prepare a strong watch in the Field.

And he sent to the watch strong warriors: Rodion Rzhevsky, Andrey Volosaty, Vasily Tupik, and others strong and courageous for this task, and commanded them to keep watch on the Bystraya River, or on the Tikhaya Sosna, with all precautions, and to ride to the Horde to obtain a tongue and to learn truly of Mamai’s intentions.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich himself at that same hour again sent messengers with letters throughout all the lands, so that they should be ready to go out against the Tatars and all should gather at Kolomna on the 31st day of July, on the commemoration of St Eudocimus.

He himself began to gather and prepare with the princes who had then come; and much soldiery assembled; but there was no news from anywhere, and those sent to keep watch in the Field were delayed, and no news at all came from them.

The Grand Prince sent a second watch detachment into the Field: Kliment Polyanin, Ivan Svyatoslav, Grigory Sudok, and others with them, and ordered them to return quickly.

They met Vasily Tupik leading a tongue to the Grand Prince, who reported that the tsar was indeed marching against Rus, having joined with Oleg, Prince of Ryazan, and with Jogaila, Prince of Lithuania, and that the tsar was not yet hastening but was waiting for autumn to join with Lithuania.

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich learned for certain that Tsar Mamai was truly coming against him with great force, and he began to comfort and strengthen his brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich and the other princes, boyars, and commanders, so that they should be strong and courageous against the Tatars. They all cried out with one voice: “We are ready to suffer for Christ, for the Christian faith, and for the wrong done to you.” And he commanded all the people to be at Kolomna on the 15th day of August, on the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos, and there to appoint a commander for each regiment.

And many gathered from all sides to Moscow to the Grand Prince. And the Belozersk princes came, strong and courageous, for battle with their hosts: Prince Feodor Semenovich, Prince Semyon Mikhailovich, Prince Andrey Kemsky, Prince Gleb Kargopolsky and Tsydonsky; the Andoma princes also came.
