On the Meeting of the Wonderworking Image of the Mother of God in Moscow

When that divine icon was already not far from Moscow, His Eminence Metropolitan Cyprian came out to meet the icon with the whole sacred council, with bishops and archimandrites, abbots and deacons, and with all the church clergy, with crosses and icons, with the Holy Gospel, with candles, with censers, with the singing of psalms and spiritual hymns. After them came the aforementioned Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and other princes, princesses, boyars and boyar ladies, and other nobles and officials, and men and women of every age, old and young, boys and maidens, the poor and the destitute, and all the countless multitude of Christians; all earnestly desired to see that divine and greatly wonderworking icon, at which many miracles were wrought. All prayed with tears and heartfelt sighs and unceasing lamentation; one might say there was no one who did not weep.

On the Public Prayer with Good Hope before the Image of the Mother of God

And they met it far outside the city. And when they beheld the most wondrous image of the Mother of God and on her most pure hands the most pure image of Jesus Christ, all fell prostrate to the ground, bathing in tears, sighing, and praying fervently. And rising from the ground and lifting up their hands, they cried out: “O most holy Lady, Virgin Mother of God, all-merciful Sovereign Lady! Now by your coming have mercy and save us, your afflicted servants, who expect help from nowhere except from you and from Christ our God born of you. Pray to Him without ceasing for us, and send down your mercies upon us, O Sovereign Lady, and deliver us and our city, and all our cities and places, from the invasion of the impious, and from the advancing enemy Temir-Aksak, who boasts that he will march against us, and from the other Tatars with him, and from all enemies who attempt against us, and from captivity, and from fire, and from the sword, from famine and all destruction, and from every misfortune and sorrow, and from internecine wars.
