The Novgorodians then installed his brother, Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich, as prince in Novgorod.

Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich of Vladimir seized Novgorodian merchants and caused them much harm; the Novgorodians were grieved and offended by this, and expelled Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich from Novgorod; then Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich of Vladimir came secretly to Novy Torg, killed many, and took many captive.

The Novgorodians sent to Smolensk to Prince Roman of Smolensk, asking him to come to Novgorod to rule as prince.

He, having heeded them, came to Novgorod on St Theodore’s Sunday and on the Sunday of the Assembly sat as prince in Novgorod.

In the same year Vladyka Ilya, Archbishop of Novgorod, founded a stone church of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God; they began building it on the twenty-first day of May and finished it on the twenty-fifth day of August, and they built it in all in seventy days.

In the same year the Novgorodians sent to Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov and asked him for his son Prince Vladimir.

In the same year Vladyka Ilya, Archbishop of Novgorod, together with his brother Gavriil, founded a stone church of the Holy Epiphany at the gates.

In the same year Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great, set out with the Chernigovians and Polovtsians in arms against Suzdal, against Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich; and his son Prince Vladimir left Novgorod with the Novgorodians and met him on the Volga at the mouth of the Tver, and they devastated all along the Volga, burning all the cities, volosts, and villages.

Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich then came out against him and met him forty versts short of Pereyaslavl, on the river Vlena, and they stood opposite each other for two weeks, engaged briefly, and again withdrew; and Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov with his son Vladimir of Novgorod and with the Novgorodians took fright at the spring flood, burned the town of Dmitrov, the volosts, and the villages; and Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich returned to Chernigov, while his son Prince Vladimir returned to Novgorod with the Novgorodians; and Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich returned to Vladimir, and Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov was received with honors in Novgorod; they honored him greatly and gave him gifts.

In the same year the Ryazan princes, Prince Vsevolod Glebovich and his brother Vladimir, sent envoys to Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich.
