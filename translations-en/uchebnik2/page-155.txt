Those envoys of Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich distorted the words conveyed to them by Prince Vsevolod through him, and having acted so shamelessly and dishonorably, departed by another route to Chernigov.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, gave his daughter Vseslava in marriage in Chernigov to Prince Rostislav, son of Yaroslav, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Oleg, on the 11th day of July.

In the same year there was a severe illness among the people, so that there was not a single household without a sick person, and there was no one even to give the sick drink or food: all lay sick; thus the Lord chastens His servants, and turning, He turns them, and saving, He saves.

In the same year a multitude of Polovtsians attacked Ryazan, did much evil, and withdrew to their own lands.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, went to Ryazan together with his brother-in-law Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, Prince Vladimir of Murom, and Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, from Kolomna against his brothers to Ryazan; and crossing the Oka River, he burned many districts and villages, and taking many captives, returned to his own lands.

In the same year the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in the city of Rostov was painted by the blessed Bishop Luka.

In the year 6696 from Adam (the year 1196 from Christ)

On the 17th day of February there was a terrible thunderclap, and it killed two people and burned a church; and great fear seized all, for it is written: He shook the earth and troubled it, and showed His people a severe [punishment] according to their deeds.

In the same year Prince Vladimir died, son of Gleb, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, forefather of the great Vladimir, nephew of Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, in the city of Pereyaslavl Russky on the 18th day of March. In the same year Prince Boris died, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.

In the same year the Novgorodians expelled their prince Mstislav, son of David, grandson of Rostislav, from Novgorod, and took Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, brother-in-law of Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, to Novgorod to rule over them; and he came to them to rule on their terms.

In the same year the foundation was laid in Novgorod for the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God in the Arkadyevsky Monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God (Arkazh). In the same year bread was expensive.
