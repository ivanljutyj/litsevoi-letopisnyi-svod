And not only this city, Moscow, was taken, but other cities and localities also were laid waste by the pagans.

The Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, with his princess and children, was in Kostroma, and his brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich was at Volok.

And his mother and his princess were in Torzhok, and Vladyka Gerasim of Kolomna was in Novgorod. Then the Tsar sent soldiers throughout the whole Russian land to conquer the grand principality.

Some went to Vladimir, cut down many people, and led them into captivity.

And others went to Zvenigorod, some to Mozhaisk, others to Volok, and still others to Pereyaslavl; and they took it and burned it with fire, and the townspeople fled to the lake in boats and waited there, abandoning the town.

And they almost caught the Grand Princess, for she was traveling from Moscow to Pereyaslavl, and from Pereyaslavl to Rostov.

And from Rostov to Kostroma to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

Other Tatars went to Yuryev, plundered and burned, cut down Christians, and led others into captivity.

And villages and monasteries and holy churches they burned with fire, ravaged the volosts, and wrought much evil on the Russian land.

And Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, was then in Tver: for he had come to Novgorod two days before the coming of Tokhtamysh, and saw discord and strife in the city, and withdrew to Tver, and waited there. The Tatars wanted to go to Tver as well, but the Divine power invisibly prevented them.

Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver sent his envoy to Tsar Tokhtamysh, named Gurlenya, with honor and many gifts.

The Tsar received his gifts with honor and released him to Tver, sending with him his yarlyk, his grant, to Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver.

Prince Vladimir Andreevich, cousin, the brother of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, stood armed near Volok with a great host that he had gathered around himself.

And some of the Tatars, not knowing him and not having learned of him there, rode against him.
