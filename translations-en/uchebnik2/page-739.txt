Prince Vasily Yuryevich went from Ustyug against the Grand Prince

That same spring Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy went from Ustyug against Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, boasting proudly, and with him were the Vyatchans and men of his cousin Prince Dmitry Shemyaka.

And the Grand Prince met him in the Rostov district at the Holy Intercession in Skoryatino. And with Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich were Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Krasny, the younger, and Prince Ivan Andreyevich Mozhaysky with his regiments.

And then Prince Ivan Baba, of the Drutsk princes, came from Lithuania to serve the Grand Prince, and he equipped his regiment with lances in the Lithuanian fashion; and all the other regiments of the Grand Prince likewise soon equipped themselves after their own custom.

But Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy wished to capture the Grand Prince by stealth; he sent to the Grand Prince and concluded a truce until morning. Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich concluded the truce, dismissed his regiments, and all dispersed for forage.

But Prince Vasily Yuryevich set out that same day, wishing to fall unexpectedly upon the Grand Prince; but watchmen came running to the Grand Prince, reporting that Prince Vasily Yuryevich had set out swiftly against him.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich sent messengers to all his camps, and he himself took a trumpet and began to sound it; and at that same hour the regiments of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich assembled.

And they went together against the enemy and fought with him, and drove Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy and his regiments. Boris Tobolin caught up with Prince Vasily and, recognizing him, seized him and began to shout; and at that hour Prince Ivan Baba of Drutsk galloped up to him and, seizing Prince Vasily Yuryevich, brought him to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich.

And the Grand Prince sent him to Moscow, and there Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy was blinded.

And when the Grand Prince went against Prince Vasily Yuryevich, he ordered his brother Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Shemyaka to be freed from fetters and sent to Kolomna. And when the Grand Prince defeated Prince Vasily Yuryevich, he came to Moscow and sent to Prince Dmitry Shemyaka in Kolomna and showed him favor.

That same year the Novgorodians convened a veche concerning the demarcation of land and sent to Bezhetsky Verkh to allot the land of the Novgorod patrimony.
