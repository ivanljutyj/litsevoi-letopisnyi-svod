After some time, certain pious people gathered there, collected the venerable bodies of the martyrs, and buried them with great reverence. Such are the glorious deeds, such are the brave victories of the valiant and invincible soldiers of the Heavenly King, and moreover of the passion-bearers of Christ who voluntarily offered themselves, valiantly contending against the evil tormentor and enemy for the Church, the bride of Christ, from Whom they received crowns of victory.

O greatly renowned martyrs and confessors, who then voluntarily confessed the true God before the impious king and tormentor, for the Church and for the people of your fatherland; now, all the more, defend us from every assault of the enemies, since you have boldness before God. Therefore preserve unshaken and steadfast the Catholic and Apostolic Church in Rus, and by your intercession avert the temptations that are to come upon us because of our sins, delivering us from every crooked path of sin, and from the threefold snare of envy, pride, and injustice. So that we may be guided by your intercession before God, and be found worthy to walk diligently in the commandments of the Lord, doing all righteousness in the fear of God, keeping brotherly love with all guilelessness and long-suffering, with humility that lifts us to God. Thus abiding, may we be delivered here from all evils, and, having been freed from the future endless torments, may we also receive the eternal good things prepared for the righteous, and enjoy the Kingdom of Heaven; by the grace and love for mankind of our Lord God and Savior Jesus Christ, to Whom be glory together with His Father without beginning, and with the Most Holy, Good, and Life-giving Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

In the same year Tsar Batu released Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov to his son Sartak; Sartak honored him and sent him back with honor to Rus, to the land that had passed to Boris from his father.

On the Repose of Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich in the Horde

In the same year Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich was in the Horde with the khan’s son, and he was slandered by Feodor Yarunovich before Tsar Batu, and he suffered much from the Tatars for the Russian land; and they released him already tormented.

And, having ridden a short distance from the khan’s son, Grand Prince Yaroslav died a painful death among the foreigners, on the thirtieth day of September.
