The Murder of Grand Prince Andrei Yuryevich Bogolyubsky in Vladimir by the Kuchkovichi

In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky was murdered. This is how his murder came about: on Friday at Mass the accursed murderers held counsel. The prince had a beloved servant named Yakim Kuchkovich, who heard that Prince Andrei had ordered his brother to be seized and executed because he had committed some offence. This same Yakim, taught by the devil, ran with cries to his brother Peter, Kuchkov’s son-in-law, and to the keykeeper Anbal Yasin, and to Efrem Moizovich, his accursed counsellors, and began to say to them: “If the prince executes him today, tomorrow he will execute us too; let us consider how to rid ourselves of this prince.” And they agreed to murder their lord and benefactor.

Night fell, and they, the accursed, having made ready and taken weapons, went against their lord and Grand Prince Andrei like savage beasts. This murder took place on the night leading to Saturday, on the commemoration of the holy and chief apostles Peter and Paul. All of them were favourites of the prince; among his murderers were the accursed Kuchkovichi. In all there were twenty of them, the accursed murderers. And coming to the prince’s courtyard, they killed the guards. With him was a youth of quite childish age, who, entering the entryway, forcefully threw open the doors, entered the prince’s bedroom where the blessed one slept, and then the servant, standing at the doors, called: “Lord, lord, great prince!” And the prince asked: “Who is it?” And he answered: “Procopius.” The prince said to him: “It is not Procopius.”

They, hearing the prince’s voice, began to batter at the doors and by force broke them down. The blessed one, leaping from his bed, wanted to take his sword, but there was no sword, because the keykeeper Ambal had carried it off that day; it was the sword of the holy and great martyr Boris.

And bursting into the prince’s bedroom, two murderers seized the prince; the prince pinned one beneath himself, and at that time the rest came in, and thinking that it was the prince thrown to the floor, they killed their accomplice.

After this they called out to the prince as he struggled with him, and because the prince was very strong, they slashed him with swords and sabres. The prince said to them: “Woe to you, accursed and impious! Why have you become like Goryaser? Why do you do evil, shedding my blood on the earth? God will avenge my bread upon you.”

The impious murderers, considering the prince dead, carried out the body of their friend and went away trembling.

But the prince, recovering from his stupor, ran out after them, began to groan from pain, and went under the porch. They, hearing his voice, turned back.
