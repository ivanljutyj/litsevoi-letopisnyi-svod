In the year 6921 from Adam (1421 from Christ)

In the month of September, Gregory, lord of Rostov—this was already the second archbishop of Rostov; the first was Theodore, nephew of Saint Sergius, abbot of Radonezh, and the second was this Gregory—by the blessing of Photius, metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, Gregory built the stone church of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God at Dorogomilovo above the Moskva River, and he renounced the lands belonging to the metropolitan in the village of Kudrino, so that they should belong to the metropolitan. In the same autumn, in the month of September, Prince Semen, surnamed Lugven Olgerdovich, grandson of Gediminas, left Novgorod for Lithuania and recalled his viceroys from the towns subject to Novgorod. In the same autumn in Novgorod the church of Saint Nicholas on the Verezha by the bridge was completed.

In the same autumn, in the month of October, on Saint Demetrius's Day, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow left the Horde, and with him Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin.

In the same autumn Tsar Kerim-Berdei began to reign in the Horde.

In the same autumn, in the month of October, on the 31st day, at night in Tver, Gorodok on the Volga burned: the church of the Most Pure Mother of God, the prince's court, much princely property, grain, and supplies.

In the same winter, in the month of December, on the 24th day, on the eve of the Nativity of Christ, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich and Prince Ivan Borisovich returned to Kashin, but the Tver outpost did not let them into Kashin; then Prince Vasily Mikhailovich went back to the Horde.

On the rupture of peaceful relations between King Jogaila of Poland and Prince Vytautas of Lithuania with Novgorod

In the same winter, in the month of January, on the 2nd day, the Polish King Jogaila Olgerdovich and the Grand Prince of Lithuania Vytautas Keistutovich, and Prince Semen Lugven Olgerdovich sent letters annulling the peace agreement to Novgorod, saying: "You promised us that if the Germans should annul with us their cross-kissing oath, peace, and agreement, then you too would annul with the Germans your cross-kissing oath, peace, and agreement and would be at one with us. Therefore we sent to you the lords Nemir and Zinovy Bratoshich to learn whether you had kept your word. But you answered them: 'Novgorod cannot act thus: if we remain at peace with the Lithuanians, then we remain at peace with the Germans.' Then we sent to take Prince Lugven away from you, and we concluded an eternal peace with the Germans, as also with the Hungarians and with all the lands bordering us; but you forgot your promise, which you gave us, that you would be at one with us.
