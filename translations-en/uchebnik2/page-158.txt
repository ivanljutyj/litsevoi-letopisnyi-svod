On the Suzdal Church

In the same year the church in Suzdal was renovated by Bishop John, which had collapsed from age and neglect; and it was entirely covered with tin. All this came about in a wondrous manner: craftsmen were found from among the church people who were there—some poured tin, others covered the roof, and others whitewashed with lime; for the eyes of their hearts had been opened by God to the church’s property, showing that one must care for it as a shepherd, not as a hireling.

In the same year Varlaam the Wonderworker founded a stone Church of the Transfiguration of the Lord God, our Savior Jesus Christ, at Khutyn.

In the same year a son, Dmitry, was born to Grand Prince Vsevolod on the 25th day of October.

In the year 6702 from Adam (1202 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod sent his steward to the Rus land and founded there the town of Gorodets on the Osetr River, restoring his patrimony.

On Pereyaslavl

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod founded the town of Pereyaslavl by the lake on the 29th day of July; in the same year the town was built of wood.

In the same year Prince Igor Glebovich of Ryazan died and was laid to rest in the town of Ryazan in the stone church of the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb.

In the same year Grand Prince of Kiev Svyatoslav died, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Oleg, great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir the Great, and he was laid to rest in the monastery in the Church of St. Cyril, which his father, Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich, and his sons built.

In the same year the surviving Novgorodians returned to Novgorod from Yugra.

In the same year the towns of Ladoga, Russa, and Gorodishche burned, and in Novgorod it kept catching fire every day in an invisible manner, from the Church of All Saints to the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God; and then people did not dare live in their houses, but lived in the fields.

In the year 6703 from Adam (1203 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, sent his men to Kiev and enthroned his kinsman by marriage, Prince Rurik Rostislavich, as Grand Prince in Kiev.

In the same year the Cumans came against Ryazan and, having done much harm, returned to their own lands.
