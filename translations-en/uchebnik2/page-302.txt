Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov was to register the lands of Novgorod. The Novgorodians learned of this and were greatly troubled.

Then the Tatar census-takers came to Novgorod with Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich and with other Russian princes; and Prince Vasily Alexandrovich of Novgorod fled from his father out of Novgorod to Pskov.

And the Tatars began to demand tribute, but the Novgorodians refused, and gave many gifts to the tsar and to his envoys, and they were sent away in peace.

And the Novgorodians killed the posadnik Mikhalka, and also killed another posadnik, Mikhalka, while Posadnik Anania died.

And they made Mikhalka Fedorovich posadnik, and Zhidyata Domozhirovich tysyatsky.

And Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich drove his son Vasily out of Pskov and sent him to the Nizovian Land; and he punished his retinue: to some he cut off noses and ears, to others he put out eyes and cut off hands—those who had advised his son to act so; and so the evildoers perished evilly; and so he calmed and pacified the Tatar census-takers.

In the same year the Lithuanians came to Torzhok in war, and the Novgorodians gathered and set out against them.

And there was a great battle, and the Lithuanians prevailed, and did much harm to the inhabitants of Torzhok.

In the same year the Tatars seized the whole Lithuanian land and went to their own land with many captives and much wealth.

In the year 6767 from Adam (the year 1267 from Christ)

Mikhail Pinyachin came to Novgorod and said: “If you do not agree to pay tribute to the Tsar of the Horde, then soon the Tatar army will come against the Nizovian Land, and it will go badly for all.” And the Novgorodians were afraid, and agreed to pay tribute, and sent their envoys to the Horde to the tsar with many gifts; and they humbly asked that he not be angry with them and that he register all their land as he wished.

In the same year census-takers came from the tsar out of the Horde to Vladimir, and took with them Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich of Vladimir, and Prince Andrei Yaroslavovich of Suzdal, and Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov, and went to Novgorod, and registered all the Novgorod and Pskov lands. Only they did not register archimandrites, abbots, priests, deacons, monks, singers, and all church servants who stand before the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and are in the house of the Lord, and serve God’s churches.
