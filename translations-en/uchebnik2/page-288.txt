He heard all this, and just as he himself could not persuade Michael either by kindness or by threat of death, so Michael would not heed the tears and entreaties of his own kinsmen that he fulfill the tsar’s command; but Michael remained of one mind.

Then Eldega rode to the tsar to report what Grand Prince Michael had said to him; and the multitude of Christians and pagans present there heard these words that Grand Prince Michael sent in reply to the tsar. Then this blessed prince Michael and his boyar Theodore began to chant the funeral service over themselves, and after the funeral service they received Communion, which their spiritual father gave them and with which he blessed them to suffer for Christ.

And Eldega came and told the tsar everything that the saint had said; and he also said: “Not only does he refuse to bow down to our gods, but he also mocks all who believe in them. He says much else besides that it is not fitting for your majesty to hear repeated.” These words so enraged the tsar that he was like one gone mad; and it was as if a huge fiery flame rose up, fanned from below by some power; and at once he cried out very loudly to the people standing before him, and ordered the saint to be handed over to the executioners.

When they heard this answer, the pagans at once rushed with one accord against the martyr, and first of all they wanted to take the prince’s belt from Michael. His grandson, Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov, likewise continually, together with the others, begged Grand Prince Michael to fulfill the tsar’s command. But Michael refused and said: “I do not wish to bear the name of Christian in words while doing pagan deeds.” Then he said: “My child Boris, Christians must honor the Lord God in faith and deeds. For faith without deeds is dead, according to the word of the apostle. And elsewhere, through the mouth of the prophet, the Lord says of such people: ‘This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me. In vain do they honor Me.’” His grandson Boris said to him: “You are for us all on earth a teacher and comforter.” Michael said: “Earthly things are nothing: they pass by, quickly vanish, like a dream and like smoke. But heavenly good exists eternally and without end.” And those with him said to him: “Prince Michael, those sent by the tsar are coming to kill you; bow down and fulfill the tsar’s command, and you will remain alive.” Grand Prince Michael and his boyar Theodore answered as if with one mouth: “We will not bow down, and we do not listen to you, and we do not want the glory of this world.” And when they saw that the murderers were coming toward them, they began to chant their own funeral service: “We are Your martyrs, O Lord; we have not renounced You, we have not departed from Your commandments; and we also suffer for You, O Christ, and endure many torments, like the other martyrs.”
