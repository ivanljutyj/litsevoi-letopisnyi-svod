Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with his brothers, having read the message, took offense and flew into a rage.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent them a message saying: “You do not even know this: as you come, so quickly will you depart. And say this to my brother Constantine: if he wishes to fight with us, then when he conquers, the whole land will be his.”

And Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with his brother Yaroslav Vsevolodovich and his other brothers became fierce and fell into a great rage. He hoped in the strength of his army, because much army had gathered; and raging, they began to feast in their tents with their boyars.

And he began to ask each of his boyars one by one: how should they go into battle with them? Each of them expressed his own opinion. But one of them, the old boyar Andrei Stanislavich, said nothing, and all thought that from old age he understood little, and they did not ask him diligently.

When they did ask him, merely in passing, “How should we deal with these enemies?” he answered: “Princes Yuri and Yaroslav! I am old and already understand little, but in my old age it is better to make peace and give the seniority to your elder brother Constantine Vsevolodovich, and to release the Novgorod districts and people, not to seize what is others’, and not to enter others’ lands, but to know and guard your own, and to stand firm in this. And do not look at the fact that their army is small while your army is large, but still do not forget that the princes of the Rostislavichi line are wise and brave, and the Novgorodians, Pskovians, and Smolenskians are zealous in battle. And about Prince Mstislav Mstislavich himself you know what he is like, and that courage has been given to him by God more than to all others, and he has very brave men and mighty bogatyrs, like lions and bears, for they do not feel wounds upon themselves. And about your elder brother Constantine, you yourselves know that the right is on his side, and he ought to be the senior. And do you not also know his bogatyrs Alexander Popovich and his servant Torop, Dobrynya Ryazanich of the Golden Belt, and Nefedya Dikun? And if you think I am mad and speak as one who has lost his mind from old age, then do as is pleasing to God and to you.”

Grand Prince Yuri with his brothers became furious and enraged at these words, and began to rush to and fro; this speech was not pleasing to him. His boyars began to say to him: “Why, prince, are you grieved over this? Do you not know that he is without sense from old age and it is not known what he says?”
