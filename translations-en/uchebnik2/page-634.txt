And from that time that church stood for 100 years until its renovation under Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver and Bishop Arseny of Tver. Many Tver bishops served the divine liturgy to the Lord in it: the first was Simeon, under Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich; 2. Andrew, also under Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich; 3. Varsonofy, under Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich and his sons Dmitry and Alexander, Constantine and Vasily; 4. Theodore, under Grand Prince Constantine Mikhailovich; 5. Theodore, under Grand Prince Vasily Mikhailovich. This Bishop Theodore placed a gilded cross on the cathedral church of the Holy Transfiguration of the Lord, and also on the side chapels of that church in honor of the holy martyr Demetrius and the Entrance of the Most Pure Mother of God he placed copper doors and made a marble floor in that cathedral church of the Transfiguration of the Lord. 6. Vasily; 7. Euthymius Vislen, under Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich; 8. Arseny the Wonderworker, who built the monastery of the Most Pure Mother of God at Zheltikovo, having formerly been great archdeacon under Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus', under Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich. Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich from his very youth cared for God’s churches; he built and adorned many churches, but he had the greatest care for the cathedral church of the Transfiguration of the Lord. He donated much wealth for its adornment and gilded its dome. And seeing it adorned by God’s grace, he wished to renew it, so that it might become as it had been before, when it was first built and whitewashed. And by his command stonemasons cut new slabs and renewed the church, and whitewashed it, and it became as it had been from the first day of its building. The Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich himself in that year was already ill with his legs.

The Ancient Tale of the Life of Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, Grandson of Mikhail

In that same year, on the 26th day of the month of August, Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, descendant of the forefather Yuri Dolgoruky, reposed, having received in the monastic and schema rank the name Matthew instead of Mikhail, and was interred in the cathedral church of the Transfiguration of the Lord by Bishop Arseny and the whole sacred council.
