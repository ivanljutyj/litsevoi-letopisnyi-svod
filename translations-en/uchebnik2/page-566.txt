His father Kestutis Gediminovich planned to take the great principality of Lithuania from Jogaila Olgerdovich. There was a great battle, and Kestutis Gediminovich was killed, and his son Vytautas at that same hour fled to the German land.

With the help of the German host he warred with Lithuania. And therefore with great joy he gave his daughter Sophia to the great prince Vasily Dmitrievich, hoping for his son-in-law’s help in the war with Lithuania. The great prince Vasily was married to Sophia Vytautovna in Moscow by Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus. That same winter a letter came from the patriarch to Novgorod the Great, according to the word of Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus, about the judgment, signed by the patriarch’s own hand—Anthony, patriarch of New Rome. The metropolitans of Nicaea, Chalcedon, and Adrianople, the Serbian, Gaianian, and Dramian metropolitans also signed this letter with their own hands. In that same year the great prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver summoned to him in Tver Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus.

And after Easter he went to Tver, and with him two Greek metropolitans: Matthew of Adrianople and Nicander of Gaia; and Michael, bishop of Smolensk, Stephen, bishop of Perm. Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus, was met by the grandson of the great prince Michael Alexandrovich, Prince Alexander, thirty versts from the city of Tver with boyars, with great honor.

On the next day Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus, was met by the eldest son of the great prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver, Prince Ivan, twenty versts from the city of Tver with boyars, with great honor.

On the third day, on Saturday after vespers, the great prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver himself, with princes and boyars, with great honor, met Cyprian, metropolitan of all Rus, five versts from the city of Tver. The metropolitan came out of his tent to meet the great prince and blessed the great prince Michael Alexandrovich, and kissed him with love. They sat and conversed for a long time about spiritual benefit.

In the morning the great prince again himself met Metropolitan Cyprian with his children, with his brothers, and with boyars at Peremir. And from there they went to the city, and there they were met with crosses before the Vladimir gates, at the church of the holy martyr George, and they kissed the holy crosses and the precious icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, entered the church of the Great Savior, and ordered the lamb to be slaughtered, and began to serve the holy liturgy.

After the completion of the divine service, the great prince with his children and with his nephews petitioned him to come to the feast together with the guests, the Greek metropolitans, with the bishops, with the elders, and with his boyars and servants, and with all who were there.
