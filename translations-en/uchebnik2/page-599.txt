In the same year the Novgorodians, with Prince Roman of Lithuania and Prince Konstantin Vasilyevich of Belozersk, went with an army against Pskov. At that time Prince Andrei Olgerdovich was in Pskov with his son, Prince Ivan, and during the raid there was a battle between the Novgorodians and the Pskovians. Prince Ivan Koporsky and Vasily Fedorovich were killed, and a great multitude fell on both sides.

And the Novgorodians returned home, at enmity with the Pskovians, and the Novgorodians took the office of posadnik from Iosif Zakharyich and gave it to Bogdan Avvakumovich.

In the same year Prince Andrei Olgerdovich came to Novgorod with the Pskovians and many regiments. And they departed from Novgorod without concluding peace.

In the same year Michael, Bishop of Bethlehem, came from Constantinople from Anthony, Patriarch of Constantinople, and brought to Novgorod two charters from Patriarch Anthony concerning taxes and expenditures, and concerning the ordination of priests, and an instruction to all Orthodox Christians.

In the same year in Moscow the daughter of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy was given in marriage to Prince Lugven Olgerdovich, grandson of Gediminas of Lithuania, on the Sunday of All Saints, June 24.

In the same year to Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Ivan, great-great-grandson of Daniel of Moscow, a son was born, Vasily.

In the same year the Germans came with an army to the city of Vilnius against Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutievich, grandson of Gediminas. And there was a great battle near the city of Vilnius. Vytautas Kestutievich of Lithuania prevailed and captured many German commanders.

In the same year the princes of Suzdal, Nizhny Novgorod, and Gorodets fled to the Horde — Vasily Dmitrievich Kirdyapa and his brother Prince Semyon Dmitrievich — to Tsar Tokhtamysh, seeking their patrimony: Nizhny Novgorod, Suzdal, and Gorodets.

And Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow sent pursuit after them; they tried very hard, but did not overtake them.

In the same year they planned in Moscow to dig a ditch from Kuchkovo Field to the Moskva River, and from this the people suffered great loss; houses were destroyed, and although they labored much, nothing came of it.

In the same year Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan went with an army to Lyubech, and returned home with a great number of captives.

In the same year Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan defeated the Tatars of Tokhtamysh’s Horde who had come raiding into the Ryazan lands.
