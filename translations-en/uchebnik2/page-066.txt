In the same year, Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, grandson of Monomakh, with his brothers, Prince Svyatopolk Mstislavich and Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and with his son Prince Mstislav, went to Galich against Grand Prince Yaroslav Volodimirkovich; and having come, they halted at Terebovl.

Prince Yaroslav Volodimirkovich of Galich wished to go against them himself; but his boyars said to him: “Our beloved lord prince! You are the only one we have; if anything should happen to you at the hands of our enemies, where then should we turn? It would be better for us never to have been born, or, having been born, to be buried in the earth, than to see such misfortune and calamity. You, prince, stay in the city, and we will go out to fight with Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, and with his brother Svyatopolk Mstislavich, and with Vladimir Andreevich, and with his son Mstislav; and if any of us remains alive and runs back to you in the city, then we, shutting ourselves in the city with you, will fight them.”

And Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev crossed over to them across the Sestra River; and both armies came together and fought fiercely, and the dead fell on both sides, and there was great confusion among them, and they did not recognize one another, and did not understand who had won: everywhere the dead lay, everywhere blood flowed.

And Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev pursued the Galicians, while his brother and his son fled; and Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, seizing Galician men, led them with him; and the Galicians, seizing Kiev men, boyars and commanders of Izyaslav, led them with them.

And Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev with a small retinue stood at the place of that battle and set up the Galician banners, and the Galicians came under their own banners; and a great multitude of them was captured.

That same night Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich became afraid, because he had remained in the field with a small retinue, saying thus: “Let us flee quickly to our own land, before the united Galicians come against us from the city.”

And he ordered the captive common people to be killed, but the commanders and boyars and the best men he led with him to Kiev; and the Galicians took his boyars and commanders and distinguished men to Galich, and others to Peremyshl, and others to Terebovl.

In the same year Rostislav Yaroslavich, Prince of Ryazan, founded in his own name the city of Rostislavl on the Oka River.

In the same year Nifont, Bishop of Novgorod, founded the Church of St Clement in Ladoga. In the same year Abbot Arkady established a monastery for himself and built in it a church of the Dormition of the Holy Mother of God.
