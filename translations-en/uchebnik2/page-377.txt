In the year 6838 from Adam (1338 from Christ)

In the Horde there died the son of Azbyak, Temir, who had killed the Tsar of Zagor, and Tsar Azbyak was in great sorrow.

In that year Tsar Azbyak favored the Bishop of Sarai, giving him everything he asked for and promising that no one would harm him in any way. In that year the Grand Prince of the Horde, Asan, was killed by his wife.

In that year Prince Feodor Ivanovich of Starodub was killed in the Horde, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Ivan, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky.

In that year Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver married in Bryansk, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-grandson of Vsevolod.

In that year the Most Reverend Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, came from Kiev to Volhynia, to the city of Vladimir.

In that year the Most Reverend Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, while he was in the city of Vladimir-in-Volhynia, consecrated Feodor there as bishop of Tver.

In that year the Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, by the blessing of his father the Most Reverend Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, founded a wondrous monastery and built in it a marvelous church in the name of the Holy Transfiguration of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ, in Moscow, near his court, and gathered there a multitude of monks. And he loved this monastery more than all other monasteries.

And he often came into it to pray, with great humility and blessing, and all marveled at his humility and his blessing. And he gave abundant alms to the monks living there, and took care of everything necessary for life: food and drink, clothing, and he fulfilled all their requests. And this monastery was held in great honor, had many privileges, and was well protected, so that no one could offend them. He adorned the church there with holy and venerable icons, filled it with holy books and sacred vessels, and every kind of ornament. And he appointed there the first archimandrite, named Ivan, a man of noble birth, intelligent and wise, well versed in the Divine Scriptures and virtuous. For his great virtue he was later consecrated bishop in the city of Rostov, and there he piously shepherded the flock entrusted to him by God, and in deep old age departed to the Lord. Some of the ancient elders say that the first was the Grand Prince Daniil Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, who had an archimandry at St Daniel’s, beyond the Moscow River, who established there a church in the name of his holy namesake.
