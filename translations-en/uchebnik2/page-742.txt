Dear readers!

This small volume includes texts from several volumes of the Illuminated Chronicle Code of Ivan the Terrible at once. We hope that this compact version of the narrative, in modern language only, will make easier and speed up the accomplishment of the most important task for every person—self-education.

Deep gratitude to the enthusiasts through whose efforts this History Textbook came into being: Nikolai Petrovich Tokarev, Anatoly Borisovich Yanovsky, and Konstantin Anatolyevich Babkin.
I wish you and your children fascinating and useful reading.

Glory to Jesus Christ!
