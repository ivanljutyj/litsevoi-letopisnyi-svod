In that year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, came from the Horde from Tsar Janibek with a grant of favour and with great honour. In that year Feodor, lord of Tver, installed copper doors on the cathedral church of the Holy Transfiguration of our Lord God and Saviour Jesus Christ in Tver.

In the year 6853 from Adam (the year 1353 from Christ)

In Novgorod the Church of the Holy Saviour at Kovalevo was founded. And a strong wind with snow arose, brought ice into the Volkhov, and tore out seven supports of the great bridge. This happened on the sixth day of September, on the commemoration of the holy miracle of the Archangel Michael.

Then the office of posadnik was given to Matfei Varfolomeevich. In that year Grand Princess Anastasia, wife of Semyon Ivanovich, reposed as a nun, in the schema, Lithuanian by birth. Her Lithuanian name was Avgusta, and in holy baptism Anastasia. And she was laid to rest in the monastery, in the Church of the Holy Saviour in Moscow, which she herself had ordered to be painted; and she patronized all the monks living there and held them in honour. In that year the Church of the Holy Saviour in the monastery was painted by the command and at the expense of Grand Princess Anastasia, wife of Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich. The masters and elders were Russian by birth, while the apprentices were Greeks: Goitan, Semyon, Ivan, and their other apprentices and company.

In that year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, married a second time, taking as his wife Evpraksia, daughter of Prince Feodor Svyatoslavich of Smolensk.

In that year his brothers also married: Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, and Prince Andrei Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel. All three brothers married in the same year.

In that year Prince Olgerd Gediminovich with his brother Kestutis Gediminovich came secretly and attacked his elder brother Narimant Gediminovich and his brother Evnutii Gediminovich in the city of Vilno.

And the people who were then in the city were frightened. And Prince Narimant Gediminovich fled from the city to the Horde to Tsar Janibek, son of Azbyak. And his other brother Evnutii Gediminovich fled to Pskov, and from there to Novgorod, and from there to Moscow to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich. And there in Moscow he was baptized and named Ivan in holy baptism.

In that year Prince Vasily Davydovich of Yaroslavl reposed as a monk, in the schema, and was laid in the Church of the Holy Saviour in Yaroslavl.

In that year Prince Vasily of Murom reposed and was laid in his patrimony, in Murom, in the Church of the Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb, in the monastery on the Ushna.
