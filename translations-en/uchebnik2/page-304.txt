In the same year the people in all the Russian cities took counsel together against the Tatars, whom Tsar Batu had made rulers in all the Russian cities, and after Batu’s death his son Sartak, and after him others.

The Russian princes then took counsel among themselves and drove the Tatars out of their cities, for they oppressed the Russians. Rich men bought from the Tatars the right to collect tribute and enriched themselves, and many poor people fell into slavery because of debts. And so the Russian princes drove out the Tatars, and killed some, and some of them were baptized in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

At that time they killed in Yaroslavl the traitor Izosima, who had renounced the Christian faith and monasticism and was a very wicked Muslim; and he received help from the tsar’s envoy Tityak, and did much evil to Christians; Orthodox Christians killed him and threw him to the dogs to be eaten.

In the same year came Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, and Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, with his brother-in-law Konstantin, and Tovtivil of Polotsk, and the Novgorodians, and they all went to war with a great army against Yuryev, a German city; and the city had three walls. And then quickly, in a single assault, they took the city and killed the Germans; and from the city they shot our valiant and very brave men; and they killed Peter Myasnikovich, and killed Yakov the Brave, the Nailmaker, and killed Ilya Degtyarev, and killed Izmail the Blacksmith, very brave and very daring men.

In the year 6771 from Adam (1271 from Christ)

A son, Mikhail, was born to Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Belozersk.

In the same year Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-forefather of the great Vladimir, went for the fourth time to the Horde to Tsar Sartak, and wintered there, and fell gravely ill, and was released to Rus.

And Alexander reached Gorodets, and received the holy angelic monastic habit and the monastic rank of schema, on the 14th day of November, and died that same night.

And they brought Alexander’s body to Vladimir and laid it in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God, on the 23rd day of the same month of November. When the Most Reverend Cyril, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, wished to place the letter of absolution in his hand, and the church servant Sebastian came up and wished to open his hand, Prince Alexander stretched it out himself, as if alive. When Metropolitan Cyril had placed the letter in his hand, Prince Alexander again bent it himself, as if alive.
