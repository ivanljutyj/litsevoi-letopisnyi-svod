The Novgorodians said to Prince Yuri’s envoys: “Prince, you are too proud.”

And the Novgorodians began to gather, and they set a palisade around the city.

And the Novgorodians sent envoys to Grand Prince Yuri with these words: “Prince, live in meekness and humility, and do not shed blood; and we, the Novgorodians, will not hand over our brothers to you; if you wish to advance and shed blood, then here we are ready to lay down our heads for the Most Holy Mother of God, and for one another, and for our brothers.”

And the Novgorodians placed guard detachments on all roads, and made a stronghold, and were ready to die for Saint Sophia and for one another.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent to them his man Ivan Verkha and Roman, the tysyatsky of Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich, with this message: “Take my brother-in-law Prince Mikhail, son of Vsevolod Chermny, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Oleg, to reign in Novgorod.”

The Novgorodians sent for his brother-in-law Prince Mikhail, son of Vsevolod Chermny; and Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with the princes went from Torzhok to his own land.

In the same year a star appeared, called a spear, stretching from east to west in the form of a spear; and it was more than seventeen days, foretelling an invasion of the godless Moabites upon Rus for our sins, which came to pass.

On the Battle on the Kalka River, on the Defeat of the Russian Princes

In the same year, because of our sins, unfamiliar peoples came, in the time of Grand Prince of Kiev Mstislav Romanovich, grandson of Rostislav Mstislavovich. Unknown godless Moabites came, called Tatars, about whom no one knows properly who they are or whence they came, what language they have, of what tribe they are, or what faith they hold. They are called Tatars, while some call them Taurmenians and others Pechenegs. Some say that these are the ones of whom Methodius, Bishop of Patara, testifies that they came from the Etrivian desert, located in the northeast; that these are the ones whom Gideon drove out; that they will appear at the end of times and seize the whole land from the east to the Euphrates, and from the Tigris to the Pontic Sea, except Ethiopia. About them we have heard that they have seized many lands: the Yas, the Obez, and the Kasog.

And the Tatars came into the land of the Polovtsians; the Polovtsians could not resist and fled; and some of them were killed, and some were driven off.
