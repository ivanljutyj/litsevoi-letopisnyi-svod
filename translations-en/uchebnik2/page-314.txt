And thus this new martyr appeared; in his sufferings he became like James the Persian.

O beloved Russian princes, do not be tempted by the vain, brief, and deceptive glory of this world, which is worse than a spiderweb; it changes with the passage of time and passes by like a phantom, and vanishes like smoke, and is all like a dream. For you brought nothing into this world, and you can carry nothing out; naked you came from your mother’s womb, and naked you will depart. Do not wrong one another, and do not be treacherous among yourselves, and do not seize what belongs to others, and do not wrong your younger kinsmen, for their angels see the face of your Father in Heaven. Love true righteousness, and humility, and long-suffering, and purity, and love, and mercy. And be filled with the joy of the saints, as this blessed Prince Roman received the Kingdom of Heaven for his sufferings, and accepted the crown from the hand of the Lord together with his kinsman, the great Prince of Chernigov, Mikhail Vsevolodovich, having suffered for Christ and for the Orthodox Christian faith; and he was killed in the month of July on the [9th] day.

In the year 6779 from Adam (the year 1279 from Christ)

Died Prince Vasily Aleksandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-forefather of Vladimir the Great.

In the same year the princes went to the Horde to the tsar: Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, his brother Vasily Yaroslavovich of Kostroma, and their nephew Dmitry Aleksandrovich of Pereyaslavl. In the same year there was a solar sign, on the fifth Sunday of Holy Lent, before noon: the sun disappeared completely and appeared again.

In the same year Princess Vasilkova died, when her son Prince Boris was with her, together with his princess and children, while Prince Gleb was then in the Horde with the tsar; she departed to the Lord quietly and meekly. Bishop Ignatius, with all the priests and with the proper funeral hymns, laid her in the Church of the Holy Savior in her convent.

In the same year died the great Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, forefather of Yaroslav, great-forefather of Vladimir the Great, while on the way from the Horde; he was great prince in Vladimir after his brother Alexander Yaroslavovich for 8 years.
