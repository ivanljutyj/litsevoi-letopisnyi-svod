His son Berdibek sat after him on the throne and killed his 12 brothers, instructed by his accursed prince and teacher, his well-wisher Tavlubiy: he killed his father and his brothers, and after some time he himself perished with his advisers.

In the same year the Most Reverend Alexis, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, came from the Horde through the mercy of God and the Most Pure Mother of God, safe and sound.

In the same year all the princes went to the Horde to the new tsar Berdibek, son of Janibek. But Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver was at odds and in discord with his nephew, Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Kholm, and they went separately. Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Kholm went to the Horde through Pereyaslavl, but there the viceroy of Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich did not let him through, and he went to Lithuania.

In the year 6866 from Adam (the year 1366 from Christ)

Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, came from the Horde and again summoned to himself two of his boyars who had left him for Ryazan — Mikhailo and his son-in-law Vasily Vasilyevich.

In the same year Prince Vasily Mikhailovich came from the Horde to Tver. In the same year a son, Ivan, was born to Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich.

In the same year a noble ambassador from the Horde, a tsar's son named Momat Khoja, came to the land of Ryazan and sent to Moscow to Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, to announce the delimitation of the land of Ryazan, so as to establish inviolable boundaries. Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich did not admit him into his patrimony. So it was.

And soon Tsarevich Momat Khoja was recalled by the tsar to the Horde, because a denunciation had been made against him. And great sedition began, because he killed the tsar's favorite in the Horde, and fled to Arnach, but there couriers overtook him, seized him, and by the tsar's command he was killed.

In the same year Prince Alexander, son of Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, great-grandson of Yaroslav, died at the residence of his wife, Grand Princess Sophia, in the St Sophia Monastery.

In the same year Prince Feodor Alexandrovich of Kholm, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Yaroslav, went to the Horde from Lithuania.

In the same year the Tver and Mozhaisk hosts took Rzhev and drove out the Lithuanians.

In the same year Feodor, Bishop of Tver, made copper doors in the Church of the Savior.
