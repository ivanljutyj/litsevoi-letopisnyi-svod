In that same winter the Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich married in Moscow on the eighth day of February, taking as his wife Princess Maria, daughter of Yaroslav Vladimirovich and granddaughter of Maria Goltyaeva.

In that same winter Ivan Dmitrievich left Tver for Galich to Prince Yuri Dmitrievich and began to urge him to take the grand principality; and Prince Yuri, after long reflection, sent for his sons, Princes Vasily and Dmitry Shemyaka, to Moscow; but they were then at the wedding of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich.

And then Pyotr Konstantinovich recognized on Prince Vasily a golden belt on chains with precious stones, which had been given to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich by Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal. We mention this because much evil came of it.

That belt, at the wedding of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, was substituted by the tysyatsky Vasily. He gave the Grand Prince a smaller one, and that one he gave to his son Mikula, and Mikula was married to Maria, the elder daughter of the same Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal.

And that Mikula gave the belt as dowry to Ivan Dmitrievich, and Ivan Dmitrievich gave it as dowry with his daughter to Prince Andrei Vladimirovich.

Later, when after the death of Prince Andrei and his return from the Horde, Ivan Dmitrievich betrothed the daughter of Prince Andrei, his granddaughter, to Prince Vasily Yuryevich, he gave him that belt; and at the wedding of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich the belt was on him. Grand Princess Sophia then removed the belt from him, and because of this Prince Vasily and Prince Dmitry Yuryevich took offense and left Moscow for Galich to their father, and plundered the city of Yaroslavl, seized the treasury of all the princes, and went to Galich.

Prince Yuri Dmitrievich marched with an army against his nephew Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich

They came to their father in Galich, and he had already gathered with all his people, intending to march against the Grand Prince. Having united with those who came to him, the prince set out with them and with a large army against Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and Ivan Dmitrievich was with them too, but the Grand Prince did not know of it.

Then his deputy Pyotr Konstantinovich came running from Rostov to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich and told him that his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich was coming against him with his sons and a large army and was already at Pereyaslavl.
