To meet death in his own patrimony in Pereyaslavl; if you, brother Vsevolod, are not satisfied with this power by which you hold the whole Russian land, and if you also want my authority, the great principality of Pereyaslavl, then, having killed me, you will receive it; but alive I will not give myself into your hands; and I will not depart from my authority, from the great principality of Pereyaslavl; and if you wish to kill me or otherwise put me to death, may the Lord God take vengeance on you in this age and in the age to come. It is no wonder that we and our lineage should suffer so, for formerly there was Svyatopolk, son of the great Vladimir, who, loving power and desiring to hold the whole Russian land, killed Boris and Gleb, and soon perished himself."
