In the same autumn in Novgorod died the Novgorod posadnik Yuri Antsifirovich, having been in this office a year, three months and ten days.

In the same autumn, through the care of the Novgorod archbishop, two churches were completed in Novgorod: the first, of St Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus; the second, of St Athanasius.

In the same winter many people died of frost, since that year the winter was very cold.

In the same winter died Grand Prince of Nizhny Novgorod Ivan Vasilyevich, grandson of Dmitry, while returning from Kolomna. And he was buried in Moscow in the church of St Archangel Michael.

In the same year Prince Daniel Borisovich of Nizhny Novgorod came to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.

In the same year, on the 10th day of the month of June, a thunderclap struck the church of St Eupatius on Rogatitsa; a terrible lightning flashed, scorched the icons, and many people were frightened by this phenomenon.

In the same month of June, on the 24th day, Archbishop John of Novgorod died and was buried in the porch of the monastery of the Holy Resurrection at Derevenitsa.

In the same year the boyar of Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, Gleb Semyonovich, with the Novgorod fugitives Simeon Zhadovsky and Mikhail Rossokhin, and with them also the Vyatchans with the Ustyuzhans, secretly sailed away in ships.

And they conquered all the Dvina and Zavolsk land, Kholmogory, and Borok, belonging to the children of Ivan Vasilyevich.

And they seized and burned Yemtsa, and took captive the Novgorod boyars Yuri Ivanovich and his brother Samson.

But the Novgorod boyar Ivan Fedotovich, his brother Afanasy and Gavriil Kirillovich, and Isaak Andreevich overtook them on an island near Morzh; they freed their brothers Yuri and Samson, seized all the captives and all the property, and released them, showing mercy.

They, however, went further and again conquered a district.

And Vasily Yuryevich, son of the Novgorod posadnik Samson Ivanovich, Gavriil Kirillovich and his brother Grigory Kirillovich, having united with the Zavolochans, rushed in pursuit of them. Some they took captive, some managed to escape, and others were killed.

And the Novgorodians with the Zavolochans went against Ustyug, conquered and plundered Ustyug, and burned it.

In the same year the Novgorodians concluded peace with the Germans under posadnik Simeon Vasilyevich and under tysyatsky Kozma Terentyevich.
