Morning came, and John convened a council and told before everyone what he had heard, and all together glorified God.

And the archbishop sent his archdeacon with the clerics of Holy Sophia and commanded that the holy icon of the Mother of God be brought to the council. The archdeacon entered the church and bowed before the holy icon of the Mother of God, and wished to take it, but the icon did not move from its place; the archdeacon returned and told the archbishop what had happened.

Then the archbishop with all the sacred assembly rose quickly and came to the church of the Holy Savior. He approached the holy icon of the Mother of God, and kneeling, began to pray, saying: “O most merciful Lady, Virgin Sovereign, Most Pure Mother of God! You are the intercessor and hope unashamed of our city, the wall and covering and refuge of Christians; for trusting in you, Lady, we sinners also pray to your Son and our God; do not deliver us to the mockery of those who war against us, hear the weeping of your people, accept the prayer of your servants, and deliver us, Lady, and our city from our enemies and from every evil.” Praying thus, they began to sing supplicatory services and the canon to the Most Holy Mother of God; and after the sixth ode, when the kontakion “Intercessor for Christians…” was read, at that time the icon moved by itself; the people, seeing this, cried with tears: “Lord, have mercy!” Archbishop John took the icon in his own hands and handed it to two deacons, and they carried it before him, while he followed after it with all the sacred assembly, performing the supplicatory canon; the people crowded and followed.

And they carried the icon up onto the fortification where now is the monastery and church of the Holy Mother of God on the Tithe; the Novgorodians could not stand outside the fortification, but only wept, each seeing his own ruin, since the Suzdalians had divided the streets among themselves throughout the city.

At the sixth hour of the day all the Russian regiments began to advance and loosed arrows at the city like rain; then, by God’s providence, the icon turned its face toward the city, and the archbishop saw tears flowing from that holy icon and caught them in his phelonion. O great and glorious wonder! How can there be tears from dry wood? These are not tears but a merciful sign; for in this way the Most Holy Mother of God prays to her Son and our God for the city and her people who hope in her mercy. By this the Most Holy Mother of God showed her intercession and defense for people in despair, and so the Lord God, through the prayers of his Most Pure Mother, had mercy on his people who had sinned. And at that hour darkness came upon the attackers, and something like ashes fell upon them, and trembling and terror seized them, and some were blinded.
