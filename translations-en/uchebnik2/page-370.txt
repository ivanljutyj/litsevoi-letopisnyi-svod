And Palaeologus, Milutin’s father-in-law, ordered him to remain without leaving in the Monastery of the Almighty. The valiant man endured with thanksgiving, becoming ever more virtuous and diligent, and remaining an example of benefit to all, both in word and deed, so that the king himself heard of him, and the patriarch, who summoned the righteous man to himself; and they marveled at his words and understanding.

At that same time the wondrous Patriarch Athanasius convened a council against a certain Barlaam, leader of the Akindinian heresy, who thought like Arius and Macedonius, and called the Lord’s Transfiguration a phantom, and caught many in his heresy. The patriarch, with the whole council, excommunicated Barlaam the heretic from the Church and delivered him with his like-minded followers to anathema. But he did not cease writing and disturbing the Church with many deceits.

Stephen was summoned to the king according to custom, and after many conversations he said: “O king most crowned by God, you have surpassed many who were before you in all virtuous manners and humility, but about one thing I do not know how you can sin, being the head and crown of the kingdom, in that for which the apostles labored and the martyrs did not spare their flesh, that is, concerning piety?” The king said to him: “How do you think we sin concerning piety, tell me, most excellent of friends?” Stephen answered: “It is evident, O king, that a shepherd who neglects the wolf’s entry into the flock and does not drive it off already becomes such a beast himself, although he bears the name of shepherd; and if he does not drive away the heterodox, then he too will be condemned as heterodox. Therefore it does not seem righteous to one who has understanding, O king, for you, honored with the rank of kingdom and appointed by Christ as shepherd of so many flocks, to keep His enemies in your flock, but, like wolves, to drive far away the soul-destroying and to sing with David: I have hated those who hate Thee, O Lord, and I have wasted away because of Thine enemies, and with perfect hatred I have hated them, and they have become enemies to me; and command them to be driven out of all your kingdom. If you do this, you will stop the Church’s dissensions, and grant deep peace to the Orthodox, and magnify the scepter of the kingdom; you will be a true king to true Christians, and a true shepherd, and from the common Master you will receive the reward — the heavenly kingdom.” Hearing this, the autocrat marveled at the man’s wisdom and rational understanding, and, having thanked and praised him much, said to those in the palace: “This man is very great in understanding and most far-seeing with the eyes of the mind, although his bodily eyes have been shut.”

And then the king ordered Barlaam brought to him bound. But someone from the palace, one of his heresy, informed him. And he quickly fled to Rome. The king ordered his like-minded followers driven out of the city and out of all cities and villages, driven with dishonor from his kingdom.
