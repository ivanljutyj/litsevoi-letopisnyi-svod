Therefore, rendering what is due to your love, I first myself confess and beseech you to adhere to the apostolic and patristic faith received from God, the Orthodoxy of true piety in the Holy Trinity; and as I myself receive the Holy Church and Holy Scripture from God and from the holy apostles, from the divine fathers, from the holy ecumenical and local councils, and from the saints especially glorified I lovingly receive and venerate from the depth of my heart with much faith, so I also ask this of you and command you to keep it; and to keep the other sacred, apostolic, and patristic commandments of God’s Church whole and unchanged, as is written in my confession, so I also hand this over to the Divine Church, when at the beginning of my hierarchal path I was ordained by the Most Reverend Matthew, Patriarch of Constantinople, and by the holy consecrated ecumenical council. Although some of them have departed from this life, yet some are still alive; to all of them I give forgiveness in Christ, and I myself ask the same of them. And to all the most holy patriarchs, who died before and are still living now, I give peace, love, and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. Also to all Orthodox tsars I give blessing and forgiveness. Also to all pious and Christ-loving great Russian princes and great princesses, who died earlier and who passed away in our years, to all of them I give blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. Also to the God-loving Russian bishops ruling within the bounds of the metropolitanate of our cathedral church, and to all others who passed away before and in our years, to all I give blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. Also to all faithful Russian princes and princesses who passed away before and in our years, to all I give blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. Also to every priestly and monastic order who died earlier, to all I give blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. Also to all Orthodox Christians who died earlier and in our years, during famine, pestilence, in waters, in fire, in battles, in captivity, by violent death, who died before and in our years, to all I give blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them. To my faithful, noble, and Christ-loving son in the Holy Spirit, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, with his mother, Grand Princess Sophia, I give peace, love, and blessing, and the final kiss, and forgiveness. And to my faithful, noble sons in the Holy Spirit, Princes George and Andrew Dmitrievich, with their princesses and children, I give blessing and forgiveness. Also to all great Russian princes ruling in their great princedoms, to all appanage and local princes with their princesses and children, I leave blessing in Christ and forgiveness.
