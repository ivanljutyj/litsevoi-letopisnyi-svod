In the same year they came with their army and captured the Novgorodian Korelian suburb.

And the Novgorodians went after them with Prince Semyon, called Lugven Olgerdovich, and, coming into the Swedish land, they conquered villages and burned them, took Swedes captive, and cut down some of them; they captured and burned the suburb of the city of Vyborg.

In the same year the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Rostov was completed, which had burned during a fire, and it was consecrated by Vladyka Gregory, Archbishop of Rostov.

In the same year in Novgorod Ivan, Vladyka of Novgorod, built a stone church of the holy confessors Gurias, Samonas, and Abibus.

In the same year in Novgorod, during the posadnikship of Grigory Bogdanovich and under tysyatsky Vasily Osipovich, the Novgorodians began to use squirrel foreheads, Lithuanian groschen, and German artugs in trade, and abolished kunas and marten muzzles.

In the same year the Zavolochians went with an army against the Murmansk people by order of the Novgorodians, together with voivode Yakov Stefanovich, and fought with them.

In the year 6920 from Adam (1420 from Christ)

In September Prince Alexander Ivanovich came from Lithuania to Tver, having received great honors from Vytautas.

In the same autumn Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver founded a town on the Novotorzhsk border.

In the same winter, on the eighth day of the month of December, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich was in his village of Strazhkovo for the feast of the Holy Conception; they were already singing the prokeimena at Vespers when at that time a huge and terrible serpent flew from Kashin; it flew from east to west toward a certain lake, shining like the dawn. And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, the boyars, and all the people in all the districts around the city saw it at the same hour. In the same winter, on the seventeenth day of the month of January, Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of Yaroslavl gave his daughter Maria in marriage to Prince Alexander Feodorovich.

In the same winter Zeleni-Saltan, son of Tokhtamysh, took the throne and drove out Prince Edigey.

In the same year there was a grain crop failure in Nizhny Novgorod. They bought a measure of rye for forty-four altyns in old money.

In the same year a harsh ambassador came to Tver from the Horde, from Tsar Zeleni-Saltan Tokhtamyshevich, summoning Grand Prince Ivan to come after him to the Horde.
