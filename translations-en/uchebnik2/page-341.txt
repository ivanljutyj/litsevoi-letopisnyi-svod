In the same year the Germans came to Pskov by sudden raid and burned the settlement, and killed many people, and besieged the city. And Dovmont, Prince of Pskov, came out against them, and there was a fierce battle near Saints Peter and Paul on the bank, such as had never been in Pskov; and many Pskovians were killed.

Yet by God’s help the Pskovians prevailed and killed the Germans, and drowned others. And Prince Dovmont of Pskov took the men of Viljandi captive and sent them to Vladimir to Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Vladimir.

In the same year Tsar Tokhta went a second time against Tsar Nogai and defeated him.

In the year 6808 from Adam (1308 from Christ)

There was a very great fire in Novgorod, with wind and whirlwind, and many holy churches and people burned.

In the same year the Swedish Germans came with a great force into the Neva River, bringing with them wise and highly skilled masters of siege engines from Rome, from the Pope of Rome, and they took a great force from the Pope to help them.

And they founded a city at the mouth of the Okhta River and named it the Crown of the Land. They fortified it with inexpressible strength and left in it a commander named Osten with a multitude of noble people.

And they departed with their commanders and with the wise papal and royal masters to their own land.

In the same year the Most Reverend Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, came to Novgorod and Pskov, and with him Simeon, Bishop of Rostov, and Andrew, Bishop of Tver. And with the two bishops, Simeon of Rostov and Andrew of Tver, he appointed Theoktistos, igumen of the Annunciation, as Archbishop of Novgorod and Pskov, on the feast of the holy apostles Peter and Paul. And there was great joy in Novgorod. In the same year in Novgorod they laid the foundation of a stone church of Saint Michael on Mikhailov Street.

In the year 6809 from Adam (1309 from Christ)

There were great winds and many dreadful storms, and strong whirlwinds, and terrible thunder, and lightning, and great rains, so that all people trembled and were terrified; and the clouds were so strong that in but a single hour the ditches and ravines became like abysses; and the winds with whirlwind tore many churches and houses of inhabitants from their foundations, and from some tore off the tops and destroyed great churches to the halfway point, and such could not be counted.
