When the Orthodox Tsar Ivan of Constantinople learned of the evil that had befallen the Christians, he was greatly saddened and grieved by what had happened, not knowing what to do. And he sent his envoys with great gifts to the Sultan of Egypt, asking him to grant relief to the Christians and to lessen his anger against them.

And the Sultan of Egypt listened to him: he released the metropolitans, bishops, abbots, priests, and monks imprisoned in dungeons, and all the Christians; he ordered all Christian churches to be opened and permitted the Christians to perform all that is established by their Christian law without oppression in Egypt and in Antioch, and in Jerusalem, and in all the nearby cities.

And he released all the Christians, and the archimandrites, and abbots, and monks, and presbyters, and all who hold sacred rank, to the metropolitans and bishops, permitted them to perform all that is established by their Christian law and custom, and promised them that they would no longer be wronged by anyone.

And for this he took from the metropolitan then ruling, on behalf of all the bishops, archimandrites, and abbots, all the Christians, and all who lived in those cities and districts of his realm, twenty thousand rubles.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver built the new town of Gorodok on the Volga.

In the year 6875 from Adam (1375 from Christ)

The former Bishop Feodor of Tver reposed in the Otroch Monastery, and he was buried in the small Church of the Entry of the Most Pure Mother of God in the same grave as Bishop Andrew.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich laid the foundation of the stone city of Moscow, and its construction began.

And Prince Dmitry began to subject all the Russian princes to his authority, and with those who did not submit to his will he began to wage war, as happened with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver. And because of this Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich went to Lithuania.

And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, and his son Prince Mikhail, and Prince Eremei summoned Bishop Vasily to Moscow for trial before the metropolitan with the help of the metropolitan’s bailiff, because he had judged them unjustly in their dispute over the appanage of Prince Semyon. Thus in Moscow, because of this trial, Bishop Vasily had to pay large court costs, and in Tver the wealthy people had to pay for the appanage of Prince Semyon.
