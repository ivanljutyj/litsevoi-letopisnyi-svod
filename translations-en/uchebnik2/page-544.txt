Also on the 5th day of the same month they went to Pigi and venerated the Most Pure Theotokos, and drank the holy healing water, and washed with it. Also on the 8th day of the same month they went to the Peribleptos monastery and kissed the shrine of the Forerunner and the head of Saint Gregory the Theologian, Patriarch, and the brow of Saint Stephen the New, and the icon of the Lord from which a voice came to Emperor Maurice, and in it were placed many holy relics. And there is a chalice there made of topaz, a precious stone; and they saw a great multitude of other holy relics.

Also on the 9th day of the month of August they went to the Church of the Pantocrator and to the sacristy. They saw the holy Gospel, written entirely in gold by the hand of Emperor Theodosius the Younger. And there they kissed the blood of the Lord that flowed from His ribs at the Crucifixion on the cross.

Also afterward the most reverend Pimen, metropolitan, fell ill and departed this life on the 11th day of the month of September in Chalcedon. And bringing his body, they laid it outside Constantinople, on the seashore opposite Galata, in the church of the Forerunner.

At that time Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev, was also in Constantinople, staying there concerning the ordering of the Russian see; and Metropolitan Pimen, while he was alive, had come to Patriarch Antony in Constantinople concerning the governance of the Russian see. But God in His providence so arranged it: Metropolitan Pimen departed this life, as we wrote before. And there the most reverend Patriarch Antony blessed Cyprian as metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus and sent him away with honor.

And he set out from there on the 1st day of the month of October, and with him Michael, Bishop of Smolensk, and Jonah, Bishop of Volhynia, and with him also two Greek metropolitans, and Theodore, Archimandrite of Simonov, the spiritual father of the Grand Prince.

They departed, and some time after their departure news came from them, saying: “The Russians drowned at sea; only one ship with the metropolitan was saved, but the ship with the bishops disappeared without trace.” And some said of them that they had drowned, while others said they had been killed by robbers; others said they had been carried to Amastris by great winds and a strong swell. Others said they were at Daphnusius.
