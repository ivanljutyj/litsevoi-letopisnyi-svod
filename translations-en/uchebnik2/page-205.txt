He placed all his hope, and God subdued all his enemies under his feet. Bishop John buried him with funeral hymns, with abbots and monks, and with all the consecrated clergy of the city of Vladimir, and his sons wept for him with great weeping, and all his boyars, and all his men, and all the land subject to him.

His sons: Prince Constantine of Rostov, Boris, Yuri, Yaroslav, Vladimir, Svyatoslav, Ivan. All the years of Vsevolod’s reign were thirty-seven. After him his son Yuri sat on the grand principality in Vladimir, and his eldest son, Prince Constantine, in Rostov and Yaroslavl.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, released the Ryazan princes and their bishop Arseny, and sent them to their patrimony in Ryazan, and Bishop Arseny with them, and all the Ryazan people went each to his own.

In the same year Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich fled from Vladimir to his elder brother Constantine in Rostov, and he began to plot war against his brother Yuri, wishing to take Vladimir from him.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri also began to plot war against his elder brother Constantine, and went against him to Rostov with his brothers Yaroslav, Vladimir, and Ivan.

And when they reached Rostov, they made peace, and they dispersed each to his own.

In the same year there were tonsures for the sons of Grand Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich of Rostov, Vasilko and Vsevolod.

In the same year there was a great famine, and they ate meat during the Great Fast.

In the same year the Pskovians expelled Prince Vladimir of Toropets from their city, and they themselves went to the lake.

And in the Fast of St Peter the Lithuanians came to Pskov; they captured many volosts and villages, ravaged and burned them, and returned home with much plunder and many captives.

In the same year Prince Vladimir Yuryevich fled from Vladimir to his elder brother Prince Constantine in Rostov.

Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with his brothers Yaroslav and Ioann went to a meeting with them, and they were at Yuryev, and made peace.

In the same year Prince Vladimir went from his brother, Prince Constantine of Rostov, to Volok, and from there went to Moscow, and having arrived, shut himself up in it.

And Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich went from Rostov, from Constantine, to Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich in Vladimir, and he gave him Yuryev-Polsky.
