The Batu Invasion in the 6745th Year from Adam
(1245th Year from Christ)

In the twenty-fifth year after the repose of Grand Prince Dmitry Vsevolod, for our sins God brought the godless Tsar Batu to ravage the Russian land. They took the glorious city of Vladimir and the cathedral church; all around it and within it they piled up wood and set it alight. There were many people there: some died from the fire, others suffocated, and the rest were cut down. And by God's permission the impious stripped all the precious covering from the very wonder-working image of the Mother of God. But her divine image, in a wondrous way, unknown how, proved intact, without any damage from the irresistible fire and from the hands of the lawless Christ-hating Hagarenes, for the confirmation of piety and the preservation of the Russian land by the unfailing grace of that holy icon, as the Mother of God herself had said of old: "My grace is with you." Then God and the Most Pure Mother of God delivered Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, father of blessed Alexander Nevsky, grandfather of Daniel of Moscow, together with his six sons and other princes, from the invasion of the impious.

After that invasion Yaroslav sat in the city of Vladimir, cleansed the church of dead bodies, and, gathering the scattered people, calmed them.

And from that time this wonder-working image of the Mother of God has had an adornment like the former one, worthy of its beauty. And this divine icon remained in the city of Vladimir for 242 years, and after that, by God's good pleasure and as the Mother of God herself willed, her healing image was transferred from the city of Vladimir to the reigning city of Moscow.

On Temir-Aksak

In the seventh year of the scepter-bearing of the Russian kingdom under the pious Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Ivan Kalita, and of Daniel, son of Alexander Nevsky. These were all grand princes for whom the Most Pure Mother of God was protectress and invincible strength, and they lovingly venerated her honorable image with faith.

It became known that a certain tsar named Temir-Aksak came from the eastern country, from the Blue Horde, from the land of Shamakhi, and began great turmoil in the Horde and in Rus, stirring up many wars, in which he won victories, overcame countless regiments, ravaged many cities and destroyed people in them, conquered many countries and lands, and subdued many peoples, principalities, and many kingdoms to himself. But Temir himself by origin was not even a tsar: neither a tsar's son, nor of tsarist, princely, or boyar stock.
