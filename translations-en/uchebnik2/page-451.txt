In the same year there was a severe drought; throughout the whole summer not a single drop of rain fell, and there was a great pestilence among horses, cows, sheep, and all livestock; afterward the pestilence came upon people as well throughout the whole Russian land.

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow broke off relations with the Tatars and Mamai; at that time there was a severe pestilence in Mamai’s Horde.

In the same year Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Mikhail, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, fled from Tver to Moscow to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

In the same year the Lithuanian army went to war against the Tatars and Temirez, and there was a fierce battle.

In the year 6883 from Adam (1383 from Christ)

Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich ordered Prince Sarai and his retinue to be separated. He burst into the lord bishop’s courtyard with his retinue, set the courtyard on fire, and began shooting people; he wounded many and put others to death.

Then they also began shooting at the bishop, and an arrow struck the mantle of Bishop Dionysius, but the Lord God preserved him; then people gathered and killed Sarayka and his retinue.

In the same year Tatars came from Mamai’s Horde, captured and burned Kashin, and killed the boyar Parfeniy Feodorovich.

And they conquered, plundered, and burned all Zapyanye, cut down many people, and took some captive.

In the same year the venerable Abbot Sergius fell gravely ill; he became sick and lay in bed during the second week of Great Lent, and he began to recover and rise from bed only on Simeon’s Day, while he spent the whole spring and summer in illness. And no one was surprised at this, seeing the sorrows and illnesses sent by God to the righteous. It is written: “Many are the sorrows of the righteous, and the Lord delivers them from all of them,” and “Through many sorrows we must enter the Kingdom of Heaven.” But if sinful people are healthy and abide in pleasures and joy, and in this world do not remain in sorrow and sadness, then torment is prepared for them in the age to come. If sinful people endure sufferings in this world, their sins will be forgiven them by God; and for the righteous, who have suffered much, crowns and ineffable glory in heaven are prepared by the Lord God.

In the same year, before the fast of Great Lent began, the son of the tysyatsky, Ivan Vasilyev, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Veniamin, fled from Moscow to Tver to Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, and with him a certain man of Surozh, Nekomat, with lies and flattering speeches.
