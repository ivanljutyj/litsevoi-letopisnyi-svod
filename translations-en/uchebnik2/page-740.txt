And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich neither sent his boyars, nor assigned the Novgorod patrimony to the Novgorodians anywhere, nor freed his land, nor began any redress.

In the same year the Lord Archbishop of Novgorod Evfimy laid the foundation at his court in Novgorod for the second time and built a stone church of St John Chrysostom over the gates.

In the same year Evfimy, Archbishop of Novgorod, laid the foundation of a stone church of St Nicholas the Wonderworker on Vezhishchi.

In the same year, by God’s permission for our sins, it was very cold, and frost killed the fruit and the unthreshed grain throughout the Russian land, and there was great dearth throughout all the land; in Novgorod many departed to the Germans as soon as the famine grew worse.

In the year 6945 from Adam (the year 1445 from Christ)

On the 20th day of September there was a great internecine battle in Novgorod.

In the same autumn in Novgorod there was high water and much ice, and at night the frozen water broke out seven bridge-cribs of the great bridge, and washed away the Zhilotutsky bridge, and caused much harm to the Novgorodians.

In the same winter the Novgorodians sent their posadnik Grigory Kirillovich to Grand Prince Sigismund, son of Kestutis, brother of Vytautas, grandson of Gediminas of Lithuania, to conclude peace, as had been established from old; and Grand Prince Sigismund Kestutievich gave them assurances, and kissed the cross before the Novgorod envoys according to the Novgorod charters for all Novgorod; and they made peace and love according to the old custom, as it had been from ancient times.

In the same winter Prince Yuri Patrikeevich Cherny came from Moscow to Novgorod from Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich to request the Black Bor tribute, and the Novgorodians gave the Black Bor to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich.
