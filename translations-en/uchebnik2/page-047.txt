Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich sent to Grand Prince Vladimir of Galich, the in-law of Grand Prince Yuri, saying: “Bring me, brother, into love and peace with my uncle, Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Manomash, for I am guilty in all things before God and before him.” Grand Prince Vladimir of Galich spoke to his in-law Grand Prince Yuri about peace, for Prince Yuri also wanted peace; but his son Prince Rostislav and Prince Yuri Yaroslavich, grandson of Svyatopolk, did not want peace; and they told Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Manomash not to make peace with him.

Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich again sent asking for peace; but his son Prince Rostislav and Prince Andrei Yaroslavich, grandson of Svyatopolk, would not allow peace to be made with him. Then his son Prince Andrei, being God-loving and man-loving and most merciful, began to entreat his father, saying: “Father, father! Why do you listen to those who do not want peace? Receive and have mercy on your nephew, Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich, and you yourself will receive mercy from God, and you will preserve your patrimony, and you will not pass enmity with him from generation to generation on to us, your sons; make peace with him and strengthen it with him by kissing the cross, and so, being in love, you will together be very strong against the infidel enemies.”

And so they made peace among themselves, and went to Peresopnitsa, into the domain of Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Manomash, and kissed the precious cross; and so they were strengthened by the precious and life-giving cross to be in concord, unity, and love, and they went their separate ways: Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich went to the great reign in Vladimir, and Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Manomash went to the great reign in Kiev.

In the same year Prince Igor Davidovich came from Ryazan to Kiev to Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich. In the same year the Polovtsians took Torshin, and ravaged and burned the volosts and villages.

In the same year the Novgorodians fought with the Suzdalians, and the Novgorodians stood on an island, and the Suzdalians stood against them, and began to build a fortification in boats; and on the third day the Novgorodians went against them to battle, and there was fierce slaughter, and many people fell on both sides. In the same year the Yam came in war against Novgorod, and the Novgorodians went out against them and killed more than five hundred of them.
