Through His most pure Mother, the Theotokos, and the great wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of all Rus, the Ishmaelite enemies were terrified and soon fled, although no one resisted them; only the Lord God terrified them and drove them away. Though He allowed time for battle and for us to be humbled, He did not abandon us utterly in His mercy. Even if a boar tramples the meadow and a solitary wild boar wounds us, He will not uproot the root of piety. So let us learn to do good and to keep the Lord’s commandments, that by repentance, compunction, confession, almsgiving, and generosity to the poor we may please God, obtain present blessings, and receive the mercies promised in the future, through the grace and love for mankind of our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and dominion forever and ever, amen.

That same winter Edigei’s army seized the Klin volost within the borders of the Tver land of the house of the Holy Savior; many people were killed, and some were taken captive.

That same year grain was very dear, so that many Christians died of hunger, while grain sellers grew rich.

And in that same year Edigei, Prince of the Horde, sent to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, saying:

“A rumor reached us that Tokhtamysh’s children live with you, and therefore we set out with an army. Further reports reached us that what is improper is being done in your cities: the tsar’s envoys and merchants from the Horde come to you, and you make envoys and merchants a laughingstock; they suffer insults and oppression among you. This is not right. When formerly this was the tsar’s ulus, and he administered the principality and the customs dues, the tsar’s envoys were honored, and merchants were not insulted or oppressed. You should have learned from the elders how everything was done formerly; but now you do not act so—is that right? Temir-Kutluy began to rule, and you became sovereign of the ulus, yet you have not been in the Horde before the tsar, you have not seen the tsar even with your eyes, nor the princes, nor noble boyars, nor common ones; you have sent no one, neither son nor brother, nor have you conveyed a word through anyone. And afterwards Shadibek reigned for eight years, and you did not visit him, nor did you send anyone to him with a word. And Shadibek’s reign has already passed; now Tsar Bulat-Saltan has begun to rule the realm, and he has already reigned for three years, yet you have neither gone to him yourself nor sent a son, a brother, or a noble boyar.

Yet you, a great prince, rule over so vast an ulus, but all your deeds are evil and unjust. Good customs and good counsels and good deeds toward the Horde existed in the time of Feodor; he was a good man who remembered the Horde’s good deeds.
