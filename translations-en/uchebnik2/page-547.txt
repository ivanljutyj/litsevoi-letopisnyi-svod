He came down; and standing below, he showed the patriarch on the ambo his hand and the crown; and the patriarch, standing on the ambo, blessed the tsar and tsarina from afar with his hand. And both of them at the same time and equally made a bow to the patriarch.

And they went to their places and sat on golden thrones, and the patriarch made his exit and entered the holy altar through the royal doors. And when the Cherubic Hymn began, the great archdeacons went and made a small bow to the tsar, only inclining their heads to their breasts very decorously and according to order.

Then the tsar, arising with fear and trembling and great attentiveness, went very decorously into the altar, and he was clothed in the sacred phelonion.

And the tsar walked before all before the holy royal doors, and during the transfer he held a lit candle in his hand. Thus he went out from the altar and entered the altar, and he walked before all; and after him, very decorously and in order, came the great sacred assembly with great beauty and honor and sacred and divine glory. And it was so decorous and reverent and beautiful that it surpassed the human mind. Long was their procession with the holy gifts: as long as the Cherubic Hymn is sung from beginning to end, so long was their procession with the holy gifts. The singing was very long and beautiful, wondrous, and moving.

After the holy divine gifts had been brought into the holy altar, the tsar censed around the holy altar and remained in the altar until holy communion.
