In the same winter Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich went from Tver to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and with Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver he broke the peace treaty, because he feared what Prince Ivan Mikhailovich had done to his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich.

At that time the winter was without snow; the spring thaw came during Cheese-fare Week, and the flood on the leave-taking before Great Lent, and the water came. Spring arrived, and people marveled, because such a thing happens rarely and is unknown to many—when it changes and in what year it happens.

In the same year, in the month of April, in Holy Week, on Great Friday, in Tver the brothers made peace among themselves: Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver with his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, and they kissed the honorable cross on it. The grand prince sent his brother Prince Vasily with love to Kashin on Bright Week, on Tuesday. In the same year there were terrible storms with thunder and lightning; many people were killed by thunder and burned by lightning strikes. In the same year in Tver the Church of Saint John the Theologian burned from a lightning strike on Trinity Week, on Tuesday, in the evening hour.

In the same year, in Peter’s Fast, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich fled from Kashin to Moscow, and Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver sent his governors to Kashin; much evil was done to Christians by exactions and robberies, because after three months of humility and love, enmity and hatred flared up between them even more.

And therefore Prince Vasily Mikhailovich fled to Moscow, not wishing harm to himself. In the same year, on Peter’s Day, Prince Alexander Ivanovich, grandson of Mikhail Alexandrovich, went from Tver to Kashin. In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Suzdal died.

In the same year Grand Prince Vytautas Keistutovich of Lithuania went to the Polish King Yakov, nicknamed Yagailo, and Yagailo went out to meet him.

And they met in the city of Milolyubne. His Eminence Metropolitan Cyprian was also present; he stayed with them for two weeks, and they showed him great honors and gave gifts to him, his boyars, and his servants, and they parted.

In the same year slanderers slandered Bishop Antony of Turov before Grand Prince Vytautas Keistutovich of Lithuania. Vytautas became angry with the bishop, and the slanderers, choosing a convenient time for themselves, accused the bishop still more and sowed enmity and discord between them.
