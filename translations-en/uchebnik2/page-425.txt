And an envoy came to him from Mamai’s Horde from Tsar Avdul with a yarlik for the great reign of Vladimir.

And the grand prince, having received the envoy with honors and bestowed gifts upon him, sent him back to Mamai’s Horde to Tsar Avdul, and himself went from Vladimir to Pereyaslavl.

In the same year Tsar Amurat, brother of Tsar Khidir, having learned together with his Sarai princes that an envoy had come from Mamai’s Horde from Tsar Avdul to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich with a yarlik, became very angry.

At that time Prince Ivan Belozersky was in Tsar Amurat’s Horde.

Tsar Amurat sent Prince Ivan Belozersky back to Rus, and together with him sent his envoy Ilyak with thirty Tatars to Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich with a yarlik for the great reign of Vladimir.

And they came to Suzdal to Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich, and he received them with great honors and joy.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew, went from Suzdal to Vladimir for the great reign, and with him were Tsar Amurat’s envoy Ilyak and thirty Tatars, and Prince Ivan Belozersky.

Then Prince Ivan Belozersky came from Tsar Amurat’s Horde with the envoy Ilyak and thirty Tatars. And Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal sat upon the great reign in Vladimir.

Having learned of this, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, with his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, and with his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, gathered a great army and went against him in war to Vladimir, and drove him out of Vladimir. And he fled to Suzdal, having remained in the great reign in Vladimir only twenty days.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich went after him with his army to Suzdal and stood with his host around Suzdal, laying waste the whole surrounding country.

And then Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich made peace with him and went from Suzdal to Nizhny Novgorod to Grand Prince Andrey Konstantinovich, his elder brother.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, having won victory over him, returned to Vladimir. He likewise won victory over Prince Konstantin of Rostov.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich drove Prince Dmitry of Galich from the principality of Galich.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich drove Prince Ivan Feodorovich of Starodub from the principality of Starodub.
