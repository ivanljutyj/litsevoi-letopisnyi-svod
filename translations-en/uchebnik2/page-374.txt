Then internecine strife began. Someone named Yunets was sent by the tsar to guard the holy place. He began worse than the first to insult and torment the hegumen and the brethren, giving them nothing they needed, but taking everything and appropriating it for himself.

The brethren with the hegumen prayed to Saint Stephen to deliver them from this calamity.

At that time Yunets went off to war. And there, in a dream, he saw as if he were in the monastery, in the church, and there appeared to him a man of fearsome appearance, adorned in royal garments, who had come out from the place where the shrine of the holy martyr stands, with a long beard streaked with grey, and approaching Yunets he struck him with a lamp on the face and chest. And from such a strong blow the lamp broke. Yunets tried to run away. But the saint, overtaking him, began to beat him with the remnant of the lamp like a spear in the lower back, along the spine, and on the right arm, saying: “This is your recompense for my monastery, so that you may unlearn to rage against my people!” He roared like a beast and, waking, leapt up.

And in suffering he began to tell everything he had seen in the dream. Since after this he became ill, although he did not want it, he was brought to the monastery, where he lay for another 7 weeks; his bones and flesh rotted at the places of the blows; his tongue and teeth fell out; and from the stench coming from him the whole monastery suffered. Many other signs too the saint worked to the glory of God.

The Greek Empire

After Andronicus Palaiologos, his grandson Andronicus Palaiologos, an Orthodox ruler, reigned in Constantinople for 13 years.

In the year 6837 from Adam (1337 from Christ)

On May 21, the Church of John of the Ladder was founded in Moscow. In that year it was completed and consecrated on September 1. Another church, in honor of the Veneration of the Precious Chains of Saint Peter, was founded on August 13 and completed on October 14.

That year Azbyak, Tsar of the Horde, sent his envoys to all the Russian princes, that they should seize Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver and send him to him in the Horde.

That year His Eminence Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus’, who was a Greek, came to Novgorod.

That year in the German city of Yuryev a noble and brave man was killed. That year the accursed enemy, the devil, put an evil thought into all the Russian princes—to hand over Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver at the command of the Tatar Tsar Azbyak—and the whole Russian land was stirred up.
