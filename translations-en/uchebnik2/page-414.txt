In the same year His Eminence Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus', appointed Basil bishop in Ryazan and Murom.

In the same year His Eminence Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus', appointed Theophylact bishop in Smolensk.

In the same year His Eminence Metropolitan Alexius appointed Ivan bishop in Sarai.

In the same year Metropolitan Alexius went a second time to Tsargrad, and Metropolitan Roman had gone to Tsargrad before him, and in that same year there was a great dispute between them there.

In the same year the son of Sizhsky, Ivan, settled with the Lithuanians in Rzhev.

That same autumn Olgerd Gediminovich ravaged Bryansk and Smolensk and captured the son of Prince Vasily of Smolensk.

In the same year Prince Ivan Feodorovich came from the Horde from the Tsar with a grant and sat to rule in Starodub, his patrimony.

In the same year Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Suzdal came from the Horde from the Tsar with honor and with a grant.

In the same year His Eminence Alexius came from Tsargrad with great honor from the patriarch and with a blessing upon all the Russian land. Metropolitan Roman also came from Tsargrad, appointed by the patriarch for the Lithuanian land and the Volhynian land.

In the same year Iranchei came to Moscow from the Horde, and with him merchants, the Surozhans.

In the same year Prince Vasily came from the Horde with a grant and sat to rule in Bryansk, and after staying there a short time, he died. And there was sedition in Bryansk from wicked men and great turmoil; the cities became desolate, and the Grand Prince of Lithuania took possession of Bryansk.

That same winter, on the third day of the month of February, in Moscow at night, when the bell was rung for Matins, the Moscow tysyatsky Alexei Petrovich was killed. He was killed terribly, by unknown persons, in an unknown manner; he was only found murdered in the square when the bell was ringing for Matins. And some said that this was done either by the counsel of the great boyars or by someone else, secretly. He was killed just as Grand Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky was killed by the Kuchkovichi. And there was a great revolt in Moscow because of that murder. And that same winter, along the last road, the great Moscow boyars departed to Ryazan with their wives and children.

In the year 6865 from Adam (1365 from Christ)
