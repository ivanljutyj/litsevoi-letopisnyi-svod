In the same year in Novgorod Ivan Morozov built the stone Church of the Conception of Saint John the Forerunner.

In the same year, fifteen versts from Mozhaysk, in the estate of Prince Andrei Dmitrievich—grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Ivan, great-great-grandson of Daniel of Moscow—a sign appeared: in Koloch a certain man named Luke, of simple origin, a poor tiller, found on a tree in a certain place an icon of the Most Pure Mother of God holding on her arm the Child, our Lord Jesus Christ. On one side of the icon, on a folding leaf, was depicted Nicholas the Wonderworker, and on the other Elijah the Prophet. And taking this icon, he kissed it with faith and placed it on a tree not far from his field.

And another man came and took the icon from him. Luke began to entreat him to leave the icon, and giving that man an oat bread, he again took the icon for himself and carried it to his house.

And at that time there had been in his house for many years a paralytic. Luke showed him the icon and told him how he had found it. The paralytic marveled and began to ask Luke to bring the icon to him. Luke brought him the icon. The paralytic touched his forehead, eyes, and lips to the holy icon of the Mother of God, and in that same hour he rose healed, as though he had never been ill.

And all who lived in that place heard of this, and began to come, bringing the sick and infirm, and all were healed. And many people began to come from everywhere, bringing the sick; paralytics recovered, and a countless multitude of miracles took place.

And people began to honor Luke as a prophet or an apostle, showing him honor and glory. Luke came with the icon from Koloch to Mozhaysk, and when he drew near the city, Prince Andrei Dmitrievich with his boyars and the whole city, from small to great, came out to meet him, even bringing out infants. Many signs and miracles took place from the icon of the Mother of God.

Then from there Luke set out with the icon for Moscow; there the metropolitan with bishops and the whole sacred council met it with crosses. Also princes and princesses with their children, boyars and voivodes, all nobles and the whole Orthodox Christian multitude came out to meet the icon. And many miracles took place: the blind regained sight, the lame began to walk, paralytics rose, the mute found speech, the deaf hearing, and those suffering from any disease recovered; what more can be said—the human mind cannot comprehend the countless multitude of miracles that occurred there.
