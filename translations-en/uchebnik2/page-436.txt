So God helped Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and his warriors, and they overcame the Ryazan men, and Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan barely managed to flee with a small retinue.

And then Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk took his seat in the grand principality of Ryazan. That same winter Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich had a son, Vasily.

That same winter Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, great-great-grandson of blessed Alexander, married, taking as his wife the daughter of Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania, named Elena in holy baptism.

That same winter Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan drove Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk from the principality of Ryazan, and himself again took his seat in the grand principality of Ryazan, seized his son-in-law Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk, and subjected him to his authority.

In that same year Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver sent his nephew Prince Dmitry Eremeevich and his voivodes with an army to Kistma, and, having captured the Kistma voivodes, the children of Ivan Tishanarov, Andrei, David, and Boris, they brought them to Tver to Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver.

In that same year Prince Mikhail Vasilievich of Kashin, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Yaroslav, sent to Moscow to conclude peace with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and annulled his agreement with Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver.

In that same year, when His Eminence Metropolitan Alexius was finishing the divine liturgy, a certain youth was healed at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus; that youth had hands contorted at his chest and could not speak, but then he spoke and straightened his hands. And Metropolitan Alexius ordered the bells to be rung and a prayer service to be served, and glorified the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos and Their holy saint.

In that same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver went to war against Dmitrov, took a ransom from the city, burned the suburbs, the surroundings, and the villages, and, having captured the boyars and people, brought them to Tver.

At that same time Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver secretly led a Lithuanian army against the city of Pereyaslavl: Prince Keistut, brother of Olgerd Gediminovich, Prince Andrei Olgerdovich of Polotsk, Prince Vytautas Keistutovich, Prince Dmitry of Drutsk, and many other princes with many warriors. From Pereyaslavl they took a ransom, burned the suburb, seized the districts and villages, burned everything, and took captives. Likewise from Kashin they took a ransom and seized the districts with villages, and led the people away captive.
