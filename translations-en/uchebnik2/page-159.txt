In the same year, Vladyka Martiriy, Archbishop of Novgorod, founded a stone church of the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Pure Mother of God over the city gates, and another stone church of the Holy Resurrection in the monastery in Lyudin End.

In the same year, Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, sent an army of Novgorodians to Chernigov against the Grand Prince of Chernigov Yaroslav Vsevolodovich and the whole clan of the Olgovichi.

The Novgorodians set out with their prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, and when they were at Novy Torg, Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich sent to them and ordered them to return home.

The Novgorodians sent to Prince Vsevolod the posadnik Miroshka, Boris Zhiroslavich, and the sotsky Nikifor, asking him for his son to reign in Novgorod, and they drove their own prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, his kinsman by marriage, away from them.

In the year 6704 from Adam (the year 1204 from Christ)

Blessed John, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir, founded in the city of Vladimir by the citadel over the gates a stone church of Saints Joachim and Anna on the first day of the month of May, during the reign of the right-believing Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky.

In the same year a son, Gavriil, was born to Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky.

In the same year, Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, married his son Prince Konstantin to the daughter of Prince Mstislav Romanovich, grandson of Rostislav, great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, forefather of Yaroslav, forefather of the great Vladimir, and they were wed in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Vladimir by blessed John, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir. Present there also was Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich himself with his princess and children, and Grand Prince Roman Glebovich of Ryazan, and with him his brothers—Prince Vsevolod Glebovich, Prince Vladimir Glebovich with his son Gleb, and Prince Vladimir of Murom, and David, and Yuri with their warriors; and there was joy and love in the city of Vladimir.

In the same year there was the tonsure of the son of Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, Prince Vladimir, in the city of Vladimir. Present there was blessed John, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir, and Grand Prince Roman Glebovich of Ryazan with his brothers and his warriors; and all remained in joy and gladness, in love and eternal union, and afterward they dispersed to their homes.
