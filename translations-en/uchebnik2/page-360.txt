In the same year the Novgorodians held a veche according to their ancient custom and elected as their archbishop Moses, archimandrite of Yuryev, if the metropolitan should bless it, and they seated him in the lord’s court until the metropolitan should send for him.

In the year 6832 from Adam (A.D. 1332)

Grand Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, went to the Horde.

In the same year the most reverend Peter, metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, installed Moses as archbishop for Novgorod in Moscow.

In the same year Tsar Azbyak sent princes to wage war on Lithuania, and they did much harm to Lithuania, and they came to the Horde with much booty.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, killed in the Horde Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, descendant of Vladimir Monomakh, descendant of Yaroslav, descendant of Vladimir the Great, during the Filippov Fast, on the twenty-first day of November, on the feast of the Entry of the Most Pure Mother of God, without the Tsar’s command, relying on the Tsar’s favor, since Tsar Azbyak honored Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver. And relying on that, he killed Grand Prince Yuri; and this was not for his own good, and he was in great disgrace with the Tsar until he should decide what to do with him.

In the same year Tsar Azbyak ordered the body of Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow to be taken to Rus and buried in his patrimony in Moscow.

In the same year Prince Alexander Mikhailovich came from the Horde, and Tatars with him, his creditors.

In the same year they brought from the Horde the body of Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow, and Metropolitan Peter buried him in the church of the holy archangel Michael in Moscow on the first Saturday of Lent.

In the same year the Germans came in arms to Pskov in ships, in boats, and on horses, and they stood for 18 days, bombarding with wall-breaking engines and advancing their siege works. And so they battered the city walls, and great sorrow came upon Pskov.

And at that time Prince David came from Lithuania with his men, and went against them beyond the Velikaya River, and took their wall-breaking engines, and their fortifications...
