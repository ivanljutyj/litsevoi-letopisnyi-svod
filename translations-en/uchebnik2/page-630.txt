And Anfal fled to Ustyug, while of the Novgorod warriors only one man was killed, and all returned unharmed to Novgorod.

In the same year Vladyka Ioann, Archbishop of Novgorod, built a stone gate-church of the Holy Resurrection and consecrated it himself. In the same year Mikhail Krup built a stone church of St Nicholas at the end of Chudintseva Street.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver and his son Prince Ivan built a stone church of the Holy Archangel Michael in Novy Gorodok.

In the year 6907 from Adam (the year 1407 from Christ)

Vladyka Ioann, Archbishop of Novgorod, said to his Novgorodians: “Make peace with Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich and do not shed Christian blood.”

And the Novgorodians sent ambassadors to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich in Moscow: Archimandrite Parthenius, posadnik Iosif Zakharyich, tysyatsky Anania Konstantinovich, and the wealthy townsmen Grigory and David; and they concluded peace with the grand prince as before. And the grand prince gave them his brother Prince Andrey Dmitrievich, and to the Pskovians he gave his son-in-law Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich of Tver.

In that same autumn Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow sent in pursuit of Prince Semyon Dmitrievich of Suzdal; they pursued him as far as Kazan, but did not overtake him.

In the same year Prince Roman Yuryevich was killed on the Shelona.

In the same year Grand Princess Sophia, wife of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, stayed with her children for two weeks in Smolensk with her father Vytautas Keistutovich and her mother; she left her father with many gifts, with icons adorned with gold and silver, and brought to Moscow a portion of the Savior’s Passion; for this there was joy in Moscow.

In the same year Grand Princess Maria, wife of Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel of Moscow, reposed in the monastic order; her name in monasticism was Photinia, and she was buried in Moscow in the Monastery of the Savior.
