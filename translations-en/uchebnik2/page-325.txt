And after Prince Mikhail, his brother Prince Feodor Rostislavovich the Black of Yaroslavl began to reign in the place that had come to him from his father; he became Grand Prince in Smolensk.

In the same year the Grand Princess of Yaroslavl, wife of Vsevolod Konstantinovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, died; her name was Marina; and she was buried in Yaroslavl.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Borisovich, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Konstantin, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, took away domains from Prince Mikhail Glebovich of Beloozero, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Konstantin, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, with sin and with injustice, and thereby wronged and oppressed his brother. O evil human greed and accursed pride! Will the Lord God endure and remain silent, and not avenge His wronged servants who cry to Him with weeping and tears!

In the same year Theognostus, Bishop of Sarai, came for the third time from the Greek land, from Constantinople; he had been sent by His Eminence Cyril, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and by Mengu-Timur, Tsar of the Horde, to the Patriarch and to the Greek Emperor Michael Palaiologos, with letters from His Eminence Metropolitan Cyril and from Tsar Mengu-Timur, and with gifts from both.

In the same year the Tatars ravaged the Lithuanians; and the Russian princes went with the Tatars as well; and they returned to their land with a multitude of captives and with booty.

In the same year there was a lunar omen at night: the whole moon disappeared, and it was not there until dawn, and when it grew light, it did not appear.

In the year 6788 from Adam (the year 1288 from Christ)

Grand Prince David Konstantinovich of Galich and Dmitrov died, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.

In the same year Ignatius, Bishop of Rostov, covered the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Rostov
