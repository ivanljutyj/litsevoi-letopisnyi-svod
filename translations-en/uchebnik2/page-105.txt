Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich marveled at his wisdom, and sent him many gifts, and begged him to return to his own lands and to grant him forgiveness for his boldness; and so in great love they were reconciled and each returned to his own place.

In the same year Prince Andrei, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, with his brother Yaroslav, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, and with his son Izyaslav, and with Yuri, Prince of Murom, went against the Volga and Kama Bulgars, and the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos helped them, and they slew many Ishmaelites, and their prince barely escaped with a small retinue; and they went and took their glorious city of Bryakhimov on the Kama, and burned their other cities. This was the second miracle from the icon of the Most Pure Theotokos, which two priests carried in vestments; likewise priests carried the life-giving Cross in vestments, and deacons in their sticharia.

This icon of the Most Pure Theotokos was brought from Tsargrad to Kiev to Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, the one which the pious Prince Andrei took with him from Kiev from his father, Grand Prince of Kiev Yuri Dolgoruky, and having brought it, he placed it in his church in the Golden-Domed [church] in Vladimir.

In the year 6669 from Adam (1169 from Christ)

They began to paint the Golden-Domed Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos and finished on the 30th day of August.

In the same year Theodore, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, reconciled Grand Prince of Kiev Rostislav Mstislavich with Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich and with his children, and all were in peace and love and concord and unity, confirmed by kissing the cross.

In the year 6670 from Adam (1170 from Christ)

The city of Rostov burned, almost entirely, and also the holy cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos, which Bishop Hilarion built, who was before Saint Leontius the Wonderworker, Bishop of Rostov; it was built of oak timbers, and was wonderful and most wondrous, such as had never been, and afterward I do not know whether there will be such.

Grand Prince Andrei of Rostov and Suzdal, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, ordered a stone church to be built in the name of the Dormition of the Holy Theotokos on the same site as the burned church. And they began to dig trenches, and found the body of the blessed Isaiah, Bishop of Rostov, wonderworker.
