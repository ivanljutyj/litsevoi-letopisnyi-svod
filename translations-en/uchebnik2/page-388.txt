And so the Tver principality fell utterly desolate. His Grace Theodore, Bishop of Tver, with the sacred council performed the funeral singing over him and laid him in the Church of the Transfiguration of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ. Thus died Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir. He sat in Pskov for 13 years, fled to the Germans and to Lithuania, and again returned to his patrimony, to Tver. And he met death in the Horde with his son Theodore at the hands of Tsar Azbyak, on the 28th day of the month of September, on the commemoration of the holy martyrs Terentius and Neonilla, of the venerable Stephen, of Savvatius the composer of canons, and of the holy martyr Paraskeva Pyatnitsa.

And so from Pereyaslavl, by the tsar’s command, Prince Ivan Korotopoliy set out with Tavlubiy and the other Tatars, and Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich also sent his host with them to Smolensk. The commanders were Alexander Ivanov and Fyodor Akinfovich. Other princes also, by the tsar’s command, went with the Tatars to Smolensk: Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Rostov, Prince Ioann Yaroslavich of Yuryev, Prince Ivan of Drutsk, Fyodor Fominsky with the baskaks, and the Mordvin princes with the Mordvins.

And approaching Smolensk, they burned the suburbs, plundered and burned the volosts and villages, and stood before the city for several days. And from there the Tatars went to the Horde with great wealth and with captives, while the Russian princes returned to their own lands safe and sound.

In the year 6848 from Adam (1348 from Christ)

Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich Kalita died, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, having become a monk, in the schema. And he was laid in the Church of the Holy Archangel Michael in Moscow, which he himself had built. But his eldest son, Prince Simeon, was not then at his father’s burial, because at that time he was in Nizhny Novgorod. His sons: Simeon, Ivan, Andrei. He ruled for 53 years.

In that year Prince Simeon Ivanovich and his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, and their brother Prince Andrei Ivanovich, went to the Horde, and many other princes were then in the Horde with Tsar Azbyak.
