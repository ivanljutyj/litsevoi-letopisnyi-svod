The Jordan River is deep, with steep banks, very formidable, and it flows into the Sodom Sea. On the Jordan there is a very great monastery of John the Forerunner, for in that Jordan Christ was baptized by John. There Mary of Egypt crossed the Jordan to Zosima as if on dry land. Beyond the Jordan River is the cell of the great John the Forerunner, and near it the cell of the holy prophet Elijah. From there a little farther is a mountain; from that mountain the holy prophet Elijah was taken up to heaven in a fiery chariot. Near there is a place on the Jordan River where the prophet Elisha struck the Jordan with Elijah’s mantle, and the water of the Jordan parted to this side and that, and Elisha crossed on dry land. Not far from there, about five stadia away, near the Sodom Sea, is the monastery of Saint Gerasimus, with whom a lion lived. And the tomb of Saint Gerasimus is behind the altar. And that beast, the lion that served him, lies buried at his feet. The Sodom Sea is where Sodom and Gomorrah were, seven cities: some the earth swallowed, and other cities the sea swallowed. Smoke still rises from that sea even now; and where the earth swallowed those cities, nothing grows there, only hot sulfur, and no grass.

To the right from Jerusalem, about two stadia away, is the monastery of Saint Elijah. There the holy prophet Elijah himself lived, and there a raven fed him. From there about five stadia to Bethlehem, where the Lord was born. In Bethlehem there is a great church, and beneath its altar is a cave; in that cave is the grotto where the Lord was born. And going into the cave, on the left side is the manger where our Lord Jesus Christ lay wrapped in swaddling clothes. And from the manger in the lower part it is about three sazhens to the grotto. At the manger there is a Frankish rite, and at the grotto a Greek rite. And in Bethlehem on the left side is a Frankish monastery, and in it the Church of the Nativity of Christ, where the Franks serve. In that Frankish monastery are the house of Joseph and the well from which our Lord Jesus Christ and the Most Pure Theotokos drank water.

From the city of Jerusalem to Nazareth is three days by the Doman road; also from the city of Jerusalem to Mount Tabor is two days and more. Ten stadia from the city of Jerusalem is the tomb of Rachel, wife of the patriarch Jacob, son of Isaac, grandson of Abraham. Two days from Damascus is the pit into which the brothers of Joseph the Beautiful threw him. And five days from Damascus to the tomb of Benjamin, brother of Joseph the Beautiful. From Jerusalem to Mount Sinai is 15 days, and from Jerusalem to Cana of Galilee, 3 days. There on Mount Zion, in her cell, the Most Pure Theotokos reposed. There on Mount Zion was the court of Annas the high priest; there our Lord Jesus Christ was held in prison, and now that prison is in the Armenian church. There is also an Armenian monastery there.

In that Armenian church, the great stone that lay on the Lord’s tomb has been made an altar. In that monastery is a hearth, by whose fire Peter, the chief apostle, warmed himself during the voluntary Passion.
