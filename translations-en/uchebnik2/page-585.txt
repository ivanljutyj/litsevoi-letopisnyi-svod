Neither into the refectory nor into his cell, but he went out of the monastery alone and set off, and no one knew of it. Night fell, and he prayed alone in the deserted forest.

And, after sleeping a little, in the morning he set out on his way. He came to the monastery on the Makhrishcha River to Abbot Stefan and asked him for an experienced brother who would show him deserted places. And he found a beautiful place very high above the Kerzhach River.

The brethren in his monastery, “which is in Radonezh,” began to be troubled about him, and many went to look for him. And one brother found him on the Kerzhach, and out of joy did not want to return to his people to tell about him. Then nevertheless he went, so that those who remained might not fall altogether into despondency. When they heard, they rejoiced and began to come to him: sometimes two, sometimes three, sometimes a multitude.

Blessed Sergius set up a cell for himself and the brethren and sent two of his disciples to His Eminence Metropolitan Alexei, asking a blessing to found a church in the name of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God. And, having received the blessing from the hierarch, he began to build the church. And many helped in this, both monks and laypeople. And princes, boyars, and nobles brought many estates and much wealth—silver and gold—for the building of the monastery. And so, by God’s grace, the monastery on the Kerzhach was soon built in the name of the honorable and glorious Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God. From the former monastery of the Holy Trinity in Radonezh, the brethren who had been anxious about him went to the city of Moscow and with tears begged Saint Metropolitan Alexei to return the Venerable Abbot Sergius from the Kerzhach to Radonezh, to his first monastery. The hierarch, having heard them, sent two archimandrites, Gerasim and Pavel, to Sergius, saying: “The church built by you awaits you, and the monastery founded with God’s help in that place. Therefore, whomever you consider worthy among your disciples, leave in your place as superior in your monastery on the Kerzhach, and return yourself to your former monastery of the Holy Trinity in Radonezh, so that those who love you there may not grieve long over separation from you. But those who hate you and do not submit to you I will soon remove with dishonor from the monastery, and no one will oppose you in your monastery, and the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God and of all the saints, and our blessing and prayers, will be with you. Do not disregard us.” Sergius promised to obey, and the archimandrites, returning, told this to Metropolitan Alexei.

And Saint Alexei soon sent priests to the Venerable Sergius, and they consecrated the church in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God, her honorable and glorious Annunciation. The Venerable Sergius chose one of his disciples named Roman and sent him to the city to the hierarch for ordination as presbyter. And he received the priestly rank.
