The city of Jerusalem was fortified thus: around it were three stone walls, and strong and brave warriors were inside the city — one would go out from the city walls against a hundred warriors and return without a single wound on his body. But when Titus the Roman surrounded the city, then seven brave Jerusalem warriors came out of the city; fighting, they cut seven streets through the warriors of Titus the Roman, reached through his countless regiments to Titus himself, the king of Rome, rivers of blood flowed, and the warriors returned unharmed to Jerusalem. But what was their strength? They could have crushed mountains, yet they could do nothing without God’s help; God’s wrath turned against them, and they were all defeated, all the strong and brave warriors fell dead. Likewise any army without God’s help can do nothing.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Lithuania, Olgerd Gediminovich, on the advice of Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, with a large army went to war against the Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow and joined with the army of Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver near Lyubutsk on the twelfth day of the month of June.

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, having gathered his army, went against them and near Lyubutsk met the enemy; first the Muscovites attacked Olgerd’s guard regiment and defeated it.

And great confusion began in the ranks of the Lithuanian army; Olgerd Gediminovich himself fled and halted beyond a ravine, and took up arms against him, but the ravine between them was formidable, with deep thickets, and therefore they could not come out to battle. And they stood there a long time, until they concluded peace and dispersed.
