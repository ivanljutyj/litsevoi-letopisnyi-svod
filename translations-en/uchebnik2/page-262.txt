When the body of Yuri was brought to Vladimir, Yaroslav with his brothers, Bishop Cyril with all the clergy, and all the people from small to great came out to meet it; and the singing could not be heard because of the great weeping. Then they brought the body into the cathedral church of the Most Holy Mother of God and laid it in the tomb, shedding many tears; there also lies Prince Yuri’s father, Grand Prince Vsevolod.

In the same year Prince Alexander Yaroslavich of Novgorod married; he took the daughter of Prince Bryachislav of Polotsk and was wed in Toropets.

In the same year Yaroslav went to Kamenets and took the city, and brought Prince Michael’s wife, together with many captives, back with him.

In the same year Prince Alexander Yaroslavich with the Novgorodians built a fortress on the Sheloni River.

About Batu
In the same year Batu began sending troops against the Russian cities.

Those sent by Batu came and took the city of Pereyaslavl-Russky, which is in the land of Kiev; and they destroyed the church of Archangel Michael; and they killed Bishop Simeon; and they took church vessels of gold and silver and precious stones; and they cut down the people, and some they led away captive; and they burned the city.

In the same year Batu sent another army against Chernigov, and the army was great.

Learning of this, Prince Mstislav Glebovich, grandson of Svyatoslav Olegovich, went out against the Tatars with his many troops; and they fought courageously, and the battle at Chernigov was fierce. And the Tatars set up siege engines against Prince Mstislav; and they threw stones at him from a distance of a bowshot and a half; yet only four strong men could lift one stone. And Mstislav was defeated, and many of his soldiers were killed.

And the Tatars took the city and burned it with fire; and they left the bishop alive; and they led him away to Glukhov; and from there they released him.

In the same year Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, campaigned against the Lithuanians and defeated them, defending the inhabitants of Smolensk, and he took the Lithuanian prince captive.

Prince Yaroslav gave orders to the people of Smolensk and made Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich, grandson of Roman Mstislavich and brother-in-law of his brother Constantine Vsevolodovich, their grand prince in Smolensk.

And Prince Yaroslav himself returned to his land with great honor.
