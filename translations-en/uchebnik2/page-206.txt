Bishop John buried him with funeral hymns, together with abbots and monks and the whole consecrated assembly of the city of Vladimir, and his sons wept for him with great weeping, and all his boyars, and all his men, and all the land subject to him.

His sons: Prince Konstantin of Rostov, Boris, Yuri, Yaroslav, Vladimir, Svyatoslav, Ivan. All the years of Vsevolod’s reign were thirty-seven. After him his son Yuri assumed the grand principality in Vladimir, and his eldest son, Prince Konstantin, in Rostov and Yaroslavl.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, released the Ryazan princes and their bishop Arseny, and sent them back to their patrimony in Ryazan, and Bishop Arseny with them, and all the people of Ryazan went each to his own place.

In the same year Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich fled from Vladimir to his elder brother Konstantin in Rostov, and he began to devise war against his brother Yuri, wishing to take Vladimir from him.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri also began to devise war against his elder brother Konstantin, and went against him to Rostov with his brothers Yaroslav, Vladimir, and Ivan.

And having reached Rostov, they made peace, and they dispersed each to his own place.

In the same year the ceremony of tonsure was held for the sons of Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov, Vasilko and Vsevolod.

In the same year there was a great famine, and they ate meat during Great Lent.

In the same year the Pskovians expelled from themselves Prince Vladimir of Toropets, and they themselves went to the lake.

And in the Fast of Peter the Lithuanians came to Pskov; they captured, ravaged, and burned many districts and villages, and returned to their own place with many captives.

In the same year Prince Vladimir Yuryevich fled from Vladimir to his elder brother Prince Konstantin in Rostov.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with his brothers Yaroslav and Ivan went to a meeting with them, and they were at Yuryev, and made peace.

In the same year Prince Vladimir went from his brother Prince Konstantin of Rostov to Volok, and from there went to Moscow, and, arriving, shut himself up in it.

And Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich went from Rostov, from Konstantin, to Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich in Vladimir, and he gave him Yuryev-Polsky.
