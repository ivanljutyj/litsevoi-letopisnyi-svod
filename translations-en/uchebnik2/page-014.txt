In the same year Vyacheslav, son of Vladimir Monomakh, began to lose Pereyaslavl, and having reached Gorodets, he returned again to Pereyaslavl. In the same year Vyacheslav went to Ryazan.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich went with the Novgorodians against the Chud; and he took the city of Yuryev on the ninth day of February.

In the year 6642 from Adam (1142 from Christ)

Markel, Bishop of Pereyaslavl, reposed on the sixth day of January.

In the same year Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich came from Ryazan to Pereyaslavl; and Prince Yaropolk Vladimirovich sent to him, saying: “Sit without agitation, do not behave like a Polovtsian.”

But Vyacheslav did not obey his brother Yaropolk Vladimirovich and went to Turov. In the same year Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh founded a city at the mouth of the Nerli on the Volga, and gave it the name Konstantin, and built a church in it; and he built many stone churches throughout the Suzdal region.

In the year 6643 from Adam (1143 from Christ)

Prince Yuri Vladimirovich asked his brother, Prince Yaropolk Vladimirovich, for Pereyaslavl, and gave Yaropolk Suzdal and Rostov and his other domains, but not all.

In the same year Izyaslav went from Turov to Minsk, and from there went to Novgorod to his brothers, and joined with the Olegovichi and the Davidovichi, and all rose up for war.

And Vsevolod Mstislavich went again to Novgorod, while Izyaslav remained at Volok; and he learned that Prince Yaropolk of Kiev was going with his brothers, Yuri and Andrei, to Chernigov, and he went there as well.

And so the Olegovichi began the struggle with the Vladimirovichi. And Prince Yaropolk of Kiev came to Chernigov with his brothers, Yuri and Andrei, against the Olegovichi, and without reaching the city of Chernigov they stood twenty days and returned back in the month of November.

That same winter the Olegovichi came with the Polovtsians and took the city of Nezhatin, and burned the villages, and took and burned Baruch, and took many captives. That same winter thunder sounded on the tenth day of December, on the commemoration of the holy martyrs Mina, Hermogenes, and Eugraphus.

That same winter Prince Yaropolk of Kiev gathered the men of Kiev, and his brother Prince Yuri Vladimirovich gathered the men of Pereyaslavl, and they stood eight days by Kiev, fighting with Vsevolod.
