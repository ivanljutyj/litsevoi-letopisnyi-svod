Grand Prince Roman Glebovich with his brothers—Prince Igor, Prince Vladimir, and Prince Sviatoslav—sent to the Grand Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, with submission and humility, returned all his retinue to him with honors, conveyed many gifts to him, and peace and concord were established between them.

In the same year Grand Prince David Rostislavich of Smolensk set out from Smolensk, and his son Prince Mstislav of Novgorod from Novgorod with the Novgorodians, and Prince Vasilko Vladimirovich from Logozhsk, and Prince Vseslav from Drutsk against Polotsk. Hearing of this, the Polotsk people were frightened and said: “We cannot resist the Novgorodians and Smolensk people; and if we let them into our land, and if we make peace with them, they will do us much harm; those coming against us will ravage our land. Let us then go to them on the border territory and make peace with them there.”

And having gathered, they all went out to meet them; they met them at the border with bowing and honors, gave them many gifts, made peace, and dispersed each to his own side.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, although he seemed to have made peace, nevertheless again began to quarrel with the Ryazan princes: Roman Glebovich and Igor Glebovich, Vladimir Glebovich, Sviatoslav and Rostislav. In the same year there were heavy rains all summer.

In the year 6695 from Adam (1195 from Christ)

A son, Boris, was born to the Grand Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, on the second day of the month of May.

In the same year Bishop Porfiry of Chernigov came to Vladimir to Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, wishing to reconcile him with the Ryazan princes of the Glebovich line: with Roman, Igor, Vladimir and Sviatoslav, and with Rostislav; and he labored much for humility and love, wishing to unite them, because Ryazan then belonged to the Chernigov diocese.

And he served in the Monastery of the Ascension of Christ on the day of the feast, and took as his helper Luke, Bishop of Vladimir, and they began to ask Vsevolod Yuryevich for peace with the Ryazan princes; he listened to them and sent Porfiry, Bishop of Chernigov, away in peace to Ryazan, and himself sent his envoys with him to the Ryazan princes, and released all the Ryazan men who had been taken captive by him.
