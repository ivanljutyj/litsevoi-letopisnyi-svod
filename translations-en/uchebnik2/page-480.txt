That same year, by the command of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the venerable abbot Sergius founded a monastery on the Dubenka River at Stromyn, and set up in it a church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God, and brought an abbot named Leonty from his own monastery, of the Holy Trinity.

And the church was consecrated on the first day of the month of December; and he gathered monks and established the monastery with all things; and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich enriched it and supplied it with all necessities, to the glory of Christ God and His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, for the salvation of his soul and the preservation of his realm.

That same winter Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gathered many warriors and sent on campaign his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich, Prince Andrei Olgerdovich of Polotsk, Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Volyn, and other commanders, boyars, and many nobles, and sent them forth on the ninth day of December, a Friday, against the Lithuanian land.

They went and took the towns of Trubchevsk and Starodub and many other regions, plundered the districts, and returned home with much wealth.

But Prince Dmitry Olgerdovich of Trubchevsk did not take up battle and did not raise his hand against the Grand Prince, but with great humility came out of the city with his princess, his children, and his boyars.

And he came to Moscow to negotiate with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, concluded an agreement with him, and gave an oath.

The Grand Prince confirmed his oath and agreement, received him with great honor and much love, and gave him the town of Pereslavl with all its revenues.

That same winter the Vyatchans, going on campaign into the Arsk land, killed the robber Ushkuiniks, and captured their commander Ivan Ryazanets, son of Stanislav, and killed him.

That same summer Ivan Vasilyevich, the tysyatsky, came out from the Horde, and having lured and outwitted him, they seized him in Serpukhov and brought him to Moscow.

And on the thirtieth day of the month of August, the commemoration of the holy martyr Felix, on Tuesday before noon, at the fourth hour of the day, Ivan Vasilyevich, the tysyatsky, was killed, cut down with a sword on Kuchkovo Field by the city of Moscow by command of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich. And there was a multitude of people standing there, and many shed tears for him and grieved over his nobility and his greatness.
