It is more insane to forsake the Creator and worship the creature, which has been given to us, people, for service! To honor the creature as God is a great insult to the Godhead. The apostle Paul also teaches me and says: “Do not become partners with unbelievers. For what fellowship has righteousness with lawlessness? What communion has light with darkness? And what agreement has Christ with the devil? And what part has the faithful with the unbeliever? And what concord has the Church of God with idols?” And how can I forsake the living God and worship the creature?” Eldega said: “If you call the sun a creature, then you are mistaken. Tell me, who can rise to such immeasurable height as to create such a great luminary that would illuminate the whole universe?”

Prince Michael said to him: “If you wish to listen to me, I will tell you who created this. God, without beginning and invisible, and His only-begotten Son and our Lord Jesus Christ, who is also uncreated and also has neither beginning nor end, and also the Holy Spirit—this is the three-personed Godhead, yet one God in essence; He created heaven and earth, the sun and the moon, which you worship, the sea and dry land, and the first man Adam himself, to whom all this was given for service. It was commanded and established by law that people should not worship anything created, whether on earth below or in heaven above, but should worship only God, who created this. And as for what the king promises me—the right to reign and the glory of this world—the king himself is not eternal and promises me a temporary kingdom, which I do not need. My God, in whom I believe, can give me the Eternal Kingdom, which will never end.” Eldega said: “If you remain in this conviction and do not fulfill the royal command, then you will die.” The blessed Prince Michael said: “This death is nothing to me—only joy and eternal dwelling with God. What more need I say! I am a Christian, and I confess the Creator of heaven and earth, and I believe in Him without doubt, and I will die for Him with joy.”

The saint uttered such a word, and this word, like a spear, pierced the enemy’s belly, and annulled the insane law of the foreigners for Christian princes, and strengthened the Church itself. This word of the Church’s warrior and victor brought him a double victory over the tormentor, since Michael neither bowed before threats nor retreated before flattery. Then the devil hastened to set double and even stronger nets for the saint: not only did he direct the tormentor’s wrath against him and raise a storm among the foreigners, but, more than from enemies, he caused the bitterest confusion from his own kinsmen.

Not only did those sent by Batu harass the saint, but many Russian princes who were with him surrounded him, tormenting their souls and grieving over what was happening, and, not desiring an unjust murder, they entreated him with flattery, reminding him of the love of a wife, the affection of children, and the beauty of this world—
