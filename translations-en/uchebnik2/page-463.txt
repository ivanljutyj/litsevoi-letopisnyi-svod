Stefan, who lived a spiritual and strict life, was a brother by the same father and mother of the blessed Abbot Sergius, who founded a monastery in the Lord at Radonezh.

And the elder Stefan began to live a spiritual life together with Alexius; they not only lived together but also stood in the same row in the choir in church, humbly singing with love in the Lord.

Also Gerontius, a renowned and honorable elder, lived a spiritual life in that same Epiphany Monastery, abiding in spiritual love in the Lord and in humility together with Stefan and Alexius.

Theognost, Metropolitan of All Rus, also loved them and often invited them to himself, showing them every honor.

Later Theognost, Metropolitan of All Rus, installed the elder Stefan as abbot in that same holy Epiphany Monastery.

And Stefan became abbot of the Epiphany Monastery and father confessor to Grand Prince Simeon, son of Ivan, grandson of Daniel, great-grandson of Alexander, and also to the tysyatsky Vasily and his brother Theodore, and to many noble boyars, for all loved him for his righteous life and humility.

Then the Most Reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of All Rus, took the venerable elder Alexius from the monastery of the Holy Epiphany to his court and appointed him vicar, so that he might help him judge church people according to the sacred canons. And the venerable elder Alexius lived at the court of Metropolitan Theognost for 12 years and three months, having lived 40 years in the monastic order.

Later Metropolitan Theognost appointed him bishop in Vladimir on the sixth day of the month of December, thinking of him as his successor to the metropolitan see, loving the elder for his great virtues. And the blessed Alexius remained bishop for 4 years, governing as a hierarch, until Metropolitan Theognost reposed.

After the repose of Theognost, by the decision of a joint council, by the decision of the bishops, of Grand Prince Ivan, son of Ivan, grandson of Daniel, great-grandson of Alexander, of the boyars, nobles, and all the people, and most of all by God's will, Bishop Alexius was sent to Constantinople to the patriarch for installation as metropolitan. And, though he did not desire it, he went to Constantinople for installation, and was installed in Constantinople by the blessed Patriarch Philotheus as metropolitan of Rus.

And when he came from Constantinople to his metropolitan see in Rus, joyful was the meeting of the Orthodox Russian metropolitan.
