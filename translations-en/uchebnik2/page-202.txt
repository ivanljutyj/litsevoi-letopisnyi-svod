In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavich began to gather an army and set out with all the Novgorodians toward Torzhok.

Hearing of this, Grand Prince Vsevolod sent his sons Constantine, George, and Yaroslav to Torzhok. When they reached Tver with their regiments, the Novgorodians were afraid and released Svyatoslav and his men to Tver, to their brothers, and the Novgorodians made peace with Grand Prince Vsevolod and with his sons.

And Prince Constantine returned from Tver to his father, Grand Prince Vsevolod, in Vladimir with his brothers, while the Novgorodians went back to their own land.

On How the Ryazan Princes Came to Moscow

Two Ryazan princes came to Moscow, Izyaslav Vladimirovich and Kir Mikhail Vsevolodovich, for they had heard that Vsevolod’s sons had gone to Tver against the Novgorodians, but they did not know that they had come to terms with the Novgorodians and had come from Tver to Vladimir to their father. And they began to ravage the villages around Moscow. Hearing of this, Grand Prince Vsevolod was greatly grieved and sent his son George against them. He came in the evening to Golubino, and Prince George Vsevolodovich sent out guards to learn about the army.

And he received news that Izyaslav was at Mersk and Kir Mikhail at Litova, and that he had sent his men out to fight.

George went by night to Mersk, came to Volochok, and from there sent a guard regiment across the Klyazma River, while he himself followed them.

At early dawn the guard regiments met, and George’s guards drove Izyaslav’s guards and chased them through the forest, beating them. George quickly followed with his regiment, came to the Drozdna River, and there attacked Izyaslav, and he fled. Some of his retinue were killed and others captured, while he himself fled across the river; and many of his retinue drowned.

Kir Mikhail heard that Izyaslav had been defeated and fled himself with his regiment. It was then Great Thursday and the Synaxis of the Archangel Gabriel.

Prince George returned with victory to his father in Vladimir, with great honor.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod Svyatoslavich Chermny and all the Olgovichi besought Matthew, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, to reconcile them and help them conclude an agreement with Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, because he wished to be at one with him and obedient to him in all things: “Why, being angry with us, did he seize the Ryazan princes and burn and lay waste their land?”
