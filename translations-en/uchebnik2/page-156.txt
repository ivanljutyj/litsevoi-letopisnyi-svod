In the year 6697 from Adam (1197 from Christ)

On the 30th day of the month of July, Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yury Dolgoruky, gave his daughter Verkhuslava in marriage in Belgorod to Prince Rostislav Yuryevich.

In the same year the stone cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God was consecrated by the blessed Luke, Bishop of Rostov, in the time of Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich and his son Konstantin, and his son-in-law Rostislav Yaroslavich. In the same year Prince Gleb Vsevolodovich departed this life on the 29th day of September.

In the same year the right-believing and blessed Luke, Bishop of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir, departed this life on the 10th day of November; and Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yury Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, prepared his body for burial with the consecrated council, and they laid his body with great honor in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God in the city of Vladimir on the 11th day of November.

In the same year a son, Yury, was born to Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich.

In the year 6698 from Adam (1198 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yury Dolgoruky, sent to Kyiv to Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich and to His Eminence Nikephoros, Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Rus, his spiritual father John, that they might appoint him to the episcopate in Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir. The Lord says: On whom shall I look, if not on the meek and humble, and on him who trembles at My words? And you shall know Me, for I am meek and humble in heart. And the Lord God looked upon him and appointed him shepherd and teacher of the land of Rostov, Suzdal, and Vladimir, and so it came to pass: he was appointed bishop on the 23rd day of January, on the commemoration of the holy martyr Clement, and he came to Rostov to his see on the 25th day of February, on the commemoration of our holy father Tarasius; Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich was then ruling in Rostov. And he came to Suzdal on the 10th day of March, on the commemoration of the holy martyr Quadratus, and he came to Vladimir on the 16th day of the same month of March, on Friday, during the eve service for Alexis, the Man of God, and Lazarus, the Friend of God.

In the year 6699 from Adam (1199 from Christ)

A son, Feodor, was born to Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich in Pereyaslavl on the 8th day of February, on the commemoration of the holy prophet Zechariah.
