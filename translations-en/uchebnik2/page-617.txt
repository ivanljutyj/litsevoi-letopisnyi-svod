On the Return to Moscow of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich

When the pious Grand Prince Vasily heard of the departure of the beast-like Tsar Temir-Aksak and of the cruel Ishmaelites who were with him, and of all that barbarian multitude, he gave great praise to God, who had granted him a bright, glorious, bloodless victory through the prayers of our quick helper, the Most Pure Mother of God, and of all the saints, and he returned to Moscow. Metropolitan Cyprian with the whole sacred council, with crosses and icons, met him with great joy, and so did Prince Vladimir Andreevich and the other princes, boyars, and dignitaries, with a great multitude of people and great joy.

The pious autocrat and the chief hierarch, and all the people with tears lifting their hands to heaven, cried out in thanksgiving, saying: “Let the strength of Your right hand be glorified, O Lord; Your right hand has crushed the enemy, and by the multitude of Your glory You have ground down our adversaries!” And when the autocrat saw the wonder-working icon of the Mother of God brought from the city of Vladimir, he fell before it with immense joy of heart, shedding tears as from a spring, and, addressing the Vladimir Icon, he said: “I thank you, O Lady, Most Pure Virgin Mother of God, sovereign lady of Christians, blameless intercessor, that you have shown us your great mercy and, beyond our hope, have gloriously delivered us, our city, all our cities, and our villages from the godless enemies. Be always with us, O Lady, never departing; shepherd your flock against every evil design; keep us unharmed, and we shall remember your help forever.”

On the Building of a Church and Monastery at the Place Where the Wonder-working Icon of the Mother of God Was Met

And the autocrat began to hold good counsel with the chief hierarch and with all the people, saying that such a wondrous miracle of the Mother of God must not be left without a feast. And soon he commanded a church of the Most Pure Mother of God to be built on the place where that wondrous icon of the Mother of God had been met, in honor of the honorable Meeting of her wonder-working image, as a memorial of the great benefaction of God and of His immaculate Mother, so that God’s works might not be forgotten by people. Metropolitan Cyprian himself consecrated this Church of the Meeting of the Most Pure Mother of God. And they established a monastery, installed an abbot in it, and gathered monks, and the grand prince provided the monastery with all necessities. And after this they established the custom of celebrating the glory and honor of the Lord God and of His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, on the 26th day of August, on the commemoration of the holy martyrs Adrian and Natalia.
