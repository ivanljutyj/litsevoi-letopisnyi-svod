But the Christian people believed their words; they pondered this and were deceived, for Tatar malice blinded them and infidel falsehood darkened them; they did not discern or remember him who says: “Do not believe every spirit.” And they opened the city gates and went out with crosses and with their prince, and with many gifts to the tsar, and with the best people, also archimandrites, hegumens, and priests with crosses, and after them boyars and great men, and then the people and the common folk. And at that very hour the Tatars began to cut them down cruelly.

And Prince Ostey had been killed before that beneath the city. Then they began to cut down the archimandrites, hegumens, and priests, although they were in vestments with crosses, and the boyars and honorable people. And there one could see holy icons dishonored and despised, trampled underfoot, stripped, and plundered.

The Tatars also went into the city, cutting down, and others climbed by ladders onto the city walls, and no one from the city resisted them, since it had no defense; and there was none to deliver, none to save. And there was a great slaughter within the city, and outside there was slaughter. They cut down so many that their hands and shoulders grew numb, and their strength failed, and the edges of their sabers were blunted.

Christian people in the city ran through the streets hither and thither, rushing in crowds, crying aloud, shouting and beating their breasts. There was nowhere to find deliverance, nowhere to escape death, nowhere to hide from the edge of the sword. Their prince and commanders failed, all their host was destroyed, and their weapons utterly vanished.

Some shut themselves in stone churches, but they did not hide even there: the godless broke down the church doors by force and cut everyone down with swords. Everywhere there was a terrible cry and wailing, so that those crying out did not hear one another; a multitude of people was crying out. They stripped the Christians and, having stripped them naked, cut them down.

And they plundered the cathedral churches, defiled the holy places of the altars, stripped the precious crosses and wondrous icons adorned with gold and silver, pearls, and precious stones; they stripped the veils embroidered with gold and set with pearls, stripped the covers from the holy icons, trampled the holy icons, and carried off the sacred church vessels for service. And a multitude of books was brought from the whole city and from the villages. And all that had been gathered in the cathedral churches in great abundance for safekeeping vanished without a trace.
