Also the divine service was held, and he partook of the holy, divine, and life-giving mysteries of Christ, for the tsar’s guards and all the princes forbade him nothing that must be done according to Christian law.

And so, after divine communion, he embraced his spiritual father, Abbot Mark, and he also embraced and kissed all the priests and the deacon, and spoke to them piteously with tears, that they should not forget him but remember him in their holy prayers.

He also summoned his son Constantine—for his son Constantine was there—and, embracing him, he said: “My son, keep pure the Orthodox faith and honor the divine churches, and honor the whole priestly and monastic order, and do not despise strangers and the poor, for this is pleasing to God.” And then he began to instruct his son and the princess orally concerning the patrimony, and concerning all who had been with him in sorrow and great grief, that they should not forget them or ever despise them. He also said: “Depart from me, all of you, for my soul is sorrowful unto death; behold, the hour of my departure is already at hand.” And he began to pour out tears like water.

And after this he little by little inclined into sleep, and again, waking, he wept. And he commanded the Psalter to be brought to him, saying: “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, and I am grieved unto death.” And opening the Psalter, he found David’s 24th psalm: “Hearken, O God, to my prayer, and despise not my supplication; attend to me and hear me in my sorrows; I am troubled by my grief at the voice of the enemy and by the oppression of sinners, for they have turned iniquity against me and in wrath they were hostile to me. My heart is troubled within me, and the fear of death has fallen upon me.” And so he spoke line by line with tears, and again, repeating, he said the same. He also spoke this verse: “Most Pure Mother of God, in the time of my life forsake me not, and do not entrust me to human help, but do thou thyself defend me and have mercy on me. All my hope I place in thee, Mother of God; preserve me in thy shelter.” And after this he began to say the Jesus prayer with tears. He said nothing else. And if anyone asked him, he did not answer, but said only this: “Do not touch me, for my soul is sorrowful unto death.”

Many hours passed, and while he was saying the Jesus prayer with tears, behold, one of his attendants ran up quickly, saying: “My lord, Grand Prince Michael, behold, Kavgady is coming from the Tsar out of the Horde, and with him Prince George Danilovich of Moscow with a multitude of people, straight to you.” He said: “Do you know why they are coming?” The youth cried out with many tears, saying: “O lord, Grand Prince Michael, they are coming to kill you.”
