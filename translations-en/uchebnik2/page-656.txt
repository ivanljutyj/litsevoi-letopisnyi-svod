Metropolitan Cyprian kept Bishop Anthony with him; but the slanderers denounced the bishop even more, Vytautas was angry, and Bishop Anthony remained in great sorrow and grief. And Metropolitan Cyprian deprived him of his episcopal rank, sent him to Moscow, and ordered him to live in a cell in the Simonov Monastery; he also ordered everyone to care for him and not to offend him in any way, only not to let him leave the monastery.

In the year 6914 from Adam (1414 from Christ)

In the month of September, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich was in Kashin. In the same autumn, on the first day of the month of November, Grand Princess Avdotya, wife of Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, reposed.

In the same autumn the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos at Zheltikovo on the Tmaka River was consecrated by Arsenius, Bishop of Tver; Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver was also present.

In the same winter, on the fifth day of the month of December, on Saturday, the right-believing Grand Princess Evpraksia, wife of Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, reposed.

In the same winter, on the first day of the month of January, Metropolitan Cyprian came from Kiev to Moscow, having stayed there a year and eleven months.

In the same winter there was a miracle in Moscow: in the house of Tyutryumov, myrrh flowed from the icons of the Most Pure Theotokos and the Wonderworker Nicholas.

In the same winter Vytautas seized the Pskov city of Kolozha by a secret raid, and sent the Pskov treaty charter to Novgorod.

And he stood for two days before Voronich, seized lands and villages, took their people and property captive, killed many, and went away.

In the same year the Pskovians, together with the Novgorodians, went to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich to complain about his father-in-law Vytautas, and the grand prince sent his brother Prince Pyotr Dmitrievich to help the Pskovians.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow and Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver annulled the peace treaty with Vytautas.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich gathered a large army and sent it under his voivodes to the Lithuanian lands, to the city of Vyazma and Serpeysk, but could do nothing against them.
