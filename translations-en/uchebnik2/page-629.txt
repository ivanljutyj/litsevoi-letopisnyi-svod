And from the town of Beloozero the Beloozero princes came out, began to petition the Novgorod commanders and the whole army, and gave them a ransom of 60 rubles. The Novgorodians took countless captives, much property, and every kind of wealth.

They also conquered and took captives in Kubena with its districts, and in Vologda, and in Ustyug.

And they stood near Ustyug for four weeks, and from there went to Galich, seized the lands around Galich, took many captives, and for some captives received ransom, while some captives they left behind, since the boats could not hold them all.

And from there they went along the Dvina to the town of Orlets, conquering lands and villages, and came to Orlets, stood before the town for four weeks, set up siege engines, and began the battle.

And the Dvina men came out of the town and with tears and lamentation began to petition the commanders and the whole Novgorod host; the commanders and the whole host accepted the petition of the Dvina men and lifted their disgrace from them. And the Zavolochye commanders, Ivan and Konon, with their retinue, were seized and put to death; and Ivan, Gerasim, and Rodion were put in fetters, as were those who had brought evil upon the Dvina land. And from Prince Feodor of Rostov they took the judicial dues and taxes that he had collected, but granted life to him and his retinue. And from the merchants of the Grand Prince they took a ransom of three hundred rubles, granted them their lives, and released them. And from their own Dvina men, for their crime, guilt, and treason, they took three thousand rubles and three thousand horses.

And so the Novgorod commanders returned to Novgorod with all their army and wealth—posadnik Timofei Yuryevich, posadnik Yuri Dmitrievich, Vasily Borisovich Sinets, the boyars, the boyars' sons, and all the Novgorod army. And all the Novgorodians rejoiced. And the traitor, the Dvina commander Ivan Nikitich, was brought to Novgorod and thrown from the bridge into the Volkhov; his brother Anfal fled along the way, while Gerasim and Rodion were granted their lives and tonsured as monks. And when Anfal fled along the way, Yakov Prokofievich set off after him with seven hundred warriors and chased him as far as Ustyug. And at that time in Ustyug the rulers were Vladyka Grigory, Archbishop of Rostov, since Ustyug at that time belonged to the Rostov diocese, and Prince Yuri Andreevich. And the Novgorod commander Yakov said to Vladyka Grigory and Prince Yuri and all the Ustyug people: "Will you stand for the Novgorod fugitive Anfal?" And the vladyka, the prince, and all the Ustyug people answered: "We do not stand for Anfal, we will not help him in anything; in this we give the Grand Prince a kiss on the cross."

And Yakov rode after Anfal, caught up with him beyond Medvezhya Gora, where he and his friends had built a fortification and fought with Yakov under the cover of that fortification. At that time the Ustyug people came with an army of two thousand men to help Anfal, forgetting their kiss on the cross.
