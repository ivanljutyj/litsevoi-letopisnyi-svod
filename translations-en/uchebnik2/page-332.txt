In the same year the Lithuanians devastated Oleshka, the church domain of the lord of Tver, and other domains.

The retinues from Tver, Moscow, Volok, Torzhok, Dmitrov, Zubtsov, and Rzhev gathered, and having overtaken the Lithuanians, they defeated them, took back the spoil, and captured their prince Dovmont.

In the same year Prince Ivan Dmitrievich of Pereyaslavl married, taking as his wife the daughter of Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov, grandson of Vasilko; he was the grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky.

In the year 6795 from Adam (1295 from Christ)

The princes of Rostov, Dmitry Borisovich, grandson of Vasilko, and his brother Konstantin Borisovich, divided among themselves the land that had come to them from their father: the elder brother, Prince Dmitry Borisovich, began to reign in Rostov, and his younger brother, Prince Konstantin Borisovich, began to reign in Uglich, and their cousin, Prince Mikhail Glebovich, grandson of Vasilko, began to reign in Belozersk.

In the same year a son, Alexander, was born to Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov.

In the same year a son, Mikhail, was born to Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Uglich.

In the same year Fedor, bishop of Vladimir, Suzdal, and Nizhny Novgorod, reposed.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, went to war against Tver, and Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav and great-grandson of Vsevolod, came out to meet him with his army; and they came to Kashin, and there they negotiated and concluded peace.

In the same year Simeon, bishop of Tver, consecrated the wooden Church of the Holy Saviour in Tver, within the stone church—for the great stone church was not yet finished—and served in it; while the craftsmen were building the stone Church of the Holy Saviour.

In the same year the foundation was laid for the Church of the Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb in Rostov, with the blessing of Ignatius, bishop of Rostov.

In the year 6796 from Adam (1296 from Christ)

His Eminence Maximus, metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, in the Church of Saint Sophia in Kiev made Iakov bishop of Vladimir, Suzdal, and Nizhny Novgorod.
