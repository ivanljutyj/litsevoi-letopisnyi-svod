Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich of Kiev, hearing this, threw into the dungeon all the Novgorodians who had come to him as an embassy with their Bishop Nifont, and he placed Bishop Nifont under guard.

In the same year the right-believing and Christ-loving Grand Prince Andrei Vladimirovich Monomakh reposed in Pereyaslavl on the 23rd day of January. And on the next day, when they were carrying him to the tomb, there was a wondrous and very terrible sign in the heavens, as if three suns were shining together, and three pillars from earth to heaven, and above them all there was something like an arc, and the moon stood by itself; and this sign remained until they buried him at the Church of Saint Michael.

In the same year the right-believing Prince Vsevolod Gorodetsky reposed on the 1st day of February. In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich of Kiev sent against Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomakh, saying: “Why do you sit in my domain of Kiev? That domain belongs to me, and you, go to your patrimony, to Pereyaslavl.”

In the same year Prince Igor Olegovich took the towns of Yuryev and took all the livestock, horses, and wealth.

In the year 6650 from Adam (1150 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomakh went from Turov to Pereyaslavl to reign, and Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich went to Turov.

Prince Igor Olegovich desired to take the throne in Pereyaslavl, and he gathered a multitude of warriors and went to Pereyaslavl, and having come, he stood there for two months; and his nephew Izyaslav Mstislavich came to Vyacheslav’s aid; and his other nephew, Rostislav Mstislavich, came from Smolensk and took four of Igor’s towns. When Prince Igor heard that his towns had been taken, he withdrew from Pereyaslavl; and Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomakh gave Pereyaslavl to his nephew, Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich, and he himself went to Turov for the grand reign. And Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich of Kiev sent his son Svyatoslav to Vladimir for the grand reign, on the 25th day of January.
