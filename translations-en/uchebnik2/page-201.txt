In the same year the Hungarians drove Prince Vladimir Igorevich out of Galich and installed his brother Prince Roman Igorevich.

In the same year Matvey Andreevich, tysyatsky of Ryazan, was killed in Kadom.

In the same year a son, Vasily, was born to Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov.

In the year 6718 from Adam (1218 from Christ)

Prince Rostislav, son of Rurik, grandson of Rostislav, sat in Galich, and Prince Roman Igorevich was driven out, on the 4th day of the month of September.

In the same year Prince Rostislav Rurikovich was driven out of Galich, and Prince Roman Igorevich with his brother was installed again.

Hearing of this, Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich sent his son Prince Konstantin with his brothers against Prince Mstislav Mstislavich to Torzhok; and Prince Mstislav Mstislavich, hearing of this, withdrew to Novgorod and from there went to Toropets, to his own volost.

That winter Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich sent his voivode Kuzma Rodionovich with an army into the Ryazan land, and they took the Ryazan river Pra downstream along the Oka, and returned to Vladimir with many captives.

In the same year the Novgorodians with Prince Vladimir and posadnik Tverdislav overtook the Lithuanians at Khodynichi and cut them down.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavich came to Torzhok, and seized Mstislav’s men, and put the posadnik in irons, and took their wealth and property, as far as his hand could reach.

Prince Mstislav Mstislavich sent an envoy to Novgorod to the Novgorodians, saying: “I bow to Holy Sophia and to the tomb of my father and to all the Novgorodians! I heard of the violence of the princes against you, and therefore I have come to you, grieving and sorrowing for my patrimony, Novgorod, that your old customs are being changed and the statutes of former princes, and I lament for you, thinking how I might help you.” And these words greatly pleased the Novgorodians.

And they sent to him with great honor, saying: “Come, good prince, to reign among us in Novgorod, and we will drive Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich away from us or seize him.”

And, having held a veche according to their custom, they placed their prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich with his men under guard in the Vladyka’s courtyard.

And Prince Mstislav Mstislavich came to Novgorod. They seated him with honor and glory and with great joy on his father’s throne, and there was great joy among all the Novgorodians, that by their desire and will a prince had come to them in Novgorod. And Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich sat under guard in the Vladyka’s courtyard.
