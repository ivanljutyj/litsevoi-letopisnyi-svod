Prince Izyaslav, son of Vladimir, grandson of Gleb, brother of those slanderers, was received by the people of Pronsk into their city of Pronsk, and they shut themselves up with him in the city of Pronsk.

Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, came and stood before the city of Pronsk.

And on the Sabbath day they joined in a great battle. The people of Pronsk rose against him with Prince Izyaslav, the third son of Vladimir, because Kir Mikhailo Vsevolodich had fled from the city of Pronsk, as was said before.

And they began to assault the city with a great host. And then they killed from the city the Novgorod posadnik Yuri Miroslavich.

Grand Prince Vsevolod sent to the people of Pronsk, saying: “Submit to me in good will. If you do not submit, you will all perish miserably.” But they did not listen to him, hoping in the strength of the city walls, forgetting the Scripture which says: “If the Lord does not guard the city, in vain does the watchman keep watch.” And much blood was shed on both sides.

When the Ryazanians with their prince Roman Igorevich, grandson of Gleb, heard that the Suzdalians and Novgorodians were standing on the island by Olgov, they equipped men against them, both foot soldiers and men in boats; and they themselves with their prince Roman Igorevich went along the bank on horseback.

Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, hearing of this, sent Oleg Vladimirovich, grandson of Gleb, to his regiments, to the boats that stood on the bank by Olgov with a great force.

And when they were at Ozhsk, news came to them that Prince Roman, son of Igor, grandson of Gleb, had come out of Ryazan and was fighting with the boatmen at Olgov.

Prince Oleg Vladimirovich rushed swiftly to the boats. The Ryazanians, seeing them, withdrew from the boats and set their regiment against them, and Prince Oleg Vladimirovich, grandson of Gleb, with his regiments stood against them, and on the other side the boatmen.

And so from two sides they began to advance upon them, and Prince Oleg, son of Vladimir, grandson of Gleb, defeated the Ryazan prince Roman Igorevich, grandson of Gleb. And they were to one another the children of two brothers.

And Prince Roman Igorevich fled to Ryazan. The Ryazanians, seeing their prince fleeing, abandoned the boats and fled into the forests.

Roman Igorevich ran to Ryazan and shut himself in, and Prince Oleg Vladimirovich returned to Vsevolod Yuryevich at Pronsk with great honor.

And Grand Prince Vsevolod stood around the city of Pronsk for two and a half weeks, and cut off the water from the townspeople, and the people in the city grew faint.
