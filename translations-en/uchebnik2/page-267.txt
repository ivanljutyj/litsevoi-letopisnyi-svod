Batu heeded Dmitr’s advice and went against the Hungarians; the kings Béla and Coloman met Batu at the Salt River.

And there was a great battle and a fierce fight; and the Hungarians were defeated; and King Coloman was killed.

King Béla fled, grievously wounded; the Tatars pursued him as far as the Danube River.

And they remained there three years; and they ravaged the lands as far as Volodava, and around the lakes.

And the Tatars returned to their own land, having done much evil to the Christians of the Russian land and to the Hungarians.

In that same year a daughter, Maria, was born to Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich.
