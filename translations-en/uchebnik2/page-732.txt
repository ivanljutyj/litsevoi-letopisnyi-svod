And our sovereign, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, gave the great reign to his son, Grand Prince Vasily, by your mercy, O free tsar; and now, my lord, for how many years he has sat on his throne by your mercy, serving you, his sovereign free tsar—this is known to you yourself."

And then Tsar Makhamet gave the great reign to Prince Vasily Vasilyevich and ordered a horse to be led under his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich. Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich did not wish to dishonor his uncle, and Shirin-Tyaginya opposed the tsar in this matter and wanted to desert him, since at that time Tsar Kichim-Akhmet had risen against Makhamet. Makhamet decided to act thus, and at Tyaginya’s request he added the town of Dmitrov with all its volosts to Prince Yuri Dmitrievich’s patrimony; and the tsar released them to their patrimonies. And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich came to Moscow on St Peter’s Day, and with them came the tsar’s envoy, Tsarevich Mansyr-Ulan, who installed the grand prince in the great reign in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God at the Golden Doors. And his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich went to his patrimony Zvenigorod, and from there to Dmitrov.

In the same year the grand prince Vasily Vasilyevich’s own uncle, Prince Andrey Dmitrievich of Mozhaysk, died in his patrimony Mozhaysk in the month of June; and he was brought to Moscow and laid in the Church of the Archangel Michael.

In the same year Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, fearing his nephew Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, left Dmitrov for Galich; and the grand prince took Dmitrov for himself, sent some of his lieutenants into exile and took others captive.

In the year 6941 from Adam (1441 from Christ)

The boyar of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, Ivan Dmitrievich, who served him zealously and with a sincere heart in the Horde and obtained the great reign for him from Tsar Makhamet, wished to give his daughter in marriage to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich. And he discussed this with the grand prince. And when they were in Moscow, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich and his mother Sofia Vitovtovna did not want this marriage, but he wished to marry the daughter of Prince Yaroslav. And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich became betrothed to Princess Maria, daughter of Prince Yaroslav, granddaughter of Vladimir, great-granddaughter of Andrey Ivanovich, and granddaughter of Marya Goltyaeva.
