In a word, no one could or dared before to approach the king and speak greetings to him, neither princes nor boyars, nor warriors.

Only the marble-cutters and tomb-makers approach him, bringing with them marble and stones of various appearance, and they enter to him and say: “Of what appearance does your majesty command your tomb to be?”—reminding him by way of parable and saying: “Man is mortal and corruptible, passing through this vain, fleeting, and soon perishing wretched life; take thought for your soul and govern your kingdom piously. The greater you are, the more humble yourself, for the mighty, and those mightier still, will be tested. For as those who blaspheme the good sin against God, so also the proud in their pride sin against God; above all, always have the fear of the Lord, and humility, and love, and mercy, and so may you be preserved and kept by the heavenly love and mercy of the Lord.” And this they pronounced, as is written there in the ordinance, and then went the princes and military commanders, consuls and soldiers, and all the nobles, speaking to him according to their custom at their crowning to the kingdom.

And after this the king received the blessing from the patriarch with great meekness and humility, and with the fear of God; and he came out of the church with great decorum, like some great hierarch; and they showered him with crosses. But the people seized them, each with his own hands. Such, according to ancient tradition, were the installations of kings to their kingdom, and so they installed him; in the same way Emperor Manuel was installed by Patriarch Antony and the Holy Council according to the ordinance handed down from of old.

The Journey to Jerusalem

This I, unworthy, and those with me were permitted to see in the holy city of Jerusalem. There is the Church of Christ’s Resurrection.

When you enter the church, on the right side there is the slab on which Christ our God was laid after He was taken down from the cross. And going left from there, there is the Lord’s Tomb. And opposite the Lord’s Tomb is the Greek service, where Greeks serve.

And on the right side of the Lord’s Tomb is the Roman service, where Romans serve. And on the galleries on the right side is the Armenian service, where Armenians serve. And on the right side of the Lord’s Tomb on the ground is the Frankish service, where Franks serve. And from there is the Syrian service, where Syrians serve. And behind the Lord’s Tomb is the Jacobite service, where Jacobites serve. And on the left side at the Lord’s Tomb is the Frankish service, where Franks serve. And from there again is the German service, where Germans serve. And from that service again is the Frankish service, where Franks serve. And under the Frankish service there is the place where the myrrh-bearing women sat and saw the angel of the Lord, where the angel rolled away the stone from the doors of the tomb.
