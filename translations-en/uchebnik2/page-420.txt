Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky showed great honors to Metropolitan Roman and, having given gifts, ordered him to be escorted to Lithuania with honor.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Lithuania, Olgerd Gediminovich, came to inspect Rzhev.

In the same year the Suzdal princes came to the Horde to Tsar Naurus — Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, and his brother Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich. Tsar Naurus received them with honor and gave the great principality of Vladimir to Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, not by patrimony nor by inheritance. Previously the tsar had given the great principality of Vladimir to his elder brother, Prince Andrei Konstantinovich of Suzdal, and now it did not fall to him. Thus Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal took the great principality of Vladimir, and he was released to Rus from the Horde by the tsar with a grant and with honor, and with the tsar’s envoy he entered Vladimir to the great principality a week before St Peter’s Day, on the 22nd day of June, not by patrimony nor by inheritance. Then in his presence in Vladimir His Eminence Alexis, Metropolitan, installed Alexis as archbishop in Novgorod. In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich sent his envoys and namestniks from Vladimir to Novgorod. The Novgorodians received them with honor and seated his namestniks in Novgorod. In the same year in Novgorod Semyon Andreyevich built a stone church in the name of Saint Theodore on Fyodorov Street. In the same year there was a terrible sign in the heavens: blood-red clouds appeared in the sky.

In the same year in Tver Theodore, Bishop of Tver, painted the small church of the Entry of the Most Pure Mother of God.

In the same year a certain Trans-Yaik tsar, Khidyr, came from the east in war against the Volga Tsardom. A great turmoil began among the Horde princes of the Volga Tsardom. And they began secretly to communicate with Khidyr, the Trans-Yaik tsar, slandering their own Volga tsar, Naurus.

And they formed a conspiracy against him. And when the Volga tsar Naurus entered battle, he was betrayed by his princes to the Trans-Yaik tsar Khidyr.

And he was killed with his son Temir; his tsaritsa Taydula was also killed, and the Horde princes, the closest advisers of Naurus, the Volga tsar, the children of Muallbuzin, and many other Tatars were slain.

And after the death of Naurus, the Volga tsar, Khidyr, the Trans-Yaik tsar who had come from the east, sat on the Volga Tsardom. And then there was great turmoil in the Horde.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Borisovich came from the Horde from the tsar, having been granted the principality of Galich.
