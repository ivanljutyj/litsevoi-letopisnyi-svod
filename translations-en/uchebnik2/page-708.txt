And Prince Konstantin sent to the German land the grand prince’s boyar Feodor Patrikievich and his own boyar Andrei Konstantinovich, and the Novgorod posadniks. They met with the master on the Narva and concluded an eternal peace according to old customs, as had been established under Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavich and under Prince Yuri Danilovich.

In the same year, in the month of April, in Great Novgorod the Volkhov greatly overflowed, and 20 supports of the bridge were carried away. There were strong storms on the river, so that from the rising waters and storms the street bridges were carried away, and many churches were washed from their foundations. Many people, because of the great flood, were forced to save themselves on the roofs of houses. And 19 monasteries were surrounded by water; the singing of the church could not be heard in them. The people were sorrowful, for they already expected the flood that occurred in the days of Noah because of human sins. The waves of the river reached the city gates at the end of the Prussian Street. If anyone does not believe this, let him turn to the ancient tales and read the old chronicles: in former times there were many floods in Novgorod, and from the surging waves the supports of bridges were carried away. And throughout all lands there were signs for our admonition, so that people might turn away from evil deeds and learn to do the will of God and keep His commandments. Wise are those people who know the ancient writings; says the wise Solomon: “He is wise who knows the ancient tales and accounts.” This flood occurred under Archbishop Simeon, in the sixth year of his episcopate; much evil was done to people: many people drowned, and much property and livestock disappeared under the water.

A Terrible Sign

In that same spring, in the month of May, on the 19th day, on Sunday, the feast of All Saints, in Great Novgorod at midnight there was a strong shaking of the air: from the south came a very dark cloud with thunder and flashing lightning, so that it was impossible to look at them; people expected that they would be burned by this heavenly fire. And the cloud stood over the city, and it seemed that it carried not rain but fire. The people decided that this was a flame consuming sinners; they were terrified and began to cry aloud: “Lord, have mercy!” They also offered many other prayers and vows to the Lord and to His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos, and to all His saints. And there began a strong rain with hail; stones fell from the clouds to the earth. Archbishop Simeon with the sacred council and all God-fearing people gathered in the church of the Holy Wisdom of God, fell to the floor, and began to pray with tears and with sighs from the depths of the heart; likewise in the other churches the priests prayed together with the people. The terrible night passed. Day came, light shone forth, stillness came, and that fiery cloud disappeared. Barely did the people come to their senses from that fear!
