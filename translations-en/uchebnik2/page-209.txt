He, having heard of the Novgorodians’ conspiracy and that they wished to expel him, gave way to wrath and went to the king in Hungary, asking him for Galich. And he left his princess and his son in Novgorod.

The Novgorodians rejoiced at being rid of their prince. And they sent Yuri Ivanovich to Vladimir to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, and to Pereyaslavl on Lake Kleshchino, inviting Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich to Novgorod, and they besought Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich to send his brother Yaroslav to them in Novgorod to reign. Yaroslav went to them in Novgorod, and upon arriving he seized Posadnik Yakim Zubets and the second posadnik, Foma Dobrynin, and imprisoned them in Tver.

Feodor Lazutich also denounced Varnava, who had much gold, and Yakun Nezhich, who had countless gold; and the prince seized those men as well.

The Novgorodians held a veche, and there was much disputing, and a shout arose at the veche, and the Novgorodians ran with weapons to Yakun’s courtyard, plundered his wealth, and burned the courtyard, and captured his wife and children; and Prince Yaroslav took Khristofor, the posadnik’s son.

The Novgorodians seized his noble man Astrap and killed him, and his son Leonty they seized, beat, cut off his nose and ears, and threw him into the water. Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich grieved over this and went from Novgorod to Torzhok, closed the route to merchants, not allowing any grain to be brought into Novgorod, and seized many merchants.

And in Novgorod a kad of rye was ten grivnas, a kad of oats three grivnas, and a cartload of turnips two grivnas. And then there was a great famine, and people gave their children into slavery for bread. And they dug many graves and threw the dead into them, because there was no one even to bury people, and the dogs could not eat all the dead.

And in the towns, and in the volosts, and in the villages, and in the fields lay the dead; and those left alive fled beyond the sea.

Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich sent Varak and Ponos to them in Novgorod to bring his princess out of Novgorod, and he seized all the Novgorodian merchants, and more than two thousand of them were captured; he bound them with iron fetters and sent them to his cities into confinement.

And he seized the Novgorodian envoys: Posadnik Yuri Ivanovich, Stefan Tverdislavich, Semyon Borisovich, Vyacheslav Klimentevich, Terenty Temnik, Manuil Yagolchevich, Yakun Dankov, and many others, renowned in all lands for glory, honor, and wealth.
