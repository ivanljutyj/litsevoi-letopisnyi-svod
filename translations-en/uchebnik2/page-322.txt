And after his brother, Grand Prince Boris Vasilkovich, Gleb Vasilkovich Belozersky became Grand Prince in Rostov.

They lived with the tsar in the Horde: Grand Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky; with his nephew Theodore; with Prince Boris Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Constantine, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod; and with Prince Theodore Rostislavovich of Yaroslavl, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of David; and with Prince Andrew Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod; and with many other princes, and with boyars, and with his servants. And Tsar Mengu-Timur wished to go to war, and commanded all the aforesaid princes, with their boyars and servants, to gather with him; and soon Tsar Mengu-Timur himself set out with the Russian princes.

And the Lord God helped the Russian princes, and they took the glorious city of the Yas people, Tetyakov, in winter, on the 8th day of the month of February, the day of the memory of the holy great martyr Theodore Stratelates and the holy prophet Zechariah the Sickle-Seer; and they took many captives and much wealth, and killed some, and burned their city.

Tsar Mengu-Timur honored the Russian princes well, and praised them greatly, and gave gifts to all, and sent them back to Rus, to the lands that had come to them from their fathers, with great honor and with gifts.

That same winter died Grand Prince Gleb Rostislavovich of Smolensk, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of David, great-great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-great-grandson of Mstislav, ancestor of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-ancestor of Yaroslav, great-great-great-ancestor of the great Vladimir.

That same winter Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, with the Suzdal and Novgorod retinue, went to war against the Karelians and captured all their land, and returned with great booty to his own land.

That same winter, in the month of February, there was a solar sign: the sun was encircled by arcs, and in the midst of the arcs a cross, and outside the arcs four suns, and above the sun a great arc, with horns toward the north. In that same year Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov came from the Horde from Tsar Mengu-Timur, having been with him at war, and with his son Michael, and with his nephew Constantine Borisovich, grandson of Vasilko; and they brought with them many captives and wealth, and were held in great honor by the tsar.

And Prince Gleb and those with him entered the city of Rostov on the Sunday of All Saints, on the 13th day of the month of June, the day of the memory of our venerable father Anuphrius the Hermit, and they gave thanks to the Lord God, and to the Most Pure Mother of God, and to all the saints.
