And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with all his brothers set out from Vladimir with a great host, with mighty regiments; for a multitude had been gathered, including peasant foot soldiers.

It is a wonder: children went against fathers, and fathers against children, and brothers against brothers, and slaves against masters!

And Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with his brothers and his force took his stand on the Gza River, while Prince Mstislav Mstislavich with Prince Vladimir Rurikovich of Smolensk and all their forces stood near Yuryev, and Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich of Rostov stood on the Lipitsa River with all his force. With him were also two valiant warriors: Dobrynya Zlatoy Poyas and Alexander Popovich with his servant Torop, glorious bogatyrs. And he sent to Yuri and Yaroslav concerning peace, but obtained no peace.

And Prince Mstislav Mstislavich also sent to his son-in-law, Prince Yaroslav, saying: “Novgorod is mine, and the Novgorodians are mine, and I am theirs; and the Novgorodians and the people of Torzhok, weeping, cry out against you to the Lord God and to His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos, and to my unworthiness, that you have wronged them. Release the Novgorod merchants and people, and the people of Torzhok, and return Volok, and withdraw from the other Novgorod volosts. And let us make peace with the Novgorodians by kissing the cross, agreeing that no further evil be done, that Christian blood not be shed, that nothing belonging to others be seized, and that we not trespass upon domains not our own; and we have no business with Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich over wrongs and violence.”

But Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich sent to them, saying: “I do not want peace, and I will not give up the merchants; what I have taken is mine, and I want to take still more—and I will execute all the Novgorodians. And in your great folly you do not realize that you have come like sheep to lions, calves to bears, like swine into a field, like fish onto dry land.”

When Prince Mstislav Mstislavich, Prince Vladimir Rurikovich of Smolensk, Prince Vladimir of Pskov, and Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich Romanovich heard this, they told Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich of Rostov about it, and all marveled.

And again they sent a final message to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, saying: “Brothers Yuri and Yaroslav! Do you not know that we are one kin and one tribe, and have come not for battle but for peace; not for bloodshed, but to stop ceaseless bloodshed. Love truth and God and your brother: for he who loves God but hates his brother, according to the apostolic word, is a liar. And you, Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, give the senior principality, the throne of the grand principality, to your elder brother Constantine Vsevolodovich, and the Suzdal land shall be yours, because you are the younger brothers; and me, Prince...”
