And he could not follow in the footsteps of his father, the holy, valiant victor, martyr and confessor Prince Vasily, called Vasilko, son of Constantine, whom shortly before the same godless Tsar Batu had tortured and put to death for the confession of Christ. And thus Prince Boris, together with the other rulers, harmed the soul of the radiant Michael, giving him bad counsel that he should fulfill the tsar’s will merely to avoid his wrath. Boris Vasilkovich’s boyars said to Grand Prince Michael: “When you return in peace to your land, then all your land and we will take this sin upon ourselves, so God will not require it of you, for He knows that you did not do this of your own will. And you, having thus outwitted the tsar, will do much good and benefit for your land and for all of us.” But the valiant sufferer tore apart the nets that the devil stretched from the mouths of foolish friends like cobwebs, hoping in the Resurrection, that instead of death he would receive Life with Christ; and against corruptible beauty he set the beauty of the Heavenly Kingdom, and against perishing wealth, imperishable treasures; and instead of fading glory he recalled Divine glory, and instead of the sweetness of life, the delight of immortal Life; and instead of the temporal he reminded of the Unchanging, and led those who were deceived to recognition of their sins; to some flatterers he answered with silence. But the wise counsellor Theodore, full of the Holy Spirit, led the radiant Michael from strength to strength, so that he would not heed the counsels of flatterers and would keep the Faith unshaken. And Theodore reminded Michael of the Gospel words which they had heard from their spiritual father: “Whoever wishes, it is said, to save his soul will lose it, but whoever loses his soul for My sake and for My Gospel, that one will save it. For what profit is it to a man if he gains the whole world and loses his soul? Or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul? For whoever is ashamed of Me and of My words in this generation? Whoever confesses Me before men, I will also confess him before My Father who is in heaven. But whoever denies Me before men, I will also deny him before My Father who is in heaven.”

The holy Prince Michael heard this with joy and himself stood inflexibly and unshakably in piety, and marveled at Theodore’s wisdom, that Theodore had such great boldness and love for God that he could not only remain firm himself but also strengthen his lord for a higher feat. Prince Boris Vasilkovich urged the blessed Grand Prince Michael to listen to the flatterers and fulfill the tsar’s command.
