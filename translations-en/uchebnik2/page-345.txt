Great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, forefather of Vsevolod, remote ancestor of Yaroslav Vladimirovich.

The Tale of the Killing of Akinf, Boyar of Tver

Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver and Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow, having quarreled over the grand principality, went to the tsar in the Horde, and there was great strife between them over this. And Prince Ivan Danilovich, after his brother Yuri, sat in Moscow.

Then he came from Moscow to Pereyaslavl and sat there in the grand principality; and a secret report came to him from Tver that the Tverites wished to come by surprise raid with an army against Pereyaslavl. He therefore encouraged all his boyars and the people of Pereyaslavl, and sent to Moscow, gathering an army.

And Akinf, boyar of Tver under Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, came against him by surprise raid beneath Pereyaslavl. And that boyar Akinf had formerly been with Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Gorodets, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod.

And Prince Ivan Danilovich came out to meet him, and the Pereyaslavl host was of one mind with him. And they stood firm. The Muscovite host also came up to him.

And they fought very fiercely. And God helped Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich, and there beneath Pereyaslavl Akinf was killed, and his son-in-law Davyd; many Tverites were slain there. But Akinf’s sons, Ivan and Fyodor, barely escaped to Tver. And there was great sorrow and grief in Tver, while in Pereyaslavl there was great joy and gladness. Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver was then in the Horde.

Boyar Akinf, wishing to earn trust and do what pleased his prince, went with the Tverites in arms to Pereyaslavl against Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich of Pereyaslavl, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, and was killed himself together with many of his warriors.

Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver and Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, went to the Horde to the tsar in dispute over the grand principality of Vladimir.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver came from the Horde from the tsar to the grand principality, and sat on the grand principality in Vladimir; and he heard what had happened to Akinf, and was greatly offended.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, went with an army to Moscow against Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and against his brothers. And there was much strife between them, and after some time they made peace.
