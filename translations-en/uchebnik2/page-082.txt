And he himself saw, and those with him, fiery rays issuing from the image of the Most Pure Mother of God and illumining all his host; and he and those with him were frightened and trembling, and they gave thanks to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother. And he ordered their cities to be set on fire, and he besieged the rest and imposed tribute on them.

The same also happened that day to the Emperor of Constantinople: fiery rays issued from the image of the Most Pure Mother of God and from the Honorable and Life-Giving Cross of the Lord, and he defeated many Persian Saracens. And so, by the blessing of blessed Luke, Patriarch of Constantinople, and of the Most Reverend Constantine, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and of Nestor, Bishop of Rostov, they established the feast of the Honorable and Life-Giving Cross on the first day of the month of August.

In the same year Nestor, Bishop of Rostov, was expelled from his throne of Rostov and Suzdal because of the feasts of the Lord, for he did not permit meat to be eaten on the feasts of the Lord if they fell on Wednesday or Friday, and likewise from Bright Week until Pentecost.

In the year 6666 from Adam (the year 1166 from Christ)

The Most Reverend Constantine, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Arkady bishop of Novgorod, and in the same year sent him to Novgorod.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrew Bogolyubsky of Rostov and Suzdal, son of Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, laid the foundations of the city of Vladimir, a very great city, much larger than the original, and in it he laid the foundation of a stone cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God with a single dome, on the eighth day of the month of April, on the commemoration of the holy Apostle Rodion, on Tuesday.

And he gave it much property, and purchased settlements with their tribute dues, and the best villages, and a tenth part of his herds, and a tenth part of the income from trade.

In the same year the blessed Princess, wife of Gleb Vsevolodovich, daughter of Yaropolk Izyaslavich, reposed; she had remained a widow after her prince for 40 years, and her age was 84 in all; and she was laid in the Caves Monastery, at the head of Saint Theodosius.
