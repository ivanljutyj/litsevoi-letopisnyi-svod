…the Bishop of Kolomna, at the city gates with the life-giving crosses and holy icons, with all the sacred council.

And they entered the cathedral church and served a prayer service to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, and Bishop Gerasim served the holy liturgy; and throughout the whole city the priests also performed prayer services and liturgies for the health of the Grand Prince, and of all the princes, and of all the Christ-loving army.

The Grand Prince remained in Kolomna four days, having rested a little from his labors, for he was greatly weary and exhausted; and he went to the glorious city of Moscow.

And he entered the city of Moscow, and his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, met him with crosses and with all the sacred council. And the Grand Prince said to the metropolitan: “Father, by the mercy of God, and of His Most Pure Mother, and of the great wonderworker Peter, and by your holy prayers, and by the venerable abbot Sergius, we have overcome the impious Mamai and defeated those with him. For he exalted himself with pride, but we with humility. You yourself saw how much gold and silver we sent him and how much we begged him. But he did not listen, but exalted himself with his pride and was put to shame.

By God’s grace we defeated them, and captured much of their spoil and wealth, and drove along with us many herds: horses, camels, yoke oxen, great bulls, which are without number, and their weapons, and armor, and clothing, and countless goods.” Metropolitan Cyprian said: “Glory to You, Lord! Glory to You, Holy One! Glory to You, King! For You have shown us Your great mercy and cast down our enemies! We magnify You, Most Holy Virgin Theotokos! For You have shown much mercy and great wonders to Orthodox Christians! We bless you, Peter, saint of Christ! For you protect your flock from misfortunes and cast down our enemies! How then shall we glorify you also, my lord, my beloved son in Christ, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, new Constantine, glorious Vladimir, wondrous Yaroslav, marvelous Alexander? What thanksgiving, honor, and glory shall we render you for striving and laboring so much for all Orthodox Christianity?”

And the metropolitan blessed him with the precious cross, and his cousin brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and after kissing each other they entered the holy church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos and served a prayer service; and the metropolitan himself served the divine liturgy with all the sacred council. And throughout the whole city the priests also served prayer services and liturgies for the health of the Grand Prince, and of all the princes, and of all the Christ-loving army.
