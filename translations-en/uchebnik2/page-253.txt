In the same year the godless Tatars came and seized the whole Bulgarian land, called Kazan; and they took their Great City and cut down all the men, women, and children, and carried some away captive.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich married off his sons: Prince Vladimir and Prince Mstislav.

In the same year a son, Gleb, was born to Grand Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich of Rostov.

In the same year Bishop Mitrofan placed a covering in the holy altar above the Lord’s throne in Vladimir and adorned it wondrously with gold and silver, under the pious Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich.

In the same year Bishop Mitrofan painted the porch of the Most Pure Mother of God. In the same year a Greek metropolitan named Joseph came from Nicaea to all the Russian land.

There was a solar sign, and it was like this: the sun darkened greatly from the west, becoming like a five-day-old moon, while from the east it remained bright; then from the east it darkened greatly, and likewise became like a five-day-old moon, while from the west it remained bright; and so it was once more; and there was fear and trembling in all who saw or heard of it.

In the same year the Germans came with a great army from overseas to Riga.

And the Germans joined with the people of Riga and Pskov and went to war against the Lithuanians, and ravaged much.

And the Lithuanians gathered and gave battle against the Pskovians and Germans; and there was a great battle; and the Lithuanians won; and so many Pskovians and Germans perished — there was also a certain number of Novgorodians — that fewer than one in ten returned to their own land.

Batu’s Invasion, in the year 6745 from Adam (1245 years from Christ)

That same winter the godless Tatars came from the east into the land of Ryazan, through the forest, with Tsar Batu, and when they came they first camped at the settlement of Onuza; and they took it and burned it.

And from there the Tatars sent their envoys — a sorceress woman and two men with her — to the princes of Ryazan, asking of them a tenth of everything: of princes, of common people, of armor, and of horses.

But the princes of Ryazan, Yuri Ingvarevich and his brother Oleg, and the princes of Murom and Pronsk, did not let the Tatars reach the city, but went out to them at Voronezh; and the princes answered them: “When we are no more, then all will be yours.”

And from there the Tatars sent envoys to Grand Prince Yuri in Vladimir; and they began to ravage the land of Ryazan and take captives; and they reached Pronsk.
