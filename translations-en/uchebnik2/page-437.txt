Thus God helped Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and his warriors, and they overcame the Ryazan people, while Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan barely managed to flee with a small retinue.

And then Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk seated himself in the grand principality of Ryazan. That same winter a son, Vasily, was born to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

That same winter Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, and great-great-grandson of blessed Alexander, married, taking as his wife the daughter of Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania, named Elena in holy baptism.

That same winter Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan drove Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk from the principality of Ryazan, and seated himself again in the grand principality of Ryazan; he seized his son-in-law Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk and subjected him to his authority.

That same year Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver sent his nephew Prince Dmitry Eremeevich and his commanders with an army to Kistma, and having captured the Kistma commanders, the children of Ivan Tishanorov, Andrei, David, and Boris, they brought them to Tver to Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver.

That same year Prince Michael Vasilievich of Kashin, grandson of Michael, great-grandson of Yaroslav, sent to Moscow to conclude peace with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and he annulled the agreement with Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver.

That same year, when the most reverend Metropolitan Alexius was finishing the divine liturgy, a certain youth was healed at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus; that youth had hands twisted at his breast and could not speak, but then he spoke and straightened his hands. And Metropolitan Alexius ordered the bells to be rung and a prayer service to be held, and he glorified the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and their holy servant.

That same year Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver went to war against Dmitrov, took tribute from the city, burned the suburbs, surrounding areas, and villages, and having taken boyars and people captive, brought them to Tver.

At that same time Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver secretly led a Lithuanian army against the city of Pereyaslavl: Prince Keistut, brother of Olgerd Gediminovich, Prince Andrei Olgerdovich of Polotsk, Prince Vytautas Keistutovich, Prince Dmitry of Drutsk, and many other princes with many warriors. From Pereyaslavl they took tribute, burned the suburb, seized the districts and villages, burned everything, and took captives. Likewise from Kashin they took tribute and seized the districts with villages, and led people away captive.
