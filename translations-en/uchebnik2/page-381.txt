In that same winter Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich, with all the princes and with a vast force, went with his army against Torzhok. He removed the Novgorod governor and sat himself in Torzhok, warring from Epiphany to the Assembly, until the Novgorod authorities sent to him with a petition Archimandrite Lavrenty, Feodor Tverdislavich, and Luka Varfolomeev. But he would not listen to their entreaties, did not accept their petition, and, refusing peace, went to Moscow.

In that year Vasily, Vladyka of Novgorod, removed the ceiling beams from the walls of Saint Sophia in Novgorod and built a stone wall over two years with the help of the Holy Spirit.

In that same winter the envoy Saraychuk came to Rus from the Horde, from Tsar Azbyak, for Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich.

In that same year, at the summons of Tsar Azbyak, Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak with the envoy Saraychuk.

In the year 6841 from Adam (1341 from Christ)

Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich came from the Horde from Tsar Azbyak with a grant and honors.

In that year Prince Boris of Dmitrov reposed in the Horde.

In that year Prince Dmitry of Bryansk came with an army and with Tatars to Smolensk against Prince Ivan Alexandrovich; they fought for a long time and then made peace.

In that year the Novgorodians sent to Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich for the second time and made peace on the old terms.

In that same year Archbishop Vasily, Vladyka of Novgorod, went to Vladimir to Theognostus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, with many gifts and with honor.

In the year 6842 from Adam (1342 from Christ)

Moisei, Vladyka of Novgorod, founded a stone church in the name of the Holy Resurrection, at Derevyanitsa, and established a venerable monastery.

In that year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went from Novgorod to Torzhok.

In that year the Lithuanians seized the Novotorzhok districts in peacetime.

And Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich gathered an army, went, and burned the Lithuanian towns of Otsechen and Ryasna and others. And then the Lithuanians killed many worthy men of Novgorod.
