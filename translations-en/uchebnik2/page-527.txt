That same summer, on the 29th day of the month of June, a son, Peter, was born to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and the venerable hegumen Sergius baptized him.

In the same year, on the 9th day of May, Metropolitan Pimen set out for Constantinople down the Volga to Sarai, and with him went Abraham, hegumen of Rostov.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gathered many troops from everywhere and sent his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich with an army against Grand Prince Oleg of Ryazan and all his land.

And then in that battle they killed many Moscow boyars and the best men of Novgorod and Pereyaslavl; then too they killed the valiant commander of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, Prince Mikhail Andreevich of Polotsk, grandson of Olgerd, great-grandson of Gediminas.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sent another army against Murom. In the same year there were empty flights.

In the year 6894 from Adam (1394 from Christ)

In the month of September, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich went to the monastery of the Life-Giving Trinity to the venerable hegumen Sergius at Radonezh and celebrated supplicatory services to the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God.

And he fed the holy brethren and gave alms, and spoke with entreaty to the venerable hegumen Sergius, asking that the venerable hegumen Sergius himself go from him as an envoy to Ryazan to Prince Oleg concerning perpetual peace and love.

That same winter, on the 15th day of December, Dionysius, Archbishop of Suzdal, reposed in Kiev. He had been appointed metropolitan to Rus by Nilus, Patriarch of Constantinople; and at that time Cyprian was metropolitan in Kiev. And when Metropolitan Dionysius came from Constantinople from the patriarch, Prince Vladimir Olgerdovich of Kiev, grandson of Gediminas, took him captive; and he sat in captivity until he reposed; and he was laid in the Kiev Caves of the great Anthony and Theodosius, and his body remains to this day whole and incorrupt. That same autumn, at the beginning of Philip’s Fast, on St George’s Day, on Sunday, Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow fled from the Horde from Tsar Tokhtamysh, on the 26th day of November.

That same autumn, at the beginning of Philip’s Fast, the venerable hegumen Sergius of Radonezh himself traveled as an envoy to Ryazan to Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan from Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow concerning perpetual peace and love, and with him went the eldest boyars of the grand prince.
