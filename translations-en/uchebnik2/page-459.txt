…the priest Mityay dwelt in great honor and glory from the Grand Prince and from all, and he began to exalt himself like a tsar, having many servants and attendants in his service. And he remained in such rank and position for many years, enjoying great glory and honor; every day he changed precious garments and shone in those costly robes to the astonishment of all. No one dressed in such garments, and no one changed costly and bright robes every day as the priest Mityay did, and all paid him honors as to a tsar. And what more can be said? No one lived in such honor and glory as he, for he was the Grand Prince’s confessor and managed things as he wished; for many years he lived such a life, and all loved him.

Deep old age came to the Saviour Monastery’s Archimandrite Ioann, surnamed Nepeytsa. He was a meek and humble man, who humbled himself for God’s sake; in his old age he gave up the archimandrite’s office and withdrew to a cell for silence. And after Nepeytsa there was no similar archimandrite in the Saviour Monastery, and all the monks and the monastery itself had need of such a one.

And then a search began for one who could become archimandrite in the Saviour Monastery in Moscow after Ioann Nepeytsa. They began to propose now one, now another; the Grand Prince proposed Mityay, because he loved him very much, as did the boyars.

Mityay learned of this and did not wish it. The Grand Prince began to speak to him with humility and tenderness: “You see that Metropolitan Alexius has grown old, and soon you will become metropolitan and lord of all Rus’. Now accept the monastic image and become archimandrite in the Saviour Monastery and my confessor, as before, and all will obey you.” The priest Mityay did so; the Grand Prince obtained his desire. Soon the priest Mityay was taken for tonsure, and of his own will, though as if by compulsion, he was brought to the Monastery of the Holy Saviour.

And he entered the Church of the Holy Saviour and sent for the Chudov archimandrite Elisey, surnamed Chechetka. At that same hour the Chudov archimandrite Elisey, surnamed Chechetka, came and tonsured the priest Mityay into the holy angelic monastic order in the church in the Saviour Monastery in Moscow. Thus before dinner he was a secular priest and was tonsured into the monastic order, and after dinner he became a superior, pastor, and teacher.

And Mityay remained archimandrite of the Saviour Monastery in Moscow for two years.

After the repose of Metropolitan Alexius, he left his archimandrite’s office by the counsel of the Grand Prince and his boyars, entered the metropolitan’s court, and began to rule on that great throne with authority and boldness, as it is fitting for a metropolitan to rule.
