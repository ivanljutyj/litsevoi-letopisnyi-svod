In that winter, during the Philip’s Fast, on the twenty-fifth day of November, Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania came with a great army against Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, together with his brother Keistut Gediminovich and the Lithuanian princes, with Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver and Prince Svyatoslav Ivanovich of Smolensk and their troops. First the army came to Volok, burned the whole settlement, captured many prisoners, but did not take the city itself, having stood three days before the city of Volok.

Then Prince Vasily Ivanovich Berezuisky was wounded at the city of Volok: he stood on the bridge before the city, and suddenly a Lithuanian struck him with a spear from beneath the bridge. Gravely wounded, he fell ill, and at that same hour took monastic vows and reposed. This prince was very brave and famed for his victories; he loved the priestly and monastic order, desired to die tonsured in the monastic order, and God did not despise his desire.

Then Olgerd went from Volok, conquering and taking lands captive, and came to Moscow on the sixth day of December, on St Nicholas’s Day; he burned the settlements around the city, stood before Moscow eight days, but could not seize the city. And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich shut himself up inside Moscow at that time.

And His Eminence Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, was at that time in Nizhny Novgorod. Prince Vladimir Andreevich gathered troops and stood at Peremyshl, fighting, and Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk came to him with troops, and with him also the army of Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan.

Olgerd conquered much, burned, killed, and took captive, and decided to turn back, but when he learned that a great army had gathered and was preparing for battle, he was frightened and began to ask for peace.

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich concluded peace with him until St Peter’s Day, but Olgerd wished to conclude peace forever and decided to give his daughter in marriage to Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and so it came to pass. And having concluded peace, the prince withdrew from Moscow, having been before Moscow eight days, and returned to his principality, going cautiously and looking around, fearing pursuit. Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich returned to Tver, having concluded peace with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

In the same year Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania went with an army against the Germans; he did much harm to the German land and returned with great booty.
