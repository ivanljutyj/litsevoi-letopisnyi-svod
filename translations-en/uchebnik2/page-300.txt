Reteshka the Novgorodian, who had gone over to the side of Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, said: “Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich has left Novgorod.”

The Novgorodians held an assembly according to their old custom: “Brothers, let us stand firmly for Holy Sophia, for the Novgorodian truth, and for the land that has come down to us from our fathers; either death or life; we will not spare ourselves.” And the Novgorodians sent their Archbishop Dalmat with his people to Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, so that he would not be angry with them.

He said to them: “Hand over to me my enemy, Ananias the posadnik; if you do not hand him over to me, I will march against you in war.” But the Novgorodians long refused this. Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich sent envoys to them with the words: “If you do not hand over to me my enemy, Ananias the posadnik, then deprive him of the posadnik’s office; and I will not be angry with you.”

The Novgorodians gathered and said among themselves: “Our traitors advised the prince to do this; let God and Holy Sophia judge them, for they are setting the prince at odds with us.” And they took the posadnik’s office from Ananias, and made Mikhail Stepankovich posadnik, and concluded peace with Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich with recognition of all their Novgorodian liberties, and after kissing the cross they came to an agreement with him.

And Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich came to Novgorod, and Archbishop Dalmat with a procession of the cross and with all the priests met him.

And so Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich gave his son Vasily to reign in Novgorod, and he himself departed from there, from Novgorod, with great honor; and there was great peace and quiet.

In the year 6764 from Adam (1264 from Christ)

The princes set out for Gorodets and Novgorod; Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov went to the Tatars with many gifts.

Likewise Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich sent his envoys to the Tatars with many gifts.

Prince Boris Vasilkovich of Rostov was with Ulavchiy, and gave the gifts, and received great honor, and returned with great honor to the place that had come to him from his father.

That same winter Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich went with the Suzdalian druzhina against the Yem tribe, and defeated it, and returned to his land with great plunder.

In that same year Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich went to Novgorod with the Suzdalian druzhina; and from there he went with the Novgorodians against the Swedish land and against the Chud tribe; and they went through impassable places, so that they could not distinguish day from night, but all the time there was darkness. And so they went and laid waste to everything.
