On July 8 a sign appeared in Novgorod, in the Church of the Holy Trinity on Ryaditina Street: the watchman heard something knock in the church, and he entered the church to see what had happened. And it was the icon of the Holy Mother of God holding the Son by the hand, standing above the doors on the north side, on another tier. And at that same hour at noon the icon came down from on high and stood apart, supported by nothing, and tears flowed from its eyes. O great miracle, how can there be tears from dry wood! But God was glorifying His Mother, accepting her prayer, saving the city. And at that same hour they summoned Vladyka Vasily, and they made an icon-case and placed that icon in the icon-case, and established a feast in memory of the holy martyr Procopius.

In that year there was a fire in Moscow, and 18 holy churches burned.

In the year 6846 from Adam (1346 from Christ)

Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver came from the Horde, from Tsar Azbyak, to the great principality in Tver, and with him came ambassadors: Prince Kindyak and Avdul.

In that year many boyars departed from Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver to Moscow, to Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich.

That same winter Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver sent his son, Prince Feodor, with the ambassador Avdul to the Horde; but with Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich they did not reach agreement and did not make peace.

In that year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went from Moscow to the Horde.
In that year the Tatars warred with Lithuania.

In that year in the Horde the Germans slandered Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver to Tsar Azbyak and filled the tsar’s ears with many grievous reports. Tsar Azbyak was greatly offended and, summoning one of his servants named Istorchey, said to him secretly: “Go to Rus and summon to me Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, but not with fury and cruelty, but quietly and meekly.”

He gathered himself and went to Rus and did as he had been commanded, saying: “The free tsar Azbyak summons you, that you come to him in the Horde with your son Feodor, so that the tsar may fulfill your will, and you will receive from him the great principality and great honor.” Then Prince Alexander learned from certain people of the tsar’s fury against him and began to reflect: “If I go to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak, I will be put to death, but if I do not go to him in the Horde, an army will come from him, and many Christians will be killed and many taken captive. And the cause of all this will be me. Better, by the will of the Lord God
