In the year 6781 from Adam (the year 1281 from Christ)

The Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, with the great baskak of Vladimir, Iargaman, and with Prince Aidar, and with many of the tsar's Tatars, laid waste the Novgorodian lands, and returned with great booty to Vladimir.

In the same year the great prince of Tver, Svyatoslav Yaroslavovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, went with the tsar's Tatars, and they laid waste the Novgorodian lands: Volok, Bezhetsk, and Vologda, and returned with great booty to Tver.

In the same year in Novgorod grain was dear, and the Novgorodian merchants who were in Vladimir, in Tver, and in Kostroma were all seized and robbed.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, went with all the Novgorodian land to war against Tver; and to Vladimir, to the Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, he sent Semyon Mikhailovich, Lazar Moiseevich, and Stepan Dushilovich with the words: "You, prince, should not desire Novgorod; and what you have taken of Novgorod's, you should give back; and as for the Novgorodian merchants who have been robbed, and those who have been taken captive and imprisoned, you should release all the Novgorodian merchants to Novgorod with all their goods, without any hindrance, and live with us in peace and love."

The Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, sent the envoys away with honor, but did not conclude peace.

Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, with the Novgorodians, came on a campaign against the town of Torzhok; in Torzhok at that time were the voivodes of the Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, and the Novgorodians were in confusion, and great fear and trembling fell upon them, and they said: "Woe to us from all sides: here is the great prince of Vladimir, and here is the great prince of Tver, and here is the great baskak of the tsar with the Tatars, and all the Nizov land is against us." And the Novgorodians wished that the Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, would come to reign over them; and they began to renounce Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.

Prince Dmitry, having learned of this, was frightened and refused to reign in Novgorod, and went away to Pereyaslavl.

The Novgorodians then sent envoys to the Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich with a petition, inviting him to Novgorod.
