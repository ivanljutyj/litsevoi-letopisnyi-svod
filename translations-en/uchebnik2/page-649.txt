The Omen

In that same winter, in Great Lent, in the month of March, an omen appeared in the west at sunset: a huge star, on top of which a ray shone, went around the earth; it was seen for twenty days rising in the east and shining in the evening in the west. This omen meant that peoples would rise against one another: Turks, Poles, Hungarians, Germans, Lithuanians, Czechs, the Horde, Greeks, Russians, and many other countries and lands would be seized by turmoil and would fight one another; besides this, pestilences began. In that same winter, in the month of March, Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich of Tver reposed, in monastic vows and the schema, on Monday of Bright Week during the liturgy; he was buried in the city of Tver, and he bequeathed his principality to the son of the grand prince, Prince Alexander Ivanovich. In that same year, on the 6th day of the month of May, Michael, lord of Smolensk, reposed and was buried at the Church of the Trinity in the Sergiev Monastery.

In that same year Prince Rodoslav Olegovich of Ryazan went with an army against Bryansk. Lithuanian princes came out to meet him: Prince Semyon Lugveny Olgerdovich and Prince Alexander Patrikeevich Starodubsky, Prince Buynoe Ivanovich. There was a fierce battle near Lyubutsk; the Lithuanians won, beat the Ryazanians, cut down many, and captured Prince Rodoslav Olegovich himself. And they brought him to Vytautas Keistutovich; he remained in confinement and great hardship for 3 years.

Then Vytautas Keistutovich took a ransom of 3000 rubles for him and released him to Ryazan.

In that same year, on the 5th day of the month of July, Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan reposed, in monastic vows and the schema, named in holy baptism Jacob and in monasticism Joachim; he was buried in his patrimony and inheritance in Ryazan beyond the Oka River in the monastery called Solotcha.

And his son Prince Feodor went to the Horde to Tsar Shadibek with gifts to announce the death of his father Oleg Ivanovich. And the tsar rewarded him, gave him his patrimony and inheritance, the great principality of Ryazan, his ulus, and released him.

He, coming from the Horde to his land, sat upon his patrimony and inheritance, upon the great principality of Ryazan.
