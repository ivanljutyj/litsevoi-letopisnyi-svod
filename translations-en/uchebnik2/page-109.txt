In the same year the pious and Christ-loving Prince Yaroslav reposed, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, and he was laid in the Church of the Holy Mother of God in Vladimir, on the twelfth day of April. In the same year Prince Svyatoslav Vladimirovich reposed, grandson of David Izyaslavich.

In the same year the Cumans campaigned against Rus and killed two bogatyrs, Andrei Zhiroslavich and his brother Shvarn, beyond Pereyaslavl; their nephew, also named Shvarn, they took captive, and having taken captive a great multitude of Christians, they withdrew to their own lands.

In the same year John, Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Rus, reposed.

In the year 6675 from Adam (1175 from Christ)

The pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince of Kyiv Rostislav reposed, son of Mstislav, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of Great Vladimir, on the twenty-first day of March, the commemoration of St James the Bishop and Confessor, as he was going from Smolensk to Kyiv; he sat on the grand princely throne in Kyiv for nine years, and they carried him to Kyiv. His sons: Svyatoslav, David, Roman.

And after him his nephew Mstislav sat on the grand princely throne in Kyiv, son of Izyaslav, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-great-great-grandson of Great Vladimir.

Later they went against their uncle, Prince Vladimir Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, to Vyshhorod, and against Prince Rurik Rostislavich; and they kissed the cross to one another and parted.

Later Prince Vladimir Mstislavich began to plot with his boyars against Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich, and his plotting became known; he exposed him and took from him a cross-kiss oath.

In the same year the Tyagroves began to send to Prince Vladimir Mstislavich, saying, “We will receive you.” He rejoiced greatly, forgot the cross-kiss oath, and sent to the Kyiv boyars, telling them of his intention, to Rakuil, Mikhail, and Zavil. They said: “O prince! You have devised this yourself and truly acted childishly; we do not think such things and will not follow you.”

He, however, became puffed up in his childishness and went to the Berendei. They said to him: “You told us thus, saying that you are with your brothers; but where are your brothers, Vladimir, and Yaroslav, and David? You ride alone, and you have deceived us like children.”
