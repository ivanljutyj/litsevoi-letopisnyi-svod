In the same year Grand Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov married his son Michael to the daughter of Grand Prince Feodor Rostislavovich of Yaroslavl; and Ignatius, Bishop of Rostov, wed them in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Yaroslavl; Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov himself was there, with his nephew Constantine Borisovich, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Constantine, and Prince David Konstantinovich of Galich and Dmitrov, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and David Yavidovich, and a multitude of boyars; and many gathered from everywhere; and there was great joy in Yaroslavl, on the fifteenth day of the month of July, the day of remembrance of the holy martyrs Cyricus and his mother Julitta.

And Grand Prince Gleb Vasilkovich, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, arranged a great feast at his place in Yaroslavl, and honored his in-law Feodor Rostislavovich the Black, and his princess and children, and his son-in-law David Konstantinovich of Galich and Dmitrov, and his boyars, and his servants.

In the same year the right-believing Andrew reposed, and his wife Ustinia.

In the same year Grand Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov sent his son Michael to the Tatars for war, with his in-law Prince Feodor the Black Rostislavovich of Yaroslavl.

In the same year Grand Prince Gleb Vasilkovich of Rostov reposed, grandson of Constantine, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of the great Vladimir; he lived forty-one years from his birth.

After the Tatar devastation, from his youth Prince Gleb served the Tatars, and saved many Christians oppressed by the Tatars, and comforted the sorrowful.

Prince Gleb gave his food and drink unsparingly to the needy, and gave much alms to the poor, and beggars, and strangers, and orphans, and widows, remembering the words of the Lord: “Inasmuch as you did it to one of the least of these my brethren, you did it to me,” and “Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.” And David says: “Blessed is the man who shows mercy and lends, and blessed is he who considers the poor and needy; in the evil day the Lord will deliver him; the Lord will preserve him and keep him alive; and he shall be blessed on the earth, and He will not deliver him into the hands of his enemies.” And David’s son Solomon says: “Sins are cleansed by alms and by faith,” and “He who gives to the poor lends to God.” And the holy prophet Daniel says: “Cleanse your sins by alms, and your transgressions by mercies—
