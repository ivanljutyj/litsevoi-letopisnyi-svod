In the same year died Ignatius, Bishop of Rostov, wonderworker.

In the same year in Rostov the Church of Saint Michael burned from thunder, on the fourteenth day of the month of June.

In the same year died the blessed Simeon, Bishop of Tver. He was virtuous and skilled in teaching, and mighty in the books of Divine Scripture; he was not timid before princes, but kept the word of Christ truly, and without respect of persons he forbade all who did wrong.

And Bishop Simeon was very honorable and prudent; he greatly honored priests and monks; he favored the poor, orphans, and widows; he interceded for the offended and delivered the oppressed; and he was terrible to all who did wrong, and all feared and trembled before him.

His sacred and blessed body was laid in the Church of the Holy Savior on the right side, on the third day of the month of February, when the memory of Saint Simeon the God-Receiver and Anna the Prophetess is celebrated.

In the same year Prince Elortai of the Horde, son of Temir, came to war against Ryazan and ravaged the lands of Ryazan, Murom, and Mordva, and after doing much evil he went back to his own land.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, began to feud with Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky; and Prince Mikhail of Tver began to gather troops.

And Grand Prince Dmitry of Vladimir also began to gather troops with his own brothers, with Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Gorodets, and with Grand Prince Daniel Alexandrovich of Moscow; also with him was Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Constantine. And the princes went to Kashin, and stood under Kashin for nine days, and laid everything waste, and burned the town of Konstantin; and from there they wanted to go to Tver; and Grand Prince Mikhail came out against them with his army; and then they began negotiations, and after concluding peace they dispersed.

In the year 6797 from Adam (1297 from Christ)

The Most Reverend Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, in Holy Sophia in Kiev made Tarasius, abbot of the monastery of Saint John the Theologian, Bishop of Rostov.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, with his mother, Grand Princess Aksinya, sent Andrew, abbot of the common monastery of the Most Pure Mother of God in Kiev, to Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and he made Andrew a bishop.
