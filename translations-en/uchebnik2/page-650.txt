RUS (1411–1432 from the Incarnation of Christ). Book 12

In the year 6911 from Adam (the year 1411 from Christ)
Savva, Bishop of Sarsky, reposed.

In the same year, Grand Prince Vytautas Kestutovich sent Prince Lugven with an army to Vyazma.

He came, captured Vyazma, and took Prince Ivan Svyatoslavich prisoner together with Prince Alexander Mikhailovich, and brought them to Vytautas.

In the same year, Prince Andrei Dmitrievich married in Moscow Agrafena, daughter of Prince Alexander Patrikeevich.

In the same year, in Tver, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver founded a small town on the Opoki. In the same year it was also built.

That same autumn, on the 10th day of the month of October, at the second hour of the night, there was a sign in the sky in the form of a huge fiery spear, and this phenomenon repeated three times. That same winter, in the month of December, Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, summoned to Moscow Arseny, Bishop of Tver.

In the same year, in Tver, in the course of one year, the stone church of St Nicholas on Gorodok was built by Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, and it was consecrated by Arseny, Bishop of Tver.

In the same year, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver went with an army against Kashin, against his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, then returned himself.

And he sent his son Prince Alexander with all the army to Kashin. And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin fled to Moscow.

And Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow reconciled them.

In the same year, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich ordered the town of Pereyaslavl to be built. In the same year Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Gorodets reposed.
