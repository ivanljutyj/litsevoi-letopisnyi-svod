In the year 6804 from Adam (1304 from Christ)

There was strife among the Russian princes. At that time an envoy came from the Horde from the tsar, Alekse Nevrui.

There was a congress of all the Russian princes in Vladimir; each prince spoke before the envoy about his grievance. On one side stood Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Vladimir, and with him Prince Fyodor Cherny Rostislavich of Yaroslavl, and with him Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Rostov. On the other side against them stood Grand Prince Danilo Alexandrovich and Grand Prince Mikhailo Yaroslavich, and with them the Pereyaslavlians as one. And here bloodshed almost ensued; but Vladyka Simeon brought them to an amicable settlement, and having divided them according to their principalities, each went his own way.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Vladimir gathered a great army, intending to go to Pereyaslavl and to Moscow and to Tver.

But Prince Mikhailo Yaroslavich of Tver and Prince Danilo Alexandrovich of Moscow gathered a great army against him, and having set out, they halted with their hosts near Yuryev. And so they did not let Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich go to Pereyaslavl.

And Prince Ivan Dmitrievich of Pereyaslavl, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, was at that time in the Horde. And when going to the Horde, he had ordered them to guard his patrimony, Pereyaslavl. And so, after exchanging messages, they made peace.

In the year 6805 from Adam (1305 from Christ)

A church of Saint Athanasius was built in Tver. In the same year it was consecrated. In the same year the small town of Zubtsov was built of timber in the Principality of Tver on the Volga.

In the same year Prince Yuri Danilovich married in Rostov, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod.

In the year 6806 from Adam (1306 from Christ)

The vestibule beneath Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver caught fire, and the entire court of Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich burned. By God's mercy Prince Mikhailo himself awoke and threw himself with his princess out the window.
