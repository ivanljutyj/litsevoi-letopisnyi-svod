But I entreat and enjoin you, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and your sons and your grandsons from generation to generation, and all pious princes, boyars, and nobles, I entreat and enjoin them to keep all this that I have written. Those who do not take care for this will be tried by the Lord God and by His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos. Therefore I entreat all Orthodox Christians and enjoin them to keep all that has been written by our humility, because I wrote by the gift of the Holy Spirit. May the mercy and grace of the Lord God and of the Most Pure Theotokos be upon my son, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, upon his sons and grandsons from generation to generation, and with their great realm, and with all pious princes, boyars, nobles, merchants, and all the Christian people, peace and salvation, and our blessing now, always, and unto endless ages. Amen. And so the Most Reverend Photius, Metropolitan of All Rus, after writing his testament and after the completion of seven weeks and seventy days, as had been announced to him by an angel of God, departed to God in the year 6939 from Adam (1439 from Christ), on the second day of the month of July, and was laid in the cathedral metropolitan church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Moscow on the right side, where stands the tomb of Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus; he shepherded the Church of God for 22 years and 10 months.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich disputed the grand principality with his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich and went to the Horde

In that same year, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich and his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich disputed the grand principality of Moscow and decided to go to the Horde to Tsar Makhamet: whoever Tsar Makhamet should grant the grand principality would be grand prince. And so Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich in the month of August began to prepare to go to the Horde. On the feast of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos, after the end of the liturgy, he ordered a prayer service to be served to the Most Pure Theotokos and to the great wonderworker Peter, shed tears, ordered much alms to be distributed to all the churches of the city of Moscow, to the monasteries and to all the poor, and likewise ordered this to be done in all his cities, and set out for the Horde that same day; and he dined in his meadow opposite Simonov [Monastery] under a canopy, and set forth on his journey.

In the year 6940 from Adam (1440 from Christ)

On the eighth day of the month of September, Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, on the feast of the Nativity of the Most Pure Theotokos, was at the liturgy in the church of the Most Pure Theotokos at Storozhi and set out after Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich to the Horde.
