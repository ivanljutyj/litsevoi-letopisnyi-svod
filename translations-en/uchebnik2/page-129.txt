He remained in perplexity, not knowing how to act. For thus the Lord God humbles and casts down the wicked and proud, giving them over to universal mockery and dishonor.

And so in Cheese-fare week Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich began to gather regiments and prepared the baggage wagons, and sent them to the other side of the Koloksha River, where Prince Gleb of Ryazan was standing; and Prince Gleb of Ryazan prepared a regiment and sent it together with his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich to the wagons; and Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich sent his son Vladimir after the wagons together with the Pereyaslavl men and with a multitude of warriors against his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich; and Prince Gleb of Ryazan with his two sons, Roman and Igor, and with his brother-in-law Yaropolk Rostislavich crossed the Koloksha River and came to Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich on Pruskova Hill. They had not come within a bowshot of Vsevolod’s regiment when his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich on the other side of the Koloksha River fell into confusion himself and threw all the host of Prince Gleb of Ryazan into confusion; this happened by God’s will, for the avenging of the injustice of Prince Gleb of Ryazan.

When Prince Gleb of Ryazan saw his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich fleeing from Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich, fear and terror seized him; after standing a little, he too fled, driven by God’s wrath, though no one was pursuing him—only God’s wrath.

And Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, seeing him driven by God’s wrath, pursued him with all his host, and some he killed, others he took captive, as also Prince Gleb of Ryazan himself and his son Prince Roman, and his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, and his tysyatsky Boris Zhidislavich, and his voivode Yakov Dedenkov, and Olstin, and Dedil, and a vast multitude of other warriors; likewise some of the Polovtsians they killed and others they captured with bare hands. Such vengeance did the Lord God accomplish. If the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, do not let even simple people be wronged, then all the more do they not permit wrongs to their own sacred and divine; even if He sometimes endures long, yet He still executes punishment: with the judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with the measure you measure, it will be measured to you; whatever a man does to his neighbor, he himself falls into the same—does this not happen in things bodily or spiritual? For, it is said, judgment without mercy is for him who has not shown mercy. This victory took place and help was given by the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God to Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich of Vladimir against Prince Gleb of Ryazan on Monday of St Theodore’s week, and he returned to Vladimir with great joy, bringing with him Prince Gleb of Ryazan and his son Prince Roman, and his brother-in-law Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.
