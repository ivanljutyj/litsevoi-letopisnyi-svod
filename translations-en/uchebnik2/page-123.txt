There was then a notable voivode with Prince Mikhalko, Vladislav, brother of Yanev; and they gave thanks to God and His most pure Mother, the Theotokos, and then hastily went against the Polovtsians, and, meeting them, struck them down, for none of them could raise a hand. Such a miracle did the Mother of God, the most pure Theotokos, show. And they asked some who were still alive: “Are there any more of your Polovtsians behind you?” They told of the great Polovtsian force coming after them, more than ten thousand. Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich placed his hope in the Lord God and His most pure Mother, the Theotokos, since at first the Mother of God had wrought a great and ineffable miracle over the godless Polovtsians and delivered them to death, and then again gave help, and now was again the helper and intercessor.

And so, mounting his horse, he went hastily against them with his small retinue. And behold, suddenly he met a great multitude of Polovtsians, and when they began to close for battle and slaughter, then suddenly all quickly fled, and threw down all their weapons of war and shields, and fled wholly naked on horseback; and also falling from their horses, they fell and died. Thus the Mother of God, the Theotokos, wrought a great and ineffable miracle, and thus the Cathedral Church of the Tithes, the most holy church, took vengeance for its wronged cities and territories. If God does not allow a simple man to be wronged, how much less will He allow the sacred house of His Mother to be wronged, but avenging, He will avenge everyone; for it is terrible to go against the goad and to offend what is sacred and divine. And so concerning the miracles of the Mother of God all people, joyfully in soul and heart, gratefully sang to the Lord God and His most pure Mother; and then there was fear and trembling throughout all the Polovtsian land at the miracle that had been wrought.

In the same year Mstislav Izyaslavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, with his brother, Prince Yaroslav Izyaslavich, with all the Volhynians, Galicians, and Kovui, came to Dorogobuzh against Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, and he was ill, and then also reposed, on the 28th day of the month of January; and they ravaged Dorogobuzh and other cities, and some they burned, and went away to their own lands.

In the same year a multitude of Polovtsians came and fought around Kiev, but the great prince of Kiev himself, Gleb, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, was ill and sent against them his brothers, Prince Mikhalko and Prince Vsevolod, sons of Yuri Dolgoruky. They drove them beyond the river Bug, and by the mercy of God and the most pure Theotokos and all the saints, some Polovtsians were slain and others fled to the Steppe, and the Russian princes returned with joy with captives to their own lands.
