The whole sacred and divine great council, together with our humility, greets you, our most noble spiritual son, and therefore we hope that you honor and revere your instructors who labor among you, teaching and admonishing you in the Lord; especially in love keep the instructions and testaments which you received from us in the Lord, and for God’s love and for our instruction and blessing you will fulfill all things concerning your bishop. Instructing you as a father, we wrote that in honoring the hierarch you honor Christ: for he bears the image of Christ and sits on Christ’s throne; do not be lazy to listen to and honor such men, so that you may enjoy good things both in the present and in the future, and be glorified most of all. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God the Father, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with your nobility. Amen.”

In the same year Prince Mstislav Yuryevich, Prince Vasilko Yuryevich, and Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich went with their mother and their children to Constantinople to Emperor Manuel; and Emperor Manuel received them with joy and gave them cities.

In the same year Prince Vladimir Mstislavich was driven out of Lutsk; but his brother, the Grand Prince Rostislav Mstislavich of Kiev, gave him Trepol and four other towns.

In the same year Prince Vladimir Svyatoslavich Yaroslavich, Grand Prince of Ryazan, reposed.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich had the holy church in Vladimir-in-Volhynia painted, and adorned it wondrously with holy and precious icons, and books, and many wondrous objects, and sacred vessels of gold and silver with pearls and precious stones.

In the same year the Novgorodians drove their prince Mstislav out of Novgorod: the son of Rostislav, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and descendant of great Vladimir.

And the Grand Prince Rostislav of Kiev, son of Mstislav and grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, gave the Novgorodians his son Svyatoslav to reign in Novgorod according to their will.
