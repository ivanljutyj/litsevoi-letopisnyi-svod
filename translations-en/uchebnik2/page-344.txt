In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Vladimir, grandson of Yaroslav, came from the Horde from the Tsar.

In the year 6812 from Adam (the year 1312 from Christ)

Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Gorodets, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-forefather of Vladimir the Great, was tonsured into the holy angelic and monastic habit and into the schema, and reposed on the 22nd day of June.

And they carried him and laid him in Gorodets, in the Church of Saint Michael the Archangel. He reigned 11 years. And his boyars, and with them also Akinf, his boyar, after his death went away to Tver to Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver.

And the two princes began to dispute over the grand principality: Grand Prince Mikhailo Yaroslavich of Tver and Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow.

And they went to the Horde to the Tsar in dispute and great strife, and there was turmoil in the Suzdal land in all the cities.
