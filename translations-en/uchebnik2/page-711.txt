In the same winter, Prince Yuri Ivanovich of Tver married a daughter of Ivan Dmitrievich.

In the same year there was a severe famine throughout the Russian land and in the Novgorod principality. Many people died of hunger, and some fled from Rus to Lithuania. Some died of hunger and frost on the way, since that winter was very cold. Some people ate dead livestock, while horses, dogs, cats, moles, and even human beings ate human beings. And in Novgorod those who died of hunger were gathered into three common graves. At that time in Moscow an okov of rye was bought for one and a half rubles, in Kostroma for two rubles, and in Nizhny Novgorod for six rubles. In the same year King Jogaila Olgerdovich of Poland and Grand Duke Vytautas Kestutovich of Lithuania went with an army to the German town of Marienburg.

In the same year the Novgorodians kissed the cross to be united in all things.

In the same year, on the eleventh day of the month of July, Princess Anastasia, wife of Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, died in Zvenigorod, and she was buried in Moscow in the Ascension Monastery.

In the same year, on the eighteenth day of the month of August, there was a fire in Moscow: the city caught fire at midnight, and by daybreak it was all over.

On the thirty-first day of the same month of August, Tsar Barak defeated Tsar Kuidat.

In the year 6931 from Adam (1431 from Christ)

In September Tsar Barak came with an army to Odoev, caused much harm, but was unable to take the town, and went away into the field with a great number of captives. And Prince Yuri Romanovich of Odoev and Grigory Protasyevich, voivode of Mtsensk, overtook him in the field and took back the captives.

In the same autumn, in the month of September, three stone churches were built in Novgorod: the Church of the Resurrection of Christ in the Pavlov Monastery, the Church of St John the Merciful on Voskresenskaya Street in the Lyudin End, and the Church of the Resurrection of Christ in the Annunciation Monastery, built by David Dmitrievich.

In the same winter Grand Princess Sophia, wife of Prince Vasily Dmitrievich and daughter of Vytautas, together with her son Vasily Vasilyevich, went to her father Vytautas in Smolensk; and Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, having let her go from Moscow, himself set out for Kolomna. And Photius, Metropolitan of All Rus, was at that time with Vytautas, but he left before Princess Sophia.

In the same year there was a pestilence throughout the Russian land. In the same year in Novgorod two stone churches were built: that of the Most Pure Mother of God at Kolmovo and that of St James on the Luga.
