On the third day the Grand Prince of Smolensk himself, Rostislav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, also came to Novgorod, and the brothers came together, and there was no evil at all, but peace and love were between them for a long time.

And he placed his son Prince Svyatoslav on the grand-princely throne in Novgorod, and in Novy Torg he placed his son Prince Davyd, while he himself returned to Smolensk.

In that same year Constantine, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, having learned concerning Nestor, Bishop of Rostov, that he had been unjustly slandered by his own household, ordered all his slanderers to be put into prison.

The Suzdal reign of Prince Andrei Yuryevich Bogolyubsky began on the throne of the grand principality of Vladimir

In that same year the people of Rostov and Suzdal took pity on their prince Andrei Bogolyubsky, son of Yury Dolgoruky, because he grieved for his father and because he had become estranged from the grand principality of Kiev, and they began to comfort him; and they all resolved to place him on his father’s throne in Rostov and Suzdal, because that Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky was loved by all for his exceedingly great virtue, which he had toward God and toward all people, and he accomplished many things and deeds worthy of remembrance, and adorned churches, and gave many riches and possessions, and raised up monasteries; and he also gave many riches and possessions; and he completed the stone church of the Holy Savior, which his father the Grand Prince Yury Dolgoruky had founded, and he had great faith and love toward God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and the name of Christ and of His Most Pure Mother never ceased to be uttered from his lips, and he always delighted in reading the holy Scriptures, and prayed in tears at prayer, and greatly honored bishops and presbyters and monks, and he planned that the metropolitan see should be in Rostov or in Suzdal, or that Vladimir should be raised up as an even greater city, which the blessed Grand Prince Vladimir, who baptized all the Russian land, had founded. And thinking thus, he placed his hope in God, for as He wills, so He does; and he always remained in prayers and tears, in a harsh life, serving Christ.

And he had great love with the Emperor of Constantinople and with the Patriarch, and sent many and frequent gifts to them, and they too had love and humility toward him, and many gifts.

For the sake of their great virtues, and the love which they had toward God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and among themselves, and toward all, the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, poured out Their mercy upon them, so that in one day they defeated the godless Ishmaelites; and in remembrance of such a miracle they established a celebration on the first day of the month of August.
