Shall I glorify you as Isaac, prepared as a sacrifice to God by his father? But you yourself offered your pure and immaculate soul as a sacrifice to God. Shall I call you Israel? But he wrestled with God in frenzy, while you fought the godless Hagarites and godless Lithuania for the holy churches, establishing the faith like a spiritual ladder. Shall I present you as chaste Joseph, the wise and spiritual fruit, who possessed Egypt? But you were chaste in mind and appeared as ruler of all the Russian land. Shall I call you Moses? But he was prince to one Hebrew people, while you had many peoples in your principality, shining with the honor of thanksgiving in many lands. The Roman land glorifies Peter and Paul; Asia, John the Theologian; India, the Apostle Thomas; Jerusalem, James the brother of the Lord; all Pomor'e, Andrew the First-Called; the Greek land, Tsar Constantine; Kiev and its surrounding cities, Vladimir. But you, Great Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, all the Russian land glorifies! And I, unworthy, cannot give your most glorious rule worthy praise because of my ignorance. Pray, holy one, for your kin and for all the people living in your kingdom. For you are there where the pastures of spiritual sheep and eternal blessedness are. For blessedness, joy, and the beauty of Paradise are to see the divine face, to stand before the face of God in those pastures. There angelic choirs sing; there is great fellowship with the heavenly powers; there sweet honor is given for heavy labors on earth; there is the assembly of wise prophets, and there the apostolic judgment, and there the countless host of martyrs. There zealous confessors receive their reward; there faithful men subdue the vain desires of this world by strength of mind; there holy women by good character overcome the male sex. And there youths who preserved purity here behold angels. There too are elders whom old age made feeble, but the power of good deeds did not destroy. Having such saints, we can live in peace and with them enjoy the joyful grace and love for mankind of your only-begotten Son. With Him you are blessed, with your most holy, good, and life-giving Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages, amen.

After his repose, his fifth son Ivan also reposed.

And his sixth son Constantine remained after his father at the age of four days. His seventh son was the eldest, Daniel, but he had reposed earlier.

In the same year, on the 21st day of the month of July, the city of Moscow caught fire from the Church of Saint Athanasius, which is inside the city; people had only just dined. And almost the whole city burned. The Kremlin burned, and little of it remained, so that by evening it was barely extinguished.
