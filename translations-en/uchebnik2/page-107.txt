In that year the weather was fair, and there was great heat and drought all summer, and all grain and vegetables burned, and lakes and rivers dried up, marshes burned out, and forests and the earth burned.

In the same year there was a great flood in Galich: by God's permission the rains increased, and suddenly in one day and night water came from the great Dniester into the marsh, and rose as far as Bokovoye Marsh, and drowned more than 300 people who were going with salt from Udechev, and many people were taken down from trees and logs that the water had scattered, and many fields were flooded; and that winter they had a dearness of grain.

And that autumn great frosts set in, yet the winter was all warm, with great rains, lightning, and terrible thunder, and many people were killed; a kad of rye was then a ruble and forty altyns, and there was among the people great sorrow and grief, and inconsolable weeping.

In the year 6671 from Adam (1171 from Christ)

The church at the Golden Gate in Vladimir was consecrated. In the same year died Feodor, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus.

In the same year died Arkady, Bishop of Novgorod, on the 19th day of the month of September, and he was laid in Novgorod, in the narthex of the Church of Holy Sophia.

In the year 6672 from Adam (1172 from Christ)

Prince Izyaslav died, son of Grand Prince Andrey Bogolyubsky, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, and he was laid in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Vladimir, on the 28th day of the month of September.

In the same year John the Metropolitan came from the patriarch in Constantinople to Kiev and all Rus. And Grand Prince Rostislav, son of Mstislav, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir, went out from the city of Kiev to meet him with bishops and all the sacred council, and with princes, and with boyars, and with all the people, and he was received with joy and honor.

In the same year the brother of Emperor Manuel, Kyr Andronikos Komnenos, fled from Constantinople to Galich to Grand Prince Yaroslav Volodimirovich of Galich. And Grand Prince Yaroslav Volodimirovich received him with honor and great love, and gave him cities and districts.
