And with gentle words and speeches, and gracious utterances, by the grace given to him from the Holy Spirit, he conversed much with him about the benefit of the soul, and about peace, and about love. And Grand Prince Oleg changed his ferocity to meekness, and grew calm, and was tamed, and was greatly softened in soul, for he was ashamed before so holy a man; and he concluded with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich an eternal peace and love from generation to generation.

And the venerable Abbot Sergius returned with honor and much glory to Moscow to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and was worthily praised, and glorified, and honored by all. That same winter the servant of God Semyon Yama reposed and was laid in Moscow, in the monastery of the Holy Ascension.

That same winter, on the 1st day of January, on the commemoration of Basil the Great of Caesarea, on Monday, at the midday meal the sun perished, and what remained of it was like a four-day-old moon, and again in the evening it was filled, and it was in darkness for two hours. In the same year Bishop Matthew the Greek of Rostov reposed. In the same year Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod went to the Horde to Tsar Tokhtamysh.

And that same summer, toward autumn, he came from the Horde to his patrimony, to Nizhny Novgorod and to Gorodets, and to Suzdal. In the same year the church of St Michael the Archangel at Gorodets of Nizhny Novgorod burned.

In the same year Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Suzdal, Kirdyapa, grandson of Konstantin, fled from the Horde from Tsar Tokhtamysh, and a Tatar envoy met him.

And having seized him, he brought him to the Horde to Tsar Tokhtamysh, and for this he received great suffering from the tsar and from the Tatars.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sent his spiritual father Theodore, archimandrite of Simonov, to Constantinople regarding the governance of the metropolitanate.

At that same time Euphrosynus, archimandrite of the Caves, went from Nizhny Novgorod to Constantinople for consecration to the bishopric of Suzdal.

That same autumn Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver came from the Horde, and with him a Tatar envoy.

In the same year Stephen Khrap, bishop of Perm, went to Novgorod the Great.

In the same year Archimandrite Joachim renewed the old monastery and the dilapidated church, filled them with icons and books, gathered monks, and erected many cells, and brought there Abbot Ivan, a zealous man; and so he established a common life in Christ.

In the same year in the great Volga Horde Tsar Tokhtamysh himself killed his tsarina, called Tovlunbeka. In the same year the Novo- shut themselves up.
