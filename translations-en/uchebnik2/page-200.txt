That same winter Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich consecrated the church of Saint Michael at his court in Rostov, on the 25th day of November, on the commemoration of Saint Clement, Pope of Rome, and Peter of Alexandria.

That same winter, on the 1st day of February, on the memory of the holy father Simeon the God-Receiver, there was a sign in the moon: it darkened wholly, and little of it remained, as at the beginning of the first day of the month, and so it remained until evening and until midnight.

That same winter the Olgovichi went to Kiev against Rurik Rostislavich, but could do nothing and returned.

In the year 6717 Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, sent his son Prince Yaroslav to Ryazan for the great reign; the Ryazanians kissed the cross to Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich and to his son Prince Yaroslav, who sat among them in the great reign. And they were not long in peace, love, and truth, and again fell into strife, not having been able to restrain themselves; they seized many boyars and men of Vsevolod and Yaroslav, and having put them in chains, and having shut some in cellars, they put them to death, and others they hanged.

And the Ryazanians began to take counsel with Prince Gleb Vladimirovich and with his brother Prince Izyaslav Vladimirovich, the princes of Pronsk, wishing to hand over Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich to them from the city of Ryazan to Pronsk.

He, hearing of this, began to hide and to fear them, and sent to his father in Vladimir. His father, hearing of this, went to Ryazan with his children and, arriving, stood by the city. And Prince Yaroslav, his son, came out of the city to meet his father. The Ryazanians also came with foolish speeches, according to their custom and disobedience.

And Grand Prince Vsevolod ordered all the people to come out of the city with all their light goods, and with all their remaining goods he set fire to the city and the whole suburb, and went to Belgorod, and burned its suburbs, and ravaged its districts and villages.

He also burned other cities with their suburbs and districts.

And he laid waste the whole land of Ryazan, and burned cities, districts, and villages, and slaughtered the livestock.

And Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich went to Vladimir with all his regiments, with his son Yaroslav, and led all the Ryazan people out with him to Vladimir, with their wives and children, and took their bishop Arseny with him to Vladimir.

And he dispersed all the Ryazanians among his cities.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, took another wife, the daughter of Prince Vasilko of Vitebsk.
