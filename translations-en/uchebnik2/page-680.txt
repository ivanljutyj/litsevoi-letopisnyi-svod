After the Tatar invasion, and after frequent pestilences, the population of the Russian land began to increase; then church metropolitanates also began to grow, the revenues of Metropolitan Photius began to increase, and what had disappeared began to be recovered—what had been seized by princes, boyars, or stolen by any other extortioners: villages and lands, revenues and dues of the house of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the holy great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis. The metropolitan began to gather and secure everything in the house of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the holy wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, in the sacred metropolitanate of all Rus: revenues and dues, and lands, and waters, and villages, and lands. Some things he bought for the glory of the name of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God for the sustenance of the poor and needy, as it is written: “Church wealth is the wealth of the poor; he who gives to the poor lends to God; blessed is he who understands the needy and poor; in the evil day the Lord will deliver him. The Lord will preserve him and keep him alive, and bless him on earth, and will not deliver him into the hands of his enemies; and sins are cleansed by alms, and as water quenches fire, so alms cleanse sins.” And the holy prophet Daniel says: “Cleanse thy sins by alms, and thine iniquities by mercies to the poor.” In that same spring, in the month of May, Prince Vladimir Andreevich died, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel of Moscow, and he bequeathed to his princess and his children that they give to the house of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, and to the metropolitan, his father the Most Reverend Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, for the commemoration of himself and his family, the village of Kudrino with its hamlets, with all its lands, as had been established in old times. His princess Elena, daughter of Olgerd Gediminovich, at the command of her husband Prince Vladimir Andreevich, together with her children, gave to the house of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, to her father the Most Reverend Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, for the commemoration of her husband and herself, her parents, children, and family, the large village of Kudrino, and with that village also the hamlets: Ananyino, Obeltsyno, Bestuzhevo, Afanasyevo, Davidovo, Diakonovo, Kraskovo, Redkino, Valchino, Zakharovo, Vydreshkovo, Rodukino, Timoshkino, and the wastelands, settlements, with all their lands, as had been established in old times.

In that same year in Novgorod died the posadnik Timofei, and also another posadnik, Osip Zakharyich.
