On the Placement of the Wonder-Working Icon of the Mother of God in Moscow, in the Cathedral Church of Her Venerable Dormition

And so, offering her many thanksgivings, they journeyed to the city with the icon of the Mother of God. They brought it into the famous church of her glorious Dormition, which was the great cathedral and apostolic church of the Russian metropolia, and placed it in an icon-case on the right side, where to this day it stands visible, venerated by all, and works innumerable miracles; it is the intercessor and guardian from all misfortunes for the reigning city of Moscow, for all the cities of the Russian realm, and for all the Christian patrimony. Temir-Aksak was then encamped in the land of Ryazan for fifteen days already and had laid everything waste by God's permission. And from there he was steadfastly preparing to enslave the whole Russian land, to torment all Christ-named humanity, and to put them to the sword. But the right hand of the Most High Providence did not permit this. Who can recount the ineffable bounties and love for mankind of God, and the intercession and mercy of His Most Pure Mother toward the Christian race!

On Tsar Temir-Aksak Being Terrified by a Vision

O most glorious miracle, brothers! O most great wonder! On that very day and hour when the whole multitude of the people went out to meet that divine icon and with tears cried out to God and to the Mother of God, praying for deliverance from the invasion of the impious, in that very hour the proud tormentor Temir-Aksak saw an unusual vision that filled him with horror. It was thus: he lay on his bed and slept. And he saw a fearful dream: there appeared to him an exceedingly high mountain, and over the mountain hierarchs were coming toward him, holding golden staffs in their hands and threatening him. And suddenly he saw above the hierarchs in the air an unusually radiant, ineffable light. In the midst of that bright light appeared a certain woman, clothed in purple robes and adorned with ineffable glory and beauty, shining with light brighter than the sun's rays. Around Her was a countless multitude of fearsome and great, most radiant warriors, serving Her as a queen. This woman, standing, stretched her hands on high and prayed for someone, while fiercely threatening Temir-Aksak. At this threatening vision he suddenly shuddered and leapt up in terror, as if at a clap of thunder; he was frightened and horrified.
