The Bishop of Kolomna, at the city gates with the life-giving crosses and with the holy icons, with the whole sacred assembly.

And they entered the cathedral church and celebrated a supplicatory service to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, and Bishop Gerasim served the holy liturgy; and throughout the city the priests also celebrated supplicatory services and liturgies for the health of the Grand Prince, and of all the princes, and of all the Christ-loving army.

The Grand Prince stayed at Kolomna four days, resting a little from his labors, for he was greatly wearied and exhausted; and he set out for the glorious city of Moscow.

And he entered the city of Moscow, and his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, met him with crosses and with the whole sacred assembly.

And the Grand Prince said to the metropolitan: “Father, by the mercy of God, and of His Most Pure Mother, and of the great wonderworker Peter, and by your holy prayers, and by the venerable abbot Sergius, we have overcome the impious Mamai and defeated those with him. For he rose up in pride, but we in humility. You yourself saw how much gold and silver we sent him and how much we begged him. But he did not listen; rather, he exalted himself in his pride and was put to shame.

By God’s mercy we defeated them and captured much of their spoil and wealth, and drove away with us many herds: horses, camels, yoked oxen, great bulls, of which there is no number, and their weapons, and armor, and clothing, and countless goods.”

Metropolitan Cyprian said: “Glory to You, O Lord! Glory to You, O Holy One! Glory to You, O King! For You have shown us Your great mercy and cast down our enemies! We magnify You, Most Holy Virgin Theotokos! For You have shown great mercy and great miracles to Orthodox Christians! We bless You, Peter, saint of Christ! For you defend your flock from misfortunes and cast down our enemies! How then shall we glorify you, my lord, my beloved son in Christ, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, new Constantine, glorious Vladimir, wondrous Yaroslav, marvelous Alexander? What thanksgiving, honor, and glory shall we render you for having striven and labored so much for all Orthodox Christianity?”

And the metropolitan blessed him with the precious cross, and his cousin, Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and having kissed one another, they entered the holy church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos and celebrated a supplicatory service; and the metropolitan himself served the divine liturgy with the whole sacred assembly. And throughout the city the priests also served supplicatory services and liturgies for the health of the Grand Prince, and of all the princes, and of all the Christ-loving army.
