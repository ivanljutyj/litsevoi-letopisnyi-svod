In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, great-great-grandson of Alexander, and Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew, quarreled over the grand principality of Moscow and sent their envoys to Tsar Amurat in the Horde.

And Tsar Amurat gave the grand principality to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow by right of his father and grandfather. And Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich went from Vladimir to Pereyaslavl and took up the reign there.

That same winter Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, with his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, and with Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, great-great-grandson of Alexander, gathered a large army in their appanages and went to Pereyaslavl against Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew. But he fled from Pereyaslavl to Vladimir, and from there to Suzdal, his patrimony, after having been in the grand principality at Vladimir only a little more than two years.

The Reign of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, with his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, and with Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Daniel, went with all their army from Pereyaslavl to Vladimir.

And Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sat on the grand principality in Vladimir on the throne of his father and grandfather, being 11 years old, surpassing all in understanding and courage. And he stayed then in Vladimir three weeks, and returned again to Moscow, and dismissed his army.

That same winter died Roman, Metropolitan of Lithuania and Volhynia.

That same winter died Vladyka Moses, Archbishop of Novgorod, on the fifth day of January, and was buried at the Church of the Archangel Michael on Skovorodka; he had governed the Church of God as archbishop for eight years.

In the same year died Daniel, Vladyka of Suzdal. In the same year died Athanasius, Vladyka of Kostroma.

In the year 6871 from Adam (1371 from Christ)

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, went from Moscow to Vladimir with his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, and with his cousin Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Daniel.
