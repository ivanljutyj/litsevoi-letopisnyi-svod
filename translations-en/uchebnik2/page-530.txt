At that time the Novgorod posadnik Grigory Yakunovich said to his lord Aleksey: “Holy hierarch of God, Aleksey, make peace among us; if not, the sword will kill many.”

And Vladyka Aleksey, with him two archimandrites, seven priests, and five laymen, one man from each end, went a second time to the grand prince.

And they petitioned him on behalf of Veliky Novgorod, saying: “Lord Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the Novgorodians will themselves seek out the guilty—those who warred against Kostroma and robbed and beat the Volga merchants, and those who went on the campaign with them; and for those on whom the princely due has fallen into arrears, they give 8,000 rubles for them, and let the grand prince take the black tribute from them.”

And then the Novgorodians took 3,000 rubles from the galleries of St Sophia and sent them to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich with Posadnik Grigory Yakunovich and Posadnik Vasily Fedorovich.

And they agreed to pay 5,000 rubles to the grand prince from the Zavolochye land. The grand prince had mercy and heeded Vladyka Aleksey, and took 8,000 rubles from the Novgorodians for the guilty, and returned from Yamen and went to Moscow.

In the same year the Novgorodians went beyond the portage: Posadnik Fedor Timofeevich, Timofey Yuryevich, Yury Dmitrievich, and with them lesser boyar sons, young men, and servants, to collect that silver, to the amount of 5,000 rubles, from the Zavolochians, which had been imposed on them in Novgorod, decided by the whole veche according to old custom, justly upon the whole Zavolochye land, because the Zavolochians had been on the Volga, and had robbed and beaten merchants, and had warred against Kostroma. And the grand prince returned from Yamen and went to Moscow, having made peace with Novgorod, and sent his namestniks and black-tribute collectors to Veliky Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians built a stone church of St Clement on Ivorova Street. In the same year the town of Orekhov burned, and in Novgorod the end of Nikitina Street.

In the same year the Horde princes entered into strife among themselves.

In the same year Grand Prince Jagiello Olgerdovich of Lithuania went to the Polish land to marry and married there, taking as his wife the princess Jadwiga, daughter of Casimir; and there he was baptized into the Roman faith, and because of this the kingdom fell to him.

And he sent into the Lithuanian land and ordered the Lithuanians to be baptized into the German faith; he baptized half of his city of Vilna.

But two of his chief Lithuanians had been baptized into the Christian Orthodox faith; he wanted to baptize them into his faith, and they did not obey him; and King Jagiello executed them with many torments and ordered them put to death.
