We are all with you in our minds and are ready to lay down our heads and shed our blood for you.” He said this wishing to flatter his lord. But they themselves had conspired with Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, ready to betray their lord to him.

When Tokhtamysh’s Tatars and the Moscow boyars approached the city, Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich did not want to let them into his city. And his eldest boyar Vasily Rumyanets said to him: “Lord prince, this is Tokhtamysh’s envoy and the boyars of the Grand Prince of Moscow Vasily Dmitrievich. They wish to confirm peace and establish eternal love, while you yourself wish to begin war. Let them into the city; after all, we are all for you—what can they do?”

So the Tatars and Moscow boyars entered the city and began ringing the bells. All the people flocked together. Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich sent for his boyars, saying: “My lords and brothers, and my dear retinue, remember the cross-kiss you gave me, and do not betray me to my enemies.” And one of them, the eldest boyar Vasily Rumyanets, answered: “Lord prince, do not rely on us; from now on we are not yours and not with you, but against you.” Thus Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich of Nizhny Novgorod was captured.

And after some time Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich came to Nizhny Novgorod and installed his viceroy in it. And Prince Boris Konstantinovich with his wife and children and his supporters he ordered sent to various places and kept very firmly in iron fetters.

And behold, an envoy came from the Horde from Tsar Tokhtamysh and summoned Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich and his envoy to the Horde. The Grand Prince went to the Tsar in the Horde and was received with honor by Tsar Tokhtamysh as none of the former princes had been. And Tsar Tokhtamysh gave him Nizhny Novgorod and Gorodets with all that belonged to them, Meshchera and Tarusa, and showed him great favor and sent him back to Rus.

In the same year in Novgorod the Great Yuri Antsiforovich founded a monastery in the name of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God at Kolmovo.

In the same year Germans came as robbers into the Novgorod districts and entered the Neva, took districts and villages on both sides of the river, three versts from the town of Oreshek.

Prince Semyon gathered with the townspeople, pursued them, and killed some, brought some into the city, while others fled.

And Semyon went to Lithuania, leaving the city. Then the Novgorodians began to dig a rampart around the Trade Wall.
