He, having come, sat in Kiev on the great reign, and sent Prince Gleb Yuryevich to the great reign in Pereyaslavl.

In that same year the Polovtsians came again and did much evil around Pereyaslavl; they burned districts and villages, and the Monastery of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God, and the Monastery of the Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb, and the Monastery of Saint Savva, and the Lettskaya chapel, in which there was a multitude of treasure, they plundered and burned, and led people away captive.

The Grand Prince of Suzdal, Yuri Dolgoruky, hearing that his nephew, the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, had reposed, began to think of a campaign into Rus; and having prepared everything, he went into Rus. And when he was near Smolensk, a messenger came to him saying: “Your nephew, the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, has fallen asleep in eternal sleep and received rest from this vain and deceitful life, and was laid in the Church of Saint Theodore in his father’s monastery; and after him his brother, your nephew, Rostislav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, sat on the great reign in Kiev together with your brother, the Grand Prince Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomakh; together and of one mind they rule on the great reign in Kiev.

And in Pereyaslavl on the great reign was Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich; and then your son Prince Gleb went against him to Pereyaslavl with Chernigov force and with the Polovtsians, and fought much, but could do nothing, and withdrawing stood on the Sula.

The Grand Prince of Kiev Rostislav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, with his son Svyatoslav and with Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, and with Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich, went to Chernigov against the Grand Prince of Chernigov Izyaslav Davidovich and against your son Prince Gleb; and when he was on the way, a messenger came to him saying that the Grand Prince of Kiev Vyacheslav had passed from this vain and deceitful life to another life.

The Grand Prince Rostislav returned to Kiev and buried his uncle, and your brother, the Grand Prince of Kiev Vyacheslav Vladimirovich Monomakh, in the Church of Holy Sophia. And again he went to Chernigov against the Grand Prince Izyaslav Davidovich and against your son, Prince Gleb, and when he was at Boloves, they came together for battle with Prince Izyaslav of Chernigov, and with your son, Prince Gleb, and with the Polovtsians.
