He was then in the German land. He had fled from Lithuania after his father Kestutis was killed. His father Kestutis Gediminovich had planned to take the great principality of Lithuania from Jogaila Olgerdovich. A great battle took place, Kestutis Gediminovich was killed, and his son Vytautas fled at once to the German land.

With the help of the German host he warred against Lithuania. And therefore with great joy he gave his daughter Sophia in marriage to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, hoping for his son-in-law’s help in the war with Lithuania. Grand Prince Vasily was wedded to Sophia Vitovtovna in Moscow by Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus. That same winter a letter came from the patriarch to Novgorod the Great, according to the word of Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, concerning judgment, signed by the own hand of Anthony, Patriarch of New Rome. The metropolitans of Nicaea, Chalcedon, and Adrianople, of Serbia, of Gaiania, and of Drama also signed this letter with their own hands. In that same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver summoned to him in Tver Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus.

And after Easter he went to Tver, and with him were two Greek metropolitans: Matthew of Adrianople and Nikander of Gaiania; and Mikhail, Bishop of Smolensk, and Stefan, Bishop of Perm. Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, was met by the grandson of Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich, Prince Alexander, thirty versts from the city of Tver with boyars, with great honor.

On the next day Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, was met by the eldest son of Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, Prince Ivan, twenty versts from the city of Tver with boyars, with great honor.

On the third day, on Saturday after Vespers, Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver himself, with princes and boyars, with great honor met Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, five versts from the city of Tver. The metropolitan came out of his tent to meet the grand prince and blessed Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich, and kissed him with love. They sat down and conversed for a long time about spiritual benefit.

In the morning the grand prince again himself met Metropolitan Cyprian with his children, with his brothers, and with boyars at Peremir. And from there they went to the city, and there they were met with crosses before the Vladimir Gate, at the Church of the Holy Martyr George, and they kissed the holy crosses and the honorable icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, entered the Church of the Great Savior, and ordered the lamb to be slain, and began to serve the Holy Liturgy.

After the completion of the divine service, the grand prince with his children and with his nephews petitioned him to come to the feast together with the guests, the Greek metropolitans, with the bishops, with the elders, and with the boyars, and with his servants, and with all who were there.
