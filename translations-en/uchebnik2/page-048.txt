Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, with his brother Vladimir Mstislavich and his son Mstislav, went against Grand Prince Volodimirko of Galich and met him as he came at Tyumash; and they stood on the River Stugna, and archers shot at one another across the river. And Volodimirko, Grand Prince of Galich, began to press hard with his force.

The Berendei and the rest of the host of Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich, seeing the countless force of Grand Prince Volodimirko of Galich, were greatly afraid and began to say to Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich: “Let us go back; for his force is great and countless, and you have few troops; you yourself know the bravery of the Galich host, and besides there are many of them; let us return quickly before they cross the river against us, and then it will be hard for us all.”

Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev said: “It is better to accept death than to have shame and an evil life.” But the Kievans began to press him still harder, saying: “Flee to Kiev and fight from the city with the whole host.” He did not wish to; and while they were speaking thus, they saw Grand Prince Volodimirko crossing the river at other places, and, terrified, the Berendei fled, then the Kievans, and Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich himself fled also.

Prince Volodimirko with his warriors pursued after them, capturing some and cutting down and killing others. Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev ran to Kiev to his druzhina and ordered them to assemble at Dorozhich; and he himself, after waiting for night, left Kiev.

Grand Prince Volodimirko of Galich, having come, stood by the teremets; and Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Dolgoruky Monomash at the same time came to Kiev with the Davidovichi and Olegovichi, and stood at Chertorya, and sent Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich and his son Prince Boris after Izyaslav Mstislavich, and pursued him as far as Chertov Forest, and, not overtaking him, turned back.

Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich, going from Kiev, took Pogoryna and installed his son Mstislav in Dorogobuzh. The Kievans, fearing Prince Volodimirko of Galich, brought Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Dolgoruky Monomash into Kiev, and he met with Prince Volodimirko of Galich in the Caves Monastery of the Holy Theotokos, and they made great love between them. And Prince Volodimirko went to his domain, to Galich, and took from him his son, Prince Mstislav.
