But I am sinful from my mother’s womb and remain in sin unceasingly. Behold, the time has come for me also to suffer in this fleeting life. All our life passes by like a shadow, vanishes like smoke, and dissolves like a dream.” And they all, grieving for him, said, “The will of the Lord be done,” and could say nothing more.

When he boarded the ship with his people, a strong wind arose. The rowers row, but the ship is thrown backward. They try even harder, but the wind strikes them in the face, and the ship again turns back. Thus, with great difficulty, the ship crossed the land of Rus.

His brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, with his boyars and servants, escorted him to Svyatoslav’s Field. But his brother Konstantin Mikhailovich was then gravely ill and could not escort him, and Prince Alexander Mikhailovich wept for him, saying: “Let him after my death be heir and gatherer of our patrimony; let him be established after all that may happen.” At that same time Prince Vasily Davydovich of Yaroslavl, grandson of Feodor, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of Davyd, and Prince Romanchuk of Belozersk went with him to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak.

When they came to the Horde, Prince Alexander Mikhailovich found his son Feodor in the Horde; weeping, they kissed each other. And his son told him of the tsar’s wrath. He said: “May the Will of the Lord be fulfilled!” And taking great gifts, he presented them to the tsar, the tsaritsa, the princes, and other Tatar dignitaries, and he remained in the Horde with Tsar Azbyak for one month. Many of the Tatars and his friends said to him: “The tsar wants to kill you, because you have been strongly slandered.” He said to them: “What can I do? If God wills that I be put to death, who will deliver me from it?”

And he began to pray and prepare for repentance and confession with his spiritual father.

The day of the memory of the holy great martyr Demetrius was approaching. And news reached him that he would soon be killed: in three days he would be put to an evil death. He said: “Lord, Thy will be done; do as Thou wilt!” And all day he went about sorrowful, praying and grieving. And the day of the memory of the holy martyrs Terentius and Neonilla, of the venerable Stephen, composer of canons, and of Savvatius, and of the holy martyr Paraskeva Pyatnitsa arrived.
