And he blessed with the cross, and sprinkled with holy water the Grand Prince and those two monks of his, Peresvet and Oslyabya, and all the princes, and boyars, and commanders.

And he said to the Grand Prince: “The Lord God will be your helper and protector, and He will conquer and cast down your enemies and glorify you.” The Grand Prince received the blessing from the venerable one and rejoiced in his heart that the venerable Sergius had said to him: “The Lord God will be your helper and protector, and He will conquer and cast down your enemies, and glorify you.” And he kept this in his mind as a kind of treasure, and told no one.

And having come to the city of Moscow, he received a blessing from his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, and told him alone what the venerable Sergius had said to him. The metropolitan said to him: “Tell this to no one until the Lord brings it to a good end.”

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich then took care and prepared himself in all things, and entered the cathedral church, and fell prostrate before the image of the Most Pure Mother of God, which the Evangelist Luke had painted, praying to receive help against the enemies. He also went to the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter and fell down with tears, and prayed to receive help and protection from the enemies and to cast down their pride and harshness. And having finished the prayer, he went to his father Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, asking forgiveness and a blessing. The metropolitan forgave him and blessed him, and overshadowed him with the precious cross, and sprinkled him with holy water.

And he sent many presbyters and deacons with precious crosses and holy water to the Nikolsky, Frolovsky, and Konstantino-Yeleninsky gates — to bless all, so that every warrior might receive a blessing from them and be sprinkled with holy water.

The Grand Prince went to the holy church of the holy Archangel Michael, and before the icons he signed himself with the sign of the cross and prayed, and at the tombs of his parents he took leave and received a blessing.

And he came out of the church, and mounting a horse, set out for Kolomna. His brother, Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, he sent by the Brazheva road, and the Belozersk princes by the Bolvanovskaya road with their host.

But the Grand Prince himself went by the road to Kotel with many forces. And it came about that the sun warmed him in front, while behind him a gentle and quiet wind blew and breathed. They divided onto different roads for this reason, that they could not all go by a single road.
