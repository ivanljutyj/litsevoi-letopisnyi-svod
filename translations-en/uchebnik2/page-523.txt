He lived in silence and stillness for many years; but when the time for conversation came to him, he was very thoughtful and exceedingly profitable, and his word was seasoned with divine salt. He was rich in virtues and wondrous in all things, and was called by the nickname Paul Vysoky. He was laid to rest honorably in the Pechersky Monastery in Nizhny Novgorod. And many grieved for him, because he was a consolation and a spiritual refuge for many who came to him; and not only presbyters and monks but also laypeople grieved for him, and the metropolitan himself and the bishops; and that Dionysius, Bishop of Suzdal and Gorodets and Nizhny Novgorod, grieved for him, since he was very learned and well disposed to good customs, and obedient in the Lord, and sweet to all, and consoling, and profitable.

In that same year Prince Ivan Borisovich, grandson of Konstantin, went to the Horde to Tsar Tokhtamysh, to his father Prince Boris Konstantinovich, for Prince Boris was then in the Horde.

In that same year Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod sent his son Prince Semyon to Tsar Tokhtamysh in the Horde, since he had come to old age and infirmity.

Beginning of the Appearance of the Image of the Most Pure Theotokos at Tikhvin.
In that same year there appeared an icon—the image of the Most Pure Theotokos, the Hodegetria—in the region of Great Novgorod, in the Obonezh Pyatina, on the Oyat, at Vychenitsy, a hundred versts from the city; and no one knew whence it had appeared. And from that icon of the image of the Most Pure there were many miracles.

And there they built a church in the name of the Nativity of the Holy Theotokos. And after some time it departed invisibly from there and appeared again on the Kozhela, twenty versts from there on Kukova Hill; and on that place they built a church in the name of the Protection of the Holy Theotokos.

And from there the icon—the image of the Most Pure—appeared at Tikhvin, above the river, on the hill, in the air; and a multitude of people gathered for this great miracle; and on that place, on the hill, they laid the foundation of a church—three courses—in the name of Her honorable Dormition, and toward night the people went home.

In the morning they came to that place and found nothing, but they saw the church framework and the icon on the other side of the Tikhvina River, and there they completed the church.

And from that time great miracles began to occur at the Most Pure Theotokos at Tikhvin to those who came with faith, and the church and the image stood in that place for seven years.

And that church burned from a candle, but the wonderworking icon was found unharmed in the ashes; and they set up another church.
