Grand Prince Roman Glebovich with his brothers, Prince Igor, Prince Vladimir, and Prince Svyatoslav, sent to Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, with submission and humility, returned all his retinue to him with honors, gave him many gifts, and peace and concord were established between them.

In the same year Grand Prince David Rostislavich of Smolensk set out from Smolensk, and his son Prince Mstislav of Novgorod with the Novgorodians from Novgorod, and Prince Vasilko Vladimirovich from Logozhsk, and Prince Vseslav from Drutsk against Polotsk. Hearing of this, the people of Polotsk were frightened and said: “We cannot resist the Novgorodians and Smolenskians, and if we let them into our land, and if we make peace with them, those coming against us will do us much harm and lay waste our land; let us then go out to them at the border and make peace with them there.”

And having gathered, they all went out to meet them, met them at the border with bows and honors, gave them many gifts, made peace, and each departed to his own side.

In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, although he seemed to have made peace, nevertheless again began to feud with the Ryazan princes: Roman Glebovich and Igor Glebovich, Vladimir Glebovich, Svyatoslav, and Rostislav. In the same year there were heavy rains all summer.

In the year 6695 from Adam (1195 from Christ)

A son, Boris, was born to Grand Prince Vsevolod of Vladimir, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, on the second day of May.

In the same year Bishop Porphyrius of Chernigov came to Vladimir to Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, wishing to reconcile him with the Ryazan princes, the Gleboviches: Roman, Igor, Vladimir, and Svyatoslav, and with Rostislav; and he labored much for humility and love, wishing to unite them, because Ryazan at that time belonged to the Chernigov diocese.

And he served in the Monastery of the Ascension of Christ on the feast day, and took as his helper Luke, Bishop of Vladimir, and they began to ask Vsevolod Yuryevich for peace with the Ryazan princes; he listened to them and sent Porphyrius, Bishop of Chernigov, in peace to Ryazan, and sent with him his own envoys to the Ryazan princes, and released all the Ryazan men whom he had taken captive.
