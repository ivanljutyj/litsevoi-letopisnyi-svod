They overcame all their German force. And they captured their German cities; only three cities did not surrender to the king and Vytautas.

And that autumn there were three battles between the Poles, the Lithuanians, and the Germans. In all three battles the Germans were defeated, but many Christians, Lithuanians, and Poles were also killed by the Germans. And they stood before Marienburg for 8 weeks, took two suburbs near it, but did not take the third—the upper city. And they remained in the German land for another eleven and a half weeks.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich ordered the town of Plyos to be built.

In the same year Chariton, Archimandrite of Kiev, built the stone church of the Three Hierarchs on Krasny Island.

In the same year the accursed Horde prince Edigei conquered the Pomor cities and caused much evil there.

In the same year in Novgorod Ioann, Vladyka of Novgorod, built a stone chapel for blessing the water every month, and a stone bakery.

In the same year the Pskovians concluded peace with Vytautas Keistutovich.

In the year 6919 from Adam (the year 1419 from Christ)

In the month of September the Tatars came on a raid against Ryazan. The Ryazanians set off in pursuit of them, defeated them, and took back the captives.

That same autumn, in the month of October, on St Demetrius’s Day, the water rose in the rivers, and a flood began everywhere. That same autumn, on the 30th day of the month of November, in Novgorod a miracle occurred in the church of the holy Archangel Michael on Skovorodka: for two days and two nights a rumble was heard in the dome.

That same winter in Novgorod there was a sign in the church of the holy martyr George at the end of Lubyanitsa: from the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God there flowed something like blood.

That same winter, in the month of January, on the commemoration of St John the Hut-dweller, a battle took place at Lyskove between the princes Peter Dmitrievich of Moscow, Rostov, and Yaroslavl, the prince of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod Daniil Borisovich and his brother Prince Ivan, and the Bulgar and Zhukotin princes. It was a cruel battle; then Prince Daniil Vasilievich was killed, and many warriors were killed on both sides. The princes of Nizhny Novgorod and the Kazan princes stood on the bones. That same winter Tsar Temir began to rule in the Horde, and Prince Edigei fled.
