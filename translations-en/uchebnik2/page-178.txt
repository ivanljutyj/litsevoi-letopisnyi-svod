Rus (1213–1224 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 4

From another chronicler. On the genealogy of the Serbian despots

It is necessary to tell also of the Serbian despots. Let us begin the account as if we have dug around the root with a spade, and show the tree that has grown from this root, and show the sweetness of its fruit to all. The great despot Nemanja, who is also Saint Simeon, from whom they trace their descent: Belourosh begot Tehomil,

Tehomil begot the blessed and great despot Nemanja, who ruled in the time of the Greek emperor Alexios the Schemamonk; he planted Orthodoxy in his land and drove out heresies. His spouse Anna was like him in all virtues, and always had compassion on priests, monks, and wanderers; she bore him three sons: Stefan, the first-crowned king, Volkan, great prince of all Zeta and the land of Hum, and Rastko, whom she loved more than all the others.

Monks came to the despot from the Holy Mountain, and among them was one Serbian monk. Rastko conversed with him, and having learned of the life of the holy fathers who lived there, he was kindled with divine desire.

And having asked his father’s leave to go hunting, he hid from all the boyars and fled together with this monk to the Holy Mountain.
