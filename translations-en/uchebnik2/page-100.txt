For, O son, having such a shepherd, do not ask for another beyond him, but consider him truly a great hierarch, and father, and teacher, and shepherd, and receive him again with love and honor into your land; let him pasture the divine flock, and not wander in foreign countries; and your nobility will not remain in that sin, but let him govern his church, according to the grace given to him from the Holy Spirit, for this is pleasing to God and people. If your nobility wishes to live in the city you have created, and if the bishop also wishes to live with you in it, let this God-loving bishop be both father and teacher and shepherd to you with you, and in that there is no dishonor to him, if you are well-disposed to him in the Lord, since that city is within the power of his bishopric of Rostov and Suzdal. If, after his vindication—by which the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God vindicated him, and the most reverend Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus and his bishops who are under his authority vindicated him, and also the great and sacred and divine council and the perfect confirmation of our humility—then, if you will not be to him as is fitting in the Lord, and will not obey his teaching and instruction, but even begin to persecute him as the hierarch, father, teacher, and shepherd given to you by Christ God, obeying other unlawful teachings, let it not be unknown to you, beloved son in Christ, that even if you fill the whole world with holy churches and monasteries, and raise up many cities without number, yet persecute the bishop, the head of that church and people, then these are not churches but barns, and there will be no reward or salvation for you. It has been written to us in detail about your pious bishop, from which we know that he is persecuted and dishonored for no other reason but only for the sake of a certain Theodore, nephew of Bishop Manuel, deprived of the church in which he was installed; he came there and taught against the bishop, contrary to the commands of the holy apostles and holy fathers, to eat meat on Wednesday and Friday if a feast of the Lord or the memory of a revered saint should occur, also throughout the whole holy Pentecost on Wednesdays and Fridays, and in many other matters he acted and taught foolishly; also he was indignant at and reproached those living purely in an unmarried life, and those who are monastics for the Lord’s sake, and those who teach philosophy in the Lord. Truly, he has completely fallen away from sense and destroyed his reason; for he did not understand or recognize true philosophy and salvation in the Lord.
