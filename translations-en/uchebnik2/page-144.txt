He began to conquer along the Volga the towns, districts, and villages belonging to the Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod Yuryevich. So he conquered them without suspecting anything, but the Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod Yuryevich secretly rose against him, having gathered a great army, the Ryazanians and Muromians; and Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich with his Novotorzhians shut himself up in the city.

They, coming up, besieged the city; there was a fierce battle and a cruel slaughter; they stood about the city for ten weeks, and the people in the city began to faint from hunger, and grief and sorrow came upon the city. Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich walked about the city and along the walls and stationed his troops against the enemies, but he was suddenly wounded by the enemies, and great grief and sorrow seized the city.

And so, remaining in grief and sorrow, the Novotorzhians surrendered to the Grand Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod Yuryevich; he, having taken the city, captured Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich and put him in fetters, and led all the Novotorzhian boyars, commanders, nobles, and merchants, with their wives and children, away with him to Vladimir.

In the same year the Novgorodians drove out from Novgorod their prince Vladimir Svyatoslavich; he went to his father Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich in Chernigov.

In the same year the Novgorodians sent to the Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich in Vladimir, asking him for a prince for themselves; he gave them his kinsman Prince Ivan, called Yaroslav Vladimirovich; they came with him to Novgorod and installed him to reign in Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians built the Church of St James on Dobrynina Street and the Church of Saints Cosmas and Damian, and of St George.

In the year 6690 from Adam (the year 1190 from Christ)

The pious Grand Princess, wife of Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, called Olga, reposed in the holy monastic habit, named Euphrosyne, on the 4th day of the month of July, and was laid in the holy Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Vladimir.

In the year 6691 from Adam (the year 1191 from Christ)

The Novgorodians drove out from Novgorod their prince Ivan, called Yaroslav Vladimirovich, kinsman of the Prince of Vladimir Vsevolod Yuryevich; he returned to his kinsman Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich in Vladimir.
