Priest Mityai lived in great honor and glory from the Grand Prince and from all, and he began to exalt himself like a tsar, having many servants and youths in his service. And he remained in such rank and position for many years, having great glory and honor; every day he changed precious garments and shone in these costly robes to everyone’s amazement. No one dressed in such garments, and no one changed costly and bright garments every day as Priest Mityai did, and all rendered him honors as to a tsar. And what more can be said? No one lived in such honor and glory as he, since he was the Grand Prince’s confessor and managed as he wished; for many years he lived such a life, and all loved him.

Deep old age came upon the Spassky archimandrite Ioann, nicknamed Nepeitsa; he was a meek and humble man, and he humbled himself for God’s sake. In his old age he left the archimandrite’s office and withdrew to a cell for silence. And after Nepeitsa there was no similar archimandrite in the Spassky monastery, and all the monks and the monastery itself had need of this.

And then searches began for one who could become archimandrite in the Spassky monastery in Moscow after Ioann Nepeitsa. And they began to propose first one, then another; the Grand Prince proposed Mityai, since he loved him very much, as did the boyars.

Mityai learned of this and did not wish it. The Grand Prince began to speak to him with humility and compunction: “You see that Metropolitan Alexei has grown old, and soon you will become metropolitan and lord of all Rus. Now accept the monastic form and become archimandrite in the Spassky monastery and my confessor, as before, and all will obey you.”

Priest Mityai did so; the Grand Prince obtained what he desired. Soon Priest Mityai was taken for tonsure, and of his own will, but as if by compulsion, he was brought to the monastery of the Holy Savior.

And he entered the church of the Holy Savior and sent for the Chudov archimandrite Elisei, nicknamed Chechetka. At that same hour the Chudov archimandrite Elisei, nicknamed Chechetka, came and tonsured Priest Mityai into the holy angelic monastic order in the church in the monastery of the Holy Savior in Moscow. Thus before dinner he was a secular priest and was tonsured into the monastic order, and after dinner he became a superior, pastor, and teacher.

And Mityai remained archimandrite of the Spassky monastery in Moscow for two years.

After the repose of Metropolitan Alexei, he left his archimandrite office by the counsel of the Grand Prince and his boyars, entered the metropolitan’s court, and began to rule on that great throne with authority and boldness, as it befits a metropolitan to rule.
