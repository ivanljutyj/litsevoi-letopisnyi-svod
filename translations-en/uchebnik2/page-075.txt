And having come to Kiev, he was joyfully received by his father; and he stayed with him in Kiev for some time; and he was troubled by the disorders of his brothers, nephews, kinsmen, and all his clan, who are always in rebellion and turmoil and shed much blood, since all desire and want the great principality of Kiev, and no one has peace with anyone, and because of this all the principalities have become desolate, and along the Danube, and along the Meta, and along the Astri foreigners have gained power and settled, while from the Field the Polovtsians have taken captives and laid waste; and he grieved much over this, and was sick in soul and heart. And he thought in the secret of his heart, saying nothing to his father, the Grand Prince of Kiev Yuri Dolgoruky, and wished to go to the great principality in Suzdal and Rostov, for there, he said, it is calmer.

And hearing of the miracles from the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, which, they say, was painted by Luke the Evangelist, and which had been brought to his father in Kiev by ship with Pirogoshcha from Constantinople, and hearing of it that it of itself comes out of its shrine and stands in the air, and this happened many times, he wished to take this icon secretly from his father with him as he went to the great principality in Suzdal and Rostov. And he went from Kiev secretly, without informing his father, and took that icon of the Most Pure Mother of God with him, and set into it more than thirty grivnas of gold, besides silver, and precious stones, and pearls; and having adorned it, he placed it in his church in Vladimir.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev Yuri Dolgoruky married off his son Prince Gleb in Rus, taking for him as wife the daughter of the Grand Prince of Chernigov Izyaslav Davydovich, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, and great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Yuryevich, son of Dolgoruky, married in Novgorod the daughter of Pyotr Mikhalkovich. In the same year the tysyatsky Andrei Glebov was killed, and he was killed in Belgorod of Ryazan at night by his own kinsmen. In the same year there were great rains.

In the same year the Tatars came to Ryazan against Khaport and did much evil: some they killed, others they led away captive.

The Greek Empire

After Manuel, his son Alexios Porphyrogennetos reigned six months, Orthodox; and after him Andronikos Komnenos reigned 2 years and 6 months, Orthodox; and after him Isaac, called Angelos, 8 years and 6 months, Orthodox; and after him Alexios Skimnin, brother of Isaac Angelos, reigned 9 years and 6 months, Orthodox; after Alexios Skimnin reigned Alexios Doukas Mourtzouphlos 2 months and ten days, Orthodox; and after him reigned Alexios Angelos, son of Isaac, 7 months and 20 days, Orthodox.
