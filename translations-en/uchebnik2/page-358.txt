In the same year, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich gathered all the force of the Lowlands and of Suzdal, intending to go with an army to Kashin. But Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver came out against him with his forces to the Volga, and there the former bishop Andrew established peace between them, and they went their separate ways.

In the same year, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich took the tribute silver from the Tver princes according to the agreement, and did not go to meet the tsar’s envoy, but went with the silver to Novgorod the Great. In the same year, to Prince Alexander Mikhailovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, a son was born named Lev.

In the same year, Prince Davyd Fedorovich of Yaroslavl died, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-grandson of Davyd, great-great-great-grandson of Rostislav, forefather of Mstislav, great-great-forefather of Vladimir Monomakh.

In the same year, while Prince Yuri was in Novgorod, the Novgorodians had a small quarrel with him.

In the year 6830 from Adam (1330 from Christ)

Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Vladimir, Moscow, and Novgorod made peace with the Novgorodians.

In the same year, the Germans came, and Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Vladimir and Novgorod went out against them with the Novgorodians. And so some of the Germans were slain, and others fled.

In the same year, Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, went to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak and received great honor from the tsar and from the princes; Tsar Azbyak granted him the grand principality of Vladimir in place of Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich. And before this, the same Tsar Azbyak had given the grand principality of Vladimir to Yuri Danilovich of Moscow in place of Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver.

In the same year, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich went with an army and with the Novgorodians to Vyborg.

In the same year, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, entreated the Novgorodians to escort him to the Horde, and he went from Novgorod to the Lower Lands.

And when he was on the Udroma, Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver came against him there. He fled to Pskov, and all his treasury was taken. And in Pskov at that time was the Lithuanian prince Davyd.

And from there the Novgorodians summoned him to themselves in Novgorod by oath, since they were then oppressed by the Germans, for the Germans were then warring against the Novgorodian lands; and they began to prepare siege engines, intending to go with Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich against the Germans.
