But with you we do not wish to argue about the faith. And if there is any grievance between you and us, we will send to you about it, and you send to us. You set forth your grievances to us, and we will examine the matter; we will set forth ours to you, and you will examine it. And let us live in peace, love, and concord. But let us not argue about the faith, stir up enmity among ourselves, or reproach one another.” And after this the king ceased to hold forth about the faith, and their proud philosophers fell silent at these short but wise words of the ruler.

In the same year the Germans came in arms against Lithuania, and there was a fierce battle, and the Germans killed forty thousand Lithuanians.

In the same year there was a great flood, such as had never been. In the same year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, married for the third time, taking as his wife Princess Maria, daughter of Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver.

And Andrei Kobyla and Aleksei Bosovolkov rode to Tver for her.

In the same year His Grace Theognostus, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, having consulted on some spiritual matter with his son Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, sent to Constantinople to the patriarch to ask for a blessing.

In the same year His Grace Theognostus, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, appointed Nathanael bishop of Suzdal. In the same year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, went to the Horde.

On the Martyr Kruglets

In those times there lived in the land of Lithuania a certain man named Kruglets, called Eustathius in holy baptism. He was killed by Grand Prince Olgerd for the Orthodox faith and was buried at the Church of Saint Nicholas in the city of Vilna together with his kinsmen, the great martyrs Anthony and John, who suffered for the Orthodox Christian faith and received the crown of martyrdom from the hands of the Lord.

In the year 6856 from Adam (1356 from Christ)

A son, Daniel, was born to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich. In the same year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, came from the Horde from Tsar Jani Beg, son of Uzbek, with great honor and a grant of favor, and with him his brother Andrei Ivanovich. In the same year in Moscow, in the cathedral metropolitan church in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God, by the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God, and through the prayers of Saint Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, at his tomb a woman received healing.
