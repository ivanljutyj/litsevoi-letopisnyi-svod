In the same year Prince Boris Konstantinovich went to the Horde. And at that time Tsar Tokhtamysh set out to war against Temir-Kutluy. Prince Boris overtook Tsar Tokhtamysh already on the way and marched with him for 30 days.

But afterward Tsar Tokhtamysh, sparing him, released him at a place called Eruktana, ordering him to return without him and to await his return in Sarai.

And he himself went to war into the land of Temir-Kutluy and conquered his far-off cities. And, without reaching Temir-Kutluy himself, he returned to his ulus.

In the same year Grand Prince Semen Olgerdovich Lugveny came to Novgorod to the suburbs.

The Reign of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.
The year 6897 from Adam (the year 1397 from Christ).

In the same year, on the 15th day of the month of August, on the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos, Prince Vasily Dmitrievich sat upon the great reign of Vladimir, upon the throne of the kingdom of his father, grandfather, and great-grandfather. And he was installed by the envoy of Tokhtamysh, Shiakhmat.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich quarreled with his uncle, Prince Vladimir Andreevich. Then Prince Vladimir Andreevich went with his son, Prince Ivan, and with his senior boyars to his city of Serpukhov, and from Serpukhov he went to Torzhok, and many people went with him, and he remained for several days in Terebenskoye until they were reconciled. In the same autumn, on the 11th day of the month of September, on the commemoration of the Venerable Theodora of Alexandria, Pimen, Metropolitan of Russia, reposed in Constantinople, and was buried there, and his property was divided up.

In the same winter, on the 11th day of the month of December, Princess Marya reposed, the wife of Prince Andrei Ivanovich, the mother of Prince Vladimir Andreevich, named Martha in monasticism. And she was laid in the church of the Holy Nativity of the Most Pure Theotokos in her honorable convent, which is on the moat. In her lifetime she herself had founded it, and in it she was buried. In the same winter, after the Epiphany, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich agreed upon peace and friendship with his uncle, Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and added Volok and Rzhev to his domains.

In the same winter, on the 26th day of the month of January, a son, Prince Fyodor, was born to Prince Vladimir Andreevich.
