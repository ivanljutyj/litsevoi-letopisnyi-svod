In the year 6755 from Adam (1255 from Christ)

Prince Alexander learned in Novgorod of the death of his father, Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, and came from Novgorod to Vladimir, and mourned his father with his uncle Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and with his brothers.

In the same year, after the death of his brother Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich became Grand Prince in Vladimir.

And to his nephews—Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, and Prince Andrew, and Constantine, and Afanasy, and Daniel, and Michael, and Yaroslav, and Vasily—Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich gave them to reign in other cities, as his brother Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich had arranged; and he did not alter his testament.

In the same year, Prince Andrew Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, went to the Horde to Tsar Batu; and Batu received Prince Andrew with honor.

How Alexander Went to the Horde, and Batu Marveled at Him, and Rendered Him Great Honor

In the same year, the impious Tsar Batu learned of the God-protected Grand Prince Alexander, of his noble courage, his invincible bravery, and his many glorious victories over all his adversaries. And Batu sent his envoys to Prince Alexander with these words: “O Prince Alexander, most renowned among the Russian rulers, I know that you know that God has subdued many peoples to me, and all submit to my power; and of all, you alone do not wish to submit to my strength. See, then, if you think to preserve your land unharmed, strive to come to me immediately, and you will see the honor and glory of my kingdom, and you will gain benefit for yourself and your land.” But the God-wise Grand Prince Alexander reasoned as his holy father Yaroslav had, who did not care for an earthly kingdom but went to the Horde and there gave his life for piety and for all his people, and for this received the Heavenly Kingdom. And so blessed Alexander, imitating the good zeal of his pious father, decided to go to the Horde for the salvation of Christians.
