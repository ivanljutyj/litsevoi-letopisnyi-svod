And the lime had not yet dried when a multitude of people gathered to admire their beauty, and suddenly the gates collapsed, and twelve people were buried under them by stones.

Grand Prince Andrew fell with tears before the miraculous image of the Mother of God, considering himself guilty of this loss of people. And when the stones were cleared, all were found alive, having suffered no harm at all, through the mercy of the Most Pure Mother of God.

Miracle of the Victory over the Bulgars

In days of old Grand Prince Andrew lived in peace and love with the Greek Tsar Manuel.

It happened once that they set out to war on the same day: Manuel against the Saracens, Andrew against the Bulgars. And Andrew took with him the Cross of Christ and the miraculous icon of the Most Pure Mother of God of Vladimir, as he usually did, considering her a helper against enemies.

Through her prayers the Bulgars were defeated: four cities were captured, the city of Bryakhimov on the Kama was taken, while the Russian host remained unharmed.

And all saw divine fiery rays issuing from the icon of the Savior Jesus Christ and His Most Pure Mother, which covered the whole army of the Grand Prince. And all understood that this was God’s help, and returned. And other Bulgarian cities they gave to fire, but some they spared in order to impose tribute on them.

On the Appearance of the Vladimir Church of the Mother of God in the Air

Tsar Manuel saw this vision at the very same time when fiery rays shone forth from the Lord’s icon, and thereafter a feast was established in glorification of the all-merciful Savior and His Most Pure Mother, on the first day of the month of August. The other miracles wrought by that icon of the Mother of God will be recounted elsewhere; here only this.

After the death of Grand Prince Andrew, his brother Grand Prince Vsevolod Dmitry Georgievich enlarged that cathedral church in Vladimir, adding four cupolas, and gilded it.

And for this Autocrat Vsevolod received such grace. Once he was beyond Suzdal with his army and saw the great church of the Holy Mother of God, in which was that miraculous icon of the Mother of God, and together with it they saw the whole church and the whole city of Vladimir standing in the air. The Autocrat and all who were with him rejoiced and glorified God and the Most Pure Mother of God.
