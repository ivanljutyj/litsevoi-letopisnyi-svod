In that same winter, in the month of January, Tsar Shadibek killed Tsar Tokhtamysh in the Siberian land.

In that same winter, during Philip’s Fast, Prince Yuri Svyatoslavich of Smolensk went from Novgorod to Moscow.

And Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich made him governor in Torzhok, and there he innocently killed his serving prince Semyon Mstislavich Vyazemsky and his princess Iuliania, because, seized by carnal desire for his wife, he took her into his house, wishing to cohabit with her. The princess, not wishing this, said: “O prince, what do you think—can I leave my living husband and go to you?” He wished to lie with her; she, resisting him, seized a knife and struck him in the muscle. He was enraged and soon killed her husband, Prince Semyon Mstislavich Vyazemsky, who served him, had shed blood for him, and was guilty of nothing before him, since it was not he who taught his wife to act thus toward the prince.

And he ordered the princess’s hands and feet cut off and her thrown into the water. The servants did what was ordered them, threw her into the water; this became a sin and a great shame for Prince Yuri. Not wishing to endure his misfortune, shame, and dishonor, he fled to the Horde.

In that same winter the Pskovians went with an army to Polotsk and, having conquered lands, returned.

In that same winter the Novgorodians invited Prince Daniil and his brother Prince Yuri to themselves and gave them the town of Porkhov.

In that same winter the Germans defeated the Pskovians.

In that same winter the Pskovians came to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich to ask help against the Germans. He sent to them his brother Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich, who stayed in Pskov a little less than a year.

In that same winter, in the month of March, the former Bishop of Suzdal, Evfrosin, reposed.

In that same spring Grand Princess Evdokia, wife of Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy, founded in Moscow the stone Church of the Holy Ascension.

In that same spring, on the 20th day of the month of May, the Lithuanians seized Odoev and burned it.

In that same year, during St Peter’s Fast, a bell tower was founded in Tver near the old bell tower by Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver. In that same year, during St Peter’s Fast, Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich of Tver went from Moscow to the Horde, wishing to obtain the grand principality of Tver in place of Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver.

In that same year the Muscovites fought with the Lithuanians.
