In the same year Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, son of the Grand Prince and grandson of Ivan, of Moscow, seeing himself detained in the Horde, thought it impossible to flee directly to Rus. And after taking firm counsel with his loyal well-wishers, he fled to the Podolian land, to Great Wallachia, to Voivode Peter.

And from there he went secretly, as an unknown man, and when he came to the German land, Prince Vytautas Kestutievich recognized him and detained him with him.

For at that time Vytautas was in the German land, whither he had fled after the murder of his father Kestutis. Vytautas had an only daughter, and he wished to give her in marriage to Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and said to him: “I will let you go to your father in your land if you take my daughter for yourself, my only child.” He promised to do so, and then Vytautas Kestutievich began to keep him in great honor until he released him to his father in Moscow.

In the same year Prince Skirgaila Olgerdovich took Polotsk, and took his brother Prince Andrei Olgerdovich captive, and killed his son.

In the year 6895 from Adam (1395 from Christ)

In the month of September, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, after the venerable Abbot Sergius had made peace and love between him and Grand Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, had great love with him; and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich gave his daughter Princess Sophia to Ryazan for Prince Feodor Olegovich of Ryazan.

That same autumn there were great frosts, and rivers and lakes froze, and snow fell; also at midnight, on the 26th day of October, in Novgorod the Great a great wind arose and carried ice from the lake into the Volkhov River, and swept away nine supports from the Great Bridge.

That same autumn Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sent his eldest boyars to meet his son Prince Vasily in the Wallachian land.

That same winter Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver conceived a strong enmity toward Bishop Evfimy of Tver, and the Grand Prince would not have him, and Bishop Evfimy withdrew to the Monastery of St Nicholas over Ruchei in the month of January.

That same winter, on the 19th day of January, his son Prince Vasily came from Lithuania to his father Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich in Moscow, and with him came Lithuanian princes, lords, and Poles, and the senior boyars of the Grand Prince who had gone to meet him.

That same winter Prince Rodoslav, son of Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, fled from the Horde. That same winter, in the month of January, a daughter, Anna, was born to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.
