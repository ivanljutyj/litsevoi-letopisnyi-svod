In the same year Bishop Antony laid the foundation of the stone church of the Holy Martyr Barbara in the monastery in Novgorod.

In the same year Archbishop Mitrofan came from Vladimir to Novgorod, and the Novgorodians escorted him to the Church of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Mother of God.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavovich set out from Novgorod for Rus; and the Novgorodians sent to Smolensk for Prince Svyatoslav Mstislavovich, grandson of Roman, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of Vladimir the Great.

In the same year the Bulgars took Ustyug by cunning.

Then the Bulgars went to the city of Unzha, and the inhabitants of the city fought them off.

In the same year the Ryazan princes Gleb and Konstantin, sons of Vladimir, grandsons of Gleb, first slandered their uncles Prince Roman Glebovich and Prince Svyatoslav Glebovich, and their brothers and nephews; and they shed much blood and killed many; and they laid waste all their Ryazan land; they also killed their brother, their nephews, and their uncles; and they fled to the Cumans. And now these accursed princes Gleb and Konstantin came with a multitude of Cumans to Ryazan, and began to ravage, take captive, burn, and cut down.

Their nephew Prince Ingvar Igorevich, having united with his brothers, went against them, and defeated them, and killed many Cumans.

And the princes Gleb and Konstantin, sons of Vladimir Glebovich, fled to the Cumans, and Prince Ingvar returned to Ryazan.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavovich, grandson of Roman, and Prince Vladimir Rurikovich, grandson of Rostislav, went to Galich against the king's son; he came out against Mstislav with the Galicians, the Hungarians, the Poles, the Czechs, and the Moravians.

And they began to fight, and Mstislav prevailed, and captured the king's son, his wife, and his son.

And after this Mstislav made peace with the king, and released his son, together with his wife and his people, and himself began to rule in Galich.

In the same year a son, Feodor, was born to Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich.

In the same year the Hungarians drove Prince Mstislav Mstislavovich, grandson of Roman, out of Galich, and the king's son again began to rule in it.

In the same year Prince Ingvar of Ryazan sent envoys to Grand Prince Georgy and to his brother Yaroslav, asking them to help him in his feud with the Cumans.
