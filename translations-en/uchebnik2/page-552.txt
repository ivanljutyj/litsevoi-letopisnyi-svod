Of the Lord, when the high priest’s maidservant asked him, saying: “Were you also with Jesus of Galilee?” Peter denied before all, saying: “I do not know and do not understand what you are saying.” And when Peter went out to the gate, another maidservant saw him and said to him there: “This one also was with Jesus of Nazareth.” Peter again denied with an oath: “I do not know that man.” After a little while, those standing there came up and said to Peter: “Truly you also are one of them, for your speech clearly betrays you.” Then Peter began to adjure and swear: “I do not know that man.” And immediately the rooster crowed, and Peter remembered the words of Jesus spoken to him: “Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times”; and going out, he wept bitterly.

A little farther from there, on Mount Zion, was the courtyard of Caiaphas the high priest. Here there is now a very great Armenian monastery. And on the road to Bethlehem a pillar still stands, where Simeon the Stylite stood with Saint Elijah. And if one goes down to the Podol, in the city of Jerusalem there was a Greek church, and now it is a Saracen mosque. There lie the holy God-bearing Joachim and Anna. And opposite stood the Holy of Holies, which Solomon built. There the prophet Zechariah, father of John the Forerunner, was killed. There is the Lord’s cell. And going further down the street, there was Pilate’s courtyard.

And beyond the Holy of Holies, in the monastery of the Holy Savior, are Solomon’s bed and Solomon’s house; there too is the church into which our Lord Jesus Christ entered. And finding in that church merchants selling sheep, oxen, and doves, and where money-changers sat, and making a whip of cords, he drove all the sheep and oxen out of the church, scattered the merchants’ money, overturned the tables, and said to the dove-sellers: “Take these away from everywhere, and do not make My Father’s house a house of merchandise.” There too is the house of David, which, like the whole city, was made of stone; around it is a wonder of wonders! In it, on one side, magpies live, but they dare not fly into the larger houses. There is a very high pillar where David composed the Psalter.

Not far from David’s house is Mount Zion. On that mountain is a wondrous Frankish monastery. The Franks own it, and Frankish monks live in it. They say that Christ Himself served Mass there and taught His brother according to the flesh, James, to serve Mass, and handed over to him the mystery of the sacred and divine services. There is the upper room where the Holy Spirit descended upon Christ’s holy apostles on the fiftieth day. There, on the left side of the church, is the place where the Lord washed the feet of His disciples. There too is the house where the Lord passed through the doors and convinced His disciple Thomas, the unbelieving, of His resurrection. In this church is the veil that was rent in two at the time of Christ’s crucifixion. In this church lies the very stone on which the Most Pure Mother of God made her prostrations. There, in the same church, are two stones on which Christ often sat.
