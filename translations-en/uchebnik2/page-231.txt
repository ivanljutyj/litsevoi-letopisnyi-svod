In the year 6728 from Adam (the year 1228 from Christ)

Mitrofan became Archbishop of Novgorod by the will of God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and of the Most Reverend Matfei, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus; and Bishop Mitrofan came to Novgorod on the seventh day of March. And Metropolitan Matfei kept Anthony, Archbishop of Novgorod, with him in Kiev and held him in honor, and later appointed him bishop in Peremyshl; and Anthony came to Peremyshl on the second day of June.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich came from Smolensk to Torzhok, and then came to Novgorod the Great.

And the cunning Satan put hatred into some Novgorod people against the Novgorod posadnik Tverdislav; and they slandered him before Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich, grandson of Roman; and Posadnik Tverdislav was then ill.

And Prince Vsevolod went from Gorodishche with all his men, in armor, with weapons, as if for battle, and rode out to Yaroslav’s Court; and the Novgorodians gathered to him with weapons, as if for battle.

And they brought the sick Posadnik Tverdislav out in a sledge to the Church of Saints Boris and Gleb; and the Novgorodians gathered under his command, the Prussians, and the Chudin End, and people from the towns; and many of them stood around him as an army in armor, with all manner of weapons; and they formed five regiments; and a great cry and murmur arose. Prince Vsevolod, seeing this, understood that they stood firmly for their posadnik Tverdislav, and did not ride there, but sent people to them with a humble request that they kiss the cross with Tverdislav; and Prince Vsevolod remained to reign over the Novgorodians with respect for their liberties.

In the same year Tverdislav ceased to be posadnik because he was ill, and at night, secretly from his wife and children, he went to the Most Pure Mother of God at the Arkadiev Monastery and became a monk. In the same year Ivanko Dmitriev was made posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6729 from Adam (the year 1229 from Christ)

The Most Reverend Matfei, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, reposed. In the same year the Novgorodians drove Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich out of Novgorod, grandson of Roman, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh.

In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavich, grandson of Roman, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, fought with the Hungarians, and killed many of them, and took the king’s son captive, and began to reign himself in Galich.
