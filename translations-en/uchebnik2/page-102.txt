Let us ask you: how does this choir differ from the angels, who on earth sing and say: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will among men? And their clothing is worthy of manliness, for they are not adorned with trailing, worn, and torn robes, but after the example of those divine angels—Elijah, Elisha, and John—and the apostles, the garments of some are made of goatskins, of others of camel hair, while others were content with skins alone; and these in ancient times sang songs in concord, and bent their knees, and entreat the all-hymned God for things that some do not even conceive, and they ask nothing in the present except forgiveness of sins, and to stand with boldness before the dread judgment seat when the only-begotten Son of God comes to judge the living and the dead, and that not one should hear that terrible voice: “I know you not; depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels”; and that with a pure conscience and many arduous deeds they may complete a harsh and mournful life in this vain and deceitful age, and cross the fierce deep by a good voyage; for these, as having pitched their tents in heaven, living far from evil, wage war against the devil, and as if exulting thus defeat him. Why do we not willingly come to such blessedness? Shall we not come to these angels, shall we not dwell together with these angels, shall we not receive the pure garments, being renewed by this marriage? Do we not consider that we are called to a wedding, to God’s wedding? Do we not heed that the invited soul must enter these chambers by good deeds and the brightness of life? Let us go then to these tabernacles, that we may learn their work, for their dwelling is in no way worse than the heavenly one; for angels come down to them, and the Lord of angels Himself. For if they came to Abraham, a man who had a wife and fed children, because they knew him to be hospitable; when they find a much greater virtue, and a man freed from the body, and in the flesh despising the flesh, they abide here much more and rejoice with the rejoicing proper to them. In this blessed Chrysostom and the other saints agree, that the life of those who are unmarried and live purely amazes all; for virginity is angelic life, and unites with angels those who love it. Therefore we instruct and admonish your nobility and piety: guard yourselves against false prophets, who come in sheep’s clothing, and do not believe every spirit, but be subject to your elders and obey, for they care for your souls, since they wish to give an account on the day of judgment.
