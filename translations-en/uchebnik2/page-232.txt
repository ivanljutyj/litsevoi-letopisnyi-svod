In the same year the Novgorodians sent their archbishop Mytrofan, and posadnik Ivanko, and noble men to Vladimir to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, asking him to give them a son to reign among them in Novgorod; and he gave them his son Vsevolod to reign according to their ancient Novgorod will.

In the same year the Novgorodians went to war with their prince Vsevolod Yuryevich against the city of Kesi; and they fought much, but did not take the city.

In the same year the city of Yaroslavl burned almost entirely, and 17 churches burned; but the prince’s court, by God’s mercy and through the prayers of blessed Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich, was saved from the fire.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich founded a city at the mouth of the Oka River and named it Nizhny Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians wished to expel their prince Vsevolod Yuryevich; and Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich learned of this and secretly from the Novgorodians fled from Novgorod by night with all his people.

The Novgorodians were grieved; and their noble men went to Vladimir to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with these words: “If you do not wish to rule Novgorod with your son, then give us your brother Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich.”

In the year 6730 from Adam (1230 from Christ)

Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich laid the foundation of a stone church of the Most Holy Mother of God in Suzdal; on the former site he destroyed the old building, since it had already begun to fall apart from age, and its roof had collapsed; for it had been built under his great-grandfather Grand Prince Vladimir Monomakh and blessed Bishop Ephrem.

In the same year the Novgorodians took Yaroslav Vsevolodovich of Pereyaslavl, brother of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, to reign among them in Novgorod according to their ancient Novgorod will.

In the year 6731 from Adam (1231 from Christ)

There was a great drought, and many forests, pine woods, and marshes burned; and the smoke was then so thick that people could not see; it was as if darkness had descended upon the earth; and birds could not fly in the air, not seeing, and fell to the earth and died; and all kinds of wild beasts came into towns and villages to people, not seeing; and fear and terror fell upon all.

In the same year a star appeared in the west; and its rays were not toward people’s eyes, but toward the south; it rose in the evening after sunset; and it was larger than all the other stars; and this continued for 7 days; and after the seventh day those rays appeared toward the east.
