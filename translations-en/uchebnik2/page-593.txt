And the lime had not yet dried when a multitude of people gathered to admire their beauty, when suddenly the gates fell, and twelve people were crushed beneath them by stones.

Grand Prince Andrew, weeping, fell before the wonder-working image of the Mother of God, considering himself guilty of the death of these people. And when the stones were cleared away, all were found alive, having suffered no harm at all, through the mercy of the Most Pure Mother of God.

The Miracle of the Victory over the Bulgars

In ancient times Grand Prince Andrew lived in peace and love with the Greek Emperor Manuel.

It happened once that they went to war on the same day: Manuel against the Saracens, Andrew against the Bulgars. And Andrew took with him the cross of Christ and the wonder-working icon of the Most Pure Mother of God of Vladimir, as he usually did, counting her as a helper against enemies.

Through her prayers the Bulgars were defeated: four cities were captured, the city of Bryakhimov on the Kama was taken, and the Russian host remained unharmed.

And all saw how from the icon of Jesus Christ the Saviour and His Most Pure Mother divine fiery rays came forth, which covered the whole host of the Grand Prince. And all understood that this was God’s help, and they returned. And other Bulgar cities they gave to fire. But some they left, to impose tribute upon them.

On the Appearance of the Vladimir Church of the Mother of God in the Air

Emperor Manuel saw this vision at the very same time when fiery rays shone from the Sovereign icon, and after this a feast was established in glorification of the all-merciful Saviour and His Most Pure Mother on the first day of the month of August. Concerning the other miracles which came from that icon of the Mother of God, it will be told elsewhere; here only this.

After the death of Grand Prince Andrew, his brother Grand Prince Vsevolod Dmitry Georgievich enlarged that cathedral church of Vladimir, completing four domes and gilding them.

And for this Autocrat Vsevolod received such grace. He was once with his host beyond Suzdal and saw the great church of the Holy Mother of God, in which was that wonder-working icon of the Mother of God, and together with it they saw the whole church and the whole city of Vladimir standing in the air. The Autocrat and all who were with him rejoiced and glorified God and the Most Pure Mother of God.
