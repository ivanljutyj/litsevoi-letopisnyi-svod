He put on himself the metropolitan’s mantle with its springs, a golden cross, and a paramand with beads and precious stones, a golden staff, and a white klobuk. And he ordered tribute to be collected as a metropolitan, and he administered justice and governed as a metropolitan. Even before this he had been elected archimandrite by lay election, and afterward he was elected metropolitan also by lay election. Metropolitan Alexei in no way wished to appoint him archimandrite. Mityay thought that it was Sergius who had advised Metropolitan Alexei not to bless him as metropolitan after himself, and he harbored anger in his soul against the venerable abbot Sergius and boasted that he would take revenge on him.

Hearing of this, the venerable abbot Sergius prayed to the Lord God with tears and a contrite heart and told all his brethren that Michael, nicknamed Mityay, had boasted that he would lay waste this holy place and drive out all without cause. But he will not receive the rank of metropolitan, because he is unworthy of it, and he will not see Tsargrad.

And so it happened. When he set out for Tsargrad, he fell ill on the way on the ship, and before reaching Tsargrad he died, just as the venerable Sergius had prophesied. And after this all who heard the prophecy regarded blessed Sergius as a great and wondrous prophet.

On the Founding of the Monastery on the Dubenka

After some time Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich came to the venerable Sergius at the monastery and said to him: “You know, father, great sorrow and grief have seized me, and there will be sorrow for all Christendom. I have heard that Mamai, prince of the Horde, has raised the whole Horde of godless Tatars, and they are coming against my patrimony, the Russian land, wishing to lay waste its holy churches and destroy our Christendom. Holy father, pray to God for our Christendom, that God may deliver us from this calamity.”

He replied: “My lord prince, it is fitting for you to care for your flock and to stand firm, to lay down your soul and shed your blood for the image of Christ, as Christ shed His blood for us and laid down His soul. So you too, my lord, must first go against them with truth and righteousness, with humility, as it has long been customary to submit to the Horde tsar. Behold, Grand Prince Vasily wished to appease the fury of the impious Tsar Ulyan with gifts and humility. And God, seeing Vasily’s humility, cast down the impious Tsar Ulyan, delivering him to death.
