But the other churches in the city and outside the city walls, and the monasteries, they plundered; we cannot name their number or describe their beauty.

And they killed and robbed a multitude of people; the Greeks and Varangians who remained they drove out of the city. Here are the names of the Italian commanders: 1. Markos from Rome, in the city of Berne, where the evil pagan Derdik lived; 2. Kondof of Flanders; 3. the Blind Doge from the island of St Mark, a Venetian.

This doge was blinded by Emperor Manuel; many philosophers begged the emperor, saying that if you release this doge in good health, he will do much evil to your realm; the emperor, not wishing to kill him, ordered his eyes to be blinded with glass, and although his eyes were not damaged, he saw nothing.

This doge devised many troubles against Constantinople, and all obeyed him; he possessed great ships, with whose help Constantinople was taken. The Latins stood before Constantinople from December until April, until they took the city.

On the 9th day of the month of May, by the authority of their bishops, they raised their Latin emperor Kondof of Flanders to the imperial throne and divided the power among themselves: to the emperor the city, to Markos the court, and to the doge the tithe. And so the kingdom of God-protected Constantinople and the Greek land perished because of the discord of emperors, and now the Latins have come to possess it.

On the Icon of the Most Pure Mother of God Hodegetria in Constantinople

The Hodegetria is an icon of the Most Holy Mother of God; this icon was painted by the Evangelist Luke; it is carried out of the monastery every Tuesday, and it works miracles to this day, healing those who come to it with faith.

A certain old monk, who had long prayed to the Most Holy Mother of God, said: “Lady! Reveal yourself to me as you were in the flesh.” And a voice came to him: “Go to the apostolic church and you will find Me painted in the altar by the cross, such as I was in the flesh.”

Once there was iconoclasm in Constantinople, and the icon of the Most Holy Mother of God Hodegetria was taken to the Monastery of the Pantokrator and hidden in the wall of the altar; a censer was lit before it, and the iconoclasts ruled in Constantinople for 60 years.

Then Orthodoxy reigned again; they began to seek the icon of the Most Holy Mother of God, and the patriarch ordered all monasteries and recluses to pray about this.

And the Mother of God appeared to a certain recluse in a dream vision: “Seek,” he was told, “the icon of the Most Holy Mother of God in the monastery…”
