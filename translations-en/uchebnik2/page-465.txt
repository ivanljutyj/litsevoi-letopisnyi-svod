And this metropolitan monastery stands to this day in Nizhny Novgorod by the grace of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God, guarded by the prayers of the holy wonderworker Alexis. Afterwards the metropolitan came to Vladimir and there founded a cenobitic monastery on the Klyazma River; he built in it a church in the name of the holy and equal-to-the-apostles great Tsar Constantine, provided for all its needs, granted it villages, and endowed it with everything, so that even now this monastery, with all its needs, is kept by the grace of Christ and of the Most Pure Mother of God and of the holy wonderworker Alexis.

And because of the saint’s great virtue, the fame of the metropolitan spread not only throughout Christian lands but also throughout the infidel Tatar lands.

At that time Tsaritsa Taidala, wife of the Horde Tsar Chanybek, suffered from a disease of the eyes. An envoy came from the Horde from Tsaritsa Taidala in the year 6875 from Adam (1375 from Christ) to the blessed Alexis, Metropolitan of all Rus, with a request that he visit the sick woman. The tsaritsa had heard of him as a holy and wise man, that God listens to his prayers, and that if the saint asks anything of God, God grants it all to him.

He, having prepared everything necessary for the journey, served a moleben in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God, during which a candle at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter lit of itself.

After serving the moleben with the whole sacred council, he broke that candle and gave it to the people as a blessing, and took part of that candle with him; and having blessed water, he set out for the Horde to Tsaritsa Taidala.

And the tsaritsa saw him in a dream in the vestments of a priest, and all his priests in priestly vestments; and as Tsaritsa Taidala saw Saint Alexis in the dream, so she ordered hierarchical vestments to be prepared for Saint Alexis and priestly vestments for all his priests.

And when in the Horde of Tsar Chanybek they learned of the saint’s approach, at that same hour Tsar Chanybek himself, son of Azbyak, went out to meet him with his son Berdibek, with his other sons, with princes and nobles, showing the metropolitan great honor.

And there was fulfilled what is written: the lion and the lamb feed together. The blessed one celebrated the moleben, during which he ordered that candle to be lit which had lit of itself, sprinkled Tsaritsa Taidala with holy water, and at that same hour she regained her sight.
