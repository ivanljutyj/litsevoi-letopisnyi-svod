Yuri’s nephew, Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich, sent his voivode with his warriors from Rostov and Ustyug to help.

Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich also sent envoys to the Murom princes, ordering them to send their sons; and David sent his son Svyatoslav, while Yuri sent Oleg with his troops to help. And all assembled on the Volga at the mouth of the Oka in boats.

And from there they went down the Volga, and when they were at the landing places opposite the city of Oshel, they disembarked.

Prince Svyatoslav arranged his regiments: the Rostov regiment on the right, the Pereyaslavl regiment on the left, and he himself stood with the Murom princes in the middle, and he left one regiment by the boats; they themselves went from the shore to the forest, and after passing through the forest they came out onto the field by the city.

And the Bulgars met them with their prince on horseback and drew up a regiment in the field; Svyatoslav advanced against them quickly; they, after standing a little, shot an arrow each at our men, and, wavering, fled to the city, and running into the city, shut themselves in.

Prince Svyatoslav quickly approached the city. A palisade had been set around the city, a strong oak fence, and then two walls, and between them a rampart had been heaped up, and the Bulgars, running along that rampart, fought back from behind the fence.

Prince Svyatoslav, coming to the city, sent men forward with fire and axes, and behind them archers and men with spears, and attacked the city, and the battle between them was very stubborn.

And they cut down the fence, hacked through the walls, and set them on fire, and the Bulgars fled into the city; our men pursued and cut them down as far as the city.

And then the Russian troops attacked the city from all sides and set it on fire; and the smoke was very thick, and the wind blew from the city toward Svyatoslav’s regiments, and people could not be seen in the smoke; and they could not endure the smoke and heat, and most of all the lack of water, and they retreated from the city and began to rest after all that had been done.

Svyatoslav said: “Let us go to the other side of the city, whence the wind blows.” And they rose and went to that side of the city, and came and stood opposite the city gates.

And the prince said to them: “Brothers and retinue, today one of two things awaits us: either good or bad; let us move quickly.” And the prince himself rushed ahead of all toward the city; seeing him, all the warriors hastened quickly toward the city.

And from that side of the city they also cut down the fence and the walls and set them on fire, and the Bulgars fled into the city; our men pursued them and cut them down.
