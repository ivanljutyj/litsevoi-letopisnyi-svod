Moses was made lord in Novgorod, upon the hierarchal throne of the great archbishopric of Christ.

In the same year there was a great pestilence in Novgorod and throughout all the Novgorod land. By God's providence a grievous, terrible, and bitter death came upon the people from the Dormition of the Mother of God until Easter.

Countless good people died. This happened not only in Novgorod but passed through all lands. Those whom God ordained died, and those whom He preserved were so filled with the fear of the Lord that all their remaining days and years they lived chastely and without sin.

In the same year there was a severe pestilence in Smolensk, in Kiev, in Chernigov, in Suzdal, and throughout all the Russian land a cruel, sudden, and swift death. Fear and great trembling fell upon all people. In Glukhov at that time not a single person remained; all died, and so it was also in Belozerye.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky sent his wife, the princess, away to Ryazan. In the same year the construction of the stone church in Nizhny Novgorod was completed.

The Spiritual Charter and Testament of Magnus, King of Sweden

In ancient times the Swedish king Magnus, before his death, wrote a testament and confirmed it with an oath, in which it was written: "I, the sinful Magnus, King of Sweden, named Gregory in holy baptism, departing from this fleeting life, write this testament in my lifetime. I bequeath to my brothers, to my children, and to all the Swedish land to live in peace and love, to avoid all lies and falsehood, and vain ostentation, drunkenness, and all demonic games, not to offend, not to do violence, not to violate the oath sworn on the cross with the Russian lands forever, because from this we gain no bodily benefit, but only destruction for our souls, because it will help us in nothing."

Once the prince, Master Belgerd, went to war and entered the Neva. On the Izhora River Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavich met him, routed his regiments, and put him to flight.

Then my brother, Prince Maskalka, also entered the Neva, built a fortress on the Okhta River, and stationed his governors there with a multitude of Germans, while he himself went overseas.

But Grand Prince Andrey Alexandrovich came, took the fortress, and beat the governors and the Germans. And after this there was no peace between us and Rus for forty years. And then on the Neva they established a perpetual peace for forty years with Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich and made a division of lands and waters—who should own what; they signed charters and affixed seals.
