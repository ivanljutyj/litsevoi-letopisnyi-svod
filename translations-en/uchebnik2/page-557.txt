Be merciful to the poor and needy and live in peace and love among yourselves.

Then he turned to his sons: "You, my sons, fear God, honor your mother, and keep peace and love among yourselves. I commit you all into the hands of the Lord my God and of His Most Pure Mother, the Mother of God, and into the hands of my princess, your mother, Evdokia. Listen to your parents and remember what is said in Scripture: 'A father's curse will destroy a child's house, but a mother's sighing will uproot it completely.' If you have the fear of God in your hearts and keep His commandments, this will be for your good: you will live long on earth, your soul will abide in goodness, your dominion will increase and spread, your enemies will fall under your feet, and foreigners will flee before your face, and your land will be relieved of its burdens, and there will be abundance of all things in it. Love your boyars and give them worthy honor according to each one's deeds, and decide nothing without their counsel; be gracious to all who serve you, do everything wisely and as is proper, and in all things ask counsel of your mother."

And he turned to his boyars and said: "Come to me, and I will tell you what I have done in my life. You know my customs and character: I was born and grew up among you, I reigned together with you, and I ruled my patrimony, the great principality, for twenty-seven years, and I am forty years old from birth. With you I went on campaigns, with you I was fearsome in battles, and by God's help I overthrew my enemies and subdued them; with you I strengthened the great principality and established peace and calm in my principality. I preserved the dominion of my patrimony; I have great honor and love for you, and together with you I governed cities and districts. I loved your children and did harm to none of you, took nothing by force, did not oppress, did not reproach anyone, did not plunder, did not commit outrage, but honored and loved all and held them in great honor; I rejoiced and grieved together with you. You were called by me not boyars but princes of my land. Now remember the words you spoke to me: 'Lord prince, serving you and your children, we must lay down our heads for you.' Now, after my departure from this brief and poor life, truly serve my princess and my children with all your heart. Rejoice with them in joy, and do not forsake them in sorrow. Let your sorrow be turned to joy. Abide in peace."

And having first summoned his eldest son Vasily to give him the seniority, he placed in his hands the great principality—the throne of his father, grandfather, and great-grandfather—with all dues, and entrusted to him his patrimony, the Russian land. And then he distributed to his sons the cities of his patrimony in portions, assigning to each where to reign, and divided the lands among them by right.
