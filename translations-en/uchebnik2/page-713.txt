RUS (1433–1451, from V.Kh.)
Book 13

The Reign of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, Grandson of the Pious Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy

After the death of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, his son Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich ascended the grand principality of Vladimir and Moscow; he was then ten years and sixteen days old.

And Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, his uncle, sent to him with threats; and he concluded a truce with him until St Peter's Day.

And during that truce, that same spring, Prince Yuri Dmitrievich sent throughout his patrimony to all his people; and when all from all his cities had gathered to him, he wished to go against his nephew, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, to Moscow. And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich united with all his forces, and with him were also his uncles, the brothers of Prince Yuri Dmitrievich.

And the grand prince went to Kostroma. Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, hearing that the grand prince was coming against him with all his forces and with his brothers the Dmitrieviches, fled to Nizhny Novgorod and shut himself up there with all his people.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich sent against him his uncle Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich with a great force. Prince Yuri Dmitrievich was frightened and fled beyond the Sura River, and stood on the bank; but his brother Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich, after standing opposite him, returned to Moscow, because it was impossible to cross the river to him.

And then Prince Yuri Dmitrievich went to Nizhny Novgorod and from there to Galich, and sent to the grand prince for a truce for a year.
