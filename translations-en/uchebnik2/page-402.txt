Then the Novgorodians, having pelted the walls of the fortress with fire, set the city ablaze. And in this way they burned some Germans, killed others, and those who remained alive they led to Moscow to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich. The Novgorodians stood under that city until they had burned it, from the Dormition of the Mother of God to the Synaxis.

In the same year Prince Temir of the Horde came with an army to the city of Aleksin of St. Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, and they burned the settlement, and with a great number of captives he returned to the Horde.

But God delivers His servants from death, by His righteous judgment, from those who wrong them, for Temir was killed by his own Tatar servants in the Horde in the same year. And so the accursed one perished miserably with his children.

In the same year a certain man named Ivan, whose one eye was diseased, was healed in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God, at the tomb of St. Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky came from the Horde with honor and with a grant for the principality of Tver, and with him an envoy. And his uncle, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Kashinsky, came from the Horde to Kashin. And there arose between them a feud that was burdensome to the people of Tver, and many people of Tver fled because of this feud.

In the same year the Germans killed Prince Narimant Gediminovich, and with him they killed many princes, voivodes, and Lithuanians.

In the same year there was a great quarrel in Tver between Grand Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich Kholmsky, who sat by the royal command on the grand principality of Tver, and his uncle, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Kashinsky, grandson of Yaroslav; matters nearly came to bloodshed between them.

In the year 6857 from Adam (1357 from Christ)

Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania sent his envoys to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich of Moscow with great gifts and with a petition, asking peace and safety for his brother Kariad Gediminovich, his boyars, and all his Lithuanian retinue. And Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich agreed to peace and love and released to him his brother Kariad Gediminovich, his boyars, and all his retinue to Lithuania.

In the same year the King of Cracow took the land of Volhynia by treachery and converted churches of the Greek rite to the Latin rite.

In the same year Theodore, Bishop of Tver, reconciled Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Kashinsky with Grand Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Tver. And, having repented, they swore love and peace between themselves. Vsevolod Alexandrovich yielded the grand principality of Tver to his uncle.
