Rus (1298–1350 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 7

In the year 6798 from Adam (1298 from Christ)

The cathedral church of the Holy Savior was consecrated by Andrey, Bishop of Tver. This bishop was by birth a Lithuanian, the son of Erden, a Lithuanian prince.

In the same year a son, Ivan, was born to Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich of Vladimir.

In the same year in Novgorod the posadnik Samoil Rodionovich was killed, and the Novgorodians gathered in armor, took Prusskaya Street, and plundered and burned their houses. In the same year there was disorder among the Tatars, but in Rus there was quiet.

In the year 6799 from Adam (1299 from Christ)

The daughter of Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavich of Tver was tonsured into the holy angelic order while still a maiden, in a convent. In the same year there was a sign in the moon: it became as if bloody and then turned into darkness.

In the same year the tsars Tokhta, Telebuga, and Solguy went to war in the Horde, and Tokhta prevailed. In the same year Tsar Nogai killed Tsar Telebuga and Saolguy.

In the same year a son, Vasily, was born to Grand Prince Konstantin Vasilkovich of Rostov.

In the same year among the Tatars there was internecine strife, great war, and slaughter, but in Rus there was quiet.

In the year 6800 from Adam (1300 from Christ)

Prince Ivan Dmitrievich of Pereslavl married—grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky—and took as his wife the daughter of Grand Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov, and was married in the Church of the Holy Savior in Pereslavl.

In the same year the cathedral Church of the Holy Savior in Tver was painted with frescoes.
