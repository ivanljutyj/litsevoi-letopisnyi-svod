So God helped Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and his warriors, and they overcame the Ryazanians, and Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan barely managed to flee with a small retinue.

And then Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk sat on the grand principality of Ryazan. In the same winter a son, Vasily, was born to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

In the same winter Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, great-great-grandson of blessed Alexander, married, taking as his wife the daughter of Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania, named Elena in holy baptism.

In the same winter Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan drove Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk from the principality of Ryazan, and himself again sat on the grand principality of Ryazan, seized his son-in-law Prince Vladimir Dmitrievich of Pronsk and subjected him to his authority.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver sent his nephew Prince Dmitry Eremeevich and his commanders with an army to Kistma, and, having captured the Kistma commanders, the sons of Ivan Tishanorov, Andrey, David, and Boris, they brought them to Tver to Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Vasilievich of Kashin, grandson of Mikhail, great-grandson of Yaroslav, sent to Moscow to conclude peace with Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and he annulled the agreement with Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver.

In the same year, when the most reverend Metropolitan Alexius was finishing the Divine Liturgy, a certain youth was healed at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Rus; that youth had hands twisted at his breast and could not speak, and then he spoke and straightened his hands. And Metropolitan Alexius ordered the bells to be rung and a thanksgiving service to be served, and glorified the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God and their holy servant.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver went to war against Dmitrov, took a ransom from the city, burned the posads, the surrounding areas, and the villages, and, having taken boyars and people captive, brought them to Tver.

At the same time Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver secretly led a Lithuanian army against the city of Pereyaslavl: Prince Keistut, brother of Olgerd Gediminovich, Prince Andrey Olgerdovich of Polotsk, Prince Vitovt Keistutich, Prince Dmitry Drutsky, and many other princes with many warriors. From Pereyaslavl they took a ransom, burned the posad, seized the districts and villages, burned everything, and took captives. Likewise from Kashin they took a ransom and seized the districts with the villages, and led the people away into captivity.
