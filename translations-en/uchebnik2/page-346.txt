In the year 6813 from Adam (the year 1313 from Christ)

There was a sign in the heavens. In the same summer there were great thunders and lightning. In the same year the most reverend Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, reposed during Philip's Fast, on the 6th day of December, on the feast of Saint Nicholas the Wonderworker. And he was laid in Vladimir, in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God.

In the same year, during Philip's Fast, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich ordered Grand Prince Konstantin Romanovich of Ryazan to be killed in his own Moscow; his father, Grand Prince Daniil Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, had captured him by some cunning in battle.

In the same year the brothers of Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow departed from their brother, Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich, from Moscow to Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver.

In the same year in Nizhny Novgorod the common people killed the boyars of Prince Andrei Alexandrovich.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Andreevich came from the Horde to Nizhny Novgorod and killed all the veche men who had killed the boyars. And they drank the same cup, for: "With the judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with the measure you measure, it will be measured to you." In the same year in autumn there was Tairova's host.

In the year 6814 from Adam (the year 1314 from Christ)

A son, Konstantin, was born to Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, of the Yaroslavichi. In the same summer there were great rains.

In the year 6815 from Adam (the year 1315 from Christ)

Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver sat on the throne in Novgorod.

In the same year Archbishop Feoktist, lord of Novgorod, left his bishopric of his own will, because of his infirmity, choosing a quiet and silent life, and went to the Annunciation Monastery. And he blessed his spiritual father David to be bishop in Novgorod in his place; and they installed him in the Vladyka's court with entreaty and honor, until a metropolitan should be installed for Kiev and all Rus; and then he would go to him to be consecrated.

In the year 6816 from Adam (the year 1316 from Christ)

Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, was installed in Constantinople by Athanasius, Patriarch of Constantinople, and received great honor from the patriarch and from the emperor.
