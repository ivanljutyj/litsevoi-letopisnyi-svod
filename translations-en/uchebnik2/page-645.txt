In the year 6909 from Adam (the year 1409 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, and Grand Prince Vitovt Keistutovich of Lithuania agreed on peace and love among themselves.

In that same autumn Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver took from his brother, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, Lake Lukoye and the Entry into Jerusalem, and gave them to his nephew Prince Ivan Borisovich. Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin sent to him Arseny, Bishop of Tver, their common father, asking for a common judgment. Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich answered: “I will not give you judgment.” And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich returned to his patrimony in Kashin.

And a son was born to him by his wife Princess Anastasia, and his mother, Grand Princess Avdotya, wife of Prince Mikhail, came to him in Kashin.

And from Sergiyev Monastery the Venerable Abbot Nikon came to him; he rejoiced at the arrival of his mother, at the birth of his son, and at the Venerable Abbot Nikon. And Grand Princess Avdotya and Abbot Nikon baptized him and gave him the name Dmitry in holy baptism.

In the same year were discovered the Passions of our Lord Jesus Christ, which the God-loving Bishop Dionysius of Suzdal once brought from Constantinople, having received them there with great faith and love and having brought them to Rus with great labor. Afterwards for some time they were in the city of Suzdal, hidden in the stone wall of the church. In that same spring they were discovered there, and they were transferred to Moscow; the whole priestly order and the whole city met them with honors and crosses.

In the same year Tsar Timur-Aksak marched with an army against the Turkish Tsar Bayezid, who had already stood with his army before Constantinople for seven years; Timur-Aksak himself wished to conquer Constantinople.

Tsar Bayezid of Turkey, hearing of this, withdrew from Constantinople and went with his army against Tsar Timur-Aksak.
