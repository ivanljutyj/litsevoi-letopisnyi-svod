In the year 6854 from Adam (1354 from Christ) they finished painting three stone churches in Moscow: in the monastery of the Holy Savior, of the Holy Archangel Michael, and of Saint John of the Ladder.

In that year in Moscow Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich with his brothers Ivan Ivanovich and Andrei Ivanovich cast three large bells and two smaller ones. And the master who cast them was Boris the Roman.

In that same year the Archbishop of Novgorod, Lord Vasily, came to Moscow to invite Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich to Novgorod to reign. But first he received a blessing from Metropolitan Feognost, and paid him honor, and brought gifts. Metropolitan Feognost blessed him, and gave him sacred cross-patterned vestments, and took from him the deacon Kirill, who surpassed all in the purity of his voice. In that year the most reverend Feognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Ivan, archimandrite of the Savior Monastery, Bishop of Rostov.

In that year there was a severe pestilence in the eastern lands: in Ornachi, in Astrakhan, in Sarai, in Bezdezh, and in other cities of that land. Among Christians and Armenians, among the Franks, the Circassians, the Tatars, and the Obezy the pestilence was such that there was no one to bury the dead.

In that year Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich with his brother Keistut Gediminovich went with an army against Novgorod and stood at the mouth of the river Nshaga. And he sent word to the Novgorodians: “I wish to meet you, and if God helps me, I wish also to do battle with you. Your posadnik Ostafy Dvoryaninets has offended me; he reviled me with such abusive words that he even called me a dog.” He stood there a little while and, beginning to wage war, took Shelona and the land from Golino to Luga and to Fsavl: he conquered and took captive many volosts, villages, and places.

And from Porkhov he took a ransom of three hundred rubles and sixty Novgorod rubles. The Novgorodians gathered and went out against him to Luga, but then returned at a run to Novgorod.

They began to summon a veche at the prince’s court and there killed the posadnik Ostafy Dvoryaninets, saying to him: “You began the enmity; because of it they are taking our whole land captive. Who told you to abuse the king?” So Ostafy Dvoryaninets was killed.

And Olgerd Gediminovich with his brother Keistut Gediminovich and with a great multitude of captives returned to his own land.

In that year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, grandson of Daniil, sent his princess Evpraksia away from him to her father, Prince Feodor Svyatoslavich, at Volok.

In that same year Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich went to Novgorod and settled there to collect tribute; he stayed there three weeks and departed for Moscow.
