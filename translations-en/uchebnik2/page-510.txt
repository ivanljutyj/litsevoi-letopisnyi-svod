Rus (1389–1397 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 10

In the year 6889 from Adam (1389 from Christ)

Pimen, Metropolitan, came from Constantinople to Rus; but Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich did not receive him; and when he was in Kolomna, they removed his white klobuk and sent him into confinement at Chukhloma. This is written in the tale of Mityay before this, in the seventh year of this decade. In the same year Dionysius, Bishop of Suzdal, sent from Constantinople with the monk Malafiy the Philosopher an icon, a copy of the image of the Most Pure Mother of God Hodegetria, called in the Russian tongue Nastavnitsa, and in Greek Hodegetria; that icon of the Hodegetria is carried forth in Constantinople on Tuesday. And so Bishop Dionysius painted an image of the same kind, both in width and in height, and sent another icon—the image of the Most Pure Mother of God Hodegetria, of the same measure in width and height—which he had copied and sent to Rus. And one was placed in the Church of the Holy Savior in Nizhny Novgorod, and the other was placed in Suzdal, in the cathedral church.

In the same year Prince Vladimir Andreyevich had a son, Prince Ivan, and he was baptized by His Eminence Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, and by the venerable abbot Sergius of Radonezh.

In the same year Tsar Tokhtamysh sent his envoy to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and to all the Russian princes, a certain tsarevich Ak-Khozya, and with him seven hundred Tatars, summoning them to the Horde. And having reached Nizhny Novgorod, he turned back, and did not dare go to Moscow, but sent some of his Tatars, not daring to go with a small retinue.

That same winter and that same spring certain signs appeared in the heavens in the east before early dawn: something like a pillar of fire, and a star resembling a spear, as it were tailed. This foretold against the Russian land the evil coming of Tokhtamysh and the bitter invasion of the heathen.

In the same year, on the Sunday of All Saints, there was a terrible thunder and a strong wind with a whirlwind. In the same year the Lithuanian prince Skirgailo with all the German force stood before Polotsk and inflicted much hardship on the city of Polotsk, and, not having taken it, withdrew.
