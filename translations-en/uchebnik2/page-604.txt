By the same enemy instigation, the horse began to rage furiously and threw off the priest's servant, broke his leg, trampled the priest's wife underfoot, and, seizing her with its teeth, dragged her along the ground until she stopped breathing.

The presbyter, in grief and sorrow, began to pray with tears to the Most Pure Mother of God before her wonder-working image. And in that hour his wife recovered, and she had no injury at all. His servant also recovered, and his broken leg became like the other.

Miracle concerning the place called Bogolyubovo

And from there they reached the city of Vladimir. And when they were not far from it, on the Klyazma River, the horses that were carrying the icon of the Mother of God stood still and would not move further. The horses were changed and the wonder-working icon was transferred to a sledge. And not a single horse could move with the holy icon.

Great Prince Andrew at that place set up two stone churches in the name of the Holy Mother of God and built a city there, giving that place the name Bogolyubovo.

On the building of the Vladimir Church, in which the icon of the Mother of God was placed

Eleven years passed. Great Prince Andrew in the famous city of Vladimir set up a wondrous large stone cathedral church in the name of the honorable Dormition of the Holy Mother of God, with a single dome. Before that there had been a wooden church there, set up by the first Russian autocrat who accepted Orthodoxy, Great Prince Vladimir. Great Prince Andrew gave that cathedral church many estates, and bought settlements for it with their dues and the best villages, and gave a tenth of his herds, and a tenth of the market revenues, and gilded its dome.

Because of his faith and devotion to the Holy Mother of God, God brought to him wise craftsmen from various lands, and he commanded skilled painters to paint this cathedral church, and placed many wondrous icons in it, and wondrously adorned it more than other churches with sacred vessels and costly books, vestments, and other seemly ornaments.

In it he also placed this most wondrous icon of the Mother of God, setting it in gold with precious stones and costly pearls. He spent more than thirty grivnas of gold on it. And from that time this icon of the Most Pure Mother of God came to be called the Vladimir Icon.

Miracle concerning the Golden Gates

Then the Golden Gates of the city were built, and there also the church of the Deposition of the Robe of the Holy Mother of God, which he wondrously adorned.
