And the voivode Dmitr received many wounds; and many strong warriors perished; and the townspeople were overcome; and the Tatars climbed onto the walls; and from great exhaustion they stopped on the city walls.

And night fell, and that night the townspeople set up another fortification around the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God.

On the next day the Tatars attacked them; and there was a fierce battle; and the people’s strength gave out; and the people ran onto the church vaults with their belongings; and from the weight the church walls collapsed.

And the Tatars took the city of Kiev on the 6th day of the month of December, the day of the memory of Saint Nicholas the Wonderworker. And the voivode Dmitr was brought, wounded, to Batu, and Batu did not order him killed because of his courage.

And Batu began asking about Prince Daniel, and they told him that he had fled to the Hungarians.

And Batu appointed his voivode in the city of Kiev, and himself went to the city of Vladimir in Volhynia.

And Batu came to the city of Kolodyazhna; and set up 12 throwing engines, and could not with their help break the city walls.

And Batu began to deceive the people; and they, having listened to his treacherous advice, surrendered and were then killed.

From there he came to Kamenets, the city of Izyaslav; and struck it with throwing engines; and took it.

And he went to Kremenets, the city of Prince Daniel; and he could not take it because of its fortifications; and he withdrew from it.

And he came to the city of Vladimir and took it; and killed all, sparing not a single person.

And from there he went to Galich; and struck it with throwing engines; and took it.

And from there he went to Chervenets; and struck it with throwing engines; and took it.

But why list the cities one after another at length! Batu took a vast, countless multitude of Russian cities; and enslaved them all; and appointed his voivodes.

And Dmitr, the Kiev thousandman of Prince Daniel, said to Batu: “You cannot linger in this land long; it is time for you already to go against the Hungarians; if you linger, their land is strong, they will gather against you, and will not let you into their land”; Dmitr said this to Batu seeing that the Russian land was perishing from the impious one.
