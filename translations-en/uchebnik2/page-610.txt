This Temir diligently studied how to eradicate the Christian faith utterly. O god-opposing designs! O harshest savageries! O wild audacity and inhuman disposition! O evil, furious wars directed against the Orthodox faith, which Christ established, the cornerstone of laws and grace, upon which the Lord founded His Church, and the gates of Hell shall not prevail against it! This faith the false-minded, inhuman Temir wished to destroy, but he himself was destroyed by God.

All Orthodox people heard how, by God’s permission, many mighty kingdoms were destroyed by the most wicked Temir in an incomprehensible manner, and many rulers and potentates were mercilessly exterminated, and countless multitudes of people were ruthlessly and in manifold ways slain, and they heard Temir’s mad words against the chosenness of the Divine inheritance. These menacing and terrible tidings circulated in Moscow every day, and all were seized with horror, bitterness, great fear, and sorrow: the former calamities that God had permitted were not yet forgotten. Thirty years had passed since the city of Moscow was taken by cunning by the deceitful Tsar Tokhtamysh. But the god-abhorrent Temir wished to devastate the Christian inheritance even more cruelly. People had heard of his deeds far away in the sunny East, and now he was already near, at the doors.

On the Courage of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich

The pious Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, hearing of the proud savagery and lawless tortures and murders of Temir, was not frightened nor terrified, but placed all his hope in almighty God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and in the prayers of the great wonderworking hierarchs of Russia, Peter and Alexei, and of the venerable wonderworker Sergius, and of all the saints; he began to arm himself with prayer, lifted up his hands, and with tears turned to God, saying: “Creator and defender, Lord, Lord! Look from Your holy dwelling; You see this barbarian and those with him, who has dared to blaspheme Your holy and magnificent name, and that of Your Most Pure all-blameless Mother, and of all Your saints! Cast down our enemies! Let them not say, ‘Where is their God?’—for You are our God, who resists the proud. O most merciful Lord! Do not permit the enemies who wish to revile us! Arise, Lord, to help us, Your servants! Take care of us and deliver our city and all our land from the Tatar invasion, and grant us, Your servants, strength and help from on high against all our adversaries!”

Invincible is Your power, and Your kingdom is indestructible, and we, kept under Your dominion, glorify You with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.
