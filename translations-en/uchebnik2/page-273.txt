There was at that time a wondrous miracle, as in ancient years under King Hezekiah, when Sennacherib, king of Assyria, came to Jerusalem desiring to seize the holy city; and suddenly the angel of the Lord went forth and killed 185,000 from the army of the king of Assyria, and rising the next day Sennacherib found them all dead. So too at the victory of Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, when he defeated the king. On that bank of the Izhora River there was no passage for the army of Grand Prince Alexander; and there was found a great multitude killed by God’s angels, and our men discovered that there lay many bodies of dead enemies who had been killed. Those who remained alive fled in disgrace; and the corpses of their own dead they threw into three large ships of the commanders, and these sank with them in Lake Ladoga.

And for the remaining enemy soldiers they dug pits and threw countless corpses into them; and many others were wounded and fled that same night.

From the Novgorodians there fell: Konstantin Lugotinich, Yuryata Pinyashchinich, Namest, Druchilo, son of Nezdila the Tanner; and in all twenty men fell, together with the Ladoga warriors; or fewer, God knows.

Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich returned with a great victory, glorifying and thanking God with these words: “I thank Thee, O most good Master, and I glorify Thy most holy Name; for I called upon Thee in the day of my sorrow, and Thou didst not forsake me, Thy servant, and Thou didst deliver us from our enemies; they stumbled and fell, but we arose and were set upright; for Thou hast mercy on all who repent fervently; and to all who seek Thee with fear and love Thou art near; and Thou dost not despise those who look to Thee alone, but dost benefit them; and to all who fall down before Thy dominion from the soul Thou givest every petition that is for their profit; and those who glorify Thee Thou dost glorify; for Thou alone art the Giver of good things, and to Thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages, amen.”

In the same year Batu’s Tatars defeated the Ugrians and took Prince Menush alive and brought him to Batu, and brought many great Ugrian commanders.

In the same year Batu’s Tatars killed Prince Mstislav of Rylsk.

In the same year the Novgorodians quarreled with Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich; and there was a great revolt in Novgorod.

And Alexander went to his father, Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich of Vladimir, with his mother, and with his princess, and with all his people.

In the same year the Germans, the Medvezhans, the Yuryevites, and the Veliadtsy, with Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, took the city of Izborsk.

And news came to Pskov that the Germans had taken Izborsk.
