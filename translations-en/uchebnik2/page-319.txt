In the same year the Tatars and Russian princes went against the Lithuanians and ravaged them, and returned with great booty.

In the same year there was a second census in Rus and in Novgorod, from the Horde by the Tsar, and they registered everyone except priests, monks, and all church servants.

In the same year they killed a bear with three legs. In the same year in Novgorod the office of posadnik was given to Mikhail Mikhailovich. In the same year there was a heavenly sign.
