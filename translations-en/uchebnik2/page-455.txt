In the same year, Mamai’s Tatars came with an army against Nizhny Novgorod, saying: “Why did you go to war against Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver?!”

And they seized all the lands of Nizhny Novgorod, and after taking many captives returned to the Horde.

In the same year, Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania came with an army against Smolensk, saying: “Why did you go to fight against Prince Mikhail of Tver?!”

And so they conquered the entire Smolensk land and took captives.

And they seized and burned the small Smolensk towns, led the people away into captivity, and returned to their own lands.

In the same year, Mamai’s Tatars came with an army against the town of Novosil, saying: “Why did you go to conquer Tver?!”

And so they laid waste the entire Novosil land and returned with a great number of captives.

In the same year they gave Maria, daughter of Prince Keistut of Lithuania, in marriage to Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver; Bishop Evfimy of Tver baptized her in the Holy Church of the Exaltation.

And on the next day Prince Ivan Mikhailovich was married in the cathedral Church of the Holy Savior by Bishop Evfimy of Tver.

And Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver rejoiced at the marriage of his son Prince Ivan.

In the same year the Korela of Sedmidesyatskaya built a new town.

In the same year the Novgorodians threw the Strigolniki heretics into the waters of the Volkhov River, saying: “It is written in the Gospel: If anyone causes one of these little ones to stumble, it is better for him that a millstone be hung around his neck and he be drowned in the sea.”

In the year 6884 from Adam (1384 from Christ)

Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow went with an army beyond the Oka River, watching for Mamai’s Tatar host.

In the same year in Novgorod, for seven days the Volkhov River flowed backward, upstream; this had already happened for the third year in a row.

In the same year Bishop Alexy of Novgorod voluntarily renounced the bishopric and settled in Derevyanitsa, and the Novgorodians were greatly offended.

And they sent to Moscow to Metropolitan Alexy with a petition, asking him to appeal to their Alexy not to abandon the Novgorod bishopric.
