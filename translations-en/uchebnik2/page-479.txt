Sign.

In that same winter, during Philip’s Fast, on the fifth day of December, a Sunday, at early dawn, there was a sign in the heavens: the moon was darkened and turned to blood, and stood in one place, and after this it began again to appear bright from the southern side, but it had darkened from the east and again entered into its brightness.

That same winter Prince Andrei Olgerdovich fled from Lithuania to Pskov, and from there to Moscow, and Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich received him with love.

In that same year Dionysius, bishop of Suzdal, went to Tsargrad in boats by the Volga River to Sarai. This account was written earlier in the account about Mityai. Since Dionysius and Mityai were at enmity, and he himself also wanted to sit on the metropolitan throne, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich forbade him to go to Tsargrad, so that he would not create an obstacle to Mityai’s installation as metropolitan. Bishop Dionysius outwitted Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich in that he would not go to Tsargrad, and concerning this he obtained from the venerable hegumen Sergius of Radonezh a guarantee to the Grand Prince, and afterwards he betrayed his guarantor, the venerable hegumen Sergius, and fled by the Volga in boats to Sarai. Then Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich saw that Bishop Dionysius had betrayed him and had betrayed his guarantor, the venerable hegumen Sergius, and had fled by the Volga to Sarai, and from there to Tsargrad; and the Grand Prince was grieved at this, and he sent Mityai, archimandrite of Spassky, to the patriarch in Tsargrad to be installed as metropolitan for Rus. Mityai went by land from Moscow to Kolomna, from Kolomna to Ryazan, from Ryazan to Mamai’s Horde. There was a tsar in Mamai’s Horde, but he held nothing in comparison with Prince Mamai, for he was only called tsar, and even that name was small and poor, for all the glory and deeds were Mamai’s. And there was much disorder in the Horde, and many Tatar princes were killed, until they had no head, and they died by the edge of the sword, and the Horde grew poor from its great strength. Mityai was detained in the Horde by Mamai for a short time and again released, and he passed through the whole Tatar land in peace and quiet, for Mamai ordered his Tatars to escort him. And they came to the Caffa Sea, and he sent the Tatars of Mamai who had escorted him back to Mamai. And Mityai with all his people went up onto the ship; and while sailing over the sea, and being already near Tsargrad, so that all Tsargrad could be seen, Mityai fell ill on the ship and reposed, and they laid him in Galata.
