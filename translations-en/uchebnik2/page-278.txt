From the icon of the Holy Savior, above the tomb of the Grand Princess, wife of Yaroslav Vladimirovich, whom her own stepson killed in the city of Medvezhya Golova, myrrh flowed from the icon for twelve days; and four lamps were filled, each of the capacity of a small flask.

The Pskovians took counsel and sent two lamps to Novgorod, to the archbishop and the Novgorodians, for a blessing, and kept two for themselves in Pskov.

O truly a wonder beyond wonders! How from dry wood, as from a spring, myrrh flows! But glory to Thee, O Lord, who hast given us, Thy unworthy servants, such a blessing. And in Thee we trust, O Lord Almighty, that Thou lookest upon us, the lowly, with Thy mercy, O Lover of mankind.

On the Venerable Varlaam, Wonderworker of Khutyn

In that same year reposed the venerable great wonderworker Varlaam, abbot of Khutyn, endowed with many virtues, whom in the world they called Aleksa Mikhailovich. He lived a holy life and fully pleased God, to Whom he departed rejoicing into Eternal Bliss. The Lord gloriously glorified Varlaam with wondrous memory and countless miracles. There is sufficient account of him in his festal service.

In that same year the Germans sent envoys to Novgorod with obeisance, saying: “What we took by spear and sword—Pskov, Vetluga, Latygoda—we renounce all of it; and as for your people whom we took captive, we will exchange them: we will release yours, and you release ours; and we will release the captive Pskovians.” And then peace was concluded, and both sides released all the captives.

In that same year the Lithuanians began to ravage the Novgorod lands; Alexander went and with his one army defeated seven enemy armies; and he killed a multitude of Lithuanians.

And those Lithuanians who were taken captive they tied to the tails of their horses and, making sport, led them along; and from that time they began to fear the name of Prince Alexander.

In that same year Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went to the Horde to Tsar Batu, and sent his son Constantine to the Khan.

Batu received Yaroslav with honor and released him, making him senior over all the Russian people; and Yaroslav came with great honor to his land.
