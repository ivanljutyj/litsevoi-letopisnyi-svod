And in Kiev they again sang the funeral service over Metropolitan Cyril, and all the Russian bishops served with all the priests, and they buried him in Kiev in the cathedral church.

In that same year Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich of Vladimir with his brothers went to war against Novgorod; the Novgorodians sent to him their Archbishop Clement with obeisance, petition, and gifts. Dmitry listened to the bishop, accepted the gifts, and returned with his brothers to Vladimir.

In that same year there was a sign in the sky: there was a fiery cloud from the west, and sparks from it fell over all the earth, and after standing a little the cloud vanished.

In the year 6789 from Adam (1289 from Christ)

Dmitry Borisovich was Great Prince in Rostov, and his brother Constantine Borisovich was with him; and there was between them a quarrel and great enmity. Their bishop Ignatius of Rostov reconciled them and brought them to agreement; but they were hostile all the more.

And Prince Constantine Borisovich departed from Rostov to Vladimir to Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav; and Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov began to gather troops and fortify the city, fearing his brothers, and was in confusion and great sorrow, and threw the whole city of Rostov into unrest.

And Bishop Ignatius went from Rostov to Vladimir and entreated Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich to keep the peace.

And Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, with Ignatius Bishop of Rostov and his boyars, went to Rostov.

And Bishop Ignatius brought the princes to peace and concord; and they held a great celebration, and all rejoiced; and Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich returned to Vladimir.

That same winter Prince Andrew Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, younger brother of Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich of Vladimir, went to the Horde to the Tsar, and wished to become Great Prince in Vladimir bypassing his elder brother; and Semen Tonilievich was his accomplice and helper, and many others.

And Prince Andrew Alexandrovich gave many gifts to the Tsar and to the great princes of the Horde, and gave wealth in full to all, and persuaded and placated everyone, and obtained from the Tsar for himself the right to be Great Prince of Vladimir bypassing his elder brother, Great Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.
