Rostislav, son of Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich, together with the Novgorod posadnik, went to Torzhok; and the Novgorodians plundered the house of Semyon Borisovich, seized his wife, and killed him.

The Novgorodians likewise plundered the houses of Posadnik Vodovik, his brother Mikhail, their son-in-law Danislav, and Tysyatsky Boris, divided all their property among themselves, and killed them. They labored gathering wealth, but these without labor took all their property for themselves, as it is written in Holy Scripture: “Some labored, and others entered into their labors,” and “A man gathers, and knows not for whom he gathers it.”

And the Novgorodians drove Prince Rostislav Mikhailovich from Torzhok to his father Mikhail in Chernigov, saying: “Your father Mikhail Vsevolodovich gave us his word that he would gather and go to war for our Novgorod grievances from the Day of the Exaltation; and now it is already St Nicholas’s Day, and nothing has been done by your father, so go from us, and we will find a prince for ourselves.” Prince Rostislav went to his father in Chernigov, and the Novgorodians sent for Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich to Pereyaslavl, saying: “If you wish to rule over us while preserving all our liberties of the Novgorod people, then come to us.”

Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich soon came from Pereyaslavl to Novgorod, and the Novgorodians met him with honor.

And the Novgorodians held an assembly, at which the prince kissed the cross, promising to rule according to all the charters of Yaroslav and all the liberties of Novgorod.

The famine spread still further, and was not only in Novgorod but throughout the Russian land, except Kiev alone. And such was the wrath of God that not only did they eat dead people, but living people killed and ate one another; and they ate horses, dogs, cats, and the like, wherever anyone found them; others ate moss, pine, elm, linden bark, and leaves. Evil people, if they learned somewhere that someone had bread, forcibly broke into that place, robbed, and killed. And the wrath of God spread; and countless people died throughout the land; and this lasted two years: in 6737 from Adam (1237 from Christ) and 6738 from Adam (1238 from Christ).

In the same year died Mstislav Davidovich, Grand Prince of Smolensk; and he was buried with honor in the city of Smolensk.

In the same year the relics of Saint Leontius the Bishop were transferred from the Church of Saint John to the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God.
