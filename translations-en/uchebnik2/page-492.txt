And for another reason: if anything should happen, let these act according to their custom. Their names are: Vasily Kapitsa, Sidor Elferiev, Konstantin, Kozma Koverya, Semion Antonov, Mikhailo Salarev, Timofey Vesyakov, Dmitry Chernoy, Dementy Salarev, Ivan Shikh. And the Grand Prince came to Kolomna on Saturday, the twenty-eighth day of August, on the commemoration of our venerable father Moses the Ethiopian. Before the Grand Prince, many commanders had gathered there and met the Grand Prince at the little River Severka. And Gerasim, Bishop of Kolomna, met him at the city gates with crosses.

And early the next morning, on Sunday, the Grand Prince ordered all the princes, boyars, and commanders to ride out to the field, and he appointed commanders for each regiment.

And the Grand Prince took the Beloozero princes with their troops into his own regiment, for they were very bold and brave; and to the right wing he appointed his brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich, giving him the Yaroslavl princes with their troops for his regiment. And to the left wing he appointed Prince Gleb of Bryansk. And to the vanguard regiment he appointed Dmitry Vsevolozhsky and Vladimir Vsevolozhsky.

The commander of the Kolomna regiment was Mikula Vasilyevich; the commander of the Vladimir and Yuryev regiment was Timofey Valuevich; the commander of the Kostroma regiment was Ivan Rodionovich Kvashnya; the commander of the Pereyaslavl regiment was Andrey Serkizovich; and Prince Vladimir Andreevich’s commanders were Danilo Belous, Konstantin Kononovich, Prince Feodor Eletsky, Prince Yuri Meshchersky, and Prince Andrey Muromsky.
