And the Grand Prince Izyaslav Davydovich came to Kiev to the great reign, and now he sits on the great reign in Kiev, while your son Prince Gleb sits on the great reign in Pereyaslavl."

At that same time the Grand Prince of Kiev, Rostislav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, fled from that battle to Smolensk, and heard that near Smolensk stood the Grand Prince of Suzdal, Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh Dolgoruky; and he was frightened and in great confusion, thinking that Prince Yuri Dolgoruky was marching against him.

He gave him peace and went to Rus, to Kiev; and having come, he stood at Starodub, and there Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich came to him, and kissed him, and held the customary conversation, and said to him that he should receive in love and peace his nephew Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich; and he gave peace and love to him also. And he commanded them to march with him to Kiev.

And having come, he stood near Kiev, and sent to Kiev to Grand Prince Izyaslav Davydovich—let him go out from Kiev: "Your great reign is Chernigov, but I must sit on the great reign in Kiev."

But Izyaslav Davydovich did not want to leave Kiev and the great reign, because the great reign of Kiev had become very dear to him. And who would not love the reign of Kiev, since all honor, and glory, and majesty, and the head of all Russian lands is Kiev, and from all the distant many kingdoms all sorts of people and merchants gather, and all manner of goods from all countries are found in it?

And the Grand Prince of Suzdal, Yuri Dolgoruky, went and stood at Moravsk, while Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich went and stood at Chernigov.

And Prince Yuri Dolgoruky again sent to Izyaslav in Kiev, saying thus: "If you do not leave Kiev, there will be great war and much blood will be shed."

He sent to him, saying thus: "It is no glory, honor, or praise to stir up war and shed blood for this fleeting and deceptive life; if you have so resolved, enter Kiev and sit in it on the great reign; for I did not seek Kiev myself, but the Kievans sent Bishop Demyan of Kanev to me with a petition, that I should go and sit on the great reign in Kiev, and I did their will, and I am now Grand Prince in Kiev; and if you have so willed, go to Kiev and sit on the great reign, and I will go back to my great reign in Chernigov, where I was before."

And having said this, he left Kiev and went to Chernigov, and sat on the great reign in Chernigov.
