A message came to Prince Andrei Alexandrovich from Rus that Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich was gathering an army, fortifying the city, and did not wish to submit to the tsar’s command and yield the grand-princely throne according to the tsar’s command.

And Prince Andrei told this to the tsar, and the tsar commanded his commanders Kavgady and Alchedai to go with Prince Andrei with a Tatar army.

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich came from the tsar out of the Horde to Murom with the Tatars, and with his counsellor and helper Semyon Tonilievich, and with many other helpers of his.

And Prince Andrei sent for Prince Mikhail Ivanovich of Starodub, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and for all the Russian princes; and they came and took counsel; and they all went to war against Pereyaslavl against Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.

Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich learned of this and fled to Novgorod, with his wife and children and boyars and all his people.

And Prince Dmitry entered the town of Koporye; the Novgorodians came out onto Lake Ilmen with feigned obeisance, for they did not want to let him leave from there, since he intended to flee overseas from there. And there were many words between them, and the Novgorodians said to Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich: “Prince, we do not want you; leave us peacefully. If you do not leave here, the Tatars will seize you and take you to the tsar in the Horde, and we will not help you.”

Prince Dmitry then set out from Koporye and went overseas; the Novgorodians did not seize him but drove him out and took from him by force his two daughters and his boyars with their wives and children, saying: “We take these from you as hostages; when all your boyars and servants have left Koporye and gone from us, then we will release these hostages”; and they dispersed on the first day of January.

But the Tatars scattered over the whole land and searched for Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, and laid waste everything around Murom, around Vladimir, around Yuryev, around Suzdal, around Pereyaslavl, around Rostov, around Tver, and as far as Torzhok and beyond, and near Novgorod; they pursued Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich and, not overtaking him, returned back.

Prince Andrei Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, entered the city of Vladimir and became grand prince.

And Prince Andrei arranged a great feast and gave many gifts to the Horde princes Kavgady and Alchedai and to the Tatars; and Prince Andrei sent the Tatars back to the Horde to the tsar; and they went to the Horde with great booty.
