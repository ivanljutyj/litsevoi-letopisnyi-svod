When you complete such a path, you will be great in God’s sight, and you will receive great blessedness and glory from Him in heaven and on earth; and you will stand with the holy martyrs of old; and in this present generation you will be called newly revealed martyrs of Christ.” Ivan instructed them from the Holy Gospel and from other books, and then blessed them for their struggle. As a companion for the journey he gave them Holy Communion of the most pure Flesh and life-giving Blood of Christ our God. When they had received Communion, the priest Ivan sent forth two valiant warriors to fight and repel the enemy’s assault on the Holy Church. And the saints entered their homes, took from their property what they needed for the journey, and peacefully said to their households: “The Lord be with you,” and then set out on their way. Of their own will they hastened to the struggle, each pondering within himself: “As the deer longs for the springs of water, so my soul longs for You, O God. My soul thirsts for the mighty and living God: when shall I come and appear before You?” And they rushed like a lion upon a deer, and like hawks upon sparrows.

They passed through many lands; and this wondrous pair appeared in the Horde, and report was made of them to the impious Tsar Batu, and it was said: “Grand Prince Michael of Chernigov has come to bow before you.” It was not without reason that the Chernigov prince was summoned. When the crafty Batu saw that by his deceit he darkened the brightest souls of the pious and encompassed them with fear, he, the madman, rejoiced that his design was coming to pass. And then, by God’s inscrutable providence, there appeared this God-sent radiant and great Prince Michael, like the last small spark of divine understanding buried under ashes. He kindled a great fire of Orthodox faith, which enlightened all the faithful, darkened the shameless Tatar countenances, obscured their godless decree, and burned up their impiety.

The lawless Batu summoned his priests skilled in sorcery and commanded them to make Grand Prince Michael be led by them through fire, and bow to a bush and the sun, and come to him with honor.

The priests came to Prince Michael and said to him: “Tsar Batu summons you.”
