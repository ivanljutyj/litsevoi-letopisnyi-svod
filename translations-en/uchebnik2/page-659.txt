He fell ill and lay ill for several days, until he reposed. Four days before his death he wrote a farewell charter, in which he forgave and blessed everyone, and himself asked forgiveness and blessing from all—behold true philosophy and humility. Therefore he also commanded, since by humility all sins are forgiven and all good is accomplished, and as he was leaving this world he commanded the bishops and all those close to him, saying: “When they place me in the coffin, read this charter aloud to the people over my body,” which was done in accordance with his instruction by the Venerable Gregory, Archbishop of Rostov, who read this charter aloud to all the people; and as he read it, tears came to the eyes of many who were there, and loud weeping and wailing was heard. In this charter was written: “In the name of the Holy and Life-Giving Trinity. I, the sinful and humble Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, seeing that my old age has come, that I have begun to be often ill, cleansing by illness, through God’s love for mankind, my sins, that my illnesses have now multiplied as never before, and that all this announces to me nothing other than death and the dread judgment of the Savior, decided that I must in my testament speak of what is needful for me. First, I confess the holy apostolic faith received from God, and the true Orthodox faith in the Holy Trinity and the other sacred apostolic covenants. I enjoin all to preserve the traditions of God’s holy Church whole and unchanged by God’s grace; what is written in my confession, that I hand on to you. When a hierarch is ordained according to custom, the apostolic rule, and the divine and sacred commandments of the holy fathers—since the apostles were appointed by God Himself, and the hierarchs by the apostles, therefore they are heirs of the apostles, and from those hierarchs other hierarchs were appointed according to the apostolic rule received from God—so too I, a sinner, was appointed by the grace of the Holy Spirit, according to the rule of the sacred commandments of the apostles, by the sacred hierarch, the Ecumenical Patriarch, in the reign of the pious Orthodox emperor of Constantinople. And since I have renounced the customary life after my consecration, while I am still alive I grant sincere forgiveness in the Holy Spirit to all. And to all the most holy ecumenical patriarchs, both those already departed and those still living, and also to the most sacred metropolitans, departed and living, I send my customary love and final kiss and entreaty, and I myself ask the same of them. To my noble and Christ-loving son, beloved in the Holy Spirit, Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Dmitrievich, I send peace and blessing and the final kiss, as also to his mother, brothers, princess, his children, and their princesses and children.
