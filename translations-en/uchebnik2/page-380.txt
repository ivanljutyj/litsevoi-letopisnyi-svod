But after some time, thanks to God and the Most Pure Mother of God, they all together, safe and sound, with Archbishop Vasily came to Novgorod on the 8th day of November. This was under Prince Ivan Danilovich, under posadnik Varfolomei and under tysyatsky Ostafii. The Novgorodians rejoiced at the return of their lord and the posadniks.

In that year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went to the Horde, and with him went Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich of Tver.

In that year there was a revolt in Novgorod: the seditious men took the posadnik office from Feodor Akhmyl and gave it to Zakhary Mikhailovich, plundered the homesteads of Semyon Sudokov, and seized the property, wealth, and villages of his brother Selifont.

In that year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich came from the Horde and showed his anger against the Novgorodians, demanding from them the Zakama silver, and for this he took Torzhok and Bezhetsky Verkh from the Novgorodians.

In that year the Novgorodians gave the posadnik office to Matfei Kozka. In the same year, in the Russian land, during a drought, much tall dorogov grew. Some call tall rye dorogov. In that year Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich, grandson of Yaroslav, came from the Horde.

In the year 6841 from Adam (1341 from Christ)

Prince Alexander Vasilyevich of Suzdal died. In that year Archbishop Vasily of Novgorod went to Pskov, and there in Pskov, at Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, he baptized his son, Prince Mikhail.

In that year Narimont Gediminovich, named Gleb in holy baptism, came to those Novgorodian towns that had been promised to him in Lithuania when they went to Metropolitan Theognost in the Volhynian land and gave their true word.

In that year the Most Reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, came to Moscow, after visiting Constantinople and the Horde.

In that year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich laid the foundation of a stone church in the name of the Holy Archangel Michael in Moscow on the square, inside the Kremlin, and in the same year it was completed and consecrated by the Most Reverend Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, on the 20th day of September, on the commemoration of the Holy Martyr Eustathius Placidas and those with him.

In that same winter Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich married his son Semyon in Moscow, who was seventeen years old. For him a princess named Augusta was brought from Lithuania, and in holy baptism she was named Anastasia.
