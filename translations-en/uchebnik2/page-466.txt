When the saint returned to Rus to his hierarchal see, they praised him, saying that his prayer was so great before God, and he remained on the hierarchal throne of the metropolis of all Rus for many years, teaching the word of God and instructing in piety, forbidding those who did wrong, appointing bishops and presbyters. On the Chudov Monastery

By divine revelation the saint founded a monastery in Moscow, and in it he built a stone church in the name of the Miracle of the Holy Archangel Michael, and in it he erected a side chapel in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God, her honorable and glorious Annunciation, and adorned the church with all kinds of beauties: icons, books, painting, golden sacred vessels, and other various ornaments.

And he established that monastery with a cenobitic rule, which is preserved in it to this day; the saint gave the monastery many villages, houses, people, lakes, fields, and lands, all that was necessary for the monastery, and not only did he not limit himself to these gifts and benefactions, but he also ordered himself to be buried in this monastery, and this was done. To this day his tomb is in the monastery, in the church, to the right of the altar, and from his holy body those who come with faith receive healing. This monastery was founded by the saint 12 years before his death.

When the saint saw himself weakened by old age and approaching his end, he summoned to himself the venerable Abbot Sergius of Radonezh. Soon, when the abbot came to the saint and conversed with him, Metropolitan Alexius ordered a cross with a paraman, adorned with gold and pearls, to be brought out and gave it to the saint. He, bowing with humility, said: “Forgive me, great hierarch of Christ, but from my youth I have not been a wearer of gold, and in old age all the more do I wish to remain in poverty.”
