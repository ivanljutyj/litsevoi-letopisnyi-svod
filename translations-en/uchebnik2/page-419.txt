And each of them returned to Rus, to his own patrimony, in peace and love.

In that same year Archbishop Moses, Lord of Novgorod, unexpectedly left the see of Novgorod and withdrew to a monastery. The Novgorodians, assembled in veche, brought the monk Alexei, the lord’s steward, into the lord’s house.

In that same year the monk Alexei was ordained deacon and presbyter by Theodore, Bishop of Tver.

In that same year Theodore, Lord of Tver, laid a marble floor in his cathedral church of the Holy Savior.

In that same year died Prince Ivan Alexandrovich of Smolensk, grandson of Gleb, great-grandson of Rostislav, great-great-grandson of Mstislav, great-great-great-grandson of David, forefather of Rostislav, great-forefather of Mstislav, great-great-forefather of Vladimir Monomakh, son of Vsevolod, son of Yaroslav, son of Vladimir. And after him his son Svyatoslav took the principality in Smolensk.

In that same year Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Kholm, grandson of Michael, great-grandson of Yaroslav, came to Lithuania.

In that same year the people of Smolensk conquered Belaya.

In that same year Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania came with an army to Smolensk, took the city of Mstislavl, and installed his deputies in it.

In that same year Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich sent his son Andrew with a large army to Rzhev, took the city, and installed his deputies in it.

In the year 6868 from Adam (1368 from Christ)

The Most Reverend and Blessed Metropolitan Alexei came from Kiev to Vladimir and then to Moscow.

And Metropolitan Roman came to Tver shamelessly, without informing the Most Reverend Metropolitan Alexei. But nothing came of his desire and design: Theodore, Bishop of Tver, would not meet with him and paid him no honor.

And having stayed a short time in the lands of Tver, and having received what was necessary from the princes, from the boyars of Tver, and from certain others, he returned to Lithuania.

In that same year Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Kholm came from Lithuania and made peace with his brothers, while Prince Vasily Mikhailovich, their uncle, renounced a third of their patrimony. And they divided the volosts.

Amen.
