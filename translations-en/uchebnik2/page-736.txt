On the Grand Prince, How He Marched Against Prince Yuri
In the year 6942 from Adam (1442 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich learned of the betrayal of his uncle Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, that his voivodes with many warriors had taken part in a battle involving his sons, and for this the prince set out against him with a huge army to Galich; but he fled to Beloozero.

And the Grand Prince took and burned the city of Galich, and led the people away into captivity, and did much evil to that land, and returned to Moscow. And Prince Yuri Dmitrievich came to Galich, sent for his sons, and began to rally against the Grand Prince, gathered a large army, and brought the Vyatchans.

Prince Yuri Dmitrievich with His Sons Marched Against Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich

And he marched against the Grand Prince that same spring, and with him went his sons Prince Vasily Kosoy and the two princes Dmitry, Shemyaka and Krasny, with a large army. Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, learning of this, marched to meet them, and with him was Prince Ivan Mozhaysky. And they met in the Rostov land at Saint Nicholas on the Hill on Lazarus Saturday, and a battle began between them; Prince Yuri Dmitrievich won, and the Grand Prince fled to Veliky Novgorod, and Prince Ivan Mozhaysky to Tver; his mother was there too, for she had also fled to Tver to her daughter.

And the Grand Prince sent Andrei Feodorovich Goltyaev to Prince Ivan Mozhaysky, that he should not abandon him but be at one with him. But Prince Ivan refused Andrei, saying to the Grand Prince: “My lord sovereign, wherever I may be, I shall everywhere be your man; but so that I do not now lose my patrimony, and so that my mother does not wander in another’s patrimony, I will not come to your aid, but I shall always be yours.”

And Prince Yuri sent Yakov Zhestov to him, summoning him to himself; and Prince Ivan Mozhaysky set out from Tver to Prince Yuri and met him at the Trinity, and went with him to Moscow.

Prince Yuri Dmitrievich came to Moscow on Wednesday of Holy Week and stood before the city for a week.

And on Wednesday of Bright Week he took Moscow, and seizing the grand princesses, sent them to Zvenigorod, and himself sat upon the grand principality.

The Grand Prince then went from Novgorod to Mologa and to Kostroma, and from there to Nizhny Novgorod.

And Prince Yuri Dmitrievich sent against him two of his sons, the princes Dmitry, Shemyaka and Krasny, and they came to Vladimir with a large army; the Grand Prince had no help from anywhere, and he wished to go to the Horde.
