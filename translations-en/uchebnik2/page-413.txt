That same year Prince Fyodor Glebovich gathered a great army and marched to Murom against Prince Yuri Yaroslavich, drove him out of the city of Murom, entered the city, and himself sat down to rule in Murom.

And the people of Murom rejoiced at him and began to hold to him, and some went with him to the Horde. But Prince Yuri Yaroslavich came to Murom a week after his departure, and gathering the remaining people of Murom, he too went to the Horde to the tsar to seek judgment against him.

And there was a trial between them before the Horde princes, and the principality of Murom fell to Prince Fyodor Glebovich, and Prince Yuri Yaroslavich was handed over to Prince Fyodor Glebovich. He kept him in confinement, and this was very grievous to him. Thus, in hardship and confinement, he died.

That same year Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich made peace with Prince Konstantin Vasilyevich of Suzdal.

That same year Prince Konstantin Vasilyevich of Suzdal died, as a monk, in the schema, and was laid to rest in Nizhny Novgorod in the Church of the Holy Savior that he had built. He reigned for about fifteen years, honorably and sternly guarding his patrimony from powerful princes and from the Tatars.

That same winter his son Prince Andrei went to the Horde to Tsar Janibek, son of Azbyak, with gifts. The tsar honored him and granted him his patrimony, giving him his father’s table—the principality of Suzdal, Nizhny Novgorod, and Gorodets.

That same year the Novgorodians made peace with Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel.

That same year an envoy of the Vladyka of Novgorod came from Constantinople, from the Most Reverend Patriarch Philotheus and from his spiritual son, the Greek Tsar John Kantakouzenos, and brought charters with gold seals concerning levies for appointments, concerning hierarchal church dues, and other various instructions. He also brought cross-patterned vestments to Moisei, Vladyka of Novgorod, and a blessing to all Novgorod.

That same year Vladyka Moisei built a stone Church of St Michael on Skovorodka. That same year Alexander, brother of Dvoryanintsev, became posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6864 from Adam (1364 from Christ)

Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, gave his daughter in marriage to Lithuania, to the son of Kariad, grandson of Gediminas.

That same year Ivan, Bishop of Rostov, died.

That same year the Most Reverend Alexei, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus’, appointed Ignatius Bishop of Rostov.
