And Svyatoslav went up the Oka River, and, arriving, stood at the mouth of the Protva River in Lyubinets Amosov, and while there planned to go to Polotsk. And Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh sent him from Suzdal fourteen thousand armored warriors as aid; but Prince Izyaslav Davydovich and his brother Vladimir Davydovich, learning of this, did not dare follow him, but returned from Dedoslavl.

In the same year Prince Ivanko Yuryevich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, reposed at Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich's on the mouth of the Protva River in Lyubinets
