Prince Gleb sent to Chernigov to Grand Prince Vladimir Davydovich and to his brother Izyaslav Davydovich, and to Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, saying: “I was forced to kiss the precious Cross to the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, and as before, so now I wish to live and act in unity with you; and whoever is a friend to you is a friend to me, and whoever is an enemy to you is an enemy to me”; and so he again strengthened his accord with them according to the old custom.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Suzdal Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh sent another son of his, Prince Rostislav, to the aid of the Olegoviches and Davydoviches against the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich.

And when he halted on the way, ambassadors came to him from the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich with honor and with gifts, summoning him to himself in Kiev and offering him cities and districts. Prince Rostislav Yuryevich, taking counsel with his druzhina, said: “Will my father be angry with me for this, that I do not go to his enemies, the Chernigov princes; for they are enemies both to my grandfather and to my uncles.”

And he went to the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich, and sent word ahead to him; he rejoiced greatly and sent his men to meet him.

And he came to the city of Kiev, and Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich rejoiced over him, and joyfully celebrated and made merry with him, and gave him the so-called City of God, and Mezhibozhye, and Kotelnitsa, and two other cities.

Hearing of this, Prince Gleb Yuryevich was afraid and went to his father, Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh, in Suzdal; and Prince Rostislav occupied Gorodets and entered it, and stationed his governors in the towns.

In the same year the Olegoviches and Davydoviches, the Chernigov princes, made peace with the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich; and because of their brother, Prince Igor Olegovich, they laid aside enmity, saying: “We cannot bring our brother, Prince Igor Olegovich, back to life; may God repay him with heavenly things instead of earthly, and instead of dishonor, with ineffable glory in heaven!”

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev Izyaslav Mstislavich sent his son Prince Yaroslav to Novgorod to rule, and removed his brother Svyatopolk Mstislavich because of his wrongdoings and gave him Vladimir.
