In the same year Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich reposed, named Demetrius in holy baptism, brother of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich; before his death he became a monk and received the monastic rank of schema; and he was laid to rest in Vladimir in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God, the Golden-Topped.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich married his nephew Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich of Rostov to the daughter of his brother-in-law Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich of Chernigov.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Konstantinovich, nephew of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, married, taking as his wife the daughter of Oleg Svyatoslavovich.

In the year 6736 from Adam (the year 1236 from Christ)

Grand Prince Mstislav Mstislavovich of Galich went from Galich to Kiev, and on the way fell ill, and became a monk, and received the monastic rank of schema, and reposed.

In the same year Prince David of Murom became a monk, and received the monastic rank of schema, and reposed.

In the same year a daughter, Feodora, was born to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich.

In the same year Prince Svyatoslav released his wife by agreement; and she went to Murom to a monastery and became a nun.

On the War with the Mordva

That same winter, on the fourteenth day of January, Grand Prince Yuri, and Yaroslav, and the Konstantinoviches Vasilko and Vsevolod went against the Mordva, and Prince Yuri Davydovich of Murom; and they came into the land of Mordva, into Purgas’s domain, and burned and spoiled the grain, and slaughtered the livestock, and burned the villages; those living in Purgas’s domain they cut down with swords without mercy; and others they took captive and sent to their homes; but the Mordva, learning of this, fled into the forests, to their fortifications; and those who did not flee they killed.

In the same year Archbishop Antony went to the Khutyn Monastery of his own will.

In the same year the Yem tribe came into the Novgorod lands with an army on ships; but the posadnik of Novgorod and Ladoga hurried against them with the Ladoga warriors and arrived before the prince and the Novgorodians; and he cut them down; there were two thousand of the attackers, and few of them escaped to their own land.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went to Pskov; but the Pskovians did not let him into the city; and he returned to Novgorod.
