In the same year, Grand Prince Vsevolod Svyatoslavich Chermny, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Oleg, great-great-grandson of Svyatoslav, great-great-great-grandson of Yaroslav, and descendant of the great Vladimir, drove the grandchildren of Rostislav out of Rus, saying: “How can I live with you in peace and love, when you have caught my brothers, beaten them and cruelly abused them, and hanged them like villains in Galich, disgraced everyone, and committed so much evil and so many accursed deeds that now there is no place for you in the Russian land?” In the same year Prince Mstislav Mstislavich set out from Novgorod with posadnik Tverdislav and the Novgorodians for Kiev, and they warred along the Dnieper against the Chernigov towns and Rechitsa, and came to Vyshgorod, fought, and captured Prince David, Prince Rostislav Yaroslavich and his brother Prince Yaropolk, and the people of Vyshgorod opened the city gates. Prince Mstislav Mstislavich entered the city and persecuted his princes; and the Grand Prince of Kiev, Vsevolod Svyatoslavich Chermny, fled from Kiev beyond the Dnieper. He had reigned in Kiev for ten years.

Prince Mstislav Mstislavich with the Novgorodians installed Prince Mstislav Romanovich as Grand Prince in Kiev, and went from Kiev to Chernigov, and stood before the city for 12 days, and they made peace, and dispersed to their homes.

In the same year Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov founded the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God on the former site of the fallen church.

That same summer, a son Dmitry was born to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodich, whose second name was Vsevolod.

In the year 6723 from Adam (1223 from Christ)

A son Dmitry was born to Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov, whose second name was Vladimir.

In the same year Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov sent Pachomius, abbot of the Monastery of Saint Peter, his spiritual father, to Kiev, to Matthew, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, to be consecrated Bishop of Rostov.

He was consecrated in the month of December, and came to Rostov in the month of January.

In the same year Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent Simon, abbot of the Monastery of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God, to Kiev, to Matthew, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, to be consecrated bishop of Suzdal and Vladimir.

And he was consecrated bishop; and he came to Suzdal on the 12th day of February.

In the same year Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich of Rostov founded a stone church on his courtyard in Rostov, of the holy passion-bearers Boris and Gleb.
