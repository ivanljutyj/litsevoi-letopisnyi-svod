On the Fervent Prayer of Metropolitan Cyprian

In Moscow he left Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan Danilovich Kalita. His Grace Metropolitan Cyprian then adorned the throne of the Russian metropolia and labored diligently day by day and hour by hour, not departing from the church, continually offering tearful prayers and bloodless sacrifices to God for the pious great prince and for the Christian army and for all Christians. He also commanded all the church clergy together with the prince, the boyars and all the nobles, and all officials, and all the people, men and women, to fast and read prayers, to forget enmity toward one another, to remember unfeigned brotherly love, and all with one mind, with sorrow in their hearts, humble thoughts, and a pure conscience, to ask God for deliverance from the approaching calamity and to await salvation, remembering the Ninevite repentance of the Israelite kings Hezekiah and Manasseh. And all listened to his good teaching and with great zeal and sincere faith, with tears and sighing from the depths of the heart, all from small to great unanimously began to purify themselves from sins by constant kneeling and repentance, resorting to God’s holy churches. And at home they prayed with exclamations, calling to their aid Almighty God and His most pure Mother, the swift helper in troubles, and the great Russian wonderworkers Peter, Alexis, and Sergius, and all the saints, and they asked to be delivered from the approaching calamities and sorrows.

On the Calling Forth of the Wonder-working Icon of the Mother of God

Our most good and most merciful Christ God, when He hears the turning of His people to Him, is always near those who call upon Him. And as He Himself willed, and His immaculate Mother, the Lady, through the God-pleasing prayers of His saints, they placed a good counsel in his mind. By God’s will Grand Prince Vasily received it, although he was far away on the bank of the great Oka River, and Metropolitan Cyprian, who was at that time in Moscow, received it also. But, thanks to God, the same thought came to both of them: to send to the city of Vladimir for the wonder-working icon of the Mother of God, which the Evangelist Luke painted, so that it might be brought to the reigning city.
