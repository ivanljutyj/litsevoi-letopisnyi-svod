In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went with the Novgorodians to Kolyvan, and they devastated all the Chud land, but did not take the city; they took a ransom, much gold, and returned again to Novgorod.

In the same year Archbishop Mitrofan of Novgorod reposed; and they laid him in the narthex of the Church of Holy Sophia; and on that same day the Novgorodians brought the monk Arseny from the Khutyn Monastery into the bishop’s court.

In the year 6732 from Adam (1232 from Christ)

Cyril the Greek was appointed Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Rus, on the 6th day of the month of January; he was skilled in the study of Divine Scripture and had the gift of teaching.

In the same year the Church of the Saviour in Yaroslavl, in the monastery, was consecrated; its foundation had been laid by the pious Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich, and his son Prince Vsevolod completed it; and it was consecrated by Bishop Cyril.

In the year 6733 from Adam (1233 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich laid the foundation of the stone Church of the Holy Saviour in Nizhny Novgorod. In the same year the Novgorodians drove their prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich of Pereyaslavl out of Novgorod; and he went to Pereyaslavl.

And again the Novgorodians took his son Vsevolod from Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich to themselves in Novgorod for the second time.

In the same year the Novgorodians went to war against the Chud, and returned with a small force, because they were defeated by the Chud.

In the same year Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich learned that the Novgorodians wanted to expel him, and secretly from them, with all his people, he went to Torzhok and began to reign there.

And there came to Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich his father Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich with all his host, and their nephew Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich with the Rostov druzhina, and Prince Mikhail of Chernigov, son of Vsevolod Chermny.

And the Novgorodians sent two men to Yuri in Torzhok with this message: “Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, you sent your son to us, but you yourself leave Torzhok, and do not lay waste the Novgorod lands.”
