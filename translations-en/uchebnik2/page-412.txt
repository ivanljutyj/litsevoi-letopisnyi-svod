In the same year the Archbishop of Novgorod, Lord Moses, sent his envoys to Tsargrad to the blessed and sweet-tongued Philotheus, Ecumenical Patriarch, and to his spiritual son Ivan Kantakouzenos with gifts and much honor, asking for blessing and protection from the injuries and violence inflicted by the metropolitan. In the same year in Novgorod a stone church in the name of Saint Simeon was built.

In the year 6862 from Adam (1362 from Christ)

There came from the Horde, from Tsar Janibek, son of Azbyak, Prince Ivan Ivanovich, grandson of Daniel, granted the great principality of Vladimir. And all the Russian princes who were then in the Horde were released, each to his own patrimony.

In the same winter Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich sat upon the great principality in Vladimir, on the twenty-fifth day of March, on the Annunciation of the Most Pure Theotokos.

In the same year the city of Moscow burned, and thirteen churches burned.

In the same year, because of the hierarchal throne, there arose a turmoil such as had never before been in Rus: in Tsargrad two metropolitans were appointed by the patriarch for the whole Russian land—Alexei and Roman. And there was great enmity between them. From both of them envoys came from Tsargrad to Feodor, Lord of Tver, and the whole priestly order had a very hard time of it.

In that year the most reverend and blessed Alexei, Metropolitan, came from Tsargrad to Moscow, and Roman, Metropolitan, also set out from Tsargrad for Rus.

In the same year Prince Konstantin Vasilievich of Suzdal married his son Prince Boris to the daughter of the Grand Prince of Lithuania, Olgerd Gediminovich.

In the same year in Novgorod three stone churches were built: the Church of the Sign of the Holy Theotokos on Ilyina Street, Saint Demetrius on Lubyanitsa; and the church of the Holy Forty Martyrs collapsed. People then caught fish in the Volkhov by the shore with their hands.

In the year 6863 from Adam (1363 from Christ)

Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Starodubsky passed away and was laid in his patrimony in Starodub. And then his brother Prince Ivan Fedorovich went to the Horde to the tsar.
