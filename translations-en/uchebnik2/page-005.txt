On the twenty-sixth day of the month of August. Tale of the Meeting of the Wonderworking Icon of the Most Pure Lady Our Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, which the divinely inspired Evangelist Luke painted, who himself saw her during her life. On how that divine icon came to the Russian land and in Kiev, and in Vyshgorod in the churches appeared gloriously in the air. And on how it came to the city of Vladimir, whence the icon is called the Vladimir Icon.

And how it came to the God-loving city of Moscow, and wrought for us a wondrous deliverance from the invasion of the godless and unbelieving Tsar Temir-Aksak. And on the founding of the monastery where that most honorable image of the Mother of God was met. Bless, Father!
