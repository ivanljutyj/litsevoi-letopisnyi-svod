"And for this evil I suffered. I violated the cross-kissing oath many times and committed violence against the Russian land. But the Lord God subdued me, and I suffered unbearably for this evil. And now I charge you, my brothers, my children, and all the Swedish land: avoid deceit, all injustice, and all the vanity of this world; for all things vanish like smoke, and pass away like a dream and like a shadow. Do not commit violence—do not attack the Russian land, do not violate the cross-kissing oath, and then you will not perish. But whoever goes against the Russian land and violates the cross-kissing oath, upon him the Lord God will send fire and water, as He punished me for my iniquities, for deceit and injustice, for my hopes and pride, for violence and mercilessness, and for all the evil done by me. And all this the Lord God brought about by His divine providence for my salvation."

In the same year a son, Semyon, was born to Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich.

In the same winter, on the sixth day of December, on the memory of our holy father Nicholas the Wonderworker, the most reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed his vicar, the elder Alexei, as bishop in Vladimir. He loved him greatly and kept him at his side, since he was his vicar. And even during his lifetime he willed that he be made a hierarch, and he blessed him after his death to the great throne as Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus.

Then, having consulted with his spiritual son, Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich, and with his brothers, Prince Ivan Ivanovich and Prince Andrei Ivanovich, with the boyars and nobles, they sent their envoys to Constantinople: from the grand prince, envoys Demenity Davydovich and Yuri Vorobyov; and from the metropolitan, envoys Artemy Korobyin and the Greek Mikhailo Shchebraty. They were to ask that no one be appointed metropolitan over them in Rus except the venerable elder Alexei, who was vicar and was virtuous and honorable in all things, and who was an elder of the most reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus.

In the same year the monk Theodoret was appointed metropolitan by the Patriarch of Tarnovo and came to Kiev.

In the year 6861 from Adam (1361 from Christ)
