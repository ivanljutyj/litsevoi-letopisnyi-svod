Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal and Nizhny Novgorod, with his brothers, Prince Boris Konstantinovich and Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich, and with his sons, gathered a great army and went out against the Horde prince. And he fled beyond the Pyana River.

They, arriving there, beat the remaining Tatars, and drowned others in the Pyana River, and killed yet others in a raid.

But the Horde prince Bulat-Temir, driven by the wrath of God, returned to the Horde with a small retinue and was killed there by Tsar Oziz.

In the same year, in Gorodets, in the monastery of St Lazarus, monks and nuns perished from thunder, and in the villages many people were also killed.

In the same year the Novgorodians sent to Moscow to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich with a petition and gifts, asking for peace. And he concluded peace with them, as had been customary in former times.

And he sent back to them their boyar Vasily Danilovich with his son Ivan, and to Novgorod he sent his governors, and peace and love came.

In the same year the Novgorod posadnik Antsifir died. In the same year they began to build and completed in Novgorod the stone church of the holy chief apostle Peter on Slavna Street.

In the same year the Novgorodians and Pskovians quarreled; the Germans, inhabitants of Vilna, came and began to fight around the town of Izborsk, and seized all the Pskov districts and villages as far as the Velikaya River.

And, crossing the Velikaya River, they came beneath the walls of the city of Pskov, burned the posad, seized and burned all the villages, but did not take the city. At that time there was in the city neither Prince Alexander with posadnik Pantelei nor other brave warriors.

And afterward the Pskovians united with the Novgorodians, marched with an army against the Germans, and many Novgorodian and Pskovian warriors were killed.

And there was killed then the brave Novgorodian warrior Zakharía Davydovich with all his regiment, and the Pskov commander Selilo Slavny. But of the German warriors a countless multitude was killed.

In the same year in Nizhny Novgorod Grand Princess Anastasia, wife of Prince Andrei Konstantinovich, took vows in the holy angelic monastic order.
