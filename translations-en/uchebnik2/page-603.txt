On the procession from Vyshgorod with the icon of the Mother of God

Grand Prince Andrew prayed for a long time, and having finished the supplicatory service, he approached the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, stretched out his hands, touched that wonder-working image of the Mother of God, and taking it as a priceless treasure, went out of the church without informing his father, Grand Prince George, having listened to the flattering counsel of the accursed Kuchkovichi.

And secretly by night they left Vyshgorod with his princess, with boyars and with many people. He took with him also the Vyshgorod presbyter, the priest Nikola, and his son-in-law the deacon Nestor with his wife. And they went, performing supplicatory services along the way. Grand Prince George in that same year departed to God on the fifteenth day of May. And he was buried at the Savior on the Berestovo.

On the miracles from the icon of the Mother of God

Many miracles were performed from the holy icon of the Mother of God then and afterward. There will be a separate account concerning them. Here we shall tell briefly. When they came to the river Vuza, the water in it rose high. And the Grand Prince sent to look for a ford. A man went, sank to the bottom of the river, and did not appear for a long time.

The Grand Prince then began to pray with tears to the Most Pure Mother of God, considering himself guilty that the man had drowned. And after the prayer that man came out of the river on horseback, holding a staff in his hand, having suffered no harm at all in the depths.
