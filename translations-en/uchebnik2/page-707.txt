In the same year, in the month of August, plague began again in Novgorod, Pskov, Torzhok, Tver and throughout the Russian land.

In the year 6928 from Adam (the year 1428 from Christ)

In September a severe pestilence spread throughout the Russian land, especially in Kostroma, Yaroslavl, Yuryev, Vladimir, Suzdal, Pereyaslavl, Galich, Plyos and Rostov. People died, the grain stood uncut in the fields, and there was no one to reap it.

And on the 15th day of the same month of September, on the feast of the Holy Great Martyr Nicetas, snow fell and continued for three days and three nights; it lay four spans deep. Frosts and SEVERE cold began, then a thaw set in. The snow melted, and few were able to reap the grain after the snow had gone. Then famine came after this pestilence throughout the Russian land, and people died of hunger.

In the same autumn, in the month of October, in Veliky Novgorod the Novgorodians began to introduce silver money into trade, and began reselling artugs to the Germans; artugs were used in trade for nine years. Before artugs, marten foreheads were used in trade, and before marten foreheads, squirrel and marten snouts.

In the same autumn, in Pskov and Novgorod, in the month of November, a severe and terrible pestilence began; the whole winter was very warm. Very few people survived in the Russian land after the pestilence and drought.

In the same year Prince Svidrigailo Olgerdovich came from the Hungarian land to the Lithuanian land to live.

In the year 6929 from Adam (the year 1429 from Christ)

In September severe frosts set in. And on the 15th day of the same month snow fell and continued for two days and two nights. A strong storm arose, it became very cold everywhere, and frosts persisted. Then the snow melted, and very little grain had been reaped. Snow fell again, frosts began, and famine came over the whole Russian land.

In the same autumn envoys came to Novgorod from the German land from Master Sylvester: the Veliad commander Gostilo, the Master’s nephew Timofei, and the Rugodiv voivode Eremei — to Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich; and the Novgorodians agreed with them to gather for a council with Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich together with the Novgorodians and the masters.
