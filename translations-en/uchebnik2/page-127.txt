In the year 6683 from Adam (1183 from Christ)

The Novgorodians drove their prince Yuri Andreevich, grandson of Yuri, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, away from themselves, and sent to Prince Mstislav Rostislavich for his son; and Prince Mstislav Rostislavich placed his son Svyatoslav to reign in Novgorod.

In Kyiv at that time Yaroslav Izyaslavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Andrei, called Vladimir Monomakh, ruled on the throne of the grand prince, and Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov attacked him by stealth, entered Kyiv, and took his retinue captive. Grand Prince Yaroslav Izyaslavich himself fled; his princess was taken captive together with his younger son and all his retinue; and he stayed in Kyiv fifteen days, and returned back to Chernigov, taking with him much of Yaroslav’s property. Yaroslav, hearing of this, returned to Kyiv and was angry with the Kievans, saying to them: “By this you have made me dependent on Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov; consider how I may ransom my wife and son.” The Kievans were at a loss what to answer; and the prince imposed tribute on them all—archimandrites, abbots, priests, monks, merchants, and the Latins living in Kyiv, and all the people of Kyiv—and this caused the Kievans much hardship, and he set off for Chernigov.

At that time there was an internecine war in Chernigov between the Prince of Chernigov and Oleg Svyatoslavich, for Oleg Svyatoslavich was seizing Chernigov territories.

Grand Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich of Chernigov made peace with Grand Prince Yaroslav Izyaslavich of Kyiv, and Yaroslav Izyaslavich went back to Kyiv, while Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich went to war against Prince Oleg Svyatoslavich, burned his territories, and, having done much harm, returned to Kyiv.

In the same year Prince Roman Rostislavich with his brothers sent to Grand Prince Andrei Yuryevich Bogolyubsky, asking for help, that he might help Prince Roman Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, to sit on the grand principality in Kyiv, saying: “Let it be known to all whom you install in Kyiv on the grand principality.”

He answered him, saying: “Wait a little, for I have sent to my brothers in Rus; when an answer comes to me from them, then I will answer you as well.”
