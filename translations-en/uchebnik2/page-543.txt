On the third day of the same month of July they went to Saint Anthony the Patriarch, bowed, and received a blessing from him. On the fourth day they went and venerated the image of the great Archangel Michael, who appeared to a youth tending cattle; the youth found much gold by that pillar and was thrown by those monks into the sea.

On the sixth day they went to the All-Pure Mother of God Hodegetria, which in Russian is called the Guide, and after bowing they kissed it with fear and trembling. And they took the pomorisimo and anointed themselves with the so-called radostoma. And they went to the great and honorable monastery of the Pantocrator and kissed the holy board of the Lord, on which, having taken Christ’s body down from the cross, they laid it, and the tears of the All-Pure were imprinted on that board. And there is a jar made of native stone, in which Christ turned water into wine, and in it they keep now and always the holy water of the Epiphany. There too they saw the heads of the holy martyrs Sergius and Bacchus, and of James the Persian.

On the eighth day they also went and venerated the image of Christ that worked the miracle for the merchant Theodore. After this, on the 16th day of the month of July, Michael, bishop of Smolensk, came to Constantinople from Metropolitan Pimen.
