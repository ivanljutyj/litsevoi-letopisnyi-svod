And they placed heavy irons on their hands and feet, and sent them into confinement in the Chud land. Then, after some time, Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh brought them to himself in Suzdal, along with their wives and children from Novgorod, and kept them with him in love and favor.

And the Novgorodians took for themselves from Suzdal Sudila Nezhatich and Strazhdtka, who had fled from Novgorod because of Svyatoslav, Yakun, and his brother Prokopy; and they gave the office of posadnik to Sudila Ivankovich.

And to the Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich they sent their bishop Nifont with their men, saying: “Give us your son Svyatoslav, but we do not want your brother Svyatoslav Olegovich, and we have already driven him out of Novgorod from the principality.”

But Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich sent his son Svyatoslav to reign over them; and he had already passed Chernigov when the Novgorodians again decided to send to Vsevolod Olegovich, saying: “We want neither your son to reign among us in Novgorod, nor your brother, nor your lineage; but we seek the lineage of Vladimir Monomakh to reign among us.”

When Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich of Kiev heard this, he quickly sent many messengers after them and brought back their bishop Nifont with all their men. And the Novgorodians, after deliberating again, said to Vsevolod: “Give us your brother-in-law, Prince Svyatopolk Mstislavich.” But Vsevolod Olegovich did not want to concede Novgorod to the Vladimirovichi Monomakhs, and he summoned his brother-in-law Svyatopolk Mstislavich and placed him under guard in Brest with the bishop and the Novgorodians, saying: “Stay here and do not worry about Novgorod; let the Novgorodians remain as they wish, and let them obtain a prince for themselves wherever they wish.” And he kept the Novgorodians with their bishop at his court for a whole year; he also threw some of them into prison.

In the year 6649 from Adam (1149 from Christ)

The Novgorodians could not endure sitting without a prince, and there were disputes and great turmoil among them, because no grain was coming to them from anywhere; moreover, Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich of Kiev ordered merchants going from Novgorod to Rus to be seized and thrown into cellars.

And there was great sorrow in Novgorod, and the Novgorodians sent to Grand Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh in Suzdal, saying: “Give us Prince Svyatopolk Mstislavich, because we do not want the Olegovichi to reign in Novgorod. If you do not give us your nephew, Prince Svyatopolk Mstislavich, then come to us yourself to reign, or send us your son Rostislav.”

And Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomakh sent them his son Rostislav. He came to them in Novgorod, and they brought him to the bishop’s court.
