We give thanks to the Most Pure Theotokos and for your good deeds. And not only has the wisdom of your writing informed us, but from your bishop Nestor himself we have many times heard many good testimonies about your Nobility, recounted before our humility, before the consecrated and divine council, and before our most sovereign son, the pious Emperor Manuel: that the city called Vladimir, which the holy and blessed Grand Prince Vladimir founded in his own name, you have now from its foundation made great, expanded, and elevated with a multitude of people; in it you have also built the church of the Most Pure Theotokos of Her honorable and glorious Dormition, and adorned it with many precious and holy icons, books, sacred vessels, and all manner of things, gold and silver, and pearls; and you have given it much property and wealth in abundance, and given villages, settlements, and volosts with tributes, and a tithe in the markets, and in grain, and in herds, and a tenth in everything, and from the pud, and weight, and length; and according to the sacred and divine canons and statutes you have dedicated all to the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos; and as your great-grandfather, the holy and blessed Grand Prince Vladimir, bequeathed you to observe, so you promise to continue, and those after you from generation to generation until the end of the world. You do not wish to be under the administration of the bishopric of Rostov and Suzdal, but to renew it as a metropolitan see and to install in it, from us, as metropolitan Theodore, who lives there with your Nobility. For the sake of your city and the holy church which you have built in it to the glory of the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos, and have endowed with all sacred beauties and wealth, you will have a reward from the Lord God and the Most Pure Theotokos; and because you have also raised many other churches and monasteries, for all this you will enjoy ineffable joy in heaven with Christ God, for you have arranged all things in a god-pleasing and wise manner. But to take this city away from the right and truth of the Rostov bishopric and to make it a metropolitan see is impossible. Let your Nobility know, if you have written as we have heard, that this city is not in another country and region, and was not newly built and now added to your principality, but is in the same land and region of Rostov and Suzdal, in which your great-grandfathers and your father were, and which you yourself possess now, in which there has been one bishopric from of old, and the most reverend Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus installs one bishop in all that land at the appropriate time; and that metropolitan himself is appointed by us, the holy and great church, and sent there to the sacred and divine metropolitan throne in Kiev and for all Rus.
