And let us together give praise to God, who works wondrous and glorious deeds that we have seen with our own eyes.”

After this, sensing his swift departure to God, having instructed the brethren in what was profitable and appointed in his place as abbot a man named Nikon, he charged him to keep the traditions and statutes of the monastery. And having partaken of the Holy Mysteries and said, “Lord, into thy hands I commit my spirit,” he reposed, departing to the Lord in the year 6900 from Adam (1400 from Christ), on the twenty-fifth day of September. In all he lived seventy-eight years from birth. And he was laid in the monastery founded by him, and he granted many healings to those who came to him in faith.

That same winter Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich renounced the cross-kissing oath with the Novgorodians and sent his uncle Prince Vladimir Andreevich, grandson of Ivan, great-grandson of Daniel, and his own brother Prince Yury Dmitrievich to Torzhok. And they warred against the Novgorodian volosts, burned them, and did much evil to those living there. At the end of that same winter, at the beginning of spring, the Novgorodians took Ustyug the Great, warred against and burned volosts and villages, shed much blood, cut down people with swords, and led others away captive. They did much evil to Christians and returned to their own lands. In that same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich renounced the cross-kissing oath with Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich, grandson of Vasily, great-grandson of Michael, great-great-grandson of Andrew, and went to the Horde to Tsar Tokhtamysh with great honor and gifts. And he began to ask that the grand principality of Nizhny Novgorod, which was under Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich, be granted to his own grand principality, Moscow. And the prince gave a great bribe to the tsar’s princes, so that they would take thought for him and intercede with Tsar Tokhtamysh. They took much gold and silver and great gifts. Tsar Tokhtamysh also took much gold and silver. And Tsar Tokhtamysh gave the grand principality of Nizhny Novgorod, which had been under Grand Prince Boris Konstantinovich, to Moscow, to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.

And Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich went from the Horde to Rus from Tsar Tokhtamysh with joy, together with the tsar’s envoy, and came to Kolomna. And from there he himself went to Moscow, while he sent the envoy of Tsar Tokhtamysh to Nizhny Novgorod with his boyars.
