In the same year the Muslims came in war to Constantinople with a great army, and they ravaged many regions and returned with great booty to their own land.

In the same year there came from the Greeks, from Constantinople, His Eminence Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all the Russian land, a Greek by birth.

In the same year the Lithuanians ravaged the Germans and returned with great booty to their own land.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich came from the Horde from the Tsar and made peace with his brother, Prince Andrey Alexandrovich of Gorodets.

In the same year His Eminence Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, went to the Horde to the Tsar, and in the same year he returned from the Horde to Rus.

...And Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, together with the Tatars, did much evil to the land of Novgorod; and the Novgorodians submitted to him, and Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich began to reign among them.

In the same year His Eminence Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, according to his custom went around all the Russian land, teaching, giving instruction, and making corrections.

Then Metropolitan Maximus came to Novgorod, and Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich met him with great honor. Afterwards Metropolitan Maximus was in Pskov, and there he was shown great honor.

In the same year Prince Andrey Alexandrovich of Gorodets brought a tsarevich from the Horde against his elder brother, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich.

The Tatars dispersed with their detachments in different directions, but Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich gathered a multitude of troops and marched against them; and the tsarevich fled to the Horde, while Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich captured Andrey’s boyars.

In the year 6794 from Adam (1294 from Christ)

Prince Roman of Bryansk came in war to Smolensk and burned the settlement around the fortress, and attacked the fortress, and ravaged districts and villages, and departed to his own land.

In the same year, with the blessing of Bishop Simeon of Tver, Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, together with his mother, Grand Princess Aksinya, consecrated anew the cathedral church that had formerly been of Cosmas and Damian; and they consecrated it in the name of the Holy Savior, and now it is the Church of the Transfiguration; and they made it of stone.
