And so Theodore the monk was appointed bishop of Rostov. This Theodore received the holy monastic habit in Kiev, in the Caves Monastery; he was very bold and shameless, and he was not ashamed before prince or boyar, for he was very strong in body, had a clear tongue and grandiloquent speech, and a malicious mind; and all feared him and trembled, for no one could withstand him; some said of him that he was from a demon, while others called him a sorcerer.

In the year 6679 from Adam (1179 from Christ)

Theodore came to Rus, to Rostov, for the bishopric, and having come to the city of Rostov, he sat on the throne. But Grand Prince Andrew Yuryevich did not wish to listen to him, but ordered him to go to Kiev to Constantine, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus; he, however, refused, according to what is written: “He did not desire blessing, and it departed from him; for when a man is permitted to fall into the pit of destruction, before all else his mind is taken from him, and humble wisdom.”

Prince Andrew Bogolyubsky, who regarded him with love, urged him to go to Kiev to the metropolitan; but he, not wishing appointment from the Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, also did not desire blessing, but said: “It was not the metropolitan who appointed me, but the patriarch in Constantinople; for I have already been appointed and blessed for the bishopric of Rostov and for the cities belonging to that bishopric by the Ecumenical Patriarch. From whom else should I seek appointment and blessing?” And so he sat untroubled on the throne of the Rostov bishopric. Grand Prince Andrew Yuryevich gave him much good counsel, but he not only did not listen to him, but reproached him and laid many insults upon him; he also shut all the churches in the city of Vladimir, and there was no church singing in all the city of Vladimir, nor in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God of the Golden Dome, in which stands the wonder-working icon of the Most Pure Mother of God; likewise in other cities and districts he shut many churches, and there was no singing anywhere.

After this he began to seek out treasures from the rich and take them, and he also tortured many princes and boyars and took their property.

After this they pointed out to him a certain very rich princely chamberlain, and he soon ordered him brought before him; but he did not submit, so the bishop ordered him tortured, and thus took much of his wealth; and unable to endure it, that chamberlain said an insulting word to Bishop Theodore, and he ordered him crucified upside down.

And so he imprisoned some, tortured others, taking their property; to others he burned the hair on their heads, their beards, and their eyes with candles; to some he cut off hands and feet; to some tongue, nose, ears, and lips; others he crucified on walls and boards; and some he cut in half.
