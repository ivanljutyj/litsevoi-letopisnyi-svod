Timur sends to him, demanding tribute and submission.

One of his chosen men said to Tsar Bayezid: “Listen to me, my lord! Give him as much as he demands, and then he will quickly depart to another place.” He answered: “It is better that I either conquer and gain everything, or die bravely.” His adviser said to him: “Then it may happen that you will suffer in both cases.” But Bayezid did not listen to him or to his other advisers, and, gathering those subject to him in the east and the west, and also the Serbian king Stefan, he went against Timur. And there was a battle. Timur won, and the Turkish Tsar Bayezid was captured and taken prisoner. His adviser came to him and said: “Do you see, my lord, have not my words come true?” And he wept bitterly that he had not accepted his advice. This happened in the year 6911 from Adam (the year 1411 from Christ).

About Timur Aksak

But Timur went southward to Egypt, and along the way he destroyed the wall of Damascus and killed its inhabitants.

And he decided to go to Jerusalem. But they said to him: “It has long been known that anyone who sets his hand against Jerusalem is punished by God.” Then the accursed one, abandoning the thought of a campaign against Jerusalem, passed through all the East, and he ordered the inhabitants there who did not submit to him to be buried in pits with their wives and children, given to beasts to be torn apart, and subjected to various executions.

Seeing the multitude of remaining weeping children, the godless Timur did not take pity on them, but ordered them to be trampled by horses, saying: “I free them from the malice and burdens of this world.” O merciless and inhuman, accursed, thrice-cursed Timur, who did such things!

And he came to a certain kingdom that had Iron Gates on the seashore and was inaccessible because of high mountains. There was only one path there, and a fortress had been placed there guarding the path, and iron gates.

Ambassadors from the king of that kingdom came to him. But Timur, having drunk his fill of blood, pretended before the ambassadors to be completely ill, as if at death’s door. And, having begun to speak with them, he began to vomit blood, as though dying.

And orders were given to release the ambassadors, saying that Timur had supposedly died. They went back joyfully and reported to their king.

But Timur at night rushed headlong forward, found an unprotected place and took it; he destroyed the kingdom and captured the king.
