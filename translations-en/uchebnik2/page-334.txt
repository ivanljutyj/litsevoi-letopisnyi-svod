In the same year Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Konstantin, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and his brother Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Uglich went to the Horde to the tsar, together with their wives, and the tsar showed them honor.

In the same year the tsar released from the Horde Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov and his brother Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Uglich with honor.
