A Brief Tale of the Blessed Grand Princess Evdokia,
in monasticism Euphrosyne, Wife of the Praiseworthy
Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy

Who will be able to describe in words the power of the Lord or tell of all His wonders, how great is His mercy from generation to generation toward those who fear Him and do His holy will? And as it is set forth in the narrative, that originally in the Russian land the foundation of piety and the confirmation of the Orthodox faith was the God-chosen autocrat, equal to the apostles, Grand Prince Vladimir, and before him was his grandmother, the holy blessed Grand Princess Olga, renowned for her wisdom. And a great multitude of people of their great realm, male and female, became followers of a like observance of piety and God-pleasing life; the first among them were the rulers themselves, and among them were many righteous ones glorified by God, whose memory shines in the world like luminaries. From them descended also the praiseworthy holy and Christ-loving Grand Princess Evdokia, who was the wife of the blessed and praiseworthy Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, called Donskoy, daughter of Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, striving in all things to conform to the God-pleasing life of her sovereign and virtuous holy spouse, living with him with one mind according to the commandments of the Lord, bearing him sons and daughters and raising them in piety. They lived in marriage for 22 years.

After the repose of her spouse, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the princess spent her life in labors for God, mortifying her flesh by abstinence, preserving chastity and purity of soul, guarding also bodily purity with all humble-mindedness, filling her God-pleasing life with love of the poor, having abandoned all vainglory, and by all this she was able to please God.

On the Appearance of the Angel of the Lord

And the princess labored diligently with fervent prayers to God for herself, for her children, and for the whole realm, and the Lord in every way fulfilled her petitions. Once, because of the princess’s virtues, the Lord sent to her His angel, who announced to her her departure from the world. Seeing the angel of God, she could not utter a single word because of the light emanating from him, and she spent many days in silence, speaking with no one.
