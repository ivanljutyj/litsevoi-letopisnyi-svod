He lived in the monastery for quite a long time. Then Metropolitan Theognost took him to his court; the saint served as his vicar for 12 years and three months. Even during his lifetime Metropolitan Theognost appointed Saint Alexius bishop of Vladimir, and he remained bishop for 4 years. After the repose of Theognost, he was appointed metropolitan in Constantinople by the blessed Patriarch Philotheus; he was then 60 years old, he remained in the rank of metropolitan 24 years, in all he lived 85 years, and he was in monastic tonsure 60 years.

In the year 6939 from Adam (1439 from Christ)

During the reign of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, under the most holy Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, the roof of a church collapsed during the sacred liturgy; the priest had not yet come out of the altar at that time, but remained unharmed; the site of that church was cleared.

And they began excavations, wishing to build a new stone church, and while digging they found the sacred body of Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, and his garments were preserved incorrupt. On the 24th day of the month of May they built the church and placed his holy body in the church in the chapel of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Theotokos on the right side, where it remains to this day, pouring forth healings to all who come with faith.

The Tale of Mityai

After the repose of the blessed Alexius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, his hierarchal place was taken by a certain Archimandrite Michael, called Mityai, who performed strange and incomprehensible actions: he clothed himself in the full metropolitan vestments, placed on himself the white klobuk and the metropolitan’s mantle with its sources and tablets, the metropolitan’s golden cross with a paraman adorned with gold and pearls, took the metropolitan’s golden staff and the metropolitan’s seal, and appointed himself to the rank of metropolitan.

Before this, Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich had asked Metropolitan Alexius to bless that Archimandrite Mityai for the metropolitanate of All Rus after his death. But Alexius did not wish to do this, since Mityai had only recently been in the monastic rank, saying: “The apostle affirms: ‘With other virtues this also is mentioned, but it is not the rule, lest, having grown proud, one fall into condemnation and the devil’s snares.’ The same apostle also says: ‘They first fall into temptation, but afterwards serve blamelessly.’ Archimandrite Mityai has only recently been in the monastic rank, and therefore it is necessary for him to pass through probation in monasticism and to learn good deeds and manners.
