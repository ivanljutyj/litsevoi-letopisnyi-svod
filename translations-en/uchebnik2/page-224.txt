At this was also Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich with his children Vasilko and Vladimir.

And the prince arranged a great feast, and honored the bishop, and the boyars, and treated all the people, and distributed alms to the poor and needy; for such was the custom of that prince—above all, to have love and mercy toward all.

In the same year Grand Prince Konstantin Vsevolodovich summoned his sons Vasilko and Vsevolod, and all the nobles and boyars, and all his counsellors, and began to speak to them thus: “All people are passers-by in this vain life: today born, tomorrow grown, and behold gray hair, old age, and death; and after death will be the dread judgment of the Savior, at which each will receive according to his deeds: some Paradise, others torment; such is all this temporary, vain life. All people from youth to old age, and to death, pass on, beginning at one end and ending at the other. Human life is like a dream vision: as a dream vision, good or bad, a man sees, but on waking remembers and understands little, and it all becomes nothing; so too all human life becomes nothing, like a dream, like smoke. For today a man is alive, and tomorrow dead. And behold, I see how quickly my end approaches. And these little children of mine I entrust to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother; and to my brother and lord Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich; let him be to them in place of me. And you, my princes, and boyars, and nobles, my lords, do not forget these children. And you, my children, have the fear of God in you; and keep the commandments of God; and do not forsake the holy churches; and honor and love the hierarchs, priests, and monks; and listen to and love bookish instruction; and obey and honor your elders; and live among yourselves in love and peace, and keep peace and love with all; and do not covet what is another’s, and do not intrude into others’ domains, but be content with your own; and from this property of yours bring a portion to God, and have mercy on the poor, and supply the needy with necessities, do not despise travelers, clothe the naked, help those in need, and do everything for the glory of God.”

After these words Grand Prince Konstantin stood up, and prayed before the image of God, and embraced his son Vasilko, and kissed him, and told him to reign in Rostov, and sent him to Rostov; likewise he embraced his second son Vsevolod, and blessed him to reign in Yaroslavl, and sent him to Yaroslavl; and said: “Go, my children, having within you the fear of God and observing His commandments, and God will preserve and save you.”
