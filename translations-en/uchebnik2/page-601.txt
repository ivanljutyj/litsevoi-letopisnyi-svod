On the 26th day of August.

The Tale of the Meeting of the Wonderworking Icon of our Most Pure Lady, the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, which the God-proclaiming Evangelist Luke painted, who himself saw her during her lifetime. How that divine icon came to the Russian land.

And in Kiev and in Vyshgorod it appeared gloriously in the air in the churches. And how it came to the city of Vladimir, whence the icon is called Vladimirskaya. And how it came to the God-loving city of Moscow, and wrought for us a wondrous deliverance from the invasion of the godless and unbelieving Tsar Temir-Aksak. And about the founding of the monastery where that most honorable image of the Mother of God was met. Bless, father!

Everyone knows how difficult and beyond our worthiness it is to praise our Most Holy, Pure, and All-Blessed Lady, the Mother of God, more honorable than all the heavenly hosts. Even the angels themselves could not devise how worthily to sing Her praises, much less we who are earthly! With fervent faith, as we are able, and as the Mother of God herself is pleased to help us, let us truly and with fitting splendor remember her and, celebrating divinely, keep festival for her most glorious, ineffable, and wondrous miraculous benefits and great mercy toward the Christian race. This applies also to this present feast of the Mother of God, whom in Her lifetime the God-proclaiming author of the Gospel of Christ, the wise Apostle Luke, painted. He painted her beautiful, depicting in the image her appearance and other likenesses: of middle stature, her holy and grace-filled face slightly rounded and rather elongated, and the Infant resting graciously on the right near the most pure face of her eternal Child, our Lord Jesus Christ; the fingers of her God-receiving hands were refined in their slenderness. Having completed this whole holy icon most beautifully, he brought it to the Mother of God herself. She, casting her most merciful eyes upon the icon, said joyfully: “My grace is with it.” And she spoke her ancient prophecy: “Behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed.” And by this unchanging promise the ineffable mercy of the Mother of God has reached even to our Russian people.

On the Transfer of the Wonderworking Icon of the Mother of God from Tsargrad to Kiev

Just as God from the beginning created the sun in the heavens, so that it should not shine in one place only, but should shine, going around the universe, and illumine the whole world under heaven with its rays, so also the image of the Most Pure Mother of God does not pour forth various miracles and healings in one place only. But going around all the countries under heaven, it enlightens the whole world, delivers from misfortunes, and heals from various ailments.
