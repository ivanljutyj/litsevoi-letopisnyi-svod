In the same year the Novgorodians besieged the city of Russa. In the same year the Novgorodians founded a stone church of the Holy Forty Martyrs.

In the same year the Lithuanians took Lovot; the Novgorodians pursued them, and a fierce battle broke out between them, and the Novgorodians recaptured part of the captives from them.

In the year 6709 from Adam (the year 1209 from Christ)

Grand Prince Igor of Chernigov, son of Svyatoslav, grandson of Oleg, reposed. In the same year the Church of the Dormition of the Mother of God in Vladimir, in the Knyaginin Monastery, was consecrated by Bishop John on the 9th day of September.

Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, sent his son Yaroslav to reign in Pereyaslavl Rusky; his brothers Konstantin, Yuri, and Vladimir saw him off.

In the same year Grand Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich of Chernigov reposed. Mitrofan was installed as archbishop in Great Novgorod on the 3rd day of July. Then too the city of Russa was rebuilt.

In the same year Princess Fevronia, spouse of Prince Mikhail, reposed and was laid in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Suzdal.

In the same year Princess Efrosinia, spouse of Prince Boris, reposed in Kideksha and was laid in the Church of the Holy Martyrs Boris and Gleb beside her father and mother.

In the same year Grand Prince Rurik Rostislavich of Kiev rose against Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich and brought the Olegovichi to Kiev to his aid, and so began to gather an army.

When Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich heard of this, he first hastened to muster the Galich and Vladimir regiments, and sent to Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, and swiftly entered the Rus land. The Vladimirians, the Black Klobuks, and the Mandiriski withdrew from Grand Prince Rurik Rostislavich of Kiev, and the warriors gathered and came to Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich; people from all the Kiev cities also gathered to Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich.

And so with all these forces Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich secretly and swiftly approached the city of Kiev.

The Kievans opened to him the Podol Gate in the Kopyrev End, and so Grand Prince Roman Mstislavich of Galich entered the Podol of the city of Kiev, and sent to the Hill to Grand Prince Rurik Rostislavich of Kiev and to the Olegovichi and to Vladimirk.
