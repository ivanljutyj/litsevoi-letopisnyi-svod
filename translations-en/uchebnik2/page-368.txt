In the same year a son, Andrew, was born to Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich of Moscow.

In that year the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos in Moscow was consecrated, on the fourth day of the month of August, by Prokhor, Bishop of Rostov; it had been founded and begun by the Most Reverend and blessed Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus.

The Most Reverend Isaiah, Patriarch of Constantinople, appointed Theognostos, a Greek, as Metropolitan of Kiev and all the Russian land.

In the same year he came to the great throne, to the metropolis in Kiev and to all Rus, and went from Kiev through many cities.

In the same year he came to Vladimir and to the glorious city of Moscow, to the Dormition of the Most Pure Theotokos and to the wonderworker’s tomb of Peter, and sat in his place, and began to live in his courtyard; and the mercy of God was with him, and the prayer and blessing of the Most Pure Theotokos and of the great wonderworker Peter. But to many other princes it was not very pleasing that the city of Moscow had a metropolitan living in it. In the same year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich of Moscow and Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav Yaroslavich, went to the Horde, and the Novgorodians sent from Novgorod to the Tsar their own envoy, Theodore Kolesnitsa.

Tsar Azbyak received them all with honor and gave the great principality of Vladimir to Prince Ivan Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and added other principalities to Moscow for him.

And to Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, he gave the great principality of Tver. And the Novgorod envoy Theodore Kolesnitsa he favored according to their petition, and ordered them together with him to seek Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav.

In the same year the earth quaked in Novgorod. In the same year Varsonofy, Bishop of Tver, departed this life.

In the same year the German city of Yuryev burned entirely, and their shrines, that is, their churches, and their palaces collapsed, and 2,530 Germans burned, and four Russians.

In the same year Prokhor, Bishop of Rostov, departed this life. In the same year the Most Reverend Theognostos, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Anthony as Bishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl.

In the same year Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich, grandson of Alexander, came from Tsar Azbyak out of the Horde with favor and great honor to the great principality of Vladimir, and sat on the great principality in Moscow; and the throne of Vladimir and many other principalities Tsar Azbyak added to him.
