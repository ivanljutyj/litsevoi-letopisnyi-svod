In the same year Grand Prince Vsevolod Chermny Svyatoslavich installed his son Prince Michael in Pereyaslavl.

In the same year Rurik Rostislavich, grieving over the Grand Principality of Kiev, allied with Mstislav Romanovich and set out with them with an army, with his sons, nephews, and Cumans, and, having come, he drove Vsevolod Chermny out of Kiev, and his son Prince Michael out of Pereyaslavl.

And Rurik sat in Kiev, and installed his son Vladimir in Pereyaslavl.

In the same winter Vsevolod Chermny, having allied with his brothers, nephews, and Cumans, went against Kiev; but Rurik could not fight him and shut himself up in Kiev.

They stood about the city for three weeks and returned, having failed to do any harm to the city, but they captured the districts and villages.

Then Prince Vladimir Igorevich went from Galich to Vladimir, drove Daniel Romanovich out of Vladimir, and installed his brother in it.

In the same winter there was a sign in the sun on the 28th day of February: the whole sun disappeared, and only a small part of it remained, the size of a one-day-old moon, and it stood thus in the sky from noon until evening; and many people gathered, looked, marveled, and prayed to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, that God would turn that sign to good.

On the same day Constantine came to his father from Novgorod; his brothers met him on the Shedaksha River—Yuri, Yaroslav, and Vladimir with Ioann—and all his father’s men and the whole city, and they kissed him affectionately.

And so he entered the city to his father, bowed to him, and his father embraced him; and there was joy in the city of Vladimir, and he stayed several days with his father in gladness.

In the same year Prince Constantine Vsevolodovich left his father Vsevolod Yuryevich for Novgorod.

In the year 6715 from Adam (the year 1215 from Christ)

In Novgorod they built the church of the holy apostle and evangelist Luke on Lubyanitsa.

On the Capture of Kiev and Other Cities by the Olgovichi

In the year 6715 from Adam (the year 1215 from Christ) the Olgovichi again set out against Rurik to Kiev: Vsevolod Chermny with his brothers and his nephews, and the Svyatopolchichi from Turov and Pinsk.
