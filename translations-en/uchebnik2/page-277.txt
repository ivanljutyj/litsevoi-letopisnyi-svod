It was then the Sabbath day, and the sun was rising; and both armies met. The Germans and the Chud, drawn up in a wedge, broke through the Novgorod troops; and there was a fierce and great battle with the Germans and the Chud; and the air shook from breaking spears, and there was a ringing from striking swords; and the frozen lake trembled; and the ice could not be seen, for it was all covered with blood.

And this I heard from an eyewitness; he told me that he saw regiments of God in the air, coming to the aid of Grand Prince Alexander. And Alexander conquered by the power of God, and of St Sophia, and of the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb, for whom our people also shed their blood. And the enemies turned to flight, and they cut them down, pursuing them as if through the air, and there was nowhere for them to flee; and they drove them seven versts across the ice to the Subolich shore; and 500 Germans fell, and countless Chud. And they took alive 50 Germans, noble men and strong commanders, and brought them to Novgorod. And some drowned in the water, and others were sorely wounded and fled. This battle was on the 5th day of April, the day of the Praise of the Most Holy Mother of God, the day of the memory of the holy martyr Claudian. Here God glorified Grand Prince Alexander before all the armies, as Joshua at Jericho. And the Germans had said, “We will take Grand Prince Alexander alive,” yet they themselves were given by God into Alexander’s hands; and no adversary at all was found against Alexander in battle.

Grand Prince Alexander returned with glorious victory; and there were many captives with his army; they led them beside the horses; they called the captives knights. And when Grand Prince Alexander drew near the city of Pskov, the abbots and priests in festive vestments met him with a procession of the Cross; and a multitude of people before the city glorified the Lord and Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavich: “Lord, You helped the meek David to overcome the foreigners, and likewise our faithful prince; with the weapon of the Cross You freed the city of Pskov from foreign and alien peoples, by the hands of Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavich.”

And the name of Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavich became famous in all lands, from the Varangian Sea to the Pontic Sea, and to the Khupozh Sea, and to the Tiberian country, and to the mountains of Ararat, and beyond the Varangian Sea, and to the Arabian mountains; even to great Rome the fame of him spread; thousands and thousands of thousands learned of him. And so Alexander came to Novgorod with great victory.
