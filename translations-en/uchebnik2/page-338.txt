In the same year, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-forefather of Yaroslav, great-great-great-forefather of Great Vladimir, was tonsured into the holy angelic image and the schema and reposed; and he was carried to Pereyaslavl and laid in the Church of the Holy Saviour; he ruled for 18 years. His sons: Prince Ivan of Pereyaslavl.

And after him his brother, Prince Andrei Alexandrovich, sat on the grand principality in Vladimir.

In the same year Grand Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov reposed, grandson of Vasilko, great-grandson of Konstantin, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, forefather of Vladimir Monomakh, great-great-forefather of Vsevolod, great-great-great-forefather of Yaroslav Vladimirovich.

And after him his brother, Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Uglich, sat on the principality in Rostov.

And in Uglich his son, Prince Alexander Konstantinovich, grandson of Boris, sat on the principality.

And in Yaroslavl Prince Fyodor Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Davyd, sat on the principality.

In the same year Prince Mikhailo Yaroslavich of Tver married; he took the daughter of Prince Dmitry Borisovich of Rostov and was crowned in the Cathedral of the Holy Archangel Michael in the cathedral Church of the Holy Saviour by Bishop Andrei of Tver; and there was great joy in Tver.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich of Vladimir, grandson of Yaroslav, married; he took the daughter of Vasilko of Rostov.

In the same year Vasily, Bishop of Ryazan, reposed.

In the same year the Swedes came and set up a town in the Karelian land.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich sent Prince Roman Glebovich with the Novgorodians, and Yuri Mikhailovich, and Andrei the tysyatsky, to the Swedish town. And when they came there on Praise Week, many Novgorodians were struck down from the city walls; Ivan Klekovich, a great and brave and glorious man, was shot from the city wall, and many were wounded; then a strong thaw set in, and there was great need for both people and horses; and they barely returned and came home, and many were dead, and Ivan died from that wound.

In the year 6803 from Adam (1303 from Christ)

Grand Prince Andrei Alexandrovich went to the Horde to the Tsar with his princess. In the same year the whole city of Tver burned down.
