He so loved the poor and the lepers that he would gaze upon them with his own eyes for a long time, and sometimes even touch them with his hands, and speak with them gently, quietly, and mercifully, and comfort them, and feed them, and satisfy them.

In the year 6668 from Adam (1168 from Christ)

The holy cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God was completed with a single dome in the new city of Vladimir, which Grand Prince Andrew Bogolyubsky of Rostov and Suzdal had built, and he gilded its dome. In accordance with his virtue and his faith in the Most Pure Mother of God, the Lord God brought to him craftsmen of every kind from all lands, and he adorned it wondrously, better than other churches, with many wondrous icons, and with golden and silver church vessels set with precious stones and pearls, and with many books, and with hangings and curtains, and he wondrously furnished it with all sorts of sacred objects. In it he also placed the wondrous icon of the Most Pure Mother of God, which they say is by the hand of Luke the Evangelist, and which had been brought by ship from Constantinople to his father in Kiev together with the Pirogoshcha. He took it from Kiev and brought it from his father, and he set upon it thirty grivnas of gold, besides silver, precious stones, and pearls. And he gave to the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God and to Her holy cathedral church of the Dormition, which he had adorned most wondrously, much wealth and property, and districts, and purchased settlements with their tributes, and the best villages with their tributes, and a tenth part of the market revenues, and of grain, and of herds, and a tenth part of everything; for here he wished to establish not only a great but also a sacred and divine metropolitan see, and to confirm the hierarchal ordinances and dues according to the holy Greek canons and the statute of the blessed and holy Grand Prince Vladimir, so that what had been established and confirmed by the Lord God and His holy apostles, and by the holy fathers, and by pious Orthodox emperors, and hallowed and dedicated by the holy and blessed Grand Prince Vladimir to the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, the Theotokos, might remain forever unshaken and unchanged.

Who can tell of the virtue of this great Prince Andrew, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, and of his gentleness, meekness, humility, and love for the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and of his service in the Lord to people, the poor, and the wretched, and of his knowledge of readings, chants, and prayerful words? For the delights of this present and fleeting life were not dear to him, but those joys and delights that are with Christ God after death; but this was hateful to the hater and enemy of mankind, the devil, and also to his servants—crafty, evil, and envious people.

In the same year the Church of the Savior was founded in Vladimir. In the same year there was a dispute with Bishop Leon, because he had been installed unlawfully.
