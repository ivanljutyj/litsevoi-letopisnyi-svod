The Reign of Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky in Kiev

And then Prince Yuri Dolgoruky of Vladimir entered Kiev with joy and gladness, and sat there in the great principality, thankfully praising God.

And he placed his eldest son, Prince Andrei the God-loving, in the great principality in Vyshgorod; and he placed his son Prince Boris in the great principality in Turov; and he placed his son Prince Gleb in the great principality in Pereyaslavl; and he placed his son Prince Vasilko in the great principality in Porosye.

In that same year the Polovtsians came and returned home with a great multitude of captives; and when they were already in the Steppe, they grew careless: the Berendei overtook them and came upon them at early dawn, when they were sleeping, and attacking them in their sleep, they killed many and took others captive.

In that same year Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomakh of Kiev went to Kanev for a princely congress against the Polovtsians; and when they assembled at Kanev, the Polovtsians began to ask for their brothers and the other Polovtsian princes whom the Berendei had captured.

And Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky of Kiev sent to the Berendei, asking them to hand over all the Polovtsian princes held captive by them; but the Berendei were angered and said: “We shed our blood for the Russian land, fighting the Polovtsians, and we would be glad if we had defeated them all; how then have you thought to give these back to their kin in their land?”

And the Polovtsians, hearing this, were offended, and taking many gifts from Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, but not making peace, they went home.

In that same year the Polovtsians came and fought against Deminsk, and came to Pereyaslavl, and did much evil: they killed some and took others alive captive, and returned home.

In that same year Prince Yuri Dolgoruky sent Prince Yuri Yaroslavich with Zhiroslav and with Vyacheslav’s grandsons against Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich to the city of Peresopnitsa; and arriving, they drove him from Peresopnitsa to Lutsk.

And Prince Yuri ordered his son-in-law, Grand Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich of Galicia, to go there against him; and Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich left his brother Prince Yaroslav Izyaslavich in Lutsk, while he himself went to Poland; and Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich and Prince Vladimir accomplished nothing against him and turned back.

In that same year the Princess, wife of Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, came to Kiev from Novosil. In that same year Grand Prince Rostislav Mstislavich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, came to Kiev to his uncle Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky and was received by him with honor.
