Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver summoned to himself Prince Afanasy Danilovich and the Novgorod boyars and began to negotiate with them about confirming an oath according to their ancient custom. They, however, did not wish to give such an oath on their own behalf.

He, falling into a rage, seized them all and sent them to Tver. And all the other people he sold off as it happened, and their horses, and their armor, and all their weapons, and all their craftsmen he took to Tver, and he laid waste Torzhok.

And in Novgorod he installed his own governors and bestowed the Novgorod office of posadnik by his own authority upon Mikhail Klimentievich and Ivan Dmitrievich. This was on the 5th day of the month of February.

In the same year Andrei, Bishop of Tver, left his bishopric and went to a monastery.

Lacuna in the chronicle (three years)

And some said to Tsar Azbyak: “If his son is killed, then his father will never come to you.” And Tsar Azbyak ordered his son released.

In the same year Kavgady sent many Tatars to intercept Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver, to put him to death wherever they found him. In that same summer, in the month of August, Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver went to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak.

In the year 6827 from Adam (the year 1327 from Christ)

Grand Prince Mikhail Yaroslavich of Tver came to the Horde to the Tsar on the 6th day of the month of September, at the mouth of the Don River by the Sea of Surozh, where the Don River flows into the sea, and that sea is called the Surozh Sea, on the 6th day of September, on the feast of the great miracle of the holy Archangel Michael; and according to custom he gave gifts to all the princes and princesses, and afterward to the Tsar himself and the Tsaritsa.

Mikhail was in the Horde with the Tsar for a month and a half, and Kavgady ceaselessly slandered him to the Tsar. The Tsar, having summoned his princes, said to them: “Judge Mikhail Yaroslavich with Yuri Danilovich, and, having judged, tell me.”

And the princes sat in judgment, and began to judge Mikhail with Yuri, and all helped Yuri, and laid many charges upon Mikhail. If Mikhail answered anything, they let his words pass by. And whatever words were true, they despised them all, but listened diligently to the words of Kavgady, for he was both judge and accuser, and took everything upon his soul, but made everyone believe him. Then they dispersed, and ordered them to be brought to trial in the following week.
