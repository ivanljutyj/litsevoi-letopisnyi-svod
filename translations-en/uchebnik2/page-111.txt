In the same year Prince Svyatoslav Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav and great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, with his brothers, and with the Smolenskians, the Polotskians, and the Suzdalians, went to Rusa.

The Novgorodians, however, gathered with Yakov Stanislavich and went against them; but they, not waiting for the Novgorodian host to come to them, returned home, having done much evil to the people of Rusa.

In the year 6676 from Adam (1176 from Christ)

Grand Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich of Kiev went against the Polovtsians with his brothers, the Izyaslavichi: with Yaroslav and Yaropolk; and with him were Prince Rurik Rostislavich and the regiment of his brother David, because Prince David himself was unwell; and Prince Gleb Yuryevich, and his brother Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich of Pereyaslavl, and Prince Svyatopolk Yuryevich, grandson of Bryachislav and great-grandson of Svyatopolk Izyaslavich, and the Chernigov princes Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich and Prince Yaroslav his brother, and Prince Oleg Svyatoslavich, grandson of Oleg Izyaslavich, and his brother Prince Vsevolod.

At that same time, on that campaign, Prince Mstislav Vsevolodkovich of the Mstislavichi died. And coming to their camps on the Ugra River, they took their wagons, and overtook them themselves by the Black Forest, and some they killed and others they took captive; and they took such a countless multitude of captives that all the warriors had more than enough, and so they returned home.

At that same time Satan put hatred into the Russian princes against Grand Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich of Kiev, since they suspected that, secretly from them, he had sent his regiments ahead on a surprise foray and had seized much plunder from the Polovtsians, and therefore their heart was unjust toward him.

Moreover, to this also evil men, the accursed boyars Petr Borislavich, Nester Zhidislavich, and Yakov Digenevich, whom Grand Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich of Kiev had sent away from himself because they and their servants had spread lying talk among the troops—those boyars and evil men, filled with the demonic spirit, began to slander Grand Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich of Kiev to Prince David Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav and great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, saying: “Grand Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich of Kiev wants to seize you”; and he told his brother Prince Rurik Rostislavich; and so distrust and hatred arose between them.
