In the same year the Konstantinovichs, princes of Rostov, asked their uncle, Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, for Kirill, archimandrite of the Nativity Monastery, to be bishop in their city of Rostov.

And the prince sent Archimandrite Kirill, with his boyar Dmitry Ivanovich, to Kiev to Kirill, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, so that he might make him bishop of Rostov.

And Grand Prince Yuri married his eldest son Vsevolod to the daughter of Vladimir Rurikovich; and they were wed in the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God in Vladimir, on the fourteenth day of April.

In the same year Prince Mikhail, son of Vsevolod Chermny, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Oleg, went from Novgorod to his brothers in Chernigov, and left his son Prince Rostislav in Novgorod, and said thus to the Novgorodians: “If God wills, all the Novgorodian truth will be fulfilled, and then I will have to take my son from you”; and he also said to them: “It is not fitting for you to live without a bishop; choose for yourselves from among the monks whom you wish, or better, whom God wills.”

And they chose three monks and cast lots, and the lot fell to Spiridon.

And the Novgorodians sent Spiridon to Kiev, with the posadnik and other noble people, to Kirill, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, so that he might make him archbishop of Novgorod.

In the same year Prince Mikhail Vsevolodovich sent ambassadors to Pereyaslavl to Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich with the words: “Renounce the city of Volok in the Novgorod region, and promise by kissing the cross to be in peace and love.” And Prince Yaroslav said: “I do not promise, and I do not kiss the cross, and I do not renounce Volok; you are by yourselves, and I am by myself”; and he detained the ambassadors all summer.

In the same year the Lithuanians ravaged the Novgorodian regions: Lyubna, Moreva, Seliger; and the Novgorodians went after them and took back the captives. In the same year the Novgorodians made Nezd Vodovik posadnik.

On the Earthquake

On the third day of the month of May, during the Holy Liturgy, when the Holy Gospel was being read in the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God in Vladimir, the earth shook; and the church, the altar, and the icons on the walls moved; and the chandeliers with candles and the lampstands shook; and the people were amazed and thought that each one’s head was spinning; and they began to tell one another what had happened to them, and wondered what it was. This happened in many churches and in the houses of lords; and it happened in other cities as well.
