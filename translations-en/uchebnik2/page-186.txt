And then he came to great Babylon. The Saracen sultan provided him with a house for rest, and together with the Christian metropolitan the saint visited the sultan, who showed them honors and ordered that he be supplied with everything necessary.

And the saint told the sultan that he wished to go to Egypt; then the sultan sent Sabbas to escort him and ordered that the saint be handed over to the Egyptian sultan.

That sultan also received the saint with honors and ordered that he be given rest in the Christian metropolitanate.

And the saint, taking a monk from the metropolitan as a guide, visited the desert places of Father Anthony and Arsenius and returned to Egypt, and told the sultan that he wished to ascend Mount Sinai. Then the sultan gave him guides, showed him honors, gave him balsam oil and a large root with no foliage, and fragrant Indian spices, sugar and dates. And the nobles, that is, the amirs, saw the great honors shown to the saint by the sultan, and some tried to touch his hands, and others his mantle, saying that no Christian had ever been so dear to us, but this is truly a man of God.

The saint came to holy Mount Sinai and came to the monastery of the Most Holy Theotokos, where Moses saw the Lord in the burning bush, who said to him: I am the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and commanded Moses to take off his shoes; and from that time the priests here serve barefoot.

And he visited the church of the holy prophet Elijah, and venerated the place where Moses saw God; on that stone, to this day, an image of the sovereign right hand marks the place where the Lord was. And with joy he returned to the monastery of the Most Pure Theotokos and remained there throughout the whole Great Lent.

And after the feast of the Resurrection of Christ he returned to Jerusalem, celebrated the holy service in the church of the Resurrection of Christ, and venerated all the holy places.

And he returned to Great Antioch, and was received with honors by the patriarch, and venerated the holy places. And then he came to the monastery of Saint Simeon on the Wondrous Mountain and venerated his venerable tomb, and donated much gold; and from there he came to Greater Armenia, seeking the relics of the saints. And he passed through all Armenia and the Turkish land, and came again to the Syrian Sea to sail to Constantinople.
