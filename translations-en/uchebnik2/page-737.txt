To the princes Dmitry, who were then in Vladimir, came news that their father Prince Yuri had died, and that their elder brother Prince Vasily had taken the grand princely throne in Moscow. Then he himself also sent to them, telling of their father’s death, of his own health, and of his reign; they answered: “God did not wish our father to reign, and we do not want you either.”

On Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich

And they sent to Nizhny Novgorod to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich. And the Grand Prince came and, having come to terms with what had happened, went to Moscow.

And Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy fled from Moscow to the Horde, having been on the grand principality for one month.

And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, having come, sat on his patrimony, on the grand principality in Moscow, and to Prince Dmitry Shemyaka he gave Uglich and Rzhev, and to the younger brother Prince Dmitry Krasny he gave Bezhetsky Verkh.

And Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy went to Kostroma and began gathering soldiers in order to go against Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich.

In the same year in Great Novgorod the elected hierarch Euthymius went to be consecrated by Metropolitan Gerasimus in Smolensk on the 26th day of May. Then, after the death of Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, there was no metropolitan in Moscow.

In the same year Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy came to Great Novgorod.

In the same year the Lithuanians warred with the Germans.

In the same year Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy left Novgorod and on the way plundered the lands along the Meta River, through Bezhetsky Verkh and Zavolochye.

In the same year in Novgorod the posadnik Grigory Kirillovich and Osip Andrianovich Goroshkov built a stone church of St John Chrysostom in Kolotka on the old foundation.

In the year 6943 from Adam (1443 from Christ).
Prince Vasily Yuryevich went against the Grand Prince

Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy came from Kostroma with a large army to Moscow; and Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, learning of this, set out against him. And they met in the Yaroslavl patrimony at Kozma and Damian on the Kotorosl on the 6th day of January, and a battle began between them; God helped Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich.
