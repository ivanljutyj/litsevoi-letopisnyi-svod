He came from confinement to Moscow, and the holy council met him with crosses, and the Grand Prince himself met him far outside the city with his children, his boyars, and a multitude of people, with honor and love.

And Metropolitan Pimen was in Moscow and in the cities subject to him, administering church affairs according to custom, and all obeyed and honored him.

That same autumn an envoy came to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich from Tsar Takhtamysh, named Karach, concerning peace and with a grant from the tsar.

The Grand Prince ordered the Christians to set up households and build the city.

That same winter Metropolitan Pimen consecrated Bishop Savva for Sarai, and with him at the consecration were Bishops Matthew the Greek of Rostov and Nikandr; Metropolitan Pimen was then in the city of Pereyaslavl, and there he consecrated him with the aforementioned bishops.

And from Pereyaslavl he went to Kostroma with the same bishops, and there he consecrated Michael as bishop of Smolensk.

And from there he came to Vladimir, and there with the same aforementioned bishops he consecrated Stephen Khrap as bishop of Perm.

From there he sent them away: Bishop Savva to Sarai, Bishop Michael to Smolensk, and Bishop Stephen Khrap to Perm.

That same winter Dionysius, Bishop of Suzdal, came from Constantinople to his episcopacy on the 6th day of January, and he received for himself an archbishopric from Patriarch Nilus, and for the bishops after him in that region—in Suzdal, in Novgorod, and at Gorodets; and the patriarch and the whole ecumenical council gave him a phelonion with four crosses. At that time he also brought out of Constantinople the Passion of the Savior and relics of many saints. That same spring the Annunciation fell on Wednesday of Bright Week, and snow lay for four weeks after the Annunciation, and there were great frosts; and everywhere people traveled with loads on sledges, as at Epiphany: such was the firm road, and the cold, and the great frosts.

That same spring Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich sent his eldest son, Prince Vasily, from Vladimir to the Horde to Tsar Takhtamysh in his place to contend for the grand princedom of Vladimir and Novgorod with Grand Prince Michael Alexandrovich of Tver; and with him he sent his senior boyars and his best trusted men; and they set out from Vladimir on the 30th day of April. And Prince Vasily Dmitrievich remained there in the Horde with Tsar Takhtamysh for three years. And they went from Vladimir by water in boats down the Klyazma into the Oka, and from the Oka down the Volga with gifts and with great honor.
