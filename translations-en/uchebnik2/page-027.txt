At that time Prince Izyaslav also came, son of Davyd and grandson of Svyatoslav, with the Polovtsian princes, who had with them a multitude of Polovtsians, and they took two towns of Prince Vladimirko of Galich, Ushitsa and Mikulin.

And Prince Vladimir went to Zvenigorod and stood on this side of the city, while Prince Vladimirko of Galich stood on the other side of the city; and, coming down from the hill, he took position on the Lebyazhye Field, and between them was the River Grezka. And Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev ordered each of his regiments to pave bridges and repair causeways.

And on the next morning he crossed the river and occupied the hills beyond Grand Prince Vladimirko of Galich. But Grand Prince Vladimirko of Galich thought they were coming against him, and he drew up in battle array with a great host before the city on the Black Marsh. The others advanced on the Sokolii Hills, with a stagnant marsh on both sides. Because of this, the regiments of Grand Prince Vladimirko of Galich could not fight against him owing to the narrowness, since the great marshes made those places impassable right up to the hills.

Then the Rus’ regiments climbed the hills and came around from Peremyshl and from Galich. The Galicians, seeing this, were grieved, saying: “We stand here, while in our homes our wives and children will be taken.” And Grand Prince Vladimirko of Galich began sending to Igor, saying: “If you reconcile me with your brother, Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev, then after his death I will help you to sit in the great principality in Kiev; and behold, God is between us, that I will not lie to you!” And so with flattering words he beguiled Igor.

And Prince Igor Olgovich began to entreat his elder brother, Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev, saying: “My dear lord! You are a father in place of a father, and my elder brother, and my lord! You told me: I will give you after my death the great principality in Kiev; and behold, Prince Vladimir of Galich is my friend and helper; I beseech you, sweetest lord and my elder brother, make peace and mercy with him.”

And Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev listened to his brother, Prince Igor Olgovich; and he made peace with Prince Vladimir of Galich. And that same day toward evening Grand Prince Vladimir of Galich rode out of the city to Vsevolod Olgovich; and Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev, with his brothers and boyars, met him; and so, after kissing, they parted to their own sides.

And Prince Vladimir of Galich gave Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev two thousand rubles of silver; and Grand Prince Vsevolod Olgovich of Kiev returned to Grand Prince Vladimir of Galich the towns he had taken from him — Ushitsa and Mikulin — and of the silver, altogether
