In the 6903rd year from Adam (1403rd year from Christ)

In Tokhtamysh’s Horde there was much discord and enmity among the great princes. And they fought one another on the Volga River. And in that battle many Tatar princes fell. This happened on September 8.

In the autumn of that same year, on September 28, Theodore, the first Archbishop of Rostov, died. Before him there were bishops, not archbishops. This Archbishop Theodore was a nephew of the blessed hegumen Sergius of Radonezh. That same autumn Cyprian, Metropolitan of all Rus, went to Veliky Novgorod with the envoy of Patriarch Anthony and asked for judgment according to the old custom. And the Novgorodians gave him judgment according to the old custom. And he remained all winter and spring in Novgorod until the Fast of St Peter. And Ivan, Lord Archbishop of Novgorod, showed great honor to Metropolitan Cyprian and to the patriarch’s envoy, and all Novgorod also showed honor and great love. And Metropolitan Cyprian, departing from Novgorod for Moscow, blessed with love his spiritual son Ivan, Lord of Novgorod, and all Novgorod. That same winter, on March 1, a son, Prince Yuri, was born to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.

That same year Prince Boris Mikhailovich died in Kashin, and he was brought to Tver and buried in the Church of St Alexander.

That same year a certain woman was healed at the tomb of Peter the Wonderworker in the cathedral metropolitan Church of the Most Holy Mother of God.

That same year, in the center of the city of Moscow, the Church of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God and the side chapel of St Lazarus were painted. And the masters were Theodore the Greek and Semyon Cherny.

That same year in Moscow, in the posad, there was a great fire; several thousand households burned.

That same year in Veliky Novgorod, Isak Antsyferov built a stone church in the name of St Michael the Archangel in the Arkadievsky Monastery.

That same year Germans came to Novgorod, to the town of Yam, with fifteen thousand troops. Prince Konstantin Vasilyevich Belozersky with the Novgorodians went out against them. And there was a battle. And God helped Konstantin Vasilyevich: some were killed, and others fled.

He returned to Veliky Novgorod with many captives and with booty, and the Novgorodians joyfully met him.

That same year, on August 24, Novy Gorodok on the Volga, near Zubtsov in Tver volost, burned down. It caught fire early in the morning on Sunday from thunder, when the market was just ending; the whole town burned down, and the Church of St Michael the Archangel burned.
