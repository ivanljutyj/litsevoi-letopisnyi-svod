From the sea, having learned that there was famine throughout the Russian land and in Novgorod, they came on many ships with all kinds of grain and with flour to Novgorod and to all the Russian land, and did much good. If the Lord God had not sent bread, and all kinds of grain, and flour through them, it is terrible to say what would have happened, since all the Russian land was already near destruction from famine.

In the same year Cyril, Metropolitan of Kiev, made the archimandrite of the Nativity Monastery Bishop of Rostov. In the same year a son, Boris, was born to Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich of Rostov.

In the same year Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich went with the Novgorodians to war against Chernigov, against Grand Prince Michael, son of Vsevolod Chermny, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Oleg; and he burned Serensk; and after standing before Mosalsk, he returned back again; he spoiled all kinds of grain and fruits; and he devastated and burned all districts and villages. Then his boyar Ivan Bosha was killed near Mosalsk, and Oldan Podvoisky; and many others were killed; and they withdrew without peace.

In the same year there was a fire in Novgorod; and such was the wrath of God that fire burned even on the water and crossed the Volkhov; and all the people looked and marveled; and many people burned and drowned.

In the year 6740 from Adam (the year 1240 from Christ)

Prince Vladimir Konstantinovich, grandson of Vsevolod, married. In the same year the Tatars came and wintered without reaching the great Bulgarian city, the so-called Kazan.

In the same year Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich sent his son Vsevolod against the Mordvins; and with him went Prince Feodor Yaroslavovich and the princes of Ryazan and Murom; and they ravaged the Mordvins, and burned districts and villages.

In the same year Archbishop Antony of Novgorod died; he held the episcopal see for 8 years before his exile; and in exile he was imprisoned for 6 years; and then he came from Peremyshl and held the see for 2 years in Novgorod; and he became very ill and became mute, and did not speak for 6 years, 7 months, and 9 days, and died; and he was laid in the Church of Holy Sophia in the porch.

In the year 6741 from Adam (the year 1241 from Christ)

The Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Suzdal was painted and faced with various red marbles.
