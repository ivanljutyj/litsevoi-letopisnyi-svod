To Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin. Their appanages were: Prince Vasily Mikhailovich’s was Kashin, and Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich’s was Kholm. Thus Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, and great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, sat upon the great principality in Tver, and they confirmed by kissing the cross that they would live in unanimity, in counsel, and in unity.

And Prince Vasily Mikhailovich came to Tver for the great principality, and began to live with his nephew, Prince Vsevolod Alexandrovich of Kholm, quietly, meekly, and peacefully, in great love and friendship. People came to them from everywhere, to their cities, to their volosts, and to all the Tver lands; the number of inhabitants grew, and they rejoiced with great joy.

In the same year, Bishop Theodore of Tver ordered the altar at the Holy Savior in Tver to be painted. In the same year, Grand Princess Maria, widow of Grand Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, who had been killed in the Horde together with his son Theodore, reposed. In the same year, the Most Reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, who had been living in Volhynia, came from the Horde.

In the same year, a son, Michael, was born to Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich, and Metropolitan Theognost baptized him.

In the same year, Prince Lyubord Gediminovich sent from Volhynia to Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich, asking for himself his niece, the daughter of Prince Konstantin Borisovich of Rostov. And he gave his niece to him in Volhynia.

In the same year, Grand Prince Olgerd Gediminovich of Lithuania sent to Moscow to Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich, asking him to give for himself his wife’s sister, that is, his sister-in-law, the daughter of Grand Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, named Uliana. And he, after consulting with his spiritual father, the Most Reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, gave his sister-in-law Uliana to him.

In the same year, there was a pestilence among the people in Polotsk.

In the year 6858 from Adam (1358 from Christ)

Prince Konstantin Vasilievich of Suzdal founded a stone church in Novgorod.

In the same year, Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich went from Moscow to the Horde with his brothers, Prince Andrew and Prince Ivan, to Tsar Janibek, son of Azbyak.

In the same year, he returned from the Horde with his brothers, from the Tsar, with a grant and with great honor.
