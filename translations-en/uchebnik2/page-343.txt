In the same year Grand Prince Ivan Dmitrievich of Pereslavl died, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and his further ancestors Vladimir Monomakh, Vsevolod, and Yaroslav Vladimirovich, on the 15th day of March. He had no children and was quiet, meek, humble, loving, and merciful, and very diligent toward the divine churches, and he greatly honored the priestly and monastic orders, and loved them and conversed with them continually, and was like a father to the poor and to strangers, and was so virtuous that many marveled at his life.

He blessed in his place, with his patrimony Pereslavl, Grand Prince Daniel Alexandrovich of Moscow, his uncle, for Daniel was own brother to his father, Grand Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich. The youngest son of Alexander (this Daniel of Moscow) was the one he loved more than the others.

And Grand Prince Daniel Alexandrovich of Moscow sat down to reign in Pereslavl, and installed his governors there.

And he drove out his elder brother, Grand Prince Andrew Alexandrovich of Vladimir.

In the same year Grand Prince Andrew Alexandrovich of Vladimir went to the Horde to the Tsar.

In the same year Prince Boris Andreevich, grandson of Alexander, died in Kostroma, on the 25th day of February.

In the same year in Novgorod they founded a stone citadel for Novgorod.

In the year 6811 from Adam (1311 from Christ)

Grand Prince Daniel Alexandrovich of Moscow, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, and his further ancestors Vsevolod, Yaroslav, and Great Vladimir, was tonsured into the holy angelic and monastic image and into the schema, and died on the 4th day of March.

And he was laid in the Church of the Holy Archangel Michael in Moscow, in his own patrimony. His sons: Yuri, Alexander, Boris, Ivan, Afanasy. But after his death the people of Pereslavl stood for his son Yuri and did not let him leave Pereslavl for his father’s burial.

That same spring Prince Yuri Danilovich of Moscow, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, went with his brothers with an army to Mozhaisk and took it, and seized Prince Svyatoslav and brought him to Moscow.
