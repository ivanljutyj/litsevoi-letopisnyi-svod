At that time Prince Yuri Danilovich himself had grief from Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, who in the Horde had obtained from Tsar Azbyak the great principality of Vladimir in Rus.

In the same year Prince Dmitry Mikhailovich of Tver, grandson of Yaroslav, came from the Horde with a grant from Tsar Azbyak for the great principality of Vladimir, and with him came a powerful ambassador, Prince Sevenchbuga.

In the same year Prince Ivan Danilovich came from the Horde, grandson of Alexander, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of Vsevolod, great-great-great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, and with him came a very powerful ambassador named Akhmyl, who did much evil to the lower towns, and they took and burned Yaroslavl, and took countless captives. And he summoned Grand Prince Yuri Danilovich to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak, while he himself went to the Horde with countless captives.
