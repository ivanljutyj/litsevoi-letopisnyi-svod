And Feodor was installed by Metropolitan Alexei as abbot in his monastery at Simonovo. He lived a virtuous life, was wise and prudent, and his monastery was held in honor and glory, and surpassed many in greatness. And he was confessor to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the tysyatsky, the boyars and nobles, and he shone with virtue and glory more than others.

It was customary to go to Constantinople on matters of church administration. At that time Pimen was metropolitan in Moscow, and Cyprian was metropolitan in Kiev.

After this, in the year 6891 from Adam (1391 from Christ), Abbot Feodor, Sergius's nephew and disciple, was with Patriarch Nilus in Constantinople on matters of administering the Russian metropolitanate. And the patriarch loved him very much, and made him archimandrite, showing him great honor, more than to other archimandrites. And the great and honorable Feodor became the first Simonovo archimandrite. And his monastery shone with glory, honor and great virtues. And so many elders shone forth in that monastery that many of them, by God's providence, occupied episcopal sees in other distant cities, and were appointed archimandrites and abbots in honorable monasteries, and lived virtuously, in honor and glory, tending their flocks in the name of the Lord.

Feodor, archimandrite of Simonovo, Sergius's nephew, was in Constantinople, and Patriarch Nilus conversed kindly with him, and loved him very much, and showed him honor, and gave him many honorable gifts, and made Simonovo Monastery patriarchal. And he gave his charters to Feodor, the honorable archimandrite, for the building of Simonovo Monastery in the patriarch's name, and gave houses, monastery villages, rivers and lakes, and other things necessary for monastic life. And they were not to be subject to the metropolitan, and the metropolitan did not own Simonovo Monastery. And so, having honored with great honor Feodor, archimandrite of Simonovo, Sergius's nephew and disciple, and having established the monastery with great love and instructed him spiritually, he sent him with honor to Rus'.

And the archimandrite returned with great glory and honor to his monastery at Simonovo. Thus Feodor's Simonovo Monastery was founded in the patriarch's name, and it grew and multiplied like a city. Feodor lived prospering in virtues, and was greatly respected by the Grand Prince, the hierarch, and all the inhabitants.

After some time the Grand Prince and the hierarch began earnestly to ask Feodor to agree to become bishop in the city of Rostov.
