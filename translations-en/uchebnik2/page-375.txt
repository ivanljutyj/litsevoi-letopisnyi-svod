And Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went with an army to Novgorod, and with him the Tver princes, the younger brothers of Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver: Prince Konstantin Mikhailovich of Tver and his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Tver, and Prince Alexander Vasilyevich of Suzdal, and they took all the Novgorodians captive. And Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich sent his ambassadors to Pskov to Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, Luka Protasyev, and the Novgorodians sent from themselves Vladyka Moses and Avraam the Tysyatsky, urging him to go to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak, saying: “Tsar Azbyak has commanded all of us to seek you and send you to him in the Horde. Go to him, so as not to bring his wrath upon all of us. Better that you suffer for all of us than that we all suffer because of you, for then he will lay waste all our land.” Alexander answered them: “Better that I suffer for all with patience and love than that I avenge myself on slanderers and traitors. Our life is nothing—we shall all vanish and go into oblivion, and each will be repaid by the Lord according to his deeds. Properly, you ought to stand for one another and not betray your brothers to the Tatars, but unite and together resist the Tatars, and stand for the Russian land and for all Orthodox Christendom. But you, on the contrary, bring the Tatars against Christians and betray your brothers to the Tatars.”

And he decided to go to Tsar Azbyak in the Horde, but the Pskovians would not let him, saying: “Our lord, Prince Alexander Mikhailovich, they are sending you to the Horde to the infidel tsar to be killed. Azbyak sent to them, and they promised Azbyak, all as one, to put you to death. Do not go to the Horde; you will give yourself over to a pointless death, but remain in Pskov. And we will all lay down our heads for you.” So all the Pskovians held firmly to him.

When the princes learned how the Pskovians stood for the prince, Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich thought it over and said to them: “If we do not bring Prince Alexander of Tver to the Horde, Tsar Azbyak will deprive us all of our patrimonies, put us to death, and lay waste the Russian land.” And Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich went against him, and with him all the Novgorodians. A great army gathered, and a multitude of forces assembled.

The troops halted at Opoki, and the princes began to deliberate among themselves, saying: “If we go against Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver, all the Pskovians, who have promised to die for him, will rise for him with all their might. The Germans border them and may help them, so that we can neither drive him out of Pskov nor take him captive.”

And they began to persuade and entreat His Eminence Metropolitan Theognostus, that, threatening excommunication and the imposition of penance, he would send him to the Horde to Tsar Azbyak. And Metropolitan Theognostus excommunicated from the church and sent a curse upon Prince Alexander Mikhailovich of Tver and upon the whole city of Pskov and upon all their land.
