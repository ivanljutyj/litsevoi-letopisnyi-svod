And after him came from the Horde the envoy Ulan Tsarevich and placed Prince Vasily Dmitrievich on the great reign.

In the same year three Tatars, the tsar's bedchamber attendants, petitioned the grand prince with a request to be taken into service, wishing to accept the Christian faith. Their names were Bakhtykhoza, Kydyrkhoza, and Mamatkhoza. They were brought to Metropolitan Cyprian, and the metropolitan himself baptized them and gave them names in holy baptism: Ananias, Azariah, and Mishael; and there was great joy, and all glorified God. In the same year Vitovt Kestutievich with the Germans fought against the Lithuanians and defeated them.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich built Novy Gorodok on the Volga; around it they dug a moat from the approach, and in Tver they completed the gate at St Basil's.

In the same year Vitovt Kestutievich with the Germans, having hired a large German force, went to war against Vilna. But he could not take it and returned to his own German land. And many Lithuanians and Germans were killed by cannon.

In the same year the Novgorodians and Ustyuzhans, joined by others, went in boats and ushkuys down the Vyatka River to the lower reaches and took Zhukotin and Kazan.

And then they again went to the Volga, plundered all the merchants, and returned home with spoil and riches.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich sent his boyars to Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, in Moscow with a petition, inviting him to come to him in Tver. Cyprian went to Tver, and with him were two metropolitans, Greek guests: Matthew and Nicander, and Michael, Bishop of Smolensk, and Daniel, Bishop of Zvenigorod, and Stephen, Lord of Perm; then also came Jeremiah, Lord of Ryazan. And the grand prince rejoiced at the metropolitan's arrival. And they barely persuaded the metropolitan's protodeacon Arseny to become bishop in Tver, since he feared hostility and war. And they barely persuaded him, and elevated him to the episcopal rank on the fifteenth day of August. And from Tver Metropolitan Cyprian went to Novgorod the Great. With him were Jeremiah, Lord of Ryazan, a Greek, and Daniel, Lord of Zvenigorod. They came to Novgorod, and Ivan, Lord of Novgorod, met them with crosses, with all the sacred council and with all Novgorod from small to great, near the great Savior in Nereditsa, and they assigned a place to stay at St John the Forerunner. And Lord Ivan with the Novgorodians honored him for a week, and they gave many gifts. On the eighth day, a Sunday, Cyprian, Metropolitan of All Rus, began to serve the divine liturgy in St Sophia.
