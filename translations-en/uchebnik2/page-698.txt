So to us, grieving for the church and the people of God, the merciful God sent the great prince Alexander, called Vytautas, sovereign of Lithuania and many Russian lands: he expelled this Metropolitan Photius and sent to Constantinople to the emperor and patriarch, asking them to appoint a new metropolitan who would care for the salvation of God’s people and be worthy of the Russian church. The emperor and patriarch did not wish to listen to the request of this righteous prince, receiving unjust revenues from Metropolitan Photius. Prince Alexander, together with us, the bishops, took counsel, as befits a true sovereign and great prince, having gathered all the princes of the Lithuanian land, the Russian lands and other countries subject to him, the bishops of our church, boyars, nobles, archimandrites, abbots, pious monks and simple priests. By the decision and will of all, by our whole assembly and desire, having gathered in Novogrudok in Lithuania, in the holy church of the Most Pure Mother of God, by the grace of God sent down to us from the Holy Spirit, we appointed to the metropolitan see of the holy church of Kyiv and all Rus Metropolitan Gregory according to the tradition of the holy apostles and God-bearing fathers. Photius we deposed and expelled from the throne of the Kyiv metropolitanate. We sent a letter to Constantinople to the emperor and patriarch, complaining about Photius and recounting his offenses and the desolation of the Kyiv metropolitanate. And we asked the emperor and patriarch to appoint Gregory as metropolitan to the Kyiv see for us. They did not agree to do this. We learned that they want to appoint as metropolitan according to their own will one who will pay them well, they want everything to happen according to their desire, so that the metropolitan appointed by them in Rus, robbing, doing violence, selling priestly offices, taking bribes, collecting a large tribute, laying waste the churches, would send money to them in Constantinople. Having learned of all this, we summoned all the bishops ruling in our region and in all our land, consulted with our lords, archimandrites, abbots and asked their opinion on this matter. We asked them: “What should we do? The emperor and patriarch do not want to appoint for us such a metropolitan who would rule in Kyiv and care for the church of God, this place being according to God’s will. Kyiv, which we possess, this city of Kyiv is the mother of Russian cities, and this cathedral church of Kyiv is the mother of Russian churches! Looking at all that is happening, we weep and suffer. Christians on all sides say: ‘Sovereign Vytautas belongs to a different faith—he does not care for the church of God, therefore the church has grown poor and fallen into desolation.’ We have heard no other words from people! And the metropolitan’s deeds all lie in plain view, from him come robbery, desolation in the churches and disorder.” And then all the lords said to us: “So it is indeed!
