In the same year the Suzdalians and Rostovians sent to Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich, saying: “Lord Prince! Do not hold anger against us in your heart because we were not in the regiment with Prince Mstislav Rostislavich. Come, Lord Prince, to us in Suzdal with gentleness, meekness, and love.”

And he went to Suzdal with gentleness, meekness, and love, and then went to Rostov with gentleness, meekness, and love, and united them all by kissing the precious cross, and returned to Vladimir, and seated his younger brother Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich in Pereyaslavl, which is on Lake Kleshchino.

In the same year Grand Prince Mikhalko, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, came with an army to Ryazan against Prince Gleb of Ryazan, and when he was on the Mersk River, envoys of Prince Gleb of Ryazan met him with homage, peace, and love, acknowledged their guilt in everything, and returned all that Prince Gleb had taken from his brothers-in-law, Prince Mstislav Rostislavich and his brother Prince Yaropolk Rostislavich, and they returned the Most Holy Mother of God, which he had taken from the Vladimir church, all down to the last zolotnik, and the books. Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich made peace with Prince Gleb of Ryazan and returned to Vladimir in love.

In the year 6685 from Adam (1185 from Christ)

The Rostovians and Suzdalians sent to Novgorod for Prince Mstislav Rostislavich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh; and he went to them, while he left his son Svyatoslav in Novgorod.

And when he had not yet reached the principality, at that time Prince Mikhalko, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, reposed, on the twentieth day of the month of June, on the commemoration of Saint Methodius, and his body was laid in the holy church of the Most Pure Mother of God, Golden-Domed, in Vladimir, which his brother Andrei, son of Yuri Dolgoruky, had built.

The Reign in Vladimir of Grand Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, Grandson of Vladimir Monomakh

After the repose of Grand Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich, the people of Vladimir sent to the city of Pereyaslavl, which is on Lake Kleshchino, for the brother of Prince Mikhalko, Prince Vsevolod Yuryevich, grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, saying: “God has taken our lord Grand Prince Mikhalko Yuryevich; come to reign after your brother in the grand principality in Vladimir.”
