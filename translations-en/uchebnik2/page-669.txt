Rigailo Olgerdovich, together with him the Vladyka of Bryansk Isaac, Prince Patrikey Zvenigorodsky, Prince Andrey Zvenigorodsky from Putivl, Prince Feodor Alexandrovich, Prince Semyon Peremyshlsky, Prince Mikhail Khotevsky, Prince Urus Tamensky, and the boyars of Chernigov and Bryansk, Starodub and Yaroslavl. Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich received him with great honors, gave him Vladimir with all its volosts, tolls, villages, grain sowings and unreaped grain, also Pereyaslavl with the same, and Yuryev-Polsky with the same, also Volok Lamsky with the same and half of Kolomna.

In the same year there was a plague among the people in many lands: Rzhev, Mozhaisk, Dmitrov, Zvenigorod, Pereyaslavl, Vladimir, Yuryev, Ryazan, Tarusa; the plague spread also to some Moscow volosts.

In the same year, in the month of August, there was drought and severe frosts. In the same year, in the month of August, Tatar envoys came from the Horde from Tsar Bulat-Saltan to Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich in Moscow.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow began gathering an army and sent to the Tatar tsar, asking for help against Vitovt, wishing to go to war against his Lithuanian land.

In the year 6917 from Adam (1417 from Christ)

On the [ ] day of September Metropolitan Photius was installed in Constantinople by Kyr Matthew, Patriarch of Constantinople, during the reign of Manuel, Emperor of Constantinople, for Rus, during the great reign of Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow.

In the same autumn, on the first day of the month of September, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow, having gathered a large army, set out against his father-in-law Grand Prince Vitovt Keistutovich of Lithuania and stood on the bank of the Ugra.

And Vitovt also came with a large army to the other side of the Ugra River; with him came Lithuanians, Poles, Germans, and Samogitians, and they stood by the Ugra for many days, then according to the old custom concluded peace and went their separate ways.

In the same autumn, in the month of October, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver set out with an army to Kashin against his nephew Prince Ivan Borisovich; the latter fled to Moscow.

And Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver, approaching Kashin, made peace with his brother Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Kashin, and his sister-in-law, the wife of Prince Boris, he took captive and brought to Tver, and in Kashin he installed his governors, imposing tribute.
