And Grand Prince Alexander blocked all the roads to Pskov and burst into the city, and captured the Germans and the Chud, and put the German governors in fetters and imprisoned them in Novgorod, and freed the city of Pskov from captivity.

And Prince Alexander went into the German land, and laid it waste, and burned it, and killed many, and took some captive.

But the Germans were proud and by that time had again gathered an army, saying to one another: “Let us go and defeat Prince Alexander, and take him alive.”

And the Germans went with their army, and when Alexander’s watchmen saw them, they were terrified and in great fear, seeing their numerous army; and they reported to Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich. When he learned of this, he was grieved, and went to the Church of the Holy Trinity, and prayed with weeping and tears.

And Alexander went into the German land—it was then winter—and when he was in the German land, he dispersed all his army in separate detachments.

And the Germans, attacking their detachments, killed the commanders Damash and Kerbet, the brother of the posadnik, men very strong and brave; and they killed many others with them, and took others alive; and some ran to Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich; and he began to muster his forces.

Having learned of this, the Master came out to meet the Novgorodians with all his bishops, and with all the multitude of the German people and the peoples subject to them, as many as were on this side of the sea, and with help from the king; and they assembled on the lake called Chudskoe.

And Grand Prince Alexander Yaroslavovich, seeing them, retreated back onto the lake; and the Germans and the Chud went after him; and the grand prince drew up his troops on Lake Chudskoe in a narrow bay by the Raven’s Stone.

And Alexander strengthened all with the power of the cross, and, raising them all after him, went against the Germans. Lake Chudskoe trembled, for there was a very great multitude of both armies. Alexander’s father, Grand Prince Yaroslav Vsevolodovich, sent to his aid Alexander’s younger brother, Prince Andrey, with a multitude of his warriors; so that Grand Prince Alexander had a multitude of brave warriors, as once King David had strong and mighty warriors. And the warriors of Grand Prince Alexander were filled with a martial spirit; their hearts were like those of lions.
