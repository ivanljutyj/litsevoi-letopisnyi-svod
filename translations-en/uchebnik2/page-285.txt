Many pagans went through the fire and bowed to the sun and the fire. In the same way the priests wished to lead the Grand Prince Michael and his boyar Theodore through it. But the Grand Prince Michael said: “It is unworthy for Christians to pass through fire and bow to the creature rather than to the Creator; we worship the Holy Trinity, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, one God, Creator of heaven and earth.” Then Michael said to his boyar Theodore: “It is better for us not to bow to what these foul ones bow to.” By this answer the valiant Michael put the shameless tormentor to shame to his face and trampled his command, and with it mocked the devil himself; and the laws of the impious pagans were brought to nothing.

And then the priests, mocked, returned in shame to the king and reported to him: “The Grand Prince Michael of Rus does not obey your command, does not go through the fire, and does not bow to your gods; but he says that it is unworthy for Christians to pass through fire and bow to the creature, and to the sun, and to idols; rather one must bow to the Maker of all things, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.” Hearing this, the king was filled with fury and seized with perplexity, and he put his hand over his mouth, and was ashamed, the accursed one, realizing his defeat, that neither by granting the right to rule nor by the craft of sorcery could he change Michael’s pious mind. Then he devised another cunning, not understanding in his vain mind that by this cunning he would bring greater exposure upon himself and add still more brightness to the ascetic.

And Batu chose from among the noble men close to him one man—by office he was a cupbearer, and his name was Eldega—and sent him to persuade the blessed one by flattery to submit to Batu, and if not, then by threats. Batu commanded Eldega to persuade Michael with these words: “Why have you violated my command and not bowed to my gods? Now there are two paths before you; choose whichever you wish. If you fulfill my command and pass through the fire, and bow to my gods: the bush, and the sun, and the idols, then you will live, and you will be lord of your great principality, and I will glorify you throughout the whole Russian land. But if you do not obey me and do not bow to my gods, then you will die a terrible death.”

Then Eldega went and delivered all the king’s words to Michael. But the Grand Prince Michael was not at all frightened by the tormentor’s fury, but with boldness spoke thus: “You, since you are king, must be bowed to and honored, since the kingdom has been entrusted to you by God, and by God’s will you have crushed our Russian kingdom also; so I ought to honor you. But to renounce Christ and bow to your gods—such a thing shall not happen; because these are not gods, but creations.
