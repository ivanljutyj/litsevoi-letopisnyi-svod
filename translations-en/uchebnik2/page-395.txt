Rus (1351–1380 from Christ).
Book 8

In the year 6851 from Adam (the year 1351 from Christ)

The whole city of Moscow burned out; 18 churches burned. This was already the fourth fire in Moscow in thirteen years. In that year Prince Ivan Ivanovich Korotopoly of Ryazan was slain. Since he had killed his brother Alexander Mikhailovich of Pronsk, he too drank this cup: he was judged by the same judgment, and the same measure was measured out to him.

In that year the Pskovians set out with their prince Ostafy on a campaign into the German land. They came to the city of Medvezhya Golova and fought there for eight days.
