Rus (1160–1181 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 2

In the year 6660 from Adam (the year 1160 from Christ)

The Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev took Gorodets of Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Dolgoruky, and burned it, and destroyed it, and resettled the people.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev, Izyaslav Mstislavich, sent his son Prince Mstislav to his son-in-law, the King of Hungary, and to the King of Bohemia, and to the Poles, calling them to his aid against the Grand Prince Volodymyrko of Galich.

The Bohemian king and the Poles did not go, but the Hungarians went; and the king went with the Hungarians and with Mstislav Izyaslavich.

The king sent to Izyaslav, saying: “Father! I bow to you; you informed me of the offense of the Galich prince, and I have already set out to you, and do you go at once; but if we do not do so, let God judge between us and him.”

Izyaslav, hearing this, gathered his druzhina and set out, taking with him all Vyacheslav’s regiment, and all the Black Klobuks, who are called Cherkasy, and the best men of Kiev, and all the Rus druzhina; when he came to Dorogobuzh, there his brother Vladimir also came to him.

And from there they went to Peresopnitsa, and there at Peresopnitsa Vladimir Andreevich came to him, and there his brother Svyatopolk also came from Vladimir with his regiment; and so all gathered and went to the king.

Izyaslav did not take Svyatopolk with him, but left him in Vladimir, and took his regiment with him.

And Izyaslav went from Vladimir with his regiments along the king’s road, by which he had come against Andrei Vladimirovich with Yaroslav Svyatopolchich; and Izyaslav came to the San River, which flows below Peremyshl, and, having crossed the San River, halted.

And there an embassy of a hundred men came to him from the king, and they said to Izyaslav: “Your son-in-law the king bows to you and has commanded you thus: I have already stood here for the fifth day, awaiting you, and do you come quickly.” Hearing this, Izyaslav set out with great zeal.
