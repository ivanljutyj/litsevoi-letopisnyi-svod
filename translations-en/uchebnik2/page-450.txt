In that same year Prince Vladimir Andreevich wished to build a monastery in Serpukhov, since this prince had a great love for the monastic and priestly order.

At that time there lived in his appanage at Radonezh a certain servant of God, a holy man named Sergius, abbot of many monasteries founded by himself; he was a father to a large brotherhood and was renowned throughout all the land, and many testimonies are known about him. Prince Vladimir Andreevich entreated him to come from Radonezh to Serpukhov to found a monastery.

He came from his monastery at Radonezh to Serpukhov, and having inspected a place suitable for founding a monastery, after praying, he laid with his own hands the foundation of a church in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God on the 9th day of the month of December, on the Conception of Saint Anna, when the holy Mother of God was conceived. And having blessed the prince, the venerable abbot Sergius returned to his monastery at Radonezh. Prince Vladimir Andreevich began to care for the building of the monastery in Serpukhov; the monastery and cells were built and filled with everything necessary—icons, books, and sacred vessels—and that monastery became cenobitic. The first abbot in that monastery was Athanasius, a disciple of the venerable Sergius, abbot of Radonezh, whom Prince Vladimir Andreevich requested from the abbot, and he remained in the abbacy at the monastery for several years.

Then he left the abbacy and went to Constantinople, acquired a cell there for himself, having made a contribution upon entering the monastic brotherhood, and began to live in silence with holy elders, and in deep old age he departed to the Lord.

In the same year in Moscow there died in monastic habit and schema the last tysyatsky Vasily Vasilyev, son of Veniaminovich; his name in monasticism was Varsonofy, and he was buried in the monastery of the Holy Epiphany.

In the same autumn, on the 26th day of the month of November, on the commemoration of Saint Alypius the Stylite and the holy martyr George, a son was born in Pereyaslavl to Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, Prince Yuri; the venerable Sergius, abbot of Radonezh, baptized him.

And there were present Grand Prince Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal, father-in-law of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Moscow, with his grand princess, with his brothers, children, and boyars; and there was then a great gathering of princes in Pereyaslavl.

In the same winter, in the month of February, Metropolitan Alexius came to Tver and installed Evfimy as Bishop of Tver on the Thursday of the Week of the Veneration of the Cross, on the commemoration of the holy Forty Martyrs, and then set out from Tver with the patriarchal envoy Cyprian for Pereyaslavl.
