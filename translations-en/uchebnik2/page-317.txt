In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, went to Novgorod; and the Novgorodians came out to meet him, bowing, and received him with honor.

In the year 6782 from Adam (1282 from Christ)

Kirill, Metropolitan, came from Kiev to Vladimir, and brought with him Serapion, Archimandrite of Pechersk, and made him bishop of Vladimir and Suzdal.

In the same year the Novgorodians saw that their bishop Dalmat was already at death, that he was already leaving life in this vain world, and they came to him in sorrow, bowing, with these words: “Our lord, father, pastor and teacher Dalmat, Archbishop, bless us a pastor and teacher in your place, that he may be among us in your stead.”

Dalmat answered them: “It is not for me to decide this, but for God and the Most Pure Mother of God; but if you ask my humble counsel, here is Abbot David; in the Church of St George there is John; and here is my spiritual father Clement; whomever you wish, choose for yourselves.” They liked his spiritual father Clement, marked by God; and Archbishop Dalmat blessed him with his own hand.

In the same year Archbishop Vladyka Dalmat of Novgorod reposed.

In the same year the Novgorodians sent Clement with leading men to Kiev to Kirill, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, so that he might make Clement bishop of Novgorod.

In the same year Grand Princess Theodora, wife of Gleb, reposed; and Bishop Ignatius of Rostov with all the priests sang funeral hymns over her; and they laid her in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Rostov.

In the same year Pavel Semyonovich, Posadnik of Novgorod, reposed.

In the year 6783 from Adam (1283 from Christ)

Grand Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich of Vladimir, grandson of Vsevolod, went to the Horde to the Tsar.

In the same year there was a solar sign: the sun was enclosed by certain circles, and in the middle of the circles were crosses; the arcs were blue, green, yellow, purple, and scarlet; and the arcs were turned with their backs inward; this was on the 3rd day of the month of May.

In the same year Serapion, bishop of Vladimir, Suzdal, and Novgorod, reposed; and they laid him in Vladimir in the Golden-Domed Church of the Most Pure Mother of God; he was very skilled in teaching and mighty in the Divine Scriptures.
