In the same year died Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver; in monasticism he was named Job.

And after him his son Prince Alexander sat on the grand principality of Tver on the first day of May, and in Kashin his uncle Prince Vasily Mikhailovich.

And from Trinity Day a great pestilence was in Moscow; it came from the Germans to Pskov, and from there to Great Novgorod, and to Tver, and reached Moscow as well, and spread over all the Russian land; in all places the pestilence was very great.

In the same year in Novgorod the whole Trade Side and the Lyudin End burned.

In the same year the Ustyuzhans waged war on the Zavolochye land of Great Novgorod.

In the same year the Novgorodians went with an army to Ustyug and took a ransom from them: fifty thousand squirrel skins and six forties of sable skins.

In the same year in Novgorod their archbishop-elect Theodosius died in his monastery of the Most Holy Trinity at Skolbsk, whom they had chosen by lot and then did not want.

In the same year the Greek emperor Manuel died in Constantinople, son-in-law of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich of Moscow.

The Grand Principality of Serbia

After the Battle of Ankara, when Timur conquered and captured and led away Tsar Bayezid, and plundered all his realm, and took captive the sister of Stefan, Grand Prince of Serbia, who was married to Bayezid.

Grand Prince Stefan, however, sends envoys to Timur and brings her out of captivity.

He himself comes to Constantinople with his brother Vuk and there receives the rank of despot from the Greek emperor Manuel, who was then still alive; and after honoring him greatly, the emperor sent him away.

Despot Stefan comes with his brother to his city of Novo Brdo, a city truly of silver and gold. After the killing of Tsar Bayezid, the Serbian land was freed from the yoke and subjugation of the infidel, and from that time Despot Stefan became autocrat and sovereign of all the Serbian land.

By God's grace, going around the places and land of his fatherland, he subdued some and subjected others to himself; and the cities and places that in former times had been stolen from Serbian authority, those he joined to himself.
