He was installed in Kiev as Grand Prince by His Eminence Metropolitan Michael, on the fifth day of March, a Sunday.

And on that day the Grand Prince Vsevolod Olegovich, with his father Metropolitan Michael, and with his brothers, and with the princes, and with his boyars, and with all the people, held a splendid feast, and set out wine, mead, and beer, and all kinds of foods and vegetables along the streets; and he distributed great alms to churches and monasteries.

In the same year the Grand Prince Vsevolod, son of Oleg, grandson of Svyatoslav, installed in the city of Chernigov as Grand Prince Vladimir, son of David, grandson of Svyatoslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of the great Vladimir.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich went against the Polovtsians and slew many, and took others captive alive.

In the year 6647 from Adam (1147 from Christ)

The Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich heard of the indignation against him of the Vladimirovichi Monomashichi and began to plot against the Vladimirovichi and the Mstislavichi, relying on the multitude of his troops, and he wished himself, with his brothers, to hold all the Russian land, and he wished to take from Rostislav Mstislavich the grand principality of Smolensk, and from Izyaslav Mstislavich the grand principality of Vladimir, and from their uncle Prince Yuri Vladimirovich Monomash the grand principality of Suzdal, and from their other uncle Prince Andrei Vladimirovich Monomash the grand principality of Pereyaslavl of Rus.

And he sent a great host against Izyaslav Mstislavich, saying: “Leave Vladimir peaceably; if you do not leave, then consider what a great host I have sent against you.” But the host of the Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich reached Gorina, and a great panic and terror fell upon them, and they were seized with great fear and fled back.

In the same year the Grand Prince of Kiev Vsevolod Olegovich brought his brother, Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich, from Kursk and went with him to Pereyaslavl, wishing to install him as Grand Prince in Pereyaslavl, and to remove the Grand Prince of Pereyaslavl Andrei Vladimirovich Monomash and send him to Kursk, also as Grand Prince.

And he sent to the Grand Prince of Pereyaslavl Andrei Vladimirovich Monomash, saying: “Go to Kursk as Grand Prince”; and the Grand Prince Andrei Vladimirovich Monomash said: “It is better for me to accept death with all my retinue on the land of my fathers and grandfathers than to take the grand principality of Kursk, since it is foreign to me.
