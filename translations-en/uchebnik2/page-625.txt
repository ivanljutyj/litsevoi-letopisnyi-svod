And when he returned to his father in Tver, to Great Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich, he told him of the great love, the gifts, and the honor that Vitovt had shown him. In the same year, by Great Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, a stone church of the Archangel Michael was built at Gorodets and consecrated by Arsenius, Bishop of Tver.

In the same year Cyprian, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Isaac as bishop of Perm, at Golenishchevo.

In the same year Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich departed from Tver to Moscow to Great Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and sent word to his uncle, Great Prince Mikhail Alexandrovich of Tver, that he renounced his oath sworn on the cross. The Great Prince received him with honor and love and gave him the town of Torzhok.

In the same year Prince Ivan Vsevolodovich of Tver married in Moscow at the house of Grand Princess Evdokia, taking as his wife Nastasya, daughter of Great Prince Dmitry Ivanovich.

In the same year Tsar Tokhtamysh went to war against the Pomor towns.

In the same year the envoy of Tsar Tokhtamysh of the Great Horde, Timer-Khoza, was in Ryazan with Great Prince Oleg Ivanovich of Ryazan, and with him were many Tatars, horses, and merchants.

In the same year Pskov envoys came to Novgorod: Prince Grigory Ostafyevich, posadnik Sysoy, posadnik Roman, and Filip Kazachkovich, and they petitioned Lord Ivan, Archbishop of Novgorod, saying: “We desire, lord, that the enmity between your children, the Novgorodians and the Pskovians, should end, and receive us, so that there may be love between us as of old.”

And Vladyka Ivan, having summoned the Novgorodians, said to them: “My children, Novgorodians, you see that the time has come when it befits you to live in love and humility among yourselves, and to cease quarreling, and to live together in love and humility with the Pskovians.” And the Novgorodians, posadnik Timofey Yuryevich, tysyatsky Nikita Fedorovich, all the posadniks and all the tysyatskys, the boyars and all Novgorod, accepted the blessing of their lord Vladyka Ivan, and ceased to quarrel with the Pskovians, and established peace and love as of old.

In the same year there was great joy for Tsar Tokhtamysh of the Great Horde: having rid himself of his enemies, he sent his envoys to all lands, glorifying his name and also collecting gold, silver, and gifts, and Tatars from everywhere.
