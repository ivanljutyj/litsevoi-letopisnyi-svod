He tortured rich women, and those who would not submit to him he boiled in cauldrons. And he was fearsome and terrible to all, and all feared him and trembled; for his voice roared like a lion’s, and in stature he was like an oak, sturdy and strong; and the purity of his tongue and his most wondrous swiftness, and his audacity and shamelessness were such that he never reckoned with anyone, but without hesitation rushed upon all and tormented without mercy.

In that same year all the people fell into sorrow and grief, and they all came to the Grand Prince Andrew Yuryevich Bogolyubsky, weeping over their misfortunes and calamities; and he sent to Feodor the bishop, saying: “All the people grieve and weep. You ought to cease from anger and change to meekness and mercy; behold, you have shut all the churches, and singing has ceased in them, and you have locked the wonder-working image of the Most Pure Mother of God.” But he not only loaded the prince with abuse and reproaches, but also uttered blasphemy against the Most Pure Mother of God.

And the Grand Prince Andrew Yuryevich Bogolyubsky ordered him seized and sent him to Kiev to the most reverend Constantine, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus.

The metropolitan ordered him to be examined concerning the evil he had done; but he, lifted up with pride and speaking improper things, reproached the divine law and uttered blasphemy against the Lord God Himself and the Most Pure Mother of God. And Constantine the metropolitan sent to him, saying: “From ages past unrepentant blasphemers have been put to death, and in the law of Moses not only blasphemers but also evildoers were killed; and moreover, in the New Testament blasphemers are delivered to death by the holy apostles and holy fathers; so you too, unrepentant, ought to accept death; for the Lord said: ‘Woe to that man by whom the offense comes, and if anyone offends one of these little ones who believe in Me, it would be better for him that a millstone be hung on his neck and he be drowned in the sea; take heed therefore to yourself and turn to repentance.’” But he became even more enraged and spoke improper and blasphemous things; and so he remained without repentance for a whole year.

The metropolitan sent to him, saying: “You have uttered blasphemy against the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God and all the saints, and thereby you have reproached God’s law, and you have tortured many people. And God’s law says: with what judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with what measure you measure, it will be measured to you; and judgment without mercy to him who has not shown mercy.” And so he ordered his tongue cut out, as of an evildoer and heretic, and his right hand cut off, and his eyes put out; and so they put him to death according to the Gospel judgment, which says: “If anyone offends one of these little ones who believe in Me, it is better for him that a millstone be hung on his neck and he be drowned in the sea.”
