A violent quarrel arose between the two boyars, and they exchanged indecent words, and the ambassadors of the Grand Prince returned empty-handed.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich gathered the people who were then about him, the Muscovites, the merchants, and all the rest, took them with him, went out to meet him, and met Prince Yuri on the Klyazma twenty versts from Moscow. With Prince Yuri there were many warriors, while the Grand Prince had very few, yet they fought them. But from the Muscovites there was no help at all, since many of them were drunk, and they had even brought mead with them to drink.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich saw that there was no help for him from anywhere and quickly fled to Moscow; and taking his mother Sophia and his princess Maria, trembling and hastening, he fled to Tver, and from Tver to Kostroma. This battle took place on Saturday, on the eve of the Sunday of the Myrrh-bearing Women, on the twenty-fifth day of April.

About Prince Yuri Dmitrievich

And his uncle, Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, coming to Moscow, sat upon the grand principality and sent his sons after his nephew, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich; and they told him that the Grand Prince was in Kostroma, and then Prince Yuri Dmitrievich himself went with his sons after him to Kostroma, taking with him a great army also. And coming to Kostroma, he took him captive; and Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, with tears and weeping, petitioned his uncle’s favorite, the boyar Semyon Morozov, for this Semyon was held by Prince Yuri Dmitrievich in great favor and love. Ivan Dmitrievich became angry at this; it greatly displeased him that he was not only showing him leniency but even wished to give him an appanage. And not only Ivan Dmitrievich alone, but many other boyars and servants also became angry at this; it displeased them all.

About Semyon Morozov

Semyon Morozov could ask much of his lord Prince Yuri Dmitrievich, and he obtained for Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich peace and love and Kolomna as an appanage. Prince Yuri Dmitrievich held a feast in honor of his nephew, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, gave him many gifts, and sent him away to his appanage in Kolomna, and with him all his boyars. And that peace and love and appanage were all arranged by Semyon Morozov, Prince Yuri’s favorite.
