And weeping, recalling his misfortunes and troubles, and, having called all this to mind, he began to weep and lament over his sins, desiring to renounce the vain worldly life, to forget the glory of this world, and to please God by a righteous life. There the prince fell ill and, after a short illness, died in the Lord; his body was escorted with honor and buried with funeral hymns. And so the line of the grand princes of Smolensk came to an end, and the Grand Principality of Smolensk lost its grand princes, and the Grand Prince of Lithuania Vytautas Kestutovich began to rule in Smolensk, and after him other grand princes of Lithuania appointed their governors there. In the same autumn, on the 3rd day of the month of October, the Germans came with an army into the Novgorod lands, and after waging war there, they returned back.

In the same winter, in the month of December, Prince Konstantin Dmitrievich came to Novgorod the Great, appointed as governor by his brother, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich.

In the same winter, on the 25th day of the month of January, Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver came from the Horde to Tver from Tsar Bulat-Saltan, and with him came the tsar’s envoy; the townspeople met the grand prince with crosses and honors, and all came out with great joy to meet him.

In the same winter there were severe frosts and snows, winds and blizzards, and the road from the Horde was very difficult and hard. In the same winter Grand Prince Ivan Mikhailovich of Tver married, taking as his wife the daughter of Prince Dmitry Eremeevich.

In the same winter Prince Yaroslav married, son of Vladimir, grandson of Andrei, great-grandson of Ivan, great-great-grandson of Daniel of Moscow.

In the same winter, on the 9th day of the month of March, Hilarion, Bishop of Kolomna, reposed.

In the same winter, on the 11th day of the month of March, a man who had been in great suffering and illness was healed at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter in the great metropolitan cathedral church in Moscow, and went home healthy. In the same winter the Germans came to conquer the Pskov land, and stood for a long time at Pskov until the Pskovians were completely exhausted, because too great a German army had come against them. In the same spring, in the month of May, they began to paint the great cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God of Vladimir by command of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich; the masters were Daniil the Icon-painter and Andrei Rublev.

In the same year Prince Ivan Vladimirovich of Pronsk came secretly with a Tatar army and drove Grand Prince Feodor Olegovich of Ryazan, grandson of Ivan, out of Ryazan; he fled beyond the Oka, and Grand Prince Ivan Vladimirovich of Pronsk began to rule in both principalities, Ryazan and Pronsk.
