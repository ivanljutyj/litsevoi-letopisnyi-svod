In the same year they finished building the stone side chapel to the Church of the Holy Savior in Moscow. In the same year a son, Dmitry, was born to Prince Ivan Ivanovich, on the twelfth day of November.

In the same year Prince Konstantin of Suzdal went to the Horde. In the same winter a son, Ivan, was born to Prince Semyon Ivanovich.

In the same winter Grand Prince Semyon Ivanovich gave his daughter in marriage in Tver to Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich of Tver.

In the same year Grand Prince Vasily Alexandrovich of Ryazan died. In the same year Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, fell grievously ill.

In the year 6859 from Adam (1359 from Christ)

In Moscow, in the metropolitan cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God, by the mercy of God, and through the Most Pure Mother of God and the prayers of the holy wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, a certain woman was healed who had been grievously ill: she had lain for two years unable to use her legs or arms. And when she was brought into the church of the Most Pure Mother of God to the tomb of the holy wonderworker Peter, at that very hour she was healed and went to her home healthy, glorifying the Lord God, the Most Pure Mother of God, and their saint, the holy wonderworker Peter, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus.

In the same year the most reverend Theognost, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, blessed Bishop Daniel to serve in Suzdal, since he had previously been excommunicated; and having received again his former hierarchal rank, he again received the hierarchal office in the great archbishopric.

In the same year Prince Yuri Yaroslavovich of Murom renewed his city, his patrimony Murom, which had fallen desolate as early as under the first princes. He built his court in the city, and his boyars, nobles, and merchants, and the common people also built their courts, and they renewed the holy churches and adorned them with icons and books.

In the same year there was a terrible storm, and it destroyed churches and mansions. In the same year in Novgorod they built a stone church of Florus and Laurus; and another stone church in the name of Cosmas and Damian they built on Kholopiya Street; and a third stone church in the name of John Chrysostom was built by the posadnik Yuri Ivanovich.

In the same year the Novgorodians went on a campaign against the Germans and returned with a great number of captives.

In the same year Novgorod envoys went to the German city of Yuryev and exchanged with the Swedish king for the Orekhov Germans the tysyatsky Abraham and Kozma Tverdislavich and the rest of their company, who had been taken overseas by Magnus, King of Sweden.
