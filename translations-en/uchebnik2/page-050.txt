When he was going to Dorogobuzh with Prince Mstislav Yuryevich, Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich fled from Dorogobuzh and came to Lutsk to his uncle Svyatopolk.

Prince Volodimirko of Galich seized all those cities and came against Svyatopolk Mstislavich at Lutsk; Svyatopolk Mstislavich and Prince Mstislav Izyaslavich shut themselves up in the city of Lutsk. He, having come, could do nothing to them and went to his domain, to Galich, and installed Prince Mstislav, son of Yury Dolgoruky, at Peresopnitsa.

In the same year, at Ryazan on the Great Vorona, they defeated the Polovtsians. In the same year the Polovtsians came to the aid of Prince Yury Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomash against Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich and did much evil to him.

That same autumn Grand Prince Yury Dolgoruky gave to his son Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky: Turov, Pineg, Dorogobuzh, Peresopnitsa; and he settled in Peresopnitsa. That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich many times sent to Prince Andrei Yuryevich, saying: “Lord and brother, bring me into peace and love with your father, Prince Yury Vladimirovich.” He sent this not sincerely, but with guile and deceit; for the envoys coming from him spied out his host, his weapons, how his city was fortified, and how and what was being built in it.

And again Izyaslav Mstislavich sent to Prince Andrei Yuryevich Bogolyubsky, saying: “I have no inheritance among the Czechs, nor among the Hungarians and Poles, only in the Russian land; ask for me peace and love from your father, Prince Yury, and let him give me Pogoryna.” But Grand Prince Yury Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomash was angry with him and did not want to receive him or give him a single volost.

That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich learned secretly that the people of Kiev wanted him to reign among them as grand prince in Kiev; likewise all Vyacheslav’s men and all the Berendei desired him for the great reign in Kiev. He rejoiced greatly and sent to them secretly, saying: “Nothing is so dear to me as your love, and I desire to see your salvation and health, and to please you in Christ in all things, and I love you so much that I will lay down my soul for you.”

That same winter the Polovtsians came into Rus, and having done much evil, went back to their own land. That same winter Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich gathered Hungarians, Poles, and Czechs; and a great host. And he went to Kiev against Prince Yury Dolgoruky Vladimirovich Monomash, and passing by Peresopnitsa, he burned Zarechesk; and having come, he encamped at Mylsk.
