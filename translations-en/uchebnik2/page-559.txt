…with estates I bless my princess, and my princess is free to bequeath those acquisitions to any one of her sons. And my children are not to interfere in this.

And those villages that my brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich took away, the villages from the village of Lytino to Berendeeva Slobodka, are to be attached to my princess’s village of Lytino. And if God should take any one of my sons because of sins, then my princess shall divide his appanage among all the remaining sons in parts. And to whomever she gives what, that shall be his, and my children are not to oppose her will. My children, the younger brothers of Prince Vasily, honor and obey your elder brother Prince Vasily
