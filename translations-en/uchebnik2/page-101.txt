They give birth to wise and active words, thereby teaching the foolish and senseless. This purity was loved by the great Forerunner, the Baptist of Christ, and he showed the rule of virginity, the harsh and God-loving life; this purity was loved by the great John the Theologian, and he was deemed worthy to lean on the breast of the Lord and became the beloved disciple of Christ God. Virginity has a fiery maidenly face; for fire is divinity, burning up bodily passions and enlightening the pure soul; it also has the bands that angels have, for a pure unmarried life is equal to the angels; the bands are the resting places of the Holy Spirit; on its head it has a royal crown, since humble wisdom reigns over the passions; the girding on its loins is an image of seniority and episcopacy, and it reveals authority; in its hand it holds a scepter—the ruler's sacred, episcopal rank; the fiery eagle's wings soaring high are prophecy, because they reveal swift understanding, for the eagle is a very keen-sighted bird: when it sees the hunter, it flies higher and becomes uncatchable; so too those who love virginity and wisdom are hard to catch for the hunter—the devil; in its left hand it holds a written scroll, in which unknown and hidden mysteries are written, which means: one ought to honor traditions and writings, and abandon foreign and unlawful disputes; the garment reveals light, the establishment on seven pillars—the seven gifts of the Spirit; its feet are established on rock, which means: "On this, says the Lord, on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it, and I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven." Virginity is honored much more; for Christ did not damage the seal of virginity, being born of a virgin; let virginity be honored, for it is higher than marriage and much more honorable is virginity. For as much as angels are higher than people and as heaven is from earth, so much is the unmarried higher than the married; virginity is the angelic life. For the Lord said in His Gospel according to Luke: "The sons of this age marry and are given in marriage, but those who are deemed worthy to attain that age and the resurrection from the dead neither marry nor are given in marriage, and they cannot die anymore: for they are equal to angels and are sons of God." And Matthew says: "In the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven." Concerning this John Chrysostom says: "And before the resurrection I will show you the spiritual hosts, the angelic choir, those who are Christ's have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires, despising things that pass away, holding to spiritual things; these are the luminaries of the universe, the angelic choir. For when the sun rises, much before the sun's rising, having risen from bed in the fear of the Lord, alert, sober, not troubled by worldly cares and vanity, not darkened by corruptible and passing things, not by earthly and vain labors, not by sorrow and desire for present..."
