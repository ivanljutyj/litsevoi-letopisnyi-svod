"They do not listen, they do not pay you tribute, and they have driven out your tributaries, and killed others, and drove me out in disgrace, and they revile you." The Tsar sent his army against the Novgorodians.

The Novgorodians sent envoys to Pereyaslavl to Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of Vsevolod; but he refused and said to them: "I do not wish to take the throne and to become higher than my uncle Yaroslav Yaroslavovich." The Novgorodians were grieved, and Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich was gathering an army.

Prince Vasily Yaroslavovich of Kostroma, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yuri Dolgoruky, great-great-grandson of Vladimir Monomakh, learned of this and sent his envoys to Novgorod with the words: "Behold, I bow to the Holy Mother of God and to the people of Novgorod. I have learned that my brother Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich is going to war against Novgorod, and my nephew Prince Dmitry Alexandrovich from Pereyaslavl, and Prince Gleb Rostislavovich from Smolensk, grandson of Mstislav, and the Tatar Tsar is sending his army against you as well; and I pity you more than that land which came to me from my father."

The Novgorodians sent envoys to Prince Vasily with obeisance and a great petition; he went to the Horde to the Tsar and turned back the Tatar army, having entreated the Tsar, telling everything in order, saying: "The Novgorodians are right, and my brother Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich is guilty." The Tsar honored Prince Vasily greatly and was glad that he had not sent the army.

Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich with his children and with the Pereyaslavl prince Gleb Rostislavovich, grandson of Mstislav, great-grandson of David, went to war against Novgorod. The Novgorodians set a palisade around the city on both sides and strengthened the city with a strong fortress; and they gathered with all the land, and joined with the Pskovians, and brought many Germans to their aid, and went out in a great host to Gorodishche, and, having inundated it, stood armed in armor.

Grand Prince Yaroslav Yaroslavovich, with other princes, learned of this, went from the other side, and came and stopped at Russe, and sent envoys to the Novgorodians with the words: "For whatever you do not love me for, whatever you accuse me of, I repent of it all and will not do so anymore, and all the princes guarantee for me to you." The Novgorodians answered him thus: "Prince, you have planned to go to war against the city of Holy Sophia, that, prince, you know; but we will all die for the city of Holy Sophia and for one another, and in this is our honor and glory; and we have no prince, but we have the Lord God, and Holy Sophia, and the truth; and we do not desire you"; and the Novgorodians stood firm.
