In the same year there was a severe drought and a lack of water, and the earth, pine forests and woods burned; everywhere there was very thick smoke, so that at times people could not see one another; and because of that smoke beasts, birds and fish in the water died, and people were in great need and also died.

In the same year in Novgorod they built a stone church of the Holy Fathers in the Prince’s Court on the site of the old church, which had collapsed. In the same year the Novgorodians added another stone wall to the city of Porkhov. In the same year there was an assembly of the Novgorodians concerning the building of the city, and four equipped a fifth.

In the year 6939 from Adam (1439 from Christ)

In the month of October the Horde prince Aidar conquered the Lithuanian land, came to the city of Mtsensk and stood before it for three weeks, but did not take the city, because the voivode Gregory Protasyev was there. Aidar swore to Gregory by his faith; and he believed him and came out of the city.

Aidar took him with him to the Horde to Tsar Makhamet. And Tsar Makhamet rebuked Aidar and did not praise him for this; and after showing honor to Gregory, he sent him with gifts to Rus'.

In that same winter Prince Yuri Dmitrievich broke the peace with Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, his nephew.

In that same spring Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich sent Prince Fyodor Davydovich Pestry with an army against the Volga and Kama Bulgars; and he, coming, conquered them and seized all their land.

In the same year there was a sign: three fiery pillars appeared in the sky. Then too there was a severe drought; the earth, pine forests and swamps burned, and there was a severe famine throughout all the Russian land.

The Tale of Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus', Who Saw the Angel of the Lord Showing Him the End of His Life

There was an apparition in the city of Moscow to Photius, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus'. He was in his bedchamber in the morning, performing his usual prayer rule on Friday of Holy Week, in the year 6938 from Adam (1438 from Christ), on the feast of the Venerable Theodore Vlasyany. And as he lay in bed resting, suddenly an extraordinary light shone in his bedchamber, and someone entered to him, in appearance like a man from the north, comely and radiant, with golden hair and a royal crown on his head, and his clothing was adorned with all manner of beauties...
