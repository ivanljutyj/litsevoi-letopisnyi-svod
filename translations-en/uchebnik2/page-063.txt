They began to consider how to go to Rus, and after deliberating everything they went to Chernigov; and having come, they stood at Gorichev near the city, having passed Kanin; it was then the Lord’s day, and therefore they did not go to the city.

Then Grand Prince Izyaslav Mstislavich of Kiev, hearing of the great gathering of many forces against Grand Prince Izyaslav Davidovich of Chernigov, sent to his aid his brother, Grand Prince Rostislav Mstislavich of Smolensk; but Rostislav was in Smolensk, fearing Prince Yuri Dolgoruky; and when Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky had passed through his domain, he then went to Chernigov to the aid of Grand Prince Izyaslav Davidovich, and entered the city with Prince Svyatoslav Olegovich and Prince Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich to Grand Prince Izyaslav Davidovich. And embracing, they kissed and wept long, hanging on each other’s necks, saying to one another: “When shall we escape vain turmoil and bloodshedding, sinful war? When shall we be delivered from the deceit and cunning of these people, for demons drag each of them from every side to hell, darkening their minds? What can stop our madness and vanity? We kiss the holy and life-giving crosses and swear terrible oaths; truly every man is vain, and vain is his course. For what does it profit a man,” said the Lord, “if he gains the whole world and loses his soul? But God is in righteousness and will repay vengeance to those who hate peace in the Lord; for it is said that he who starts war perishes.”

He also sent to reconnoiter the strength of the arriving warriors; and he saw a countless force, both Christians and Polovtsians, and they ordered all the people sitting in the ostrog to flee from the ostrog into the detinets. On the next day Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky with his sons, and with Grand Prince Rostislav Mstislavich of Ryazan, and with Temir, great prince of the Polovtsians, and with Svyatoslav, and with Dulep, and with Berdash — Polovtsian princes — and with all the Polovtsians; and the great multitude of the host surged like the sea, and it was fearful to see it in armor, shining bright as water in the sun.

And they went to the city and halted before reaching the town of Semyn. The Polovtsians swiftly rushed to the city and took the ostrog and set it on fire, and also set fire to the whole posad, and made it like an open field around the city; and so, coming up, they surrounded the whole city of Chernigov and loosed such a multitude of arrows that the sky was not visible. The townspeople fought firmly against them.

The Chernigov princes, strengthened with the townspeople and gradually overcoming fear, went out from the city to fight them and killed many of them.

Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky Yuryevich rushed at them courageously; they advanced against him and knocked him from his horse, and he barely escaped to his father Prince Yuri Dolgoruky, and was severely wounded.
