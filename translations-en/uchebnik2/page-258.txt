And already at the end of the month of February a messenger came to Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich, and to his brother Svyatoslav Vsevolodovich, and to their nephews: to Vasilko Konstantinovich, and to Vsevolod Konstantinovich, and to Vladimir Konstantinovich. And the messenger reported that the Tatars had taken the city of Vladimir, and had burned the bishop, and the grand princess, and the princes, and all the people, and had killed others; and Prince Yuri’s eldest son, Vsevolod, with his brothers, had been killed by the Tatars outside the city; and now they are coming against Yuri.

But Yuri, and his brother, and his nephews, hearing this, cried out with loud weeping; and there was great sorrow; and fear and trembling fell upon all. And Yuri said these words: “O Lord Almighty, is this pleasing to Thy love for mankind? But I know that all this has come about because of our sins. The will of the Lord be done. Blessed be the name of the Lord from henceforth and forever.

And why have I alone remained alive, without these new martyrs! But grant me, O Lord, to suffer for Thy holy name, and for the Christian faith, and for the people, and join me to these new martyrs.” And Yuri waited for his brother Yaroslav, but he did not come.

And Yuri commanded his voivode Zhiroslav Mikhailovich to gather troops, strengthen the people, and prepare for war.

On the Slaying of Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich by Batu

And Yuri sent a brave man, Dorofei Semenovich, and with him three thousand men, to find out about the Tatars; but he had not gone far when he returned again with these words: “My lord prince, the Tatars are already coming against us.”

Learning of this, Prince Yuri mounted his horse with his brother Svyatoslav and with his nephews, with Vasilko, and with Vsevolod, and with Vladimir, and with his men, and they set out against the pagans.

And Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich began to draw up his regiments; and then suddenly the Tatars came out to the River Sit to meet Prince Yuri; and Prince Yuri, laying aside all sorrow, moved against them.

And the two armies met, and there was a great battle and a cruel slaughter; and blood flowed like water; and by the will of God the Tatars overcame the Russian princes; and there Grand Prince Yuri Vsevolodovich of Vladimir was killed; and many of his voivodes, and boyars, and his host were cut down.

And his nephew, Prince Vasilko Konstantinovich of Rostov, the Tatars took alive and led him with them as far as the Sherensk Forest; and there they set up their camps.
