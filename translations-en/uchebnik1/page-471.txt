And to the true, holy, God-given faith these are greatly blinded, darkened, and foolish; the wretched ones attend to falsely named worship and to seductive “fabricated-wordism,” and by evil heresy are drawn to hardness of heart, to deceit and incontinence, and to foul vainglory; and, as those removed from the good, they do not lend their ear to saving words and pay no heed at all, for they have rejected the true living God, clearly proclaimed by the law and the prophets, and they honor one who rages against the Godhead and is clearly possessed by demons, and what he has said, greatly invented and demonic, they receive most gladly. Which God-inspired writing foretold the impious and poison-making Bohmit? Or which God-befitting prophet foretold that he would be a teacher of piety? These wretched ones deny every holy inspiration and teaching, in which people from Adam to the coming of Christ gladly listened to the prophets and accepted them, and by these clearly proclaimed Christ. But these wretched ones reject God and follow the evil sinner, of whom there are no testimonies or portents at all, as a God-taught teacher, and accept him as a teacher of the true faith, and, as a benefactor, make him God—O unworthy and evilly named ones. Let those merciless and demonic ones examine those same empty words, impiously and demonically uttered by this Bohmit. What proclamation will they have, what God-instructed words? For the Divine Word clearly teaches and brightly declares that truly the living God is not the cause of all evil, and man is self-willed in his salvation and perdition. But the thrice-wretched and thrice-cursed, fierce alike to God and man, evil-shaped enemy, by the diligence and malice of the man-deceiving demon, and also by the will of his untiring tongue and foolish speech, boldly designates God as the cause of all good and evil. And all that comes from man’s fall, or from the enemy’s temptation, or from his own sloth, or from inexperience—all this is received from divine diligence: so this sinner and empty-talker declares. Bohmit also teaches that he who does not care for wealth, and is humble-minded, and does not return evil for evil, rejects the sweet life. But he who is in wealth, and is high-minded, and loves all sweetness from wealth, and attacks fiercely against attackers, to him will be salvation of soul and participation in Paradise. O madness, and harmful mind, and humiliation, and laughter, and poison, and murder, and destruction to those evilly caught by him, even to this day seduced!
