As the Evangelist testifies, this same Herod the Ascalonite, son of Antipater, an Idumean by birth, son of his mother Cypros, who was freed from Arabia, from the pagan peoples, was already named king of Jerusalem and Judea, and he changed his life to evil. What had been said was fulfilled, since Herod himself was a foreigner and was circumcised, and began to rule over Judea, and appointed a foreigner, Ananedon, as high priest. In their days, according to the divine prophecy, the power of Judah ceased even in his former possessions and in the matter of the priests’ service. For then the Pontian, a Roman commander, came and captured Jerusalem and defiled the sanctuary, even the place where no one was permitted to enter, and he entered the temple. Of those who received authority from their forefathers, even until that time, of kings and high priests together, the last was Aristobulus, whom they bound and sent to Rome. The Romans made the whole land of Judea their own. And so at first the foreigner Herod, as has been said, took possession of the Jewish peoples; but when the manifest coming of Christ drew near, the Jewish high priests and rulers truly dwindled, according to the priestly prediction. For the high priests were from Judah on the mother’s side, since the great Aaron took as his wife the daughter of Amminadab, who was descended from Judah. After this, those who came from Judah and from Levi were kinsmen. Therefore Elizabeth, being descended from Aaron, was called a kinswoman of Mary the Mother of God. Both high priests who were from Judea dwindled away, and Herod was a foreigner and, as has been said, was set as king over the Jews when the Nativity of Christ drew near.

And the succession of the high priesthood, which had come down from the forefathers, was destroyed, according to Daniel’s prophecy, that the Lord is cut off. The high priests were near the rulers even to the end of the line. They began the rebuilding of the temple even before Darius, and finished it under Hyrcanus, and 483 years passed. Daniel also testifies and says: And he shall know and understand, speaking from the going forth of the word, that it shall return and Jerusalem shall be built, even until the Lord Christ: seven weeks and sixty-two. For sixty and two weeks give 483 years, and they receive dominion from the good rulers, of whom the last was Hyrcanus, of the Parthian people. Herod received the kingdom from Augustus, and his men were with him even until the final captivity of Jerusalem. From there also came the tradition of the high-priestly line, Jewish and Samaritan, that is, Maccabean, and the last high priest Hyrcanus, whom Herod killed. Thereafter lawless Jews became priests; some were unworthy, sometimes for a short time, and sometimes gradually they bought the place of high priest from the Romans, even until the final destruction.

Herod had sons with the same name, three. The firstborn was Antipas, who cut off the head of John the Forerunner; under him the Lord was crucified. This Antipas Herod took Herodias, the wife of Philip his brother.
