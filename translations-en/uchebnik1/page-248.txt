Vespasian also hung in the temple he had built the veil from the Jerusalem temple, to his own glory, and all the garments adorned with gold. The books of the law of Moses Vespasian ordered to be kept in his palace.

And to Judea Vespasian sent Lucilius Bassus as governor, and he received the army from Cerealis and went against the fortress of Herodium, and called upon the townspeople to surrender peacefully, and they obeyed him.

Then Bassus gathered all kinds of troops, and also received the Tenth Legion, and decided to conquer the fortress of Machaerus. It was very necessary to subdue this fortress, since the Jews could rise up and gather in this fortress, hoping in its stronghold. For Machaerus inspired in those living in it a firm confidence in safety, and deterred those approaching it from attack by its formidable appearance and natural defenses. For Machaerus stood on a very high cliff and was surrounded by strong walls. This cliff was inaccessible from all sides, for the chasms around it were bottomless and could neither be crossed nor filled in. From the west a gorge stretched for 60 versts and ended at the Asphalt Lake, and at this gorge rose the top of the fortress. On the south and north sides the gorges were smaller in size than on the west, but in depth were the same. The eastern gorge was no less than 100 cubits deep, and it ended at the mountains which were opposite Machaerus.

Alexander, king of the Jews, saw the stronghold of this place, and built a fortress there, which the Roman commander Gabinius destroyed in the war with Aristobulus. And when after this Herod became king, he intended to carefully develop this place, since the Arabian lands came close to it. It was convenient to station guards in Machaerus and from there send troops to ravage the Arabian lands. Therefore Herod enclosed a large area on this cliff and built a fortress there. And Herod set walls not only on the slopes but also on the hill itself, and erected towers 160 cubits high.

In the middle of the fortress Herod built a palace, majestic and beautiful. And he made many reservoirs, like houses, for storing water in them, so that the inhabitants would continually have water in abundance and there would be no shortage of it. Herod also placed in Machaerus all kinds of weapons, and everything necessary for waging war, and all kinds of food and clothing. And in every way Herod prepared the fortress of Machaerus so that for many years it would be possible not to fear conquest by besieging enemies.
