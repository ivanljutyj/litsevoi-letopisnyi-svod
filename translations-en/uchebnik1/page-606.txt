Vremennik, that is, the Chronicle, during the hierarchate of Lord Nikephoros, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, under the rule of the Orthodox pious Great Prince Vladimir Monomakh, son of Vsevolod, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of the great and equal-to-the-apostles holy Vladimir, named Vasily in holy baptism, who baptized all the Russian land. I wrote all this for love of the Lord God and the most pure Mother of God, and His saints, and for the sake of my fatherland—the Russian land—for salvation and benefit to all. And I beseech all who read these books to pray for me in their holy prayers, that I may hear from the Lord God the sweet and joyful voice on the day of the Great Judgment, and be delivered from endless torments, and receive the promised good things from the Lord through the prayers of the most pure Mother of God and all the saints. Amen.

In the same year Mstislav Vladimirovich went from Novgorod with the Novgorodians against the Chud and took the city of Medvezhya Golova on the day of the Forty Martyrs.

In the same year Mstislav laid in Novgorod a city wall larger than the former one, and Pavel, posadnik of Novgorod, founded a stone fortress in Ladoga.

In the year 6625 from Adam (1125 from Christ)

Yaropolk Vladimirovich built a town and gave it to the Druchane whom he had taken captive. In the same year Mina, Bishop of Polotsk, reposed, on the month of July on the 20th day.

In the same year Leon Diogenes, son-in-law of Vladimir, went against Tsar Alexios; and several Danubian cities were given to him, and in Dristra, a Danubian city, two Saracens sent by the Tsar treacherously killed him, on the month of August on the 15th day.

In the same year Yaropolk went to the Polovtsian land to the river called the Don, and took great booty there, and took three Polovtsian cities: Balin, Cheshlyuev, and Yusogrov, and brought Yasians with him, and took captive a Yasian woman as his wife. In the same year Oleg Svyatoslavich reposed. In the same year Vladimir Vsevolodovich founded a stone church on the Alta of the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb, for there they had been killed.

In the same year Vladimir Monomakh brought his son Mstislav out of Novgorod and seated him in Belgorod, and Vsevolod Mstislavich sat in Novgorod.

In the same year there was a sign in Novgorod at St Sophia from thunder: on May 14, at the tenth hour, while they were singing vespers, one of the deacons was struck by thunder, and the whole choir and the people fell prostrate, but rose up alive; and in the evening there was a sign on the moon.
