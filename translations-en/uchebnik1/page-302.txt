Awakening at these words, the wise King Constantine saw the physicians standing around him according to custom, to anoint his scabs as always, and the king said to them: “No longer shall human cunning enter into me, but let the Hand of Almighty God help me.” And having said this, during the day he sent to the divine Sylvester and commanded him to come with many honors.

And when he entered the chamber, the king rose and, meeting him first, bowed and kissed him, saying: “Welcome, honored Sylvester. Having found you in good health, we rejoice.” The holy Bishop Sylvester answered: “Peace to you from heaven, and salvation from God and victory shall be given.” The holy King Constantine told him of the night vision: “Two men appeared to me; but I beg you, tell me, do you have any gods named Peter and Paul, from whom I learned all about you?” The bishop answered: “We have one God, Creator of heaven and earth, and Peter and Paul are His closest servants.” The king said: “I beseech you, O bishop, if you have images of them, or with the help of some icon painters make me their visible images, so that I may know whether they were truly sent to me by God.”

And at once the bishop commanded his deacon to bring the stiphars—their portraits on boards. And the deacon brought him the images of the holy apostles. When the king saw the venerable icons, he recognized them and cried out in a loud voice: “Truly, those whom I saw with my own eyes in the vision that appeared to me cannot be depicted more truthfully; and they said to me: ‘Send for Bishop Sylvester, and he will show you the font of salvation; having washed in it, you will receive healing from your scabs.’ And now show me what they spoke of, that with this I may wash soul and body and be saved.” Blessed Sylvester ordered him to fast for seven days and to give alms, and having blessed him and catechized him, he departed from him.

On the Saturday on which the fast appointed for Constantine ended, in the evening, preparing for the usual service, Saint Sylvester ordered a water font to be made without delay and said to the blessed king: “This water, O king, which you now see, through the prayers to the Holy and Life-giving Trinity acquires divine power, which will cleanse the body from all impurity and leave it outside, making it brighter than the sun. Enter, then, this honorable and consecrated bath, and you will be cleansed from all the sins you have committed.” And with these words and with many prayers he blessed the holy water.
