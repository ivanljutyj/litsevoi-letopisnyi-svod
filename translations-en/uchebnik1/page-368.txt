But if someone is a flatterer, and does not magnify himself, and is not proud, this still does not mean that he is humble-minded. Therefore those who are deeply free must conduct themselves fittingly and keep themselves so, and be magnanimous but not proud; firm but not quick to anger; merciful but not servile; humble-minded but not timorous; free, and not a slave. Chrysostom himself says likewise: "So it is fitting for the pastor and teacher to be varied. 'To be varied' means to be neither a flatterer nor a tormentor, but, filled with great freedom and boldness, to discern when one ought to condescend, if circumstances require it and good will come of it, and equally when one ought to be fierce.
