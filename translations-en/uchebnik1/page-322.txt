And after remaining there some time, he promised to tell. The queen released him, and they came with Macarius the patriarch to a certain place. The patriarch prayed, and Judas also prayed in Hebrew, and God indicated the place of the Cross. At that place there was a mound of refuse and stones heaped up by the Jews. Later, on that same mound, Hadrian, king of Rome, erected a temple of Artemis and set up her statue in it.

The holy Queen Helena ordered many craftsmen to destroy the pagan temple to its foundation and to cart away the refuse. During the digging a fragrance of incense began to issue forth, and soon the Holy Sepulchre and the place of Kranion, also called the Place of the Skull, appeared on the eastern side, and near them three buried crosses were discovered. Having dug them up, they also found the Holy Nails.

All were perplexed, and the blessed queen grieved: which of them was the Life-giving Cross of Christ? At that time a certain dead woman was carried out. Immediately the holy bishop and Judas began in turn to lay each cross upon the dead woman, to learn which of them was the Lord’s Cross. And soon the dead woman revived by the power of the Divine Cross.

Then the blessed queen joyfully took the Venerable Wood and kept a part for herself, as also the Holy Nails; and the rest, placing in a silver reliquary, she gave to Archbishop Macarius to preserve as a memorial for later generations.

Then Judas also, with many Jews, believed and was baptized, and was named Cyriacus, later becoming patriarch of Jerusalem; and he died martyred for Christ under Julian the Apostate.

Then Queen Helena ordered churches to be built at the holy places which our Lord Jesus Christ visited for our salvation, having wrought most glorious miracles. The blessed and truly holy Queen Helena first ordered a church to be built in Jerusalem, where the Wood of the Venerable Cross was found, and in the name of her son she built the Church of Saint Constantine.

She also built a beautiful church at the Lord’s Sepulchre in the name of the Resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ. She also erected a church at the place of Kranion, where the Venerable Cross was found, making its steps of marble.

In the eastern part of the Holy City, which descends to the garden of Gethsemane, where the tomb of the Holy Mother of God rested, she wisely also built a church in that place in the name of the Most Holy Mother of God, establishing Her all-glorious tomb within the church’s altar.
