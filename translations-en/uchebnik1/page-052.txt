Pilate was a close friend of Tiberius Caesar, and he appointed him governor over the Jews.

Pilate told Tiberius Caesar of all that had been done by the Lord: that Jesus, called Christ, had been crucified and had risen on the third day, and that miracles were being done by his disciples. King Tiberius, hearing of this, marveled and believed in the Lord, and wished at once to honor Him with royal rank. But his nobles forbade Tiberius and called the Word of Christ madness. The Caesar, however, kept this word in his heart and destroyed those who slandered the Christians.

Tiberius Caesar also learned that Herod Antipas had consented to the death of Christ, and that because of the sin of adultery much evil had been in that land, and that Herod Antipas had killed John the Baptist, a righteous man, because he had sharply rebuked him for transgressing the law. And Tiberius was greatly enraged, and he commanded that the adulterer be bound and brought to Rome, and he cast him out of his rule, and took away his property, and sent him from that land with Herodias to Spain, where he ended his life badly. The dancing girl the earth swallowed alive. This Herod fell into illness, and was ill for a month, and was killed in his palace, and after eight months his wife learned of this.

Annas and Caiaphas served as high priests in the land of Judea. Forty years after the coming of our Savior and God, in the reign of that Tiberius Caesar, Saint Paul departed from Antioch the Great, where he had first preached the true word in the square which in those parts is called the Jawbone. And with Barnabas Paul went to Cilicia; Peter, however, came from Jerusalem to that Antioch the Great, and taught the Word, and ordained, and received bishops. And he called himself an heir there, and Peter trusted those Christians who were from the Jews, but he did not like to receive those who had believed from among the Gentiles, and so Peter left them and departed from there.

Saint Paul, after this, came to Antioch the Great and, having learned this about Saint Peter, dispelled all doubts and received all equally and loved all, bringing them to the faith, as the wise Clement and Tatian the Chronicler wrote. And King Tiberius died his own death in his palace when he was 70 years old; he had grown old, and he reigned 23 years, and after the Ascension of the Lord Tiberius reigned 5 years. This same Tiberius killed Longinus the centurion with two soldiers.

The Fourth Reign, of Gaius, son of Tiberius, who reigned in Rome. In the year 5538 from Adam (the 38th year from Christ)

After Tiberius, Gaius his son reigned, the fourth, and he was 35 years old, and he reigned 3 years and 7 months. This was a fierce and proud man. He was tall, slender, handsome of face, ruddy, with a barely visible mustache.
