No one among men could kill Marcian before the appointed time. Marcian was tall, with straight hair.

This Marcian was made emperor by Theodosius’s sister, the empress Pulcheria, with the boyars and the emperor. And charging Marcian, Pulcheria said: “Will you preserve my virginity until my death?” He then promised her.

And when Marcian became emperor, he took as his wife Theodosius’s sister, the Lady Pulcheria; she had remained a virgin for fifty-four years.

On the Punishment

In the reign of Marcian, the city called Tripolis, on the Phoenician coast, received punishment from God’s wrath in the month of Garpaeus, that is, September, at night.
