In the same year in Novgorod, Abbot Anthony founded a monastery and a stone church of the Holy Mother of God. In the same year Dobrynya, posadnik of Novgorod, died.

In the year 6626 from Adam (the year 1126 from Christ)

Vladimir went against Yaroslav Sviatopolchich to the city of Vladimir, and made peace with him; and Vladimir withdrew from him, having severely rebuked him, because Yaroslavets was angry with his granddaughter, Mstislav's daughter.

In the same year Lazarus, Bishop of Pereyaslavl, died on September 16, and in his place Nikifor, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus', appointed Sylvester as bishop on the first day of January.

In the same year Vladimir Monomakh married off his son Andrei, taking for him the granddaughter of Turkan, prince of the Cumans.

In the year 6627 from Adam (the year 1127 from Christ)

Yaroslavets Sviatopolchich sent away his wife, Mstislav's daughter, Vladimir's granddaughter; and for this Vladimir Monomakh went against him to Vladimir; he fled to Hungary, and his troops abandoned him.

Vladimir sent his son Roman to Vladimir to reign; but he died there on January 15. And Vladimir Monomakh sent another son, Andrei, to Vladimir to reign.

In the same year Gleb Vseslavich died. In the same year Abbot Kyriak and Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich founded a stone church of the Holy Martyr George in the monastery in Novgorod the Great.

In the same year the Church of the Most Holy Mother of God was completed in the Antoniev Monastery in Novgorod.

In the year 6628 from Adam (the year 1128 from Christ)

Yaroslav campaigned against the Cumans beyond the Don and, not finding them, returned.

And George Dolgoruky, his brother, campaigned against the Bulgars, took much booty, and defeated their army.

In the same year the Torks and Berendei fled from the Russian land; and so they rushed hither and thither and perished.

In the same year Boris came from Kiev to be posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6629 from Adam (the year 1129 from Christ)

Metropolitan Nikifor of Kiev and All Rus' died in the month of April.
