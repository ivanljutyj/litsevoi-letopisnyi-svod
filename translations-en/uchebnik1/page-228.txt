And Titus stood on the right side of the inner temple, and from there a bridge led to the city, which now separated the Romans from the Jews, so that both sides crowded around their leaders, waiting to see how the negotiations would end. Titus ordered his soldiers not to take up weapons or raise them against the Jews, and he placed an interpreter, and he was the first to speak to the Jews. “Have you not had your fill of the misfortune of your fatherland? You have considered neither our strength nor your own weakness, but have destroyed the people, the city, and the temple in senseless struggle, in demonic pride. Therefore you deserve destruction. You have opposed the Romans ever since Pompey took you, and now you have even gone to open war. Did you trust in your numbers? Yet even a small part of the Roman army has overcome you. Were you waiting for allies? But no one will wish to join you against us. Did you hope in your courage? But even the Germans, the bravest of all peoples, are our slaves. Did the strength of your walls inspire in you the desire to fight us? But even the Britons, sitting behind the strongest wall—the ocean—bow before Roman arms. Was it strength of soul, or endurance, or wisdom that moved you to war? But even the Carthaginians, who were stronger and wiser than all, were captured by us. It was the Romans’ own love of humanity that moved you against the Romans. For when we captured you, we allowed you to possess your land, and set a king over you from your own kin, and preserved for you the laws of your fathers, and gave you a free life, and did not forbid you to collect tithes from all the lands for the offering to God, and did not hinder you from growing rich. But you turned our wealth against us. You received so much good from us and yet rose up against us. Like furious serpents, you spat venom upon those who caressed you. At first you were able to insult the Romans owing to Nero’s negligence. Then my father Vespasian came into your land, but he did not wish to take vengeance on you, but to punish you.
