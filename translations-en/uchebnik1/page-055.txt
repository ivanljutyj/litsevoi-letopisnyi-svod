And Phineas, a priest of those Jews in Palestine, learned of this and gathered a multitude of Galileans, Jews, and townsmen, about 230,000, and came suddenly to that city from the city of Tiberias, and killed many, entering with armed men, and this Phineas again withdrew to Tiberias.

Gaius Caesar learned of this and was enraged at the nobles who were in Antioch, Pontius and Varius, and mocked them before all, and took all their property, and turned their courts in Antioch into royal property, and they came to be called royal palaces. Thereafter in that Antioch Gaius brought these nobles in chains, because they had neither suppressed the city riot nor opposed the army of the priest Phineas before the city.

Gaius also sent soldiers to Tiberias, a Palestinian city, and seized that Phineas, the Jewish priest, and cut off his head, as a ruler-tyrant, and destroyed many Jews and Galileans. And he set the head of the priest Phineas on a stake outside the city of Antioch on the far side of the Orontes River, and rebuilt what had been burned in the city, sending gold.

But all the military commanders saw what Gaius Caesar was doing and could not endure it, and killed him when he was washing in the bath, at the command of his nobles; he was 39 years old. In the time of Gaius there was Theudas, mentioned in the Acts of the Apostles; he was a deceiver, and many obeyed him, and he said: “I will cross the Jordan and make a passage for the people.” And in this he sinned, and he was killed, and all who were with him. And the tower of Siloam in the city fell on eighteen men.

The Fifth Reign, of Claudius, who reigned in Rome

In the year 5542 from Adam (the 42nd year from Christ). After Gaius, Claudius Caesar Germanicus reigned, under the governors Cassius and Silanus. This Claudius Caesar reigned 14 years and 9 months. He was short, grey-haired, white, and quiet. He founded a city and called it by his own name, Claudiopolis, near the ocean.

This King Claudius gave precedence to the Green faction. In his reign, by God’s wrath, the city of Ephesus and Smyrna fell, and many cities of Asia, and he sent them much gold for rebuilding. And he lightened for the Antiochenes the tax they paid on smoke, so that they could restore the covered galleries that Tiberius Caesar had built.

Under Claudius there was a great famine throughout the whole world, as the prophet Agabus foretold. Then the Jews shut themselves up in the city and killed one another on the roads. The king learned of this and sent an order to the kings of the peoples to kill the Jews without mercy. And many of those people died, and their houses and children were given over to plunder. And they were expelled from Italy and Rome. All this happened to them because they had crucified the Lord.
