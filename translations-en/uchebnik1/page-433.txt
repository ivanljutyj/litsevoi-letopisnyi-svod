Not only there, but also in houses and in covered cells, and in churches there were unburied dead. Many died unknown in the streets of strangers as they went to their own, since all their close kin died suddenly. That pestilence lasted two months: July and August.

There were also very great thunders and terrible lightning, so that people lying down were burned by lightning. There was also a great earthquake throughout the world, so that the whole universe suddenly fell, and the sea came out of its shores for two leagues, so that very many ships sailing perished from the movement of the water, beyond number.

In Arabia, Palestine, Mesopotamia, and Antioch many cities fell, and many people and cattle perished.

And in Constantinople then many churches fell, and many houses, and the city walls, and in that collapse a great multitude of people died; and that earthquake lasted 40 days and 40 nights.

And fire appeared from heaven in the form of a spear, stretching from east to west. And again there was an earthquake, so that two walls of Constantinople collapsed, on the east side and on the west, and many churches and houses fell and were destroyed even to the ground, and many lands were laid waste, so that it was not easy to recognize them; and the earth shook ceaselessly for 10 days and nights.

Grieving over this, the emperor came to church at the Nativity and Theophany without a crown, and did not make his usual dinner and supper for 12 days, but gave all food, wine, and silver to the poor.

Soon there was another pestilence from a disease called vuvon, or carbuncle, and this pestilence was no less than the former, and for four months people died from this pestilence throughout the city and around it.

In Antioch there was a great earthquake lasting an hour, and a terrible gnashing was heard from heaven, and buildings fell upon one another, and 30,000 people died.

The patriarch and all the people went with crosses and prayed. And it appeared to a certain man in a dream that all people should write on the gates and doors of their houses: “Christ is with us, stop.” And when this was done, immediately the shaking of the earth ceased.

And a great bright star appeared on the western side, casting rays upward, which they called Lampadia, that is, the Shining One, and it remained 20 days and nights, shining. There was also throughout the world rule of the people and much killing. After some time there was a movement of the stars from evening even until morning, so that all marveled greatly and said that the stars were falling. And after a little time again the sun shone without rays, like the moon. And there were strife, diseases, and deaths among people, and they did not cease.
