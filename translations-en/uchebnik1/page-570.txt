The king had great trust in the Prophet Elijah. And on the day of the feast, when he was dining with his princes, in that room hung a cage with a bird called pstak, though some call it a parrot; it imitates with its voice any word it hears from a man. And the bird then heard how a man wept and said, “Leomu, Leomu,” and began to pronounce clearly and distinctly: “Leon, Leon!”

When the princes heard this, they began to weep and with tears to plead for Leo, saying: “This irrational animal is above us; it demands and seeks its master, and cries out against our shame. But we, rational men, do not remember him who has been shut in prison so long.” The king was moved by these words, forgave his son, and made him emperor, as he had been before.

When the emperor, according to custom, made his entrance, the people, seeing him, cried out: “Glory to Thee, O Lord!” The emperor turned to the people and said: “Is it not for my son’s sake that you glorify God? Because of him you will fall into many sorrows, and evil days lie ahead for you to endure.”

And Santavarin slandered Andrew, the domestikos of the Scholai, before the emperor, saying that he sympathized with Leo; and he was removed from power, and Stupiot was appointed domestikos.

And when he came to Tarsus, he was cruelly defeated, and all turned away from him. The city was called Tarsus because Bellerophon, having come there, fell and injured his shoulder, or from Mount Tersius in Taurus it was earlier called Tersos, because after it had been waterless, it was flooded by a flood. After this Andrew again became domestikos.

About the Russian Prince

The tribes called Rus, who are also the Cumans, lived by the Euxine Pontus and began to attack the Roman land.

And they gathered to go against Constantinople, but the higher providence prevented this, and the wrath of God was upon them, and then their princes Askold and Dir returned with nothing.

Basil, who had fought much against the Hagarenes and Manichaeans, made peace with the aforementioned Rus. And he turned them to Christianity, and they promised to be baptized.

And they asked for an archbishop for themselves, and the emperor sent him to them, and when they gathered to be baptized, they again doubted.
