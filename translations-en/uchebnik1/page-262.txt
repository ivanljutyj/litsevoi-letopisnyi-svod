Rome. Byzantium (78–345 from the Incarnation of Christ).

And from Adam to the capture of Jerusalem by Titus there passed 5575 years (75 years from Christ).

And from the first building of the temple by Solomon and of the city — 1088 years; from the second building of the temple by Zerubbabel and Jesus, son of Josedech — 596 years; after the capture of Jerusalem by Antiochus — 248 years; after the Ascension of the Lord — 42 years. Eusebius wrote that the Jews crucified Christ on their feast, and on a feast they all perished, and received recompense for the murder of the Savior and God. Eusebius described this as the third capture of Jerusalem. Titus, having announced the victory, came to Rome.

Vespasian, after the devastation of Judea, set up in Great Antioch of Syria large copper cherubim, which his son Titus had found nailed in the temple of Solomon. When the temple was destroyed, Titus took them from there and brought these seraphim to Antioch as a sign of the victory over the Jews that occurred in his reign. And they set up a tall copper idol in honor of the moon, with four bulls looking toward Jerusalem, for Titus took the city by night, when the moon was shining. In the 4th year of Vespasian’s reign Titus took Jerusalem, and after the capture of Jerusalem Vespasian died five and a half years later.

The 11th reign, of Titus, son of Vespasian, who began to reign in Rome in the 5581st year from Adam (the 81st year from Christ).
After Vespasian, his son Titus reigned three years.

And in the hot season, having spent the whole day on the road, he was severely scorched by the sun, so that blood flowed from his nose, and he became gravely ill. And while he was still breathing, his brother Domitian laid him in the coffin at the command of Titus’s wife.
