And he accepted the heresy of the single will, that is, the Monothelite heresy. Then, not only because of such heresy but also because of sin against the law of God—for he took as wife his sister’s daughter, that is, his niece, the daughter of his sister—Heraclius fell into a grievous dropsical illness. By whatever a man sins before God, not being ashamed before the face of God, by that same he receives punishment. And Heraclius’s face was defiled by filth issuing through his water passage. By whatever a man sins against nature, by that same he receives punishment. When the king wished to release water, he placed a board on his belly; since the water did not come out, thereupon his shameful member turned and discharged water onto his face. And his illness dragged on very painfully, and in it he died.

The Reign of Constantine, Son of Heraclius

After Heraclius, his son Constantine reigned for 4 years, who showed himself in few things as a sole autocrat, and soon hid himself, and vanished before his time, having said and done nothing worthy of memory, being young in body. As they say, Pyrrhus secretly mixed deadly poison for Constantine at the instigation of Martina, his stepmother, and Heraclonas, her son. This Pyrrhus was patriarch of the city of Constantine, a destroyer, a wicked man, a poisoner, a sorcerer, who after Sergius the heretic received the patriarchal throne.

Concerning a Certain Rich Man Who Gave Alms, and Then, Repenting of What He Had Done, Took the Gold Back and Died

Under that Emperor Heraclius, a certain rich man in the city of Constantine fell seriously ill and, fearing death, distributed 30 litras of gold to the poor.

And after that occurrence the rich man became healthy again, and, heeding diabolical deception, repented that he had given away the gold, and told this to his close friend, also a rich man. He said to him: “By no means, O man, accept this evil thought, lest you anger God, who strengthened you and healed you for the sake of your alms. But if you accept this evil thought and anger God, He will bring sudden death upon you, and you will depart from this world without repentance.”

But the rich man who had given the alms did not listen to this advice, but was about to flee from his friend in anger. And his friend said to him: “Since you do not listen to what I have said for your salvation, I will give you another piece of advice.” He asked: “What other?” And so the truly merciful man answered: “Come with me to the church and say: ‘Not I, Lord, gave this alms, but it is his,’ and I will give you 30 litras of gold.” And then that wretched man agreed to this, and came to the church, and received the gold into his hands, since he had said everything as his friend asked him; but as he was going out of the church doors, he fell down dead.
