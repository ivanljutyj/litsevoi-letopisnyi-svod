The tsar came to him with his mother, and they asked him: “Why have you withdrawn?” With many tears he said: “It would have been better for me never to have sat upon the patriarchal throne of this church, for it is cut off from the other holy patriarchal thrones and is cursed.” Hearing this, the tsar and tsaritsa withdrew in sorrow.

And they sent a patrician to him, so that he might hear what he was saying. To them, at the end of his exhortation, he said with boldness: “If there is no ecumenical council and the scandal of our faith is not corrected, you will have no salvation.” They said to him: “Why, then, did you write, when you were chosen to the patriarchate, that holy icons should not be venerated?”

And he said to them: “For this reason I, weeping, have resorted to repentance, and I pray that God not torment me as a priest and pastor who kept silent and did not preach the truth for fear of your anger and of murder.” Hearing this, they withdrew, having within themselves much perplexity and questioning among themselves about all these words, which reveal the truth and expose the foul heresy. The patriarch reposed in peace and Orthodoxy, leaving behind great lamentation among the tsars and the Orthodox people of Tsargrad, for he was a wholly honorable man, respected, and adorned with every virtue. Therefore many also firmly trusted him.

And in his place Tarasius was appointed, by rank an asekretis, that is, a secretary. At his instruction the tsar and tsaritsa gathered representatives from Rome and from the other patriarchal thrones and from the whole world, and having assembled in the reigning city, in the Church of the Holy Apostles, they began to read the divine writings and to bring them to the hearing of all the tsars and people.

And the disciples of the teacher Constantine Copronymus rose up against them, drew their swords, and rejected the council.

At that time the tsar brought them out unarmed, arranging a feast in the city of Malagina, and exiled them to confinement in their own lands.

But the divine Tarasius he sent with the previously named bishops to the city of Nicaea, to hold a council there. And there was a holy and ecumenical council, and the church received her ancient and first beauty.
