The Tale of the Elevation of the Bread of the Holy Mother of God

After the awe-inspiring Resurrection of the Savior, even until the Descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles, even until the separation of the holy apostles for preaching, all the apostles were together. And when after prayer they reclined and ate, they left the Lord’s place unoccupied, and thereafter, from the portion, or bread tablet, which they ate, they placed Christ’s part in the place. And when they finished eating, they rose and gave thanks, and took the bread tablet, which they called the Lord’s portion and which lay in the place, and elevated it upward, and said: Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, and the rest. And instead of “O, great Name...” they said: Christ is risen, until the Ascension. From the Ascension even until the Dormition of the Mother of God they said: O great Name of the Holy Trinity, Lord Jesus Christ, help us. Each of them did this likewise if he found himself somewhere alone. When the apostles made a worldwide assembly on the clouds for the Repose of the Lady, they performed the customary burial, that is, matins and consolation for those who had come, and after they had arranged everything, they rose, and the bread tablet, which they called Christ’s, they elevated as usual, and before it they said “O, great Name...” O most glorious miracle and great wonder! — she who was dead appeared alive on the clouds, and luminous angels stood before her and moved through the air, greeting her. She said: I am with you all the days of your life; I have received this joyful gift from my Son. Having seen this miracle, the disciples marveled and instead of “Lord Jesus Christ...” sang “O Most Holy Mother of God, help us!” And then they came to the most pure body, opened the tomb, and found it empty, and were assured that she truly lives in the body. As before her Son, Who rose from the dead, so she too rose, having previously departed, and reigns in Heaven with Christ. When the unbelieving Jewish robber boldly wanted to overturn the bier of the Divine Vessel, immediately an angel cut off his hands, and they fell off; then he immediately believed, and was healed by Peter, and glorified God. And the apostles glorified her, and having glorified and given praise to God, they departed each to his own place. The years of the Mother of God are counted thus: at 7 years her parents brought her to the church; she spent six and a half years in the Lord’s church, and six months in the house of Joseph the Betrothed, and she was the worldwide joy of the Annunciation. At 15 she gave birth to Emmanuel; with her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, she spent 33 years, which added together make 48 years. After the Ascension of her Son she remained in the house of John the Theologian on holy Zion for 24 years. In all she lived 72 years. After the Dormition of the Mother of God all the apostles scattered. John went to Ephesus. Matthew the Evangelist says that until the captivity of Jerusalem none of the apostles went far away.
