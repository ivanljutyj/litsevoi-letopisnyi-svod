At his time Eupsychius from Cappadocia was martyred; Faith, Love, Hope, and their mother Sophia from Italy were also martyred, along with the slave Arinda, and Eleutherius from Rome, son of father Sidor and mother Anthia, and Philetus the Senator and Lydia his wife, and their children Macedonius and Theoprepia, and Romud, Commentarisius, Anthilophus, Ducas, and Pausilopus. Heretics. In his time the leaders of heresy Cendron, Valentinus, Marcion, Montanus, Saturnilus, Carpocrates, and Aquila became known.

The 16th Reign: Antoninus, who began to reign in Rome in the year 5633 from Adam (the 133rd year from Christ)

After Hadrian, Antoninus Pius reigned 22 years. In his time Polycarp, disciple of Saint John the Evangelist, and Justin the Philosopher, and Dionysius, bishop of Corinth, were martyred, and Meletina from Marcian, a Phrygian city, and Theodore who was in Perga, and his mother Philippa, and with them two soldiers, Socrates and Dionysius. Heretics. And Tatian and Bardesanes, and Priscilla and Maximilla, false prophets from Phrygia, became known as founders of heresy.

The 17th Reign: Marcus, who began to reign in Rome in the year 5655 from Adam (the 155th year from Christ)

After Antoninus Pius, his son Marcus reigned 19 years together with Verus, his son-in-law. He was virtuous, very God-fearing, and most wise. But he died of poison. In his time fire from heaven appeared from time to time. In his time there was Abercius, a bishop equal to the apostles, from Hierapolis in Phrygia.
