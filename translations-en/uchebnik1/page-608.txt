In the 6630th year from Adam (the 1130th year from Christ)

There was a sign in the sun on the tenth day of the month of March; and there was a sign in the moon on the 20th day of the same month.

In the same year Princess Mstislavova died, on the 12th day of January. In the same year Daniel, bishop of Yuryev, died, on the 9th day of September. In the same year a metropolitan named Nikita came from Tsargrad to Holy Sophia, to Kiev and all Rus.

In the same year Amphilochius, bishop of Vladimir, died; and the earth shook a little. In the same year the Poles captured Volodar, Vasilko's brother.

In the same year Mstislav Vladimirovich married in Novgorod the daughter of Dmitry Zavidovich.

In the 6631st year from Adam (the 1131st year from Christ)

Blessed Sylvester, bishop of Pereyaslavl, died on the 12th day of April, on Maundy Thursday.

In the same year Prince David Mstislavich died in Chernigov; and after him Prince Yaroslav, his brother, took his place in Chernigov.

In the same year Nikita, metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Simeon bishop in Vladimir-Volynsky. In the same year Feoktist, bishop of Chernigov, died on the sixth day of August.

In the same year Prince Yaroslavets, son of Svyatopolk, grandson of Izyaslav, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of great Vladimir, came to the city of Vladimir against Prince Andrey, son of Vladimir Monomakh, grandson of Vsevolod, great-grandson of Yaroslav, great-great-grandson of great Vladimir, with a multitude of warriors, with Hungarians and Poles, and with Czechs, and with Pinyans, and with the Rostislavichi, Volodar and Vasilko; and they besieged Prince Andrey in the city of Vladimir with a multitude of troops.

Prince Andrey Vladimirovich shut himself in the city of Vladimir and fought them stoutly; but his father Vladimir Monomakh was gathering soldiers in Kiev and did not manage to send him help quickly from Kiev.

Prince Yaroslavets rode around the city of Vladimir and strongly threatened Prince Andrey and the townspeople: "Tomorrow I will take your city and put you all to cruel torture without mercy."

And in the morning, when the sun was rising, many warriors began to prepare for the assault on the city; but Prince Yaroslavets, not waiting for his retinue, went toward the city accompanied by two warriors.
