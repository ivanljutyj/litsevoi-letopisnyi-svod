And then by the following improper deed the Hellenic priests, known for cunning, true servants of the devil, nearly seduced him—the crafty ones—for they said, vile as they were: “A bath must be made in the Roman Capitolium, filled with the blood of innocent infants, and heated so that steam rises, and he must bathe in the warm foaming blood in order to recover, and he will be able to be cleansed.” Out of Hellenic evil-mindedness and inhumanity, fierce vile madmen, by such healing you promise to cure one man by slaughtering many infants! What the child-killers proposed did not please Tsar Constantine, and the tsar said: “Let it not be according to your crafty and loathsome counsel.” But the Hellenes, evil-doing criminals and workers of abominations, tempted the tsar’s ear with the promise of health, compelling him by temptation to descend to the devil.

And having heard that word, the tsar remembered it and sent soldiers throughout all dioceses to gather children for his healing.

And from all the dioceses, by the tsar’s command, a multitude of children was brought. On the appointed day, when the tsar was going to the Capitolium and the soldiers had prepared to kill the children, the mothers of the infants met him with uncovered heads, bared breasts, crying and lamenting, filling the air with grief and wailing. The tsar asked about them: “What is the cause of their weeping?” And learning that these were the mothers of the children doomed to be killed, he was overcome by the monstrosity of the sin, and sighing from the depth of his heart, he began to weep and to say in a loud voice: “The lord of the Roman kingdom has shown the source and root of piety. And for its sake I will not set an example for all, lest they adopt it. I would sooner prefer the salvation of innocent infants to my own health. From my youth I have not given bitter, inhuman orders, and these little children will establish piety for their mothers in Rome. It is better to die for the salvation of innocent children than by the murder of the young to obtain and renew my life. Let them give the mothers their beloved children, and let the sweetness of their sons return to the mothers, and let them sweeten the grief that has been caused.” And having said this to the boyar retinue, he returned and entered the palace, not only giving their children back to their mothers, but also ordering gifts, carts, and food to be given to them for the journey back to their homes. And they, returning, entered their homes with joy and gladness. And after this, that same night the tsar saw a vision: there appeared to him the apostles of Christ, saying: “We are Peter and Paul, sent from the Lord God to give you an image of salvation and health.
