The maiden, adorned with virginity, was seized because she reviled kings and idols. Taking her, they tortured her greatly, and since she did not submit to their impiety, they sold her for fornication, ordering the keeper of the brothel to take her and to bring in three gold pieces a day for her. But he who wished to receive the gold handed her over to those who desired her. Learning of this, lovers of women, sinners, began coming to her and burst in on her, offering silver. But she, pleading, at first said: “I have a foul-smelling discharge; I am ashamed of it, since it will infect you too. Therefore it is better that you wait a little—a few days—until I am healed, and I will always be in your power.” Thus she deceived them, and with prayers she entreated God to deliver her from such defilement and to preserve her virginity without disturbance. And the Lord God heard her prayers and saved her thereafter in the following manner. Once a certain noble and very pious young man came to her master’s harlots and gave him five gold pieces, saying: “Leave this maiden with me until morning.” And entering the place where she was kept, he said to her: “Rise and go in peace.” And he clothed her in his own garment and covered her head, and she went out undefiled, and unexpectedly the woman found herself free. By the end of the next day what had happened was discovered, and the young man was condemned to death. And when this happened, the word of the Lord was fulfilled which says, “Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friend.” Likewise a certain other monk was seized by these inhuman and impious men. After many tortures and wounds, they finally devised the following impiety to corrupt the chastity of the righteous man. Having placed a bed in a garden and bound the saint to it, they left a harlot with him, so that in this pleasant place he might be unable, against his will, to avoid the harlot’s advances and be drawn into unlawful union. But the divine man, while the woman, embracing him, spoke indecent words and inflamed him with sweetness, bit off his own tongue and spat it in her face, causing himself pain and weeping instead of sweetness, and he astonished her with the flowing blood and filled her with shame. Thus the Hellenes learned what true monastic chastity is. The impious woman too was defeated. And the torturers Diocletian and Maximian, because of the strength and steadfastness of the Christians and the multitude of the slain, issued a decree to tear out the right eye of captured Christians, so as not only to cause them pain but also to dishonor them, making them outcasts among the Roman inhabitants. But God’s judgment struck them with a punishment that justly overtook them: Diocletian was killed at a council of nobles, and Maximian’s entrails poured out.
