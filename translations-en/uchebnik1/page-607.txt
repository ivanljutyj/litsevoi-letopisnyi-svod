In the same year in Novgorod hegumen Anthony founded a monastery and a stone church of the Holy Mother of God. In the same year Dobrynya, posadnik of Novgorod, died.

In the year 6626 from Adam (the year 1126 from Christ)

Vladimir went against Yaroslav Svyatopolchich to the city of Vladimir and made peace with him; and Vladimir withdrew from him, having strongly rebuked him, because Yaroslavets was angry with his granddaughter, Mstislav’s daughter.

In the same year Bishop Lazar of Pereyaslavl died on the 16th day of September, and in his place Nikifor, Metropolitan of Kiev and All Rus, appointed Bishop Sylvester on the first day of January.

In the same year Vladimir Monomakh married his son Andrei, taking for him the granddaughter of Turkan, prince of the Polovtsians.

In the year 6627 from Adam (the year 1127 from Christ)

Yaroslavets Svyatopolchich sent away his wife, Mstislav’s daughter, Vladimir’s granddaughter; and for this Vladimir Monomakh went against him to Vladimir; he fled to Hungary, and his soldiers deserted him.

Vladimir sent his son Roman to Vladimir to reign; but he died there on the 15th day of January. And Vladimir Monomakh sent another son of his, Andrei, to Vladimir to reign.

In the same year Gleb Vseslavich died. In the same year hegumen Kiriak and Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich founded a stone church of the Holy Martyr George in the monastery in Novgorod the Great.

In the same year the church of the Most Holy Mother of God was completed in the Antoniev Monastery in Novgorod.

In the year 6628 from Adam (the year 1128 from Christ)

Yaroslav went against the Polovtsians beyond the Don and, not finding them, returned.

And George Dolgoruky, his brother, went against the Bulgars, took much booty, and defeated their army.

In the same year the Torks and Berendei fled from the Russian land; and so they rushed here and there, and perished.

In the same year Boris came from Kiev to be posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6629 from Adam (the year 1129 from Christ)

Metropolitan Nikifor of Kiev and All Rus died in the month of April.
