The dog went, and when Simon was teaching someone magical deceits, it stood in the midst and said in a human voice to Simon: Peter, servant of God Most High, wishes to enter to you. Hearing the dog speaking in a human voice, they marvelled and said: Who is this Peter of whom the dog speaks, and what is his power, that he made the dog speak in a human voice and made it a messenger? And Simon said to those standing and marvelling at Peter: People, let this man not frighten you. For I too will command the dog to make answer to him in the same human voice. And Simon said to the dog: I command you: go and tell Peter in a human voice that Simon says to him, let him enter.

And Peter entered to Simon, and Simon the Egyptian and Peter contended, working miracles, and Peter overcame Simon Magus. And Peter performed healings, and many believed Peter and were baptized. Thus in Rome a great tumult arose, and a storm because of Simon and the miracles wrought.

And learning of the tumult, Agrippa the governor told King Nero, saying: There are in this city certain ones who work miracles against each other: one has called himself Christ, and the other says that he is not Christ but a sorcerer, and calls himself a disciple of Christ. And then King Nero ordered Simon Magus and Peter brought to him, and Pilate brought from prison. And they brought them to the king. And Nero asked Simon: Are you Christ? He said: I am. And Peter said: This is not Christ, for I am a disciple of Christ, and in my presence He ascended to Heaven. And after this Nero summoned Pilate and asked him about Simon: Is this the one whom you handed over for crucifixion? And stepping forward, Pilate said: This is not Christ. Likewise Nero asked Pilate about Peter: And do you know this one, is he truly a disciple of Christ? Pilate said: Yes, the Jews brought him to me as a disciple of Christ, and he denied it, saying that he was not a disciple of Christ, and I released him. And then Nero ordered them driven out: Simon because he lied, calling himself Christ, which he was not, and Peter because he denied Christ, as Pilate exposed him. And they drove them from the royal chambers, and they remained in Rome, working miracles against each other.

Simon ordered a great bull brought and said into the bull’s ear, and then the bull died, and this was wondrous. Peter, having prayed before all, caused the bull to come to life, which was an even greater miracle. Simon saw that Peter was prevailing, and did not wish to contend with Scripture and the Word of God, and departed with great shame and defeat. This vile Simon knew many things through diabolical action, but knew nothing through spiritual teaching.
