He blessed his child. He was distinguished by bodily purity and also by virtue: from his youth he fasted so strictly that four copper coins a day were enough for his food. Over many years of his life he was accustomed to sleep on the ground or on a mat, and at night he slept little, devoting himself more to the study of divine words; and giving himself to this, in hunger and without rest, and keeping himself in nakedness, he so subdued bodily desires that he looked completely withered, since he abstained from wine, oil, and other things. Therefore he was also famous, matching his words with deeds; and he turned many Greeks away from the worship of idols, persuading them to be crowned with martyrdom. And since great fame spread about him, many came to him from afar; and not only Greek philosophers but also intelligent heretics were drawn to piety; and he strengthened many Christians in the faith, teaching them. Among them also the aforementioned Ambrose repeatedly entreated him, forcing him to come to Caesarea, and assigned to him eight shorthand writers and many calligraphers, so that he might make an interpretation of the divine books, providing him with everything necessary for this. He, having obtained leisure, dictated to the shorthand writers, and the scribes, trained in calligraphy, copied 6,000 books onto skins. He interpreted all divine Scripture over 18 years. They say that he wrote 6,000 books, showing such an example of zeal for interpreting divine words that Ambrose, bearing witness to Origen’s great diligence, wrote to a certain person, speaking of things dedicated to God: “The sincerely devoted Ambrose often remembered you. But although I considered myself diligent and thirsting for God’s word, he put me to shame by his diligence and love for the holy teachings, so far surpassing me in this that I shall have to die because of his immense zeal. For it is impossible to dine without conversation, nor after dinner may one stroll and give rest to the body, but all the time we are compelled to philosophize, checking what has been written, having no opportunity all night for bodily rest, so that we often have to sleep in the evenings. And he detained the scholars; I do not speak of the fact that even in the morning, until the ninth hour, and they say until the tenth hour. So let all who wish to suffer of their own will devote their time to interpreting divine words, adding this to reading.” So, having interpreted all divine Scripture, he died at the age of 69.
