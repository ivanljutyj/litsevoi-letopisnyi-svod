Concerning the Magi

In the year 5502 from Adam (the 2nd year from Christ), Herod the local ruler, that is, the King of the Jews, in time learned that some time had passed, and that Magi had come from Persia to the land of Judea, as envoys from the summer east, to the left of Jerusalem, for there Persia borders Judea. The star was not like the other stars, but over the earth it moved not according to custom, as if by some compulsion, never having appeared before, said Basil the Great. And John Chrysostom says: where the Birth of Jesus was pure and ineffable, it was not in a cave, nor in any new dwelling, but Jesus was a young Child, as Matthew the Evangelist himself testifies. Indeed, when it came to pass according to the revelation—Joseph, the all-pure Virgin, and Him born of her without seed—when from the foreign and strange Magi we hear the fear of God: who He was, and for what reason, and that He made a descent to mankind—not only these, but all Jerusalem, and all those rulers of the Jews.

On the 8th day they circumcised the Child and gave Him the name Jesus, which is interpreted Savior, and they returned to Nazareth.

After 40 days had passed, they brought the Child to Jerusalem and presented Him to the Lord with gifts. Then Simeon the elder received Him in his arms and blessed Him. Then there was a sign to Joseph to flee to Egypt.

And Herod ordered the Magi to be seized, and said: for the Magi have come from Persia. Herod learned that the Magi had received the news from the star that appeared to them, which announced to them in the east the incarnation of Christ the Savior, and they were bringing gifts to Him as to a great king and victor, and they brought gold, frankincense, and myrrh: gold as to a king, frankincense as to a holy one, and myrrh as to a dead man. And having come to Jerusalem, they asked: Where is the newborn King of the Jews? And the Jews were in confusion. They learned about the Magi, and took them, and brought them in to the king. And Herod asked them: Why have you come to the land of Judea, envoys? And the Magi told him about the course of the stars, and about the wonder, and that a great King had been born to the world, and that they had brought gifts to Him as to a great King and God, for we have seen His star in the east.
