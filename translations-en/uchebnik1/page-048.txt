And from there Jesus departed and went to Capernaum, and there He healed the paralytic. And many others were healed everywhere, when they were instructed by His word, and they brought them what was needed. Many did His disciples instruct and baptize. Jesus did not baptize, but only taught and healed the sick.

And Zebedee died, and a son of Zebedee came to Jesus and said to Him: command me to go and bury my father. Christ did not permit him then, but later permitted him to go.
