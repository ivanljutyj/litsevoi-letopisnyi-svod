…and, having wallowed in accursedness, they will be taken and perish utterly.
Therefore one must greatly marvel at such manifest madness and insensibility of theirs. They live but a short time and make themselves out to be wise, but to what a most irrational seduction, sliding, and fall they hasten! For it behooves the senseless and beast-like to understand their teaching about the resurrection and to expose this foul noose, since for the sake of food and drink, visible and corruptible, they take so much in this life and are compelled again to serve corruption and to die, following their teaching. For it is impossible for the present food and drink to remain without change and altogether incorruptible, and to abide in immortality. And where then is the hope of eternal and incorruptible life for those faithful to Divine Scripture? And from this let the foolish and wretched understand that to this day they remain unchanged from their forefathers in godless seduction, but they think that they serve idols in a pious manner, and remaining thus, these foolish and corrupted ones can in no way understand the falseness of their deceitful faith, of the evil demon from the beginning. They also serve idols, and among the pagans they bow to the so-called Aphrodite, which means “sweetness,” to the star, for that is the day-star, that is, the morning star, and thus they fable, calling it “iskuvar” in their evil-speaking tongue, which means “great, mighty,” and thus they remained even to this day, and they called the goddess Aphrodite. And let not certain ones who speak lies think that they are declaring a great mystery, for darkness is this foul word and their abominable prayer: “allauyaquvar,” which is interpreted “God, God”; “au” is interpreted “greater, highest”; “quar” as “great, mighty, that is, the moon and Aphrodite God,” so it comes out: “God, God, greater, mighty, that is, the moon and Aphrodite God.” And this declares their final delusion, and they say “alla” falsely and ignorantly in their tongue, which shows madness before rational people who have understanding. We have made this known for the sake of information about these swine-like people, who know nothing else but sweetness and despondency. From the word just adduced concerning those who are senselessly and ceaselessly ailing, let us return to the narrative we left off.

After the death of the God-hating Bokhmit, there appeared in the south a star called “dokit,” that is, “spear,” proclaiming the power of these Arabs; it remained 30 days, stretching from the south even to the north in the manner of a spear. In that same year these Arabs, formerly called Saracens, made war, and having left Arabia, a great multitude of them came into the land of Damascus.

Baanis and Basiliscus, who were commanders in the East, learned of this and rushed against them. When the regiments fought, the Christians were completely defeated. And by God’s permission a great wind blew against the Greeks, and because of the great dust they could not see the enemy. And for this reason the Greek regiments were utterly defeated.
