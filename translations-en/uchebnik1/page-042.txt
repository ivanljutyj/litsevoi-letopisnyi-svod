Herod, at their request, sent to John and found him reproving even more.

And there was a feast, and when they had drunk their fill, the daughter of that Herodias came and danced and pleased Herod, and Herod swore to her to give whatever she asked, even to half the kingdom. She, instructed by her mother, asked for the head of John the Baptist.

And the king sent an executioner, beheaded him in the prison, and brought his head on a platter; and the king took it and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.

His disciples came, took his body, and buried it. Saint John was then thirty-one years and two months old; he suffered on the twenty-ninth day of August.

This Herod the king, the second brother of Philip, went out from the city of Sebaste to Pania, a Jewish city.

And a certain rich woman came to him; since she had been healed by Christ, she wished to set up His image, but did not dare to do so without the king's command.

And that woman asked Herod to permit her to set up a bronze image of Christ the Savior in that city.

She set forth her request thus: To the honorable Herod, ruler and establisher of laws for the Jews and Greeks of the country of Trachonitis, a petition and request from Veronica, noblewoman of the city of Paniada.

May righteousness, love of mankind, and other good deeds preserve your divine head.

Therefore I too, knowing this, turn to you with good hopes and perhaps obtain what I ask.

As for the meaning of this present petition, what happened to me will tell of it.

I suffered from a heavy flow of blood, and having exhausted my property and wealth, I found no healing.

Having learned of Christ's wondrous healings—He raised the dead and brought them back again to this light, cast demons out of a man, and healed all with festering wounds—I too came to Him as to God.

And I drew near to Him, while a crowd stood around Him, and I was afraid to tell Him of my persistent wound, fearing lest He be disgusted by the filth of my illness and be angry with me, and lest an even greater infirmity then come upon me.

And I thought to myself that if I could touch the hem of His garment, I would be healed.

And having thought so, I made my way through the crowd and secretly received healing by touching the hem of His garment.

And the bleeding stopped, and I became completely well. But He at once, as one who knows the thoughts of my heart, cried out: Who touched Me? Power has gone out from Me. I was terrified and groaned, thinking that a greater illness would return to me, and I fell at His feet, watering the ground with tears, and with boldness confessed everything.
