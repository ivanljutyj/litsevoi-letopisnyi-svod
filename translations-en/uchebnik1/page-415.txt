Athalaric was king, from the royal house of the Amals; in Africa Hilderic was king; in Inner India Caleb reigned over the Aksumites and Himyarites, being a Christian; in Lazica Zamala reigned. In the aforementioned year of Justinian's reign, a stratelates, that is, a commander, named Citt was appointed by him in Armenia. In former years Armenia had no stratelates, but only satraps, duces, comites, and princes.
