Dear readers!

This small volume contains texts from several volumes of the Illustrated Chronicle Compilation of Ivan the Terrible. We hope that this compact version of the narrative, rendered only in modern language, will make easier and speed up the accomplishment of the most important task for every person—self-education.

A low bow to the enthusiasts through whose efforts this Textbook of History came into being: Nikolai Petrovich Pokarev, Anatoly Borisovich Yanovsky, and Konstantin Anatolyevich Babkin.

I wish you and your children exciting and useful reading.

Glory to Jesus Christ!
