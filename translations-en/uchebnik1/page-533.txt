“Now, if it is fitting, then know that in the morning I will accuse you and tell the tsar everything, and then you will perish.” And he gave this message to Papias with the words: “Deliver this to such-and-such and such-and-such and say: ‘Come this night in clerical garments, hiding small swords beneath them.’”

When the time of the service came, the court singers came to sing the canon of the Nativity of Christ. Papias opened the doors for them, and with them entered Michael’s accomplices, dressed in clerical garments, and they hid in the corners of the church.

The tsar’s singers began to sing and had already sung half the service when the tsar came to matins and also began to sing. His voice was strong and loud, but it sounded harsh and discordant. The tsar always thought that his singing was pleasing to the hearers, and he usually began to sing first. And now, as soon as he entered the church, he began loudly to sing the troparion “They Reproached the King of All with Love.”

And immediately, suddenly, the conspirators surrounded him from all sides and drew their swords. Seeing this, the tsar hid in the altar, and they, rushing in after him, stabbed him to death.

They cut off his head and hands and cast his body out for public view. Thus ending his foul and impious life, he also destroyed his worthless, accursed soul. Leo was killed by the sharp sword, receiving bitter retribution for the deeds of his vile and senseless life. Condemned to death by the conspirators who dared to commit this evil in the holy church, in his life the thrice-accursed hater of God, who had done so much evil and drunk so much Christian blood, himself received an evil death at the hands of murderers.

After this his foul body was wrapped in plain, coarse cloth, placed in a prepared boat, and transported to the so-called First Island, where it was buried. But the daring men who did this did not know that they had taken such a sin upon themselves only to their condemnation. “For he had stretched out firmly, as the word says, these abominable and evil-named men, truly kindling the lawless things that were in them, so that they were brought forth, and in other episcopates, if indeed anything was preserved and known anywhere, and some were nowhere to be known. Together the eastern and western ends, following the Gospel and the apostolic, fulfill and preach, so that wherever you, Christians in another region, that unusual and lawless thing, and to increase Christian piety, the God-shameful command is reached, as much as was taken into memory by evil and foul assemblies, but only by this slave woman, captive and playing with divine authority. In him also the sinful coming upon earth for those living earthly, crafty, and proud, the mind helps, where the knee has power pleasing to itself, the word loves—”
