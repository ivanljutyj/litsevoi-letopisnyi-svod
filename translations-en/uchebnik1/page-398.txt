And Leontius set out from the city of Tarsus, and he came, and Leontius, the apostate emperor, entered Great Antioch alone, without Illus, in the consulship of Theodoric.

And Leontius reigned in Antioch, being an apostate, and remained there 12 days, and entered the palace of that city, and appointed a pagan as administrator.

And Leontius left Antioch and warred at the city of Chalcedon for one month and a half.

And Emperor Zeno learned of this, and sent many soldiers and the general John the Scythian.

And Illus the Great, who lived in Cilicia, heard of this, and wrote to Leontius in Antioch. And immediately Leontius left Antioch and fled, and both entered, Leontius with Illus, and also Pamprepius, a supporter of Illus, into Papurius, a fortress in Syria.

Verina died a natural death. Pamprepius, accused of apostasy, was killed up there, and his corpse was cast into a ditch.

Illus and Leontius were long besieged and were captured by John the Scythian. And the heads of Illus and Leontius were brought to Emperor Zeno, and for the spectators to see were impaled on spears, that is, on spear shafts, and were stuck on those spear shafts for many days; and all the people of Constantine’s city came out to see them.

In the reign of this Zeno, Peroz reigned among the Persians.

And the townspeople of the Green faction at the hippodrome, when there was a spectacle there, began to fight with the Blues with stones. Phasius, a prince and hypatus, was also a spectator; and they struck him on the head, and drove him from the hippodrome.

And the prince looked back at the one who had struck him with a stone, and recognized him, and sent for him, and took him, and brought him to his chambers, to the bath called by the name Urvikia, and the hypatus then began to beat the one who had struck him with a stone.

And the people of the Green faction learned of this, and they rushed to the hypatus’s palace against the prince, and set fire there, and burned the building called the xystus, and the whole street as far as the hypatus’s palace burned down.

And the local Olympic games were abolished. And the prince Valasius fled to another city, and began to live in a suburb of Antioch called Hippocephalus, and was deprived of his rank; and Conrat was in his place.

And the city riot did not cease for six months, and again the Antiochenes who were on the Green side came, and there was strife at the hippodrome, and many were killed, and the so-called Sabinians burned the synagogue. The Jews were at the spectacle on the Blue side. And the Greens plundered everything that was in the synagogue, and those living there.
