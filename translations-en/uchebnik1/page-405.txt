Queen Boa seized a certain king, Stirax, and sent him bound to Constantinople to Justinian. During the reign of this Justinian, Nememppian was martyred.

The Sixteenth Reign: Justinian the Great, who reigned in Constantinople

After Justin, Justinian the Great, his brother's son, reigned for 39 years. He renovated the great church of Holy Sophia in the twenty-second year of his reign, giving it new beauty and grandeur. At first this church was built by Constantius, the son of Constantine the Great.

The Tale of the Building of the Great Church of God, Holy Sophia, in Constantinople, Which the Pious Tsar Justinian Built

Such is the building in Constantinople of the great Church of God called Holy Sophia. The great Tsar Constantine first raised it, in form similar to the churches of Saint Agathonikos and Saint Akakios. And having completed this, he established many other churches as well, until the years of the reign of Theodosius the Great.

And when the Second Ecumenical Council was held in Constantinople, the Arians rose up and burned the covering of Holy Sophia.

Patriarch Nektarios sat in the old church of Saint Irene, which Tsar Constantine had built. Two years passed during which Holy Sophia stood uncovered. Tsar Theodosius ordered Rufinus the magister to cover it with marble.

Thirty-two years after Tsar Theodosius, and two hundred years after Tsar Constantine, and after one thousand and six hundred men were killed at the hippodrome, God breathed into the mind of the pious Tsar Justinian to build the Church of Holy Sophia, such as had not been built since the time of Adam.

Tsar Justinian wrote to his commanders, governors, and judges in all lands to collect tribute in the established amount, in every country of the east and west, south and north.

Having collected the tribute, they sent it to the tsar from all fifty islands. And the tribute was great wealth: there was six hundred centenaria of gold and two thousand centenaria of silver; and one centenarium contains one hundred twenty litrai.

And the tsar ordered searches everywhere for church columns and column bases, and capitals, and door partitions, and apses, and marbles, and other things needed for the church. And everything the tsar commanded they took from old pagan churches and from old baths and houses.
