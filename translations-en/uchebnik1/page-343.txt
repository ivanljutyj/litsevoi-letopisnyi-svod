Miracle of the Blind Man

Shortly before this, a blind man was brought to Macarius’s hut, which was in the desert three stadia away. And the blind man came there, and with great difficulty they brought him, and they did not find Macarius in the house, and they were in great despondency. Then with great faith the blind man said to his guides: “I beg you, lead me to that side of the wall where the elder used to rest.”

When they came near, the blind man took a little dry clay with which the wall had been plastered, and put it in his hands, and asked that water be drawn for him from the well from which the elder drank. Then the blind man mixed the clay with water and anointed his eyes with it, and washed his eyes with the drawn water, and suddenly received such sight that he needed no guides, and, healed, he came home by himself. Later this man came with all his household to the elder and gave thanks to God, and told how he had been healed.

Miracle of the Hyena, Whose Cubs Received Sight

The cave was near Macarius’s house, and a hyena gave birth in it. One day, since the hyena’s cubs remained blind, the beast carried them out to Macarius and laid them at his feet. Macarius understood that the beast was pleading for the blindness of the cubs, and prayed to God that He would grant them sight. And God gave the cubs sight, and they went to the cave after their mother. And soon afterward the beast returned with its cubs, carrying a ram’s skin, and there was still wool on it, as a gift in gratitude. The beast brought the skin to the elder in its teeth, and laid it at the door, and went away.

Luke exiled these divine fathers to the islands of the Egyptian lakes, where not a single Christian lived. And the ship with the monks drew near to the island. About the Maiden Who Was Healed. The daughter of the idolatrous priest of that country was tormented by a demon and lost her reason, and came to the ship in which the holy elders were, and cried out with impious wails, and raged so. And the elders ordered the unclean spirit to come out, and gave the maiden back to her parents.

While the elders lived on that island, they soon converted its inhabitants to Orthodox Christianity. Learning of this, Luke was afraid that the elders would convert everyone to Christianity, and secretly ordered them to go to their dwellings in the desert.
