By origin she was whole, since the people were pious. But now it was pitiful to behold her, for she had been torn suddenly from top to bottom when our Lord Jesus Christ was crucified. And many other signs were then.

On the Resurrection of the Lord

Our Lord Jesus Christ rose on the third day, at the seventh hour of the night, when it began to grow light, on Sunday, the first day of April. And the Lord appeared to the apostles and to many other saints on holy Mount Zion, with the doors shut, and standing in the midst He said: Peace be unto you... and the rest. There He also assured Thomas.

And Jesus dwelt with them 40 days, and ascended into Heaven on the 10th day of May, on Thursday, at the 9th hour of the day; the holy apostles beheld, and a multitude of people saw how He ascended on the clouds. And the holy angels said: Men of Galilee, why do you stand gazing into heaven? This is Jesus, who has ascended into Heaven.

The Holy Mother of God saw the Ascension of her Son into Heaven and urged herself to greater fasting and kneeling, so that Andrew of Jerusalem, Archbishop of Crete, wrote that even now on the marble are discernible the traces of her kneeling on holy Mount Zion, and that place is shown where the Mother of God rested on the stone and briefly took natural sleep; she had honor and awe from all people, and glory not only from the faithful but also from Jews and pagans. And no one among the Jews, or the princes, or the high priests dared to approach, or speak, or do anything to her. And there, where she was, in the house, she gave healing to the sick and health, freed the possessed from unclean spirits, and diligently gave alms to the poor, to widows, and to orphans.

Some said that not one of the twelve apostles went far away or departed from Jerusalem during the life of the Mother of God. And they remained in sorrow and in the persecution of the church, and after the murder of Stephen the First Martyr all dispersed through the Jewish and Samaritan cities, except the twelve apostles, as Luke the Evangelist writes.

There also the Holy Spirit descended upon the holy apostles on the fiftieth day, on the 20th day of May, on Sunday, at the 3rd hour of the day, on holy Mount Zion; and from there the apostles were sent to various places, and returning, they gathered there again.

This Pontius Pilate reigned in Judea, whom that Tiberius Caesar named ruler of the people, who destroyed the kingdom of Judea and set over them a prince whom he himself wished. And Herod ruled over the Jews.
