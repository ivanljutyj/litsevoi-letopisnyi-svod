And then she departed, rejoicing and making merry, having succeeded in accomplishing everything. And after walking two leagues, she found the house of Mary Magdalene and the place where Christ our God had healed her of seven demons. And here she erected a church of Mary Magdalene.

Having completed the circuit of the venerable places in the vicinity of Tiberias, the blessed one ordered that bright churches be built with diligence in every place.

She had clergy at her disposal, and taking them, she entered the city of Tiberias. In the middle of the city she found the house of the mother-in-law of the Apostle Peter, who had been healed of a fever by the touch of the Lord Christ. And here she built a beautiful church of the holy chief Apostle Peter.

Twelve leagues from Tiberias she ascended the northern mountain, where Melchizedek blessed Abraham, and found the place on which Christ our God was transfigured, setting up here too a church in the name of our Lord and Savior and of the holy apostles Peter, James, and John. And she appointed clergy for the services and for singing to the glory of the honorable church, giving them a great abundance of wealth for the ministry.

Coming down from the mountain, she went east to Nazareth to find the place and house in which the Holy Mother of God received the Annunciation and the joy from the angel Gabriel, and here she built a most glorious church of the Holy Mother of God.

From the city of Nazareth she reached the village of Cana of Galilee and found the house where the marriage of Simon the Canaanite took place, and where Christ our God turned water into wine; and here she erected a holy church.

After traveling six leagues to Bethlehem, here too she built a great cruciform church of the cell type in the name of Christ our God, enclosing within it, beneath the altar, the Manger and the Cave.

Departing from there, she found the place where the holy infants were slain by Herod, and having honored this cave as was fitting, she built a church in the name of the Holy Infants.

From there she went down to the holy pasture and found the place where the holy angels announced to the shepherds the Nativity of our Lord Jesus Christ, and said: “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace.” Here she built a beautiful church in honor of the Holy Mother of God Mary and Joseph the Betrothed.

Then she went to Holy Zion. Holy Zion is situated south of the Holy City. Here the blessed Saint Helena buried Theodora, the wife of Consta.

For the church that she built—and this church was a great cell church—she laid a roof of tin instead of tiles, and shut the entrance.
