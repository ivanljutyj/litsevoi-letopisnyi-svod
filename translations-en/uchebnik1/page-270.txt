The 20th Reign, of Julian, Who Became Emperor in Rome in the Year 5686 from Adam (the 186th Year from Christ)

After Pertinax, Julian Didius reigned for 4 months. And he was killed by a certain man of the rank of cubicularius, at the palace fountain, while he was looking at the fish.

The 21st Reign, of Severus, Who Became Emperor in Rome in the Middle of the Year 5686 from Adam (the 186th Year from Christ)

After Didius, Severus reigned for 17 years and 8 months. He conquered his opponents in Britain and enclosed the island from sea to sea with a wall of 1000 stadia. In his time Leonides the bishop, father of Origen, was martyred, and Iaia from the city of Bizad, and with her nine others. Heretics. And Clement the Stromatian, and Sumachus, and Origen became renowned.

He, having taken Byzantium, destroyed its walls, and gave it the first building of the hippodrome, and arranged hunts and a theater for the townspeople.
