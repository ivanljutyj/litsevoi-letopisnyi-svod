There is no salvation for you unless you find helpers. For Ananus, having consulted with the people, sent envoys to Vespasian, so that he might come quickly and seize the whole city. To the people Ananus announced a purification, so that in the morning he might enter the temple ostensibly for worship and the offering of sacrifice, and attack you suddenly. And I was sent to negotiate peace with you, so as to seize you treacherously while you are unarmed. You ought to take thought for the salvation of your lives: either make peace with Ananus or send for help. But I tell you that if the hope of forgiveness warms you, you have no sense, for the people will not forget what lawless deeds you committed against them.” With this lie John frightened the Zealots; he spoke to them ambiguously about help from outside, but his words pointed to Idumea.

John added to this that Ananus was gnashing his teeth, wishing to tear apart the leaders of the Zealots. Having heard all this, Eleazar son of Simon and Zachariah son of Phalek, both priests by birth, consulted with the other Zealots about what ought to be done, for a difficult time had come upon them, since they themselves were shut in and could not send for help from outside. Nevertheless, they decided to send to the Idumeans.

They quickly wrote a letter and sent it to the Idumeans, saying: “Ananus is deceiving the people and handing the capital over to the Romans. We have separated from him for the sake of freedom and are now shut in and sit inside the temple. Guards stand around us. Little time remains for us to save ourselves. If you do not help us soon, Ananus and our enemies will overcome us, and the city will be surrendered to the Romans.” The Zealots gave this letter to two men, loyal, resourceful, and swift-footed. The Zealots knew that the Idumeans were a violent people, prone to rebellion and upheaval, and that at a word they would immediately take up arms and come.

When the Idumeans received the message from the Zealots, they were thrown into confusion and sent men everywhere with calls to war; and they gathered as one, twenty thousand men, and went to Jerusalem.

Ananus did not notice how the envoys from the Zealots had gone out, nor did the guards. But the arrival of the Idumeans did not escape their eyes, and they managed to close the city gates and post guards on the walls. Jesus, the high priest who was second after Ananus, stood on the tower and said: “I am amazed how fortune helps the wicked. For you have come to the aid of impious people. I did not know that you were of the same mind, since you are helping the Zealots. If anyone honestly examines their life, he will find that each of them deserves a thousand deaths. For they are cursed by the whole land, and have defiled themselves in everything, lost their property, and vented their madness and greed on the surrounding cities. Now at last they have secretly penetrated into the holy city and defiled undefiled places, and without fear they drink their fill in the sanctuary with wine and with the wealth of the slain...
