In the same year in Novgorod, Hegumen Antony founded a monastery and a stone church of the Holy Mother of God. In the same year Dobrynya, posadnik of Novgorod, died.

In the year 6626 from Adam (1126 from Christ)

Vladimir went against Yaroslav Sviatopolchich to the city of Vladimir and made peace with him; and Vladimir departed from him, having severely rebuked him, because Yaroslavets was indignant at his granddaughter, the daughter of Mstislav.

In the same year Lazarus, Bishop of Pereyaslavl, died on the sixteenth day of September, and in his place Nikephoros, Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, appointed Bishop Silvester, on the first day of January.

In the same year Vladimir Monomakh married his son Andrei to the granddaughter of Turkan, prince of the Polovtsians.

In the year 6627 from Adam (1127 from Christ)

Yaroslavets Sviatopolchich sent away his wife, the daughter of Mstislav, the granddaughter of Vladimir; and for this Vladimir Monomakh went against him to Vladimir; he fled to Hungary, and his troops deserted him.

Vladimir sent his son Roman to Vladimir to reign; but he died there on the fifteenth day of January. And Vladimir Monomakh sent another son, Andrei, to Vladimir to reign.

In the same year Gleb Vseslavich died. In the same year Hegumen Kiriak and Prince Vsevolod Mstislavich founded a stone church of the Holy Martyr George in the monastery in Novgorod the Great.

In the same year the church of the Most Holy Mother of God in the Antoniev Monastery in Novgorod was completed.

In the year 6628 from Adam (1128 from Christ)

Yaroslav went against the Polovtsians beyond the Don and, not finding them, returned back.

And his brother Georgy Dolgoruky went against the Bulgars, took much plunder, and defeated their army.

In the same year the Torks and Berendei fled from the Russian land; and thus they rushed here and there and perished.

In the same year Boris came from Kiev to serve as posadnik in Novgorod.

In the year 6629 from Adam (1129 from Christ)

Metropolitan Nikephoros of Kiev and all Rus died in the month of April.
