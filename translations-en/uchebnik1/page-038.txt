Jerusalem. Herod inquired of them the time of the star, and said to the magi: If you find Him, come and tell me, that I also may go and worship Him.

And the magi went, led by the star which they had seen in the east, and found Jesus and His Mother in the city of Bethlehem, and fell to the ground and worshipped Christ the Savior, and said among themselves: The star, which we honor as a god, shows a God greater than itself. And they brought Him gifts as to God, and having received an answer from an angel in a dream not to return to Herod, they departed by another way to the land of Persia, and did not obey Herod.

When the magi had departed, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph, saying: Take the Child and His Mother and flee to Egypt, and remain there until I tell you, for Herod seeks the Child to destroy Him.

He rose, took the Child and His Mother, and departed by night to Egypt, and was there until the death of Herod. Joseph entered the sanctuary of their god Avdul, and immediately all the idols fell. The priests saw and trembled, and fell down before the Savior, so that the church would not fall on them. And they wished to paint an icon of His likeness. The icon painter undertook to paint it, but could not finish the image of Christ. And all began to entreat the Savior to command that this be done, and that His icon be completed. Christ bowed down to it, and the icon itself was at once completed. The Egyptians keep this icon to this day. Many mighty kings attempted to take it or make a copy of it, but could not.

And after this, when Herod had been mocked, he was enraged, and questioned the Jewish high priests: “Where is Christ born?” and learned, and having sent soldiers, he slew all the infants in Bethlehem, the city of the Jews, from two years old and under, according to the time. And they sought to kill John.

And Herod also sent soldiers to Zacharias, John’s father, into the sanctuary, with the words: Where is your son John? And Zacharias said: I do not know. Am I my son’s keeper? And then the soldiers killed him between the church and the altar. And his blood flowed out and at once became like stone. There are two reasons for his murder: some say because he hid John from Herod, and others also say that the Saint died for the sake of the Virgin, the Mother of God. For Zacharias again brought her into the place where virgins were not permitted to enter, and was condemned to death, since he openly transgressed the law of the scribes, and since he told everyone about her, that she was a virgin, as she was a virgin even before the Nativity.

Elizabeth was in Bethlehem, and received John, and went into the wilderness, and hid with her child for forty days in a certain cave, and then went back up alone. John was brought up there, and remained so until his appearance to the people of Israel in the wilderness.

And Joseph departed to Egypt, and spent there with his sons and his daughters, and with the Mother of God, and with Christ, five years, and again had a revelation, and returned, and came to Nazareth of Galilee.
