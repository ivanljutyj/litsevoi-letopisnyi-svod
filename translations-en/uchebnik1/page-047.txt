King Herod heard this petition, and marveled at the miracle, and feared the mysterious healing, and said to her: “The healing that was with you, O woman, surpasses what we ask for in the pagan temples. Go then, and as you wish, set up an image to Him, glorifying with love the One who healed you.”

And then this woman set up in the midst of her city an image of our Lord and God Jesus Christ, made of copper with some part of gold and silver mixed in. This image is in the city of Paneas to this day; many years ago it was moved from the place where it stood in the middle of the city into the holy church. The record of her memory was found in this Paneas in the possession of a certain Vasa, one of the Jews who had become Christians. There were written the lives of all the saints of the land of Judea, as Clement wrote.

On one day Jesus went out into Galilee with three of His disciples: with Andrew, and with Peter, and with John the son of Zebedee, and He found Philip of Bethsaida, and took him with Him, and John, and Nathanael. And He came to Cana of Galilee, and there was a wedding there. Jesus was invited to the wedding, together with His most pure Mother and His disciples. And when the wine ran out, Jesus made wine from water. The bridegroom was named Simon, and he afterward followed Christ, having lived with his wife two years; and his wife died.

This is Simon the Cananaean, one of the twelve, who was a zealot. And from there Jesus came to Bethsaida, a city in Galilee, and entered Peter’s house, and his mother-in-law was sick with fever, and immediately Christ, having come, healed her, and returned to Nazareth. Joseph, the betrothed and guardian of the Mother of God, departed in old age, full of days, having lived, as they said, 100 years. His sons James and Jude followed Christ and went through all cities and lands, preaching the Kingdom of God and healing every disease and every infirmity.

Peter’s mother-in-law, with her daughter and with Peter’s wife, followed Him and were with the Mother of God. They departed from there and went to a Galilean city where there is a lake called Gennesaret. In that same place many trees had been planted.

That land is beautiful, rich in oil, abundant in wine, with much fish and all kinds of food, and the trees bear much fruit. That region is beautiful, and much fruit grows there; wine and oil are there in abundance.

Jesus came to the house of Zebedee with His mother and His disciples, and stayed there with Zebedee, who had a wife named Salome and two sons, James and John, whom Jesus received as disciples.
