And to the surviving inhabitants of these cities King Justin gave much gold, and he called Edessa the city of Justin.

In the 7th year and the 10th month of Justin’s reign, in the 4th indiction, Antioch the Great, in Syria, fell from the wrath of God. This was the fifth destruction, on the 29th day of the month Artemisios, that is May, at the 6th hour of the day, when Olybrius was consul in Rome. Of this destruction it is not possible to tell with lips. People buried by buildings, by God’s command, were burned by fire issuing from beneath the earth. Sparks of fire came forth from the air and, like lightning, set alight whatever they found; the dust of the earth boiled from the heat, and the earth turned to coals, and the foundations of buildings cracked, weakened by the earthquake and burning in the fire. Fire met those who fled, and pressed those hiding in houses. It scattered the dust of the fields through the city, and that fire encompassed everything, as if it had received a single command from God and was kindled in every body at the same time.

And a terrible and wondrous thing was seen — fire coming down instead of rain, and rain bringing hot flame; fire poured down as rain, and the rain was like scorching flame and devoured those crying out on the earth. And Christ-loving Antioch was destroyed; nothing remained in so glorious a city, except the buildings adjoining the mountain in a single row.

From the spring called Olympias, and as far as the gates, one could clearly see the ruined walls standing there, threatening death. Many of these walls fell and killed those living there and passers-by. Not a single building, nor any house, nor any city dwelling remained that was not destroyed. Neither a holy church, nor a monastery, nor any holy place remained, but all was destroyed and fell; of the buildings still standing, some crumbled completely, others for the most part. From the depths below came something like sea sand, and it poured over the surface of the earth, having the wetness and stench of sea water. The great church that King Constantine built, and the like of which was not in Greece, when God’s wrath came and all fell, this one remained standing for five days after the punishment, and suddenly it too caught fire and crumbled to the ground. Likewise the great church of the Archangel Michael and the church of the Holy Mother of God Mary collapsed to the ground.

Zeno built the church of the Archangel Michael. These two venerable houses stood wholly unharmed by the earthquake, but after God’s wrath they suddenly burned together. And the church of the Holy Prophets and of Saint Zacharias afterwards crumbled to the ground. And other churches that did not fall from the earthquake crumbled to their foundations from fire.

There perished in Antioch then, of townspeople and foreigners, men and women, children and old men, 200,050 souls, as those who knew said.
