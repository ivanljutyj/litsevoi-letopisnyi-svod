Then the son of Theron said to Herod: “Alexander, your son, sent by night to my father and asked him to kill you, and he agreed to the murder.” Some considered these words of Theron’s son a lie that the son had invented to save his father, while others considered them the truth.

And Herod gathered the common people and ordered the accused commanders to be handed over to them, and Theron, and his son, and the man who cut his hair, and at once they killed them with stones and sticks.

And Herod sent his sons Alexander and Aristobulus to Sebaste, near Caesarea, and ordered them to be strangled with a rope, and ordered their bodies to be carried to the city of Alexandria and buried at the tomb of Alexander, their grandfather on their mother’s side. Such was the end of Alexander and Aristobulus. Antipater now had all the rights to inherit the throne. And the Jewish people began to hate him, for all knew that it was he who had arranged the death of the brothers. And great fear seized Antipater, for he saw the growing children of the murdered brothers. Alexander and Glaphyra, daughter of Archelaus king of Cappadocia, had two sons, Tigranes and Alexander; Aristobulus by Berenice, daughter of Herod’s sister Salome, had three sons: Herod, Agrippa, and Aristobulus, and two daughters: Herodias and Mariamne.

And Herod sent Glaphyra back to her homeland, to Cappadocia, with her property, after he had killed her husband Alexander, his son.

And Berenice, the wife of Aristobulus, Herod gave in marriage to Antipater’s uncle. This marriage was needed by Antipater, the son of Herod, in order to reconcile with Salome, who was at enmity with him. Antipater also gave gifts to Pheroras and pleased him in every way.

And to Caesar’s officials and commanders Antipater sent countless riches to Rome, and all of Saturninus’s close associates in Syria received gifts from him. But the more Antipater gave, the more he was hated. Since he did not give out of generosity, but feared everyone, and out of this fear he spent. So those who received gifts from Antipater did not become devoted to him with all their heart, and some of those whom he gifted even remained his enemies.

Every day Antipater’s gifts became more costly, since he saw the king’s mood. The king showed love to the orphans left after the sons he had killed. Herod repented of killing his sons, and therefore cared for their offspring. And Herod gathered his relatives and his nobles, and placed the children before them, and his eyes filled with tears, and he said: “My kin and my nobles, a merciless spirit that brings misfortune has taken from me the fathers of these children, and nature compels me to show mercy to these orphans.
