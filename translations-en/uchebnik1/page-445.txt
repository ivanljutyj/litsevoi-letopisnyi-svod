Of the patriarch, and the whole people, and having instructed them sufficiently, Justin handed over his scepter-holding and autocracy to Tiberius while still alive.

And having lived a little longer, Justin fell asleep in the never-waking and common sleep. O death, sparing no one, like a stone you covered such a man. O abyss, you do not spare even good nature, but lay all together, buried in graves.
