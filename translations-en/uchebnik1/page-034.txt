In Jerusalem, Herod inquired of them the time of the star, and said to the Magi: “If you find Him, go and tell me, so that I too may go and worship Him.”

And the Magi went, led by the star which they had seen in the East, and they found Jesus and His Mother in the city of Bethlehem, and fell to the ground and worshipped Savior Christ, and said among themselves: “The star reveals a God greater than itself, though we worship it as a god.” And they brought Him gifts as to God, and having received an answer from an angel in a dream not to return to Herod, they went by another way to the land of Persia and did not obey Herod.

When the Magi had departed, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph, saying: “Take the Child and His Mother and flee to Egypt, and stay there until I tell you, for Herod will seek the Child to destroy Him.”

He arose, took the Child and His Mother, and by night departed to Egypt, and was there until Herod’s death. Joseph entered the sanctuary of their god Avdul, and immediately all the idols fell. The priests saw it and trembled, and fell down before the Savior so that the church would not fall upon them. And they wanted to paint an icon of His likeness. The icon painter undertook to paint it, but could not finish the image of Christ. And they all began to entreat the Savior that He command this to be, and that His icon be completed. Christ bowed toward it, and the icon itself was immediately completed. The Egyptians keep this icon to this day. Many powerful kings attempted to take it or make a copy of it, and could not.

And after this, when Herod had been mocked, he was enraged and asked the Jewish high priests: “Where is Christ born?” And he learned, and having sent soldiers, he killed all the infants in Bethlehem, the city of Judea, from two years old and under, according to the time. And they sought to kill John.

Herod also sent soldiers to Zachariah, John’s father, in the sanctuary, with the words: “Where is your son John?” And Zachariah said: “I do not know. Am I my son’s keeper?” Then the soldiers killed him between the church and the altar. His blood flowed out and immediately became like stone. There are two reasons for his murder: some say because he hid John from Herod; others also say that the saint died for the Virgin Mother of God. For Zachariah again led her into the place where maidens were not permitted to enter, and he was condemned to death because he had clearly transgressed the law of the scribes, and because he told everyone about her that she was a virgin, as she had been a virgin even before the Nativity.

Elizabeth was in Bethlehem, and she took John and went into the desert, and hid with her child for 40 days in a certain cave, and returned alone. John was raised there, and remained so until his manifestation to the people of Israel in the desert.

And Joseph went to Egypt and spent five years there with his sons and daughters, with the Mother of God, and with Christ, and again had a revelation, and returned and came to Nazareth of Galilee.
