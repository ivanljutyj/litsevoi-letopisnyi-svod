“…spend upon their insatiable bellies. But you, Idumeans, are not like that; you are wise and peaceable, and your weapons adorn you so. You can help their capital when in due time your help is needed against foreigners. We would call that a wretched happiness which has fallen to the robbers, in that you have come to set them free. It is curious what weighty reason the robbers invented to incite you against us. Yet you ought to know the truth and not believe fabricated slanders. How can we be surrendering to the Romans if we are at war with them? We ought first to have made peace.

If all Galilee were still intact, and all the other cities too, peace would still be possible. But even if we desire peace, the Romans do not need it. And it is shameful for us to ask for peace. Better a glorious death than to live in captivity. But if the Zealots slander us so, let them say who was sent to the Romans, or who served as envoys, or whom they caught going there and back, or from whom a letter was intercepted. Let them tell how we hid it from all who live with us, yet did not hide it from these lawless men who are shut in and surrounded by guards. But they have felt that they will not escape the wrath of the Lord. For they have provoked the Lord and sinned, and therefore they have lied to you. Even if this city perishes, it will perish not by anyone else but by these very Zealots. You have taken up arms for righteousness, so help to destroy the unrighteous. They have carried out their judgment not by word but by hand: they seized innocent people from among the citizens, bound them, put them in chains, and, listening neither to their defenses nor to their pleas, killed them. If you wish, enter the city, but not as enemies, and see what the Zealots have done: houses laid waste and plundered, people cut down, women wailing, weeping and lamentation throughout the city, and there is no one who has not experienced the malice of the Zealots. They have gathered defilement from all the land and drawn it from all the cities into Jerusalem, as if they had gathered all diseases from the feet, the hands, and every part of the body and cast them upon the head. And in Jerusalem they chose the temple, and now it is a refuge for them, and nothing more, hiding their malice and protecting them from enemies. This place is worshiped by the whole world, and it is honored in all its ends, both among our people and among foreigners. Now this place is trampled by beasts that were born right here. You can verify what I have told you: enter, only without weapons, and see everything for yourselves. You can also return to your own. If you think that we have given ourselves over to the Romans, then you can stay outside the city and guard the gates and the roads, and if our deceit is revealed, do not spare us. If none of these possibilities suits you, then do not be surprised that the gates remain closed before you, since you are armed.” Such were the words of Jesus the high priest.
