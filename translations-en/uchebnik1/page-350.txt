How will you receive the Body of the Lord with such hands? How will you receive the Honorable Blood with your lips? For because of one word from these lips, spoken in lawless fury, so much blood has been shed. Therefore depart and do not try to increase the first lawlessness with a second, and do not come to Communion, and accept these spiritual bonds, and God Most High be witness to this.

Hearing this, the emperor, weeping and wailing, withdrew to the imperial chambers that were in Mediolanum and remained in them for eight months, repenting greatly and weeping.

At the feast of Christ's Nativity, Rufinus the magister saw that according to custom the emperor did not wish to make the proeleusis, that is, the procession, and said to him: "I will go, my lord, to the priest and entreat him to release you from these bonds, if you command." Theodosius said: "I know Ambrose's severity, so that he will not listen to you." Rufinus spoke much and urged the emperor, and said: "I will persuade Ambrose." And the emperor ordered Rufinus to go to him.

Hope also arose in Theodosius, and he did not restrain himself long, and followed Rufinus. Ambrose saw Rufinus and said: "You imitate canine shamelessness, O Rufinus, making yourself a partaker in so great a murder! But I, as before, say that again I will not let Theodosius enter even the forecourt of the Sanctuary."

Hearing this, Rufinus quickly sent a messenger to the emperor with this news. The emperor was walking and was on the way to the church, and hearing this, said: "I will go to the church and at once receive the torments I deserve."

And Theodosius came to the church and begged the bishop to release him from the bonds. The bishop said: "You do this by the power of your authority and rise in war against God." The emperor said in a contrite voice and with many tears: "I have not come in fury to the holy feast, but I have come to entreat you to release me from the bonds." Ambrose said: "What repentance have you shown after so many lawless deeds? With what medicines have you healed the incurable sores?" The emperor said: "It is your task to prepare the needed healing, and mine to accept its bitterness."

Then Ambrose said: "Since, overcome by fury, you swiftly and without investigation committed such bloodshed, write a law that nothing be done in fury and without investigation, but that all human cases concerning riots and murders be justly investigated for up to thirty days." The emperor ordered such a law to be written and sealed it with his ring.

Then Ambrose released Theodosius from the bonds and allowed him to enter the church and pray not standing nor kneeling, but lying prone on the ground.
