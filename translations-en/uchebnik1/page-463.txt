And those Ethiopians, that is, the demons, seized me and beat me much and brought me down to the earth. And then the earth opened, and we went down into a certain narrow and dark place, stinking and very foul, and descended even to the underworld, into the prisons of hell, where the souls of sinners are shut up, in the land of eternal darkness, where there is neither light nor life for men, as it is written, but eternal sickness and endless sorrow, inconsolable weeping, unceasing sighing, gnashing of teeth. There they always say, "Woe!" for each of those there, remembering his evil deeds, considered how to answer the incorruptible Judge and how to endure endless torment, and was cruelly tormented by conscience. And all there, moreover, reflecting that they are separated from God and from the glory of His saints, and condemned with the devil and with his demons, ceaselessly and inconsolably grieve and sigh. And that misery which is there cannot be told, nor can that sickness be expressed with the tongue. And they groan there with all their heart, but there is none to have mercy on them; and they weep, and there is none to deliver them; they pray, and there is none to help them. With them I too was shut up and placed in dark and narrow places.

And I remained there weeping from the third hour to the ninth, and I saw two angels who had come there again, and I began to entreat them to lead me out of that fearful prison and misery, so that I might repent before God. They said to me: "You call on us in vain and pray in vain, for none of those who have come here goes out or is released until the resurrection of the whole world." I earnestly pleaded and said: "Have mercy on me, holy ones, for I did not know that this would happen to me; therefore from now on I will repent and will be of benefit to many, if I ascend to earthly life." And one angel said to his companion: "Will you stand surety for him?" And the other angel said: "I will." And I saw how the surety angel stood surety with all his heart.

And then the angels took me and led me out to my tomb, to my body, and said to me: "Enter there from whence you departed." And I saw my soul's nature shining like a pearl, and the dead body like black stinking mire. Therefore I resisted and said: "I will not enter the body because of its gloom." The angels urged me and said: "It is impossible for you to repent unless in the body in which you sinned." But I pleaded not to enter the body. Then the angels said to me: "Enter. If you do not enter, we will turn you back to the prison from which we brought you. Enter, so that you yourself may be saved and be of benefit to others." Then I saw how I entered through my mouth and revived the body, and began to cry out: "Have mercy on me." All received very great benefit and feared this fearful confession, and each lamented his own sin.
