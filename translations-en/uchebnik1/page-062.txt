A smile was on his face; he was wise, eloquent, sweetly speaking from the Holy Spirit and healing.

This king Nero sent soldiers throughout all Judea and Jerusalem and did evil to everyone; he killed many and sent soldiers, because, grieved, they cried out, reproaching Nero for cutting off Pilate’s head, as though he had avenged Christ, and that Christ had been crucified not only by him but as by one ruling as governor; and therefore they were angry with him as with a tormentor. And Annas and Caiaphas were then killed by the soldiers.

This Nero received into his kingdom another country on the Pontus, sending Polemon his military commander, and made it an eparchy, naming it Pontus Polemoniacus. This Nero greatly loved the Veneti.

About James

James the Great, the brother of God, was pure from his mother’s womb; he drank neither wine nor any strong drink, never ate anything that had a soul, and had no need of washing. His knees were calloused like a camel’s, because he always knelt and prayed to God. Because of this great righteousness and wondrous life they named him the Just, and also the people’s bulwark and justification, for he was renowned, honored, and very glorious. It was the feast of Passover, and a multitude of people gathered in the church, and the scribes brought James up to the church’s raised platform, wishing to make him reject the preaching of Christ, and said in a loud voice: We beg you, righteous one, tell the people not to be deceived and not to follow the so-called Christ. We all listen to you, knowing that you are righteous and without hypocrisy. He answered and said: Why do you ask me about Christ? He Himself sits at the right hand of God in heaven. But the lawless high priests and Pharisees cried out: O righteous one, you too have been deceived. And they said to one another: We have done badly, that we have brought such testimony upon ourselves concerning Jesus, and have given many an occasion to believe in Him.

And behold, the unbelievers were enraged and threw James down. But he, kneeling, prayed for them. And one of the sons of Rahab came near and heard him and said to them: What are you doing?
