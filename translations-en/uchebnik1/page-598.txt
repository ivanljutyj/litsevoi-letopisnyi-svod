The Reign of Grand Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich Monomakh in Kiev, Grandson of Yaroslav (1122–1133 from the Incarnation of Christ)

The Crowning of Vladimir Monomakh with the Royal Crown

After the death of the Grand Prince of Kiev Svyatopolk Izyaslavich, Vladimir Vsevolodovich Monomakh, grandson of Yaroslav, great-grandson of the great Vladimir, entered Kiev. And the people of Kiev received him with great honor.

And on Sunday he sat upon the grand principality, on the throne of his father Vsevolod in Kiev, on the 20th day of the month of March; and the people rejoiced, and the unrest subsided.

In that same year Vladimir began to take counsel with his boyars, wishing to go against Tsargrad. The boyars answered him: “The emperor’s heart is in God’s hand, and we are in your hand.”

And the Grand Prince, having gathered an army, sent his commanders against the Tsargrad region of Thrace; and having taken abundant plunder, they returned safe to their own land.

At that time there was in Tsargrad the Emperor Constantine Monomakh, and at that time he was fighting the Persians and the Latins;
