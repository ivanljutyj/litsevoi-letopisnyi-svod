The blessed great Emperor Justinian lived 17 years after the building of Hagia Sophia until his repose. The great and noble Emperor Justinian handed down the troparion to the church to be sung. This troparion begins: “Only-begotten Son, Word of God.” In the time of Justinian, Antioch began to be called Theopolis, that is, the City of God. The Hypapante, that is, the Meeting, also began to be celebrated, and the Meeting was not reckoned among the feasts of the Lord. For the divine Chrysostom says: “In six days God made all His works, as it is written, and on the seventh day He rested. Therefore in the last days, when the Word of God deigned to seek and save the lost and to become incarnate, in the same manner, according to the number of the days of the whole creation, the Lord also handed down to us the feasts of His dispensation: the first feast is the Incarnation of Christ from the holy Virgin Mary, and after the Conception, the Nativity; the second is the Illumination, that is, Baptism; the third is the saving day of the Passion; the fourth is the glorious Resurrection, in which our Savior and Deliverer also was in hell and raised with Himself the righteous and those who believed; the fifth is the Ascension into Heaven, which took place on Thursday; the sixth is the Coming of the Holy Spirit; the seventh is the expected Resurrection of all the dead, the great and glorious day, when the feast will be true with great joy and gladness for those who will inherit what ‘eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither hath it entered into the heart of man, what God hath prepared for them that love Him.’” Thus does the venerable Chrysostom recount these things.

After these events, in the reign of that Justinian, the sea went out from its bounds for three stades into the regions of Africa, and destroyed many villages and hamlets, and drowned many people, and then returned to its bounds.

And the people called the Greens, that is, the Prasini, rose against the Blues, that is, the Veneti, and wrought much outrage, robbery, murder, and arson in the city, and named Hypatius emperor. And many people gathered at the hippodrome, and the hippodrome was filled with people, and Justinian ordered his men and soldiers—some above and others below—to shoot at those sitting in the hippodrome, and 30,000 people were killed, among them Hypatius, because he had placed the imperial crown upon himself.

From the 4th Council to the 5th, 100 years. The 5th Council

The Fifth Council was of 165 holy fathers, in Constantinople, in the 26th year of the reign of the Great Justinian, under the holy Vigilius, Pope of Rome. And a letter was sent by the emperor, confirming the right faith.
