But He said: Suffer it now. And Jesus was baptized on the 6th day of January, at the 7th hour of the night. And the Forerunner saw the Holy Spirit of the Lord descending like a dove and remaining upon Him.

After this Herod Antipas took the wife of Philip, his brother. And for this reason all the teachers of the Law loathed Herod and did not dare to reprove him; and only this man, whom they called wild, came to Antipas in fury and said: Since you have taken your brother’s wife, lawless one, and your brother died a merciless death, so you too shall be cut down by the Heavenly Sickle, for God’s Providence will not be silent, but you shall die in bitter sorrow in other lands, since you are not raising up seed for your brother, but serve fleshly lust and commit adultery, for four children were born to your brother.
