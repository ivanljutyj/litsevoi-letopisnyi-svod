Queen Voa seized a certain king, Styrax, and sent him bound to Constantinople to Justinian. In the reign of this Justinian, Nemempian was martyred.

Reign 16th, of Justinian the Great, who reigned in Constantinople

After Justin, Justinian the Great, his brother’s son, reigned 39 years. He renewed the great church of Holy Sophia in the 22nd year of his reign, giving it new beauty and grandeur. At first this church was built by Constantius, son of Constantine the Great.

Account of the Building of the Great Church of God, Holy Sophia, in Constantinople, Which the Pious Tsar Justinian Built

Such is the building in Constantinople of the great church of God called Holy Sophia. The great Tsar Constantine first raised it, in form similar to the churches of Saint Agathonikos and Saint Akakios. And, having completed this, he established many other churches before the years of the reign of Theodosius the Great.

And when the Second Ecumenical Council was held in Constantinople, the Arians rose up and burned the covering of Holy Sophia.

Nectarius the patriarch sat in the old church of Saint Irene, which Tsar Constantine had built. Two years passed during which Holy Sophia stood uncovered. Tsar Theodosius commanded Rufinus the magister to cover it with marble.

Thirty-two years after Tsar Theodosius, and two hundred years after Tsar Constantine, and after 1,600 men were killed at the hippodrome, God inspired the mind of the pious Tsar Justinian to build the church of Holy Sophia, such as had not been built since the time of Adam.

Tsar Justinian wrote to his governors, officials, and judges in all lands to collect tribute at the established rate, in every land of the east and west, south and north.

Having collected the tributes, they sent them to the tsar from all fifty islands. And the tribute was great wealth: there were 600 kentenaria of gold and 2,000 kentenaria of silver; and one kentenarion contains 120 litrai.

And the tsar commanded that it be learned everywhere where church columns and column bases, capitals, door partitions, apses, marbles, and other things needed for the church might be found. And all that the tsar ordered they took from old idol temples and from old baths and houses.
