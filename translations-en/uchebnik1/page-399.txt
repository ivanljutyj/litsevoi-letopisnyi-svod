Theodoret, Count of the East, learned of this and was angry, and pacified both factions of the citizens of Antioch the Great. A certain monk walled himself in and blocked up the wall from the inside, and through a small window he conversed with those who came to him and blessed him.

Taking this as a pretext, the Greens of that city, since they were the elder faction, spread out because of the monk, being near the city wall, and set fire to the Jewish synagogue, and raked out the bones of the dead Jews, and brought much dry brushwood alongside that synagogue and set it alight, and poured the bones onto the fire.
