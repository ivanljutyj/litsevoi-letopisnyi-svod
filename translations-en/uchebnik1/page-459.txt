Therefore, concerning Christ’s second legislation, the great Isaiah proclaimed: “Out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem, and He shall judge among the nations.” By Zion Isaiah here means the land and the metropolis situated in it, that is, the Jewish capital. From there, as from a certain enclosure, adorned swift horses, the apostles were sent into all the universe. From there signs began to be performed. There was the saving Passion of Christ, and the Resurrection, and the Ascension, and the beginning of our salvation. There the ineffable Theology began to be preached. There the Father first appeared, and the Only-begotten Son was made known, and the great grace of the Spirit was given. And incorporeal things there began to praise, and gifts, and powers, and the promises of future good things. And all this Scripture calls “His beauty,” for the goodness of God and beauty are His benevolence, and love of mankind, and all other good works. There too the Lord healed the sin of Adam’s transgression; there too was the swift, as it were winged, gathering of the saints, “a meeting for the Second Coming,” which He foretold, saying: “Where the corpse is, there the eagles gather.” “Nor was it unknown to the chief of all men that not even Noah’s grave could contain him, since after the flood glory was given from him.” Therefore the Lord, knowing the beginning of human death, accepted His Passion at the so-called Place of the Skull; in that same place all corruption began for mankind, and from there the preaching of the Kingdom of Heaven took its beginning. Likewise Athanasius, who spoke of God, says concerning Christ’s Passion: “For this reason Christ endured His Passion not somewhere else, nor in any other place, but in the place where, as the Jewish teachers say, Adam’s grave was. ‘There, after the curse, he was buried, I affirm. Therefore I marvel at the peculiarity of the place.’ It was fitting for God to renew the first man and to endure His Passion in that same place. And since Adam heard: ‘Earth you are, and to earth you shall go,’ it was fitting that he be laid there again: in the place where he received the curse, there he would also receive release.” And the Great Ephrem also said of Cain: “And Cain, groaning and trembling, was condemned, possessed by conscience, and he will suffer also in eternal torment, for ‘groaning and trembling shall you be on the earth’; and he shows to all in that land the place of condemnation and the valley of lamentation, as Holy Scripture says.” It is not the earth for the sake of judgment, nor judgment for the sake of the earth, for not the earth sinned, but man. The earth becomes the execution of God’s judgment, as a throne for judges and a weapon for executioners; and it can be, it is said, that judgment can also be in temporal things. And human flesh is now mortal, but then it will be immortal. And Paradise, by sensory understanding, has also an eternal perception. It was not said of Paradise in a parable, it was not planted as a figure, being eternal.
