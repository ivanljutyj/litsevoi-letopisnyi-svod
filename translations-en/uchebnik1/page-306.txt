Whose star shone in the heavens before His birth, proclaiming the Born One to all the universe? Or was there any other king who, scarcely able to call for father or mother, received kingdoms and victories over enemies? What other king was there in Israel on whom the nations placed their hope, and whom no one could resist? Since Jerusalem has stood, the Jews have suffered defeats in wars, and those who warred against Israel inflicted calamities on them: the Egyptians drove them out, and the Syrians repelled their attacks. For did not David war against the land of Moab and war against Syria, and did they not submit to him? Josiah guarded the surrounding cities, Hezekiah feared Sennacherib’s treachery, the Amorites resisted Joshua son of Nun, and Amalek opposed Moses. And in general it was not the custom of these peoples to be at peace with Israel, as to this day, as the facts testify.” The scribes answered: “We do not deny this, for all is so written, but we await the God the Word, who has never yet come.” Sylvester said: “How is it that you did not heed Daniel, who clearly foretold the time of the coming of the Divine Savior? For the angel said to him: ‘Seventy weeks are appointed for thy people and thy holy city, to finish sin and cleanse iniquity, and to seal up sins, and to cleanse from much iniquity, and to bring everlasting righteousness, and to seal up the vision, and to anoint the Holy of Holies. For the manifestation and understanding, from the going forth of the word to speak and to build Jerusalem, even unto Christ the Lord, seven weeks and sixty-two weeks.’ But you invent other excuses, referring what is written to a future time. And what can you add to this, or even say against it? Since Christ is the Anointed One, He will be called the Holy of Holies. And until His coming Jerusalem stood, but after that all prophetic visions will cease in Israel. And in ancient times David, Solomon, and Hezekiah were anointed, but Jerusalem stood in its place, and prophets prophesied, also those who were anointed and holy people, but they were not called the Holy of Holies. When was there no longer prophet or vision in Israel, if not now, when the Holy of Holies, Christ, foretold by the prophets, has come? For it is truly a great sign, foretold and understood from ancient times, of the coming of God the Word: that from then on Jerusalem would not stand, and there would be no prophets, and visions would cease to be revealed to them. And this is understandable, since He has come of whom were the prophecies and signs; and why would these prophecies and signs now be needed? Therefore they prophesied until the Foretold One came, who delivers from all sins.
