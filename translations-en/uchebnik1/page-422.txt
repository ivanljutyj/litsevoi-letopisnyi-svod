The divine fathers, crying out, said: “We anathematize all those of whom it has been said, together with all who have spoken and thought, or think, or shall think such things, even to the end of time.” Likewise concerning Theodore of Mopsuestia and others, this was then said in the imperial writing: “It was the care of our orthodox and pious ancestors who reigned before us that at all times heretical shoots be cut off by councils of venerable fathers, and the right faith be purely preached in the world, and the Holy Church of God be preserved.”

Concerning the First Council. Thus Constantine the Great opposed Arius, who blasphemed and said: “The Son is not consubstantial with God the Father, but is a creature, and was created out of nothing.” Constantine assembled 318 holy fathers at Nicaea, and himself came then, and when Arius had been anathematized, he strove to establish the Orthodox faith. Therefore the divine fathers confessed: “The Son is consubstantial with God the Father.” Even to this day we so sing and believe.

Concerning the Second Council. Under Theodosius the Elder, Macedonius rejected the divinity of the Holy Spirit, while Apollinarius and his disciple blasphemed against the dispensation of God the Word and said: “God the Word did not receive a human mind, but was united to flesh having an irrational soul.” Theodosius assembled 150 holy fathers in Constantinople, and himself took part in the council, and anathematized the aforementioned heretics, and destroyed their impious teaching and those of one mind with them, and established the preaching of the Orthodox faith.

Concerning the Third Council. Under Theodosius the Younger, the impious Nestorius said that God the Word is one thing and Christ another; and he argued that God the Word is the Son of God the Father by nature, while Christ is the Son of God by grace; and this Nestorius denied that the Holy Mary is the Mother of God. Theodosius assembled 200 holy fathers in council at Ephesus, and sent for the boyars who wished to remain at the council, and ordered Nestorius also to come, and the holy fathers to make inquiry concerning him. There was a thorough investigation, and they condemned Nestorius and anathematized those of one mind with him.
