He promised with an oath, for if he should see a bishop causing scandal, he would immediately cover him with a purple robe, so that it might not harm the faces of those who saw.

And after distributing many gifts to all the churches and commanding the rulers of the regions to honor the bishops, he released them all, rejoicing.

They gave us the rule to celebrate holy Pascha, which we also observe. Some people, however, formerly celebrated it on the fourteenth day of the moon.

One of the philosophers, instructed by certain Hellenes, began to accuse the Christ-loving Emperor Constantine of not observing the customs of the Roman emperors, since he adhered to a new faith. It happened that Saint Alexander the bishop was conversing with this philosopher. The blessed man Alexander said to him as he blasphemed: “I command you in the name of Jesus Christ, our true God, to be silent and not to teach.”

At the same moment, with that word, the philosopher of the Hellenic faith fell silent and was mute as one without voice; and in fear and trembling he came to the holy icon to ask that the curse be removed from his tongue and for the gift of holy baptism. By such a sign and miracle the Lord brought the philosopher and all his household to understanding, calling to salvation the one who had dissuaded others from the right faith, and now showing him to be its true champion.

At that time also Macarius, Archbishop of Jerusalem, came to the council, and the emperor commanded him, when he returned, to find the place of the Holy Resurrection of Christ our God, and the place of the Skull — Golgotha, and the Life-giving Wood, that is, the Precious and Life-giving Cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, on which for the salvation of the human race He suffered and wrought deliverance for us.

About Empress Helena, and How She Went to Jerusalem to Find the Precious Cross

In that same year he crowned Helena, his God-wise holy mother, and adorned her with earrings, as a queen. And she saw a vision commanding her to go to Jerusalem and to bring to light the divine places buried by the pagans.

She asked her son Constantine for permission to fulfill the command that had come to her from God. Having heard it, he sent her away to Jerusalem with great wealth.

Macarius was then patriarch of Jerusalem, and he received the empress with fitting honor. Blessed Helena, having summoned all the Jews, asked them to show her the Precious Cross; but since they refused, she began to threaten them with death.
