In Jerusalem, Herod inquired of them the time of the star, and said to the Magi: If you find Him, come and tell me, so that I too may go and worship Him.

And the Magi went, led by the star which they had seen in the East, and found Jesus and His Mother in the city of Bethlehem, and fell to the ground and worshiped the Savior Christ, and said among themselves: The star reveals a God greater than itself, though we honor it as a god. And they brought Him gifts as to God, and having received a command from an angel in a dream not to return to Herod, they went another way into the land of Persia, and did not obey Herod.

When the Magi had departed, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph, saying: Take the Child and His Mother and flee to Egypt, and remain there until I tell you, for Herod seeks the Child to destroy Him.

He arose, took the Child and His Mother, and departed by night to Egypt, and was there until the death of Herod. Joseph entered the sanctuary of their god Avdul, and at once all the idols fell. The priests saw it and trembled, and fell down before the Savior, lest the church fall upon them. And they wanted to paint an icon of His likeness. The iconographer undertook to paint it, but could not finish the image of Christ. They all began to entreat the Savior to command that this be so, and that His icon be completed. Christ bowed toward it, and the icon itself was at once completed. The Egyptians keep this icon to this day. Many mighty kings attempted to take it or make a copy of it, but could not.

And after this, when Herod had been mocked, he was enraged, and questioned the Jewish chief priests: Where is Christ born? And he learned, and having sent soldiers, he slaughtered all the infants in Bethlehem, the city of Judea, from two years old and under, according to the time. And they sought to kill John.

Herod also sent soldiers to Zacharias, John’s father, in the sanctuary, saying: Where is your son John? And Zacharias said: I do not know. Am I my son’s keeper? Then the soldiers killed him between the church and the altar. His blood flowed out and at once became like stone. There were two reasons for his murder: some say because he hid John from Herod; others say that the saint was killed for the sake of the Virgin, the Mother of God. For Zacharias had again brought her into the place where virgins were not permitted to enter, and he was condemned to death, since he had openly transgressed the law of the scribes, and since he told everyone about her that she was a virgin, as she had been a virgin before the Nativity.

Elizabeth was in Bethlehem, and she received John, and went into the wilderness, and hid with her child for 40 days in a certain cave, and then returned alone. John was brought up there, and remained so until his manifestation to the people of Israel in the wilderness.

Joseph went to Egypt and spent five years there with his sons and daughters, with the Mother of God, and with Christ; and again he had a revelation, and returned and came to Nazareth of Galilee.
