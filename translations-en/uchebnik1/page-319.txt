Inside the church, on the western side, was the house of the holy disciples, in which they hid, fearing the Jews; “and Christ came to them, the doors being shut.”

On the left side of the church stood the purple column to which Christ, our God, was bound, beaten by the Jews. At the right side of the altar was the place of the Holy Washing of the Feet and of the Descent of the Holy Spirit on Holy Pentecost.

On the left side of the altar, where the tomb of the holy prophet David was, blessed Helena established the making of prosphora for the holy oblation.

She also built in the courtyard of Caiaphas a church of the holy apostle Peter. From Golgotha, the place of the Holy Resurrection, to Holy Zion is one stadion. And many other churches of God did Saint Helena, the first Christian empress, set up in the holy places: at the pit of the prophet Jeremiah, and at the spring of Siloam, and in the village of the Potter’s Field at the burial of the poor, by the oak of Mamre where the Savior appeared to Abraham, on the holy lithostrotos, that is, the stone pavement, a church of the holy lord John.

These honorable and holy churches of God and of our Savior God in the holy places, eighteen churches, the wise and Christ-loving and truly magnanimous mother of blessed Constantine ordered to be built soon. The blessed one, having gone around them all and honored them with faith, as she had promised Saint Macarius, patriarch of the Jerusalem church, took with her a part of the Honorable Cross, which she kept for herself, and the Holy Nails of Christ’s Body, and returned to her son, the glorious Emperor Constantine, in Byzantium.

And she told her son much about the Christian faith; he met her with joy and, having placed a part of the Life-giving Wood in a golden reliquary, entrusted it to Saint Archbishop Alexander for safekeeping.

Blessed Patriarch Metrophanes had died by that time. Blessed Emperor Constantine forged some of the Holy Nails onto his helmet and fastened the others to the reins of his horse’s bridle, so that what was spoken by the prophet might be fulfilled: “In that day it shall be upon the bridle of the king’s horse: Holiness to the Lord Almighty.”

This holy Emperor Constantine wrote to the truly renowned blessed Macarius, vicar of the Jerusalem church, asking him to labor in the building of Christ’s churches, and sent a prince with sufficient means, to create for universal joy churches in the holy places that would have no equal in beauty in all the universe.
