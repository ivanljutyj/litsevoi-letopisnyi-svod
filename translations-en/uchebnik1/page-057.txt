The Tale of the Elevation of the Bread of the Holy Mother of God

After the dread Resurrection of the Savior, even until the Descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles, even until the holy apostles were divided for preaching, all the apostles were together. And when after prayer they reclined and ate, they left the Lord's place unoccupied, and apart from the portion, or bread tablet, which they ate, they placed the part of Christ in His place. And when they finished eating, they rose and gave thanks, and took the bread tablet, which they called the Lord's portion and which lay in the place, and elevated it upward, and said: Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, and so forth. And instead of "O, great Name..." they said: Christ is risen, even until the Ascension. From the Ascension until the Dormition of the Mother of God they said: O great Name of the Holy Trinity, Lord Jesus Christ, help us. Each of them did this likewise if he found himself alone somewhere. When the apostles on clouds held a worldwide assembly for the Repose of the Lady, they performed the customary burial, that is, both matins and consolation for those who had come, and after they had arranged everything, they rose and, as usual, elevated the bread tablet which they called Christ's, and before it said: O, great Name... O most glorious miracle and great wonder! She who was dead appeared alive on the clouds, and radiant angels stood before her and moved through the air, greeting her. She said: I am with you all the days of your lives; I have received this joyful gift from My Son. Having seen this miracle, the disciples marveled and, instead of "Lord Jesus Christ...", sang "O Most Holy Mother of God, help us!" And then they came to the most pure body, opened the tomb, found it empty, and were convinced that she truly lives in the body. Just as before her Son, who rose from the dead, so she too rose, having first reposed, and reigns in Heaven with Christ. When the unbelieving Jewish robber dared to overturn the bier of the Divine Vessel, an angel immediately cut off his hands, and they fell off; at once he believed, was healed by Peter, and glorified God. The apostles glorified her, and having glorified and given praise to God, they departed each to his own place. The years of the Mother of God are counted thus: when she was seven, her parents brought her into the church; she spent six and a half years in the Lord's church, and six months in the house of Joseph the Betrothed; and there was the worldwide joy of the Good Tidings. At fifteen she bore Emmanuel; with her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, she spent thirty-three years, which, added together, gives forty-eight years. After the Ascension of her Son she dwelt in the house of John the Theologian on holy Zion for twenty-four years. In all she lived seventy-two years. After the Dormition of the Mother of God all the apostles dispersed. John went to Ephesus. Matthew the Evangelist says that before the captivity of Jerusalem none of the apostles went far away. The Gospel written by him was written in the first years.
