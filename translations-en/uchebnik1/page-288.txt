Galer did much that was foul and evil, exceedingly much, and in addition declared a ruthless and inhuman persecution against Christians throughout the whole East. Then many of the virtuous were martyred: 3,650 martyrs in the Nicomedian crevices; Theodore and Mnana, and Ulian, and Okean from the village of Kandavlia; Minodora, Mitrodora, and Nymphodora, cousins; Papa from the city of Alexandria; Maxim, Theodotus, and Aclipiades from the city of Markiyan; John the Confessor from Egypt; Sergius and Bacchus; Eulampius and Eulampia, his sister, in the city of Nicomedia; Mnason, bishop of Cyprus; and Uar from Egypt and with him 6 martyrs; Nester from the city of Selunsk; Kiryena and Uliana from Tarsus in Cilicia; Domnin and Theodotin; Timothy the Philosopher; Dorotheus, Katerius, Silvanus, Pomphilus and with him 3 virgins; Alexander of Selunsk; Mina, Victor, Vincent from the land of Egypt; Anthony, Nikephor, German, Demetrius from the village of Ladaudia; Roman and with him the infants Anthimus and Phalelei; Christopher and Euphemia and her children; This same Maximian received a sign of the torment awaiting him, as was worthy of his abomination. Strong pains seized him, destroying all his flesh from the great flame within his entrails. His flesh melted like wax. Moreover he went blind, and having become blind, the wretched one, scorched still more strongly, was baked as on a griddle, the most foul, so that his bones dried up and the likeness of the human form vanished. Besides, rotting painfully and putrefying cruelly, he gave off such a stench as if a dead man were decomposing in a grave. He, barely breathing through all this, sighed from the depth of his heart and called for death. And when his end drew near, he said thus of himself, the sinner: “Woe to me, wretched and worthy of lamentation, what audacity I raised against Christians! And because of my sin I suffer a torment I deserve.” Having said this with great pain, in suffering he gave up the spirit...
