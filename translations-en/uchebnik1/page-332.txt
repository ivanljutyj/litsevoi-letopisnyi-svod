To his youngest son Constans he left the plain of Gaul, called Italy, and Rome itself; and he also left his only daughter, named Helena, whom he had by Fausta, daughter of Herculius Maximian, who later was the wife of Julian the Transgressor.

On the Repose

Having made this testament, the holy Emperor Constantine, having passed over into the ageless and endless kingdom, ascended from the body to heaven, receiving in peace the end of this life on the twenty-first day of May, having lived sixty-five years in all. Some say that Constantine was killed by Dalmatius Anavalianus, his brother by Theodora, daughter of Herculius, through his treachery—he poured a magical poison for him, in order to take the kingdom from him. But he was deceived in his hope and did not obtain what he desired.

Then the commanders, eparchs, and all the other local princes placed the emperor in a golden shrine and carried him into Constantinople, while all his army went before him and after him, weeping bitterly over the loss; and as for a gentle father who had accepted his trial, all lamented him. His body received such great honors as was fitting for one who had been emperor so long, while the princes, still children, awaited his return from Rome. It is superfluous, I think, to write what I have already written about him, as I think. By his example one can easily see and learn how the Giver of all good things glorifies those who love Him. If anyone believes this, let him also now have faith in what is written about the honorable shrine of Constantine, from which visible healings of every kind occurred, according to what my Lord said: “Those who glorify Me I will glorify; those who despise Me shall be despised.” But God, working great miracles, glorified His holy servants, Emperor Constantine and his mother Helena, for their righteousness and great virtue. Vouchsafe to those reigning successors who truly believe in You to imitate their faith and virtue until the end of the Greek dominion, and let people live in quietness and gladness and remain in joy.
