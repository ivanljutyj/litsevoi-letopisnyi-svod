A smile was on his face; he was wise and gracious in speech, sweetly speaking from the Holy Spirit and healing.

This Tsar Nero sent soldiers throughout all Judea and to Jerusalem, and did evil to all, and killed many; and he sent soldiers because, grieved, they cried out, reproaching Nero for cutting off Pilate’s head, as though he had avenged Christ, and for what had come upon him—not only that, but as though he were the ruling governor, they had crucified Christ, and for this they were angry with him as with a tormentor. And then Annas and Caiaphas were killed by the soldiers.

This Nero also took to his kingdom another country on the Pontus, sending Polemon his commander, and made it an eparchy, calling it Pontus Polemoniacus. This Nero loved the Veneti greatly.

About James

Great James, the brother of God, was pure from his mother’s womb; he drank neither wine nor any other intoxicant, and he never ate anything in which there was a living soul, and he did not require washing. His knees were worn like a camel’s, because he always knelt and prayed to God. Because of this great righteousness of his and his wondrous life he was called righteous, and also the support and justification of the people, since he was famous, revered, and very glorious. Now it was the feast of Passover, and a multitude of people gathered in the church, and the scribes brought James up to the church’s elevation, wishing to reject the preaching of Christ, and said in a great voice: We beg you, righteous one, tell the people not to be led astray and not to follow the so-called Christ. We all listen to you, knowing that you are righteous and without hypocrisy. He answered and said: Why do you ask me about Christ, who himself sits at the right hand of God in heaven? The lawless high priests and Pharisees cried out: O righteous one, you too have been deceived. And they said to one another: We have done badly, that we have brought such testimony upon ourselves concerning Jesus, and have given many occasion to believe in Him.

And behold, the unbelievers were enraged and cast James down. But he, kneeling, prayed for them. And one of the sons of Rahab approached and listened to him, and said to them: What are you doing? The righteous man is praying even more for you. And a fuller came up and struck James with a club, while others said with a laundry bat, and then the righteous James died. And they buried him by the church and set up his image.

After James’s martyrdom, Jerusalem too was soon taken captive. And Joseph said: This happened to the Jews because they killed the righteous James, who was the brother of Jesus called Christ, since he was righteous, and the Jews killed him. But against these Jews, after our Lord’s Ascension, no attack came for forty years, for Christ long endured the lawlessness of their blasphemy, so that those who blasphemed such things might understand.
