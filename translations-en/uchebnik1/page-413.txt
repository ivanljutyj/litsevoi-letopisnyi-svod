After eight years and another nine months and five days of Justin’s reign, the Divine Justinian reigned with him, his wonderful kinsman, peacefully, with Empress Theodora. This Justinian was installed and crowned to the kingdom by the Divine Justin, his father’s brother, in the month Xanthicus, that is April, on the 4th day, indiction 5. And it was the 5th and 70th and 700th year, according to the reckoning of Antioch the Great, under the consul Mavortius, who was consul in Rome. This Emperor Justin also sent gold for construction and built much.

These emperors made a persecution against God’s enemies, the Manichaeans, and tortured many. With them the wife of the patrician Andronicus was also tortured, and others with her.

And from that time the spectacles ceased. There were issued for the renewal of the city 30 kentenaria, and for the renewal of the churches 10 kentenaria.

At that same time it happened that the Divine Justin fell ill from three wounds, which he had received in his leg from an arrow in battle, and Justin died in the month Laous, that is August, on the 1st day, on Sunday, at the 3rd hour, indiction 5. When Justin died, he was 76 years old. All the years of his reign were 9 years and 22 days, and with his brother’s son he reigned 4 months.

There are from the beginning of the reign of Augustus Octavius, dictator, to the end of the second consulship of this Justinian, when the 7th indiction ended, 6059 years. Thus from the first-created Adam to the imperial consul the Divine Justin, to the end of his consular term, there were all together 6407 years, to the end of the indiction. The years in Antioch on the Orontes, the Syrian city of God, began from Caesar Julius. According to the chronology accepted in Alexandria on the Nile, the Egyptian city, it was the fifth year, and the 40th, and the 200th, beginning from Diocletian. According to the chronology accepted among the so-called Syrian Macedonians in Apamea, it was the 40th year, beginning from Seleucus Nicator. We found this number of years in the writings of Timothy and Theophilus, wise chroniclers, who spoke alike. In the chronicle of Eusebius Pamphilius we find the number of years from Adam to the consulship of Justinian, at the end of the 7th indiction, to be 6032 years. Truly, I think, the children of Theophilus, Timothy, and Clement count the years, each having composed his account in his own writing. “They set down the sixth thousand years; each added in his own writing the past seventh thousand, that good may come into the world from many with the present good seventh indiction.” There are from the Creation of the world to the second consulship of the Divine Justinian years, as said above.
