Concerning one Egypt, there is information about such church tribute, besides the sacred vessels. From the villages there comes, for every church item, 2,000 and 200 centenaria of gold.

Emperor Justinian alone began and alone completed the construction of Hagia Sophia. Amazement seized those who then saw such a church, shining with light; it was all radiant with gold and silver and pearls and every other adornment. The floor caused no little wonder in those who saw it, for because of the various marbles it seemed like the sea and like a river constantly flowing, and it was as though there were four rivers in the church, named after the four rivers that flow out of Paradise. And a law was laid down: that everyone who for his sins was excommunicated should stand upon these rivers.

Justinian also made a baptistery near the well, and a porch, and stone lions bringing forth water from their mouths for the washing of simple folk, that is, laymen.

On the right side of the church Justinian made a well of sea water, raised by one span, so that the water might rise. He also set a stair for the priests to ascend, so that the water came directly to the face of him who received the water. And he made twelve lions, twelve hinds, and twelve each of eagles, hares, calves, and ravens, and water issued from their mouths for the washing of the priests. That place he named the Lions’ Place. There too Justinian erected a gilded house, and he wished to sleep in the church. But who can tell of the magnificence of which we have already spoken, and of the exceedingly great beauty of such a church? The great Emperor Justinian completed the creation of Hagia Sophia with all its sacred vessels on the twenty-sixth day of the month of September.
