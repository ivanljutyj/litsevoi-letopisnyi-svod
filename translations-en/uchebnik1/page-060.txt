The Tale of the Elevation of the Bread of the Holy Mother of God

After the awesome Resurrection of the Savior, even until the Descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles, even until the division of the holy apostles for preaching, all the apostles were together. And when after prayer they reclined and ate, they left the Lord's place unoccupied, and from the portion, that is, the bread tablet which they ate, they placed Christ's portion in that place. And when they finished eating, they rose, gave thanks, and took the bread tablet, which they called the Lord's portion and which had lain in the place, and elevated it upward, and said: Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee. Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, and the rest. And instead of "O, great Name..." they said: Christ is risen, even until the Ascension. From the Ascension even until the Dormition of the Mother of God they said: O great Name of the Holy Trinity, Lord Jesus Christ, help us. Each of them did this if he found himself alone somewhere. When the apostles, on clouds, made a worldwide assembly for the Repose of the Lady, they performed the customary burial, that is, both Matins and consolation for those who had come, and after they had arranged everything, they rose, and the bread tablet, which they called Christ's, they elevated as usual, and before it they said "O, great Name..." O most glorious miracle and great wonder! She who was dead appeared alive on the clouds, and radiant angels stood before her and moved through the air, greeting her. And she said: I remain with you all the days of your life; I have received this joyful gift from her Son. Seeing this miracle, the disciples marveled and instead of "Lord Jesus Christ..." sang "O Most Holy Mother of God, help us!" And then they came to the most pure body, opened the tomb, and found it empty, and were assured that she truly lives in the body. As before her Son, Who rose from the dead, so she too rose, having departed before this, and reigns in Heaven with Christ. When an unbelieving Jewish robber boldly tried to overturn the bier of the Divine Vessel, an angel immediately cut off his hands, and they fell off; he at once believed, was healed by Peter, and glorified God. And the apostles glorified her, and having glorified and given praise to God, each departed to his own place. The years of the Mother of God are counted thus: at seven years her parents brought her to the church, and she spent six and a half years in the Lord's temple, and six months in the house of Joseph the Betrothed, and then came the universal joy of the Good Tidings. At fifteen she gave birth to Emmanuel, and with her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, she spent thirty-three years; adding these, we get forty-eight years. After the Ascension of her Son she remained in the house of John the Theologian on holy Zion for twenty-four years. In all she lived seventy-two years. After the Dormition of the Mother of God all the apostles scattered. John went to Ephesus. Matthew the Evangelist says that until the captivity of Jerusalem none of the apostles went far away. The Gospel written by him was written in the first years.
