Alexander, however, in no way wished to receive Arius and said to the emperor: “The one whom 318 Fathers of the Church condemned and anathematized, I cannot receive.” And the emperor said: “See to it, then, O bishop, that you do not accept Arius under compulsion, for I will order a council to be assembled to examine both of you.”

But Saint Bishop Alexander set out quickly and withdrew from the emperor’s sight, saying, “The will of the Lord be done.” And the bishop entered the church, and prayed all night before the Holy Altar, and with tears said: “Lord God, do not allow the shameless wolf and ravening beast to enter Your Holy Church, but soon judge the unjust one according to justice.”

On the bursting of Arius’s belly

On the very next day all assembled for the council in the church. Arius, however, as he was going with his like-minded followers to the council, stopped at the house of a certain man situated at the lower part of the square where clothing was sold, and suddenly Arius’s belly burst and his entrails came out. Then all Arius’s like-minded followers dispersed, put to shame. But the good shepherd, Bishop Alexander, joyfully celebrated the holy service in the church.

After Alexander’s repose, pious Christians appointed the priest Paul as patriarch. Then Magnentius rose up in Rome against Constans and killed him.

Then Constantius alone remained to reign over the whole Roman realm, and the Arians grew bold under him, and at their advice Constantius drove the Great Athanasius of Alexandria from the episcopal throne.

And Constantius drove Paul from Constantinople and exiled him to the village of Kukuz in Armenia and shut him in prison. And there, during the Divine service, the Arians strangled Paul with the omophorion in which he was serving.

And Constantius appointed Macedonius the Spirit-fighter as patriarch in Constantinople. This impious man tortured many pious people with various tortures in many lands.

Constantius, however, became afraid because of the vastness of his domains and appointed Gallus Caesar, the nephew of his father Constantine and brother of Julian. But Gallus began to rise against Constantius, and they seized him, and afterward Gallus was killed by Constantius’s command.

On Julian the Apostate

Julian, Gallus’s brother, was a reader in the church of Antioch, and he wished to build a church to the martyr Mamas. And when they were laying the foundation of the church, the earth would not receive it, but on the next day the whole foundation was found cast out of the ground, which foretold Julian’s future apostasy and exposed his foul mind and wretched soul.
