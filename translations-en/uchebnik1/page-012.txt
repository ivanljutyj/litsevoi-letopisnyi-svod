And Ermilai reigned in Rome in place of his father Romun, while Urvan reigned in Byzantium, the city of the mother.

Claudius reigned in Alexandria; he ruled over the tribe and descendants of Khuzifa, that is, Olympias, daughter of Phol, king of Ethiopia, and the eastern lands — Macedonia, Hellas, and Rome; the Hellenic, or Greek, kingdom descends from the tribe of the land of Mir, that is, from the same Ethiopian kingdom. Ethiopia shall stretch out her hand to God in the last days, according to the prophetic word. For blessed David understood by spiritual understanding that from Khuzifa, that is, from Olympias, daughter of Phol, would come the Greek kings; and David proclaimed and said that the Ethiopian hand stretches out to God. Some said that this was spoken by Saint David about the Ethiopian king. But those who say so lie, because that kingdom shall be raised up which will find the Great and Precious Wood of the Holy and Precious Cross, honored in the midst of the earth. Therefore Saint David the father said thus, that Ethiopia is, and her hand stretches out to God, because there is no people or kingdom under heaven that could overcome the Greek kingdom. For the Greek kingdom has a defense on which all the ends of the universe are firmly marked, from east to west, from south to north; and no power will be able to defeat the Greeks while the Precious Cross intercedes for them. But let us return again to what we set aside earlier.

The First Reign in Egypt

After Alexander’s death, 57 years. Alexander entrusted all Egypt and Libya to Ptolemy the Hare to hold and reign over; for so the son of Lagus was nicknamed. He was an astrologer and reigned 42 years.
