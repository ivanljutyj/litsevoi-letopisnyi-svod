And he accepted the single-will heresy, that is, the Monothelite. Later, not only because of such heresy, but also because of sin against the law of God—for he took as his wife his sister’s daughter, that is, his niece, the daughter of his sister—Heraclius fell into the evil disease of dropsy. For by whatever a man sins before God, not being ashamed before God’s face, by that he receives punishment. Heraclius’s face was defiled by filth issuing through the urinary passage. By whatever a man sins against nature, by that he receives punishment. When the emperor wished to pass water, he would place a board on his belly; since the water would not come out, his shameful member was turned by this and discharged water onto his face. And his illness dragged on very painfully, and in it he died.

The Reign of Constantine, Son of Heraclius

After Heraclius, his son Constantine reigned for 4 years. He showed himself in few things as a sole autocrat, and soon hid himself and vanished before his time, saying and doing nothing worthy of memory, being young in body. As they say, Pyrrhus mixed deadly poison for Constantine at the instigation of Martina, his stepmother, and Heraclonas, her son. This Pyrrhus was patriarch of Constantinople, a destroyer, an impious man, a poisoner, a sorcerer, who after Sergius the heretic received the patriarchal throne.

About a Certain Rich Man Who Gave Alms, and Then, Repenting of What He Had Done, Took the Gold Back and Died

In the reign of that Emperor Heraclius, a certain rich man in Constantinople fell ill, and, fearing death, distributed 30 litras of gold to the poor.

And after that occurrence the rich man became healthy again, and, obeying the devil’s deception, repented that he had distributed the gold, and told his close friend, also a rich man. He said to him: “By no means, O man, do not accept this evil thought, lest you anger God, who strengthened you and healed you for the sake of your alms. If you accept this evil thought and anger God, He will bring sudden death upon you, and you will depart from this world without repentance.”

But the rich man who had given the alms did not listen to this advice, but was about to run away from his friend in anger. And his friend said to him: “Since you do not listen to what I have said for your salvation, I will give you another counsel.” He asked: “What other?” And so the true almsgiver answered: “Come with me to the church and say: ‘Not I, Lord, gave this alms, but this man,’ and I will give you 30 litras of gold.” And then that wretched man agreed to this, and came to the church, and received the gold into his hands, since he had said everything as his friend asked; but when he was going out of the church doors, he fell down dead.
