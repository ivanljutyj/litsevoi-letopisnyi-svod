Horrified by this pernicious abomination, he said: “You shall not lie with a male as with a woman, for it is an abomination; both shall be put to death, for such a one is accursed, and both shall be stoned.” Those who with untiring will and deeds labor ceaselessly to preserve such a custom the Great Cyril rebuked at length, saying: “Not for the sake of torment do these accursed ones do this, as women do; they are men, so they are considered. For they have changed nothing for the better in nature, but for the sake of fornication they corrupt the God-created image and the masculine form; the corruptor corrupts many by his will, or, compelled by others, they endure this; but as murderers of nature and rebels against their kind, foul and offensive to life and living, they become so. Like harlots they make love without number, rejoice in foul sins, having mingled a foul, corrupt life with an accursed life, double-hearted and hated, or having a record with lawless generations. They stand far from the courts of the saints and from the holy assembly, as a loathsome and God-shameful judgment, so let them be driven away. For by an improper and evil will, having turned the good and divine work to evil, and having changed spiritual censing into a forbidden deed, forced to serve, they are not only worthy according to the word of the Law to receive torment, but according to the Gospel and apostolic answer they will be suddenly cast into the so-called outer darkness. Of such Moses spoke: ‘For a eunuch completely cut off shall not enter the church of the Lord.’ For one sees the houses of their nobles filled with such wondrously formed faces, wearing golden necklaces on their necks; by birth they are male, but they have the appearance of the female sex and a mincing gait. Like harlots, nodding their heads this way and that, they laugh unrestrainedly, showing forth a pursuit of fornication. Thus with men, as though they were women, softly sleeping and being pressed, they are corrupted; with women, as guardians together with the chaste—that is, as co-mothers—sitting, shamelessly wailing, they do foul things. These women, corrupted and pressed by foul and improper men, are defiled; for the sake of vice they do improper deeds and mockery; and these, as has been said, defile and injure accursed women with sins like demonic dogs, and even more bitterly and evilly—thus they become guilty, partakers of endless torment, corrupting the corruptible. O folly, O deceit, O contagion! But men in authority, receiving them as chaste, believe them and bring them into their homes; and they, having been brought in and having received boldness, having little by little caught the chaste men who rightly apply themselves to virtue, wickedly cast themselves into the foul pit of Sodom and gave themselves over to fire. Therefore such persons are utterly mocked by the laws and the Gospel word and by the excellent and pious life, as being hated by God and unclean. For they chose and loved such a life; as much as came upon them, they destroyed cities; having corrupted and defiled the natural seed, they corrupt the well-minded strength of man, of age, of the male sex
