He betrayed Constantinople to Gaiseric; and when Olybrius came to Rome, leaving his wife Placidia and daughter in Constantinople, Leo wrote through the magister to Anthemius, emperor of Rome: “I have killed Aspar and Ardabur, so that no one may oppose my commands. Do you also kill your son-in-law Ricimer, so that he may not command you. I have also sent to you the patrician Olybrius; kill him too. And reign, commanding, and not being commanded.”

Ricimer distributed his soldiers and stationed them at all the gates of Rome and at the otishie, that is, at the harbor, in order to search those coming into Rome as to what they were carrying. When the illustrious Olybrius came from Leo to Anthemius, and they searched that magister, they found Leo’s letter, which had been sent to Anthemius.

And the letter of Emperor Leo was brought in to Ricimer, and he showed it to Olybrius. And at once Ricimer sent to Gundobad, his sister’s son, and he came from Gaul, for he was commander of the Gauls there.

This Gundobad came against Emperor Anthemius and killed him when he was in the holy church of Saint Peter; and immediately Gundobad went back.
