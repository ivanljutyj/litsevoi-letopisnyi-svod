And he accepted the single-will heresy, that is, the Monothelite. Then, not only because of such heresy, but also because of transgression against the law of God—for he took as his wife his sister’s daughter, that is, his niece, the daughter of his sister—Heraclius fell into a grievous dropsical illness. For by whatever a man sins before God, not being ashamed before the face of God, by that he receives punishment. And Heraclius’s face was defiled by filth issuing through the urinary passage. By whatever a man sins against nature, by that he receives punishment. When the king wished to release water, he placed a board on his belly; since the water did not come out, from this his shameful member was turned back and discharged water onto his face. And his illness dragged on very painfully, and in it he died.

Reign of Constantine, Son of Heraclius

After Heraclius, his son Constantine reigned for 4 years, who in few things showed himself a sole autocrat, and soon hid himself and vanished before his time, having said and done nothing worthy of memory, being young in body. As they say, Pyrrhus mixed deadly poison for Constantine at the instigation of Martina, his stepmother, and of Heraclonas, her son. This Pyrrhus was patriarch of Constantinople, a destroyer, an impious man, a poisoner, a sorcerer, who after Sergius the heretic received the patriarchal throne.

On a Certain Rich Man Who Gave Alms, and Then, Repenting of What He Had Done, Took the Gold Back and Died

Under that king Heraclius, a certain rich man in Constantinople fell ill and, fearing death, distributed 30 litras of gold to the poor.

And after that event the rich man became healthy again, and, obeying diabolical deception, repented that he had distributed the gold, and told this to his close friend, also a rich man. He said to him: “In no way, O man, accept this evil thought, lest you anger God, who strengthened you and healed you for the sake of your alms. If you accept this evil thought and anger God, He will bring sudden death upon you, and you will depart from this world without repentance.”

The rich man who had given alms did not listen to this advice, but was about to run away from his friend in anger. And his friend said to him: “Since you do not listen to what I said for your salvation, I will tell you another counsel.” He asked: “What other?” And so the true merciful man answered: “Come with me to the church and say: ‘Not I, Lord, have given this alms, but it is his,’ and I will give you 30 litras of gold.” And then that wretched man agreed to this, and came to the church, and received the gold in his hands, since he had said everything as his friend asked him; but when he was going out of the church doors, he fell down dead.
