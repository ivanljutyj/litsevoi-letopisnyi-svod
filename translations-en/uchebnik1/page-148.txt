There is no salvation for you unless you find helpers for yourselves. For Ananus, having consulted with the people, sent envoys to Vespasian so that he might come quickly and seize the whole city. To the people, however, Ananus announced a purification, so that in the morning they might enter the temple ostensibly to worship and offer sacrifice, and attack you suddenly. And I was sent to arrange peace with you, in order to seize you treacherously when you were unarmed. You must therefore take thought for the salvation of your lives: either make peace with Ananus, or send for help. But I tell you that if the hope of forgiveness warms you, you have no sense, for people will not forget what lawless deeds you committed against them." With this lie John frightened the Zealots, and he spoke to them vaguely of help from outside, but his words pointed to Idumea.

And to this John added further that Ananus gnashed his teeth, wishing to tear apart the leaders of the Zealots. Having heard all this, Eleazar son of Simon and Zachariah son of Phalek, both priests by birth, consulted with the other Zealots about what ought to be done, for a hard time had come upon them, since they themselves were shut in and could not send for help from outside. Nevertheless they decided to send to the Idumeans.

And they wrote a letter quickly and sent it to the Idumeans with these words: "Ananus deceives the people and is handing the capital over to the Romans. We have separated from him for the sake of freedom and are now shut in, sitting inside the temple. And guards stand around us. Little time remains for our salvation. If you do not help us soon, Ananus and our enemies will overcome us
