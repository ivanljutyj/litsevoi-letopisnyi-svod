Besides gold, they bring food to the Romans and feed them four months of the year. But when you rise up for war, where will you receive help? From the land? But the whole world is subject to the Romans. Perhaps from God? But God is on their side too, for it is not without God's Providence that they have obtained such power. Your faith is not suited for war. You may hope for help from God only by observing the Law, but in order to fight you will have to transgress the Law and thereby provoke God to anger. If you keep the custom of the Sabbath and take up no work on that day, you will soon be conquered, as your forefathers were by Pompey. For that Pompey waited for the day when all the Jews were celebrating, and on that day attacked them. But if you depart from the ancestral Law for the time of war, then I do not understand what you are striving for. For your one aim is to depart in nothing from the Law. How then will you call God to help you if you voluntarily refuse to serve Him? When someone takes up arms, he does so in hope either of divine help or of human help. If both these sinews are cut, then taking up arms means inevitable destruction; by this you will drag yourselves to execution like prisoners condemned to death. It would be better for you to cut the throats of your children and wives with your own hands and burn our beautiful fatherland. This is mad, but so you will avoid shameful defeat and condemnation. O friends, a ship ought to remain in harbor if it is clear that a storm is coming, and not leave the harbor in bad weather. For he who perishes by unexpected misfortune is worthy of pity, while he who rushes of himself into manifest destruction deserves shame and condemnation. If any of you thinks that the Romans, having won the war, will spare you, he is cruelly mistaken, for the vengeance taken on you will be an example to keep fear in other peoples. And the holy city will be burned, and your whole race will be killed, and if anyone survives, he will have nowhere to flee, since all lands are subject to the Romans, and therefore they will be afraid to receive you among them. The disaster will fall not on you alone who live here, but on all Jews who live in other countries. For in the whole world there is no city in which a part of our people is not found. And because of your revolt all of them will be cut down by foreigners; through the folly of a few, all cities will be filled with Jewish blood. Therefore I beg you: spare your women and children, and your capital, and the holy places, and the holy temple, and the Law, and the adornment of the temple. After their victory the Romans will spare none of these. I call as witnesses the holy ones, and God's holiness, and our common fatherland, that I have done everything possible to save you and to benefit you. If, after considering all that has been said, you incline toward peace, I will gladly join your prudence; but if you choose war, I will not go with you to this certain destruction."
