A smile was on his face; he was wise, well-spoken, sweetly conversing and healing from the Holy Spirit.

This king Nero sent soldiers throughout all Judea and to Jerusalem, and did evil to all, and killed many, and sent soldiers, since they, grieved, cried out, reproaching Nero for having cut off Pilate’s head, and as though he had avenged Christ, and that He had come to him not only so, but as one having authority as a governor, they had crucified Christ; and for this they were angry with him as with a tormentor. And then Annas and Caiaphas were killed by the soldiers.
