By these words the king was as if pierced, and he began to torture all the maidservants in his house, and one of them cried out under torture: “O God, King of heaven and earth, punish the mother of Antipater, the leader of us all and the cause of all evils!” The king seized upon these words and continued torturing, wishing to find the truth. This woman revealed to him that Antipater’s mother loved Pheroras and had fornication with him, and that Antipater, Pheroras, and the maidservants secretly gathered, and when they came from the king they feasted together all night, admitting neither slave nor free, neither man nor woman. So spoke this maidservant.

Then the king ordered each maidservant to be tortured separately, and as they were beaten they all with one voice reported the same as this woman had said. It was also discovered that the departure of Antipater to Rome and of Pheroras to his region had been planned by them themselves, for many times they had said among themselves: “After the death of Alexander and Aristobulus, Herod will fall upon us and our wives. He who did not spare Mariamne and the children born of her, will he spare anyone? It is better for us to keep far away from this savage beast.”

It was also discovered from Pheroras’s maidservants under torture that Antipater had many times tearfully complained to his mother and said: “I am already grey, yet my father grows younger every day, and my end will come before I begin to reign. If my father dies before me—O when will that happen, and whom will the Lord grant to see it!—even then my glory and pleasure will be short, and it will soon end. For there grow to my destruction and my children’s the heads of the hydra—the offspring of Aristobulus and Alexander. And the hydra is such a beast that though Hercules cut off a hundred of its heads with his sword, the last head remained, and in place of the severed ones new ones grew. At last Iolaus came to Hercules’s aid. Then Hercules began to cut off the hydra’s heads, and Iolaus immediately cauterized with a brand the places where the heads had been; thus they stopped the growth of this beast’s heads. So I cut off Aristobulus and Alexander, but there is no benefit to me in this, for in their place now are their sons, and I have no helper Iolaus. And I do not know how to fulfill my desire. Because of all these descendants of Herod I have no opportunity to hand over power in my kingdom, after my reign, to an heir from among my children. After my death Herod ordered his son by Mariamne, Herod, to reign. But I think that in his old age he has lost his mind if he hopes that even after his death his words will remain in force.
