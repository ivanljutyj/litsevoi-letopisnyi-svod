After the Apostle Peter, after he had left the episcopate in Antioch, Evod became bishop there and became patriarch. And in his time they began to be called Christians and were named by this name; before, Christians had been called Nazarenes and Galileans.

In the eighth year of Claudius’s reign, the Jews made great persecution against the apostles and their disciples, and took counsel to raise an army against the Romans. Claudius first sent against them a commander named Kintion, who brought destruction upon the Jews. Claudius also appointed over them a ruler named Felix, and he swept the Jews away.

In the 6th year of Claudius’s reign, Luke wrote the Gospel, in the year 5548 from Adam (the 48th year from Christ), and 15 years after the Ascension of the Lord. And in the 18th year after the Ascension of the Lord, in the 9th year of Claudius’s reign, at the Jewish feast of Pentecost, the priests again heard a Voice speaking in human language from the inner altar, which is called the Holy of Holies: “Let us depart from here.” This Voice came three times, and hearing it, these priests cried out that there would be a sacrifice. And all learned of this. And immediately from then began the destruction of the Jews.

To this Emperor Claudius Pilate himself wrote and sent a letter to Rome, and wrote as follows: From Pontius Pilate to my lord Emperor Claudius. This word is true, and I myself have experienced it. The Jews took pleasure in their envy, and handed over to terrible torment and dishonor Him of whom their fathers had prayed that their God would send them from Heaven His Holy One, who for their sake would be called King; and He promised to become incarnate of a Virgin on earth. And when I was ruler over the Jews, the God of the Hebrews sent Him to them, and the Jews saw that He gave sight to the blind, cast demons out of all, raised the dead, commanded the winds, walked with dry feet on the waves, on the sea, and performed other signs and wonders. And when many out of the Jewish darkness understood and began to believe that this is the Son of God, then the former teachers of the law, the scribes, and the Jewish Pharisees envied, opposed, and could not endure this, and seized Him and told me lies about Him, that He had done many things against their law. But I believed their such words, and beat Him, and handed Him over into their power. And they crucified Him on the cross, and buried the Dead One, and set a guard from the servants of the councillors to guard His tomb, and sealed His tomb; and on the 3rd day He rose from the tomb. And so greatly was the Jewish madness inflamed that they gave a bribe to my servants, saying: Say that His disciples stole the body by night. But the servants, having received the silver, could not hide the truth about Him, how it all happened, but immediately testified about His Resurrection from the tomb, and told that they had received a bribe from the Jews, and that those were deceiving the king, hoping that he would pass on this Jewish lie.
