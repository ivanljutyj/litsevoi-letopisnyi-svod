The emperor decided to leave in Jerusalem for guard the tenth regiment, horsemen and foot soldiers. Since the war had ended, Titus wished to honor all his soldiers for their bravery, for their skill in battle, and for the victory, and to give gifts to the victors and show honor according to their deserts. And a high place was made for the emperor, on which he sat, in the middle of the first camp. And Titus stood in that place with his commanders. When all had fallen silent, the emperor said to his soldiers:

Word on wisdom, with instruction and gratitude, to the Roman soldiers, each of whom Titus wisely honored

“I express great gratitude to you, Romans, for your devotion and for the obedience that you showed before me in hard and frequent trials, together with your bravery. You have increased the power of your fatherland. You have shown all peoples that neither a multitude of troops, nor the strength and greatness of a city, nor the beastlike courage of enemies could withstand Roman power. And the war has been crowned with a glorious end, though it lasted a long time. I have asked nothing else of you better than what I have already received. But something more glorious than all this you have done—that you appointed us with my father to reign and to set in order the Roman affairs. The Romans gladly joined your decision and praised you for choosing us. You are all wonderful to me and dear to my heart, for you showed your bravery beyond measure and never grew lazy. Therefore I now will give honors and gifts to all of you as is fitting, and to those who distinguished themselves—the most magnificent, for you have adorned your life with bravery and deeds and glorified my army by your successes. And whoever labored more than others shall receive more honor and gifts. I wish to honor the most valiant soldiers and the most obedient, and to punish those who sinned against our law.” And Titus ordered his scribes to read out the names of all in order, who had performed what feat and in what place. The scribes called everyone by name. Each of those who heard his name and his feat came forward and stood before Titus, and he praised him for his fortitude, for bravery, and for diligence, and placed a golden wreath on his head, and hung a golden necklace on his neck, and gave him a long golden spear, and a silver banner, and allotted him a portion of the captured gold, silver, and precious fabrics, and changed the rank of the distinguished man to a higher one. And so the emperor rewarded all in order.

And Titus honored each of his soldiers according to his valor, and according to custom blessed the whole army, and then all the soldiers cried out, and with trumpets, pipes, flutes, and drums gave loud-voiced praise to their emperor, so that it might seem the earth shook.
