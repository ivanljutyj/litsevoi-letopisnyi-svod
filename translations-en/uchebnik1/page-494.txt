And these women entered the church and stoned the impious patriarch, calling him a hireling and not a shepherd; a wolf and not a pastor.

The patriarch reported this to the emperor, who ordered all those women to be put to the sword. And many of the Orthodox were given over to death, while others hid in the deserts.

And Emperor Leo raised a great persecution, and many priests, monks, and simple folk, that is, laymen, he tormented with many imprisonments and wounds and put to death.

Therefore Gregory, occupant of the throne of Great Rome, refused to obey the imperial authority of this Leo and fled from his impious heresy; and, having sworn to preserve peace, he was received under German authority together with all Italy, and at that time did not give tribute to the emperor; and he cursed Anastasius, the false patriarch of Constantinople, and those with him. Under this Emperor Leo were John of Damascus, Claudian, Theodore, Victor, and holy women: the virgin Pherbutha and her sister.

And Pope Gregory, first occupant of the apostolic throne, by two letters to the emperor affirmed the veneration of icons, but could not turn the emperor from his madness; rather the emperor became even more fierce, showing himself a second Belshazzar, as the writing of ancient people says: “I have chosen from among all these a raven, that is, a crow, and I will show one blacker than blackness.”

In those years there was a great and terrible earthquake; many churches and houses of Constantinople fell, and many monasteries collapsed.

Under this emperor countless people died in Thrace. And the earth quaked for two years, so that the sea in some places overflowed its bounds.

At that time the most foul fierce beast, like a “cunning cave-dweller,” and not like a man, Leo died of preloga, that is, diarrhoea, like one of the princes.

Masalma, of whom was said above, came to the city of Abydos, ravaging much.

And Masalma hastened to Constantinople and wrote to Suleiman his chief counsellor, that he should come quickly in ships.

On the 15th day of August Masalma besieged Constantinople, encircling the landward side of the city, having previously laid waste the Thracian country greatly.

On the first day of September Suleiman arrived, having both very great ships and war ships with bulwarks, that is, fortifications, and very great dromons, 1800 and 700.

Emperor Leo sent ships with fire against the Saracens, and burned them with fire, and destroyed their terrible ships.
