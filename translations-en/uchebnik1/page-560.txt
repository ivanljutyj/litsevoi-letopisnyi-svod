When Nikephoros Maleinos learned of the designs of George and Subatios, he decided to capture them in the following way. He secretly circulated a message among their soldiers, advising them to desert their commanders and leave them alone.

When George was seized and brought to Constantinople, the emperor ordered him blinded.

After this he was placed in Mili, and there he sat with outstretched hand begging alms, and passersby threw small coins to him.

Thirty days later Subatios was also seized by Nikephoros Maleinos at Kelinitsa, in the monastery where he had taken refuge. When he was brought to Constantinople, the emperor was at the palace of Saint Mamas.

And the emperor ordered George Piganin to be brought to meet Subatios, and, placing in his hand a clay censer containing sulfur instead of incense, he ordered him to cense. After this Subatios was blinded in one eye and his right hand was cut off, and then, having been placed in Lamlte, he was given a cup so that passersby might throw alms into it. Three days later the guards led Subatios and George to their homes.

On the Murder of Emperor Michael

On September 1, in the fifteenth indiction, a son, Leo, was born to Michael by Eudokia Ingerina. In honor of his birth the emperor arranged a chariot race at the Hippodrome located next to the palace of Saint Mamas. The emperor himself rode for the Blues; for the Greens, the logothete Constantine the Armenian, father of Thomas, patrician and former logothete of the drome; for the Whites, Chila; and for the Reds, Kras.
