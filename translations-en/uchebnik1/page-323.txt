Many say that they themselves saw the place at the foot of the mountain called the Valley of Lamentation, and the Kedron brook, and the place of Her rest, and the vineyard of Asaphat, in which Jesus Christ, our God, was betrayed. Here also was the place where the Lord prayed on the night in which He was betrayed. There too the wondrous Helena went aside and ascended Mount of Olives, to the east, where the Lord our God ascended, and in faith she set up there a church to God and our Savior.

She went down also to the tomb of the blessed and holy Lazarus, which is located two furlongs from the city, and kneeling before the tomb of the venerable Lazarus, watering the lid of the tomb with warm tears, she commanded a church to be built in the name of Saint Lazarus.

And she came to the river Jordan, in which Christ our God was baptized, having no sin, for our salvation. The blessed Helena crossed the Jordan and found the cave in which the Forerunner lived, and she set up a church in the name of John the Baptist.

Opposite the cave there is a high place from which Saint Elijah was taken up; and there too she commanded a beautiful church to be set up in the name of the prophet Elijah.

And she went to the city of Tiberias, which is four days’ journey away. In the environs of Tiberias were twelve thrones, where Christ our God sat and taught. In this same place Christ multiplied seven loaves and fed four thousand.

And here also is the village of Capernaum, where Christ our God lived; in it is also the house of John the Theologian, where Christ supped with the disciples.

Here too the miracle with the centurion took place; here also the bed of the paralytic was lowered from the roof through the attic. In those places the holy and blessed Helena commanded venerable holy churches to be built for glory and for the veneration of the saints.

And having crossed the river flowing there with the gifts she had received from the tsar, and with the most noble nobles of Constantinople, she found a stone bearing the image of the cross, on which Christ healed the woman with the issue of blood, and here she ordered a beautiful church to be built in the name of the Honorable and Life-giving Cross. And rising from there, she came to Palestine to a place not far from the aforementioned cross and called “Seven Wells,” where many springs gushed forth, and where Christ our God worked the miracle with five loaves and two fish. And here she set up a wondrous church.
