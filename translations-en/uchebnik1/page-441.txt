Justin could not make columns like the former ones, but sent Priscus to the patrician Manasius on the island, and there he hewed marbles, so that they might have the image of the earth, and the blue marbles the image of rivers flowing down to the sea.

When they were cutting away the upper platforms and wanted to remove the wooden supports, they filled the church with water to a height of five cubits, and then they threw boards into the water, and the foundations of the church did not crack.

And after this Justin adorned the church with mosaic. For this reason some say that Justin built Holy Sophia. Such is the true account of God’s church of Holy Sophia, which is in Constantinople.

About the Son of a Jew. In the reign of that Justin, a certain Jew in the city of Constantine had a glassmaking trade. He had an only child, and he sent him to learn letters at the Great Church.

The vessel-keeper, a church servant, having gifts from the holy altar gathered in abundance, summoned the boys to eat them. With them the Jew’s boy also entered. When they were dismissed, they went to their parents.

The Jewish boy also came to his father, and his father said to him: “Why did you delay, child?” The boy answered: “I went with the Christian children to the church, and I ate too, father, and for that reason I delayed.” The wicked Jew, like a beast, flew into a rage when he heard that word in his house, and after dinner took his son and entered the workshop, that is, the glassmaker’s shop, and threw him into the glass furnace, and, shutting the doors, went away.

But his wife, knowing her husband’s mad rage against the boy, quickly came to the workshop and, bending to the crack in the door, heard the boy’s voice inside the furnace, and broke open the door, and went in, and brought her son out of the furnace unscorched. She said: “Who put you into the furnace? And how did you not burn?” And the boy said: “My father put me in. But a certain woman clothed in purple came and extinguished the flame, saying: ‘Do not fear, boy.’”

And the mother of this boy heard and learned this, and hearing it, marveled, and took her son, and went to Patriarch Menas, and told him everything, and asked that she and her child become Christians.

The patriarch brought both before the emperor—the father and the boy. After the investigation, the emperor instructed the Jew at length in the Christian faith and urged him to become a Christian, but he could not convert him to Christ. Then the emperor ordered the Jew’s head cut off, since he was the murderer of his own child.
