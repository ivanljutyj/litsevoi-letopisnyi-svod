And through the prayers of the saints He truly shows the best protection from those who wish to harm, and delivered the pious youth from a painful murder, granting the young man keen perception. And he, having uncovered the secret machinations, by divine light and good reason, which destroys hidden evil, destroyed their vain frenzy.

At night, having convinced some that he was joining their faith, and having eluded his enemies by his intelligence, he suddenly mounted a swift horse and, leaving Nicomedia, came to his father in Britain and Gaul, finding him in a mortal illness and drawing up his will. And among the many children he had, he sought and summoned Constantine, but since he was still absent, he repeatedly asked that he be found, praying to God. For he was a man meek in all things and very quiet, hating idols and loving Christians.

And soon Constantine came and told his father everything: how he had taken part in battles in Egypt and Palestine, and how, riding out before the regiment and before the soldiers, he had always inevitably defeated all his opponents completely with his weapon. Then he also told of Maximian's treacherous intention in his thoughts, and of envy, and how he had almost died in chains, and of his dangerous escape from the guard, saying: "Not only did this Maximian intend to kill me by deceit, but with him also the vile Diocletian, for having summoned all their commanders and senators, they decided in an unlawful council to kill me. And although they consulted secretly from me, I learned of it. And if it had not been for God's Providence, I would not have learned of the conspiracy being plotted against me and would not have obtained salvation, like David, for I would have perished at the hands of those impious men." But Consta, pitying the youth and yielding to the law of nature, expressed his love for him with warm tears long held back.

Having heard this, Consta said: "You have renewed yourself in body like an eagle; you have chosen a worthy, God-given king."
