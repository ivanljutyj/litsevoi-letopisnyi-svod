The Lord Christ celebrated the Passover with His disciples at the Last Supper.

There too, they say, the Lord sent the disciples to prepare the Passover, and when He said, “Go to a certain man,” He sent them to John. Some say that he was the owner of the house, for John never departed from Christ, but was with Him until the very crucifixion. There they also stayed for Passover and celebrated the Last Supper; there too the Mother of God stayed from the time John took her to himself, for when he stood at Christ’s cross he heard this from Him.

On the Crucifixion of Our Lord Jesus Christ

Our Lord God Jesus Christ was crucified in the year 5533 from Adam (the 33rd year from Christ), on the 30th day of March, on Friday, at the 6th hour of the day. From the 6th hour to the 9th there was darkness over all the earth, of which the prophet Zechariah says: In that day there shall be no light; and that day is known to the Lord, neither day nor night, and toward evening there shall be light. Our Lord Jesus Christ was crucified in the third indiction. The Passover of the Jews was then on March 31, on Saturday. Golgotha is the place where the Lord was crucified, and a round, high rock, which is called Golgotha.

Below, under the crucifixion, where Adam’s head lies, the place is called Kranievo, which is the Place of the Skull.

When the soldiers had crucified Him, they divided His garments and cast lots for His seamless robe. When the Lord gave up His spirit into the Father’s hands, then the earth shook and the rocks split. Then that rock of Golgotha also split. And one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out through that cleft onto Adam’s head and washed away the sin of Adam the forefather and of all the human race.

And the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the holy departed arose, as it is written in the Gospel. Nicodemus also writes of the Savior’s passion in a work whose title is: The Acts of the Holy Trinity. There he says that two sons of Simeon the God-receiver also rose, Charin and Lentius, who proclaimed what Christ had done in Hades, and many others.

On the Temple Veil

Inside the temple were doors of pure gold, 55 cubits high and 16 wide. Before them hung a veil, or katapetasma, equal to the doors in width and height. It was made of Babylonian cloth and adorned with hyacinth, fine linen, scarlet, and purple; it was wondrous in appearance and skillfully made, for scarlet prefigured fire, fine linen earth, hyacinth air, and purple the sea; for they are like those elements in appearance.
