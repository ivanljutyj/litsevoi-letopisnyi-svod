On the Dormition of the Holy Mother of God

In the reign of this Nero, in the first year, the Holy Mother of God reposed—the Most Holy, Ever-Virgin, and All-Immaculate Mother of God. Fifteen days before her Dormition she prophesied of her departure. And thirteen days before, the archangel Gabriel appeared and announced the departure of the Mother of God from the world and the coming of the Lord.

And the archangel summoned all the apostles. They came to her and by the Holy Spirit beheld her departure. And many others came to her. Many women also came: Mary Magdalene, the daughters of Joseph, and others of her kin. And she delivered to them many words and awesome mysteries which, laying them up in her heart, she had kept: both the angel’s greeting and his vision, and the earlier vision she had seen when she prayed in the church, as it is said in the apostolic writings, that a covenant was to be made. As the apostle Bartholomew declares, she cared above all for self-control, and gave her two garments to two poor widows, and said to them: “I wish to watch over not only you but the whole world.”

When her hour came, Christ appeared to all, and at His word and at the radiance of the light proceeding from Him all fell face down on the earth and were as dead. And He said to them: “Peace be to you.” And they were struck with fear amid their joy, and heard the singing of invisible angels. The apostles stood bowed down and marveled at the miracles. The apostles also sang as if in a sweet sleep, and opened their mouths. And the Mother of God gave up her spirit to her Son and God, and she was seventy-two years old. And again the angels sang and departed with a sound to Heaven. The holy apostles, as Dionysius the Areopagite reports, each sang his own song, and not all together, since all marveled at the singing of Hierotheus.

And after the burial rites they made a tomb in Gethsemane and laid the Mother of God in the tomb.

Gradually all had been there over three days, and the apostle Thomas came, bringing the girdle of the Holy Mother of God, and entered the room where the apostles were sitting. They began to rebuke him, saying: “When our Lord Jesus Christ rose from the tomb on the third day and appeared to His disciples, you were not found with us; and the Lord appeared again a second time in the church and on the eighth day assured you. And now, at the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God, where were you?” He said: “When I was caught up on the clouds and saw the Holy Mother of God coming to the heavenly gates with the angelic choir, she gave me her girdle.” They kissed the girdle of the Most Holy Mother of God and went with joy to the tomb, and did not find her body, only the burial clothes. And the holy, undefiled, and all-immaculate body was hidden from their eyes.
