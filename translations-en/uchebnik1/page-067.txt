In that same festival they brought an ox for sacrifice, and it gave birth to a lamb in the middle of the church; this was beyond natural birth and showed great distress and groaning in the city; and the newborn lamb signified the Lamb of God, Christ, whom the Jews rejected, and this was a clear announcement to them of the imminent and bitter murder and destruction of all.

And the Jews refused to obey the Romans and to pay tribute.

About the warrior

And Nero sent Vespasian, his son, from Achaia, with his son Titus, that is, with his grandson, to fight against Jerusalem, with a very great force.

In the days of the Passover festival, all from Judea came to Jerusalem, and by the Romans they were shut up in the metropolis, that is, the mother of cities; but beforehand, by God's revelation, those who believed in Christ had gone out of the city, and the wicked sinners remained alone as in a prison, and the holy servants of God separated themselves, as before, from the evil-natured and accursed inhabitants of Sodom. For the divine Abraham prayed for the cities of Sodom and said: Do not destroy the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous may not be as the wicked. Therefore the Lover of Mankind God also said: If ten are found in Sodom, I will leave the wrath that is upon them. But none were found, and therefore they quickly perished. But the godly Jeremiah prayed for Jerusalem, and God said to him again: Run to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see and understand, and seek in its broad places, and if you find a man who executes judgment and seeks faith, I will be merciful to him, says the Lord. But even so it was scattered, and grew poor, and suffered want, receiving God's wrath, and not at another time but on feast days; and this was very just for them: on the days in which they sinned before the Lord, on those same days they suffered for the murder of Christ.

Nero, however, ruling his realm, fell into unworthy and vile deeds, and did things unworthy of kings: he hummed, sang abominable songs, and danced for all to see, and to all these unrighteous deeds he added blasphemy, and became the first persecutor of the Divine Word. After this a conspiracy was formed against him, and it was reported to him, and for this he fell into great anger, and killed his mother, his sister, his wife, and many others of his close kin; moreover, he killed noble men in Rome, fearing that they would rise up. And so from great anger he became like a beast, went into the field, and with his own hands cut himself, as was ordained by God's judgment. The Greeks also depict this, who record wonders and heavenly things, as well as events. And Vespasian besieged Jerusalem.
