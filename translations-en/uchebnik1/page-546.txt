Recognizing him, the empress followed them. And when they reached the Bronze Gates, she saw there a certain Wondrous Man sitting before the image of Christ, before Whom they also placed Theophilos. Falling at His feet, the empress began to entreat Him for Theophilos. But this Glorious One, after a pause, opened His mouth and said: “O woman! Great is your faith! Therefore know that for the sake of your tears and your faith, and also for the sake of the prayerful petitions of My servants and My priests, forgiveness is granted to your husband Theophilos.”

After these words He ordered those who had brought Theophilos: “Untie him and give him to his wife.” And taking her husband, she departed rejoicing.

Immediately awakening, Empress Theodora told the patriarch and those with him about her vision. And when they began to offer prayers and petitions for the emperor, Patriarch Methodius took a clean scroll, wrote on it the names of all the heretic emperors and with them the name of Emperor Theophilos, and placed it in the Great Church beneath the holy altar, secretly from all.

On the morning of the fifth day, during prayer, Patriarch Methodius saw in the Great Church a fearsome Angel, who announced to him: “Your prayer is heard, bishop; Emperor Theophilos has received forgiveness. From now on, do not trouble God about him any longer.”

Then the patriarch, wishing to know whether what he had seen was true, decided to look at the scroll which he had placed beneath the holy altar. And taking it, he saw that by the judgments of God the name of Theophilos had been completely erased, while the names of all the other emperors remained. He told this to the empress. And the empress rejoiced exceedingly, having received this news.

On that same day many hermits came, saying that Theophilos had been forgiven. And to the patriarch himself, when he was serving on Saturday in the church and praying for Theophilos, a voice from above was heard by all: “For the sake of the prayers of My hierarchs I have granted forgiveness to Theophilos. But hereafter do not dare to pray for such, for I will not listen to you.”

Empress Theodora rejoiced, having received tidings about her husband, and sent to the patriarch and all the people, ordering a council to be convened, appointing it for the First Sunday of Great Lent, so that all might gather with crosses and venerable icons in the Great Church for the restoration to her of the beauty of the sacred images, and so that the new miracle concerning Emperor Theophilos might be proclaimed to all, and so that all who had gathered with candles, and the empress with her son Michael, might pray for the establishment of Orthodoxy.
