Miracle 2: Concerning the Gold That Was Lacking

This happened on the right side of the church, where now the precious icon of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ is set, when the builders had finished their work on the upper part of the wall and had set up the upper columns, and had begun to build the passages and had covered them all around; and the emperor was grieved because of the shortage of gold, since the construction was not yet completed. And it happened on the Sabbath day, when the emperor was standing high on the building and they wished to raise the top, at the fourth hour of the day, a eunuch in white garments appeared to the emperor and said to him, “Why are you grieved, lord?” And the emperor said, “Because of the construction, for I cannot finish the building of the church.” And the eunuch said, “Tomorrow, without delay, command your nobles to come to me, and I will give you as much gold as you wish.”

The next day the eunuch came and said to the emperor, “Whom do you command to receive the gold?”

Then the emperor sent with him Strategius the Magister and Basilides the steward and Theodosius the patrician, also called Kolokynthos, that is, the Pumpkin, and with them fifty other servants with twenty mules. The eunuch took them and went away from the Golden Gate. And when they came to the place called the Tribunal, there appeared newly built chambers. And they went with the eunuch into the chambers, and the eunuch opened one chamber for them, and it was full of gold, so that the doors could scarcely be opened; and each of them filled a sack.

And those sent by Justinian received the gold and brought it to the emperor. The eunuch, however, remained in the chambers. The emperor received the gold and counted it, and there were 180 centenaria of gold. And Justinian was amazed and said to them, “In what place was this?” And they told him what had happened. And the emperor went with those who had brought the gold to the place called the Tribunal, and found there not a single chamber or house. And the emperor understood that it was an angel of God, and behold, His gift; and Justinian glorified God.
