Gratian left the western regions to himself and his brother, and commanded Theodosius to care for the eastern ones. The pious and valiant Gratian governed many things. Maximus the tyrant came from Britain against Gratian, for he was a brave duke. In Lugdunum Gratian was killed, betrayed by his own men rather than captured by the strength of his enemies. Valentinian, Gratian's brother, was in Italy; because of his brother's murder he was frightened and feared to fight Maximus. And Maximus offered him peace, which Valentinian himself also wanted, and so he accepted it with joy.

Justina then, Valentinian's mother, had been poisoned with the Arian heresy, to which she had inclined even during her husband's lifetime. By her eloquence she seduced her son, and joyfully revealed her heresy to him, and urged Valentinian to expel Ambrose of Milan by military force, since he opposed Arianism
