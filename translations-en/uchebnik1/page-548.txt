Some urge their husbands to give alms. Others forbid their husbands to give alms, though this is beneficial to all who grieve in their life. A man has one soul, and the time for life is granted to him but once. Do not hope, then, to be saved by the gifts of others. Knowing this, let us apply all diligence to cleanse ourselves from our sins in these holy days of Great Lent, glorifying the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit and His holy confessors, now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.

On the Slander against Patriarch Methodius. The heretics who secretly confessed iconoclasm, seeing that Annius had been removed from the patriarchal throne and Methodius raised to it, bribed a certain harlot with gifts and sent her to Patriarch Methodius, and they accused him of adultery.

Then, wishing to end the scandal, he exposed his private parts before everyone. And all saw them completely withered. He told them: “Once, when I was in Rome, the demon of fornication troubled me. And I began to pray to the holy apostle Peter, that he deliver me from such torment. He, appearing to me at night, touched my loins with his right hand and said: ‘You shall have this no more.’ When I awoke, I could scarcely move; from that time my flesh has remained deadened until now, as you see.” And all glorified God.

While Michael was small, his virtuous mother, Empress Theodora, cared for him, governing the heavily burdened ship of the kingdom wisely, like a skilled helmsman.

When Michael reached youthful age, he became immoderately addicted to sensuality, drunkenness, and fornication, becoming like Nero. He squandered the entire treasury on the actors, buffoons, chariot drivers, and other impious men surrounding him, greedy for exquisite food and drinking already in the morning; spending all his days with harlots, amusing himself and committing adultery, like the formerly reigning Sardanapalus, the last successor of Ninus, Michael also drank and sang indecent songs. And he ended his vile life like Actaeon, torn apart by his own dogs, which attacked him as a beast.

Into what pits of passions did the actors surrounding Michael, with shaved beards and whitened faces, cast him! It reached the point that he himself rode in a gold-forged chariot drawn by four horses.

Meanwhile the royal treasury was being depleted; and all the rabble surrounding the king grew rich. Thus unworthily and dissolutely Michael spent his life, often appearing alone, without bodyguards or armor-bearers, at the city hippodrome, shamelessly amusing himself with spectacles and rejoicing in horse races. He was not even ashamed to take into his arms the children of the charioteers participating in the races, like a child-loving father.
