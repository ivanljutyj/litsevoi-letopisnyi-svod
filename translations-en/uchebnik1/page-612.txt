Dear readers!

This small volume includes texts from several volumes of the Illustrated Chronicle of Ivan the Terrible. We hope that this compact version of the narrative, rendered solely in modern language, will ease and speed up the accomplishment of the most important task for every person—self-education.

A low bow to the enthusiasts by whose efforts this History Textbook came into being: Nikolai Petrovich Pokarev, Anatoly Borisovich Yanovsky, and Konstantin Anatolyevich Babkin.

I wish you and your children fascinating and useful reading.

Glory to Jesus Christ!

Chairman of the Board of the Society
Trian Sperlshov
