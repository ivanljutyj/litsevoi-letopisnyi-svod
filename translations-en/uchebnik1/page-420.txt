The tsar strove with all his heart toward God, and at midday he did not sleep, but had great care and diligence for the holy church, so that he came many times and watched how the stone was cut and the woodworking. And the tsar saw all who worked with Troilus the bedchamber attendant—there were three hundred of them—and he took great care for them, and ordered that everything necessary be given to them, and besides their wages he thanked them and rewarded them. Every Sunday he gave them once or twice a gold piece, that is, a gold coin, or more. The tsar clothed himself in fine white garments, and on his head he had a dark cloth, that is, a veil, and in his hand he had a staff.

Justinian raised the eastern, western, northern, and southern galleries and covered them, as he did the other galleries. He raised a wall fifteen fathoms high. And no silver pieces were brought from the tsar's chambers on the Sabbath day at the third hour. And the chief ordered all the workmen and craftsmen to go to the tsar for the midday meal. The aforementioned Ignatius, chief builder over all, also went, leaving his son Isaiah, fifteen years old, on the wall to guard the building tools, there where they were building, on the right side.

The First Miracle: On the Appearance of the Angel of the Lord. When the youth Isaiah was sitting on the wall, there appeared to him a young eunuch, clothed in a bright garment, beautiful in appearance, and as if sent from the tsar's chambers, that youth said to Isaiah: "Why do the builders not finish the work of God, but, leaving the work, have gone to dine with the tsar?" Isaiah said to him: "My lords will come soon." The eunuch said: "No, but rather go and tell them that I urge them to complete the work. Let them not delay there." The youth answered: "I cannot leave the tools, lest something of them disappear." The eunuch said to him: "Go quickly and tell them to come quickly; and I swear to you, in the name of Holy Sophia, the Wisdom of God the Word, in whose name this church is being built, that I too will be a guard appointed to this church, and I will not depart from here until you return here, for I am commanded to remain here and guard the glory of God." Hearing this, the youth went quickly to his father and to the other workmen, leaving the angel of the Lord there guarding the construction.

The youth Isaiah told his father and all the others what had happened. His father arose and led him to Tsar Justinian. The tsar questioned the youth and summoned all his eunuchs and youths, and showed them all to Isaiah one by one, saying: "Is it not this one? Perhaps this one?" The youth said: "None of these eunuchs is like that eunuch who was in white robes, and from whose countenance fire seemed to come forth."
