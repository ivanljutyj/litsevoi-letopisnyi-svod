Then Pilate sat on the tribunal in the great hippodrome and summoned the people to himself as if to answer them, while he himself ordered armed soldiers suddenly to surround the Jews. The Jews looked in great fear at the army that had unexpectedly surrounded them in three ranks. And Pilate, threatening them, said: “I will cut you all down if you do not accept the emperor’s image,” and he ordered the soldiers to draw their swords. But the Jews all with one accord fell to the ground, stretched out their necks, and cried aloud: “We are ready for slaughter! Better that we be slaughtered like sheep than that we depart from the Law.” And Pilate marveled at how they feared God and kept the Law pure, and he ordered the standard to be carried out of Jerusalem.

Concerning Jesus Christ

Then there appeared a certain Man, if indeed He can be called a man. His nature and appearance were human, but His deeds were divine. He performed wondrous miracles, because of which I cannot call Him a man. But looking at His visible nature, I would not call Him an angel either. And everything He did, He did by some invisible power, by word and command. Some said of Him that the one who first gave us the Law had risen from the dead. And He showed many healings and much wisdom. Others thought that God had sent Him. But He did many things that contradicted the Law, and did not observe the Sabbath according to the ancestral custom; yet He did nothing unclean on the Sabbath, nor did He labor, but accomplished everything by word alone. And many people followed Him and attended to His teaching. And many souls sought some great deed, thinking that by this the Jewish people would be freed from Roman dominion.

He usually stayed outside the city on the Mount of Olives. There He bestowed healings on people. And there gathered to Him one hundred fifty servants and many people. And they saw His power, that He did whatever He wished by a word, and they said to Him that He should go into the city and kill the Roman soldiers and Pilate, and reign over them. But He did not listen to them.

Then the Jewish rulers learned about Him. And the chief priests assembled and said: “We are weak and feeble to resist the Romans. The bow is already drawn against us. Let us go and inform Pilate of what we have heard, and we will know no trouble. But if he learns it from others, he will deprive us of our property and cut off our heads, and exile awaits our children.”

And the chief priests went and informed Pilate, and he sent his soldiers, and killed many of the people, and brought this Wonderworker, and questioned Him, and understood that He was a righteous man, not a villain and rebel, and did not desire royal power, and released Him, for He had healed Pilate’s dying wife.
