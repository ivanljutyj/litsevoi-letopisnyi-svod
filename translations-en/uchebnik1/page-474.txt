There stands a tree, from which comes healing of diseases, in the place where, as they say, Joseph, when he took Christ with the Most Holy Mother of God Mary and made his journey into Egypt, bowed down to the ground, and the tree bowed down. In the same manner that tree remains bowed even to this day. In the northern borders of that land there is a root of fiery appearance, called vatgarit, from which light shines toward evening; no man can dig it up, for this root will soon kill the digger if he but touches it with his hand. If one digs around that root and ties it to a dog, the dog will drag out that root, following after the departing man, and the dog will die at once. When the demon-possessed touch that root, they are released from the demon, for the demon is driven out by that root. There is another city of Galilee, two stades distant from the sea, having rivers and islands. In it there is a place measuring one hundred cubits from edge to edge, in which there occurs a natural wonder worthy of marvel: when the winds sweep the sand lying outside the river into the river, at once in that place that sand is turned into glass. Many people resort there and draw out that glassy sand, and again the wind, blowing, fills that place. Moreover, that land produces opobalsam, that is, the juice of the balsam tree, in great abundance, and it produces many other things needful for people. And it is hard to depart from there, for that land is more excellent than all. Truly God gave it to His beloved Israel for a satisfying portion. “God brought them into the mountain of His sanctuary, into this mountain which Thy right hand hath acquired.” Blessing it, in the holy good promised land, in many and various ways from of old God the Word appeared to the prophets upon the throne borne up by the cherubim, and spoke, and moreover wrought divine manifestation in various ways and in a manner befitting God, as He also said of this: “I have multiplied visions, and I have been vouchsafed to the hands of the prophets.” Afterwards the Lord also appeared in that holy land in the flesh and lived with men, fulfilling all things according to divine prophecy, as He also says: “Mother Zion, a man is flourishing, and a man is born in her, and the Most High founded her.” In that same land the Lord also endured the saving Passion, having wrought salvation for the whole world in its midst, so that certain witnesses called it the navel of the earth. And Christ Himself called it the heart, saying: “As Jonah was in the whale’s belly three days and three nights.”
