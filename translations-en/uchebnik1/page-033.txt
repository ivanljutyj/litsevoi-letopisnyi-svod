And the magi went, led by the star which they had seen in the East, and found Jesus and His Mother in the city of Bethlehem, and fell to the ground and worshiped Christ the Savior, and said among themselves: The star, which we honor as a god, shows a God greater than itself. And they brought Him gifts as to God, and having received a warning from an angel in a dream not to return to Herod, they went by another way into the land of Persia, and did not obey Herod.

When the magi had departed, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph, saying: Take the Child and His Mother and flee to Egypt, and be there until I tell you, for Herod seeks the Child to destroy Him.

He rose, took the Child and His Mother, and departed by night to Egypt, and was there until the death of Herod. Joseph entered the sanctuary of their god Avdul, and immediately all the idols fell. The priests saw and trembled, and fell down before the Savior, so that the church would not fall upon them. And they wished to paint an icon of His likeness. The icon painter undertook to paint it, but could not finish the image of Christ. And all began to entreat the Savior to command that this be done, so that His icon might be finished. Christ inclined toward it, and the icon itself was at once finished. The Egyptians keep this icon to this day. Many powerful kings attempted to take it or to make a copy of it, but could not.

And after these things, when Herod had been mocked, he was enraged, and questioned the Jewish chief priests: Where is Christ to be born? And he learned, and sending soldiers, he slew all the infants in Bethlehem, the city of Judea, from two years old and under, according to the time. And they sought to kill John.

Herod also sent soldiers to Zechariah, John’s father, in the sanctuary, with the words: Where is your son John? And Zechariah said: I do not know. Am I my son’s keeper? And then the soldiers killed him between the church and the altar. His blood flowed out and at once became like stone. There are two reasons for his murder: some say because he hid John from Herod; others likewise say that he, a saint, died for the Virgin Mother of God. For where maidens were not permitted to enter, Zechariah led her in again, and he was condemned to death because he had openly transgressed the law of the scribes, and because he told everyone of her that she was a virgin, as she had been a virgin before the Nativity.

Elizabeth was in Bethlehem, and took John, and went into the wilderness, and hid with her child for forty days in a certain cave, and then went back up alone. John was brought up there, and so he remained until his manifestation to the people of Israel in the wilderness.

Joseph went to Egypt and spent five years there with his sons and daughters, and with the Mother of God, and with Christ, and again had a revelation, and returned, and came to Nazareth of Galilee.
