“If this is now suitable, then know that in the morning I will accuse you and tell the tsar everything, and then you will perish.” And he gave this message to Papias with the words: “Deliver this to such-and-such and say: ‘Come this night in the garments of clerics, hiding small swords under them.’”

When the time of the service came, the court singers came to sing the canon of the Nativity of Christ. Papias opened the doors to them, and with them entered Michael’s accomplices, dressed in clerical garments, and hid in the corners of the church.

The imperial singers began to sing and had already sung half the service when the emperor came to matins and also began to sing. His voice was strong and loud, but it sounded harsh and unmelodious. The emperor always imagined that his singing was pleasing to the hearers, and he usually began to sing first. And now, as soon as he entered the church, he began loudly to sing the troparion “They Reviled the King of All with Love.”

And at once the conspirators suddenly surrounded him from all sides and drew their swords. Seeing this, the emperor hid in the altar, and they, bursting in after him, stabbed him to death.

They cut off his head and hands and threw his body for all to see. Thus ending his vile and impious life, he also destroyed his worthless, accursed soul. Leo was killed by a sharp sword, receiving the bitter recompense for the deeds of his abominable and absurd life. Condemned to death by the conspirators who dared to commit this evil in the holy church, in his life the thrice-accursed hater of God, who had done so much evil and drunk so much Christian blood, himself received a wicked death at the hands of murderers.

After this his vile body was wrapped in plain, coarse cloth, placed in a prepared boat, and carried to the so-called First Island, where it was buried. But the bold men who did this did not know that they had taken such a sin upon themselves only for condemnation. “He stretched out and held firmly, as the word says, these abominable and evil-named men, truly inflaming those who were in them, legislating wretchedly how they were to be brought out, and in other episcopates, if indeed anywhere it was preserved and known, while some knew it nowhere. Together the eastern and western ends, following the Gospel and apostolic teaching, accomplish and preach, so that wherever you, Christians of another region, have that unusual and lawless thing, to increase Christian piety, the shameful command is attained—as much as was not taken into memory by evil and vile assemblies, but only by this slave girl, captive, and playing with divine power. In him also the sinful coming upon the earth for those living earthly, crafty, and proud, the mind helps, where the knee is powerful and beloved to itself, the word love—”
