He turned to God for help, and stood straight before the troops, and prayed: “O God Almighty, look upon me. Thou knowest that for the name of Christ, Thy Son, and for vengeance for the righteous, I have undertaken this war. If I lie, punish me; but if I have come here in righteousness, trusting in Thee, stretch forth Thy right hand to Thy servants, lest the nations ever say: ‘Where is their God?’” And the commanders of Theodosius were assured of God’s help to them.

There was also a distinguished man named Bacurius, renowned for faith and piety, for virtues of the soul, and distinguished in body; and when all around him took up arms against him, he wounded one with a spear, another with arrows, and cut down with a sword, and those struck by him fell. This Bacurius broke up the close-formed detachments of the enemy, and amid the multitude of the fallen he broke through the defense, and passed over the bodies of the dead, and advanced toward the tormentor himself.

And there was a very strong wind when Eugenius was brought to the king’s feet with his hands bound behind his back, and in that place both his life and the war came to an end.

At that time, caring for the future, Theodosius immediately sent to the eastern lands, where he kept his children securely while he himself went to war.

In the East he commanded Emperor Arcadius to guard the kingdom previously given to him and to prevail over Rufinus.

He honored Honorius with the same rank and commanded him to come quickly to the Western kingdom. When Honorius came to his father, Theodosius received him, kissed him, and handed over to him the scepter of the Western kingdom.

Theodosius himself maintained his reign for 17 years, and was a righteous king, and received honor in old age, and departed from this life, having governed the kingdom well.

The Eighth Reign: Arcadius, Son of Theodosius, Who Reigned in Constantinople

After Theodosius, his son Arcadius reigned for 30 years. He erected a column on Xerolophus, that is, on the Dry Hill, and on that column he set up his own statue.

And he founded a city in Thrace and named it the City of Arcadius. In the time of Arcadius there was a great earthquake throughout all the earth, during which the earth was violently shaken for 7 days.

On the Installation of John Chrysostom as Patriarch

When Archbishop Nectarius departed this life, by common consent Arcadius brought John Chrysostom from the city of Antioch to install him as bishop in Constantinople.
