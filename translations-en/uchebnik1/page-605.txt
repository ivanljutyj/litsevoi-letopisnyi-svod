Foreseeing his own departure, he appointed his disciple Peter as their abbot and departed to the Lord. And they laid him in his monastery. When much time had passed, some began to oppose Abbot Peter, inclining toward their ancient customs and introducing dissensions and heresies.

Saint Hilarion appeared to them in a dream and wounded many of them with a staff, while others he put to death. In the morning those who had been wounded suffered, and those whom he had put to death were found dead, so that others might not take from them the deadly poison. The remaining monks, terrified with fear, remained in obedience, keeping the saint’s rules.

By royal and episcopal judgment, after much time had passed, his venerable relics were taken from the earth and placed in the church. And they saw, as it were, two springs of fragrant myrrh flowing from his eyes.

Then, when the Greek realm declined, the Bulgarians had a king, Kaloyan, who took under his rule many Greek cities, Thrace, Macedonia, Triballia, and Dalmatia; and hearing of the miracles of Saint Hilarion, he transferred his venerable relics to his capital city, Tarnovo, where they work many miracles to the glory of God.

In the year 6623 from Adam (1123 from Christ)

The stone church in Vyshgorod was consecrated on the first day of May, a Saturday. On the second day of the same month all the brothers, the Russian princes, transferred the relics of the holy passion-bearers Boris and Gleb into the stone church; and Vladimir Monomakh ordered that rich silks, precious fabrics, soft leather, and squirrel skins be thrown to the people who were pressing hard, so that they might easily reach the church; and on the fourth day they placed them in a stone coffin in their place.

In the same year Prince Oleg Svyatoslavich died, on the eighteenth day of August. In the same year Voigost laid the foundation of the church of Saint Theodore Tiro between two streets in Novgorod.

In the same year Vladimir Monomakh built a bridge across the Dnieper. In the same year Vladimir went to the city of Minsk.

On the eighteenth day of January his sons took the city of Drutsk, while he himself stood by the city of Minsk.
