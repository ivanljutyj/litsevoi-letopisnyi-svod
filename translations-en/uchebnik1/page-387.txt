The Eleventh Reign, of Leo the Great, who reigned in Constantine’s City

After Marcian, Leo the Great reigned 18 years and 8 months. The boyars installed him. He was handsome. During his reign Anthemius reigned in Rome, installed by Marcian.

On the Punishment

In the reign of Leo the Great, Syrian Antioch received punishment. This was the fourth punishment. In the month of Gorpiaeus, that is September, on the 20th day, there was a light at night on the Lord’s Day. The year, according to the Antiochene reckoning, was the five hundred and sixth, in the consulship of Patricius. And Patricius gave the Antiochenes much gold for many needs.

In the reign of this Emperor Leo a sign appeared in the sky—a trumpet-shaped cloud stood for 40 days. When a certain icon-painter dared to paint Christ in the likeness of Zeus, his hand immediately withered, and Gennadius the patriarch healed him. During the reign of this Leo there were Matrona of Perge in Pamphylia, and Daniel the Stylite from the Syrian Mesopotamia, and the monks Basil and Procopius.

In the reign of Leo the Great, Isocasius the quaestor was accused as a pagan; he was a native of Aegae in Cilicia. He was a ruler in Antioch the Great and long remained in his office with glory, for he was very wise. And Isocasius was taken by the emperor’s command, when he was in Constantinople, and the belt of his office was removed from him, and Isocasius was sent to Chalcedon, a city on the far side of the Bosporus, opposite Constantine’s City, to Theophilus, prince of Bithynia, who was also ordered to interrogate Isocasius.
