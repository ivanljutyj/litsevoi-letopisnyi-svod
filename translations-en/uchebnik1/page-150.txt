There will be no salvation for you unless you find helpers. For Ananus, having taken counsel with the people, sent envoys to Vespasian so that he might come quickly and occupy the whole city. To the people Ananus announced a purification, so that in the morning they might enter the Temple ostensibly for worship and the offering of sacrifice, and attack you suddenly. And I was sent to negotiate peace with you, so as to treacherously seize you unarmed. You must take thought for the salvation of your lives: either make peace with Ananus or send for help. But I tell you that if the hope of forgiveness warms you, you have no understanding, for people will not forget what lawless deeds you committed against them. With this lie John intimidated the zealots, and he spoke indistinctly to them about help from outside, but his words pointed to Idumea.

And John added to this that Ananus was gnashing his teeth, wishing to tear apart the leaders of the zealots. Having heard all this, Eleazar son of Simon and Zechariah son of Phalek, both priests by birth, consulted with the other zealots about what should be done, for a difficult time had come upon them, since they themselves were shut in and could not send for help from outside. Nevertheless they decided to send to the Idumeans.

And they wrote a letter quickly and sent it to the Idumeans with these words: "Ananus is deceiving the people and handing the capital over to the Romans. But we have separated from him for the sake of freedom and are now shut in and sit inside the Temple. And guards stand around us. Little time remains for us to be saved. If you do not help us soon, Ananus and our enemies will overcome us, and the city will be surrendered to the Romans." And the zealots gave this letter to two men, loyal, resourceful, and swift-footed. The zealots knew that the Idumeans were a violent people, prone to rebellions and revolutions, and that once a word was said to them, they would immediately take up arms and come.

And when the message from the zealots was brought to the Idumeans, the Idumeans were thrown into confusion and sent men everywhere with calls to war, and they gathered as one, twenty thousand men, and went to Jerusalem.

But Ananus did not notice how the envoys from the zealots had gone out, nor the guards. But the arrival of the Idumeans did not escape their eyes, and they managed to close the city gates and place guards on the walls. Jesus the high priest, who was second after Ananus, stood on the tower and said: "I am amazed how fortune helps the wicked. For you have come to help impious people. I did not know that you were of the same mind, since you help the zealots. If anyone honestly examines their life, he will discover that each of them deserves a thousand deaths. For they are cursed by the whole land, and have defiled themselves in everything, have squandered their property, and have tested their frenzy and greed on the surrounding cities.
