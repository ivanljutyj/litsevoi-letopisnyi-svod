Herod, at their request, sent to John and found him reproving him even more.

And there was a feast, and when they had drunk, the daughter of that Herodias came and danced and pleased Herod, and Herod swore to her to give whatever she asked, even to half the kingdom. She, at her mother's instruction, asked for the head of John the Baptist.

And the king sent an executioner, and he beheaded him in prison and brought his head on a platter, and the king took it and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.

His disciples came and took his body and buried it. Saint John was then 31 years and 2 months old; he suffered on the 29th day of August.

This Herod the king, the second brother of Philip, went out from the city of Sebaste to Pania, a Jewish city. And a certain rich woman came to him; she wanted, since she had been healed by Christ, to set up His image, and she did not dare do that without the king's command. And that woman asked Herod to permit a bronze image of the Savior Christ to be set up in that city. She set forth her request thus: To the honorable Herod, ruler and establisher of laws for the Jews and Greeks of the Trachonite country, a petition and request from Veronica, a noblewoman of the city of Paniada.
