That Kirei came to the tsar and began to speak many false words and slanders against the Grand Prince on the king’s behalf, and he brought many gifts to him, and also to his princes, to Temir and to the others, from the king, and made obeisance and said that “the free tsar should show favor and go against the Prince of Moscow with all his Horde, and I from here with all my land, because my land suffers many hardships from him.” And Prince Temir and the others who stood for the king stirred the tsar against the Grand Prince. But the thought of the accursed did not come to pass, for man cannot hinder the counsel of God.

The tsar kept Kirei with him for the whole year, having nothing with which to send him back to the king.

On the Signs in Veliky Novgorod. In this year and in the past year there were many signs in Veliky Novgorod, of which we heard from the local inhabitants. They say that at one time a great storm came and knocked the cross from Holy Sophia; and again: blood appeared on two coffins; and after that also: at the Holy Savior on the Khutyn, the Chersonese bells rang by themselves. And yet another sign, terrible and worthy of wonder: in the convent of the holy Great Martyr Euphemia, from the icon of the Most Holy Theotokos abundant tears flowed like streams from its eyes. Many God-fearing people of Novgorod who saw this wept and prayed to God; but others, having hearts of stone and not fearing God, regarded it as nothing and fell into mockery, not knowing their destruction and the wrath of God coming upon them. Such signs never portend good, but only famine, or pestilence, or bloodshed, or captivity, as also in former times, when there were kings of Israel, prophets and others before them, after them, and to this day. And when signs appear on the sun or on the moon, or in the stars, or in any other creature, which are unusual, then all this is written down in the chronographic books called “Krinitsa,” and also in the chronicle, in the days of which tsar or prophet it happened, saying thus: in the years and days of King David, or Solomon, and the prophet Isaiah, or King Hezekiah, such and such happened. They also appear in our times, which our own eyes have seen.

On the Repose of Blessed Jonah, Archbishop of Novgorod; on Vladyka Theophilus and on the Capture of Novgorod

That same autumn, on November 8, on the Synaxis of the Archangel Michael, the blessed Archbishop of Veliky Novgorod, the wonderworker Jonah, departed to God. Together with Metropolitan Jonah the Wonderworker, he had prophesied to Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich that his noble son Grand Prince Ivan would abolish all the free customs of the Novgorod people and bring them under his will, which came to pass. And his body was laid in the monastery of the Otenskaya Hermitage.
