And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus' and his mother, the Grand Lady Elena, consulted with the boyars about this, that for this reason he should release Tsar Shigalei from confinement.

And the Grand Prince sent Prince Nikita Borisovich Turenin to Tsar Shigalei at Beloozero, and with him boyar sons.

On December 12 Tsar Shigalei arrived from Beloozero in Moscow, and the Grand Prince ordered the tsar to appear before him.

And Tsar Shigalei came before the eyes of the great sovereign, fell before the Grand Prince and went down on his knees, and petitioned Grand Prince Ivan for his offense. And the tsar said: "Your father, my lord Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus', took me, a little child, and favored me as a father favors a son, and granted me, set me as tsar in Kazan, and favored me with his great favor; and through my sins there arose in Kazan discord among the princes and the Kazan people, and I again came to my sovereign, your father, in Moscow; and your father, my lord, favored me and gave me towns in his land; and I through my sins offended against my sovereign and fell before him into a great offense by my proud mind and cunning thought; and God did not permit my crime and cunning thought, and your father, my lord, punished me for my crime and laid his disgrace upon me, humbling me; and now you, my lord, remembering your father's great favor to me, have shown mercy to me, and God put it into your heart that you, my lord, have favored me not according to my sins."

And the Grand Prince ordered the tsar to rise, and the great sovereign favored him, called the tsar to dine with him, and ordered him to sit on the right side of him on another bench.

And the Grand Prince favored Tsar Shigalei, gave him a fur coat, and sent him to his quarters. And Tsar Shigalei petitioned the Grand Prince that the great sovereign would order him to appear before his mother, Grand Princess Elena, and petition the Grand Lady.

And the pious tsaritsa, the Grand Lady Elena, consulted about this with the boyars, that it was fitting for the tsar to appear before her, because the Grand Prince was still young, and the presentation of the royal scepter of the great Russian power had all been laid by God upon her and entrusted to her by God for the preservation and keeping of all Orthodox piety, for mercy to the good and for vengeance on the evil.

And on the 9th day of January, the Grand Prince ordered Tsar Shigalei to appear before him and before his mother, the Grand Princess.
