And the commanders came out with all their men, by God’s grace, healthy, and took their places.

And to Ivangorod the sovereign sent his clerk Shestak Voronin and ordered him to fire from all the guns on Rugodiv.

On the Sending of a Petition to the Sovereign from the Master of Livonia

In the same month the Master, the Archbishop, the Bishop, and the whole land sent the German Stepan Lystarev to petition the tsar and sovereign grand prince for a safe-conduct for ambassadors, and that the sovereign show them favor, order his army to be restrained and give them peace, so that in all things they might make amends before the sovereign.

And the tsar and grand prince wrote back to the Master and the whole land that he had already sent a safe-conduct earlier, and that they themselves should come in accordance with that safe-conduct and send ambassadors to petition for their offenses; the road would be safe both for arrival and departure; and for that time the sovereign ordered his army to be restrained.

On the Arrival of Roman Olferev from Lithuania

In the month of April there arrived from Lithuania, from the king, the envoy of the tsar and grand prince, Roman Vasilyev, son of Olferev. The tsar and grand prince had sent him to learn how the Crimean tsarevich had conquered Lithuania, and so that the king might consider for himself: for many years Christian blood has been shed by heathen hands, and the Crimean tsar takes tribute from the king; what profit is there in Christians perishing at heathen hands? And the tsar and grand prince for many years, through all ambassadors and envoys, reminds him that he should wish to rise together with him for Christian freedom. And the king wrote back to the tsar and grand prince that he desires peace for Christendom, and that the sending from the tsar and grand prince is pleasing to him, and that he would soon send his envoys, and with them would send word in full detail as to how they should accomplish that matter. And Roman said: the king’s marshal Ostap Volovich ordered him to meet with him in the Prechistaya Church, and in other churches the Vilnius voivode and the same Ostap, and they spoke of many state matters, that the king wishes them well, desires to conclude an eternal peace and stand together against the Crimean tsar, and they questioned Roman about everything, how the tsar and grand prince now stood with the Crimean tsar; and Roman told them that the Crimean tsar had released our sovereign’s ambassador and his own envoy
