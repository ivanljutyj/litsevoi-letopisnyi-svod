It has come to our hearing that your former ill-wishers do not cease whispering flattering words into your ear and advise you not to resist the adversaries, but to retreat and deliver to the wolves the rational flock of Christ’s sheep. Attend to yourself and to all the flock over which the Holy Spirit has set you: God-loving, all-sovereign ruler, I beseech your majesty, do not heed such advice, but heed the words of the universal teacher Paul, who said: The wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who hold the truth in unrighteousness; but they became vain in their thoughts, and their foolish heart was darkened; professing themselves to be wise, they became fools; and as they did not see fit to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do things that are not fitting. And again, the Lord Himself says: if your eye offends you, pluck it out; or He commands that hand or foot be cut off. But understand by this not the visible and tangible hand or foot or eye, but your neighbors who advise you to do an unjust deed: cast them away and drive them far from you, that is, cut them off, and do not listen to their advice. And what do these false deceivers, who think themselves Christians, advise you? Nothing other than to cast away your shields and, without any resistance to these wretched barbarians, having betrayed Christianity and your fatherland, to wander as exiles through foreign lands. Consider, O great sovereign, from what glory to what dishonor they would bring your majesty! And when such a multitude of people has perished and so many churches of God have been ruined and defiled, who would be so hard-hearted as not to grieve over their destruction? Fear also, O shepherd! Will not God require their blood at your hands, according to the words of the prophets? And where do you hope to flee, and where will you rule, having destroyed the flock entrusted to you by God? Hear what the prophet says: Though you make your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down, says the Lord; and another says: Whither shall I go from Your Spirit, and whither shall I flee from Your face? If I ascend into heaven, You are there; and in the uttermost parts of the sea the hand of God guides me and His right hand holds me. Where then are you departing, good shepherd? To whom are you leaving us, like sheep having no shepherd? But we hope that the Lord will not cast away His people and will not forsake His inheritance. Do not listen, sovereign, to those who wish to turn your honor into dishonor and your glory into disgrace, so that you become an exile and are called a betrayer of Christians. Cast off all fear and be strengthened by the help of the Lord, by His authority and His power; for one shall overcome a thousand and two — ten thousand, according to the prophetic word: For their gods are not as our God.
