On the Capture of Belgrade

In the same year the Turkish Sultan took Belgrade from the Wallachian voivode Stephen.

In the year 6991 from Adam (the year 1491 from Christ).

On the Consecration of Two Side Chapels in the Cathedral of the Archangel Michael

The Church of the Resurrection of Christ, a side chapel of the Cathedral of the Archangel Michael, was consecrated on October 13.

On the 27th day of the same month the church of the holy apostle Aquila was consecrated.

On the Death of the Grand Prince of Ryazan Vasily Ivanovich and the Reign of His Son

In that same winter, on January 6, during the liturgy, the Grand Prince of Ryazan Vasily Ivanovich reposed; and his son Prince Ivan succeeded to the grand principality.

The Marriage of the Young Grand Prince

In that same winter, on January 12, the Grand Prince of All Rus Ivan Ivanovich, son of the Grand Prince of All Rus Ivan Vasilyevich, married and took as his wife Princess Elena, daughter of the Wallachian voivode Stephen.
