And the boyar Prince Vasily Semyonovich, earnestly asking God’s mercy for this, and by the tsar’s command took care of it; they dug day and night, and in ten days dug under the bridge where they go for water; and Prince Vasily himself with his companions understood, when he heard Tatar voices, that they were already walking over them to get water, and informed the sovereign of this.

Year 7061 from Adam (the year 1561 from Christ)

In September, on the 4th day, the sovereign ordered boyar and commander Prince Vasily Semyonovich and Alexei to place 11 barrels of gunpowder under the secret passage.

And on Sunday, at the liturgy, when the deacon was reading the Holy Gospel and when at the end he proclaimed: “And there shall be one flock and one shepherd,” at the same time as those words the secret passage was blown up with the Kazan people who went for water, and the city wall sagged and collapsed, and many Kazan people in the city were killed by stones and logs that fell from the great height when the gunpowder blew them up. And the people in the city were numb with fear.

And the great host with cross-bearing banners, divinely inspired, boldly, as if borne on clouds, swiftly galloped to the city walls and into the city itself, and roaring like lions, fiercely killed the godless Tatars and captured countless alive.

And they made much discord in the city: some wanted to submit to our sovereign; but others, the traitors, began to dig for water and did not find it, but dug out only a small stinking stream, and until the capture they took water there with difficulty; from that same water they had sickness: they swelled and died from it.

But the pious tsar all days and nights calls God to his aid and rides about the regiments, and also around the city and to all the towers, and orders the commanders to stand firm and with caution guard the sovereign’s cause. He favors and strengthens all, praises their labors, and promises to reward them, and does all this for many days and nights.

And they fired incessantly at the city from cannons. They knocked the Arsk Gates down to the foundation, and knocked down the remnants, and killed many people with upper fire, with fiery and stone cannonballs, firing all night so that the pagans would not sleep.
