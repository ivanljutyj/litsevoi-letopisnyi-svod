On the Sending from Leonty Mansurov

On the 5th day of the same month Leonty Mansurov sent from the Volga, from Perevoloka, service Tatars and his own men, and he wrote the same as Ismail had written: Tsar Derbysh has betrayed us, and he has killed those princes, Begul and his companions, who had agreed to serve the tsar and grand prince faithfully. And they attacked him for three days with all their people, but he sat in a small fortified town by the Volga and fought off the Astrakhan men, and went up the river in boats, and came to the Cossacks with a force of 350 men; and altogether there were 500 men with him, and those have now been killed, while others drowned and perished of hunger along the way.

On the Dispatch of Ismail’s Messenger

In the same month the tsar and grand prince sent Ismail’s messenger Bekchura back to Ismail, and with him he sent 50 Cossacks with arquebuses, headed by Ulanka and his companions; and he wrote to Ismail that he was sending his army along the Volga to Astrakhan, and across the steppe on skis he had sent 500 men to the Volga to Ismail for the Astrakhan affair, with ataman Lyapunk Filimonov and other atamans and their companions.
