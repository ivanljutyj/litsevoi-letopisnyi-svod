RUS (1483–1491, from V.Kh.) Book 16

On the Capture of Kaffa and the Crimea

In the same year the Turkish sultan sent an army in ships and galleys against Kaffa, and, coming, took it in the month of June, and took other cities in the Kaffa Perekop.

In the same year the Turks took Kaffa and killed many Muscovite merchants, and captured others, and others, after plundering them, gave them for ransom.

They besieged Az-Girey’s Horde, the Crimea, and Perekop, so that they would pay tribute, and installed among them Mengli-Girey, the younger son of Az-Girey.

And two of their brothers, also sons of Az-Girey, fled; and a commander came, but the tsar himself was not there.

In the same year the Kazan Tatars killed forty Ustyuzhans on the Kama who were going to Tyumen to trade.

In the year 6984 from Adam (the year 1484 from Christ)

On the 6th day of the month of September, an envoy came to the grand prince from King Casimir, named Bogdan, and Vasko Lyubich.

On the 12th day of the same month, at midnight, a fire broke out in Moscow in the settlement beyond the Neglinnaya, between the churches of St. Nicholas and All Saints; and many households burned, and both those churches burned down. On the same day the Lithuanian envoy left Moscow.

On the 27th day of the same month, at the third hour of the night, Nikifor Basenkov burned with all his household on the Arbat; only all of them escaped with their lives.

On the 30th day of the same month, on Saturday, at the second hour of the day, the sun was eclipsed: a third of it vanished, and it became like a moon with horns; but no one in Moscow saw this, while many saw it in Kolomna and its environs.

On the 2nd day of the month of October, at the fourth hour of the day, a fire broke out in Moscow inside the city near the Timofeev Gates. And the grand prince himself, coming with many people, extinguished it.
