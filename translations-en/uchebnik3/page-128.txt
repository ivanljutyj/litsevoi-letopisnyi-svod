RUS (1468–1483 according to V.Kh.).
Book 15

Concerning Grand Prince Vasily, how he went to Novgorod the Great in peace with his children. In the year 6968 from Adam (the year 1468 from Christ)

The Grand Prince went to Novgorod the Great in peace, and with him were his sons, Prince Yuri and Prince Andrei the Big. The Novgorodians did great honor to the Grand Prince and to his sons.

And from Novgorod the Grand Prince sent his son Prince Yuri to Pskov on Cheese-fare Sunday, because the Germans had offended them.

Prince Yuri arrived, and the Pskovians received him with great honor, and set up a throne in Holy Trinity, and put into his hands the sword of Prince Dovmont, and gave him many gifts.

Prince Yuri then sent his army into the German lands to wage war. The Germans heard that the Grand Prince had sent his son against them with a great force, and they sent their envoys to Prince Yuri and petitioned him, and made peace with the Pskovians on Pskovian terms.

The Grand Prince came from Novgorod to Moscow on the commemoration of the Forty Holy Martyrs of Sebaste; and Prince Yuri came from Pskov on the Friday of the sixth week, on the eve of Lazarus Saturday.

In the same year, on the thirteenth day of June, at the sixth hour of the day, a terrible, menacing, and dark cloud appeared from the west, and a very strong storm began. All the people were in despair and sought where to hide from such terror, because it was very dark, and the whirlwind was terrible, and because of the rising dust nothing could be seen. The people began to pray to the Lord God and the All-Pure Mother to destroy that cloud. But Almighty God, Lover of Mankind, heard the prayer of His servants: in that same hour the cloud passed through the city, and calm and brightness came, as before.

On the next morning, the fourteenth day, there was great calm all day; but in the evening, already at the fifteenth hour, a menacing cloud appeared from the south, covering half the sky, with a terrible storm and strong whirlwind; the lightning was so great that the earth and all the churches seemed to be in flame, and the thunder was terrible and great.
