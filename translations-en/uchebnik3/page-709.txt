The Swedish ambassadors Isaak Nikolaev and his companions were released from Moscow.

The truce with the boyar, voivode, and namestnik of the Livonian land Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov was concluded in the city of Yuryev. And for their sovereign, King Erik, before Mikhail they kissed the cross on the truce charters, on this condition: when Mikhail’s ambassador comes to King Erik, then King Erik, before Mikhail’s ambassador, shall affix his seal to those truce charters, and King Erik shall kiss the cross on those charters that he will keep the truce with the boyar, voivode, and namestnik of the Livonian land until the appointed term and act according to what is written in the truce charters.

In the same year the tsar and grand prince commanded that a seal be made for his patrimony, the Livonian land and the city of Yuryev; and on the seal the device: a double-headed eagle, and at the eagle’s right foot the armorial seal of the Master of Livonia, and at the left foot the armorial seal of the Bishop of Yuryev; around the seal the inscription: “Seal of the boyar of His Tsarist Majesty and of the boyar, namestnik, and voivode of the Livonian land.” And with that seal he commanded the Yuryev boyar and voivode to seal truce charters with the Swedish king and to seal charters to other states as well.

In the same month, August, on the 24th day, at night, in Novgorod on Smolensk Street, by the Church of the Resurrection of Christ, the homestead of Prince Nikita Odoevsky caught fire; and three wooden churches burned: the Church of Cosmas and Damian in Smolensk Lane, the Church of Saint Demetrius, martyr of Christ, on Ilyinskaya Street, the Church of Saint Paraskeva Pyatnitsa in Vvedensky Lane, and 33 homesteads and seven cells.
