To the pious tsar and to our humility, and to all who on earth serve the Lord in His commandments and will receive joy in this life, and also to all the heavenly powers, of whom it is written: “There is great joy in heaven among the angels, and especially to God Himself, over one repentant sinner.” If any of you has forgotten the fear of God and the tsar’s commandment and our spiritual teaching, and does not begin to repent of his sins, but from now on and henceforth begins to shave his beard or trim his beard and moustache, or falls into foul Sodomitic sins with youths, or begins to fall into adultery and fornication with captive women and maidens, and afterwards they are exposed, then all such persons shall be in great disgrace from the pious tsar, and from our humility and from the whole consecrated council they shall be excommunicated according to the sacred canons from the holy church and from every sacred thing. And therefore I wrote to our God-loving assembly for no other reason, but only seeking the benefit of our only-begotten immortal souls according to the Lord’s commandment: and that you, O children beloved in Christ, noble princes and boyars, voivodes and boyars’ sons, and all the Christ-loving army of the pious Tsar Ivan, from now on and henceforth with God’s help would all strive to correct this, as much as your strength can, and henceforth would struggle with God’s help bravely and courageously for the holy churches and for our holy Orthodox faith against the godless Hagarenes, I wrote about everything according to the command of the pious Tsar Ivan. And may the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Theotokos, and the prayer and blessing of the great wonderworkers, and the conciliar blessing of our humility abide with your nobility forever. Amen. Written in Moscow in the 7060th year from Adam (the 1560th year from Christ), on the 21st day of the month of May. And on that day the archpriest was dismissed.

On the Assignment of Voivodes

In the same year, in the month of May, the sovereign confirmed his campaign and ordered the regiments to be assigned: in the large regiment — boyar and voivode Prince Ivan Fedorovich Mstislavsky and servant and voivode Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Vorotynsky; and in the vanguard regiment — boyar and voivode Prince Ivan Ivanovich Pronskoy and Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Khilkov; and in the right-hand regiment — boyar and voivode Prince Pyotr Mikhailovich Shchenyatev and Prince Andrey Mikhailovich Kurbsky; and in the left-hand regiment — voivode Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Mikulinsky and Dmitry Mikhailovich Pleshcheev.

And the sovereign sends his voivodes to Kolomna, Prince Ivan Mstislavsky with his comrades, and ordered them to gather with their men, and the right-hand regiment to Kashira.

And in the guard regiment — boyar and voivode Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany and Semyon Vasilyevich Sheremetev; he ordered them to gather in Murom with men from the Moscow towns.
