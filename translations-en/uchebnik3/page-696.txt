To the rest, to the whole sacred monastic and priestly order, to hieromonks and priests, to archdeacons and protodeacons and deacons, and to the whole priestly and monastic order who serve at the Lord’s altar, and who are within the bounds of the Russian metropolia, and to all the church clergy, I leave peace and blessing and forgiveness, and from them I require the same—to receive blessing and forgiveness.

And to all Orthodox Christians with their wives and children who live within the bounds of the Russian metropolia, I leave peace and blessing and forgiveness, and I myself require the same from them, blessing and forgiveness, in this age and in the age to come.

To the pious tsars, great princes of Russia, and to the most reverend metropolitans of all Rus, and to the God-fearing archbishops and bishops, and to the right-believing princes and boyars, and to all Orthodox Christians who have died in our years within the bounds of the Russian metropolia, to them also I give forgiveness and blessing, and I pray to the Lord God that He forgive them all their sins, because they, like all people, sinned; and I myself ask the same of God and of them—to receive blessing and forgiveness.

If I have forbidden anyone anything, either through inadvertence or for a just cause, and he did not seek absolution from us, and death occurred in that forgetfulness; or if I taught anyone and he disobeyed, all those I declare, in the Holy Spirit, absolved and forgiven and blessed, and I pray to the God who loves mankind to remit their guilt, except those priests whom I will, according to the divine canons, censure and remove from the great priesthood of God for their own understanding and spiritual benefit; and it is not in my power to admit them to the priesthood, but God alone knows all things. If anyone murmured against me all the days of my life, or someone of the episcopal, priestly, and monastic order openly rose against me, and any of the laity joined them, and if any of them acknowledge it and, coming to me, confess, they will immediately receive forgiveness, and they will be forgiven and blessed, and may the Lord God not impute it to them, and may He remit it to them.

If anyone, whether out of shame before me, or having fallen into forgetfulness, or through negligence because of his pride (murmured against me), or fearing me, or from weakness of mind—whoever it may be: priests, monks, or laypeople, male or female—may they be forgiven by the Lord God and blessed, and may it not be imputed to them, because this is mine, and it is in my power to absolve it.

And those who loved us and showed us mercy for God’s sake in our needs—Christ-named people, nobles, men and women—may they also be blessed and shown mercy by the God who loves mankind; may the Lord give them according to their heart, in place of corruptible things, incorruptible.
