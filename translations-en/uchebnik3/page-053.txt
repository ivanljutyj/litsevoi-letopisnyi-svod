Highest in the Lord Jesus Christ majesty, with many wishes: we ask the metropolitan concerning the vindication of Isidore and the aforementioned church property, that you receive him joyfully; and therefore, with wishes and much diligence, we inform you about him, so that in all matters which you see from him concerning church dues you may be a diligent helper to him with all your strength; then praise and glory will be rendered to you by people, and from us a blessing, and from God you will receive an eternal gift. Given at Florence in the ninth year of our priesthood.
