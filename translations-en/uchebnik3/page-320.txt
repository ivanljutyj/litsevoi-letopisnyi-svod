Concerning Krichev and Dubrovna

In the same month, on the 13th day, townspeople and common folk came to the grand prince from Krichev and Dubrovna, asking that the grand prince show them favor and bid them serve him, and that the towns of Krichev and Dubrovna be under the sovereign. And the grand prince showed favor, took them under his name, and ordered them to be brought to the kissing of the cross.

Concerning Glinsky

Then the grand prince sent his servant Prince Mikhail Glinsky with many people to guard his patrimony, the city of Smolensk and other cities, from his enemy Sigismund, King of Poland.

And in Borisov and in Minsk and on the Drutsk fields stood the grand prince’s governors: boyar and governor Prince Mikhailo Ivanovich Bulgakov, and his brother Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Bulgakov, and his other governors with men.

But Prince Mikhail Glinsky, forgetting God and his word, broke his cross-kissing oath, betrayed the sovereign grand prince, and began to correspond with the king and with all the Polish and Lithuanian lords, and incited them against the grand prince’s people, while he himself wished to flee to the king.

The grand prince’s governors and Prince Mikhail Bulgakov with his companions, having learned of Glinsky’s treason—that he was corresponding with the king and his lords and inciting them against the grand prince’s people, while he himself wished to flee to the king—seized him and sent him to the grand prince. And the grand prince, having had Glinsky bound in chains, sent him to Moscow and ordered him imprisoned.

And after sending away the traitor Glinsky, in order to thwart his designs, he sent to the Drutsk fields his boyars Grigory Fedorovich and his equerry and boyar Ivan Andreevich, and his other governors with men, so that they might join Prince Mikhail for reinforced protection. And he ordered them to assemble with the governors, with boyar and governor Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Bulgakov and with the other governors, and to stand on the Dnieper, and to join with those men who had been sent to Minsk and to Borisov and to the Drutsk fields. And when they should gather with all the men, the grand prince ordered Prince Mikhail Bulgakov and all the governors to follow after him. And he himself began to establish order in his patrimony, the city of Smolensk, as ought to be in his state.
