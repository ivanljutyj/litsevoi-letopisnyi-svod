This Tsaritsa Irene and her son Constantine later lived unrighteously and fell away from the kingdom not of their own will.

After them Nikephoros Genikos received the kingdom. He died, and after him his son Stavrikios remained tsar for one year and two months. And he lay in his chamber because of a disease of the thigh, and then Michael Rangabe, Nikephoros’s son-in-law, was named tsar by all, while his brother-in-law Tsar Stavrikios, son of Nikephoros, did not know of it. Hearing of this, Stavrikios wept, but received help from nowhere and took monastic vows together with his wife Theophania. All of them, from the Seventh Council to this day, venerate the holy icons.

The beast-named Leo the Armenian rose against Tsar Michael Rangabe, wishing to seize his kingdom; but Tsar Michael Rangabe yielded to him his royal throne, which many desired to obtain, and clothed himself in the monastic habit.
