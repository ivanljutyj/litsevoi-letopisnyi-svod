And Prince Mikhail with his companions came to the sea to the small town of Khtorchbor, in Russian White Town, and they took Belgorod on the Sea and killed people; and Prince Mikhail with his companions besieged the town, while he himself went to Rakobor.

Prince Pyotr Shuysky wrote that he had conquered the Kes places and captured German commanders: Indrik, Kashpir, and other Germans.

In the same month the commanders from Yuryev, Prince Pyotr Ivanovich and his companions, sent Prince Nikita Kropotkin and his companions. They sent Prince Nikita to conquer the Kes district, and Prince Nikita and other chiefs conquered many surrounding places.

And the Germans Indrik and Kashpir with many people came out against them, but Prince Nikita and his companions routed them utterly and captured the German commanders Indrik and Kashpir, and brought other Germans to the commanders.

And the commanders sent the same chiefs to conquer Govya and Golbin, and the chiefs conquered many surrounding places and killed many Germans, and the chiefs themselves assembled at Yuryev before the commanders, God granting, safe and sound.

The sovereign ordered the commanders, Prince Pyotr Shuysky and his companions, to come to him.

And the tsar and grand prince ordered the boyars and commanders, Prince Pyotr and his companions, to come to him, and to Yuryev for a year the sovereign sent his commanders: boyar Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Kurlyatev and okolnichy Vasily Dmitrievich Danilov, and with Prince Dmitry the commanders Prince Pyotr Shchepin, Mikhail Petrovich Golovin, Fyodor Ivanovich Buturlin, and Prince Anton Romodanovsky. And the sovereign ordered Prince Dmitry’s commanders to divide the gates and guard their own and the land’s business.

And in all, the sovereign’s commanders took twenty German towns with their districts and villages in the year 7066 from Adam (the year 1566 from Christ), and in all the towns they set up Christian churches and established Orthodoxy and piety.
