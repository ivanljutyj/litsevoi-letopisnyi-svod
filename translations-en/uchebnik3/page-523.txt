…they will receive ineffable joy, according to what the divine apostle said. What eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor has entered into the heart of man—what God has prepared for those who love Him—on the day from the Rewarder, the righteous Judge, the Lord God, they will receive crowns of patience and the rest of eternal life in endless ages; amen. The perfect blessing of our humility and the conciliar prayer be upon your God-saved host, that all Orthodox together may suffer for the Christian faith; and by the grace of the Holy Spirit I give forgiveness and blessing in this age and in the age to come, that they may stand uncondemned before the terrible judgment of the good and merciful Lover of mankind, the Lord God, and with all the saints and righteous receive the good portion, and be accounted worthy of the heavenly kingdom, which we pray all of us may obtain. We also thank and praise, O pious Tsar, your great royal discernment in this matter and your courage, and the wisdom given you by God to stand manfully for the holy churches and for the great wrong done to the Orthodox Christian faith, and to strive in a royal and courageous manner for the fatherland for God’s sake. Take care also, O Tsar, to keep these four holy Gospel commandments: courage, wisdom, justice, and chastity, together with righteous judgment and mercy toward those who sin, according to what Christ said in the holy Gospel: “Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy,” and so on; then He also said: “Seek first the kingdom of heaven and its righteousness, and then all things shall be added unto you. For it is written: ‘Gray hair is wisdom to men, and the age of his old age is an undefiled life’; and again: ‘Give a wise man an occasion, and he will be wiser; tell the righteous man, and he will receive and understand the law of good thought.’” And if you live thus, O Tsar, the years of your life will be increased. With this, grace and peace, and mercy, and together with this salvation from the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ and His all-pure Mother, prayer, protection, and the prayers and blessing of all the saints, and also the great petition and blessing of our humility, and conciliar prayers, and universal prayers will always be upon you, our great and noble sovereign, the Orthodox and God-crowned Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Autocrat of all Rus, and upon your Christ-loving army. May the Lord multiply the years of your life, and may the Lord exalt your royal right hand over all your foes and enemies, and may the Lord establish your kingdom in peace and eternally from generation to generation and forever; amen. Written in Moscow in the year 7060 from Adam (the year 1560 from Christ), on the 13th day of July.”

The pious Sovereign, having read the metropolitan’s writing, received it with gratitude and inscribed it within himself on the tablets of his heart; and he ordered the letter to be read to Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and also to all the boyars and voivodes, and having read it, all received it as most fitting.

The Sovereign then dismissed the metropolitan’s boyar, and with him sent his own letter, and in the letter this was written.
