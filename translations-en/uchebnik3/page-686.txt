On the Moscow Fire

In the same year, on July 13, a Thursday, the Synaxis of the Archangel Gabriel, the Church of the Resurrection of Christ at the Good Belfry on Nikitskaya Street beyond the Neglinka in Moscow caught fire, and many households burned.

That same summer, a messenger of King Sigismund of Lithuania, Andrei Stanislavov, came to the sovereign tsar and grand prince from Lithuania with a letter. The king wrote to the tsar and grand prince that he should not make war on the Livonian land and should stand firmly by the truce until the end of the truce. And as for the cities that the tsar and grand prince had taken in the Livonian land, he should not mention them in correspondence with the king, so that because of such misfortunes there should be no more bloodshed. And the great tsar sent the Lithuanian prince’s envoy back to the king.

In the same month, on the 17th day, a Wednesday, the Feast of the Holy Great Martyr Marina, at the seventh hour of the day, the household of Prince Fyodor Pozharsky caught fire on the Arbat, by the Church of the Deposition of the Robe. And a great multitude of churches and households burned from the Uspensky ravine near the open place to the wood yard, and the whole bank as far as the Church of St Clement in Chertorye, and as far as Semchinskoye village, and to the Church of the Most Pure on Mogilitsy, and all of the Arbat, and beyond the Arbat as far as Novinsky Monastery. And the Tsaritsa and Grand Princess Anastasia was ill at that time, and the tsar and grand prince escorted the grand princess to his village of Kolomenskoye with great difficulty, because her illness was very grave.

And the tsar and grand prince came to the fire and labored much with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and the boyars to stop the fire, and in many places they defended the households by force; and at the Church of St Leontius on the Uspensky ravine, having stood against the fire with many people and stationed nobles of his court and streltsy on the mansions, by God’s grace with great labor they defended the mansions from the fire, and thus much of the settlement was saved that night.

In the same month of July, on the 19th day, a Friday, the Feast of the Holy Martyr Macrina, at the fifth hour of the day, fire broke out in Moscow in the Great Posad in Romodanovskaya Sloboda, and twenty households burned.

On the same day at the eighth hour of the day, the household of the clerk Rusin Kolobynin caught fire beyond the Neglinka on Dmitrovskaya Street, and that day a great multitude of churches and households burned; and with difficulty the tsar and grand prince, with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, the boyars, his court, the streltsy, and a multitude of people, by God’s grace extinguished the fire with great labor.
