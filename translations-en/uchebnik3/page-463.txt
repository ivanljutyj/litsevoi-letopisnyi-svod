And then he approaches the metropolitan to receive his blessing; and having received the blessing, he goes to his place, while the metropolitan stood in the choir at his place with the sacred assembly in all his hierarchal vestments.

And he ordered the archdeacon to begin a supplicatory service to the Most Pure Mother of God and to the Wonderworker Peter. And after the completion of the supplicatory service and “It is truly meet,” after the “Trisagion” and the troparia, the metropolitan and the grand prince went to the places prepared for them and sat in their places.

And after sitting a little while, they stood up, and the metropolitan ordered two archimandrites and an igumen to bring to him from the analogion, on a golden platter, the cross (with a particle) of the Life-giving Wood, on which our Lord Jesus Christ was crucified, and which the Greek tsar Constantine Monomachos had sent for the installation of the great Russian princes, together with the barmy and the cap, with the Ephesian Metropolitan Neophyte and other envoys.

And the metropolitan took the life-giving cross from the golden platter and laid it upon the grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich, and read the prayer aloud for all to hear: “O Lord our God, King of kings and Lord of lords, who through the prophet Samuel chose Your servant David and anointed him king over Your people Israel, do You now also hear our prayer, though we are unworthy, and from Your holy dwelling vouchsafe to anoint Your faithful servant, the grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich, whom You were pleased to raise up as king among Your people, which He purchased with the precious Blood of Your only-begotten Son, with the oil of gladness; fence him with the power of Your life-giving cross, place upon his head a crown of precious stone, grant him, O Lord, length of days, give also into his hands the scepter of kingship, set him upon the throne of righteousness, fence him with the whole armor of the Holy Spirit, strengthen his arm, subdue to him all barbarian peoples, instill in his heart the fear of God and a merciful eye toward the obedient, preserve him in the immaculate faith, show him to be a trustworthy guardian of Your holy catholic Church, that he may judge Your people with righteousness and Your poor with judgment, save the sons of the needy, and be an heir of Your heavenly Kingdom.”

The exclamation. “For Yours is the dominion, and Yours the kingdom, and the power, and the glory of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, both now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.”

And they prayed.

And the metropolitan ordered the same two archimandrites to bring to him from the analogion the diadem, that is, the barmy, and the metropolitan made the sign of the cross over the grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich with the cross, and placed the barmy upon him. And the metropolitan said this prayer secretly: “O Lord Almighty and King of the ages, an earthly man, made king by You, has bowed his head to pray to You; O Master of all, preserve him under Your shelter, uphold his kingdom, help him through You always to do what is pleasing to You, cause righteousness and abundance of peace to shine forth in his days, that in his gentleness life may be quiet and silent.”
