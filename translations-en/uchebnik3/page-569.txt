By God’s permission, for the sins of Christians, such a misfortune happened soon after the pious tsar’s return from Kazan to his God-preserved Russian realm, to Moscow.

And soon the sovereign, with the tsaritsa and their newborn infant, Tsarevich Prince Dmitry, went to the monastery of the Life-giving Trinity and Sergius the Wonderworker, to enlighten their newborn child with holy baptism.

And the sovereign came to the monastery of the Life-giving Trinity and Sergius the Wonderworker. And in the church of the Life-giving Trinity, at the relics of the wonderworker, they baptized his son, Tsarevich Prince Dmitry; and Archbishop Nikandr of Rostov baptized him.

And soon the sovereign, with the tsaritsa and their newborn infant, Tsarevich Dmitry, returned to Moscow. And the sovereign ordered the boyars, in his absence, to take care of the Kazan affair and to hold their feeding posts. But they, exhausted by such a great effort and labor, were in no hurry to perform even a small feat; they desired wealth and began to argue about the feeding posts, and they postponed concern for the ordering of Kazan.

And then the clerk of the tsar and grand prince, Ivan Mikhailov, reminded the sovereign about the testament. The sovereign ordered the testament to be drawn up, for this was always kept ready by the sovereign.

Having drawn up the testament, he began to speak to the sovereign about the kissing of the cross, so that Prince Vladimir Andreevich and the boyars might be brought to kiss the cross in the name of Tsarevich Prince Dmitry.

And the boyars began to speak to Prince Vladimir Andreevich, that the prince should not be stubborn, should obey the sovereign, and should kiss the cross; and Prince Vladimir Vorotynsky spoke first, along with the clerk Ivan Mikhailov. And Prince Vladimir Andreevich became very angry and said to Vorotynsky: “You should not scold me, nor dictate anything to me, nor speak against me.” And Vorotynsky answered the prince: “I, sire, have pledged my soul to my sovereign, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus, and to his son Tsarevich Prince Dmitry, that I am to serve them honorably in all things. And my sovereigns ordered me to speak with you, and I serve them, my sovereigns, but I do not wish to serve you; and I have spoken with you on behalf of them, my sovereigns. And if it comes to it, by their sovereign command I am ready even to fight with you.”
