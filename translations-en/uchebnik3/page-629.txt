On sending the okolnichy Prince Dmitry Semenovich Shastunov, Pyotr Petrovich Golovin, and Ivan Vyrodkov to found a city at the sea mouth

In the same year, in April, the tsar and grand prince sent his okolnichy Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Shastunov, Pyotr Petrovich Golovin, and Ivan Vyrodkov to Ivangorod, and ordered a city to be built on the Narva, below Ivangorod at the sea mouth, for a ship harbor; and he ordered a prohibition to be proclaimed in Novgorod and in Pskov and in Ivangorod, that no one should go to the Germans with any goods; but if Germans came into the patrimony of the tsar and grand prince, the sovereign ordered them to trade in his land, except for prohibited goods, and he ordered that no vexations be caused to the Germans.

On the sending to the sovereign from Astrakhan

In the same year, in the month of May, Ivan Cheremisinov and Mikhail Kolupaev sent from Astrakhan the serving Tatar Baichura. And with him they wrote that many Astrakhan people had recognized their dependence before the tsar and sovereign and had come to the city to them, and had given an oath that they would serve the tsar and grand prince and pay yasak as they had formerly paid to the Astrakhan tsars. And Ismail, prince of the Nogai, sent to Ivan and Mikhail his sons, Magmet with his brothers, and they gave their oath for their father and for all the murzas that they would keep loyalty to the tsar and grand prince in all things, and with Ivan they became sworn brothers. And Ivan and Mikhail released them to Prince Ismail, and with them they sent serving Tatars. And Yunus-murza, son of Prince Yusuf, the Nogai muradin, was himself with them and gave them an oath in the same matter for his brothers, that they would serve the tsar and grand prince, and that the tsar and sovereign would favor them, and not order war against them, and would permit them to roam in their old places. And the Nogais roamed and wintered below Astrakhan, and traded all winter in Astrakhan freely and amicably. And from many lands they negotiated with them, from Shevkal, Shamakhi, Derbent, and Urgench, concerning brotherhood and love, and in spring they wish to come with many goods to Astrakhan. And with Bachura Ak-murza, son of Yusuf, sent a messenger, and he thanked the sovereign for his favor, declaring his service and that of his brothers.
