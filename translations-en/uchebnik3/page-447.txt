When the trading was finished, the Grand Prince released the Nogai envoys to their sovereigns. And together with them the Grand Prince sent his Cossacks to Koshum-Murza and Oraz-Murza; and the Grand Prince wrote to the murzas in letters that they should keep honorable friendship. In the same month, on the 22nd day, Thursday, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus set out for the Life-Giving Trinity in St Sergius Monastery, for the day of the memory of the Wonderworker Sergius, to pray, and with him his brother Prince Yuri Vasilievich and many boyars; and they heard the all-night vigil, matins, the moleben, and the liturgy, and he bestowed alms on the abbot and the brethren; and he came to Moscow in the same month on the 29th day, Thursday. That same autumn, on November 7, there came to the Grand Prince in Moscow Nogai envoys from Prince Shikh-Mamai, from Koshum-Murza, from Ismail-Murza, and from other murzas—Prince Murat with companions—with letters. And the prince and murzas wrote in the letters that the Grand Prince should grant them favor and keep them in friendship with him. And the Grand Prince promised to keep them in friendship with him, and released them to their sovereigns; and together with them the Grand Prince sent his Cossacks, Riazan Bakov with companions, to Prince Shikh-Mamai and the murzas with letters.

The Grand Prince Grants Favor to Prince Vladimir

That same winter, on December 25, Sunday, the Nativity of Christ, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Sovereign of All Rus, at the intercession of his father Metropolitan Ioasaph and of his boyars, granted favor to Prince Vladimir Andreevich and his mother Princess Eufrosinia: he allowed them to behold his eyes, and gave him his father's patrimony, and ordered that other boyars, a palace steward, and court sons of boyars, not his father's, should be with him.

On the Arrest of Prince Ivan Belsky

That same winter, on January 2, the Grand Prince's boyar Prince Ivan Fedorovich Belsky was seized without the Grand Prince's knowledge, by counsel of the boyars, because the sovereign Grand Prince kept him near himself and among his chief counselors, and also Metropolitan Ioasaph. And the boyars were indignant at this against Prince Ivan and the Metropolitan, and began to plot evil against them with their confidants, and to send messages to Prince Ivan Vasilievich Shuysky in Vladimir. And Prince Ivan Shuysky was then in Vladimir, sent to stand guard against the Kazan people.

And Prince Ivan Shuysky in Vladimir brought many sons of boyars to kiss the cross, that they would be in their counsel. And the boyars set a term for Prince Ivan Shuysky and his advisers: that they should come from Vladimir to Moscow on January 3, Monday.

And in that night from Sunday to Monday, by the counsel of their like-minded men, they seized Prince Ivan Belsky in his courtyard and imprisoned him.
