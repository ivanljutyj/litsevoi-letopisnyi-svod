And the sovereign ordered them to go straight to the Sura River and cross upstream of him, because of the press of people, and he ordered them to join him beyond the Sura River in the fields.

The fourteenth camp was on the Sura River below Barancheev hillfort; and many bridges had been built over the Sura River. And there the hill people met the sovereign. And the sovereign came to the Sura River below Barancheev hillfort; and there the sovereign was met from the Sviyaga by the commanders Nikita Trofimov and Roman Pivov, and with them the hill people Yantudu-Murza, Buzke, and Kudaberdei with their companions; and they told the sovereign that the boyars Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky and Danilo Romanovich with their companions had gone against the remaining hill people, and all the hill people had petitioned the sovereign and joined the town of Sviyazhsk.

And the sovereign gave praise to God that He shows His grace and the impious are put in fear. And the sovereign fed the hill people and satisfied them with a word, showed them favor, forgave their guilt, and sent them back to the town of Sviyazhsk, and ordered bridges to be laid over the rivers and difficult passages to be cleared along the road; and they did so, laying bridges over all the small rivers.

And the sovereign sent Vaska Teterin to the commanders and ordered them to meet him two days’ journey from the town of Sviyazhsk, upstream along the Sviyaga River.

On the Coming of the Commanders to the Sovereign

And the sovereign crossed the Sura River, and there in the fields his sovereign’s commanders came to him, the boyar and commander Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Mstislavsky with his companions.

And the sovereign ordered the forward regiment to go before his own regiment, behind the yertoul, and he ordered the right-hand regiment to go on his right; and he ordered the great regiment to go behind his regiment, and behind it the guard regiment; and the sovereign ordered the left-hand regiment to go to the left of his tsar’s regiment. The fifteenth camp was on the small river Kivata; there the pious tsar spent the day on the feast of the Transfiguration of our Lord Jesus Christ; and on that day the sovereign partook of the ineffable mysteries of Christ, and so did Prince Vladimir Andreevich. And the sovereign is vouchsafed the grace of the Holy Spirit, and arms himself with piety against the impious and with joyful hope, by the grace of Christ, and continues his journey. The sixteenth camp was at Yakla, the seventeenth camp at Chivla.

And there many hill people met the sovereign and petitioned him concerning their defection, saying that they had fallen away from the sovereign out of fear, because the Kazanians had compelled them.

And the sovereign showed them favor, forgave their offense, invited them to eat, and satisfied them with food and drink; he sends them away to their villages and orders them to be ready…
