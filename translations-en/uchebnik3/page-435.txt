There is nothing bad in his thoughts; but if Prince Andrey does not believe the Vladyka and the archimandrite and his spiritual father, and flees, then the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess therefore sent after Prince Andrey their boyars Prince Nikita Vasilyevich and Prince Ivan Fedorovich Ovchina Obolensky with many people.

And they sent to meet Prince Andrey’s boyar Prince Fyodor Pronsky, ordering that he be seized and brought to Moscow.

And when Prince Fyodor was being taken, at that time one of Prince Andrey’s boyar sons, Sudok Dmitriev, son of Satin, escaped and ran to his prince; and he told Prince Andrey that his boyar Prince Fyodor Pronsky had been seized, and a rumor had reached him that “the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess have sent their boyars Prince Nikita and Prince Ivan Ovchina Obolensky with many people to seize you.”

And at that time Prince Andrey’s boyar son Yakov Verigin galloped from Volok to Staritsa and said that the Grand Prince’s boyars Prince Nikita and Prince Ivan Ovchina Obolensky with many people had arrived at Volok: “And they are coming to take you.”

And Prince Andrey immediately fled with his princess and son on the second day of May; and he rode about sixty versts from Staritsa to the Bernovo villages of Novotorzhsky district.

And from Bernovo Prince Vasily, son of Prince Fyodor Goluboy, fled and told the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess that Prince Andrey had not yet decided to what place he should flee.

And Prince Andrey did not go beyond the frontier from Torzhok, but went to Novgorod the Great, wishing to shut himself up in Novgorod.

And he wrote letters to the Grand Prince’s boyar sons, landowners, and sent them to the pogosts; and he wrote in the letters: “The Grand Prince is young, but the boyars hold the state, and whom should you serve? I will gladly reward you.”

And many other boyar sons came to him to serve him—landowners and men from the pogosts.

And news of this came to the Grand Prince and to his mother the Grand Princess that Prince Andrey was going to Novgorod and sending letters to boyar sons, calling them to serve him, and some boyar sons had already come to serve him; and those letters were brought to the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess with the prince’s seals.

And the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess immediately sent for the boyars, and ordered Prince Nikita to overtake Prince Andrey and to hurry to Novgorod before Prince Andrey; and they ordered the city fortified and the namestniks to bring all the people to kiss the cross.
