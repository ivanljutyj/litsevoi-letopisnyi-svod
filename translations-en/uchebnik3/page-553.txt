The court voivode Ivan Vasilyevich Sheremetev; and there beyond Kazan were the head of the tsar’s regiment, Mikhailo Voronoy, and Ivan Ugrimov, voivode of Prince Vladimir Andreyevich.

And the boyars and voivodes, by God’s mercy, slew a great multitude of the infidels. And they slew such a multitude there: from the Kazan River to the forest, and in the forest many lay dead, and only a few escaped, wounded with many wounds.

On the Slaughter of the Infidels

And now, with the help of our almighty Lord Jesus Christ, and by the prayers of our Lady Theotokos, and by the help and intercession of the great Archangel Michael, and by the prayers of the other incorporeal powers and great wonderworkers, and by the prayers of the kinsmen of our Orthodox tsar and of all the saints, our pious and God-crowned Orthodox tsar and great prince, autocrat of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, fought against the impious and overcame them utterly. And he took the Kazan tsar Ediger-Magmet, and took their banners and the kingdom, and seized the populous city of Kazan.

But the tsar commanded that women and small children be taken captive, while all the fighting men were to be slain for their treason. And such a multitude of Tatar captives was taken that all the Russian regiments were filled: every man had Tatar captives.

And many thousands of Christian captives were set free. And such a multitude of the slain lay in the city that throughout the whole city there was nowhere to step except upon the dead. Behind the Tsar’s court, where they had taken to flight, and on the city walls, and along the streets, piles of the dead lay level with the city walls; and the moats on that side of the city were full of the dead; and along the Kazan River, and in the river, and beyond the river across the whole meadow, the dead lay.

The Tsar Gives Praise to God

Seeing such mercy of God upon himself and upon all his Christ-loving army, the pious tsar and great prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, raised his hands to the Lord, offered grateful prayers, and spoke thus: “Glory to Thee, most merciful Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, who has given us victory over our enemies! Thy hand, O Lord, has been glorified in strength; Thy right hand has crushed our enemies! What shall I render unto Thee, O Lord, for all the good that Thou hast rendered unto us? Glory to Thee, most merciful Lord, that Thou didst not despise the supplication of Thy servant! Thou didst hear the words of my heart and my tears, and didst fulfill our petitions, and didst pour out Thy mercy upon us, and didst destroy our adversaries utterly! O most merciful Lady Theotokos, glory to Thee, for by thy prayers and intercession our enemies have been defeated! O most merciful Lady Theotokos, thou with all the saints—”
