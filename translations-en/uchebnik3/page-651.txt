RUS (1566–1575 from V.Kh.).
Book 23

On the Sending of the Tsar and Grand Prince to the German Land to the Boyar Prince Peter Ivanovich

In the same month the sovereign sent to the German Land to the boyar Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky the boyar Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany, and ordered them to form five regiments. In the Large Regiment were Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky and the commander Prince Fedor Ivanovich Troekurov, and also in the Large Regiment with Prince Peter Ivanovich was the commander Andrei Ivanovich Shein; in the Advance Regiment were the boyar Prince Andrei Mikhailovich Kurbsky and the commander Daniil Fedorovich Adashev. In the Regiment of the Right Hand were the boyar Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany and Bogdan Yuryev, son of Saburov. In the Regiment of the Left Hand were Prince Peter Shchepin Obolensky and Vasily, son of Vasily Razladin Kvashnin. In the Guard Regiment were Prince Grigory Ivanovich Temkin and Prince Grigory Golova Zvenigorodsky. And he ordered them to go to the German Novgorodok, to Kostr, and to Yuryev and to campaign as far as the Merciful God would help.

On the Taking of Novgorodok

On the 6th day of the month of July the boyars and commanders Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky and Prince Andrei Mikhailovich Kurbsky, and all the commanders, sent to the tsar and grand prince Prince Bogdan Romodanovsky, Elizar Rzhevsky, and Fedor Solovtsov, [to report] that by God’s mercy the commanders had taken Novgorodok, having stood before it for three weeks, and the Germans had fought very fiercely and stood to the death.

And the commanders ordered the streltsy commanders Timofei Teterin and Andrei Kashkarov to set up siege towers around the city and to move the cannon up to the city, and with the cannon they knocked down the shooting tower and destroyed much in the city.

And the streltsy commanders set the siege towers right by the city, and the streltsy entered the city and drove the Germans out of the city into Vyshgorod.

And in Vyshgorod they killed many Germans with cannon, and they took the city on June 30, Thursday. And the Master and the Bishop of Yuryev were standing at that time
