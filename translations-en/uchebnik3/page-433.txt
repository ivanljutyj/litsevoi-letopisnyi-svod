And before that, in the year 7042 from Adam (the year 1542 from Christ), in January, after the death of the Grand Prince, without leaving Moscow for his patrimony, after the fortieth-day memorial rites, Prince Andrei petitioned Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother Grand Princess Elena, and he asked for additional towns for his patrimony beyond those granted by his father's blessing and beyond the testament. And the Grand Prince and his mother Grand Princess Elena did not add towns to his patrimony.

And they honored him as formerly, after the deaths of grand princes, gifts were given to brothers; and to him they gave even more than that: they gave him fur coats, cups, and ambling horses with saddles.

Prince Andrei went to his own patrimony in Staritsa and began to hold anger against the Grand Prince and against his mother Grand Princess because they had not added towns to his patrimony.

And evil people attached themselves to this, and they told the Grand Prince tales about Prince Andrei, and told Prince Andrei tales about the Grand Prince, that the Grand Prince wanted to seize him.

And the Grand Prince and his mother Grand Princess Elena sent their boyar Prince Ivan Vasilievich Shuisky and their clerk Menshoy Putyatin to Prince Andrei to persuade him about this, that those words were not true, and that Grand Prince Ivan and his mother Grand Princess Elena had nothing bad in their thoughts.

Prince Andrei, by the command of the Grand Prince and his mother Grand Princess, came to Moscow and had audience with the Grand Prince and his mother, and petitioned the Grand Prince and his mother through his father Metropolitan Daniel: "A rumor had reached him that the Grand Prince and his mother Grand Princess had wanted to place their disfavor upon him." And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and his mother Grand Princess Elena said to Prince Andrei:

"Rumors also reach us about you that you hold anger against us; you should stand firm in your word and not listen to the speeches of evil people; and we have nothing bad in our heart against you; and name to us those people who set us at odds, so that henceforth there may be no evil between us." And Prince Andrei did not name anyone by name, but said that it had only seemed so to him.

And Grand Prince Ivan and his mother Grand Princess Elena gave Prince Andrei their firm word that the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess had nothing bad in their heart against Prince Andrei; and they sent him away to his patrimony with great reassurance. And Prince Andrei came to his patrimony, but did not set aside suspicion and fear, and held anger in his heart against the Grand Prince and against his mother the Grand Princess because they had not added towns to his patrimony.
