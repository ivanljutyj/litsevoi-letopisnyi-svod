From the Holy Forty Martyrs, from the monastery of Xeropotamou, came the elder Isaiah, a hieromonk, a Serb. And with the metropolitan came the patriarchal deacon Dionysius, a Greek. Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign of all Rus, received Metropolitan Gregory and the elders of the Holy Mountain of Athos with great honor and commanded them to stay in the Monastery of the Holy Archangel Michael of his honorable and glorious miracle, feeding them and supplying them from his royal table.

Also the chief hierarch Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, showed great love and honor to Gregory, Metropolitan of the city of Zichna, and to the elders of the Holy Mountain, and calling them to himself, he often conversed with them about divine spiritual words.

On the Repose of Prince Simeon, Brother of Grand Prince Vasily

That same summer, on the 26th day of June, on Saturday, at the twelfth hour of the day, the pious Prince Simeon Ivanovich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily, reposed. And his body was laid in the Church of the Archangel Michael in Moscow, where his forefathers also lie, between Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, the eldest son of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, and his uncle Prince George Vasilyevich. And he was buried on Sunday, and Grand Prince Vasily was at his burial, and with Grand Princess Solomonia they escorted his body with many tears. And Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, sang over him with bishops, archimandrites, and hegumens and with the whole consecrated council. Also present at his burial were Metropolitan Gregory, who had come from the patriarch, and the elders of the Holy Mountain of Athos.

On the Bishop of Kolomna

That same summer, in June, Mitrofan, Bishop of Kolomna, left his bishopric because of infirmity.

On the Rains

That same summer, during the Fast of Saints Peter and Paul and after the Feast of Saint Peter, great rains increased, and in the rivers there was more water than in spring; this was, for our sins, a chastisement from God for our own salvation.

Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus commanded his father Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, together with bishops, archimandrites, hegumens, and the whole consecrated council, to pray to the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ and to His most pure Mother, the holy Mother of God Mary, and to the holy great wonderworkers of Rus; and to sing molebens for God’s mercy, for order in the land, for the warmth of the sun, and for fair weather. And to all the people, Orthodox Christians, they enjoined fasting and prayer with pure repentance and tears. And so it came to pass.
