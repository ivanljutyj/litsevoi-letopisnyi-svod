RUS (1544–1559 from the Birth of Christ). Book 20

In the year 7044 from Adam (1544 from Christ)

In November, on the 25th day. Tsarevna Kovgorshad, Prince Bulat, and all the Kazan land betrayed Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich: they killed Tsar Yanalei, whom Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich had given them as tsar in Kazan, and took for themselves as tsar in Kazan Tsarevich Safa-Girei from Crimea.

In the same autumn, in October, by command of the Grand Prince, the earthen town of Pochep was built on the old site.

In the same autumn, in December, on the 12th day, the Grand Prince showed favor to Tsar Shigalei, released him from exile—he had been confined at Beloozero—lifted his disgrace, and allowed him to behold his eyes.

In the same month a wooden town was built in Meshchera on the Moksha River.

In the same winter, on January 6, Kazan Tatars came to Nizhny Novgorod and to Balakhna, burned the posad, and cut down many fugitive people on the Volga.

And the Grand Prince sent from Murom Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky and other commanders with troops, and the Kazan Tatars went away.

In the same month the Grand Prince ordered an earthen town to be built in Rzhevsky on the Lithuanian border, named it Zavolochye, and ordered the Rzhev households to be moved into it.

In the same month, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, a small town was built in Kostroma district, Buy-Gorodok on the Korega.

In the same winter, on February 20, Meatfare Sunday, Savva Slepushkin, an elder of the Osifov Monastery, was consecrated by Metropolitan Daniel as bishop in the city of Smolensk.

On February 27 of the same month, Lithuanian commanders, Andrei Nemirov with many men and a great artillery train, with cannons and handguns, came to the town of Sebezh and advanced close to the town. By God’s mercy they did the town no harm, but with their own cannons they killed their own men.
