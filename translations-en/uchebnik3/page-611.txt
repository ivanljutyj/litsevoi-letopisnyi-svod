On the Royal Journey to the Trinity and to Pereslavl

In the year 7065 from Adam (1564 from Christ), on the 10th day of September, the tsar and grand prince set out with his tsaritsa and grand princess Anastasia, and with his son the tsarevich Ivan Ivanovich, and with him also his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, to the Trinity and to the wonderworker Sergius. And the sovereigns celebrated the Exaltation of the Precious Cross in the monastery at the Trinity, and having sufficiently provided for the brethren, the sovereigns went from there to Pereslavl to pray to the great wonderworker Nikita. And in the monastery of the wonderworker Nikita the sovereigns celebrated with warm faith and great zeal, and gave the monastery many lands for the sustenance of the brethren, and commanded the abbot to establish a community, since formerly this had been a separate monastery, and they gave large sums of money for gathering the brethren, so as to build a great monastery in honor of God and His servant the wonderworker Nikita. And from there the sovereigns came to the Life-Giving Trinity for the commemoration of the wonderworker Sergius, and celebrated here according to the royal custom with great piety and devotion, and provided sufficiently for the brethren, and with alms from their royal hands they bestowed gifts on all the brethren; the tsar himself also gave sufficient alms with his own hands in the hospitals and almshouses. And the sovereigns came to Moscow in the same month on the 29th day.

On the Arrival to the Sovereign from Ivan Cheremisinov

In the same month, at the Trinity, there came to the tsar and grand prince from Astrakhan, from Ivan Cheremisinov and Mikhail Kolupaev, and from the flotilla commanders, from Fyodor Pisemsky and from Timokha Teterin, the boyar’s son Ontonko Potulov with companions, and the Cossack ataman Arkhipko, and the Vyatka man Shelom. And the heads wrote from Astrakhan as follows: that they had come to Astrakhan, and the city turned out to be empty; the tsar and the people had fled. And the heads remained in Astrakhan and fortified the city, and having secured it completely, they went after the tsar for five days from Astrakhan to Machaki by the sea; and they found all the Astrakhan vessels, cut them up and burned them, but did not catch the people, because they had gone further to the shore. And another time the flotilla commanders Fyodor Pisemskoy and Timofei Teterin went and found the tsar about 20 versts from the shore, and advanced against the tsar by night, and killed many people in his uluses.
