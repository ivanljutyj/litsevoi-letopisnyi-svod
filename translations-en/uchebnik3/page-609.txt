And the tsar and sovereign gave praise to God, that the merciful God had avenged them for innocent Christian blood, and sent rewards to the voivodes.

And at that time the sovereign sent to Novgorod his viceroy, boyar Mikhail Vasilyevich Glinsky, and ordered him to write to the king, recounting his wrongs, and if he wished for peace, he should send ambassadors to him; and he should petition the tsar and sovereign that the sovereign order the truce to be kept according to the old custom.

In that same month on the 15th day the sovereign sent back to the Nogais, to the prince and murzas, their ambassadors, and sent his own ambassadors: to Prince Ismail, Andrei Tishkov; to Roslan, Grigory Velin; and to Bai-murza and Shikh-Mamai’s children, Istoma Matyukov; and he instructed them that, according to their petition, the sovereign wished to act as God would help.

On the Birth of Tsarevna Evdokia

In that same month on the 26th day, on Wednesday of the second week of Lent, to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus was born a daughter, tsarevna and grand princess Evdokia, from his tsaritsa and grand princess Anastasia.

On the Baptism of the Tsarevna

And in that same Lent, on the fourth Sunday, she was baptized in the Chudov Monastery of the Archangel Michael, and Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, received her from the font, and Andrei, archpriest of the Annunciation, officiated.

On the Sending of a Messenger from Ismail

In the month of March on the first day Prince Ismail sent from the Nogais his messenger Bikchura, and wrote to the tsar and grand prince that Tsar Derbysh in Astrakhan had betrayed the tsar and grand prince, had conspired with the Crimean tsar and with the Nogai murzas, with Isuf’s children and with other murzas who had been driven out of the Nogai lands, and had driven the envoy Leonty Mansurov out of Astrakhan; Ismail had set out against Astrakhan, and the tsar and grand prince should send his army.

On the Treason of the Ar People

And on that same day boyar Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky wrote from Kazan that the Ar people and the coastal people had betrayed the sovereign and killed the streltsy who had been sent to them for protection with Mamich-Berdei; and Mamich-Berdei had taken to himself the tsarevich who had come to them from the Nogais, and was living on the Meadow Side, and about one hundred Nogais had come with him.
