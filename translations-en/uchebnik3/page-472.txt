On Thursday, the Feast of the Presentation of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ, he spent the night at Yelnya, 15 versts from Nizhny Novgorod, and the next day, Friday, he came to Robotka Island. And by God’s will great warmth and wetness set in, and water covered all the ice on the Volga, and many cannons and guns fell into the water, since much river water had come out onto the ice. And no one could cross on the ice, and many people drowned in the ice holes, because they were not visible under the water.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince stood on Robotka Island for 3 days, waiting for a chance to continue the journey, but there was no way.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince sent to Kazan his boyar and voivode Prince Dmitry Fyodorovich Belsky and many other voivodes with many people, and ordered them, joining with Tsar Shigalei, to go to Kazan.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince himself returned to Nizhny Novgorod with many tears, because God did not aid his journey; and he came to Nizhny Novgorod on February 10, and the Tsar and Grand Prince came to Moscow on March 7.

The voivodes of the Tsar and Grand Prince joined with Tsar Shigalei at the mouth of the Tsyvil in Cathedral Week, on Friday, February 18, and came to the city of Kazan. And the Kazan Tsar with many people met Tsar Shigalei and the Grand Prince’s voivodes on Arsk Field; and in the advance regiment was then Prince Semyon Mikulinsky with his comrades, and he advanced with the advance regiment and defeated many Kazan people, and drove the Tsar himself into the city, and they stood around the city of Kazan for 7 days, fighting. And in that battle they captured Azik-bogatyr and many others. And from Kazan the voivodes of the Tsar and Grand Prince returned, God granting, all healthy, and sent to the Tsar and Grand Prince with that news Ivan Mikhailov, son of Yuryev, and Nikita Sheremetev, on March 11. And near Kazan, from among the nobles of the Tsar and Grand Prince, they killed Grigory Vasilyev, son of Sheremetev.

In the same winter, on January 13, from Friday to Saturday, at the third hour of the night, many rays appeared in the sky on the midnight side, as if fiery, and stood in the sky all night until dawn. In the same winter, on February 9, from Thursday to Friday at night, many rays appeared in the sky on the northern side and went out before matins.

In the same autumn, on April 22, in the third week after Easter, Michael, former archimandrite of the Chudov Monastery, was consecrated bishop of Ryazan by Metropolitan Makary.
