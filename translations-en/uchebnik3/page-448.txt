And ahead of him went his son, Prince Pyotr, and Ivan Bolshoy Sheremetev, and with them about three hundred men; and in that council were the boyars Prince Mikhail and Prince Ivan Kubensky, Prince Dmitry Paletsky, the treasurer Ivan Tretyakov, and princelings, and many nobles and sons of boyars, and the Novgorodians of Great Novgorod, the whole city.

And the next day, on Monday, they sent Prince Ivan Belsky to Beloozero into confinement.

And Prince Ivan Belsky’s counsellors they seized and sent to various towns: Prince Pyotr Mikhailovich Shchenyatev to Yaroslavl.

And Prince Pyotr was taken from the sovereign’s room through the back doors; and Ivan Khabarov was seized and put in the cellar beneath the chamber at the court of Foma Golovin, and from there he was exiled to Tver.

And they began to subject Metropolitan Joasaph to great dishonor and disgrace (and to throw stones about his cell). Metropolitan Joasaph could not endure this and left his court for the Trinity metochion.

And the boyars sent town sons of boyars (Novgorodians of Great Novgorod) to the Trinity metochion with shameful words. And with great disgrace they reviled him and nearly killed him; the Trinity abbot Alexius barely persuaded them, by [the name of] Sergius the Wonderworker, to spare him from death (and the boyar Prince Dmitry Paletsky).

And there was a great tumult in Moscow at that time, and they frightened the sovereign, and the metropolitan was exiled to the Kirillov Monastery.

And when Prince Ivan Belsky was being taken, the boyars came to the sovereign’s bedchamber at an untimely hour, three hours before dawn, and made [them] sing before the crosses; and at that time Metropolitan Joasaph came to the sovereign in his room, and the boyars came after him into the room with noise. And the metropolitan was exiled to the Kirillov Monastery.

(And in that spring, in the month of May) the boyars sent to Beloozero Petrok Yartsov, son of Zaitsov, and Mitka Ivanov, son of Klobukov, and Ivashka Elizarov, son of Sergiev, to kill Prince Ivan Belsky in prison; and they, setting out secretly, without the knowledge of the Grand Prince, by the boyars’ arbitrary will, killed Prince Ivan Belsky.
