In the great church of the Meeting of the Lord he established an archpriesthood and numbered it among the cathedral churches. He also ordered the church and chambers to be built quickly, so that his children might settle in that courtyard the sooner.

To his brother, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, the tsar and grand prince ordered a place to be cleared in the courtyard of his uncle, Prince Yuri Ivanovich of Dmitrov, behind the Church of St John, which is under the bells; and he ordered the courtyard of Mikhail Yuryevich Zakharyin and other courtyards to be demolished and the place cleared as far as the lane of the Chudov Monastery, at the rear gates of that same monastery; and he ordered a church to be built in the prince’s courtyard, on the gallery, the Church of the Entry of the Mother of God.

After the death of his father, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich lived with his princess at the tsar and grand prince’s in separate chambers, in their father’s courtyard, and his whole household was supplied from the sovereign treasury and the palace, from the treasury of the sovereign tsar and grand prince and from the palace departments, all undivided.

From this time the tsar and grand prince began to establish for his brother, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, a household from his own towns and volosts, with which his father, the grand prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, had blessed him: with treasures and treasury, towns, villages, and volosts. He also appointed for him his own boyars and a majordomo, clerks, courtiers, table attendants, stewards, and all kinds of officials, as befits every sovereign rank in every household.

And for Tsar Alexander Safa-Gireevich of Kazan, the tsar and grand prince ordered a place to be cleared in the courtyard by the Church of Nicholas the Wonderworker of Gostun — the courtyard of Prince Pyotr Mikhailovich Shchenyatev, and the courtyards of the Morozovs; and on that place he ordered a courtyard to be built for Tsar Alexander.

Alexander lived in the tsar and grand prince’s courtyard, in the chambers at the Saviour on the Palace. He also ordered that boyar sons and clerks be appointed for him from among all the palace servants, and that officials be granted support from his treasury; and he ordered that he be given sufficient household provisions from the palace in all things until he should reach full age, for at that time he was still young.

In the same year, on August 14, a Wednesday, his father and intercessor Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, together with Bishop Matthew of Sarsk and Podonsk, and all the archimandrites and boyars, came to the tsar and grand prince to petition him, and all petitioned the tsar and grand prince.

In the same year, on October 3, Grigory Semenov, son of Pleshcheev, came to Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus from the Pyatigorsk Circassians. The tsar and grand prince had sent him to the Circassians, to Prince Temryuk Aidarovich, on an embassy, to protect him from enemies among the Circassians who had deserted him and who were causing him trouble. And Grigory reported that he had come to Astrakhan in the year 7071.
