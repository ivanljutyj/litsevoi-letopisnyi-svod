And the Kazan princes, in addition to that sworn charter and record, gave another record upon themselves: that in Kazan with Tsar Shigalei they would safeguard the grand prince’s interests, and that they and their children would remain unwavering from the great sovereign with all the Kazan land until their death, and that they would take no other tsar. And on that they gave a separate oath to the great sovereign on behalf of the seid, the princes, and all the people of the Kazan land.

And the great sovereign Vasily sent Tsar Shigalei and the Kazan envoys back to Kazan on the 8th day of March. And he sent to Kazan to install the tsar on the throne Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky, and his Tver butler Mikhail Yuryevich, and with him his clerk Ivan Teleshov.

They, having gone to Kazan, by command of the grand prince installed Tsar Shigalei on the throne in April, and brought the seid, the ulans, the princes and karaichis, the murzas, the mullahs, the sheikhzades, and all the people of the land to the oath: that they would remain unwavering from the sovereign grand prince until their death.

On the Journey of the Grand Prince

That same spring, in the month of May, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich left the city of Moscow for the wonderworker Nicholas at Ugresh, and from there to Ostrov, and lived there until the Eve of the Petrine Fast.

And having come to Moscow, he lived all summer at Vorontsovo until autumn itself.

On the Miracles of St Alexis the Wonderworker

That same summer, in the month of July, on the 13th day, they brought from a village a certain maiden named Anna, a Christian daughter, with paralyzed legs, who had lain in that ailment for seven years. And they brought her to the wonder-working shrine where the body of St Alexis the hierarch lies, and through the prayers of the saint she was healed, became well, and went away.

Miracle of the Blind Man

That same summer, in the month of July, on the 3rd day, a Sunday, they brought to the wonder-working coffin Zlobin from the village of Bulatnikovo, an old man named Semion, whose eyes were blind and who was altogether very ill. And falling with faith before the body of the wonderworker St Alexis and making a prayer, he at once became well.

On the Blind Woman

In that same month of July, on the 8th day, they brought to St Alexis the Wonderworker a certain blind woman named Solomonia; and after a prayer service, they brought her to the shrine of the wonderworker St Alexis, and through his prayers she became sound in her eyes.
