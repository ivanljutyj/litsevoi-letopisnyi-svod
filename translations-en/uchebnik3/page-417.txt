And Prince Andrey did not go abroad from Novotorzhsk district, but went to Novgorod the Great, wishing to establish himself in Novgorod.

And Prince Andrey ordered letters to be written to the grand prince's boyar scions and landowners, and also sent them to the pogosts; and he wrote in the letters: "The grand prince is young, and the boyars hold the state; whom then should you serve? But I am ready to reward you."

And many other boyar scions, landowners, and men from the pogosts came to Prince Andrey to serve.

And the news came to the grand prince and to his mother, the grand princess, that Prince Andrey was going to Novgorod and was sending letters to boyar scions, summoning them to serve him, and that some boyar scions had already come to serve him; and these letters, with the prince's seals, were brought to the grand prince and to his mother, the grand princess.

And Grand Prince Ivan and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, at once sent for the boyars and ordered Prince Nikita to intercept Prince Andrey and to hurry to Novgorod, to overtake Prince Andrey; and they ordered the city to be fortified and the governors to bring all the people to the oath; and when Prince Andrey should come to Novgorod, Prince Nikita, having strengthened himself with the people and with the governors, was to stand against Prince Andrey as much as God would help, and not to let him burn the posad; and if Prince Andrey should be strong in men, Prince Nikita was to remain in the city and guard the grand prince's cause, and be of one mind with the archbishop and the governors.

And they wrote to Prince Ivan Ovchina, ordering him to gather men and follow Prince Andrey. And at that time the boyar scions, the Novgorod landowners, had gone to service, but they were ordered to be in Moscow.

And the grand prince and his mother, the grand princess, sent men along the roads to meet them, and also into the pyatinas to the boyar scions, ordering them to hurry after Prince Ivan Ovchina.

And Prince Ivan Ovchina, having gathered many men, went after Prince Andrey; and Prince Andrey turned left from Zayatsky Yam toward Staraya Russa.

And Prince Ivan Ovchina overtook Prince Andrey at Tyukhli, about five versts from Zayatsky Yam. And Prince Andrey prepared for battle with Prince Ivan, armed his men, and drew up his regiments.

And he advanced with his regiments against Prince Ivan. And Prince Ivan appointed commanders over the grand prince's men against Prince Andrey and drew up the regiments; and having set the regiments in order and placed his help in God, he went against Prince Andrey. And Prince Andrey saw the regiments of the grand prince and did not wish to fight with the grand prince's men.
