Concerning the Letter from Kazan from Prince Peter

And Prince Peter Ivanovich wrote from Kazan that Yenebek and his companions had sent to him and apologized for their offenses, and the Bashkirs came acknowledging their guilt and paid the yasak.

Concerning the Dispatch from Cheboksary from Prince Peter

And Prince Peter Semyonovich wrote from Cheboksary that the children of Mamich-Bardei and the sotsky Kaka, and all the other people, acknowledged their subjection to the sovereign and came to him and took the oath, and he sent Daniil Chulkov to administer the oath to all the common people.
