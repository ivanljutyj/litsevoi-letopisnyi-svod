Message from the Commanders

In the same month the commanders sent Mamysh Ododurov and reported that they had overtaken the Master’s commanders; Boris Kolychev and Timofei Teterin had been with the commander as a patrol, and they overtook them at the main fortresses.

The commanders ordered the patrol to fight them, and the forward regiment hastened to it, while the other regiments began to go around the fortress.

When the Germans saw the men and did not wait for their commanders, they fled, and the patrols chased them for about fifteen versts, caught and killed a few men, and took many carts and stallions.

The commanders then sent the captains Grigory Papin Saburov and his companions to get news of the Master. The captains went about fifty versts, took prisoners, and reported that the Master had fled to Kes and that his men had scattered from him. The commanders, calling upon God for help, went to Yuryev.

Message from the Master

In the same month the Livonian Master Gedir sent his man Indrik to say that the old Master Viglim had been deprived of power and that he himself had been made Master, and that the tsar and sovereign should show him favor and restrain his army.

The tsar and grand prince dismissed him and wrote back to the Master: if the Master wished to receive the sovereign’s favor, let him come himself with a petition; and after examining the petition, the sovereign would show him favor, and this letter would serve as his safe-conduct for the journey.

The Capture of Yuryev

In the month of July, on the twenty-fifth day, Prince Boris, son of Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany, and the streltsy head Timofei Teterin came from the commanders and reported in the commanders’ name that, by God’s mercy, they had taken the city of Yuryev on the eighteenth day of July.

When the commanders came to Yuryev, they rolled the cannon out of the boats, and the streltsy dug in before the siege towers and drove the Germans from the city walls.

The Germans came out against the forward regiment, and the captains with the boyar sons were sent against them; they killed many and captured some. There were other sorties as well, and everywhere, God granting, they beat them. They fired with cannon for six days and destroyed the city wall, and killed many people in the city with cannon.

The Bishop of Yuryev and all the posadniki, the ratmans, the bishop’s boyars, palace officials, estate holders, and all the common people submitted to the commanders, surrendered the city, and entered into eternal servitude to the tsar and sovereign.
