And the tsar comes to the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God and to the chief hierarch Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, and tells the metropolitan what has happened. And the metropolitan advises the sovereign thus: “Let the relics of all the saints be brought to the cathedral church, a service be performed before them, and water be sanctified over them, and by you, the sovereign, and by our humility let some of the priests be sent to the Sviyaga, to the (church of) the Most Pure (Mother of God) of Her Honorable Nativity and to all the church (people), so that prayer services may be performed there also, and they may sanctify water, and unite them together, and sanctify the city by a cross procession and with the holy waters, and fence all the people with the cross and sprinkle them with water; let Christ, through the prayers of His saints, appease His righteous wrath. It is also needful to send instruction to those living in the city, (saying) wherein the people have sinned, so that they renounce their wickedness; also the tsar and all of us ought to live according to the commandments of Christ, so as not to anger our Master, for it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.”

To the pious tsar it was sweet to accept the hierarch’s advice and instruction, and the tsar and the hierarch came together to the church of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Mother of God, which was not far from the tsar’s treasury, and they take the relics of the holy fathers out of the vessels and place them on a platter, and the metropolitan and all the consecrated council receive this platter, and they carry the relics on their heads to the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, and perform prayer services for a long time, and sanctify water from all the relics and with the cross of the Life-giving Wood, on which our Lord Christ was crucified, who delivered the human race from the torment of hell.

The pious tsar comes to the wonderworking image of the Most Pure (Mother of God) and to the wonderworking and healing-bearing relics of the holy fathers and with tears for a long time made prayer, only the One God knows of this; and having completed the prayer, the tsar and the hierarch choose a distinguished man learned in God-inspired Scripture, the archpriest of the Archangel Cathedral, Timothy, and send him to Nizhny Novgorod, and command him to go together with the boyars of the great prince and the tsar’s army; and the metropolitan sends with him holy water and instruction to the voivodes and to all who live there.
