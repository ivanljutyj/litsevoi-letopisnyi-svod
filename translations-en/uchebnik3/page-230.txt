Misfortunes from the godless barbarians. God was pleased to act thus because of our sins, and you, our sovereign, have come to the reigning city of Moscow, to the All-Merciful Lady, the Mother of God, and to the holy wonderworkers for help and protection, and to your father the metropolitan, and to your mother the grand princess, and to the right-believing princes and pious boyars for good counsel—how to stand firmly for Orthodox Christianity, for your fatherland, against the godless infidel. And you, our sovereign, obeying our entreaties and good counsels, promised to stand firmly for our pious Orthodox faith and to defend your fatherland from the infidels; but flatterers whisper into the ear of your authority that it is necessary to betray Christianity. And the metropolitan, with all the God-loving council, blessed you, our sovereign, and signed you with the precious Cross, and said: “May God preserve your kingdom by the power of His precious Cross and grant you victory over your enemies, and subdue under your feet all your adversaries, like Constantine and David of old, through the prayers of His Most Pure Mother and all the saints.” Only be courageous and strong, my spiritual son, as befits a good soldier of Christ, according to the Gospel word of the Lord: you are the good shepherd; you lay down your life for the sheep; but the hireling, who is not the shepherd, whose sheep are not his own, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep and flees, and the wolf seizes and scatters them; the hireling flees because he is a hireling and does not care for the sheep. But you, sovereign spiritual son, not as a hireling but as a true shepherd, have risen to deliver the rational flock of Christ’s sheep entrusted to you by God from the wolf that has now come, and the Lord God will strengthen you and help you and all your Christ-loving army; and to us all together saying, “Amen, that is, so be it.”

With the Lord helping you, our sovereign, who has laid all this upon your heart, and as a good true shepherd, having called to your aid God and His Most Pure Mother, and His saints, and the hierarchal blessing, and the prayer of all the people, you have armed yourself with the power of the Precious Cross and go forth against the accursed spiritual wolf, the dreadful Akhmat, striving to save from his jaws the rational flock of Christ’s sheep. And after your setting out, our sovereign, the hierarch metropolitan together with us, your Grace’s men of prayer, with all the God-loving councils, continually offers prayer, and in all holy churches supplications and the holy service are always served for our whole fatherland for the sake of your victory; and all Christians continually pray to God that He grant you victory over your enemies, whom we hope to overcome with the help of the All-Merciful God. But now we have heard that the infidel Akhmat is already approaching and destroying Christianity, and especially boasts against you and your fatherland; yet you humble yourself before him, send to him and ask him for peace; but he, accursed, breathing anger, does not heed your request.
