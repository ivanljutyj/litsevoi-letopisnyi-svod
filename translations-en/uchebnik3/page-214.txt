After these speeches, in the archbishop’s courtyard the Grand Prince’s boyars began to administer the kissing of the cross to the Novgorod boyars and well-to-do townspeople, and merchants, and others, on that on which they had petitioned the Grand Prince according to the charter which the archbishop’s clerk had written, and the lord had signed with his own hand and affixed his seal, and one seal from each of the five ends.

And the Grand Prince sent to the five ends his boyar sons and his clerks; and they brought them all to the kissing of the cross on the same charter: all the people kissed the cross, and the boyars’ wives, and widows, and the boyars’ people.

And there was among the Novgorodians a charter confirmed among themselves with fifty-eight seals, and that charter the Grand Prince’s boyars took from them at the time of the kissing of the cross in the archbishop’s courtyard.

On January 18, Sunday, all the Novgorod boyars, and boyar sons, and well-to-do men petitioned the Grand Prince for service, and having completed everything, they went out from him.

And the Grand Prince sent after them Ivan Tovarkov to Kazimir and to his brother Korob, and to Feofilat Zakharyin, and to Luka and Yakov Fedorov, and to Ivan Kuzmin, and to Ivan Zakhariin, son of Grigoriev, and to Mikita Esipov, and to Mikhail Berdenev, and to Vasily Esipov, and to Feodor Telyatev, and to Rodion Norov; and the Grand Prince ordered him to say to them: “On which charter you kissed the cross to the grand princes, according to that charter you should act for your sovereigns, and according to that kissing of the cross; and if anyone hears from his brother, a Novgorodian, about the grand princes, good or evil, you are to tell that to your sovereigns the grand princes; and if the grand princes begin to speak with you about any of their affairs, or the Grand Prince’s boyars speak with any of you about the affair of the grand princes, you are not to divulge that sovereign affair, according to that kissing of the cross.” And all those boyars agreed to that on which they had kissed the cross to them, their sovereigns the grand princes.

And on the same day the archbishop petitioned the Grand Prince that he would grant this and order his bailiffs to be given in the districts and villages, because the Christian refugees did not dare to leave the city; and the Grand Prince ordered bailiffs to be given to them.
