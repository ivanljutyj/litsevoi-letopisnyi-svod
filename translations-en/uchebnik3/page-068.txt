This queen Irene and her son Constantine later lived unrighteously and fell away from the kingdom not by their own will.

After them the kingdom was received by Nikephoros Genikos. He died, and after him his son Stavrikios remained emperor for one year and two months. And he lay in his chamber because of a disease of the thigh, and then Michael Rangabe, the son-in-law of Nikephoros, was named emperor by all, while his brother-in-law Emperor Stavrikios, the son of Nikephoros, did not know of it. When Stavrikios heard of this, he wept, but received help from nowhere and was tonsured a monk together with his wife Theophano. All of them, from the Seventh Council to this day, venerate the holy icons.

The beast-named Leo the Armenian rose up against Emperor Michael Rangabe, wishing to seize his kingdom; but Emperor Michael Rangabe yielded to him his imperial throne, which many desired to obtain, and put on the monastic habit.

And so Leo became emperor. He wished to destroy the whole family of Emperor Michael Rangabe and cut off the genitals of his son Niketas, tonsured him a monk, and named him Ignatios; later he became a hierarch in Constantinople. After some time this beast-named Leo the Armenian became a fierce iconoclast, and so by God’s permission there began the second period of iconoclasm, which began because of our sins, and again God’s churches were left without adornment.

Against this same beast-named Leo the Armenian rose Michael Traulos of Amorium, and he killed him and accepted the scepter of the kingdom, but he too did not venerate the holy icons. And although he did not venerate icons, he ordered the release of people imprisoned because of the holy icons, while those who did not submit to his heresies he ordered expelled from the city, but not tortured or imprisoned. And he reigned after Leo the Armenian 8 years and 9 months.

And after him his son Theophilos received the kingdom. He raged against the icons, was more furious than the others, and subjected many saints who did not submit to him to various tortures and death.

The Seventh Council was convened in the reign of Emperor Constantine and his mother Irene, in the year of the pontificate of Pope Adrian of Rome, in Constantinople in the year of the pontificate of Patriarch Tarasios.

After Pope Adrian of Rome, there was Pope Leo in Rome; the relatives of the former Pope Adrian envied this Pope Leo and drove him from the sacred throne and from Rome.

And Pope Leo was greatly grieved by this event; he left his throne and found help nowhere, and sent to Constantinople to Emperor Constantine, the son of Leo, and his mother Irene, asking them for help, but did not receive it.
