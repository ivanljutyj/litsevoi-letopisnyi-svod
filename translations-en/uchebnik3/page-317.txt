On the Construction of the Church of Saint Athanasius

In the same year, in the city of Moscow at the Frolov Gates, Yuri Grigoriev, son of Bobykin, built a brick church of Saint Athanasius of Alexandria.

On the Taking of Smolensk, How the Grand Prince Went for the Third Time to Smolensk and Took Smolensk

In the same year, on the eighth day of June, the Grand Prince of All Rus’ Vasily Ivanovich set out from Moscow with an army for the third time for the city of Smolensk, and with him his brothers Prince Yuri and Prince Semyon Ivanovich. And in Moscow he left his brother Andrei Ivanovich.

And ahead of him he sent to the city of Smolensk his commanders Prince Boris Ivanovich Gorbaty, and Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Gorbaty-Kiselka, and his boyar and equerry Ivan Andreevich, and many other commanders with many men.

And those commanders, having come, surrounded the city. And at that time there was in the city of Smolensk the commander and viceroy of the king, Pan Yuri Sologubovich.

And in the month of July the Grand Prince himself came with his brothers to the city of Smolensk with many forces and with a great train of cannon and arquebus ordnance.

And having placed large cannon and arquebuses around the city, he ordered them to strike the city from all sides, to make great assaults without respite, and to fire upon the city with fiery cannon. And from the roar of cannon and arquebuses, from human shouting and wailing, and also from the answering cannon and arquebus fire of the city’s people, the earth trembled so that they could not see one another, and the whole city, amid flame and billowing smoke, seemed to rear up. And great fear fell upon the townspeople.

And then they began to shout and cry from the city that the great sovereign should show mercy, stay his sword, and order the fighting to cease, and that they wished to submit to the sovereign and surrender the city. And the great sovereign soon ordered the fighting to cease.
