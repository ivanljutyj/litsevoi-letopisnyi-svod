Thanksgiving and Praise for the Joyous Birth, after Barrenness, Granted by God through Prayer to the Autocratic Tsar and Great Prince Vasily, of a Son, the God-crowned Tsar and Great Prince Ivan

Who can tell of the Lord’s power and of all His wonders? Even in ancient times it happened that, at the request of His righteous saints, blessed fruit was granted from their barren loins: to Abraham from Sarah—Isaac; to Elkanah from Anna—Samuel; to Manoah from his wife—Samson; to Zechariah from Elizabeth—the great John the Forerunner; to Joachim and Anna—the most pure Mother of God, our hope and intercessor and the salvation of the world. And many others in the Old Testament and in the New Grace opened barren wombs by prayer. So here too: the most generous great Giver of gifts, Christ, our true God, in His benevolence and mercy, who visited His people in despair and had mercy on His world, heard the diligent prayers of His servant, the pious autocrat, the Christ-loving Great Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich. This most glorious among tsars, Great Prince Vasily, was childless for a long time. And when he was already more than fifty years old, the Lord wished to glorify him. Yet this mighty sovereign did not lessen his zeal in prayers to God, and did not doubt, though he had been childless for a long time, and, not growing despondent, diligently asked for this; he did not cease scattering wealth to the poor and went about the cities and honorable monasteries in the lands of his kingdom.

And he raised up new wondrous monasteries and holy churches in the name of God and of the most pure Mother of God, and renewed churches of All Saints and adorned the depictions of holy icons; and he kindly bestowed gifts on monks dwelling in his kingdom and coming from other lands, inspiring all to prayer.

What a wonder! How, from such exceedingly great royal height, could he come to such voluntary humility of mind that he walked on foot with his royal feet even through the most distant deserts, with his God-loving Grand Princess Elena and with his nobles. And as he himself was drawn to God by faith and love, so God gave him such a helper, who in wisdom and understanding followed in all things her autocratic, God-wise spouse; always placing her hope in God, being strengthened by faith and rejoicing in hope; for it is known that faith conquers, and hope does not put to shame.
