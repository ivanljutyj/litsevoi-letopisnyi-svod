And he came to the monastery of the Life-giving Trinity and to the Venerable Wonderworker Sergius, and the abbot with the brethren and with the priests and all the choir singers met the Grand Prince at the monastery gates with icons and lamps.

This was seventy days before his departure to God. He was with his Grand Princess Elena and with his noble children in the most renowned monastery of the Life-giving Trinity on the commemoration of the great wonderworker among the venerable, Sergius, and he celebrated the wonderworker’s memory according to the order, and performed a prayer service, and confirmed the brethren there in their rights.

And from the Trinity the Grand Prince rode with the Grand Princess and with his children to his patrimony at Volok Lamsky to take his ease; the Grand Prince rode to Volok to his village of Ozeretskoye and reached his village of Ozeretskoye.

And there, as if after some visitation from God, he began to ail; and there appeared on his leg a sign of illness, a small sore on the left side in the upper part of the leg, on the bend near the private parts, the size of a pin’s head: it had no head, nor was there pus in it, but it was dark red. And then all the more he began to think to himself that his passage was approaching from this brief life to eternal life, blessed rest; and from that time he turned more earnestly in his thoughts to God in prayer.

From Ozeretskoye Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich went to the village of Nokhabnoye.

From Nokhabnoye he rode with difficulty, afflicted by illness, to Pokrovskoye in Funikovo; and there he celebrated the feast of the Holy Mother of God.

And from there he rode to his village of Pokrovskoye, and there he stayed two days.

On the third day he came to Volok with difficulty, in great illness, on the Sunday after the Protection.

The physicians cared for him greatly, but could not help him at all.

And on that same day there was a feast in honor of the Grand Prince at Ivan Yuryevich Shigona’s, the steward of Tver and Volok.

In the morning, on Monday, with enormous difficulty the Grand Prince reached the bathhouse; and he sat at table in the bedchamber with great difficulty.

In the morning, on Tuesday, the weather was fine for the sovereign to hunt; and he sent for his huntsmen, for Fyodor Mikhailov, son of Nagoy, and for Boris Vasilyev, son of Dyatlov.

And he did not desist, wishing to go hunting, and he rode to his village of Kolp, afflicted by illness and sorrowing; while riding to that village, he hunted a little.

When he came to Kolp, he sat at table with difficulty; and he sent for his brother, Prince Andrei Ivanovich, inviting him to hunt with him.
