On Kokshenga

Grand Prince Ivan and the tsarevich with him, having gone to Kokshenga, took their towns, and ravaged all that land and took captives. They went as far as the mouth of the Vaga and to Osinovo Pole, and from there returned back all safe with great booty.

In the same year, on the fourth day of the month of June, the Grand Prince married his son, Grand Prince Ivan, to Maria, daughter of Grand Prince Boris Alexandrovich of Tver.

In the same year a son was born to the Grand Prince on the first day of the month of August and was named Andrey.

In the year 6961 from Adam (the year 1461 from Christ)

On the ninth day of the month of April, Moscow and the whole Kremlin burned down.

On the Capture of Tsargrad by the Godless Akhmet the Turk, Son of Amurat. Concerning This Same Tsargrad Let Us Also Make a Beginning: by Whom It Was Founded, and Why It Was Called Byzantium, and by Whom It Was Named, and by Whom It Was Called Tsargrad

In the year 6803 (1303 from the Incarnation of Christ) a certain king arose in Egypt named Nectanav, of the lineage of King Philix, who ruled the world. And this King Nectanav was a sorcerer, and he begot Alexander of Macedon by Olympias, the wife of Philip. And this Alexander ruled the whole world; Alexander lived thirty years and died. After her son's death, Alexander's mother returned to her father, to the Ethiopian king Phola.

Phola gave her in marriage to Viz, a kinsman of Aktan. Viz begot a daughter by her, and gave her the name Antia, and founded a city in Sosveni; and he named that city after his daughter's name and his own name — Byzantium, which is now called Constantinople.

In the year 5803 there reigned in Rome God's helper, the great Constantine Flavius, who with great zeal gathered Christians from everywhere and began to strengthen and spread the Christian faith, to adorn God's churches and raise up new glorious ones, and to smash idols and turn their houses to the glory of God. And in addition he established many laws, so that only Christ's hierarchs and Christians should own the idolatrous shrines and care for them; to fast on Wednesday and Friday in remembrance of Christ's Passion; and on Sunday to celebrate Christ's Resurrection. But he did not permit the Jews to offer sacrifices or to condemn anyone to crucifixion.
