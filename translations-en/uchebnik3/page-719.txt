In the same year, by the command of the Sovereign Tsar and Grand Prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, Autocrat, the painting of the Church of the Annunciation of the Most Pure, which is on the passages, was renewed; and he ordered the icons to be adorned with gold and stones.

In the same month of August, on the 26th day, there was a great flood, as in spring, and on the Moscow River the floating bridge was carried away, and all the people from Zarechye traveled to the city on ferries and in boats; and in the courtyards near the bank, buildings were swept away.

In the same year stone churches were built in the New Town: at the Iosifovsky metochion, the Church of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Theotokos; the Church of the Holy Myrrh-bearing Women in the New Town by the Ustyug courtyard; the Church of the Life-giving Trinity by the Old Fields.

In the year 7075 from Adam (the year 1575 from Christ)

On the first day of September, at Mozhaisk, at the Dobreysky post station, a deadly pestilence appeared: people were dying of sores. And the Sovereign Tsar and Grand Prince ordered a strong guard and sentries to be established around that place, and he ordered that no people from those places be allowed through to Moscow or to the Moscow towns. And by God’s mercy, in the same year that pestilence subsided in those places.

In the same month of September, on the 3rd day, Yakov Zmiyev arrived from Crimea, and with him there arrived from Crimea to the Tsar and Grand Prince messengers from Tsar Devlet-Girei, the Tsar’s messenger Kormish-Murza, the Tsar’s falconer, and messengers from the tsareviches, the tsaritsas, and the murzas. And in all they numbered 130 persons, apart from the Turkish merchants.

In the same month of September, on the 10th day, Pimen, Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, wrote from Great Novgorod to the Sovereign Tsar and Grand Prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, that in Great Novgorod a deadly pestilence had appeared in the suburb on sixteen streets, and many people were dying of sores. And the deadly pestilence lasted in Novgorod and in the pyatinas for eight months, and by God’s mercy it ceased from the first day of May.

In the same month of September, on the 15th day, Vladyka Simeon and Boyar Petr Vasilyevich Morozov with his companions wrote from Smolensk that in the month of September a plague had appeared in the city of Smolensk and in the suburb, and many people were dying of sores. And such was God’s punishment in the city and in the suburb that people of the priestly and monastic order died out, townspeople with their wives and children, and boyar people; countless of them died, and many homes became empty, and many churches stood without singing; likewise in the district the pestilence was great, and this affliction lasted from September to the month of March; but God in His mercy preserved the service people who were in the district.
