The God-loving Archbishop Macarius, true shepherd of Christ’s flock, at the word of the sovereign Grand Prince immediately appointed this humble monk Theodosius in the cathedral church of Holy Sophia in Veliky Novgorod, on the feast of the Entry of the Holy Mother of God into the Church, on the twenty-first day of November, according to the sacred rite as reader, subdeacon, and deacon, and ordained him priest; and he also read over him the prayer of rank that is proper for abbots. And having instructed him in spiritual matters—how to persevere in fasting, in prayers, and in vigils, and in all virtues, and how to care for his flock unto salvation, as much as God grants—he sent him to the monastery of the God-pleasing Transfiguration and the great Wonderworker Varlaam, with his boyar sons and with the choir singers, doing honor to the holy church by the spiritual singing of the honorable monastery.

(9) On the Loplyane, how they were baptized and built holy churches

That same winter, from the Kola River, from Tutoloma, Loplyane of the Murman Sea came to Veliky Novgorod and petitioned the Most Reverend Macarius, asking for antimensia and priests to consecrate the churches of God and that they themselves might be enlightened by holy baptism.

The God-loving Archbishop Macarius sent a priest and a deacon from the cathedral church of Holy Sophia, and they, having gone, consecrated churches of God: the Annunciation of the Holy Mother of God, and that of the Wonderworker Nicholas, in St Philip’s Fast; and beyond Svyatoy Nos they baptized many of the Loplyane themselves in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit into our Orthodox and holy faith.

On the Building and Consecration of the Church of the Dormition of the Holy Mother of God in Veliky Novgorod

In that same year the sovereign Grand Prince of All Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, sent to Veliky Novgorod to his pilgrim Archbishop Macarius and to his clerks Yakov Shishkin and Afanasy Funikov, and commanded them to build a wooden church.

And by the command of the sovereign Grand Prince they built a church in the stone city, by the wall, in the name of the Dormition of the Holy Mother of God, and in the side-chapel in the name of St John, namesake of the sovereign Grand Prince.

And the Most Reverend Macarius himself consecrated it very orderly and honorably on the twelfth day of March.

On the Building of Two Churches

In that same summer they made two stone churches: one of the Holy Apostle James, son of Zebedee, and the other of the Conception of St John the Forerunner, with side-chapels.
