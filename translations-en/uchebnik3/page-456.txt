And the sovereign rewarded the commanders and the sons of boyars with his great bounty: whomever petitioned him about anything, he rewarded them all according to their petition, for he himself had a noble desire to suffer for Orthodoxy.

And from that time discord began in Kazan: the tsar stopped trusting the princes, “for they had brought in the Grand Prince’s commanders”; and he began to kill them.

And many of them left Kazan for the Grand Prince, while others went to other lands.

On the Grand Prince’s Journey to the Monasteries

That same spring, on May 21, Thursday of the seventh week after Easter, the Grand Prince went to the Sergius Monastery for the feast, to pray to the Life-Giving Trinity, and with him were his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich and Prince Vladimir Andreyevich; and at the feast of the Life-Giving Trinity they heard the all-night vigil, matins, prayer service, divine liturgy, and vespers; and in the refectory he gave the abbot and the brethren ample food and alms; and from the Trinity he went to pray to the wonderworkers in Pereslavl, and from Pereslavl he sent his brothers back to Moscow, while he himself went to Rostov and Yaroslavl, and to Beloozero to the Kirillov Monastery, and to the Ferapontov Monastery, and to Vologda to the Savior on the Pryluk, and to the Korniliev Monastery, and to Pavlov, and to Boris and Gleb on the Ustye; and in all those monasteries he fed and gave ample alms, and he came to Moscow on the 7th day of July.

On the Envoys from Kazan

In the same year, on July 29, Kadysh-knyaz and Chur Narykov sent to the Grand Prince from Kazan a son of the boyars, Vaska Davydov son of Bortev, a Galich man, with the request that the sovereign send an army to Kazan, and they would hand over the tsar and thirty Crimeans who were with the tsar in the city.

And the Grand Prince sent them word with his favor, that they should seize and hold the tsar, and the Grand Prince would send his army to them.

In the year 7054 from Adam (the year 1554 from Christ)

On September 3 the Grand Prince ordered Afanasy Buturlin punished: his tongue was cut out at the prison for his guilt, for disrespectful words. In the same month, on the 15th, Tuesday, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus set out for the Life-Giving Trinity at the Sergius Monastery to pray at the memory of the wonderworker, and with him was his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich; and the Grand Prince heard the all-night vigil, matins, prayer service, and liturgy, and gave the abbot and brethren ample food and alms; and from there the Grand Prince went with his brother to his hunting at Sloboda.
