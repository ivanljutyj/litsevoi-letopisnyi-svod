And Islam detained Nebolsa and did not let him go to the grand prince, lest he bring him word that he had broken his oath and committed treason, lying to his own ruin.

And when he came to the grand prince’s borderland, to the Seversky Donets, he then released Nebolsa, while Islam himself with the tsareviches went to Ryazan.

And Nebolsa came to Moscow on the twelfth day of August and told the grand prince that Tsar Islam, Tsar Safa-Girey, and other Crimean tsareviches with all their people, and also many people who had come to them, were marching on the grand prince’s borderland, on Ryazan.

And the grand prince, having learned of this, immediately began to muster against the tsareviches and sent for his brothers, Prince Andrei and Prince George.

And on that same day he sent ahead of himself to Kolomna, to the Oka River, to the bank, his commanders: Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky, and his boyar and commander Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky, and Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Gorbaty, and Mikhail Semenovich Vorontsov; and at Kolomna were the grand prince’s commanders Prince Ivan and his brother Prince Semyon Fedorovich Belsky, and other commanders with their men.

And the grand prince himself left the reigning city of Moscow on the fifteenth day of August; having heard the divine liturgy on the feast of the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God and having sung a prayer service, he fell before the image of the Savior Almighty and, praying with tears and calling upon His Most Pure Mother for help, said: “O most merciful and all-bountiful Lover of mankind, Jesus Christ, Son of God, my hope and my deliverer! See now those who wrong and oppress Your inheritance; arise, O Lord, to help us, and do not allow our godless and accursed enemies to prevail over us: for You are our God, and besides You we know no other God, and we call upon Your name, and from You we all seek help. Do not forsake me, O Lord my God, through the prayers of Your Most Pure Mother, the ever-virgin Mother of God, and of Your holy saints!”

And he came to the tomb of the wonderworker the great hierarch Peter, and kissed his precious shrine; his eyes filled with tears, and he prayed, saying: “O Master, do not despise Your servants and do not forsake the supplication of those who with God place their hope in You, but bring a change to the wars and sorrows that have come!” And then he received a blessing from the metropolitan and went against his foes, the Crimean tsareviches, and with him was his brother Prince Yuri Ivanovich.

And as he departed, the grand prince commanded the city commanders to set up cannons and arquebuses in the city and the city people to bring their property within the city.
