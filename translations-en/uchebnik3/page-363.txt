About Tsar Safa-Girey

That same summer, on May 17, Postnik Golovin came to the grand prince from Kazan and told the grand prince that Tsar Safa-Girey had wanted to kill Ivan Polev and his companions and had become an enemy to the grand prince.

And when the Kazan envoys came from the grand prince with letters—and the grand prince wrote in the letter to the tsarevna, the princes, and all the Kazan land that the sovereign wished to favor and protect them and had set aside his anger against the tsarevna, the princes, and all the Kazan land—then the tsarevna and Kachigaley-murza, and Prince Bulat at their head, and the ulans, princes, seids, murzas, and all the Kazan people gathered in one place and did not let Tsar Safa-Girey be killed, but by the grand prince's instruction they expelled him and the tsar's counselors, the Crimeans and Nogais, from Kazan; and others who had incited the tsar to harm they killed: Prince Rasta with his children, and Ali, son of Shukor, and other princes.

And afterward they expelled him and his tsaritsa from Kazan to the Nogais, to her father Mamai. And to the grand prince they sent a petition.

And in the same month, on the 19th day, Kulchura and Foruzat came to the grand prince from Kazan with letters from Tsarevna Gorshedna and from Kachigaley-murza, and from Prince Bulat, and from the ulans, and from the princes, and from the seid, and from the murzas, and from all the people of the Kazan land.

And they wrote in the letters to the grand prince: "By the sovereign's instruction we have expelled Tsar Safa-Girey from Kazan; and the sovereign, wishing to favor and protect the Kazan land, wants to give us Tsar Shigaley as tsar in Kazan; but we are afraid of Tsar Shigaley; let the sovereign favor us and give us Tsarevich Yanaley, son of Tsarevich Shig-Avliyar, as tsar in Kazan."

And the great sovereign sent Ostanya Andreyev to Kazan, and with him he released Kulchura and Foruzat.

And he conveyed to the murzas, the princes, and the ulans, headed by the tsarevna, and to all the Kazan land his great favor: that he wished to favor and protect them, and was giving them Tsar Yanaley for Kazan, and wished to call him his brother and son. And the grand prince ordered Tsar Shigaley to be with him in Moscow. And to Tsarevich Yanaley in Gorodok the grand prince sent Fyodor Demid Semenov's son Vorontsov, and ordered him to be in Vasile-gorod on July 22.
