On the Founding of the Church of the Wonderworker Alexis and the Refectory in the Chudov Monastery

In that year Gennady Gonozov, archimandrite of the Chudov Monastery, founded a stone church in the name of the Wonderworker Alexis in the Chudov Monastery and a stone refectory.

On the Capture of Kiev by the Crimean Tsar.
In the year 6992 from Adam (1492 from Christ)

On September 1, at the word of Ivan Vasilievich, Grand Prince of all Rus, Menli-Girey, Tsar of the Crimean Perekop Horde, came with all his force against the royal realm, took and burned the city of Kiev, captured the Kiev voivode Pan Ivashko Khotkevich as a prisoner, and took countless captives; and he devastated the land of Kiev because the king had failed to fulfill his obligations, because he had brought Akhmat, Tsar of the Great Horde, with all his forces against Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and had sought to destroy the Christian faith.

On the Sending of Voivodes against Yasyk and Yuzshan

The Grand Prince sent his voivodes, Prince Fyodor Romanovich Kurbsky and Ivan Ivanovich Saltyk Travin, against Yasyk and Yuzshan.

Setting out with the Vologdians and Ustyugians, they hastened against Yuzshan; he, after fighting them briefly, fled into impassable places and precipices.

On Yuzshan’s Petition and How the Grand Prince Granted Him Favor

A year later Yuzshan, son of Yasyk, of the Gogulichi, came to the Grand Prince under a safe-conduct, having negotiated with Philotheus, Bishop of Perm; and the Grand Prince granted Yuzshan favor and ordered him to pay tribute to him.

On the Installation of an Archbishop in Novgorod

On the 4th day of the same month, Sergius, formerly Simeon, protopope of the Mother of God, was installed as archbishop of Novgorod and Pskov.

On the Birth of Prince Dmitry,
Son of Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich

In the same autumn, on October 10, in the 10th hour of the night, on Friday, on the commemoration of the holy martyr Evlampius, a son was born to Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich and was named Dmitry on October 26.
