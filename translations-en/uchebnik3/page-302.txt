On the Release of the Envoys to Kazan.
In the year 7016 from Adam (the year 1516 from Christ)

On September 8 Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' released the Kazan envoy Barash-Seit, and with Tsar Magamet-Amin he concluded peace, brotherhood, and friendship; and he sent to the tsar in Kazan his envoy, the boyar and okolnichy Ivan Grigoryevich Poplevin, and the clerk Alexei Lukin, with peace, friendship, and brotherhood, as had been with his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus'.

The Grand Prince Sent His Commanders to Mstislavl

In the same month, September, on the 14th day, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' sent his commanders Prince Vasily Danilovich Kholmsky and Yakov Zakharyich to Mstislavl to conquer Lithuanian lands.

On the Placement of the Relics of the Great and Appanage Princes in the Church of the Archangel Michael

In the same autumn, on October 7, the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus', on the advice of his father Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus', ordered places to be prepared and the relics of his forebears, the great Russian princes, to be transferred.

Metropolitan Simon, with the whole consecrated council, sang the funeral hymns, and they were laid in the new church of the Holy Archangel Michael, which his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus' had founded. By the southern wall, from Saint Simeon, was laid Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich the Dark; and beside him, his son Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus'; and beside his father Grand Prince Ivan was laid Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' himself, in monasticism as Varlaam. And on the other side of the same southern doors, along the same southern side, by the wall, they laid the relics of Grand Prince Ivan Daniilovich; and beside him they laid his eldest son, Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich the Proud; and beside Grand Prince Simeon Ivanovich the Proud they laid Prince Yuri Vasilyevich. And behind them, at their heads, along the same wall, by the wall, they laid Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, the second son of Grand Prince Ivan Daniilovich; and beside him they laid his son Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Donskoy; and next to him, Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of Uglich, son of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich.
