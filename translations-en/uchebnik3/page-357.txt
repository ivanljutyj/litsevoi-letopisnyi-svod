On the Installation and Consecration of the Wooden Church of St John the Forerunner at Old Vagankovo

In the same month of August, the right-believing and Christ-loving great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, ruler of all Rus, built at the Annunciation on Old Vagankovo a votive wooden church of the Beheading of the Head of John the Baptist of the Lord, and in it two side chapels: of the Holy Apostle Thomas and of St Peter the Wonderworker. The great sovereign did this in fulfillment of his vow, and with his own royal hands he took part before all the craftsmen, and after him they began to build and completed it in a single day.

On the Consecration

On that same day it was also consecrated. At that consecration were the Grand Prince with the Grand Princess Elena and their son Prince Ivan, the boyars, and a multitude of people.

On the Installation of Archimandrite Siluan at the Yuriev Monastery in Veliky Novgorod

In that same year the sovereign, the Grand Prince and autocrat of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, sent to his pilgrim Archbishop Macarius in Veliky Novgorod and commanded the archbishop to ordain his archdeacon, the monk Siluan, as priest, to confer on him the priestly rank and the palitsa, and to send him as archimandrite to the Yuriev Monastery.

And the God-loving Archbishop Macarius, at the word of the sovereign Grand Prince, ordained the humble elder monk Siluan as priest in the cathedral church of Holy Sophia in Veliky Novgorod, and laid upon him the rank and the palitsa, on the second day of October. And he sent him with his boyar sons and with the choristers to the monastery of the glorious Great Martyr George as archimandrite, doing honor to the holy church and the honorable monastery with spiritual songs.

On the Fire in Veliky Novgorod

That same summer, by God's permission, there was a fire on Yanev Street by the prisons before Matins. It burned on Doslan Street, and the church of the Holy Forty Martyrs burned down. And all three prisons burned: from two prisons the Lithuanians were brought out, but from the third, the city prison, they did not manage to bring them out, and 145 people burned in the prison.

And the God-loving Archbishop Macarius ordered a pit dug by the church of the Holy Archangel Gabriel at the end of Khrenkova Street, and he himself sang the funeral hymns, and with him the priests of the Cathedral of Holy Sophia and the priests and deacons of the whole city, and they buried them.
