He ordered the execution of the boyar sons: Vladimir Elizarov, son of Gusev; Prince Ivan Paletsky Khrul and Poyark, Runo’s brother; and Shchavy Skryabin, son of Travin; and the appointed deacon Fyodor Stromilov; and Afanasy Yaropkin. They executed them on the ice and cut off their heads on December 27.

The Grand Prince Bestowed the Grand Principality on His Grandson

That same winter, on February 4, a Sunday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus blessed and installed his grandson Prince Dmitry Ivanovich in the grand principality of Vladimir, Moscow, and All Rus, and his elevation to the throne took place in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God in Moscow. With the blessing of Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus, Archbishop Tikhon of Rostov, and the bishops Nifont of Suzdal, Vassian of Tver, Protasius of Ryazan, Abraham of Kolomna, Euthymius of Sarsk, and the whole sacred council, they placed on him Monomakh’s barmas and cap, and his uncle Prince Yuri Ivanovich showered the prince three times with gold and silver: before the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure, before the Church of Archangel Michael, and before the Church of the Annunciation. This took place in the 7006th year from Adam (the 1506th year from Christ), on February 4, on the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee.

On the Installation of the Grandson in the Grand Principality

In the middle of the church they prepared a special dais on which hierarchs are installed, and on that dais they set three chairs: for Grand Prince Ivan, for his grandson Dmitry, and for the metropolitan.

And when the time came, the metropolitan and archbishop, the bishops, archimandrites, abbots, and the whole assembly vested themselves in sacred robes, and ordered an analogion to be placed in the middle of the church, and on it they placed Monomakh’s cap and barmas.

And when the Grand Prince entered the church with his grandson, the metropolitan with the whole assembly began to serve a moleben to the Most Pure Mother of God and to the wonderworker Peter; and after “It Is Truly Meet,” the “Trisagion,” and the troparia, the metropolitan and the Grand Prince sat in their places, while the grandson stood before them by the dais on the upper step, not ascending the dais.
