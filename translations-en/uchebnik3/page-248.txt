On the Repose of Metropolitan Gerontius

In the same month, on the 28th day, Wednesday, at the 11th hour of the day, the most reverend Metropolitan Gerontius reposed and was buried in Moscow in the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure, on the north side.

On the War with Vyatka

In that same spring, on June 11, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus sent his army against Vyatka for their failure to fulfill their oath: Prince Daniel Vasilyevich Shchenya, Gregory Vasilyevich Morozov, and other commanders with a great host.

They, having come, took Vyatka and its towns, and brought the Vyatchans themselves to the kissing of the cross and brought the Ar people to the oath, and took notable people among the Vyatchans with their wives and children with them, as also the Ar princes, and so returned back.

And the Grand Prince settled the zemstvo people from among the Vyatchans in Borovsk and Kremenets and gave them lands, and settled the trading people from among the Vyatchans in Dmitrov; and the Ar princes the Grand Prince granted favor and released to their own land, and the rebels he put to death.

On the Consecration of the Church of the Annunciation on the Porches

In the same year, on August 9, on the memory of the holy apostle Matthew, the church of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God was consecrated in the courtyard of the Grand Prince on the porches.

On the Consecration of the Chapel of Saint Basil in the Church of the Annunciation

In the same month, on the 20th day, on the memory of the holy prophet Samuel, the church of Saint Basil of Caesarea was consecrated, which had been a chapel in the Annunciation.

On the Death of Matthias, King of Hungary.
In the year 6998 from Adam (1498 from Christ)

Matthias, King of Hungary, died, and with the Grand Prince he had remained in love and peace and brotherhood.

And Maximilian, King of the Romans, wished also to have power over the Hungarian land, but the Hungarian lords and the whole country did not wish to accept him.

Maximilian sent his army against Hungarian Belgrade and captured it; and the Hungarian lords sent to Vladislaus, King of Bohemia, son of Casimir, King of Poland, and invited him from the Kingdom of Bohemia to the Kingdom of Hungary.
