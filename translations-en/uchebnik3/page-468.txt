They brought the metropolitan to his own monastery at Novoe barely alive. And in the city all the households and chambers were burning, and the Chudov Monastery burned down entirely; only the relics of the holy great wonderworker Alexis were preserved by God’s mercy.

And 18 elders burned in the cellars and chambers, and 8 servants; and all the monastery’s supplies burned.

And the Ascension Monastery also burned down entirely; 10 nuns burned in it, and the Church of the Ascension burned, with its icons and church vessels, and many people; the archpriest carried out only one image of the Most Pure (Mother of God). And all the households in the city burned, and the roof on the wall, and the cannon powder that was on the wall, and in those places the city walls were blown apart.

And in the other town all the shops with their goods burned, and all the households in the town; God preserved only 2 churches—the Nativity of Christ on the Moat and the Nativity of the Most Pure (Mother of God)—and at the Nikolsky crossroads about 10 shops. And in many stone churches the icons and church vessels burned.

And beyond the town—the Great Settlement by the Neglinnaya River, the Cannon Yard, the Church of the Holy Righteous Ancestors of God Joachim and Anna, and Rozhdestvenskaya Street, and the Nativity Monastery, and [the Church of] St Sergius as far as the St Nicholas Drachevsky Monastery; and along Sretenskaya Street to the Church of St Stephen; and along Ilyinskaya Street to the Church of St Florus in Myasniki, and along Pokrovskaya Street to the Church of St Basil; and along Varvarovskaya Street to the Church of All Saints and St Paraskeva, and the Nativity of the Most Pure (Mother of God), and St Nicholas Podkopaevsky, and St Florus at the stables, and the Grand Prince’s stables, and to the Vorontsov court, and to the Church of St Elijah at Sosenski; and along Velikaya Street by the Moskva River—the churches of St Nicholas in Koshelev and St Andrew, and the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross by the Moskva River, and Cosmas and Damian, and all Kulishki; and near the Yauza as far as the Vorontsov garden, and the courtyards as far as the stables burned, up to Smolivaya Street; and all kinds of gardens burned, and in the vegetable plots all kinds of vegetables and herbs.

And from the town beyond the moat, on the square from the Church of the Transfiguration of the Lord, the households as far as the Church of All Saints did not burn, along Varvarovskaya Street as far as Kulishki; but behind those households all the households burned, and the Church of the Chains of St Peter and St Simeon, and Cosmas and Damian, and beyond the Church of St Vladimir to the Church of St Elijah and to the Vorontsov garden. And at the third hour of the night the fiery flame ceased. This happened because of our sins; it was sent upon us from God as punishment; for the multiplication of our sins God did not spare such a multitude of holy churches and His holy icons; this fiery flame came by God’s wrath.
