Concerning the Turkish Ambassador

That same spring, on May 28, there came to Moscow to the Grand Prince of All Rus Vasily Ivanovich, from Constantinople, from the Turkish Sultan Salim-Shah-Khandiker, an ambassador named Kemalbi, Prince of Mingun, concerning brotherhood and love.

On the Renovation of the Icon of the Most Pure Mother of God of Vladimir

By the command of the faithful Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign autocrat of all Rus, on the advice and blessing of the most reverend Varlaam, Metropolitan of All Rus, the image of our Most Pure Lady, the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, the Vladimir Icon, was renovated and adorned; it was painted with her blessing, during her lifetime, by the God-inspired Luke, Apostle and Evangelist. Every year, on the 21st day of May, they go in procession to the Meeting of the Most Pure Mother of God.

The faithful Prince Andrei Bogolyubsky, son of Grand Prince Yuri Dolgoruky Vladimirovich, brought this same wondrous icon of the Most Pure Mother of God from Constantinople and placed it in the cathedral church of the Holy Mother of God in Vladimir in the year 6662 from Adam (1162 from Christ); and it stood in Vladimir for 240 years.
