And the Tsaritsa of Shigalei petitioned the Grand Prince’s boyars to intercede with the Grand Lady that the Grand Princess might receive her.

And Grand Lady Elena graciously granted her an audience and ordered her to be admitted to her presence.

And when Tsaritsa Fatma-Saltan arrived at the palace, the Grand Princess ordered Agrofena Ivanova, wife of Volynsky, to meet her at the sledge, together with young noblewomen.

And when the tsaritsa reached the middle of the staircase, the Grand Lady ordered her own noblewoman Agrofena, wife of Vasily Andreevich, to meet her there, together with young noblewomen as well.

And when the tsaritsa entered the vestibule before the hall at the Church of Saint Lazarus, the Grand Lady graciously honored the tsaritsa: she herself met her in the vestibule before the hall, exchanged courtesies with her, and went with her into the hall.

And when the Grand Princess entered the hall, the Grand Prince came into his mother’s hall to see the tsaritsa; and the tsaritsa rose to meet him and left her place, and the Grand Prince said to the tsaritsa, “Tabugsalam,” and exchanged greetings with her.

And the Grand Prince sat in his place beside his mother, at the tsaritsa’s right hand, and the boyars with him on both sides. And the Grand Princess’s noblewomen were: Princess Nastasya, wife of Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky, and Elena, wife of Ivan Andreevich, and Agripena, wife of Vasily Andreevich, and Agripena, wife of Vasily Volynsky, and many other noblewomen.

And that day Tsaritsa Fatma-Saltan dined with the Grand Lady in the hall at Saint Lazarus, while the Grand Prince dined with the boyars in his mother’s quarters. And at the Grand Princess’s table the tsaritsa sat on the right side in the corner, and on the Grand Princess’s left side there sat on the long bench Princess Nastasya, wife of Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky; and below her sat Elena, wife of Ivan Andreevich, and Agripena, wife of Vasily Andreevich, and other noblewomen; and on the bench sat Princess Marfa, wife of Prince Dmitry Fyodorovich Belsky, and below her Agripina, wife of Ivan Volynsky, and other noblewomen, while other noblewomen sat on the bench. And Ivan Ivanovich Chelyadnin served as carver to the Grand Lady, and Prince Vasily Repnin was carver to the tsaritsa, and all the table attendants and cupbearers were at the Grand Princess’s table.

And after the meal the Grand Lady presented the cup to the tsaritsa, and there bestowed gifts upon her, and sent her back to her residence, and the Grand Lady ordered her noblewomen to escort her out just as they had met her.

That same autumn, on November 28, Danilko Smagin came from Kazan to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and to his mother, Grand Princess Elena, bringing letters from the tsar and from the Kazan princes.
