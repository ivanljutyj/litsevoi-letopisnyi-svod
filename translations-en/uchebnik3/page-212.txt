And he ordered them that same day to present the list of what all Great Novgorod was to kiss the cross to them, the Grand Princes; and they petitioned that the Grand Prince would deign to send that list into the city and order it to be presented to all Great Novgorod.

And the Grand Prince sent that list into the city with the under-secretary Odinets, and ordered it to be presented to Novgorod in the archbishop's chamber.

On the 12th day of January, a Monday, the archbishop came to the Grand Prince, and with him the aforementioned posadniks and wealthy townsmen, and they petitioned that they might speak with the boyars; and the Grand Prince sent boyars to them for negotiations. And concerning the Grand Prince's Yaroslav's Court they answered thus: "That, sir, is the court of our sovereigns, the Grand Princes; if they wish to take that court of theirs, that is before God and before them; and if our sovereigns wish to take a place in the neighborhood opposite that court of theirs, that too, sir, is before them; and as for the oath list, sir, which our sovereign the Grand Prince has ordered to be presented to his patrimony, Great Novgorod, according to which Novgorod is to kiss the cross to its sovereigns, the Grand Princes, their patrimony knows that list, and to all of it they kiss the cross to their sovereigns; and may our sovereigns, the Grand Princes, show favor to their patrimony as God lays it on their hearts; and their patrimony, Great Novgorod, bows before its sovereigns and places all hope in God and in them, its sovereigns, the Grand Princes."

And the Grand Prince ordered the Novgorod secretary to write out the oath record from his list word for word, according to which the Novgorodians were to kiss the cross to them, the Grand Princes; and he ordered the archbishop to sign that list with his own hand and affix his seal, and to affix a seal from each of the five ends to that list. And on the following day, Tuesday, the Grand Prince ordered the archbishop, the posadniks, and the wealthy townsmen to be with him at the Trinity in Paozerye.

On the 13th day of January, Tuesday, the archbishop came to the Grand Prince, and with him many boyars of Novgorod, wealthy men, and merchants; and the archbishop brought the oath record with him, which the archbishop's secretary had written, and the archbishop signed it with his own hand and affixed his seal, and they affixed a seal from each of the five ends.

And many boyars of Novgorod, wealthy men, and merchants kissed the cross on that record to the Grand Princes at the Trinity in Paozerye before the Grand Prince's boyars on the 13th day of January, Tuesday.

And the boyars said to the Novgorodians at the kissing of the cross concerning the Pskovians: "Because the Pskovians served the Grand Princes, you should not take any revenge upon them."
