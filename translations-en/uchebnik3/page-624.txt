The children of Yusuf, Yunus-murza and Ali-murza with their brothers, wrote and sent orders to petition the tsar and grand prince, and asked Prince Ismail that the tsar and sovereign should grant them the same favor as Prince Ismail. And Prince Ismail and all the Nogai murzas, having united, asked the sovereign for the following: now they are all as one; will the sovereign order them to go against the Crimea themselves or to send their brothers and kinsmen, and as the tsar and grand prince commands them, so they will do.

About a Messenger from the Crimea

In the same month a messenger, Karatzhan, came from the Crimea to the tsar and grand prince from Devlet-Giray, tsar of the Crimea, and the tsar and grand prince’s messenger Yushko Moshkov, a serving Tatar; and the tsar released all the captives for ransom, those whom he had captured in battle when he fought with Ivan Sheremetev, fifty men, Ignaty Bludov, the Yakhontovs and others; and three hundred merchants of Arbadazar with their goods. And Tsar Devlet-Giray wrote to the tsar and grand prince that he had already set aside all evil deeds, and that the tsar and grand prince should make peace with him and send good ambassadors, such as could create love between them, and that there should be someone to believe.

And the ambassador of the tsar and grand prince, Fedor Zagryazhsky, wrote that the tsar had been gathering troops all summer and had asked help from the Turkish tsar. He awaited the arrival of the tsar and grand prince against him in the Crimea; and that same autumn, at the Intercession, Prince Dmitry Vishnevetsky took the town of Islam-Kirmen from him and killed the people, and carried the cannon off to his own town on the Dnieper.

And from the other side the Pyatigorsk Cherkesses took two towns, Temryuk and Taman, and the Cherkess princes Tazdrui and Sibok with their brothers, who had been at the tsar and grand prince’s in Moscow, came; and when the tsar learned that the tsar and grand prince would not go to the Crimea that year, he wished to make peace with the tsar and grand prince without deceit, and said he wanted to send ambassadors, Prince Kangala or Murat-murza, son of Suleshev. And the captive Ignaty Bludov and his companions said the same.

About the Arrival of Tsarevich Takhtamysh

In the same month Tsarevich Takhtamysh came from the Nogai Horde, brother of Tsar Shigalei; he had been in the Crimea for many years, and they had intended him for the throne and wanted to kill Devlet-Giray, and the tsar, learning of this, guarded against it, while Takhtamysh fled to the Nogai Horde to Prince Ismail, and Ismail sent him to serve the tsar and grand prince, and with him sent his own ambassador Bikchura.

About the Arrival of Kadysh from Astrakhan

On the same day the serving Tatar of the tsar and grand prince, Kadysh Kudinov, arrived from Astrakhan from Ivan Cheremisinov and Mikhail Kolupaev.
