And he arrived in Pskov on January 24, a Thursday, and bowed to the Life-giving Trinity; and he ordered his patrimony, Pskov, to be brought to the oath of the cross; and he showed favor to the captured Pskovians, released them from confinement, and ordered the best people to go to Moscow to live, and sent their veche bell to Moscow.

And in Pskov he left two governors: Grigory Fedorovich and Ivan Andreevich Chelyadnin, and he arranged everything as it ought to be in his realm.

And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich rode from Pskov after the Council to Novgorod the Great; and from Novgorod he set out in the third week of Lent and arrived in Moscow in the fifth week of Lent, on March 17.

On the arrival of the Grand Prince's envoy from the Crimea

In the same year, in July, the envoy Vasily Grigorievich Poplevin came from the Crimea.

On the arrival in Moscow of the Crimean Tsaritsa Nur-Saltan

In the same month, on July 21, a Sunday, Nur-Saltan, tsaritsa of the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girey, daughter of Temir, came to Moscow, and with her came Menli-Girey's son Saip-Kirey-Saltan. And Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, met her with honor, together with the boyars. And from Tsar Menli-Girey envoys Shiolashikh-Zoda and Lagim-Berdiduvan came to the Grand Prince. And the tsaritsa came to see her children, with Tsar Magamet-Amin of Kazan and Tsar Abdyl-Letif, who serve the Grand Prince.

And the Grand Prince sent the tsaritsa away to Kazan on August 20, a Tuesday; and the tsar's envoys remained in Moscow; and to Tsar Magamet-Amin the Grand Prince sent Ivan Kobyak as envoy.

On the building and consecration of the church at Old Simonovo

In the same year, at Old Simonovo, a brick church was built in the name of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God. It was consecrated on September 1, a Sunday, by His Grace Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, in the presence of Archimandrite Varlaam.

On the Grand Prince's journey.
In the year 7019 from Adam (1519 from Christ)

On September 8, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, left Moscow for Pereslavl, and after himself he ordered the Grand Princess to set out after him. And he was in Pereslavl, and then he was in Yuriev, in Suzdal, in Vladimir, and in Rostov.

And the Grand Prince arrived in Moscow on December 5, a Thursday.
