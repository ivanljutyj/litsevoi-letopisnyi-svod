On the arrival of the Circassian princes to serve the sovereign

In the same month the Circassian princes came to serve the sovereign and petitioned for their future provision: Prince Mashuk Kanukov, Chuguk-Murza, Tokhta-Murza, and Tatar-Murza, who had served the Crimean tsar and was the brother-in-law of the Crimean tsar, since the senior tsaritsa of Tsar Devlet-Girei was his own sister and the daughter of Tarzatyk-Murza, and with him their people. And on their way to the sovereign they came against the uluses of the fugitive Nogai murzas on the Don and took Biichei-Murza, son of Bieriuchei-Murza, and brought him to the sovereign. And the tsar and sovereign showed them favor and provided for them.

The sovereign showed favor to the Nogai murzas

And the sovereign showed favor to the Nogai murzas: he released Magmet-Kazymurza, Saltan Magmetev, and Bechi-Murza Biyurakov, and to the fugitive murzas he sent the serving Tatar Baib erya Tyuyushev, and wrote to them that they should submit to Prince Ismail. And the tsar and grand prince does not order his Cossacks to come against them and orders them to nomad on this side of the Don, along the Khoper and the Medveditsa.

And Tsarevich Takhtamysh also wrote to them, because those murzas were brothers to him, and urged that those captured murzas, Magmet and Bechi, should obey the tsar and grand prince and acknowledge their dependence before Prince Ismail, so that their yurt would not be lost.

On the founding of the city

In the same year, in July, a city was founded at the mouth of the Narva River on the Rozsenya channel by the sea, as a haven for sea-going ships, and Petr Petrov and Ivan Vyrodkov built it.

On the sending from Astrakhan

In the same year, in July, Ivan Cheremisinov and Mikhail Kolupaev sent Vaska Vrazhsky from Astrakhan with the Circassian murza Kavklych Kanukov.
