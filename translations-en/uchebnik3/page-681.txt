And he wrote about his envoys and about friendship, so as to make peace with the tsar and grand prince.

And the tsar and grand prince sent to the tsar service Tatars as well, Sofka Tutaev and his companions, with a letter, and he took one Tatar from the Crimean envoys and sent him to the tsar. And he wrote: as soon as the tsar abandons his follies and can be believed, the tsar and grand prince will make peace with him; and that he had sent men from the sea to scout out the road; Tinbai-Murza with his companions had come from Astrakhan, and he should not think about that; if there are good deeds, then those matters will fall away. And therefore he should judge what is more profitable for him: to make peace or to make war; he leaves that to his will, let him choose the better himself. And with that he released them to Crimea.

The Coming of Tinbai-Murza to the Milky Waters

And Tavkei said that Tinbai-Murza, son of Ismail, with his companions had come to the Milky Waters, and to the Sheep Waters, and to the Horse Waters, and had ravaged many uluses; and many Nogais joined him.

And Tsarevich Kalga Magmet-Girei pursued him, and the Nogais killed many of the tsarevich’s men, and they themselves withdrew unharmed, and drove off about forty thousand horses. And when Danila came from the sea against the uluses, then great fear came upon them from the coming of the tsar and grand prince, and all fled to the mountains, thinking that the sovereign had come; and such great fear of the sovereign fell upon them (because) from the sea and from the Field an approach to the Crimea is possible in many places, and they cannot protect themselves, so that the whole land came to the tsar to ask that he make peace with the tsar and grand prince, because from the Dnieper the uluses suffer great hardship.

The Sovereign Sent Vishnevetsky to the Domain in Cherkasy

In the month of February the tsar and grand prince, at their petition, sent to Cherkasy his voivode Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Vishnevetsky, and together with him he sent the Cherkasy princes: Prince Ivan Omashuk, and Prince Vasily Sibok with his brothers; and he sent Christian priests with him, and ordered them to be baptized according to their petition and their promise to fight with the Crimean tsar.

The Sovereign Sent the Voivodes

The tsar and sovereign and grand prince, at the petition of the Kabardian princes, because of Shevkal’s lawlessness, sent Voivode Ivan Semenovich Cheremisinov with his companions against Shevkal and to Tyumen; and with Ivan he sent Christian priests, according to their promise and petition, to baptize the Kabardian Cherkess.
