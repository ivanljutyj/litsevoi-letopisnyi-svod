In the same autumn, in November, there were great rains and a great flood, and the ice passed as in spring, and many losses were caused to people.

In the same winter, on 29 December, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus could not endure that the boyars were committing disorder and willfulness without the Grand Prince’s command, and by their own counsel and with their like-minded advisers had committed many murders at their own will (and had done many dishonors before the sovereign), and had committed many wrongs against the land during the sovereign’s youth; and the Great Sovereign ordered their chief adviser, Prince Andrei Shuysky, to be seized and ordered him to be handed over to the kennelmen.

And the kennelmen took him and killed him, dragging him toward the prisons, opposite the Rizopolozhensky Gate in the city.

And his advisers (Prince Fyodor Shuysky, Prince Yuri Temkin, Foma Golovin, and others) he sent away; and from that time the boyars began to have fear of the sovereign (and obedience).

In the same winter, on 22 January, at the seventh hour of the day, the metropolitan’s huts burned in the metropolitan’s courtyard; and the metropolitan had then gone to Mozhaisk and to the Pafnutyev Monastery. In the same winter, in February, Bishop Dositheus of Krutitsy reposed.

In the same month, on the 21st, Jonah Sobina, former archimandrite of the Chudov Monastery, was consecrated by Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, as bishop of Suzdal. In the same month, on the 24th, on Cheese-fare Sunday, Savva, former archimandrite of the Simonov Monastery, was consecrated by Metropolitan Macarius as bishop of Krutitsy. In the same winter, on 3 March, on Monday of the second week of Holy Lent, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus set out to pray to the Life-giving Trinity at the Kalyazin Monastery and to the wonderworker Macarius, and with him were his brother Prince Yuri and a multitude of boyars; and the Grand Prince prayed, and satisfied the abbot and the brethren with alms and food abundantly, and from there went to his sovereign hunt in Zabolotye for bears. And from there to the Life-giving Trinity at the Sergius Monastery; and he came to Moscow in the same month on the 18th, on Tuesday.

In the year 7053 from Adam (the year 1553 from Christ)

On 16 December the Grand Prince placed his disgrace upon Prince Ivan Kubensky because he (with other boyars) had not wished well to the Grand Prince, the sovereign, and had committed many wrongs against his realm, and had practiced great bribe-taking, and had caused many seditions, and had destroyed many boyars without the Great Sovereign’s command.
