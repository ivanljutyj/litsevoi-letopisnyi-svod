In the same year there was a sign in the sun and in the moon. When the sun was about to rise, at the first hour of the day, it rose upon a bright cloud, and suddenly darkness came, visible to all people but coming from unknown whence, and hid the whole sun; and for a long time it was not there, and for a long time it gave no rays, until the third hour and later; and the moon appeared in three circles on a cloud for a long time, after the morning hymns had been sung.

On the marriage of Prince Andrei Ivanovich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich

That same winter, in February, on the 22nd day, the Sunday of the Publican and Pharisee, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich married his brother, Prince Andrei Ivanovich, to Princess Evfrosinia, daughter of Prince Andrei Khovansky; and Daniel, Metropolitan of all Rus, wed Prince Andrei Ivanovich and Princess Evfrosinia in the cathedral church of the glorious reigning city of Moscow.

And at the wedding of his brother, Prince Andrei Ivanovich, was the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich with Grand Princess Elena, and with his brother Prince Georgy Ivanovich. And at the wedding the Grand Prince showed favor to his brother, gave gifts to Prince Andrei and his princess Evfrosinia; and Grand Princess Elena and the Grand Prince’s son Prince Ivan Vasilievich also gave them gifts; and there was an honorable feast.

On the arrival of envoys to the Grand Prince from the Tsar and from all the land of Kazan with a letter petitioning concerning cannons

On the 22nd day of the same month there came to the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich at Moscow, from Tsar Yanalei of Kazan, his envoy Agish-Prince with a letter from the tsaritsa and from the princes and from all the people of the Kazan land.

And in the letter the tsar, the tsaritsa, the princes, and all the Kazan land wrote that the great sovereign should show them favor and not order the cannons to be taken from them out of Kazan, because the sovereign’s Kazan land has many friends, but also has enemies.

And the Grand Prince showed favor to his brother and son Tsar Yanalei, and to the princes and all the people of the Kazan land, and did not order the cannons to be taken from Kazan. And the Grand Prince sent Agish away on March 6, and with him sent a letter to the tsar and to the tsaritsa, and to the princes, and to all the Kazan land.

On the journey of the Grand Prince to St Nicholas of Zaraysk

On March 10 the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich left Moscow to pray at St Nicholas of Zaraysk on the Osetr, and came to Moscow on the 19th day of the same month.
