And he was installed and consecrated by the most holy Metropolitan of All Rus in the same month of September, on the 20th day, on a Sunday; and present at his installation were Tikhon, Archbishop of Rostov, and the bishops Nifont of Suzdal, Simeon of Ryazan, Vassian of Tver, Abraham of Kolomna, Silouan of Sarai, and Philotheus of Perm; and Gennady, Archbishop of Novgorod, sent his letter of concord concerning the worthiness of his installation. And when the Divine Service had been completed and the time had come to raise him to the rank of metropolitan, the great prince said: “The Almighty and Life-giving Holy Trinity, which grants us the sovereignty of all Rus, bestows upon you the holy great throne of the hierarchate, the metropolitanate of all Rus, through the laying on of hands and the consecration of the holy fathers, the archbishops and bishops of the Russian realm; and receive, father, the staff of pastoral care, and ascend the seat of hierarchal seniority in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and of His Most Pure Mother, and pray to God and His Most Pure Mother for us and for our children and for all Orthodoxy; and may the Lord God grant you health and long life for many years, unto ages of ages.” The metropolitan’s reply: “May the Almighty and All-Sovereign right hand of the Most High preserve, O Lord God, your God-established sovereignty, and may it abide victorious with all the Christ-loving armies that obey you and with the other peoples forever, throughout all the days of your life. Be well, be well, be well, doing good, pious lord and autocratic great sovereign of ours, for many years.” And the deacons sing Many Years to the great prince.

In that same autumn the Greek boyar Fyodor Laskar and his son Dmitry came from Constantinople to serve the great prince.

On the Campaign of the Great Prince to Novgorod

In that same autumn, on the 20th day of October, a Tuesday, Great Prince Ivan Vasilyevich set out from Moscow for his patrimony, Great Novgorod, and with him were his grandson Prince Dmitry and his son Prince Yury; and in Moscow he left his son Vasily with the younger brothers in the care of Grand Princess Sophia.

And the great prince came to his patrimony, Great Novgorod, on the 11th day of November, a Tuesday; and Gennady, the archbishop of that city, met the sovereign great prince outside the city with crosses and censers, as befitted their realm, with archimandrites and abbots, and priests, and the whole consecrated council of Great Novgorod. And the great prince’s lieutenants in Novgorod at that time were Prince Daniil Alexandrovich Penkov and Prince Semyon Romanovich, and they too met the sovereign with all the people of that city.
