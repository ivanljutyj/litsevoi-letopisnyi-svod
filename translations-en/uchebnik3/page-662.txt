In the year 7067 from Adam (the year 1567 from Christ)

In the month of September, Peter Ivanovich and his comrades wrote to the sovereign: when they were going from Yuryev according to the sovereign’s instruction, they sent the commanders Boris Kolychev and his comrades to Golbin, and the archbishop’s men were standing there at an outpost, and Boris, without reaching Golbin, hid with all his men, and sent Bogdan Rzhanikov ahead to the city with a few men.

And in the settlement they killed many and took alive thirty-four of the best Germans; and of ours were killed in that battle: Bogdan Rzhanikov and Semyon Fedorov, son of Bykov, and Vladimir Pechatnikov.

He also sent the commander Gregory Papin Saburov and his comrades against the city of Govya; and Gregory, arriving there, took Govya and killed many Germans, while the sovereign’s men, God willing, all remained healthy. And the sovereign ordered the voivodes to come to him.

On the dispatch from the German Land to the Sovereign from Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky

And Prince Peter Ivanovich and his comrades came to the sovereign at the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery, and the tsar and sovereign favored them with loving and gracious words, praising the help and power of Almighty God and giving thanks to the All-Powerful God for His ineffable gift, and he praised their righteous honorable service and promised them great favor. And he ordered them to ride after him to his village of Alexandrovskaya Sloboda, and in the sloboda the sovereign granted the boyars and all the voivodes fur coats and goblets, Argamak horses and horses, and gave them armor, and generously granted them lands and maintenance, and in everything showed his great favor; also he granted all the boyars’ sons with his great favor, with fur coats and ladles, cloths and money, horses and armor, maintenance and estates, and in everything the sovereign showed his great favor.
