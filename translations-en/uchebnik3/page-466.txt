Wedding of the Great Tsar

In the same winter the pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus decided to marry and chose as his bride Anastasia, daughter of his okolnichy Roman Yuryevich.

And the Russian tsar and great sovereign married on the 3rd day of February, on Thursday of the Week of All Foods, and Metropolitan Macarius of All Rus married them in the cathedral church of the Dormition of our Most Pure Lady the Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, Honorable and Glorious, in the reigning city of Moscow; and there was great joy over the sovereign’s marriage.

In the same month, on the 17th day, Friday of Cheese-fare Week, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus went on foot to pray to the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergiyev Monastery with his tsaritsa Anastasia and with his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and he celebrated a service of supplication, and gave gifts to the brethren there, and came to Moscow on the 5th day of March, the second Saturday of Holy Lent.

Of the Fire in the City

In the same spring, on the 12th day of April, Tuesday of Bright Week, at the ninth hour of the day, a shop in the dry-goods row in the Marketplace caught fire; and the shops in all the rows of the city of Moscow burned with many goods, from the Nikolsky crossroads to the river-side city wall; and the great prince’s merchant courtyards, and household courtyards, and much property burned, from Ilyinskaya Street to the city wall, from the small square and the city wall along the river, churches, the Epiphany and St. Elijah monasteries. And by the Moscow River, in the gun tower, cannon powder caught fire, and from this the tower was blown apart and bricks were scattered along the bank of the Moscow River and into the river itself. On the following night ten courtyards burned in Chertorye, toward Dorogomilovo.

Of the Fire beyond the Yauza

In the same month, on the 20th day, Wednesday of the second week after Easter, at the tenth hour of the day, fire broke out beyond the Yauza in Bolvanovye, and the Gonchary and Kozhevniky settlements by the Moscow River burned, and the Church of the Savior in Chigasov Monastery burned out; the painting in that church was beautiful, by the icon-painter Dionysius; and Lyshchikovo burned as far as the Yauza and along the Yauza at its mouth toward the Moscow River.

Of the Bell

In the same spring, on the 3rd day of June, when they began ringing for Vespers, the ears of the bell called Blagovestnik broke off, and it fell from the wooden bell tower, but did not break.
