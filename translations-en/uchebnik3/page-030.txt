In the year 6950 from Adam (the year 1450 from Christ)

Tatars of the Great Horde came to the Ryazan borderlands, did much evil, and left with captives.

In that same autumn Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich became angry with Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Shemyaka and went against him to Uglich, but he fled to Bezhetsky Verkh in Novgorod land, and the grand prince turned back to Kiasovo and came to Moscow.

And then Koludar Irezhsky was beaten with knouts. And afterward Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Shemyaka, and with him Prince Alexander Chertorizhsky, came almost at that same hour to Moscow; and Zinovy, abbot of the Trinity, reconciled them and concluded a peace agreement between them.

In that same autumn there was a fire in Moscow.

In that same winter Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Shemyaka, fearing Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, sent to Veliky Novgorod asking that he be accepted on their terms; and the Novgorodians answered him: “If you wish, prince, to come to us, come, and we will be glad to see you.”

That winter was fierce with intolerable frosts and did much harm to animals and people.

In that same spring there were violent thunders and terrible lightning, strong winds and whirlwinds, and all people were terrified.

In that same spring, after a thaw there came a prolonged frost with snow; much snow fell, and as soon as it had melted, winds rose and severe frosts and winds began, and sorrow seized the people. In that year grain was dear.

In that same year in Novgorod Vladyka Evfimy built the stone Church of the Holy Transfiguration of the Savior in Rusa on the old foundation and consecrated it himself, and established a monastery, and endowed it abundantly with icons, books, church vessels, and all necessities.

In that same year in Novgorod Vladyka Evfimy built the stone Church of St Nicholas in his own courtyard.

In that same year in Novgorod there was a great fire in Podolye; twelve stone churches burned, and the whole district burned as far as St George, and many Christians burned, and it stopped at Lubyanitsa.

And afterward in that same month there was a fire in Novgorod; the Zapolye of Nikitina Street burned, and misfortune befell many people: those who had escaped the first fire with their property now all burned with their property; and great sorrow seized the Christians.
