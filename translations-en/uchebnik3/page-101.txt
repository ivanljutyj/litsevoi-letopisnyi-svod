In the year 6961 from Adam (1461 from Christ)

The godless Mehmed, son of Murad and grandson of Mehmed, ruled over the Turks; and he was then at peace with the Orthodox Emperor Constantine, son of Manuel, who reigned in Constantinople.

And yet the godless Mehmed raised war against Constantinople not according to right order, but contrary to established agreements and contrary to oaths, and brought a vast host by land and sea, and began to wage war. And coming suddenly, he surrounded the city with many forces.

The emperor, with the nobles then there and all the people of the city, realizing his intent, did not know what to do, since at that time there were few people in the city, and the emperor's brothers were not there.

And the emperor sent envoys to Sultan Mehmed, wishing to see everything and speak of peace. But he, the accursed, deceitful, and faithless one, sent the envoys away empty-handed, and himself with a wondrous army that inspired fear moved by land and sea and approached the Imperial City in the month of December, ordering cannons and guns and wall-breaking devices to be fired and preparations made to storm the city.

The people in the city, Greeks and Franks, riding out of the city, fought the Turks, not allowing them to set up wall-breaking engines; but still, the sultan's power was great and heavy, and the Greeks could do them no harm, because one fought against a thousand and two against ten thousand.

Seeing this, the emperor ordered the nobles and magnates to divide among the soldiers the city walls, towers, and gates, and also all the people, and to set war bells on all sides, so that each of them might know and guard his own side, and arrange all that was needed for war, and fight the Turks from the wall, and not go out or ride out of the city. He also ordered cannons and guns to be placed at places convenient for the assault, in order to protect the walls.

And the emperor himself, with the patriarch and the empress, and with the hierarchs, and the whole sacred assembly, and a multitude of women and children, went about the churches of God, making supplications and prayer services, saying: “Lord, Lord! A terrible nature and ineffable power has come upon us; seeing it, the ancient mountains trembled, and earthly creation shook, the sun and moon were terrified, and their brightness perished, and the stars of heaven fell. But we, wretched ones, despising all this, sinned and acted lawlessly before You, Lord, and angered You a thousandfold, and provoked Your Divinity, forgetting Your great gifts and despising Your commandments, and turned away Your vehement mercies and bounties toward us, and turned to evil deeds and lawlessness, by which we have departed far from You.
