And King Frederick wrote to the tsar and grand prince: the cities in the Livonian land, in Wiek, which the tsar and grand prince assigned to King Frederick in the peace charter—those cities and others King Eric of Sweden took from him, and King Frederick wishes to recover those cities from King Eric of Sweden. And other Livonian cities are held by the King of Sweden, but by the truce charter those cities were assigned to the tsar and grand prince; and the tsar and grand prince should permit him to take those cities, for without the knowledge of the tsar’s majesty the Danish king does not risk attacking. Furthermore King Frederick wrote to the tsar and grand prince that in the truce charter two manors, Kolki, were written as an exception, and the tsar and grand prince should yield those two manors, Kolki, to King Frederick.

And the tsar and grand prince wrote back to the Danish King Frederick by his envoy Zakharías that he should win back from the King of Sweden his own cities that are in Wiek, but the Livonian cities that are held by the King of Sweden, though by the truce charter they were assigned to the tsar and grand prince—King Frederick should not demand those cities; and concerning Kolki, King Frederick should send his great ambassadors to the tsar and grand prince, and then he will receive an answer on that matter. And he dismissed Zakharías on the 5th day of May.

On the Repose of the Most Reverend Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus

In that same month, on the 31st day of December, the Most Reverend Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, reposed, and was buried in the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos in Moscow on the 1st day of January. At his burial were the tsar and grand prince of All Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and princes, boyars, magnates, and a multitude of people. Present then at his burial were Nikandr, Archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl; Afanasy, Bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa; Filofei, Bishop of Ryazan and Murom; Varlaam, Bishop of Kolomna and Kashira; Matfei, Bishop of Sarsk and Podonsk; archimandrites and hegumens with the consecrated council.

And he ordered this document to be read at his burial aloud for all standing there:

“In the name of the holy, consubstantial, life-giving, and indivisible Trinity, I, the servant of God, the humble Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, formerly Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, seeing that old age has come upon me, for I have fallen into frequent and various illnesses with which I am now afflicted, being chastised by God in His love for mankind for my sins. And my illnesses have multiplied as never before, and I have deemed it proper that I must declare certain things in this testament.
