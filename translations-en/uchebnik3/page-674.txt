Until this time it had been bloody; until then the trumpet had not sounded, summoning the army of the Orthodox. But now, at the petition of our sovereign, God has wrought an incomprehensible miracle of Christ through his wise, firm mind and labor: for two and a half weeks his royal army went by sea in small boats and in ships, and they took ships and fought, for even the air served them according to the sovereign’s faith in God. And a small army, having suddenly attacked the Great Horde and waged war, avenging the blood of Christians on the impious, withdrew in safety.
