In the year 6972 from Adam (1472 from Christ)

Great Prince Ivan and his mother, Grand Princess Maria, sent Prince Vasily Ivanovich to Ryazan, to his patrimony, to the great principality.

That same winter, in the month of January, the same Prince of Ryazan, Vasily, came to Moscow and took as his wife the Great Prince’s sister, named Anna; and they were married in the Church of the Most Pure in Moscow.

And in that same week, on the commemoration of the Three Hierarchs, he went with his princess to his patrimony, Ryazan.

On the installation of Joseph as Metropolitan in Caesarea Philippi

That same winter, on 4 March, Metropolitan Theodosius installed a certain Jerusalemite named Joseph, brother of the Patriarch of Jerusalem, as Metropolitan of Caesarea Philippi. And that brother, the patriarch, had gone to Moscow for alms, since they had great hardships from the Egyptian Sultan, and before arriving he died in Caffa.

And this Joseph wished to be in his place, and our metropolitan and the bishops of the Russian land enthroned him here, and he went back, having collected much alms, and did not reach his own land.

In the year 6973 from Adam (1473 from Christ)

On 13 September Metropolitan Theodosius left the metropolitan see and went to the Chudov Monastery in Moscow.

On the installation of Philip as Metropolitan

Great Prince Ivan sent for his brothers and for all the bishops of his land, and also for archimandrites and venerable hegumens.

And when the princes, the brothers of the Great Prince, and all the bishops of the Russian land, and the whole sacred council, archimandrites, hegumens, archpriests and other priests had assembled, by the will of the Great Prince and his brothers and all the bishops then present at that election and of the whole sacred council, they elected Philip, Bishop of Suzdal, as Metropolitan of all Rus.

At his installation there were bishops: Tryphon, Archbishop of Rostov; Evfimy, Bishop of Bryansk, to whom the Suzdal see was then also given; David of Ryazan; Goronty of Kolomna; Vassian of Sarai. And those who did not come to his installation sent their envoys and letters and all gave their written assent together. And he was installed as Metropolitan of all the Russian land in the month of November. In that same year the godless Tsar Makhmut went against the Russian land with the whole Horde and was on the Don.
