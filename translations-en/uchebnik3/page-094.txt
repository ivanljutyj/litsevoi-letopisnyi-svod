All the best men were seized; the prince himself barely escaped, almost the entire foot army was killed, and the city shut its gates.

News came to the Grand Prince at Borok that his victory was won; and he gave thanks to God and His Most Pure Mother and the great wonderworkers, and, having served a thanksgiving prayer in the church of Saint John the Forerunner, he went to Galich. When he came to Galich, the townspeople submitted to him; and he pacified the city, and, having installed his governors throughout that whole patrimony, he went to Moscow. And he came to Moscow during Cheesefare Week; and Shemyaka fled to Novgorod the Great.

In the same year Vladimir Khovrin in Moscow, on his own courtyard, built a stone church in the name of the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross, on the site of the first stone church, which had burned when there was a fire in the Suzdal region.

And Metropolitan Jonah founded on his courtyard a stone chamber, in which was a church of the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Holy Mother of God.

In the same year, on the fifth day of the month of August, there was a great cloud in Moscow and terrible thunder, and it struck the stone cathedral church of the Archangel Michael. And on the thirteenth day of August there was a great storm and it broke the cross off that same church of the Archangel Michael.

In the same year, when the Grand Prince was in his patrimony at Kolomna, news came to him that Tatars were coming from the Field, Malberdei and Ulan, and other princes with them with many Tatars.

And the Grand Prince sent against them his Tsarevich Kasim with Tatars, and with him his voivode Konstantin Alexandrovich Bezzubtsev with the men of Kolomna.

And they caught up with them on the Bityuk River in the Field, and killed many Tatars, while others fled. Then Romodan Zinoviev was killed.

In the year 6959 from Adam (1459 from Christ)

Prince Semyon Olelkovich was in Moscow at the home of his grandmother Grand Princess Sophia and of Grand Prince Vasily, his uncle. This was in spring, on the day of the Great Martyr George, on Great Friday.

On the Swift Tatars

In the same year news came to the Grand Prince that Tsarevich Mozovsha was coming against him secretly from the Horde of Seyid Ahmad, and the Grand Prince set out against him to Kolomna without having had time to gather his forces. When he was near Brashevo, news came to him that the Tatars were already near the bank. And the Grand Prince returned to Moscow, and all the men who were with him he sent to the bank with his voivode, Prince Ivan Zvenigorodsky, so that the Tatars would not cross the Oka River so quickly. He, however, took fright and turned back by another route, not following the Grand Prince.
