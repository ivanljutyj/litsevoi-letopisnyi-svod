Message from the Pious Tsar and Grand Prince, Sovereign Ioann Vasilievich, Autocrat of All Rus, to Metropolitan Macarius of All Rus

“To our most reverend father Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, Tsar and Grand Prince of All Rus Ivan Vasilievich bows low. Thou didst write, lord, to us in thy letter, teaching us to be freed from sins and to fight, established in piety; and we, lord, bow low before thy favor and thy enlightened words, and, calling upon the all-merciful God for help, placing our hope in the most pure Mother of God and in the great wonderworkers, and in thy holy prayers and strengthening, we set out on the journey with Prince Vladimir Andreevich and with the boyars and with all the Christian host. And from Murom we set out on Wednesday, the day of the Prophet Elijah. And to thee, our lord and father, pastor and intercessor with God for our souls, and to all the Orthodox assembly we bow low, that thou wouldst be pleased to pray to God and to His most pure Mother and to the great wonderworkers, that God may not remember the many sins of our ignorance, and by your holy prayers may the Lord grant remission of our known and unknown transgressions, and may the Creator make for us a peaceful and untroubled path; and may He bring our foreign enemies into submission and to death; and may He free the poor Christians who, because of our sins, suffer great misfortunes at their hands, and return them to their own; and may God, through thee and us, cover our realm both at the time of our departure and henceforth with His grace from all enemies warring against piety, and keep it unharmed. And may the all-merciful Lord shield and strengthen us with His grace; may God grant us to return, having delivered Christendom, to our fatherland and to see the image of the Most Pure and the relics of the great wonderworkers, and thy holy things, and all our own with all our host, to the joy of Orthodox Christians. And us, lord, with Prince Vladimir Andreevich and with the boyars and with all the Christian host, all together forgive and bless, and help by prayer; and we bow low to thee, our lord and father.”

Concerning Shigalei and the Dispatch of the Voivodes

The Sovereign, meanwhile, organizing his warriors and caring for their arms, so that all might be prepared for battle, as the prophet said: “He prepared himself and was not dismayed.”

And the Sovereign sends his stolnik Fyodor Ivanovich Umny to Tsar Shigalei. And the Tsar came to the Sovereign in Murom, and the Sovereign sent him by boats to Kazan, and with him he sent the voivode Prince Pyotr Andreevich Bulgakov and his companions, and many boyar sons and streltsy.

And the Sovereign ordered all the host to cross the Oka River at many crossings.
