This was why: since by God's judgment and by the sovereign will of the tsar and grand prince, the brother of the tsar and grand prince, Prince Yuri Vasilievich, had taken as his wife the daughter of Prince Dmitry Paletsky, Prince Dmitry Paletsky sent Vasily to Princess Efrosinia and her son Prince Vladimir, asking that the princess and her son do them the favor of granting Prince Yuri Vasilievich and his wife, Prince Yuri’s princess, an appanage according to the grand prince’s testament, and that they would not oppose Princess Efrosinia and Prince Vladimir’s accession to the throne and were ready to serve them.

And he ordered it to be found out at whose instigation this opposition had arisen, since such a thing cannot happen without instigation; and he ordered his clerk Vasily Grigoriev, son of Zakharov Gniliev, to find out about it, because he was among the close men of the sovereign.

But the clerk Vasily, in some unknown manner, slandered the sovereign’s boyars Prince Ivan Kubensky and Fyodor and Vasily Vorontsov in this matter.

And the grand prince ordered Prince Ivan Kubensky, Fyodor Vorontsov, and Vasily Mikhailov, son of Vorontsov, to be executed: their heads were cut off on the twenty-first day of July, on Saturday.

And Ivan Petrov, son of Fedorovich, he ordered to be seized and exiled to Beloozero, and ordered him to be placed under guard.

But the treasurer Nikita Funikov fell gravely ill early and rose only when the sovereign had recovered considerably, and then he kissed the cross, after all the other people.

And concerning Prince Dmitry Kurlyatev and Nikita Funikov, it was said that they had been in communication with Princess Efrosinia and with her son Prince Vladimir, and had wanted him on the throne, while they did not want the tsarevich Prince Dmitry on the throne because of his infancy.

And they dragged them out through the front doors into the square and beyond the city, and laid them before the stake at which executions are carried out.

At this council were the archpriest of the Annunciation, Fyodor Barmin, Prince Fyodor Shuysky, Prince Yuri Temkin, Ivan Petrov Fedorov, Grigory Yuryevich Zakharyin, Fyodor Nagoy, and many others.
