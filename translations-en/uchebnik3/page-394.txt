For many years, and to your seed from generation to generation, from now and forever. I also entrust to you this scepter, the sovereignty of Great Russia, the great principality of Vladimir, Moscow, and Novgorod, and the great state of all Rus! May you be blessed in the great state of our realm from now and forever!” And so he blessed him, kissing him with tears.

And he sent him to his chambers. And then the Grand Prince commanded Agrofena: “Agrofena, do not step away from my son Ivan by a single span.”

Then the Grand Princess herself came to her autocratic spouse, weeping bitterly, shedding fiery tears and speaking tender words piteously; his brother Prince Andrei Ivanovich could barely restrain her on one side, and on the other the noblewoman Elena, wife of Ivan Andreevich Chelyadnin. The Grand Princess threw herself about and wept bitterly, and her tears flowed incessantly from her eyes like a great fountain; there were many tears, weeping, and wailing among all the people. The Grand Prince comforted her, saying to her: “Wife! Cease, do not weep; my illness has eased, nothing pains me, I thank God,” for the Grand Prince was already insensible. And having comforted her a little with brief words and calmed her for a time, he ordered her to stop weeping.

And the Grand Princess stopped weeping and began to say: “Sovereign Grand Prince! To whom are you leaving me, and to whom, sovereign, are you entrusting the children?” The Grand Prince answered and said: “I have blessed my son Ivan with the state and the great principality, and I have written for you in my testament, as in the former testaments of our fathers and forefathers, according to what is fitting, as for former grand princesses.”

And the Grand Princess began to petition him concerning her son Prince Yuri, that he also bless him. And the Grand Prince sent for his son Prince Yuri. And they brought Prince Yuri, and there he blessed his son George with a cross, for Prince Yuri was then very small, one year old; and he laid upon him a golden cross of Paisios, and ordered that after his death that cross be taken to his boyar Mikhail Yuryevich; and he endowed him with a patrimony, bequeathing to him as inheritance the town of Uglich and other towns and districts as was fitting.

He bequeaths the whole governance of the entire Russian realm: to hold sovereign power, to order all things according to God, and to judge under the scepter of Great Russia together with his son, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan, until he grows from infancy to manhood—to his mother, his own Grand Princess Elena; for the great sovereign knew her to be God-loving and merciful, gentle and truthful, wise and courageous, and her heart was filled with all royal understanding, and such a gift was bestowed on her by God.
