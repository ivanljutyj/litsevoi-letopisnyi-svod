And what was written in my confession, that I also handed down when at the beginning I was ordained to the episcopate according to custom and the apostolic rule and the ordinances of the divine and sacred holy Fathers. For as the apostles were appointed by God, so bishops were appointed by the apostles and became apostolic heirs; and after those bishops, according to the God-given apostolic rule, I, a sinner, was also appointed by the grace of the Holy Spirit, according to the rule of the holy apostles and the sacred canons and by the laying on of hands of my lord the most reverend Daniel, Metropolitan of all Rus, and of the whole consecrated council then with him, the archbishops and bishops of the Russian metropolis, by the command of the all-sovereign tsar and sovereign grand prince Vasily Ivanovich, autocrat of all Rus, I was installed to the archbishopric of Great Novgorod and Pskov. And I ruled the throne of Holy Sophia, the ineffable Wisdom of the Word of God, for 16 years. And in the year 7050 from Adam (1550 from Christ), the great first-throned lord, the most reverend Joasaph, Metropolitan of all Rus, left the Russian metropolis and withdrew to the Kirillov Monastery for a life of silence. And, I do not know by what judgments of God, I, the humble one, was compelled and chosen not only by the whole council of the Russian metropolis but also by the pious and Christ-loving tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich himself, autocrat of all Rus. I, the humble one, long refused, as the divine Scriptures testify, and could not disobey, but was compelled and installed upon the great throne of the Russian metropolis by the most reverend Archbishop Dositheus of Rostov and Yaroslavl and all the bishops of the Russian metropolis then with him and the whole consecrated council. And I remained among the metropolitans for 21 years and nine and a half months. And in those times many sorrows befell me: now from a great fire, now from various illnesses. And I repeatedly reflected and desired to renounce the whole episcopal name and its affairs and to take upon myself the yoke of my Christ and to remain in the humblest state until my last breath. And because of my sins I could not obtain a suitable time for departure into a life of silence: now by the wise restraint and merciful benevolence of the Christ-loving tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich, sovereign and autocrat of all Rus, now by the compulsion of all the hierarchs of the Russian land and the whole consecrated council. God so willed, and now my life has drawn near its end, and I do not know what will be hereafter, since the future is unknown to people, and nothing lies before me but death and the dread judgment of my Lord Christ; and therefore I entreat all and give peace and bless and forgive all.
