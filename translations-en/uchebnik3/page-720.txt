In the same month of September, on the 21st day, a Saturday, the tsar and grand prince set out with his tsaritsa and grand princess and with his children, with Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor, to pray at the Life-giving Trinity and to the venerable wonderworker Sergius. And from the Trinity to Alexandrov Sloboda and on a progress, and from the Sloboda to Volok Lamsky, to Vyazma, to Tsarev Pochinok, and to other villages of Vyazma district. And the sovereign tsar and grand prince arrived in Moscow on November 17.

In the same month of September, on the 25th day, a Wednesday, Nikandr, Archbishop of Rostov, reposed in Moscow, and was laid at the Trinity in the Sergius Monastery.

In the same month of October, on the 28th day, from Monday to Tuesday, at the third hour of the night, at the full moon there was a renewal of the moon: it almost disappeared, and became barely visible as if at its end, and then it waxed until the fifth hour of the night, and at the fifth hour it became full.

In the same month of October there arrived from the Pyatigorsk Cherkess, from Prince Temryuk Aidarovich, Prince Ivan, son of Prince Dmitry Dashkov, and Dyak Ivanov, son of Rzhevsky, with their comrades. And they had been sent by the sovereign to the Cherkess at Temryuk’s petition to guard him from his enemies; and they lived among the Cherkess with Prince Temryuk from the Nativity of John the Forerunner to the Feast of the Mother of God; and they warred against many Cherkess places, the villages of Shapshuk and his brothers, and took many captives and goods. And many Cherkess princes, having gathered, went against Prince Ivan Dashkov and Dyak Rzhevsky with their comrades and fought them; and the sovereign’s men killed many Cherkess and wounded others.

In the same summer, by the command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, a town was built in his patrimony in Polotsk district, on the Dvina River, on the Vilna side, at the mouth of the Ula River, forty versts above Polotsk.

In the same summer, by the command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, a town was built on the Field on the Orley River.

In the same year, in November, on the 6th day, the sovereign’s envoy Utesh Kapustin with his comrades arrived from the Nogais, from Prince Tinekhmat and from Urus-Murza. And he said that the boyars’ sons who had gone to other Nogai murzas said that they had done everything according to the sovereign’s instruction, and that the care and honor shown by Prince Tinakhmat and the murzas had been great.

In the same year, in December, on the 10th day, a Tuesday, a church was consecrated, the chapel at the Annunciation on the Gallery, the Entry into Jerusalem; and it was consecrated by Philip, Metropolitan of all Rus, and with him Athanasius, Archbishop of Polotsk, and bishops, archimandrites, hegumens, and the whole consecrated council. At the consecration there was also the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and his children the tsareviches: Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor.
