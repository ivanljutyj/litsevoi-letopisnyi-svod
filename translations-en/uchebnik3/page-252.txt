They concealed heresies; Ivashko Maksimov and another Ivashko, son of Protopope Alexei, took to flight. But their malice was not hidden from Archbishop Gennady, for the archbishop, enlightened by God, by great effort, like a cunning hunter with various nets, caught their malicious activity and evil mystery and learned in detail about all their God-opposing heresies, and tried in every way to correct them. But seeing their hard-hearted disposition and disobedience, which he alone could not correct, he announced this in Moscow to his lord and father, the Most Reverend Zosima, Metropolitan of All Rus. These foolish rotten reasonings became known to the most pious Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Autocrat of All Rus, and by his command a council was then convened in Moscow concerning them.

In the year 6999 from Adam (1499 from Christ), on the 17th day of October, by the command of the pious and Christ-loving Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Sovereign of All Rus and autocrat of many others and true champion of Orthodoxy, a council was convened at the Metropolitan's court. And at this council were present: Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich in place of his autocratic father; the Most Reverend Zosima, Metropolitan of All Rus; Archbishop Tikhon of Rostov; and the bishops: Nifont of Suzdal, Simeon of Ryazan, Vassian of Tver, Prokhor of Sarsk, and Filofei of Perm; and Afanasy, Abbot of the Trinity-Sergius Monastery; and the hermits and virtuous elders Paisius and Nil; and many archimandrites, abbots, protopopes, priests, deacons, and elders, and all the rest of the sacred council of the Russian metropolitanate. And so they gathered to struggle for the truth against those shameless corrupters of the Christian faith, against the Novgorod heretics, the monk Zakharia and his companions, and all their sympathizers, who wished to corrupt the pure and immaculate Orthodox faith in Christ our God, glorified in the Trinity, and to destroy Christ's flock—Orthodox Christianity—which they did not overcome and could not overcome, but were struck as if by a stone, and were themselves crushed and perished, and all their wisdom was swallowed up, because they had seduced many simple people with their foul heresies.

They were brought before that same council and questioned about their heretical villainy; but they, the accursed, at first lied much, concealing their lawlessness, and denied their heresies, but were convicted by truthful testimony. And so the accursed themselves poured out all the poison of their madness and openly laid bare all their apostate deeds. And they began to say improper things: they said of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, thus: "How can God come down to earth and be born of a Virgin like a man?
