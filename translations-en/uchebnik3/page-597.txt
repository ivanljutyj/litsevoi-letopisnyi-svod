And the next day in the morning, on Sunday, in the Church of the Dormition of the Mother of God there were abundant miracles from the image of the great wonderworker Nicholas, and from Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of All Rus, and Jonah; and every day there were healings from the image of the great wonderworker Nicholas.

And Metropolitan Macarius himself restored the icon of Nicholas the Wonderworker, for he had been trained in icon painting, and with him Andrew, archpriest of the Annunciation, with great desire and faith, fasting and prayer. From his image wonderworking most of all superabounds for those who ask with faith, and many icons were painted from it of the same size and likeness, and from them all there were many miracles for many believers.

On the Founding of the Stone Church of the Protection of the Most Holy Mother of God

In the same month the pious and Christ-loving tsar and great sovereign ordered the founding of a stone church of the Protection of the Most Holy Mother of God with nine domes, which had formerly been wooden, in memory of the capture of Kazan, at the Frolov Gates above the moat, with a chapel to that same church of the Life-giving Trinity above the moat, dedicated to Nicholas the Wonderworker of Vyatka; and before this, two years earlier, one church of the Trinity had been founded, and the sovereign ordered that the Protection of the Most Pure Mother of God and chapels be added to that same church.

On the Erection of the Wooden Church of Nicholas of Vyatka

In the same month the pious tsar and great sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus ordered the erection, with great faith and much desire he commanded to erect a wooden church of the holy wonderworker Nicholas of Vyatka and to paint from his icon another icon of the same kind.

And the newly painted image of the holy wonderworker Nicholas was placed in the newly erected church by the Protection of the Holy Mother of God above the moat. And Metropolitan Macarius consecrated it with the whole council of the Russian metropolis on the 29th day of July, on Monday; at that consecration were also the pious tsar Ivan Vasilyevich and his brother, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and a multitude of boyars and people. The merciful God shows His mercy to people through the wonderworking image of Saint Nicholas, His saint. There were many healings that day, and after that great miracles; and from the newly painted image of the wonderworker sight was granted to the blind, and walking to the lame, and recovery to the paralyzed, and healing of all ailments was abundant for those who came with faith.
