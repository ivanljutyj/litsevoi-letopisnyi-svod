And Vladyka Varsonofy of Smolensk, the princes and boyars of Smolensk, the townspeople, and all the city residents came out of the city to petition that the great sovereign show favor to his patrimony and inheritance, set aside his disgrace and wrath, allow them to appear before his eyes, and command them to serve him.

And the great sovereign showed favor to his patrimony and inheritance: to Vladyka Varsonofy of Smolensk, the princes, boyars, townspeople, and all the city people he forgave his disgrace and wrath, and ordered them to appear before his eyes and commanded them to serve him.

On July 31 the Smolensk princes and boyars opened the city, and they themselves went to the tents to petition the great sovereign and appear before his eyes, and there they swore allegiance to the great prince and kissed the cross. And the great prince forgave them, spoke his gracious word to them, and invited them to feast.

And he sent into the city of Smolensk his boyar and commander Prince Daniil Vasilyevich Shchenya and his other commanders with many people, ordering them to bring the rest of the people—the princes, boyars, townspeople, and all the people of the city of Smolensk—to the oath and to speak to them the sovereign’s gracious speech.

On August 1, the Feast of the Procession of the Honorable Cross, Vasily Ivanovich, Great Prince of All Rus, with his brothers and boyars, and with all his commanders and people of various ranks, entered the city of Smolensk with great glory. And Bishop Varsonofy of Smolensk, with archimandrites and abbots, priests and deacons, and with the whole cathedral clergy, taking the wonder-working icon of the immaculate Most Pure Mother of God together with the honorable crosses and many holy images of other saints, came out to meet him. The noble princes and nobles, elders with youths, mothers and maidens, monks and nuns, and all the people of the city of Smolensk, small and great, men, women, and children, with bright eyes and pure souls, with great love and zeal, met the sovereign great prince outside the city in the suburb.

And the right-believing great sovereign, with his brothers and all the boyars and commanders, crossed himself before the honorable and holy icons and, having received the blessing of Bishop Varsonofy of Smolensk, went after the crosses to the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God and of her glorious Dormition.

And there they immediately began to sing a prayer service. After the prayer service was completed, the protodeacon, ascending the ambo, began in a loud voice to proclaim Many Years for the great prince, and then the bishop with all the consecrated cathedral clergy, and also the church clerks on both choirs, sang Many Years to the great prince.

And the bishop, having blessed the great prince with the honorable and life-giving cross, said: “By the grace of God, rejoice and be well, Orthodox Tsar Vasily, Great Prince of All Rus, autocrat, in your patrimony and inheritance, the city of Smolensk, for many years.”
