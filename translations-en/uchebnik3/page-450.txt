In the same year Vassian of Kolomna left the episcopacy. In the same year, on the 2nd of July, a Sunday, Feodosii, former archimandrite of the Novospassky Monastery in Moscow, was installed by Metropolitan Macarius as bishop of Kolomna.

On the 8th day of the same month the grand prince’s envoy from Astrakhan, Fedor Nevezhin, came and told the grand prince that Tsarevich Ideger of Astrakhan was coming to serve the grand sovereign, and had stayed with Tsar Shigalei at Kasimov. And with Fedor there also came to the grand prince an Astrakhan envoy from Tsar Abdyl-Rakhman, Prince Ishim and his companions, with proposals for firm friendship.

On the 14th of the same month the grand prince sent to Kazan Isenkildei, a man of Safa-Girei, and with him he sent his own man to the tsar in Kazan with a letter, Nikita Chuvatov; and the grand prince wrote in the letter that he wished to make peace with the tsar, and the tsar should send his great envoys, good men.

On the 15th of the same month the grand prince sent his pilgrim Archbishop Feodosii of Novgorod to his patrimony, Great Novgorod.

On the 16th of the same month Tsarevich Idiger arrived to enter the grand prince’s service, and the grand prince showed the tsarevich favor and took him into his service.

On the 20th day of that month the grand prince’s envoy, Prince Alexander Vasilievich Kashin, came from the Crimea and brought to the grand prince a sworn charter from Tsar Saip-Girei; and with him the tsar sent his men with letters, Togmach and companions, and the tsar wrote in the letters of brotherhood and firm friendship. And Prince Alexander Vasilievich Kashin gave five hundred rubles as ransom for himself and got out (without an envoy and without an embassy, while Ostanya Andreev remained in the Crimea in the envoy’s place).

In the same year, on the 12th of August, the grand prince sent away the Astrakhan envoys Ishim and companions; and with them the grand prince sent his Cossacks, Itaish and companions, to Tsar Abdyl-Rakhman in Astrakhan with letters; and the grand prince wrote in the letters that he wished to have firm friendship with him.

In the same month Archbishop Dosithei of Rostov reposed and was laid in Rostov.
