On the Release of the Envoys to the Crimea
In the year 7028 from Adam (1528 from Christ)

On the 8th day of September, the Great Prince of All Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, having honored the Crimean envoys, Prince Appak and Magmed-Pasha, released them to their sovereign. And together with them the Great Prince sent to the Crimea to his brother, Tsar Magmed-Girey, his envoy Feodor Klimyantiev with friendship and love.

On the Release of Metropolitan Gregory and the Elders of the Holy Mountain

In the same month of September, on the 11th day, the Great Prince released Metropolitan Gregory, who had come to the great sovereign from Theoleptus, Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople, for alms; and he released the elders of the Holy Mountain: Sava the prohegumen of the Russian Monastery of the Great Martyr Panteleimon, and Isaiah from the Monastery of the Holy Forty Martyrs. And the great sovereign Vasily, by the grace of God, sent sufficient alms to the patriarch and to the Holy Mountain, and also bestowed upon Metropolitan Gregory and the elders of the Holy Mountain money, horses, clothing, and all other necessities.

On the Vladimir Icons

On the 15th day of the same month of September, on Thursday, the right-believing and Christ-loving Vasily, by God’s grace sovereign of All Rus, on the advice of his father Varlaam, Metropolitan of All Rus, sent the holy icons to the glorious city of Vladimir, about which it was previously said how and why they had been brought from Vladimir to the most renowned city of Moscow for renewal and adornment, since over many years they had become worn, and how they had been renewed and adorned with silver and gold. And His Holiness Varlaam, Metropolitan of All Rus, with bishops, archimandrites, and abbots, and with all the sacred council, saw off the holy icons with psalm-singing and prayer services. And after them followed the pious great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, Autocrat of All Rus, with his princes and boyars. And all the inhabitants of the glorious city of Moscow, a great multitude of men and women with infants, with great joy and prayer escorted the holy icons beyond the posad, where they also erected a new church in the name of our Most Pure Lady the Mother of God and of her honorable and glorious Meeting and Farewell.

And Metropolitan Varlaam came with the bishops and with all the sacred councils, consecrated the newly erected church, sang a prayer service, celebrated the divine liturgy in it, and sent the holy icons on to the city of Vladimir.
