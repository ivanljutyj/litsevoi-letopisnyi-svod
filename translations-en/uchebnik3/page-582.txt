On the Sending to the Tsar and Grand Prince from Astrakhan

In the same year, on August 29, his voivode Prince Yuri Ivanovich Pronsky Shemyakin and his associates sent from Astrakhan to the Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus a messenger, Prince Vasily, son of Prince Ivan Barboshin. The sovereign was then in his village of Kolomenskoye, celebrating his birthday, for there each year the metropolitan with the council and many boyars celebrated at the tsar’s. And Prince Vasily said to the tsar and grand prince: the voivode Prince Yuri Ivanovich Pronsky had sent him to announce to the sovereign the greatness of God, how God had wrought His mercy, glorified His holy name, magnified the pious tsar, cast down and put to shame the enemies of Christ’s Cross, the Basurmans, and freed captive Christians.

On the Arrival of the Voivodes at Perevoloka

The voivodes came to Perevoloka, the portage from the Volga to the Don, on June 29, and sent ahead of themselves Prince Alexander Vyazemsky and Danila Chulkov, and with them boyar sons and atamans with Cossacks, to seek out Astrakhan people and get tongues.

And Prince Alexander met Astrakhan men above Black Island; they were rowing in ushkuys to scout about the host of the tsar and grand prince, and their leader was Sakmak. Prince Alexander fell upon those men, and by God’s mercy and the good fortune of the tsar and grand prince he routed those men utterly; not a single man of them escaped, and Sakmak himself they took alive, and many others, and brought them to meet the voivodes. And the tongues, Sakmak and his companions, told the voivodes that Tsar Emgurchey had sent them to find out about the Moscow host, and that Tsar Emgurchey himself was stationed five versts below the city of Astrakhan; in the city, they said, few people remained, while all the people were dwelling on the islands in their uluses.

And by the order of the tsar and grand prince the voivodes left the large boats and themselves went hastily to the city of Astrakhan.

And when the voivodes rowed up to Black Island, Prince Alexander Vyazemsky sent other tongues to them, and those tongues said the same about Tsar Emgurchey.

And when the voivodes came opposite Bolshiye Sarai, where the Great Horde had been, other tongues were brought to them there, and those tongues said the same about Tsar Emgurchey.
