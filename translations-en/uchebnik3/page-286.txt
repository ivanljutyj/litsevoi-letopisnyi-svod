In the year 7009 from Adam (1509 from Christ)

In January an ambassador from Vladislav, King of Hungary, named Matthias, came to Moscow.

On the Polish Ambassador

In that same winter, in February, an ambassador from Albrecht, King of Poland, named Olekhno Skaruta, came to Moscow.

On the Lithuanian Ambassador

And from Alexander, Grand Prince of Lithuania, an ambassador named Stanislav Narbutov came.

On the Death of the Grand Prince’s Daughter

That same month, on the 19th day of February, the daughter of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, the pious Princess Feodosia, wife of Prince Vasily Danilovich Kholmsky, departed this life, and she was laid in the Church of the Ascension in Moscow.

On the Death of the Grand Princess of Ryazan

In that same spring, in April, on the Wednesday of Bright Week, the pious Grand Princess Anna of Ryazan, sister of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, departed this life at the sixth hour of the day.

The Bishop of Perm Leaves the Throne

In that same spring, in April, Philotheus, Bishop of Perm, left his bishopric and came to the Kirillov Monastery.

On the Healing at the Tomb of Saint Maximus the Holy Fool

In that same year, at the tomb of Saint Maximus the Holy Fool, God healed a man who had a crooked leg, on the 23rd day of April.

On the Seizure of the Merchants

In that same spring the Germans in Yuryev seized more than 200 Novgorod and Pskov merchants of the Grand Prince, plundered their goods, and sent them into confinement in various towns.

And the whole Livonian land gathered and set out against the Grand Prince’s patrimony, the city of Pskov, and after ravaging the Pskov volosts they returned to their own land.
