On the Battle of Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania with Stephen, Voivode of Wallachia

In that same year King Albrecht of Poland, son of Casimir, and his brother Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania went with an army against Stephen, Voivode of Wallachia.

Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, having learned that they were marching with an army against Stephen, Voivode of Wallachia, quickly sent by relays to his son-in-law Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania his envoy Loban Zabolotsky and his clerk Volk Kuritsyn, so that Grand Prince Alexander would not break his agreement and would not march with an army against the Grand Prince’s matchmaker, Voivode Stephen. But Grand Prince Alexander acted deceitfully: he himself returned, while he sent the Russian princes with an army to help his brother King Albrecht.

King Albrecht of Poland, having entered the Wallachian land, began to take captives, burn, cut down, and plunder; and he came up to the city of Sochava and battered down the city wall with cannons.

Stephen the voivode lured him into fortified places, and defeated him, and took his cannons and treasury, and killed many lords, and took some alive as captives, and slew about 40,000 of his troops; and the king himself with a few soldiers barely escaped to the Russian princes, because they did not take part in that battle. And the king returned to his own land with great shame.

On the Arrival in Moscow of Grand Princess Anna

In that same summer, in August, Grand Princess Anna of Ryazan, sister of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, came to Moscow.

The Grand Prince met her with honor at Vspolye, at Bolvanovo, with his grandson, his children, and the boyars; likewise Grand Princess Sophia with her daughter-in-law Grand Princess Elena and with the boyar ladies. And Grand Princess Anna stayed in Moscow until Epiphany, and after Epiphany the Grand Prince sent her away with great honors and many gifts, and Prince Yuri with the boyars escorted her to the St. Nicholas Ugreshsky Monastery. And the Grand Prince sent her away so soon because of the wedding, since at that time Grand Princess Anna of Ryazan was giving her daughter in marriage to Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Belsky, and the wedding was in Ryazan in January.
