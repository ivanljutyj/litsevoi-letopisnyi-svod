And on that day the great sovereign Vasily celebrated brightly and joyfully with his father Varlaam the metropolitan, and with the bishops, archimandrites, his princes, and boyars, having made a great feast, glorifying God and His most pure Mother, and he ordered sufficient alms to be given to priests and the poor throughout the whole city. And from that time they established the celebration of the feast of the Most Pure on the fifteenth day of September.

On the Consecration of the Archbishop of Rostov

That same winter, on the ninth day of February, on Thursday, Ivan, archimandrite of Simonov, was consecrated by Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, as archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl.

On the Consecration of the Bishop of Kolomna

That same month, on the fourteenth day of February, on Tuesday, Tikhon, hegumen of Ugresh, was consecrated by Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, as bishop of Kolomna.

On the Consecration of the Bishop of Vologda

That same month, on the sixteenth day of February, on Thursday, Pimen, hegumen of Solovetsky, was consecrated by Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, as bishop of Vologda and Perm.

On the Sending of the Grand Prince to the Turkish Sultan Concerning His Health

That same winter, in March, the Grand Prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, sent his man Boris Golokhvastov to the Turkish Sultan Salim-Shag-Saltan, son of Baazit-Saltan, to tell him of his health and to see him. And he sent him to Azov by the Don, and from Azov he went by sea to Kafa, and from Kafa to Tsargrad by the Black Sea. And from Tsargrad he went overland to Adrianople, and from Adrianople he caught up with the sultan at Demotika, and there the sultan ordered Boris to be with him, and appointed him great honor and sufficient sustenance, and from there sent him to the Serbian land, to the Danube.

And he crossed the Danube below Kilia, and from Kilia to Belgorod, and there he crossed the great river Dnieper, and from Belgorod to Takov, and there he crossed the Dnieper; and from there he came to Perekop, to the Crimean tsar Magmed-Girey. And the tsar sent him after Prince Yuri Pronsky, and Boris caught up with Prince Yuri at Putivl, and came to Moscow with Prince Yuri on the eighth day of January.
