At that same time Metropolitan Daniel, with the brothers of the Grand Prince and with the boyars, went to the Grand Princess to comfort her; and the Grand Princess, seeing the Metropolitan and the boyars coming to her, became as if dead, and lay for about two hours, and barely regained consciousness. The Metropolitan, the Grand Prince’s brothers, and the boyars comforted her; and then all went away from the Grand Princess.

And with the Grand Prince remained Abbot Joasaph of the Trinity and the elder Misail Sukin; and they began to dress him and to comb his beard, as befits one in the monastic order, and they placed under him a bier from Chudov Monastery, and laid his body upon the bier.

And when the Grand Prince had reposed, then the elders of Joseph Monastery began to dress him, and the Grand Prince’s stewards were sent away. And then his cross clerks with the protopope began to sing matins for him, and the hours, and the canons, and the canon for burial, and they sang everything as had been done when he was alive.

And then many people went to him to take leave: boyar sons, and young princes, and merchants, and funeral attendants, and all the people who had not been with him; and there was great weeping and wailing among all the people.

On the morning of Thursday, at the first hour of the day, Metropolitan Daniel ordered the great bell to be rung.

And his boyar Mikhail Yuryevich, after speaking with the Metropolitan and with the Grand Prince’s brothers and with the boyars, ordered a grave to be dug in the Archangel Cathedral beside his father, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, opposite the image of our venerable father Simeon the Year-Guide.

And after speaking with the Metropolitan, Mikhail Yuryevich sent for the tent-keeper Rusin Ivanov, son of Semyon, to take the measure from him, from the Grand Prince, and ordered him to bring a stone coffin.

Then Metropolitan Daniel came, and with him Bishop Vassian of Kolomna and Dositheus of Krutitsy; but other bishops were then in their own regions and therefore did not arrive in time. The archimandrites then were Jonah of Chudov and Philotheus of Simonov, Zosima of Andronikov, the abbot of the Trinity, the abbot of Joseph, all the Moscow abbots and all the Moscow protopopes, and the priests.

Then came the Grand Prince’s brothers, Prince Yuri and Prince Andrei Ivanovich, and all the boyars, and all the people, weeping and wailing bitterly; and then they ordered his singing clerks, the large company, to stand at the doors of the chamber, and they began to sing the great “Holy God.”

Then the elders of the Trinity and of Joseph took the body of the Grand Prince, the monk Varlaam, and carried it into the front room; and there were many tears and much wailing among the people who had not seen him.
