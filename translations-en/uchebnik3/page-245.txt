On the Consecration of the Church of the Deposition of the Robe

In the same year, on August 31, the church of the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Pure was consecrated in the metropolitan’s court.

On the City of Vladimir

In the same year the city of Vladimir was founded, and the clerk Vasily Mamyrev founded it.

On the Birth of Simeon, Son of the Grand Prince
In the year 6995 from Adam (the year 1495 from Christ)

On March 21, at the seventh hour of the day, a son, Simeon, was born to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich.

On the Capture of Kazan

In that same spring, on April 11, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus sent his commanders to Kazan: Prince Daniil Dmitrievich Kholmsky, Prince Alexander Vasilyevich Obolensky, Prince Semyon Ivanovich Ryapolovsky, and Prince Semyon Romanovich, on Maundy Thursday; and the Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin the Grand Prince sent off on the Tuesday of the second week after Easter, April 24.

And the Grand Prince’s commanders came with their troops to the city of Kazan on May 18, Thursday, in the fifth week after Easter. And they took the city of Kazan on July 9, and captured the Kazan Tsar Alegam with his mother and tsaritsa, with two brothers and a sister, and with his princes, and brought them to Moscow.

On July 20 news came to the Grand Prince that his commanders had seized the Bulgar city of Kazan and captured the Kazan Tsar Alegam; and Prince Fyodor Khripun Ryapolovsky galloped in with that news.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, by his will, placed Tsar Magamet-Amin on the throne in Kazan, and put the rebellious princes and ulans to death together with other rebels; and Tsar Alegam with the tsaritsa the Grand Prince sent into confinement in Vologda, while his mother, brothers, and sisters the Grand Prince sent into confinement to Beloozero, to Kargolom.

On the Relocation of the Novgorodians

In the same year the Grand Prince transferred from Veliky Novgorod to Vladimir 50 families of the best Novgorod merchants.

On the Chamber

In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, Mark Fryazin built a great chamber in the Grand Prince’s court, where the terem had stood.
