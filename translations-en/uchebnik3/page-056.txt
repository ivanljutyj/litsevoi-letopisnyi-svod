As the God-seer Prophet Moses said: “There is nothing to add to them, nor anything to take away from them.” And the divine Apostle Peter, praising them, sings: “Into which the angels desire to peer.” And the Apostle Paul says: “If we or an angel from heaven should preach to you anything beyond what we preached to you, let him be accursed.” Since all this is true and has been testified to us, rejoicing in it as one who has received great profit, let us receive the divine rules with delight and wholly and unshakably hold to the decrees of these rules, set forth by the all-praised apostles, the holy trumpets of the Spirit, and by the holy six ecumenical councils, assembled in various places to set forth these commandments, and by our holy fathers. Since all of them were enlightened by one and the same Spirit, having legislated what is profitable. Whom they delivered to anathema, we also anathematize; whom they cast out, we also cast out; whom they delivered to excommunication, we also excommunicate; on whom they laid penance, on them we also lay penance.

From the 19th Epistle of Nikon

Nikon says in his 19th epistle, naming the number, in what years he lived: “Now in our days, in the year 1072,” and thus he named the year in which he lived. And he also says in his 40th epistle about the Franks: “This is the Frankish people, which is called Germans in the divine writings: first they destroyed Rome and rooted out the ancient Romans, the Vandal people. That people consisted of various heretics: Macedonians, Nestorians, Arians, and similar ones. And from that time various heresies existing among the Romans penetrated into Rome. Now Rome belongs to the Franks, who, living in Rome, profess various heresies. Whoever wishes to learn more about the fall of Rome and the disappearance of the Vandal people and these heresies, let him read the Life of Saint Melania the Roman, or the Patericon called Lausiac, and there he will find what he needs. About the Frankish fall let him read the book of epistles of Photius, Patriarch of Constantinople, and there he will find a detailed exposition by that same Patriarch Photius. Because there Photius speaks in detail about everything, about unleavened bread and other things, and also Patriarch Photius sets out in detail the exposition of the holy Creed. The Franks, in order to compose their false teaching, bring their books and answer and argue as they wish, as in other heresies they violate, just as the Armenians, wishing to compose their teaching, falsely expounded the 24th canon, saying thus: ‘We command the prosphora to be offered on the same day; this is acceptable,’ because when they bring it on the same day, then they think to make the offering. But the sacred canon is not like that, but says that a layman who cuts off his member is excommunicated for 3 years as an enemy of his life. But they perform and say and violate, wishing to fulfill their desires.
