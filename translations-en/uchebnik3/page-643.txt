The tsarevich conquered the king's Volhynia, though Christians also live in Volhynia. That is our sovereign's truth and beginning, that he stands for the peace of Christians. Both the voivode and the marshal Ostap said that the king was pleased with all this, and the king is sending his envoys to the tsar and grand prince.

Concerning the Report from the Voivodes from Ivangorod

In the same month the voivodes wrote from Ivangorod that from Rugodiv they were incessantly shelling Ivangorod and killing people; and they sent to Rugodiv with a complaint that they were shooting contrary to the safe-conduct charter and causing discord, while they themselves asked for a term of two weeks, firing from cannons and killing people; but the Germans refused them: "The little prince is shooting; we cannot restrain him."

And the tsar and grand prince wrote to the voivodes and to the clerk Shestak Voronin and ordered them to fire from all cannons at Rugodiv, but he did not order them to wage war on all the other border regions. And they fired for a week from all cannons, both from direct fire and from the upper tier, with stone and incendiary shot, and brought them into great distress and killed people.

And on Great Saturday the Rugodiv posadniki came out to them and petitioned the voivodes that the sovereign show them mercy, forgive their guilt, and take them into his subjection, and they would not stand for the little prince; he had done wrong to his own ruin, and they had withdrawn from the Master and from the whole Livonian land, so that they might be allowed to go to the sovereign, and for that time they would give other people to the voivodes as hostages.

And the voivodes, Prince Grigory Andreevich Kurakin and Ivan Andreevich Buturlin, and the clerk Shestak Voronin sent Bogdan Rzhanikov about this, that the Rugodiv people were petitioning the sovereign and giving themselves over to the sovereign's entire will. And they sent the ratmans and posadniki, the burgomasters, and their own palatniki, the brothers of the posadniki Yakim Kromysh and Zakhar Vandin with their companions, to petition the sovereign; and in Ivangorod they placed as hostages with the voivodes their palatniki, the best people Ivan Bely and Ashpir Chermny, so that during that time, until the sovereign showed them favor, they should not communicate with the Master or seek help for themselves, but should be in the sovereign's will.
