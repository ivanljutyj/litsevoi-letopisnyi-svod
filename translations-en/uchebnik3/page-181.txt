And from there he went to the table for dinner. And in the middle of his dinner a fire broke out in Moscow inside the city near the St. Nicholas Gate, at the fifth hour of the day, between the churches of the Presentation of the Mother of God and Cosmas and Damian, and almost the whole city burned. It burned as far as the court of the grand prince, and as far as the Spassky Monastery, and as far as the court of Prince Mikhail Andreevich, and along the lower part to the court of Fyodor Davydovich; they barely stopped it by the third hour of the night, since the grand prince himself stood at all the dangerous places with many people. Ten stone churches alone burned, and the eleventh, the Ascension, burned inside as well, except for the stone walls, and those 10; and 12 wooden churches burned; and two wooden annexes of the Archangel were pulled down, the Resurrection and St. Akila.

In the same month, on the 21st day, the grand prince's envoy Dmitry Lazarev came running from the Horde.

On the Grand Prince's Journey to Novgorod

In the same month, on the 22nd day, a Sunday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich set out for Novgorod the Great in peace with many people, and in Moscow he left his son, Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich.

And on St. Demetrius's Day the grand prince entered Volok, and ate and drank at the house of his brother Prince Boris.

And on the first of November, a Wednesday, he entered Torzhok. On the 5th day at Volochok the Novgorodians Kuzma Yakovlev and his companions met the grand prince with complaints against their own brothers, the Novgorodians.

And there too Vasily Nikiforov Penkov met him with gifts from Vladyka Feofil.

On the 7th day of November the grand prince stood at Vira, where the Novgorod podvoisky Nazar met him with gifts, and there also Loshinsky met him with his cousin Fyodor, also with gifts.

On the 14th day, a Tuesday, at Ozhegi on the Khoriva, his governor Semyon Borisov and his steward Roman Alekseev met the grand prince.

On the 15th day, a Wednesday, at Volma the Novgorod posadniks met the grand prince: Feofilat Zakharyin, Yakov Fedotov, Kuzma Fefilatov; and from the prosperous townspeople: Ulian Plyusnin and Fyodor Ievl, and Matfey Derevyazhnik, with gifts from Novgorod and from themselves; and there also Fyodor Isakov met him with gifts.
