Yuri Ivanovich and his companions, at the upper reaches of the Narrow Mochak on the Karaibulak; and by God's mercy and the good fortune of the tsar and grand prince they killed many people and freed many Russian captives.

And the Astrakhan people began to petition the voivodes. Prince Karaiklesh arrived and began to petition the voivodes, Prince Yuri and his companions, on behalf of the princes, ulans, murzas, and all Astrakhan people, that their tsar, the grand prince, show them favor, not order them slain and scattered, but order them to serve him, the tsar and grand prince, and Tsar Derbysh-Alei, and that they would give an oath on this, that they would serve the tsar and grand prince honorably.

And Iklesh himself gave the oath on this. And the voivodes released Iklesh to the princes and ulans and to all the Astrakhan people to tell them of the sovereign's favor, that the tsar and grand prince did not order them slain and scattered.

And the Astrakhan people — Prince Ishim, Prince Alei, Prince Kourat-Klesh, and many other people — gave an oath to the tsar and grand prince that they would serve the tsar and grand prince and Tsar Derbysh-Alei honorably, and give up all Russian captives, wherever they had been brought from and in whatever horde they had been bought.

And they went in boats with the voivodes to petition Tsar Derbysh-Alei. The tsar showed them favor: he ordered the best people to live in the city with him, and sent the common people back to the uluses with their wives and children.

And the voivodes sent charters to the uluses to the common people, that, having come to the city, they should give an oath to the tsar and grand prince and Tsar Derbysh-Alei.

And many people from all the uluses came to the tsar and voivodes and gave the oath, and gave up much Russian captivity. And from all the uluses there were 500 princes and murzas, and seven thousand common people; and they gave the oath for the whole land. And the Astrakhan people said of Tsar Emgurchei that he had fled to Azov, and with him about twenty men.

And detachments from the voivodes were sent throughout the whole Astrakhan land, and those Astrakhans who had fled to the sea, to the islands, were all reached and brought to the city.

And the remaining people were overtaken a day's journey from Tyumen, and captured, and brought to Astrakhan.

And Enguvat-azi arrived, and with him many mullahs and azis and three thousand people of all kinds, and they gave an oath to the tsar and grand prince and to Tsar Derbysh: he was at that time their chief man.

And the voivodes, Prince Yuri Ivanovich and his companions, entered the city and brought the whole Astrakhan land to the oath, and entrusted it to Tsar Derbysh-Alei.

And Tsar Derbysh-Alei and the whole Astrakhan land, having united with the tsar, pledged themselves to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and to his children.
