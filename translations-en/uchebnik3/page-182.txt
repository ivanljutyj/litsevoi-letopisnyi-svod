On the seventeenth day, Friday, at the mouth of the Volma the Grand Prince stood at Vlukovo; there many posadniks met him: Zakharia Ovin, his brother Kuzma, his son-in-law Ivan Kuzmin, and Zakharia’s son Ioann, and Kuzmin Vasily; and the thousand-man Mikhailo Berdenev, and the boyar Grigory Mikhailov.

After those, at the same camp, other posadniks: Afanasy Gruz, his brother Timofei, Ivan Shenkursky, Nikita Afanasov, and Kuzma, Gruz’s brother, with his son Timofei, and Matfei; the thousand-man Pavel Telyatev, his brother Fedor; and the prosperous Ivan Konstantinov with his companions.

And on that same day they met the Grand Prince: Nikita Esipov, son of Grigoriev, and Semen Fedorov, son of Samsonov, and the thousand-man Esip Maksimov, and Fedor Semenov; the prosperous townsmen — Okinf Vasiliev, Konstantin Kolesnitsyn, Fedor Lotoshkov, Pavel Fedorov, his brother Grigory — these are prosperous, and Nazar the podvoisky.

And on that same day they met the Grand Prince: the posadnik Alexander Samsonovich, and the posadniks’ sons: Yuri Ivanov, son of Grigoriev, Ivan Esipov Goroshkov, Ivan Dmitriev from among the prosperous, and Yuri Marinin, Martemian Bozhin.

And having come out of Vlukovo, the Grand Prince stood at Rydin on the river Kholova; at that camp the Archbishop of Great Novgorod, Feofil, met him a hundred versts from the city, and with him Prince Vasily Vasilievich Grebenka Shuysky, and the senior posadnik Vasily Ananyin, and the posadnik Ivan Afanasov, and the posadnik Kirill Yakovlev, and the senior thousand-man Vasily Esipov, and Vasily Nikiforov, and Isaak Semenov Shenkursky, and the archimandrite of the Yuriev Monastery Feodosy, and the hegumen of Khutyn Nafanail, and the hegumen of Vezhitsy Varlaam, and the treasurer Sergei, and the confessor Evfimy; from the lord — two barrels of wine, one red and the other white; and from all those — a skin of wine each.

And after those, Vasily Glazoemtsov and Rodion Norov and the Novgorod boyars and prosperous people bowed to the Grand Prince: Kirill, and his brother Mikhailo, the sons of Ivan Makariev, Nikifor Khmelev, Samuil Derevyazhnik, Alexei Skokukhin, Nikifor Stefanov, Alexander Klimentiev, Ivan Vafromeev, Mikhailo Boguslavl, Nikita Grigoriev, Matfei Konstantinov Domozhirov, Vasily Filippov — a skin each; Luka and Vasily Polinaryin — a skin; Kiprian Grigoriev with two children — two skins; Ivan Ananyin and Alexander Lukin Slyakov — a skin; Vasily Anfimov the podvoisky — a skin.
