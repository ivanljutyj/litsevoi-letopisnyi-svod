May we live in all piety and purity. Exclamation. “Thou, King of peace and Savior of our souls, and to Thee we send up glory, to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit.”

And after “Amen” the metropolitan ordered the same two archimandrites to bring him the cap from the analogion, and the metropolitan took the cap, that is, the crown, and crossed the grand prince with the cross, saying: “In the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit,” and placed the cap on him. And the metropolitan ordered the litany to be proclaimed: “Have mercy on us, O God”; and the metropolitan read the prayer to the Most Pure Mother of God: “O Most Holy Virgin, Lady Mother of God.”

And after the prayer the tsar sat in his place, and the metropolitan in his, and the archdeacon ascended the ambo and proclaimed Many Years to the Russian Tsar Ivan Vasilievich and Many Years to all the consecrated council of the Russian metropolis.

And after the Many Years the metropolitan greeted the great tsar: “By God’s mercy, rejoice and be well, Orthodox Tsar Ivan, autocrat of all Rus, for many years”; and the metropolitan bowed to the tsar.

And then the archbishops and bishops and the whole council bowed and greeted the great tsar, and the boyars greeted the great autocrat; the metropolitan performed the dismissal of the supplicatory service, and then the liturgy began.

And after the completion of the liturgy, the great Tsar Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of Great Rus, went out from the Church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God, and velvets and silks were spread along the path from the church to the chambers whither the great autocrat was going. And when the great tsar came down from his place, at the church doors his brother Prince Yuri Vasilievich showered him with gold coins, and after him the great tsar’s boyar and equerry Prince Mikhail Vasilievich Glinsky carried a gold dish with gold coins; and opposite the Archangel Cathedral he showered him, and on the middle stair he also showered him.

The Tsar’s Journey

In the same month, on the 25th day, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus rode to the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery to pray and to venerate the relics of the wonderworker Sergius, and to hear the supplicatory singing; and he ordered the abbot and all the elders of the great monastery to pray to God for his health, for God’s mercy, and for the strengthening of his kingdom; and, having bestowed alms and provisions on them, he arrived in Moscow in the same month on the 28th day.

In the same month, on the 30th day, Sunday, Cyprian, former abbot of the Epiphany Monastery in Moscow, was installed by Metropolitan Macarius as bishop of Perm and Vologda.
