On the Envoys

In the same year, in August, there came from the Crimea the envoy of the Grand Prince, the okolnichy Mikhail Vasilyevich Tuchkov; and together with him there came to the Grand Prince from the Crimean Tsar Magamet-Kirei an envoy named Anchura-Duvan with brotherhood and friendship.

On the Death of the Archbishop of Rostov

In the same month of August, on the 28th day, Archbishop Vassian of Rostov reposed at Dorogomilovo, and he was laid to rest in Rostov.

On the Death of the Bishop of Suzdal.
In the year 7024 from Adam (the year 1524 from Christ)

On November the 12th day, Bishop Semion of Suzdal reposed, and his body was laid to rest in Suzdal.

On the Envoys

That same winter, in December, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, sent back the Crimean envoy Yanchura-Duvan; and together with him the Grand Prince sent to the Crimea to Tsar Magamet-Kirei, son of Menli-Girei, his envoy, a close man, Ivan Grigoriev, son of Mamontov, with friendship and brotherhood; and by God’s will Ivan Mamontov reposed in the Crimea.

That same winter, in March, the envoy of the Grand Prince, Vasily Andreevich Korobov, came from Tsargrad to Moscow.

And Vasily Kopyl set out from Tsargrad for Holy Mount Athos with alms from Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, while Varavin remained in Tsargrad to carry out the needs of the Grand Prince.

On the Death of Serapion, Former Archbishop of Novgorod

That same spring, on March the 16th day, Serapion, former Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, reposed, and his body was laid to rest at the Trinity in the Sergiyev Monastery.

On the Envoys

In the same year, in June, the Grand Prince sent back the envoy of the Danish king Kristiern, David the Old; and together with him the Grand Prince sent to the Danish king his clerk Nekras Kharlamov, and he also sent with him a treaty charter to the king.
