On the Ambassadors from the Master of Livonia

In the same month there came to the tsar and grand prince from the Master of Livonia and the Bishop of Yuryev ambassadors: Fedor Fershtirbertrekh, brother of the Master, Klaus, Simon, Melkhir, and the bishop’s envoys, the Yuryev archimandrite Velva, and Ganus Ivan, Vlas Beka. But Klaus and the archimandrite died on the way.

Fedor and his companions were received by the tsar and sovereign and petitioned the sovereign to order their embassy to be heard. And the tsar and sovereign ordered his okolnichy Aleksei Fedorovich Adashev and the clerk Ivan Mikhailov to hear their embassy. The ambassadors petitioned that their land had been conquered and that they could not pay tribute because they had nothing, and asked the sovereign to show them mercy, not to order tribute taken from them, and that in other tributes they would make correction henceforth. And the tsar and grand prince ordered this answer given to the ambassadors: the tsar and sovereign had shown them mercy and given a safe-conduct for the ambassadors. And after the safe-conduct the sovereign forbade fighting, but they from Rugodiv for another two weeks fired on Ivangorod and killed people; then the tsar and grand prince ordered firing on Rugodiv and war against them. But God sent His mercy: the voivodes took Rugodiv, and the sovereign ordered military operations against other towns as much as God would help them. But they cannot be trusted: whatever they swear to, in all of it they lie. And if the Master wished, he himself and the bishop should petition for their offenses and impose tribute on all their land. And the sovereign dismissed the ambassadors without success.

On Sending the Voivodes to Syrensk

In the same month the sovereign ordered his voivodes Daniil Fedorovich Adashev, Pavel Petrovich Zabolotsky, Ivan Sharapov-Zamytsky, and the clerk Shestak Voronin to go from Rugodiv to Syrensk with artillery. And to Daniil and his companions there came commanders from Novgorod with men: Boris Kolychev and Vasily Razladin Kvashnin. And they set out from Rugodiv to Syrensk.

On the Arrival at the Town of Syrensk

In the same month of May, on the 25th day, Daniil Fedorovich Adashev with his companions came to Syrensk, five versts away at Skamya, on June 2, and by the sovereign’s instruction sent word to the boyars in Pskov, and to Prince Fedor Troekurov in Novgorod, that he with men and cannon was five versts from Syrensk, and that they should by the sovereign’s instruction go to Syrensk.
