All those commanders went with their regiments across Lake Ilmen on the ice.

And all the commanders from both sides came in one night, from Monday to Tuesday, and occupied Gorodishche and all the monasteries near the city.

On November 25, Tuesday, at Sytino the grand prince ordered an answer to be given to the lord and the Novgorod envoys through Prince Ivan Yuryevich, together with Vasily and Ivan Borisovich.

Prince Ivan Yuryevich said thus: “Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus answers you, his pilgrim the lord, and the posadniks and well-to-do people, thus: that you, our pilgrim, and you, posadniks and well-to-do people, petitioned me, the grand prince, concerning our patrimony Great Novgorod, saying that we, the grand princes, have laid our wrath upon our patrimony Novgorod.”

And then Vasily Borisovich said: “The grand prince says to you, his pilgrim the archbishop, and to the posadniks and well-to-do people and to all who are with you: you know yourselves that you sent to us, the grand princes, from our patrimony, from all Great Novgorod, your envoys Nazar the podvoysky and Zakhar the veche clerk, and called us, the grand princes, your sovereigns; and we, the grand princes, according to your sending and your petition, sent to you, the lord, and to our patrimony Great Novgorod our boyars Fyodor Davydovich, and Ivan and Semyon Borisovich, ordering them to ask you, our pilgrim, and our patrimony Novgorod: what sovereignty of ours, the grand princes, do you desire in our patrimony Great Novgorod? And you refused this before us, and said that you had not sent envoys to us about it, and laid upon us, the grand princes, the claim that we were committing violence against you, our patrimony; but not only have you laid falsehood upon us, your sovereigns, but many other disobediences of yours toward us, the grand princes, and much disrespect of yours are being done by you; and we restrained ourselves in this, awaiting your turning to us, but you again appeared to us as deceivers; and for that we could no longer endure you and your malice and wantonness, and we resolved to march with an army against you, according to the Lord’s word: if your brother sins against you, go and reprove him between you and him alone; if he listens to you, you have gained your brother; if he does not listen to you, take with you two or three witnesses, for at the mouth of two or three witnesses every word shall be established; if he does not listen to them, tell the church; if he begins not to care for the church either, he shall be as a pagan and a tax collector; but we, the grand princes, sent to you, our patrimony: cease your wickedness and evil deeds, and we, according to our former favor, favor you, our patrimony.
