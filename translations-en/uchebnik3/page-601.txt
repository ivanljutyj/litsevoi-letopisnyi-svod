And after waiting for all the men from the Field, the sovereign set out for Moscow; and he came to Moscow, and the sovereign rewarded the voivodes and boyar sons who had fought the Crimeans. And altogether with the voivodes there were four thousand boyar sons, and with their men, and Cossacks, and streltsy, and baggage-train men, thirteen thousand; and of these six thousand were sent to the baggage trains; and on the night before Thursday only about five hundred men came up to them, while all the rest, with all the mounted men, rode off to the borderlands unharmed; and in the battle two thousand boyar sons were killed and captured, and thirty-four streltsy, and five thousand boyar retainers.

On the sending from Lithuania to the Metropolitan

In the same month of July, the Bishop of Vilna Paul and the Voivode of Vilna, Lord Nicholas Radivilovich, sent from Lithuania to Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus', and to the boyar Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Shuisky, with a letter, the merchant Demeshko, asking that the Metropolitan pray and incline the mind of Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus' to desire peace with the King.

On the sending from the Metropolitan to Lithuania

And on the twenty-fifth day of August the Metropolitan sent to Lithuania his clerk Savluk Turpeev with letters, and he wrote concerning agreement and peace, and from the Tsar and Grand Prince he sent a letter of safe conduct for the King's ambassadors.

On the sending from Astrakhan

In the same month of August, the streltsy head sent from Astrakhan the centurion Stefan Kobelev, and he reports that Grigory Kaftyrev had arrived with the streltsy and Fedka Pavlov with the Cossacks, and the ambassador Pyotr Turgenev; and Tsar Derbysh and all the Astrakhan people had fled from the city, because it had been falsely told them that the Tsar and Grand Prince had sent an army against them and ordered them all to be beaten, and out of fear they fled; and the Crimean Tsar had sent to them three tsareviches and his prince Chegilek, with cannon and handguns.

And Grigory communicated with Tsar Derbysh and with all the Astrakhan people, and told them that their Tsar and Grand Prince had shown them favor, and had sent his envoy Leonty Mansurov to Derbysh, and had released the tsaritsas, and had released their ambassadors Iklesh and Tonotar, and the sovereign had remitted their tribute for the whole year and forgiven them.
