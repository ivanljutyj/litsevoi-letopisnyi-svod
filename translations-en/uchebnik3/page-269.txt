About the Lithuanian Envoy

After their petition, Prince Alexander of Lithuania sent to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich to plead concerning their crime, that the Grand Prince, for his sake, should pardon the German merchants.

About the German Envoy

Also from the Prince Master an envoy came to the Grand Prince, from all the Livonian land and from 70 overseas cities, to plead about the same thing, that the Grand Prince should show favor, release the German merchants, and order negotiations about land affairs on the Narva River, on the island.

Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus, at the intercession of his son-in-law Grand Prince Alexander and at the petition of the Master, the overseas Germans, and all the Livonian land, pardoned their merchants, released them from prison, and sent them to their land in the year 7003 from Adam (1503 from Christ), in April.

On January 6, Tuesday, Lithuanian envoys came to Moscow from Grand Prince Alexander Andreevich Kazimir to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus, to take his daughter Grand Princess Elena to Grand Prince Alexander: Prince Alexander Olshansky Otoksta, Pan Jan Zaberezsky, and Pan Yuri Zinovievich.

The Grand Prince Sent His Daughter Princess Elena to Lithuania to Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania

In the same month, on the 13th day, Tuesday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent his daughter Princess Elena to Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania, and Abbot Simon of the Trinity blessed Princess Elena, because there was no metropolitan then; and he sent with her his envoys together with their wives, Prince Semyon Ivanovich Ryapolovsky, Mikhail Yakovlevich Rusalka, and clerk Vasily Kuleshin, and many other princes and boyar children of his court.

And they came to Vilna on February 15, on the Sunday of the Prodigal Son, and first Grand Princess Elena came to the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God of our Greek confession, and there they served prayer services, and went from there to the wedding, and they were wed in the Church of St. Stanislaus of the Roman confession by the Bishop of Vilna in Latin, and by the Grand Prince’s priest Thomas according to the Greek rite.

And from there Grand Prince Alexander, having honored the envoys of the Grand Prince and the boyar children of his court, sent them away with great honors.
