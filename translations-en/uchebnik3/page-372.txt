On the Capture of Tsar Shigalei

That same winter, in December, the Grand Prince captured Tsar Shigalei, who had been at Kazan.

On the Consecration of the Church of the Ascension of the Lord in Kolomenskoye.
In the year 7041 from Adam (the year 1541 from Christ)

On Tuesday, the 3rd day of September, in Kolomenskoye, the stone church of the Ascension of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ was consecrated by Daniel, Metropolitan of All Rus, and Archbishop Kirill of Rostov, and Bishop Vassian of Kolomna, and Dositheus of Krutitsy, and archimandrites, and hegumens, and the consecrated council. And at that consecration were Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich with Grand Princess Elena, and with his son Prince Ivan, and with his brothers: Prince Georgy and Andrey Ivanovich.

And the sovereign made a great, bright, and joyful celebration, and the Metropolitan with the council, the Grand Prince’s brothers, and the boyars feasted at the Grand Prince’s in Kolomenskoye for three days honorably and joyfully. And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich bestowed gifts on the Metropolitan and his brothers. And on the fourth day he came to Moscow.

On the Envoys

In the same year, on the 2nd day of September, the Grand Prince sent Prince Burnak to Tsar Yanalei in Kazan, and with him he sent Vasily Alekseev, son of Pushkin, to the Tsar with a charter.

On the Crimean Envoys

In the same month of September the Grand Prince sent back the Crimean envoys and a courier to their sovereign.

On the Nogai Envoys

And in the same month of September, on the 4th day, the Nogai envoy Bauk with his companions came to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus, and with them were merchants with horses from Koshum Murza and from other murzas.

And they petitioned the great sovereign that the Grand Prince show them favor and permit the Nogai merchants to trade.
