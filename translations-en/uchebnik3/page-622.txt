On the Arrival of the Metropolitan from Constantinople

In the same month there came from Constantinople, from Patriarch Dionysius, the metropolitan Joasaph of Cyzicus to the tsar and grand prince, and he brought from the patriarch to the tsar and sovereign and to the tsaritsa relics of the martyr George and of Panteleimon, and of the apostle Barnabas. The patriarch wrote asking for alms because of the oppression by the Turkish people; and the metropolitan said to the tsar and sovereign on behalf of Patriarch Dionysius that Patriarch Dionysius of Constantinople, with the archbishops and bishops and the whole Council of Constantinople, had decided in council to pray to God for the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus, as for the former pious tsars, and with their own hands they signed that they would forever commemorate and pray to God for Tsar Ivan, and whoever does not begin to pray to God for them, let him be excommunicated.
