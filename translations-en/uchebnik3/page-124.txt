Grand Prince Ivan and the princes Yuri, Andrei, and Boris; and Andrei the Younger, still a child, was carried in arms. And all crossed themselves.

The Grand Prince, having shed many tears, as did also the metropolitan, and the others take from the icon case the wonderworking icon of the Most Holy Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary and entrust it to Bishop Misail of Smolensk, who had asked for it; and besides that, many other icons as well, smaller than that one, adorned with gold and stones and pearls, of the same captivity, though not requested by the bishop, and the Grand Prince gave them all to him.

Of these, His Eminence Metropolitan Jonah took one icon, the image of the Most Holy Lady with the Infant, and said: “O bishop, son and brother of our humility! This image of the Most Holy Mother of God shall be for a blessing and for a remembrance of the day when you, having come, found the priceless treasure that for many years was hidden from you, and left it to my lord and son, the Grand Prince, to his princess and their children, as a remembrance of this day.” And he gladly did everything according to his words.

And the metropolitan summoned the bishop; both took it in their hands and blessed with it the Grand Prince and his princess and their noble children, and gave it into the hands of the Grand Prince. The Grand Prince received the image of the Most Holy Mother of God, kissed it with tears, and ordered them to go out of the church with the image of the Most Holy Mother of God which he was sending to Smolensk. And they went, carrying the wondrous icon of the Most Holy Mother of God; after it followed the metropolitan with the consecrated council, carrying with them also that icon which was left as a blessing to the Grand Prince. After them went the Grand Prince with his children, with the princes and with the boyars. At Moscow then there was a great multitude of soldiery, and all of them followed, and all the people of the glorious city of Moscow.

The Grand Prince escorted it to the Church of the Annunciation, which is called “on Dorogomilovo,” two versts from the city.

And from there he returned to the icon that remained with him, and as he went he also celebrated prayer services. And coming to the Church of the Annunciation, he ordered it to be placed in the place where it had stood before, when he sent off that icon.

And he ordered another icon to be painted for that place, taking its measure and repeating the image; and before the one that was left, he ordered the priests to sing a prayer service every day: an akathist with ikoi. This was on the eighteenth day of January, a Sunday, on the commemoration of our holy fathers Athanasius and Cyril.

On the Campaign of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich against Veliky Novgorod with an Army

That same winter, on the nineteenth day of January, a Monday, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, because the Novgorodians had violated the treaty, set out against Novgorod with an army.
