And on the eve of the Sunday before St Nicholas’s Day, at night, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich was visibly anointed with oil.

And he commanded his spiritual father, Alexei, archpriest of the Annunciation, and Gregory, priest of the Annunciation, to serve the Divine Liturgy on Sunday in the Church of the Nativity of the Holy Theotokos and to bring the Holy Communion to him.

Then there was a wondrous miracle, which the Lord wrought according to his true piety: before that the Grand Prince had been unable to turn from the side on which he lay, but they had turned him; and he ordered that he be told when the Gifts were being carried, and for himself he ordered a chair to be brought to the bed. And when the Holy Communion had been brought to him, he rose himself, though Mikhailo Yuryevich lifted him a little; and the Grand Prince sat in the chair, and Archpriest Alexei brought him the Holy Gifts, and Priest Gregory the dora; and then he stood up on his own feet, and with great reverence and tears reverently partook of the Most Pure Body and Blood of Christ our God; he also took a little of the dora and of the bread of the Most Pure Theotokos, and of the warm water, and of the kutia, and tasted a little of the prosphora.

And then he lay down again on his bed and summoned his father, Metropolitan Daniel, and his brothers, Prince Yuri Ivanovich and Prince Andrew Ivanovich, and all his boyars, for at that time many boyars had come together from their patrimonies, having heard of the sovereign’s infirmity. And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich began to speak to his father, Metropolitan Daniel, and to his brothers Prince Yuri and Prince Andrew, and to all the boyars: “I commend my son Ivan to God and to the Most Pure Theotokos and to the holy wonderworkers, and to you, my father Daniel, Metropolitan of All Rus, and I give him my realm, with which my father, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus, blessed me. And you, my brothers Prince Yuri and Prince Andrew, should stand firmly by your word, on which you kissed the cross, and by the agreements that were between us; and you, my brothers, should stand together concerning the ordering of the land and military affairs against the enemies of my son and your own, so that the hand of Orthodox Christians may be exalted over the Basurman and Latin peoples; and you, boyars and boyars’ sons and princelings, should stand together with my son and with my brothers against the enemies, and serve my son as you served me, honorably.”

And he began to speak to his boyars: “You know yourselves that our realm of Vladimir, Novgorod, and Moscow descends from Grand Prince Vladimir of Kiev; we are your sovereigns by birth, and you are our hereditary boyars; and you, brothers, stand firm, so that my son may be established as sovereign in the realm, and so that there may be justice in the land.”

The Grand Prince grieved greatly and grew weak; he did not feel his illness, and his wound did not increase, but only the stench from it was grievous.
