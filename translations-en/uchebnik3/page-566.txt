And the pious tsar showed favor to Tsar Alexander Safa-Kireevich and ordered him to live in his own royal house. And he ordered that he be taught letters, since he was still young, so that he might become accustomed to the fear of God and learn the Christian law.

Concerning the Kazan Tsar’s Message to the Metropolitan (About His Wish) to Petition

In the same month the Kazan Tsar Ediger-Magmet, who had been taken captive in Kazan, sent to the Metropolitan and petitioned him to intercede with the Tsar and Grand Prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich: that the sovereign would show him favor, grant him property, and not order him executed for his ignorance; and that the sovereign would show him favor, bring him into the true Christian law, order him baptized, and accept his true desire for the faith of Christ.

And the Metropolitan sent archimandrites and abbots to him over many days to determine whether he wished to accept the Christian law under compulsion; but he affirmed with an oath that he desired with love to believe truly in Christ and cursed the false Muhammad and his foul laws.

And Metropolitan Macarius petitioned the Tsar and Grand Prince on his behalf, and the sovereign showed him favor for the sake of the Christian faith, received him, and permitted him to be baptized.

Concerning the Baptism of the Kazan Tsar

That same winter, on February 26, the second Sunday of Lent, the Kazan Tsar Ediger-Magmet was baptized on the river at the Tainik, and in holy baptism was named Simeon. At the baptism were the pious Tsar and Grand Prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, and Metropolitan Macarius, and the brother of the Tsar and Grand Prince, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and the Bishop of Krutitsy, and the whole council, archimandrites and abbots, and archpriests, and a multitude of boyars. And Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, received him from the font, while his spiritual father Amos, archpriest of St Nicholas Gostunsky, and Bishop Savva of Krutitsy performed the priestly office.

The Tsar and Grand Prince Shows Favor to Tsar Simeon

And the Tsar and Grand Prince showed favor to Tsar Simeon: he gave him a court in the city and appointed Ivan Petrovich Zabolotsky in the place of a boyar at his court, and all officials according to the sovereign’s rank, and maintained him not as captives are kept, but as a tsar and tsar’s son, according to his dignity, and as appanage princes.
