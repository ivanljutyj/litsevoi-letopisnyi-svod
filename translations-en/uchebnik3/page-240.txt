RUS (1491–1510 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 17

On the Death of the Grand Princess of Tver

That same winter the Grand Princess, wife of Grand Prince Mikhail of Tver, died.

On the Death of Prince Vasily Yaroslavich

That same winter Prince Vasily Yaroslavich died in chains.

On the Death of Princess Elena

That same spring, on April 2, Princess Elena, wife of Prince Andrei Vasilievich, died.

On the Lots for the Novgorod Archbishopric

That same summer, on July 17, the Grand Prince of All Rus Ivan Vasilievich, having consulted with his father Metropolitan Gerontius, with Joasaph, Archbishop of Rostov, and with the bishops—Simeon, Bishop of Ryazan, Gerasimus, Bishop of Kolomna, and Prokhor, Bishop of Sar—placed upon the throne lots bearing the names of Eliseus, Archimandrite of the Spassky Monastery, Gennady, Archimandrite of the Chudov Monastery, and Sergius, elder of the Trinity, for the archbishopric in Veliky Novgorod.

And the Metropolitan himself served with all those bishops and archimandrites, and the lot bearing the name of Sergius was drawn for the archbishopric in Veliky Novgorod.

On the Negotiations of the Grand Prince's Boyars with the Germans

That same year the Grand Prince sent Novgorod boyars to Rugodiv for negotiations with the Germans; and they concluded a peace with the Germans for twenty years and brought them to kiss the cross.
