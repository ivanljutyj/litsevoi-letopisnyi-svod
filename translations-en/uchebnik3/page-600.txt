And the commanders set out hurriedly after the tsar along his route, and met the tsar on Wednesday at midday at Sudbishchi, and fought with the tsar until evening, and trampled the tsar’s advanced regiment and his right and left wings, and took the banner of the Shirinsky princes, and fought until night; and there the regiments stood all night.

And the commanders sent back for the heads and boyar sons, that they should hasten to them, for they were fighting the tsar. And few men galloped to them; all the rest turned toward the frontier with the whole baggage train, each whither was nearer for him, to Ryazan and to Mtsensk.

And in the morning, on Thursday, they fought until the fifth hour of the day, sending regiment against regiment in fierce and stubborn battle, and many Crimean Tatars in his advanced regiments were killed.

And the Crimean Tsar came with his regiment and all his men, and routed the commanders and killed many men, and himself went toward Tula.

And the tsar and grand prince set out hastily for Tula, marched all night, and came to Tula on Saturday at sunrise.

And there came to the sovereign the commanders, boyar Ivan Vasilyevich and okolnichey Lev Andreevich, and they told the sovereign that they had sent many men against the baggage train and had taken the tsar’s baggage train, and had fought with the tsar for a day and a half. And of the men who had been sent against the baggage train, many did not come to them, but went with the baggage train to the frontier districts; and the tsar trampled and routed them, and killed many men, and many men fled from the battle, throwing off their weapons.

And all the commanders, God be thanked, are safe and well; Dmitry Pleshcheev and Bakhteyar Zyuzin arrived that same day.

And okolnichey Aleksei Danilovich Basmanov and Stepan Sidorov came upon the baggage train of their regiments in an oak grove and ordered the alarm bell to be rung there and the zurnas to be played, and many boyar sons and boyars’ men and streltsy, some five or six thousand, gathered to him, and there they halted.

And the tsar assaulted them with all his men and with cannon and with arquebuses until evening. And by God’s mercy Aleksei Danilovich held out there against the tsar, and many Tatars were killed with bows and arquebuses.

And the boyar sons whom the Crimean Tsar had captured told him that the tsar and grand prince was in Tula and that they were awaiting his coming against the tsar; and the Crimean Tsar went hastily back and crossed the Sosna River the next day.

And Aleksei came to the sovereign in Tula on Sunday with all his men, God be thanked, safe and well; but Stepan was wounded there at the abatis by a wall gun in the knee, and in the battle he was wounded by a spear, and he lay ill for five weeks, and he died as a monk and in the great schema, in Moscow.
