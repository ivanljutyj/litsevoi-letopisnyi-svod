Throughout all the world, in the realm and dominion of your royal fatherland, grandfatherland, and great-grandfatherland, of your great royal nobility and lordship, Orthodoxy shines, at which the proud serpent, the all-wicked enemy the devil, always rages and raises fierce war against it through impious tsars, your foes—the Crimean tsar and their accomplices, the impious peoples of the Crimean and Kazan Tatars, who look to the bottom of hell, where they will inherit unquenchable fire and outer darkness. But we, your humble pilgrims, O pious Tsar Ivan, always thank and beseech the Lord God and His Mother, the most pure Theotokos, intercessor for all the world, and ask the ineffable bounties of His great mercy—help and strengthening against them for your royal noble host and all your Christ-loving army of Orthodox people, that the Lord may send you from above, to your aid, His swift Archistrategos Michael, intercessor and commander of the holy heavenly powers, who in ancient times was a helper and defender to Abraham against Chedorlaomer, king of Sodom, who had with him three hundred thousand warriors. And Abraham with three hundred and eighteen of his household, by God’s power and the help of the great Archangel Michael, defeated them. The same Archistrategos Michael was a helper to Joshua son of Nun when he surrounded the city of Jericho, in which there were seven Canaanite kings, and by God’s command, through Archangel Michael, the city walls fell of themselves to their foundations, and Joshua son of Nun cut down the kings and all the people. He was also a helper to Gideon against the Midianites, of whom there were a thousand thousand, while Gideon had three hundred of his warriors, and he conquered, for he had with him by night lanterns with candles, and the Midianites cut themselves down in the panic caused by the archangel. Likewise, in the days of the pious King Hezekiah, Sennacherib, king of Assyria, with his warriors surrounded the city of Jerusalem and reviled the God of Israel; and Hezekiah prayed to God, and the Lord God sent Archangel Michael, and in one night he killed in the Assyrian camp 185 thousand Assyrian warriors. And to your royal nobility and host, and to your piety, the same holy Archistrategos Michael is a true defender and champion against impious nations. Also now, O pious Tsar, we, your pilgrims, with God’s help have hope in the all-merciful Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ and in His Mother, the most pure Theotokos, intercessor for all the world, and on the prayers of the great Archistrategos Michael and all the saints we rely and pray together with tears for his help to you, pious Tsar, in your present host and to all your Christ-loving army against your adversaries, the impious peoples of the Ishmaelite race, that he may soon subdue all your visible and invisible enemies, and that the Lord God may fulfill your royal desire and the wish of your heart.
