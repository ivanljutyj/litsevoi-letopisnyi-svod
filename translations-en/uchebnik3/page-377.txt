On the Cloud, Thunder, and Lightning

That same summer, on May 24, Saturday, at the thirteenth hour of the day, a cloud came from the west with great thunder and lightning, and lightning shattered the onion dome beneath the cross at the church of the holy righteous passion-bearers Boris and Gleb, and pierced the church dome and the floor; and on that same Arbat lightning killed a woman and a girl.

On the Arrival of the Kazan Envoys to the Grand Prince to Petition Concerning the Marriage of the Kazan Tsar and Other Affairs of the Land

That same spring, on July 6, the Kazan envoys came to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich in Moscow—Appay-ulan, Prince Kadysh, Otuch’s brother, Kutlubulat Prince of Gorodets, and Evtek-bakshi—and they petitioned the Grand Prince on behalf of Tsar Yanalei and Bulat, and Prince Tabai and the ulans, and the princes, and the karachis, and all the people of the Kazan land, asking that the great sovereign show them favor, release the tsar, and give him leave to take as wife the daughter of Yufsa-murza of the Nogai, and also concerning other great affairs of the land.

And the Grand Prince showed favor to Tsar Yanalei, his brother and son, gave him leave and permitted him to take the daughter of Yufsa-murza of the Nogai as his wife, so that the Kazan land might be at peace; and he also set the affairs of the land in order, and sent away the Kazan envoys, Appay-ulan and his companions, in August on the [0] day.

On the Former Kazan Tsar Saip-Girey, How He Came to the Throne in the Crimea, and on Islam Tsarevich, How He Became Kalga in the Crimea

In that same year, in July, the Grand Prince’s Cossack Yangandyr Kozhukhov came from the Crimea and told the Grand Prince: Saip-Girey, who had been tsar in Kazan, had become tsar in the Crimea, and Islam Tsarevich had become kalga. And together with him there came to the Grand Prince from Tsar Saip-Girey his man Prince Saltanyar and companions, and from Islam his man Boyan with letters.

And the Grand Prince sent the men of Tsar Islam back to their sovereigns. And together with them the Grand Prince sent to the tsar his boyar’s son Vasily Sergeev, son of Levashov, to congratulate Tsar Saip-Girey on his accession to the throne.

On the Mission to Wallachia

And together with Vasily the Grand Prince also sent through the Crimea to Peter, voivode of Wallachia, Feodor Leontiev and Yushka, a man of the voivode; and the Grand Prince sent them off on July 19.
