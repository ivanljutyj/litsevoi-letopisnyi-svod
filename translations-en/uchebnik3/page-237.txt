And Prince Vasily Fyodorovich Shuysky and other commanders of the grand prince set out in pursuit of the Master and chased them for 50 versts, but did not catch up; they only captured his baggage train.

And Prince Ivan Bulgak and Prince Yaroslav from Vyshgorod of Veliad secretly took two thousand rubles for themselves and withdrew from the city with the army, without taking Vyshgorod; and from there they returned with a great number of captives and booty, and all came to the grand prince safe and sound.

On the Veliad Bells

Then the Pskovians brought eight Veliad bells to the grand prince.

On the Capture of the Novgorod Boyars

In the same year the grand prince captured the Novgorod boyars: Vasily Kazimir and his brother Yakov Korob, and Mikhail Berdenev, and Luka Fedorov.

In the year 6990 from Adam (1490 from Christ)

On the eighth day of September, two churches were founded at the Archangel Michael in the side-chapel: the Resurrection of Christ and the Holy Apostle Aquila.

On the Birth of Dmitry, Son of the Grand Prince

In the same year, on the sixth day of October, on the memorial day of the holy Apostle Thomas, a son was born to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich and was named Dmitry.

On Prince Fyodor Belsky

In the same year Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Belsky fled from Lithuania, from King Casimir, to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, but did not manage to take his wife with him; and the grand prince showed him favor and gave him the town of Demon as a patrimony and Moreva with many volosts.

In the same year Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich was in Vladimir.

On the Hungarian Envoy

In the same year an envoy named Ivan arrived to the grand prince from the Hungarian King Matthias with a proposal of love and brotherhood; and the grand prince, having shown honor to the envoy, sent him back with love to his sovereign, and together with him the grand prince sent his own envoy Fyodor Vasilyevich Kuritsyn; and he set out on the journey, taking with him the treaty of brotherhood and love between the grand prince and King Matthias.
