And the commanders advanced upon the city with all their men. To the Ruzskie Gates they ordered the streltsy heads Timofei and Andrei to advance with the streltsy, while to the Kolyvanskie Gates advanced commander Ivan Andreevich Buturlin, and with him heads with boyar scions; and the Germans fought fiercely against them.

And the streltsy heads seized those gates from them and climbed into the town, and through those gates entered Aleksei and Daniil, and through the Kolyvanskie Gates Ivan Buturlin, and they beat many Germans.

And all gathered in Vyshgorod, and the commanders assaulted Vyshgorod until evening from all sides and from the cannon from Ivangorod and Rugodiv; from their own cannon they fired upon Vyshgorod.

And the Germans sent to petition that the commanders show them mercy and release the princelet together with the people who had come; and the commanders released the princelet and the Germans, and took Vyshgorod and Rugodiv by God’s mercy and by the petition to God of our sovereign the tsar and grand prince and by his justice, with all their armament, cannon great and small, 230 in all, and with arquebuses and with German goods; and all the common people petitioned and gave oath to the sovereign that they would be in servitude to the tsar and grand prince and to his children forever.

Miracle from the Icon of St Nicholas

And the city caught fire, they say, in this way: a German was brewing beer and split the icon of Nicholas the Wonderworker, and with it fanned the fire, and flame spread and burned everyone’s houses.

And when the commanders seized the gates and entered the city, they saw in the fierce flame the icon of the Most Pure Theotokos lying face toward the fire, and they took the image unharmed; and in another place they found the icon of Nicholas the Wonderworker. And from the time the icons were found, the fire began to abate, and the beaten Germans fled to Vyshgorod. And Rugodiv was taken on the Renewal of Constantinople and on the commemoration of the holy martyr Mokei, on Wednesday of the fifth week after Pascha.

And the tsar and grand prince, giving praise to God on this account, came to the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure (Theotokos) and of the Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers, and served a moleben for such an ineffable gift and miracle of Christ our God and of His Most Pure Mother and the great wonderworkers.
