Report from Kazan from Prince Pyotr Shuysky

In the same year, in April, Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky wrote from Kazan that he had ordered the Arsk and coastal Tatars to build the city of Laishev on the Kama, and Prince Pyotr established newly baptized people and streltsy in it, with boyar sons at their head for protection against the coming of the Nogai people, and he ordered the newly baptized to plow arable land there, and at the city of Kazan and in empty villages he ordered all Russian people and newly baptized people to plow arable land.

Report from Sviyaga from Ivan Petrovich

In the same month Ivan Petrovich wrote from Sviyaga that the meadow people had come against the mountain people, with Akhmetek the bogatyr at their head together with his companions, and Ivan sent boyar sons against them, with Prince Iosif Kovrov at their head together with his companions, and the mountain people, the streltsy, and his own people; and they routed the meadow people utterly and took Akhmetek the bogatyr alive.

Report from Kazan, Sviyaga, and Cheboksary

And they wrote from Kazan, Sviyaga, and Cheboksary that the meadow people had come to the Arsk places and to the mountain side in many places, and by God’s mercy they were beaten in all places; and every day they send from Kazan, Sviyaga, and Cheboksary to wage war on the meadow side, and everywhere, God granting, they fight and return safe all winter and spring.

Report from Ivan Petrovich from Sviyaga

In the same year, in the month of May, Ivan Petrovich wrote from Sviyaga that the meadow people had sent a petition concerning their offenses, asking the sovereign to show them favor, forgive their guilt, and establish them in subjection as the mountain people, and to order yasak collected as former tsars had collected it. And the tsar and sovereign sent to Kazan and Sviyaga his stryapchy Semyon Stepanov Yartsov, and ordered the meadow people to be shown favor, their guilt forgiven, and to be brought to oath.
