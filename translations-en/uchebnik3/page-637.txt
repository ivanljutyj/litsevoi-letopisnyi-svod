On the Ordering of Regiments for the German Land

And the sovereign sent out: in the great regiment, Tsar Shigalei, boyar and voivode Mikhail Vasilyevich Glinsky and Daniil Romanovich, and the Circassian princes, Sibok with his brothers; and in the advanced regiment, Tsarevich Takhtamysh, boyar and voivode Ivan Vasilyevich Sheremetev the Elder and Aleksei Daniilovich Basmanov, and the Circassian princes, Prince Ivan Maashik with his brothers; and in the advanced regiment also, Daniil Adashev, and with him the Kazan people from Kazan, Sviyaga, and Cheboksary, and the Cheremis, and the newly baptized; and in the regiment of the right hand, Tsarevich Kaibula, voivode Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany and okolnichy Ivan Vasilyevich Sheremetev the Younger; and in the right hand also, Prince Yuri Repnin, and with him the Gorodets people, the seid and princes and murzas; and in the left hand the voivodes were boyar Prince Pyotr Semenovich Serebryany and Mikhail Petrov, son of Golovin; and in the guard regiment the voivodes were Prince Andrei Mikhailovich Kurbsky and Pyotr, son of Petrov; and all the Novgorod and Pskov people with all the voivodes, and many from the Moscow towns. And the tsar and voivodes were sent out from Moscow, and the sovereign ordered the men to assemble in Novgorod.

On the Arrival to the Sovereign of Ambassadors from the Livonian Master

In the month of December there came to the tsar and grand prince ambassadors from the Livonian Master: Klaus, Thomas, and Melchior, and from the Bishop of Yuryev: Ellert and Christopher, Vlas Beka, to petition and they brought the tribute with them.

And the tsar and grand prince ordered his okolnichy Aleksei Fedorovich Adashev and his clerk Ivan Mikhailov to hear the embassy and draw up an agreement concerning the tribute. And the ambassadors petitioned in the name of the Master and the Bishop and all the land, that the sovereign abolish the per capita tribute of a grivna per head and order taxes to be taken as the sovereign might grant. And the tsar and sovereign granted them favor and ordered agreement to be made on how to take it and what to take for past debts. And they acknowledged their guilt and agreed to pay for past debts and for the present levy 45,000 and 18,000 rubles, and henceforth to pay tribute from Yuryev of 1,000 Hungarian gold pieces year after year continually, and they would conclude an agreement on other matters, but they did not say everything; they began to ask for a delay, so that the sovereign would recall his army; they wanted to get the army withdrawn and to lie again thereafter.

And the sovereign dismissed them from Moscow without result, and ordered his army to march into their land because of their falsehood and violation of the cross-kissing oath.
