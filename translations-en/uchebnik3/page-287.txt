On the Arrival of Envoys from the German Land

In that same summer, in the month of August, Yuri Staroy and Tretyak Dolmatov came to Moscow from the Danish king, and together with them came the Danish king’s envoy named David.

In that same summer, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, the old Church of Archangel Michael in Moscow was dismantled—the one that Saint Alexis the Wonderworker, Metropolitan, had founded and built in the year 6873 from Adam (1373 from Christ)—and a new church was founded on the same site under Archimandrite Theognost.

On the Town of Mstislavl. The Grand Prince Sent [Forces] to Conquer Lithuanian Lands, and a Battle Took Place near the Town of Mstislavl. In the Year 7010 from Adam (1510 from Christ)

The Grand Prince sent his voivodes with Prince Semyon Ivanovich Mozhaisky and Prince Vasily Ivanovich Shemyakin, his boyar Prince Alexander Vladimirovich Rostovsky and his boyar Semyon Ivanovich Vorontsov, and Grigory Fedorov, son of Davidovich, with many men to conquer the Lithuanian lands.

And the voivodes came to the town of Mstislavl on November 4, a Thursday, and Prince Mikhail Izheslavsky, son-in-law of Prince Yuri Lugvenevich, came out of the town to meet them, together with the voivode of the Grand Prince of Lithuania Alexander, Ostafy Dashkovich, with the Grand Prince’s court, guard, and soldiers.

And the regiments joined battle, and by God’s mercy the Moscow regiments of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich prevailed, and they cut down many Lithuanians, about 7,000 soldiers, and took many others captive, and captured their banners, while Prince Mikhail barely escaped into the town. And the Grand Prince’s princes and voivodes, after standing by the town, ravaged the surrounding area and returned to Moscow with a great number of captives.

In that same autumn Bishop Abraham of Kolomna died.

In that same autumn, in September, an envoy came from the Sultan of Kaffa, Ashikhzoda, son of Baazit-Saltan, named Alakoz, proposing peace and friendship.

In that same autumn, in October, the Grand Prince sent Fyodor Mikhailov, son of Kiselev, to Tsar Mengli-Girey in Crimea.
