Everyone who confesses God without falsehood: what He wished to say, that He also handed down to the disciples.

The council at Carthage also commands thus: nothing else is brought into the holy place except what the Lord handed down, that is, bread and wine mixed with water. Chrysostom, who wrote a commentary on the Gospel of John, says that when you approach the altar and partake of the Blood of Christ from the cup, you should think that your lips touch the side of Christ, and you drink from His side; and from the side of Christ not only blood but also water flows, as proof that two natures are united in Him. Concerning unleavened bread, it is said by the law of Moses, by the holy evangelists, and also by the divine apostle Paul and by Gregory the Theologian: “The old has passed away, all things have become new.”

If unleavened bread is still brought into the sacred and chief mystery of our salvation, then listen to Chrysostom, who speaks thus concerning the Old and New Testaments: “There is the letter, here is Christ; there the ark, here the Virgin; there the rod of Aaron, here the cross; there the lamb, here Christ; there the unleavened breads, here the bread.” What testifies more clearly than this that the apostles received and handed down to us not unleavened bread but bread?

From Other Books Concerning Peter the Stammerer

After many Roman hierarchs there was Peter the Stammerer, pope of the great Roman see.

This lawless Peter the Stammerer rejected the Christian faith, stirred up all Italy, and planted many evil heresies in all the Roman lands: he commanded every priest to have seven wives, and did not regard the keeping of concubines as a sin, saying that every priest may have seven wives, and as many concubines as he wishes, let him have that many; and he permitted organs to be played in church, and ordered every priest and deacon, and every man and woman, to shave the beard and the loins; and to eat large and small snakes; and to bathe in the same bathhouse with women; and to eat all unclean food, and to eat with dogs from the same vessels; and he ordered the meat of strangled animals to be eaten; and instead of incense he ordered censing with moss; and to eat kites and wolves. And many other abominations did this lawless Peter the Stammerer, the Roman pope, sow together with his like-minded Romans; and therefore they were rejected from the church and delivered to anathema.
