Grand Prince Ivan and the princes Yuri, Andrei, and Boris; and Andrei the Younger, still a child, was carried in arms. And all crossed themselves.

The Grand Prince, shedding many tears, as did the metropolitan and the others, took from the reliquary the wonder-working icon of the Most Holy Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary and handed it to Bishop Misail of Smolensk, who had requested it; and besides that, many other icons, smaller than it, adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls, from the same captivity, though not requested by the bishop—all these the Grand Prince gave to them.

Of these the Most Reverend Metropolitan Jonah took one icon, the image of the Most Holy Lady with the Child, and said: “O bishop, son and brother of our humility! This image of the Most Holy Mother of God shall be for a blessing and for a remembrance of the day when you, having come, found the priceless treasure that for many years had been hidden from you, and left it to my lord and son, the Grand Prince, to his princess and their children as a remembrance of this day.” And he joyfully did everything according to his words.

And the metropolitan summoned the bishop; they both took it in their hands and blessed the Grand Prince and his princess and their noble children with it, and gave it into the hands of the Grand Prince. The Grand Prince received the image of the Most Holy Mother of God, kissed it with tears, and ordered them to go out of the church with the image of the Most Holy Mother of God that he was sending to Smolensk. And they went, carrying the wondrous icon of the Most Holy Mother of God; after it followed the metropolitan with the sacred council, carrying also the icon that was left as a blessing to the Grand Prince. After them walked the Grand Prince with his children, with the princes and the boyars. In Moscow at that time there was a great multitude of troops, and they all followed, and all the people of the glorious city of Moscow.

The Grand Prince escorted it to the Church of the Annunciation, called “on Dorogomilovo,” two versts from the city.

And from there he returned to the icon that remained with him, and as he went he also had prayer services celebrated. And when he came to the Church of the Annunciation, he ordered it placed in the place where it had stood before, when he had sent that icon away.

And he ordered another icon painted for that place, having its measure taken and its image copied; and before the one that was left, he ordered the priests to sing a prayer service every day: the Akathist with its ikoi. This was on the 18th day of January, a Sunday, on the commemoration of our holy fathers Athanasius and Cyril.

On the Campaign of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich with an Army against Veliky Novgorod

That same winter, on the 19th day of January, a Monday, Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, because the Novgorodians had violated the treaty, set out with an army against Novgorod.
