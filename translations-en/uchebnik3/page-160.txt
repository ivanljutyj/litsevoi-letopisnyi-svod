Sign of the Star

In the same month of December, after the Nativity of Christ, a great star appeared in the sky, and from it came a very long, thick, bright ray, brighter than the star itself; and it rose at the sixth hour of the night from the summer sunrise and moved toward the summer west, and the ray stretched forward from it, and the end of that ray was spread out like the tail of a great bird.

Concerning the Star

In the month of January, after the Baptism, another tailed star appeared above the summer west; its tail was thin and not very long, and darker than the ray of the first star. And to whatever place the first star came three hours before sunrise, at that same place this other one appeared three hours after sunset and departed westward.

Concerning Great Perm

That same winter the Grand Prince sent Prince Fyodor Pyostry against Great Perm to wage war on them for their violation of the treaty. And he took Great Perm.

The Grand Prince Sent Fryazin to Rome for Tsarevna Sophia

That same winter the Grand Prince, after taking counsel with his father Metropolitan Philip, with his mother Grand Princess Maria, with his brothers, and with his boyars, sent Ivan Fryazin to Rome for Tsarevna Sophia on January 16 with letters and an embassy to the Pope and also to Cardinal Bessarion.

The former Pope Paul had died, and it was said that there was now another pope named Callistus.

When they entered that land, they learned that the pope’s name was Sixtus, not Callistus; and after considering this among themselves, they rewrote it: erasing the name of that Callistus, they wrote Sixtus.
