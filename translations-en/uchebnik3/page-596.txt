And on the next day, in the morning of Sunday, in the Church of the Dormition of the Mother of God there were inexhaustible miracles from the image of the great wonderworker Nicholas, and from Peter the Wonderworker, Metropolitan of All Rus, and from Jonah; and every day there were healings from the image of the great wonderworker Nicholas.

Metropolitan Macarius himself restored the icon of Nicholas the Wonderworker, for he had been trained in icon painting, and with him Andrew, Archpriest of the Annunciation, with great desire and faith, fasting and prayer. From his image the working of miracles most abundantly overflows to those who ask with faith, and many icons were painted from it of the same size and likeness, and from all of them miracles occurred for many of the faithful.

On the Founding of the Stone Church of the Intercession of the Most Holy Mother of God

In that same month the faithful and Christ-loving tsar and great sovereign ordered the founding of a stone church of the Intercession of the Most Holy Mother of God with nine domes, which had previously been of wood, in memory of the taking of Kazan, at the Frolov Gate above the moat, with a chapel to that same church of the Life-giving Trinity above the moat, to Nicholas the Wonderworker of Vyatka; and before this, two years earlier, one church of the Trinity had been founded, and the sovereign ordered an additional church of the Intercession of the Most Pure Mother of God and chapels to be added to that same church.

On the Building of the Wooden Church of Nicholas of Vyatka

In that same month the faithful tsar and great sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus ordered the building of a wooden church of the holy wonderworker Nicholas of Vyatka, and with great faith and much desire he commanded that an icon like the one from his icon be painted.

And the newly painted image of the holy wonderworker Nicholas was placed in the newly built church at the Intercession of the Holy Mother of God above the moat. And Metropolitan Macarius consecrated it with the whole council of the Russian metropolitanate on the twenty-ninth day of July, a Monday; and at that consecration were the faithful tsar Ivan Vasilyevich and his brother, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and a multitude of boyars and people. The merciful God shows His mercy to people through the wonderworking image of Saint Nicholas, His servant. There were many healings that day, and afterward great miracles; and from the newly painted image of the wonderworker sight was granted to the blind, walking to the lame, recovery to the paralyzed, and inexhaustible healing of every ailment to those who came with faith.
