On the Sending of Envoys to Lithuania

That same autumn, in November, the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich sent to Lithuania as envoys Mikhail Yuryev, son of Zakharyin, and the clerk Vasily Tretyak Dolmatov.

On Prince Semyon Ivanovich, and How He Wished to Flee to Lithuania

That same winter, in January, Prince Semyon Ivanovich conceived the idea of fleeing to Lithuania from his brother, the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich. And the Grand Prince, having learned of this, sent to him and ordered him to come before him, and intended to impose his disgrace upon him.

Prince Semyon Ivanovich began to petition the sovereign Grand Prince concerning his guilt, and Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus pardoned him, at the intercession of Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, and of his brothers and the hierarchs: he forgave him his guilt, and replaced all his people, boyars, and boyar sons.

On the Arrival of Envoys from Lithuania

That same winter, in March, the Grand Prince's envoys Mikhail Yuryev, son of Zakharyin, and the clerk Vasily Tretyak Dolmatov came from Lithuania.

On the Arrival of Lithuanian Envoys

In the same month of March, on the 30th day, Lithuanian envoys from King Sigismund came to the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich: Lord Stanislav Glebovich, and Voytko Klochko, and the scribe Bugush Bogovitinov; and the Grand Prince dismissed the Lithuanian envoys on April 4.

On the Death of Metropolitan Simon

In the same month of April, on the 26th day, a Sunday, Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, fell ill, and at the wish of the Grand Prince he sent for Serapion, former Archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov, blessed him, received absolution from him, and sent him back to the Trinity-Sergius Monastery.

Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, reposed on April 30, on the night from Tuesday to Wednesday, in the first hour of the night; and his body was placed in the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God in Moscow.

On the Arrival in Moscow of the Crimean Tsaritsa from Kazan

That same spring, on June 22, Tsaritsa Nur-Saltan came from Kazan to Moscow.

On the Installation of Varlaam as Metropolitan to the Metropolitan See

In that same summer, on the 27th day of the month of July, a Sunday, Varlaam, archimandrite, was brought to the metropolitan court and named Metropolitan of all Rus.
