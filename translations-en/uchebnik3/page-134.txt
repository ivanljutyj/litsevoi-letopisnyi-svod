In the same year the stone Church of the Ascension in Moscow was renovated by Maria, wife of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich. That church had been founded by Evdokia, wife of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, 62 years earlier. Under her a little of it was built, and in the same year she reposed and was laid in that same church. After many years Grand Princess Sophia began to complete that church and built it up to the ring on which the top was to rest, but the top was not raised. After many fires the stone around it burned and the vaults shifted. Grand Princess Maria wished to dismantle it and build a new one, but inside it was all sound. Vasily Dmitriev Ermolin with master masons devised this: they did not dismantle the whole church, but broke away all the burnt stone, took down the shifted vaults, and finished it all with new stone and fired brick, and raised the vaults, and completed it all so that everyone marveled at this unusual work.

On Kazan.

In the year 6976 from Adam (1476 from Christ).

And from the Exaltation Tsarevich Kasim marched against Kazan, and with him were the Grand Prince’s commanders, Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Obolensky Strita with a great host, and others. They came to the Volga where they intended to cross, and there the Kazan Tsar Ibrahim met them with all his princes and his host and did not let them cross to his side. The Tsarevich had been summoned to the throne by the Kazan princes Abdul-Mamon and others with deceit: he, believing them, knew nothing of their cunning, and asked the Grand Prince for a host, hoping to obtain what had been promised him. Achieving nothing, he returned. Their journey was hard, because the autumn was cold and rainy, and provisions began to run short. Therefore many Christians ate meat on fast days, and their horses died of hunger, and many threw away their armor, but they all came away safe, each to his own home.

And after their departure the Kazan Tatars at once went secretly to Galich. They took few captives and did no harm to the towns and districts, for all were under siege in the town.

And the Grand Prince sent garrisons to the towns: to Murom and Nizhny Novgorod, to Kostroma, and to Galich, and ordered them to remain under siege and guard against Kazan.

In the same autumn the Church of the Ascension was consecrated by Metropolitan Philip on the third of November.

On the Cheremis.

In the same autumn Grand Prince Ivan sent Prince Semyon Romanovich against the Cheremis, and with him many boyar sons of his court.
