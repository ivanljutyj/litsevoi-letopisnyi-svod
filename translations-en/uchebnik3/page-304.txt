On the Consecration of the Bishop of Krutitsy

That same winter, on January 23, Dositheus was consecrated Bishop of Krutitsy by the Most Reverend Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus.

Death of the Bishop of Suzdal

That same winter, on March 8, Nifont, Bishop of Suzdal, reposed in Moscow, and he was laid to rest in Suzdal.

On Glinsky

That same winter Prince Mikhail Lvovich Glinsky departed from King Sigismund and sent to petition the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus for service.

And the Grand Prince showed him favor, took him into his service with his patrimony, together with his brothers and associates; and the Grand Prince sent to him his clerk Guba Moklokov and brought him and his brothers to kiss the cross on the condition that he serve faithfully and wish good in all things to the sovereign Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus.

And the Grand Prince sent to him his voivode Prince Alexander Alenka, brother of Alabyshev, and with him boyar scions of Murom.

On the Death of the Bishop of Tver

That same spring Vassian, Bishop of Tver, reposed on May 23.

On the Moscow Moat

That same spring Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich ordered Aleviz Fryazin to build a moat of stone and brick around the city of Moscow and to repair the ponds around the city.

On Nizhny Novgorod

That same spring the Grand Prince ordered the foundation of the stone city of Nizhny Novgorod, and the master was Pyotr Fryanchushko Fryazin.

On the Grand Prince’s Move to the New Court

That same spring, on May 7, the second Sunday after Easter, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus with his Grand Princess Solomonia moved to live in the new brick court, which his father the Grand Prince had founded.
