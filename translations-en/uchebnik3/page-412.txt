In the same month a wooden town was built in Meshchera on the Moksha River.

That same winter, on January 9, the Kazan Tatars came to Nizhny Novgorod and to Balakhna, burned the posad, and killed many fugitive people on the Volga.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus sent from Murom Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky and his other commanders with men; and the Kazan Tatars went away.

In the same month the Grand Prince ordered an earthen town to be built in Rzhev district on the Lithuanian border, named it Zavolochye, and ordered the Rzhev households to be transferred there.

In the same month, by the command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, a small town was built in Kostroma district, Bui-gorodok on the Karega River.

That same winter, on February 20, on Meat-Fare Sunday, Savva Slepushkin, an elder of the Joseph Monastery, was consecrated by Metropolitan Daniel as bishop in the city of Smolensk.

In the same month, on February 27, the Lithuanian commanders Andrei Nemirov with many men and with great artillery, with cannons and handguns, came to the town of Sebezh and fiercely assaulted it; and by God’s mercy they did nothing to the town, but with their own cannons they killed their own people.

And at that time the Grand Prince’s men came out from the town against them, killed many Lithuanian men, and took their banners and drums; and they withdrew to their own lands with great shame.

And the Grand Prince and his mother, the Grand Princess, ordered in honor of this to build in that town a church of the Life-Giving Trinity, and in it three side chapels: the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, the Protection of the Mother of God, and Sergius the Wonderworker.

That same winter the town of Temnikov was moved to another place, on the Moksha River as well.

On the Miracle of the Most Pure Mother of God

That same spring, on the seventh day of April, on Friday of the sixth week of Holy Lent, at the fifth hour of the day, in the church of the honorable and glorious Entry of the Most Pure Queen, Virgin Mary, Mother of God, beyond the marketplace, in the stone church built by Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, God had mercy, and His Most Pure Mother granted healing from her image to a paralyzed woman who had no use of her arms and legs, and she immediately became well, in the third year of the reign of the great sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich and his mother, Grand Princess Elena.
