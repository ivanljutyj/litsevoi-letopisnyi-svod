Many Germans fell in battle beneath the city, and others were wounded; at that time Prince Vasily Yuryevich of the Suzdal princes was in the city of Yam.

And the Germans went away to their own lands with great wealth.

The Novgorodians then wished to march with an army led by Prince Ivan Vladimirovich, grandson of Olgerd, beyond the Narva River to fight the Germans.

But at that time many horses began to die among the Novgorodians in the cities and districts, and people began to fall grievously ill; then the Novgorodians ceased making war and did not go beyond the Narva River.

At the same time the Pskovians sent their envoys to Novgorod to confirm the peace treaty and love; and they learned that in Novgorod there was a plague among horses and a terrible sickness among the people, and that the Novgorodians had returned from the war against the Germans; and then the Pskov envoys returned from Novgorod without confirmation of the peace treaty.

In that same year the Karelians went with an army against the Murmans, defeated them and conquered them, and returned home.

In the year 6953 from Adam (1453 from Christ)

In the month of September Tatars came to the Ryazan borderlands. In that same autumn the Tatars conquered the Mordva.

In that same winter Tsar Ulu-Makhmet came with an army to Murom from Old Nizhny Novgorod, and Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich went against him, and the tsar fled back to Old Novgorod. Then Alexander Konstantinovich, son of Ivan, was shot, and the warriors froze their hands in battle.

And an army of 7,000 Lithuanians came to Kaluga, and Prince Mikhailo Andreevich fought them with four hundred warriors; and then Prince Andrei Lugvitsa of Suzdal was killed, while Sudok and Koninsky were led away to Lithuania.

From Another Chronicle Concerning the Same

In that same winter Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich secretly sent two tsareviches with an army against the Lithuanian cities of Vyazma, Bryansk and other cities. Setting out, they fought much, killed and put people to death, and burned; they came almost to Smolensk and took many captives. They did much evil to the Lithuanian land and returned with great wealth. Having learned of this, the Grand Prince of Lithuania Casimir Jagiellonovich sent his lords with an army against Mozhaisk and other cities.
