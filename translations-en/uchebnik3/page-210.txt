On December 30, Tuesday, Prince Vasily Shuysky came from Novgorod to serve the Grand Prince; and the Grand Prince received him, honored him, and gave him gifts.

On January 1 the Archbishop, with the posadniks and the well-to-do townsmen, came to the Grand Prince and declared to him the volosts: Velikie Luki and Rzheva Pustaya; but he did not take them.

On the fourth Sunday the Archbishop, with the same aforementioned people, came to the Grand Prince and declared ten volosts: four of the Archbishop’s, and three of the Yuryev Monastery, and at Demon the Blagoveshchenskaya volost, and the Antonovskaya volost, and the Tubas volost; and those in Torzhok—lands of the Archbishop, of monasteries, of boyars, and of all Novgorodians, whosesoever the lands might be—they renounced all of them to the great princes, to their patrimony, Torzhok. But the Grand Prince did not take those ten volosts.

And they petitioned that the sovereign himself decide how he should favor his patrimony and how many volosts he should take, for his patrimony relies on God and on him.

And the Grand Prince ordered his boyars to tell them: “I am to take half of all the volosts—the Archbishop’s and the monasteries’—and the Torzhok volosts, whosesoever they may be.” And they answered: “We shall tell that, lord, to Novgorod.”

On January 6, the Epiphany, Tuesday, the Archbishop, with the posadniks and the well-to-do townsmen, came to the Grand Prince with a petition and with half of the Archbishop’s volosts; and all the Torzhok volosts—the Archbishop’s, the monasteries’, and the boyars’, whosesoever they were—they renounced all for the Grand Prince; and they petitioned concerning the monasteries, that he would show favor and take from six monasteries half of their volosts and lands: from Yuryev, from the Blagoveshchensky Bogoroditsky, from the Arkadyevsky Bogoroditsky Monastery, from the Antoniev, from St. Nicholas in the Nerevsky End, and from St. Nicholas on Skovorodka; but the other monasteries the sovereign should favor and not take their lands, because they are poor and have little land.

And the Grand Prince ordered the Archbishop and the posadniks to go into the city and write into a register half of the Archbishop’s volosts and also the monasteries’; and they were not to conceal anything, and whatever they concealed would become the lands of the great princes.

On January 7 the Archbishop, with the posadniks and the well-to-do townsmen, came to the Grand Prince and brought the lists for half of the Archbishop’s volosts, and also for the monasteries’.

And the Grand Prince showed favor: he did not take half of the Archbishop’s volosts, but took ten volosts: Ladoga Porog and the Porog land on both sides
