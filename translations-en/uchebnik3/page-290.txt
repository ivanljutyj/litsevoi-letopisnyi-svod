RUS (1510–1536 from the Incarnation of Christ).
Book 18

On the Taking of the Horde by the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girey

In the same year the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girey defeated Shiakhmat, tsar of the Great Horde, and seized the Horde.

In the same year the Grand Prince's ambassador David Likhorev came from the Horde.

In that same summer, on the 14th day of June, a Thursday, the Grand Prince sent his son Prince Dmitry with an army to the city of Smolensk to conquer Lithuanian lands.

On the Battle with the Germans at Smolin

In the same year the Livonian Germans approached Pskov, while the Grand Prince's voivodes were then in Novgorod with their men.

And the voivodes went from Novgorod to Pskov: in the main regiment, Prince Daniil Vasilyevich; and in the main regiment, another voivode, Prince Ivan, son of Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky; and in the main regiment, a third voivode, Prince Boris Tebet-Ulanov; in the advanced regiment, the Novgorod viceroy Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky; and in the advanced regiment, Prince Peter Loban; in the right hand, Prince Iosif Dorogobuzhsky and Peter Zhitov; in the left hand, Prince Vladimir Andreevich Mikulinsky; in the guard regiment, Semyon Karpovich, Mikhailo Andreevich Kolychev, and Prince Semyon Romodanovsky.

And the Germans heard that the Grand Prince's voivodes were coming to Pskov, and they went away from Pskov.

And the Grand Prince's voivodes overtook them at Smolin; and Daniil Nashchekin and Lev Kharlamov told the Grand Prince's voivodes that the Germans were fleeing.

And many of the Grand Prince's men from the regiments galloped off, and the order of the regiments was broken.
