The people, having taken the oath, disperse to the villages. “And send your light baggage train with food, and with it about 100 Cossacks; they will be useful at the tsar’s court for any task.”

And the boyars sent off the baggage train, and with it sent 70 Cossacks, and 72 arquebuses were sent with the Cossacks and the boyars’ men.

On the Treason of the Kazanians

In the same month the boyars Prince Semyon Ivanovich and Ivan Vasilyevich Sheremetev, and Prince Pyotr Serebryany with their comrades, rode to the city of Kazan; ahead of them went Ivan Sheremetev, and in the guard regiment was Prince Fyodor Romodanovsky, and in the guard regiment also were all those Kazanians whom the tsar had brought out.

And when they came to the Volga at Irokhov Island, there they met the boyars from Kazan: Prince Shamsa and Khankilday, and many other Kazanians. All greeted them with affection and petitioned the boyars to go into the city, saying that they were all the sovereign’s slaves and subject to his will.

And the boyars met the tsaritsa also on the Volga and sent her to the city of Sviyazhsk to Tsar Shigalei.

And boyars’ sons ride to Kazan and from Kazan to the voivodes, and say that all the people rejoice at the sovereign’s favor, while Ivan Cheremisinov is still bringing others to the oath.

And when the voivodes came to Bezhbolda, Prince Islam and Prince Kebyak rode ahead of the voivodes into the city, and Alekei Murza, brother of Chura Narykov; but the boyars did not keep watch over them, because all their princes had betrayed the sovereign.

And having entered the city, they shut it, and told all the people that they knew for certain everyone was to be killed, and that they had heard this from the Gorodets Tatars and from Tsar Shigalei himself. But there had been no order from the sovereign to the boyars to do any harm, nor had the boyars themselves even thought of it; and the boyars had sworn that nothing bad would come from them, and that all would be favored and supported in everything, as in the city of Sviyazhsk. But the enemy of the Christian race, who from the beginning hated good, rejoices in human blood and stirred up all the Kazanians. And the boyars came to the city, and Ivan Chermisinov and Prince Kulalei met them on the Bulak and said: “We have seen nothing bad so far, but now, when the princes came running from you, they began to speak evil words, and the people became troubled; all the princes rode out of the city to you with us, only Chapkun remained in the city, and the others are putting on armor.”
