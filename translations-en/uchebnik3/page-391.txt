And his boyar Mikhail Yuryevich began to speak to him, comforting the sovereign: "Sovereign Grand Prince, it would be needful to prepare vodka and pour it into the wound, and squeeze it out; but, sovereign, seeing you so exhausted, perhaps, sovereign, one should pour vodka for six days, or at least two, so that, sovereign, there may be at least a little relief of your illness; perhaps then the vodka may be let in too."

The Grand Prince called Nikolai and began to say to him: "Brother Nikolai! You came from your land to me and saw my great favor toward you; can you do anything so that my illness may be relieved?"
