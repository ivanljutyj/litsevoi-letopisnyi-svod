On the Novgorod Priests

In the same year the Novgorod priests were beaten with the knout, because the lord Gennady sent them from Novgorod the Great, since they, being drunk, had mocked the icons; and the prince sent them back to the lord.

On the Resettlement of the Novgorodians

In the same winter the grand prince sent men, and more than 7,000 Novgorod men were brought from Novgorod to Moscow, because they had wanted to kill the Novgorod viceroy Yakov Zakharyich; and Yakov uncovered and hanged many other conspirators.

On the Rostov Archbishops, How One Left the Archbishopric and Another Was Appointed. In the Year 6997 from Adam (1497 from Christ)

The Rostov lord Ioasaph, formerly Prince Obolensky, left the archbishopric, and Tikhon was appointed archbishop in Rostov.

On the Roman Envoy and the Sending of an Envoy to Rome

In the same year the envoy Nicholas came from the Roman emperor Frederick with an offer of love and friendship, and the grand prince, having received that envoy with honors, sent him back to his sovereign with love; and together with him the grand prince sent his envoy Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot to Emperor Frederick with love.

On the Resettlement of the Novgorod Boyars

In the same winter the grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich brought many boyars and well-to-do people and merchants from Novgorod the Great, more than a thousand souls in all, and he favored them, giving them estates in Moscow and in Vladimir, and in Murom, and in Nizhny Novgorod, and in Pereyaslavl, and in Yuryev, and in Rostov, and in Kostroma, and in other cities.

And to Novgorod the Great, to their estates, he sent many Moscow merchants and boyar sons, and from other cities of the Moscow patrimony many boyar sons and merchants, and he granted them lands in Novgorod the Great.

On the Death of the Kolomna Lord

In the same year, on May 16, the Kolomna lord Gerasim died in the Paphnutiev Hermitage.
