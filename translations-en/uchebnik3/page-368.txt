And Tsarevich Islam took an oath before Prince Mikhail Ivanovich to the Grand Prince that he would be steadfast to the great sovereign and to his son, Prince Ivan Vasilievich, and would do no harm to Grand Prince Vasily and his son, Prince Ivan, or to their borderlands.

On the arrival of the Lithuanian envoys, and the truce

That same winter, on the seventeenth day of March, Lithuanian envoys from King Sigismund came to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus in Moscow: Ivan Bogdanov Sapiega, Matfei Voitekhov Klyuchnikov, and Pavel Nurshev, a scribe; and they concluded a truce with the Grand Prince for one year, from the Nativity of Christ in the year 7041 from Adam (1541 from Christ) to the Nativity of Christ in the year 7042 from Adam (1542 from Christ); and the Grand Prince released the Lithuanian envoys to their sovereign.

On the arrival of the Grand Prince’s envoy from Crimea with a charter

And in the same month, on the thirtieth day, the Grand Prince’s envoy Stefan Ivanov, son of Zlobin, came from Crimea and brought to the Grand Prince from Tsar Saidet-Girey a sworn charter such as the Grand Prince had desired. And he told the Grand Prince that Tsar Saidet-Girey had agreed with the Grand Prince on brotherhood and great friendship, and on the sworn charter the tsar and his kalga, Tsarevich Devlet-Girey, and other tsareviches, ulans, and princes had sworn to the Grand Prince before the Grand Prince’s envoy Stefan. And the tsar and the tsarevich, the ulans and princes, pledged to be united with the Grand Prince against the Grand Prince’s enemies.

On the arrival of the Crimean envoys

That same spring, in April, envoys from Tsar Saidet-Girey came to the Grand Prince in Moscow from Crimea—Avelshikh with his companions—and brought to the Grand Prince a charter like the one the envoy of the great sovereign, Stefan, had brought.

On the sending of an envoy to Wallachia

That same spring, on the third day of May, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of All Rus, sent the under-secretary Ivashka Elizarov, son of Sergeev, to Wallachia through Crimea to the Wallachian voivode Peter.
