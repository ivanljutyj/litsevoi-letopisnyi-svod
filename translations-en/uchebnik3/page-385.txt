At that time the Grand Princess did not wish to leave him.

The Grand Prince took leave of her and gave her the last kiss; and at once he ordered her to be led away to his royal children.

And the great sovereign Vasily lay down, ailing, at death’s door. Piteous indeed was it then to see those standing before him; all was filled with tears and weeping at that time; and the autocrat himself felt utterly spent.

Then the Grand Prince sent for Vladyka Vassian and for Elder Misail Sukin, and ordered him to bring the monastic garb according to his former vow.

The great sovereign asked whether the abbot of the Kirillov Monastery was in Moscow, because he had long desired to be tonsured at the Kirillov Monastery; and they told him that the Kirillov abbot was not then in Moscow.

And he sent for Joasaph, abbot of the Trinity-Sergius Monastery. Abbot Joasaph soon came to him.

And he ordered him to enter the chamber with the Holy Gifts, and ordered him to stand opposite himself; and he ordered his steward Fedor Kuchetsky to stand beside the archpriest, because Fedets had once seen the death of his father, the Grand Prince.

And then he ordered his cross-clerk Daniel to sing the canon to the Great Martyr Catherine and the canon for the departure of the soul, and he ordered the parting prayer to be said for him. And as he began to say the canon, he dozed a little and seemed to fall asleep, and at once awoke as if from sleep; and as though seeing a certain vision, he spoke certain words of good hope.

The Grand Prince sent for the icons of the Most Pure Mother of God and of St Nicholas; and soon they brought the icons of the Most Pure and of Nicholas the Wonderworker.

And he summoned his Tver palace steward Ivan Yuryevich Shigona, and sent him to his spiritual father, Archpriest Alexius, and ordered him to bring him the reserved Gifts from the church; and he ordered him to ask whether he was accustomed to that matter, when the soul is parted from the body. The archpriest replied that he had had little occasion to see it.

Elder Misail Sukin came to the great sovereign Vasily and brought him the monastic garb.
