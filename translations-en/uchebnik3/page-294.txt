The church of the holy great archangel Michael, because of the dilapidation of the building erected by the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich in the year 6841 from Adam (1341 from Christ), was dismantled; on the same site they laid the foundation of a new church of the holy archangel Michael and then removed the relics of the great appanage princes.

On Another Church

At that time another church was also dismantled, the church of John of the Ladder under the bells, built by Grand Prince Ivan Danilovich in the year 6836 from Adam (1336 from Christ); and they laid the foundation of a new church of St John in the old place.

On the Sending from Magamet-Amin

In the same spring the Kazan tsar Magamet-Amin sent to the Grand Prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilievich a letter concerning certain matters of the town prince Shyaisup; and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, according to his firm word, sent to him in Kazan concerning those matters his envoy Mikhail Klyapik, so that he should not yield to all those words.

On the Envoy Klyapik and the Destruction of People in Kazan

And in the same year, on the 24th day of June, on the Nativity of St John the Forerunner, the godless and evil-believing Kazan tsar Magamet-Amin, while in friendship and under oath with the Grand Prince, forgot his word, violated the oath charter, seized the Grand Prince's envoy Mikhail Klyapik in Kazan, and seized the Grand Prince's merchants, and cut some down, while others, after robbing them, he sent to the Nogai.

In the same year Ivan Oshcherin came from Crimea, and with him came Alabata-ulan and Tetlain-Iberdei Duvan.

On the Marriage of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus. In the Year 7014 from Adam (1514 from Christ)

On the 4th day of the month of September, on Thursday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus married his son Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, and took for him the daughter of Yuri Konstantinovich Saburov, named Solomonia; and the Most Reverend Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, crowned Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus and Grand Princess Solomonia in the cathedral great church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Mother of God in the renowned and glorious city of Moscow.

On the Arrival of Tsar Magamet-Amin with an Army

In the same month of September the impious Kazan tsar Magamet-Amin came with an army against Nizhny Novgorod and stood under the city for two days
