In the same year, in May, Tsarevich Kaibula, son of Tsar Akhkubek, came from Astrakhan to the sovereign; and the tsar sovereign granted him favor, gave him the town of Yuryev with its tribute, and permitted him to marry, taking for him the niece of Tsar Shigalei, the daughter of Tsar Yanalei.

In the same year the town of Shatsk was established in Meshchera, and the voivode Prince Dmitry Semyonovich Shastunov was appointed to guard it, while Boris Sukin built it.

The pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat of Great Russia, began to take counsel concerning help for Orthodox Christianity against the godless and evil traitors, the Kazan Tatars.

In that same spring, in the month of April, the pious tsar places his hope in God, who created heaven and earth and all that is on it, and who knows the secret of all men, and in His most pure Mother of God, and in the great wonderworkers, and speaks thus: “O God, who created heaven and earth and all that was created by You, and You, Lover of mankind, who knows the hidden things of men, You know that I have thought of nothing else, but only desire peace for Christians! The enemies of Your cross, the evil Kazan people, are occupied in nothing else but devouring the flesh of Your humble servants, reviling Your holy name, which they cannot know, and defiling Your holy churches. Take vengeance on them, O Master! As the prophet says: ‘Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, but unto Your name give glory.’ Guide us, O Lord, on the path of salvation and grant me the opportunity to suffer for Your holy name and for the Christianity entrusted to me.” And the sovereign summons to himself his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich and Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, and his boyars and voivodes, and takes counsel with them about his campaign against Kazan. At the council many different things were said, that the sovereign should not himself take part in the campaign but should send others, since at that time the sovereign had many enemies: the Crimean khan and the Nogais. The sovereign said only one thing: “In no way can I see Christianity perishing, which was entrusted to me by my Christ; and how shall I be called — I and the people whom the Lord has given me — while others are our enemies?
