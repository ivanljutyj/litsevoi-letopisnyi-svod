With the help and assistance of the great Archangel Michael and the other incorporeal powers, and through the prayers of the holy and great John the Forerunner and of the chief apostles Peter and Paul, and of the beloved apostle John the Theologian, and of the seventy holy apostles, and through the prayers of the great wonderworker Nicholas, and through the prayers of the great three hierarchs and wonderworkers Basil the Great, Gregory the Theologian, and John Chrysostom, and of Peter and Alexis, and Jonah and Leontius the wonderworkers, and with the assistance of the holy passion-bearers of Christ and our helpers George and Demetrius, and Andrew, and Theodore Stratelates, and of your pious kinsmen the great and Equal-to-the-Apostles Grand Prince Vladimir and the holy passion-bearers Boris and Gleb, and through the prayers of our venerable and God-bearing fathers the wonderworkers Sergius and Barlaam, and Cyril, and Paphnutius, and of the holy Grand Prince Alexander Nevsky, and through the prayers of all your pious kinsmen and all other saints, that the Lord God may fence you in and strengthen you by the invisible power of the precious and life-giving cross, and send down from above His great mercy upon you, that you may be saved. But now only we beseech you, pious Tsar Ivan, and your brother Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and all your nobles, princes and boyars, and commanders, and all your Christ-loving army, that you remain in purity and humble wisdom, and in chastity, and repentance, and the other virtues. Especially we pray and bless that with you, pious Tsar, all your nobles and all your Christ-loving army may remain in love and obedience, in fear, and in peace, and in unity against enemies, in all things according to God’s will. If the heart of the tsar is in God’s hand, then all ought according to God’s will and the tsar’s command to walk and obey with fear and trembling, as the divine Apostle Peter said: “Fear God, honor the tsar”; and again the same apostle said: “Not without reason does the tsar bear the sword, but for vengeance on evildoers, for the praise of virtues.” And again we pray and bless your God-lovingness, from now and henceforth let all guard themselves from pride and drunkenness and from downfall of soul and body; let no one think the sin of pride and drunkenness small, but great: according to the divine apostle, every drunkard and fornicator, and the proud, has no place in the kingdom of heaven; and again the same apostle said: “Drunkards and fornicators, and extortioners will not inherit the kingdom of God”; and again the same divine apostle said: “Do not be deceived, neither fornicators, nor adulterers, nor sodomites, nor bestialists, nor those who defile themselves by hand, nor drunkards, nor extortioners will inherit the kingdom of God.” For many righteous men, strong and brave, and kings fell through pride and drunkenness: as righteous Noah became drunk, uncovered himself, and was mocked, so too righteous Lot became drunk and fell a cruel fall with his two daughters.
