And suddenly many Germans met them on the river Seritsa, and the Germans drowned them; the Grand Prince's commanders had not managed to arm themselves.

And in that same battle they killed the commander Ivan Borisovich Borozdin; they killed him with a cannon; and Prince Daniil withdrew and stationed himself in Pskov.

And after this the Grand Prince sent Prince Daniil Vasilyevich Shchenya and Prince Aleksandr Vasilyevich Obolensky, and other commanders with many men, to conquer the German Livonian lands for their wrongdoing, because they had been conquering his patrimony Pskov and had seized his merchants in Yuryev.

The Grand Prince's commanders, the two Princes Daniil, set out and began to conquer the German land, to take captives, to burn, to cut down, and to carry off into captivity, and at the third hour of the night on 24 November a fierce battle began.

And the Germans suddenly approached with a great force, with cannons and arquebuses, and by God's mercy the Grand Prince's commanders overcame them: some they killed, and others they took captive, and few of them escaped by flight; and then in the battle they killed Prince Aleksandr Vasilyevich Obolensky.

And the commanders marched near Kolyvan, and came out to Ivangorod, and ravaged the German land.

In that same winter, in December, Nogai envoys came to Moscow: from Musa-murza, Prince Yagal, and from his brother Yamgurchei-murza, Prince Uyadam, with an offer of peace and friendship.

In that same winter, in December, an envoy, Prince Khazseger, came to the Grand Prince from the Tsar of the Great Horde, Shiakhmat, son of Akhmut, with an offer of friendship and love.

On the Capture of Tsar Abdyl-Letif

In that same winter, in January, the Grand Prince sent Prince Vasily Nozdrovaty and dyak Ivan Teleshov to Kazan and ordered the seizure of the Kazan Tsar Abdyl-Letif for his falsehood; and they did so: they seized the tsar and brought him to Moscow.

And the Grand Prince sent him into confinement at Beloozero, and to Kazan the Grand Prince granted the throne to the former Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin, son of Abreim, and gave him as his tsaritsa his sister-in-law, the wife of the former Kazan Tsar Alegam.

And together with the tsar the Grand Prince sent to Kazan Prince Semyon Borisovich Suzdalsky and Prince Vasily Nozdrovaty.

In that same winter, in March, on the Thursday of the fifth week of Lent, the Grand Prince sent back the envoy of the Tsar of the Great Horde, Shiakhmat, and with him he sent to Tsar Shiakhmat his own envoy, the equerry David Likhorev, with an offer of friendship.
