And when Ismail defeated the prince, he gathered Syuyundyuk with his companions and sent him to the tsar and grand prince, and sent word with him that to the end of his life he would be inseparable from the tsar and grand prince.

By the command of the tsar and sovereign, all the Russian archbishops assembled

In the same year, by the command of the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, and by the blessing of Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, all the Russian archbishops, archimandrites, and abbots assembled (for a council) concerning the various church orders and many matters for the strengthening of the Christian faith.

And the tsar and grand prince, and Metropolitan Macarius with all the archbishops and bishops, and with the whole sacred council of Rus, according to the sacred canons elected for the Kazan kingdom, for the strengthening of the faith, and decreed that there should be an archbishop; and at Sviyaga there should be an archimandrite and abbots; and in Kazan under the bishop, an archimandrite and abbots likewise; and the archbishop should have under his authority the city of Kazan with its surrounding uluses, the city of Sviyaga with the Mountain Side, Vasil-gorod, and all the Vyatka land. And the pious tsar and grand prince Ioann decreed: to the bishop and all churches, the tithe promised to God from all revenues of the Kazan land; and initially the metropolitan and all bishops and monasteries will help the Kazan bishop with money and grain.

On the Installation of Archbishop Guriy in Kazan

On the third day of February, Archbishop Guriy was installed for the Kazan kingdom and the city of Sviyazhsk, who had formerly been abbot of Selizharov Monastery; and he was chosen by lot.
