The great Prince Andrei Yuryevich Bogolyubsky, grandson of Monomakh, with a single dome. Having many times before seen that exceedingly great and lofty church, wondrous in craftsmanship, the most reverend Metropolitan Philip burned in spirit and was possessed by desire, and wished to see such a church of the Most Holy Mother of God built in Moscow, where was the healing-bearing tomb of our father among the saints, the most reverend Metropolitan Peter the Wonderworker, and of the other Russian metropolitans.

Before that, he summons stonecutters and sends them to the city of Vladimir to inspect that church and take its measurements.

They, having gone there, saw the Church of the Most Pure, marveled at the beauty of its building, its size, and its height; and having measured its width and height and that of the altar, they returned to Moscow.

And they measured the space around the church in Moscow and set to work.

But that former church had become dilapidated, and its vaults, which had been supported by wood, had shifted. They began to dig trenches in the month of April; after digging the trenches, they first packed the base with wood, then filled those trenches with stones over it.

When this was finished, the smaller porch and the altar of the first church were dismantled.

And in that same month, on the 30th day, on the memory of the holy Apostle James, brother of John the Theologian, at the second hour of the day, the most reverend Metropolitan Philip with all the consecrated assembly, having put on sacred vestments and taken the precious Cross and holy icons, went to the founding of the church and ordered all the bells to be rung.

On the Finding of the Relics

There came also the right-believing and Christ-loving Great Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, and his son Grand Prince Ivan, and his mother, and his brothers, and the boyars and nobles, and all the multitude of the people of the glorious city of Moscow. And so they celebrated a prayer service, and before all the metropolitan with his own hands lays the foundation where the altar is to be, and also at the sides and corners, and after that the masters begin the construction of the building. This church was founded after the founding of the former church, which the most reverend Metropolitan Peter founded under Prince Daniil Ivanovich 146 years less three months ago.

And when it had been built to the height of a man, then they began to dismantle the former church entirely to its foundation; and when they had carried out all the stones from it and the floors down to the tombs of the most reverend Russian metropolitans Cyprian, Photius, and Jonah, they dug around their shrines.
