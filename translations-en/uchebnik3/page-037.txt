We ask of you an exposition of the faith, beloved fathers, the good bequest of our fathers who assembled in the Reigning City. Imagine if someone entrusted a deposit to you and you did not return it: how would you deal with him? The handing down and exposition of the fathers’ faith which you have received admits neither additions nor alterations; it was concluded and sealed by the holy fathers, and those who dare to introduce changes are driven off, while those who do even more are punished with execution. Although the change just mentioned seems insignificant to you, a brief word about it will do no harm. Moreover, such a union will bring much benefit, since it will unite all Christians; but will this change be significant? That is the very question. We sin greatly in striving for this; something necessary for it will be added, and something will be taken away because of it. You will even find brothers scattered in the faith and honoring love. We beseech you, brothers and most honorable lords, as we have asked you before, for the sake of the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ, who loved us wicked, sinful, and despairing, and laid down His soul for us! Let us return to the good concord that formerly existed between us and the holy fathers, when all held to concord and there were no schisms among us. Let us receive one another as brothers, let us not put to shame the memory of our common fathers, let us honor their ordinances, fear their prohibitions, and keep the traditions, so that with one mind, in concord and unanimity, we may glorify the all-honorable and magnificent name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. And now it would be good for you, O Latins, to know the truth of God and not speak falsehood. Do not lie against the holy fathers, do not cite false writings, do not divide the Holy Spirit, hold to the teaching concerning unleavened bread, believe in the holy seven councils, fulfill the covenants of the holy fathers and do not reproach them or reject them; do not convene or name an eighth council, but diligently examine the teachings of the most blessed seven popes, be guided by the holy books, carry out the instructions of the ecumenical teacher John Chrysostom, act according to the rules of the holy apostles and God-bearing fathers of all the holy seven councils, fulfill the true writings of the saints, and do not utter what is flattering and false. And so, according to the divine law of the holy canons and the ordinance of holy church singing, let us rightly offer petitions and prayers to the Almighty and most merciful God, and glorify the memory of the holy apostles and holy fathers: Basil the Great, Gregory the Theologian, and John Chrysostom, and all the holy fathers of the holy seven councils, and the most holy ecumenical popes from Sylvester of Rome to Adrian; and may all these saints pray to God for us and bring us to true understanding. And the Lord God will open the mind of wisdom and show to all His true divine word. And may the Lord soften the spiritual fields of your hearts, so that you may be able to hear and understand the truth and walk the right path; and may our thoughts not stumble either. And to you, honorable Pope Eugene, it is fitting to speak of this.
