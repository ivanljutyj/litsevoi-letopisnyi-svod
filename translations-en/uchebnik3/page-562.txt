In assembly we pray to God and give praise to His great grace! And what can be said here, except that we say to Him: wondrous is God in glory, working miracles, who has shown His glory upon you, pious tsar, who granted you bright victories over your enemies—over Devlet-Girey, the impious Crimean tsar—and has now, thanks to you, our sovereign, preserved His Christ-named flock from the invasion of the foreign Hagarene. You, our sovereign, by God’s grace and by your great labors, together with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and all your Christ-loving army, manfully defending yourselves, with God’s help struggled for piety. You, tsar, rose up in royal might against your adversaries, the impious, impious tsars and oath-breakers, the Kazan Tatars, who always innocently shed Christian blood, defile and destroy God’s holy churches, and carry Orthodox Christians away captive and scatter them over the face of the whole earth. And you, pious tsar, strong in battles, placed unwavering hope and faith in Almighty God and showed great exploits and labors, and strove to multiply the talent given to you and to free the plundered flock of your pasture from captivity. And the Master saw your steadfast faith and purity, your unfeigned love and prudent discernment, courage, manliness, and chastity, and that you did not hesitate to suffer unto blood—and more than that I say, you gave your soul and body for our holy, pure, and most honorable Christian faith and for the holy churches, for the flock of Orthodox Christians entrusted to you, for their shed blood and for those carried away captive and tormented by all kinds of calamities from them and defiled by manifold passions. According to your faith and great ineffable labors, God granted you His mercy: He gave the city and kingdom of Kazan into your hands; and His grace shone upon you, as upon former pious tsars who did the will of the Lord. He granted the pious and equal-to-the-apostles Tsar Constantine to gain victory over enemies by the Cross; and to other pious tsars; to your forefather, Grand Prince Vladimir, who enlightened the Russian land with holy baptism, He granted victory over many foreigners; to the praiseworthy Grand Prince Dmitry on the Don, to defeat the barbarians; and to Saint Alexander Nevsky, to defeat the Latins. And upon you, pious tsar, God’s grace came from above; He granted you the reigning city of Kazan with all its surroundings, and by His grace and the power of the Cross He crushed the serpent nesting there and hiding in its holes and evilly devouring us. And through you, pious tsar, He uprooted this impiety and planted grace: you erected the life-giving Cross, raised up holy churches, and by your royal hand delivered many captive Christians from bondage.
