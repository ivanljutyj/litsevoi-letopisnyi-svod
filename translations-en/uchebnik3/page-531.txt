And the sovereign granted Kamai-Murza and his companions his great favor.

On the voivodes’ deliberation as to how to take position by the city

And the sovereign summoned to himself Prince Vladimir Andreevich and Tsar Shigalei, the boyars and voivodes, and told them of Kamai’s arrival and of what Kamai had said to the sovereign. And the sovereign took counsel as to how to advance on the city.

And they resolved: the sovereign himself and Prince Vladimir Andreevich should take position on Tsarev Meadow near Otuchyova Mosque, and Tsar Shigalei beyond the Bulak by the cemetery. And the tsar was to march in the great regiment, and on the Arsk field the great regiment and the advance regiment were to take position, and Prince Yuri, boyar and voivode of Prince Vladimir Andreevich; and the right-hand regiment beyond Kazan across the river, with many Cossacks with them; and the guard regiment at the mouth of the Bulak, and the left-hand regiment above it.

And he ordered the court voivodes, and commanded word to be sent to all regiments, that throughout the whole army they should prepare siege towers for every ten men, and that every man should prepare a log for the palisade, and, God willing, to surround the city and fortify it with siege towers and palisade.

And the sovereign commands all boyars and voivodes, heads and boyars’ sons in all regiments, that without his royal command and, in the regiments, without the voivode’s command, no one should ride out to skirmish against the city until the time comes; and the sovereign forbids this with a great and terrible word, that they not do daring deeds until fortifications are established around the city.

On the twenty-third day of August the sovereign set out for the city of Kazan

The sovereign set out from Teren-Uzek, having drawn up the regiments, for the city, and commanded Prince Yuri Shemyakin and Prince Fedor Troekurov to go, and with them before the regiments went streltsy and foot Cossacks; and also ahead of all regiments the streltsy heads, and with them their sotniks, each with his hundred, and the atamans with the sotniks and Cossacks, divided according to rank; and he also commanded the advance regiment and the great regiment, Tsar Shigalei and the voivodes, to go to the Arsk field up the Bulak, by Kaban Lake.

And he commanded the right-hand regiment to take position below the city beyond the Kazan River, and commanded the left-hand regiment and the guard regiment to take position opposite the city beyond the Bulak.

And the sovereign himself went behind the great regiment, and behind himself he commanded the artillery and the guard regiment and the left-hand regiment to go.
