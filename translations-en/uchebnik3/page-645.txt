On the dispatch from Ivangorod by Alexei Basmanov

In the same month they wrote from Ivangorod — boyar Alexei Daniilovich Basmanov with his comrades, with Andryushka Zacheslomsky: the voivodes’ sentries stood beyond Rugodiv on the Kolyvan road and lay in wait for many people; along the Kolyvan road many people, horse and foot, were going to Rugodiv with cannon; and Alexei with his comrades sent the voivodes beyond the river: Afanasy Mikhailov, son of Buturlin, and Pauk Zabolotsky, and Ivan Sharapov, son of Zamytsky, and the streltsy heads Timofei and Andrei, so that they would not let the sentries be trampled down and would withdraw the sentries to their own side beyond the river.

And the voivodes, having withdrawn the sentries beyond the river, were crossing to the Ivangorod side, and they began to cross five versts below Rugodiv; but the Germans had sent all the equipment on to Rugodiv, and they themselves, horse and foot, came to the crossing against Afanasy and his comrades. Altogether there remained on their side, of those who had not managed to cross, about 100 men, while about a thousand Germans, horse and foot, came against them.

And God showed His mercy: they beat many Germans and drove them five versts to Rugodiv itself, and took 33 men from them.

And the captives told the voivodes that the people of Rugodiv had betrayed the tsar and grand prince: they had sent Yakim and Zakhar to the tsar and grand prince, and had at once sent to the Master that he should not give them up; and the Master sent a Kolyvan princeling and another of Velya, and with them 1,000 horse and 700 foot with arquebuses, and men with cannon; and the people of Rugodiv swore among themselves and kissed the cross that they would not surrender to the tsar and grand prince.

On the capture of Rugodiv

In the same month the voivodes sent from Rugodiv — boyar Alexei Daniilovich and Daniil Fedorovich with their comrades — Andrei Ivanovich Saburov and Prince Ivan Petrovich Okhlyabinin, and the streltsy head Andrei Kashkarov with a report to proclaim the greatness of God, how the Merciful God had shown His mercy to our sovereign over Rugodiv and Vyshgorod.

And Andrei with his comrades said in the name of the voivodes to the sovereign: in May, on the 11th day, Wednesday, fire broke out in Rugodiv and began to burn in many places; and the voivodes sent to them that they should stand by the word on which they had petitioned the sovereign, humble themselves before the sovereign, and let them into the city; but the Germans refused them and would not do that for which Yakim and Zakhar had kissed the cross on their behalf.
