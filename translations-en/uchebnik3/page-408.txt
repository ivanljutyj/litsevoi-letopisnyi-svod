He was laid in the Cathedral of Michael the Archangel, on the left side, at the head of his brother, Prince Yuri Ivanovich.

On Vladimir

In the same month of April, on the 13th day, a Thursday of the seventh week of Holy Lent, at the ninth hour of the day, the town of Vladimir caught fire between the Nativity Monastery and the Andreev Gates, in an empty place; and the wall from the Klyazma River burned, and 170 wall sections and four towers were destroyed, and half the roof of the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God burned, and the whole roof of the church of St Demetrius burned, but the icons, church vessels, and books were all carried out intact by God's mercy; and half of the Nativity Monastery burned, and of the town there remained, by God's mercy and the protection of His Most Pure Mother, 265 wall sections and seven towers.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus' and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered that place in the town of Vladimir to be rebuilt with timber, and the remaining walls, wherever they were, to be repaired; and in the same month, on the 19th day, Istoma Kurtsov was sent to build the town. And in the same year, in August, the town of Vladimir was completed, in the third year of his reign.

On Velizh

In the same month of April, on the 19th day, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered a town to be established in Toropets district, at the Velizh hillfort, the town of Velizh; and it was completed in the month of July, in the third year of his reign.

And by God's mercy, the great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus' and his mother, the great sovereign lady Elena, sent from Velizh their commanders to wage war on the Lithuanian land: Prince Ivan Ivanovich Barbashin and their other commanders.

In the year 7046 from Adam (1546 from Christ)

On April 3, a Wednesday of the fifth week of Holy Lent, at the second hour of the day, the blessed Grand Princess Elena reposed, daughter of Prince Vasily Lvovich Glinsky, wife of the blessed Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich.

And she was laid in the Church of the Ascension of our Lord Jesus Christ, beside Grand Princess Sophia, wife of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich.

On the enmity among the boyars of the Grand Prince and on the murder of the clerk Fyodor Mishurin

That same autumn, through the devil's machinations, there was enmity among the Grand Prince's boyars: Prince Vasily and Prince Ivan Vasilievich began to feud.
