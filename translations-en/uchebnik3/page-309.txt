Pan Voytekh Yanovich, and the scribe Pan Bogush Bogovitinov, came to negotiate about the lords who had been taken in battle at Vedrosha, so that they might be released.

And the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich showed them favor and released them.

On the establishment of the town at Tula

In the same year, by the command of the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich, they built a wooden town at Tula.

And after that, in the fifth year, they built a stone town.

On the installation of a bishop for Suzdal

In the same year, on the 21st day of August, a Tuesday, Simeon, archimandrite of Andronikov, was installed as bishop of Suzdal by His Eminence Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus'.

On the installation of a bishop in Tver

On the 24th day of the same month of August, Nil, abbot of the Epiphany, was installed as bishop in Tver by the same Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus'.

On the journey of the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich to Veliky Novgorod and on the taking of Pskov
In the 7018th year from Adam (the 1518th year from Christ)

On the 23rd day of September, a Sunday, the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich set out from Moscow for his patrimony, Veliky Novgorod; with him were his younger brother Prince Andrey Ivanovich, and Tsarevich Peter, and Vladyka Mitrofan of Kolomna, and Varlaam, Archimandrite of Simonov.

On the taking of Pskov

And that same winter many posadniks of Pskov, merchants, and men from all Pskov came to Novgorod to the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich to petition the sovereign against his viceroy, Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Obolensky Repnya.

And the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich, having investigated that the Pskovians were petitioning unjustly against his viceroy and had not treated him honorably, laid his disgrace upon them for this and ordered all the Pskovians in Novgorod to be seized. And to Pskov he sent Tretyak Dolmatov and ordered him to tell the Pskovians that the sovereign wished to be himself in his patrimony and to venerate the Life-Giving Trinity, and would himself govern and favor his patrimony, and that the veche bell was to be taken down. The Pskovians did everything according to the sovereign's will.

And the Grand Prince of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich set out from Veliky Novgorod for his patrimony, Pskov, on the 20th day of January, a Sunday.
