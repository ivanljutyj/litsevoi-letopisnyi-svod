In the same month a messenger named Erin came to the tsar and grand prince from the Roman emperor and supreme king Ferdinand with a letter; and he wrote about the Livonian Germans, asking that the tsar and grand prince not wage war against them, calling them his subjects of the Roman Empire.

And the tsar and grand prince sent the emperor's messenger back to him with honor, and sent letters with him to the Emperor, telling of his realm, which God had given him: that if the Emperor wished to be in love and brotherhood with the tsar and grand prince, as the former emperors Maximilian and Charles and other emperors were with the former sovereigns, the Russian tsars, with the grandfather and father of our sovereign, then he should send his great ambassadors from among his counsellors; and then they would conclude an agreement on all matters.

Report from the voivodes of Yuryev

In the same month the boyar Prince Andrei Ivanovich Kaftyrev sent Prince Pyotr Kropotkin from Yuryev, and wrote to the sovereign that he had sent Vasily Vasilyevich Rozladin and other heads with him with men to the small town of Tarvast; and in Tarvast at that time was the old master Velim. And Vasily with his companions came to the suburb of Tarvast; the Germans came out to meet them, and Vasily and his companions routed the Germans and burned the suburb of Tarvast. He stood by the suburb for a day, but campaigned for three days; and he came with all his men, God granting, safely with a great many captives.

Report from the voivodes of Yuryev

In the same month Prince Andrei wrote from Yuryev that he had sent Prince Gleb Obolensky and his companions to ravage the environs of Velyan. And Prince Gleb campaigned ten versts from Velyan, and when he started to withdraw, the Germans caught up with him and fought with him; and there they killed Ivan Vasilyev, son of Rozladin, and eight boyar sons, and many Germans were killed as well, and they withdrew safely with all the captives.

Report from the voivodes from the German land

In the same month the boyars and voivodes Prince Ivan Fedorovich Mstislavsky and Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky with their companions sent to the sovereign from the German land Prince Vasily Barbashchin and Prince Dmitry Shevyrev. They wrote to the sovereign that, by the sovereign's instruction, they had dispatched the boyars and voivodes. In the central regiment: the boyar Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany and the okolnichy Nikita Romanovich Yuryev; in the advanced regiment: the boyar Nikita Vasilyevich Sheremetev; in the guard regiment: the boyar Ivan Menshoy Vasilyevich Sheremetev; and with them many men as well for the war in the German land.

And they themselves came with all the men and with cannons to the German town of Alist. And Prince Vasily with his companions came to them at Alist safely.
