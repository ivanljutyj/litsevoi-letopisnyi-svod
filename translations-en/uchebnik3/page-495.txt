“…Kazan can be [so], and while I live Kazan will be faithful to the tsar and grand prince, but after me—who can know what will be?” And Prince Dmitry, according to the sovereign’s instruction, said to the tsar that the Mountain Side should not be given to Kazan but to him, the sovereign; God Himself had given it to him: “And if the Mountain Side is given back, how can the city of Sviyazhsk stand? You yourself know how much dishonor and loss our sovereigns have suffered from Kazan: even now they keep Christian captives in bondage, and having sworn an oath to our sovereign, they lie; and many Gorodets people who came with you, as you know, keep Christian captives in bondage; and if the Kazanians are the same, how can our sovereign endure seeing Christians in captivity? And if, sire, the boyars of the grand prince, Prince Yuri Golitsyn and his companions, installed you as tsar of Kazan, and all the land swore that they would release all the captives, and if they do not release even one person, then the sovereign must stand up for his Christians as much as the Merciful God helps him; and if, sire, our sovereign does not stand up for them, then he, sire, will have to answer to God for that.” And the tsar said: “If he does not give me the Mountain Side, how am I to live in Kazan? Or should I flee to the sovereign grand prince?” And Prince Dmitry and Alexey said to the tsar: “If you are to flee to the sovereign, then strengthen his city with Russian people.” And there was much disputing about this, and the tsar said: “I am a Muslim, and I do not wish to stand against my faith, but neither do I wish to betray my sovereign, the tsar and grand prince; I have nowhere else to go, so I will go to the tsar and grand prince; if treachery from the Kazanians awaits me, I will destroy the wicked yet and come to the sovereign.”

And he sent Prince Dmitry and Alexey to the sovereign, and with the tsar Prince Dmitry left Ivan Cheremisinov with his streltsy to guard the tsar against the Kazanians and not to leave the sovereign without news.

And when Prince Dmitry came to the Sviyaga, the princes who lived on the Sviyaga, Chapkun and Burnash and their companions, said to Prince Dmitry: “They hear from the land that when spring comes, all the Kazanians want to betray the sovereign and do not love Shigalei; let the sovereign consider his own affair as is best for him, but we have given the sovereign our truth and speak truthfully, that the Kazanians will certainly betray him.”

And Prince Dmitry came to the tsar and grand prince and reported to the sovereign everything from Tsar Shigalei.

And the tsar and grand prince sent Semyon Yartsov to Tsar Shigalei and ordered him to say to the tsar that he should live taking heed of the Kazanians.
