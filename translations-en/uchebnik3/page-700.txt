And Ivan, Archbishop of Novgorod, and Leontius and Isaiah and Ignatius, the wonderworkers of Rostov, are depicted in white klobuks. But the metropolitans of the Russian metropolitanate who were on that most high throne after the first-throne metropolitans and wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, as well as the Rostov archbishops and bishops, all wore black klobuks. And we have not found in the writings why the white klobuks were set aside. And our pilgrim Pimen, Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, wears a white klobuk, and the former Novgorod archbishops also wore white klobuks, but there is likewise no writing as to on what occasion the Novgorod archbishops wear white klobuks. And also our pilgrims the Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov and the Archbishop of Kazan and Sviyazhsk seal blessing and dispatch letters with red wax, while the metropolitan seals blessing and dispatch letters with black wax. And the metropolitan is the highest rank of the Russian metropolitanate, head of all archbishops and bishops. But in that high-throne rank there is no precedence over archbishops and bishops: the metropolitan wears a black klobuk, and all the hierarchs wear black klobuks, while the Archbishop of Novgorod wears a white klobuk. And we, the great sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, with all our pilgrims, the most reverend archbishops and bishops, and with the most honorable archimandrites, and with the igumens, and with the whole sacred council, were moved in spirit and decreed and confirmed that from this time and henceforth, whoever God and the Most Pure Mother of God and the Russian metropolitans Peter, Alexis, and Jonah the wonderworkers are pleased to have be on the great degree of the Russian metropolitanate, or whatever other metropolitans may be on that throne, for him we have established the ancient honor: that he wear a white klobuk with vestments and a cherub, as the former first-throne metropolitans Peter the wonderworker and Alexis the wonderworker wore white klobuks, and the Rostov bishops Leontius, Isaiah, and Ignatius the wonderworkers also wore white klobuks. And also the metropolitan is to seal blessing, ordination, and dispatch letters with red wax; and on the seal let there be the Most Pure Mother of God with the Child, and on the other side let there be a blessing hand, and around it let there be an inscription with the metropolitan’s name. And the Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov is to wear a white klobuk and seal with red wax according to the former custom; and the Archbishop of Kazan is also to seal with red wax. And according to this decree the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus with his pilgrims, the archbishops and bishops, archimandrites and igumens, and with the whole sacred council, ordered this charter to be written for confirmation. And to this charter the sovereign ordered his seal to be affixed.
