In the same month of February, on the twenty-fourth day, the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and his son Tsarevich Ivan, with his prayerful clergy, with the archbishops and bishops, and with the whole consecrated council, chose for the house of the Most Pure Theotokos and for the great wonderworkers Peter and Jonah, as metropolitan, the elder Afanasy of Chudov Monastery, who had formerly been protopope of the Annunciation and confessor of the tsar and grand prince, and led him up to the metropolitan’s court.

And on the fifth day of March, the third Sunday of Great Lent, by the command of the pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of all Rus, the elder Afanasy, who had formerly been protopope of the Annunciation, was installed to the metropolitanate by the Russian archbishops and bishops: Pimen, archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov; Nikandr, archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl; Trifon, archbishop of Polotsk; Afanasy, bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa; Filofei, bishop of Ryazan and Murom; Semion, bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk; Varlaam, bishop of Kolomna and Kashira; Matfei, bishop of Sarsk and Polonsk; Ioasaf, bishop of Vologda and Perm; and the whole consecrated council. And Guriy, archbishop of Kazan, had died at that time; and Akakiy, bishop of Tver and Kashin, was at his own place in grave illness and sent a letter about it, that he was of one mind with the archbishops and bishops and the whole consecrated council concerning the metropolitan installation. At his installation were the tsar and grand prince, and his children, Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor, and Prince Vladimir Andreevich.

After the completion of the Divine Liturgy and the removal of the service vestments from the metropolitan, the archbishops and bishops placed upon him a golden collar icon and a mantle with springs, and a white klobuk, and led him up to the hierarchical stone throne.

And the tsar and grand prince, approaching the throne, spoke the word of the tsar and grand prince to the metropolitan: “The almighty and life-giving Holy Trinity, which grants us the autocracy of the Russian kingdom over all Russia, entrusts to you this holy great throne of the great wonderworker Peter, the archbishopric and metropolitanate of all Russia of the Russian kingdom, through the ordination and consecration of the holy fathers, the archbishops and bishops of our autocratic Russian kingdom, and the staff of pastorship. Father, receive and ascend to the throne of seniority in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and of His Most Pure Mother, to the throne of the great wonderworker Peter, and pray to God and His Most Pure Mother and the great wonderworkers Peter, Alexei, and Jonah and all the saints for us and for our tsaritsa, for our children, and for all Orthodoxy.”
