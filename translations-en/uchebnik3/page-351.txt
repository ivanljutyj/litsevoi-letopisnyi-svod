On the Arrival of Mstislavsky

In the same month of July, Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky came from Lithuania to enter the service of the Grand Prince.

On the Arrival of the Lithuanian Ambassadors.
In the year 7035 from Adam (the year 1535 from Christ)

Lithuanian ambassadors came to the Grand Prince from King Sigismund, while the Grand Prince was hunting at Mozhaysk: Pyotr Stanislavich, voivode of Polotsk, and the scribe and marshal Bogush Bogovitinov; and they concluded with the Grand Prince a truce for six years.

On the Dismissal of the Ambassadors

And from Mozhaysk the Grand Prince dismissed the Lithuanian ambassadors, and he also dismissed the Emperor’s ambassador and the Pope’s ambassador from Mozhaysk.

And to King Sigismund the Grand Prince sent his ambassadors: his okolnichy Ivan Vasilyevich Lyatsky and his clerk Elizar Ivanov, son of Tsyplyatev.

And to the Roman Emperor and King and to his brother Ferdinand he sent his ambassadors Ivan Lyapun Osinin and Andrey Volosaty; and to the Pope he sent Eremey Trusov and Sharap Lodygin.

On the Grand Prince’s Journey to Tikhvin

That same winter the Grand Prince was at the Monastery of the Mother of God in Tikhvin on a pilgrimage.

On Glinsky

That same winter, in February, the Grand Prince showed favor to Prince Mikhail Glinsky and released him from confinement. That summer the Grand Prince lived in his village of Vorobyovo until the Dormition of the Holy Mother of God.

On the Sending to Kazan

In the same year the Grand Prince sent his ambassador Andrey Fedorov, son of Pilyemov, to the Tsar in Kazan.

On the Building of Stone Churches

In the same year the Grand Prince built a stone church with side chapels in his courtyard in the name of the Transfiguration of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ; and he built another stone church at the Frolov Gates, of the holy martyr George; and he built a third stone church, of the holy martyrs Boris and Gleb, on the Arbat, beyond the Neglinnaya.
