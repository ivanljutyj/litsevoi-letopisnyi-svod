And on the thirteenth day of the same month, on Thursday, the grand prince sent Vasily Ivanovich Obolensky Striga with many warriors, and with them the princes, Tsarevich Daniyar, with many Tatars, ordering them to go to Volochek and along the Msta. And in that year Prince Mikhailo Olelkovich was in Novgorod.

Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich set out with an army against Great Novgorod

After this the grand prince began to have prayer services celebrated in the churches and to send abundant alms throughout his land: to churches and monasteries, to priests, monks, and the poor.

In the cathedral church of our most holy Lady, the Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary, the grand prince, coming to the wonder-working icon of the Most Pure Mother of God of Vladimir, offered prayer, shedding abundant tears: “O Lord and Master, most holy and eternal King! You know the secrets of men’s hearts and know that not by my own desire, not by my own will do I dare to do this—to shed much Christian blood on the earth—but I dare for the sake of Your true divine law”; and also before the wonder-working image of the Most Pure, which the wonder-worker Peter himself painted. Then he comes to the tomb of our holy father the wonder-worker Peter, Metropolitan, performing a prayer service and shedding tears, asking help and protection from them, and also from the other hierarchs lying in that church: the most reverend metropolitans Theognost, Cyprian, Photius, and Jonah.

Having prayed and gone out from there, he comes to the honorable Chudov Monastery of the Archangel Michael, and, entering the church, performs a prayer service. And so the champion of piety, praying everywhere to the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God, calling to his aid the invincible power of the honorable and life-giving Cross of the Lord, and the great intercessor and swift helper of Christians in battles, the glorious commander of the heavenly powers, the Archangel Michael, and the holy great John the Forerunner, and the assemblies of the holy prophets and apostles, and the hierarchs, and the holy, gloriously victorious martyrs, the venerable and righteous, and all the saints with much compunction. Then again he enters the chapel of the same church of the Annunciation of the most holy Mother of God, where was the healing-bearing tomb in which lay the wonder-working relics of our holy father the wonder-worker Alexis, Metropolitan of Russia; and there also he prayed with many tears.

And then again he comes to the church of the Archangel Michael of his honorable assembly and of the other incorporeal powers, and there also performs a prayer, asking help and intercession from them.
