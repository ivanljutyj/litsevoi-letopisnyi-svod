And when the godless traitor to the sovereign, Hetman Ostrozhsky, returned from Opochka in disgrace, the Great Sovereign Vasily ordered the ambassadors of King Sigismund, Jan Shchit and Bogush, to appear before him for negotiations, and after hearing their speeches he sent them back to their sovereign, King Sigismund, with nothing, on November 15.

On the Release of the Ambassadors to Rome

That same autumn, on November 18, the Great Sovereign Vasily released the ambassador of Emperor Maximilian, Sigismund Herberstein, to his sovereign, and with him the Grand Prince sent to his brother, the elected emperor and supreme king of the Romans, Maximilian, his secretary Vladimir Semyonov, son of Plemennikov.

On the Envoy from Prince Vasily Shemyachich

That same autumn, in November, his servant Prince Vasily Ivanovich Shemyachich sent to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign of all Rus, his man Mikhail Yanov with news that the Crimean Tatars had come to the borderland, to their patrimony, the Putivl lands. And Prince Vasily went after them, and by God's mercy and the good fortune of the Sovereign Grand Prince he overtook them beyond the Sula, and slew many Tatars, and took others alive, and sent prisoners to the Grand Prince.

On the Death of the Kazan Tsar Abdyl-Letif

That same autumn, on November 19, Abdyl-Letif, Tsar of Kazan, departed this life.

On Metropolitan Gregory and the Elders from the Holy Mountain

That same spring, on March 4, on Thursday of the third week of Lent, Vasily Kopyl and Ivan Varavin came from Constantinople to Moscow. And together with them there came to the Great Sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign of all Rus, from Constantinople, from the Ecumenical Patriarch Theoliptus, Metropolitan Gregory the Greek, from the city of Zhikhno in the Constantinople district. And together with him there came to the Great Sovereign and to the chief hierarch Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, elders from the Holy Mountain of Athos to petition the Great Sovereign concerning their poverty and for alms. From the monastery of the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God at Vatopedi, three elders: Maximus the Greek, and Neophyte the hieromonk, a Greek, and Lawrence the Bulgarian.
