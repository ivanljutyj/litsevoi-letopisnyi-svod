On the Birth of Prince Yuri

In the same month of October, on the 30th day, on Wednesday, on the commemoration of the holy martyrs Zinovy and Zinovia, at the seventh hour of the day, a son was born to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich by his Grand Princess Elena Glinskaya, and he was named George; and the Great Sovereign rejoiced and glorified God.

And he was baptized in the Church of the Epiphany at the Trinity Court in the glorious reigning city of Moscow by Asaf Skripitsyn, abbot of the Trinity, and by Elder Daniel of Pereslavl, on November 3, Sunday.

At that joy of the Great Sovereign of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, were his brothers Prince George and Prince Andrew Ivanovich, and his princes and boyars; and there was great joy in the reigning city of Moscow over the joy of the sovereign and the birth of a son to the sovereign.

On the Arrival from Kazan of the Grand Prince's Envoys Who Installed Tsar Yanalei as Tsar in Kazan

That same autumn, on November 7, the Grand Prince's envoys Yakov Grigorievich Poplevin and Afanasy Kuritsyn came to Moscow; the Grand Prince had sent them to Kazan to install Tsar Yanalei as tsar and to bring the tsar and the whole land of Kazan to oath. And the tsar in Kazan, before the Grand Prince's envoys, gave an oath on the oath charter and affixed his seal to the oath record, that he would be unwavering in loyalty to the Great Sovereign and would guard his interests. Also the tsarevna swore, and the princes and the people of the whole land of Kazan gave oath on the oath records, that they would be unwavering from the Grand Prince with the whole land of Kazan until their death and that of their children; and that they would not take any tsar or tsarevich without the knowledge of the Great Sovereign. And to the oath records the princes and people who were literate put their hands, and those who were not literate affixed their seals; and Yakov Poplevin brought the charters and oath records to the Grand Prince.

On the Arrival from Wallachia of the Grand Prince's Envoy Ivashko Elizarov, and on the Sending by Voivode Peter of Wallachia to the Grand Prince of a Petition for Protection

That same autumn, on November 7, the Grand Prince's servant Ivashko Elizarov arrived to the Grand Prince from Wallachia. He had traveled through Crimea and told the Grand Prince that Tsar Saidet-Girey had been expelled from Crimea, and Islam had been taken as tsar in Crimea.
