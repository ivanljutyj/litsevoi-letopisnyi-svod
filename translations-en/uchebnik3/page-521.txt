He went, but being a saint, he prayed to God, and from that same dry bone, the jawbone of an ass, he brought forth water and quenched his thirst; and afterward, having wrought many victories, he became proud, and then drank to excess, and fell, and was blinded, and became a laughingstock and a sport for foreigners. And who was higher than King David in virtues? But he dozed a little, forgot himself, fell through wrath and suffered much, and until his last breath, day and night, he washed his bed with tears. Who was more blessed than Solomon the wise? But in old age, at the end of his life, he became proud, fell, and perished. Who was more beloved of God than Israel? But because of a fall it became a killer of God. As it is written: the people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play, and there fell in one day twenty-three thousand; some the earth swallowed, others fire consumed. And much is described in the divine Scripture, in the New and the Old, how many perished from pride and drunkenness and falling. And if pride, wine, and falling destroyed the righteous and holy, the brave and strong, not only ordinary people but also praised kings and prophets, then especially should we, weak and abiding in sins, withdraw from such things and flee as from a serpent! But those who preserved humble wisdom and purity not only in virginity but also after lawful marriage wrought many victories, and were glorified and saved. Like the pious first king Constantine and the pious kings Theodosius the Great and Theodosius the Younger, and the other Greek kings, who preserved purity in lawful marriage in an Orthodox manner and wrought many victories and were glorified by God, so also in our Russian kingdom your pious and equal-to-the-apostles holy forefather, Grand Prince Vladimir of Kiev, and his sons, and grandsons, and great-grandsons, and all Russian tsars down to your God-crowned reign, preserving purity according to the law of marriage, wrought many victories over the impious and were glorified by God. You yourself, O Tsar, know the bravery of your forefathers: of the God-crowned Tsar Vladimir Monomakh and the brave Grand Prince Alexander Nevsky, and of your other kinsmen, what victories they wrought over the impious and how they were glorified by God. But for you, Great Tsar Ivan, and for your brother Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, and for all your Christ-loving army, now is the favorable time, with God’s help, to care for the truth and to have great concern. If it should happen that you endure sorrow for Christ, it will not be without recompense for you, for paradise, they say, is sweet and the reward is great, according to the divine apostle; who is worthy to receive the crown of praise: the one who wrought these victories, or the one who rested? For labors give birth to glory, and toils obtain crowns. According to the word spoken by Christ in the holy Gospel, he who endures to the end shall be saved.
