And the Grand Prince, having honored him, sent him back to his sovereign, the Sultan of Caffa, Makhmet-Shih-zoda, and with him the Grand Prince sent his envoy Andrei Semenovich Lapenok on the 16th day of March; and he sent them off on boats along the Mecha River to the Don, and along the Don to Azov; and at the same time he sent back Kazimir of Crimea.

Also, together with Prince Semyon, there came an envoy from Crimea named Azikhaley, son of Azbaba.

About the Danish Envoy

In that same winter, in February, an envoy came to Moscow from the Danish King Ivan, a chaplain named Ivan.

In the same month of February Alyosha Golokhvastov returned from Tsargrad, whom the Grand Prince had sent with letters to the Turkish Sultan Baazit-Saltan; and the Sultan, having honored him, sent him back to the Grand Prince.

Marriage of the Grand Prince's Daughter Feodosia

In the same month of February, on the 13th day, a Thursday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich gave his daughter Feodosia in marriage to Prince Vasily Danilovich Kholmsky, and Metropolitan Simon married them in the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God.

In that same spring, on April 2, the Grand Prince sent Yuri Manuilovich the Greek and clerk Tretyak Dolmatov on an embassy to the Danish King Ivan, and he also sent the Danish envoy, the chaplain, back with them.

On the Sending of Envoys and Merchants to Crimea

In the same month of April, on the 17th day, the Grand Prince sent back the Crimean envoy Azikhal, and together with him he sent his envoy Prince Ivan Semenovich Kubensky to the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girey, and with him he also sent many Muscovite merchants.

And they set out across the Field, and Tatars, Azov Cossacks Uguz-Cherkas and Karabay, attacked them and robbed them in the Field; some merchants they took captive, others they killed; and some merchants, together with Prince Ivan and the Crimean envoy, rode on yasak horses and reached Crimea; while the boyar sons who escorted them all returned safe to Moscow. And Prince Ivan Kubensky died in Crimea.

About the Wallachian Envoy

In that same spring a Wallachian envoy came to the Grand Prince, named Fedor Isaev, governor of Khotin.
