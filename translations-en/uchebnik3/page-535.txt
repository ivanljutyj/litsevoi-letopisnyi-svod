And on Friday of the same month, the 26th day, the sovereign ordered a large fortification to be built opposite the Tsarev, Arsk, Atalykov, and Tyumen gates.

And he orders from the large regiment the other voivode, the servant Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Vorotynsky, with his regiment to dismount, go on foot to the city, and roll the turas, while the great voivode, boyar Prince Ivan Fedorovich Mstislavsky, with his regiment is to remain on horseback.

And from his own regiment the sovereign sends selected commanders on horseback with boyar sons, and the sovereign ordered them to be on guard; if mounted men come out against Prince Mikhail, Prince Ivan is to act as God helps.

And from his own regiment the sovereign also sent boyar sons, choosing those stalwart and skilled in warfare, and with them boyar retainers, assigning to each boyar son one hundred men, and he ordered them to be with Prince Mikhail on foot.

On the setting up of the turas by the city of Kazan

That same day, in the evening hour, according to the sovereign’s order Prince Mikhail went to the city, and according to the sovereign’s order he arranged the men: in front he ordered the streltsy commanders Ivan Cheremisinov, Grigory Zholobov, Fyodor Durasov, and Dyak Rzhevsky with their streltsy to go to the city, and many Cossack atamans with Cossacks, and commanders with boyar retainers, and he ordered the turas to be rolled to the appointed place, while he himself with the boyar sons went on foot behind them.

On the sortie of the Kazan people from the city

And when they went to the city, many Kazanians climbed out, mounted and on foot, and both sides, Christians and Tatars, fought stoutly for a long time, and from the cannons they struck the city and the gates without cease, and the streltsy fired from arquebuses; likewise from the city they fired cannons and arquebuses, and there was a great and most terrible battle. From the cannon fire and the thunder of arquebuses, and from the voices and shouting of people, and from the clatter of weapons, no one could hear what was said to one another; everywhere there was a great thunder, and there was flashing from the multitude of cannon fire and arquebus shots, and smoke and fumes.

And God helped the Orthodox: they overcame the godless and killed many Tatars, and drove them into the city, and the city bridges were filled with the dead.

And the streltsy commanders and streltsy stood, and the commanders with boyar retainers and Cossacks, along the moat around the city, and fired from arquebuses and bows.

And Prince Mikhail Ivanovich set up the turas and poured earth at fifty sazhens from the city, from the Bulak River to the gates, around the whole city from the assault places; and he ordered the commanders to lead all the men to the turas, and before the turas he ordered the streltsy and Cossacks to entrench themselves in the ditches opposite the city.
