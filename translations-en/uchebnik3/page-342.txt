On Those Sent by the Roman Pope

In the same year there was at the grand prince in Moscow a man from the Roman pope with a letter, by name Paul.

On the Building of the City of Tula

In the same year the building of the stone city at Tula was completed, and it was called by its former name—the city of Tula—and the river beneath it is also the Tula.

In the year 7029 from Adam (1529 from Christ).

On the Death of Prince Dmitry, Brother of the Grand Prince

Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily, reposed on the fourteenth day of February.

On the Finding of the Relics of Makary of Kalyazin

In the same year, on the fourteenth day of May, in the patrimony of the right-believing Prince Georgy Ivanovich of Moscow, at Kashin, in the Kalyazin Monastery, they began to dig trenches for the foundation of a church of the Holy and Life-Giving Trinity, and they found the relics of the venerable abbot Makary, who had founded that place, whole and uncorrupted, and his vestments had not decayed; and, seeing this and marveling, they glorified God, who glorifies His saints.

On the Betrayal of the Kazan Princes

That same spring the Kazan seyid and ulans and princes broke their oath to Grand Prince Vasily, took for themselves from the Crimea the tsarevich Sahib Giray as tsar in Kazan, and sent Tsar Shigalei with the tsaritsa out of Kazan, and, having plundered the merchants from the grand prince’s land, kept them with them.

On the Treacherous Union of the King with the Crimean Tsar

And afterward, when peace had been made, the king secretly joined with the Crimean Tsar Magamet-Giray and gave him a great host in aid against the grand prince.

The grand prince, however, at that time had peace with all the surrounding tsars and kings and with other lands. And then the godless Tsar Magmed-Giray seized a time convenient for his treacherous and evil cunning, knowing that the grand prince expected war against himself from nowhere, and was not then preparing to march against anyone; and many of his fighting men were at that time in their own places without fear.
