On the War of the Crimean Tsar against Astrakhan, and on His Slaying in Astrakhan

In that same year the godless Tsar Magmed-Kirei set out from Perekop, and having come with his brothers and his sons to Astrakhan, and having overcome it, he became very proud.

And the Nogais living in Astrakhan took counsel together, and killed the tsar and his accursed son, and slew the remaining Crimean enemies.

On Shemyachich

That same spring Shemyachich was captured.

On Vasily Podzhogin

That same spring Tsar Saip-Girei in Kazan did much evil to Christians, shedding blood like water, and killed the envoy of the Grand Prince, Vasily Yuryevich Podzhogin.

On the Grand Prince’s Campaign to Nizhny Novgorod and on the Founding of the Town of Vasil on the Sura River

And the Grand Prince Vasily, grieving over this, set out with his brothers against the second tormentor, the Kazan tsar, and arrived at Nizhny Novgorod on the 24th day of August.

And he sent Tsar Shigalei to Kazan by the Volga with a river host, and with him his voivodes, and also a mounted host overland with many men, and he ordered the environs of Kazan to be laid waste.

On Vasil-grad

And at that time he ordered a wooden town to be built at the mouth of the Sura River and named it Vasil-grad.

And the Grand Prince’s voivodes, having laid waste the environs of Kazan, returned safe and brought many captives with them.

On the Grand Prince’s Departure from Nizhny Novgorod. In the 7032nd Year from Adam (1532nd Year from Christ)

On the 15th day of the month of September, the great sovereign returned from Nizhny Novgorod with his brothers to Moscow.
