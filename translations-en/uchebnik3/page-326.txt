And Shausein-seit also wrote with his own hand the documents concerning that in which the tsar and all the land of Kazan were to give the oath.

And the grand prince dismissed the envoy Shausein-seit. And together with him, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, sent to Tsar Magamet-Amin in Kazan with those documents his okolnichy Mikhail Vasilyevich Tuchkov, his oruzhnichy Nikita Ivanovich Karpov, and his dyak Ivan Teleshev.

And Tsar Magamet-Amin and all the land of Kazan swore allegiance to the grand prince on those documents before his envoys, and the tsar dismissed the grand prince’s envoys, and together with them the tsar sent to the grand prince his own envoy, the same Shausein-seit, with great entreaty—to petition for his brother. And the grand prince, for the sake of Tsar Magamet-Amin, showed favor to his brother, released Tsar Abdyl-Letif from exile, removed his disgrace from him, set aside his anger, and showed him favor by giving him the town of Kashira in his land.

On the Appointment of a Bishop in Suzdal.
In the year 7025 from Adam (the year 1525 from Christ)

On February 10, a Tuesday, by the good pleasure of God and the council of hierarchs, at the command of the right-believing Vasily Ivanovich, by the grace of God sovereign of all Rus and grand prince, by the most reverend Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, and by the whole sacred council, Gennady, former archimandrite of the Nativity of the Holy Theotokos in Vladimir, was appointed bishop in Suzdal.

On the Appointment of a Bishop to Ryazan

In the same month of February, on the 12th day, a Thursday, Sergius, archimandrite of the Andronikov Monastery, was appointed bishop to Ryazan by Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus.

On the Envoy of the Prussian Master

That same winter, in March, there came to Moscow to the great sovereign Vasily, from the Grand Master of Prussia, Albrecht, his envoy of the German nation, named Feodoriscus Shimbork, to petition that the great sovereign Vasily show him favor and protect him. And that against his foe, the King of Poland, he would be at one with him and defend him from the king.
