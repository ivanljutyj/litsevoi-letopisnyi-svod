On November 4, at the camp at Bolblovy, there came to the Grand Prince from Grand Prince Mikhail of Tver his commander Prince Mikhail Fedorovich Mikulinsky with Tver regiments to aid the Grand Prince against the Novgorodians.

And the Grand Prince honored him and ordered him to follow after him along his route.

On November 8, in the Church of the Savior at Yaglino, the Grand Prince ordered the Novgorodians Fedor Kalitin and Ivan Markov, who had come for a safe-conduct, to appear before him; and they came to him and began to petition him on behalf of Feofil, Archbishop of Novgorod, and of Great Novgorod for a safe-conduct for travel, calling the Grand Prince sovereign—that the sovereign might show favor and give a safe-conduct so that the archbishop and the Novgorod posadniks might come to him, petition him, and depart freely.

And the Grand Prince granted it and gave his safe-conduct.

On November 10, at Ivanichi, the third envoy sent for a safe-conduct, Grigory Mikhailov Sovkin, met the Grand Prince; and on the 11th, at St Nicholas in Lokotsk, the Grand Prince ordered him to come before him. And he petitioned on behalf of the archbishop and Novgorod, calling the Grand Prince sovereign, and asked for a safe-conduct; and the Grand Prince ordered his secretary Vasily Dalmatov to reply, speaking in the Grand Prince’s words: “As for his petition to us on behalf of our pilgrim and our patrimony, I have granted it and given a safe-conduct to the first envoys sent for it, Ivan Markov and Fedor Kalitin; and the archbishop and the Novgorod envoys shall, under that safe-conduct, come and depart from us freely.”

And Grigory Sovkin petitioned that the Grand Prince would show favor and appoint an escort with whom they might pass through his host; and the Grand Prince granted it and appointed Mikhail Pogozhy as escort, and released them together from Lokotsk.

On November 19, at Paliny, Ivan Vasilyev, son of Nikiforov, met the Grand Prince, together with Luka Klimentyev.

And at that same camp at Paliny the Grand Prince arrayed the regiments and ordered where each was to be: he ordered his brother Prince Andrei the Younger to be in the advance regiment, and under him he ordered his commanders Prince Daniil Kholmsky with the Kostromans and Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Obolensky with the Vladimirians to be. And in the right-hand regiment the Grand Prince ordered his brother Prince Andrei the Elder to be with him, and under him he ordered the Tver commander Prince Mikhail Fedorovich Mikulinsky with the Tver men, and his commanders Grigory and Ivan Zhit Nikitich, and with them the men of Dmitrov and Kashin.
