And the tsar and grand prince ordered his okolnichy Alexei Fyodorovich Adashev to question Ismail’s envoys about what they desired, and ordered him to decide with them how that matter should be done.

And by the tsar’s and sovereign’s command and by decision, the okolnichy Alexei and the dyak Ivan agreed that the sovereign tsar and grand prince should send Tsar Derbysh to Astrakhan, and many of his voevodes in boats down the Volga and with the artillery train, while Smail should go by the steppe, or send his sons and nephews to Astrakhan; and if, God willing, they take the Astrakhan yurt, then the tsar’s and grand prince’s voevodes should install Tsar Derbysh in Astrakhan, and Smail his son or nephew. And Smail at that time, or whenever it is possible for him, should come against his brother Prince Isup in war because he is not loyal to the tsar and grand prince, dishonors the tsar’s and grand prince’s envoys, and at that time keeps the envoy Syundyuk Tulusupov in chains; and Ismail-murza with his sons and nephews should be at one against all enemies of the tsar and grand prince: wherever the tsar and grand prince sends him, there he should go.

And the tsar and grand prince, placing his hope in Almighty God, began to consult with the boyars how he should deal with the Astrakhan Tsar Emgurchey for all his offenses: concerning which he had sent his envoys and Prince Shikhim to petition, in all that he had broken faith, and had robbed the tsar’s and grand prince’s envoy Sevastyan. And the tsar and grand prince recalled his ancient patrimony, how his forefather the holy-departed Great Vladimir, who enlightened the Russian land with holy baptism, divided his possessions into parts among his children, and gave that Astrakhan, then called Tmutorokan, to his son Mstislav, in which also a church of the Most Pure had been built. And many Christian sovereigns, reposed since the time of Vladimir, kinsmen of the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, ruled those places; but God permitted, because of Christian sins and for the non-observance of the law of Christ, and because of many internecine wars of the Russian sovereigns, that it was taken by the impious tsars of the Horde, who were called the Great Horde. And for many years the impious tsars established themselves in that Horde. And with many calamities and blood the Orthodox sovereigns suffered from them and were crowned with martyrs’ crowns. Among them also the blessed Grand Prince Mikhail of Chernigov and his boyar Fyodor, and other sovereigns suffered evil, and down to the blessed Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, grandfather of the pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus.
