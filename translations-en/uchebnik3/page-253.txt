And they stood as if beside themselves, like mutes, at the council before the grand prince, the metropolitan, the archbishop, the bishops, and the entire sacred council of the Russian metropolia, and shut themselves up in those heresies of theirs, in their foul deeds, and were as if beside themselves.

But the pious and Christ-loving true champion of Orthodoxy, like the second pious Emperor Constantine, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, sovereign of all Rus and autocrat of many other lands, with his father Zosima, most holy metropolitan of all Rus, with the archbishop and bishops, and with the entire sacred council of the Russian metropolia, having examined their foul heresies according to the authentic copies presented by Archbishop Gennady and sent to Metropolitan Gerontius, and according to the Moscow testimonies, excommunicated those heretics — the Novgorod archpriest Gavriil, the monk Zakharia, and the priest Denis, and their aforementioned companions and sympathizers — from the catholic and apostolic Church according to the rules of the holy apostles and holy fathers, deposed them from their rank, and sent them into exile and prisons, while some they sent to monasteries.

And they clearly affirmed the pure, holy, and immaculate Orthodox Christian faith and glorified the Holy Trinity, indivisible in the one Godhead in three persons, glorifying the Father, hymning the Son, worshiping the Holy Spirit, confessing the Trinity in Unity and Unity in Trinity with heart and lips forever. Amen.

Against these same God-opposing Novgorod heretics and against other heresies, the venerable Joseph, abbot of Volokolamsk, wrote words of denunciation from the testimonies of the Divine Scriptures, which are gathered into a separate book of his for the confirmation of the Orthodox.
