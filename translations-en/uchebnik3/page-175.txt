But Theophilus, Archbishop of Novgorod, and Gennadius, Bishop of Tver, sent their envoys and signed together with the other bishops, saying: “Whomever the Lord God and the Most Pure and the great wonderworkers, and also Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, and our brothers the bishops, and the consecrated council wish, he shall be metropolitan to us all.”

The council was held in Moscow.

On the Installation of Gerontius as Metropolitan

And they chose Gerontius, Bishop of Kolomna, as one worthy to govern the flock entrusted to him by God; he was brought to the metropolitan court on June 4, a Friday, and installed on the 29th day of the same month, a Tuesday, on St Peter’s Day.

That same summer, on July 25, the priest-monk Nikita Semeshkov, son of the archpriest of the Church of the Archangel Michael in Moscow, was installed as Bishop of Kolomna by Metropolitan Gerontius.

In the same year, Metropolitan Gerontius built at his court in Moscow a gate of fired brick, and also laid the foundation of a palace at his court.

In the same year, an envoy from Pskov came to petition the Grand Prince that he would show favor and defend them from the Germans, for their truce had already ended, and the Germans were marching against them.

In the year 6982 from Adam (1482 from Christ)

The Grand Prince sent Prince Daniil Dmitrievich Kholmsky to the aid of Pskov, and with him many of his regiments.

And they came to Pskov, and the Germans heard that the Grand Prince’s commanders had come to Pskov’s aid with many men, and they began sending their envoys to Pskov with offers of peace; and peace was concluded on Pskov’s terms for twenty years.

That same autumn the Muscovites took Lyubutsk and a great number of captives.

And that same autumn the people of Lyubutsk came secretly against Prince Semyon Odoevsky; he joined battle with them, but he had few men with him; and they killed this one Prince Semyon there, while all the others remained unharmed.
