And the other associates of Prince Dmitry and Prince Ivan were seized, plundered, and put in irons. And the townspeople were brought to swear allegiance to Grand Prince Vasily, and they began to fortify the town.

And the Grand Prince went to Volok against Shemyaka and Ivan of Mozhaisk with a great host. They were in confusion: the Grand Prince was coming against them from Tver, and news came to them that the tsareviches were coming, and Prince Vasily Yaroslavich with a huge force, that Moscow had already been taken, and the people were fleeing from them—and they fled to Galich, and from there to Chukhloma.

And taking with them the Grand Prince’s mother, Grand Princess Sophia, they fled to Kargopol.

And the Grand Prince went after them, and sent his Grand Princess back to Moscow, and, having come, he halted under Uglich. Prince Vasily Yaroslavich came there to him as well, and with him the Grand Prince’s boyars previously named; he stood under Uglich and took it. A Lithuanian, a brave man, Yushko Dranitsa, was killed under the town of Uglich.

And from there the Grand Prince went to Yaroslavl; and there the tsareviches Kasym and Yagup came to him.

From Yaroslavl the Grand Prince sent his boyar Vasily Fedorovich Kutuzov to Prince Dmitry, asking him for his mother, Grand Princess Sophia, saying to him: “Brother, Prince Dmitry Yuryevich, what honor or praise is it to you that you keep my mother, and your aunt, in captivity? You wish by this to take revenge on me, but I am already on my throne, on the great principality.” And having sent him, he himself went back to Moscow and came to Moscow on the 17th day of February, Friday of Cheese-fare week. The Grand Prince’s boyar came and delivered these words to Prince Dmitry, and all the others. Prince Dmitry, having consulted with his boyars, said: “Brother, why should I torment not merely my aunt, but my lady, the Grand Princess? I myself am fleeing, and people are needed by me and are already weary; rather than guarding her, it is better to let her go.” And so they decided to release her from Kargopol; and with her Prince Dmitry sent his boyar Mikhail Fedorovich Saburov, and boyars’ sons with him. The Grand Prince learned that his mother had been released and would soon arrive, and went to meet her. And they met at the Trinity in the Sergius Monastery.

And from there he went with his mother to Pereyaslavl. And Mikhail Saburov and the others beat their brows to the Grand Prince, and, without returning to Shemyaka, remained with the Grand Prince to serve him.

In the year 6956 from Adam (1456 from Christ)

In the Philip’s Fast, the Kazan tsar Mamutyak sent all his princes with a great force to wage war on the Grand Prince’s patrimony, Vladimir, Murom and other towns.
