In the year 6974 from Adam (the year 1474 from Christ)

On the fifth day of October, at the first hour of the night, the moon vanished completely for two hours. That same spring, on the eighth of April, Bishop Vassian of Sarai died.

There was a great pestilence in Pskov and in Novgorod from Easter, and it began to abate from Philip's Fast. In that same year, on May 14, snow fell a span deep and lay for two days; on the twenty-sixth of the same month, snow lay for a day; and on the eighteenth of August there was frost, and another frost was on the twenty-seventh of the same month and killed the spring crops.

In the same year died the princess, wife of Prince Pyotr Dmitrievich, after long living as a nun and in the schema in Moscow at the Ascension Monastery, named Euphrosyne in monasticism.

In the year 6975 from Adam (the year 1475 from Christ)

On November 1, as it was freezing, Lake Rostov began to howl; this lasted two weeks, and at night it gave the people in the city no sleep: it was as if six or eight were threshing, and at last it would knock with a prolonged sound; such a thing had not happened for many years.

That same winter, on January 14, there was a cruel frost, and many people died along the roads in Moscow and in other towns, through districts and villages.

That same spring, on April 14, a son, Ivan, was born to Prince Vasily Ivanovich of Ryazan in the city of Moscow.

On the Death of Grand Princess Maria, Wife of Prince Ivan Vasilievich

That same spring, on the twenty-second day of April, on Wednesday of the fourth week after Easter, between Wednesday and Thursday at the fifth hour of the night, there died in the city of Moscow the faithful and Christ-loving, good and humble Grand Princess Maria, daughter of Grand Prince Boris Alexandrovich of Tver. Metropolitan Philip sang the customary hymns over her, and she was laid in the monastery in the Church of the Holy Ascension, above her late mother-in-law, Grand Princess Maria; Grand Prince Ivan was then in Kolomna. That same spring, on the fifth of May, snow fell to the depth of half a knee and lay for three days; and on the fourth day it melted by evening. And in the month of June, on the second, there was frost.

In the same year, on the sixth day of August, Tryphon, Archbishop of Rostov, renounced his bishopric, for he was very ill. He shepherded God's Church for five years and two and a half months and withdrew to the Monastery of the Savior.
