On the Sending from Astrakhan

In the same year, on April 13, the son of Tsar Derbysh-Alei, Tsarevich Yantimer, sent from Astrakhan his man Tinbulat with a letter. And in the letter he wrote that Yamgurchei-Tsar and the sons of Prince Isuf, Yunus-Murza and Alei-Murza and Ak-Murza, and all those murzas who had been driven out of the Nogais, and Kazyi-Murza, and the Crimean Tsar had sent with them Shigai-Bogatyr Antulov, and with him Crimeans and janissaries, had come to Astrakhan.

And they attacked the city; and Tsar Derbysh and all the Astrakhan people, having repaired the artillery on the city and prepared the Cossacks with handguns of the tsar and grand prince, fought with them, and many of them were killed at the city by cannon and handgun fire, and they drove them off.

And Tsar Derbysh-Alei sent his son Tsarevich Yantimer after them. And he pursued them, and from the pursuit sent to the sovereign tsar and grand prince with glad tidings his man Tinbulat, that his foe Emgurchei-Tsar had come, and by God’s mercy and by the good fortune of the tsar and grand prince, the city of Astrakhan and its people, Astrakhanians and Russians, God be thanked, were well, and let this be known to the tsar and sovereign.

He also wrote that from many hordes many had come into the tsar and grand prince’s realm of Astrakhan, both envoys and merchants, and let this too be known to the sovereign tsar and grand prince.

And in April the tsar and grand prince showed favor to Tsarevich Yantimer’s man and released him. And to the tsarevich he wrote with great favor, and to Tsar Derbysh and all the land he wished to send an envoy with favor in the spring.
