In a single hour a great multitude of people burned, 1,700 persons of male and female sex, and infants; a multitude of people burned along Tverskaya Street and along Dmitrovka, and in the Great Settlement, along Ilyinskaya Street, in Sady. God brought all this upon us for our sins, for many transgressions and iniquities; by His righteous judgment God leads us to repentance—some by fire, some by famine, some by the coming of warriors, some by pestilence. Earlier memorial books of the time record: such a fire had not occurred in Moscow since Moscow came to be so named, glorious and honored through the great princes in their realm; before this Moscow had not been so populated as now, but multiplied in people in the years of the pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of all Rus.

And after the fire the tsar and grand prince was in his village of Vorobyovo with his tsaritsa, Grand Princess Anastasia, with his brother Prince Yuri, and with boyars; and he ordered the churches and chambers on his court to be rebuilt, those that had collapsed from the fire, and wooden mansions to be built.

On the Murder of Prince Yuri Glinsky

In the same month, on the 26th day, a Sunday, on the fifth day after the great fire, the common people of the city of Moscow, out of great sorrow over the fire, rebelled as if mad, came into the city, and in the square stoned to death the tsar’s and grand prince’s boyar Prince Yuri Vasilievich Glinsky, and killed many sons of boyars.

And on the second day after the fire the tsar and grand prince came to visit Metropolitan Macarius at Novoye, and with him were boyars, and by the enemy’s instigation they began to say that by sorcery human hearts had been taken out and soaked in water, and that water had been sprinkled, and from that all Moscow had burned. The tsar’s and grand prince’s confessor, the Annunciation protopope Fyodor, and boyar Prince Fyodor Skopin-Shuisky, and Ivan Petrov Fedorov began to speak these words. And the tsar and grand prince ordered the boyars to carry out an investigation. The boyars came to the square by the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Mother of God and gathered the common people, and began to ask: who set Moscow on fire? They began to say that Princess Anna Glinskaya with her children and people had practiced sorcery: she took out human hearts and put them in water, and rode about Moscow with that water and sprinkled it, and from that Moscow burned down. And the common people said that at that time the Glinskys were near the sovereign and in his favor, and that violence and robbery were done to the common people by their people, and they did not desist from it. And Prince Mikhail Glinsky was then with his mother on the sovereign’s bounty in Rzhev; and Prince Yuri Glinsky had then arrived, and when he heard such speeches about his mother and himself, he went to the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God.
