And they carried him onto the porch, and behind him walked those with candles and censers, singing “Holy God”; and there was inconsolable weeping.

And when they carried him to the square, there were so many tears and cries from the people that even the ringing of the bells could not be heard, as though the earth were groaning.

The Grand Princess was carried from her chambers in a sledge by the boyars’ sons themselves to the staircase, and with her walked the boyars: Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky, Mikhail Semyonovich Vorontsov, Prince Mikhail Lvovich Glinsky, Prince Ivan Fedorovich Ovchina; and the noblewomen then with the Grand Princess were Princess Anastasia, wife of Prince Fyodor Mstislavsky, niece of the Grand Prince, and Princess Maria, wife of Prince Ivan Danilovich Penkov, and the noblewomen: Elena, wife of Ivan Andreevich Chelyadnin, and Agrofena, wife of Vasily Andreevich, and Feodosia, wife of Mikhail Yuryevich, and Agrofena, wife of Vasily Ivanovich, and Princess Anna, wife of Prince Vasily Lvovich Glinsky.

The sovereign, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, left the earthly kingdom and took the monastic habit, and in the monastic rank was named Varlaam; and he departed on the 3rd day of the month of December, from Wednesday to Thursday, at the close of the month, at the tenth hour of the night; he was in the sovereignty, in the great principality, after his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, 28 years and 5 weeks; and all the years of his life were 56 years and 8 months and 9 days. And his body was laid in the glorious and renowned city of Moscow in the church of the holy and glorious Michael the Archangel beside his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, where his forefathers lie.

And he blessed for the great principality his son, the sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, who was at that time three years old.

And all the princes and boyars, and officials, and all Christians throughout all the cities of the patrimony of his sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus kissed the life-giving cross to him.

On the Capture of Prince Yuri Ivanovich

In the same month, on December 1, Prince Yuri Ivanovich sent his clerk Tretyak Tishkov to Prince Andrei Shuysky, and Tretyak said to him from the prince that he should come to serve Prince Yuri. And Prince Andrei answered Tretyak: “Your prince kissed the cross to the Grand Prince yesterday, to wish him well, and now he calls people away from him.” And Tretyak said to Prince Andrei: “The boyars brought Prince Yuri to the kissing of the cross under lock, but they themselves gave Prince Yuri no surety on the Grand Prince’s behalf: is this a kissing of the cross? This is a forced kissing of the cross.”

And Prince Andrei told Prince Boris Ivanovich Gorbaty about this; and Prince Boris told the boyars about this; and the boyars told the Grand Princess.
