On the 29th day of May, on Friday, on the commemoration of the holy venerable martyr Theodosia the Virgin, at the beginning of the second hour of the day the metropolitan ordered the bell to be rung, and the whole sacred council gathered to him—Prokhor, Bishop of Sarai, and the archimandrites, and the archpriests, and the abbots, and all the priests of the city of Moscow—and they began to sing funeral hymns.

And then came the right-believing and great prince Ivan with his son the great prince Ivan, and his mother, and his brothers Yuri and Andrei, and Boris, and Andrei, and their princes, and the boyars, and all Orthodox Christian people of the glorious city of Moscow, men and women, small and great. And after they had sung the funeral service, as is proper, for the departed, then the metropolitan ordered the reliquaries with the honorable relics of the hierarchs to be brought to the place prepared for them, from the dismantled church into the newly founded one. And first they took the reliquary with the relics of Metropolitan Cyprian and placed it in a shrine in the wall on the right side.

Then they took the reliquary with the relics of Metropolitan Photius and placed it together with Cyprian in one place.

After this they came to the tomb of the most reverend Metropolitan Jonah, and when the lid was removed from it, immediately a strong fragrance came forth throughout the whole church, so that all who were there perceived it. His relics were all whole and intact, for the flesh had adhered to his bones, and his joints had not shifted; and the vestments and omophorion and the rest of all these three hierarchs had not decayed over so many years after their repose; and so then all the multitude of Orthodox Christians who were there and saw it shed many tears at the glorious sight and thanked the Lord God and His Most Pure Mother, who had glorified His servants.

And taking the reliquary of the most reverend Metropolitan Jonah, they placed it on the left side in a shrine with his honorable relics. Their translation took place on one day: after the repose of Metropolitan Cyprian, 65 years and 9 months less 18 days; of Photius, 40 years and a year less 34 days; and of Jonah, 11 years and two months. And when the church was being dismantled, then from the wall of the church of the Holy Great Martyr Demetrius they took the relics of Prince Yuri Danilovich, Great Prince of All Rus'.

And, having placed them in a wooden reliquary, they set them on the tomb of Metropolitan Theognostus, where was the Church of the Veneration of the Precious Chains of the Holy Apostle Peter.

And when the church was being built, they prepared a place in the same church of the Great Demetrius, in the wall on the same side.

And having transferred them to the prepared place and sung the funeral service, they laid them there; and at the translation were the metropolitan with all the aforementioned sacred council, and the great prince with his son, and a multitude of people; and it was 147 years after his murder.
