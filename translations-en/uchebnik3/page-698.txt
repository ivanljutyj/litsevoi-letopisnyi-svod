And some boyars and voivodes who were in that campaign, boyar scions, streltsy, and boyar retainers went to Polotsk with all their people.

And in that action they killed and captured boyar scions, and up to one hundred and fifty men disappeared without trace.

The Vyazma voivodes, the boyars Prince Vasily and Prince Pyotr Semyonovich Serebryany with their comrades, came at the appointed time to Orsha, to Boran, and there news came to them that the Lithuanians had not let the voivodes Prince Pyotr Shuysky and his comrades through from Polotsk to Orsha; and they began war throughout the Lithuanian land and ravaged Lithuanian places: Dubrovin, Orsha, Drutsk, Berezyna, Kopos, Shklov, Mogilev, Radoml, Mstislavl, and Krichev; they burned the king’s villages and hamlets as well; and the separate detachments sent out in many places defeated the outposts of the Lithuanians, took tongues, and captured many people with their property.

And they came to the Smolensk borderland on the 9th day of February with all their people, by God’s grace, in good health, and they sent to the sovereign with that news Nikita Ivanov, son of Ochin Pleshcheyev; and Nikita arrived on the commemoration of the wonderworker Alexis, on the 12th day of February.

In the same year, on the 9th day of February, the Tsar and Grand Prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilyevich and his son Tsarevich Ivan, with his holy fathers, with Pimen, Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, with Nikandr, Archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl, with Trifon, Archbishop of Polotsk, with Afanasy, Bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa, with Semyon, Bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk, with Filofey, Bishop of Ryazan and Murom, with Varlaam, Bishop of Kolomna and Kashira, with Matfey, Bishop of Sarsk and Podonsk, with Ioasaf, Bishop of Perm and Vologda, and with his boyars decided that, after the departure from this life of his father and pilgrim Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, the Church of God is widowed and the throne of Great Russia of the most reverend former metropolitans and wonderworkers Peter, Alexis, and Jonah is without a shepherd. And whoever God, the most pure Mother of God, and the Russian wonderworkers Peter, Alexis, and Jonah favor to be metropolitan in the house of the Most Pure, that metropolitan shall wear a white klobuk with lappets and a cherubim; and letters of blessing and dispatch shall be sealed with red wax; because the Archbishop of Novgorod wears a white klobuk, and the Archbishop of Novgorod and the Archbishop of Kazan affix seals in red wax.

And according to that ordinance the Tsar and Grand Prince ordered a charter to be written and hung his seal, and the archbishops and bishops set their hands to that charter.
