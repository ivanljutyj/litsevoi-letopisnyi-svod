At the very top of that church there was an unusual vision, like a light, outstretched cloud or thin smoke streaming upward, pure as frost, bright and shining like the sun; and then in the fineness and brightness of that cloud they saw a likeness of the image of the blessed and great prince Alexander, rising on horseback up toward the heavens. And the people who saw this, seized with great fear and terror, began to ring the bells throughout the whole city.

On the Fire

And at noon of that same day a great fire began, on the 23rd day of May, a Monday, so that the whole city of Vladimir burned with all its houses and suburbs; in the city nine churches burned, and in the suburb thirteen churches burned; and many calamities then befell the Christian townspeople.

Moreover, in that city the aforementioned monastery of the Nativity of the Most Pure also burned; a multitude of monks and laypeople with much property had gathered into that church, but by God’s permission everything inside that church burned up together with the people.

But the wonder-working relics of the holy and righteous great prince Alexander Yaroslavich, although they suffered a little from the fiery visitation, were preserved by God, just as the shroud that was in his tomb remained undamaged by that inconstant fire; for it is known that the Lord, according to the words of the prophet, preserves the bones of His servant, and not one of them will be harmed.

And so by God’s grace, from his honorable tomb even to this day many miracles and healings occur, of which it is said here also: “When our pious tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilyevich, sovereign and autocrat of the whole Russian, Kazan, and Astrakhan realms, grandson of the elder great prince Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat of all Rus, guided by God, for his ancestral inheritance, and especially for the Christ-named possession, conquered the Kazan kingdom and received it into his dominion, and enlightened it with Orthodoxy, and freed countless captive Christians.

And also when he was going to Kazan with his army and was in the city of Vladimir, in the monastery, in the church of the Nativity of the Most Pure Mother of God, lifting up his prayers to God, at that same time in that church, during the conciliar prayer, it happened that I, the most sinful Afanasy, priest of the Annunciation, was present at the healing-bearing tomb of the blessed and great prince Alexander. And I saw near the church platform, on the tomb of the blessed one, a small hole, and in order to learn what that hole meant, I put my hand into that hole and felt how some kind of ointment moistened my hand, on which for a long time there had been a small wound.
