On the Release of Grand Prince Vasily by Shemyaka

In the year 6955 from Adam (1455 from Christ), Prince Dmitry Shemyaka went to Uglich, wishing to release the Grand Prince and his children. There went with him all the bishops, and the honorable archimandrites, and the abbots.

Having come to Uglich, he released the Grand Prince and his children, repenting and asking forgiveness. And the Grand Prince humbled himself before him and laid all the blame upon himself, saying: "I should have suffered more than this for my sins and many iniquities, and for breaking the oath on the cross before you, before all my elder brothers and before all Orthodox Christendom, which I have ruined and even wished to ruin utterly; I deserve beheading, but you, my lord, have shown mercy to me and have not destroyed me for my lawlessness; I repent of my evil." Having said this and much else besides, which is beyond number and impossible to describe. As he spoke, tears flowed from his eyes like streams, so that all who were there marveled at such humility and compunction, and all wept as they looked at him.

Then Prince Dmitry made a great feast in honor of the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess and their children: there were present all the bishops of the Russian land, and many boyars, and sons of boyars. And he honored the Grand Prince and gave many gifts to the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess and their children, and gave the Grand Prince Vologda with all that belonged to it as a patrimony, and released the Grand Prince with the Princess and their children. The Grand Prince came there and, after staying a little, went with all his people to the Kirillov Monastery, intending to feed the brethren there and give alms. For such a sovereign could not be kept confined in so distant a wilderness. Hearing of this, the Grand Prince's boyars and sons of boyars and many people fled from Prince Dmitry and Prince Ivan to the Grand Prince.

After staying at Beloozero, the Grand Prince wished to go away, and then many people came to him. And the Grand Prince did not return to Vologda, but went to Tver, having beforehand sent word to the Grand Prince of Tver, Boris Alexandrovich.

When he came to Tver, the Grand Prince of Tver gave him rest with him and showed him great honor and many gifts.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich then betrothed his eldest son, Prince Ivan, to Maria, daughter of Grand Prince Boris. And many boyars with their people came to the Grand Prince in Tver.

And when Prince Dmitry was still releasing the Grand Prince, Prince Vasily Yaroslavich, being in Lithuania, did not know of it, and he and the Grand Prince's boyars decided, leaving their wives and children in the Lithuanian land, to go and seek the Grand Prince, so as somehow to free him from Uglich; and they decided that at a set time all should meet in the Lithuanian volost at Patsyn.
