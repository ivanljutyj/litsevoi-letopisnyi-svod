And Tsar Shigalei and the commanders sent them to the city of Kazan: “You have sworn allegiance to the sovereign; go, show your oath to the sovereign, fight his foe”; and with them they sent Pyotr Turov and Alexei Ershov to keep watch. And many Hill people gathered, and they set out, petitioning that they be ferried across, because it was impossible throughout that summer to cross from the Hill side to the Meadow side; and the tsar and commanders ordered the Cossacks stationed at those ferries to ferry them across below Tarloshaya and at the Kamenny ferry.

And they set out for the city in the month of June and came to the Arsk field by the city; and all the Kazan people, Crimeans and Kazanians, came out to meet them and fought them bravely, and there were casualties on both sides.
