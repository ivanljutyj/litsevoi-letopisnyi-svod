In the same winter, on the tenth day of March, on the first Sunday of Great Lent, Trifon, former archimandrite of the Simonov Monastery, was installed by Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus', as bishop of Suzdal.

On the seventeenth day of the same month, on the second Sunday of Great Lent, Nikandr, abbot of the Sergius Monastery, was installed by Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus', as archbishop of Rostov.

On the death of the Kazan Tsar

On the twenty-fifth day of the same month of March, a report came to the tsar and grand prince that the Kazan Tsar Safa-Girey had died in Kazan, having killed himself in his chambers. And the Kazanians and Crimeans, having united, enthroned his son, Tsarevich Utemish-Girey, two years old, in the Kazan Tsardom, and sent many envoys to Crimea to ask for help and for a grown tsar. And the Cossacks of the tsar and grand prince, Urachko and his companions, killed the Kazan envoys, took their charters from them and sent them to the sovereign, and did not let a single man through to Crimea.

In the same summer, on the sixth day of July, Tsar Utemish-Girey sent from Kazan to the tsar and grand prince his man Bakshanda with a letter, and wrote in it about peace.

And the tsar and grand prince sent his letter to Tsar Utemish-Girey with the same Bakshanda, and wrote: if he wants peace, let him send good men.

On the eleventh day of the same month, the tsar and grand prince sent to the King his envoys Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov and Pyotr Vasilyevich Morozov, and the clerk Bakaka Mitrofanov, to conclude a truce and bring the King to the oath.

And Mikhail Yakovlevich and his companions concluded the truce and received the oath from the King, and brought the truce charter to the tsar and grand prince in Vladimir in the year 7058 from Adam (the year 1558 from Christ), in the month of December; and at that time the tsar and grand prince was going on his campaign to Kazan.

On the birth of the Tsarevna

In the same year, on the tenth day of August, from Saturday to Sunday, at the fourth hour of the night, a daughter, Tsarevna Anna, was born to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus' by the Tsaritsa and Grand Princess Anastasia, daughter of Roman Yuryevich.
