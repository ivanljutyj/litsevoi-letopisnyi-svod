Message from the right-believing Tsar and Great Sovereign
Ivan Vasilievich, Autocrat of All Rus, to the Most Holy
Patriarch Joachim of the Holy Mount Sinai and Judge of the Universe

“By the mercy of God, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus, of Vladimir, Moscow, Novgorod, Tsar of Kazan, Tsar of Astrakhan, Sovereign of Pskov, Grand Prince of Smolensk, Grand Prince of Tver, Yugra, Perm, Vyatka, Bulgaria, and possessor of the Livonian land, the city of Yuryev and others, Sovereign and Grand Prince of Novgorod of the Low Land, of Chernigov, Ryazan, Volotsk, Rzhev, Belsk, Rostov, Yaroslavl, Belozersk, Udora, Obdora, Kondia and others, and Lord of all the Siberian land and the northern country, to the honorable and most honorable father in God, shepherd and teacher of Orthodox precepts, and confessor of God’s majesty shown in the Holy Gospel: ‘If they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them,’ to the invincible passion-bearer, the most excellent warrior, adorned with crowns of good victory, the great standard-bearer, glorified by God in miracles, shining with the adornment of God’s word brighter than the sun, Joachim, great pope and patriarch of the great city of Alexandria, judge of the universe, I offer praise and extol your virtues. The writing of your virtues, sent to us together with the elders of Holy Mount Sinai, we received with joy and are filled by the words of the honey-flowing streams of your tongue; but we do not measure up to the praises you have bestowed upon our rule, for it is the One God who is glorious in miracles, for through Him all things came to be, and without Him nothing comes to be, for He does whatsoever He wills. We sing and extol His most great mercies shown to us and His bounties, for He is glorious and shall be glorified. We beseech Your Holiness that day and night we may be remembered in your prayers to our Lord Jesus Christ, glorified in the Trinity, who created all things, and to the Intercessor of our salvation, the Most Glorious Mother of God, and to all the saints; and through your prayers may we be granted eternal life, delivered from enemies visible and invisible, and may our kingdom be preserved from every evil; and may the Christian race everywhere be delivered from captivity to foreign godless peoples, and may the horn of the Orthodox be exalted and return to its original state and tranquility. May God grant us to see the honorable face of your brightness, so dear to us, and with zeal to kiss your hands as a precious gift, and permit us to delight in the teaching of your words more than honey and honeycomb, for we desire to hear your venerable voice as a thirsty deer desires to quench its thirst. Now, as a gift to God and for the comfort of Your Holiness, I have sent you, with Archdeacon Gennady of St Sophia in Novgorod and with my merchant Vaska Pozdyakov, furs to the value of 1,000 Hungarian gold pieces; and, according to your request, to the archbishop”
