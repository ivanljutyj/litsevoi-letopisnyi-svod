In the same month of January there came to the Tsar and Grand Prince from the King of Lithuania an envoy, Vasily Zagorovsky, with the message that the Tsar and Grand Prince’s voivodes were building towns on the King’s land, and had already built the towns of Usvyato, Ula, and Sokol, and that the Tsar and Grand Prince should not build towns in the King’s land. And under the agreement between the King’s envoys, Yuri Alexandrovich Khotkevich and his comrades, and the Tsar and Grand Prince’s boyars, Vasily Mikhailovich Yuryev and his comrades, the Tsar and Grand Prince’s envoys Fedor Ivanovich Kolychev Umnogo and his comrades did not come to Orsha for the Nativity of Christ and the Epiphany.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince wrote to King Sigismund in his charter with the Lithuanian envoy Vasily Zagorovsky that, by the decision of the Tsar and Grand Prince, the envoy, boyar and namestnik of Suzdal Fedor Ivanovich Umnogo Kolychev and his comrades had not come to Orsha because plague had begun in Smolensk; and when the plague in Smolensk should pass, then the Tsar and Grand Prince’s envoys would be there in Orsha. But towns the Tsar and Grand Prince ordered to be built in his patrimony, in the Polotsk povet, which God had given him, the sovereign.

In the same year, in April, on the 1st day, the Tsar and Grand Prince released the Nogai envoys of Tinakhmat, Ishtora, Bekchura, and Topas. And together with them the Tsar and Grand Prince sent to Prince Tinakhmat with his favor Fedor Matveev, son of Elizarov; to Urus-murza, Ivan Maltsov, son of Danilov; and to Tinaley-murza, son of Koshum, Stepan Shigin, son of Priklonsky; and with them, by stanitsa, serving Tatars; to Asanak-murza, son of Koshum, a stanitsa of serving Tatars, Yansuba Useinov and his comrades. And the Nogai envoys Ishtora and his comrades and Fedor Elizarov and his comrades set out from Moscow in spring by water, in the month of April.

On the same day the Tsar and Grand Prince released the Samarkand envoy Khozya-Khurt and his comrades, and the Bukhara King Yabdulya’s Khozya-Isup and his comrades, and with them sent charters to their sovereigns concerning trade.

On the same day the envoy of the Urgench King was released. But he was not received by the Tsar and Grand Prince, because the Urgench King had plundered the sovereign’s people, the under-clerk Akseny Ivanov and his comrades, and had taken the sovereign’s gifts; and the Tsar and Grand Prince ordered his officials to accept the charter from the Urgench envoy. And to the Urgench King he sent his royal charter, saying that he should search out the sovereign’s gifts and send them to the sovereign, and return all that had been plundered from the sovereign’s merchants. And then the sovereign would favor him as before, and command that the road be opened to his guests and merchants without hindrance, and that envoys might come.
