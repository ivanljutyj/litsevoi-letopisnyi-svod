On the Sending from the Field by Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Vishnevsky

In the same year, in the month of April, his voivode Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Vishnevsky sent from the Field to the tsar and grand prince fourteen Crimean captives and wrote that he had defeated the Crimeans on the Aidar near Azov. There were two hundred and fifty of them, and they had intended to go to wage war against the Kazan lands, and Prince Dmitry utterly defeated them, took twenty-six alive, and sent fourteen to the sovereign, while keeping twelve with him as guides; and they said that the tsarevich had come to the Crimea weary, having lost horses and men.

And also Mishka Cherkashenin, going to Prince Dmitry, defeated the Crimeans above the Seversky Donets and sent four Crimean captives to the sovereign, and those captives said the same.

On the Ambassadors to Urgench

On the 24th day of the same month the sovereign sent the ambassadors to Urgench to the tsar, and to Samarkand, and their people to the tsar in Bukhara.

On the Sovereign’s Sending of Roman Pivov to Lithuania

In the same year the sovereign sent Roman Pivov to Lithuania to King Sigismund Augustus for the examination of matters. And Roman was with the king and returned in the same year, and brought letters from the king to the sovereign. And in the letters the king wrote that he wished to send his man to the sovereign.

And Crimean Tatars Came to Pronsk

In the same year Crimean Tatars came to Pronsk, and Vasily Buturlin defeated them and sent sixteen captives to the sovereign.

On the Dispatch to the Sovereign from the Dnieper from Daniil

In the month of July there came to the tsar and grand prince from the voivodes from the sea, from Daniil Adashev, from Ignaty Zabolotsky and his companions, Prince Fyodor, son of Prince Ivan Khvorostinin, and Savva Tovarishchev with news, and they told the sovereign from the voivodes that, according to the sovereign’s instruction, they had gone by sea against the Crimean uluses. And when they came near Ochakov, there they took a ship and defeated Turks and Tatars, and took some other people with them as guides on the sea, and came to the island of Chula on the sea. And there, on the channels, they took another ship and took all those people with them as guides as well.

And they came to the Crimean uluses, to the island of Yarlagash, and there captured and slaughtered many camel herds.
