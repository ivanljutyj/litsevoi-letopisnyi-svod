The Grand Princess, however, kept Prince Vasily Romodanovsky, Prokopy Skurat, and Dmitry Peshek with her in Vilna; but Grand Prince Alexander soon sent them as well to Moscow, and began to compel Grand Princess Elena to accept the Roman faith.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' sent on this matter to Lithuania to his son-in-law Grand Prince Alexander his envoys, Boris Vasilyevich Kutuzov and the clerk Andrei Maik, so that he would not compel his daughter Grand Princess Elena to abandon the Greek faith for the Roman.

On the Stone Wall in Moscow

In the same year they laid the city stone wall in Moscow by the Neglinnaya River, not on the old foundation but adding to the city.

In the same year the Grand Prince ordered the churches and courtyards beyond the Moskva River opposite the city to be demolished, and ordered a garden to be planted in those places.

On the Dispatch of the Army against the Swedish Germans and on the War before the City of Vyborg.
In the year 7004 from Adam (the year 1504 from Christ)

Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' sent his commanders from Moscow with an army against the Germans, into the Swedish land, against the city of Vyborg: in the Grand Regiment was Prince Daniil Vasilyevich Shchenya, in the Advance Regiment Prince Pyotr Nikitich Obolensky, and in the right wing Pyotr and Vasily Borisovich.

And from Great Novgorod the Grand Prince sent his commanders against Vyborg: in the Grand Regiment Yakov Zakharyin, in the right wing Fyodor Konstantinovich Bezzubtsev, and in the left wing Prince Timofei Aleksandrovich Trostensky.

And from Pskov the Grand Prince sent Prince Vasily Fyodorovich Shuysky with the Pskovians to conquer German lands as well.

The Grand Prince's commanders came into the German land, and they conquered, burned, and seized the German land, but did not take the city of Vyborg.

At that same time, before the city of Vyborg, Ivan Andreyevich Subota Pleshcheyev was killed by an arquebus shot.

On the Elevation of Simon, Abbot of the Trinity, to the Metropolitanate

In the same year, on the 6th day of the month of September, by God's grace and the will of the sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus', and by the counsel of the holy fathers, archbishops and bishops, archimandrites, and the whole consecrated council, Simon was chosen and named to the most holy metropolitanate of all Rus'.
