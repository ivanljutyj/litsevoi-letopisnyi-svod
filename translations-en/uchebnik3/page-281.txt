On Lands

In the same year, with the blessing of Metropolitan Simon, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich took in Novgorod the Great the church lands, the archbishop's and the monasteries', under his authority and distributed them to boyar children as service estates.

The grand prince sent his commanders Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Belsky and others to Kazan in the year 7007 from Adam (1507 from Christ), in the month of March, after receiving news from the Kazan tsar Abdyl-Letif.

News came to the grand prince from the Kazan tsar Abdyl-Letif that Tsarevich Agalak, brother of Mamuk, was marching against him, and with him Urak, prince of the Kazan princes.

Hearing of this, the grand prince sent his commanders to aid Kazan: in the mounted host, in the great regiment, Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Belsky; in the vanguard regiment, Prince Semyon Romanovich Yaroslavsky; in the right hand, Yuri Zakharyich; in the left hand, Dmitry Vasilievich Shein; and in the river fleet host he sent his commanders Prince Ivan Alexandrovich Suzdalsky Barbasha, Mikhail Konstantinovich Bezzubtsev, and Andrei Vasilievich Saburov.

But Agalak and Urak, hearing that the grand prince's commanders were coming against them with an army, fled back to their own lands, and the grand prince's commanders returned to Moscow.

In the year 7008 from Adam (1508 from Christ)

In the month of September, Tatars, Horde and Azov Cossacks, came against Kozelsk and seized the Kozelsk village of Oleshnya; and Prince Ivan Peremyshlsky, the Odoevsky princes, and the sons of Pyotr Pleshcheev, Vasily and Ivan, overtook them, beat them, and took back the captives, and other Tatars they captured and brought to Moscow to the grand prince.
