And the relics of the appanage princes were laid out thus: by the western wall, in the corner, Prince Andrei Ivanovich, third son of Grand Prince Ivan Daniilovich; and beside him his son Prince Vladimir Andreevich Don; and from them along the front wall they laid Prince Georgy Dmitrievich, and with him in one coffin his children, two sons, Prince Vasily Yuryevich Kosoy and Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Krasny the Younger, and the gravestone slab over them was one; and from them along the same wall they laid the brothers of Prince Yuri, Prince Andrei Dmitrievich and his brother Prince Peter Dmitrievich, in separate coffins; and further they laid Prince Ivan Vasilievich the Elder, son of Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily Vasilievich the Dark; and further from him was laid Prince Semyon, son of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus; and next they laid Prince Georgy Vasilievich and his brothers, Prince Boris Vasilievich and Prince Andrei Vasilievich the Younger, brothers of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus, and they lie in separate coffins; and further they laid Prince Ivan Vladimirovich and Prince Yaroslav Vladimirovich, children of Prince Vladimir Don, and they lie in separate coffins. And on the left side by the northern doors, entering the church from the square to the right, they laid Prince Andrei Vasilievich the Elder of Uglich; and at his head is Prince Andrei Vladimirovich, third son of Prince Vladimir Andreevich; and at Prince Andrei’s head lies Prince Yuri Ivanovich of Dmitrov; and at Prince Yuri’s head lies his brother Prince Andrei Ivanovich Staritsky; and beside Prince Andrei Ivanovich Staritsky they laid his son Prince Vladimir Andreevich; and beside Prince Vladimir Andreevich they laid Prince Vasily Vladimirovich. And on the other side by the same doors, from the altar, before the cover, they laid Prince Vasily Yaroslavich. And entering the church by the great doors, on the left by the pillar lies Tsarevich Peter, son-in-law of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, son of Tsar Abreim; and beside him, at the same left pillar, they laid Alexander Safakireevich, Tsar of Kazan; and entering the church by the great doors, on the right by the southern doors they laid Prince Yuri Vasilievich, brother of the Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of All Rus.
