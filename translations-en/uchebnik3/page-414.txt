On the arrest of Prince Andrei Ivanovich.
In the year 7045 from Adam (the year 1545 from Christ)

By the devil’s action and through the incitement of wicked people, they stirred up discord for the Grand Prince: they began to slander Prince Andrei to the Grand Prince and to his mother Grand Princess, saying that Prince Andrei held anger against the Grand Prince and against his mother the Grand Princess because they had not added to his patrimony, and that therefore he wished to flee.

And to Prince Andrei they slandered the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess, saying that they wished to seize him.

And before that, in the year 7042 from Adam (the year 1542 from Christ), in January, after the death of the Grand Prince, without leaving Moscow for his patrimony, after the fortieth-day memorial, Prince Andrei petitioned Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother Grand Princess Elena, asking for more towns for his patrimony than were due by the blessing of his father and by the testament. And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and his mother Grand Princess Elena did not add towns to his patrimony.

And they honored him as had been done previously after the death of grand princes for their brothers, and they gave him even beyond that: they gave him fur coats and goblets, and ambling horses with saddles.

And Prince Andrei went to his own town of Staritsa, and began to hold anger against the Grand Prince and against his mother the Grand Princess because they had not added to his patrimony.

And wicked people joined in and slandered Prince Andrei to the Grand Prince, while to Prince Andrei they spoke against the Grand Prince, saying that the Grand Prince wished to seize him.

And Grand Prince Ivan and his mother Grand Princess Elena sent to Prince Andrei their boyar Prince Ivan Vasilievich Shuysky and their clerk Menshoy Putyatin, to persuade him that those words were untrue, and that in the mind of Grand Prince Ivan and of his mother Grand Princess Elena there was nothing evil.

And Prince Andrei came to Moscow without fear and was with the Grand Prince and with his mother the Grand Princess, and he petitioned the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess about this through his father Metropolitan Daniel, saying that a report had reached him that the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess wished to place their disgrace upon him.
