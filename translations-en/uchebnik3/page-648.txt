Concerning the Envoys from the Livonian Master

In the same month envoys came to the tsar and grand prince from the Livonian master and the bishop of Yuryev: Fedor Fershtirbertrekh, the master’s brother, Klaus, Simon, Melchir, and the bishop’s envoys — the Yuryev archimandrite Velva, and Ganus Ivan, Vlas Beka. And Klaus and the archimandrite died on the way.

And Fedor with his companions were with the tsar and sovereign and petitioned the sovereign to order their embassy to be heard. And the tsar and sovereign ordered his okolnichy Alexei Fedorovich Adashev and the clerk Ivan Mikhailov to hear their embassy. And the envoys petitioned that the land had been conquered, and they could not pay tribute because they had nothing, and asked that the sovereign show them mercy and not order tribute to be taken from them, and that in other tributes they would make amends hereafter. And the tsar and grand prince ordered this answer to be given to the envoys: the tsar and sovereign had pardoned them and had given a safe-conduct for the envoys. And after the safe-conduct the sovereign forbade war, but they from Rugodiv still shot at Ivangorod for two weeks and killed people; then the tsar and grand prince ordered shooting at Rugodiv and war with them. But God sent His mercy: the voivodes took Rugodiv, and the sovereign ordered military operations to be conducted against other towns as God would help them; and they cannot be trusted: in whatever they give their oath, in all of it they lie; and if the master wishes, let him himself, and the bishop too, petition for their offenses and impose tribute on all their land. And the sovereign dismissed the envoys without result.

Concerning the Sending of Voivodes to Syrensk

In the same month the sovereign ordered his voivodes Daniil Fedorovich Adashev, Pavel Petrovich Zabolotsky, Ivan Sharapov-Zamytsky, and the clerk Shestak Voronin to go from Rugodiv to Syrensk with the artillery train; and commanders came to Daniil and his companions from Novgorod with men: Boris Kolychev and Vasily Razladin Kvashnin. And they went from Rugodiv to Syrensk.

Concerning the Arrival at the Town of Syrensk

In the same month, on the 25th day of May, Daniil Fedorovich Adashev with his companions came to Skamya, five versts from Syrensk, on the second day of June, and according to the sovereign’s instruction sent word to the boyars in Pskov, and to Novgorod to Prince Fedor Troekurov, that he with men and cannon was located five versts from Syrensk, and that they should, according to the sovereign’s instruction, go to Syrensk.
