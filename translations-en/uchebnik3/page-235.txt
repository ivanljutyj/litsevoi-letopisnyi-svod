They took him to Jerusalem, and there he died. But Judas did not waver and did not say: “I am not a king, nor of royal lineage; how shall I oppose a king?” Instead he placed his hope and trust in God, and he defeated the king. He took him captive, ordered him executed, seized their land, and subjected them to the sons of Israel. And again, whenever the sons of Israel sinned before the Lord God, He delivered them into the hands of their enemies, and they were in slavery to them. But when they repented, He chose for them a ruler from their tribe—I speak of Othniel, Ehud, Deborah with Barak, and Gideon, who with three hundred warriors destroyed many thousands of Midianites, and Samson, who killed a thousand men with an ass’s jawbone. And God chose many other rulers for them and delivered them from slavery to foreigners, and the foreigners were in slavery to them. But now the same Lord exists, and if we repent with all our soul and renounce sin, the Lord will appoint you, our sovereign, over us, as in ancient times He appointed Moses and Joshua and many others who freed Israel.

The Lord will give us you as the deliverer of the new Israel, the Christian people, from this accursed new Pharaoh who exalts himself over us all, the impious Ahmad. But the Lord will bring down his boasting under your feet, and He will send you helpers, His angels and holy martyrs, and they will sweep them away, and they shall perish. And the prophets would say: “Draw the bow and prosper, and reign for truth, meekness, and righteousness; and thy right hand shall guide thee wondrously. Thy throne is established in righteousness, meekness, and true judgment; the Lord shall send thee the rod of strength from Zion, and thou shalt prevail in the midst of thine enemies.” Thus says the Lord: “I have raised thee up, a king of righteousness; I have called thee in righteousness and taken thee by the right hand and strengthened thee, that the nations may obey thee; and I will destroy the strength of kings. I will open doors before thee, and cities shall not be shut; I will go before thee and level the mountains, and I will shatter the gates of brass and break the bars of iron.” And then the Lord God will give unshakable and blameless royal power into your hands, O sovereign established by God, and to the sons of your sons from generation to generation and forever. So we too, by prayer from pure faith toward God, day and night in prayers, supplications, litias, and assemblies of hierarchs, by our divine oblations, will make the necessary and fitting commemoration of your pious dominion and your royal victory at the liturgy, that your enemies may be subdued under your feet and that you may prevail over them in battles. Let the impious lands that rush into battle be scattered, blinded by God’s lightning, and let them, like hungry dogs, lick the earth with their tongues, and let the angel of the Lord drive them away. We rejoice and are glad, hearing of the valor, strength, and victory of your son, given to him by God, and of the great courage and bravery of your brothers, our sovereigns, who have set out against the godless Agarines.
