On Starodub

On the 10th day of the same month, April, by the command of the great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of all Rus, and of his mother the great sovereign lady Elena, they began to build the town of Starodub of earth on the same old site; and it was completed that same summer on the 20th day of July.

That same spring the town of Starodub was built of earth on the same old site.

And at that time the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess sent their commanders Prince Ivan Vasilievich Goreinsky and their other commanders to wage war in the Lithuanian land against the town of Lyubech.

And the commanders of the great sovereign and his mother went into the Lithuanian land, fought against Vitebsk, burned the suburb, and ravaged many districts and villages, and took many people captive and much property from the Lithuanian people, and with God’s help came out of the Lithuanian land all safe and sound.

On Ustyug

On the 20th day of the same month, April, by the command of the great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and of his mother the great sovereign lady Elena, the town of Ustyug was built of wood, entirely new, and Danilo Zagryazhsky built it.

On the Yaroslavl Fire

In the same year, on the ninth day of the month of July, a Sunday, at the eighth hour of the day, in the town of Yaroslavl the church of St Nicholas the Wonderworker caught fire, and from that the whole town of Yaroslavl burned, and the wooden churches within the town, and the households burned down.

And the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother the Grand Princess Elena ordered that in the same month, on the same old site, on the burned ground, the town of Yaroslavl be built of wood; and it was completed that same summer. And that same summer the wooden town of Yaroslavl was set up on the old site, on the burned ground. In the same year they began to build an earthen town at Balakhna, and it was completed in the forty-fifth year on the 8th day of October.

In the same year Kazan Tatars came to the Kostroma lands; and the Grand Prince’s commander Prince Pyotr, son of Prince Vasily Zasekin Pestroy, stood at the outpost.

And he attacked the Kazan people without waiting for his own men, and the Tatars scattered them and killed Prince Pyotr himself and Menshik Polev.

And the Tatars heard of the Grand Prince’s great commanders with many men, and were frightened, and went away.
