And the Kazan people attacked the mounted host of the grand prince, and in more than one place; and by God’s mercy the grand prince’s commanders defeated them, crossed the Volga to the Kazan side, and joined with the ship commanders.

And on the tenth day of the month of July, a Sunday, having called God to their aid, they set out for the city of Kazan with many people.

And the Kazan tsar Safa-Girey with all the Kazan people, and to them came from the Nogais Mamai, the eldest son of a murza, with many people, and Prince Yaglych with many people, and the Astrakhan people came out of the city against them, and there was a great battle.

And by God’s mercy the grand prince’s commanders defeated the tsar and took their fort on the Bulak River, and killed many Kazan people, and took others alive.

Also in the fort they killed countless Tatars, their wives and children, and Cheremis; and they took others captive, and took cannons and arquebuses from them; and the tsar fled into the city with a few people.

And the grand prince’s commanders stood around the city and ordered the city to be fired upon with cannons and arquebuses.
