Prince Tinekhmat took the princely seat after his father, Prince Ismail; and to his brother Urus-murza, the nuradin, son of Ismail, [he sent] Vasily Petrov, son of Fedchishchev; to Asanak-murza, son of Koshum; to Tekhkovat — Ivan Elizarov, son of Mikhnov; to Tinbay-murza, son of Ismail — Roman Matveyev, son of Khvoshchinsky; to Tinaley-murza, son of Koshum — Ivan Vasilyev, son of Sherefedinov; and with them a company of service Tatars; and to the Circassians with a letter to Kazy-murza and his companions — the service Tatar Krym Taishev and his companions.

In the same month of May, on the 19th day, the monastery of St Nicholas the Old in Novgorod burned, and eight cells burned, and two wooden churches, and the tops of St Nicholas the Wonderworker burned.

In the same summer, in June on the 21st day, the tsar and grand prince sent to the Kabardian Circassians, to Prince Temryuk Aidarovich, Ivan Petrov, son of Novosiltsev, with his bounty.

In the same month of June, on the 27th day, Devlet-Girey, Tsar of the Crimea, sent to the tsar and grand prince his courier Karash-prince; and together with him sent their couriers the kalga Magmet-Girey tsarevich and Aldy-Girey tsarevich, and other tsareviches, and karacheis, and ulans, and princes, and murzas, thirty-one persons in all. And the tsar and grand prince was at that time at Mozhaysk.

And Afanasy Nagoy sent from the Crimea to the tsar and grand prince the service Tatar Yansuba with companions. And on arriving, Karash said that Tsar Devlet-Girey had sent with him to the tsar and grand prince a letter and gifts, an argamak and a boyar's son Voloshanin. And he said that to Ovech'i Vody had come the Kanev Cherkasy of the Lithuanian king and had routed them, and in that rout they took from him the tsar's letter and the presents, the argamak and Voloshanin.

And as for the oral message to the tsar and grand prince from Tsar Devlet-Girey, Karash said that Tsar Devlet-Girey had gone against the Wallachian voivode at the command of the Turkish sultan and had ravaged the Wallachian land and killed many people, and had installed Alexander the voivode in the town of Azbar and had subjected the whole Wallachian land to the sultan's will, and had come from Wallachia to the Crimea in good health with much booty and rejoiced at it; and in his joy he had sent to tell the tsar and grand prince his close man, Karash-prince Yausal.

And the kalga and Aldy-Girey and other tsareviches, and the mother of the tsar and tsaritsa, and the karacheis and ulans, and princes and murzas wrote to the tsar and grand prince that they were working for the good cause, that Tsar Devlet-Girey should be in firm friendship and love with the tsar and grand prince; and Devlet-Girey had agreed with the tsar and grand prince on firm friendship and love.

And the service Tatar Yansuba said that Afanasy Nagoy had sent letters with him to the tsar and grand prince, and those letters had been taken from him by the Kanev Cherkasy in the same rout when they routed Karash and his companions.
