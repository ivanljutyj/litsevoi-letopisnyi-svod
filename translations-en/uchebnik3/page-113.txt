Of all creation visible and invisible, who for us, ungrateful and evil-natured, came down from heaven and became incarnate, and shed His blood for us, look now, O Sovereign King, from Your holy dwelling upon Your humble servants and accept our sinful prayer, incline Your ear and hear our words, who are perishing utterly. We have sinned, O Lord, we have sinned before You by abominable and shameful deeds, having done every kind of vile thing to ourselves, to heaven and earth, and to this very temporal life, and are unworthy to look upon the height of Your glory. We have provoked Your grace and angered Your divinity, transgressing and trampling Your commandments and not obeying Your ordinances; but You Yourself, O King and Sovereign, lover of mankind, gentle, long-suffering and greatly merciful, said to Your prophets: “I do not desire the death of the sinner, but if he turns, he shall live”; and again: “I came here not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance”; for You do not wish, O Sovereign, to destroy the work of Your hands, You do not desire human perdition, but You wish that all be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth. So we also, unworthy, the creation and handiwork of Your divinity, let us not despair of our salvation, but hoping in Your countless mercy, we fall down and follow with all our heart and seek Your mercy. Spare, O Lord, spare those whom You redeemed with Your life-giving blood, and do not deliver us to the enemies and rivals of Your dominion, and deliver us from present troubles, and from the evils and misfortunes that have come upon us, vouchsafe us according to the multitude of Your mercy, and rescue us by Your wonders, and give glory to Your name, and let Your enemies be put to shame, and let every power be ashamed, and let their strength be crushed, and let them understand that You are our God, the Lord Jesus Christ, to the glory of God the Father.”

With such and many other supplicatory words praying every day, they hoped for their salvation. Also all the people came to the holy churches of God, weeping and wailing, and raising their hands to heaven, asking mercy of God. But as much as they had formerly been vouchsafed graces and gifts of God and benefits of the most pure Mother of God, so much now, because of our sins, were they deprived of the pardon of God’s bounties. For “When,” He said, “you stretch out your hands to Me, I will turn My eyes away from you; and if you come to show yourselves to Me, I will turn My eye away from you”; and again: “What you do, My soul hates.” We too are now worthy of such an answer because of our sins, and our supplications and prayers are unacceptable to God.

The Turks, as was said earlier, engaged the Greeks in battle every day without respite. And the accursed Mehmed, having gathered his soldiers, assigned them places for the attack: to Karabchei — against the Imperial Palace and the Wooden Gate, and to Kalikharia; and to the beylerbeys: to the eastern one — against Pigia and the Golden Place, and to the western one — against all of Korsun.
