“The mighty one has girded himself with strength; the Lord puts to death and gives life, and gives strength to our princes, and exalts the horn of His Christ; and again the Lord is near to all who call upon Him in truth; and truly He will not delight in the strength of a horse, nor take pleasure in the legs of a man; the Lord takes pleasure in those who fear Him and hope in His mercy.” Listen to what Democritus, the most ancient of philosophers, says: “A prince ought to reason soberly about all that happens, and against his adversaries have strength, courage, and bravery, and toward his retinue have love and sweet greeting.” Remember what was said by the unlying lips of the Lord our God Jesus Christ: “If a man gains the whole world and harms his soul, what will he give in ransom for his soul?” And again: “Blessed is the man who lays down his life for his friends.” And now, as we hear, the godless tribe of the Agarenes has already drawn near to our land, to your fatherland. Already they have seized many lands neighboring us and are moving against us. Go out therefore more swiftly to meet them, calling God to your aid and the Most Pure Mother of God, the Helper and Protectress of our Christianity, and all His saints. Follow the example of your forefathers who ruled before you, the great princes, who not only defended the Russian land from the impious but also subdued other countries to themselves; I mean Igor, Svyatoslav, and Vladimir, who took tribute from the Greek emperors, and also Vladimir Monomakh, who fought so much against the accursed Cumans for the Russian land, and many others, of whom you know better than we do. And the praiseworthy great prince Dmitry, your great-grandfather, what courage and bravery he showed beyond the Don over those same accursed barbarians—he himself fought ahead of all and did not spare his life for the deliverance of Christians. And the Merciful and Lover of Mankind God saw his firm intention, that he wished to suffer not only to blood but even to death for the faith of Christ and for the holy churches and, as a true pastor, for the rational flock of Christ’s sheep entrusted to him by God, to become like the ancient martyrs. For the holy martyrs, for the love of God, went to sufferings and wounds as to a banquet. So this God-loving and steadfast man counted death as gain. He did not doubt, did not fear the Tatar multitude, did not turn back, and did not say in his heart: “I have a wife and children and much wealth; if they seize my land, I will settle somewhere else.” But without hesitation he rushed to the feat and rode forward, face to face against the accursed rational wolf Mamai, to snatch from his jaws the rational flock of Christ’s sheep. Therefore, for his boldness, the Merciful God did not delay to give him help, did not remember his former sins, but quickly sent His help—angels and holy martyrs—to help him fight against his enemies. Therefore he who undertook the feat for the Lord is to this day deemed worthy of praise and glory not only from people but also from God.
