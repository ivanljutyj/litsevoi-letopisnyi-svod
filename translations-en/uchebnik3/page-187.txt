And the Grand Prince, for the sake of his pilgrim the archbishop and at the petition of his patrimony Great Novgorod, pardoned those guilty people, remitted the executions, and ordered the bailiffs to recover from them the plaintiffs’ losses, one thousand five hundred rubles, and for their guilt before him he ordered them to be taken from each separately according to the charter; and the bailiffs took all of it from them. And he judged many other boyars, and investigated, and showed favor to and defended the wronged against the powerful.

On December 6, Wednesday, St Nicholas’s Day, the Grand Prince dined and drank at Prince Vasily Shuysky’s; gifts: three bolts of Flemish cloth, three bolts of silk fabric, thirty gold ship-coins, two gyrfalcons, and a falcon.

On December 10 the posadniks were brought to Moscow: Vasily Ananyin, Bogdan, Fedor, Ivan Loshinsky, and Ivan with his son Alfery.

On December 14, Thursday, the Grand Prince feasted at the archbishop’s; gifts: 150 gold ship-coins, five bolts of Flemish cloth; a stallion, and for the send-off a barrel of wine and two barrels of mead.

On December 15 there was a feast at Kazimir’s; gifts: a gold ladle, two grivnas, one hundred gold ship-coins, two gyrfalcons.

On December 17 there was a feast at Zakhary Grigoriev’s, and gifts to the Grand Prince: 20 gold ship-coins and 5 bolts of Flemish cloth, and from his son Ivan ten gold ship-coins, a bolt of Flemish cloth, and two walrus tusks.

On December 19, in honor of the Grand Prince, there was a feast at the senior posadnik Vasily Esipov’s; gifts: 30 gold ship-coins, three bolts of red-yellow Flemish cloth.

On December 21, Thursday, there was a feast in honor of the Grand Prince at Yakov Korob’s; gifts: two hundred gold coins, a gyrfalcon, and a walrus tusk; and from his son Ivan a gyrfalcon, a bolt of red-yellow Flemish cloth, and a walrus tusk.

On December 23, Saturday, there was a feast in honor of the Grand Prince at Luka Fedorov’s; gifts: 20 gold ship-coins, three bolts of Flemish cloth, two walrus tusks.

On December 25, the Nativity of Christ, there was a feast at the Grand Prince’s; the archbishop was there, and Prince Vasily Shuysky, and the posadniks, and all the thousand-commanders, and wealthy townsmen, and many merchants; and he drank with them, and for a long time, until evening.

On December 28, Thursday, there was a feast in honor of the Grand Prince at Gorodishche at Nastasya’s, wife of Ivan Grigoriev, and at her son Yuri’s; gifts: 30 gold ship-coins, 10 bolts of Flemish cloth, two forties of sables, two walrus tusks.
