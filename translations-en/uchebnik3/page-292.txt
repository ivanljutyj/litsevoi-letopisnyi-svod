They also decided that priests and deacons should not take payment for ordination or for church places, according to the rules of the holy fathers; and they wrote a confirmed charter on this, and set their hands to it, and affixed seals.

In the same year there came the envoy Alliar from Tsar Shiakhmat, and the envoy Khozya from his brother Khozya-Saltan, and from Bogatyr-Saltan the envoy Kem-Urus.

On the illness of the Grand Prince

In that same summer, on the 28th day of the month of July, on the commemoration of the holy apostles Prochorus and Nicanor, Timon and Parmenas, the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus began to be ill; for whom God loves, He chastens.

On the consecration of the Church of the Archangel Michael.
In the year 7012 from Adam (1512 from Christ)

On September 6 the church of the holy and great Archistrategus Michael, in honor of his honorable miracle at Chonae, was consecrated in Moscow by the most reverend Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, and Gennadius, Archbishop of Novgorod, and bishops.

On the envoy

In the same month of September, on the 20th day, the Grand Prince sent Ivan Oshcherin to Crimea.

On the journey of the Grand Prince

In the same month of September, on the 21st day, a Thursday, the Grand Prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilievich, with his son Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich and with his children, set out from Moscow; and that autumn he was at the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery, and from there he went to Pereslavl, Rostov, and Yaroslavl, and returned to Moscow that same autumn on November 9, a Thursday.

On the death of Princess Ulyana, and on the wedding of Prince Fyodor Borisovich, and on the death of Prince Ivan Borisovich

In that same autumn, in November, Princess Ulyana, wife of Prince Boris Vasilievich, reposed; and then Prince Fyodor Borisovich married after her death, and his younger brother Prince Ivan Borisovich fell ill at his wedding and reposed, and he was laid in the Church of the Most Pure in the Joseph Monastery.

In the same year, on the day of the Ascension, by command of the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus, Archimandrite Mitrofan laid the foundation of a brick refectory in the Andronikov Monastery.

In the same year grilles were set up along the streets in Moscow.
