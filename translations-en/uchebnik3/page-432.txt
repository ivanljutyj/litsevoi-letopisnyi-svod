And at that time the Grand Prince’s men came out of the town against them, and they killed many Lithuanian men and took their banners and drums; and the Lithuanians withdrew to their own lands in great shame.

And the Grand Prince and his mother, the Grand Princess, ordered that for this reason a church of the Life-Giving Trinity be built in that town, with three side chapels in it: the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, the Protection of the Mother of God, and Sergius the Wonderworker.

In the same spring the town of Temnikov was moved to another place, on the Moksha River.

In the same spring the town of Starodub was built of earth, on its old site.

And at that time the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess sent their commanders, Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Gorensky and other commanders, to wage war on the Lithuanian land, against the town of Lyubech. And the Grand Prince’s commanders went and waged war on the Lithuanian land; and they returned safe to their own lands.

In the same spring the town of Ustyug was built of wood. In the same summer, in July on the 18th day, the town of Yaroslavl burned from the church of St Nicholas. And it was rebuilt in the same summer. In the same year they began to build an earthen town at Balakhna, and it was completed in the 45th year, in October on the 8th day.

In the same year many Kazan Tatars came against the Kostroma lands. And the Grand Prince’s commander Prince Pyotr Zasekin-Pestroy, son of Prince Vasily, was stationed at an outpost.

And he attacked the Kazan people without waiting for his own men; and the Tatars routed them and killed Prince Pyotr himself and Menshik Polev.

And the Tatars heard that the Grand Prince’s great commanders were coming with many men; and they were frightened and went away.

In the same summer, in August on the 3rd day, on Thursday, at the third hour of the day, Prince Yuri Ivanovich, brother of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, died in prison; and he was laid in the Church of the Archangel Michael in Moscow.

In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, a town was founded on the Prona.

In the year 7045 from Adam (the year 1545 from Christ)

Through the devil’s action and the incitement of wicked men, confusion arose for the Grand Prince: they began to slander Prince Andrei to the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess, saying that Prince Andrei bore anger against the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess because they had not added to his patrimony, and that he intended to flee.

And to Prince Andrei they reported against the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess, that they wanted to seize him.
