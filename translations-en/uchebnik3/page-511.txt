On How the Tsar Mounted His Horse

And the sovereign mounts his horse and sets forth, guided by God, and the sovereign set out for his village of Kolomenskoye: there he dined. And during the meal the sovereign showed favor to all who were with him.

And he went to his village of Ostrov; there he spent the night. And news came to the sovereign during his journey: a scout, Ivashko Strelnik, galloped from Putivl from Aydar Volzhin and said that many Crimean men were marching toward the sovereign’s borderlands—it was unknown whether it was the tsar or the tsarevich—and had already crossed the Seversky Donets.

The sovereign, without hesitating in the least, goes to Kolomna and takes with him Prince Vladimir Andreevich, though from Ostrov Prince Vladimir had been supposed to return. And the sovereign comforts all with words of hope: “Let us place our hope in God, and also in our own righteousness; before the Crimean tsar I am guilty of nothing; he, roaring, wishes to swallow up Christianity, but the Merciful God will do what He Himself wills.”

On the Sovereign’s Arrival in Kolomna

And the sovereign came to Kolomna on the 19th day of the same month, a Sunday, and there Aydar Volzhin arrived and said that many Crimean men were coming, and they were expected at Ryazan and Kolomna, while others were passing through the sovereign’s borderlands; but they did not know whether it was the tsar or the tsarevich.

And the sovereign ordered Tsar Shigalei to be with him, and the sovereign sends regiments to the bank: he ordered the great regiment to stand at Kolychevo, the vanguard at Rostislavl, and the left wing at Golutvin; and as soon as the Crimean tsar should arrive, the sovereign resolved to engage him in direct battle.

He sends Tsar Shigalei by boat to Gorodok, since the tsar was a man of large body and could not ride quickly on horses; the tsar was very prudent, but not brave in battles and not accommodating to his retinue.

The sovereign was hastened by messengers: the scout Vaska Aleksandrov arrived and said that many men were approaching Ryazan, and many others were passing through the borderlands.
