On the Tsar’s Journey to the Trinity and to Pereyaslavl

In the year 7065 from Adam (the year 1564 from Christ), on the 10th day of September, the tsar and grand prince set out with his tsaritsa and grand princess Anastasia and with his son Tsarevich Ivan Ivanovich, and with him also his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, to the Trinity and to the wonderworker Sergius. And the sovereigns celebrated the Exaltation of the Honorable Cross in the monastery at the Trinity, and having sufficiently satisfied the brethren, they went from there to Pereyaslavl to pray to the great wonderworker Nikita. And in the monastery of the wonderworker Nikita the sovereigns celebrated with warm faith and great zeal, and gave the monastery many lands for the sustenance of the brethren, and commanded the hegumen to establish a communal rule, since previously this had been a monastery with separate cells, and gave large sums of money for gathering the brethren, so as to build a great monastery in honor of God and His saint the wonderworker Nikita. And from there the sovereigns came to the Life-Giving Trinity for the commemoration of the wonderworker Sergius, and celebrated here according to the royal custom with great piety and devotion, and provided sufficiently for the brethren, and bestowed alms from their royal hands upon all the brethren; likewise the tsar himself gave sufficient alms with his own hands in hospitals and in almshouses. And the sovereigns arrived in Moscow in the same month on the 29th day.

On the Arrival to the Sovereign of Messengers from Ivan Cheremisinov

In the same month, to the Trinity, there came to the tsar and grand prince from Astrakhan, from Ivan Cheremisinov and Mikhail Kolupaev, and from the flotilla commanders, from Fyodor Pisemsky and from Timokha Teterin, the boyar’s son Ontonko Potulov with his comrades and the Cossack ataman Arkhipko, and the Vyatka man Shelom.
