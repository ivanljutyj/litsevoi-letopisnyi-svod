He was then still a very little child, only three years and three months old from birth. And the great sovereign Vasily leaned somewhat upon his bed, and shedding tears from his eyes like a stream, he took from himself the cross of Peter the Wonderworker and placed it against the cross of his son; and he began to bless his son with the cross, and having blessed him, he laid that cross upon him. And he said to him: “May the mercy of God and of the Most Pure Mother of God and the blessing of Peter the Wonderworker be upon you! As Peter the Wonderworker blessed with this Holy and Life-giving Cross our forefather the great prince Ivan Danilovich and all our lineage, and all who came from his loins, the great princes of Vladimir and all Rus, even until now and unto us—with the same blessing I also bless you, my eldest son, with this Holy Cross! May this Holy Cross be to you for the driving out of our enemies and assailants; for many of other faiths attempt against Orthodoxy, against our realm, entrusted to us by God, wishing to destroy it, but they could not overcome the power of the Cross; for we, the faithful, have as protection the precious Cross and the prayers of the saints; and may the blessing of Peter the Wonderworker be upon you, and upon your children, and upon your grandchildren from generation to generation; and may my blessing be upon you, and upon your children, and upon your grandchildren from generation to generation.

Moreover, I bless you with the blessing of our forefather the great prince Vladimir Monomakh, with the precious Cross from the very Life-giving Tree on which, for our salvation, Christ our God endured voluntary Crucifixion; for that cross was sent from Tsargrad by Tsar Constantine Monomakh to the great prince Vladimir Monomakh; may the invincible power of this precious and Life-giving Cross be to you a firm weapon and everywhere a bright and most glorious victory over all enemies visible and invisible! To this also receive the royal crown of Monomakh, and the diadem, and the scepter, and the rest of the royal regalia of Monomakh, with which Tsar Monomakh was crowned, and with which we, the great princes, are crowned to the great autocracy of the Russian kingdom. Receive then this blessing of the royal inheritance, O my dear and long-desired child! Although you are still very young, yet by the grace of almighty God entering into age, reign autocratically in place of me, your father, piously governing with the scepter of the Cross-bearing banner of the whole Russian kingdom, our eternal fatherland. And may you be blessed according to the prophecy of the great hierarch and wonderworker Peter, metropolitan of all Rus, with a good blessing from above from the all-holding right hand of the merciful Lord, lover of mankind God, whose ineffable grace and exceedingly great mercy, and also of the Most Pure Mother of God, always inseparable, may be with you! And the help and protection of the archangels, and the God-pleasing prayers of the holy great Russian wonderworkers and all the saints, and parental blessing, and prayer may speed you everywhere in every good work.
