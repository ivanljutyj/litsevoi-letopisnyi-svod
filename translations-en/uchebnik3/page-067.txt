Of Saint Ignatius

If anyone is found to fast on Saturday or Sunday, except the Saturday of the Lord’s Burial, he is a Christ-killer.

Rule 42 of the Holy Council of Laodicea

It is not fitting during holy Great Lent to marry or to celebrate birthdays.

A Brief Account of the Latins, How They Fell Away from the Orthodox Patriarchs and Were Deposed from Their Primacy, from the Memorial Books, and of the Iconoclast Emperors, Heretics, Who Reigned in Constantinople

In the reign in Constantinople of Leo the Isaurian, who by God’s permission held the imperial scepter, he began to spread the iconoclast heresy and had great audacity. After he himself ended his life in torment, his son Constantine Copronymus ruled, and he was a patron of the heresy. And he died foully.

And after him his son Leo the Khazar was raised to the throne. And he died, and after him his wife Empress Irene and his son Constantine became heirs to the kingdom.

They, instructed by Tarasius, Patriarch of Constantinople, convened the Seventh Council in the year 6305 from Adam, and in the year 805 from the Incarnation of the Lord, in Nicaea of Bithynia, in the presence of 367 sacred men, proposing to elect as presiding heads of the sacred and great host: Tarasius, most renowned of God’s hierarchs—this divine and excellent man, and sacred helmsman of the reigning city; who else besides him is worthy to head the great holy Roman Church? And another priest, Peter, and the abbot of the monastery of Saint Sabas located there, who represented the apostolic throne of the Roman Church, Pope Adrian, then adorned with hierarchical dignity; together with them John and Thomas, men known for their monastic life, shining with priestly honor, and representatives of the apostolic and great thrones of all the eastern regions and possessing episcopal righteousness.

Then Emperor Constantine, son of Leo, grandson of Constantine Copronymus, with his mother Irene, wife of Emperor Leo, at the Seventh Council with the holy God-bearing fathers deposed and uprooted the iconoclast heretics, and again the Church received the holy icons and was adorned with her beauty.
