And if the Rugodivians did not lie, the tsar and grand prince ordered Aleksei and Daniil to be in Rugodiv; but if they lied to them, the sovereign ordered them to attend to his territorial affair as much as the Merciful God would help. And on the first day of May Yakim and Zakhar came to Moscow, and the tsar and grand prince ordered his okolnik Aleksei Fedorovich Adashev and his clerk Ivan Mikhailov to receive the Rugodivian posadniks Yakim and Zakhar and to ascertain their petition: what and what sort of sovereign’s favor they desired? And Yakim and Zakhar first petitioned that they should not abandon the Master, but that the sovereign would grant them favor according to his will, and they petitioned the sovereign.
