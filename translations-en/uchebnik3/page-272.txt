On the War with the Swedish Germans

In that same winter, on January 17, there was a second dispatch of troops against the Swedish and Kayan Germans; the Great Prince sent his commanders from Novgorod: in the large regiment — Commander Prince Vasily Kosoy and Andrey Fedorovich Chelyadnin; in the forward regiment — Prince Alexander Vladimirovich Rostovsky and Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Repnya; in the right wing — Dmitry Vasilyevich Shein and Grigory Fedorovich Davydovich; in the left wing — Semyon Karpovich and Andrey Ivanovich Korobkov. The commanders came against the Germans, into the Swedish realm, the Gamskaya land; they went as far as the Gametsky town and laid waste the German land, burned and cut down, and took many captives, and beat their outpost. And on March 6 they came to the Great Prince in Novgorod, all safe, with a great number of captives.

On the Great Prince’s Arrival from Novgorod to Moscow, on the Great Frosts and the Great Flood

In that same winter the Great Prince set out from Novgorod on March 10 and came to Moscow on March 24, on Thursday.

That winter was very harsh; the frosts were severe, and there was much snow; and in spring in Moscow and everywhere there was a very great flood; such a flood had not been remembered for many years.

On the Kayans

In that same spring the Great Prince sent Prince Ivan Fedorovich and his brother Prince Pyotr Ushasty to the Kayan land, against ten rivers; with them was an army: Ustyuzhans, Dvinians, Onezhans, and Vazhans; and these are the names of the rivers they conquered, there were eight: Kem, Torma, Kolokol, Ovluy, Sigovaya, Snezhnya, Gavka, and Putash. And those who live along the Limeng River petitioned the Great Prince and came with the commanders to Moscow; and the Great Prince, having shown them favor, released them.

On the Arrival of Shiban Tsar Mamuk at Kazan

In that same spring, in May, news came to the Great Prince Ivan Vasilyevich from Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin that Shiban Tsar Mamuk was coming against him with a great force, and that the Kazan princes Kalimet, Urak, Sadyr, and Agash were committing treason.
