On the Appointment of a Bishop in Kolomna

In the same autumn, on November 18, Thursday, Avraamy, abbot of Ugresh, was installed as bishop in Kolomna by the most reverend Zosima, Metropolitan of All Rus, and by the archbishop and bishops; but Gennady, Archbishop of Novgorod, did not send his letter for his installation.

On the Sign

In the same autumn, on November 8, at the fourth hour of the night from Monday to Tuesday, a sign was revealed in Great Novgorod: from the summer east to the summer west, on the northern side between east and west, the sky became bright, like a bright, clear evening dawn, and in that dawn there appeared pillars like sunbeams, great and bright; and the rays illumined the whole city and the suburban fields like the light of a great fire; and the pillars came together and parted, and there was a coming together and parting of those pillars repeatedly during that night, for about three hours or a little more, and they disappeared; and after this three great and very high pillars appeared and stood for a long time, for thirteen hours; and when it was already time for matins, clouds came from the east and darkened that brightness, and then after an hour the usual morning dawn and light rose; and from Yuryev Monastery they saw what seemed like the glow of a great fire and thought that the whole city was burning.

On the Arrival of Envoys from Wallachia

In the same winter, on January 1, Ivan Mitkov, son of Likharev, came from Wallachia, and with him came Stetsko, envoy of the Wallachian voivode Stephen, and they came from Wallachia by the Field.

On the Sign

In the same month, on January 9, at the second hour of the night from Sunday to Monday, a star with a tail appeared in the heavens; its tail stretched eastward for two sazhens, and it remained in the sky until the sixth hour of the night.

On the Envoy

In the same winter, on February 15, the envoy Varmtsar came from Master Simeon.

In the same spring, in March, Peter Antony Fryazin laid the foundations of two towers: one at the Frolovsky Gate and the other at the Nikolsky Gate; and he laid the Nikolsky Tower not on the old foundation, and built the wall as far as the Neglinnaya.

On the Ore

In the same spring, on March 26, the Grand Prince sent the Germans Ivan and Viktor to Pechera to search for silver ore, and with them he sent Andrey Petrov and Vasily Ivanov, son of Boltin.
