To Macarius, the okolnik Andrei Alexandrovich Kvashnin; and Metropolitan Macarius and Bishop Savva of Krutitsy with his council came to Vladimir, and the Metropolitan blessed Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich for the land’s cause against the perjured Kazanians.

The Metropolitan instructs and blesses the boyars and voivodes, the princes and all the men of the Tsar’s host, and said to them: “Lords and my children, for God’s sake hear our humble words! The Tsar and Grand Prince, having called to his aid God and the Most Pure Mother of God and the holy great wonderworkers, is going on his land’s cause to Kazan, and he must guard his land’s cause as much as the Merciful God and the Most Pure Mother of God help him; and you, lords and my children, would serve the Tsar and sovereign with the heartfelt desire of all your soul, for the holy churches, for Orthodox Christianity; and do not strive against one another with pride, but with Christ’s love for Christ’s flock, so as to be crowned with martyrs’ crowns by God the Rewarder and to receive honor from the earthly Tsar; for the proud man only removes himself from God and men. And the sovereign wishes to reward you for your service and to protect you for your fatherland, and you would serve as much as God helps you, and let there be no discord or vengeance among you, but unite in unfeigned love against the enemies and stand courageously; and if anyone is not pleased to be with another because of lineage in battle against enemies, then forget this and help accomplish the sovereign’s land’s cause, looking upon one another not in wrath but in love; and when you return from the sovereign’s land’s cause, then if any of you wishes to reckon with another about lineage, the sovereign will allow you to reckon. And the blessing of our humility be upon you.”

On the 20th day of the month of December, the Tsar and Grand Prince sent with the army to Nizhny Novgorod the boyar Vasily Mikhailovich Yuryev and the okolnik Feodor Mikhailovich Nagoy; and he ordered Shigalei and Tsarevich Ediger, and the voivodes who were gathering with men in the towns, to be in Nizhny Novgorod in the month of January.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince himself set out from Vladimir on the 7th day of January, on Tuesday, and the Tsar and Grand Prince came to Nizhny Novgorod on the 18th day of January, on Thursday; and from Nizhny Novgorod the Tsar and Grand Prince set out on the 23rd day of January, on Thursday, and before him went the armed regiments in order. And with the Tsar and Grand Prince went Tsar Shigalei and the Tsarevich, and the boyars, and Prince Ivan Fedorovich Mstislavsky, and Prince Petr Mikhailovich Shchenyatev, and Prince Semyon Ivanovich Mikulinsky, the remaining princes and courtiers of the Tsar’s court.
