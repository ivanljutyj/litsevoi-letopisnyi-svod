On the Arrival from Lithuania of the Metropolitan’s Clerk Savluk

In the same month of November, the metropolitan’s clerk Savluk Turpeev came from the lords of Lithuania and brought the metropolitan a letter from the voivode of Vilna, and Bishop Paul had died among them; and it was written in the letter that the king was sending his great ambassadors to the tsar and grand prince, and they would be in Moscow by the Nativity of Christ or by the Epiphany.

On the Arrival from Astrakhan

In the same month of November there came from Astrakhan the streltsy head Grigory Kaftyrev, and the ambassador Peter Turgenev, and the ambassador of Tsar Derbysh, Temir. And Grigory and Peter told the tsar and grand prince that his ambassador Leonty Mansurov had come to Astrakhan in good health with the tsaritsas. But Tsar Derbysh was disloyal before the tsar and grand prince: he was in correspondence with the Crimea and had transported to himself the murzas expelled from the Nogais by Prince Ishmael, and had united with them, so that he might stand together against Ishmael with Yusup’s children.

On the Arrival of Ambassadors from the Nogais

In the same month ambassadors came from the Nogais to the tsar and grand prince: from Prince Ishmael his ambassador Yelbolda, and from Roslan-murza, Kulchan, and from Beim-murza, son of Shikh-Mamai, with his brothers, Devlet-Khozya, and from many other murzas; also the service Tatars Devlet-Khozya Useenov and Baikesh, and they went to Kazan; and ambassadors from them to Kazan, to the Kazan namestnik the boyar Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky, Baiterek and his companions, also came; and to the town of Sviyazhsk, to the namestnik the boyar Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany, ambassadors also came. And Prince Ishmael and all the murzas instructed them to petition the tsar and grand prince and to complain of Tsar Derbysh, that he was disloyal before the tsar and grand prince and had brought them into great need, and that their sovereign should defend them from Derbysh and place his own people in Astrakhan, just as in Kazan. And the prince and all the murzas sent an oath letter with the service Tatars, with Devlet-Khozya and Baikesh. Before them Prince Ishmael and all the Nogai murzas swore to the tsar and grand prince that wherever the tsar and grand prince should send them, they would go there everywhere; that the friend of the tsar and grand prince would be their friend, and the foe their foe, and that against all foes they would stand together as one.

On the Sending of Governors against the Swedish King

In the same year, in the month of December, the tsar and grand prince sent his governors against the Swedish King Gustav for his injustice: in the large regiment, the boyar and governor Prince Peter Mikhailovich Shchenyatev and the boyar and governor Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Paletsky.
