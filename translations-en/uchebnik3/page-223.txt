The relics of Metropolitan Theognostus were placed in the Church of the Holy Apostle Peter above the platform, built around with brick, against the same wall as Peter the Wonderworker, where they had lain before.

Then the relics of Prince Yuri Danilovich were brought in a wooden coffin and laid in the Church of Saint Demetrius in the side-chapel, in the ground, level with the platform, and a tombstone was set over him.

Then the metropolitan with the censer, and the hierarchs, and all the priests with him, and the Grand Prince with his son and with all those previously named came to the coffin of the Most Reverend Metropolitan Philip; for he lay apart from the new church on the left side, having been laid in that church, and having opened the coffin, they saw him lying whole in body, just as before the Most Reverend Metropolitan Jonah, and his vestments had not decayed at all, though 6 years and 5 months less 8 days had already passed since his death; and seeing this, they glorified God, who glorifies His saints.

And they took him in a stone coffin, and brought him into the church, and placed him on the side where Metropolitan Jonah had been laid.

And then they began Vespers, and likewise a memorial service for all those metropolitans and for Grand Prince Yuri.

And in the morning, on the 28th day, on Saturday, the metropolitan with the bishops and archimandrites, archpriests, abbots, and priests served the liturgy in the same church; likewise in all cathedral churches and in monasteries, and in all churches in the city and outside the city.

And at all of these they gave alms from the Grand Prince, and food to monks and nuns and all the poor.

And the metropolitan, with the bishops and the aforementioned priests who had served with him, and with all the cathedral clergy, dined at the Grand Prince's; and the Grand Prince himself stood before them with his son.

Then the Grand Prince said to his father Metropolitan Gerontius: "Consider, father, with the bishops and the other priests how to cover Metropolitan Philip with a tomb, or how it would be fitting to arrange it." And so he lay uncovered for 12 days, and on the 13th day they built a brick tomb over him.

On the Grand Prince's peaceful campaign to Novgorod.
In the year 6988 from Adam (1488 from Christ)
