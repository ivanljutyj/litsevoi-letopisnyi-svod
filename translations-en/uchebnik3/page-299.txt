The tsarevich and the Grand Prince’s voivode Fedor Mikhailov, son of Kiselev, set out through the Field for Murom. Tsar Magamet-Amin sent a pursuit after the tsarevich and Fedor, and they overtook them forty versts from the Sura. The tsarevich and Fedor joined battle with the Tatars together with the boyars’ men, and with God’s help they defeated the Kazan Tatars, took some captive, and themselves returned safe with all their men.

On the Church of the Wonderworker Nicholas Lnyanoy

In the same year, on June 21, by command of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus, in the city of Moscow they laid the foundation of a brick church of the holy and great wonderworker Nicholas on the place where the old wooden church of Nicholas Lnyanoy had formerly stood—it had been so named from the beginning—and they completed it in the same year in nine weeks.

On the Crimean Envoys

In the same year, on August 1, envoys from the Crimean Tsar Mengli-Giray came to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus: Prince Kazimir-Kiyat and Prince Makhmetsha, together with Vasily Naumov, and they brought oath charters from Tsar Mengli-Giray to the Grand Prince concerning friendship and brotherhood, but not such as the Grand Prince needed.

And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus ordered Prince Kazimir and his companions to write an oath charter such as had been signed with his father, Grand Prince Ivan. Kazimir and his companions wrote such a charter as the Grand Prince needed; they set their hands to that charter and affixed their seals, and they gave an oath to the Grand Prince on this, that they would obtain the same sworn charters from Tsar Mengli-Giray.

On the Death of Alexander, Grand Prince of Lithuania

In the same year, in August, news came to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus that his son-in-law Alexander Andreevich, King of Poland and Grand Prince of Lithuania, had died in Vilna; and the Grand Prince sent Ivan Kobyak to visit his sister Elena.

And his younger brother Sigismund Andreevich took the Kingdom of Poland and the Grand Principality of Lithuania.
