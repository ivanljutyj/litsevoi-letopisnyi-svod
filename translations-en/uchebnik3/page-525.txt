On the Campaign of the Voivodes against the Cheremis

Ivan Voksherin came to the sovereign from Sviyaga, from the voivode boyar and voivode Prince Alexander Borisovich Gorbaty and his companions, and reported to the sovereign that the boyars had campaigned against the mountain traitors: in the great regiment was boyar Prince Semyon Ivanovich Mikulinsky; in the advanced regiment were boyar and court steward Daniil Romanovich and Boris Saltykov; in the guard regiment was boyar Prince Pyotr Semyonovich Serebryany; and they ravaged the mountain villages. The voivodes’ ertouls fought with the mountain people, and men fell on both sides, and they routed the mountain people; in that battle they killed Prince Alexander Dmitriev, son of Zhizhemsky. And all the mountain people, down the Sviyaga River and along the Volga, made submission to the voivodes, swore an oath to the sovereign, and went to the town of Sviyazhsk with their wives and children.

On the Organization of the Regiments

The sovereign gave praise to God that without great bloodshed God had granted victory, and being moved to tenderness, he commanded in his own regiment his voivodes-boyars Prince Vladimir Ivanovich and Ivan Vasilyevich to appoint in his tsar’s regiment, in every hundred, a commander over the military sons of boyars from among the sons of great fathers, excellent young men skilled in military affairs, for the great work of opposing the enemy and for various missions. And every son of a boyar must know his commander, and when it comes to action, each should arm himself for battle and not be troubled about anything else.

All the voivodes did so according to the sovereign’s instruction, choosing sons of great fathers and the sovereign’s stolniks who could serve in military affairs, and they assigned the sons of boyars to them. The sovereign also sends orders to all the voivodes in the regiments that they should organize them likewise, so that there be no confusion in the regiments when they begin the action.

Dispatch of the Ertoul

And the sovereign sent ahead of himself in the ertouls, the advance detachments, on the fifteenth day of July, Prince Yuri Ivanovich Shemyakin and Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Troekurov, and with them sons of boyars; and he sent conscripted laborers after them, and ordered bridges to be built over the rivers and marshes.

On the Tsar’s Campaign from Murom, July 20, Wednesday, on Elijah’s Day

The sovereign came to the Church of the Nativity of the Most Pure and to the great wonderworkers; and he served a prayer service with great tears, and called upon almighty God for help, and the Most Pure Mother of God, and the great wonderworkers, and also his holy kinsmen, and also the prayers of the most reverend hierarch Metropolitan Macarius and the prayers of the whole all-holy Orthodox council and of all Christian people; and the sovereign set out on the journey.
