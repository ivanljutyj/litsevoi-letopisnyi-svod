In that same year in Novgorod, Euthymius, Archbishop of Novgorod, built a stone church in the name of Saint Anastasia. In that same year he also built a small stone cell in his courtyard.

In the year 6949 from Adam (the year 1449 from Christ)

On the first day of the month of September. Eugene, Pope of Rome, after the completion of his council rejoiced in the agreement with the Greek emperor John of Constantinople, son of Manuel, and with all the metropolitans, Greek bishops, monks, philosophers, princes and boyars, with all the Greeks; especially did he greatly honor Isidore, Metropolitan of Russia.

And he sent the emperor back to Constantinople with all who were with him; he also sent Isidore, Metropolitan of Russia, with great honors.

Isidore, having set out from Rome for the Russian metropolitan see, came to the city called Buda on the fifth day of March, and from there sent his letters to the Polish, Lithuanian, and German lands and to all Orthodox Christian Rus.

Epistle of Isidore, Metropolitan of Russia

“Isidore, by the grace of God most reverend Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, legate of the see proceeding from the apostolic side, to Poland, Lithuania, and Germany, to all and each Christian faithful to the faith of Christ: eternal salvation from the Lord Jesus Christ, and, in connection with the union of the faith, peace and grace. Rejoice and be glad, all who now live in the Lord, that the Eastern and Roman churches, for so long divided and at enmity, have now by a true union been joined into their original unity, into peace, stillness, and love, and have come to the state of ancient unity of rule without any division. All people who bear the name of Christ, both Latins and Italians, and all others belonging to the holy catholic great Church of Constantinople, as well as Russians, Serbs, Vlachs, and all other Christian peoples who truly believe in Christ Jesus, the Son of God and God, who created all things, both heavenly and earthly; in Him are all our life and all our hope, both in this age and in the age to come—receive this most holy union and unity of rule with great spiritual joy and honor. I ask all of you who believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, who has shown mercy to us, that you have no division with the Latins, since we are all servants of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ and are baptized in His name.
