And Prince Dmitry, having heard that the children of the Grand Prince had come and seated themselves in Murom with many people, did not wish to send [troops] against them, fearing the indignation of all the people against his rule. But he himself also considered and desired to see Grand Prince Vasily in his own domain.

Concerning the Children of the Grand Prince

And Prince Dmitry devised this: he summoned to Moscow Jonah, Bishop of Ryazan, and when he came, he promised him the metropolitanate and began to say to him: “Father, would you go to your bishopric in the city of Murom and take the children of the Grand Prince under your epitrachelion; and I am glad to favor them, and I will release their father the Grand Prince, and I will give them a sufficient patrimony, so that they may have abundance in everything.”

Concerning Lord Jonah of Ryazan

And Lord Jonah went to Murom in boats with those words of Prince Dmitry, and, having come to Murom, he began to speak his words to the boyars, to the children of the Grand Prince, to the three Ryapolovsky princes and to the others with them. The boyars, having deliberated much, decided thus: “If we now do not obey the hierarch and do not go to Prince Dmitry with the children of the Grand Prince, then he will come with an army, take the city, and, seizing them, will do with them whatever he wishes, and also with their father the Grand Prince and with us all. And what sense is there in our firmness if we do not obey the words of the hierarch?” And they said to Lord Jonah: “Although you have come with such words from Prince Dmitry to our sovereigns, the children of the Grand Prince, and to us, nevertheless we dare not do this—to let the children of the Grand Prince go with you without guard. But go to the cathedral church of the Nativity of the Most Holy Mother of God, take them from the veil onto your epitrachelion, and then we will let them go with you, and we ourselves will go with them.” And Lord Jonah promised to do so.

And entering the church, he began a moleben to the Most Pure, and after completing the moleben, he took them from the veil at the Most Pure onto his epitrachelion, and went with them to Pereyaslavl to Prince Dmitry, who was there then. He came to Pereyaslavl on the sixth day of May.

Concerning the Treachery of Shemyaka

And Prince Dmitry honored them a little with flattery, invited them to dinner, and gave them gifts.

And on the third day after this, with the same Lord Jonah, he sent them into confinement to their father in Uglich. He went with them to their father, left them there, and returned to Prince Dmitry, who ordered him to go to Moscow and take his seat at the metropolitan’s court. And Jonah did so.
