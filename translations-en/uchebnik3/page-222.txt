After this, the Great Prince also came with all those previously named.

And the Metropolitan began a prayer service to Peter the Wonderworker, and when the time came for the troparia that are read before the final litany, the Metropolitan himself and the hierarchs with him bade the Great Prince and his son take up together with them the shrine in which were the healing-bearing relics of our holy father Peter, Metropolitan and Wonderworker, and they carry them to the place prepared for them near the altar.

And when they had set them down, the Metropolitan said the Great Litany, which is said at the conclusion of all prayer services, and also the dismissal.

And all made the sign of the cross at the saint’s shrine, and all went to their places; and so they began the Liturgy. Having accomplished all that was due to the glory of the saint on that day, they then established that his translation be celebrated in the following years on the same day; and the translation that had been celebrated before, on July 30, they abolished.

The Great Prince then ordered alms to be distributed to the priests throughout the whole city of Moscow, to all the monasteries, and to the poor.

On the Translation into the New Church of the Relics of the Most Reverend Metropolitans Cyprian, Photius, and Jonah the Wonderworker, and of the Relics of Prince Yuri Danilovich

On the 27th day of the same month, on Friday before Vespers, at the ninth hour of the day, the Most Reverend Metropolitan Gerontius, with all the previously named bishops, comes to the Church of Saint John of the Ladder with the whole sacred council and with priests from all the city of Moscow; and the Metropolitan ordered the bells to be rung.

And then the Great Prince came with his son and boyars, and the whole multitude of Orthodox Christians of the city of Moscow.

And the priests took the stone shrine with the relics of the Most Reverend Metropolitan Cyprian, carried it from that church into the new Church of the Most Holy Theotokos, and placed it in the prepared place, on the right side by the front wall in the corner.

And then they bring the relics of Metropolitan Photius in a stone shrine as well; and placed them in the same row with Cyprian; and set stone tombs over them.

Then they took the wooden shrine in which lies the Most Reverend Metropolitan Jonah the Wonderworker in the body, and, having carried it with great honor, placed it on the other side, opposite those metropolitans, upon the platform.
