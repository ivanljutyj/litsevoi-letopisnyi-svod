And he came to them in Oshla, thank God, safe and well; and Ivan Petrovich reported that foot Cheremis had come in the forest against the guard regiment, against Prince Vasily Tokmakov, and Prince Vasily, thank God, had defeated them utterly.

And from the senior voivodes there was war in the volosts: in Shumursha and Khozyakovo, and in Oshla, and in both Mazary, and in the two Orshi volosts, Malaya and Bolshaya, and in Bisht, and in Kukshuli, in Soroka, Kunshi, and in Vasilyukov Balak, and in the Mamich-Berdeeva volost, and the Kileeva volost, and the Kikina volost, and in Kukhyal Kokshi, Bolshaya and Malaya, and the Syzal volost, and Dmashi, and Monam, and Kermechi, and Ulyazy — and in all those volosts there was war from the voivodes, and they took and killed many people.

And they were on the Meadow Side at war for two weeks, then came out to the Volga, and went to Kazan and back along the Volga, and came to the sovereign, thank God, safe and well.

Writing to the Tsar and Grand Prince from Kazan

On March 1 the boyar and voivode Prince Mikhail Glinsky and his companions wrote to the tsar and grand prince from Kazan that the meadow people had come to the Arsk side to wage war; and the Arsk people, having built forts, fought them off, and with them in the forts were the streltsy of the tsar and grand prince, and with arquebuses they killed many of the meadow people.

And the meadow people ravaged Tatar villages; and they went to the Meadow Side.

Concerning the Sending from the Town of Sviyazhsk

In the same month Prince Mikhail Vorotynsky and his companions sent Fedka Baskakov from the town of Sviyazhsk: Fedka had been sent as a commander over the mountain people to the Meadow Side to wage war, and seven hundred mountain people went on skis; and they came unexpectedly, and after waging war they killed and captured people and killed livestock; and they came back, thank God, safe and sound.

Concerning the Dismissal of the Nogai Envoys

In the same month the sovereign, in his grace, sent them back to the Nogais; and he sent his own men to Prince Smail — Ignaty Zagryazhsky; to Kasai Murza — Myasoed Vislov; to Arslan — service Tatars; and the tsar and grand prince ordered that they be at one with the tsar and grand prince against all his enemies; and for the defeat of Prince Yusuf he sent with his envoys his sovereign bounty to the prince and the murzas.
