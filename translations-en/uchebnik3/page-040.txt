The Greek emperor John and the Ecumenical Patriarch Joseph agreed to take part in the last session of the council in the Latin city of Florence; and so they did: having received much gold, they went to their city of Florence. In the same year Tsar Mahomet came secretly to Moscow on the third day of the month of July, a Friday, with a large army.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich wished to march against him, but did not manage to assemble; nevertheless he set out. And seeing that he had few soldiers, he returned and went beyond the Volga, leaving in Moscow his commander Prince Yuri Patrikeevich with a countless multitude of Christians.

Tsar Mahomet, having come to Moscow and stood there for 10 days, went away without doing any harm to the city; but he did much harm to the Russian land: returning back, he burned Kolomna, took many people captive, and killed many.

In the same year in Novgorod, Archbishop Euthymius of Novgorod built a stone granary for grain in his own courtyard. In the same year in Novgorod, Lord Euthymius renovated the Church of Saint Sophia and whitewashed it. In the same year in Novgorod, Lord Euthymius built a stone bell tower in the city on the old site.

In the same year in Novgorod, Lord Euthymius, Archbishop of Novgorod, found the body of Lord John of Novgorod, under whom the Suzdalians were encamped before Novgorod.

In the same year in Veliky Novgorod, Lord Euthymius gilded the coffin of Prince Vladimir Yaroslavich, grandson of the great Vladimir who baptized the Russian land, and inscribed it. He also painted the coffin of his mother and laid a cover on it, and established that their memory be celebrated every year on the fourth day of the month of October.

In the year 6948 from Adam (1448 from Christ), in the month of September.
The Murder of Sigismund

Grand Prince Sigismund of Lithuania, brother of Vytautas, son of Kestutis, grandson of Gediminas, was killed in Lithuania by Prince Alexander Czartoryski and his brother Prince Ivan; he reigned for 9 years. This prince was harsh and merciless, more greedy for gold and silver than all people, and he destroyed many Lithuanian princes, cut them down, drowned them in waters, and killed them in prisons; and he mercilessly destroyed a multitude of great lords and boyars, local people, and merchants; and therefore the Lord God punished him with an evil death.
