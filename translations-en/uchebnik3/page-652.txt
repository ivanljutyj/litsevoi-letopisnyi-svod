For three weeks they stood at Kerepeti, having gathered with all the land, thirty versts from Novgorodok. And the voevodes, having set Novgorodok in order and left men in it, intended to go fight the Master and the Bishop and to pursue against them the sovereign’s and the land’s cause, as much as the Merciful God would help. And the sovereign sent Ignaty Zabolotsky to the voevodes with a reward, with gold coins.

On the Flight of the Master and the Bishop

In the same month the boyars and voevodes Prince Petr Ivanovich Shuysky and all the boyars and voevodes sent Prince Fedor Obolensky-Cherny and Andrei Zacheslomsky to the tsar and sovereign with news: two Latvians had come running to the voevodes and said that the Master and the Bishop, as soon as they heard that the voevodes had taken Novgorodok, had fled from Kerepeti, and that few people remained in Kerepeti.

The voevodes sent ahead the voevodes Andrei Ivanovich Shein and Daniil Fedorovich Adashev to Kerepeti to learn about the Master and to detain them until the voevodes came; and the senior voevodes went hastily after them as well.

On the Capture of the Town of Kerepeti

And the voevodes Andrei and Daniil, by God’s mercy, took the town of Kerepeti and left in it the voevode Vasily Razladin and Semyon Nashchekin, while they themselves went that same night after the Master, and they sent word of this to Prince Petr and Prince Andrei.

On the Voevodes’ Campaign after the Master

In the same month Ushchaty-Cheglokov and Afanasy Novokreshchenov came from the voevodes, from Prince Petr and Prince Andrei, and told the sovereign: when the voevodes Andrei Shein and Daniil Adashev went after the Master, the Master went to the left, while the Bishop went with his men toward Yuryev.

And in the morning the voevodes overtook the Bishop twenty-five versts from Yuryev and defeated the Bishop utterly, and pursued him to the very suburb of Yuryev; and the Bishop entered Yuryev with few men; and in that battle they captured twenty-seven Germans, Yuryev townsmen, the best men from the Bishop’s court, and took many carts with armor and powder and all sorts of supplies.

And the voevodes went back to Kerepeti; and ten versts from Kerepeti lies the town of Kurslav; and the voevodes Andrei Ivanovich Shein and Daniil Fedorovich Adashev went to the town of Kurslav and took the small town of Kurslav, and left in it as commanders Nikita Novokreshchenov and Tikhon Tyrtov.

And the common people in Kerepeti and in Kurslav submitted to the voevodes, and the voevodes ordered them all to be brought to the oath.

And Andrei and Daniil themselves gathered at Kerepeti with the senior voevodes, with Prince Petr and his comrades; and they sent to find out about the Master.
