On the Ambassadors

In the same month of March the grand prince sent with an embassy the Greek Dmitry Ivanov, son of Ralev, and Mitrofan Fedorov, son of Karacharov, overseas to the Italian lands on his business; and together with them he sent Mikhail Pogozhy on an embassy to Krakow to Albrecht, King of Poland, to order that the grand prince’s ambassadors be allowed through his Polish land.

And from Krakow the ambassadors went to Vladislav, King of Hungary, and from there to Venice.

In the same month of March the grand prince sent to Constantinople to Abazit-Saltan with letters Alesha Golokhvastov.

On Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich.

In the year 7007 from Adam (the year 1507 from Christ) began the reign of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich.
The Throne of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov

In the same month of March, on the 21st day, a Thursday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus granted his son Prince Vasily Ivanovich and named him sovereign grand prince, and gave him Veliky Novgorod and Pskov as a great principality.

On the Grand Prince’s Court

In that same spring, in the month of May, the grand prince ordered the laying of his stone court, stone and brick chambers, and beneath them cellars and ice-houses, on the old court by the Church of the Annunciation, and a stone wall from his court to the Borovitskaya Tower; and the master was Aleviz Fryazin from the city of Milan.

In that same spring, on the 1st day of June, the grand prince sent Vasily Tretyakov, son of Dolmatov, with an embassy to Lithuania, with carts.

In that same spring, on the 12th day of June, Euthymius, Bishop of Krutitsy, reposed. In the same summer, on the 14th day of July, a Sunday, the Epiphany abbot Tryphon was installed by Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, to the Sarskaya episcopate at Krutitsy.
