And outside the city, beyond the Rizopolozhenskie Gates, Tsaritsa and Grand Princess Anastasia met the wonder-working images, and with her Princess Uliana, the wife of Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and with them a multitude of boyarynyas; and having signed themselves with the cross before the images of the Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers, and having lovingly kissed all the icons and the venerable crosses, and having received the blessing of the metropolitan, they followed the images together with the tsar and the metropolitan into the city and into the cathedral apostolic church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God. And after serving the prayer service, the metropolitan served the Divine Liturgy with all the cathedral clergy, and after the liturgy the tsar went to his royal house, rejoicing and thanking God and the Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers.

The Capture of the German Towns of Rakobor and Porkhol

On the same day Prince Daniil Rostovsky and his companions came to the tsar and sovereign from the governors in Yuryev, and they said the following: the boyars and governors, Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky and his companions, sent the commanders Boris Kolychev and others to Rakobor and Porkhol.

And the commanders took Rakobor and Porkhol, and released the petty princes from the towns; and all the common people joined the sovereign and swore an oath that they would be inseparable from the sovereign forever. And Boris Kolychev and his companions sent from themselves Prince Nikita Priimkov and his companions to conquer the Koluvan places, and Prince Nikita fought within ten versts of Koluvan and took people captive, seized much property, and they came to Rakobor safe and sound with all their people.

The Capture of the German Town of Layus

In the same month Pavel Zabolotsky and Zhdan Veshnyakov wrote to the tsar and grand prince from the town of Layus: they had gone from Syrensk by the sovereign’s command to Rakobor and met a German outpost from Layus, and they fought those Germans and defeated them utterly; and they came to Layus and took Layus, and released the petty prince and other Germans from the town, while all the common people joined the sovereign and swore an oath for the whole district that they would be inseparable from the sovereign.

Prince Mikhail Repnin wrote to the sovereign that he had conquered the Naidu, Koluvan, and Velian places, and slew German people, and captured the town of Khtorchbor
