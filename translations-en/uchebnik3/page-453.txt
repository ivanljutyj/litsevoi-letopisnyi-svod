In the year 7052 from Adam (1552 from Christ)

On 9 September the boyars of the great prince (Prince Ivan and Prince Andrei Mikhailovich Shuysky, and Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Shuysky, and their advisers, Prince Dmitry Kurlyatev and Prince Ivan Shemyaka, and Prince Ivan Turuntai Pronsky, and Aleksei Basmanov Pleshcheev and other advisers) fell into turmoil among themselves before the great prince and before the metropolitan in the Dining Chamber at the great prince’s council. Prince Andrei Shuysky and the Kubenskys and their advisers (and Paletsky was also with them in that council) seized Fyodor Semyonov, son of Vorontsov, because the great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich favored and protected him.

And they struck him on the cheeks, and tore the clothes off him, and wanted to kill him against the sovereign’s command. /And with difficulty the metropolitan begged them to spare him from death./

And the sovereign sent to them the metropolitan and his boyars, Ivan and Vasily Grigorievich Poplevin Morozov, so that the Shuyskys would not kill Fyodor.

And they led him from the great prince’s porch with great shame, beating and pushing him, to the square, and sent him beyond the Neglinnaya to the courtyard of Ivan Zaitsev.

And the sovereign again sent to them the metropolitan and boyars to the Shuyskys: if Fyodor and his son Ivan could not be in Moscow, then Fyodor and his son Ivan should be sent to serve at Kolomna. And the Shuyskys did not want that, but decided to send Fyodor and his son Ivan to Kostroma. And by the sovereign’s decree he ordered Fyodor and his son Ivan sent to Kostroma. And when the metropolitan went from the sovereign to the Shuyskys, Foma Petrov, son of Golovin, stepped on the metropolitan’s mantle and tore the mantle on the metropolitan.

And they sent him to serve at Kostroma with his son Ivan. And in the same month, on the 16th, on Sunday, the great prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus went out to the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery to pray at the memory of the wonderworker Sergius, and with him his brother Prince Yuri Vasilievich and the boyars, and from there to Volok and to Mozhaisk, and he came to Moscow on Tuesday, 1 November.

In the same month they finished the Church of the Resurrection of Christ on the square near the Church of St John under the Bells; and it had been founded by the command of the great prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus in the year 7040 from Adam (1540 from Christ). And the church master was Petrok Maly Fryazin; he began it and completed it without a staircase.

And the staircase and doors, by the command of the pious tsar and great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus, were added to that same Church of the Resurrection of Christ in the year 7060 from Adam (1560 from Christ) by Moscow craftsmen.
