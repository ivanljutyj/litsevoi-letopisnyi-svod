In the year 6961 from Adam (1461 from Christ)

There reigned over the Turks the godless Mehmed, son of Murad and grandson of Mehmed; and he was then at peace with the Orthodox Emperor Constantine, son of Manuel, who reigned in Constantinople.

Yet the godless Mehmed raised war against Constantinople not according to right order, but contrary to the established agreements, and in violation of oaths he brought a vast host by land and by sea and began to wage war. And coming suddenly, he surrounded the city with many forces.

The emperor, with the nobles then present there and all the people of the city, perceiving his intent, did not know what to do, since at that time the city was thinly populated, and the emperor’s brothers were not there.

And the emperor sent envoys to Mehmed Sultan, wishing to see everything and speak of peace. But he, the accursed, cunning, and faithless one, sent the envoys away with nothing, while he himself with a wondrous army filling all with fear moved by land and by sea and came against the reigning city in the month of December, ordering cannon and arquebuses and battering engines to be fired and preparations made to storm the city.

The people in the city, Greeks and Franks, riding out from the city, fought with the Turks, not allowing them to set up siege devices; but still the sultan’s force was great and heavy, and the Greeks could do them no harm, because one fought against a thousand and two against ten thousand.

Seeing this, the emperor ordered the nobles and megistanes to divide among the soldiers the city walls, towers, and gates, and also all the people, and to set the war bells on all sides, so that each of them should know and guard his own side, and arrange all that was needed for war, and fight the Turks from the wall, and not go out or ride out from the city. He also ordered cannon and arquebuses to be placed in places convenient for the assault, in order to protect the walls.

And the emperor himself with the patriarch and the empress, and with the hierarchs, and the whole sacred council, and a multitude of women and children went about the churches of God, making supplications and prayers, saying: “Lord, Lord! A fearsome nature and ineffable power has come upon us, at the sight of which the ancient mountains trembled, and the earthly creation shook, the sun and moon were terrified, and their brightness perished, and the stars of heaven fell. But we, wretched ones, despising all this, sinned and acted lawlessly before Thee, O Lord, and a thousandfold angered and provoked Thy Divinity, forgetting Thy great gifts and despising Thy commandments, and turning away Thy fierce mercies and bounties toward us, and turned to evil deeds and lawlessness, by which we have far departed from Thee.
