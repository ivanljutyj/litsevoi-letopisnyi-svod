All the people who were in the city gave praise to God and to His most pure Mother, the speedy helper, and having celebrated a service of supplication to the great wonderworkers, they took rest from the evil that had befallen them.

At that very hour Grand Princess Sophia sent to her son Grand Prince Vasily, for on that same Friday at sunrise he had crossed the Volga at the mouth of the Dubna.

Hearing of this, the Grand Prince first wept, and then, thanking the Lord God and His most pure Mother and all the saints, he immediately returned to Moscow.

And entering the city, he came to the church of the Most Holy Mother of God, and fell before the image of the Lord, shedding many tears and saying: “I thank Thee, Master, that Thou didst not deliver Thy flock of Orthodox Christians to the godless raw-eaters!” He also fell weeping before the image of the Most Pure One and prayed likewise, and then likewise at the tomb of Peter the Wonderworker; and after a service of supplication he received the blessing from the hand of his father Metropolitan Jonah.

And after this, leaving the church, he kissed his mother and his son Yuri and the others.

He also went to all the cathedral churches, celebrating services of supplication and pouring out tears, thanking God who loves mankind and His most pure Mother and all the saints for the miracle that had occurred.

Then he comforted the city people also, saying: “This has befallen you because of my sins, but do not lose heart; let each of you build houses in your places, and I am glad to favor you and grant you relief.”

Then he went to dinner with the metropolitan, with his mother, with his children, and with the boyars; and there was rejoicing on that day after the sorrow that had befallen us for our sins.

In the year 6960 from Adam (1460 from Christ)

News came to the Grand Prince that Prince Dmitry Shemyaka was advancing on Ustyug. The Grand Prince, having spent the Nativity of Christ in Moscow, set out against him from St Basil’s Day; and at Epiphany he was at the Trinity in the Monastery of St Sergius, and from there went to Yaroslavl.

From Yaroslavl he sent his son Grand Prince Ioann to Kokshenga against Prince Dmitry, while he himself went to Kostroma.

And from Kostroma he sent Tsarevich Yagup, son of Mamutyak, so that they might join forces against Prince Dmitry.

And before that he had sent Prince Vasily Yaroslavich, and with him his boyars Prince Semyon Ivanovich Obolensky and Fyodor Basenok, and many others, and his court to Ustyug.
