In the same month of July, by command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, the town of Usvyato was founded in Usvyatskaya volost of Ozerishche district.

In the same month of July, the boyar Prince Fyodor Kurakin, the clerks Andrey Beznosov and Leonty Ananyin wrote from Veliky Novgorod that a pestilence had begun in Veliky Novgorod, in Shelonskaya pyatina, on July 19.

In the same month of July, on the 24th day, Wednesday, the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, and his son Tsarevich Ivan, with his clergy, the archbishops and bishops, and with the whole consecrated council, selected for the house of the Most Pure Mother of God, to the holy great throne of the great wonderworkers Peter and Jonah, to the Russian metropolia, after Metropolitan Athanasius, the hegumen of the Solovetsky Monastery, Philip; and they installed him that same day in the metropolitan's court.

And in July, on the 25th day, Thursday, by command of the pious tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat, the hegumen of Solovetsky, Philip Kolychev, was installed on the holy great throne of the great wonderworkers Peter, Alexius and Jonah, to the Russian metropolia, by the Russian archbishops and bishops: Pimen, archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov; German, archbishop of Kazan and Sviyazhsk; Nikandr, archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl; Eleutherius, bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa; Philotheus, bishop of Ryazan and Murom; Simeon, bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk; Varlaam, bishop of Kolomna and Kashira; Galaktion, bishop of Sar and Podon; Joasaph, bishop of Perm and Vologda; and the whole consecrated council. The Polotsk archbishop had died at that time, and the Tver bishop Acacius was then ill, and moreover was old and remained in Tver, and with the archbishops and bishops he was of one mind in the election of the metropolitan. At the installation were the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, and his children Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Fyodor and Prince Vladimir Andreyevich.

That same summer, in August, on the 11th day, Athanasius, vladyka of Suzdal, was installed by Philip, metropolitan of all Rus, and the whole consecrated council as archbishop in Polotsk.

In the same month, on the 25th day, the tsar and grand prince sent to the Swedish land to King Erik a messenger, Touza Savlukov, with a letter.

In the same month of August there appeared a deadly pestilence in Veliky Novgorod, Polotsk, Ozerishchi, Nevl, Luki Velikiye, and Toropets; and many people died from the plague sores. In Polotsk, Toropets and Luki, in the settlements and in the district, the priests died out. And there was no one to bury the dead; and priests were sent to those towns from other towns.
