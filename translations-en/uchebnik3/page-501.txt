On the Sending of the Voivodes to Sviyaga

And the sovereign sent the voivodes by boats to Sviyaga, and he ordered the boyars and voivodes Prince Alexander Borisovich Gorbaty and Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky and other voivodes to guard his business and await him, the sovereign; and with the equipment the sovereign sent boyar Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov, and he sent many of his supplies to feed the army, and he sent his stewards with all the household supplies.

And to the Kama the sovereign sent boyar Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Glinsky and okolnichy Ivan Ivanovich Umny, and with them boyar sons, Cossacks, and streltsy; and from Vyatka he ordered Pauk Zabolotsky to go to the Kama with the Ustyug and Vyatka volostels, and he ordered Grigory Sukin to go with the Vyatchans.
