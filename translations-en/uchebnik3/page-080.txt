For our sins, in this war many of our warriors were killed by the Tatars, although there were then rather few Tatars.

From Belev the Tsar went to Nizhny Novgorod and settled in Old Nizhny Novgorod, and much evil was done by him; and from Old Nizhny Novgorod he went to Murom.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich learned of this; on the Epiphany he was in Vladimir, and he set out against the Tsar with all his brothers and his people toward Murom. When Tsar Ulug-Muhammad learned of this, he fled back to Old Nizhny Novgorod, where he lived.

And the forward regiments of the Grand Prince beat the Tatars under Murom, at Gorokhovets, and in other places, while the Tsar remained in Murom; and the Tatars, while the Tsar was marching against Murom, captured Lukh. And the Grand Prince returned and went to Suzdal, and from there he went to Vladimir, and from Vladimir he came to Moscow toward evening on Good Friday. In that same year a great sign occurred in the city of Suzdal in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God.

On the Battle at Suzdal

In that same spring news reached Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich in Moscow that Tsar Ulug-Muhammad had sent his sons Mamutyak and Yagub against him from Old Nizhny Novgorod, where they were.

After the fast-breaking before Peter’s Fast, the Grand Prince set out against them with his army from Moscow.

He came to Yuryev-Polsky, and there the Novgorod voivodes joined him—Prince Fyodor Dolgoldov and Yushka Dranitsa. They had set fire to the town by night and had themselves fled by night, because they were exhausted from hunger: they had eaten all their grain supply, and they no longer had strength to endure hunger and the pressure from the Tatars, and therefore at night they burned the town and fled.

Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich was in Yuryev on St Peter’s Day and from Yuryev went to Suzdal. Then his brothers joined him, having set out from their patrimonies: Prince Ivan Andreevich Mozhaisky, his brother Prince Mikhail Andreevich Vereysky, and Prince Vasily Yaroslavich, grandson of Vladimir, great-grandson of Andrei, great-great-grandson of Ivan Danilovich of Moscow; and many other voivodes and people came to him. Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich came to the city of Suzdal with his brothers and all his army, and they encamped on the Kamenka River on the sixth day of July, a Tuesday.
