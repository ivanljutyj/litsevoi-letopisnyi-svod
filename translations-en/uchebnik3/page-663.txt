On the report to the sovereign from the German land from Prince Mikhail

In that same month Prince Mikhail Petrovich wrote to the sovereign that he had captured the German town of Tolchbor at sea.

On the report to the sovereign from the voivodes

In the month of October the voivodes Prince Mikhail Petrovich and Semen Yartsov wrote that they had taken the town of Kinevel and killed many German people in it.

On the report to the sovereign from the German land from Pavel Zabolotsky

In that same month Pavel Zabolotsky wrote to the sovereign: he had gone from Layus and taken the small German town of Polchev and burned it all, and carried all its equipment and bells to Layus.

From the German land the voivodes wrote to the sovereign

In that month Prince Mikhail and Semen Yartsov also wrote that they had gone against the posad of Kolyvan and killed German people in the posad of Kolyvan and withdrawn; and the Germans overtook them, and they, turning back, trampled the German people and took captives from them, and they themselves came with all their men to Rakobor. And the sovereign sent them a reward.

On the report from Yuryev to the tsar from Prince Dmitry Ivanovich

In the month of October Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Kurlyatev wrote from Yuryev that the master had gathered, and the archbishop with all his people, and foreign people with them, had come to the small town of Ryngol, and in it sat commander Rusin Ignatyev, and with him forty boyar sons and fifty streltsy.

And the tsar and grand prince ordered Prince Mikhail and Semen to go to Ryngol from Rakobor, and from Pskov the Circassian princes Prince Ivan Maashik and Prince Vasily Sibok with their brothers, and from Vyshgorod Fedor Sheremetev, and from Krasnaya Foma Tretyakov, and from Yuryev the sovereign ordered Prince Petr Shchepin and Mikhail Golovin to go; and he ordered them to march in three regiments: in the main regiment Prince Mikhail Petrovich and Prince Petr Shchepin, in the forward regiment Fedor Vasilyevich and Semen Yartsov, in the guard regiment Foma Tretyakov and Mikhail Petrov; and he ordered the Circassian princes to be in the forward regiment as well, and ordered the voivodes to assemble in the forward regiment with the Circassian prince Ivan Maashik.
