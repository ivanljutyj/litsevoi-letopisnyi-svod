On the Signs in the City of Kazan

A certain Tatar holy fool in Kazan, walking about the city, began suddenly to shout and incessantly say: “The Tatars shall not live here, but only Russian people.” The Tatars wanted to kill him, but the tsar did not allow them to kill him, and ordered him to be shut up in an empty house. But he broke out of that house, crying out and cursing the Tatars and foretelling their destruction.

A Child from a Cow

At another time in the city of Kazan it was clearly seen by the Tatars that a child of human form was born from a cow, which many of those assembled saw.

And suddenly the child turned into a man; he seemed to be armed, and said to those looking at him: “Submit without deceit to the Muscovite sovereign! If you do not, you will perish.” And the Tatars decided to kill him, but he became invisible.

A Wonder at the Fishing

Once the Kazan fishermen were fishing on the Volga and drew up the net, in which they saw a living old man lying and saying to them: “Make haste to entreat the Muscovite tsar for your wrongdoing; he is merciful and will have pity on you. If you do not do so, you will be destroyed by him.” They thought this was sorcery of the Russian people and wanted to kill him; but he, barely having come out of the net, dived into the river.

A Wonder in the City

In the city of Kazan at that time there were many huts dug into the ground as protection from Russian fiery shooting, and Tatars went into one earthen hut and saw it warm and fragrant, as though it were a Christian hut; they saw also a stove full of wheat loaves, and in one half of that hut stood a nobleman’s bed, on which lay a grey-haired man and said: “Do not resist the Muscovite tsar, but better entreat him, for he is very merciful and will do you no harm. If you do not obey him, only a small number of you will escape destruction.” They said among themselves: “This is Russian sorcery; let us kill him, and nothing will happen to us.” And that man became invisible, nor was there bed, nor loaves in the stove, nor fragrance; only a stench did they feel.
