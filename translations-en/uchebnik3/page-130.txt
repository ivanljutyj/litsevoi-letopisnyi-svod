That same spring Theodosius, Archbishop of Rostov, was installed to the metropolitanate by the Russian hierarchs of our land of Moscow: Philip of Suzdal, Euphrosynus of Ryazan, Gerontius of Kolomna, and Bassian of Sarsk; while Jonah, Archbishop of Novgorod, and the Bishop of Tver sent envoys with their letters, saying: “Whomever the Lord God and His most pure Mother, and the great wonderworkers, and our lord the great prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and our brothers the Russian bishops, and the consecrated council desire, let him be our metropolitan”; and they all signed together in agreement.

In that same year the great prince Vasily Vasilyevich built in Moscow the stone church of the Nativity of John the Forerunner at the Borovitsky Gate, where before there had been a wooden one. They say that this was the first church in Moscow: on that place there was a pine forest, and that church was built of logs in that forest. There was also then the cathedral church under Metropolitan Peter, and the metropolitan’s court was right there, where now is the court of Prince Ivan Yuryevich.

In that same year the great prince went to Vladimir, intending to go against the Kazan tsar. When he was in Vladimir, envoys came to him from Kazan and asked for peace.

The Miracle of Saint Alexius. In the year 6970 from Adam (the year 1470 from Christ)

In the month of January, in the monastery of the holy Archangel Michael, at the tomb of the holy wonderworker Alexius, a monk of that monastery named Naum was healed. From infancy he had a withered leg and walked on a wooden leg, serving in that monastery in the kitchen and bakery. One night he came to the icon of the saint which is painted by the saint’s tomb and began to beseech the saint, saying: “God gives many miracles and healings to many through you; but I have labored many years from my infancy for the brethren in your monastery, and you do not accept my request.” And in that same hour his leg straightened, and throwing away the wooden leg on which he had walked, he went healthy to his cell, where he had been before.

That same winter Bishop Euphrosynus Zvenets of Ryazan died, and David, formerly treasurer of Metropolitan Jonah, was appointed in his place as Bishop of Ryazan, on the first day of February. That same winter many boyar sons of Prince Vasily Yaroslavich, having conspired, kissed the cross among themselves, that they would come secretly to Uglich, free their prince, and flee with him.

Their plot became known to the great prince, and he ordered all to be seized and executed: to be beaten with the knout and to have hands cut off, noses cut off, and others to be beheaded. By such execution they executed Volodya Davydov, Parfen Brein, Luka Posviyev, and others.
