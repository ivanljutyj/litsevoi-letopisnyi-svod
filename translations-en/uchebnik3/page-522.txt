Nor does the tsar send his warriors into battle, but only when he himself comes. Now it is fitting for you, O Tsar, together with those whom you in your wisdom choose to take with you, to strive yourself, so that with God's help you may conquer and cast down these adversaries. For when God wills, the natural order is broken; and where the tsar's coming is with God's help, there even the established order passes away. But however great you may be, humble yourself, remembering the words of him who said: "In glory be humble, in sorrow be wise." Above all remember, O Tsar, the Saviour's word from the Gospel: that everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted. Therefore, as you are humble, the Lord God will subdue all your enemies under your feet, and the Lord will send you helpers—His angels and all the holy martyrs—and your adversaries will fall into confusion, and you will overcome your enemies, and God's word spoken by the prophet will be fulfilled in you, the pious tsar: "I have raised you up, O king, in righteousness, and called you, O king, in righteousness, and taken you by your hand, and strengthened you. Let the nations obey you; I will destroy every stronghold of kings and open the doors, and cities will not be shut before you; I will go before you and level the mountains, and break the bronze doors and shatter the iron bars." May the Lord give a firm, honorable, and strong kingdom into your hands and to the sons of your sons from generation to generation and forever, O God-established tsar. And if, O pious tsar, in the army of God for your most honorable deed and piety, and for victory over your adversaries, it should happen that any Orthodox Christian in that battle suffers to the shedding of blood for the holy churches and for the holy Christian faith and for the multitude of Orthodox people, and afterwards remains alive, then by the shedding of their blood they will cleanse their former sins, by which after holy baptism they sinned and were defiled; for all this they will be forgiven by the Lord God. And they will receive not only forgiveness of sins from God for the shedding of their blood, but also a special reward in this age—length of years and health—and in the age to come they will receive a special recompense for the shedding of their blood. And if it should happen that any of the Orthodox Christians now, in that royal army of yours, not only sheds his blood but suffers even to death for the holy churches and for the Orthodox Christian faith and for the multitude of Orthodox people whom Christ redeemed from torment by His precious blood, and His Christ's word will be fulfilled: "There is no greater love than to lay down one's soul for one's brother"—then that person, according to the word spoken by the Lord God, will receive a second baptism of martyrdom and will be cleansed by the shedding of his blood, and will wash from his soul the filth of his sins, and will cleanse his soul from sins, and will receive from the Lord God instead of the corruptible the incorruptible and heavenly, and instead of labor—dwelling in the heavenly city of Jerusalem. And for armed combat and bodily suffering—the receiving of eternal blessings; and for beheading by the sword and piercing by the spear—with the martyrs and angels...
