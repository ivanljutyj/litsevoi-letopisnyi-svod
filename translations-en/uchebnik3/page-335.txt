The Miracle of Saint Alexius concerning the Deaf Man

In the same month of February, on the 24th day, on a Thursday, at the shrine of Saint Alexius, Metropolitan of all Rus and wonderworker, God had mercy on a deaf man named Athanasius.

Concerning Kazan

In the same year, in the month of December, on the 29th day, there came to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, from Kazan, Kul-Derbysh with a letter from the seid and from the ulans, from the princes and from the karachis, and from the ichkes, and from the murzas, and from the molnas, and from the shikhzodas, and from all the Kazan people. And they wrote in the letter to the great sovereign that God's will had been accomplished, that Magamet-Amin, Tsar of Kazan, was no longer among the living, that he had died, and they besought the great sovereign: "The land of Kazan is God's, and it is also yours, sovereign Grand Prince, and we are God's slaves and also yours, the sovereign's; and do you, sovereign, show us favour, take thought for us and for all the land of Kazan, and take thought for a sovereign, how we are to be henceforth."

And on the 6th day of January, the Grand Prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, sent to Kazan his envoy, the Tver butler Mikhail Yuryev, son of Zakharyich, together with the clerk Ivan Teleshov, and he commanded them to convey to the ulans, and the princes, the karachis, the murzas, the molnas, the shikhzodas, and all the people of the Kazan land, headed by the seid, that the great sovereign showed them favour and wished to protect them, and was giving them as tsar in Kazan Shigalei, son of Tsarevich Shikh-Avlear.

And they, having learned of the favour of the great sovereign, that he was giving them Tsarevich Shigalei as tsar in Kazan, sent together with Mikhail Yuryevich to Moscow to beseech the great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, headed by the seid and on behalf of the ulans, the princes, the karachis, and all the people of the Kazan land, the envoys Abibazei, and Karacha-Bulat, Prince Shirin, and Shaisup, prince of the land, and Bazyuk the bakshi, that the sovereign Grand Prince might show favour to them, all the aforementioned, and give them as sovereign tsar in Kazan Tsarevich Shigalei.

And the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, by God's grace sovereign of all Rus, showed them favour, gave them Shigalei as tsar in Kazan on the 1st day of the month of March, and they agreed concerning friendship and brotherhood with one another just as it had been with Tsar Magamet-Amin. And the great sovereign ordered the oath letters to be written, such letters as ought to be between the great sovereign and Tsar Shigalei.

And Tsar Shigalei gave another written pledge upon himself to the sovereign Grand Prince, that he, being in Kazan, would guard his cause and would not depart from his word, and would stand with his Kazan land until the end of his life. And Tsar Shigalei gave the oath on that letter and on the pledge to the great sovereign.
