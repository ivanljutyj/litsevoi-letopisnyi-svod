The Chuvash want to serve the sovereign. Tsar Shigalei sent his household steward Prince Shabas Shamov, and the boyars sent Ivan Fedorov, son of Shishkin.

And after this envoys came to Tsar Shigalei from the whole Mountain Side to petition that he permit them to come and petition the Tsar and Sovereign and Grand Prince.

And the Tsar and the commanders sent to the sovereign the Mountain men Magmet Buzubov and Akhku-bek Tutaev with their companions, and with them they also sent Grigory Semenov, son of Pleshcheev.

And Magmet and his companions petitioned the sovereign in the name of the whole Mountain Side—from the princes and murzas, the hundred-princes and ten-princes, the Chuvash, the Cheremis, and the Cossacks—that the sovereign forgive them his wrath and order them to live by the town of Sviyazhsk; and they swear to the sovereign according to their faith that they and their children will not depart from the sovereign and will in no way break away from Sviyazhsk to Kazan; and that the sovereign favor them, lighten their yasak, and give them his charter of grant as to how they are to live henceforth. And the sovereign favored them, remitted his wrath toward them, and did not order them to be conquered, and agreed to take them to live in his town of Sviyazhsk; and he gave them a charter of grant with a gold seal, and remitted their yasak for three years; and he rewarded Magmet and his companions with great favor, fur coats, and money.

On the Novgorod hierarch

In the same spring, on the fourteenth day of June, on the third Sunday of Peter's Fast, Serapion Kurtsov, former abbot of the Sergius Monastery, was consecrated by Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, as archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov.

On the Suzdal hierarch

In the same month of June, on the eighteenth day, on Thursday of the fourth week of Peter's Fast, Afanasy, former abbot of the Kirillov Monastery, was consecrated bishop of Suzdal.

And the Tsar and Sovereign sent to Tsar Shigalei and to the commanders, with his favor and with gold pieces, the attendant Ignaty Veshnyakov; and he ordered Tsar Shigalei and the commanders, having brought the whole Mountain Side to oath, to send them to the city of Kazan; and with them they were to send boyar sons and Kazan princes to observe whether they would serve the sovereign honorably, and by this their oath would be tested.
