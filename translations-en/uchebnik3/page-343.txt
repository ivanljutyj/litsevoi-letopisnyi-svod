A Sign in the Cathedral Church

Shortly before the coming of the impious to the Russian borders, many of those able to foresee saw many terrible visions and fearful signs for our correction in the fear of God. God revealed to certain people, and to the righteous naked ascetic Vasily: one night he came to the great cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God and long stood sorrowfully on the ground by the front doors of the church, secretly praying to God with tears. There were also some others there who heard what seemed a great noise inside that great church, and the church doors opened, and the wonder-working image of the Mother of God, the Vladimir Icon, moved from its place, and a voice was heard saying that she wished to leave the city with the Russian hierarchs; and then flame blazed throughout the church, issuing through all the doors and small windows, and the whole church seemed ablaze, and soon the fire became invisible.

After some time had passed, the most wicked, godless, proud Crimean tsar Magmed-Girey, forgetting his oaths, began to make war against us: he remembered neither former friendship nor his oath, but gathering with his brothers and children, with the Crimean people, with the Great Horde beyond the Volga, with the Nogais, and with all his godless host, secretly and swiftly reached the Russian borders, the patrimony of the Grand Prince.

And word came to the Grand Prince that the godless Crimean tsar Magamet-Girey had become proud and had come to the Russian borders with the harshest cruelty; he had successfully crossed the great Oka River and had ravaged the Kolomna lands by God’s permission for our sins. And he defeated and took captive many Christians, both male and female, and shed much blood, and committed many defilements and violations, and burned many villages and holy churches, and plundered and burned the venerable monastery of St Nicholas on the Ugresha.

And the accursed one planned with all shamelessness to reach quickly the God-protected city of Moscow himself and to set the suburbs aflame. But Divine power forbade them this and did not allow them not only to insult or in any way harm it, but did not even let them approach. Then many people began to gather for the siege.

The Grand Prince Set Out for Volok

The pious autocrat, Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, sent everywhere for many of his warriors. The Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich himself went to the city of Kolomna with his brothers and there awaited forces from Veliky Novgorod and from other places.
