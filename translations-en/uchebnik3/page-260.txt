On the Grand Prince’s Move from His Own Court

In the same spring, on April 5, the Grand Prince moved from his old court to the new court of Prince Ivan Yuryevich together with Grand Princess Sophia, with the children, and with his daughter-in-law Grand Princess Elena, and with his grandson Prince Dmitry; and he ordered his old wooden court to be dismantled and began to build a stone court.

On the Release of the Wallachian Envoy

In the same month, on the 6th day, the Grand Prince released the envoy of Voivode Stephen Mushat to go to his sovereign in Wallachia.

On the Sending Off of the Envoys to Rome

In the same month Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich dismissed the envoy of the Roman King Maximilian, Yuri Delator; and together with him Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent his own envoys to the Roman King — Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot, Mikhail Klyapik Yaropchin, and Ivan Volk Kuritsin.

The City of Vladimir Was Built of Timber

In the same spring the Grand Prince sent his clerk Vasily Kuleshin to build the wooden city of Vladimir according to the plan of Vasily Mamyrev; and they built it in two months.

On Ivangorod

In the same spring, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, they founded a quadrangular town on the German border opposite the German city of Rugodiv on the Narva River, on Maiden Hill, on the steep bank, and named it Ivangorod.

On the Fryazin Ivan

In the same spring, on May 17, the Fryazin Ivan Spisatel, a chaplain, a tonsured monk of the white monks of the Augustinian order, renounced his order and abandoned monasticism, and married, taking as his wife the wife of Alexei Serinov, and the Grand Prince granted him a village.

On the Death of King Casimir

In the same spring, on May 23, died the King of Poland and Grand Prince of Lithuania Andrei Casimir; and his son Alexander ascended the grand princely throne of Lithuania, and his son Albrecht ascended the Polish throne, while his eldest son Vladislav had been taken to the Czech kingdom during his lifetime, and from the Czech kingdom he was taken to the Hungarian kingdom.
