And their counsellors—Prince Ivan Fedorovich Belsky, brother of Prince Semyon, and Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Vorotynsky with their children—the Grand Prince and his mother, the Grand Princess, ordered to be seized and, bound in irons, placed under guard.

In the same year, on the 19th day of August, Prince Mikhail Lvovich Glinsky was seized because he wished to hold the great realm of the Russian tsardom together with his accomplice Mikhail Semyonovich Vorontsov; and God did not permit them to accomplish this. And for that Prince Mikhail Lvovich was placed in the chamber where he had sat before; and he died, and was buried in the Trinity Monastery.

In the same year, on the 28th day of August, there came from the Nogais from Shidyak Murza and from other seventy murzas seventy envoys, and many merchants with them; and all of them with the merchants numbered 4,700, and their horses 50,000.

In the year 7043 from Adam (1543 from Christ)

On the 1st day of September came the commanders of King Sigismund, Andrei Nemirov and other commanders, with many people to Starodub with cannons and arquebuses.

And the governor of Starodub, Prince Fyodor Vasilyevich Telepnev, came out of the city, fought with the Lithuanian people and killed many people, and captured the commander of the hired soldiers, called Sukhodolsky, and 50 arquebusiers, and sent them to the Grand Prince.

And the Lithuanian commanders went away from the city; and going from Starodub, they burned the settlement at Radogoshch, and from that the city burned, and in the city the governor Matvei Lykov burned.

In the same month the same royal commander Andrei Nemirov came to Chernigov with many people and with artillery, and began to assault the city, and they began to shoot at the city with cannons and arquebuses.

But the Grand Prince’s commanders, Prince Fyodor Semyonovich Mezetsky, sent many people out of the city by night against the Lithuanian people; and the Grand Prince’s people defeated the Lithuanian people and took the cannons and arquebuses.

And in the morning the commander Andrei Nemirov with the remaining cannons quickly went away from the city back home.

In the same month, September, Lithuanian people came, the commander Prince Alexander Vishnevetsky, and with him many people, to Smolensk to the settlement, and they had set fire to the settlement. And the governor Prince Nikita Vasilyevich Obolensky sent many boyar sons of the Moscow land and also Smolensk men out of the city against the Lithuanian people; and the Grand Prince’s people fought with the Lithuanian people, and did not let them burn the settlement, and drove them away from the city, and the Lithuanian people soon went back by the same road.
