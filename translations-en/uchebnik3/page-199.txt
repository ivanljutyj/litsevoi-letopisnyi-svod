And in his own regiment the Grand Prince ordered to be the voivode Ivan Yuryevich, and Vasily Obrazets with the men of Borovsk, and Prince Semyon Ryapolovsky with the Suzdalians and Yuryevians, and Prince Alexander Vasilyevich with the Kalugians and Aleksinians, and with the Serpukhovians and Khotunians, and with the Muscovites and Radonezhians, and with the Novotorzhians, and Prince Boris Mikhailovich Obolensky with the Mozhaiskians and Volochians, and with the Zvenigorodians, and with the Ruzhans, and Vasily Sabur with the Galichians and Yaroslavlians, and with the Rostovians, and with the Uglichians and Bezhichians; and in his own regiment he also ordered all the Pereslavlians and Muromians to be.

And from the same camp, from Palin, the Grand Prince sent his voivodes to Novgorod to seize Gorodishche and the monasteries, so that they would not set fire to them.

And he ordered Prince Daniil Kholmsky with the Pereslavlians and Kostromans to go to Bronnich, and Prince Ivan Striga with the Vladimirians, and Fyodor Davydovich with the Kolomnians, and Grigory and Ivan Nikitichi with the Dmitrovians and Kashinians, and Prince Semyon Ryapolovsky with the Suzdalians and Yuryevians; and he ordered them to stand at Bronnich and wait for tidings from him; and his other voivodes — at Lake Ilmen, at Vozvadne and at Uzhin, likewise awaiting tidings.

On November 21, the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos into the Temple, the Grand Prince stood at St Nicholas in Tukhol, and from there sent his envoy Petyulya Payusov to Pskov, and with him he sent the Pskovian Khariton; and the Grand Prince ordered his viceroy Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky with the Pskovians to go on his service, against Novgorod with an army, with cannons and handguns and crossbows, and with all the equipment used for assaulting a city: “And when you come to the mouth of the Shelon, you shall send word of this to me, the Grand Prince, at once; and I will then tell you where you are to be with the Pskovians.”

On November 23, Sunday, the Lord Archbishop of Novgorod, Feofil, came to the Grand Prince at Sytino, and with him the Novgorod posadniks: Yakov Korob, Fefilat Zakharyin, Luka and Yakov Fedorov, Luka Isakov Polinaryin; and from the prosperous townsmen: Alexander Klimentov, Efimy Medvednov, Grigory Kiprianov Arzubiev, Filipp Kilsky, and the merchant Yakov Tsarishchev.

And the archbishop began to petition the Grand Prince, saying: “Lord Sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus! I, lord, your pilgrim, and the archimandrites, and the hegumens, and all the priests, all seven cathedrals of Great Novgorod, beat their brows to you, their sovereign Grand Prince.
