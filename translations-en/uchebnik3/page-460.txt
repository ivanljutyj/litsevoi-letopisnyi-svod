In the year 7055 from Adam (the year 1555 from Christ)

On the 15th day of September, Wednesday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus set out from Moscow to the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery for the day of the memory of the wonderworker Sergius; and he heard the all-night vigil, matins, a prayer service, and the liturgy, and gave the brethren alms and sufficient provisions, and from there he traveled through his villages, to Vorobyevo, Denisyevo, Pochinki, and to Mozhaisk to pray to St Nicholas, and he went to the monasteries: to Volok, Rzhev, Tver, Novgorod the Great, Pskov, to the Pechersky Monastery, and returning back he was in Pskov and Novgorod, and in the (monastery) of the Most Pure (Mother of God) at Tikhvin; and he came to Moscow on December 12, Sunday.

In the same autumn, on the 20th day of September, Kazan princes came from Kazan to the Grand Prince to serve: Prince Kulush, Tereul, Burnash, and the brothers of Chura Narykov, seventy-six persons in all; but the tsar had beaten others in Kazan—Prince Chura Narykov, Prince Baubek, Prince Kadysh—and many others he beat, and he began to rule Kazan together with the Crimean princes.

In the same autumn, on the 6th day of December, the mountain Cheremis, Tugai and his companions, two Cheremis, sent to the Grand Prince to petition him that the sovereign show them favor and send an army against Kazan, while they wished to serve the sovereign with his voivodes.

In the same winter the Grand Prince sent Prince Alexander Borisov Gorbaty and his other voivodes to conquer Kazan places at the petition of the mountain people.

And the Grand Prince's voivodes went as far as the mouth of the Sviyaga and conquered many Kazan places, and brought one hundred Cheremis to Moscow.

On the counsel of the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus with his father Metropolitan Macarius, and on the Grand Sovereign's wish to marry, and on the blessing to sit in the tsardom, in the great principality

In the same winter, on the 13th day of December, Monday, Grand Prince Ivan began to consider marrying and took counsel about it with his father Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus.
