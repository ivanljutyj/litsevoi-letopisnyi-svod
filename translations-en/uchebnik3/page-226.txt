On the Moscow Fire

On the 29th day of March, at the sixth hour of the night, from Thursday to Friday, Moscow caught fire inside the city near the Church of Peter the Wonderworker, which is at the Ugreshsky court, because there were cookhouses outside the city beneath the city wall, and those cookhouses caught fire, and from that the roof on the wall caught fire, and likewise the buildings that were in the city, while all the people were asleep; and they began shouting from Zarechye that the city was burning, but no one in the city had seen it. And it burned all that night and four hours of the day, until the Grand Prince himself with many people pulled down the burning structures and extinguished it.

On the Falling of Stars

In the same month, beyond the Oka River, on the 30th day, at the fifth hour of the night, in the Aleksin area many saw that stars fell from the heavens to the earth like rain; and striking the earth, they scattered like sparks and became invisible.

On the Bells

In the same month, on the 31st day, Saturday, at Little Matins, many heard that the Moscow bells in the square sounded of themselves, as they sound after ringing; and those people in the courtyards who heard it heard it as though the ringing of the Simonov bells was sounding.

On the 11th day of April, from Thursday to Friday at night, they sounded again; and Gridya, the metropolitan's keykeeper, heard it, and on that Friday early in the morning his steward Sukhan told the metropolitan, and Vladimir and his son Golova were there; and Archpriest Fyodor of the Annunciation was there at that time as well.

That same spring Nikita was installed as Bishop of Kolomna.

That same summer they dismantled the old church at the Trinity court, for it was very rotten; and they laid the foundation of a new Church of the Epiphany in the city on the same spot.

On the Brother-in-Law

That same summer Andrei, the Grand Prince's brother-in-law, son of Thomas, Despot of the Morea, came from Rome to Moscow.

That same spring the Crimean Tsar Aidar was captured and sent into confinement at Vologda.

On Tsar Akhmat, How He Came to the Ugra

That same summer news came to the Grand Prince that Tsar Akhmat was coming with the whole Horde, and with tsareviches, and ulans, and princes, and moreover was in agreement with King Casimir; for the king had urged him against the Grand Prince, wishing to destroy Christianity.
