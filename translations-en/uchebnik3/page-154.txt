And then the whole multitude of Orthodox Christians went to the tomb of the saint, desiring only to touch the tomb of the saint.

Some reverent priests, during the bringing of the honorable relics of the saint, saw a white dove above his tomb, soaring very high, until they covered the relics of the saint, and then it became invisible.

When the time of the liturgy came, the metropolitan himself served in the chamber, in the Church of the Deposition of the Robe in his courtyard, and he ordered the bishops and all the priests to serve in the Cathedral of the Archangel.

And then the grand prince ordered sufficient alms to be distributed to all, also to all the cathedral churches, and throughout the city—to priests and monasteries and the poor.

And the bishops and princes, and the brothers of the grand prince, and the archimandrites, and the senior priests, and many boyars ate at the table of the grand prince; and all the cathedral clergy ate and drank in his courtyard. And from that day they established this feast of the translation of the relics of our holy father the Most Reverend Metropolitan Peter, Wonderworker.

About Fryazin, How He Went with the Tsarevna from Rome

In that same year, on the 23rd day of the month of May, Ivan Fryazin came to Rome to Pope Sixtus and to Cardinal Bessarion; and Ivan Fryazin and those with him were shown great honor by the pope and by the tsareviches, the sons of Thomas, Andrew and Manuel, and were given great gifts; and they were there 32 days.

On the 24th day of June, Tsarevna Sophia was sent from Rome to the grand prince; and with her they sent as envoy from the pope Legate Anthony, and with him many Romans; and from the tsareviches an envoy with her—Dmitry Manuilovich with many Greeks; and many other Greeks also went with her to serve her.

And they went not by the route by which Fryazin had traveled, but through the papal domain toward

And the pope sent his letters to all cities, and also to the places through which they were to pass, even as far as the patrimony of the grand prince, to Pskov; and he wrote to them that all the princes of those lands, and honorable lords, and bishops, and all the lands wherever the tsarevna might come, should meet her and show her honor, and give provisions, and carts, and guides, and to all those who were going with her, even as far as the patrimony of the grand prince.

And according to those papal letters all those lands showed great honor to Tsarevna Sophia and to all who were with her.

In that same year, on the 26th day of June, news came to the grand prince from Perm that the commander Prince Fyodor Pestroy had taken the Perm land.
