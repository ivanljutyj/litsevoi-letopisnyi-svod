The princes and the whole land of Kazan sent their envoys, Prince Urazly and Andyrchey-Afyza, to petition that the sovereign show them favor and release Tsar Shigaley to them without delay. And Ostafy Andreev said to the Grand Prince that in Kazan the seid and the ulans, princes, murzas, and the whole land of Kazan had sworn an oath to the Grand Prince, that they would be inseparable from the Grand Prince and Tsar Shigaley until their death.

In the same spring, on April 7, the Grand Prince released Tsar Shigaley to Kazan, and with him he sent to install him as tsar his boyar Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky, and with him boyar Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Paletsky and clerk Postnik Gubin. And boyar Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky with his companions installed the tsar on the throne on June 13, on the Sunday of the Holy and Life-Giving Trinity. And they came to the Grand Prince in Kolomna, and the Grand Prince was then in Kolomna. And Tsar Shigaley sent from himself to petition the Grand Prince concerning his tsardom Chapkyn-murza, and he released Ignaty Yakhontov and his companions to the Grand Prince, and sent great envoys: Alaberdey-ulan, Prince Tevekel, and the bakshey Agish-Mollazada, and the courier was Chepkun-murza Otushev.

And after the arrival of the Grand Prince’s boyars, news came to Kazan to Tsar Shigaley that the Kazanians had brought Tsar Safa-Girey to the Kama, and had betrayed the Grand Prince and Tsar Shigaley, and Tsar Shigaley fled from Kazan.

And when news came to the sovereign that the Kazanians had betrayed him and Shigaley had fled from Kazan to unknown places, the sovereign sent Lev Andreevich Saltykov to meet the tsar in the Field, and with him boyar sons and Tatars, and to Vyatka he sent Alexei Danilovich Basmanov.

And Lev met the tsar in the Field; and Tsar Shigaley, when he fled from Kazan down the Volga by boats, seized horses on the Volga from the Gorodets Tatars and sent his courtier to the Grand Prince with a letter, and wrote in the letter that the Kazanians had betrayed the Grand Prince, had taken Tsar Safa-Girey into Kazan, and had wanted to hand him over to Tsar Safa-Girey, and he had escaped from them from Kazan, and had gone through the Field to the town and reached the outskirts on August 15, thank God, in good health, and he had stayed in Kazan for a month.
