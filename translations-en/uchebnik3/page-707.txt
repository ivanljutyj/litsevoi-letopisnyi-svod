Tsar and Grand Prince sent Karasha and his companions back to the Tsar in the Crimea on August 16, and with him the Tsar and Grand Prince sent to Tsar Devlet-Girei his courier Andrei Mikitin, son of Myasnoy, and a detachment of serving Tatars under Keldish Devlecharov and his companions. And the Tsar and Grand Prince wrote to the Crimean Tsar with Andrei Myasnoy about peace and love, asking that he quickly release his envoy Mirat-murza, son of Sulesh, and with him the envoy of the Tsar and Grand Prince, Afanasy Nagoy, and that before the envoys he send with news Fedor Pisemsky and Andrei Myasnoy. And the Tsar and Grand Prince orders his envoy Yanboldy and his companions, and his own envoy Fedor Ignatievich Saltykov, to be released from Kaluga to Putivl, and the envoys to exchange on the Seim River.

That same summer, on July 8, the Tsar and Grand Prince came from his tour to Moscow with the Tsaritsa Grand Princess and with Tsarevich Ivan. And the sovereign was on tour at the Trinity in the Sergius Monastery, and in Pereslavl-Zalessky; and from Pereslavl in his villages, in Sloboda, in Ozeretskoye, and in Mozhaisk and in Mozhaisk district in new villages, in Vyazma district in the surrounding villages, in Vereya, in Vyshgorod.

In the same month of July, on the 13th day, Tatar couriers came to the Tsar and Grand Prince from the Nogais, from Prince Tinekhmat and from the Nogai murzas: from Prince Tinekhmat his courier Ak-kerei, and from Urus-murza the courier Asmach, and from other murzas.

In the same month of July, on the 22nd day, the commander of the Tsar and Grand Prince, Prince Yuri Ivanovich Tokmakov, went from Nevel to the Lithuanian town of Ozerishche with mounted men, with foot men and with boat men, and he took men with him in boats, and took light cannon with him; and he stood by the town four days, and waged war around the town and on all roads at the abatis that led to Ozerishche.

And Lithuanian men came from Vitebsk to Ozerishche to help, led by rotmisters, and with them twelve thousand mounted and foot, and they began to clear the abatis.

And Prince Yuri Tokmakov sent the artillery train away in boats, and also sent the foot men ahead to Nevel, while he himself with mounted men and streltsy went to meet the Lithuanian men, fought with them and routed the forward regiment utterly, and took some fifty mounted prisoners from them.

And when they fell upon him with all their men, Prince Yuri killed the prisoners and came to Nevel with all his men in safety.

That same summer, on July 25, the commander Vasily Andreevich Buturlin went from Smolensk into the Lithuanian land to Mstislavl with boyar sons, with serving Tatars and with Kazan, Mountain, Cheboksary, Astrakhan, Nogai, and Gorodets Tatars, and with Mordvins, and they waged war.
