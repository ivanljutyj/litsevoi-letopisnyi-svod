On the Death of the Grand Prince of Ryazan

In that same spring, on the 29th day of May, a Friday, at the third hour of the day, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of Ryazan died, and after him there remained his son Prince Ivan, four and a half years old.

On the Lithuanian War with Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania, and on the Arrival of Russian Princes to Grand Prince Ivan

In the same year tidings came to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus that his son-in-law Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania had begun to compel his daughter Elena, his grand princess, to pass from the Greek confession to the Roman, contrary to the kissing of the cross and his confirmed charter.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus sent to him his envoys Prince Vasily Romodanovsky and clerk Vasily Kuleshin on this matter, that he should not compel his daughter Elena, his grand princess, to pass from the Greek confession to the Roman, but should leave her in the Greek confession according to his confirmed charter.

And Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania sent word back to the Grand Prince with his envoys that he refused to refrain from compelling his daughter to accept the Roman confession.

And after that, in the year 7008 from Adam (1508 from Christ), Prince Semyon Ivanovich Belsky came to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich to petition him that the Grand Prince would show him favor and take him into service with his patrimony, since now, he said, they were being compelled to renounce the Greek confession; Grand Prince Alexander, he said, had sent to his grand princess on this matter the apostate from the Orthodox Greek confession, Vladyka Joseph of Smolensk, and the Bishop of Vilna, and Benedictine monks, urging her to pass to the Roman confession, and was compelling the Russian princes and all people in the vicinity of Vilna, and all Russians who adhered to the Greek confession, to pass to the Roman confession.

And to Vladyka Joseph of Smolensk he promised the Kyiv metropolitanate for that, but God sent upon him an infirmity, a paralysis, and he was installed in the metropolitanate while remaining in that infirmity, and he remained in that rank for one year, and died.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, because of that need, showed favor to Prince Semyon Ivanovich and took him with his patrimony, with his formal renunciation; and Prince Semyon Ivanovich came to the Grand Prince on the 12th day of April, on Palm Sunday.
