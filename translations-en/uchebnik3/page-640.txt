At Babiy Gorodok they burned the suburbs, killed many people, and captured a countless multitude of prisoners. Their military raids took place near the Lithuanian border, extending 150 versts along and 100 versts across.

And they joined Tsar Shigalei and the commanders near Yuryev, by God’s grace, safe and sound.

And the tsar and the commanders went toward Yuryev, and along the way they fought on both sides, and they captured the town of Koster, and many of the people who fled from it they killed, and they sent commanders to many places and against many towns, and they burned the suburbs and killed many people.

And when they came near Yuryev, then German foot and horse soldiers, about 500 men, came out of the town and met the commanders and advanced against the forward regiment; and in it were the commanders Prince Nikita Priimkov and Dmitry Pushkin, son of Shaferik, and the boyar and commander Ivan Vasilyevich Sheremetev sent the commander Vasily Veshnyakov, and with him boyar sons. And the commanders Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany and Ivan Menshoy Sheremetev sent boyar sons from the right-hand regiment, and from the forward regiment, and many Cherkasy from the right-hand regiment, and the boyar sons came up and routed the Germans utterly and fought them to the very town, and brought 54 men to the commanders. And the tsar and the commanders stood before Yuryev for 3 days.

Then they went beyond the Umovzha River to Rakobor and sent ahead of themselves Prince Ivan Movkoshev and Bogdan Saburov with their comrades.

And many mounted Germans came out from Rakobor, as also from the town of Muki, and all of them were routed utterly and many others were taken captive; and at the town of Muki they killed many people and burned the suburbs. And the tsar and the commanders went to the right toward the sea, and led the army along the Riga and Kolyvan roads, and fought along the road for 50 versts, and to Kolyvan for 30 versts.

Then they sent commanders to the town of Kongud: Roman Pleshcheev and Shafra Cheglokov, and at Kongud they burned the suburbs and killed many people and captured many prisoners.

Then against the town of Layus they sent streltsy commanders Timofey Teterin and Grigory Kaftyrev, and with them were their centurions and streltsy, and commanders with boyar sons: Mikhail Cheglokov and Semeyka Veshnyakov, and Fedor Uzki, and Tatars and Cherkasy and Mordva. And many gathered at Layus, and the commanders came to the town, burned the suburbs, killed many people, more than 3000 men, and took a multitude of prisoners, and stallions, and all kinds of goods.

Then they sent against the town of Askilus and against the town of Porkel, and at those towns they burned the suburbs and killed many people and captured prisoners.
