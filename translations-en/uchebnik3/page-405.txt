On the 16th day of the same month there came to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, sovereign of all Rus, at Moscow, envoys from Stockholm from the Swedish king Gustav: Gutman Lavrov, Matyash Lavrov, and Olbrecht Ovkimov. They congratulated the great sovereign upon his great realms and petitioned the Grand Prince concerning a truce, that the sovereign might show favor and order his patrimony, Veliky Novgorod and Pskov, to conclude a truce on the same terms as had been under his father, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich. And the great sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich showed favor to the king and dismissed his envoys, and ordered the truce to be concluded with the Novgorod viceroy in his patrimony in Veliky Novgorod on the same terms on which truces had been made with his father, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus.

On the 21st day of the same month there arrived to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus from Crimea Vasily Sergeev, son of Levashev, with his companions; he had been sent by Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich to Crimea to congratulate Islam upon the throne.

In the same month there came to the Grand Prince envoys from Stockholm from the Swedish king Gustav, Gutman Lavrov and Matyash Lavrov, to petition that the sovereign might show favor and order his patrimony, Veliky Novgorod and Pskov, to conclude a truce on the same terms as had been under his father. And the Grand Prince showed favor to the king and ordered the truce to be concluded.

In the same month, on the 21st day, there came to the Grand Prince from Crimea Vasily Sergeev, whom Grand Prince Vasily had sent to Islam to congratulate him upon his accession.

That same winter, in February, on Thursday of the first week of Lent, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, together with her father Daniel, Metropolitan of all Rus, and with the bishops, sang a prayer service and with great tears entreated the miracle-worker Alexis that it might please the saint to have his relics translated into a new shrine.

Their father Daniel the Metropolitan himself, together with the bishops, archimandrites, and abbots, honourably translated him from the old shrine into a new silver shrine; and the Grand Prince himself and his mother, the Grand Princess, with the boyars stood there, performing the prayer service and with great tears entreating the saint.

That same winter, in February, there came to the Grand Prince from Crimea from Tsar Islam Temesh-Knyaz Kitai and Tetcherbei Bakshi.

That same winter Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent to Crimea to Tsar Islam his envoy Prince Vasily, son of Prince Semyon Mezetsky.

That same winter, on March 8, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus sent to Crimea to Tsar Islam his son of a boyar, Daniil Dmitriev, son of Zagryazhsky, with letters concerning friendship and brotherhood.
