He collected all this from the beginning to this moment from various epistles of Kyr Peter, and set forth how the meat-eating of ordinary people is regarded as foul eating; he did not accept it as foul eating, but as a delicacy of bear meat, and such improper meat-eating he proposed to monastic life. Having given heed to passionate thoughts, he adopted the Frankish heresy, passionately introduced the doctrine according to his sinful will, and set forth the ascetic words of the divine Basil and the Life of Pachomius the Great, the God-bearer.
