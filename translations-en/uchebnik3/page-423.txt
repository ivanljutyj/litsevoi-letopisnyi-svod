And he sent to Meshchera to Tsar Shigalei, that with the princes and the murzas and with all the people he should go to Vladimir as well.

And from Kostroma he ordered his voivode Fyodor Ivanovich Shuysky with his companions to go to Vladimir with all the people, to join Tsar Shigalei.

In July a scout from the Field, Alexei Kutukov, came to the Grand Prince and told the Grand Prince that he had seen many people on this side of the Don, on the Snova; regiments marched all day long, and he did not wait for their end.

And with that news the Grand Prince sent to the bank to Prince Dmitry Fyodorovich Belsky and his companions, and ordered them to send for the voivodes to Ryazan and to Ugra, and to Serpukhov, and along the whole frontier, so that they should join him at once.

On the 28th day of July Tsar Saip-Girey came to the town of Osetr, and many Tatars assaulted the town; and Nazar Glebov with the townspeople fought the Tatars beyond the suburbs, and many Tatars were killed.

And Nazar Glebov captured nine Tatars alive and sent them to the Grand Prince.

And those Tatars told the Grand Prince that the Crimean Tsar Saip-Girey himself had come, and with him his son, Tsarevich Minli-Girey, and the whole Crimean Horde, and Prince Semyon Belsky, and from the Nogai Prince Baka with many people, and the Turkish Tsar’s people, with cannons and arquebuses, and many people from other hordes and lands had come.

And the Grand Prince sent with that news to the voivodes on the bank, that they should guard his cause firmly.

And he ordered Tsarevich Shigalei and Prince Yury Bulgakov to go from Pakhra to the bank as well, to join the voivodes.

And from himself he dispatched to Pakhra his voivode Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Shchenyatev and his equerry Ivan Ivanovich Chelyadnin, and with them many people from his court, and ordered them to stand on the Pakhra.

And the Grand Prince himself went to the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure, and fell down before the image of the Most Pure painted by Luke the Evangelist, and fell to his knees, and shedding tears began to pray: “O most holy Lady, Mother of God, Sovereign Lady, show mercy to the Christian race! You had mercy on our great-grandfather Grand Prince Vasily at the invasion of the impious, of the godless Tsar Temir-Aksak; so now send your mercy upon us, his children, and deliver us and the whole Christian race from the godless Tsar Saip-Girey! He has come against me and against the whole Russian land, boasting; send, O Queen, your mercy, lest the impious say: Where is their God, in whom they trust?”
