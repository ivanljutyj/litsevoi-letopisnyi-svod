On the Release from Moscow to the Crimea of the Crimean Tsaritsa Nur-Saltan.
In the year 7020 from Adam (1520 from Christ)

On the 5th day of December, a Friday, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, released from Moscow to the Crimea the Tsaritsa Nur-Saltan, wife of Tsar Menli-Girey, and Saip-Kirey-Saltan, son of Menli-Girey. And together with her the grand prince sent to Tsar Menli-Girey his envoy, his okolnichy Mikhail Vasilievich Tuchkov; and to escort the tsaritsa to Putivl he sent Prince Mikhail Danilovich Shchenyatev.

That same winter a envoy, Shausein-Seit, came to Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, from the Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin for negotiations concerning a firm peace and friendship.

And the grand prince ordered his boyars to speak with him about it. And the grand prince's boyars and the envoy of Tsar Magamet-Amin, Shausein-Seit, agreed that Tsar Magamet-Amin should be with the grand prince in firm peace and agreement, and Shausein-Seit wrote the sworn charter; and on that charter Shausein-Seit gave an oath before the grand prince's boyars that Tsar Magamet-Amin in Kazan, before the grand prince's envoy, would swear that according to that charter the tsar would fulfill everything for the grand prince until his death.

And the grand prince sent to the tsar with that charter, together with Shausein-Seit, his envoy, okolnichy Ivan Grigorievich Morozov, and his dyak Andrei Kharlamov.

And in Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin, before the grand prince's envoy, gave an oath on that charter, and affixed his seal to it, and released Ivan to the grand prince.

That same winter, in February, the Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin sent to Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, his man Buziuk-Bakshei and asked that the grand prince send him another trusted man, namely Ivan Andreevich.

The tsar wrote to the grand prince: for all the evil deeds he had formerly committed, he wished to confess them to the grand prince, and henceforth to be with the grand prince in a firm oath, in eternal peace, in friendship and love.
