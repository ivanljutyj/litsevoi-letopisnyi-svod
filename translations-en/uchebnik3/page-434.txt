Wicked men joined in with this and began to speak against Prince Andrew to the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess, saying that Prince Andrew meant to flee. But the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess did not believe those words, because before that many false reports had been spoken concerning matters between them. And at that time Kazan was not at peace with the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess.

And the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess sent for Prince Andrew to go on the campaign against Kazan. But Prince Andrew did not go to the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess; he declared himself ill and asked that a doctor be sent to him. The Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and his mother the Grand Princess Elena sent the physician Theophilus to him.

And when Theophilus returned, he told the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess that Prince Andrew’s illness was slight: he said there was a sore on his thigh, yet he lay in bed.

But the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess were in great doubt about this, because Prince Andrew had not come to Moscow to them for the great Kazan campaign.

And the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess sent their envoys to Prince Andrew to inquire after his health and about other matters, and secretly to find out about Prince Andrew: whether there was any report of him, and why he had not come to Moscow.

And those envoys, when they arrived, told the Grand Prince that he had newly come people who did not always live with him, but they dared not speak; while those who were acquainted with them told them secretly that the prince lay abed because he dared not go to Moscow.

And Prince Andrew, because of the words of wicked men, did not himself dare to go to Moscow, but sent to Moscow to the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess his boyar Prince Fyodor Dmitrievich Pronsky. But Prince Fyodor Pronsky had not yet arrived in Moscow from Prince Andrew at Staritsa to the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess.

And at that time a son of a boyar of Prince Andrew, Prince Vasily, son of Fyodor Goluboy, of the Rostov princes, from Staritsa, secretly sent by night to the Grand Prince’s boyar Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina his man Eremka with news that Prince Andrew would certainly flee in the morning.

And Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina told the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess about this, that in the morning Prince Andrew would flee.
