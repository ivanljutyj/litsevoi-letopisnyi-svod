“If you want, I will give you chambers; or if any supplies have burned, then take everything that is mine”; he thought that he was weeping about that.

But he, after much weeping, began to grow weak in body, for his arm began to fail, and then his leg. And to the Grand Prince, who was present there, the metropolitan began to say: “Son! God has so willed concerning me; let me go to a monastery.” But the Grand Prince did not allow his will to be fulfilled, that he should go beyond the city to a distant monastery; and they took him to the nearby Epiphany Monastery, to the Trinity courtyard. And when they had brought him there, he at once sent for his spiritual father, and partook of the holy mysteries, and ordered himself to be anointed with oil.

He instructed the Grand Prince and spoke of only one thing: that the church be completed, for at that time it was built only to the half, to the great band, where they had begun to make icon cases for the saints on all three walls.

Then he began to give instructions about that same church business to Vladimir Grigorievich and his son Ivan Golova, and he instructed them concerning what he had prepared for the completion of the church — “but only take care, for it is already prepared”; likewise to the other builders of that church he spoke of all this without ceasing, and concerning the people whom he had hired for that church work, ordering that they be released after his death.

To all who came to him — princes and princesses, boyars, priests, and all Orthodox Christians — he gave peace and blessing, forgiveness, and the last kiss, and he himself also asked forgiveness of all; and so that day passed.

And on the 5th day of April, that same night, when the first hour of the night was ending, he departed to God. Many spoke of this, that they saw a vision in the church. After his death they found under his shirt on his body great iron chains, which can be seen even now on his tomb; but before that they were unknown to his spiritual father, to his cell attendant, and to anyone else.

On the 7th day of the month of April they placed him in a coffin in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God, which he himself had begun to build, with psalmody and with many tears. Present then at his burial were the Grand Prince and his mother, and his son, and a multitude of boyars and nobles, and all the people of the city of Moscow. Of the bishops, only Prokhor of Sarsk was at his burial, together with the Moscow archimandrites, archpriests, abbots, and all the priests of the city of Moscow. And his coffin was near the northern church doors, where the coffin of His Grace Metropolitan Jonah was, entering the northern church doors on the right side.
