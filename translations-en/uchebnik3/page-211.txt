And in the half of Yuryev Monastery that the grand prince took there were 720 obzhas; and at Arkadievsky, 333; and at the Annunciation, 253; and at the St Nicholas monasteries of the Nerevsky End, 251; and at St Anthony’s Monastery, 50 sokhas; and at St Michael’s Monastery, 100 obzhas less three. And of the villages that had been held by Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky, the grand prince took six villages for himself, and in them there were eighty sokhas less two.

On Thursday, January 8, the vladyka came from Novgorod to the grand prince with the posadniks, the prosperous townsmen, and the black people to petition: that the sovereign would show favor to his patrimony, so that the Christian people might not perish, for it was hard in the city—pestilence among the people and famine.

And the grand prince ordered his boyars to speak with them about the tribute, that they should declare the tribute from all the Novgorod volosts: half a grivna per sokha, seven dengas.
