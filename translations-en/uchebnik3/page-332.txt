On the Renewal of the Holy Icons

In that same year the most holy Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, having taken counsel with his lord and son, the faithful Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, sent to the city of Vladimir and commanded the priests of Vladimir to bring the old holy icons to the glorious city of Moscow, to restore and renew them, since over many years they had grown old and dilapidated.

At the same time the faithful and Christ-loving Grand Prince Vasily, having received a blessing from his father Metropolitan Varlaam, went to the Life-Giving Trinity, to the monastery of the venerable Sergius the Wonderworker, to pray and receive a blessing, wishing to set out on his affair against his foe Sigismund, King of Poland. And he commanded his father Metropolitan Varlaam to meet the holy icons of Vladimir with honor: with crosses, with the whole council, and with the people.

When the holy icons were brought to the glorious city of Moscow, news came to the metropolitan on July 2, a Friday, the feast of the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Pure Mother of God. Then the most holy Metropolitan of all Rus, Varlaam, with bishops and archimandrites, with hegumens and the whole sacred council, went out to meet the holy icons with precious crosses, with honors, with psalmody and supplications. And also a great multitude of the people of the glorious city of Moscow: princes and boyars, merchants, elders with youths, mothers and maidens, monks and nuns, men, women, and infants, with great joy met the holy icons in the field beyond the posad. And the metropolitan himself with the council met the holy icons of Vladimir at the Sretensky Monastery in the posad: the image of the Lord God our Savior Jesus Christ Almighty, of wondrous Greek workmanship, and the image of the holy and all-honored Queen, the Virgin Mary, Mother of God, Mother of that same Christ our King and God.

And having performed there the supplicatory singing and divine service, they went into the city and placed the holy icons in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God, glorifying and thanking Christ our God and His Most Pure Mother.
