And the merciful God looked down from the height of heaven and poured out the bounties of His goodness upon us, His ungrateful servants, and not according to our sins granted us His grace and help against the adversaries and victory): the reigning place, the populous city of Kazan, with all who dwell in it, He delivered into our hands, and He drove out the falsehood of Mohammed and planted the life-giving Cross in the desolate abomination of Kazan. And all the infidels living in it perished without trace in a single hour by the judgment of God, and the Kazan tsar Ediger-Magmet alone was found alive among us. And by God’s gift and by His holy will, and by your holy prayers, the city of Kazan, formerly impious, has now, God granting, been sanctified in the name of the Life-giving Trinity, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, the indivisible Divinity; and we walked along the city walls with crosses, and we raised up a cathedral church in the name of our most pure Mother of God (her honored Annunciation), according to your former blessing and counsel. And we raised other churches in the name of the Lord and His saints. And by God’s mercy and your prayers, from all the limits of Kazan all the people of the land, the Ar and Lugovaya people, have submitted to us and promised to pay us tribute. And there we, by God’s grace, to preserve the cities and lands, left our voivodes and many men. And we ourselves, with the great gift of God, have come in health hither to the image of the most pure Mother of God, to the relics of the great wonderworkers, to your holiness, and to our ancestral place. And we bow our heads before you, our father and man of prayer, and before all the sacred council together with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and all our host, for your labors and prayers, for through your prayers God has wrought these great wonders.” And before the sacred council the pious tsar, with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and all the host, bows down to the ground. “And now I bow my head before you, that you may show favor: strive and pray to God for our sinning and for the ordering of the land, so that through your holy prayers the merciful God may send us His mercy and preserve the flock
