On the Repose of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus’

In that same autumn, on the twenty-seventh day of October, from Monday to Tuesday, in the first hour of the night, the right-believing and Christ-loving Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Sovereign of All Rus’, departed this life; he had spent forty-three years and seven months in the sovereignty, in the great reign, after his father Grand Prince Vasily Vasilievich. And in all he was sixty-six years and nine months old.

And they laid his body in the new church of the holy and great Archangel Michael, which the prince had founded during his lifetime in the most renowned and glorious city of Moscow, where his forefathers also lie.

The Beginning of the Sovereignty of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus’

In that same autumn, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus’, after his father, examined the oath charters of Tsar Mengli-Girey which had been made with his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus’ concerning friendship and brotherhood, and they had been written also for them, the children; and after consulting with his brothers and boyars, he sent his close associate Vasily Naumov as envoy to Tsar Mengli-Girey in the Crimea on the seventh day of December to announce to the tsar the repose of his father and to agree with him concerning friendship and brotherhood according to the oath charters.

On the Baptism of Tsarevich Peter

In that same autumn Tsarevich Kudaikul, son of the Kazan Tsar Abreim, sent a petition to the Most Reverend Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus’, that he would grant him favor by interceding with the Sovereign Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus’, so that the sovereign would grant him favor and order him to be baptized into the Orthodox faith.

And through the metropolitan’s intercession, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich ordered the tsarevich to come before him; and he began to petition him with tears concerning baptism.

And by command of the Grand Prince, on the twenty-first day of December, a Sunday, the Most Reverend Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus’, ordered Archimandrite Afanasy of the Savior to baptize Tsarevich Kudaikul on the Moscow River, at the secret passage, in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, into the Orthodox faith of the Greek confession, and he gave him the name Peter in holy baptism.

Present at that baptism was also the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich himself, Sovereign of All Rus’, with his younger brothers and with the boyars.
