And on that same day there came to the grand prince from his Pskov viceroy, Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, and from the Pskov host the Pskov posadnik Vasily Epimakhov, with the news that his, the grand prince’s, viceroy Prince Vasily Vasilyevich with the Pskov host had come for the work of his sovereign, with everything with which he had ordered them to come to him, and asking where he would order them to take up position. And the grand prince ordered Prince Vasily to take up position at Biskupitsy, and the Pskov posadniks with the leading men he ordered to take up position in the village of Fedotya, belonging to the wife of Isak Polinaryin, and the other young Pskovians he ordered to take up position at the Trinity Monastery on Varyazh, and also at Klopsk.

And in the same month, on the 6th day, the grand prince ordered his master Aristotle Friazin to build a bridge over the Volkhov River below Gorodishche; and that master built such a bridge below Gorodishche on boats on that river that even when the grand prince, having prevailed, returned to Moscow, the bridge still stood. But let us return to what came before.

In the same month of December, on the 7th day, the lord archbishop came to the grand prince from Novgorod with the same aforementioned posadniks and wealthy men, and with them five commoners from the five Ends: from the Nerevsky End, Avram Ladozhanin; from the Gornychansky End, Krivoy; from the Slovensky End, Zakhar Brekh; from the Zagorodsky End, Khariton; and from the Plotnitsky End, Fedor Lytka.

The lord archbishop, the wealthy men, and the commoners petitioned about the same matter as before, that the sovereign would show mercy and would grant that they be allowed to speak with his boyars; the grand prince ordered his boyars Prince Ivan Yuryevich and Fedor Davydovich, and Prince Ivan Striga, together with the boyars Vasily and Ivan Borisovich, to speak with them. And the lord archbishop with all his posadniks, wealthy men, and commoners petitioned.
