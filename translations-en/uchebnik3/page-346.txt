The Same Miracle Was Seen by Another Widow

And yet another certain widow, a kinswoman of Ivan Tretyakov, the tsar’s treasurer, who was then sitting in the upper chamber of his house, which was near the same Frolov Gates, saw through the window the same wondrous vision.

Vision to the Sexton at Dorogomilovo

There was also then known another apparition after this wondrous vision, at the Church of the Annunciation of the Holy Theotokos at Dorogomilovo, where stands the house of the archbishops of Rostov, above the Moskva River. Once the sexton was going to that church and saw the great hierarch Leonty the Wonderworker hastening to the church and saying to him: “Hurry, hurry, open the church to me, into which I am going; I will put on my consecrated raiment, so that I may immediately overtake the most holy metropolitans who are going with the sacred council from this city.”

And immediately he entered the church, clothed himself in all the splendor of a hierarch, and quickly went toward the city. Some relate that in that church at Dorogomilovo, from ancient years, the sacred vestments of the wonderworking hierarch Leonty had been kept, and from that time they were not there, and have not been found anywhere to this day, for the confirmation of the glorious miracle. O, the great mercy of God! O, the immediate deliverance of Christians from enemies!

For as soon as people thought to turn to repentance, and placed all hope in God and in the Most Pure Theotokos, and called upon the great Russian wonderworkers and all the saints in prayer, the most merciful God vouchsafed some to see such wondrous visions. And on that same day messengers came saying that the impious Tatars with their godless tsar, seized by great fear, had fled, pursued by no one, for the power of God drove them out through the unfailing intercession of the Most Pure Theotokos and the prayers of all the saints.

How God Did Not Permit the Tatars, Who Wished to Burn the Suburbs of Moscow, and of Their Flight

Let us not be silent about this miracle either. When the godless tsar Magamet-Girey with all his host swiftly wished to attack the city of Moscow, he sent troops ahead of him, ordering them to burn the suburbs of the city of Moscow.
