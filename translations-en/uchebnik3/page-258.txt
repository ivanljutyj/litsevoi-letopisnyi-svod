On the Treason and Imprisonment of Prince Andrei Vasilievich and His Children

In the year 7000 from Adam (the year 1500 from Christ).

On the 20th day of September, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus renounced the oath he had sworn on the cross to his brother Prince Andrei Vasilievich because of the latter’s treason: for he had betrayed him, violated the cross-kissing oath, plotted against Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, his elder brother, together with his brothers Prince Yuri and Prince Boris, and Prince Andrei, and had brought them to kiss the cross that they would stand together against the Grand Prince, their elder brother; and he sent his letters to Lithuania to King Casimir, allying with him against the Grand Prince; and he himself, together with his brother Prince Boris, departed from the Grand Prince; he sent his letters to Akhmat, Tsar of the Great Horde, summoning him with an army against the Grand Prince and the Russian land; and he did not send his commander and his troops with the Grand Prince’s army against the Horde Tsar, and by all this he committed treason against the Grand Prince, violating the cross-kissing oath.

And for this the Grand Prince ordered him seized and imprisoned in Moscow at the Treasury Court; and he sent to Uglich for his children, Prince Dmitry and Prince Ivan; on that same day he ordered Prince Vasily, son of Prince Ivan Yuryevich, and with him many boyar sons to be seized and imprisoned in Pereyaslavl; and they did so.

And to Uglich and Mozhaisk the Grand Prince sent his governors, for at that time Mozhaisk belonged to Prince Andrei.

But to his brother Prince Boris Vasilievich of Volotsk and to his children, because of their guileless nature, he did no harm in anything.

On the Market

In that same year, in September, on St Sergius’s day, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich moved the market from the Trinity to the town of Radonezh.

On the Tver Land

In that same year Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich sent to survey the Tver lands in the Moscow manner, by sokhas; and Tver was surveyed by Prince Fyodor Alabysh, Staritsa was surveyed by Boris Kutuzov, Zubtsov and Opoki were surveyed by Dmitry Peshkov, Klin was surveyed by Pyotr Lobash Zabolotsky, Kholm and Novy Gorodok were surveyed by Andrei Karamyshev, and Kashin was surveyed by Vasily Karamyshev.
