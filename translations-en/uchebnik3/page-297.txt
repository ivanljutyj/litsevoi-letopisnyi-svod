On the Consecration of an Archbishop in Novgorod

That same winter, on January 15, a Thursday, Serapion, abbot of the Trinity-Sergius Monastery, was consecrated archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov by His Eminence Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus.

On the Consecration of an Archbishop in Rostov

And on the 18th day of the same month, a Sunday, Vassian, archimandrite of Simonov, was consecrated archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl by Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus.

On the Wedding of Tsarevich Peter

In that same month of January, on the 25th day, a Sunday, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus granted favor to the newly baptized Tsarevich Peter and gave his sister, Grand Princess Evdokia, in marriage to him; and Athanasius, archimandrite of the Savior, married Tsarevich Peter and Grand Princess Evdokia in the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God of Her Honorable Dormition in the glorious city of Moscow.

On the Envoys

That same winter, envoys from Alexander, King of Poland—Pan Yuri Glebovich, the scribe Ivan Sopezhin, and Ivan Fedorov, son of Plyuskov—came to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus on February 15, a Sunday.

On the Campaign against Kazan of Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and the Grand Prince’s Commanders

That same spring, in April, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus sent his brother Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and his commanders to Kazan against Tsar Magamet-Amin.

In the river fleet, in the great regiment, were Prince Dmitry Ivanovich and with him the Grand Prince’s commanders Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Belsky, Dmitry Vasilyevich Shein, and Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Paletsky; and in the great regiment also, with the artillery, were the commanders Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Shkurlya, Andrey Vasilyevich Saburov, and Dmitry Ivanovich Ralev. In the advanced regiment were Prince Mikhail Fyodorovich Kurbsky and the commander of Prince Yuri Ivanovich, Prince David Daniilovich Penkov. In the right-hand regiment under Prince Dmitry were Prince Fyodor Borisovich Volotsky and the Grand Prince’s commanders Prince Mikhail Fyodorovich Mikulinsky and Prince Vladimir Andreyevich Mikulinsky, and in the left-hand regiment were Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Repnya and Pyotr Ivanovich Zhitov.
