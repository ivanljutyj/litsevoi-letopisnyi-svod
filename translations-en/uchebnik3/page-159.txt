And Fryazin came to the Grand Prince with that Trevisan, calling him a little prince of Venice and his nephew, and said that he had come to him on his own business and as a guest; but they concealed the truth from the Grand Prince.

At that time Anton brought letters from Pope Paul to the Grand Prince, saying that the Grand Prince’s ambassadors might freely travel to Rome through all the Latin, German, and Italian lands and through all those lands, if they swore allegiance to his papacy, even unto the end of the age; and that he should send his people for the tsarevna Sophia, daughter of Thomas, Emperor of Amorea.

That same autumn Metropolitan Philip ordered stone to be prepared for the building of the church of the Holy Mother of God.

That same autumn, on the eighth day of November, Philotheus was consecrated Bishop of Perm by Metropolitan Philip.

On the thirtieth day of the same month, Theophilus, designated Archbishop of Great Novgorod, came to Moscow to be consecrated, and with him came the posadniks Alexander Samsonovich and Luka Feodorovich.

That same winter, on the eighth day of December, Theodosius, Archimandrite of Chudov, was consecrated Bishop of Ryazan by Metropolitan Philip; at his consecration were Vassian, Archbishop of Rostov; Evfimy, Bishop of Suzdal; Gerontius of Kolomna; Prokhor of Sarai; and Philotheus of Perm.

Concerning Theophilus, Archbishop of Novgorod, and how he was consecrated

On December 15, a Sunday, Theophilus, designated Archbishop of Novgorod, was consecrated by His Grace Philip, Metropolitan of All Rus; at his consecration were all the previously named Russian bishops, archimandrites, archpriests, honorable abbots, and the whole consecrated council of the glorious city of Moscow.

After his consecration he petitioned the Grand Prince on his own behalf and on behalf of all Great Novgorod, with the posadniks and the tysyatskys and all those who had come with him, concerning the captives, Kazimir and his other companions.

The Grand Prince accepted their petition and dismissed them all with honor; and there were thirty of them in all in Moscow.

And he released the archbishop himself on the twenty-third day of the same month.

That same winter they carried stone to Moscow for the building of the church of the Most Holy Mother of God.
