The Slavkova and Nikitina streets, with all their street residents, and Isak Polinariin’s sons, Luka and Vasily; and Vasily Ananyin, the posadnik, with the others previously named, began to answer the complaints against them; and at the Grand Prince’s was then his pilgrim, Archbishop Theophilus, and also the Novgorod posadniks. And he began to judge them, and judged them, and investigated, and acquitted the complainants, and convicted all those who had attacked, beaten, and robbed.

And the Grand Prince ordered Vasily Ananyin, Bogdan Esipov, Fedor Isakov, and Ivan Loshinsky to be seized; and the boyar sons took them: Vasily Ananyin and Ivan Tovarkov by Bogdan Rusalka, Fedor Isakov by Nikita Beklemishev, and Loshinsky by Zvenets.

And all their companions the Grand Prince ordered his bailiffs to release on strong surety for the fifteen hundred rubles demanded by the plaintiffs, and for their own guilt without a fixed term; and the archbishop stood surety for them.

And then the Grand Prince sent Ivan Afanasov and his son Alfery away from him, and ordered them seized too, because they had plotted to give Veliky Novgorod over to the King; and Vasily Kitay seized Ivan, and Yury Shestak seized his son.

Then on the third day, a Tuesday, Archbishop Theophilus came to the Grand Prince at the Gorodishche, and with him the posadniks, to petition on behalf of all Novgorod concerning the arrested boyars, that he would show favor, have mercy, remit their punishment, and release them on surety.

But the Grand Prince did not accept the petition of the archbishop and Novgorod, and answered them thus: “It is known to you, our pilgrim, and to all Novgorod, our patrimony, how much grief was caused by those boyars even before this; and now whatever evil there is in our patrimony is all done by them; how then can I show them favor for this evil of theirs?”

And on that same day he sent them in chains to Moscow for confinement with his bailiffs; and he ordered them, chained, to be sent to various towns.

On the ... day of December, a Friday, the archbishop came to the Grand Prince at the Gorodishche, and with him many posadniks: Vasily Kazimir with his brother Yakov, Zakhar Ovin with his brother Kuzma, Fefilat Zakharin, Afanasy Astafyev Gruz, Luka and Yakov Fedorov, and other posadniks and tysyatskys, boyars, and many prosperous townsmen, to petition the Grand Prince on behalf of all Veliky Novgorod concerning the guilty men, Grigory Tuchin, Vasily Nikiforov, Matvey Seleznev, and all their companions whom the archbishop had taken on his own surety, that the Grand Prince would show favor, have mercy, remit execution for those guilty people, order their fine to be taken from them according to the charter, and order the plaintiffs’ losses to be recovered from them.
