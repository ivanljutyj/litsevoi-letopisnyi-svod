Ismail, and Belek-Bulat-Murza and other Nogai murzas from Astrakhan, and Ivan sent the Kutumov sons and Astrakhan people to fight against the Crimea; and they drove off the tsar’s herds, and ravaged all the uluses of the Shirin princes, and took many people captive and killed them, and with all the people, God granting, returned in health.

And Ismail sent his envoys to the sovereign to petition for favor, that help be sent to him from Astrakhan against the Crimea, and that henceforth the sovereign provide them help against the Crimea and send Cossacks to the Don.

The tsar and sovereign sent back his envoys: Petr Sovin to Prince Ismail, and Semyon Maltsov to his children; and having sent the envoys, he ordered it to be said to Ismail that he would send Cossacks to the Don, and that he should wage war in the Crimea as his strength allowed.

A message from the Don from the murzas

In the month of April the murzas Tokhtar Urazlyev with his brothers sent from the Don to the sovereign Akbolta-Murza, son of Ivak-Murza, and their envoys Karamysh and companions to petition that the sovereign show them favor and permit them to nomadize between the Don and the Volga. And that he render them help against the Crimea. And they petitioned Prince Ismail that they wished to fight in the Crimea together with him.

And they went, they say, after Ismail’s son to fight the Crimean uluses and came to Perekop; but in those places there were no uluses, for they were beyond Perekop; and other Crimean uluses and the Nogais had all been driven beyond the Dnieper, to the Lithuanian side. And they went beyond the Dnieper and found all the Nogai uluses, and all joined Tortar-Murza and companions, and ravaged the Crimean uluses, both near Belgorod and near Ochakov, and along the Bug and the Great Ingul and the Little Ingul rivers, and all the Nogai uluses and the trans-Dnieper region crossed over; together with them they also ravaged the Crimean people.

And they went back past Perekop, and there was no sortie against them from Perekop: all the Crimeans sat under siege from them all winter. And with them were the sovereign’s Cossacks with firearms from the Don, and they came to the Don in health.

Sending to the Don

In the month of May the tsar and grand prince, at the petition of Takhtaramurza and companions, sent to the Don Tyagriberdey-Murza Kepchak the Nogai, whom the Cherkassian Cossacks had previously brought from the Dnieper. And with him he sent Daniil Chulkov, and with him many Cossacks, and ordered them to be together with the murzas and to await news from Ismail, and together to fight the Crimean uluses. And he sent the envoys Tokhtar Karamysh and companions with them as well, and sent with them his charter of favor, permitting them to nomadize between the Don and the Volga, promising that from the sovereign’s people they would suffer no harm.
