On the Consecration of That Cathedral Church

In the month of August, on the 12th day, the great and cathedral apostolic church in the most eminent city of Moscow was consecrated by the most reverend Metropolitan Gerontius; with him at the consecration were Archbishop Vassian of Rostov, Bishop Euthymius of Suzdal, Bishop Prochorus of Sarsk, and the archimandrites, archpriests, and abbots of Moscow with the whole sacred council. Present there then were also the pious Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, his brother Prince Andrei the Younger, their boyars and nobles, and all Orthodox Christians. When the church had been consecrated, great joy came upon the city of Moscow, because after the dismantling of the first church seven years and four months had already passed.

The grand prince ordered that on that day alms be distributed throughout the whole city of Moscow and to the surrounding monasteries, to priests, monks, and all the poor. The metropolitan with the bishops and archimandrites ate and drank with him at his dinner, as did all his boyars and nobles; and the priests of all seven cathedrals ate and drank in another chamber in his courtyard.

The Transfer of the Relics of Metropolitan Peter and the Other Hierarchs into the New Church

In the same month the grand prince sent to his father the metropolitan, asking him to summon the bishops, archimandrites, and other priests to consider how and on what day the transfer of the relics of our holy father, the most reverend Metropolitan Peter the Wonderworker, and of the other hierarchs should take place.

The metropolitan summoned the aforementioned bishops and priests, of whom we have written earlier, and took counsel with them: that Peter the Wonderworker should be taken from the stone coffin and laid in a wooden reliquary still in the same church in which he lay, in St John under the Bells; and on the 23rd day of the same month, before Vespers, a prayer service should be sung there, and then the healing-bearing relics of Peter the Wonderworker should be carried into the new and great church of the Dormition of the Holy Theotokos, and placed in the middle of that church, and Vespers and a prayer service and Matins with praises should be sung; and when the time of the Liturgy came, after singing a prayer service, on the 24th day of the same month, he should be brought and placed in the place prepared for him by the holy altar above the platform, and not covered with anything, and so the Liturgy should be served. And concerning those aforementioned hierarchs, the Russian metropolitans Theognostus, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, and Philip, they decided thus: that on the 27th day of the same month, Friday, before Vespers, all of them should be brought into the new church to the places prepared for them, and after Vespers had been sung, a memorial service should be sung for them, and in the morning on Saturday, with the bishops and the other priests, the Liturgy should be served for them.
