And the Grand Prince, learning of this, set out against him, taking with him the Metropolitan, the bishops, and his brothers, and the tsareviches with all his force.

And when he came to the Volga, the Grand Prince sent out his brothers and the tsareviches with all their forces; and he came to Rudino; and Prince Dmitry crossed over to their side and made peace.

In that same year a son was born to the Grand Prince in the month of July and was named Boris. In that same year the swift Tatars of Sedi-Akhmat reached the Pakhra, and they then captured the wife of Prince Vasily Obolensky, and did much evil to Christians, cutting them down and taking them captive.

When Tsarevich Kaisym learned of this, he set out against them from Zvenigorod, and they scattered over the land; and those he met he struck down and took back the captives; and they, seeing this, fled back.

On the Battle near Galich. In the year 6958 from Adam (1458 from Christ)

The Grand Prince set out against Prince Dmitry, wishing to go to Galich, and word came to him that the latter had gone to Vologda.

And the Grand Prince went by way of the Iledam and the Obnora, intending to march against him at Vologda.

When he was at the Monastery of St Nicholas on the Obnora, word came to him that Dmitry had again returned to Galich.

And the Grand Prince returned down the Obnora and up the Kostroma River. And he came to Zhelezny Borok, to the Monastery of John the Forerunner, and learned that Prince Dmitry was in Galich, that he had many men about him, was fortifying the town and preparing cannon, and had a foot army, while he himself stood before the town with all his force.

The Grand Prince, learning of this, placed his hope in the Lord God and in His most holy Mother, in the great wonderworkers and in the power of the precious Cross, and began to send out his princes and commanders with all their forces; his chief commander was Prince Vasily Ivanovich Obolensky, and there was a great multitude of other princes and commanders.

Then he sent out the tsareviches and all their princes with them. They came before Galich on the 27th day of January; and Prince Dmitry stood on the hill below the town with all his force, not moving from the spot.
