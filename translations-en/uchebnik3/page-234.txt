We then angered God, and God was angry with us and punished us, as a child-loving father, according to the apostle: “Whom the Lord loves, He chastens; a father beats the son whom he loves.” And this is the same God then, now, and forever, who drowned Pharaoh and delivered the Israelites and performed glorious deeds. Repent, Sovereign, from all your heart, and flee under His mighty hand, and promise with all your mind and all your soul to renounce what was before, when it happened to you, as a man, to sin. It is human to sin, that is, to fall, and through repentance to rise; it is proper to angels not to fall, and to demons not to rise but to despair. And you will execute righteous judgment in the midst of the earth. One must have love for neighbors, oppress no one, and be merciful, so as to see God as merciful on the day of the Dread Judgment. Do not repent in words alone, but ponder it in your heart; otherwise God will not accept such repentance—only if what is in words is also in the heart. As the prudent thief on the cross was saved at once, for one word alone, because he truly, with all his heart, recognized his sin and cried to the Creator: “Remember me, Lord, when You come in Your kingdom”; and the Merciful and Generous Lord not only forgave him his sins but made him an heir of paradise. Imitate such repentance, for true repentance consists in renouncing sin. If we repent so, the Merciful Lord will have mercy on us, and not only will He free and deliver us, the Christian people of the new Israel, from this new Pharaoh, the impious Akhmat, son of Izmail, as once He delivered the Israelites from the fierce and proud Pharaoh, but He will subdue them to us. Likewise the Israelites once sinned before God, and God gave them into slavery to foreigners; but when they repented, God chose rulers for them from their tribe and delivered them from slavery to foreigners, and the foreigners were in slavery to them. Likewise, when the Israelites were in slavery in Egypt, the Lord delivered them from Egyptian slavery through Moses His servant. Then the Lord granted them Joshua son of Nun, who led them into the promised land, took twenty-nine kingdoms, and settled there. And after this the sons of Israel sinned before the Lord God, and the Lord God delivered them into slavery into the hands of their enemies. And again they repented, and the Lord God raised up for them Judah from their tribe, and he defeated the Canaanite and the Perizzite and captured their king Adoni-bezek. And Judah commanded that Adoni-bezek’s hand be cut off at the wrist and his foot.
