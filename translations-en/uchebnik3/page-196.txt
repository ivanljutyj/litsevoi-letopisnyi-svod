The Grand Prince set out against Novgorod with his army

On the ninth day of October, Thursday, on the commemoration of the Holy Apostle James Alphaeus, the Grand Prince set out from Moscow for Novgorod to punish them by war for their crime, and he left his son, Grand Prince Ivan, in Moscow; and his brother Prince Andrei the Younger set out together with the Grand Prince.

To Tsarevich Danyar, Kasym’s son, he ordered to go to Klin four days ahead of him, and then to Tver, and then to Torzhok.

The Grand Prince himself went to Volok, and his brother Prince Andrei the Younger was with him until then; and the Grand Prince heard Mass at Volok on October 14, and ate and drank at the house of his brother Prince Boris Vasilyevich. While the Grand Prince was still at Volok, Grand Prince Mikhail of Tver sent to meet him his boyar’s son Khidyarshchik to deliver provisions from his patrimony.

The Grand Prince went from Volok to Mikulin, and then to Torzhok. His brother Prince Andrei the Younger went to the town of Staritsa, and then to Torzhok.

At the first camp from Volok, at Lotoshino, Prince Andrei Borisovich Mikulinsky met the Grand Prince on behalf of the Grand Prince of Tver, to invite the Grand Prince to dine.

On Thursday, October 16, the viceroy Vasily Kitay sent word to the Grand Prince that another envoy had arrived in Torzhok for a letter of safe conduct from the archbishop and from all Novgorod, a well-to-do man Ivan Ivanov Markov; and the Grand Prince ordered that he too be held in Torzhok until his arrival.

On Sunday, October 19, the Grand Prince entered Torzhok; and at that same time the Novgorodian boyars Luka Klementyev and his brother Ivan came to the Grand Prince in Torzhok and petitioned the Grand Prince to take them into service.

On Tuesday, October 21, the Grand Prince sent from Torzhok Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky as voivode and viceroy to Pskov, and as prince to Pskov, according to their petition, with which they had sent Vasily and Nikifor Pechatnikov.

On October 23 the Grand Prince left Torzhok and marched with his army against Novgorod; and he went from Torzhok to Volochek, and from there he went between the Yazhelobitskaya road and the Meta. To Tsarevich Danyar he ordered to go from Torzhok along the other bank of the Meta, and with him the Grand Prince’s voivode Vasily Obrazets Borisovich.
