The great prince ordered his brother Prince Yuri to go to Mozhaisk, while the great prince himself came to Moscow on the fifth day of October.

And he laid disgrace upon his boyars for their wrongdoing: upon Prince Ivan Kubensky, Prince Peter Shuysky, Prince Alexander Gorbaty, Fyodor Vorontsov, and Prince Dmitry Paletsky; and having settled his affairs, he went from Moscow to Mozhaisk on the ninth of that same month of October, and the great prince came to Moscow on the fourteenth of November.

That same autumn, in December, at the intercession of his father Metropolitan Macarius, the great prince restored his favor to his boyars Prince Ivan Kubensky, Prince Peter Shuysky, Prince Alexander Gorbaty, Fyodor Vorontsov, and Prince Dmitry Paletsky.

On the Great Prince's Journey to the Vorya

On the twenty-seventh of that same month the great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus set out from Moscow for his royal hunt on the Vorya, and thence to Vladimir.

And in Vladimir, on January 17, there came to the great prince from Kazan, from the great prince's envoy Ignaty Yakhontov, the boyar's son Rudak Bulatov, son of Mordvinov, with a letter. And the Kazanians sent to the great prince, together with that same boyar's son, their own man Gamet-Shikh with a letter from Abeyurgan-seid, Prince Kadysh, and Chura Narykov. And the great prince listened to the letters of his envoy Ignaty, and Ignaty wrote in the letter that the Kazanians had driven Tsar Safa-Girey out of Kazan and had killed many Crimean people.

And the great prince ordered the Kazan envoy Gamet-Shikh to come to Moscow with the letter, while the great prince himself came to Moscow on the twenty-third day of January and ordered the Kazan envoy to appear before him with the letter. And the Kazanians wrote in the letter that the seid and ulans, the princes, murzas, shikhs, shikhzadas, dolyshmans, Cossacks, and all the land of Kazan petitioned the sovereign to show them favor, to set aside his anger against the land of Kazan, and to grant them a tsar, Shigaley, in Kazan, and to send his boyar's son to Kazan to bring the seid and ulans, the princes, and all the land of Kazan to the oath. And the great prince sent Ostafy Andreyev to Kazan to the seid Beyurgan, to Kadysh, to Chura, and to all the land of Kazan with his gracious word, so that he might bring them to the oath; and together with Ostafy the great prince sent back the Kazan envoy Gamet-Shikh with a letter, and in the letter he wrote to them of his favor.
