On the Nogai Envoys

In the same year the Nogai murzas sent their envoys to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign of all Rus, to petition him that he grant them favor and permit Nogai merchants to travel to Moscow with horses and all manner of goods.

On the Great Fire in Novgorod

In the same year there was a severe drought, and many towns burned, and many villages also, and forests, and grain, and grass burned; and in Veliky Novgorod half the city burned, the whole Trade Side burned, and 2,700 people burned in Novgorod for our sins.

On the Completion of Churches

In the same year they completed the Church of St Michael the Archangel on the Square, and St John under the Bells, and St John the Forerunner at the Borovitsky Gate; and the master of the churches was Aleviz the New, and of the bell tower Bon Fryazin.

On Peace with the Lithuanian King Sigismund.
In the year 7017 from Adam (1517 from Christ)

On September 19, a Tuesday, Lithuanian envoys came to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus from King Sigismund: Pan Stanislav Glebovich, voivode of Polotsk; marshal and secretary Pan Ivan Sopezhich; marshal Pan Vojtech Narbutovich; and scribe Ivashko Bogdanov Sopezhich, proposing peace, love, and an eternal treaty. And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus concluded peace and an eternal treaty with King Sigismund; and the Russian cities with their volosts, his patrimony, and the service princes—Prince Vasily Ivanovich Shemyakin, Prince Vasily Semenovich Starodubsky, and the princes of Novosil, Odoev, Vorotynsk, Belev, Trubetsk, and Masalsk with their patrimonies—he assigned to his side, and the treaty charters were written according to the will of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus.

And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, having honored the king’s envoys, released them to the king on October 10, and ordered his voivodes to go from Bryansk to Moscow and to disband the army.

And the king went from Smolensk to Vilna. And the grand prince ordered Prince Mikhail Glinsky to be with him in Moscow and granted him favor: he gave him Yaroslavets as a patrimony, and Borovsk for his maintenance.
