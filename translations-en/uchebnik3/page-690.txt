In the same year, in November, on the 1st day, there arrived to the tsar and grand prince from Shamakhi, from Abdulla, king of Shamakhi, the envoys Kozemak, Sudaley, and Arslan-aga; and with them were letters to the tsar and grand prince from King Abdulla. And in his letters King Abdulla wrote to the tsar and grand prince: as the tsar and grand prince command him to serve, so he is glad to serve the tsar and grand prince; he also wrote about merchants, asking that the tsar and grand prince order the road opened for merchants and allow them free trade.

In the same month of November, on the 2nd day, there arrived to the tsar and grand prince from Denmark to Moscow the ambassador of the tsar and grand prince, Prince Anton Mikhailovich Romodanovsky, and Ivan Mikhailov, son of Viskovaty, and clerk Pyotr Grigoriev, son of Sovin; the sovereign had sent them to the Danish king, King Frederick, to conclude an agreement concerning Livonia; and they brought to the tsar and grand prince the concluded treaty charter.

In the same month of November, on the 20th day, there arrived to the tsar and grand prince from Lithuania the envoy of King Sigismund Augustus, Andrei Khoruzhev, with a letter. And the king wrote to the tsar and grand prince that from Polotsk the tsar and grand prince's people were encroaching upon the king's land in many places: on volosts, villages, and estates; and they had come in war against the Lukoml princes and had ravaged Lukoml. And constantly they were ravaging his people by war, and were warring on Bobonichi, Orekhovnoe, Plyusnoe, Churaki, Glubokoe, Usvee, Berezveche, Lastovitsi, Zalesye, Zadorozhye, the village of Golomyslo; and many other places they had ravaged. And in the same letter the king wrote about his ambassadors, that he was sending his ambassadors to the tsar and grand prince, and if the ambassadors did not conclude peace with the tsar and grand prince, but a truce should result, then the tsar and grand prince should release his ambassadors to him according to the safe-conduct charter with all their people and property, without quibbling.

And the tsar and grand prince wrote back to the king in his letter with the king's envoy Andrei Khoruzhev, that concerning those volosts, villages, estates, and Koloml, about which the king had written, all that belonged to the Polotsk district, and the people of the Polotsk district who had been disobedient to the tsar and grand prince's governors, the governors had punished them for their guilt. And they had acted justly: every man is free to punish and reward his own; and the king's ambassadors, according to his sovereign safe-conduct charter, might come and go back with all their property without any hindrance, even if the term of the truce should expire.

And he released that courier from Moscow in the same month, on the 29th day.
