By God's wrath the forests and mosses began to burn; and in many places villages burned down with their livestock; and in the forests many beasts and birds perished in the fire; and there was such a great smoke and darkness that the sun's radiance in the clouds could not be seen because of the smoky gloom.

And in the city there was great distress from the stench of smoke, and people did not know where to go.

And those sailing on the lakes and rivers were in great uncertainty: in the darkness they did not know where to sail.

And the God-loving Archbishop Makarii summoned the archimandrites and abbots, the priests and city officials, and all the people, and going around the city with crosses, they held a service of supplication, asking the Lord God for great mercy and temperate air and rain.

And Archbishop Makarii ordered supplication services to be sung daily in all the churches. And the Lord God heard the prayer of His servants, sent great rain upon the earth, and the burning, the smoke, and the darkness ceased.

On the founding of the church and refectory in the Antoniev Monastery

In the same year, on the second day of August, under Abbot Gerontius, a stone church of the Meeting of our Lord Jesus Christ was founded in the Antoniev Monastery, and there also an altar of the venerable Anthony, and a stone refectory.

On the fire in Pskov

In the same year, in August, by God's permission there was a great fire in Pskov, and the whole middle town, where the Moscow merchants live, burned down, and many holy churches burned. The Pskovians could not recall such a fire in many years.

On the repose of the venerable Alexander of Svir

That same summer, on the 30th day of August, Abbot Alexander reposed, who founded the venerable monastery on the Svir River, and having lived there in labor, he departed to the Lord in the monastery which now, by his blessing, is called Alexander's Hermitage.

On the journey of the Grand Prince to the Trinity in the Sergius Monastery.
In the year 7042 from Adam (the year 1542 from Christ)

On the 22nd day of September, a Sunday, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus set out from Moscow with Grand Princess Elena and their children to pray to the Life-giving Trinity and to the venerable wonderworker Sergius.
