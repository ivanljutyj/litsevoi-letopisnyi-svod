On the sending, how the Grand Prince sent his commanders with an army into the Field to aid the Crimean Tsar against the Horde tsars

In the same spring, in the month of May, news came to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich that the Horde tsars Seit-Akhmet and Shig-Akhmet were marching with an army against Tsar Ming-Girey of the Crimea.

The Grand Prince then dispatched to the aid of the Crimean Tsar Ming-Girey his commanders into the Field against the Horde — Prince Pyotr Nikitich Obolensky and Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Repnya Obolensky, and with them many boyar sons of his court; and together with his commanders he sent Tsarevich Satylgan, son of Merdoulat, with the ulans and princes, and with all the Cossacks.

And to the Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin he ordered to send his commanders with an army together with the tsarevich and the Grand Prince’s commanders; and to Prince Andrei Vasilyevich and Prince Boris Vasilyevich, his brothers, he ordered to send their commanders with a force together with his commanders.

And Prince Boris Vasilyevich sent his commanders with the Grand Prince’s commanders, but Prince Andrei Vasilyevich did not send commanders or his force.

And the Grand Prince’s commanders came together with Tsarevich Satylgan and with the commanders of the Kazan Tsar, Abash-Ulan and Bash-Seit, in the Field, and the commander of Prince Boris also caught up with them there, and they went together against the Horde.

And the Horde tsars learned of the Grand Prince’s great army in the Field and, frightened, turned back from Perekop; and the Grand Prince’s army returned home without battle.

On the Church at the New

In the same spring, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, the Spassky Archimandrite Afanasy Shchedry laid the foundation of a stone church at the New in honor of the Transfiguration of our Lord Jesus Christ.
