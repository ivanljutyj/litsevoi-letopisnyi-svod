We petition you, lord sovereign, to take pity on your patrimony, to restrain your sword and quench the fire, and that Christian blood be no longer shed. Lord sovereign, have mercy! And I, lord, your pilgrim, with the archimandrites, abbots, and all the priests of the seven cathedrals, petition you, our sovereign. And also, lord sovereign, you were angry with the Novgorod boyars and took them from Novgorod to Moscow on your first visit; would you, lord sovereign Grand Prince, show favor and take pity, and release those boyars to your patrimony, to Great Novgorod; and I, lord, your pilgrim, with all the aforementioned priests, with the seven cathedrals, petition you, our sovereign.’

And the posadniks and prosperous townspeople petitioned thus: ‘Lord sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus! Your pilgrim, lord, Archbishop Theophilus, stands before you and petitions you, his sovereign Grand Prince; the posadnik, lord, stepennoy Foma Andreevich, and the old posadniks, the tysyatskys, stepennoy Vasily Maksimov and the old tysyatskys, and the boyars, and the prosperous townspeople, merchants and common folk, all Great Novgorod, your patrimony, free men, petition you, their sovereign, that, lord sovereign Grand Prince, you show favor and take pity on your patrimony,’ and the rest as the lord archbishop had said before: ‘And your patrimony, Great Novgorod, petitions you, its sovereign.’ Afterwards they also petitioned concerning the captured Novgorodians, just as the lord archbishop had.

After this Posadnik Luka Fedorov also petitioned and said: ‘Lord sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, your pilgrim the lord archbishop, and the posadniks and prosperous people on behalf of all Novgorod, have petitioned you, their sovereign; this, lord sovereign, is the petition before you; and would you, sovereign, show favor and order your boyars to speak with them, while your pilgrim the lord archbishop and all Novgorod, your patrimony, petition you, their sovereign.’

That day the Grand Prince summoned them to dine with him, and they dined with him that day.

And in the morning the archbishop with all the aforementioned was at the Grand Prince’s brother’s, Prince Andrey Menshoy, with gifts, and they petitioned him to intercede for them before the Grand Prince.

After that, in the morning, they came to the Grand Prince to petition for favor, and they also petitioned that he show favor and order talks with the boyars.
