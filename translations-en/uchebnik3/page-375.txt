And the Grand Prince was in a truce with the Lithuanian King Sigismund, and Peter, the Wallachian voivode, petitioned Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of All Rus', that the great sovereign grant favor and send to the Turkish Sultan, so that for the sake of the great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich the Turkish Sultan would defend them from the Lithuanian King Sigismund.

On the Arrival from the Crimea of the Cossack Temesh, and on Islam, That He Did Not Take the Throne

That same autumn the Grand Prince's Cossack Temesh Kadyshev arrived from the Crimea and told the Grand Prince that Islam had not taken the throne but was standing at Sangir. And Islam sent to the Turkish Sultan to ask that Tsar Saip-Girey be made tsar. And Islam sent his man Budai-Murza with companions to the Grand Prince.

On the Sending to Kazan with a Charter

That same autumn, on December 23, the Grand Prince sent Konstantin Timofeev, son of Zamysky, to Kazan to Tsar Yanalei, to the tsarevna and to the princes, with a charter.

On the Disgrace of Tsar Shigalei

That same winter, in January, Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of All Rus', laid his disgrace upon the former Kazan Tsar Shigalei because he had violated his oath and had begun to correspond with Kazan and with other states without the Grand Prince's knowledge, secretly. And for this the Grand Prince ordered that Kashira and Serpukhov be taken from him; and, having seized him, to exile him to Beloozero with the tsaritsa and place him under guard.

On the Arrival from Lithuania of the Grand Prince's Envoy, and on the King's Man, and on Igumen Gavriil of the Holy Mountain

That same winter, in January, the Grand Prince's envoy Vasily, son of Alfery, arrived from King Sigismund from Lithuania. And with him came the King's man Ivashko Glagol with a charter. And he told Grand Prince Vasily that there had been with the Grand Prince, from the Holy Mountain, from the Panteleimon Monastery, his place of prayer, Igumen Gavriil, who had come for alms, and the great sovereign had granted favor, released him to his place of prayer and sent alms with him to his monastery. And when the igumen was going through the Lithuanian land, he was robbed in Minsk.

And the Grand Prince released the King's man, Ivashka Glagol, and with him sent a charter to the King about the igumen, that he should release him without delay.
