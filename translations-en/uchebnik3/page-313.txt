And the Grand Prince, at the Tsar’s request, sent to him in Kazan his boyar and equerry Ivan Andreevich Chelyadnin, and deacon Elizar Sukov, on carts. And Tsar Magamet-Amin fully revealed his secret to Ivan Andreevich and agreed with the Grand Prince under a firm oath concerning eternal peace, friendship, and love, and sent Ivan and his own envoy Ausein-Seit back to the Grand Prince. And they arrived in Moscow in March.

On the Coming of the Crimean Tsareviches to the Borderlands

That same spring, on the 8th day of May, news came to the Grand Prince that the sons of the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girei, Akhmet-Kirei and Burnash-Kirei, had come secretly with many men to the Grand Prince’s borderlands, to Belev, Odoev, Vorotynsk, and Aleksin.

And the Grand Prince immediately sent against them his voivode and boyar Prince Daniil Vasilievich Shchenya and many other voivodes. But the Tatars, having learned of the Grand Prince’s voivodes, were frightened and soon withdrew.

On the Grand Prince’s Disgrace upon Tsar Abdyl-Letif

At that same time the Grand Prince of All Rus Vasily Ivanovich laid his disgrace upon Tsar Abdyl-Letif for violating the treaty, ordered that he be placed under bailiffs, and took Kashira away from him.

On the Coming of Magnut, the Crimean Tsarevich, to Ryazan

In that same summer, in July, Magnut, the Crimean Tsarevich, came to Ryazan. And news came to him that the Grand Prince’s voivodes were standing on the Osetr—Prince Aleksandr Vladimirovich Rostovsky and other voivodes with many people—and that voivodes were standing on the Upa: Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Bulgakov and Ivan Andreevich and many other voivodes with many people.

And, hearing this, Tsarevich Magnut did not go into the land, but turned back to the borderlands. And the Grand Prince’s voivodes went after him into the Field as far as Sernava, but did not overtake him.

On the Coming of Tsarevich Burnash to Ryazan.
In the year 7021 from Adam (the year 1521 from Christ)

In October Tsarevich Burnash-Kirei, son of Menli-Girei, came to Ryazan with an army, took the ostrog and attacked the city, but achieved nothing against the city and withdrew.
