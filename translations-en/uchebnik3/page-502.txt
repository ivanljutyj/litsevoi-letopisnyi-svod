On the Campaign

And the sovereign went to the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God of the reigning city of Moscow, of Her Honorable and Glorious Dormition, and lovingly falls before Her wonderworking image, and with weeping for a long time offers prayers, and places his hope in Her, and entrusts into Her hands the city and the people and all the kingdom entrusted to him by the almighty hand of Her Son and God, and clearly declares: “You, O Lady, cover this kingdom with Your honorable robe and free it from all evils, guide us on the path of salvation, and grant us to behold Your image in joy and all that is Yours.”

Also, falling with tears before the wonderworking icons of the Lady and of all the saints, he prays; he comes to the wonderworking relics of the wonderworkers Peter and Jonah, and likewise prays long with tears—that only the One God knows.

And he receives blessing from the first hierarch Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, and says to the metropolitan, the archbishop, the bishops, and the whole consecrated council: “Be so good, lords, bestir yourselves at this present time to fasting and prayer, and entreat the Creator and His Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers, that Christ may not be angry with us to the end, and may not remember the sins of my youth and my ignorance, and may not bind according to my sins such a multitude of people, but may look upon His people with His mercies and grant you help and strengthening, and may not deliver us to our enemies, and may free the many Christians tormented by the godless Kazanians, and may work for us help according to His holy will. And I, lords, bow my head before you and beseech your holiness. But you, my lord father Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, strive, as much as God grants you, to entreat our Master Christ to protect our kingdom in all things; instruct our brother in good deeds, and also instruct the boyars remaining here in all things; also, lord, care spiritually in all things for my wife, Tsaritsa Anastasia, who is with child.” The metropolitan and the hierarchs say to the sovereign with tears: “It is fitting for you to stand manfully for the flock entrusted to you and to multiply the talent given to you, so that you may not be called a hireling but a true shepherd, who gives his soul for the sheep; but only, O Tsar, to whom are you leaving us, orphans? Good shepherd, light of our eyes, where are you going?” The sovereign said to them: “We place all our hope and trust in the Most Pure, and I trust in your holy prayers: ask and receive.” The metropolitan blessed the sovereign with the life-giving cross and said: “May the grace of the Holy Spirit be with you, Orthodox Tsar, and with all your Christ-loving host.”

And having given the kiss in Christ, the sovereign receives blessing from all standing there, and with such blessing and prayer the sovereign goes out from the church.
