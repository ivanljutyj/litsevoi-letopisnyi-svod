Now Metropolitan Isidore in his charters began to be styled legate of the throne proceeding from the apostolic side, of Poland, Lithuania, and Germany, and he ordered carried before him a Latin cross, that is, a cross, and three silver maces in honor of the Latin law; in prayers and in the service of the divine liturgy, instead of the holy ecumenical patriarchs, he first commemorated, magnified, and glorified Eugene, Pope of Rome, to whom for gold he handed over the holy faith of Greek Orthodoxy.

And he came to Moscow in the third week of holy Great Lent, and on that day he served a prayer service for the Grand Prince and for all Orthodox Christianity and served the divine liturgy, and first commemorated Eugene, Pope of Rome.

After the end of the divine service he ordered his protodeacon, in a sticharion with an orarion, to enter the ambo and read aloud the charter of the eighth heretical and unrecognized council, in which it was written abominably to God and apostatically that the Latin heresy, dividing the Holy Trinity, asserts that the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and from the Son, and, philosophizing, joins unleavened bread, asserting that both in unleavened and in leavened bread the Body of Jesus Christ must be transformed. And concerning the dead it was written thus: “Whoever in true faith and confession of God has accepted death with humility, but did not manage to bring forth the fruit of repentance for his sins, as the spiritual fathers enjoined, such shall be cleansed after death by torments.” But all this he did, philosophizing, under the guise of the true faith of Orthodoxy for the sake of heresy, in order to deceive and sever Christendom from the divine law. And so he recounted everything that had happened at the council.

And Eugene himself, Pope of Rome, sent with the metropolitan his epistle to the faithful Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich, writing in his charter:

Epistle of Eugene, Pope of Rome, to the Grand Prince concerning Metropolitan Isidore

“Eugene, bishop, servant of the servants of God, sends salvation and apostolic blessing to the most exalted prince Vasily Vasilyevich of Moscow and all Rus, the great tsar. We thank the Almighty Lord God that now, after many labors, by the grace of the Holy Spirit the Eastern Church has united with us, which leads to the salvation of the souls of many people and to your glory and praise. And to this unity and concord have contributed the help and assistance of our most honorable brother Isidore, your Metropolitan of Kiev and all Rus, and legate of the apostolic throne, who labored much for the union.
