By command, and by the service of the governors and of all boyar sons, not one man of our tsar and sovereign perished; but before the arrival of Prince Peter, in many places along the Volga and Kama the Kazanians and Crimeans who lived in Kazan, the Nogais and Bukharans, were killed, and war continued until the petition through many villages, but everywhere by God’s mercy the sovereign’s people were preserved.

In the same year Tsar Shigalei sent Prince Yuri Golitsyn to the sovereign and sent with him the confirmed sworn charters to the sovereign; and in Kazan, by the sovereign’s instruction, boyar Ivan Ivanovich Khabarov and clerk Ivan Vyrodkov remained with the tsar to oversee the release of captives and for other administrative affairs.

And the boyars and governors Prince Yuri Mikhailovich and Daniil Romanovich with all their companions and with the people who had been sent with them went up the Volga to the sovereign.

And governor Prince Dmitry Khilkov went by the Mountain side to Murom, while on the Sviyaga in the New Town there remained boyar and governor Prince Semyon Mikulinsky with his companions and with those people who had to remain with him for a year.

In the same month of the year 59, Prince Ishim with his companions came from Astrakhan, from Tsar Yemgurchei, to Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, and petitioned the tsar and grand prince in the name of Tsar Yemgurchei that the tsar and grand prince grant him favor, order him to serve him together with his Horde, and favor him as he did Tsar Shigalei and other tsars who serve him.
