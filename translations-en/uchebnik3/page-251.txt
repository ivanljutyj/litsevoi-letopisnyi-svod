On the Appointment of Metropolitan Zosima.
In the year 6999 from Adam (1499 from Christ)

By the command of the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, the God-loving archbishops and bishops assembled in the renowned city of Moscow. The pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich announced to them the election of a metropolitan. Present there were Archbishop Tikhon of Rostov and Bishops Nifont of Suzdal, Prokhor of Sarai, and Filofei of Perm; and Archbishop Gennady of Novgorod sent his letter to his brethren the archbishop and bishops: “Blessed be the Lord God, whomsoever the Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers, and also our sovereign Grand Prince Ivan and you, my brethren, the Orthodox archbishops and bishops, desire; and I am with you, my brethren, in this divine and great matter of one will and desire.” And by the grace of God and the election of the Holy Spirit, and by the will of the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich and of all the Orthodox bishops, they elected Zosima, archimandrite of Simonov, as worthy to govern the flock entrusted to him; and he was brought to the metropolitan court on the twelfth day of the month of September, and was consecrated to the metropolitanate of all Rus on the twenty-sixth day of the same month, on a Sunday.

On the Ambassador from Chagatai

In the same month, on the twenty-eighth day, an ambassador named Uruz-Bogatyr came to the Grand Prince from Chagatai, from Sein-Saltan, proposing love and friendship.

Council in Moscow against the Novgorod Heretics

Then also the following event took place in Great Novgorod: by the enemy’s instigation, great confusion spread from a certain Jew and seized people weak in many things, in whom the devil himself had clearly taken up residence. Many of them obeyed his pernicious lie, were corrupted in mind, and fell away from the truth. Heeding the fables of evil men and heretics who falsely brought falsehood against God, they turned to Jewish laws, astrology, and pagan sorcery, and fell away from the grace and truth of the Gospel; and they did not believe in the incarnation of the Son of God, our Lord Jesus Christ, born of the Most Pure Ever-Virgin Mary, and they did not venerate the holy icons, and they held the Body and Blood of Christ as nothing, and committed many other heresies and abominable deeds that cannot even be described. These are the names of those corrupt men: the monk Zakharia, Gavriil the protopope of Saint Sophia, and Denis the priest of the Archangel, and Maxim the priest of Saint John, and Vasily the priest of the Intercession, and Makar the Nikolsky deacon, and Gridya the clerk of Boris and Gleb, and Vasyuk, Denis’s son-in-law, and Samukha the Nikolsky clerk, and others with them.
