On the Birth of a Son to Prince Yuri Vasilyevich

In the same month a son, Prince Vasily Yuryevich, was born to Prince Yuri Vasilyevich of Moscow, Uglich, and Kaluga, by his princess Uliana, daughter of Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Paletsky; he was baptized in the Chudov Monastery, and Metropolitan Macarius received him from the font; and at his baptism were present Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and Prince Vladimir.

On the Dispatch from the Voivodes in the German Land to the Sovereign from Prince Semyon Ivanovich Mikulinsky and His Comrades

In the month of March there came to the tsar and grand prince from the German Land, from his boyars and voivodes, from Prince Semyon Ivanovich Mikulinsky and his comrades, Fedor Andreev, son of Karpov, and from Tsarevich Takhtamysh, Semen-murza Kitai, and they told the sovereign that they had gone into the German Land toward Riga to conquer lands according to his sovereign’s command. And they went into the German Land to the German towns of Alist and Golbin and Chesvin and laid waste a stretch seventy versts wide, and in places a hundred.

And from Chesvin German men came out against the forward regiment, and by the forward regiment they were beaten utterly, and the German voivodes Gedert and Ganus were defeated, and the third, Yanatuv, the archbishop’s seal-bearer, was taken captive, and thirty-four of the best estate-holders were taken alive.

Then they went to Rovnoe and past Kesi, and conquered the Kesi lands, and to Riga.

And at Riga they stood three days, and fought along the sea on both sides of Riga, and then went up the Dvina on both sides of the Dvina toward Kurkonos, while the fighting men went along the Prussian land, along the border with the Lithuanian land, and the voivodes came out near the Lithuanian border at Pochka.
