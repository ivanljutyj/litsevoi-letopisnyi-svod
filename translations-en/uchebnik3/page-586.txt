He spoke on the king’s behalf of matters befitting the sovereign’s praises. And while at the sovereign’s court, the envoy Yuri petitioned, asking to go to the metropolitan, so that the metropolitan would order him to be received privately; and the metropolitan ordered him to come to him.

And he petitioned the metropolitan, saying that he petitioned without instruction, for the sake of the Christian law and faith, that the metropolitan would show favor and petition the tsar and grand prince that the sovereign not shed Christian blood and would make peace with the king. And he was with the metropolitan three times, and each time petitioned for the same. And the metropolitan said to him that from the beginning they ask God for mercy, for humility and peace, and remind pious tsars and entreat them to piety and meekness: “But now you—so you say—speak of yourself and not by instruction; and that is not how such a matter is begun: if there should be a sending from the king to the tsar and grand prince or to us, then we too will gladly petition in the Holy Spirit our son and lord the tsar and grand prince, that he not shed Christian blood and would desire peace with the king, as befits his tsarist majesty.”

And the tsar and grand prince, having honored the king’s envoy and shown him sufficient favor, sent him back to the king.

On the Envoys from Ismail

In the same year, on the first day of February, envoys came to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus: from Ismail, prince of the Nogais, the envoy Bayterek; from Kasay-Murza, his envoy Ishekey; from Aryslan, his envoy Bulak; and from other murzas. Ismail also sent the tsar and grand prince’s envoys, the service Tatars Syundyuk Gulusupov with his companions, who had been with Prince Yusuf and his children.

And the envoys of Ismail and the other murzas spoke to the tsar and grand prince on behalf of the prince and the murzas; they congratulated him on the Astrakhan tsardom and petitioned him for the fact that the tsar and grand prince, at Ismail’s request, had installed Derbysh in Astrakhan.

And Ismail and the other murzas, by command of the tsar and grand prince, killed Prince Yusuf and beat many murzas, and Ismail became prince among the Nogais, Kasay became tekhovat, and Oraslan became nuradyn; and they drove out all the remaining children of Yusuf and his nephews, and the whole Nogai Horde looks to them.

And the prince and the murzas wish to be at one with the tsar and grand prince against all enemies, and to remain unwavering from the tsar and grand prince until the end of their lives; and the tsar and grand prince would favor them by ordering that they be given free trade in Moscow, Kazan, and Astrakhan. And Syuunduk Tulusupov told the tsar and grand prince that he and his companions had been in captivity with Prince Yusuf, and how Ismail and Kasay with Uruslan came against Prince Yusuf and his children.
