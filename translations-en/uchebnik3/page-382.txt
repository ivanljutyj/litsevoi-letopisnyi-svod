And when, with God's help, the top of the church was completed, then the archbishop himself entered the church, and with him the cathedral clergy of St Sophia, and the venerable abbots, and the priests of the whole city, and he himself consecrated it in the name of the holy and glorious apostle Mark, by lot. For before that there were no churches of the holy apostles and evangelists Matthew and Mark in the whole city, and by lot they established the church of St Mark, and he celebrated the holy liturgy at the second hour of the night.

The archbishop came out of the church and, having instructed the abbots and priests and all the people how they should remain in fasting and prayer and in all virtues, he dismissed them.

And by God's grace the pestilence gradually ceased. And from that pestilence in Veliky Novgorod more than three thousand souls of Orthodox Christians, male and female, died.

The miracle of St Varlaam the Wonderworker at Khutyn concerning the candle

In the same year, in the month of April, on the 22nd day, on the feast of the holy and all-praised apostle and evangelist Mark, in the second week after Easter, from Thursday to Friday, at night, by God's mercy and the prayers of our venerable father Varlaam, a miracle occurred at his holy tomb, where his much-healing body lies at Khutyn: the candle lit itself, though it had been lit by no one. And Theodosius, the abbot of that monastery, reported this to Archbishop Macarius; and the archbishop ordered a portion of that fire and of the wax of that candle to be sent to the sovereign Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich in Moscow, and this was done.

And Archbishop Macarius kept a portion of that wax for himself. And that candle burned ceaselessly day and night for nine weeks, and did not diminish at all.

On the ambo in Veliky Novgorod

In the same summer, on the 15th day of July, the God-loving Archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov, Macarius, installed in the cathedral church of St Sophia in Veliky Novgorod a most wondrous ambo, filled with every beauty: on it were thirty saints from the top in three rows, for the veneration of all Christians. And the whole ambo was most excellently adorned with various carvings and gold leaf, and below the ambo were made, as it were, twelve wooden figures of men, adorned with various colors, in garments, who with fear hold this sacred object on their heads; and it was very pleasant to behold.

On the drought in the Novgorod land, which was by God's wrath

In the same summer, by God's permission, there was a great drought throughout the Novgorod land; springs and streams, wells and marshes dried up, and much livestock died in the villages from thirst.
