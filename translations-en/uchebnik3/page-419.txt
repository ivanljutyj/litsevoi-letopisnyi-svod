Concerning Vladimir

In the same month of April, on the 13th day, Thursday of the seventh week of holy Lent, at the ninth hour of the day, the city of Vladimir caught fire between the Nativity Monastery and the Andreyev Gates, in an empty place, and the wall from the Klyazma River burned, and 170 wall sections and four towers were destroyed, and half the roof of the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God burned, and on the church of Saint Demetrius the whole roof burned, but by God's mercy the icons, church vessels, and books were all carried out intact, and half of the Nativity Monastery burned, and by God's mercy and the protection of His Most Pure Mother there remained of the city 265 wall sections and seven towers.

And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered that place in the city of Vladimir to be rebuilt in timber, and the remaining walls to be repaired; and in the same month on the 19th day Istoma Kurtsov was sent to build the city. And in the same year, in August, the city of Vladimir was completed in the third year of his reign.

Concerning Velizh

In the same month of April, on the 19th day, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered a city to be built in Toropets district at the Velizh hillfort, the city of Velizh; and it was completed in the month of July in the third year of his reign.

And by God's mercy the great sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and his mother, the great sovereign lady Elena, sent their commanders from Velizh to wage war on the Lithuanian land: Prince Ivan Ivanovich Barbashin and other commanders.

In the year 7046 from Adam (the year 1546 from Christ)

On April 3, Wednesday of the fifth week of holy Lent, at the second hour of the day, the blessed Grand Princess Elena, daughter of Prince Vasily Lvovich Glinsky, wife of the blessed Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, reposed.

And she was laid in the Church of the Ascension of Our Lord Jesus Christ beside Grand Princess Sophia, wife of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich.
