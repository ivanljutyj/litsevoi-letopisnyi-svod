The Grand Prince's commanders began to ravage the king's realm from the Smolensk frontier: Dubrovna, Orsha, Druchesk, Borisovo, Prikhaby, Sokolen, Bobynichi, Zaborovie, Svesi Novye, Borovichi, and other places.

The Novgorodian commanders, Prince Boris Ivanovich Gorbaty and his comrades, set out from the Grand Prince's patrimony, from Opochka, and began to ravage the Polotsk, Vitebsk, and Bryaslavl districts, Osinovets, Senno, and Latygosh.

And the Grand Prince's commanders joined together in one place and ravaged to within fifty versts of Vilna, and in places within forty.

Then they turned from there toward the Polotsk districts and went to the German frontier, burning and ravaging, slaying and taking captives.

And the Grand Prince's commanders came out to Opochka, into the Pskov land, all safe and sound, with many captives; and they came from the Lithuanian land to Opochka on March 1, the Sunday of the fourth week of the holy Fast.

That same autumn, in November, the Grand Prince sent his commanders from Starodub to ravage the Lithuanian land with many men: in the large regiment, Prince Fyodor Vasilyevich Telepnev Ovchina Obolensky and Prince Ivan Timofeevich Trostensky; in the forward regiment, Prince Konstantin Ivanovich Kurletev; and in the guard regiment, Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Kurlyatev.

The Grand Prince's commanders, Prince Fyodor Ovchina and his comrades, ravaged the king's land: Rechitsa, Svisloch, Gorval, Petrov Gorodok, Mozyr, Sluchesk, Rogachev, Boruchesk, Turov, Bryatin, and Lyubeichi.

They also ravaged as far as Novgorodok Lithuanian; they burned suburbs and volosts, ravaged villages, and took people captive; and by God's mercy the Grand Sovereign's commanders and men came out of the Lithuanian land all safe, with many captives and with booty.

On the Archbishop

That same winter, in the month of January, on the ... day, there came to the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' and to his mother, Grand Princess Elena, from their patrimony, their man of prayer, Archbishop Macarius of Great Novgorod and Pskov, to congratulate the Grand Sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich upon his great realm; he blessed the Grand Prince and bowed low before him, and presented him with a sapphire cross set in gold, and a crystal casket bound with gilded silver and enamel, and many other gifts.

And the Grand Prince honored Archbishop Macarius and released him to his patrimony in Great Novgorod on the 28th of the same month, on Thursday of the Meatfare Week.
