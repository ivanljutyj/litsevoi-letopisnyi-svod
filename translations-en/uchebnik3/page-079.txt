They, coming, conquered five cities, took captives, and burned them, and having caused much evil, returned to their own land with great wealth.

And Grand Prince Vasily Vasilievich then set out against the Tatar Tsar Ulu-Makhmet to Murom, and many Christians died then from the frost, and others the Tatars killed, and they laid waste the whole land.

And God helped Grand Prince Vasily; the Tatars fled to Old Nizhny Novgorod, whence they had come, and many of them were killed.

From Another Chronicle concerning the Same

The Lithuanians came to Kaluga—Pan Sudivoy, Pan Radivill Osikovich, Andryushka Mostilovich, Yabub Ralovich, Andrei Isaakovich, Nikolai Nemirovich, Zakharii Ivanovich Koshkin—and with them was an army of 7,000 warriors; and they stood before Kozelsk and Kaluga, but could accomplish nothing, and from there came to Sukhodrev. The Mozhaisk people of Prince Ivan Andreevich heard of this, though Prince Ivan Andreevich himself was not then in Mozhaisk, and a hundred Mozhaisk men gathered, and their voivode was Prince Andrei Vasilievich Lugvitsa, of the Suzdal princes. The people of his brother Prince Mikhail Andreevich also heard of this, and a hundred of them gathered too, though Prince Mikhail Andreevich was not then at Vereya; and their voivode was Sudok. The people of Prince Vasily Yaroslavich also heard, and sixty of them gathered, though Prince Vasily Yaroslavich was not then at Borovsk; and their voivode was Zhinev.

And they met the Lithuanians at Sukhodrev, and battle began between them, and there they killed the voivode of Prince Ivan Andreevich of Mozhaisk, Prince Andrei Vasilievich Lugvitsa of the Suzdal princes, and Karacharov, and four other men; and they took Yaropka and Semyon Rzhevsky captive, and took captive the voivodes of Prince Ivan Andreevich of Mozhaisk, and the voivodes of his brother Prince Mikhail Andreevich of Vereya: Sudok, Filipp Shchekin, and Koninsky, and five more young warriors; and they killed two hundred Lithuanians.

The Grand Prince Marched with an Army against Tsar Ulu-Makhmet

And then Grand Prince Vasily Vasilievich set out with all his brothers to Vladimir; with him went Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Shemyaka, Prince Ivan Andreevich of Mozhaisk, Prince Mikhail Andreevich of Vereya, and Prince Vasily Yaroslavich, and so with all his princes, boyars, and voivodes, and with all his people the Grand Prince advanced against Tsar Ulu-Makhmet, who was in Old Nizhny Novgorod. Before this he had been driven from the Field and expelled from the Great Horde by his brother Kichi-Akhmet, and he had come to Belev and remained in Belev.
