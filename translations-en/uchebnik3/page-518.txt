And the sovereign comes to the cathedral church of the Nativity of the Most Pure in that city, and to the great wonderworkers Prince Peter and Princess Fevronia, and goes around all the holy places with prayer.

And to Murom there came to the sovereign from the metropolitan the metropolitan’s boyar, bringing to the grand prince a letter, strengthening the tsar and all his host to struggle for piety, and the letter read thus:

Epistle to the right-believing Tsar and Grand Prince, Sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus, from the Most Holy Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus

“Blessing of the Most Reverend Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, in the Holy Spirit to the beloved son of our humility. To the God-honored and God-crowned Tsar, Sovereign and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Autocrat of All Rus! We, together with your pilgrims, and our children in Christ and the servants of our humility, with archbishops and bishops, with archimandrites and hegumens and with all the sacred councils of the Russian metropolis, collectively bless you, sovereign, right-believing tsar and grand prince, and bow our heads before you! And we pray with all the sacred councils and with all Orthodox Christians to the most merciful God, Creator and Maker of all, and to His Most Pure Mother, the Virgin Theotokos, intercessor for the whole world, for the well-being of God’s holy churches and for the many years of your bodily health and for the salvation of the soul of your great nobility, and of your noble and Christ-loving Tsaritsa, Grand Princess Anastasia, and also for your brothers Prince George Vasilievich and his princess and Prince Vladimir Andreevich, that the Lord God may multiply the years of your life and grant you sons, heirs of your kingdom. Also we pray to God for all your princes and boyars, and voevodes, and sons of boyars, and for all your Christ-loving host of Orthodox people who struggle for piety, and for your present undertaking, that with God’s help and intercession you, tsar, may stand manfully and in a tsar-like manner firmly with all your Christ-loving host against your adversaries, the godless Kazan Tatars, your traitors and apostates, who shed innocent Christian blood and defile and destroy holy churches. And for this especially you, pious Tsar John, with Prince Vladimir Andreevich and with all your Christ-loving host, ought well and bravely and manfully to strive with God’s help for God’s holy churches and for all Orthodox Christians, innocently led into captivity and plundered, and tormented by all misfortunes from them, and defiled by various passions. And out of compassion for all of them, and especially for our holy and pure and most honorable Christian faith
