Then the brothers of the great prince, as well as the boyars and all the voevodes, each according to his rank, came to greet the great prince and sovereign upon his patrimony and ancestral inheritance, the city of Smolensk.

Likewise the Smolensk princes and boyars, the townsmen and all the townspeople pronounced a greeting to the great sovereign, and among themselves with the voevodes and boyars of the great prince, and with all the people began to greet and kiss one another, rejoicing with great love, as brothers of one faith, exulting toward one another.

The wives did likewise among themselves, and the children, rejoicing both in the victorious cross and in the pious Orthodox great sovereign, uttering grateful exclamations for deliverance and liberation from the evil Latin falsehood and violence, and they rejoiced in their true pastor and teacher, the Orthodox great sovereign, and kissed one another.

And seeing the mercy of the most pure Mother of God and the grace of the life-giving cross, the people throughout the city of Smolensk rejoiced and made merry.

When the time for the Divine Liturgy came, the great sovereign, having heard the holy service, went with his brothers and with the boyars and with all the voevodes to his court and sat in his place; and his brothers, and the boyars, and the voevodes, and the Smolensk princes, and the boyars greeted him according to rank; and the great prince spoke his word to them concerning their health and bade them sit.

And the great prince, having summoned to him the Smolensk princes, boyars, and townsmen, delivered to them the speech appointed for such occasions and spoke of his complete favor to them, and gave them as voevode and namestnik his boyar and voevode Prince Vasily Vasilievich Shuysky, and invited them to feast with him; and all ate at the great prince's. And after this he began to grant them clothing, sables, velvets, precious stones, silks and golden satins, and monetary grants: to each according to his worth, and also to the boyars' sons, and service people, and townsmen, each according to his fitness, and also to the mercenary hetmans and mercenaries he granted.

And the namestnik of the king, Pan Yuri Salagubovich, he released to his sovereign, the king, and ordered him escorted to Orsha.

On the Sending of Voevodes to Mstislavl

In the same month of August, on the 7th day, the great prince sent his boyar and voevode Prince Mikhail Danilovich Shchenyatev, and Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Vorotynsky, and his other voevodes, and the Smolensk princes and boyars with many people to Mstislavl against Prince Mikhail Mstislavsky.

And Prince Mikhail, having heard of the arrival of the great prince's voevodes, met them and petitioned that the great sovereign prince would show favor and take him into his service.
