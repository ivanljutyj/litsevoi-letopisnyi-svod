The bishop they released to live in a monastery at Mukovo, and the foreigners who had been hired to fight against our sovereign they sent overseas; and they inventoried the equipment and the bishop’s property, and those people who remained to serve the sovereign they took into service; they took 552 cannon, large and small, and they took powder and much other property for the sovereign as well.

And the tsar and grand prince, having given thanks to God for this, sent Ivan Chulkov to the voivodes with a reward, and he ordered the bishop to come to him and also ordered the best townsmen to be sent to him.

In the same month Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Okhlyabinin sent word that the commoners from four small towns—Ryngol, Kongot, Kovlet, and Ryanden—had sent to petition him; their petty princes had fled. And the sovereign showed them favor and forbade making war on them; and Prince Pyotr with his companions sent commanders with boyar sons, and the commanders stationed themselves in all the small towns and brought the commoners to the oath.

On the message from the voivodes at Sviyaga

In the same month of July the voivodes wrote from the town of Sviyazhsk: the Crimeans had come in war against the Mountain Side, and the people of the Mountain Side, having gathered, Kochak-Prince and his companions, routed them utterly and freed all the captives; and there were 300 Crimeans.

In the same month Ivan Klushin wrote from the Volga: the Crimeans had come against the fishermen on the Volga below Uvek; Ivan marched against them, and with him were the Vyatchans, and he routed them utterly; there were 100 Crimeans, and 6 of them escaped.

On the arrival of the miraculous icons from Rugodiv to Moscow: the icon of the Most Pure Mother of God and another icon—of Nicholas the Wonderworker

In the month of August, on the 9th day, two icons came from Rugodiv to Moscow: on one icon was the image of the Most Pure Mother of God Hodegetria with the Pre-Eternal Child; on the other icon was the image of Nicholas the Wonderworker and Blaise the Great, and Cosmas and Damian.

And with those holy icons Archbishop Pimen of Novgorod sent the Yuryev archimandrite Vartholomew and the Sophian protopope Dmitry, who had consecrated the churches and the town in Rugodiv and Vyshegorod; they came with the icons.

And the tsar and grand prince, and his son Tsarevich Ivan Ivanovich, and his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and Metropolitan Macarius with the whole consecrated council, and the entire royal synclite, and a multitude of people met the icons at the Church of St. Paraskeva Pyatnitsa of Rzhev.
