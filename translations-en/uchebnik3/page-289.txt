In the same month of March, the Grand Prince sent Alexei Zabolotsky to Crimea.

In the same spring, in April, Pan Petryash Epimakhovich came with soldiers from Polotsk into the Pupovichi volost, and the Grand Prince’s boyar sons struck down many soldiers and took some captive. In the same spring, on March 9, the Germans came against Ivangorod.

Then they killed Loban Kolychev and twenty other men, and Mikhail Smolka Ivanov, son of Sliznev, because Loban was stationed at the outpost in Ivangorod with few men, while many Germans had come.

On the Disgrace of the Grand Prince’s Grandson Dmitry

In the same spring, in April, on the 11th day, Monday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich placed disgrace upon his grandson Grand Prince Dmitry and upon his mother Grand Princess Elena, and from that day he ordered that they not be commemorated in the ectenies and at the litias, and that he not be called Grand Prince, and he placed them under guard.

On the Great Reign of Grand Prince Vasily.
In the year 7010 from Adam (1510 from Christ)

On the 14th day of April, Thursday, on the memory of our venerable father Martin, Pope of Rome, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus bestowed favor upon his son Vasily, blessed him, and seated him on the great principality of Vladimir and Moscow and as autocrat of all Rus, with the blessing of Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus. In the same spring, on May 1, Sunday, Abbot Nikon of the Pavlova Hermitage was installed to the bishopric of Kolomna.

On the Installation of the Bishop of Perm

In the same month, on the 5th day, Thursday, Nikon, Abbot of the Glushitsky Dionysiev Monastery, was installed to the bishopric of Perm by Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus.

In the same month of May, on the 8th day, the Grand Prince dismissed the Kaffan envoy Alakoz, and together with him he sent his own envoy to the Kaffa Sultan Shikhzade, Alyosha Golokhvastov.

In the same month Ivan Mamonov came from Crimea.

In the same year, on June 9, Thursday, the Grand Prince went to live in Vorontsovo and remained there until the commemoration of Peter the Wonderworker in December.

In the same year Fedor Kiselev came from Crimea on June 28, Tuesday.
