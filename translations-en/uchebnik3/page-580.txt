And the commanders went up the Volga and came to the tsar and grand prince in good health by the feast of the Annunciation.

A hierarch was appointed to Ryazan

In the same year, in March, on the 17th day, Guriy, abbot of the Trinity, was appointed hierarch of Ryazan.

A son, Tsarevich Ivan, was born to the pious tsar

In the same year, in the month of March, on the 28th day, in Bright Week, on the night from Wednesday to Thursday, at three o’clock at night, a son, Tsarevich Ivan, was born to the pious tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus from the pious tsarina Anastasia, daughter of Roman Yuryevich; and there was great joy at his birth.

And he was baptized in the third week after Easter, in April, on the 15th day, in the Chudov Monastery in Moscow, at the wonder-working relics of Alexis the Wonderworker, in the church of the Most Pure Mother of God of Her honorable Annunciation; and Metropolitan Macarius of all Rus received him from the font, and Andrew, archpriest of the Annunciation, performed the sacred service.

On the sending to the tsar and grand prince

In the same year, on the 21st day of April, his envoy Fedor Zagryazhsky sent to the tsar and grand prince the service Tatars Baibyarya Tuishev and his companions; and with them the tsar sent to the tsar and grand prince to say that the tsar and grand prince had sent him too few gifts, and that he was therefore angry with Fedor, but if the tsar and grand prince sent him more gifts, he would make a firmer peace.

In the same year the tsar and grand prince ordered a town to be built in the Field opposite Tula, the town of Dedilov, and Vasily Petrov, son of Yakovlevich, guarded it, and Prince Dmitry Zhizhemsky built it.

In the same year, in May, envoys came to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus from the Master of the Livonian Order: Ivan Bokostr and Artem, and the scribe Venedikt, and from the Bishop of Yuryev, Hermann, envoys, Vladimir and his companions, to petition that their sovereign show favor and order his governors of his Novgorod and Pskov domains to grant a truce.

And the tsar and grand prince sent to them with an answer his okolnichy Alexei Fedorovich Adashev and the clerk Ivan Mikhailov, and ordered them to say that for many years they had not paid the tsar’s tributes from the Yuryev region and had oppressed merchants, and had appropriated Christian churches and Russian lands, and did not give them back to the merchants of the tsar and grand prince; and the sovereign was angry with the Master and the Bishop and all the Livonian land for such non-fulfillment of the agreement by them, and had not ordered his governors to grant a truce.
