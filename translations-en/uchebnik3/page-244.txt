Menli-Girey of the Crimea seized him and sent him to the Turkish tsar in Kafa, and sent his younger brother against the ulus of Prince Temir to scatter the remainder of the Horde.

On the war between the Horde tsar and the Crimean tsar

In the same year the Horde tsar Mahmud, son of Akhmat, with Prince Temir suddenly marched against Tsar Menli-Girey and recovered from him his brother Murtaza, son of Akhmat; Menli-Girey himself secretly fled from the battlefield from his army, while Mahmud brought Murtaza and installed him as tsar.

Menli-Girey then sent to the Turkish sultan; the sultan sent him troops as aid and sent to the Nogais, ordering them to conquer the Horde.

On the capture of Tver. In the year 6994 from Adam (1494 from Christ)

On the 8th day of September Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich came with his son Great Prince Ivan Ivanovich, with his brothers, and with his voivodes, and with all his forces before the city of Tver and surrounded the city.

In the same month on the 10th day, Saturday, they set fire to the settlements around the city of Tver; and on the 11th day, Sunday, princes and boyars came to the Great Prince from the city of Tver, Tver traitors, and beat their foreheads before him, asking to enter his service.

And that same day at night, seeing his defeat, Great Prince Mikhail Borisovich of Tver fled from the city of Tver to Lithuania; and on the 12th day, Monday, the Tver hierarch Vassian and Prince Mikhail Kholmsky with his brothers and his son, and other princes and boyars, and all the common people came to Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich and opened the city before him. And the Great Prince sent into the city his boyars Yuri Shestak and Konstantin Malechkin, and his clerks Vasily Dolmatov, Roman Alekseev, and Leonty Alekseev, and ordered all the townspeople to be brought to the oath and protected from his army, so that they would not be plundered.

And on the 15th day, Thursday, Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich with his son Great Prince Ivan Ivanovich were in the city of Tver and heard the liturgy in the Church of the Savior, and the prince granted that land to his son Great Prince Ivan Ivanovich.

And on the 18th day, Sunday, Great Prince Ivan Ivanovich entered the city of Tver to live; and Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich came to Moscow, having taken the city of Tver, in the same month on the 29th day.

On the death of Prince Mikhail Vereisky

In the same year, in the third week after Easter, Prince Mikhail Andreevich Vereisky died; and his body was laid in the Pafnutev Monastery. And Great Prince Ivan Ivanovich was present at his burial.
