On Ambassadors to the Sovereign from Shevkal

In the same month ambassadors came from Shevkal with gifts and a petition, asking that the tsar and grand prince defend them from his own slaves, the Circassian princes.

On the Arrival to the Sovereign of a Tatar from Siberia

In the month of November a serving Tatar, Sobana Rizanov, came from Siberia, and with him an ambassador from the Siberian prince Ediger with tribute, with sables and squirrels, and he said that they would arrive in winter with the long-standing tribute.

On the Report from the Voivodes from the German Land to the Sovereign

In the same month the voivodes, Prince Mikhail and his companions, wrote that the Master had sent his brother Yagan Ketler for provisions and had sent many German men with them as a guard.

And the voivodes came to the outpost and routed the German outpost utterly, and took the Master’s brother Yagan Ketler and the Master’s armorer and equerry, and captured many chieftains and manor lords; and in that battle they took one hundred and six alive, and in many campaigns they beat the Master’s men and took in all the campaigns 260 men and killed about 3,000, and all the prisoners said that the Master was battering down the city wall, and that no gunpowder for the cannon remained in the city.

On the Report to the Sovereign from Yuryev from Prince Dmitry Kurlyatev

In the same month Prince Dmitry Kurlyatev wrote from Yuryev that the Master had taken the city of Ryngol, having stood under it for six weeks. And Prince Mikhail Repnin also wrote that the Master had taken Ryngol and come against the voivodes, and had trampled the voivodes, Prince Mikhail and his companions, and killed boyars’ sons and two boyar retainers, and captured Ivan Likov and Fyodor Pisessky alive; and the Master went to Kes, leaving Ryngol; and the common people again joined the tsar and grand prince.
