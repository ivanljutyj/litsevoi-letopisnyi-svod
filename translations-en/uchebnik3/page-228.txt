And our men killed many with arrows and handguns, while their arrows fell among ours and wounded no one; and they drove them back from the bank.

For many days they attacked, fighting, and did not prevail; and they stood waiting for the river to freeze. Great frosts came then, and ice began to form on the river; and there was fear in both armies, each fearing the other.

Then the brothers came to the grand prince at Kremenets, Prince Andrei and Prince Boris; and the grand prince received them with love.

When the river froze, the grand prince ordered his son, the grand prince, and his brother Prince Andrei, and all the voivodes with all their forces to come to him at Kremenets, fearing the coming of the Tatars, so that, united, they might do battle with the enemy.

But all those remaining in the city of Moscow were in fear, and pondering in their minds the common peril, they expected help from no one, but only to Almighty God, our Lord Jesus Christ, and His most pure Mother, the glorious Mother of God, with tears and sighs they prayed unceasingly.

Then there occurred the glorious miracle of the Most Holy Mother of God. When our men withdrew from the bank, the Tatars, possessed by fear, fled, thinking that the Russians had yielded the bank to them and wished to fight; while to ours it seemed that the Tatars had crossed the river after them and were pursuing them; and they came to Kremenets.

The grand prince with his son and brothers and all the voivodes went to Borovsk, saying: “On those fields we will give them battle”; and they listened to evil people, lovers of silver, rich and big-bellied, traitors to Christians, helpers of the infidels, who said: “Flee, you cannot stand against them in battle”; for the devil himself spoke through their mouths, he who in ancient times, entering the serpent, deceived Adam and Eve. And it was wondrous then: the miracle of the Most Pure was accomplished—they fled from one another, though no one was pursuing.

The tsar fled to the Horde; and the Nogai Tsar Ivak came upon him, took the Horde, and killed him. A single tsarevich wished to take the borderlands beyond the Oka River.

The grand prince sent his two brothers, the two Andreis, but the Tatars heard of it and fled. And so God delivered the Russian land from the impious Tatars through the prayers of the Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers. O brave and courageous sons of Russia! Strive to preserve your fatherland, the Russian land, from the impious; do not spare your heads! Let your eyes not see the captivity of your homes, the killing of your children, and the outrage of your wives and daughters, as other great and glorious lands suffered from the Turks, which are called Bulgarians, Serbs, and Greeks.
