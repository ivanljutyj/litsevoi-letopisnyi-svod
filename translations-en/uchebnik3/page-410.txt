And when the Tsar came to the court of the Grand Prince, the Grand Prince ordered that the Tsar be honored and met at his sledge by his boyar Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky, and by the boyar and equerry Prince Ivan Fedorovich Telepnev, and by the clerks Fedor Mishurin and Menshoy Putyatin. And the great sovereign sat with his mother in the chamber at St Lazarus, and with the Grand Princess sat the boyar women: Princess Nastasya, wife of Prince Fedor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky, and Alena, wife of Ivan Andreevich, and Agrofena, wife of Vasily Andreevich, and many other boyar women; and the boyars sat on both sides.

And when the Tsar and the boyars with him came into the entryway before the chamber, the Grand Prince showed him favor and honored the Tsar: the great sovereign himself met him in the entryway, together with his boyars. And the Grand Prince went with the Tsar to his mother, the great sovereign lady, in the chamber.

And the Tsar, entering to the great sovereign lady, bowed to the ground and said: “Great sovereign lady Grand Princess Elena, my sovereign, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, took me while I was young, showed me favor, and reared me like a whelp, and with his great favor he favored me as a father does a son, and set me as Tsar in Kazan; and through my sins the Kazan people drove me from Kazan, and I came again to my sovereign, and my sovereign once more showed me great favor and gave me towns in his land; and I betrayed my sovereign, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus, and broke my oath, and am guilty in all my affairs before my sovereign; and you, my sovereigns, have shown favor to me, your slave, forgave me such an offense, had mercy on me, your slave, and granted me, your slave, to behold your sovereign eyes; and I, your slave, as I have now given oath to you, my sovereigns, according to that oath desire to serve firmly until my death and to die for your sovereign favor; and I also desire to die as my brother did, so that I too may wash away that stain before you, my sovereigns, and lay down my head in your sovereign service.” And the Grand Prince ordered the Tsar to sit.

And the great sovereign lady Elena ordered Fedor Karpov to speak these words to Tsar Shigalei: “Tsar Shigalei! Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus laid his anger and disgrace upon you, and our son Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and we have shown you favor, shown our mercy, and granted you to behold our eyes; now forget your past, and henceforth fulfill your promises and oaths; and we wish to maintain our great favor and protection toward you.” And the Tsar bowed to the ground to the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess.

And the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess showed him favor, gave him gifts, and sent him back to his residence, and ordered the boyars to escort him just as they had met him.
