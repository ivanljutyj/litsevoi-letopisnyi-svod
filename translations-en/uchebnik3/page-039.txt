At once the Italians and Latins were thrown into confusion. And Pope Eugene, rising from his place, went out, and after him all the cardinals, archbishops, bishops, Italians, Germans, and all the Latins who did not wish to listen to Mark’s words; only the Greeks and Russians remained.

And then Tsar Ivan Manuilovich and Patriarch Joseph asked Mark: “What evil thing did you say to Pope Eugene and the cardinals, that they left their places?” Mark, Metropolitan of Ephesus, answered them: “Listen, pious Tsar Ivan, and you, Ecumenical Patriarch Joseph: if anyone does unrighteousness, he cannot hear the truth; of such the prophet says: ‘Shut the mouths of those who speak falsehood.’ I only said to them that if anyone departs from the commandments of the holy apostles and of the holy seven councils of the holy fathers and of the holy seven popes, the ecumenical teachers, he will be given over to anathema; and therefore they fled from their places. Remember how in Constantinople you yourselves said about this, that in essence the Latins are not Christians. How can they be Christians? Now this has been confirmed: if they cannot hear the truth, how can they walk the right path? And how can we be in unity with them, in one church that rejects the truth of God? But your majesty knows, O Tsar, that if I had said even a word to them about how far the Latins have departed from the holy Church of God, or how they have renounced the Orthodox faith, they could not have listened to such rebukes and would have fled from this city because of their errors. Now, Tsar Ivan, you have heard what the pope says: he does not wish to accept the seven holy ecumenical councils, and he does not wish to commemorate you, the Tsar, in prayers, nor to call the patriarchs his brothers, but he commands that his own name, Pope Eugene’s, be commemorated in churches throughout all the land. But if we commemorate Pope Eugene first, then soon he will command us to serve on unleavened bread. Therefore, O Tsar, I do not wish to take part in this council, I do not wish to commemorate Pope Eugene first, and I command all not to renounce the holy seven councils and the commandments of the holy fathers, because it is written in the holy canons: if anyone adds anything to the commandments of the holy ecumenical teachers or takes anything away from them, he will be accursed. And this I fear greatly, and I do not recognize an eighth council, and I do not commemorate Pope Eugene, and I do not accept his precepts. And whoever commemorates Pope Eugene in Orthodox churches, to them be anathema, that is, a curse.” The Latin chaplains heard this, and, put to shame, fled at his words.

And then the Greek Tsar Ivan said to him: “Holy father, cease.” And rising, he returned to his chambers.
