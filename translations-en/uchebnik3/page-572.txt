And after that, on the following day, after the sovereign had brought his close boyars to the kiss of the cross, in the morning the sovereign summoned all his people and began to tell them to kiss the cross to his son, Tsarevich Prince Dmitry, and to kiss it in the Front Chamber, because the sovereign was very feeble and it was hard for him to bring them to the oath in his presence; and he ordered his close boyars to be there—Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Mstislavsky and Prince Vladimir Ivanovich Vorotynsky and their associates.

And to those boyars who had kissed the cross before this, the sovereign began to say: “The boyars have sworn to me and to my son Dmitry that they will serve us; and now the boyars do not wish to see my son on the throne. And if, by God’s will, I am no more, then, I beg you, remember that to which you kissed the cross to me and to my son; do not let the boyars destroy my son in any way; flee with him to a foreign land, wherever God directs.”

And the sovereign said to Danila Romanovich and Vasily Mikhailovich: “And you Zakharins, what have you taken fright at? Or do you think the boyars will spare you? You will be the first to be dead at the boyars’ hands! Die for my son and for his mother, and do not let the boyars give my wife over to outrage!” And all the boyars were terrified by that harsh word of the sovereign and went to the Front Chamber to kiss the cross. And when the boyars went into the Front Chamber, the tsar and grand prince sent after them his boyar Prince Vladimir Ivanovich Vorotynsky and other boyars of his, and sent his clerk Ivan Mikhailov with the cross. And the boyars began to kiss the cross. And when the boyars came to kiss it, the boyar Prince Vladimir Ivanovich Vorotynsky stood by the cross, and the clerk Ivan Mikhailov held the cross.

And as they began to kiss [the cross], boyar Prince Ivan Ivanovich Pronsky Turuntai came and began to say to Prince Vladimir Vorotynsky: “Your father, and you after Grand Prince Vasily, are the first traitors, yet you are bringing [people] to the cross.” And Prince Vladimir answered him: “I am a traitor, yet I am bringing you to the kiss of the cross so that you may serve our sovereign and his son, Tsarevich Prince Dmitry. But you are an honorable man, yet you do not kiss the cross to our sovereign and his son, Tsarevich Prince Dmitry, and do not wish to serve them.” And Prince Ivan Pronsky kissed [the cross] hurriedly.

And after the kissing of the cross, boyar Prince Dmitry Fyodorovich Paletsky sent to Princess Efrosinia and to her son Prince Vladimir his brother-in-law Vasily Petrov, son of Boris Borozdin; Vasily was married to Prince Dmitry Paletsky’s sister, and Vasily’s own sister was married to Prince Khovansky, and Khovansky’s daughter, Princess Efrosinia, mother of Prince Vladimir, was Vasily’s niece.
