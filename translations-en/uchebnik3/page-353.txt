RUS (1537–1549 from the Incarnation of Christ)
Book 19

On the Grand Prince's Journey to the Wonder-workers.
In the year 7037 from Adam (the year 1537 from Christ)

The Grand Prince spent the autumn in his village of Novaya Sloboda, and during the fast he set out to the wonder-workers: to Pereslavl, to Rostov, to Yaroslavl, to Vologda, to Beloozero, to the Kirillov Monastery.

On the Kazan Envoy Tabai

In the same year Kazan envoys came to the Grand Prince at Novaya Sloboda from Safa-Kirei, Tsar of Kazan: Prince Tabai, Prince Danai, and Obreim-bakshei. They petitioned the Grand Prince, saying that in whatever matters the Tsar had been wrong before the Grand Prince, in all those matters the Tsar wished to make amends before the Grand Prince and take an oath, and was sending his chief envoys to the Grand Prince.

And the Grand Prince dismissed the Kazan envoys. And the Tsar and the whole land of Kazan took an oath before the Grand Prince's envoy Andrei Pilyemov on a sworn charter; and he also sent more of his envoys to the Grand Prince: Prince Mamush, Prince Kurt, and Prince Shemerden Churakov.

And the Grand Prince sent his envoy Prince Ivan Fedorovich Paletsky, so that the Tsar would fulfill all matters for the Grand Prince according to the sworn charter. But the Tsar soon broke his word and oath, and inflicted great dishonor and shame on the Grand Prince's envoy Andrei Pilyemov, and Prince Ivan Paletsky did not go from Novgorod to the Tsar in Kazan.

And the Grand Prince could not endure the Tsar's violation of the oath, and consulted about it with his brothers and boyars.
