And on his right he ordered Prince Semyon Ivanovich Ryapolovsky to go between his road and the Meta, and with him the Suzdalians and Yuryevites.

And on his left from Torzhok to the town of Demon he ordered his brother Prince Andrey Menshoy to go, and with him the Grand Prince’s voivode Vasily Saburov with the Rostovites and Yaroslavlites, and Uglichites, and Bezhichans who serve the Grand Prince; and he also ordered his mother’s voivode Semyon Fedorovich Peshko with her court to go with them.

And between the Demon road and the Yazhelbitskaya road he ordered Prince Alexander Vasilyevich and Prince Boris Mikhailovich Obolensky to go; and with Prince Alexander—the Kalugans, Aleksinites, Serpukhovites, Khotunians, Muscovites, Radonezhites, Novotorzhites, Bernovites and Glukhovites; and with Prince Boris—the Mozhaiskites, Volochans, and Zvenigorodites, and Ruzhans who serve the Grand Prince.

And along the Yazhelbitskaya road he ordered Fedor Davydovich to go, and with him the boyars’ sons from the Grand Prince’s court, and all the Kolomnians; and along that same road he ordered Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Obolensky to go, and with him his brothers, all the Obolensky princes, and also many boyars’ sons from the Grand Prince’s court.

And the Grand Prince ordered both envoys sent for the safe-conduct, the Novgorodians Fedor Kalitin and Ivan Markov, to be led after him from Torzhok.

On the 26th day of October, at Volochek, the Novgorod posadnik Grigory Mikhailovich Tuchin met the Grand Prince and beat his brow, asking to enter his service.

On the 28th day of October, at Bereza, a prosperous Novgorod man, Andreyan Savelyev, came to the Grand Prince to serve.
