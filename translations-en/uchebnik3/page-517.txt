On the third day of the month of July

On Sunday the pious tsar sets out on his journey, and he takes with him Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and orders him to be with him in the regiment; and to Ryazan and to Meshchera the sovereign sent the great regiment, the vanguard, and the regiment of the right hand.

And the sovereign comes to the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure, and falls before the icon of the Most Pure that had been on the Don with the most glorious Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, and prays with tears, and receives the blessing of Bishop Feodosius.

And the sovereign set out for Vladimir, and traveled to Vladimir for five days, and arrived at Vladimir on the eighth of July, a Friday.

And he went into the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, and then went to the Monastery of the Nativity of the Most Pure (Mother of God) and to the healing-bearing tomb of his forefather, the holy and great Prince Alexander, and continued earnest prayer, asking help in the undertaking he had begun.

And he visited all the holy places and the monasteries that were there, and prayed to God the Creator and to His Most Pure Mother and to the great wonderworkers, and received blessing from his parents, the sovereigns who had reposed before him; and he received blessing from the superiors, the archimandrites, abbots, and archpriests, and with great entreaty asked the superiors to fast and pray for the forthcoming undertaking.

And then the Archangel archpriest Timofey, who had previously been sent with holy water, came to the sovereign at Vladimir from Sviyaga, reporting to the sovereign of God’s bounties and of the long-suffering of the Lover of Mankind toward our sins. And he told how he had come to Sviyaga, and in the Church of the Nativity of the Most Pure and in other churches had sung molebens, and with the priests dwelling there had consecrated water, and having gathered the waters into one, had gone with crosses around the city and throughout the city, and had sprinkled with the holy water all, both the commanders and all Christians dwelling in that city, and with zeal and tears had prayed to God and organized cross processions. “And God saw your unwavering faith, and that of your father, and the prayers of your pilgrim the hierarch Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, and the prayers of the whole sacred council and the petition of all Christians, and He showed His mercy: the disease ceased, and God stopped the sudden deaths, and the sick are rising.” The sovereign gave thanks to God for His love for mankind and sent the archpriest back to the metropolitan.

And the sovereign himself set out from Vladimir on the tenth of July, a Sunday, for Murom, and arrived at Murom on the thirteenth of the same month, a Wednesday.
