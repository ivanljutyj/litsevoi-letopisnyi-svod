And he summoned his boyars to him—Prince Ivan and Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky, and Shigona, and Pyotr Golovin, and his clerks Menshoy Putyatin and Fyodor Mishurin; and those boyars were with him from the third hour until the seventh, and he gave them orders concerning his son, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, and concerning the ordering of the land, and how to govern the state after him.

And the boyars went from him, while Mikhailo Yuryevich and Prince Mikhail Glinsky and Shigona remained with him; and those boyars were with him until nightfall.

And he gave them orders concerning his Grand Princess Elena—how she should be without him and how the boyars should come to her—and he gave them orders about everything, how the realm should be ordered without him.

And then his brothers, Prince Yury and Prince Andrey Ivanovich, came to him and began to urge him to eat a little something. The Grand Prince tasted a little of some almond porridge, but only raised it to his lips.

And his brothers went from him; and he commanded that his brother Prince Andrey Ivanovich be brought back to him; at that time Mikhailo Yuryevich and Prince Mikhail Glinsky and Shigona were with him.

And the Grand Prince began to say to them: “I see myself that my life is drawing near to death; I wish to send for my son Ivan and to bless him with the cross of Peter the Wonderworker, and I wish to send for my wife, the Grand Princess Elena, and to take leave of her.” After this speech the Grand Prince changed his mind and said: “I do not wish to send for my son, Grand Prince Ivan, because my son is small, and I lie in my great infirmity, and lest my son be frightened of me.” Prince Andrey and the boyars began to speak to the Grand Prince and to urge him: “Sovereign Grand Prince! Send for your son, Grand Prince Ivan, and bless him; and send, sovereign, also for the Grand Princess.”

Then the Grand Prince sent for the Grand Princess; and before the Grand Princess’s arrival he ordered his son, Grand Prince Ivan, to be brought, because of the Grand Princess’s weeping; for the great sovereign understood that his illness was mortal.

And he himself placed upon himself the cross of Peter the Wonderworker; and at that time with him were Mikhail Yuryevich and Shigona, and among his attendants at that time was Ivan Ivanovich Chelyadnin, and his brother-in-law Prince Yury Glinsky.
