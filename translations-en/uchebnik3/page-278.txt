And after the speech of the Grand Prince, the metropolitan ordered the Prince’s grandson to ascend the dais, and, having risen, he blessed him with the cross; and the one being installed bowed his head; and the metropolitan laid his hand on his head and said a prayer so that all might hear it: “Lord our God, King of kings and Lord of lords...”; and after the prayer the metropolitan ordered two archimandrites to bring the barmy to him from the analogion, and, taking them, he handed them to the Grand Prince and made the sign of the cross over the grandson, and the Grand Prince placed the barmy on the grandson.

And the metropolitan began to say the prayer secretly:

“Lord Almighty and King of the ages, as an earthly man, created by Thee, the King, I bow my head, praying to Thee, Master of all: preserve him under Thy shelter, preserve his kingdom, teach him always to do what is pleasing to Thee, show forth in his days righteousness and abundance of peace, so that in the calm of his kingdom we may live our life quietly and silently in all piety and purity.” Exclamation: “For Thou art the King of the world and the Saviour of our souls, and to Thee we send up glory to the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.”

And after “Amen,” the metropolitan ordered two archimandrites to bring the cap to him from the analogion, and, taking it, he gave it to the Grand Prince.

And the metropolitan made the sign of the cross over the grandson with the cross, saying: “In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit”; and the Grand Prince placed the cap on the grandson; and the metropolitan blessed the grandson.

And then they intoned the litany: “Have mercy on us, O God, according to Thy great mercy,” according to custom, and the prayer to the Most Pure: “Most Holy Lady, Virgin Mother of God, Mistress.”

After the prayer the metropolitan and the Grand Princes sat in their places, and, having ascended the ambo, the archdeacon proclaimed aloud the many years for Grand Prince Dmitry; and the priests in the altar and the deacons sang the many years according to custom. And after the many years the metropolitan and the archbishop, and the bishops, and the whole assembly, having risen, bowed and congratulated both Grand Princes. And the metropolitan said to Grand Prince Ivan: “By God’s grace, rejoice and be well, Orthodox Tsar Ivan, Grand Prince of all Rus’, autocrat, with his grandson Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of all Rus’, for many years.” And then the metropolitan said to Grand Prince Dmitry: “By God’s grace, be well, lord and son Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich of all Rus’, with his sovereign and grandfather Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat of all Rus’, for many years.”
