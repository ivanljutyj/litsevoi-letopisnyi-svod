Of the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus, in the first year of his reign.

And the Grand Prince Vasily placed in the church the icon of the holy and great wonderworker Nicholas of Gostun, adorning it with gold and precious stones and pearls; from it many healings occurred and continue to occur to this day for all who come with faith.

On the arrival of the elders from the Holy Mountain

In that same autumn, in November, elders came to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus in Moscow from the Holy Mountain, from the Panteleimon Monastery, seeking alms: Archdeacon Pakhomy and the monk Iakov. And the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus gave them sufficient alms and sent them away from Moscow on the ninth day of May.

On the envoys

In that same autumn, in December, the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus sent away the Crimean envoys, Prince Kazimir-Kiat and his companions, and with them he sent to the Crimean Tsar Menli-Girei his own envoy, the okolnichy boyar Konstantin Grigorievich Zabolotsky.

On the appointment of a bishop in Kolomna

In that same winter, in February, on the Sunday of the Prodigal Son, by His Grace Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus, the archimandrite Mytrofan of Andronikov was appointed bishop in Kolomna.

On the Lithuanian envoys

In that same winter, on the twenty-first day of March, Lithuanian envoys came to Moscow to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus from the King of Poland and Grand Duke of Lithuania Zhigimont: Pan Yan Nikolaevich, the kuchmistr Pan Petr Yanov, and the scribe Bogdan Sopega.

On the envoy from the Kazan Tsar with a proposal of peace

In that same winter, in March, the Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin sent to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus his man Abdul with a letter and a petition, asking that the Grand Prince show him favor, forgive his offense, and conclude peace with him.

In that same month of March, on the twenty-fifth day, the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus sent away the Tsar’s man Abdul, and with him he sent his own man Aleksei Luchin, commanding him to say to the Tsar that he should release his envoy Mikhail Klyapik, whom the Tsar had detained, together with his companions.
