By the command of the tsar and sovereign all the Russian archbishops assembled

In the same year, by the command of the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus and by the blessing of Makary, metropolitan of all Rus, all Russian archbishops, archimandrites, and abbots assembled (in council) concerning the various church orders and many matters for the strengthening of the Christian faith.

And the tsar and grand prince, and Metropolitan Makary with all archbishops and bishops, and with the whole sacred council of Rus, according to the sacred canons, elected for the Kazan kingdom, for the strengthening of the faith, and decreed that there should be an archbishop, and at Sviyaga an archimandrite and abbots, and in Kazan at the vladyka’s seat an archimandrite and abbots as well; and that the city of Kazan with its surrounding uluses, the city of Sviyaga with the Mountain Side, Vasil-gorod, and the whole Vyatka land should be under the archbishop’s authority. And the pious tsar and grand prince Ioann decreed: to the vladyka and to all churches, the tenth part of all revenues of the Kazan land promised to God; and at first the metropolitan and all vladykas and monasteries shall help the Kazan vladyka with money and grain.

On the installation of Archbishop Gury in Kazan

In the month of February, on the third day, Archbishop Gury was installed for the Kazan kingdom and the city of Sviyazhsk, who had formerly been abbot of the Selizharov monastery; and he was chosen by lot.
