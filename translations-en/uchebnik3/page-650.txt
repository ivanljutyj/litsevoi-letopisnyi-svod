On the Arrival to the Tsar and Grand Prince of Yunus, Prince of the Nogai

In the same month Yunus, prince of the Nogai, came to the tsar and grand prince to petition that the sovereign grant him favor, take thought for him, and set him upon the state, upon the Nogai principality and upon his father’s yurt as of old.

On the Sending from Rugodiv by Alexei Basmanov

In the same month the boyar and namestnik Alexei Danilovich Basmanov and the voevoda Ivan Andreevich Buturlin sent Karp Yazykov and Grigory Myakinin from Rugodiv: the little town of Adezh submitted to the tsar and sovereign and surrendered the town; and the voevodes sent the heads Dmitry Pushkin and Druzhina Karaulov, and Mamysh Syudodurov, and Istoma Blazhenkov, and with them boyar sons and streltsy; and the heads came to the town and established the town, disposed the equipment, inventoried the supplies and cannons, and left Druzhina Karaulov in the town, and with him boyar sons and streltsy; and many common people joined the Rakoborsk volosts, which were close by there.

The Passing of Tsarevna Evdokia

In the same month, through our sins, the tsarevna Evdokia, daughter of the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, passed away; and on that same day she was buried in the Ascension Monastery beside the tsar’s parents, and she was two years and three and a half months old when she died.

On the Envoys from Lithuania from King Sigismund

In the same month envoys from King Sigismund Augustus of Lithuania came to the tsar and grand prince—Jan Yuryevich Volchkov, equerry of Vilna, and the scribe Luka Garaburda—and they spoke on the king’s behalf to the tsar and grand prince, asking that they unite together against the infidel Basurman tsars for Christian freedom. And the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, having heard their embassy, sent them back to the king and promised love and unity against the Tatars, and gave the envoys a safe-conduct for his great ambassadors.
