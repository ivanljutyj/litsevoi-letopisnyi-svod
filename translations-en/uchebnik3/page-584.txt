And from their regiments they left boyar children, streltsy, and Cossacks; while the voivodes themselves went in two directions, because they had no reliable news of the tsar, where the tsar had gone. And the voivodes set out: Prince Yuri Ivanovich Pronsky, Mikhailo Petrovich Golovin, and Stefan Sidorov, voivode of the guard regiment; while the voivodes of the advanced regiment, Ignaty Mikhailovich Veshnyakov and Shiryai Kobyakov, went to Chagan and to Kazan on the sea.

And to Bally the voivodes sent, having chosen commanders, Prince Vasily Koltsa-Masalsky and Yakov Kuzmin; and ahead of themselves the voivodes sent to the sea Prince Alexander Vyazemsky, and with them Nizhny Novgorod boyar children and ataman Fedka Pavlov.

And to Ivanchuk the voivodes sent commander Poluekt Timofeev, and Prince Davyd Gundorov they sent to Bolshoy Ivanchuk on the sea as well.

And those commanders killed people and took others alive; and those tongues said that the tsar had gone to Mochak, and the Astrakhan people had gone to Mochak too. And the voivodes went after the tsar to Mochak.

And they came to White Lake, and here the tongues began to say that the tsar could not flee to White Lake, but would flee to Tyumen, and all the Astrakhan people should have fled after the tsar.

And the voivodes, according to the news from those tongues, returned to the sea and rode over all those roads by which the tsar might flee to Tyumen; and on the sea the voivodes rowed out.

And they sent to search the islands for the tsar and the Astrakhan people; but they did not come upon the tsar and the Astrakhan people on the islands.

And they sent commanders to many places, and the commanders killed people in many places and took tongues.

And they brought Bogaty the Prince with his comrades, and those tongues said that the tsaritsas were going to Baztsyzh-Mochak, and many people had gone after the tsaritsas to Baztsyzh-Mochak.

And ataman Fedcha Pavlov, interpreter Fedcha Ryzhkov, and Sava Zhderinsky found the tsaritsas and the Astrakhan people in Baztsyzh-Mochak, and by God's mercy and the tsar's sovereign good fortune they caught the tsaritsas: they took the chief tsaritsa Tevkel, daughter of Kel-Magmet Murza; another tsaritsa, Kandaza, daughter of Tsar Krym-Shavkal; and Ertugana, daughter of Tsar Emgurchei; and Tsar Emgurchei's younger wife Elyakshi, pregnant. And when they took her, she gave birth to a son, Tsarevich Yaryshty; and Mergivana, wife of Tsarevich Aibulat; and the tsarevich's daughter, Tsarevna Baibiche; and they killed many people and took others alive.
