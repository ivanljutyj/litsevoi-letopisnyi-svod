And he released Yanalei to Kazan, and with him Prince Tabay and his companions and all the Kazan people; and the great sovereign ordered his okolnichy Yakov Grigorievich Morozov and his dyak Afanasy Kuritsyn to go to Kazan with the tsar, to install the tsar on the throne and to administer the oath in Kazan to the tsar, the tsarevna, the princes, and all the people of the Kazan land; and the Grand Prince sent with them the charter and the oath records on which the whole Kazan land would take the oath.

They, having arrived in Kazan, by command of the great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, by God’s mercy sovereign and autocrat of all Rus, installed Tsar Yanalei on the throne on the 29th day of July, on Saturday.

And they sent Ostanya Andreev to the Grand Prince (with the news) that they had installed the tsar on the throne in Kazan. And the tsar sent Prince Bibey, the huntsman, to petition the great sovereign that by his sovereign’s favor he had been installed on the throne in August.

The Grand Prince’s envoy Ivan Polev arrived from Kazan, and together with him the tsar sent to the Grand Prince his armorer Prince Burnak with a letter; and in the same month the Grand Prince sent Prince Bibey back to Kazan to the tsar, and with him sent letters to the tsar, to the tsarevna, and to the princes.

On the Renovation of the Icons Brought from Rzhev

In the same year, by command of the pious and Christ-loving great sovereign of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, two wonder-working icons were brought from Rzhev to Moscow: of the Venerable Paraskeva and of the Holy Great Martyr Paraskeva Pyatnitsa, which had become dilapidated over many years.

And the pious sovereign, with great faith and desire, ordered them repaired, similar icons painted, and a beautiful church built in their name at the New, near the Church of the Intercession of the Holy Theotokos.

And having renovated the holy old icons and adorned them with silver and gold, and having adorned the new ones as well, the great sovereign escorted the old icons with honor to where the church in their name had been built.

On the Consecration of the New Church

And they came with crosses, and he ordered the newly built church consecrated, a prayer service sung, and the Divine Liturgy celebrated in it; and he ordered the newly painted images placed in their church, while the old holy icons he sent back to Rzhev and ordered them escorted.

Consecration of the Church of the Great Martyr Paraskeva.
In the year 7039 from Adam (the year 1539 from Christ)

On the 27th day of November, the church of our holy venerable mother Paraskeva and of the Holy Great Martyr Paraskeva Pyatnitsa was consecrated.
