Concerning Gennadius, and How He Left the Archbishopric in Novgorod

In the same year, in June, Gennadius, Archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov, left his see through his own infirmity, and still more unwillingly, because, having come from Moscow to Veliky Novgorod to his see, he began to take fees from priests for ordination even more than before, breaking his promise and acting on the advice of his like-minded favorite, the clerk Mikhail Ivanov, son of Aleksei. And having investigated this matter, the Grand Prince and the Metropolitan removed him from the see to Moscow, and after remaining in the Chudov Monastery in Moscow for two and a half years, the Archbishop died.

In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, they dismantled the old church of Cosmas and Damian in the city opposite the church of Archangel Michael and laid the foundation of a new one.

In the year 7013 from Adam (1513 from Christ)

In November the ambassadors of the Grand Prince came to Moscow: Aleksei Zabolotsky from Crimea, Dmitry Ralev and Mitrofan Karacharov from Overseas, and they brought with them many masters of silver and wall work, and cannon-makers. Then also Alesha Golokhvastov returned from Kaffa.

On the Investigation of the Affairs of Heretics and Their Execution

In the same winter, Grand Prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilievich and his son Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, with his father Simon, Metropolitan of all Rus, with the bishops and the whole council, investigated the affairs of the heretics and ordered them to be put to death. And they burned in a cage the clerk Ivan Volk Kuritsyn and Mitya Konoplev, and Ivashka Maksimov, in December on the 27th day; and they ordered Nekras Rukavov’s tongue to be torn out and burned him in Veliky Novgorod.

And in the same winter they burned Archimandrite Cassian of Yuryev and his brothers, and many other heretics they burned, and some they sent into exile, and some to monasteries.

On the Death of Grand Princess Elena Voloshanka

In the same winter, on January 18, on Saturday, Grand Princess Elena Voloshanka died, in confinement under Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, and they laid her in the Church of the Ascension in Moscow.
