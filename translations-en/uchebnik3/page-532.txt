And the sovereign himself, and Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and all the boyars and commanders dismounted from their horses; and they began to sing the prayer service before the most pure image of the Savior and the honorable cross at the departure of the troops.

The Tsar's Speech to Prince Vladimir

When the pious tsar looked at the banner on which was the image of the Creator and the life-giving cross, he continually shed tears and unceasingly sent up prayers to God.

After the prayer service ended, the sovereign summoned Prince Vladimir Andreevich, his boyars and commanders, and all his soldiers who were in his regiment, and began to speak with compunction: "The time has come, Prince Vladimir Andreevich and all boyars and commanders, for our exploit, to strive for piety! Strive, I pray you, to suffer with one mind for piety, for the holy churches and for our holy Orthodox Christian faith, calling the merciful God to aid, without any doubt, for our fellow Orthodox Christian brothers, who by misfortune have for many years been captive and grievously suffer from the godless Kazanians. Let us remember Christ's word: 'No one has greater love than this, to lay down his life for his friends'; let us fall with pure hearts before our Creator Christ and ask Him for deliverance for the poor Christians; let Him not deliver us into the hands of our enemies, and let not our enemies rejoice at our destruction. And serve us too, as much as God helps you; do not spare your heads for piety, for there is no death, but there is life; if we do not die now, we shall die all the same, and how are we to deal with these godless ones hereafter? I myself have come to this with you; I have chosen for myself that it is better to die here than to live and see, because of my sins, Christ blasphemed and the Christians entrusted to me by God grievously tormented by the godless Kazanians. And if the merciful God sends us His mercy, seeing your steadfast faith, which conquers by grace, and grants help according to His will, then I will gladly reward you with great favor and my love, and fill you all with all sufficiency and favor you in every way, as much as the merciful God helps; and if any of us suffers, I will care for their wives and your children to the end of the age."

Prince Vladimir's Reply to the Tsar

Prince Vladimir Andreevich and all the boyars and commanders answered with one mind, with tears and much weeping: "We see you, sovereign, firm in the true law and sparing not yourself for Orthodoxy, and strengthening us for this..."
