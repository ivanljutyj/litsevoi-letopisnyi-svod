The Tatars, however, set up wooden frames opposite those gates and the breached places and filled them with earth.

On the Burning of the Bridges

The bridges at the Tsarev Gates, the Atalykov Gates, and the Nogai Gates burned all night; the city wall also burned through: the earth was scorched and crumbled, for the whole city wall had been covered with earth and sand.

(9) On Preparation for Purification

The pious tsar ordered all the regiments to prepare for the assault on Sunday, and all purified themselves and confessed to their spiritual fathers, being vouchsafed worthy grace, for the day was approaching on which all were to drink the common cup.

On the Filling of the Moats and the Building of Bridges

On the first day of the month of October, a Saturday, the Feast of the Protection of the Most Holy Theotokos, the sovereign ordered the moats filled with wood and earth and many bridges built, and ordered incessant firing from the great ordnance; and they fired all day and battered the city wall to its foundation.

On the Sending to the City of Kazan

The sovereign, the pious Christian tsar, not wishing to shed human blood, sent to the city Kamai-Murza the Kazanite and the mountain people, so that they might petition the sovereign, so that the tsar’s people in the city, seeing God’s mercy, might petition him and submit to his royal will and hand over the traitors; and the sovereign would forgive them his anger and do them no harm. But the Kazanians in the city said all as one: “We do not petition! There are Russians on the walls and in the tower, but we will set up another wall; either we shall all die or we shall hold out.” They answered like foolish madmen, for God had blinded them through their malice, so that they did not understand that they ought to repent before the tsar. The Orthodox tsar exclaimed: “Most merciful God! Behold them with Your heart: I sent to them with all submission, but they chose blood rather than peace; let their affliction turn upon their heads, and let blood be upon them and upon their children.”

On the Deliberations of the Commanders for the Assault on the City

And after this the pious tsar ordered all people to be ready in the regiments, preparing to assault the city. And he selected a multitude of his host and ordered all the boyar men to appoint commanders, as before.
