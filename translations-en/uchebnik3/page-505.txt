At the same time, keep and guard also the spiritual with all care, always remembering the hour of death and the Last Judgment at the second coming of Christ. More especially ought you to refrain from gluttony and drunkenness, and from every indecent mockery and vain laughter; and what is not pleasing to you, do not do to others. Again I beseech you, my children, by no means to despise church singing, but always to come to the church of God with the fear of God and a pure conscience, harboring neither enmity nor anger against anyone, to hear the divine singing with all reverence and attention, setting aside every worldly care, standing in church as you hope to stand in heaven; and bringing alms from your righteous labors to the church ministers, and also to the poor, then you will receive reward from God. In your homes, receiving a blessing from the priests when you eat, beware of empty conversations and shameful words, and of scoffing and all laughter, since here the angels of God stand invisibly present, and from foul conversations the angels of the Lord
