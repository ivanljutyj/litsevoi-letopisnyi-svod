In the same month of February, on the 7th day, there came an envoy of the Great Sovereign Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus', Ivanko Buinosov, who had been sent with Ignaty Yakhontov to Kazan; and with him the tsar also sent his own man with a letter. And the tsar wrote in the letter that the Grand Prince should send his great envoys, good men, of higher rank than Ignaty Yakhontov, and the tsar would send his own great envoys, men of rank.

In the year 7048 from Adam (the year 1548 from Christ)

On February 3 two wonder-working icons were brought from Rzhev to Moscow: the image of the Most Pure Hodegetria, and the other icon, the Honorable Cross. And the Most Reverend Metropolitan Iosaf with all the consecrated council, and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' with his brother Prince Yuri and with the boyars and all the people, met the wonder-working icons with crosses near the metropolitan's New Monastery.

And the Grand Prince ordered a church to be built on that place in the name of the Most Pure Mother of God.

In the same year tidings came to the Grand Prince about the Crimean Tsar, that he wished to march against his borderlands.

And on July 20, a Sunday, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' set out from Moscow for Kolomna.

In the year 7049 from Adam (the year 1549 from Christ)

In May, Bulat-prince and all the Kazan land sent from Kazan to the Grand Prince five Tatars, Chabykey and his companions, asking that the Great Sovereign forgive their guilt and send his commanders with troops to them: “And by this we will serve the Grand Prince: we will kill or seize the tsar and hand him over to the commanders; for life under the tsar is very hard for the Kazan people: he has taken the tribute from many princes and given it to the Crimeans; and there is great extortion upon the people of the land: he amasses treasure and sends it to the Crimea.”

And the Grand Prince sent back to Bulat and to all the land that he had favored them, forgiven their guilt, and was sending his commanders with troops to them.

And for the Kazan affair he sent his boyar and commander Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Shuysky and other commanders, and many court and town people from 17 towns; and he ordered the commanders to stand at Vladimir and exchange messages with Kazan.
