Lyapun and his comrades killed many of Tsar Derbysh’s people, and captured many, and with the captives awaited the leaders of Ivan Cheremisinov. And the captives said that after Derbysh was sent, the Crimean Tsar sent Atman-Duvan to Astrakhan, and with him 700 Tatars and 300 Janissaries with arquebuses, and besides that he also sent cannons for the guarding of Astrakhan.

On the sending of the icon of the wonderworker Nikola of Velikoretsk

On the 3rd day of the same month the renovated icon of Nikola of Velikoretsk was sent to Vyatka; Makary, Metropolitan of all Rus, and Andrei, protopope of the Annunciation, renovated it, since both the metropolitan and Andrei had been taught icon painting. And they adorned it with gold and pearls and precious stones, and the Tsar and Grand Prince with his Tsaritsa and with his son Tsarevich Ivan, and the metropolitan, and Prince Yury Vasilievich with his princess, and the boyars and nobles and a multitude of people accompanied the icon.

The Tsar and Grand Prince and his Tsaritsa and Tsarevich Ivan accompanied it as far as the Church of the Mother of God at Yamy and there heard Mass; and from the icon of Nikola miracles occurred without ceasing, and those afflicted with many illnesses were healed.

And from there the sovereign, having sent off the image of the lamp of the wonderworker Nikola with great honor and love, returned to his royal court; and Prince Yury Vasilievich accompanied the icon as far as Rostokino. And the icon was sent toward Vyatka by way of Ustyug, and many mercies from the image of Nikola were poured out through towns and villages.

On the arrival of envoys from Lithuania

In the same month of August envoys came from Lithuania, and they had been with the King in Lithuania at Vilno and copied out the truce charters, and brought the King to oath on the same terms on which his envoys in Moscow had sworn to our sovereign.

On Vasil-gorod

In the same year Vasil-gorod was rebuilt: it was diminished from below and set upon the hill.

On the gilding of the cupola of the church in the Sergius Monastery

In the same year, on the 11th day of the month of October, the top of the Church of the Holy and Life-Giving Trinity in the monastery of the venerable wonderworker Sergius was gilded by the command of the pious and Christ-loving Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat of all Rus, with great faith and much desire and heartfelt love, loving the adornment of the church.
