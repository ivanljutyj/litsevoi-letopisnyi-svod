And by all of them is thy name glorified, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages, amen.” “Do Thou, O Master, sanctify this place with Thy holy name.”

And the pious tsar and grand prince commanded a thanksgiving service to be sung in his regiment under his banner, after the meal rendering thanks to God; and immediately he commanded the life-giving cross to be set up with his own hands together with the protopope, and a church of the Image Not Made by Hands of our Lord Jesus Christ to be built on the place where the tsar’s banner had stood during the taking of the city.

Greeting to the Sovereign on the Kazan Tsardom

And then Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and all the boyars and commanders greeted the sovereign: “Rejoice, Orthodox tsar, who by God’s grace hast conquered the foes! Be well, sovereign, for many years in the God-given Kazan tsardom! Thou art truly our God-given protector from the godless Hagarites; by thee now the poor Christians are freed forever, and the impious place is sanctified by grace. And henceforth we ask mercy of God to multiply the years of thy life and to subdue all thy foes under thy feet; and may He give thee sons as heirs to thy tsardom, that we may live in quiet and peace.”

The Tsar’s Answer

And the sovereign answered them with compunction: “God has done this through thy care, Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, and through the suffering of all our army and the prayer of all the people; may the Lord’s will be done!”

Tsar Shigalei Congratulates the Sovereign Tsar

Also Tsar Shigalei, who had come, greeted the sovereign: “Be well, sovereign, who hast conquered the foes in thy patrimony, in Kazan, forever.”

The Tsar’s Answer to Tsar Shigalei

And the pious sovereign answered him: “Lord tsar, it is known to thee, our brother: I sent to them many times, that they might desire peace; and their cruelty is known to thee, with what evil cunning they lied for many years; and the merciful God showed us His righteous judgment and His mercy, and took vengeance on them for Christian blood.”

On the Tsar’s Entry into the City

And the sovereign commanded one street to be cleared to the Tsar’s Court from the Muraleev Gates, and the dead to be removed; and they barely cleared it.
