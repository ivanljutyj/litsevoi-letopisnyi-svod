On the Uglich Fire

In the same summer, in June, the whole town of Uglich burned, and the posad burned, and beyond the Volga more than 500 households, and 15 churches burned.

On the Embassy to Wallachia

In that same year, on July 28, the grand prince released the envoy of Voivode Stephen Stets; and together with him the grand prince sent his own envoy to Stephen in Wallachia, Skurat Zinovievich, and ordered them to go through Lithuania.

On the Church on the Simonov Courtyard

In that same year they built a stone church of the Entry of the Most Pure Mother of God on the Simonov courtyard near the Nikolsky Gate, and a stone chamber.

On the Great Chamber

In that same year the Italian architects Mark and Peter Antonin built the grand prince's stone Great Chamber on the square.

On the Building of the Novgorod Fortress

In that same year they built a stone fortress in Novgorod the Great on the old foundations.

On the Frolov Tower

In that year Peter Anthony the Italian built the Frolov Tower.

On the Envoys

In that same year, on August 30, the grand prince's envoys returned to Moscow from Maximilian, King of the Romans: Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot and the clerk Vasily Kuleshin; and with them there came to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich an envoy from Maximilian, King of the Romans, son of Emperor Frederick, Yuri Delator; and they brought from the king a treaty charter with a gold seal, and the grand prince's charter, also with a gold seal, was returned to him, and they brought the king to kiss the cross on those same treaty charters; and the grand prince's envoy brought Maximilian to kiss the cross on those same treaty charters.
