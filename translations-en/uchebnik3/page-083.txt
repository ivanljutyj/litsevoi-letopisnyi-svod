And the clerk Fyodor with Bigich at that same time set out from Murom on a boat, while the horses were led along the shore.

And Andrew went as far as Ivan’s village of Kiselevo, between Novgorod and Murom, and met Plishchka Obraztsov with the horses of Bigich and Fyodor. And Andrew told them that the grand prince had been released to the grand principality; they turned back from there, intercepted Fyodor near Dudin Monastery, as he was sailing with Bigich on the Oka River. And Fyodor, hearing this, returned with Bigich to Murom.

And when they came to Murom, Prince Vasily Ivanovich Obolensky seized Bigich and put him in chains. And Prince Dmitry, learning of this, fled to Uglich.

And the grand prince came to Murom and, after staying there a little while, set off for Vladimir; and there was great joy in all the Russian cities.

On St. Demetrius’s Day the grand prince came to Pereyaslavl; there were his mother, Grand Princess Sophia, his grand princess Maria, and his sons—Princes Ivan and Yuri—and all his princes and boyars, and boyars’ sons, and a multitude of his warriors, gathered from all the cities.

On the Earthquake

And in that same autumn, on October 1, when the grand prince was released from Kurmysh, at the sixth hour of the night the city of Moscow shook—the Kremlin, the whole posad—and the churches trembled. People were asleep at that time and did not hear what was happening; but many were not asleep and heard it, were frightened, and despaired of their lives; in the morning, with many tears, they told those who had not heard of what had happened. And in that same autumn, on the eve of Philip’s Fast, the grand prince came to Moscow on November 7, and stayed at his mother’s court outside the city in Vagankovo; and afterwards from there he came into the city to the court of Prince Yuri Patrikeevich.

On the Evil Counsel of Shemyaka against Grand Prince Vasily

The devil put it into Prince Dmitry Shemyaka’s head to desire the grand principality; he began to send to Prince Ivan of Mozhaisk, saying: “The tsar has released the grand prince, but he swore to the tsar that the tsar would sit in Moscow and in all the Russian cities and in our patrimonies, while he himself would reign in Tver.” And so, having conspired together, by the devil’s prompting they devised evil with their wicked counsellors, who then were the Konstantinoviches and their other boyars, who did not wish good to their sovereigns and to all Christendom.
