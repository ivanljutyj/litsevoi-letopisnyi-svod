In the same month of December, on the 22nd day, there came to the tsar and grand prince from the Cherkasy Prince Matlov Temryukovich, brother-in-law of the tsar and grand prince, and with him thirty men, to petition the sovereign tsar and grand prince on behalf of his father, Prince Temryuk Aidarovich, that the sovereign should show favor and, for protection from his enemies, order a town to be built on the Terek River at the mouth of the Syuyuncha River.

In the same month of December, on the 27th day, there came to the tsar and grand prince from the Nogais envoys from Prince Tinakhmat—the envoy Topas—and from Urus Murza the envoy Masyl, and envoys from other murzas, and from their wives and children.

On the same day there came to the tsar and grand prince from Abdullah, Tsar of Bukhara, the envoy Khozya-Isup, and from Azim, Tsar of Urgench, the envoy Beli-Isup.

On the same day there came to the tsar and grand prince the envoy of Said, Tsar of Samarkand, Khozya-Furt. And those envoys came to the tsar and grand prince from their sovereigns with petitions and gifts, asking that the tsar and grand prince show them favor and permit their merchants to go to Astrakhan and Kazan and to other cities of his realm.

In the same month of December, by command of the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, the town of Sokol was built in Polotsk district on the Drissa River at the Nishchevskoye Ustye, thirty versts from Polotsk, seventy versts from Sebezh, twenty-five versts from Drissa, and seventeen versts from Konets.

In the same year, in January, on the 12th day, Sunday, the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, moved into his new court outside the city, opposite the Rizopolozhenskie Gates.

On the same day, January 12, there came to the tsar and grand prince from Crimea, from Tsar Devlet-Girey, couriers Ilyushka and companions, eleven men in all, with the request that the sovereign should wish to be in firm friendship and brotherhood with Tsar Devlet-Girey, and that he should march against the enemy of the tsar and grand prince, the King of Lithuania.

And in February, on the 1st day, the tsar and grand prince sent the Crimean couriers Ilyushka and companions back to Tsar Devlet-Girey, and with them he sent his own courier Semyon Olyabyev to the tsar.

In the same month of January, on the 14th day, Tuesday, Akakiy, Bishop of Tver, reposed; and he had been bishop in Tver for twenty-four years.

In the same month of January, on the 19th day, Cornelius was installed as Archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl by Philip, Metropolitan of all Rus, and the whole consecrated council; before that he had been treasurer at the metropolitan court; and still earlier he had been abbot at the Kolotsk Dormition Monastery. At his installation there were present the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and the tsareviches, Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor.
