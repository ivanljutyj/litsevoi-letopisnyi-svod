The peasants of those towns and districts also committed many treacherous deeds and murders of people; and when they leave their feeding posts, the peasants pursue them with many lawsuits; and therefore much bloodshed occurred and defilement of souls, such as is not fitting even to hear in the Christian law; and many governors and rulers lost their old possessions, property, and ancestral estates. The pious tsar had this custom: “The beginning of his wisdom is the fear of the Lord.” In all things he keeps himself pure before God, observes church standing with fear and trembling, says nothing and thinks nothing during the holy singing, but only corrects his conscience before God; and each day he does not separate himself from the divine church rule he has received, nor from solitary prayer; then judgment and unfeigned justice for all. Royal hunts, the chase, and all else that belongs to royal custom he abandoned entirely, and endeavored in Christ to do His will in all things, and to preserve and order the state entrusted to him in all due form and truth, and to protect it from all non-Orthodox Basurmans and Latins. By his bravery and courage, and by the nobility given him by God, he strove to fight for piety every day and hour. He does nothing else, but cares only how to establish the law and the most radiant and pious Christian faith in all the states entrusted to him; also against unbelievers in all years and times he arms himself and conquers, takes up arms to free Orthodoxy from the hands of the impious; and not only does he not spare his royal neck, always enduring hardship and not caring for it, but he strives even more for the shedding of his righteous royal blood, for the deliverance of our own brothers, Orthodox Christians; and every other delight, and no amusements, enter his royal mind, only the deliverance of Christians, simply put, only the law of Christ and military affairs. His love for God and for all under his hand—for nobles, middle ranks, and lesser—is equal toward all: he loves all according to their station, favors and encourages all worthily according to rule, for their labors, and rewards them according to their origin and service; not one has remained forgotten in the matter of his salary, and he wishes to see no one wronged by anyone. And God showed such a tsar and sovereign to Orthodox lands, granted him as an imitator of former pious tsars and brave sovereigns; more than on others Almighty God showed His grace on him, and made him a conqueror of Basurmans and infidels; and as God created him, so accordingly he strives to set all under him blameless before God in the Christian law and to protect them from all evil deeds; as it is said in the Holy Gospel: “The good shepherd who lays down his soul for the sheep, and the true shepherd, not a hireling, provides for all their souls, and also to arrange food and clothing in truth; and he wishes to hear from God at the terrible second coming the righteous voice:
