On the Captivity of the Novgorod Boyars

In that same winter the Grand Prince took captive the noble Novgorod boyars and boyars’ wives, and ordered their treasury and all their villages to be registered to himself, while to them he gave estates in Moscow, town by town; and other boyars who harbored evil intent against him he ordered imprisoned in the towns.

On the Birth of the Grand Prince’s Daughter Elena

In that same spring, on April 8, a daughter was born to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Princess Elena, at the first hour of the day.

On the Founding of the Church of the Annunciation, the Chamber, and the Destruction of the First Foundation

In that same spring, on May 6, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich laid the foundation of a stone church of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Mother of God in his court, destroying the first foundation which his grandfather Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich had built; and behind the church he laid the foundation of a chamber.

On the Novgorod Archbishop, How He Left the Archbishopric

In that same summer, on June 27, Sergius, Archbishop of Novgorod, left the archbishopric and came to the Trinity-Sergius Monastery, to the place of his tonsure.

On the Founding of the Church of the Deposition of the Robe

In that same year Metropolitan Gerontius laid the foundation near his court of a stone church of the Deposition of the Robe of the Most Pure.

On the Beginning of the Building of a Stone City in Veliky Novgorod

In that same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, they began to set up the stone walls of the Detinets in Veliky Novgorod on the old foundations on the Sophia Side.

In the year 6993 from Adam (1493 from Christ)

On December 9, Niphon, Archimandrite of Simonov, was installed as bishop in Suzdal.

On the Installation of an Archbishop in Novgorod

In that same month, on the 12th day, Gennadius, Archimandrite of Chudov, was installed as archbishop in Veliky Novgorod and Pskov.

On the Fire in Moscow

In that same spring, on April 12, on Tuesday, the city of Moscow burned.
