And then God's providence restrained them thus: when the godless envoys came, but were still far from the city, they saw a field full of countless Russian troops. The Tatars saw this, and great fear fell upon them; and they returned and in trembling ran to their tsar, telling him of the great Russian host. The tsar was amazed, and, not believing them, grew angry with them, and in fury called them cowards; and soon he sent others. And those, having gone, saw an even greater Russian host, and were more terrified than the first.

And, possessed by great fear, they ran to the tsar and told him that they had seen another host on the sides of the suburbs. The tsar was perplexed, for he knew from Russian captives who had been with him that it was impossible for such a numerous host to gather together so quickly.

And a third time he sent one of his close men to learn the truth. And he went, and saw a countless multitude of Moscow troops, and was terrified, and, trembling, quickly ran with cries: “O tsar! Why do you delay? Let us flee faster; I do not know whether we shall be able to escape, for with savage swiftness an immeasurable multitude of troops from Moscow is coming against us!”

And such great fear fell upon the tsar and all the Tatars that they fled without looking back, and as they ran they said to one another: “Run, run faster and do not delay, for the Rus, breathing fury, are pursuing us; they want to overtake us and defeat us!”

And, possessed by such fear, they ran back to their own lands, bringing with them many Russian captives; and they drove all their own ulus people living beyond Perekop into the fortified town, for fear of the Russian host.

About Metropolitan Varlaam, How He Left His Throne

In the same year, Metropolitan Varlaam left his holy office on December 17. That same winter Daniel, abbot of the Joseph Monastery, was appointed to the metropolitan see on February 27.

About the Appointment of a Bishop to Ryazan

That same winter, on March 23, Metropolitan Daniel appointed Jonah as bishop to Ryazan.

About the Appointment of a Bishop to Tver

On the 30th day of the same month, Akakios was appointed bishop to Tver.

In the year 7031 from Adam (1531 from Christ)

In September Lithuanian envoys came to Moscow: Pan Peter Stanislavov, and Bogush Bogovitinov, and the scribe Ivashko Gornostaev, and they concluded a five-year truce with the grand prince.
