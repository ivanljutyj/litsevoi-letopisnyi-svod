This was why: since by God’s judgment and by the sovereign will of the tsar and grand prince, the brother of the tsar and grand prince, Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, took as his wife the daughter of Prince Dmitry Paletsky, Prince Dmitry Paletsky sent Vasily to Princess Eufrosinia and her son Prince Vladimir, asking that the princess and her son graciously grant Prince Yuri Vasilyevich and his wife, Prince Yuri’s princess, an appanage according to the testament of the grand prince, and that they would not oppose them, Princess Eufrosinia and Prince Vladimir, in the matter of the throne, and were ready to serve them.

And he ordered it to be found out at whose instigation this resistance had occurred, since without instigation such a thing could not be; and he ordered his clerk Vasily Grigoriev, son of Zakharov Gniliev, to inquire into it, because he was among the sovereign’s close associates.

But the clerk Vasily, by some unknown means, slandered before the sovereign his boyars Prince Ivan Kubensky and Fyodor and Vasily Vorontsov in this affair.

And the grand prince ordered the execution of Prince Ivan Kubensky, Fyodor Vorontsov, and Vasily Mikhailov, also son of Vorontsov: their heads were cut off on the twenty-first day of the month of July, on Saturday.

And Ivan Petrov, son of Fedorovich, he ordered seized and exiled to Beloozero, and ordered him placed under guard.

And the treasurer Nikita Funikov fell ill early and rose only when the sovereign had recovered considerably, and then he kissed the cross, after all the people.

And it was said of Prince Dmitry Kurlyatev and of Nikita Funikov that they had been in correspondence with Princess Eufrosinia and with her son Prince Vladimir, and wanted him for the throne, but did not want the tsarevich Prince Dmitry on the throne because of his infancy.

And they dragged them out through the front doors into the square and beyond the city, and laid them before the stake on which executions are carried out.

Present at this council were the archpriest of the Annunciation, Fyodor Barmin, Prince Fyodor Shuysky, Prince Yuri Temkin, Ivan Petrov Fedorov, Grigory Yuryevich Zakharyin, Fyodor Nagoy, and many others.
