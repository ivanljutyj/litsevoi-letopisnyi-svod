On the Tsar’s Campaign against Kazan.
In the year 7058 from Adam (1558 from Christ)

Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus could no longer tolerate the Kazan oath-breakers for their many violations of the oath and their iniquities, since they made godless vows, promising to abide in the will of the sovereign tsar and grand prince, but did not abide in their promises for any time at all, and only wrought falsehood like evil beasts, taking many Christians captive through their godless perjury, defiling many churches, and laying them waste. His pious realm grieved greatly over this and could not endure it from them; because of their godless perjury the tsar took counsel with his father Metropolitan Macarius, with his brother Prince Yuri Vasilievich, with Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and with the boyars, wishing to march against the Kazan Tsar Utemish-Girey, son of Tsar Safa-Kirey, and against the evil oath-breaking Kazanians. And he declared his royal decision: the sovereign wished to go himself, and he sent his commanders ahead of him and ordered them to muster with their men: in the large regiment, to the commander Boyar Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky and to Prince Vladimir Ivanovich Vorotynsky in Suzdal; to the commander of the vanguard regiment, Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky, he ordered mustering in Shuya, and to another commander of the same vanguard, Prince Vasily Fedorovich Lopatin, he ordered mustering in Murom; to the commanders of the right-hand regiment, Boyar Prince Alexander Borisovich Gorbaty and the Uglich dvoretsky Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany, he ordered mustering in Kostroma; to the commanders of the left-hand regiment, Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Vorotynsky and Boris Ivanovich Saltykov, he ordered mustering in Yaroslavl; to the commanders of the guard regiment, Boyar Prince Yuri Mikhailovich Bulgakov and Prince Yuri Ivanovich Kashin, he ordered mustering in Yuryev.

And the pious Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus himself set out from the reigning city of Moscow for the capital city of Vladimir on the 24th day of November, a Sunday, and with him went his brother Prince Yuri Vasilievich and many boyars. In Moscow he ordered his brother Vladimir Andreevich and the boyars to remain. And the Tsar and Grand Prince arrived in Vladimir on the 3rd day of December, a Tuesday.
