At first God freed the Christians of Rus' from Basurman slavery, and that Great Horde was destroyed by them. And those Horde tsars began to live in Astrakhan, while the Great Horde became empty, and its place—in the district of the city of Astrakhan, two days' journey up the Volga—is called Great Sarai. And the tsar and grand prince, trusting in almighty God and His great mercies, and in the prayer of His most pure Mother and the great wonderworkers, and for his own offense and disgrace, because Tsar Yamgurchei had broken his oaths and plundered the ambassador, and at the petition of the Nogai murzas, resolved to send his army against Astrakhan.

The Tsar and Grand Prince Sent Tsar Derbysh Against Astrakhan

And the tsar and grand prince sent Tsar Derbysh against Astrakhan, and with him he sent his commanders Prince Yuri Ivanovich Pronsky Shemyakin and his comrades, and ordered him to march in three regiments: in the large regiment—Prince Yuri Ivanovich and Mikhailo Petrovich Golovin; in the forward regiment—the bedchamber steward Ignaty Mikhailovich Veshnyakov and Shiryai Kobyakov; in the guard regiment—Stefan Grigoriev, son of Sidorov, and Prince Andrei Bulgak Boryatinsky; and with them courtiers of the tsar's court and selected boyar sons from various towns, and streltsy and Cossacks; and with Prince Yuri he ordered Prince Alexander Ivanovich Vyazemsky to be with the Vyatchans. And the sovereign ordered Prince Yuri and his comrades to march when the ice broke.

Concerning the Sending to Ismail Murza

In the same year, in March, the sovereign sent back to the Nogai Horde, to Ismail Murza and all the murzas, their ambassadors, and sent his own ambassador to Ismail, Mikula Brovtsyn, and instructed Nikula in all his affairs, how he should act, as the tsar and grand prince had decided.

A Bishop Was Installed at Krutitsy

In the same month, on the 11th day, in the fifth week of Holy Lent, Nifont, archimandrite of the Novospassky Monastery, was installed as bishop at Krutitsy.

Stupa and Myagkoy Arrived at Moscow
