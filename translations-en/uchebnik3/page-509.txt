But God granted them, for their piety and for their suffering for Orthodoxy, after their departure from this deceptive world, heavenly and endless joy and gladness instead of earthly things: to be with their Lord and to stand before Him with the angels, and to rejoice with all the righteous. For Holy Scripture says: “Eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor has it entered into the heart of man, what God has prepared for those who love Him and keep His holy commandments.” But to you, wife, I command that you in no way grieve over my departure, but I bid you remain in great spiritual labors, often come to the holy churches of God, offer many prayers for me and for yourself, give abundant alms to the poor and to many needy, and command that those under our royal disgrace be pardoned, and command those confined in prisons to be released, so that we may receive special mercy from God—I for courage, and you for good deeds.” And hearing this from her sovereign, the pious tsar, concerning his departure, the pious tsarina was wounded by unbearable sorrow and could not stand from her great grief, had the pious tsar not held up his spouse with his own hands, for she wanted to fall to the ground; and she was long speechless and wept bitterly; and scarcely could she restrain herself from her violent tears and say to her sovereign, the pious tsar and Grand Prince Ivan: “You, my pious sovereign, keep the commandments of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ; if you wish to lay down your soul for the Orthodox faith and for Orthodox Christians, how then shall I endure my sovereign’s departure? Or who will bring me word and tell me of this great mercy from God concerning my pious sovereign—that the pious tsar and autocrat of all Rus has received mercy from the All-Merciful Almighty God, and with all his Christ-loving army fought the impious and overcame them, and returned healthy to his realm? O All-Merciful God! Hear the tears and lamentation of Your servant; grant me to hear this and to see my sovereign healthy, praising Your mercy and rejoicing. Do not remember, Master, our many sins, but show us Your mercy according to Your great mercy, according to Your many bounties. And you, O Most Merciful and Most Bountiful and Mighty Helper of the Christian race, Queen and Lady, Mother of the Heavenly King and Lord, Most Pure Mother of God! Hear the prayer of Your servant, stir yourself to prayer to Christ our God, born of You, that He may grant my sovereign victory over his adversaries and return him healthy, and grant me to see him praising Your mercy, for by Your, O Lady, intercession and prayer he has overcome his enemies.” The pious tsar, having comforted his tsarina with words, admonition, and a kiss, departs from her.
