And with them he left his great nobles and many boyar sons, and streltsy, and Cossacks.

The Ar people petitioned the sovereign

In the same month on the 10th day Nikita Kazarinov and Kamaimurza arrived, and with them many Ar people, and they petitioned the tsar and sovereign that the sovereign show them mercy; and they, the whole land, petition the sovereign and give yasak. And the tsar and sovereign granted the Ar common people, ordered yasak to be taken from them as it had been under Magmedelim the tsar, and commanded his boyar Alexander Borisovich to have them brought to the oath and to have yasak taken from them, and to administer them in all matters.

The Meadow people petitioned the sovereign

On that same day Meadow people from Yak and from many places came to the sovereign and petitioned the sovereign on behalf of all the Meadow people, that just as the Ar people they desired the sovereign’s favor. And the tsar and sovereign granted them as he had the others: he commanded his boyar to bring them to the oath and to administer justice; and on that same day all the common people gave the oath.

In the same month, on the day, the sovereign decided with Prince Vladimir Andreevich and with all the boyars to go to Moscow: the sovereign himself to go by the Volga in boats, and he sent on the horse route his servant and voivode Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Vorotynsky with his companions, to go to Vasilgorod along the bank.

On the departure from Kazan

And on that same day the tsar and sovereign heard a prayer service at the Annunciation of the Most Pure Mother of God and, having placed all hope in the merciful God and His Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers, said: “You, our Master Christ, have done this. Do You also preserve the city and the people in Your name!” And he commanded his boyar and voivode Prince Alexander Borisovich with his companions to do everything according to his royal instruction, and went to the Volga; and on that day he spent the night on the bank of the Volga.

And the next day the sovereign sat with Prince Vladimir Andreevich in the ushkuy boats and rowed up the Volga, and rowed to the town of Sviyazhsk that same day, and spent the night there.

And he commanded his boyar and voivode Pyotr Ivanovich to administer the Mountain people and take yasak, ordered that they be protected in all things, and ordered every kind of justice to be done over the Mountain people; and in disputed matters between the Mountain people and the Kazan people the sovereign ordered the Kazan voivodes to communicate with the Sviyazhsk voivodes and the Sviyazhsk voivodes with the Kazan voivodes.
