The divine and ecumenical seven councils and the divine icons; and about this speaks kyr Peter, Patriarch of Antioch, that not all will obey the word of the pope, and they answer passionately that it has already been said. And about this from the beginning to this place.

Canon 55 of the Holy Sixth Council of Trullo

"Since we have learned that those living in Rome in the holy fast of Great Lent fast on Saturdays contrary to the received church tradition, the holy council has decided that in the Roman Church the canon should be inviolably observed, which says: 'If anyone among the clergy is seen fasting on the Holy Week or on a Saturday, except for one, he shall be deposed; if a layman, he shall be excommunicated.'"

Canon 56 of the same council

"We have also learned that in the Armenian country and in other countries, on Saturdays and Sundays of the holy Great Lent, some people eat eggs and cheese. But it has been deemed good that the Church of God throughout the whole universe, following one rule, observes the fast and abstains from every slaughtered animal, as well as from eggs and cheese, which are likewise the fruit and product of that from which we abstain. If clergy do not observe this, they shall be deposed; if laypeople, they shall be excommunicated."

From the Life of Saint Simeon the Wonderworker of the Divine Mountain

Saint Simeon told us about the holy Great Lent. An angel appeared to him and said, "When I was still an infant and was in the first place of my dwelling, I heard that people do not observe the holy Great Lent, eating cheese and eggs. And before those who wondered at this, the angel of God appeared, holding in his hand a cup, a goat, and a knife; and taking it, he slaughtered the goat before me and let the blood into the cup; and I saw the blood, and it was curdled and became white as milk. And he said to me: 'This is the cheese that people eat during the holy Great Lent; this is the blood of the goat. Therefore command the brethren who gather to you not to eat cheese and eggs during the holy Great Lent, for this is like dragons and serpents, it consists of impurity.' And he mentioned the prophet Isaiah, who said: 'Break the eggs of the asp and weave the spider's web, and whoever wishes to taste of those eggs, break the shell and you will find a basilisk in it.' I was compelled to say this, not considering food unclean, which He has cleansed—for it is not right to consider anything unclean at all, since it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer for those who receive it with faith—but for profit, as monks, I have confessed these things to you, so that you may increase sanctification for yourselves in the holy days, which I have arranged to be kept also by those who were with me before you."
