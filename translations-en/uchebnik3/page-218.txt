With tears then they prayed to God and the Most Pure Mother of God, and to the great wonderworkers Leontius and Cyril, that God would quell the strife, and that they might live in obedience to their hierarch, the Archbishop of Rostov, as their venerable elder Cyril had lived.

On the Miraculous Conception and Birth of the Great Prince Vasily Ivanovich, Autocrat of All Rus'

And so, glorified by God, the Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat, reigned prosperously. And God granted him abundant fruitfulness: by his first wife, the Grand Princess Maria, daughter of the Grand Prince of Tver, a son was born to him, the Great Prince Ivan, his namesake, called the Young; and by his second wife, the Grand Princess Tsarevna Sophia, three beautiful daughters were born; but at that time she had not yet succeeded in bearing a single son. The Great Prince Ivan and his Grand Princess Sophia grieved over this and prayed to God that He would grant them to bear a son as heir to their kingdom, which they received.

And for this reason, once this Christ-loving Grand Princess Sophia, out of great faith and heartfelt desire, after good counsel with her pious spouse, the autocratic Great Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus', laboriously resolved to walk on foot from Moscow to the most renowned great monastery of the Most Holy and Life-Giving Trinity and of the great luminary, the venerable wonderworker Sergius, to pray for the birth of a son.

She set out and reached the monastery village called Klementyevo, and when she descended from there into the valley that was near the very monastery, she suddenly saw with her own eyes a reverend and comely monk coming to meet her, in whom by the icon she recognized the venerable wonderworker Sergius, holding in his hands a small male child, whom he suddenly thrust into the bosom of the Grand Princess, and then became invisible.

She, from such an unusual vision, trembled and began to grow faint and to sink toward the ground. The noble women who were with her supported her with their hands, and they themselves were greatly terrified, not knowing what had happened to her.

She sat down and began to search with her hands in her bosom for the child thrust there, and did not feel him, and then understood that this was a visitation of the great wonderworker Sergius.

And she rose to her feet, and with good hope and in health came to the monastery. And she prayed for a long time, and lovingly kissed the healing relics of the venerable Sergius, tenderly falling down before his honorable shrine, hoping to obtain what she had asked.
