On the Installation and Consecration of the Wooden Church of Saint John the Forerunner at Old Vagan'kovo

In the same month of August, the faithful and Christ-loving great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus set up at the Annunciation on Old Vagan'kovo a votive wooden church of the Beheading of the Head of the Lord's Forerunner John, and in it two side chapels: of the Holy Apostle Thomas and of Saint Peter the Wonderworker. This the great sovereign did, fulfilling his vow, and he took part with his own royal hands before all the craftsmen, and after him they began to build and completed it in one day.

On the Consecration

It was consecrated on that same day. Present at that consecration were the grand prince with Grand Princess Elena and their son Prince Ivan, the boyars, and a multitude of people.

On the Installation of Archimandrite Siluan at the Yuriev Monastery in Veliky Novgorod

In that same year the sovereign, the grand prince and autocrat of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, sent to his pilgrim Archbishop Makary in Veliky Novgorod and commanded the archbishop to ordain his archdeacon, the monk Siluan, as priest, to invest him with the rank and the politsa, and to send him as archimandrite to the Yuriev Monastery.

And the God-loving Archbishop Makary, according to the word of the sovereign grand prince, ordained the humble elder, the monk Siluan, as priest in the cathedral church of Holy Sophia in Veliky Novgorod, and invested him with the rank and the politsa on the second day of October. And he sent him with his boyar sons and with the choristers to the monastery of the glorious Great Martyr George as archimandrite, rendering honor to the holy church and the venerable monastery with spiritual songs.

On the Fire in Veliky Novgorod

That same summer, by God's permission, there was a fire on Yaneva Street by the prisons before Matins. It burned on Doslana Street, and the church of the Holy Forty Martyrs burned down. And all three prisons burned: the Lithuanians were brought out of two prisons, but from the third, the city prison, they did not manage to bring them out, and 145 people burned in the prison.

And the God-loving Archbishop Makary ordered a pit dug by the church of the Holy Archangel Gabriel at the end of Khrenkova Street, and he himself sang the funeral service, and with him the priests of Saint Sophia Cathedral and the priests and deacons of the whole city, and they buried them.
