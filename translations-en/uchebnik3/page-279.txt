And then the Grand Prince’s children bowed and congratulated both Grand Princes, and after them the boyars, and all the people.

The Metropolitan’s Instruction:

“Lord and son, Grand Prince Dmitry! By God’s will your grandfather the Grand Prince has granted you and blessed you with the grand principality; and you, lord and son, keep the fear of God in your heart, love truth and mercy and just judgment, be obedient to your sovereign and grandfather the Grand Prince, and care with all your heart for all Orthodox Christianity; and we bless you, our lord and son, and pray to God for your health.” The Grand Prince’s speech to his grandson: “Grandson Dmitry, I have granted you and blessed you with the grand principality; keep the fear of God in your heart, love truth and mercy and just judgment, and care with all your heart for all Orthodox Christianity.” And then the Metropolitan and the Grand Princes came down from the platform, and the Metropolitan performed the dismissal of the prayer service, and then they began the liturgy.

After the liturgy was completed, Grand Prince Ivan went to his own quarters, while Grand Prince Dmitry, in the cap and barmy, had only just come out of the doors of the Church of the Most Pure when Prince Yury, son of the Grand Prince, showered him there three times with gold and silver coins; and the Grand Prince’s children walked beside him, and all the boyars. He also showered him three times with gold and silver coins before the Church of the Archangel and before the Church of the Annunciation.

On the Arrival of the Russian Envoy from Tsargrad

That same winter, in February, Mikhail Pleshcheyev came from Tsargrad to Moscow.

On the Disgrace and Imprisonment of the Boyars of Prince Ivan Yuryevich and Others. In the Year 7007 from Adam (1507 from Christ)

In the month of January the Grand Prince ordered his boyars Prince Ivan Yuryevich with his children and Prince Semyon Ivanovich Ryapolovsky to be seized; and he ordered Prince Semyon Ryapolovsky to be executed: his head was cut off on the Moskva River just below the bridge on February 5, a Tuesday. But he pardoned Prince Ivan Yuryevich and ordered that he not be executed, and sent him to take monastic vows at the Trinity; and his son Prince Vasily Ivanovich Krivoy he sent to the monastery at Kirillov on Beloozero.
