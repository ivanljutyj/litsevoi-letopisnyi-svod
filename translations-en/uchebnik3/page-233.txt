He astonished the angels and gladdened people by his courage, and those who struggled alongside him unto death received from God the remission of sins and were honored with martyrs’ crowns, just as the ancient martyrs who suffered for the faith from tormentors and died for the confession of Christ’s faith. So these warriors too, in the last times, died for the faith and for God’s churches and received crowns on a par with the first martyrs. Those who were then wounded by the enemies and remained alive after the victory washed away their former sins with their blood and became valiant victors over the enemy; they became worthy of great praise and honor not only from people but also from God. So now too, if you follow the example of your ancestor, the praiseworthy Grand Prince Dmitry, and strive to deliver Christ’s flock from the spiritual wolf, then the Lord God, seeing your boldness, will also help you and subdue your enemies under your feet. And you will stand before God, who will preserve you, as a sound and unharmed victor, and the Lord God will cover your head with His shade in the day of battle. If you yourself, O strong and brave tsar, and all your Christ-loving army suffer to blood and unto death for the Orthodox faith of Christ and for God’s holy churches, as faithful children of the Church, in whose walls you were born through the spiritual and incorruptible bath, holy baptism, for which you also suffered unto death and were baptized with a second baptism, as martyrs, by your own blood, then blessed and most blessed will you be in the eternal inheritance, having received this baptism; and after it you will not be able to sin, but you will receive from Almighty God incorruptible crowns and ineffable joy, which eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor has it entered into the heart of man. Just as the first martyrs and confessors, so these last also shall be—as the Lord said—the first last and the last first. But if you argue and say: “We have a prohibition received from our forefathers not to raise a hand against the tsar, so how can I break the oath and rise against the tsar?” Listen, then, God-loving tsar: if an oath was given under compulsion, then we are permitted to forgive and absolve from such oaths, and we forgive, absolve, and bless—as the most holy metropolitan, so also we and the whole God-loving council—since you will go against him not as against a tsar but as against a robber, a predator, and a fighter against God. It is better for you to lie and obtain eternal life than to remain faithful to the oath and perish—that is, to let them into our land for the destruction and extermination of all Christianity and for the desolation and defilement of holy churches. Do not imitate accursed Herod, who did not want to break his oath and perished. Or was it some prophet who prophesied, or some apostle or hierarch who taught you to obey this God-abhorred and vile self-proclaimed tsar, O great Christian tsar of the Russian land?
