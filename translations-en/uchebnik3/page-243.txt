On the Birth of the Daughter of the Grand Prince, Feodosia

In that same spring, on May 29, a daughter was born to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, Princess Feodosia, at the third hour of the night.

On the Repose of the Nun and Grand Princess Martha

In that same summer, on July 4, the pious Grand Princess Maria, mother of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich, departed this life; she was named in the monastic rank Nun Martha and was laid in the Church of the Ascension in Moscow.

On the Founding of the Tower and the Secret Passage

On July 19 a tower was founded in Moscow on the river, and beneath the tower a secret passage was constructed; Anton Fryazin built it.

On Ivan Ralo

In that same year Ivan Ralo Paleolog came from Constantinople to serve the Grand Prince, with his wife and children.

On the Campaign of the Grand Prince with an Army to Tver

In that same year, on August 21, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich renounced his oath of the cross to Grand Prince Mikhail Borisovich of Tver because of his wrongdoing, because he had sent letters to the Lithuanian King Casimir and had roused him with an army against Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich; and for this he went against him to Tver with an army, with his son Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich, with his brothers Prince Andrei and Prince Boris, with his voivodes and with many forces.

In that same month Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich sent a messenger to his patrimony, Great Novgorod, to his boyar and Novgorod governor Yakov Zakharyich, ordering him to go to Tver with all the Novgorodian forces.

On the Birth of Ivan, Son of the Grand Prince

In that same winter, on February 13, a son, Ivan, was born to Grand Prince Ivan by the Greek woman.

On the Horde Tsar Murtaza

In that same winter the Horde Tsar Murtaza, son of Akhmat, came to the Crimean Tsar Mengli Giray, wishing to winter with him, because there was a severe famine then in the Horde.
