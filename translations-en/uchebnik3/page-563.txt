And Christ, the rewarder who does not forget, for your labors in His name and for His people, fulfilled His word: “Good and faithful servant, you were faithful from youth; I will set you over many.” And further the Lord God granted you from your loins to bear a firstborn son to your tsaritsa, the great princess Anastasia, the tsarevich Dmitry Ivanovich. But we, your pilgrims, what can we say to God against His great mercy and gift to you, the pious tsar, His faithful servant? Only we say: Great Lord, there are no words to praise Your wonders! To you, tsar, we can only bow our heads, and what praises shall we bring you? You, with God’s help, delivered us from the barbarian invasion by your noble valor, and also destroyed their dwellings to the foundation and freed our poor captive brethren from bondage. And with the delivered brethren we say to you: Rejoice, pious tsar, and be glad, chief of the pastors who brought them to Christ! Hail, our sovereign, pious tsar, with your tsaritsa the great princess Anastasia and with your God-given son the tsarevich Dmitry, and with your brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, and with your boyars, and with all the Christian host saved by God in the ruling city of Moscow and in all your kingdoms and in the God-given kingdom of Kazan to you in this year and in previous years, from generation to generation for many years! And to you, tsar, pious sovereign, for your labors, with the consecrated council and with all Orthodox Christians, we bow our heads.” And Archbishop Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, with the whole council and with all the Christian people, fall to the ground before the tsar and from heartfelt joy shed tears. And they shed many grateful and joyful tears over God’s gift and over the tsar’s coming in health.

And then the pious tsar changed his military attire and put on his royal raiment: he placed on his neck and breast the life-giving cross and on his head the cap of Monomakh, that is, the royal crown, and on his shoulders a diadem.

And he went after the crosses and the wonder-working icons with the metropolitan on foot into the city, and came to the cathedral apostolic church of the most pure Mother of God, of Her honorable Dormition.

And he falls down before the wonder-working image of the Mother of God, which the divine apostle Luke the Evangelist painted, and before the much-healing relics of Peter the Wonderworker and Jonah the Wonderworker, and uttered many grateful prayers with tears, and, having received the blessing from the metropolitan, went to his royal chambers.
