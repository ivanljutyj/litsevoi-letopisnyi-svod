The Grand Prince ordered them to be answered as in the first reply: “You sent Nazar and Zakhar and called us sovereigns, and therefore we sent our envoys to ask you what sort of rule you wanted from us. And you renounced that and accused us of lying. Since we could not endure that, having prayed to the Lord God and His most pure Mother and all His saints, we have marched against you, taking God as our help and the power of the life-giving Cross of the Lord. And if our patrimony Great Novgorod wishes to petition us, the great princes, they know how to petition.”

And the Archbishop and those with him petitioned that the Grand Prince permit them to go into the city and then come again under the same safe-conduct charter; and the Grand Prince permitted them.

On that same day Tsarevich Danyar came before the city, and with him the Grand Prince’s voivode Vasily Obrazets with the men of Borovsk; and the Grand Prince ordered Tsarevich Danyar to take position at the Kirillov Monastery of St Andrew of Sitetsk, also on the Gorodishchenskaya side; and he ordered the Tsarevich’s attendants Prince Pyotr Obolensky and Prince Ivan Zvenets to take position at the Kovalev Spassky Monastery; and he ordered Vasily Obrazets with the men of Borovsk to take position at the Spassky Monastery at Volotovo.

On the third day of the same month Prince Andrei Vasilyevich the Elder came to the city, and the Grand Prince ordered him to take position at the Resurrection Derevyanitsky Monastery; and he ordered the Tver voivode Prince Mikhail Fedorovich to take position with his regiment at St Nicholas Monastery on Ostrovka.

On Friday, December 5, Archbishop Feofil came from Novgorod to the Grand Prince, and with him the posadniks and wealthy townsmen who had been with him before; and with the Grand Prince then were his brothers Prince Andrei and Prince Boris, and Prince Andrei the Younger.

And the Archbishop and all who were with him petitioned, as they had petitioned before, that the sovereign show mercy; and they declared themselves guilty in that for which they had sent Nazar and Zakhar, and from which they had renounced before the Grand Prince’s envoys.

And the Grand Prince ordered them to be answered: “And since you, Archbishop, and all our patrimony Great Novgorod have declared yourselves guilty before us, the great princes, and have renounced those words that you sent to us, and now yourselves testify against yourselves, and ask what our rule should be in our patrimony Novgorod; then we, the great princes, want our rule to be such as it is in Moscow. So we want it to be in our patrimony Great Novgorod!”

And the Archbishop with all his posadniks and wealthy men began to petition that the Grand Prince would show favor, permit them to go into the city and consider it, and by the same safe-conduct charter order them to come to him.
