On the Sending of the Voyevodas to the Sviyaga

And the sovereign sent voyevodas by boats to the Sviyaga and ordered them to guard his business and to wait for him, the sovereign—boyars and voyevodas Prince Alexander Borisovich Gorbaty and Prince Peter Ivanovich Shuysky, and other voyevodas; and with the equipment the sovereign sent boyar Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov, and sent many of his supplies to feed the army, and sent his stewards with all the household supplies.

And to the Kama the sovereign sent boyar Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Glinsky and okolnichy Ivan Ivanovich Umny, and with them boyar sons, Cossacks, and streltsy; and from Vyatka he ordered Pauk Zabolotsky to go to the Kama with the Ustyug volostels and Vyatka men, and ordered Grigory Sukin to go with the Vyatchans.

And he ordered Prince Mikhail to place boyar sons, streltsy, Cossacks, and Vyatchans at all the crossings along the Kama and Vyatka; and he ordered the Sviyaga voyevodas likewise to place men at all the crossings along the Volga, so that military men should not go to Kazan or from Kazan.

That spring, in April, Mikhalko Shipilov came galloping to the sovereign from the Sviyaga voyevodas, from boyar and voyevoda Prince Semyon Ivanovich and from all the boyars and voyevodas, with letters; and in the letters it was written that boyar and dvoretsky Daniil Romanovich had come to them, who had sent Tsar Shigalei with the tsaritsa upstream according to the sovereign’s order, and that the hill people were in turmoil, many were negotiating with the Kazan people, and they saw little truth in them, and there was great disobedience among them, and along the Tsivil the hill people did not go to the town on the Sviyaga, and by their sins a great sickness had come upon the sovereign’s people—scurvy—and many had died of sores, and others were dying and lay sick: boyar sons, streltsy, and Cossacks.

And the sovereign sends to Nizhny Novgorod, ordering boyars Prince Alexander Borisovich and Prince Peter Ivanovich to go quickly to the Sviyaga with those men who had assembled to them early, and he ordered boyar Mikhail Yakovlevich to wait for the equipment, and ordered boyar Semyon Konstantinovich to wait for the remaining boyar sons and go together with the equipment.
