In the same year, in August, on the second day, a Friday, Crimean people came to the borderland of the tsar and grand prince, at Potega, and at their head was Devey-Murza with Crimean people; there were three thousand of them in all. And the next day they went away.

And the commanders of the tsar and grand prince—boyar Ivan Dmitrievich Belsky, Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Glinsky, and other commanders—went after them from Tula. They overtook them on the third day in the Field, on the Don, at early dawn.
