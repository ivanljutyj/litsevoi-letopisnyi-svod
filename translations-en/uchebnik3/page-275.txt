On the Capture of Ivangorod by the Germans

In the same summer, on August 19, a Friday, the Germans came from overseas, from the city of Stockholm of the Swedish state, from Prince Sten Sture, by raid on 70 ships into the Narva River below Ivangorod, and began to assault the city with cannons and firearms, and set fire to the courtyards in the city, shooting fire, since they had no opponent.

But the dashing governor and Ivangorod namestnik Prince Yuri Babich, filled with a warlike and courageous spirit, did not resist the enemies and did not encourage the townspeople, but soon took fright and fled from the city; and in the city there was no governor left, and the people were few, and there was no military supply in the city; while Prince Ivan Bryukho and Prince Ivan Gundor stood with men not far from the city and saw the city being seized by the Germans, but did not go to the city’s aid.

And the Germans seized the city, and found no one in it opposing them, and soon cruelly plundered property and goods, and cut down people, and took others away with them into captivity; and so they soon returned and fled from the city to the sea.

On the Envoys to Crimea and Tsargrad

In the year 7005 from Adam (1505 from Christ), in September, the Grand Prince sent his envoys to Crimea to Tsar Mengli Giray, Prince Ivan Zvenets, who also reposed there, and to Tsargrad to the Turkish Sultan Bayezid-Sultan, son of Mehmed-Sultan, he sent Mikhail Andreevich Pleshcheev, and with him he sent many merchants from Moscow, and they went by the Field. Before Mikhail there had been no envoys from the Grand Prince to the Turkish Sultan, and Mikhail was sent with letters concerning friendship and love.

In the same autumn, on the 18th day of September, a Sunday, the stone church of the Transfiguration of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ in the monastery at Novy was consecrated by the Most Reverend Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus, under Archimandrite Afanasy Shchedry.

In the same winter, on the 18th day of December, a Sunday, the elder Evfimy from the Trinity-Sergius Monastery was appointed Bishop of Sarsk.

On the Appointment of a Bishop in Ryazan

In the same winter, in March, in the third week of Lent, Protasius was appointed bishop in Ryazan by Metropolitan Simon.
