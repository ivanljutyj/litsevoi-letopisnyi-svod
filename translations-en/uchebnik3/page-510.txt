On the Campaign

And the sovereign went to the cathedral church of the Most Pure Mother of God of the reigning city of Moscow, of Her Honorable and Glorious Dormition, and lovingly falls down before Her wonder-working image, and with weeping for a long time offers up prayers, and places his hope in Her, and commits into Her hands the city and the people and all the realm entrusted to him by the almighty right hand of Her Son and God, and says plainly: “Do Thou, O Lady, cover this realm with Thy precious mantle and deliver it from all evils; guide us on the path of salvation and grant us to see Thy image in joy and all that is Thine.”

Likewise, falling down with tears before the wonder-working icons of the Lady and of all the saints, he prays; he comes to the wonder-working relics of the wonder-workers Peter and Jonah, and there too, praying long with tears—that is known to God alone.

And he receives blessing from the chief hierarch Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, and says to the metropolitan and the archbishops and bishops and all the consecrated council: “Be so kind, lords, bestir yourselves at this time to fasting and prayer, and pray to the Creator and His Most Pure Mother of God and the great wonder-workers, that Christ may not be angry with us to the end, and may not remember the sins of my youth and my ignorance, and may not, because of my sins, bind such a multitude of people, but may look upon His people with His mercies and grant you help and strengthening, and may not deliver us to our enemies, and may free the many Christians tormented by the godless Kazanians, and may work help for us according to His holy will. And I, lords, bow my head before you and beseech your holiness. But you, my lord father Macarius, Metropolitan of all Rus, strive, as much as God gives you, to pray to our Master Christ to protect our realm in all things; instruct our brother in good and gracious deeds, and likewise instruct in all things the boyars who remain here; also, my lord, take spiritual care in all things of my wife, Tsaritsa Anastasia, who is with child.” The metropolitan and the hierarchs say to the sovereign with tears: “It is fitting for you to stand manfully for the flock entrusted to you and to multiply the talent given to you, so that you may not be called a hireling but a true shepherd, who lays down his soul for the sheep; but only, O Tsar, upon whom are you leaving us, your orphaned ones? Good shepherd, light of our eyes, where are you going?” The sovereign said to them: “We place all our hope and trust in the Most Pure One, and I trust in your holy prayers: ask, and receive.” The metropolitan blessed the sovereign with the life-giving cross and said: “May the grace of the Holy Spirit be with you, Orthodox Tsar, and with all your Christ-loving army.”

And having exchanged the kiss in Christ, the sovereign receives blessing from all who stood there, and with such blessing and prayer the sovereign goes out of the church.
