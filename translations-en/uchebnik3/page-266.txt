At that time the grand prince was staying at the Church of St Nicholas in Podkopayi by the Yauza, in Christian courtyards.

On the Arrival of the Grand Prince's Envoys from Rome

In the same year, in July, the grand prince's envoys returned to Moscow from the Roman King Maximilian: Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot, Mikhailo Klyapik, and Ivan Volk Kuritsyn.

(9) Metropolitan's Cells

In the same year Metropolitan Zosima built three stone cells with lower stories on his courtyard.

On the Fire in Ryazan. In the Year 7002 from Adam (1502 from Christ)

In September the whole city of Ryazan burned. That same autumn Ivan Andreyevich Subota Pleshcheyev came from Wallachia, and Konstantin Zabolotsky from Crimea; they traveled by the Field, and Tatars, Horde Cossacks, robbed them on the Field.

On the Death of Prince Andrei Vasilyevich Bolshoy

That same autumn, on November 6, Prince Andrei Vasilyevich Bolshoy reposed; he had sat in prison in Moscow, at the grand prince's Treasury Court, for 2 years and 47 days; and he was laid in the Church of the Archangel Michael in Moscow by the northern doors.

On the Grand Prince's New Court

In the same month, on the 10th day, a Sunday, the grand prince went into the city to live in the new court; after the fire he had been staying at the Church of St Nicholas in Podkopayi, beneath the stable, in Christian courtyards.

On the Sending of Envoys to Crimea

In the same month, on the 16th day, the grand prince sent off the Crimean envoy, and together with him sent his own envoy Konstantin Malechkin to the tsar Menli-Girey in Crimea.
