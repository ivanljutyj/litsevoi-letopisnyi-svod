Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, brother of the tsar and grand prince of All Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, grandson of the grand prince of All Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, was 31 years old, and he was laid in the Church of the Archangel Michael. At his death and burial were the tsar and grand prince of All Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, and Prince Vladimir Andreevich, boyars and many nobles, and a multitude of people. And he was buried by Matthew, Bishop of Sarsk and Podonsk, and archimandrites and hegumens with the whole consecrated council. Metropolitan Macarius was then gravely ill, at his last breath.

In the same month of November, on the 26th day, the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus exchanged lands with Prince Vladimir Andreevich; he took in exchange from the prince Vyshgorod on the Petrov with its districts, and in Mozhaisk district the princely volosts: Oleshnya volost, Voskresenskaya volost, Petrovskaya volost. And the sovereign gave Prince Vladimir the town of Romanov on the Volga with its districts, except Rybnaya Sloboda and Poshekhonye.

In the same month of November, on the 28th day, there came to the tsar and grand prince of All Rus, Ivan Vasilyevich, from the Pyatigorsk Cherkasy to Moscow Semyon Yartsov, son of Narmatsky; the sovereign had sent him to the Cherkasy to Prince Temryuk Aidarovich as an envoy. And with Semyon there came to the tsar and grand prince from Prince Temryuk his messenger Kaylybai.

That same winter, in December on the 4th day, on the commemoration of the Holy Great Martyr Barbara and our Venerable Father John of Damascus, died Guriy, Archbishop of Kazan and Sviyazhsk, and he was buried in Kazan in the Monastery of the Transfiguration of our Lord Jesus Christ; this Guriy was the first archbishop in Kazan, and his time as archbishop in Kazan was “…”.

In the same month of December, on the 5th day, there came to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus from Lithuania, from Sigismund Augustus, King of Poland, the cupbearer of the Grand Duchy of Lithuania and starosta of Belsk, Pan Yuri Alexandrovich Khotkevich, and the starosta of Slonim, Pan Grigory Bogdanovich Volovich, and clerk Mikhailo Garaburda, and with them 70 royal nobles and two servants with them.

And the envoys spoke in the treaty about conditions that could not be accepted, and this was refused them, and they requested a truce from the month of December to the first day of July, for half a year; and they wanted to record the Polotsk povet and the lands beyond the Dvina toward Polotsk in the truce charter for five versts. And they wanted to assign the Livonian land for the time of the truce in this way: which towns in Livonian land were held by the tsar and grand prince, those towns should be recorded in the truce charter as held by the tsar and grand prince, and which Livonian towns were held by the king, those should be recorded as held by the king. And if the tsar and grand prince does not wish to record the Livonian towns in the truce charter in this way, then the Livonian lands should not be written into the treaty charter by either side.
