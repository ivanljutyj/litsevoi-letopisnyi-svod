The prince sent the Germans Ivan and Victor to the Pechora to search for silver ore; and they found silver and copper ore in the patrimony of the Grand Prince on the Tsilma River, half a day’s journey short of the Kosma River, and seven days’ journey from the Pechora River; and the place where they found it was ten versts in extent, and it was 3,500 versts from Moscow; and they found the ore in the ninety-ninth year, on the 8th day of August.

On the Consecration of the Church of the Entry [of the Most Holy Theotokos] at the Simonov Court

In that same year, on the 13th day of November, the Church of the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos at the Simonov Court by the St Nicholas Gate was consecrated.

On the Arrival of Envoys from the Crimea to the Grand Prince

In that same month of November, Prince Vasily Romodanovsky came from the Crimea, and with him came an envoy from the Crimean Tsar Mengli-Giray to the Grand Prince.

On the Arrival of the Roman Envoy

In that same autumn, on the 18th day of the month of November, an envoy came to the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus from the Roman King Maximilian, son of Emperor Frederick, namely Yuri Delator, and he brought the Grand Prince to kiss the cross on the treaty charters.

On the Vologda Churches, How They Were Given to the Perm Diocese

In that same year, in February, by the command of the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, His Eminence Metropolitan Zosima and Gennady, Archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov, transferred from the metropolitanate and the Novgorod archbishopric their churches situated in Vologda, in the town and in the posad, to Philotheus, Bishop of Perm, into the Perm diocese.

On the Iberian Envoy

That same winter an envoy named Murat came from the Iberian land to the Grand Prince from Prince Alexander.

On the Arrival of Envoys from Wallachia

In that same month of February Skurat Zinovievich came from Wallachia, and with him came an envoy to the Grand Prince from Stephen, Voivode of Wallachia, named Mushat.

On the Dismissal of the Crimean Envoy

That same spring, in March, the Grand Prince dismissed the Crimean envoy, and with him he sent Loban Kolychev to the Crimea to Tsar Mengli-Giray.
