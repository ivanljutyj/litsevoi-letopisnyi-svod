At that same time the Grand Prince brought his boyar Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Shuysky into the deliberation concerning his testament. And the Grand Prince began to deliberate with those boyars and to make arrangements concerning his son Grand Prince Ivan, and concerning Grand Princess Elena, and concerning his son Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and concerning his testament.

And soon after that his brother Prince Yuri Ivanovich came from his appanage to Moscow to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich in great sorrow, for he had heard that his brother Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich was gravely failing.

Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich was then very ill. Then there came to the Grand Prince his father Metropolitan Daniel, and Vladyka Vassian of Kolomna, and Elder Misail Sukin, and his spiritual father Alexei, Archpriest of the Annunciation.

And he began to deliberate with his father Metropolitan Daniel, and with Vladyka Vassian of Kolomna, and with Elder Misail Sukin, and with his spiritual father Archpriest Alexei, about being clothed in the monastic habit, since his mind had long inclined toward monasticism, and he steadfastly desired to be clothed in the monastic habit. And he charged the Metropolitan with an oath, saying: “I know that no one wishes me to become a monk; but you, father, be not ashamed before anyone, but do according to God’s will and make me a monk according to my vow, though I am a sinner.”

And also when the Grand Prince was at Volok, he instructed Elder Misail Sukin and his spiritual father Alexei: “See that you do not do this, Elder Misail and Archpriest Alexei, that you lay me in white garments; even if I were well, my mind and my heart’s desire incline toward monasticism.” And at Volok the Grand Prince ordered Elder Misail to prepare monastic garments for him.

Also, while he was on the way, journeying to Moscow, he summoned his Tver dvoretsky Ivan Yuryevich Shigona and his clerk Menshoy Putyatin and began to instruct them according to his intention concerning his monasticism—that they should not lay him in white garments.

And the Grand Prince ordered the priest Gregory of the Annunciation to serve secretly in the Cathedral of the Annunciation, in the chapel of St Basil the Great; and at the Liturgy there were Vladyka Vassian of Kolomna, Elder Misail Sukin, and Archpriest Alexei; and the Gifts were brought to the Grand Prince by Vladyka Vassian of Kolomna and Misail Sukin.

On Wednesday night, toward Thursday, the Grand Prince was secretly anointed with oil, and there were Vladyka Vassian of Kolomna, Misail Sukin, Archpriest Alexei, and Gregory, priest of the Annunciation; and no one knew of it.
