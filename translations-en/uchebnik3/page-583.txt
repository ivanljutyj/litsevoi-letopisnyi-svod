With them many nobles, the tsar’s household retainers, and boyar sons, and sent them to the tsar’s camp, where Tsar Yemgurchei was stationed. And they themselves went to the city.

And they came to the city on the second day of July; and at that time there were few people in the city. And the governors moored above the city and below it.

And coming out of the boats, having called God to their aid, they went to the city. And the Astrakhan people fled from the city; and by the aid of Almighty God and the prayers of His most pure Mother and the great wonderworkers, and by the nobility and firm strength of the pious tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus, and by his wise guidance and his royal fortune, his governors, Prince Yuri Ivanovich Pronsky and his comrades, took the Astrakhan kingdom and city.

And they drove out the foot people, and captured others alive. And the city of Astrakhan was taken on the second day of July, on the feast of the Deposition of the Honorable Robe of our most pure Lady the Mother of God, which is in Blachernae.

On the Arrival of the Commanders at the Tsar’s Camp

On that same day the commanders, Prince Alexander Vyazemsky and his comrades, came to the tsar’s camp; and on that same day the tsar fled before them on horseback, having learned of the campaign against Astrakhan; and he sent the tsaritsas with their children by boat to the sea. And all the people fled in boats, and others on foot, in various directions.

And those whom they found in the tsar’s camp, by God’s grace they killed many, and took cannons and firearms.

And Ataman Fedka Pavlov overtook an ushkuy with the tsar’s maidens, and the tsar’s drums and many firearms were in it, and he took all that and killed the people.

On the Enthronement of Tsar Derbysh-Aley in Astrakhan

And on that same day the governors, Prince Yuri Ivanovich and his comrades, by the order of the tsar and great prince, enthroned Tsar Derbysh-Aley in Astrakhan, and those people whom they had captured they made swear allegiance at their request.

And they sent to Nogai to Smail-Murza to say that the governors, by God’s grace, had taken Astrakhan; and they sent to Nogai the serving Tatar Yanbulat and Aideyar, a man of Tsar Derbysh-Aley; and that the Nogais could migrate to Astrakhan without fear, for by God’s mercy and by the command of the tsar and great prince they had taken Astrakhan and enthroned Tsar Derbysh on it, and had arranged everything as the tsar and great prince had ordered through his envoy Nikula to Ismail.

And the governors themselves went after Tsar Yemgurchei, and they went in two parties, and sent commanders to all the islands, because the people had fled in different directions, and there was no certain news of the tsar, to which place he had fled.
