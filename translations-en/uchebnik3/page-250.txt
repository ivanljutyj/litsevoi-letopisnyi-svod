And he was laid in Moscow in the Church of the Archangel Michael, where his forebears also lie: he lived only 32 years and 20 days.

And the Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich ordered that physician, Master Leon, to be seized, and after the fortieth-day memorial for his son, Grand Prince Ivan, he commanded that the physician be executed and his head cut off; and his head was cut off at Bolvanovye on the twenty-second day of April.

On the Arrival of Ambassadors from Rome

In the same year, on the ninth day of July, the Grand Prince’s ambassador Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot came from Rome, and with him came an ambassador from the King of the Romans Maximilian, son of Emperor Frederick, named Yuri Delator, with an offer of love, friendship, and brotherhood.

On the Birth of the Grand Prince’s Son Andrei

In the same year, on the fifth day of August, a son was born to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich and was named Andrei.

On the Sending to Wallachia

In the same summer, on the fourteenth day of August, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent his ambassador Ivan Mitkov, son of Likhorev, to the voivode Stephen in Wallachia, and ordered him to go through the Field.

On the Dismissal of the Ambassadors to Rome

In the same month of August, on the nineteenth day, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, having honored Yuri Delator, ambassador of the King of the Romans Maximilian, sent him back to his sovereign; and together with him the Grand Prince sent his own ambassadors to the King of the Romans Maximilian, son of Frederick—Yuri the Greek Trakhaniot and his secretary Vasily Kuleshin—to conclude a treaty of love with him.

On the Construction of Two Towers and a Wall in Moscow

In the same summer Petr Antony Fryazin built two towers: one near the Borovitsky Gate and the other by the Konstantino-Eleninsky Gate, and he built a wall from the Sviblov Tower to the Borovitsky Gate.

On the Sending Back to His Own Land of Andrei, Brother-in-Law of the Grand Prince

In the same winter the Grand Prince sent back to his own land his brother-in-law Andrei, son of Thomas, Despot of Morea, brother of Grand Princess Sophia.
