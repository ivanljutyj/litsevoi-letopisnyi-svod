And when they had finished trading, the grand prince released the Nogai envoys to their sovereigns. And together with them the grand prince sent his Cossacks to Koshum-murza and to Oraz-murza; and the grand prince wrote to the murzas in charters that they should live in honorable friendship. In the same month, on the 22nd day, Thursday, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus set out for the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery, for the day of the memory of the Wonderworker Sergius, to pray, and with him his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich and many boyars; and they heard the all-night vigil, and matins, and a prayer service, and the liturgy, and he bestowed alms on the abbot and the brethren; and he arrived in Moscow in the same month on the 29th day, Thursday. That same autumn, in November on the 7th day, there came to the grand prince in Moscow Nogai envoys from Prince Shikh-Mamai and from Koshum-murza, and from Ismail-murza and from other murzas, Prince Murat with companions, with charters. And the prince and the murzas wrote in the charters that the grand prince should show them favor and keep them in friendship with him. And the grand prince promised to keep them in friendship with him, and released them to their sovereigns; and together with them the grand prince sent to Prince Shikh-Mamai and to the murzas his Cossacks, Ryazan Bakov with companions, with charters.

The Grand Prince Shows Favor to Prince Vladimir

That same winter, on December 25, Sunday, on the Nativity of Christ, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, Sovereign of All Rus, at the petition of his father Ioasaf the Metropolitan and of his boyars, showed favor to Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and his mother Princess Evfrosinya: he granted them the sight of his eyes, and gave him his father's patrimony, and ordered that other boyars, and a majordomo, and court sons of boyars—not his father's—should be with him.

On the Capture of Prince Ivan Belsky

That same winter, on January 2, the grand prince's boyar Prince Ivan Fedorovich Belsky was seized without the grand prince's knowledge, by boyar counsel, because his sovereign the grand prince kept him near himself and among the chief counselors, and also Metropolitan Ioasaf. And the boyars were indignant at this against Prince Ivan and against the metropolitan, and began to plot evil against them with their advisers, and to communicate with Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Shuysky in Vladimir. And Prince Ivan Shuysky was then in Vladimir, having been sent to stand guard against the Kazan people.

And Prince Ivan Shuysky in Vladimir brought many sons of boyars to the kiss of the cross, that they would be in their counsel. And the boyars set a term for Prince Ivan Shuysky and his counselors—to be in Moscow from Vladimir on January 3, Monday.

And in that night from Sunday to Monday, by the counsel of their like-minded men, they seized Prince Ivan Belsky in his courtyard and put him under guard.
