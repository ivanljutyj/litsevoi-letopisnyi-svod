On the Archbishop of Novgorod Who Was Deposed from the Throne

In the same winter Archbishop Serapion of Novgorod was deposed from the throne because he did not bless Joseph.

On the Sending Off of the Envoys to Crimea

In the same winter, on the first day of March, Thursday, the Grand Prince sent off the Crimean envoys, Prince Magmetsha and his companions, and with them the Grand Prince sent to Crimea to Tsar Mengli-Girey his own envoy Vasily Grigorievich Poplevin.

The Arrival from Lithuania of the Grand Prince’s Envoys

In the same winter, on the first day of March, the envoys of the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich came from Lithuania: Grigory Fedorovich, Ivan Andreevich, Mikhailo Stefanovich, and the clerk Nikita Guba Moklokov. And before them the King of Poland and Grand Prince of Lithuania Sigismund kissed the cross on the treaty charters to the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich, that he would fulfil all that was written in them until his death. And they brought this treaty charter to the Grand Prince from the King under his seal.

On the German Envoys and the Truce

In the same winter, on the eighth day of March, Thursday, the German envoys came to the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich from the Master of Livonia, from the Archbishop of Riga, from the Bishop of Yuryev, and from the whole land of Livonia, named Ivan Gildorp, Chancellor Ivan Oledenzen, Ivan Kaver, and Kristern Siuge, and they petitioned the Grand Prince on behalf of the Master and the whole land for a truce.

And the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich granted the request of the Master of Livonia and the whole land of Livonia, and ordered his Novgorod viceroys and the Prince of Pskov to conclude a truce with them for fourteen years from the day of the Annunciation, and he also ordered his people to trade with them according to the old custom, and to break with the King of Lithuania.
