On the Sending of the Charter of the Sovereign Grand Prince to Veliky Novgorod to the Archbishop and the Clerks, and on the Measuring of the Streets, the Barriers, and the Fire Watchmen

In the same year, in July, the sovereign grand prince and autocrat of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, sent charters to his pilgrim Archbishop Makary in Veliky Novgorod, and sent his clerks to Novgorod: Yakov Shishkin and Afanasy Funikov, and the palace clerk Mitya Veliky, and ordered them to measure out the streets on the Sofia Side.

And they began to measure out Great Street from the Vladimir Gates to its end, and all the streets from the field straight to the bank, and the plots throughout the burnt area; and they ordered barriers to be set up throughout the whole city, and fire watchmen to be stationed by the command of the sovereign grand prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich.

On the Barrier Watch and on Evil People, on Plunderers in Veliky Novgorod

That same autumn they began to post guards at the barriers on the ... day of October. And whereas previously there had been in the city many evil people, robbery, theft, murders, and all manner of evil deeds, from that time God’s mercy began: a great calm set in throughout the whole city, free of evil men, plunderers, and murderers. Many evil people fled because of the city guards and vanished without trace. And others turned to repentance or took up a trade.

On the Church at Kolmovo

That same autumn, by the blessing of Archbishop Makary, they laid the foundation of a stone church of the Life-Giving Trinity and a stone refectory in the monastery at the Dormition of the Mother of God at Kolmovo.

On the Pestilence in Kolyvan

In the same year there was a pestilence in Kolyvan, in the German land.

On the German Ambassador and on Peace with the Germans

That same autumn a German ambassador arrived in Novgorod and in Moscow and petitioned the sovereign grand prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, for peace; and the sovereign grand prince showed them favor and concluded peace with them for twenty years, as had been before.
