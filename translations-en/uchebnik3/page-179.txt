On the Rostov Princes

That same winter the Rostov princes sold to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich their patrimony, the entire half of Rostov: Prince Vladimir Andreevich and his brother Prince Ivan Ivanovich, with all their children and nephews. Having bought it from them, the Grand Prince gave that half to his mother, Grand Princess Maria.

In the same month, on the 16th day, the relics of the wonderworker Peter were translated from the Church of the Most Pure to St John under the Bells, and those of the other metropolitans: Theognostus, Cyprian, Photius, and Jonah.

And on the 27th day the Venetian master Aristotle began to demolish the new walls of the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God that had not fallen, and that day he broke two pillars, and also the front doors, and he broke much of the front walls.

In the same month, on the 23rd day after vespers, a cloud rose, and thunder at first small and then great, from the south, with lightning, and great rain; and there were frosts and cold until the second day of May, and from that day rains began every day.

In the month of May, on the 28th day, at night a daughter, Feodosia, was born to the Grand Prince, who later married Kholmsky.

In the month of June the Venetian architect Aristotle began to dig trenches for the foundation of the Church of the Most Pure Mother of God: the depth of the trenches was two sazhens, and in some places deeper still.

In the same month, on the 10th day, at the first hour of the day, a fire broke out beyond the river in Moscow near the Church of St Nicholas, called Borisovskaia, and many courtyards burned, and that church burned down.
