They do not venerate either the holy icons or the great saints. They do not accept our father, the hierarch and teacher Gregory the Theologian, Basil the Great, and the divine John Chrysostom together with the other saints and their teachings; and they do many other things that are hard to enumerate in detail. So they live and are raised in such customs; and how is it that they dare to proclaim an unrighteous and forbidden revelation and to forbid, and how do they wish to be regarded as righteous among all the Orthodox? I do not think so; but if those who reason thus are right in acting so, let them receive their portion.” And this is said in the epistle of Kyr Michael, Patriarch of Constantinople. Concerning unleavened bread, Kyr Peter of Antioch spoke well in his epistle to the Metropolitan of Venice; he says: “If we still communicate with unleavened bread, then we will be holding to the shadow of the law of Moses and will have a Jewish table, and not the rational and living Flesh of God.”

And again in the same epistle the same Kyr Peter the patriarch writes concerning unleavened bread that they become embittered and argue and resist, although the tradition concerning unleavened bread comes from the holy apostles Peter and Paul. And the patriarch answers everyone, saying that the Body of Christ at the time of the Lord’s crucifixion clearly showed all things, when water and blood flowed together from His most pure ribs, from His Flesh pierced by the spear. The living Holy Spirit is in His divine Flesh, which the faithful receive in the bread changed by the Holy Spirit into the Flesh of Christ; we live in Him and receive the living and divine Flesh; we abide not only under the law but also under grace, and we offer unleavened bread in vain: for this is a new creation in Christ, and the old has already passed away. This is as Paul says: “All things have become new.” Why do you want to eat unleavened bread and not be circumcised, although Christ Himself was circumcised? Why do you not keep the Sabbath according to the ancient tradition?

And listen to Paul, who said, addressing the Galatians: “Behold, I, Paul, say to you: if you are circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.” Those whose malice is subtle and whose destruction is more subtle, scattered in hell and receiving retribution with equal folly and Mosaic ingratitude—I hate them all from my soul, and I turn away in heart together with those of like mind, and I abhor their helpers, and I curse them, and I count them apostates from God; I submit to the seven holy councils, I forbid the abominable defilement of the soul’s command and departure from the truth, and removal from the highest help. With them I shake off and renounce every other heresy that has risen against the Church before the councils or after them, that begins to cut off and tear at the Lord’s people; false teachings confuse minds and in every way violate the right word of the royal command; I shake them off and renounce them.
