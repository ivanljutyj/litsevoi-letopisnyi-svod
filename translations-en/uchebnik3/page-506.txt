If such evil is done by you, I grieve greatly over it, and much sorrow and grievous sickness have seized my heart, and I wept bitterly: O woe! Alas! You have turned God’s great mercy into wrath and His gentleness into fury! Look and understand the sword and the fury of God’s wrath: what a multitude of people perishes in these times through various passions and punishments, pestilence, and drowning. And if you do not cleanse yourselves, what mercy do you expect from God hereafter? But I beg all of you with all my soul and with tears: turn away from such an evil custom! Was it not because of this lustful passion and much impiety and transgression of God’s law that God sent the Flood upon the world, burned the land of Sodom and Gomorrah with fire, and destroyed the great city of Nineveh? And in the time of Moses the prophet, when the Israelites walked through the wilderness, what grace God granted them against their adversaries—this the Lord has also shown to you now: when they began to commit fornication, then they could not defeat their adversaries, but were always themselves defeated; and Phinehas saw those committing fornication and pierced both, and pleased God, and the slaughter ceased, and this was reckoned to him as righteousness from generation to generation forever. But you, O my children, honorable nobles and all entrusted to you, if anyone among you does such things, you will bring not only God’s wrath upon yourselves. See from what glory into what dishonor you turn aside, and from what honor into what disgrace you are brought down: once you were terrifying to your enemies, and now your enemies laugh at you; and the city and people who surrendered into your hands have now begun to resist you; and those who wished to serve our pious tsar have now rushed to battle against your multitude. Hear the prophet saying: “If you do not turn, I will whet My weapon against you, and I will bend My bow, and prepare instruments of death. And if you do not listen to Me, you will take up weapons but flee, pursued by no one. But if you listen to Me and do My will and keep My commandments, your enemies will fall beneath your feet, and no one will rise against you, and I will bless you and multiply you, and you will obtain earthly blessedness all the days of your life and receive eternal life.” The mouth of the Lord spoke this in ancient times, and now, if, my children, the word of God finds room in you, and our humility and tender supplication and divine instruction—accept this and turn away from all the evil deeds I wrote of above, and with true repentance flee to God with a contrite heart and humble spirit, laying aside all pride and injustice, correcting yourselves by penances received from spiritual fathers, and cleansing yourselves with tears and alms, and you will receive forgiveness of sins and find God merciful; for the Lord Himself said: “Turn to Me, and you shall find.” And if the miracle is true, repent, for true repentance and the renunciation of sin are pleasing not only
