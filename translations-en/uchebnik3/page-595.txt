In Moscow, the tsar and grand prince, the sovereign, ordered the tsarevich to be baptized together with his mother, and the tsarevich was named Peter, and his mother’s name was Ulyana.

And the tsar, the great sovereign, showed favor to the tsaritsa and ordered her to be given in marriage to Zakhary Ivanovich Pleshcheev, and he ordered the tsarevich to be nursed by his mother until he reached manhood.

On the Marriage of Prince Ivan Dmitrievich Belsky

That same autumn, on the 8th of November, Thursday, Tsar and Great Sovereign Ivan Vasilievich, Autocrat of All Rus, showed favor and married Prince Ivan Dmitrievich Belsky, giving him his niece, the granddaughter of Tsarevich Peter and the great-granddaughter of his grandfather Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus, and the daughter of Prince Vasily Vasilievich Shuisky; and the sovereign gave her away from himself, from his own court.

On the Arrival of Elders from the Holy Mountain

That same winter, in December, on the 24th day, there came to the pious tsar and Christ-loving sovereign autocrat Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus elders from the Holy Mountain of Athos, from the Serbian Hilandar Monastery: hieromonk Seliverst, hieromonk Prokhor, hieromonk Aggaeus, deacon Gennady, and monk Anophry; they petitioned the pious tsar, great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich, in the name of all the brethren of the Hilandar Monastery, that their sovereign grant them and take their Hilandar Monastery with all its brethren under his royal name, so that he might have a second place of prayer on the Holy Mountain. “And we, the poor, would pray to God for your royal health, and for your pious tsaritsa, and for your noble son; and we, sovereign, are poor monks of the Holy Mountain, we ransom the Christian faith from the heathen sultans, and we have no villages, sovereign, but we ourselves cultivate vineyards and dig the earth, and do everything necessary for the monastery; the brethren do all kinds of work and receive food from their own labors, and do not remain idle, and give ransoms from the monasteries to the Turkish Sultan for the sake of piety and the Christian faith.” And the Christ-loving and great sovereign granted the elders of the Holy Mountain, Seliverst with the brethren, took their Hilandar Monastery under his name, and sends his royal provision to the monastery for church adornment and monastic building, and sufficient alms for the brethren.

That same winter, in March, Bishop Gury of Smolensk left his bishopric because of infirmity and withdrew to a monastery.

On the Installation of the Vladyka of Smolensk

That same winter, in March, on the 17th day, on the third Sunday of Great Lent, Simeon, former abbot of Kirillov, was installed as bishop in Smolensk by Metropolitan Macarius.
