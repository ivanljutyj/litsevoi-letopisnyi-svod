and they sent the commander Ivan Petrovich with his companions, according to the sovereign’s instruction, to Vetluga and Rutki, and Ivan Petrovich went through many volosts and waged war.

And he came to them at Oshla, by God’s grace, safe and sound; and Ivan Petrovich reported that foot Cheremis had come in the forest against the guard regiment, against Prince Vasily Tokmakov, and Prince Vasily, by God’s grace, defeated them utterly.

And the senior commanders waged war in the volosts: in Shumursha and Khozyakovo, and in Oshla, and in both Mazaras, and in two Orsha volosts, Malaya and Bolshaya, and in Bishta, and in Kukshuli, in Soroka, Kunshi, and in Vasilyukovo Balak, and in the Mamich-Berdeeva volost, and the Kileev volost, and the Kikin volost, and Kukh-yal Kokshahi, Bolshaya and Malaya, and the Syzal volost, and Dmashi, and Monam, and Kermechi, and Ulyazy—and in all those volosts there was war by the commanders, and they took and killed many people.

And they were on the Meadow Side at war for two weeks, then went out to the Volga, and went to Kazan and back along the Volga, and came to the sovereign, by God’s grace, safe and sound.

To the Tsar and Grand Prince they wrote from Kazan

On March 1 the boyar and commander Prince Mikhailo Glinsky with his companions wrote to the Tsar and Grand Prince from Kazan that the Meadow people had come to the Arsk side to wage war; and the Arsk people, having built forts, fought them off, and with them in the forts were streltsy of the Tsar and Grand Prince, and with arquebuses they killed many Meadow people.

And the Meadow people waged war on Tatar villages; and they went to the Meadow Side.

On the sending from the town of Sviyazhsk

In the same month Prince Mikhailo Vorotynsky with his companions sent Fedka Baskakov from the town of Sviyazhsk: Fedka had been sent as a commander of the Mountain people to the Meadow Side to wage war, and seven hundred Mountain people went on skis; and they came unnoticed, and after waging war they killed and captured people and killed livestock; and they came, by God’s grace, safe and sound.

On the release of the Nogai envoys

In the same month the sovereign graciously released [the Nogai envoys] to the Nogai; and he sent his own to Prince Smail—Ignaty Zagryazhsky—and to Kasai Murza—Myasoed Vislov—and to Arslan—service Tatars; and the Tsar and Grand Prince ordered that they be at one with the Tsar and Grand Prince against all his enemies; and because Prince Yusuf had been defeated, he sent with his envoys to the prince and the murzas his sovereign bounty from the treasury.
