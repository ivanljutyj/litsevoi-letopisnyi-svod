And here is the confirmed charter concerning the metropolitan’s white klobuk:

For the sake of piety and by the grace of the man-loving consubstantial Trinity, by the good pleasure of the Father and the assistance of the Son, and by the operation of the Holy Spirit, and for the sake of the compassion and mercy of our most holy and most blessed Lady, the Mother of God and ever-virgin Mary, and for the prayers of the great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, and Jonah, and Ivan, and Leonty, and Nikita, and of our venerable and God-bearing fathers Sergius and Varlaam, and Cyril, and of all the saints, we, the great sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, who have assumed the scepters of the Russian kingdom, in our care and concern for all the goods of the pious Russian kingdom, and also for the welfare of the holy churches of God and the holy monasteries and places! It happened in our years that the first hierarch of the most holy Russian metropolis, our father and intercessor, the metropolitan of all Rus, Macarius, paid the common debt to the common Lord and King Christ, having completed on earth his appointed span. And now the cathedral and great apostolic church of the most pure Mother of God, of her honorable and glorious Dormition, the whole great Russian metropolis, and the throne of the great luminary and wonderworker Peter are widowed, and therefore there is no little sorrow for us and for all Orthodox Christianity. And therefore we sent to all the regions of our royal autocracy, throughout the regions of the most holy Russian metropolis, commanding our intercessors, the most reverend archbishops and bishops, and the most honorable archimandrites, and hegumens, and the entire sacred council, to assemble. Let them, according to the rules of the divine apostles and holy fathers, elect the most worthy for this great throne of the great wonderworker Peter, one able to govern according to the tradition of the holy and divine apostles and holy fathers. And when our intercessors assembled, we held counsel with those our intercessors, with the archbishops and bishops: with Pimen, archbishop of Great Novgorod and Pskov; with Nikandr, archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl; with Trifon, archbishop of Polotsk; with Afanasy, bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa; with Semion, bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk; with Filofei, bishop of Ryazan and Murom; with Varlaam, bishop of Kolomna and Kashira; with Matfei, bishop of Sarsk and Podonsk; with Iasaf, bishop of Perm and Vologda; and with the archimandrites and hegumens and the entire sacred council, concerning the fact that our father and intercessor the metropolitan wears a black klobuk. But the former Russian first hierarchs and wonderworkers, metropolitans Peter and Alexis, and Ivan, archbishop of Novgorod, and Leonty, and Ignaty, and Isaiah, wonderworkers of Rostov, wore white klobuks.
