And on the morning of October 1, Monday, on the feast of the Most Pure, of her honorable and glorious Protection, the most reverend Metropolitan Macarius came with crosses and the whole cathedral assembly to the newly built church and consecrated the church of the Most Pure Lady our Mother of God, of the honorable Protection; present at the consecration was the pious and Christ-loving tsar with his brother Prince Yuri and with the boyars, and a multitude of people.

And the sovereign celebrated that day with honor and joy, with his father Metropolitan Macarius, and with the bishops, archimandrites, abbots, and the whole council of the Russian metropolis; and he fed a great multitude of the poor and ordered sufficient alms to be given to them.

On the Consecration of the Church

In the same month of October, on the seventh day, the church of the great wonderworker Nicholas of Gostun was consecrated, because it had been painted and adorned with all church appurtenances; and it was consecrated by Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus. Present at that consecration were the pious tsar and great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus with his brother Prince Yuri, and with the boyars, and a multitude of people.

On the Tsar's Journey to Cherkizovo

In the same month, on the 8th, Monday, Tsar and Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich set out from Moscow to the village of Cherkizovo, and from there he wished to see the Klin forests, and from there to Volok, and from Volok to Mozhaisk, and from there to Goroden, the village of Prince Vladimir Andreevich. And Prince Vladimir Andreevich met the great sovereign, and the tsar and grand prince favored Prince Vladimir Andreevich, ate bread and feasted in Prince Vladimir's village of Goroden, and from there to his own village of Denisyevo, and from Denisyevo to Krylatskoye; and in his village of Krylatskoye he ordered a church to be consecrated.

On the Bringing of the Astrakhan Tsaritsas to Moscow

In the same month, on the 18th day, Thursday, there were brought to Moscow to the pious tsar and great sovereign Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus the Astrakhan captives, the wives of Tsar Emgurchei — Tevkel and Kanzadu, and the younger wife Elyakshi, who had been taken at Astrakhan.

And the Russian tsar and great sovereign ordered the Astrakhan tsaritsas to be honored, not as captives but as free women, and to be met by his treasurers outside the settlement.

And the sovereign ordered them to be held with honor and sufficient provisions to be given from his royal cellars and chambers.
