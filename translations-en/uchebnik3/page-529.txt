And he also greeted the mountain people and invited them to dine, and many ate with the sovereign in the tents and across the fields—boyar children and mountain people. The twenty-first camp was on the Ityakovo field, and from Ityakovo the sovereign went to Sviyazhsk.

And the sovereign came to the city of Sviyazhsk

On Saturday, August 13, the sovereign’s boyars who were in the city—Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Shuysky and Semyon Konstantinovich Zabolotsky with their companions—and the boyar children, the streltsy, and all the people met him. And the Orthodox Christians rejoiced with inexpressible joy, seeing the sovereign come in health from his long journey, and they meet the sovereign at the city gates with crosses.

And the sovereign comes to the Church of the Nativity of the Most Pure and offers up thanksgiving prayers that she has vouchsafed him, the sovereign, to see her image in the newly enlightened place; he also comes to the wonder-working image of the wonder-worker Sergius and asks help and deliverance for the Christian people from the impious; and the deacons sang Many Years to the sovereign, while Prince Vladimir Andreevich and all the princes and boyars wished the sovereign health in the patrimony granted him by God in the newly enlightened place.

And the sovereign rode out to the meadow and stood in tents on the meadow below the city of Sviyazhsk. And the sovereign commanded Tsar Shigalei to be with him.

And the sovereign took counsel with Prince Vladimir Andreevich, with Tsar Shigalei, with the boyars, and with the voivodes about how he, the sovereign, should accomplish his affairs. And the sovereign resolved to go to the city of Kazan without delay, and to send letters to them: if they wish to petition the sovereign without bloodshed, then the sovereign will show them favor. Our sovereign shows humility, for he knows that the Lord resists the proud and gives grace to the humble, and he bids Tsar Shigalei send a letter in his own name to Tsar Ediger-Magmet, that he should believe him in this, not be on guard against our sovereign, and ride out of the city to our sovereign; the sovereign will show him favor.

And the pious tsar sends letters to Mullah Kul Sharif and to all the Kazan land, that they should petition the sovereign; and whoever began the evil deed and stirred up the land and laid the evil deed upon the sovereign and his boyars—let them not stand for those, and the sovereign will show them favor. And on August 15 the sovereign sent away the Kazan merchant.

On August 16 the sovereign ordered the forward regiment, the right-hand regiment, and the great regiment to cross the river opposite the city of Sviyazhsk and to take position on the meadows on the Kazan side.
