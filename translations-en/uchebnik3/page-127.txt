In the year 6966 from Adam (1466 from Christ)

In the city of Murom the entire kremlin burned down on September 29. That same autumn, on October 20, at the ninth hour of the night, fire broke out in Moscow inside the city, near the Church of St Vladimir at Khovrin, and much burned, up to a third of the city, while the rest God preserved.

That same winter, on February 15, Wednesday of St Theodore's week, when they began to sing the Hours, a son was born to Grand Prince Ivan and was named Ivan. That same winter Archbishop Euthymius of Great Novgorod died.

On Vyatka

In the same year the grand prince sent his army to Vyatka with Prince Semyon Ryapolovsky, and without accomplishing anything Semyon returned.

In the same year a stone Church of the Presentation of the Most Holy Theotokos was built in Moscow at the Simonov metochion by the Nikolskie Gates.

In the year 6967 from Adam (1467 from Christ)

The Annunciation fell on the feast of Easter.

In the same year the Tatars of Sedi-Akhmat went against Rus, threatening it; and Grand Prince Vasily sent his son Grand Prince Ivan with many forces against them to the opposite bank.

The Tatars came to the bank, but the grand prince did not let them across and beat them off; and they fled.

And in memory of that threat Metropolitan Jonah built a stone church in the name of the Praise of the Most Holy Theotokos, attached to the cathedral church of the Most Pure, at the altar near the southern doors. That same winter, in February, the hieromonk Jonah was appointed archbishop of Great Novgorod by Metropolitan Jonah and the Russian bishops.

In the same year the grand prince sent his army to Vyatka under Prince Ivan Yuryevich, Ivan Ivanovich, and Prince Dmitry Ryapolovsky with great force.

They went and took two towns, Orlov and Kotelnich, and brought the rest to kiss the cross for the grand prince; and they returned to Moscow.
