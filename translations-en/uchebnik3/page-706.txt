Prince Tinekhmat sat in the principality after his father Prince Ismail; and to his brother Urus-murza, the Nuraddin, to Ismail’s son — Vasily Petrov, son of Fedchishchev; to Asanak-murza, son of Koshum; to Tekhkovat — Ivan Elizarov, son of Mikhnov; to Tinbay-murza, to Ismail’s son — Roman Matfeev, son of Khvoshchinsky; to Tinaley-murza, to Koshum’s son — Ivan Vasiliev, son of Sherefedinov; and with them a stanitsa of serving Tatars; and to the Cherkasy with a letter to Kazy-murza and companions — the serving Tatar Krym Taishev and companions.

In the same month of May, on the 19th day, the monastery of St Nicholas the Wonderworker the Old in Novgorod burned, and eight cells burned, and two wooden churches, and the tops of St Nicholas the Wonderworker burned.

In the same summer, in June on the 21st day, the Tsar and Grand Prince sent to the Kabardian Cherkasy, to Prince Temryuk Aidarovich, Ivan Petrov, son of Novosiltsov, with his bounty.

In the same month of June, on the 27th day, Devlet-Girey, Crimean Tsar, sent to the Tsar and Grand Prince his messenger Prince Karash; and with him together sent their messengers the Kalga Tsarevich Magmet-Girey and Tsarevich Aldy-Girey, and other tsareviches, and qaraches, and ulans, and princes, and murzas, thirty-one men in all. And the Tsar and Grand Prince was at that time in Mozhaysk.

And Afanasy Nagoy sent from the Crimea to the Tsar and Grand Prince the serving Tatar Yansuba and companions. And on arriving, Karash said that Tsar Devlet-Girey had sent with him to the Tsar and Grand Prince a letter and gifts: an argamak and a boyar’s son, Voloshanin. And he said that the Kaniv Cherkasy of the Lithuanian King had come to Ovech’i Vody and routed them, and in that rout took from him the Tsar’s letter and the presents, the argamak and Voloshanin.

And as for the oral message to the Tsar and Grand Prince from Tsar Devlet-Girey, Karash said that Tsar Devlet-Girey had gone against the Wallachian voivode at the command of the Turkish Sultan and had warred on the Wallachian land, and killed many people, and installed Alexander as voivode in the town of Azbar, and subjected all the Wallachian land to the Sultan’s will, and had come from Wallachia to the Crimea in good health with much booty and rejoiced at it; and in his joy he had sent to tell the Tsar and Grand Prince his close man, Prince Karash Yausal.

And the Kalga and Aldy-Girey and other tsareviches, and the Tsar’s mother and the Tsaritsas, and the qaraches and ulans, and princes and murzas wrote to the Tsar and Grand Prince that they strove for the good cause, that Tsar Devlet-Girey should be in firm friendship and love with the Tsar and Grand Prince; and Devlet-Girey had agreed with the Tsar and Grand Prince on firm friendship and love.

And the serving Tatar Yansuba said that Afanasy Nagoy had sent letters with him to the Tsar and Grand Prince, and the Kaniv Cherkasy had taken those letters from him in the same rout when they routed Karash and companions.
