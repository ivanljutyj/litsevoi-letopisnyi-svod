With Simeon, Bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk, with Philotheus, Bishop of Ryazan and Murom, with Joseph, Bishop of Kolomna and Kashira, with Galaktion, Bishop of Sar and Polonsk, with Joasaph, Bishop of Vologda and Perm, with the archimandrites and abbots and with all the consecrated council, and with all the boyars and officials, and also with the princes and boyar sons and service people, and with the guests and all the trading people: the Lithuanian envoy, Pan Trotsky Yuri Alexandrovich Khodkevich and his companions, did not wish to conclude a truce without the Livonian lands. And the Livonian lands should be written into the truce charter: those towns that are under the tsar and grand prince should be written as under the tsar and grand prince; and those German towns that are under the king—should they be written as under the king? And if those German towns are written into the truce charter as under the king, how are we henceforth to stand for those towns? And is it fitting now to stand for that? And Prince Vladimir Andreevich, and all the council archbishops and bishops, and the boyars and officials of the tsar and grand prince, and the princes, and boyar sons, and guests, and merchants, and trading people resolved that the tsar and grand prince should in no way yield the towns of the Livonian land to the Polish king, and should stand firmly on that. And to that resolution list the archbishops and bishops, and archimandrites, and abbots set their hands.

And the boyars and officials, princes, and boyar sons, and guests, and merchants kissed the cross to the sovereign on their words. And the metropolitan was not at that resolution, because Metropolitan Athanasius at that time had left the metropolitan see.

In the same year, in the month of July, on the 17th day, the tsar and grand prince sent the Lithuanian envoy Yuri Alexandrovich Khodkevich and his companions back to Lithuania to the king. And the Lithuanian envoys left Moscow in the same month of July, on the 22nd day, a Monday. And when sending off the Lithuanian envoys, the tsar and grand prince said that he was sending to his brother King Sigismund Augustus his great envoys: Fedor Ivanovich Umny Kolychev, and he was recorded as namestnik of Suzdal, and Grigory Ivanovich Nagoy, and he was recorded as dvoretsky of Volok, and clerk Vasily Yakovlev, son of Shchelkalov, “to finish those matters which between us, brothers, were not completed.”

And boyar Vasily Mikhailovich Yuryev with his companions went to the envoys with the reply. And with the Lithuanian envoys, with Pan Trotsky Yuri Alexandrovich Khodkevich and his companions, they concluded an agreement and wrote records between themselves, that between the sovereign tsar and grand prince and the king there should be no war on either side while the sovereign’s envoys are with the king. And they wrote the term until the Nativity of Christ or the Baptism of Christ, the year 7075 from Adam (the year 1575 from Christ), and to those records they attached their seals.
