On the Installation of Joseph as Bishop of Smolensk

That same winter, on February 15, by the grace of the Holy Spirit and by hierarchal election according to the Divine rules of the holy apostles and holy fathers, by the command of Vasily Ivanovich, by the grace of God sovereign of all Rus', autocrat and Grand Prince, Joseph, archimandrite of the Chudov Monastery, which is within the city in Moscow, was installed by the most reverend Barlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus', and by the whole consecrated council of the Russian metropolia as bishop in the city of Smolensk. And the Grand Prince sent him from Moscow to his patrimony, the city of Smolensk, in March, in the fourth week of Lent.

On the Ambassadors

That same winter, on March 15, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus' released the Turkish ambassador Prince Kemel, and together with him the Grand Prince sent to Tsargrad as an embassy to the Turkish Salim-Shah-Saltan his close man Vasily Andreev, son of Korobov; and to the Holy Mountain of Athos the Grand Prince sent with alms Vasily Kopyl Spiashchy and Ivan Varavin.

On the Ambassadors

That same spring, in April, the great sovereign of all Rus' Vasily Ivanovich released the ambassadors of Emperor Maximilian, Doctor Yakov and Mavrep, to their sovereign. And together with them the Grand Prince sent to his brother, the Roman King Emperor Maximilian, his ambassador Alexei Grigoriev, son of Zabolotsky, and clerk Alexei Maly.

And to the Danish King Christiern the Grand Prince sent Ivan Mikulin, son of Yary, and palace clerk Vasily Bely.

On the Grand Prince's First Journey to Volok

That same spring, in April, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus' was for the first time in his patrimony at Volok Lamsky, having gone hunting.

On the Death of the Crimean Tsar Mengli Giray

That same spring, in May, news came to the Grand Prince from Crimea that Tsar Mengli Giray had passed away, had died, and after him his eldest son Magamet-Kirei sat on the throne in Crimea.
