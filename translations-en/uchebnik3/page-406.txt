That same winter, on the 8th day of March, ambassadors came to the Grand Prince from the Livonian Master, from Hermann: Ivan Loden and Fedor Ontanov, and Ganus, to congratulate the Grand Prince upon his great realms.

In the same month of March, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered the old coins to be remade with a new stamp, because among the old coins there were many clipped coins and much alloy; and this was a great burden to Christendom: in the old grivenka there were two and a half rubles, while in the new grivenkas they ordered three rubles to be made.

And the counterfeiters, those people who forged and clipped coins, they ordered to be investigated, and after investigation some were executed; and they forbade the old coins to circulate henceforth.

In the same year, on the 16th day of May, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, ordered a stone city to be built beside the earthen city; and on that day they ordered their father Daniel, Metropolitan, with all the consecrated council, to go with crosses and icons through those places where the city was to be, and to sprinkle them with holy water; and they ordered the master Petr Maly Fryazin to lay the foundation of the wall.

In the same year, on the 15th day of July, the Grand Prince sent Semen Davydov, son of Kurtsov, to Perm to build a city, for the old one had burned.

In the same year, people of the Grand Prince’s traitors, Prince Semen Belsky and Ivan Lyatsky, came running from Lithuania and said to Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of all Rus and his mother, Grand Princess Elena, that the King had gathered many of his people and had hired men from other lands, and was sending them to wage war against the Grand Prince’s Smolensk patrimonies.

And the Grand Prince and his mother, the Grand Princess, hearing of this, took counsel with the boyars about how to send their commanders against the Lithuanian people.

And they sent their commanders with many men against the Lithuanian people: in the great regiment, his boyar Prince Vasily Vasilievich Shuysky and Prince Daniil Dmitrievich Pronsky; in the forward regiment, his boyar and equerry Prince Ivan Fedorovich Telepnev Obolensky and Prince Vasily, son of Prince Fedor Okhlyabinin; in the regiment of the right hand, Prince Andrei Dmitrievich Rostovsky and Prince Nikita Borisovich Turenin; in the regiment of the left hand, Prince Vasily Ivanovich Repnin and Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Troekurov; in the guard regiment, his boyar Prince Ivan Danilovich Penkov and Vasily Petrovich Borisov.
