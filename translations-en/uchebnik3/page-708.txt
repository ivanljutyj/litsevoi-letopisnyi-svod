And by God’s grace they came to Smolensk with all their people in good health.

In the same year the chapel of St Basil the Great of Caesarea was renovated in the Church of the Annunciation of the Holy Mother of God, which is on the Seni; and before that the altar had been on the porch by the Treasury Court. In the same year the sovereign tsar and grand prince ordered the porch at the Church of the Annunciation, outside the doors, above the stairway from the square, to be painted.

That same summer, in July, the Bukharan tsar Abdula sent to the tsar and grand prince his ambassadors Kenebek-ulan and Chalym-molla, and with them he wrote to the tsar and grand prince asking that he grant him his petition—that he permit his people to travel to his realm to trade.

That same summer, on August 2, Erik, King of Sweden, sent to the tsar and grand prince his ambassadors Isak Nikolaev, Ivan Lavrentiev, and the scribe Frants Erikhov with companions to petition for a truce with Livonia, with the boyar and voivode, the governor of Livonia, Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov.
