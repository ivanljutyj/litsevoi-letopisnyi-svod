And so, the Grand Prince, trusting in God, having taken up arms, went against them with all his forces for their pride and disobedience, not as against Christians, but as against pagans and apostates from Orthodoxy.

The Grand Prince came to Volok on the very feast of the Nativity of the Forerunner. Also the brothers of the Grand Prince set out, each from his own patrimony: Prince Yuri Vasilyevich from his patrimony, Prince Andrei Vasilyevich from his patrimony, and Prince Boris Vasilyevich from his patrimony, and Prince Mikhailo Andreyevich with his son, Prince Vasily, from his patrimony.

In the God-saved city of Moscow he left his son, Grand Prince Ivan; and with him he left in Moscow his brother, Prince Andrei the Younger.

On the feast of the holy supreme apostles Peter and Paul he came to Torzhok.

And there came to him the commanders of the Grand Prince of Tver, Prince Yuri Andreyevich Dorogobuzhsky and Ivan Nikitich Zhito, with many people to help the Grand Prince against the Novgorodians.

And from Pskov to Torzhok there came to the Grand Prince envoys Vasily and Bogdan with Yakushka Shabaltsev; and they were sent with the news that they had renounced their oath to Novgorod, and that all were ready.

The Grand Prince sent Bogdan from Torzhok to them, and with him Kuzma Korobyin, that they should go to Novgorod without delay; but Vasily they did not let go from him.

The Grand Prince himself went from Torzhok to Novgorod the Great.

The brothers of the Grand Prince all with many forces, each from his own patrimony, went by different roads to Novgorod, ravaging and burning and carrying people off into captivity. The same did the Grand Prince’s commanders, each in the place where he was sent.

The Plundering of the Novgorod Land

The commanders previously sent by the Grand Prince, Princes Daniil Dmitrievich Kholmsky and Fedor Davydovich, going through the Novgorod lands where they were ordered to be, sent their soldiers out to many places to burn and plunder, and take captive, and execute without mercy for their violation of the treaty with their sovereign, the Grand Prince. The commanders reached Rusa, took captives, and burned those places. And, plundering and burning, they came close to Novgorod, to the river Shelona, and arrived at a place called Korostyn, on the shore of Lake Ilmen. The Novgorod land was filled with lakes and marshes, and therefore in summertime never did any mounted host of the former great princes.
