At that time the Grand Princess did not wish to leave him.

The Grand Prince took leave of her and gave her a last kiss; and at once he ordered her to be led away to his royal children.

And the great sovereign Vasily lay down, ailing, at the point of death. Pitiful indeed was it then to see those standing before him; everything was filled with tears and wailing at that time; the autocrat himself felt utterly exhausted.

Then the Grand Prince sent for Vladyka Vassian and for Elder Misail Sukin, and ordered him to bring the monastic habit according to his former vow.

The great sovereign asked whether the abbot of the Kirillov Monastery was in Moscow, for he had long desired to be tonsured in the Kirillov Monastery; and they told him that the Kirillov abbot was not then in Moscow.

And he sent for Joasaph, abbot of the Trinity-Sergius Monastery. Abbot Joasaph soon came to him.

And he ordered him to enter the room with the Holy Gifts, and ordered him to stand opposite him; and he ordered his steward Fyodor Kuchetsky to stand beside the archpriest, because Fedyets had once seen the death of his father, the Grand Prince.

And then he ordered his private chapel clerk Daniel to sing the canon to Great Martyr Catherine and the canon for the departure of the soul, and he ordered the farewell prayer to be said for him. And when they began to say the canon, he became somewhat oblivious and as if fell asleep, and immediately awoke as from sleep; and as though seeing a vision, he spoke certain words of good hope.

The Grand Prince sent for the icons of the Most Pure Mother of God and of St Nicholas; and soon they brought the icons of the Most Pure One and of Nicholas the Wonderworker.

And he summoned his Tver palace overseer Ivan Yuryevich Shigona, and sent him to his spiritual father, Archpriest Alexei, and ordered him to bring him the reserved gifts from the church; and he ordered him to ask him whether he was accustomed to that matter, when the soul is separated from the body. The archpriest replied that he had had little occasion to see it.

Elder Misail Sukin came to the great sovereign Vasily and brought him the monastic habit.
