On the Arrival of Messengers from the Commanders in the German Land

In the same year, in the month of February, on the seventh day, Shemyaka, Prince Dmitry Gagarin, arrived from the commanders in the German land, and Urazly-Murza Konbarov from the tsarevich, and they reported that the commanders had crossed the border into Smolino and Lebyazhye and had sent men to ravage on both sides, and had also sent men to the town of Kinovep, and the Germans fled from the town and set it on fire.
