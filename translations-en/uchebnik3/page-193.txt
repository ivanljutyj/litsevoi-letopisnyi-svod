That same winter, on the 16th day of February, on Cheese-fare Sunday, at the seventh hour of the night, in the Chudov Monastery the refectory and the cell of Archimandrite Gennady burned.

That same winter, on the 23rd day of February, on the Assembly, the posadnik Zakharia Ovinov came from Novgorod to the grand prince with the grand prince's bailiff and with many Novgorodians: to answer for some whom he had wronged, and to bring others for investigation. And this had not happened from the beginning, since their land began and since grand princes began to be, from Rurik in Kiev and in Vladimir and down to this Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich; but he had brought them to this.

Then, at that same fast-eve, other posadniks came—Vasily Nikiforov, Ivan Kuzmin—and many other posadniks, and many prosperous townspeople, and villagers came, and monks, and widows for the same purpose: to seek answer for wrongs; and likewise widows, and nuns, and all the wronged, a great multitude of them.

On the 20th day of March, on Wednesday of the fifth week of Lent, after the service, at the seventh hour of the night, the courtyard of Prince Andrei Menshoi caught fire, and the courtyards of both Princes Andrei burned; and the small buildings around their courtyards, belonging to the priests of the Archangel, were torn down, for the grand prince himself and his son and many boyar sons were defending the place, because the grand prince had not yet gone to bed after standing in church through the Great Canon of Andrew.
