Nothing: Alexei Danilovich Basmanov and Bakhteyar Zyuzin, in the guard regiment Dmitry Mikhailovich Pleshcheev and Stepan Sidorov; and he ordered them to come to Mamailus, to contrive how to send against the Crimean herds, while themselves guarding that. And they set out from Belev on Trinity Day, and went by the Muravsky Road.

And when the voivodes had passed the upper reaches of the Mzha and Kolomak, a watchman from the Holy Mountains came running to them, and the stanitsa scout Lavrenty Koltovsky sent his comrade with news that the Crimean Tsar had crossed the Donets with many men and was going toward the Ryazan or Tula borderlands.

And the voivodes sent to the Tsar and Grand Prince with the news that the Crimean Tsar was going against his borderland, while the voivodes themselves turned back and followed the trail. And Ivashko Daryin with his comrades rode up to the Tsar and Grand Prince from the voivodes with the same news, on Friday in June.

And the Tsar and Grand Prince that same day sent the voivodes to Kolomna—Boyar
