And after hearing the prayer service and the divine liturgy, he commanded his father Varlaam the metropolitan to put in order and renew the holy icons, which had grown old over many years.

His Holiness Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, commanded that the holy icons be renewed there in his chambers, and out of great faith he himself many times touched them with his own hands and labored over the holy icons. And soon they renewed the holy icons, which became as though new, and even better, and they covered and adorned them with silver and gold, and provided them with icon cases and veils.

On the Arrival of Ambassadors from Rome

That same summer, on the 27th day of July, there came to Moscow the secretary of the great sovereign, Vladimir Semenov, son of Plemyannikov, whom the great prince had sent to his brother Maximilian the emperor. And together with him there came to the great sovereign Vasily in Moscow, from his brother the chosen emperor and supreme king of Rome Maximilian, his ambassadors, by name Frutyushko and Kolla, and Ontoni.

And the great prince, having honored them, sent them away. And in the year 7027 from Adam (the year 1527 from Christ), in January, the emperor Maximilian departed this life.

In the Year 7027 from Adam (the Year 1527 from Christ)

In November the great prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, sent to the Crimea to Tsar Magmed-Kirei his ambassador Prince Yuri Pronsky.

On the Elder Clement the Greek

In that same year, in the month of August, there came to Moscow to the great prince and sovereign of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, from Mount Sinai, where Moses the God-seer received the law from God, the elder Clement the Greek, seeking alms; and the great prince, having granted him favor, sent him away.

On the Church of the Ascension of the Lord

In that same year, also in August, by the command of the faithful great prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, they dismantled the old church of the Ascension of the Lord God and our Savior Jesus Christ in the Ascension Monastery within the city of Moscow, because of its age, and they laid the foundation for a new one.

On the Consecration of the Church of Saint Leontius, Bishop of Rostov, Wonderworker, beyond the Neglinnaya

In that same year, on the 12th day of the month of September, on Sunday, the church of Saint Leontius, wonderworker of Rostov, was consecrated by the most reverend Varlaam, metropolitan of all Rus, beyond the Neglinnaya in Moscow.
