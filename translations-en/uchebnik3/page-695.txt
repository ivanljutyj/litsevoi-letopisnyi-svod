To the pious and God-crowned and Christ-loving tsar and sovereign Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat of all Rus, beloved son of the holy church and of our humility, I leave peace, bless, and forgive, and I myself ask to receive from him his royal peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

Likewise I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness to his God-loving and Christ-loving tsaritsa, Grand Princess Maria, born of the Holy Spirit in the holy font, my goddaughter through our humility, and I myself ask the same of her—to receive peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

Likewise I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness to their God-given and beloved children, the right-believing and Christ-loving tsarevich Ivan Ivanovich and the right-believing tsarevich Feodor Ivanovich, born in the holy font of the Holy Spirit through our humility, my godchildren in Christ, my beloved children; peace and blessing and forgiveness, and I myself ask from them to receive their royal peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

To the right-believing Prince Vladimir Andreyevich, with his princess and their children, I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them—to receive peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

To the newly illumined in Christ Tsar Alexander Safa-Kireevich, born of the Holy Spirit in the holy font, my godson through our humility, I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of him—to receive peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

To my God-loving sons and brothers in the Holy Spirit, all archbishops living within the bounds of the Russian metropolis, I leave peace, blessing, and love, and I myself ask the same of them—to receive peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

To the God-loving archimandrites and honorable abbots of all the holy great monasteries I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them—to receive peace, blessing, and forgiveness.

To the God-loving boyars and princes, with their wives and children, I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness, and I myself ask to receive the same from them—forgiveness and blessing.

To the boyar sons and clerks, and merchants with their wives and children and with the rest of the Christ-loving army, I leave peace, blessing, and forgiveness, and I myself ask the same of them—to receive forgiveness and blessing.

To all the rest together, archimandrites and abbots, and sacred archpriests and honorable abbesses with their sisters, I leave peace and blessing...
