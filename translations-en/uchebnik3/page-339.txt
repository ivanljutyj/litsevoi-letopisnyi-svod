And from Starodub Seversky the Grand Prince ordered his namestnik and voivode Prince Semyon Fyodorovich Kurbsky, Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Telepnev Obolensky, Prince Pyotr Fyodorovich Okhlyabinin, and his other voivodes to march with many men.

And the Grand Prince’s voivodes marched from Smolensk to Orsha: in the large regiment were boyar and voivode Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Shuysky and okolnichy and voivode Andrei Vasilyevich Saburov; in the forward regiment were Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Vorotynsky and Prince Pyotr Fyodorovich Okhlyabinin; in the right forward regiment was Tsarevich Agdovlet; in the regiment of the right hand were Prince Vasily Andreyevich Mikulinsky and Andrei Nikitich Buturlin; in the guard regiment were Prince Semyon Fyodorovich Kurbsky and Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Telepnev Obolensky; and in the left-hand regiment were Prince Fyodor Vasilyevich Telepnev and other voivodes with many men.

And from Orsha the Grand Prince’s voivodes marched to Mogilev and Minsk.

And from Minsk they marched straight to Vilna, and they warred and took captives as far as Vilna itself and as far as Krev, thirty versts from Vilna.

And against the Grand Prince’s voivodes gathered the Lithuanian lords: the Vilna voivode Nikolai Nikolaev, the Troki voivode Olbracht Martynov, the Gorodetsky voivode Yuri Nikolaev Radivilov, the elder of Samogitia Yan Nikolaev Radivilov, Prince Mikhail Izheslavsky, and many other Lithuanian lords with many men.

And the Grand Prince’s voivodes, having called God to their aid, marched straight against them, and they beat their forward men, killing Prince Vasily Polubensky Vorotynsky, Chizh, a nobleman of the king, and many other people; and they captured a great man, a nobleman of the king, named Ray, and seized many other noblemen of the king, and took the baggage train of the Goroden voivode Yuri Nikolaev.

And with those great lords the Grand Prince’s voivodes wished to fight, but the Lithuanian voivodes fled from the Grand Prince’s voivodes behind fortifications.

And when the Grand Prince’s voivodes reached those fortifications, they came to difficult-to-access places, and they did not follow them because they encountered strongholds and difficult terrain.

And they warred and took captives in the Lithuanian land: Logeysk, Minsk, Krasnoye Selo, Molodechno, Markovo, Lebedevo, Krev, Oshmona, Medniki, Myadel, Korenskoye, Berezovichi, Vyazyn, Radoshkovichi, Borisovo, and many other places; they took captives as far as Vilna itself, and on both sides of Vilna, and beyond Vilna.

And having warred and taken captives throughout the Lithuanian land, the Grand Prince’s voivodes returned with all their men, and, God granting it, they came safely to the sovereign Grand Prince, and brought out countless captives.
