And to Tsarevich Shigalei and Prince Yuri Bulgakov he ordered to go from the Pakhra to the bank as well, to join the commanders; and from himself he sent to the Pakhra his commander Prince Vasily Mikhailovich Shchenyatev and his equerry Ivan Ivanovich Chelyadnin, and with them many men of his court, and ordered them to stand on the Pakhra.

And the Grand Prince himself went into the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Mother of God, and fell before the icon of the Most Pure Virgin, which Luke the Evangelist had painted, and falling on his knees and shedding tears, began to pray: “O most holy Lady, Mistress Theotokos, show mercy to the Christian race! You had mercy on our great-grandfather Grand Prince Vasily at the coming of the impious, from the godless Tsar Temir-Aksak; so now send Your mercy upon us and deliver us and all the Christian race from the godless Tsar Saip-Girey!”

“He has come against me and all the Russian land, boasting; send, O Queen, Your mercy, that the impious may not say: Where is their God, in whom they trust?” And he went to the tomb of Peter the Wonderworker with his brother Prince Yuri, and began to shed tears and call upon the wonderworker for help, and said: “O wonderworking Peter, look upon us orphans! We were left as little children from the bosom of our father and from the womb of our mother; we have no consolation for ourselves anywhere on earth; and now a great calamity has come upon us from the infidel; and it is fitting for you to pray for us, for God lit you for us as a bright candle and set you on the candlestick; God gave you to our family and to all Orthodox Christendom as a firm guardian; do not abandon us in the time of our sorrow, pray to God for us and for all the Christian race, that He may deliver us from the impious!” And he went into the church and began with tears to pray and to venerate the holy icons.

And he received the blessing from his father Metropolitan Joasaph, and went to his court; and he ordered his father Metropolitan Joasaph to go with him.

And he came into the chamber where he sat with the boyars, and said to his father Metropolitan Joasaph: “Know, father, this present calamity that is upon us, that the Crimean Tsar has come to our land, to the bank of the Oka River, having gathered many hordes with him; and do you take counsel with the boyars concerning us—whether we should be here in the city or go out somewhere.” And the boyars began to say: “Even before this, for our sins God permitted the infidel to come against Christendom; tsars have stood before the city of Moscow, but the grand princes did not remain sitting in the city.”

And other boyars said: “When, for our sins, tsars stood before Moscow, then our sovereigns were not little children; they could bear great hardship and think for themselves and help the land; but when Edigey came and stood before Moscow, Grand Prince Vasily Dmitrievich then left in the city Prince Vladimir Andreevich and his own brothers Prince Andrei and Prince Peter Dmitrievich, while the Grand Prince himself rode away…”
