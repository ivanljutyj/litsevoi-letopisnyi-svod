And they set loopholes along the walls. And sallying out from the city, they fought with the Tatars, and from the city they fired cannon shot at the Tsar’s regiments.

The Tatars came many times by night with combustibles and fire and wished to take the city, but by God’s protection and through the prayers of the Most Pure Mother of God and the great Russian wonderworkers they failed to harm the city in any way, and they withdrew from the city to their camps.

But the people who had not managed to enter the city fled to the Oka River to fortified places, and those who fled to the village of Kuzminskoye the Tatars seized there at the ferry.

The Tatars, seeing that the fugitives were crossing the Oka, some having embarked in boats and others swimming across on horses, seized the fleeing people there beyond the river.

And again the Tatars turned back after the Tsar, because in those places military men had never been, and there was no guard there at all, since impassable, forested country began.

In the same month of October, on the 6th day

The Tsar and Grand Prince came to Moscow from Suzdal, from a tour, having received Crimean news and wishing to go to Kolomna on his own and the land’s business against the Crimean Tsar. And he traveled to Moscow hastily, with great difficulty, and he left his tsaritsa and grand princess and his children, Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor, in Alexandrovskaya Sloboda.

And when the Tsar and Grand Prince arrived in Moscow, the voivodes wrote from Ryazan that the Tsar had left Ryazan hastily on October 5. And then the Tsar and Grand Prince postponed the campaign, although the Crimean Tsar had broken his oath, concerning which he had petitioned the Tsar and Grand Prince and sworn fidelity, and had come secretly to the sovereign’s borderlands, wishing to wage war on the sovereign’s land, to cross the Oka River, to take Christians captive and shed their blood. But the merciful God, not wishing to give the Christian race over to the wolves for final destruction, sent fear and terror upon the Hagarite race, and the Tsar with all his people left Ryazan hastily.

In the same month of October, on the 7th day, the Tsar and Grand Prince ordered his tsaritsa and grand princess Marya and his children, Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Feodor, to travel from Alexandrovskaya Sloboda to Moscow.
