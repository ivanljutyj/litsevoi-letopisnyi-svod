In the same month of July, by the command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, the town of Usvyato was founded in Usvyatskaya volost of Ozeritsky district.

In the same month of July, they wrote from Veliky Novgorod—Boyar Prince Fyodor Kurakin, and clerks Andrei Beznosov and Leonty Ananyin—that a pestilence had begun in Veliky Novgorod, in the Shelon pyatina, on July 19.

In the same month of July, on the 24th day, a Wednesday, the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and his son Tsarevich Ivan, with his pilgrims, the archbishops and bishops, and with the whole consecrated council, elected to the house of the Most Pure Mother of God, to the holy great throne of the great wonderworkers Peter and Jonah, to the Russian metropolia, after Metropolitan Afanasy, Abbot Philip of the Solovetsky Monastery; and on that same day they brought him to the metropolitan court.

And on July 25, a Thursday, by the command of the pious tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, autocrat, the Solovetsky abbot Philip Kolychev was installed on the holy great throne of the great wonderworkers Peter, Alexis, and Jonah, to the Russian metropolia, by the Russian archbishops and bishops: Pimen, Archbishop of Veliky Novgorod and Pskov; German, Archbishop of Kazan and Sviyazhsk; Nikandr, Archbishop of Rostov and Yaroslavl; Eleutherius, Bishop of Suzdal and Tarusa; Philotheus, Bishop of Ryazan and Murom; Simeon, Bishop of Smolensk and Bryansk; Barlaam, Bishop of Kolomna and Kashira; Galaktion, Bishop of Sarsk and Podonsk; Joasaph, Bishop of Perm and Vologda; and the whole consecrated council. But the Polotsk archbishop had died at that time, and the Tver bishop Akakiy was then ill, and moreover was old and remained in Tver, and he was of one mind with the archbishops and bishops concerning the election of the metropolitan. At the installation were the tsar and grand prince of all Rus, Ivan Vasilievich, and his children Tsarevich Ivan and Tsarevich Fyodor, and Prince Vladimir Andreevich.

That same summer, in August on the 11th day, by Philip, Metropolitan of all Rus, and the whole consecrated council, the Suzdal lord Afanasy was installed as archbishop in Polotsk.

In the same month, on the 25th day, the tsar and grand prince sent to the Swedish land, to King Erik, the messenger Touza Savlukov with a letter.

In the same month of August, a pestilence appeared in Veliky Novgorod, Polotsk, Ozerishchi, Nevl, Velikiye Luki, and Toropets; and many people died from the sores. In Polotsk, Toropets, and Luki, in the posads and in the uyezd, the priests died out. And there was no one to bury the dead; and priests were sent to those towns from other towns.
