And Prince Andrei, because of the words of wicked men, did not dare go to Moscow himself, but sent to Moscow to the Grand Prince and to his mother the Grand Princess his boyar Prince Fyodor Dmitrievich Pronsky.

And Prince Fyodor Pronsky had not yet arrived in Moscow from Prince Andrei in Staritsa, to the Grand Prince and to his mother the Grand Princess; and at that time Prince Andrei’s boyar’s son Prince Vasily, son of Prince Fyodor Goluboy of the Rostov princes, secretly sent by night from Staritsa to the Grand Prince’s boyar Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina his man Eremka with the news that Prince Andrei would certainly flee in the morning.

And Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina told the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess that Prince Andrei was fleeing in the morning. And the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess sent to Prince Andrei Vladyka Dositheus of Krutitsy, and Philotheus the Archimandrite of Simonov, and his spiritual father Simeon the Protopope of the Savior; and the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess ordered their word to be given to Prince Andrei that they had no evil at all in their thoughts against him.

And if it should happen that Prince Andrei did not believe the Vladyka and the Archimandrite and his spiritual father and fled, then the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess had for that reason sent after Prince Andrei their boyars Prince Nikita Vasilievich and Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina-Obolensky with many men, and ordered them to stand at Volok.

And to Prince Andrei’s boyar, Prince Fyodor Pronsky, the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess sent men to meet him, and ordered them to seize him and bring him to Moscow.

And when they were seizing Prince Fyodor, at that time Prince Andrei’s boyar’s son Sudok Dmitriev, son of Satin, escaped and ran to Staritsa and told Prince Andrei that his boyar Prince Fyodor Pronsky had been seized, “and a rumor had reached him that the Grand Prince and the Grand Princess had sent their boyars Prince Nikita and Prince Ivan Ovchina-Obolensky with many men to catch you.”

And at that time Prince Andrei’s boyar’s son Yakov Verigin galloped from Volok to Staritsa and said that the Grand Prince’s boyars Prince Nikita and Prince Ivan Ovchina-Obolensky had come to Volok with many men: “And they are coming to seize you.”

And Prince Andrei at that very hour fled with his princess and his son on the second day of May; and he rode from Staritsa about sixty versts to Novotorzhsky district, to the Bernov villages.

And from Bernovo Prince Vasily, son of Prince Fyodor Goluboy, fled from Prince Andrei and told the Grand Prince and his mother the Grand Princess that Prince Andrei had not yet decided to what place he should flee.
