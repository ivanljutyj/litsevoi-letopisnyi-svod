On the Bell Heard in the City of Kazan

Soon afterward the pious tsar himself heard with his own ears a bell ringing from the city of Kazan, like the voice of the great bell of the Simonov Monastery, and each person at the window of his chamber also heard the ringing.

The Great Artillery Train Is Brought Up to the City

And on Saturday, 27 August, the sovereign ordered his boyar Mikhail Yakovlevich Morozov to roll the great cannons up to the siege towers.

And the sovereign sent his okolnichy Pyotr Vasilyevich Morozov and ordered him to be at the siege towers that Prince Mikhail was setting up. And Boyar Mikhail Yakovlevich brought up the artillery and arranged it as it should be; and they began incessantly to strike the city with wall-breaking shot and with upper fire-cannons; they killed many people with the cannons. And also the streltsy in the trenches before the siege towers did not let people be on the walls or come out of the gates, and killed many.

The Attack of the Kazan Men on the Advanced Regiment

On that same day the sovereign sent Okolnichy Ivan Mikhailovich Vorontsov to inspect the places where the great regiment was to stand. And at that time Karamysh-ulan, son of Kudaygul-ulan, to whom those gates had been entrusted, came out of the Crimean Gates; they wanted to seize a prisoner for questioning from the regiment of Prince Ivan Mstislavsky, but the regiment had not yet been assembled.

And Prince Ivan Fedorovich hastened to meet them and defeated them, and Karamysh-ulan was taken alive; Prince Ivan himself was wounded by two arrows; all the other Christians were unharmed.

And Prince Ivan Fedorovich sent Karamysh-ulan to the sovereign tsar and grand prince.

The tsar ordered him to be severely tortured. And Karamysh said that many people had been killed on Friday, and that the Kazan tsar Ediger-Magmet and all the land had shut themselves up in the city unto death and did not wish to submit to the sovereign, for, like Pharaoh, God had hardened their hearts, that His holy name might be glorified in us.

The Attack of the Kazan Men on the Advanced Regiment

And on Sunday, 28 August, many Kazan men came from the forest onto the Arsk Field and suddenly attacked the advanced regiment, and they killed Tretyak Loshakov, who had been posted as a guard on the side of the forest.
