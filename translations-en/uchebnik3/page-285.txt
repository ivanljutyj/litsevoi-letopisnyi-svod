On the Capture of Dorogobuzh and the Battle on the Vedrosha

That same spring the grand prince sent his commander, the boyar Yuri Zakharin, with many men to Dorogobuzh, and Yuri took Dorogobuzh.

Learning of this, Alexander, Grand Prince of Lithuania, gathered a large army and sent against Yuri Zakharin his commanders, many lords and hetmans with many men; and Ivan Vasilievich, Grand Prince of All Rus, sent to Yuri’s aid his commander, the boyar Prince Daniil Vasilievich Shchenya, with the Tver force.

And the commanders of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich met the Lithuanian commanders on the Mitkovo Field, on the Vedrosha River, on the fourteenth day of the month of July, a Tuesday, the feast of the Holy Apostle Aquila, and there took place between them a great battle and fierce slaughter. And by God’s mercy and that of His Most Pure Mother, the commanders of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus overcame the Lithuanian commanders, killed many of Grand Prince Alexander’s men, and took alive many other commanders and hetmans and lords’ sons—Prince Konstantin Ostrozhsky, Lord Grigory Ostyukovich, and Lord Marshal Litovar, and many others—and sent them to the grand prince in Moscow.

And from the battle Mikhail Andreev, son of Pleshcheev, galloped to the grand prince in Moscow on July 17, a Friday, and told the grand prince of the commanders’ welfare and of God’s help against the Lithuanian force, and there was then great joy in Moscow.

On the Fire in Moscow

In the same year, on the seventeenth day of August, a Monday, at the eighth hour of the day, a fire broke out in Moscow at Bobr in the Great Posad, and it burned from the Moskva River to the Neglinnaya, and the cannon sheds, and the Nativity Monastery.

On the Coming of the Nogai Tatars to Kazan

In the same year the Nogai Tatars Musa-Murza and Yamgurchei-Murza came with many men to the city of Kazan against the Kazan tsar Abdyl-Letif, son of Abreim, and stood before the city for three weeks; and the grand prince’s commanders were then in Kazan with the tsar: Prince Mikhail Kurbsky and Prince Petr Loban Ryapolovsky with small retinues.

The Kazan tsar ordered a stockade to be built around the city, and every day they went out from the city to fight the Nogais, and by God’s permission all the Nogais soon withdrew to their own lands.

On the Capture of the City of Toropets

In the same year the grand prince’s commanders took the city of Toropets.
