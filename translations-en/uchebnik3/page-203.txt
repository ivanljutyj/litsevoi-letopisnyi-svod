Then Ivan Borisovich began to speak to them: "The great prince says thus to you, Vladyka, his pilgrim, and to the posadniks and well-to-do townsmen: you petitioned me, the great prince, that I lay aside my hostility toward my patrimony; and you have made representations concerning the Novgorod boyars, against whom I had previously been angered, that I should pardon and release them. But it is known to you, Vladyka, our pilgrim, and to you, posadniks and well-to-do men, and to all Novgorod, that all my patrimony, Great Novgorod, petitioned me, the great prince, concerning those boyars, saying that much harm had come from them to our patrimony, Great Novgorod and its volosts—raids and robberies, when they plundered people's property and shed Christian blood. And you, Luka Isakov, were yourself among the plaintiffs then, and you too, Grigory Kiprianov of Nikitina Street. And I, the great prince, investigated together with you, my pilgrim, and with you, posadniks, and with my patrimony, Great Novgorod, and found that much evil is done by them to our patrimony, and I wished to execute them; but you, Vladyka, and you, our patrimony, petitioned me, and I refused to execute them. And now you make representations concerning those guilty men; and if you do not petition us in a proper way, how are we to show you favor?"

After all these speeches Prince Ivan Yuryevich said: "The great prince says to you: if our patrimony Novgorod wishes to petition us, the great princes, its sovereigns, then they, our patrimony, know how to petition us, the great princes."

After that the Vladyka and the posadniks and the well-to-do townsmen petitioned the great prince for a bailiff, that he would show favor and order them to be escorted to the city; and the great prince ordered his bailiff Run to escort them; and he had met them from the great prince as a bailiff when they were going from Novgorod to the great prince.

On November 27, a Thursday, the great prince came up to the city across Lake Ilmen over the ice; and with him was his brother Prince Andrei the Younger.

And on that same day Prince Vasily Mikhailovich of Vereya came up to the city.
