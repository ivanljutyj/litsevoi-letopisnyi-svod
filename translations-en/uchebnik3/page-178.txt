On the 14th day of the same month, an envoy came from Rome to the Grand Prince from the tsareviches, the children of Thomas, despot of Morea, the Grand Prince’s brothers-in-law, with gifts, named Dmitry the Greek; and he was dismissed that same year.

On the 19th day of the same month, the Grand Prince dismissed Karachuk, envoy of the Great Horde, and with him he sent his own envoy Dmitry Lazarev; and he also dismissed the Venetian envoy Ivan Trevisan with them, and helped him in everything—with men, horses, and gifts.

In the year 6983 from Adam (1483 from Christ)

On September 1, an envoy named Bogdan came to the Grand Prince from Lithuania, from King Casimir. On the 19th of September, which was the 8th according to the heavenly (lunar) calendar, at the sixth hour of the day, slush came, snow with rain, and then heavy snow began to fall; at night there was frost, and on the next day as well, and then the snow melted; and on November 6 snow fell on the frozen ground, and the river froze on November 8.

On the 9th day of the same November, the Grand Prince’s huntsman Grigory Perfushkov saw two suns at the second hour of the day, when he rode into the field with birds: the true sun went on its own course, while another, unusual sun went higher than it in the midst of the sky, as the ordinary sun goes in midsummer, and it was bright with whiteness, but there were no rays from it; and not only he saw this, but others who were with him. That same autumn, Metropolitan Gerontius built a brick chamber on his courtyard upon four stone lower chambers, and he entered it in November on the [3rd] day.

And on that same day the Grand Prince’s envoy Nikita Beklemishev came from Crimea from Tsar Mengli-Girey, son of Haji-Girey, and with him the tsar’s envoy Devletek-Murza with many gifts. And he delivered the embassy on the 16th day of the same month: “To the Grand Prince from the tsar, love and brotherhood; and whoever is a friend to you, Grand Prince, is also a friend to me, the tsar; and whoever is an enemy to you is also an enemy to me; likewise our children and grandchildren shall be in the same love and brotherhood.”

In that same spring, on the 26th day of March, on Easter Day, the Grand Prince’s envoy Semyon Tolbuzin came from Rome and brought with him a master, an architect who builds churches and halls, named Aristotle; he is also a renowned cannon-founder, a master at casting them and firing them; and in casting bells and all other things he is a great craftsman.

And on the 27th day, on the morning of Easter Day, the Grand Prince dismissed his envoy Alexei Starkov to the Crimean Tsar Mengli-Girey, and also his envoy Devletek-Murza.

And on the second of April the Grand Prince dismissed to Lithuania his envoys Vasily Kitay and Fedets Mansurov.
