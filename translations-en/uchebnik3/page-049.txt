But you, Latin peoples, receive all who confess the Greek faith without any hesitation: for they are all baptized, and their baptism is holy and is accepted by the Roman Church as true and equal; and as baptism in the Roman Church is, so it is in the Eastern Church, so that from now on there may be no wicked doubt and disagreement among you. But since both the Latins and the aforementioned Greeks have now joined the one Church, they must offer prayers and supplications to the Lord God with a pure and contrite heart, so that all may be one. And when Greeks are in Latin land or in a place where Latin churches are situated in the Latin and Roman land, let them all attend the divine service with the boldness of a contrite heart and receive the Body of Jesus Christ with a contrite heart and give honor, as in their own churches in their own lands, wherever each lives; and let them come to Latin priests for confession and receive from them the Body of our Lord Jesus Christ God. And the Latins also must go to Greek churches and listen to the divine services, and with warm faith and a contrite heart adore the same Body of Jesus Christ, because the Body of Jesus Christ is true, and is also consecrated by the Greek priest in leavened bread, just as it is consecrated by the Latin presbyter in unleavened bread; and therefore both unleavened and leavened may be used. Latins may also come to Greek priests for confession and receive from them the holy and divine communion, since all is one and true. Thus the great ecumenical council decided at its session, after many deliberations and examinations of the holy Divine Scriptures, which took place in the venerable and great church in the city of Florence, in the year of the Lord's incarnation 1439, on the 6th day of June."

On the Death of Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Krasny

In that same autumn, on the 22nd day of September, Prince Dmitry Yuryevich Krasny reposed; and there was something wondrous in his illness. First deafness struck him, and the illness gradually took hold of him; his illness was very severe, and for many days he could eat nothing.

Then he wished to receive communion; on Sunday, the 18th day of September, the liturgy was being served, and the priest came with the Holy Gifts, and then blood began to flow from him, from both nostrils, as if swift streams were flowing, and so much of it flowed that it was impossible to give him communion, and the priest stood with the communion in the entryway, waiting. Then the blood began to lessen; his spiritual father, a hieromonk named Iosiy, stopped his nostrils with paper; the prince, rising, met the divine communion at the doors of the chamber.
