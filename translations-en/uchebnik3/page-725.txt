In the same month of July, on the 29th day, the tsar and grand prince sent from Moscow Domanukov’s man to Totuy Temryukovich, and together with him the tsar and grand prince sent to Prince Temryuk Aidarovich a stanitsa of service Tatars, Uraz Ryazanov and his companions, with letters.

In the same summer, in the month of August, by the command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus’ Ivan Vasilyevich, a town was built in his sovereign patrimony, in the Polotsk district, beyond the Dvina River, toward the Vilna border, on Lake Susha on an island; the sovereign ordered that town to be called Kopie. From Polotsk along the Ulsk road to that town is 70 versts, and from the Lithuanian town of Leplya twenty-five versts, from Lukoml 20 versts.

And having come secretly to that place, the voivode Prince Yuri Ivanovich Tokmakov, acting by the sovereign’s design, examined those places and established the town, and settled on the island with all his people, and ferried the artillery, the town timber, and his supplies to the island; and he quickly built the town with the town service people who were to serve there for a year, and by the sovereign’s order he fortified the town with levy laborers.

And people, the local inhabitants, came to the town to serve the sovereign tsar and grand prince. And for protection from the Lithuanian people, the tsar and grand prince ordered the boyar Prince Pyotr Semyonovich Serebryany and his companions to stand near that town.
