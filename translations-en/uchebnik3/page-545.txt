He ordered the towers strengthened, and behind the towers they stationed boyar scions, streltsy, and Cossacks.

The Tatars, seeing that by God’s mercy the Christians had already taken their stand at the ditches of the impious, and that between the city wall and the Tsar’s towers there was only one ditch three sazhens across and seven sazhens deep, fought without cease, firing from cannons and arquebuses and bows, and hurling stones.

And at dinner time, when they saw that many Russians had dispersed to eat and that there were few men at the towers, they crawled out of all their holes and from behind the taras and suddenly attacked the towers; the Christians faltered and fled from the towers.

On the Driving Off of the Pagans and the Strengthening of the Towers

The Tsar’s commanders courageously took heart and attacked the Tatars; and all the Christians saw that their commanders were fighting the Tatars, and all rushed at the Tatars. The Orthodox who hurried from all places prevailed, and the pagans threw themselves into their ditches, while the Tsar’s soldiers killed them in the ditches, and they fled through their holes into the city.

And so the Orthodox held out and strengthened their towers. And in that battle men fell on both sides, and many of the commanders themselves were wounded: Prince Mikhailo Ivanovich was wounded by many weapons, but his strong armor could not be pierced, and he was slightly wounded in the face; the okolnichy and commander Pyotr Morozov was gravely wounded in the face, and they carried him from the battle, but afterward he revived; commander Prince Yuri Ivanovich Kashin was wounded in the chest; also the streltsy commanders and many boyar scions were wounded.

And at the same time Prince Zeinesh with all the Nogais and with many Kazan people came out of the Zboylivye Gates against the towers of the vanguard regiment and the guard detachment; but those towers were not close to the city, and the commanders saw the attackers and let them come close to the towers, and the streltsy fired arquebuses at them. The commanders also attacked them, beat them, drove them to the city ditches, and killed many of them; and all the Orthodox were preserved by God.

The pious Tsar sent his okolnichy Alexei Danilovich Pleshcheev to Prince Mikhail as reinforcement, and in place of Pyotr, the treasurer Foma Petrov; he also sent as reinforcement the commanders of his Tsar’s regiment with boyar scions and many commanders with boyar retainers.

The pious Tsar, riding around the regiments around the city and coming to the towers, strengthened all, so that they should not weaken in the battles while Christ sends His mercy; and he visited and showed favor to the wounded commanders; and all saw the sovereign, prudent and courageous in bravery, who had armed himself with courage for battle.
