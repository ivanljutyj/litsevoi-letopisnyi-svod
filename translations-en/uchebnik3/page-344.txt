The Prayer of All the People to God

At that same time the Most Reverend Varlaam, Metropolitan of All Rus, and the other hierarchs, and the whole sacred council, and the monastic order, and a great multitude of the whole people, from nobles to common folk of every age, male and female, and not only in Moscow but throughout the whole Russian land, all prayed, each for his own sins, and all grieved and repented, and together in the holy churches, and in homes, and in cells they ceaselessly prayed to God, the Most Pure Mother of God, and the great Russian wonderworkers, and all the saints, and did not abandon hope of salvation.

A Wondrous and Merciful Vision to a Blind Nun

And so then the Most Merciful Christ our God, to whom they turned, showed a most glorious miracle, incomprehensible to the human mind, through the prayers of His Most Pure Mother and the holy great hierarchs, venerable fathers, and Russian wonderworkers, which God desired to show. There was a certain blind nun who had spent many years in virtues until old age, living in the maiden convent of the Ascension of Christ, where great princesses took vows and were buried. It happened then, when all the people were praying as was said above, and this nun earnestly addressed her prayers to God for deliverance from this present sorrow. And suddenly she heard as it were a loud noise, a terrible whirlwind, and the ringing of the square bells, and by divine impulse she was caught up in mind and found herself outside the monastery. And her spiritual eyes were opened together with her bodily eyes.

The Vision of the Council of Hierarchs and Others

And she saw a great and wondrous vision, not as in a dream but as if awake: behold, there came out of the city through the Frolov Gate an innumerable, light-radiant assembly of saintly men in sacred vestments, many metropolitans and bishops. Among them she recognized the great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, Jonah and Leontius of Rostov, and many other priests and deacons and other clergy, who carried the wonderworking image of the Most Pure Mother of God, the Vladimir icon, and other icons, and precious crosses, and Gospels, and other holy things with censers and candles, and with lamps, and with ripidas, and with banners, all in order as should be done during a litia, that is, during cross processions. After them followed a countless multitude of people of every age, male and female.

The Venerable Sergius and Varlaam

And then from the great Ilyinsky marketplace the great Sergius the wonderworker, great among the venerable, quickly went to meet them, and from the other side came the Venerable Varlaam, wonderworker of Khutyn.
