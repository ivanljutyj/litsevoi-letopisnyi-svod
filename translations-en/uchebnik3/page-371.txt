On the Pestilence in Pskov

In the same year, by God's permission, there was a great pestilence in Pskov. It began in the month of July and lasted until the Nativity of Christ. The city governors and dignitaries left the city because of the people's weeping and the fear of death, and hid and lived in villages and secret places.

And hearing of this, the God-loving Archbishop Macarius began to instruct the abbots and priests, and he himself went to the cathedral church of Holy Sophia and performed a prayer service, and washed the relics of the saints, and sent holy water throughout all Novgorod and Pskov.

And hearing of that, the sovereign Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich and his father Daniel, Metropolitan of all Rus, sent holy water from Moscow from themselves for the sanctification of Orthodox Christians. And from that time God's mercy was revealed upon Pskov, and little by little the pestilence ceased.

On the Erection of the Church of St. Nicholas the Wonderworker in Veliky Novgorod on the Archbishop's Court

In the same year, in the autumn, a stone church of St. Nicholas was erected on the old foundation in the archbishop's courtyard, next to the Church of the Holy Confessors, by the command of His Grace Archbishop Macarius; and the supervisor was Dmitry Syrkov.

On the Great Bridge across the Volkhov in Veliky Novgorod

That same autumn they built the great bridge across the Volkhov, and 200 Moscow rubles were given for it from the Grand Prince's treasury, and the supervisor was Filat Bobrovnik.

On the Crimean Tsar's Gathering against the Borderlands and the Grand Prince's Sending of Commanders to the Bank

In the same year, in May, news came to the Grand Prince of all Rus Vasily Ivanovich from the Crimea that the Crimean Tsar was gathering many men, boasting that he wanted to march against the Grand Prince's borderlands.

And the Grand Prince, hearing this, sent to Kolomna, to the bank, his commanders: Prince Ivan and Prince Semyon Fyodorovich Belsky; and his boyars: Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Gorbaty, and Mikhail Semenovich Vorontsov, and other commanders, and with them a countless multitude of princelings and nobles of his court and boyar sons from many cities.
