On the Consecration of Two Churches

In the same autumn, on November 5, a Sunday, the Church of John the Forerunner at the Borovitsky Gate was consecrated.

In the same month of November, on the 8th day, the Church on the Square in the name of the Holy Archangel Michael was consecrated by His Eminence Simon, Metropolitan of All Rus'.

On the Death of Prince Vasily Kholmsky

In the same autumn, in November, Prince Vasily Daniilovich Kholmsky was seized, imprisoned at Beloozero, and died there.

On the Envoys to the King in Lithuania with a Proposal of Peace

In the same month of November, on the 26th day, a Sunday, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' sent to King Sigismund in Lithuania his envoys with a proposal of peace, love, and an eternal treaty: his boyar, okolnichy, and vicegerent of Novgorod Grigory Fedorov, son of Danilovich, and his equerry Ivan Andreevich Chelyadnin, and his falconer Mikhail Stefanovich Klyapik, and his clerk Nikita Guba Moklokov.

On the Kazan Tsar. The Grand Prince Released Abdyl-Letif from Exile

In the same winter, in January, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' showed favor to the Kazan Tsar Abdyl-Letif, son of Abreim, released him from exile and forgave him his offense, and granted him favor, gave him the town of Yuryev with everything pertaining to it, and established him in brotherhood and love at the intercession of the Crimean Tsar Mengli-Girey, since Tsar Mengli-Girey and his Tsaritsa Nur-Saltana, mother of Tsar Abdyl-Letif, and Mengli-Girey's eldest son, Tsarevich Magamet-Kirey, had vouched to the Grand Prince for Tsar Abdyl-Letif; and his envoys, Magmedsha and his companions, gave oath on Mengli-Girey's soul, and Abdyl-Letif himself also gave oath that he would serve the sovereign Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus' and wish him well in all things.

On the Death of Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the Grandson

In the same winter, on February 14, the right-believing Grand Prince Dmitry Ivanovich, the grandson, reposed in prison, and his body was placed in the church...
