The Miracle of the Blind Maiden

And on that same day Saint Alexis the Wonderworker took pity on and healed a blind maiden named Anna: he granted her to regain sight in one eye, the left, and she began to see everything. And many who came with faith received healing without number—some openly, others unseen, but both kinds are known only to God.

The archimandrite of that same monastery, Jonah, reported these miracles to the most holy Varlaam, Metropolitan, and to the great sovereign and autocrat Vasily.

Having heard of these miracles from the archimandrite, the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, together with his father the most holy Varlaam, Metropolitan of All Rus, and with bishops, archimandrites, and hegumens, with the whole council and all the ecclesiastical order, came with psalmody, candles, and censers to the monastery of Saint Michael the Archangel, to the tomb in which the holy body of the hierarch and wonderworker Alexis was laid, and performed a service of supplication; and they gave great praise to almighty God and to His saint, the wonderworker Alexis.

Many Muscovites flocked to the wondrous miracles of the holy and great wonderworker Alexis. The pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich also came, and seeing such miracles, he fell down and bowed to the holy and divine body of the hierarch and wonderworker Alexis, and rejoiced with great joy, and sighing from the depth of his heart, said: “I praise and glorify You, my God, and Your most pure and all-undefiled Mother, and Your saint the wonderworker Alexis, who by the grace of Your Holy Spirit renewed his relics with wonderworking, and deemed me, Your servant, worthy in my lifetime to see such miracles in the land of my dominion, and as it were gave us a second source of grace in our city of Moscow, working wondrous miracles for us.” And they celebrated joyfully, giving thanks to God and His most pure and all-undefiled Mother, and His saint the holy wonderworker Alexis.
