And if the Rugodivians did not lie, the tsar and grand prince ordered Alexei and Daniil to be in Rugodiv; but if they lied to them, the sovereign ordered them to attend to his realm’s affair as much as the Merciful God would help. And on the first day of May Yakim and Zakhar arrived in Moscow, and the tsar and grand prince ordered his okolnichy Alexei Fyodorovich Adashev and his dyak Ivan Mikhailov to receive the Rugodivian posadniks Yakim and Zakhar and to ascertain their petition—what they were asking and what kind of sovereign’s favor they wanted. And Yakim and Zakhar first petitioned that they should not have to fall away from the Master, but that the sovereign would show them favor according to his will, and they petitioned the sovereign. And Alexei and Ivan told them that, contrary to the safe-conduct charter, they had fired upon the sovereign’s city and people, and they themselves, out of their own necessity, had petitioned that they had separated from the Master and wished to be entirely in the sovereign’s will. “And now the sovereign’s will is: you are to hand over the princelet from Vyshgorod and surrender the town of Vyshgorod to the voevodas of our sovereign, and the sovereign will show you favor, will not remove you from your homes, and will not violate your ancient customs and trade; but the voevodas of the tsar and sovereign shall rule Vyshgorod and Rugodiv and all the Rugodivian lands as the Master and the princelet formerly ruled over you, and otherwise that matter shall not be.” And Yakim and Zakhar submitted to the sovereign in all that; and the tsar and grand prince ordered them to appear before him, and they petitioned the sovereign that he take Vyshgorod and Rugodiv and all the Rugodivian land into his subjection, and concerning the princelet, as the sovereign would please: whether he ordered him taken to himself, or showed him favor and ordered him released.

And the tsar and grand prince showed favor to the Rugodivian ratmans and posadniks, and burgomasters, and polatniks, and all the Livonian land, took them into his name, and on that gave his grant charter as to what the sovereign’s favor toward them should be; and Yakim and Zakhar kissed the cross to the sovereign for all the Rugodivian land, that they would serve the sovereign and build cities, and seek no other sovereign besides the tsar and grand prince and his children.

And the tsar and grand prince showed them favor and sent them to Ivangorod with Fedor Pisemsky, and wrote to the voevodas that the sovereign had shown favor to the Rugodivian land and ordered them defended from the Master.
