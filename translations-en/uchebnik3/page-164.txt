They carry the coffin with the relics of the saint into the shrine that had been made in the new church, on the left side, where the tomb of Metropolitan Jonah had been near the northern doors of the church. And when the shrine had been placed on the spot where the relics of the saint had formerly been, level with the platform of the new church—for the platform in the new church was a man’s height higher than in the former church—and on that platform the shrine was made, where the relics of the saint were to be placed.

The bishops assembled: Archbishop Vassian of Rostov, Bishop Euthymius of Suzdal, Bishop Theodosius of Ryazan, Bishop Gerontius of Kolomna, and Bishop Prochorus of Sarai.

And on the 30th day of the same month the metropolitan announced to the grand prince that the time had already come to bring the relics of the saint on the following morning, on the first day of July. The grand prince entrusted everything to his will and to that of the consecrated council.

On that same day, July 1, at the second hour of the day, he ordered the bells rung for vespers; and all the bishops and archimandrites, archpriests, abbots, and priests gathered to the metropolitan.

And they entered the new church to the coffin of the most reverend Metropolitan Peter the Wonderworker. Then the right-believing Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich came, and his mother the Grand Princess Maria, and his son, and his two brothers, both named Andrei, and the boyars, and the nobles, and all Orthodox Christians. And then they decided to celebrate the transfer of the relics of the saint as on his repose, which falls on December 21, having performed the evening singing and then a prayer service to the Most Holy Theotokos and the Wonderworker; and after completing this they dispersed. In that night the Christ-loving princes came one after another to the coffin of the saint, performing prayer services, as did also the nobles and other God-loving people.

When the fifth hour of that night was ending, they began to ring for matins; and all the aforementioned, who had also been at the evening service, gathered and began matins, as on the repose of the Wonderworker Peter.

At the second hour of that day, July 1, having completed matins, they ordered all the bells rung and began the prayer service.

The grand prince with his son and brothers came to the coffin of the saint; and they touch it with fear and trembling, and with them the princes and boyars and many priests. And they carried it to the prepared place, where it had been before; but then that place had been inside the sanctuary near the prothesis, while in the present church it was before the doors of the holy prothesis, yet in the same former place. And when they had placed the reliquary with the relics of the saint, having sung a prayer service, they crossed themselves and venerated the relics of the saint with fear and joy, for the coffin of the saint was then uncovered, and his holy relics were visible to all. And after this the hierarchs and princes and nobles dispersed.
