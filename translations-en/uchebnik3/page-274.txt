And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, at their petition and that of all the land, forgave the Kazan princes their disfavor and offenses, showed them favor, did not send Tsar Magamet-Amin to them in Kazan, and named as tsar for them in Kazan Tsarevich Abdyl-Letif, son of Abreim, the younger brother of Tsar Magamet-Amin.
Tsar Mamuk heard of the Grand Prince's leniency toward the Kazan princes, and soon departed from Kazan to his own lands, and died on the way.

About Tsar Abdyl-Letif

In the year 7005 from Adam (1505 from Christ), in April, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich showed favor to Tsarevich Abdyl-Letif, son of Abreim, and released him from Moscow to Kazan to reign, in place of his elder brother Magamet-Amin, and with him sent to Kazan Prince Semyon Daniilovich Kholmsky and Prince Fyodor Ivanovich Paletsky.

They came to Kazan in the month of May and enthroned Abdyl-Letif, and brought all the Kazan princes and ulans and the people of the land to oath to the Grand Prince according to their faith.

And in that same spring the Grand Prince showed favor to the former Kazan Tsar Magamet-Amin, gave him Kashira, Serpukhov, and Khotun with all their dues, and released him from Moscow on the ninth day of May; but even there he did not change his customs, lived greedily, and inflicted violence on many.

About the Envoys to Wallachia

In that same year Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent his envoys to Voivode Stephen in Wallachia, Ivan Oshcherin and Luka Voloshanin; after completing their embassy, they set out back, and on the way near Terebovl they were robbed by Yapancha-Saltan, son of the Crimean Tsar, together with his Cossacks.

They returned to Voivode Stephen; Voivode Stephen sent his envoy about this matter to the Crimean Tsar Mengli-Girey; the Crimean Tsar ordered his princes and Cossacks to investigate and return the loot; after investigating, they sent back a little, but the greater part was lost.

And they came to Moscow in the year 7005 from Adam (1505 from Christ) in August; and with them came an envoy from Wallachia, Ivan Pitar, and the Grand Prince released him on the third day of the month of September.

And together with that same Wallachian envoy there came to the Grand Prince Abbot Paisius and three elders from the Holy Mountain seeking alms; and the Grand Prince showed them favor, gave them a sufficient amount of alms, and to other monasteries.
