And Maximilian, King of the Romans, concluded peace and an agreement with Vladislav, renounced Belgrade in favor of Vladislav, while Vienna and the whole Austrian land were given to Maximilian.

On the Arrival of Prince Dmitry Vorotynsky

In that same autumn, in December, Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Vorotynsky came to Moscow from his patrimony, away from the Lithuanian King Casimir, to serve Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich.

On the Arrival of Prince Ivan Peremyshlsky and Prince Ivan Belevsky with His Brothers

In the same year, Prince Ivan Mikhailovich Peremyshlsky came to Moscow from his patrimony, and Prince Ivan Belevsky with his brothers Prince Andrei and Prince Vasily came from their patrimonies, from King Casimir, to serve Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich.

On the Arrival from Rome of Andrei, Son of Despot Thomas, and on the Envoys and Craftsmen

In that same winter there came from Rome to Moscow the brother of Grand Princess Sophia, named Andrei, son of Thomas, Despot of Ammoria; and with him came the envoys of the Grand Prince, Dmitry and Manuel, sons of Ivan Ralev, and they brought with them to the Grand Prince craftsmen: an architect named Peter Anthony and his apprentice Zamantoy, builders of walls and palaces; a cannon master Yakov with his wife; a silversmith Christopher with two apprentices from Rome; the German Olberd from Lübeck; Karl with an apprentice from Milan; Peter Raik, a Greek from Venice; the chaplain of the White Monks of the Augustinian Order, Ivan Spisatel, an organist; and the Jewish physician Master Leon from Venice.

On the Death of Grand Prince Ivan Ivanovich

In that same winter, on the seventh day of March, from Saturday to Sunday, at the eighth hour of the night, there reposed the pious and Christ-loving Grand Prince Ivan, eldest son of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich of All Rus, by his first wife Grand Princess Maria, daughter of Grand Prince Boris Alexandrovich of Tver.

And he was afflicted in his legs with gout, and having examined him, the Jewish physician Master Leon, boasting, said to his father Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich: “I will cure your son the Grand Prince of that illness, and if I do not cure him, then order me to be put to death.” And Grand Prince Ivan Vasilievich believed his words and ordered him to treat his son Grand Prince Ivan.
