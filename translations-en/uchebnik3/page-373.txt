On the Arrival from the Indian Land of the Merchant Khozya-Hussein with a Letter

There came to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus in Moscow, from the Indian land, the merchant Khozya-Hussein. And he brought a letter from Babur-pasha, sovereign of the Indian land; and Babur-pasha wrote that Grand Sovereign Vasily Ivanovich should be in friendship and brotherhood with him, and that people should go between them in both directions to inquire after health.

And the grand prince released Khozya-Hussein to Babur-pasha, and wrote with him in a letter that he wished people to travel between them. But about brotherhood he did not write to him: it was unknown who he was in the Indian state, whether a sovereign or an official, and how the grand sovereign might not suffer dishonor in that if he were an official of that land. And therefore the grand sovereign did not write to him about brotherhood.

On Tsar Shigalei of Kazan, and How the Sovereign Granted Him Favor

In the same month of September, Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign autocrat of all Rus, granted favor to the former Kazan Tsar Shigalei, gave him Kashira and Serpukhov with all dues, and released him from Moscow in the same month on the twenty-first day.

On the Grand Prince's Journey

In the same month on the twenty-second day, a Sunday, the grand prince set out from Moscow to pray to the Life-Giving Trinity in the Sergius Monastery, for the commemoration of the Wonderworker Sergius, and from there he was in Sloboda and came to Moscow on October 3.

On the Appearance of a Star

That same autumn in October a star appeared at morning dawn two hours before daybreak, above the winter sunrise, and from it shone a great and broad ray southward; it appeared in the same place from the first day of October until November 9.

On the Sending to Lithuania

In the same month of October on the eighth day, the grand prince sent to Lithuania to King Sigismund Vasily Alferyev, son of Nashchekin, with a letter concerning certain affairs of the land.
