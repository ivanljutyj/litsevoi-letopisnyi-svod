When at birth he opened his mother’s womb, not only the sight of him but also the report of it gladdened hearts. Everywhere great thanksgivings and prayers for him were heard from all, addressed to God, both from the hierarchs and the whole consecrated council and monastic order, and from the whole royal court and from all noble officials and dignitaries, and from all people, male and female. Distant monks, hermits, and great ascetics, and others worn out by old age and barely breathing—they too, moved in spirit, hastened on foot from distant wildernesses and came to bow to the infant wrapped in swaddling clothes, who had come from his mother’s womb of the royal line, desiring to share in the joy of the king to whom they had come, bowing and saying: “Blessed are you from God, O pious tsar! And it will be well with you, for your queen is like a fruitful vine, and your son is like a new planting, set in the heart of your great kingdom. For God has saved His anointed and prepared him to sit on your throne from your loins.” The tsar also answered them, saying: “I think, your reverences, O honorable fathers, that through your prayers God has fulfilled my desire and God has granted me that for which I asked and prayed, so that His mercy may be established upon us from generation to generation forever.”

And so the tsar, having sufficiently honored the elders, and having shown them honor at his royal table, and having delighted them with wise words, kindly sent each back to his place, supplying them with all necessities.

On the Turmoil of the Kazan People, and How They Requested Yanalei from the Grand Prince as Their Tsar

And so the autocratic Grand Prince Vasily, reigning with thanksgiving, in the spaciousness of his heart nurtured the heir of his kingdom, the son and tsar Ivan given to him by God, whom God destined finally to subdue both the Kazan kingdom and others. And then a great fear and trembling fell upon Kazan, both upon Tsar Safa-Girey himself and upon the princes and all the Kazan people, according to their many sorcerous predictions, of which we shall speak later.

In the year 7039 from Adam (the year 1539 from Christ)

There came to the Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich, sovereign of all Rus, the Kazan envoys Prince Tabai, Prince Tevekel, and Ibreim-bakshi, and they petitioned the Grand Prince on behalf of Tsar Safa-Girey and the princes and all the people of the Kazan land, that the great sovereign would show them favor, forgive the tsar his anger, and not hold his disgrace against the princes and the whole Kazan land.
