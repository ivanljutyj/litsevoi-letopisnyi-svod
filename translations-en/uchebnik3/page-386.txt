And then, on Saturday, on St Demetrius’s Day, at the sixth hour of the night, he commanded Menshoi Putyatin secretly to bring him the testamentary charters.

And he admitted to his council concerning the testamentary charters his Tver butler Ivan Yuryevich Shigona and his clerk Menshoi Putyatin; and the grand prince began to consider whom else to admit to that council and to whom to entrust his sovereign’s charge; and the boyars were then at Volok with the grand prince: Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky and Prince Ivan Vasilyevich Shuysky, and Prince Mikhail Lvovich Glinsky, and his butlers, Prince Ivan Ivanovich Kubensky and Ivan Yuryevich Shigona.

Then also the grand prince’s brother Prince Yuri Ivanovich came to the grand prince; but the grand prince concealed his illness from him; and he stayed with him but a short while.

And he sent him away to his patrimony in Dmitrov; he did not wish to go, but the grand prince sent him.

Then also, before the commemoration of the wonderworker Varlaam of Khutyn, on that night much pus came out of his sore, so that more than a basinful of pus came out, and the core more than a span and a half, but the core had not yet come out of it entirely. The grand prince rejoiced at this, hoping for relief from his illness.

And then he sent to Moscow for Hetman Jan; Jan soon came and began to apply the usual ointment to the sore; from Jan’s ointment the discharge diminished somewhat.

Then also the grand prince sent to Moscow for the elder Misail Sukin; his illness was grave; and he sent for his boyar Mikhail Yuryevich; the elder Misail and his boyar Mikhail Yuryevich soon arrived to him.

And the grand prince began to deliberate with the boyars; and then his boyars were with him: Prince Dmitry Fedorovich Belsky and Prince Vasily Shuysky, and Mikhail Yuryevich, and Prince Mikhail Lvovich Glinsky, and his butlers — Prince Ivan Ivanovich Kubensky and Ivan Yuryevich Shigona, and his clerks — Grigory Menshoi Putyatin and Elizar Tsypliatev, Afanasy Kuritsyn, Tretyak Rakov. And the grand prince began to consider how he should go to Moscow; and the grand prince decided with the boyars that he should go from Volok to the Joseph Monastery to pray to the Most Pure.

Then the grand prince set out from Volok to the Joseph Monastery to pray to the Most Pure, and he ordered that he be carried with great difficulty.

He spent the eve of the fast in his village at Bui-gorod, and his brother Prince Andrei Ivanovich was with him as well.
