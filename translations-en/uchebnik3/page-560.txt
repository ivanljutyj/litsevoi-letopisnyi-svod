And for many years our father and grandfathers, and we as well, armed ourselves to avenge them, sent our commanders, and we had no success because of our sins. And now, with God’s help, according to your counsel, we went against them. And at that time our enemy, the Crimean Tsar Devlet-Girey, was approaching us, wishing, after our departure for Kazan, to destroy Orthodoxy utterly. And you, our father Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus’, with your children in the Holy Spirit, the archbishops and bishops and abbots, and with all the sacred councils, did not despise our entreaty and remembered the word of our Lord Christ: “Watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation”; and you fulfilled Christ’s word by vigil and prayers, by labors and thirst, and by all-night standing. You also ordered that in all holy places and among all the Christian people, after our departure, a fast and prayer be kept against the foreigners, to Almighty God and to the Lord Christ and to His most pure Mother and to the great wonderworkers, for Christian deliverance and for our sinfulness. And the merciful God, through the prayers of His most pure Mother and of the great wonderworkers, and through your great labors and prayers and those of the archbishops and bishops and of all the sacred council of Russia, heard the prayers of all the people and granted us help: the Crimean Tsar Devlet-Girey returned, driven by no one, but only by the wrath of God and by your prayers. And, not waiting for us, he soon returned; and as for those of his men who met ours, God showed us His mercy over them: our commanders killed many Crimean people and brought many alive to us. And we now give thanks to God; and to you, our father and man of prayer Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus’, and to the archbishops and to all the sacred council, we bow low. And after the departure of the Crimean Tsar, placing our hope in Almighty God and in the prayers of His most pure Mother and of the great wonderworkers, and trusting in your holy prayers, we dared to fulfill what had been said before: having armed ourselves, we went against the fierce bloodthirsty Kazanians with Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and with all our host. And, by God’s grace, we arrived safely with the help of the merciful God and of His most pure Mother, and through the prayers of the great wonderworkers, and through your holy great labors and vigil and prayers, and through the prayers of our departed parents, and also through the care, courage, and bravery of Prince Vladimir Andreyevich and of all our boyars and commanders, and through the zeal and suffering of all our Christian army for our immaculate, true, holy Christian faith and for the holy churches,
