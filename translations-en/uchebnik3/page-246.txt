On the Construction of a Tower

That same year Mark Fryazin built the Beklemishev Tower at the corner, downstream along the Moscow [River].

On the Laying of the Tower
In the year 6996 from Adam (1496 from Christ)

On the 27th day of May, Anton Fryazin laid the foundation of a tower upstream along the Moscow [River], where the Sviblov Tower had stood, and beneath it a secret passage was constructed.

On the New Town at Beloozero

That same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, they built a new town at Beloozero, in Kargolom, 10 versts from the old town.

On the Cannon Pavlin

That same year, on the 12th day of August, Pavlin Fryazin Debbosis cast a great cannon.

On the Moscow Fire

That same month, on the 13th day, after dinner, in the 9th hour of the day, the Church of the Annunciation on the Marsh caught fire, and it burned from the city to Kulishki, almost to the Church of All Saints, and to the Pokrov in the Gardens, and to the Neglinnaya; in all, 42 churches burned.

On Sending the Ralev Sons to Rome

That same year the Grand Prince sent the Ralev sons, Dmitry and Manuel, to Rome, Venice, and Mediolanum.

On the Treason

That same year his servant Obrazets told Prince Andrei of Uglich that the Grand Prince meant to seize him. He wished to flee Moscow in secret, and barely abandoned that thought, and sent to Prince Ivan Yuryevich, ordering him to ask the Grand Prince what the Grand Prince intended to do to him. But Prince Ivan refused.

He himself went and confessed; and the Grand Prince swore by heaven and earth, and by God, the Creator of all creation, that this had never even been in his thoughts. And he asked from whom he had heard of it; but in reality the Grand Prince’s servant Munt Tatishchev had come and jested thoughtlessly, and he took it for truth, wishing to tell Prince Andrei, because before that Prince Andrei had borne anger against him.
