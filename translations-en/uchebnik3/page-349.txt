In the large regiment: Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Gorbaty and Mikhail Yuryevich; and in the forward regiment Prince Semyon Fedorovich Kurbsky and Ivan Vasilyevich Lyatsky; and in the right-hand regiment Prince Semyon Dmitrievich Serebryany and Prince Pyotr Okhlyabinin; and in the left-hand regiment Prince Yury and Prince Vasily Vasilyevich Ushaty; and in the guard regiment Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Kubensky and Prince Ivan Shamin.

And in the mounted host, in the large regiment: boyar Ivan Vasilyevich Khabar and Mikhail Semyonovich Vorontsov; and in the forward regiment: Vasily Andreyevich Sheremetev and Fedor Semyonov, son of Kolychev; and in the right-hand regiment: Prince Pyotr Ivanovich Repnin and Dmitry Buturlin; and in the left-hand regiment: Prince Ivan Fedorovich Ovchina and Prince Andrey Kropotkin; and in the guard regiment: Ivan Vasilyev, son of Loshakov, and Chulok Zasekin.

The impious Tsar Sahib-Giray of Kazan learned of the mighty reinforcement, the great muster of troops, and the formidable host of the Grand Prince, and fled from Kazan in great shame. And the Kazanians took as tsar into Kazan Safa-Giray, the Crimean tsarevich.

And Tsar Shigalei and the Grand Prince's ship commanders came to Kazan and stood on the bank of the Volga, above Kazan, on the Kazan side.

And the Kazanians learned of the mounted commanders, that they were coming to Kazan; having gathered, the princes and murzas and all the land of Kazan met the Grand Prince's commanders twenty versts from the city of Kazan, on the Sviyaga.

And there was a battle between them, and God helped the Grand Prince's commanders. And in that battle they killed many princes and murzas, and Tatars, and Cheremis, and Chuvashes, and took many other princes and murzas alive.

And the mounted host joined the ship host, and they began to assault the city, and killed many Tatars before the city. And the Kazanians, seeing their defeat, petitioned the Grand Prince's commanders and gave an oath, surrendering to the will of the sovereign Grand Prince.

On the Ambassadors. The year 7033 from Adam (the year 1533 from Christ)

That same autumn ambassadors came from Kazan: Appay-ulan and Prince Bagty-Kildey and others, and they petitioned the Grand Prince on behalf of all the land of Kazan concerning their guilt and concerning Tsar Safa-Giray. And the sovereign, by their petition, granted them favor and sent his ambassador Prince Vasily Danilovich Penkov and his clerk Afanasy Fedorov, son of Kuritsyn, to Kazan.

On the Consecration of Two Bishops to Kolomna and Vologda

That same spring, on April 2, Bishop Vassian Toporkov was consecrated to Kolomna, and to Vologda Alexei, abbot of the Kirillov Monastery, on April 9.
