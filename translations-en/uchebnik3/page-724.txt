And he ordered them to go with the ambassador to Lithuania, with the boyar Fyodor Ivanovich Umnoy Kolychev and his companions, and the sovereign ordered the ambassadors to speak about this, so that they would let them through their land.

In the same year, on the 9th day of April, there was a renewal of the moon: it vanished from the eighth hour of the day and was filled until the eleventh hour; and at that time there was darkness, a person saw another person as green, and the sun became like a three-day-old young moon.

In the same year the tsar and grand prince sent with his gifts from his treasury his guest merchants and merchants to the maritime states: to Antwerp, to the burgomasters and town councillors he sent the guest merchant Ivan Afanasyev and the merchant Timofey Smyvalov; and to Hormuz — the merchants Dmitry Ivashev and Fyodor Pershin; to the English land, to Queen Elizabeth — the merchants Stepan Tverdikov and Fedot Pogorelov. And the tsar and grand prince sent his charters to those lands to all rulers concerning passage and protection.

In the same year in July, by the command of the sovereign tsar and grand prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilyevich, the icon of the Mother of God of Vladimir, painted by the Evangelist Luke, was restored and adorned with gold and many precious stones; it stands in the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Mother of God in the God-protected city of Moscow; and the former metropolitan Afanasy restored it.

That same summer, on the 5th day of July, the tsar and grand prince of all Rus Ivan Vasilyevich released the Polotsk voivode Stanislav Stanislavovich Dovoyna to Lithuania in exchange for Prince Vasily Temkin, and he ordered Prince Vasily, according to the sovereign’s command, to receive 10,000 Hungarian gold pieces from Dovoyna. And for the exchange the sovereign sent from his side Prince Ivan Tevelkeleevich and the clerk Iosif Ilyin. And for protection Vasily Kolychev and Mikhailo Kumbulov with many men were sent to the Smolensk border.

On the same day ambassadors came to the tsar and grand prince from the Nogais: from Tinekhmat — the ambassador Karmysh Tleshev, and from Urus-murza — the ambassador Aytugan Tuzarov, and ambassadors from all the murzas, and with them Nogai merchants with horses; and all the ambassadors and guests numbered 500 people, and the horses two thousand five hundred. And the sovereign ordered that they be given provisions according to the former custom.

In the same month of July, on the 16th day, the tsar and grand prince sent on his service the Nogai murzas: Ibreim-murza and Yel-murza, sons of Isup, and Temir-murza and Bezan-murza, sons of Urazly, and other murzas, and with them Nogai people who serve the sovereign. And he ordered them to be at Luki for protection against the Lithuanian side.
