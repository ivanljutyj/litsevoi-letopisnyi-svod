On Sending to the Uluses

And the sovereign himself sent to all the uluses, to the black yasak-paying people, granted yasak charters, so that they should come to the sovereign without fearing anything. And whoever does evil, God will avenge; and the sovereign will show them favor, and they should pay yasak as they did to the former Kazan tsars.

The Ar People Sent to the Tsar Sovereign to Petition

And the Ar people sent to the sovereign their Cossacks Shemai and Kubish to petition with a letter, asking that the sovereign show favor to them, the black people, forgive his anger, and order the yasak to be collected as under the former tsars. And he should send to them a son of a boyar who would speak the tsar’s word of favor to them and gather them, because they had fled in fear; and they, having sworn an oath to the sovereign, would ride to the sovereign. And the tsar and grand prince sent to them the son of a boyar Nikita Kazarinov and the Kazan murza Komay.

And from the Meadow side the Cheremis also came to the sovereign to petition, and the sovereign showed them favor.

On the Consecration of the City

In the same year, on the 4th day of October, the city of Kazan was cleansed of the multitude of corpses, and the sovereign rode into the city, and chose a place in the city, and set up a cross upon it with his own tsar’s hands, and laid the foundation in that place of a church in the name of our most pure Lady the Mother of God, her honorable Annunciation. And the archpriest Andrei sang a moleben with the abbots and priests, and they blessed the waters.

And the sovereign tsar went with crosses along the city walls, and consecrated the city in the name of the Holy Life-Giving Trinity, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, and of his most pure Mother of God and the great wonderworkers.

On the Consecration of the Church

In the same month, on the 6th day, the sovereign consecrated the cathedral church of the Annunciation of the most pure Mother of God, and Andrei the archpriest consecrated it, together with Afanasy, the Nativity archpriest from Sviyaga, with the abbots and priests.

On the same day the tsar and sovereign chose the governors whom he left behind him in Kazan: the great boyar and governor Prince Alexander Borisovich Gorbaty, whom he ordered to govern in place of the tsar, and the boyar and governor Prince Vasily Semenovich Serebryany, and many other governors.
