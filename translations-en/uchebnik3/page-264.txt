About the Masovian Envoy

In the same spring, on 3 May, an envoy named Ivan came from the Masovian land from Grand Prince Konrad.

About the Bishop of Sarsk

In the same spring, on 4 May, the elder Siluan, former abbot of Ugresh, was consecrated by His Eminence Metropolitan Zosima to the bishopric of Sarsk and Podonsk at Krutitsy.

About the Mission to Venice and Milan

In the same year in May the Grand Prince sent the Greek Manuel Angelov and Daniil Mamyrev to Venice and Milan.

About the Dismissal of the Masovian Envoy

In the same month of May Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich dismissed the Masovian envoy Ivan to his land; together with him he sent to the Masovian Prince Konrad his envoys Osanchuk Zabolotsky and Tretyak Vasilyev, son of Dolmatov.

About the German Envoy and the Sending of Envoys to the German Land

In the same summer, in June, an envoy came from the German land to the Grand Prince from the Danish King Ivan with an offer of love and brotherhood.

And the Grand Prince, having shown honor to the envoy, dismissed him to his land; and together with him the Grand Prince sent his envoys to the Danish King Ivan with an offer of love and brotherhood, Dmitry Grek Ralev Paleolog and Dmitry Zaitsev.

About the Lithuanian Envoy

In the same year envoys came from Lithuania from Grand Prince Alexander: Andrei Olekhnovich and Voitko Yanovich Klochko.

About the Building of a Town at Luki

In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, a wooden town was built at Velikiye Luki on the old foundation.

About the Courtyards in Moscow and the Churches

In the same year, by command of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich, churches and courtyards beyond the Neglinnaya were demolished, and a measure of 110 sazhens was set from the wall to the courtyards.
