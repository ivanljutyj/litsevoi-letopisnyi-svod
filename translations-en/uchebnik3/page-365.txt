At that consecration there were the Grand Prince with the Grand Princess, and with his son Prince Ivan, and with the boyars; and from that time the great sovereign decreed that every year they should go with crosses to their church on the twenty-seventh day of November and celebrate it with honor.

On the Death of the Bishop of Suzdal

That same winter, on the twenty-third day of March in the year 39, Gennadius, Bishop of Suzdal, reposed in Moscow, and he was laid to rest in Suzdal.

On the Fire in Nizhny Novgorod

In that same year, in July, at the third hour of the night, on the night from Thursday to the tenth Friday after Easter, fire broke out in Nizhny Novgorod in the posad above the Church of Cosmas and Damian. And the fire spread to the city, and the roof on the city wall caught fire, and from that roof the cannon powder in the Ivanovo Tower caught fire; and from that the tower fell, and within, the Grand Prince’s court burned, and many other courts burned.

And on that same Friday, at the seventh hour of the day, cannon powder suddenly caught fire in Moscow in the Uspensky Ravine, at the Alevizonsky Court; for the townspeople were making it at that court. And in one hour more than two hundred of the makers of that powder burned; but by God’s keeping the fire did not touch that court or other courts.

On the Building of the City of Kolomna

In that same year the stone city of Kolomna was completed.

On the Building of the City of Chernigov

In that same year, by the command of the great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich, by God’s grace sovereign and autocrat of all Rus, the wooden city of Chernigov was built.

On the Building of the City of Kashira

In that same year a wooden city was built at Kashira, and a stone one on the Osetr.

On the Appearance of a Star

In that same year, in August, a great star appeared in the sky above the summer sunrise, and for a long time at the morning dawns a great ray shone upward from it in the mornings, and it moved not by its usual course but toward the south. And afterward, in the same month, that same star appeared at the evening dawn, after sunset, red, and a red ray shone upward from it above the summer west.
