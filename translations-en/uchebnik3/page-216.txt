On the 6th day of February the Grand Prince ordered Grigory Kiprianov Arzubiev seized and all his property taken.

On the 7th day of February the Grand Prince ordered the captured Novgorodians sent to Moscow: Marfa Isakova with her grandson, and Ivan Kuzmin Savelkov, and Akinf with his son Roman, and Yuri Repekhov, and Grigory Arzubiev, and Mark Pamfiliev; and he ordered their property registered to himself.

On the 8th day of February, Sunday, on the Assembly Sunday, the Grand Prince in Novgorod heard mass in the cathedral of Holy Sophia, and dined at his own place on Paozerye, and with him were his brother Prince Andrei the Younger, and the archbishop, and many Novgorod boyars, and prosperous townsmen.

On the 12th day of February, Thursday, the archbishop came to the Grand Prince from Novgorod with gifts before mass, and presented a gold chain weighing five grivnas, and a gold cup of ten grivnas, and a gold cup of one and a half grivnas and ten zolotniks, and a gold ladle of one grivna and three zolotniks, and a silver-gilt tankard of thirteen grivnas, and a folding gilt goblet of fourteen grivnas, and a silver dish weighing eleven grivnas, and a gilt goblet of six and a half grivnas, and a gilt belt with great fittings, weighing eight and a half grivnas, and a hundred gold coins.

On the 17th day of February, Tuesday, early, the Grand Prince set out from Novgorod, and his first stop was at Yamny. And to that stop the archbishop came after him to see him off, and presented a barrel of wine and a stallion; and the Novgorod boyars and prosperous men presented skins of wine and mead; and they all ate and drank with him; and the Grand Prince gave gifts to the archbishop and the boyars, and dismissed them.

On the 5th day of March the Grand Prince came to Moscow on Thursday, in the fifth week of Lent.

And after himself the Grand Prince ordered their veche bell brought from Novgorod to Moscow. And it was brought, and raised onto the bell tower on the square—to ring with the other bells. And since Great Novgorod and the Russian land came to be, such coercion had not been upon them from any grand prince, nor from anyone else.

That same winter the Kazan tsar went against Vyatka, and took much captive, and cut down, and plundered, despite his oath; and the towns swore allegiance to him.

That same winter, on the 6th day of December, the hieromonk Vassian, archimandrite, son of Prince Ivan Vasilievich Strigin Obolensky, was appointed by Metropolitan Gerontius in Moscow to the see of Tver.

That same spring the Annunciation fell on Wednesday of Bright Week. On that same day the ice passed on the Moscow River, and on the next day they began crossing it on horseback. That same spring Elder Joseph began to build a monastery at Lamsky Volok in the wilderness, in the year 6986 from Adam (1486 from Christ).
