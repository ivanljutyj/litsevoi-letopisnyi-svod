On the Sovereign’s Journey to Volok

In the same month of September, on the 14th day, on the Exaltation of the Precious Cross, the great prince of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, set out from Moscow to Volok Lamsky for his hunt; and he returned to Moscow on St Demetrius’s Day.

On the Consecration of the Church of the Entry
on Sretenskaya Street in Moscow

That same autumn, on November 21, a Sunday, the church of the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos into the Temple on Sretenskaya Street in Moscow was consecrated by His Eminence Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus.

The Miracle of Saint Alexis the Wonderworker concerning a Paralytic

In the same month of November, on the 28th day, a Sunday, in the city of Moscow, in the monastery of the holy Archangel Michael, at the tomb of Saint Alexis the Wonderworker, through his prayers God, glorifying His servant, had mercy and healed a paralyzed man named Vasily, who had no use of his arms and legs and unexpectedly recovered. This miracle God revealed through His servant Saint Alexis in the days of the pious and Christ-loving great prince and sovereign of all Rus, Vasily Ivanovich, and of the most holy Varlaam, Metropolitan of all Rus, and of Jonah, archimandrite of that most honorable monastery.

The Miracle concerning a Woman

That same winter, on the 12th day of February, on the memory of our father among the saints Alexis, Metropolitan of all Rus and Wonderworker, through the prayers of the Most Pure Queen, the Virgin Theotokos Mary, in the church of her honorable Entry, in the newly built church beyond the Market, which Vasily Ivanovich, by the grace of God sovereign of all Rus, built during his reign, God had mercy during a moleben to His Most Pure Mother and healed Ivan Shiryaev’s wife, named Elena: she had a withered arm and no use of her leg, and she became well at the first hour of the day.

The Miracle of Saint Alexis concerning a Blind Man

And on that same day before the liturgy, at the tomb of Saint Alexis, Metropolitan of all Rus and Wonderworker, through his holy prayers God had mercy and forgave a poor blind wanderer named Ivan, granting him to see everything, and at once his eyes were healed. The pious great prince Vasily Ivanovich, together with his father Metropolitan Varlaam, seeing these miracles, gave thanks and glorified God and His Most Pure Mother and the holy hierarch Alexis the Wonderworker.
