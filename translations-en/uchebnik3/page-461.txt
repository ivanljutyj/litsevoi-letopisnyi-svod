And on Tuesday all the boyars gathered at the metropolitan’s, and for those who were in disgrace he sent the metropolitan, and together with the metropolitan all the boyars gathered at the grand prince’s, and they came out from the grand prince joyful.

And on the 17th day of December, on Friday, the metropolitan and all the boyars were at the grand prince’s; and the grand prince said before all the boyars to the metropolitan: “By the mercy of God and of His Most Pure Queen, the Mother of God, and by the prayers and mercy of the great wonderworkers Peter and Alexis, Jonah and Sergius the wonderworker, and of all the holy Russian wonderworkers, I have set my hope on them; and having received blessing from you, my father, I have decided to marry; and by your blessing I have considered and wish to marry where God, His Most Pure Mother, and the wonderworkers of the Russian land are pleased. And I thought to marry in other kingdoms, at a king’s or some tsar’s, to take a bride; but I, father, have abandoned that thought and do not wish to marry in other states, because after the death of my father, Sovereign Grand Prince Vasily, and of my mother, I remained young. And if I bring myself a wife from another state, and if our customs differ, then there will be vanity between us; and I, father, have thought it over and decided to marry in my own state, on whom God is pleased, according to your blessing.” And the metropolitan and the boyars wept for joy, seeing that a sovereign so young in years, consulting no one except God’s providence, wished to fulfill such a good intention. And the metropolitan said to him: “God, the Most Pure Mother, and all the wonderworkers bless you, tsar and sovereign, to accomplish such a great and good intention; and I, a sinner, bless you to marry where, sovereign, you intend according to God’s ordinance; and may God’s mercy abide on you, sovereign, and our blessing, though we are sinners, and may God accomplish your thought, fulfilled for a good deed!” And the boyars also spoke to the sovereign and praised his good counsel for a good deed.

And thereupon the grand prince began to speak to his father in God, Metropolitan Macarius, and to his boyars: “By your blessing, my father the metropolitan, and by your boyar counsel, before my marriage I wish to seek the order of my former ancestors: as our ancestors, tsars and grand princes, and our kinsman Grand Prince Vladimir Vsevolodovich Monomakh, sat for tsardom, for the grand princedom, so I too wish to perform that order and to sit for tsardom, for the grand princedom. And you, lord, my father Metropolitan Macarius, bless me to accomplish that deed.” And Metropolitan Macarius, hearing this from the sovereign grand prince, blessed him to seek the order of his former ancestors, and blessed him to sit for tsardom, for the grand princedom; and the boyars, hearing that, rejoiced—
