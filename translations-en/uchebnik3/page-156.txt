And the boyars went with them and petitioned the brothers of the grand prince.

The brothers of the grand prince—Prince Yuri, Prince Andrei, Prince Boris, and Prince Mikhailo Andreevich with his son—and their boyars petitioned the grand prince for them.

The grand prince accepted their entreaty for the sake of his brothers and his boyars, took pity on them, and for their sake showed them favor, and ordered the designated monk Feofil, the posadniks, the tysyatskys, and the others to come into his presence.

They came to him and bowed to the ground, entreating and begging mercy for their offense—that they had senselessly opposed him—that he might show them his great mercy, who had sinned before him; that the sovereign might show favor and take pity on them, turn back his anger, not because of their petition but because he showed his own compassion toward those who had sinned, and might no longer order them to be executed, plundered, burned, and taken captive.

The merciful autocrat, the grand prince, took pity on them, showed them his mercy, accepted their petition, and assuaged his anger. And immediately he ordered that burning and taking captives cease, and ordered the captives who were there to be released, and those who had been sent away and led away to be returned as well. And they petitioned the grand prince with sixteen thousand silver Novgorod rubles, and this was apart from the grand prince’s brothers and other princes, boyars, voivodes, and all who pleaded for them. And their whole land was laid waste and burned as far as the sea. Those who marched against them were not only those who were with the grand prince and with his brothers, but foot troops from all their lands; and the Pskov land fought them on its own: they had not had such wars since their land had existed.

On the Pskovians

As for the grand prince sending Savastian Kushelev against the Pskovians, he met them beyond Porkhov, while they were going from their town of Dubkov to Porkhov, having taken six cannon from it.

Savastian told them of the grand prince’s health and of the victory over the Novgorodians, and that the grand prince had ordered them to go quickly to Novgorod.

The Pskovians released Savastian from Porkhov to go to the grand prince, and with him their envoys Kuzma Sysoev and Stefan Afanasev Vinkov.
