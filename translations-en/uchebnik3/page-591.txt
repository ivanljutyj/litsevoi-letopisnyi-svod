And he petitioned the metropolitan, saying that he petitioned without instruction, for the sake of the Christian law and faith, asking the metropolitan to show favor and to petition the tsar and grand prince that the sovereign not shed Christian blood and that he make peace with the king. And he was with the metropolitan three times, and each time petitioned for the same. And the metropolitan told him that from the beginning they ask God’s mercy for humility and peace, and remind pious tsars and exhort them to piety and meekness: “But now, you say, you speak on your own and not by instruction; and that is not how one begins a matter: if there should be a sending from the king to the tsar and grand prince or to us, then we too will gladly petition in the Holy Spirit our son and lord, the tsar and grand prince, that he not shed Christian blood and that he desire peace with the king, as befits his tsarist power.”

And the tsar and grand prince, having honored the king’s envoy and shown him sufficient favor, sent him back to the king.

Concerning the Envoys from Ismayil

In the same year, on the first day of February, envoys came to the tsar and grand prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus’: from Ismayil, prince of the Nogais, the envoy Bayterek; from Kasai-Murza, his envoy Ishekey; from Aryslan, his envoy Bulak; and from other murzas. Ismayil also sent the tsar and grand prince’s envoys, the serving Tatars Syundyuk Tululusupov and his companions, who had been with Prince Yusuf and his children.

And the envoys of Ismayil and the other murzas spoke to the tsar and grand prince on behalf of the prince and the murzas, congratulated him on the Astrakhan tsardom, and petitioned him because the tsar and grand prince, at Ismayil’s request, had installed Derbysh in Astrakhan.

And Ismayil and the other murzas, by command of the tsar and grand prince, killed Prince Yusuf and slew many murzas, and Ismayil became prince among the Nogais, and Kasai tekhovat, and Oraslan nuradin; and all the remaining children of Yusuf and his nephews they drove out, and the whole Nogai Horde looks to them.

And the prince and murzas wish to be at one with the tsar and grand prince against all enemies, and to remain steadfastly with the tsar and grand prince until the end of their lives; and they asked that the tsar and grand prince show them favor and order free trade to be granted in Moscow, Kazan, and Astrakhan. And Syundyuk Tululusupov told the tsar and grand prince that he and his companions had been captives with Prince Yusuf, and how Ismayil and Kasai with Uruslan came against Prince Yusuf and his children.
