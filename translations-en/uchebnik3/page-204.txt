On November 29 Prince Boris Vasilyevich came before Novgorod, on the third day after his brother the grand prince, and the grand prince ordered him to take position in Krechnevo, a village of the archbishop, downstream along the Volkhov.

On November 30, Sunday, the grand prince ordered all the commanders to send half their men for provisions and keep the other half with them; and their term for collecting provisions was ten days, and on the eleventh day, Thursday after St Nicholas’s Day, all were to be before the city, wherever anyone might be.

That same day the grand prince sent Sevastyan Kuleshev to meet his Pskov viceroy Prince Vasily and to meet the Pskov host, to tell them to go without delay, with cannons and all supplies, according to the first order; and Sevastyan met them at the village of Soltsa on the Sheloni.

On the fourth day of December, a Monday, the Archbishop of Novgorod Feofil came to the grand prince in Paozerye at the Church of the Trinity, and with him the posadniks: Yakov Korob, Fefilat Zakharyin, Luka and Yakov Fedorov, and Luka Isakov; and from the well-to-do: Alexander Klimentyev and Filip Kilsky, Grigory Kiprian
