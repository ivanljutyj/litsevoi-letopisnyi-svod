For even before that, the sons of Mengli Giray—the tsareviches—had come with an army against the borderlands of the Grand Prince, likewise at the Queen’s instigation, and others too had broken many oaths. And the king began to violate the treaty charters with the Grand Prince and his cross-kissing oath.

And he ordered his lords in Vilna to seize the Grand Prince’s sister, Grand Princess Elena, wife of King Alexander, and to keep her under guard beyond Vilna at Bereshty. And Queen Elena died in that confinement, God knows for what reason.

And the Grand Prince, concerning those violations and concerning his sister Queen Elena, sent his ambassadors and couriers to King Sigismund repeatedly, but the king did nothing to correct his violations, and concerning the Queen, Grand Princess Elena, he gave no answer either.

On the first campaign of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich to Smolensk

And Vasily Ivanovich, Grand Prince of all Rus, not wishing to endure the many deeds of King Sigismund, sent to him and renounced the cross-kissing oath. And the Grand Prince himself with his brothers set out with an army against his land. And he left Moscow on the 19th day of December, on a Sunday.

And he came to the Lithuanian land, to the city of Smolensk, in January, and brought many sorrows and losses to the city of Smolensk, and having taken many captive in the Lithuanian land, returned.

And he returned to Moscow in March, on the third week of Lent.

On the sending to Tsargrad

That same winter the Grand Prince sent to Tsargrad to the Turkish Sultan Selim-Shah-Khandiker his man Mikhail, son of Ivashov, with a letter of love.

On the death of Princess Evdokia

That same winter, in February, the blessed Grand Princess Evdokia, wife of Tsarevich Peter, died, on Tuesday in Theodore’s Week, and her body was laid in the Church of the Ascension in Moscow, in the Kremlin.

On the death of Prince Fyodor of Volotsk

That same spring, in May, the blessed Prince Fyodor Borisovich of Volotsk died, and his body was laid in Joseph’s Hermitage, in the monastery.
