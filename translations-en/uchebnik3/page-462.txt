On the Crowning of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus as Tsar

In that same winter, on the 16th day of January, on the commemoration of the Veneration of the Precious Chains of the Holy Apostle Peter, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus came out into his timber Dining Chamber and summoned all his boyars into the chamber, and he ordered his voivodes, princelings, and all his officials to assemble before the Dining Chamber in the vestibule in festive golden garments.

And before this he had sent to the Metropolitan and ordered that it be done in the church thus: in the middle of the church set up an adorned analogion with a cloth, on which to place upon a golden platter the holy and life-giving cross, the holy barmas, and the tsar’s cap; and he ordered a tsar’s place to be prepared for him in the middle of the church, where he was to be seated for the tsardom and great principality.

And in the middle of the church they set up a place for the Grand Prince, such as is used when hierarchs are installed, and on it two chairs adorned with golden silk fabrics and provided with cushions, and at its foot they spread velvets and damasks.

And by the blessing of Metropolitan Macarius, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich of All Rus took with his own hands the holy and life-giving cross and his royal regalia upon a golden platter, and having covered it with a golden cloth bearing a cross studded with pearls and precious stones, he gave it to his spiritual father, Archpriest Fyodor of the Annunciation; and he took the platter with fear and trembling and set it upon his own head. And he sent him with the holy life-giving cross, the holy barmas, and the tsar’s crown to the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God, and with him he sent his equerry Prince Mikhail Vasilyevich Glinsky, his two treasurers, Ivan Ivanovich Tretyakov and Fyodor Ivanovich Sukin, and two clerks, Yakov and Vasily Grigoryev, sons of Zakharyin.

And after that, about half an hour later, the Grand Prince came out of the timber Dining Chamber and went in his royal order to the Cathedral Church of the Dormition; and before him walked his confessor, the Annunciation Archpriest, with the cross and holy water, sprinkling crosswise as far as the cathedral church; and behind the Grand Prince walked his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and the boyars, princelings, and boyar sons.

And when the Grand Prince entered the church, the choir clerks began to sing the Many Years to the Grand Prince, and the Grand Prince began to walk about the church praying and venerating the holy icons and the relics of the great wonderworkers.
