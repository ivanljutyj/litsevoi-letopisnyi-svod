On the Sending from Astrakhan

In that same year, in April on the 13th day, Tsarevich Yantimer, son of Tsar Derbysh-Aley, sent from Astrakhan his man Tinbulat with a letter. And in the letter he wrote that Yamgurchey-Tsar had come to Astrakhan, together with the sons of Prince Isuf, Yunus-Murza and Aley-Murza, and Ak-Murza, and all those murzas who had been driven out of the Nogais, and Kazy-Murza; and the Crimean tsar had sent with them Shigay-Bogatyr Antulov, and with him Crimeans and janissaries.

And they attacked the city; and Tsar Derbysh and all the Astrakhanis, having set the ordnance in order on the city and prepared the tsar and grand prince's Cossacks with arquebuses, fought them, and beat many of them at the city with cannons and arquebuses, and drove them off.

And Tsar Derbysh-Aley sent his son, Tsarevich Yantimer, after them. And he pursued them, and from the pursuit sent to the sovereign tsar and grand prince with joyful news his man Tinbulat, that his foe Yamgurchey-Tsar had come, and by God's mercy and by the fortune of the tsar and grand prince, the city of Astrakhan and its people, Astrakhanis and Russians, God be thanked, were safe and well, and that the tsar and sovereign should be informed of it.

And he also wrote that from many hordes many had come into the tsar and grand prince's kingdom of Astrakhan, envoys and merchants, and that the sovereign tsar and grand prince should be informed of it.

And in April the tsar and grand prince favored Tsarevich Yantimer's man and dismissed him. And to the tsarevich he wrote with great favor, and to Tsar Derbysh and to all the land he wished to send an envoy with favor in the spring.
