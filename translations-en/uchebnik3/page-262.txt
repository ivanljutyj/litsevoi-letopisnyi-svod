And after them came the Smolensk voivode of the Grand Prince of Lithuania Alexander, Lord Yuri Glebovich, and Prince Semyon Ivanovich of Mozhaisk to the towns of Serpeysk and Meshchevsk with a large army, so that the townspeople could not resist them and surrendered their towns.

And having learned of this, Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent against them his nephew on his sister’s side, Prince Fyodor Ivanovich of Ryazan, and his voivodes Prince Mikhail Ivanovich Kolyshka and Prince Alexander Vasilyevich Obolensky, and other voivodes with a large army, and Inka Izmailov, voivode of the Grand Prince of Ryazan Ivan Vasilyevich, came with an army.

The Smolensk voivode, Lord Yuri Glebovich, and Prince Semyon Ivanovich of Mozhaisk, having learned of the army of the Grand Prince coming against them, left princes and many lords under siege in the towns, but they themselves were frightened and fled to Smolensk.

And the army of Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich came to the town of Meshchevsk; the townspeople, frightened, could not resist and opened the town; and they captured the Lithuanian garrison of Grand Prince Alexander in the town, the Smolensk okolnichy Krivets and many other princes and lords, Lithuanians and Smolensk people, and administered to the common black land people the oath of allegiance to the Grand Prince.

And from there the army of the Grand Prince went to the town of Serpeysk; they resolved to fight bravely and did not want to surrender the town.

The voivodes of the Grand Prince ordered the soldiers to attack the town courageously with cannons and handguns, and having attacked the town, they took it by force, and captured in the town Ivan Fyodorov, son of Plyuskov, and many other princes and lords, Lithuanians and Smolensk people belonging to the court of Grand Prince Alexander of Lithuania; and having plundered it, they burned the town, and administered the oath to the local people.

And departing from there, they took and burned the town of Opakov, and administered the oath to the local and common black people.

And so they returned, and brought to Moscow the Lithuanians and Smolensk people who had been under siege, and the notable people of the town; and the Grand Prince sent them into confinement in his towns.

On the Capture of the City of Vyazma

And at the same time Grand Prince Ivan Vasilyevich sent his voivodes with an army to the town of Vyazma, led by Prince Daniil Vasilyevich Shchenya; and they took the town of Vyazma and administered the oath, and brought the Vyazma princes and lords to Moscow; and the Grand Prince granted them their own patrimony, Vyazma, and ordered them to serve him.
