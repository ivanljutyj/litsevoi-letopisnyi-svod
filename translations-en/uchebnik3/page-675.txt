On the Arrival of Vishnevetsky from the Don

In the same month Vishnevetsky came from the Don, and with him the Circassians sent Ichuruk-murza, a Circassian. All the Circassians petitioned that the sovereign grant them, give them his voivode in Cherkassy, and order them all to be baptized.

Consecration of New Churches

In the month of October, on the first day, the church chapels were consecrated in the New City by the Frolovsky Bridge, which were built to proclaim God’s miracles concerning the capture of Kazan, in memory of the days on which, with God’s help, the Orthodox tsar gained victories over the infidels: the Church of the Life-Giving Trinity, the Entry into Jerusalem, Nicholas of Velikoretskoye, Cyprian and Justina, Varlaam of Khutyn, Alexander of Svir, and Gregory of Greater Armenia. And at the consecration were the tsar and grand prince, his tsaritsa and the tsareviches Ivan and Feodor Ivanovich, and his brother Prince Yuri Vasilyevich, and the Kazan tsars Alexander and Simeon, and many boyars; and Macarius, Metropolitan of All Rus, consecrated with the whole sacred council. And the great Church of the Intercession in the middle was not ready by that time.

Entry of the Grand Prince into Mozhaisk

In the same month the tsar and grand prince went to Mozhaisk to Nicholas the Wonderworker and to the monasteries to pray, and to refresh himself in the villages, and with him were his tsaritsa and his children, the tsareviches Ivan and Feodor Ivanovich.

Message from Yuryev from the Voivodes

In the same month the boyar Prince Andrei Ivanovich Rostovsky with comrades wrote from Yuryev to the tsar and grand prince that the Livonian Master had gathered his people and hired overseas Germans, and, without waiting for the term which the sovereign had granted him, a month before the first day of November, had come into the sovereign’s land in the Yuryev district, to the Sangat manor, by war.

The tsar and grand prince sent his boyar Alexei Danilovich Basmanov to Pskov to the boyar and voivode Prince Yuri Ivanovich Temkin, and ordered him to release from Pskov the voivodes Zakhar Pleshcheyev and Grigory Nagoy, and from Krasny Gorodok and Vyshgorod Zamyatya Saburov and Alexei Skryabin, and ordered them to be ready with men and to set out against the raiding parties.
