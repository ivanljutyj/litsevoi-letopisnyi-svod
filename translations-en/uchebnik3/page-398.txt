And then they gave communion to the great sovereign Vasily Ivanovich of the Holy Mysteries, the life-giving Body and Blood of Christ our God.

And then his face was enlightened like light, and at the same time his soul departed in peace to God; and Shigona, standing near him, saw his spirit depart like a little wisp of smoke. There was then weeping and wailing among all, and mighty groans from the great to the simple, even throughout all the land. His face was enlightened like light and became white as snow; after his death, because of the wound, the foul stench ceased, and that chamber was filled with fragrance. The great prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus' departed in the monastic rank and was named Varlaam, in the year 7042 from Adam (1542 from Christ), on the 4th day of December, on the commemoration of the Great Martyr Barbara, from Wednesday to Thursday at the tenth hour of the night, on the eve of Barbara's day.

And that same night they clothed him in the full monastic habit; and Metropolitan Daniel himself took cotton wool and poured a little water on it, and wiped him from the waist.

Then there was weeping and inconsolable wailing among all the people, and amid the great outcry it could not be heard what they said to one another. Moreover, the Grand Princess did not yet know of the death of the great prince; the boyars restrained the people from weeping so that it would not be heard by the Grand Princess in her chambers.

Then Metropolitan Daniel took the brothers of the great prince, Prince Yuri and Prince Andrei, into the front room and brought them to kiss the cross, that they should serve the great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' as they had his father and grandfather, and should well promote every matter concerning him and all his realm without any cunning or guile; likewise they should serve his mother, the Grand Princess Elena; and they were to live in their own appanages, and stand truly by that on which they had kissed the cross to the great prince Vasily Ivanovich of all Rus' and by the agreements between them and the great prince Vasily; and they were not to covet the realm under the great prince, nor call people away from the great prince to themselves, and against the enemies of the great prince and their own, the Latin and Muslim faiths, they should stand together firmly as one.

And the boyars and boyars' sons, and princes, and nobles were likewise brought to kiss the cross, that they should wish good to the great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus' and to his mother, the Grand Princess Elena, and should truly wish good to all the land in all things, and against the enemies of the great prince and of all the land, of the Latin and Muslim faiths, stand together as one, and not seek another sovereign besides the great prince Ivan Vasilyevich.
