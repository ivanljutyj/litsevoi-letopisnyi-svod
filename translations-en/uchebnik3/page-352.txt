On the Coming of the Crimean Tsareviches to the Oka River and the Victory over Islam. In the year 7036 from Adam (1536 from Christ)

On the 5th day of September, Islam, Crimean Tsarevich, and Yusup Tsarevich, son of Yepancha, and two Tsareviches, sons of Akhmat the Lame, and many Mirzas came secretly to the bank of the Oka River, wishing to cross the river.

And the Grand Prince himself went out against them and took position in Kolomenskoye.

At that time the voivodes were on the bank with few men: Prince Vasily Semenovich Odoyevsky, Prince Ivan Ivanovich Shchetina, Prince Fyodor Vasilyevich Lopata, and Prince Ivan Fyodorovich Ovchina, and they stood on the bank against Islam; and Prince Fyodor Mikhailovich Mstislavsky arrived at the bank in time.

And they began to fight fiercely with Islam on the bank, and they did not let Islam cross the river. And Islam immediately went away from there, while the Grand Prince's boyar sons crossed the river and killed many Tatars.

And they captured Islam's favorite, Yanglych-Mirza, a great man; and Tsarevich Islam quickly went to the Crimea.

On the Wedding

That same autumn the Grand Prince showed favor to Prince Ivan Penkov and gave him his sister-in-law Marya in marriage.
