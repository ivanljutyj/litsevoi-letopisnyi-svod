At that same time, out of grief after that fire, the Novgorodians seized many people without cause, saying: “You secretly set fires for your own profit and have brought such misfortunes and calamities!” And they burned many Christians in the fire, and threw others from the bridge into the Volkhov, and stoned others to death. God alone knows the secret of human hearts, whether what happened was just—that they accepted such a cruel death—since such calamities come for our sins, so that people might turn from their evil deeds and repent and live according to God’s will, that they might receive good in the present and in the future.

In the year 6951 from Adam (1451 from Christ), on the 15th day of September.

About Isidore, and How He Fled from Moscow to Rome

Metropolitan Isidore, darkened by the darkness of his unbelief, did not wish to renounce union and agreement with Latinism, did not submit to confinement in the Chudov Monastery, and secretly at night departed, taking flight with his disciples, the monks Gregory and Athanasius; and they fled from Moscow to Tver, and from Tver to Lithuania, and then to Rome, to their pope, taught and guided by the devil to their own destruction.

But the God-wise and most prudent Grand Prince Vasily Vasilyevich did not send to bring him back, and did not wish to detain him, as a foolish, insane, and God-abhorrent man, so as not to touch his sins and so that no harm would come to him.

And so Metropolitan Isidore went to Rome to Pope Eugene, and afterward from afar Isidore did much, like the evil and destructive serpent the devil, cruelly persecuting the holy Church of flourishing piety in the Russian realm, turning Christianity into Latinism and uniting and reconciling them. From such a cunning enemy the Lord God and the Most Pure Mother of God saved the holy Church in Rus, safe and untroubled, through the exposure of the most wise and God-instructed great sovereign Vasily Vasilyevich, who, in accordance with his piety, was a flourishing great and most wise tsar of all Rus, to whom the Lord God entrusted to understand wisely, to comprehend all things, and to fulfill God’s will and observe all His commandments.

Rule 1 of the Holy Seventh Ecumenical Council, Which Assembled for the Second Time in Nicaea

Having received the priestly rank by divine revelation and by fulfilling the directives of the sealed canons, which we joyfully accept and sing with David, the father of God, to the Master God: “In the way of Thy testimonies I have delighted, as in all riches.” And again: “Thou hast commanded righteousness, Thy testimonies are forever; give me understanding, and I shall live.” And the prophetic voice commands us to preserve God’s instructions for all ages and to live according to them, since they are unshakable and indestructible.
