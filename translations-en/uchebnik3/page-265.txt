On the Moat

In the same year, by command of the Grand Prince, a moat was dug from the Borovitskaya Tower to the Moscow River.

On the Wooden Wall in Moscow

In the same year they built a wooden wall from the Nikolskaya Tower to the secret passage on the Neglinnaya.

On the Coming of the Tatars to the Ryazan Lands

In the same year Tatars, Horde Cossacks, came secretly to the Ryazan lands, seized three villages, and soon went back.

On the Fire from Lightning

In the same summer, on the sixteenth day of July, Tuesday, at the first hour of the day, thunder with lightning set fire to the top of the great dome, the boards beneath the iron, of the cathedral Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure Mother of God in Moscow; and inside the church a little burned at the royal doors, half of the veil burned on the ambo, and two wooden blocks were smashed beneath the ambo; but the top was soon extinguished, and by God’s mercy no harm was done to the church. On the same day, in Vladimir also, a storm broke the cross at the Church of the Annunciation of the Most Pure.

On the Great Fire in Moscow

In the same month of July, on the twenty-eighth day, Sunday, at the seventh hour of the day, the Church of St Nicholas on the Sand caught fire from a candle, and at that hour a strong storm arose, and the fire was thrown to the other side of the Moscow River, to the Church of All Saints, and from there beyond the Neglinnaya to the stone Church of St George; and at that hour the storm arose, and it began to burn in many places, and the suburb beyond the Neglinnaya burned out from the Church of the Holy Spirit to Chertolie and to the Church of Boris and Gleb on the Arbat, and to Petrovskaya Slobodka; and beyond the Moscow River it burned from the Church of St Sophia to Joachim and Anna. And from Zarechye the fire entered the city, and the court of the Grand Prince and Grand Princess caught fire, and from there the granaries on Podol caught fire, and the new court of the Grand Prince behind the Church of Archangel Michael burned down, and the metropolitan’s court burned out, and at the Church of the Dormition of the Most Pure the altar was scorched beneath the German iron, and in the city all the huts burned out where they had not managed to put up buildings after the spring fire; and the Church of John the Forerunner at the Borovitsky Gate burned and collapsed, and in the church the priest burned, and beneath the church the treasury of the Grand Princess burned out; and the Borovitskaya Tower burned out, and along the city wall all the roofing burned out, and the new wooden wall at the Nikolsky Gate. And from the city the market caught fire, and from there the suburb along the Moscow River burned out as far as Zachatyevskaya
