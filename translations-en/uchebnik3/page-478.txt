In the same year tidings came to the great prince concerning the Crimean tsar, that he intended to be on the border of his lands on the 20th day of July, a Sunday. The tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus departed from Moscow for Kolomna, and from there to Ryazan, and came to Moscow on the 23rd day of August, a Thursday.

In the same month died Tsarevna Anna, daughter of the tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, aged one year less twenty-one days; and she was laid in the New Maiden Monastery.

In the year 7059 from Adam (the year 1559 from Christ)

In the month of September, by the command of the pious and Christ-loving tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilyevich, autocrat of all Rus, with great desire and great faith, the great dome of the cathedral church of our Most Pure Lady, Mother of God and Ever-Virgin Mary, of Her Honorable and Glorious Dormition in the reigning city of Moscow, was gilded, in the 18th year of his sovereignty and the 4th year of his tsardom.

In the same autumn, on the 26th day of October, by the command of the pious and Christ-loving tsar and great prince Ivan Vasilyevich of all Rus, a great bell for the call to worship was cast, and on the 6th day of January it was placed by the Archangel Cathedral behind the altar on a wooden bell tower.

In the same winter, on the 26th day of December, the Nogai murzas, Uraslan-murza and Otai-murza and Teylyak-murza, and the remaining murzas came with many people against the Meshchera lands and against Old Ryazan.

That same winter, on 26 December, the Nogai murzas, Uraslan-murza, Otai-murza, and Teylyak-murza, and other murzas came with many people against the Meshchera lands and against Old Ryazan.

And the voevodes of the tsar and great prince from Ryazan, Prince Pyotr Mikhailovich Shchenyatev and Prince Alexander Vorotynsky, and from St Nicholas of Zaraysk Prince Dmitry Ivanovich Punkov, joined together, and they came against the Nogai people in many places, and everywhere by God's mercy they defeated them, and they captured Teylyak-murza and captured many alive. And the voevodes from Yelatma, Prince Konstantin Ivanovich Kurlyatev and Semyon Sheremetev and Stepan Sidorov, likewise defeated the Nogais in many places.

And the Ryazan and Meshchera voevodes came together and went as far as the Shatsk Gates, and everywhere by God's mercy they defeated them; and there were great snows and frosts, and many froze to death.

And the grand prince's various Cossacks defeated those who lagged behind in many places, as far as the Volga. And Araslan-murza and Otai came to the Nogai, and with them in all there were about fifty foot Nogais, and all the rest by God's mercy perished miserably.
