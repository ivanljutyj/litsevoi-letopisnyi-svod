On the consecration of the Church of the Annunciation in Novgorod the Great

In the same year, in Novgorod the Great, the stone Church of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Mother of God was consecrated in Euthymius's Annunciation Monastery, and Archbishop Macarius himself consecrated it on the 6th day of October.

On the consecration of the Church of the Holy Trinity at Kolmovo

In the same year, the stone Church of the Life-giving Trinity was consecrated in the monastery at Kolmovo, by the blessing of Archbishop Macarius.

On the consecration of the Church of Cosmas and Damian on the Gzen

That same autumn, the wooden Church of the Holy Unmercenaries and Wonderworkers Cosmas and Damian was consecrated in Kuznetsy on the Gzen, on the 16th day of October.

On the pestilence in Novgorod the Great and on the building and consecration of the Church of Saint Mark

That same autumn, in October, the following happened in Novgorod the Great: a disease began to appear among the people; it began with a boil, and from this many people began to die, and there was a great pestilence.

By God's providence, the most reverend Archbishop Macarius began to instruct the abbots and priests and all the people in repentance, and he ordered a wooden church to be built, and they joyfully accepted the good counsel of the hierarch.

On the 8th day of the month of November, on the Synaxis of the Archangel Michael, the God-loving Archbishop Macarius himself entered the cathedral church of Holy Sophia with the abbots and all the priests of the whole city; and he began to perform a prayer service from the second hour of the day, and he ordered all the city people to fell timber and hew the church.

And the people joyfully began: some to fell timber, others to hew, and still others to erect the church.

And the priests of the whole city, of the six cathedral churches, by the blessing of Archbishop Macarius, placed the Tetraevangelion in the holy sanctuary upon the altar table and adorned it with precious cloth and silver, as is fitting upon the altar table; and the Christ-named people adorned the church with a Deesis and holy icons, and venerable crosses, and sacred books. And it was wonderful to behold. And the most reverend archbishop performed the prayer service even until evening.
