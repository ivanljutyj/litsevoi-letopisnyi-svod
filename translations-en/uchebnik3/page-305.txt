On the Church of the Annunciation

Then the faithful Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus, with great faith and desire, ordered the church on his court—of the All-Honorable Queen, the Virgin, the Most Holy Mother of God Mary, the Most High, ineffable Mountain of God, higher than the heavens—of Her Honorable and Glorious Annunciation, to be painted in gold; he also ordered all the church icons to be adorned and covered with silver, gold, and pearls—the Deesis, the feast icons, and the prophets; he also ordered the church domes to be covered and gilded. The master was Feodosii, son of Dionysius, with his brothers.

On the Fire

In that same spring, on May 21, a Sunday, an hour before evening, fire broke out in the Great Posad; the posad and the marketplace burned from Pan's Court to the Neglinnaya River, to the Cannon House, and nearly reached the Church of the Presentation.

And in the same month, on the 22nd, a Monday, Chertolie burned, and the Church of the Annunciation at Koz'ya Boroda, and that of St Alexius, and Semchinskoye as far as Vspolye.

On Sending the Voivodes to Fight in the Lithuanian Land

In that same spring Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus sent his voivodes, Yakov Zakharin and his other voivodes, with a large army to conquer the Lithuanian lands because the king had violated his obligations; and from Veliky Novgorod the Grand Prince ordered his boyar and voivode and viceroy Prince Daniil Vasilievich to set out with an army to conquer the Lithuanian lands, and he commanded them to join forces in the Lithuanian land.

And the voivodes of Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus began to conquer and seize the Lithuanian land, to burn and cut down, and they came close to Vilna.

King Sigismund learned of the arrival of the Grand Prince's voivodes in his land and set out against the voivodes to Orsha, and from Orsha to Smolensk. And Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus ordered his voivodes to retreat to Bryansk in order to await the voivode Prince Vasily Daniilovich Kholmsky, sent by the Grand Prince from Moscow to Bryansk with an army, also against the king.

And King Sigismund sent from Smolensk to Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus news concerning the passage of his envoy, and Grand Prince Vasily Ivanovich of All Rus gave the king a charter for the envoy's passage.
