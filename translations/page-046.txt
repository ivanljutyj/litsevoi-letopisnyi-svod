...set into a stone tablet, as Josephus also wrote on the second leaf from the beginning of his books: that Seth's descendants, being pious men, foreseeing then the coming destruction and upheaval for mankind, made two pillars, one of stone and the other of brick, and wrote upon them the heavenly knowledge from their grandfather Seth, reasoning thus: if the human race perishes by water, then the stone pillar will remain, and what is written on it will be a memorial for those who come after; and if it perishes by fire, then the brick one will remain, as it better endures fire, and what is written on it will be a memorial for the people who survive. And the stone pillar remained on the Syrian mountain, and stands even to this day, as Josephus relates.
