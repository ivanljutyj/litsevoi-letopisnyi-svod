And the Lord God took the man whom He had formed, and placed him in the paradise of delight to till it and keep it. And the Lord God commanded Adam, saying: Of every tree which is in paradise you may eat for food; but of the tree of knowing good and evil, you shall not eat of it; for in whatever day you eat of it, you shall die by death. And the Lord God said: It is not good for the man to be alone; let Us make him a helper corresponding to him. And God formed yet out of the earth all the beasts of the field and all the birds of heaven, and brought them to Adam to see what he would call them; and whatever Adam called a living soul, this was its name. And Adam gave names to all the cattle, and to all the birds of heaven, and to all the beasts of the earth.
