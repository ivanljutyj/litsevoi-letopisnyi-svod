...the sky is hidden. The earth, yielding good fruits, yields its fruits, and growing and multiplying, through winter's sprouting and rain's watering, makes good fruits, but does not completely require these helpers for existence. But then hailstorms and frosts often destroy the fruits of the earth. Still not much is saved, still not fully established. But now it is consolidated, and surrounded by the sea, and held by the waters. The sun in its good radiance emits light-beamed rays, and those who do not have it are made sorrowful. But as if in lower dwellings, they have no other rest. Further, as it goes behind the earth, it immediately casts shadow upon the inhabited world. Still, as the sky is closed to it, it happens. To the lunar circle and the dawn it shuts, and the light is covered. And the moon alone, solely, is perfectly a net... But then those that decrease, and then those that increase. Still, as much as it approaches the partition, so much to the creation is small. When it approaches the companion [conjunction], then from another path it hides the sky, overtaking its face, still in the day and after day, the same even if of one of the monthly courses, lying underneath, raised up, illuminated by the sun, being as vessels; and those faces of equal measure are not all equal, but star after star, in speed they surpass, and in glory, and in radiance, and in magnitude.
