And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the earth, and daughters were born unto them, that the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose. And the Lord God said: My Spirit shall not abide in these men forever, for they are flesh; yet their days shall be a hundred and twenty years. There were giants on the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men, who were of old, men of renown.
