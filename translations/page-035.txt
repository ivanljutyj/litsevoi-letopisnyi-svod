And Noah built an altar unto God, and took of all clean cattle, and offered burnt offerings upon the altar. And the Lord God smelled a sweet savor, and the Lord God said unto Noah: I will not again curse the ground any more for man's works; for the imagination of man's heart is diligently set on evil from his youth; neither will I again smite any more every living flesh, as I have done. All the days upon the earth, seedtime and harvest, winter and heat, summer and spring, day and night shall not cease—
