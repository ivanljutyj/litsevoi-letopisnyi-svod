And the Lord God took the man whom He had formed, and put him into the Paradise of delight, to till it and keep it. And the Lord God commanded Adam, saying: Of every tree that is in Paradise you may eat for food; but of the tree of knowing good and evil, you shall not eat of it; for in whatever day you eat of it, you shall surely die. And the Lord God said: It is not good for the man to be alone; let us make for him a helper comparable to him. And out of the earth God formed further all the beasts of the field and all the birds of the heaven, and brought them to Adam to see what he would call them; and whatever Adam called a living soul, that was its name. And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to all the birds of the heaven, and to all the beasts of the earth.
