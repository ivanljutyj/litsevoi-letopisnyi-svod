And Cainan begot Salah. And Salah begot Eber. And to Eber were born two sons: the name of one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.
|||
And Joktan begot Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, Obal, Abimael, Sheba, Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. And their dwelling was from Mesha, as you go toward Sephar, a mountain of the east. These were the sons of Shem, according to their families, according to their languages, in their lands, and according to their nations. These were the families of the sons of Noah, according to their generations, in their nations; and from these the nations were divided on the earth after the flood. And the whole earth was of one language and of one speech [illegible].
