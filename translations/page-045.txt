After Cronus, his son Ninus reigned for 52 years. He built a very great city in Syria, a three days' journey across, and named it Nineveh after his own name. From him was born Zoroaster, that is to say, the star-gazer, the famous Persian astronomer.
|||
After Ninus, Thuras reigned over Syria for 30 years, who named himself Ares after the name of the great star. To him they first set up an idol and worshipped him as a god, whom the Persians also worship. And Ares translates as the god of war. Thus they also honored other men as gods, naming them after the names of the major stars.
