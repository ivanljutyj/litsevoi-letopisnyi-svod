And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, on the first day of the first month, the water dried up from the face of the earth; and Noah uncovered the roof of the ark which he had made, and saw that the water had dried up from the face of the earth. And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry. The Lord God spoke to Noah, saying: Go out of the ark, you and your wife, and your sons, and your sons' wives with you. And every flesh, from cattle even to birds, and every creeping thing moving upon the earth with you; and be fruitful and multiply upon the earth.
