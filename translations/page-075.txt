More honorable than the earth I shall call the sun, for with its rays it illuminates the whole universe; yet neither shall I call it God, because when night arrives it is darkened by darkness. Nor again shall I call the moon or the stars God, since at times during the night their light too is darkened. But hear this, Terah my father, that I may proclaim to you the God who created all things: for He is the true God, who having crimsoned the heavens, and gilded the sun, and made bright the moon and with it the stars, also dried up the earth; and in the midst of many things He placed you yourself at the head, and has now sought me out in the confusion of my thoughts, even if it is heavy upon itself. And it came to pass, as Abraham was speaking such things to his father Terah in the courtyard of his house, Terah his father heard this and looked upon Abraham with heavy eyes, for Abraham's word was not pleasing to him; rather, he loved the delusion handed down to him by Nahor his father.
