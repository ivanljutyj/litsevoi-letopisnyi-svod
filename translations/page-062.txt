...and Dionysus [the patron of] drunkenness; Hermes, of theft; and Athena, of wisdom. And Zeus was the inventor of the creation of punishments; Asclepius, of healing; and Apollo, of music; Artemis, of hunting beasts; Hera, of clothing; Aristaeus, of honeycombs; Triptolemus, of wheat seed. Indeed, all these inventors of foul deeds, as well as the creators of crafts, men honored as gods, and set up idols to them, and worshipped as gods the inventors and teachers of such foul deeds to them.
