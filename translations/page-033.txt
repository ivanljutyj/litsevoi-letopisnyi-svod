And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, on the 1st day of the first month, the water dried up from the face of the earth; and Noah removed the covering of the ark which he had made, and saw that the water had dried up from the face of the earth. And in the 2nd month, on the 27th day of the month, the earth was dry. The Lord God spoke to Noah, saying: Go forth from the ark, you and your wife, and your sons and your sons' wives with you, and all flesh, from cattle to birds, and every creeping thing moving upon the earth with you; and be fruitful and multiply upon the earth.
