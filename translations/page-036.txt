And Cainan begat Sala; and Sala begat Eber. And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.
And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, Sheba, and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were the sons of Joktan. And their dwelling was from Mesha, even until one comes unto Sephar, a mount of the east. These are the sons of Shem, in their tribes, after their tongues, in their lands, and in their nations. These are the tribes of the sons of Noah, after their generations, after their tongues, in their lands, and in their nations. These are the tribes of the sons of Noah, after their generations, after their tongues: and from these were the islands of the nations dispersed on the earth after the flood.
