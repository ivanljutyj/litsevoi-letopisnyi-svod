This Serug was indeed the first to begin associating with idols, being the eighth descendant of righteous Noah. Honoring the ancient men as inventors of good things, and as mighty in battles, and as pioneers of wisdom, he made sculpted images of them. Many of those born after him, not understanding this and not perceiving Serug's intention, honored the soulless bodies as gods, and bowed down to idols made by human hands, and conceived of sacrificing each to his own god. The Egyptians became corrupted more than others: they honored blind figs [moles/shrews], and apes, and birds, and goats, and oxen, and dogs, and crocodiles. Above all, they honored a pied bull having many spots, having a mark on its tongue and on its tail, and on its forehead the likeness of a two-horned moon; and they rejoiced and celebrated as though a god had newly come among them, and fed it with abundant foods. As idolatrous delusion spread widely, the whole race fell away into utter delusion. The Hellenes deified foul men, naming them after the great stars and the elements: thus they called Hephaestus fire, and Demeter earth, and the watery expanses of the ocean they called Poseidon, who was a helmsman of ships; and Aphrodite lustful desire, and Ares wrath.
