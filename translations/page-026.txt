And Enoch pleased God. And Enoch lived after the birth of Methuselah two hundred years, and begat sons and daughters. And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years; and Enoch pleased God, and he was not found, for God translated him.
|||
And Methuselah lived an hundred and eighty-seven years, and begat Lamech. And Methuselah lived after the birth of Lamech eight hundred and two years, and begat sons and daughters. And all the days of Methuselah which he lived were nine hundred and sixty-nine years.
|||
And he died.
