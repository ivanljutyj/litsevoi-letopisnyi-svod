Abram gathered to depart, and to test the gods of his father, whether they could help; and Abram took fire and set fire to the temple where the idols of his father stood. And Haran, the brother of Abram, seeing this, went thither out of zeal, wishing to rescue the idols. And Haran himself burned there, and died before his father. For before this, a son did not die before his father, but a father before his son. And from this time, sons began to die before their fathers.

Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves, the daughters of Haran, sisters of Lot. The name of Abram's wife was Sarah, the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah. And Sarah was barren, not bearing children.
