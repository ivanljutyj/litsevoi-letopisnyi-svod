And Adam called his wife's name Life, because she is the mother of all living. And the Lord God made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins, and clothed them. And God said: Behold, Adam has become as one of us, to know good and evil; and now, lest at any time he stretch forth his hand, and take of the tree of life, and eat, and live forever. And the Lord God sent him forth from the paradise of delight, to till the earth from which he was taken. And He cast out Adam and caused him to dwell opposite the paradise of delight, and stationed the cherubim and the flaming weapon which turns every way to guard the way of the tree of life.
