After Cronus, his son Ninus reigned for 52 years. He built a very great city in Syria, a three days' journey across, and named it Nineveh after his own name. From him was born Zoroaster, that is to say, the star-observer, the famous Persian astrologer.

After Ninus, Phorus [Thuras] reigned over Syria for 30 years, who called himself Ares after the name of the great star. To him they first set up an idol and bowed down as to a god, whom the Persians also worship; and Ares is interpreted as the god of war. Thus they also honored other men as gods, calling them by the names of the major stars.
