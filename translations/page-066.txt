When I finished the services of the sacrifice of my father Terah to his gods of wood and stone, and of silver, copper and iron.
|||
Entering into the shrine for the service, I found Marumath by name, carved of stone, fallen flat at the feet of the iron god Nakhihan. And it came to pass when I saw this, my heart was troubled within me, and I thought in my mind that I could not return him to his place alone, I Abraham, because he was heavy, being made of a great stone. So I went and told my father, and he came down with me, and going again, both of us wanted to return him to his place. But his head fell off from him, while I was still holding his head. And it came to pass when my father saw that the head of Marumath had fallen off from him, he said to me: "Abraham!" And I said: "Here I am." And he said: "Bring me an axe from the small house."
