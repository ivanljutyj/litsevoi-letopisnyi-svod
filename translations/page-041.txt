These are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and sons were born to them after the flood. The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tiras, Tubal, and Meshech. And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. And the sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. From these the islands of the nations were divided in their lands, every one according to his language, and families, and in their nations.
