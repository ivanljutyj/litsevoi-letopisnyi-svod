ENGLISH:
And to Enos [Enoch] was born Irad; and Irad begat Mehujael; and Mehujael begat Methushael, and Methushael begat Lamech. And Lamech took unto himself two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah.
|||
Adah
|||
And Adah bore Jabal: he was the father of those who dwell in tents raising livestock. And his brother's name was Jubal: he was the one who introduced the psaltery and the gusli. And Zillah, she also bore Tubal-cain: and he was a hammerer, a smith of copper and iron. And the sister of Tubal-cain was [illegible].

NOTE:
This page depicts the genealogy of Cain's descendants down to Lamech, his two wives Adah and Zillah, and the births and crafts of their children—Jabal with livestock, Jubal playing music, and Tubal-cain forging metal (Genesis 4:18–22).
