ENGLISH:
And Nahor likewise began to make idols, just as his father Serug had done. Moreover, the devil strove greatly to deceive the Hebrew race, so that they would forget the grace of God and worship idols, which indeed came to pass. For Nahor, not understanding that his father Serug had made statues in honor of valiant men—this Nahor, not understanding, honored them as gods, and bowed down to them, and sold those idols throughout the whole Hebrew race. Likewise, passing Gentiles bought them, and having forgotten the grace of God, worshipped the idols. And the devil rejoiced again and again, having ensnared the deceived people.
