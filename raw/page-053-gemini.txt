ENGLISH:
13
|||
A multitude of people, having obeyed Nimrod's counsel, began to build the tower of Calneh, exceedingly great and very rich in height, almost reaching the circle of the moon, and a city around it; while being wrought, it was not completed, for vain was their labor.

NOTE:
Nimrod enthroned commands the people to build the Tower of Babel (the tower of Calneh) reaching up toward the moon in the sky.
