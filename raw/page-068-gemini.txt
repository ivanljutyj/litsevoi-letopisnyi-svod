ENGLISH:
For I shall not call the sun god, as that is a creation of the Heavenly One; it was made as a service for humankind. Nor shall I call the earth "queen," although the sun with its rays illuminates the entire world. But I shall not call even that god, for when night comes, darkness obscures it. Nor again shall I call the moon's light god, as it also in the night times is obscured. But hear, Terah, my father, let me tell you a story about the fire. For He is the true One, Who indeed created the heavens, and adorned the sun, and illuminated the moon, and with it the stars, and dried the land; and in the midst of the waters He placed you yourself to rot, without you seeking His name. After the confusion of my mind, if you are angry with me in your heart, it was not so...
To Abraham thus speaking, he fell to the ear of Terah his father. Hearing this, Terah his father looked upon Abraham with unkind eyes, because Abraham spoke to him without love. But Abraham had recognized the evil delusion handed down to him by Nahor, his father.

NOTE:
Abraham disputes the worship of idols with his father Terah and sets fire to the house of idols.
