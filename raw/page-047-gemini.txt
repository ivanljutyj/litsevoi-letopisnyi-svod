ENGLISH:
And Cainan begat Sala; and Sala begat Eber. And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; for in his days was the earth divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.
|||
And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, Sheba, and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were the sons of Joktan. And their dwelling was from Mesha, as thou goest unto Sephar, a mount of the east. These are the sons of Shem, after their families, after their tongues, in their lands, after their nations. These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, after their tongues, in their lands, and in their nations. These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, after their tongues; and by these were the islands of the nations divided in the earth after the flood. And the whole earth was of one lip, and one voice to all. And it came to pass, as they journeyed...

NOTE:
This page recounts the biblical genealogy of Shem's descendants through Eber, Peleg, and Joktan, and the unity of human speech after the Flood (Genesis 10:24–11:2), illustrated with a miniature depicting Eber with his family and descendants.
