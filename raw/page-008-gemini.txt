ENGLISH:
And Enos begat Cainan. And Cainan begat Mahalalel. And Mahalalel begat Methuselah. And Methuselah begat Lamech. And Lamech took unto himself two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah.

And Adah begat Jabal: he was the father of those who dwell in tents and breed cattle. And his brother's name was Jubal: he was the one who showed the organ and the harp. And Zillah, she also begat Tubal-cain: and he was a hammerer, a smith of brass and iron. And the sister of Tubal-cain was Naamah.

NOTE:
The page depicts the genealogy of the descendants of Enos and the children of Lamech, who founded animal husbandry, music, and metallurgy.
