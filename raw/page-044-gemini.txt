ENGLISH:
These are the generations of Shem. Shem was 100 years old when he begot Arphaxad, in the second year after the flood. And Shem lived after he begot Arphaxad 500 years, and begat sons and daughters, and died.
|||
And Arphaxad lived 135 years, and begot Cainan. And Arphaxad lived after he begot Cainan 330 (430) years, and begat sons and daughters, and died.

NOTE:
The life, death, and burial of the biblical patriarchs Shem and Arphaxad.
