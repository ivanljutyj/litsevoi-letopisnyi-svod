ENGLISH:
And Asshur went forth, and built the city of Nineveh, and the city of Rehoboth, and Calah, and Dasem, between Nineveh and Calah; this is the great city.
|||
And Meraim begat Ludim, and Ennom, and Lavaim, and Nephtalim, and Prosinim, and Khalasonim, from whence came forth Philistim and Caphephorim.

NOTE:
Biblical scenes from Genesis 10:11–14 showing Asshur founding Nineveh and neighboring cities (top) and Mizraim (Meraim) with his descendants (bottom).
