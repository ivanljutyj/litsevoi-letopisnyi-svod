SLAVONIC:
И жиⷮ рагавъ . лѣⷮ . рл҃в . и роди асероуха . и жиⷮ
рагавъ рожь асероуха . лѣⷮ . сз҃ . и роди ины и
дщерѧ, и оумре ჻

ENGLISH:
And Ragau lived 132 years and begot Aseruch. And after begetting Aseruch, Ragau lived 207 years, and begot others and daughters, and died.

NOTE:
Miniatures depicting the family and burial of the biblical patriarch Ragau (Reu) at the top, and the emergence of idolatry—honoring statues and worshiping beasts—in the days of Serug (Aseruch) at the bottom.
