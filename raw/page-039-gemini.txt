ENGLISH:
And Noah began to be a husbandman on the earth, and he planted a vineyard. And he drank of the wine, and was drunken, and slept, and he was uncovered in his house. And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and went out and told his two brethren.
|||
And Shem and Japheth took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and went backward.

NOTE:
The page depicts the biblical story of Noah planting a vineyard, becoming drunk, and his sons Shem and Japheth covering his nakedness.
