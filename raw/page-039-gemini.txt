SLAVONIC:
Видѣ сїа всесилныи гъ бъ . сниде видѣти
града и стлъпа иже създаша снове члчстїи .

ENGLISH:
The Almighty Lord God saw these things; He came down to see the city and the tower which the sons of men had built.

NOTE:
The destruction of the Tower of Babel beneath God Sabaoth and two angels blowing horns, with the builders tumbling down in the collapsing brickwork before a seated ruler (Nimrod).
