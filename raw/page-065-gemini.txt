ENGLISH:
And Terah began to do the same deed that he had seen from his father Nahor: and he bowed down to idols and offered sacrifices before them, calves and young bulls, and did everything pleasing to the devil. Having seen this, Abram entered into deep reflection, saying within himself: "These gods are wood, by which my father Terah is deceived. These gods have no soul in themselves: having eyes, they do not see; and having ears, they do not hear; and having hands, they do not touch; and having feet, they do not walk; having nostrils, they do not smell; and there is no voice in their mouths. Therefore I think in truth that my father Terah is deceived." Having thought these things, Abram—sitting over those carving the gods of his father Terah and the gods of his brother Nahor, when I was testing which God is in truth the mighty one—Abram, at the time of the falling of my lot...
