ENGLISH:
And Abram went, as the Lord had told him; and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he departed from Haran. And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all their possessions that they had gathered, and the souls whom they had acquired in Haran, and they set out to go to the land of Canaan.
|||
And Abram passed through all the land, to the place of Shechem, to the high oak. And the Canaanites were then living in that land.

NOTE:
Abram departing from Haran with his family and building an altar to the Lord in the land of Canaan.
