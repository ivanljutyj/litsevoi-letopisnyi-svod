ENGLISH:
And to Enoch was born Gaidad; and Gaidad begat Maleleel; and Maleleel begat Methuselah; and Methuselah begat Lamech. And Lamech took unto himself two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the second Zillah.
|||
[illegible]
|||
And Adah bare Jabal: he was the father of those who dwell in tents keeping cattle. And his brother's name was Jubal: he was the one who introduced the psaltery and the gusli. And Zillah, she also bare Tubal-cain, and he was a hammer-beater, a smith of copper and iron; and the sister of Tubal-cain was [Naamah].

NOTE:
Genealogy of Cain's descendants from Enoch to Lamech, Lamech with his two wives Adah and Zillah, and the births and crafts of their children Jabal, Jubal, and Tubal-cain (Genesis 4:18–22).
