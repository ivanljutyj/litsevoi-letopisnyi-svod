SLAVONIC:
И҆ жиⷡ рага́въ · лѣⷮ · р҃лв · и҆ родѝ а҆сероу́ха · и҆ жиⷡ
рага́въ ро́жⷣь а҆сероу́ха · лѣⷮ · с҃з · и҆ родѝ сн҃ы и҆
дще́рѧ , и҆ оу҆́мре :~

ENGLISH:
And Ragau lived 132 years and begot Serug. And after begetting Serug, Ragau lived 207 years, and begot sons and daughters, and died.

NOTE:
The upper miniature depicts the family and burial of the patriarch Ragau (Reu), while the lower miniature illustrates the introduction of idol and animal worship in the days of his son Serug.
