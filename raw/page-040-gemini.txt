ENGLISH:
And they covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces being turned away, they did not see their father's nakedness. And Noah awoke from his wine, and knew what his younger son had done to him. And he said: "Cursed be Ham; a servant shall he be to his brethren." And he said: "Blessed be the Lord God of Shem, and Ham shall be his servant. And may God enlarge Japheth, and may he dwell in the dwellings of Shem; and may Ham be a servant to them."
|||
And he lived after the flood 350 years. And all the days of Noah were 950 years, and he died.
