And Eber lived 130 years and begot Peleg. And Eber lived 210 years and begot sons and daughters, and died.

And Peleg lived 130 years and begot Ragau. And Peleg lived, having begotten Ragau, 204 years, and begot sons and daughters, and died.

NOTE:
Two two-part miniatures depicting the lives, descendants, and burials of the biblical patriarchs Eber (top) and Peleg (bottom) from Genesis 11.
