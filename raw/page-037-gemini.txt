ENGLISH:
...with every living soul that is with you, from birds to cattle, and every wild beast of the earth that is with you, of all that came out of the ark, for every living creature of the earth. And I will establish My covenant with you, so that all flesh shall no more die from the waters of a flood, and there shall no more be a flood to destroy the whole earth.
|||
The Lord God said to Noah: This is the sign of the covenant which I give to you, between Me and you, and between every living soul that is with you for eternal generations: I will set My bow in the clouds, and it shall be a sign of eternal life between Me and you and the earth. And it shall be, when I cover the earth with clouds, My bow shall appear in the cloud, and I will remember My covenant which is between Me and you and...

NOTE:
God appears in the clouds under a rainbow to establish His covenant with Noah and his family after the Flood (Genesis 9:10–15).
