ENGLISH:
And Terah took Abram his son, and Nahor, and Lot the son of Haran, and Sarah his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son; and he led them out of the land of the Chaldeans, to go into the land of Canaan.
|||
And they came even unto Haran, and dwelt there. And all the days of Terah in Haran were two hundred and five years, and he died.
|||

NOTE:
This page depicts Terah leading Abram, Nahor, Lot, and Sarah out of the land of the Chaldeans (top miniature), followed by their settlement in Haran and the death and burial of Terah (bottom miniature).
