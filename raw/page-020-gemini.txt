ENGLISH:
And Lamech said to his wives: Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; wives of Lamech, hearken unto my words: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. For sevenfold vengeance is taken for Cain, and for Lamech seventy times sevenfold.

NOTE:
The miniature illustrates the legend of Lamech shooting Cain in the reeds with a bow guided by a youth, striking the youth dead, and confessing the killings to his wives Adah and Zillah (Genesis 4:23–24).
