ENGLISH:
When I was completing the services of the sacrifices of my father Terah to his gods of wood and stone, and silver, and brass, and iron,
|||
Having entered the temple for the service, I found the god named Marumath, carved out of stone, fallen face down at the feet of the iron god Nakhin. And it came to pass, when I saw it, my heart was troubled; and I thought in my mind that I, Abram, could not return him to his place alone, because he was heavy, made of a great stone. So I went and told my father, and he came in with me. And the two of us scarcely moved him to return him to his place, and his head fell off from him while I was still holding his head. And it came to pass, when my father saw that the head of Marumath had fallen off from him, he said to me, "Abram!" And I said, "Here am I." And he said to me, "Bring me the small axes from the house." And I brought them to him, and he hewed...

NOTE:
The miniature illustrates the apocryphal story from the Apocalypse of Abraham, showing young Abram and his father Terah attempting to lift the fallen stone idol Marumath whose head breaks off, and Terah hewing a new stone statue with an axe.
