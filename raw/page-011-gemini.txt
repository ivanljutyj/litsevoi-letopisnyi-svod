ENGLISH:
...the second is Gihon; this encompasses the whole land of Ethiopia. And the third river is Tigris; this goes toward the Assyrians. And the fourth river is Euphrates. And the Lord God took the man whom He had formed, and brought him into the paradise of delight to work it and keep it. And the Lord God commanded Adam, saying: Of every tree that is in paradise you shall eat for food; but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, you shall not eat of it; for in whatever day you eat of it, you shall die the death. And the Lord God said: It is not good for the man to be alone; let us make for him a helper suitable to him. And God formed further out of the earth all the beasts of the field, and all the birds of heaven, and brought them to Adam to see what he would call them; and whatever Adam called a living soul, that was its name. And Adam gave names to all the cattle, and to all the birds of heaven, and to all the beasts of the earth.

NOTE:
Biblical narrative of Genesis 2 (rivers of Eden, God's command to Adam, naming the animals) above a miniature depicting the creation of Eve from sleeping Adam's side and God presenting Eve to Adam in Paradise.
