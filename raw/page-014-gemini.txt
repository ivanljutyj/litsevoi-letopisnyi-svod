ENGLISH:
[walk]ing in paradise, and I was afraid because I am naked, and I hid myself. And He said to him: Who told you that you are naked, unless you have eaten from the tree of which I commanded you not to eat of this one alone? And Adam said: The woman whom You gave to be with me, she gave me from the tree and I ate. And God said to the woman: What is this you have done? And the woman said: The serpent deceived me and I ate. And God said to the serpent: Because you have done this, cursed are you above all cattle, above all beasts of the earth; upon your breast and belly shall you go, and earth shall you eat all the days of your life. And I will put enmity between you and between the woman, and between your seed and between you: he shall watch your head, and you shall watch his heel. And to the woman He said: Multiplying I will multiply your sorrows and your sighings; in sorrows you shall bring forth your children, and your turning shall be toward your husband, and he shall rule over you. And to Adam He said: Because you heeded the voice of your wife, and ate from the tree of which I commanded you of this one alone not to eat, from it you have eaten: cursed is the earth in your works; in sorrows you shall eat of it all the days of your life. Both thorns and thistles shall it grow for you, and you shall eat the herb of the field. In the sweat of your face you shall eat your bread until you return into the earth from which you were taken; for earth you are, and into earth you shall go.

NOTE:
A text-only page arranged in a tapering funnel ending, containing the biblical account of Genesis 3:10–19 in which God questions Adam and Eve after the Fall and pronounces judgment upon the serpent, Eve, and Adam.
