ENGLISH:
And to Enos was born Gaidad [Irad]. And Gaidad begat Maleleil [Mehujael]. And Maleleil begat Methusael, and Methusael begat Lamech. And Lamech took unto himself two wives: the name of the one was Adah, the name of the second was Zillah.
And Adah bore Jovid [Jabal]; he was the father of those who dwell in tents and raise cattle. And his brother's name was Joval [Jubal]; he was the one who introduced the psaltery and the harp. And Zillah, she also bore Thovel [Tubal-Cain]; and he was a hammerer, a smith of copper and iron. And the sister of Thovel was [illegible].

NOTE:
Genealogy of Cain's descendants down to Lamech, his two wives Adah and Zillah, and their children practicing livestock herding, music, and metalworking (Genesis 4:18–22).
