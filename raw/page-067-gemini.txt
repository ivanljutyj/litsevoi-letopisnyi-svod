ENGLISH:
...another Marumath from another stone without a head; and the head that had broken off from Marumath, along with the rest of Marumath, he smashed. He made five other gods, and gave them to me and commanded me to sell them outside on the city road.
|||
And I saddled my father's donkey and loaded them upon it, and went out to the highway to sell them. And behold, merchants from Fandana of Syria were coming with camels, going into Egypt to buy kokonil from the Nile from there. And I questioned them and they told me; and while I was conversing with them, one of the camels grunted, and the donkey was startled and bolted and threw off the gods, and three of them were crushed, and two remained. And it came to pass when they saw these things, since they revered gods, they said to me, "Why did you not tell us that you had gods, so that we might..."

NOTE:
A miniature illustrating the apocryphal story of young Abraham receiving idols from his father Terah, traveling with a donkey to sell them, meeting Syrian merchants with camels, and witnessing the idols break.
