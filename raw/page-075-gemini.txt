And Terah took Abram his son, and Nahor, and Lot the son of Haran, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son; and he led them forth out of the land of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan.

And they came even unto Haran, and dwelt there. And all the days of Terah in Haran were 205 years, and he died there.

NOTE:
This page depicts Terah leading Abram, Sarai, Nahor, and Lot out of the land of the Chaldees toward Canaan, their settlement in Haran, and Terah's death and burial there (Genesis 11:31–32).
