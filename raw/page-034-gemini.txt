ENGLISH:
And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, on the first day of the first month, the water dried up from the face of the earth; and Noah uncovered the roof of the ark which he had made, and saw that the water had dried up from the face of the earth. And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry. The Lord God spoke to Noah, saying: Go out of the ark, you and your wife, and your sons, and your sons' wives with you. And every flesh, from cattle even to birds, and every creeping thing moving upon the earth with you; and be fruitful and multiply upon the earth. And Noah went out, and his wife, and his sons, and his sons' wives with him; and all the beasts, and all the cattle, and all the birds, and all...

NOTE:
The miniature depicts Noah looking out from the uncovered roof of the ark as the Flood waters recede, and disembarking onto dry land with his family and the animals at God's command (Genesis 8:13–19).
