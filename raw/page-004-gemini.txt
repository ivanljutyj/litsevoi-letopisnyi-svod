ENGLISH:
And to Enoch was born Irad; and Irad begat Mehujael; and Mehujael begat Methushael; and Methushael begat Lamech. And Lamech took unto himself two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah.
|||
[illegible]
|||
And Adah bore Jabal: he was the father of those who dwell in tents and keep cattle. And his brother's name was Jubal: he was the one who invented the psaltery and the harp. And Zillah, she also bore Tubal-cain, and he was a hammerer, a smith of copper and iron; and the sister of Tubal-cain was Naamah.

NOTE:
Genealogy of Cain's descendants through Lamech, depicting Lamech with his two wives Adah and Zillah, the births of their children, Jubal playing music, Jabal with livestock, and Tubal-cain forging metal (Genesis 4:18–22).
