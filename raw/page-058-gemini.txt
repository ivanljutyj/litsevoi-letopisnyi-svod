ENGLISH:
And Cainan lived 130 years and begat Sala. And after begetting Sala, Cainan lived 403 years and begat sons and daughters, and died.
|||
And Sala lived 134 years and begat Eber. And after begetting Eber, Sala lived 270 years, and died.

NOTE:
Two two-part miniatures illustrating the Genesis 11 genealogy, showing the biblical patriarchs Cainan and Sala (Shelah) with their families alongside scenes of their burials in stone sarcophagi.
