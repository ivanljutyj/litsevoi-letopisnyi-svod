ENGLISH:
And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year of Noah's life, on the first day of the first month, the water dried up from the face of the earth; and Noah removed the covering of the ark that he had made, and saw that the water had dried up from the face of the earth. And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry. And the Lord God spoke to Noah, saying: Go out of the ark, you and your wife, and your sons, and your sons' wives with you. And every living thing of all flesh, of cattle and of birds, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth with you, and breed and multiply on the earth. And Noah went out, and his wife, and his sons, and his sons' wives with him, and all the beasts, and all the cattle, and all the birds, and all...
