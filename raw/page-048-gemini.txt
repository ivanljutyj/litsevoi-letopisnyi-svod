And as he grew in years, he became greater and better in mind and understanding. And as he rested his mind in peace and reasoned deeply in his heart, he said thus to himself: "What power is this? This indeed is not the true God, but what my father makes." And he looked up to the height of heaven and saw the sky adorned with the sun, the moon, and the stars in their radiance. And he said in his spirit: "O woe is me! And woe that it is not permitted for the human mind to reach it personally!" And he reasoned again within himself: "Is it covered from me by a thin veil? Or did these clouds cover and hide its face, which goes to the sunrises, walking on another path? And seeing this..."

NOTE:
Young Abraham contemplates the heavens, realizes his father's idols are false, and seeks the true God.
