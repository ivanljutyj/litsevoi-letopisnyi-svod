ENGLISH:
And the heaven and the earth were finished, and all their adornment. And on the sixth day God finished His works which He had made, and God rested on the seventh day from all His works which He had made. And God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, because on it He rested from all His works which God had begun to make.

This is the book of the generation of heaven and earth, when it came to pass, in the day that God made heaven and earth, and every green plant of the field before it was upon the earth, and every herb of the field before it sprang up; for God had not rained upon the earth, and there was no man to till it; but a spring went up out of the earth, and watered the whole face

The page presents Genesis 2:1–6 with a miniature depicting God the Father in heaven above and Christ-Logos in Eden creating Adam, Eve, and the plants of Paradise.
