And Abram was in great sorrow and in extreme despondency, as he found no peace for his mind, and grieved greatly in his heart: "What then shall I do? Whom shall I call God? For these are not true gods which my father makes." Looking up to the height of heaven, he saw the sky adorned with the sun and moon and the shining of stars, and said in his heart: "Are these then gods, which the human mind is not permitted to attain?" And he pondered in his heart and said: "If then the sky is God, it would not be covered by clouds; how would clouds dare to cover and obscure the face of God? Moreover, mist with steaming vapors makes the sky unseen, and smoke rising from a multitude immediately [obscures] the sight..."

NOTE:
The miniature depicts young Abraham questioning his father Terah's idol-making (carving, feeding, and testing idols) and gazing up at the sun, moon, and stars in search of the true God.
