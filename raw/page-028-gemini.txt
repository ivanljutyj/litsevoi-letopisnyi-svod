ENGLISH:
And it came to pass, when men began to be many upon the earth, and daughters were born unto them, that the sons of God, seeing the daughters of men that they are fair, took unto themselves wives of all whom they chose. And the Lord God said: My spirit shall not abide in men forever, because they are flesh; yet their days shall be a hundred and twenty years. Now giants were on the earth in those days, and afterward also, when the sons of God went in unto the daughters of men, and they bore unto themselves mighty ones; and these were the giants, who from of old were men of renown.
