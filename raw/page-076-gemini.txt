ENGLISH:
And Abram, coming to himself, said: "I shall test my father's gods, whether they are able to help." And Abram took fire and set ablaze the temple where his father's idols stood. Seeing this, Haran, Abram's brother, being zealous for the idols, wanted to snatch the idols out; and Haran himself burned up there, and died before his father. For before this a son had not died before his father, but a father before his son; and from this time sons also began to die before their fathers.
|||
And Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves, the daughters of Haran, the sisters of Lot: the name of Abram's wife was Sarai, the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah. And Sarai was barren, bearing no children.

NOTE:
The page recounts Abram burning his father's idols, the death of Haran, and the marriages of Abram and Nahor, illustrated with a miniature of their wedding feast.
